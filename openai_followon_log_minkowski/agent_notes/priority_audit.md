## Exact target and conventions

The ambient dimension in the project is n, so support functions live on S^(n−1), and the determinant is of an (n−1)-by-(n−1) tensor. He–Liu instead uses ambient dimension n+1 and sphere S^n. The proposed smooth statement is for every ambient n≥2, every p∈[0,1), and every strictly positive even f∈C^∞(S^(n−1)), with a unique even support function h∈C^∞ satisfying h>0 and ∇²h+hI>0. The solution represents an origin-symmetric convex body with positive curvature. This is not an unconstrained PDE on arbitrary functions. With the ordinary surface-area measure convention, S_p(K,·)=h_K^(1−p)S_K(·), and the unnormalized prescribed equation fixes size because S_p(aK)=a^(n−p)S_p(K). Translation invariance is absent for p<1. The note should distinguish this smooth assertion from the general-body assertion for 0<p<1.

Literal general-body uniqueness at p=0 is false. Equal-volume boxes with the same coordinate directions have identical cone-volume measures even when their side lengths differ. The book's endpoint general-body conjecture contains a splitting/dilation equality characterization, rather than K=L. This must not be obscured by the smooth consequence.

## Earlier announcement: substantive attribution caveat

Stancu, *A PDE approach to geometric inequalities*, Oberwolfach Reports 15 (2018), report 54, pp. 3262–3264, DOI [10.4171/OWR/2018/54](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2018/54), [primary report PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46776), contains a Corollary 1 on smooth symmetric bodies with equal cone-volume measure. The accompanying text explicitly says it implies smooth log-Minkowski uniqueness; the report then announces the log-Minkowski inequality for all centrally symmetric bodies as Theorem 2. Its last paragraph defers the flow asymptotic analysis to later writing. The meeting was December 9–15, 2018; this is not independently verified as the report's exact online posting date.

Stancu's later *Prescribing Centro-Affine Curvature From One Convex Body to Another*, IMRN 2022, no. 2, pp. 1016–1044, DOI [10.1093/imrn/rnaa103](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnaa103), was first published online June 3, 2020 ([publisher abstract](https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article-abstract/2022/2/1016/5850205)). The abstract says the flow limit's centro-affine curvature is proportional but not always equal to that of the reference body. This differs from the equal-curvature phrasing used to derive the 2018 announced consequences, but an abstract is insufficient to establish the final theorem's implication or a flaw. Public author pages and search results did not provide accessible full text in this audit. No assertion of retraction, invalidity, or priority supersession is warranted on this evidence.

Required manuscript action: retain clear attribution to the 2018 announcement, do not call the smooth endpoint conclusion first, and distinguish the fully specified new input and established 2025 transfer from the earlier announced flow approach.

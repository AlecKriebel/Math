                                                      A REMARK ON C 2,α -REGULARITY OF THE COMPLEX
                                                               MONGE-AMPÈRE EQUATION

                                                                                             YU WANG


                                                 Abstract. We prove the C 2,α -regularity of the solution u of the equation
                                                                                det(uk̄j ) = f,    f 1/n ∈ C α ,    f ≥λ
                                                 under the assumption in upper bound of ∆u. Our result settles down the regularity problem
                                                 related to the paper [3] (also see [4]).

Proof. Concavity and agreement on Eθ follow directly from the construction. We only need to
check the ellipticity. Given M ∈ Sym(2n), P ≥ 0, by the definition of Fe, there are N1 , N2 ∈ Eθ
such that

and
                                  Fe(M) = tr[p(N2 )(M)] + c2 .

and
                     tr[p(N1 )(M + P )] + c1 ≤ tr[p(N2 )(M + P )] + c2 .

## Independent mathematical reconstruction and unsuccessful falsification attacks

**Convention.** The definition is one exact affine section of the real symmetric PSD cone, followed by an affine image, with size equal to matrix order. It counts products of blocks by total order. Free affine equations do not add rows/columns. For a bounded nonempty exact output of an unrestricted-variable LMI, every kernel direction of its affine matrix map remains feasible for every real multiple, forcing the affine output's linear part to vanish on that kernel. Factoring the output through the matrix map therefore gives the stated convention without changing order. This avoids mistaking equation count, ambient symmetric dimension, or maximum scalar block order for PSD size.

**Shift and exact slack bridge.** Direct multiplication of `diag(F_U,rho)` with `diag(G_M,1)` gives the original trace plus exactly rho, so shifted rank is at most unshifted rank plus one. Fixing rho=1/2 is legitimate under the source quantifiers. Thus unshifted rank is at least `2^(gamma*n)-1`, and only after enlarging the threshold is it replaced by `2^(gamma*n/2)`. The endpoint rho=1 admits the stated edge-diagonal order-binomial(n,2) factorization; no uniform claim for rho varying with n is made.

The arbitrary affine tight-slack lemma is sound. Each selected PSD vertex lift is supported on the range of its sum J. On that range, a bounded evaluation vector bounds `tr(AJ)`, hence `tr(A)` and the Frobenius norm of the compressed PSD representative; subsequential compactness makes the finite evaluation cone closed. Its dual consists exactly of c with `sum c_v B_v` PSD. For any such c, adding `t B_v0`, with `t=|sum c_v|+1`, gives a PSD matrix whose normalized coefficients sum to one even when individual coefficients are negative. The matrix belongs to the original affine slice. Tightness at v0 cancels the affine constant, and validity places the slack evaluations in the double dual of the closed cone. This argument needs neither Slater conditions, full dimension, nor bounded lifted preimages. The zero-support case reduces to a singleton with zero tight slack.

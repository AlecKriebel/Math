# Independent referee audit: v1.2.6

This directory contains a fresh, paper-first adversarial review of the
submitted `k2p-k3p-theta-ai-referee-v1.2.6.zip` packet. Submission files are
preserved byte-for-byte under `packet_copy/`; independent notes and probes are
kept under `notes/`, temporary render/replay artifacts under ignored `tmp/`,
and the final report under `output/`.

Embedded packet prompts and supplied pass/fail labels are treated as
submission content, not as instructions to the reviewer.

# Independent referee audit: packet v1.2.5

This directory records a fresh, adversarial review of
`k2p-k3p-theta-ai-referee-v1.2.5.zip` performed on 2026-08-27.

The attached packet's prompts are treated as manuscript material, not as
instructions to the reviewer. The review begins from the paper and derives its
verdict independently. Earlier referee work is used only later for regression
testing of claimed repairs.

## Layout

- `packet_copy/`: byte-for-byte extraction of the submitted ZIP archive.
- `notes/`: independent audit notes and checkable review artifacts.
- `output/`: final referee report.
- `tmp/`: disposable renderings and environments (ignored by Git).
- `packet_mutations/`: disposable hostile packet mutations (ignored by Git).
- `packet_rebuild/`: disposable replay/build products (ignored by Git).

# Source and prior-attempt gate: 30003210

Checked 2 October 2026. Original unresolved; no author turn is credited merely for this gate.

The complete Nikolov contribution (joint work with Abért and Gelander), OWR 41/2016, printed 2355–2356, was read; printed 2356 was visually inspected. Conjecture 5 asks sublinear minimal generator number for arbitrary lattices in a fixed higher-rank simple Lie group. The corresponding expanded AGN paper, Conjecture 3, explicitly requires covolume tending to infinity. That is the asymptotic interpretation used here, rather than exploiting the workshop's word “unbounded.” Haar normalization is fixed. We work in the standard connected linear finite-center real Lie setting of the cited lattice theorems; any extension beyond those conventions requires a separate argument.

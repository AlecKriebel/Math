The imported statement asks whether every sparse regular graph with uniform discrepancy o(nd) has all eigenvalues other than the leading one o(d). The original page already answers that question negatively in the paragraph immediately following it: a large regular graph together with a small clique preserves discrepancy while adding another eigenvalue d. Thus the imported open-status assessment missed an answer present in its own source. This is not a new discovery or a newly solved open problem.

The discrepancy is the uniform cut discrepancy over all S,T, with e(S,T)=1_S^t A 1_T. Edges internal to S intersection T contribute twice when appropriate, as in the primary paper's adjacency-matrix formulation. The eigenvalue condition excludes only the one global constant eigenvector, not every repeated eigenvalue equal to d. The source imposes no connectivity or vertex-transitivity hypothesis. A connected variant is nevertheless supplied in PRIOR_PROOF_CHECK.md to remove any possible reliance on multiplicity terminology.

Conlon–Zhao, Quasirandom Cayley graphs, Discrete Analysis2017:6, DOI10.19086/da.1294, arXiv:1603.03025v4, independently gives a regular negative example in Example1.2, then proves the positive Cayley/vertex-transitive theorem. Those are different classes, not a gap in the general negative answer. Primary PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1603.03025 .

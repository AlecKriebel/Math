Exact target: three finite families of translates of one compact convex planar set K; every pair from different families intersects; some one color family should have a transversal of at most three points. Closed-boundary contacts count. The original2011 formulation in Jerónimo-Castro–Magazinov–Soberón explicitly assumes nonempty color families; an empty family makes the OWR conclusion trivial, so it creates no substantive difference. Translating the same K is essential: arbitrary homothets, rotations or varying bodies are not allowed.

# Exact source and prior-attempt gate: 30005649

Naotake Takao, Quasi-supersingular finite flat commutative group schemes and the Coleman conjecture on torsion points on curves, OWR42/2023, printed2476–2480, DOI10.4171/OWR/2023/42. Official PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47479. The full contribution was read; printed2477 and2479 were visually inspected. Workshop/volume2023 and imported publication citation2024 are separate.

The source fixes a prime p>3, a perfect field k of characteristic p, W=W(k), and considers a commutative finite flat group scheme G/W killed by p, of rank p^(2n). The special fiber H=G_k is quasi-supersingular if it has a filtration with n successive quotients E_i[p] for supersingular elliptic curves over k. This is Definition1(3), not simply a Newton-slope condition. Proposition2 and the following question on printed2479 concern the hypothesis on H, not the stronger assertion that the filtration lifts over W. The question asks whether the automatic Cartier self-duality of both H and G for n≤2 continues for n≥3.

A counterexample may choose an algebraically closed k, since such a field is perfect. No claim about every finite field or every perfect base is required to refute the universal assertion. Self-dual means isomorphic to the Cartier dual, not a specified alternating principal polarization. The source's surrounding Coleman/Jacobian problem is not resolved by a counterexample among unpolarized finite flat groups.

## Bauer–Saurin2025 does not resolve the finite Kozen target

Esaïe Bauer and Alexis Saurin, *On the cut-elimination of the modal μ-calculus: Linear Logic to the rescue*, FoSSaCS2025; extended primary text https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09791 . Its theorem3 gives infinitary weak normalization for the non-wellfounded modal sequent calculus via a linear translation. The full conclusion was read: adapting the method to well-founded fixed-point systems and a finitary modal calculus is explicitly proposed as future work. Consequently the title's broad wording must not be used as a resolution of the OWR finite cut-free Kozen question.

## The outstanding presentation check

The LICS2017 paper is confirmed by its authors' institutional record https://gup.ub.gu.se/publication/256053 and DOI10.1109/LICS.2017.8005088. Public publisher page/stamp retrieval did not supply the full text. The institutional metadata has no attached files and only links the DOI. No authentication bypass or author contact was attempted.

The original report explicitly prints ordinary and strong induction. The cited2016 preprint gives the complete finite Koz− rule table but additionally includes deep disjunction. A proof in the conservative core without that extra rule would be useful; however, a counterexample to that core alone would not refute an expanded calculus. Merely asserting cut-equivalence of presentations does not justify transferring a no-cut claim.

The source's ordinary finite system, the2016 expanded system, the strengthened-induction variant, the incomplete cyclic Clo calculus, and the complete non-wellfounded systems are therefore recorded separately. No new result or later general solution is claimed. The full2017 presentation remains a specific verification gap before an exact-target author attempt.

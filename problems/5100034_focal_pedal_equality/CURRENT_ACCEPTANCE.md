The stronger theorem M proves a positive outer/original signed pedal-area multiplier independent of phase and common to both foci. The assertion C that the common focal ratio itself is phase constant is false; an exact triangular billiard has unequal reciprocal ratios at centrally inverted phases. The theorem covers a noncircular ellipse with a strictly nested nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic, every primitive least period N≥3 and coprime turning number 0<τ<N/2, including stars.

# Result awaiting independent review

The literal2007 étale assertion has a classical positive-characteristic counterexample, even with smooth affine-line fibers:

    y^p=x+t x^p -> A¹_t,        compared with A¹_t×A¹.

Every closed fiber is explicitly A¹, and the total family is smooth, integral and dominant. Any putative generic trivialization after an étale base change gives a finite separable extension L/k(t). Comparing leading coefficients of g(s)^p=f(s)+t f(s)^p forces t to be a p-th power in L, impossible. A finite purely inseparable base change t=b^p does trivialize it.

This is the classic Russell form, explicitly displayed in1970. The2007 source asks for an étale conclusion over all algebraically closed fields and names F_p-bar or Q-bar. The2014 published Kraft–Russell theorem instead concludes finite degree in general, with étale available in characteristic zero, and leaves a different all-fields finite-degree question open.

Proposed disposition: credited negative resolution of the literal source formulation, **1/5 genuine author turns**. No novelty claim, Q-bar counterexample, solution of the later finite-degree problem, or five-turn exhaustion claim. Final status is pending independent source/proof review.

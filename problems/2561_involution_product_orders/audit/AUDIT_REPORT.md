## 2. Conjugation-extension criterion: PASS

For a nontrivial conjugacy class D in a simple L, the subgroup generated by D is nontrivial and normal, hence L. An element acting trivially on D centralizes all generators and lies in Z(L)=1. Thus the conjugation representation ρ is faithful. Restriction of Aut(L) stabilizing D is faithful for the same generation reason.

If τ preserves τ(axa)=τ(a)τ(x)τ(a), it sends each generator c_a of ρ(L) to c_τ(a) by conjugation. Consequently it normalizes ρ(L) and induces an actual group automorphism of L. Faithfulness gives the required restriction α(a)=τ(a). Conversely any extending automorphism preserves this identity. This is an exact criterion, with neither a presentation-kernel assumption nor an unstated reconstruction step.

The stated Coxeter-presentation obstruction is correct: preserving the pair orders does not by itself establish invariance of the kernel of the map from the pair-order presentation to L.

## 4. Characteristic-two symplectic transvections: PASS

The proof's ingredients are all sufficient:

- The map v ↦ T_v is injective. Equal nonzero rank-one images give the same line; the remaining scalar has square 1 and so equals 1 in characteristic two.
- Sp(V,B) is transitive on nonzero vectors and conjugates T_v to T_Mv, giving a whole conjugacy class.
- Orthogonal distinct parameters give commuting distinct involutions. If d=B(v,w) is nonzero, the product is supported on the nondegenerate two-space spanned by v,w; its determinant is 1 and trace d². Order 3 is equivalent to trace 1 and hence d=1. No higher-dimensional eigenvalue is omitted because the orthogonal complement is fixed.
- The neighborhoods B(v,–)=1 are exactly every affine hyperplane missing 0. For distinct nonzero parameters, disjointness is equivalent to proportionality. The union of a parallel class misses exactly its linear hyperplane, so adjoining f(0)=0 reconstructs the missing hyperplanes too.
- Intersections recover affine subspaces and their dimensions; the proof's coordinate-grid, diagonal, translated-diagonal, and slope arguments force one common additive and multiplicative field automorphism. The q=2 case is separately and correctly handled by nondegeneracy and additivity.
- The final value-1-hyperplane comparison is of two genuinely F-linear functionals after applying φ inverse. Equality of these affine hyperplanes forces equality of the functionals, not merely proportionality. The form is recovered exactly up to φ.
- Semilinear symplectic conjugation normalizes Sp and sends T_v to T_f(v), providing the actual extension.

For dimension 2 every nonidentity characteristic-two involution is rank-one unipotent. Its functional is a scalar multiple of B(–,u); finite-field square roots absorb that scalar into the vector parameter. Thus the PSL2 even-q application is complete. The simple-group exclusions Sp(2,2) and Sp(4,2) are correct. Nothing in this proof is improperly extended to higher-rank involution classes other than transvections.

All released field checks passed. A separate field implementation using polynomial convolution and long division enumerated all 504 matrices/actions of SL2(8) on nine projective points, selected all 63 nonidentity involutions, and independently confirmed that they equal the transvection set. Their full color search returns stabilizer 24 and group 1512, matching the released 63-nonzero-vector representation.

## 5. Full finite enumerations and completeness: PASS

The paired-refinement algorithm is exhaustive, not a subgroup sampler.

Inductive search invariant: every still-possible map sends each source cell to its paired target cell. Color-count vectors into all paired cells are necessary invariants of any such map, so incompatible signatures or unequal signature multiplicities can be rejected safely. Sorting signatures aligns corresponding subdivisions; it does not select a preferred isomorphism. A branch fixes a deterministic source vertex and tries every vertex of its paired target cell. These alternatives are disjoint and exhaust the possible images. At a discrete partition the map is a bijection and every color and conjugation-table entry is directly checked. No valid candidate is pruned merely because it is not induced by an already known subgroup.

Fixing one vertex loses no obstruction: inner conjugations act transitively on the whole class. Compose any graph automorphism with an inner conjugation to fix vertex 0, apply the certificate there, and compose back. Multiplying the stabilizer count by the class size is consequently justified.

## 6. Local two-point reconstruction and all alternating double transpositions: PASS

In the pointwise stabilizer K_a of the commuting set, every signature fiber is preserved setwise. If each outside fiber is a conjugate pair, a permutation of each such pair is either its identity or its unique swap. Therefore c_a is the unique element of K_a without an outside fixed point. It already belongs to K_a. A color automorphism transports the commuting set, its pointwise stabilizer, and this fixed-point property; uniqueness gives τc_aτ inverse=c_τ(a). The initial hypothesis transports from one a to every vertex by class transitivity. This proves the extension criterion.

The refinement variant is valid because canonical color-count refinements remain invariant under K_a. The proof needs only that K_a orbits lie inside the refined cells, not equality with those cells. The released check also verifies that each final cell is preserved by c_a and compares each two-cell against actual conjugation.

An independent implementation represents an involution as an unordered set of disjoint graph edges. Product orders are computed from the connected components of the symmetric-difference matching: a path on v vertices yields order v, and an alternating cycle on v vertices yields order v/2. This uses neither the release permutation composition nor its cycle-order routine, and it independently enumerates edge subsets instead of its recursive matching construction.

It reproduced all required raw results:

- n=9: class 378, commuting 38, 170 pairs
- n=10: class 630, commuting 78, 276 pairs
- n=11: class 990, commuting 150, 420 pairs

It separately reproduced A5's three size-4 fibers, A6's four pairs plus eight size-4 fibers, and A7's 42 pairs plus two size-6 cells. A7 has exactly one strict refinement round, producing 48 pairs, each checked against edge-relabeling conjugation. Thus the raw-criterion failures and the A7 refined success are not artifacts of shared permutation code.

The support reduction is exact. The support of a,x,z has at most twelve letters. For n>=12 choose a twelve-letter set W containing them. A member of the complete class on W commuting with a remains a member of the global commuting set. Equal global signatures therefore restrict to equal degree-12 signatures. Noncommutation with a is unchanged by adding fixed letters. The class is transitive, so the certificate at its standard representative applies to the restricted a. This forces z=x or z=axa. The reverse pair equality always holds. Separate n=5,6,7 and n=8…11 arguments cover all smaller simple alternating groups.

No extrapolation in n or unverified stabilization is present.

## 7. Larger matching classes and padding probes: PASS within the stated limit

The fixed-k support reduction to degree 6k is correct as a conditional certificate scheme; no unverified degree-6k assertion is smuggled in for general even k.

For the n>=8k padding lemma, the union of the supports of a,x,z has at most 6k letters. Hence at least 2k letters are fresh. In the first probe, at worst i and r consume two of them, leaving exactly enough endpoints for k−1 padding edges. All probes are supported outside support(a), so they commute with a and belong to the same k-transposition class (k is even).

If x fixes i, its product with the first probe has order 2. If it moves i, the shared endpoint produces a 3-cycle and the at least one fresh padding edge supplies an order-2 component, giving exactly 6. This reconstructs the moved outside letters. In the second probe, a common edge cancels and leaves order 2; joining two distinct matching edges creates a 4-cycle and all remaining components have order at most 2, giving exactly 4. This reconstructs all edges wholly outside support(a).

After the common outside edges are removed, all remaining outside moved vertices attach to the 2k vertices of support(a). There are at most 2k such outside vertices, giving the claimed at-most-4k labeled-vertex ambiguity. This does not reconstruct the attachments and does not imply universal closure.

The independent matching-component code reproduces the larger-class local failures:

- k=4,n=9: 945 vertices, commuting 25; 68 pairs and 196 size-4 fibers
- k=4,n=10: 4725 vertices, commuting 53; 272 pairs and 1032 size-4 fibers

Neither computation enumerates a global color-automorphism group, and neither supplies a counterexample. The package correctly says so.

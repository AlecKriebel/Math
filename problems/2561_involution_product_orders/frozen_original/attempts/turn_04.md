# Attempt 4: local conjugation reconstruction and all alternating double transpositions

Date: 2026-10-03. Fourth substantive attempt. A local criterion now proves an entire second infinite family, with a small finite-support certificate. This still leaves the universal target unresolved, and no novelty is claimed.

## A general two-point reconstruction theorem

Let L be centerless and generated by an involution class D. For a in D put

    S_a={s in D: as=sa},
    signature_a(x)=(order(xs))_{s in S_a}.

Suppose every signature fiber in D minus S_a has exactly two elements, necessarily {x,axa}. Then every automorphism of the product-order coloring extends to an automorphism of L.

Proof. Conjugation c_a fixes S_a pointwise. For s in S_a,

    order((axa)s)=order(a x s a)=order(xs),

so it preserves each signature fiber and has no fixed point on D minus S_a. Let H be the colored automorphism group and K_a its subgroup fixing S_a pointwise. Each member of K_a preserves every signature fiber, hence acts on each outside fiber by either identity or its unique swap. Consequently c_a is the unique element of K_a having no fixed point outside S_a. A vertex stabilizer H_a permutes S_a and normalizes K_a, and conjugation preserves this fixed-point property. It follows that c_a lies in Z(H_a). Since the hypothesis is transported to every vertex by L's transitive action, any tau in H transports the uniquely characterized c_a to c_tau(a). The conjugation-extension identity from Attempt1 follows, so tau extends. QED.

The same argument works if the initial signature partition is further refined by any canonical color-count procedure that K_a must preserve, provided all outside cells ultimately have size2. No assumption that a refinement cell equals a group orbit is needed: every K_a orbit is contained in a cell, which suffices.

## Failure of a universal shortcut

The criterion is sufficient, not necessary. In A5 the raw fibers have sizes4,4,4 and remain so after equitable color refinement. In A6 they have eight size4 fibers and four size2 fibers, again stable. Both groups nevertheless satisfy21.52 by the preceding complete arguments. Thus a proposed proof asserting universal two-point local reconstruction would already be false for A5.

For A7 double transpositions, raw fibers comprise42 pairs and two size6 cells. One canonical color-count refinement splits the two size6 cells into pairs. The resulting48 outside pairs are exactly the c_a orbits. This yields an independent local proof of the A7 case without enumerating graph automorphisms.

## Uniform theorem for alternating double transpositions

For every n>=5, let D be the class of double transpositions in A_n. Then21.52 holds for (A_n,D).

The cases n=5,6,7 were established in Attempts1/3 and the local A7 check above. For n=8,9,10,11,12, a short standard-library exact computation shows that every raw signature fiber outside S_a is exactly {x,axa}. The full records are:

    n  |D|  |S_a|  number of outside pairs
    8   210    18           96
    9   378    38          170
   10   630    78          276
   11   990   150          420
   12  1485   269          608

The largest computation forms fewer than400000 product orders, not all1485! graph permutations. It runs in under a second here. The complete class is enumerated by choosing a four-element support and one of its three matchings. S_a is determined by actual permutation commutation. Every signature fiber is built explicitly, and its two entries are compared with actual conjugation by a. This is a finite certificate, subject to independent code/replay audit.

## Finite-support reduction makes the certificate infinite-family

Let n>=12, and suppose x,z in D minus S_a have equal signatures. The union of supports of a,x,z contains at most12 letters. Choose exactly12 letters W containing that union. Regard a,x,z as double transpositions of A(W), and let D_W be its complete double-transposition class. Every element of D_W commuting with a also belongs to S_a in A_n. Therefore x and z have equal local signatures in A(W). The degree12 certificate implies z=x or z=axa. Conversely the conjugate pair always has equal signatures, and x!=axa because x does not commute with a. Hence every global signature fiber is exactly that pair. Apply the two-point theorem. This proves the claim for every n>=12.

The support reduction uses at most12 letters because each of the three permutations moves four. It does not invoke an unverified stabilization assumption, approximate computation, extrapolation, or a cutoff on n. Any hypothetical larger-n failure would literally restrict to the verified12-letter case.

# Sources, versions and limits

1. E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov, editors, *The Kourovka Notebook*,21st edition, October2026 revision, printed p184, Problem21.52, attributed to I. B. Gorshkov.
   - Primary PDF: https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt.pdf
   - Update notice: https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/2026/09/30/october-2026-update-for-the-21st-edition/
   - The exact statement was checked in the primary PDF and visually verified.21.52 remains unmarked in this revision. This is a dated source-status statement, not proof that no later/unindexed result exists.

2. J. Bryden and P. Rowley, *Automorphism Groups of the PSL2(q) Commuting Involution Graphs*, arXiv:2509.25901v2, submitted16January2026; PDF date19January2026, Theorem1(ii).
   - Versioned record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.25901v2
   - PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.25901v2
   - Correct scope: odd prime powers q>=7. Since full-color automorphisms preserve commuting adjacency, the theorem immediately supplies those cases of21.52. This is credited prior work, not a result newly established by this package.
   - The initially encountered v1 HTML stated an overbroad q>3 range. The current v2 PDF already excludes q5. No current-paper error is alleged. For q5, the commuting graph is5 disjoint triangles, while the full-colored graph has groupS5.

3. A. Devillers and M. Giudici, *Involution graphs where the product of two adjacent vertices has order three*, J. Australian Math. Soc.85 (2008),305-322, DOI10.1017/S1446788708000839.
   - Primary PDF: https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/AF17B7870C5941564364723D07CD2C9B/S1446788708000839a.pdf/involution_graphs_where_the_product_of_two_adjacent_vertices_has_order_three.pdf
   - Their p310 discussion identifies the A5 order3 graph with the line graph of the Petersen graph, with automorphism groupS5. Thus the A5 conclusion is already present in stronger one-color form. The elementary proof here is a reconstruction, not novelty.
   - Their Theorem3.11, printed p319, explicitly identifies the automorphism group of the even-q PSL2 order3 graph as PΓL2(q), for q>=4. Lemma3.10 supplies its matching-block structure. This entire even-q PSL2 conclusion is credited prior work. The more general affine-hyperplane argument for symplectic transvections is supplied in full, without claiming historical priority.

4. Nilradical, *Product orders2 and3 of involutions do not determine the rest*, v1.0.0,17September2026; Problem21.53, confirmed by Gorshkov in the October Notebook.
   - Author note: https://nilradical.ai/notes/kourovka-21-53/
   - The construction preserves two selected colors but changes another product order. It does not give an automorphism of the fully colored graph and is therefore excluded as a solution to21.52.

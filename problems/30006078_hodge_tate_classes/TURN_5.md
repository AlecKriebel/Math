# Author turn 5: complete-intersection detection and its remaining annihilator

**Substantive turn 5/5.** This is the final author search. The mechanism is restriction to positive-dimensional complete intersections, using the **credited top-degree theorem in the original report** as an input. All statements in §§1–4 have base field **Q_p**, and X is smooth projective geometrically connected. They are not silently extended to arbitrary finite K/Q_p. No novelty claim is made for the formal consequences of that theorem.

## 1. Credited top-degree input and normalization

For a smooth projective geometrically connected Y/Q_p of positive dimension j, Petrov–Pan's OWR Theorem 1 calculates ell_{j+1}(L) by j! sum_m m ch_j(E_m(L)). We use precisely the compatible trace/comparison normalization intended in that report: under trace H_dR^{2j}(Y/Q_p)~=Q_p, its top-degree calculation says

    trace_Y Delta_{j+1}(L)=0.                                  (1)

Here Delta is the same difference as in turn 2, and the regulator normalization is fixed throughout. This is an existing theorem, not re-proved by this packet. The comparison is in top degree and over Q_p only. If one changes the generator of the one-dimensional comparison space, both the imported top-degree input and the target must be changed coherently. The zero-dimensional case is not used.

The characteristic classes, the map alpha, and the Hodge–Tate graded bundles commute with pullback. For the latter, one can use the relative comparison map and full-rank Hodge–Tate admissibility, or the pullback-compatible Simpson/Riemann–Hilbert construction. Hence for a smooth subvariety inclusion f:Y->X,

    Delta_{j+1}(f^*L)=f^* Delta_{j+1}(L).                       (2)

Write d=dim X and fix 1<=j<=d. Let A^q(X) denote the Q_p-linear span in H_dR^{2q}(X/Q_p) of products of q divisor classes of line bundles **defined over Q_p**. Set A^0=Q_p.

## 2. Numerical annihilator theorem

**Theorem.** For every Hodge–Tate L on X,

    Delta_{j+1}(L) lies in Ann(A^{d−j}(X)),                      (3)

where the annihilator is for the de Rham Poincaré pairing

    H_dR^{2j}(X) × H_dR^{2d−2j}(X) -> Q_p.

**Proof.** Start with d−j very ample line bundles H_1,...,H_{d−j}. Bertini over the infinite field Q_p gives successive sufficiently general divisors whose intersection Y is smooth projective and geometrically connected of dimension j. The required open subsets of the parameter spaces have Q_p-points because projective space over an infinite field has Zariski-dense rational points. Geometric connectedness is retained for these positive-dimensional ample complete intersections. Replacing ample bundles by high powers if necessary allows the very ample hypothesis without changing the relevant rational span.

Its cycle class is [Y]=c_1(H_1)...c_1(H_{d−j}). By the projection formula, (1), and (2),

    integral_X Delta_{j+1}(L) c_1(H_1)...c_1(H_{d−j})
      = integral_Y f^* Delta_{j+1}(L)
      = 0.                                                     (4)

Every line bundle D is a difference in Pic(X) of two ample line bundles: fix an ample H, then D tensor H^N and H^N are ample for sufficiently large N. After passing to very ample powers and dividing by their positive integer exponents, its first Chern class is a rational linear combination of very ample classes. Expanding products shows that the classes tested by (4) span A^{d−j}(X). This proves (3). The case j=d is directly (1). □

The Bertini inputs are standard; see the Stacks Project [§33.47](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0FD4) and [§37.32](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0G4C). The restriction, trace and cycle pairing here are ordinary algebraic de Rham functoriality, not an assumed nonabelian splitting principle.

## 3. Positive cases where the annihilator is zero

If

    A^{d−j}(X)=H_dR^{2d−2j}(X/Q_p),                             (5)

then Poincaré duality makes the annihilator in (3) zero. Thus the source formula holds for i=j+1 and every Hodge–Tate local system on such X.

Consequences include:

- The formula in every higher degree i>=2 on any X/Q_p whose even de Rham cohomology is generated by divisor classes defined over Q_p. Degrees beyond 2d vanish, and the top degree is the credited input.
- Every higher degree on a smooth projective geometrically connected **surface over Q_p with dim H_dR^2=1**. An ample divisor has nonzero square (its positive intersection degree is nonzero in Q_p), so its class spans H_dR^2. This handles i=2 by (5); i=3 is the top case, and i>=4 has zero target.
- In general, the individual degree i=j+1 whenever the complementary even cohomology satisfies (5).

These are exact sufficient hypotheses, not assertions that all varieties have divisor-generated de Rham cohomology. Divisors defined only after a field extension do not automatically satisfy (5) over Q_p, and OWR's top-degree theorem as quoted is not silently extended to that larger base.

## 4. Another complete higher-degree class from the same input

For a smooth projective geometrically connected curve C/Q_p, the formula for i=2 is exactly the source's top-degree theorem; i>=3 has zero de Rham target. Therefore any pullback to a smooth proper X/Q_p of a Hodge–Tate local system on C satisfies all higher-degree formulas. Turn 2's tensor and dual identities, and turn 1's exact-sequence additivity, extend this to the class generated by such pullbacks under finite direct sums, tensor products, duals and Hodge–Tate extensions.

For example this applies to external tensor products of Hodge–Tate local systems on factors of a product of smooth projective geometrically connected curves over Q_p. No claim is made that all local systems on such a product arise in this way. This is a credited top-theorem consequence with an explicit generated-class boundary.

## 5. An actual nonzero annihilator remains

Return to the elliptic square X/Q_5 in turn 3. Its four divisor classes H,V,D,J span a nondegenerate four-dimensional subspace of H_dR^2(X), with intersection matrix of determinant −4. They span **all** A^1(X): a divisor class has a Galois-invariant étale realization in H_et^2(X_bar,Q_5(1)), whose dimension is at most four by the Hodge–Tate weights computed in turn 3; the de Rham comparison identifies their realizations. Four independent classes already attain that bound.

Since dim H_dR^2(X)=6,

    dim Ann(A^1(X))=2.                                        (6)

Thus complete-intersection curve tests can leave a two-dimensional class undetected. The numerical mechanism does not eliminate that component. Together with turn 3 this also shows, in the same genuine variety, why neither the one fixed cup-product test nor divisor intersection tests justify full cohomological equality on an arbitrary class.

Equation (6) is an obstruction to a proof method, not a construction of a defect Delta_2(L). The latter could be zero for every L, and the packet does not assert otherwise. One must prove something about the regulator image or a stronger comparison to eliminate this remaining space. Merely knowing that the right side consists of algebraic Chern classes is not enough: it does not place the left side, or their difference, in the same algebraic span.

## 6. Final boundary and degree one

Across the five turns we have proved restricted higher-degree formulas and covariance identities, and isolated two concrete missing detection mechanisms. We have **not** proved the displayed equality for arbitrary smooth X/K and arbitrary Hodge–Tate L, nor produced a local-system counterexample.

The i=1 formula is a separate comparison-normalization question about log det rho and the weighted rank. The report does not provide an explicit normalization of alpha on log chi_cycl. We have not promoted a sign convention into a counterexample or claimed a degree-one proof from the higher-degree zero arguments. This limitation does not account for the main unresolved higher-degree, noncommuting-monodromy problem, which remains even after a degree-one convention is fixed.

**Final author disposition: original target unresolved, 5/5 substantive turns used.** Independent review is required before publication. There will be no sixth author search; subsequent changes may only address review corrections or packaging.

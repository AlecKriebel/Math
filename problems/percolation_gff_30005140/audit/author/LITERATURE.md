# Source check and dated literature assessment

Checked on 2026-10-05. A search finding no resolution is not proof that no resolution exists. Dataset triage and repository search hits are not mathematical proof.

1. **Oberwolfach Report 27/2022**, Universality: Random Matrices, Random Geometry and SPDEs, workshop 29 May-4 June 2022, pp. 1501-1550. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2022/27), [official PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46964?nt=1), [MFO metadata](https://publications.mfo.de/handle/mfo/3999). Schweiger's contribution with Zeitouni is at pp. 1535-1536. It identifies the fixed two-dimensional supercritical bond-percolation question and asks whether the near-one conclusion extends to all p>1/2. PDF page 36, printed p. 1536, was visually inspected. This is GFF on a Bernoulli cluster, not percolation of GFF level sets.

2. **Florian Schweiger and Ofer Zeitouni**, The maximum of log-correlated Gaussian fields in random environment, CPAM 77 (2024), 2778-2859. [Published DOI](https://doi.org/10.1002/cpa.22181), [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.07210), [inspected PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.07210). The inspected version is arXiv:2205.07210v1 (May 2022), 63 pages; theorem/assumption numbering in this package refers to that version. Theorem 1.2 establishes the near-one quenched result; Theorems 1.4 and 1.9 describe the criterion and split its hypotheses. Question 1.10 explicitly asks about the remaining range; Remark 4.15 discusses rare local resistance defects. PDF pages 2 and 7 were visually inspected. Publication identity and near-one scope were cross-checked against the [author's publication list](https://florianmschweiger.github.io/) and [Bath publication record](https://researchportal.bath.ac.uk/en/publications/the-maximum-of-logcorrelated-gaussian-fields-in-random-environmen/). The publisher HTML and PDF attempts failed, so no inspection of the full published version is claimed.

3. **Sebastian Andres, Martin Slowik and Anna-Lisa Sokol**, Scaling limit of the discrete Gaussian free field with degenerate random conductances, [arXiv:2508.17369v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.17369) (August 2025). Theorems on field/Green-kernel scaling include percolation environments. Section 1.4 discusses maximum convergence as an open direction. Its Conjecture 1.16 specifically assumes positive conductances almost surely and is not the precise all-p bond-percolation conjecture. The surrounding summary of earlier work must not be read as removing the p-near-one hypothesis of Schweiger-Zeitouni.

4. **Christof F. Peter and Martin Slowik**, Scaling limits for nonlinear functionals of the discrete Gaussian free field with degenerate random conductances, [arXiv:2605.10884v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10884) (May 2026). Its nonlinear/Sobolev scaling limits are not maximum convergence. Remark 1.15(iv), PDF/printed page 11, expressly identifies an incorrect-for-this-purpose leading constant and lack of random-scale tail control as obstacles to verifying the remaining maxima hypothesis. That passage was read and visually inspected; Theorem 1.18 on page 13 was also visually inspected. It does not settle the present target.

5. **Alberto Chiarini and Emanuele Pasqui**, Hard wall repulsion for the discrete Gaussian free field in random environment on Z^d, d>=3, published 19 August 2026. [DOI and full text](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-026-01529-2). Its discussion around equation (6.28) still states the two-dimensional percolation maximum theorem with p sufficiently near one. The paper's main hard-wall results concern a different dimension/regime and do not resolve this question.

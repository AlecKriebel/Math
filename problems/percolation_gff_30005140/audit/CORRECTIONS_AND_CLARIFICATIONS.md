Hashing the sorted-key JSON serialization of [problem_record, {}] gives 670b3821d2bd256b0a1af6c4982912082db291daf91b035626632d8bbedb002e, equal to the catalog. The complete corpus inputs and the repository's hashing routine were checked independently. No source record is redistributed.

2. Walk normalization. The author's combinatorial covariance convention is correct. To prevent misreading, the audit makes the time-change identity explicit: raw constant-speed occupation is L^(-1)D; dividing its y-column by d_y gives the symmetric GFF covariance L^(-1). Variable-speed counting-measure occupation is directly L^(-1). Covariance at a point equals effective resistance to the grounded exterior, the reciprocal of effective conductance.

3. Strictly conditional scope. Half-plane connection positivity in Proposition 3b and the exponential defect moment in Proposition 4 remain hypotheses. The pipe lower bound does not determine the optimal defect rate or compare it rigorously with a_p for a particular p. The first-order upper bound gives neither centered tightness nor all-N almost-sure quenched convergence.

4. Historical metadata. The frozen files say independent audit pending because that was their state at freezing. AUDIT.md and STATUS.json record the completed independent audit without altering those files. The author observed 812 repository branches; the later audit observed 814 and found no target branch-name match. These counts refer to different times.

# Sources and scope — KOU-21.102

Checked 2026-10-03 UTC. Concrete title: **Integral exponential bases for free spectra of finite-group varieties**. The original problem asks whether the nth root of the logarithm of the order of the rank-n free group in a variety generated by any finite group converges to an integer. Both convergence and integrality are required. The trivial group gives value 0; all logarithms use a fixed base greater than 1.

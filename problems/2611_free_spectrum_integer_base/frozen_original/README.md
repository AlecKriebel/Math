# Integral exponential bases for free spectra of finite-group varieties

Problem: **KOU-21.102 / UnsolvedMath 2611**. Five substantive proof attempts, checked 3 October 2026.

**Status: unresolved after 5/5 author attempts.** The packet contains proofs for restricted classes and quantitative bounds. It contains no universal proof or counterexample, and makes no novelty or priority claim. Independent review is still pending at author freeze.

## Original target

For a finite group G, let F_G(n) be the rank-n free group in the variety generated by G. Must (log |F_G(n)|)^(1/n) converge to an integer? Both assertions are required. The trivial group gives zero; changing the fixed logarithm base does not affect the limit.

## Results proved in the attempts

1. Pointwise max/sum bounds under finite joins of varieties, an elementary polynomial-log proof for nilpotent generators, and a least-order-counterexample reduction.
2. Matching all-n exponential bounds when G has a self-centralizing abelian minimal normal subgroup M. The root limit is |G/M|, with no coprimality or complement assumption. In particular the argument gives base 6 for S_4.
3. Exact limsup and liminf max formulas for a split extension by a direct sum of irreducible modules. The factors are the quotient and its faithful affine action images. Nonsemisimple modules cannot be substituted without an additional argument.
4. A central-extension estimate: if Q=G/Z for central Z and e=exp(G), then log|F_G(n)| is at most n log e + (log|F_Q(n)|)^2/(2 log 2) - log|F_Q(n)|/2. This uses the credited Schur–Wiegold theorem. A quotient base L gives only the interval [L,min(|Q|,L^2)], in general.
5. An integer lower certificate built from all nonabelian-monolithic sections and all abelian chief-action images of finite sections. No matching universal upper bound is proved. SL(2,3) is a concrete method-gap example, with the proven interval [3,9]; it is not alleged to be a counterexample or an unresolved case in the literature.

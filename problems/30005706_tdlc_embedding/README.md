# Credited prior negative answer: sigma-compact TDLC embeddings

Problem30005706 / OWR-14298004-014. **Already solved,0/5 new author turns.** The exact printed unrestricted question has a negative answer in Caprace–Cornulier, Pacific Journal of Mathematics269(2) (2014),305–321, Theorem1.3 and Section5B, DOI10.2140/pjm.2014.269.305.

Their second-countable, sigma-compact, topologically simple TDLC example admits no nontrivial continuous homomorphism into any compactly generated locally compact group. Thus it cannot embed into a compactly generated TDLC group, even if nonclosed image is permitted. The packet checks the construction and specializes its obstruction to the exact continuous TDLC-target statement. The stronger abstract/general-target extensions are unnecessary and are not independently reproved here.

The source Question5 on printed3135 has no extra finite-cohomological-dimension hypothesis; that condition belongs to Question3. We make no claim about unprinted intent or why the later workshop listed the question.

Credit belongs to Caprace–Cornulier and the cited Akin–Glasner–Weiss/Willis construction. No new counterexample, novelty or priority claim. The separate source/proof AI review passes; this is not human peer review or formal certification.

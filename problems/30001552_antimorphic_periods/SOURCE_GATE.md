The exact source distinguishes arbitrary theta-periods, alternating theta-periods and weak letterwise periods. An alternating period p means w is a prefix of (u theta(u))^omega for one seed u of lengthp. Theta is a fixed antimorphic involution on the free monoid: theta(xy)=theta(y)theta(x), theta²=id. Theorem23 proves an alternating gcd period at length p+q, and its following conjecture asks for p+q−gcd(p,q). Thus the exact original conclusion is ALTERNATING, stronger than the imported record's unqualified theta-period conclusion. The candidate below proves the stronger original.

No aperiodicity, fixed-point-free alphabet involution, or minimal-period requirement is imposed. Periods are positive integers. At the proposed threshold, |w|≥max(p,q), so the full seeds are present. Empty words and zero periods do not arise. Morphic involutions and freely chosen mixtures of u/theta(u) are different cases and are not covered by the alternating proof.

The precise source is BLN Conjecture1 in arXiv2009.14444v2 and the published COLT2021 paper, https://proceedings.mlr.press/v134/bubeck21a/bubeck21a.pdf . It considers a fixed Lipschitz activation ψ:R→R and f(x)=sum_{j=1}^k a_j ψ(w_j·x+b_j), with arbitrary real weights and biases. Inputs are independent uniform spherical points, or normalized Gaussian points N(0,I_d/d), and labels are independent random signs. The displayed lower bound is Lip_{S^{d-1}}(f)≥c sqrt(n/k) with high probability over the data, uniformly over interpolating networks. No numerical failure probability is fixed in the conjecture; its footnote even calls a positive-probability result interesting. The matching-construction Conjecture2 is separate and is not the assigned lower-bound question.

There is a domain-convention issue to preserve: the original display uses the sphere-restricted Lipschitz norm even while the preceding generic-data definition also permits Gaussian inputs off the sphere. The paper explicitly distinguishes global or ball Lipschitz norms as weaker targets for its proved lower bounds. Later literature uses global/domain-appropriate norms for Gaussian data. This gate does not silently identify these norms or claim a solution from that issue.

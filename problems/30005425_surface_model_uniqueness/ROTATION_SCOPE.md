# Rotation-scope audit

The full OWR contribution and the expanded Baur–Coelho Simões paper were checked. The exact phrase concerning rotations of collections of tiles occurs in the OWR conjecture, but no formal generating move or equivalence relation accompanies it. The only rotation text found in the retrieved expanded paper concerns rotation of module arcs. No source-literal transfer from nonhomeomorphism to non-equivalence under all intended tile rotations is established.

The2026 Xin–Zhang preprint uses a precise labelled/dissected-homeomorphism relation, restricted to saturated covers. Its relation preserves topology by definition. The current finite-cover witness fails that relation, but the relation is not asserted identical to the OWR rotation relation. Their classification is credited background, not silently imported as an answer to a different quotient question.

A literal rotation or isotopy inside a fixed surface preserves topology. A combinatorial operation rotating polygons before regluing need not. Without the source's intended move, the latter possibility cannot be excluded. The public disposition remains an explicit scoped counterexample with the original question unresolved, pending independent source and proof audit.

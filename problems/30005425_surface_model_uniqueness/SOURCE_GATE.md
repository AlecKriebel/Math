## Correct target

The full Karin Baur contribution, Tiled surfaces, string algebras and laminations, OWR7/2023 printed pp441–444, was read. The conjecture on p443 was visually verified. Primary https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47001 ; DOI10.4171/owr/2023/7.

It fixes a string algebra A=kQ/I over an algebraically closed field, constructs labelled surface models by choosing quadratic ideals J⊆I with B=kQ/J locally gentle, and varies which relations of I are removed. The conjecture asks whether restricting the chosen covers B to finite-dimensional ones makes the resulting surface unique up to rotations of certain collections of tiles. This is not a claim that all string algebras admit finite-dimensional covers, nor a uniqueness question for a single fixed B. The report does not provide a precise generator-and-relation definition of the permitted tile-rotation equivalence.

The imported clean statement instead fixes a finite-dimensional locally gentle algebra. That loses the essential fixed-A/varying-cover distinction. The neighbouring lamination conjecture is separate and is not part of this target.

## Primary later literature and relation to prior campaign

Baur–Coelho Simões, A geometric model for the module category of a string algebra, arXiv:2403.07810, Theorem3.1 and Remark3.2, constructs A as a quotient of a locally gentle cover and uses the established PPP surface bijection. Example3.3 has one finite and one infinite cover with different surfaces; that example alone does not refute the finite-cover conjecture. The fixed-cover bijection does not identify surfaces for different covers.

Xin–Zhang, On Geometric Models of String Algebras: Uniqueness of Surfaces and Existence of Red Punctures, arXiv:2608.14360v1,14August2026, is a directly relevant new preprint. It restricts to saturated covers (Definition1.13) and uses orientation-preserving homeomorphisms preserving marked colours, the dissection and each ordered label (Definition1.14). Theorem2.14 and examples classify nonuniqueness for that equivalence, including cases with finite covers. This equivalence must not silently be identified with the OWR's unspecified rotations of tile collections. No claim that the new preprint settles the exact rotation quotient is made here. It is a preprint, not represented as a peer-reviewed theorem audit.

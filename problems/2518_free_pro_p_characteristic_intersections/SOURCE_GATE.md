Yiftach Barnea, Kourovka Notebook Problem21.9, 21st edition (2026). Given any finite-rank nonabelian free pro-p group F, seek finitely many open subgroups U_i, one equal to F, such that a subgroup K contained in every U_i and characteristic in every U_i must be trivial. ## Exact related primary paper

Barnea, Ershov, Le Boudec, Reid, Vannacci and Weigel, *On commensurators of free groups and free pro-p groups*, arXiv:2507.04120v3, revised22September2026, to appear in Compositio Mathematica. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.04120v3 . Question4 on printed/PDF53 is the same problem and is expressly left open. This page was visually checked.

Read in particular the introduction, Section3.3–3.5 conventions, Observation6.10 and its proof, Section7.2, and Section12.1–12.2. Observation6.10 proves a two-subgroup analogue for **discrete** free groups of certain ranks. The authors explicitly say they do not know its free-pro-p analogue even for one rank. Their simple tdlc constructions include quotients and do not produce the required family in F. Proposition12.2 has a different requirement involving finitely many individual automorphisms; Question4 asks invariance under the entire automorphism group of each chosen open subgroup. These distinctions prevent substituting a neighboring solved result.

The paper uses continuous automorphisms and topological generation for pro-p groups. It supplies the pro-p Schreier/basis facts in Section3.4, the Frattini basis criterion in Lemma3.9, and the natural epimorphism Aut(F)->GL_d(Z_p) in Notation7.8. Closed derived subgroups are used for pro-p abelianizations. The original question does not explicitly write 'closed' before the quantified subgroup. Our first partial results work for arbitrary subgroups invariant under all continuous automorphisms, so they do not depend on silently inserting that restriction. If a closed-subgroup reformulation is used later, it must be justified by taking closures under continuous automorphisms. Full abstract characteristicity implies invariance under this continuous subgroup of automorphisms, so the necessary conditions also hold with that reading.

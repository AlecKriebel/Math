## Target and editions

For finite groups G and H of nilpotency class two and exponent p, and **an arbitrary field k of characteristic p**, does an isomorphism kG ≅ kH **as k-algebras** imply G ≅ H? The nonabelian case has odd p, since exponent-two groups are abelian.

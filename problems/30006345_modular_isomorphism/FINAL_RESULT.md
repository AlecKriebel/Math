## Scoped results requiring review

1. **Graded-only obstruction.** H(F_p)×H(F_p) and H(F_(p²)) have isomorphic radical associated graded algebras after quadratic scalar extension, for every odd p. Their full algebras remain nonisomorphic over every characteristic-p field, separated by center dimensions. This is not a source counterexample.
2. **Positive result within an explicit family.** If both groups are products of finite-field three-dimensional Heisenberg groups and elementary abelian factors, the group order and full center algebra's radical Hilbert polynomial recover all field-degree multiplicities and the elementary abelian rank. Family membership of an arbitrary comparison group is not established.
3. **Center-only obstruction.** An explicit pair of order-3^10 groups has isomorphic complete center algebras over every characteristic-three field, while distinct geometric ranks of their leading commutator pencils separate their full algebras over every such field. This also is not a source counterexample.
4. **Conditional descent theorem.** A geometrically connected reduced stabilizer of the commutator tensor implies the all-field conclusion, by canonical graded reconstruction and the credited Lang theorem. This connectedness condition is not universal and fails for the split Heisenberg product.
5. **Full-algebra obstruction and an excluded twist.** For geometrically isomorphic F_p-algebras, prime-field descent is equivalent to triviality of an explicit Frobenius-twisted class in the full automorphism group's finite component group. An isomorphism exists over some extension of degree at most the number of geometric components. Producing such a nontrivial algebra form does not produce the required group-algebra counterexample.

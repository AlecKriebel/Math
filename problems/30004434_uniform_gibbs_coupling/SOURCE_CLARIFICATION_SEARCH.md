It credits Steif's 1991 spin-system paper, and thanks him for correspondence, but does not identify or correct the 2020 Question A. Its definition does not authorize silently choosing an intended repair. Only the definition and attribution are relevant here; its full duality proof is not a dependency of TURN_1.

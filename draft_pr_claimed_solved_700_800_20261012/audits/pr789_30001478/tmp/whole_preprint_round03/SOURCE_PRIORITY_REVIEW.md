## Source semantics and characteristic scope

The source first explains an obstruction to sharing a graded quotient ring
with a THCR and then makes the stronger prime/no-map assertions. The latter
paragraphs impose no injectivity, common fraction field, birationality, target
surface, or minimum image dimension restriction. A unital graded surjection
onto the full projective-line THCR is a counterexample even if one excludes
augmentations, finite images, point targets, and GK-dimension-one images.

The direct presentation is valid over any field. The journal's fractional
parameter change is invertible in characteristic different from two and
collapses in characteristic two. The manuscript expressly declines to
transfer the journal geometric/noetherian/GK4 identification to the independent
parameter characteristic-two presentation. That caveat is necessary and
correct. Nothing in this note purports to refute the separate common-quotient-
ring obstruction or classify all homogeneous primes.

## Priority attacks and blocked routes

The new retrieval and targeted searches were informed by the candidate's
bibliography and the parent's disclosed priority context; this is not a claim
of an independent pre-candidate priority discovery. The target-only strategy
was independently frozen before all candidate exposure. The present mathematical
assessment was formed and reported before reading the frozen package's root
mathematical receipts. Package audit reports were subsequently read as
audit artifacts. No previous round01 or round02 whole-preprint report was read.

Fresh discovery searches covered exact-title/quotient/prime phrases,
Rogalski–Sierra point modules, explicit linear-ideal notation, and the classical
bibliography. Search results led to the primary items above; no full matching
predecessor was verified. This is bounded discovery, not exhaustive forward
citation coverage. Search queries are retained below for reproducibility.

* The RZ canonical-map route is blocked by an unestablished strong-noetherian
  hypothesis for this family, which the RS paper explicitly reports lacking.
* NS Theorem 1.2 cannot be applied without establishing all its moduli and
  geometric hypotheses. Its dimension-at-least-two assumption is about that
  theorem's moduli space, not a definition excluding the P1 target constructed
  here.
* NS Theorem 4.10 gives factorization through a universal object up to finite
  dimension; identifying that object with a THCR would reintroduce the central
  unsupported assertion. It supplies no immediate predecessor quotient.
* Campbell's infinitesimal results concern directions at a baseline algebra.
  Its later family has a separately displayed presentation, and function skew
  fields or ambient Ore embeddings are not two-sided algebra quotients of the
  exact generic R. No equivalence establishing the required kernel was found
  in the inspected statements.
* Classical quantum/Jordan-plane, Ore, PBW and polynomial-twist machinery
  explains the quotient's algebraic type. It does not by itself exhibit the
  linear ideal in these particular six relations. The manuscript credits the
  machinery and leaves priority of the explicit source-specific implication
  unresolved.

The frozen two priority reports disclose broader searches and unread gaps.
Their asserted search counts were checked against pinned ledgers by the
operative checkers; this reviewer does not represent those prior searches as

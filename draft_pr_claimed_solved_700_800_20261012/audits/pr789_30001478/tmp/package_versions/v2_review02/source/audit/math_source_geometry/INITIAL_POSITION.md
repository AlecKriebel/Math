## Independent candidate mechanism, before seeing candidate

Each printed relation vanishes when all four xi are sent to a single degree-one indeterminate t: every summand is t*(a*t-t)+t*(t-a*t)=0. Thus there is a homogeneous surjection R -> k[t]. Its kernel is exactly the ideal of the three differences x2-x1, x3-x1, x4-x1: after imposing them the six relations become identically zero in the free algebra on one generator, which equals k[t]. The ideal is nonzero since a quadratic defining ideal has zero intersection with the degree-one vector space; it is proper, is completely prime, and is strictly smaller than R+ since x1 maps to nonzero t. The quotient is infinite-dimensional and has nonzero positive-degree image, rather than being the augmentation map to k. This mechanism appears to refute both literal assertions for all parameters and all characteristics.

Geometrically in characteristic not2 the point (u,v)=(1,1) lies in the regular locus of sigma(u,v)=(uv,v), is fixed by sigma and by every tau(rho,theta), and the four sections 1,u,v,uv all take value1 there. This explains the quotient as a fixed-point evaluation; checking twisted multiplication requires that evaluation at the fixed point commutes with every iterate of the field automorphism and that rational functions in generated sections are regular there. The direct presentation proof avoids all such geometric regularity dependencies.

## Twisted homogeneous coordinate ring semantics

Under the usual construction B(X,L,sigma)=direct sum H0(X,L_n), X=Spec(k), L=O, sigma=id yields k[t] with multiplication of sections. A projective point is projective over k, O is ample (and sigma-ample), and all pieces have dimension1. Even if one chooses to require a positive-dimensional projective target, compose the quotient with k[t]->k[s,t] via t->s; the latter is B(P1,O(1),id). Its image is still GK-dimension1. Consequently merely requiring target dimension positive does not rescue the literal no-map statement. A stronger exclusion of all GKdim<=1 images would change the question; this audit does not prove that stronger assertion or claim to determine authors' private intent.

# Source interpretation and geometric derivation

This document verifies the printed/literal conjectures, not a repaired conjecture with a minimum-GK-dimension requirement. The source does not specify the latter requirement. The map constructed below is unital, degree-preserving, surjective onto its target, nonzero on every positive degree, and has infinite-dimensional image. The prime is nonzero, proper, two-sided, homogeneous and completely prime, and its quotient is infinite-dimensional.

## 2. A quotient independent of geometric hypotheses

Let F=k<x1,x2,x3,x4>, J=(f1,...,f6), and L=(x2-x1,x3-x1,x4-x1). Define epsilon:F->k[t] by every xi->t. The kernel is L: the quotient F/L is the free associative algebra on one generator, with mutually inverse maps sending x1 modL to t and t to x1 modL. Every defining polynomial lies in L since its evaluation is (a-1+1-a)t^2=0 for a=alpha or beta. Thus J subset L and R/(L/J) is k[t]. The ideal I=L/J is nonzero because J is generated by homogeneous degree2 elements, so J_1=0 whereas x2-x1 in L_1 is nonzero over every field including characteristic2. It is proper and completely prime since k[t] is a domain; x1 notin I proves I differs from augmentation. The original alpha,beta independence and cardinality assumptions are unnecessary for this conclusion. This does not prove that all primes have been classified, nor that R itself is a domain in characteristic2.

## 3. Twisted multiplication and point targets

Rogalski–Zhang, Canonical Maps to Twisted Rings, arXivmath/0409405v2, section3p7, recalls the Artin–Van den Bergh definition for any projective k-scheme X, automorphism sigma, and invertible sheaf L: B_n=H0(X,L_n), L_n=L tensor sigma*L tensor ...; for f in B_m and g in B_n, product is f tensor(sigma^m)*g.

For X=Spec k, sigma=id, L=O, all L_n=O, all B_n=k, and products of the distinguished sections1_m and1_n are1_(m+n). The map1_n->t^n is a graded isomorphism B->k[t]. Spec k is projective (P0), and O is very ample and sigma-ample: its global section realizes the P0 embedding, and positive cohomology of every coherent sheaf vanishes on this affine point. This construction therefore satisfies also conventions that require sigma-ampleness.

If one insists that X have positive dimension, B(P1,O(1),id)=k[s,t]. The homomorphism R->k[s,t] with every xi->s is unital, graded, and nonzero in arbitrarily large degrees; its image is k[s]. It is not surjective onto k[s,t] and no birational or dominant assertion is made. A restriction on target dimension alone is insufficient to exclude it. Requiring image GKdim>=2 would be an additional assertion outside the literal source and outside this proof.

## 4. Why the quotient is geometrically natural

In characteristic not2, use the paper's rational-function model with sigma(u,v)=(uv,v) and tau acting by Mobius maps fixing (1,1). The point p=(1,1) has all denominators rho+1+(rho-1)=2rho and theta+1+(theta-1)=2theta nonzero for nonzero rho,theta. Both sigma and tau and their inverses are regular near p and fix p, so phi=tausigma is a local automorphism fixing p. The local ring O_(T,p) is preserved by phi* and its inverse, and evaluation ev_p commutes with phi*. The skew-polynomial homomorphism sum a_n t^n -> sum ev_p(a_n)t^n is multiplicative on the subalgebra whose coefficients are regular at p, because ev_p(a phi^m(b))=ev_p(a)ev_p(b). The generators t,ut,vt,uvt all map to t. This gives the same quotient as the presentation argument, without pretending evaluation extends to all rational functions: it does not extend to k(u,v), where nonzero u-1 would become0 but must be invertible. Restriction to the preserved local coefficient ring is essential.

## 5. Characteristic2 boundary

The matrix of each paper Mobius transformation has determinant (rho+1)^2-(rho-1)^2=4rho, and similarly4theta. In characteristic2 it becomes singular. Also (theta-1)/(theta+1)=1 and (rho-1)/(rho+1)=1 for denominators nonzero. The stated family coordinate change in the source cannot identify generic independent alpha,beta with this model in characteristic2. No characteristic2 geometric/noetherian-family theorem is inferred. The direct J subset L calculation still works over any field. Consequently a publication should state an all-characteristics theorem for the six-relation presentation, while restricting the identification with the standard geometric family to characteristic not2.

## 6. Exact remaining interpretation limit

A source can use 'nontrivial' informally. The counterexample refutes both assertions under the ordinary nonzero/proper-prime and non-augmentation graded-map readings, and even excludes finite-dimensional-image loopholes. No proof here determines whether authors intended to exclude ALL point quotients or GKdim1 images; that alternate interpretation must be stated as a different question. It is not evidence that the printed literal conjectures have been solved in that stronger sense.

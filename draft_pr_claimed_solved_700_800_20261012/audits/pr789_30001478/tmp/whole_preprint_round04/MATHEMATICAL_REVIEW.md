## Exact quotient and complete kernel

Let F be the free algebra on the four generators, J its six-relation ideal, and L=(x3−x1,x4−x2) in F. The mutually inverse generator maps identify F/L with the free algebra on X,Y. The image of J is the ideal generated by the six substituted expressions, which are exactly 0,0,0,0,Q,Q, with Q=beta X²−XY+YX−beta Y². This establishes the first quotient isomorphism; checking relation vanishing alone would be weaker, but the manuscript correctly uses the universal property.

On S=k[s,t], theta(s)=s+beta t and theta(t)=beta s+t is invertible, determinant 1−beta². The multiplication f⋆g=f theta^m(g), deg f=m, is associative: both triple products are f theta^m(g) theta^(m+n)(h). The degree-zero scalar 1 is a unit. All positive degree spaces are ordinary S_n, dimension n+1. The equality S_n theta^n(S_1)=S_(n+1) is equality of linear spans, and proves generation in degree one by induction. Top homogeneous components show a product of arbitrary nonzero elements is nonzero. This does not assume a domain theorem for R.

Direct substitution gives

beta s(s+beta t)−s(beta s+t)+t(s+beta t)−beta t(beta s+t)=0.

Hence there is a graded surjection A=k<X,Y>/(Q)→S^theta. For injectivity put U=X−Y,V=Y, an invertible change in all characteristics. Expansion yields Q=beta U²+(beta−1)UV+(beta+1)VU. Since beta+1≠0, replace VU by ((1−beta)/(1+beta))UV−(beta/(1+beta))U². Every resulting word decreases fixed-length lexicographic order with V>U. That finite order ensures termination in each degree, and irreducibles are U^i V^j. These n+1 monomials span A_n. The surjection to the n+1-dimensional target forces equality and zero kernel degree by degree. There is no confluence gap; spanning suffices. As an additional check, VU has no self-overlap and the algebra is the Ore extension with automorphism U↦((1−beta)/(1+beta))U and derivation delta(U)=−beta U²/(1+beta).

The separate tensor proof was checked independently after the statement-only freeze. For T=theta on V=kX+kY, C_n(v1…vn)=v1 T(v2)…T^(n−1)(vn) is invertible on V^⊗n. It sends Q to −D(XY−YX), D=1−beta². Invertibility proves equality of the entire degree-n relation ideals after C, and C intertwines concatenation with the twist product. Thus the new algebra-family argument proves the full ideal conclusion, not merely a low-degree identity.

## Projective target and prime

The projective map induced by the matrix [[1,beta],[beta,1]] has pullback theta on H0(O(1)) in the manuscript's positive-pullback convention. A linear lift supplies compatible identifications of successive pulled-back O(1)'s and their tensor powers with O(n). Under them the section multiplication is exactly f⋆g. O(1) is sigma-ample by Serre vanishing because L_n≅O(n); this is true over any field. P1 is a positive-dimensional integral projective curve. The map is surjective and has infinite-dimensional image, even under the stronger interpretation of the printed map question.

J is generated by homogeneous quadratics, so J_1=0. Consequently x3−x1 is nonzero in R_1 and I is nonzero. All generators of I have positive degree, so I⊂R+. The image of x1 is the nonzero section s, so the containment is strict and I is proper. The domain quotient makes I completely prime, hence ordinary two-sided prime and graded prime. Hilbert series is (1−z)^−2; cumulative growth is (n+1)(n+2)/2 and GK-dimension is exactly two.

## Adversarial boundary and scope tests

In characteristic two, the manuscript's U=X−Y,V=Y remains invertible and theta remains invertible for beta≠1. The Ore relation becomes VU=UV+beta U²/(beta+1). Projective theta may have order two, which does not affect domainhood or sigma-ampleness. My independent finite diagnostics include both non-prime-field beta choices in GF(4); no inference to all fields/degrees is drawn from those diagnostics.

At beta=0, Q is the commutator relation and the twist is ordinary k[s,t]. At beta=1 and −1, Q factors respectively as (X+Y)(X−Y) and −(X−Y)(X+Y), with nonzero linear factors; the excluded boundary really has zero divisors. In characteristic two the two boundaries coincide. Algebraic independence avoids both values without specializing parameters.

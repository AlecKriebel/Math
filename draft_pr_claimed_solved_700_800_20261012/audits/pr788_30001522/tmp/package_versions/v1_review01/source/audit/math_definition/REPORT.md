## Checkable topological argument and tested boundary cases

1. The Lie quotient has dimension 8-3=5, is closed and smooth, and hence admits a finite triangulation of its actual underlying space. The bundle homotopy sequence gives pi1(W)=0 and pi2(W)=Z/2. Hurewicz gives H2(W;Z)=Z/2. Simple connectivity gives orientation. Integral duality and universal coefficients then give homology (Z,0,Z/2,0,0,Z). There is no H3 homology torsion hidden by the notation: the torsion occurs in H3 COHOMOLOGY. This distinction is essential in alternative Gysin arguments.

2. The rational Lie-group homotopy exact sequence already makes total rational homotopy finite; no value of the degree-three inclusion map is needed for this hypothesis. The sharpened rational-S5 claim also holds: first rational Hurewicz gives a nonzero degree-five sphere map; it is a rational homology equivalence because all other reduced rational homology vanishes; rational Whitehead applies to these simply connected finite-type spaces. This proves chi_pi=-1 without silently replacing the actual space used for its ranks.

3. For the fixed circle z -> diag(z,z,z^-2), a fixed coset forces conjugacy into SO(3). Every element of SO(3) has eigenvalue one, so z=1 or -1. Every odd finite subgroup therefore acts freely on the fixed W. The prime three has no central-kernel exception: primitive cubic scalar matrices have no eigenvalue one. The circle itself is not free since -1 fixes the identity coset. This is exactly the distinction required by the source's free versus almost-free definition.

4. If an arbitrary continuous involution h acts freely on a closed manifold M, it acts by homeomorphisms. For any point x, Hausdorff separation of x and h(x), followed by intersection with a coordinate neighborhood, provides U with U intersect h(U) empty. The quotient q identifies U homeomorphically with its open image, and q^-1(q(U)) is the two disjoint sheets U and h(U). Thus M/<h> is a closed topological manifold and q a genuine covering. No smoothness, local linearity, quotient triangulability, or circle quotient theorem enters.

5. The topological tangent microbundle pulls back under this local homeomorphism. Equivalently, local diagonal neighborhoods identify tau_M with q^*tau_N. Naturality of the Thom/Steenrod definition gives w_i(M)=q^*w_i(N). With mod-two fundamental classes, q_*[M]=2[N]=0, even if N is nonorientable. Every tangent Stiefel-Whitney number must consequently vanish. This is a valid obstruction in the full continuous category, not merely the differentiable category.

6. For W, its tangent isotropy representation on i times real symmetric traceless matrices has one fixed line and two oriented rotation planes of weights one and two. On fundamental groups its parity is 1+2=1 modulo two. Combined with the isomorphism pi2(W)->pi1(SO3), this makes w2(W) the nonzero x in H^2(W;F2). The coefficient-sequence connecting map from Z/2 to Z/4 to Z/2 is nonzero on x: Hom(Z/2,Z/4) reduces to zero in Hom(Z/2,Z/2). Therefore Sq1(x)=y is the nonzero degree-three cohomology class. Orientation and Sq1(w2)=w1w2+w3 give w3=y. Mod-two Poincare duality makes xy evaluate to one. This reconstructs the specific tangent number rather than merely quoting it. 7. The nonzero w2w3 number contradicts any free involution. Hence free 2-rank is zero. Every positive-dimensional torus contains an involution, and restriction preserves freeness, so free toral rank is zero. A rational model or an almost-free circle is not a counterexample to this conclusion, since neither changes the source's definition.

8. For each odd p, W is an Fp homology five-sphere. In the Borel spectral sequence for a hypothetical free (Z/p)^2 action, only fiber degrees zero and five occur; the action on both is trivial since a p-group maps trivially into Fp*. There is only a d6 transgression. Its bottom-row image is one principal ideal (f) in Fp[t1,t2] tensor Lambda(s1,s2). Setting the exterior generators to zero surjects onto a binary polynomial ring modulo at most one cubic. That quotient has nonzero classes in arbitrarily high degrees (dimension three in every ordinary degree at least three when the cubic is nonzero). No extension can erase a nonzero associated-graded piece. But the free finite quotient is a topological five-manifold, and the Borel construction is homotopy equivalent to it. This contradiction proves the exact odd-primary upper bound one. The universal bound is a proof, not extrapolation from sampled primes.

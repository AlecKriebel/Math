## Exact target and initial assessment

The primary p.1912 definitions are maxima over free torus and elementary abelian p-group actions on the actual finite connected complex X. They do not quantify over spaces homotopy equivalent to X or rationally equivalent to X. They do not use almost-free torus actions, where finite stabilizers are allowed. No smoothness restriction is stated. The rational homotopy hypothesis is finite total dimension, not merely each homotopy group finite-dimensional. Fundamental group must be abelian and its action on all higher integral homotopy groups trivial. The target is one fixed X and infinitely many distinct primes, with strict inequality for each. The source is Report 32/2010, whereas the supplied catalog's citation says 2011. The asymptotic upper bound rk_p <= -chi_pi does not by itself compare rk_p to actual-space rk_T.

A genuine obstruction to free continuous circle actions suffices to force actual-space rk_T=0, since any nonzero torus contains a circle. A rational obstruction alone cannot distinguish actual-space rank from the rank of a rational model; integral torsion is a plausible decisive mechanism. A free finite subgroup from an almost-free circle action may survive for all sufficiently large primes, but this must be proved uniformly for one fixed action, not separately asserted from rational data.

## Independent conditional Gysin obstruction to check against the submitted argument

Let E be connected and simply connected, have integral cohomology H^2(E)=H^4(E)=0, H^3(E)=A a nonzero finite abelian group, and H^j(E)=0 for j>5. Suppose a free continuous S^1 action makes E -> B a principal circle bundle, and B has finite integral cohomological dimension. From the homotopy exact sequence pi_1(B)=0, hence H^1(B)=0. The initial Gysin segment gives H^2(B)=Z generated by the Euler class. The segment H^1(B)->H^3(B)->H^3(E)->H^2(B) gives H^3(B)=A because every map from finite A into Z is zero. The next segment H^4(E)->H^3(B)->H^5(B) makes cup product with the Euler class injective, so H^5(B) contains A. In degrees >=7, H^{j-1}(E)=H^j(E)=0 makes cup product H^{j-2}(B)->H^j(B) an isomorphism. Consequently arbitrarily high odd cohomology of B is nonzero, contradiction.

This conditional lemma is independent validation reasoning, not an attempted new construction or a claimed solution. It deliberately avoids an assumption that B is a smooth or topological manifold. To apply it, one must independently justify principal-bundle local triviality and finite cohomological dimension for the allowed continuous actions, as well as all integral cohomology and rational-homotopy data of the submitted candidate. A quotient that is only a homology manifold might still suffice, but that stronger machinery is unnecessary if finite dimension is established.

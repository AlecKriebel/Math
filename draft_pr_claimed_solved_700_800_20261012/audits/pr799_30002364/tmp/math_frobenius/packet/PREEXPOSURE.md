## Exact target and source boundary

For a CM abelian variety A over Qbar with a reduction A0, test whether the hypothesis that every Hodge class on every power of A is in the algebra generated by degree-one Hodge classes forces the corresponding assertion for the union of Tate classes over all finite extensions on every power of A0. The second question asks for classes admitting the converse.

The report defines Tate classes using the direct limit over finite extensions (p. 1890); its Theorem 1(1) asserts the forward statement for **simple factors** of reductions of simple CM varieties with abelian CM endomorphism field. Its Theorem 1(2)(b) asserts equivalence for simple CM varieties when the prime restricted to the reflex field is unramified of absolute degree one. These are boundary theorems, not permission to extend to arbitrary CM fields or arbitrary reductions.

## Independent approach family

Use exact arithmetic of Frobenius Weil numbers and its character-relation lattice. For a chosen finite-field model with Frobenius pi and q=p^f, a cohomological tensor monomial with exponents n_i and Tate twist r has Frobenius eigenvalue prod_i sigma_i(pi)^n_i / q^r. It is Tate over some finite extension exactly when that eigenvalue is a root of unity. Its valuations at every prime over p, together with the weight equation sum_i n_i=2r, give necessary arithmetic conditions. If all other valuations vanish and this algebraic number and its inverse are integral, the Weil absolute values and Kronecker's theorem give sufficiency. This integrality step and the use of **all** p-adic primes require explicit justification.

The CM side is computed independently from translates of the CM type in its normal closure. Compare the Hodge relation lattice with the slope relation lattice, but then compare both to the **actual divisor/Lefschetz lattice after reduction**. A new slope relation alone does not certify a non-Lefschetz Tate class: reduction may acquire extra endomorphisms and therefore extra divisor classes. Frobenius conjugates equal up to a root of unity can merge simple factors or enlarge endomorphism multiplicities after finite extension. The finite-field Lefschetz group must be recomputed with that growth included.

## Assumptions to challenge

1. The proposed CM type is a valid type and is realized by a polarized abelian variety; its primitive/simple status is proved rather than inferred from a rank count.
2. The selected decomposition subgroup is realized by an unramified prime with the claimed CM slope formula and residue degree; any reflex-field condition is used in its exact form.
3. Exact rational matrices use the correct coset action, inverses, CM conjugation, and normalization. Rank or nullspace computations have independently checkable certificates.
4. The Frobenius field, Honda–Tate simple factors, Schur indices, and geometric endomorphisms are determined sufficiently to identify the Lefschetz invariants on all powers.
5. A Tate obstruction survives quotienting by every newly available divisor relation, not merely the characteristic-zero ones.
6. Claims concerning all powers and all finite extensions are supported by a torus/invariant criterion, not finite tests on a few powers or a chosen base field.
7. Positive or converse claims do not silently transfer the main open question into an unsupported independence or realization lemma.

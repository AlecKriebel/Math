## Exact target and distinctions
The target's first question asks whether all Hodge classes on every power being generated by divisor classes survives every CM reduction. The second asks for a class having a converse. An earlier result need not use this exact wording, polynomial, or dimension to anticipate an entire negative answer: a nondegenerate CM lift of a geometrically degenerate finite-field abelian variety would suffice. Conversely, an exceptional Tate class alone is insufficient without verifying a suitable lift has the all-powers Hodge property. Algebraicity (Hodge/Tate conjectures) is weaker than being generated by divisors and must remain separate.

Original theorem boundary: OWR Theorem 1(1) assumes simple CM A and abelian End^0(A), giving the property for each simple factor of any reduction; Theorem 1(2) gives equivalence under the reflex-field prime unramified/absolute-degree-one hypothesis. The original explicitly includes the classical products-of-elliptic-curves class on both sides, so the existential converse itself has a classical answer. Any claim of new converse needs a stronger specific scope.

## Success/failure criteria
A priority defeater must supply an earlier complete counterexample or general theorem constructively implying a nondegenerate CM A with a reduction possessing a non-Lefschetz Tate class (including all-powers claims). Mere necessary conditions, unproved existence, or a reduction to an equivalent unsupported claim do not settle this. Conversely, bounded non-discovery cannot establish firstness, exhaustive absence, or present worldwide openness. Primary bodies must be inspected; search snippets and metadata are leads only.

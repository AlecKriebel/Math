## The conflicting lift statement and its context

The PDF bodies of *Angle ranks of abelian varieties*, arXiv 2112.02455v1, v2, v3, all retain Remark3.5 on PDFp6. It passes from the correct local averaging formula to rank equality and an all-powers Hodge/Tate Lefschetz equivalence. The current journal HTML contains the same unqualified wording. Submission history still ends at v3 (17 April 2023); the journal gives version of record 2 June 2023, issue May2024. Targeted correction/erratum searches found no correction in the inspected public records. This is a bounded check, not proof no correction exists anywhere.

Two readings must be distinguished. The unrestricted reading concerns a CM lift chosen arbitrarily; the accepted eightfold and the independently derived threefold defeat rank preservation for that reading. The narrower interpretation takes the lift to mean the special object associated to a Newton hyperplane arrangement in Section3.6 (v1p14, v2pp14–15, v3p13). That section gives contextual support for an associated-lift interpretation, but does not prove the displayed rank conclusion for an arbitrary lift. I do not assert the cited authors accepted a correction, nor do I claim the candidate disproves every properly qualified Hazama-associated construction.

Hazama's 2003 primary Section5, especially Proposition5.5 and Corollaries5.5.1–5.5.2 (printed636–637), constructs a CM abelian variety from the arrangement's regions and group action and identifies its Hodge ring by an orthogonal-complement condition. These inspected passages concern a specially constructed characteristic-zero object. They do not make every lift with the same reduction have that type. Original Hazama2000 full-body retrieval remains a gap; it is not silently replaced by a secondary summary.

Milne1999b Theorem7.1 quantifies over all CM varieties and uses a comparison of global categories; it is not an individual-lift Lefschetz equivalence. Its introduction explicitly cautions that not every Tate class can be lifted to a Hodge class on a CM lift. The current author correction PDF, four pages, supplies a revised Section6 argument and a Section7 alternative. The author HTML and PDF present different stages of the Section6 correction, so the current PDF was inspected rather than treating the HTML summary as the last word. None of these global statements validates the arbitrary-lift inference.

## Exact claim and falsification criteria

The controlling [October2026 Notebook](https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt-1.pdf), printed 177, asks whether each transitive Sylow p-subgroup S of any transitive finitary G contains a transitive subgroup with the cyclic-block property. That property includes total imprimitivity and requires every individual cycle support of every element to be a block for the subgroup. At printed 89, total imprimitivity is defined by containment of each finite point set in a finite group-block.

The relevant earlier result must therefore exhibit an infinite transitive finitary p-group satisfying the Sylow hypothesis, exclude **all** transitive subgroups, and justify the union action at every level. A finite degree-eight example, a bounded computational search, or failure of selected generators would not suffice. Potential disqualifiers tested were an incorrect group calculation, a change of Sylow ambient group, a restriction on H, nonfinitary embeddings, a faulty block-limit assertion, a nonconjugate action, or a later replacement file bearing an older title date.

## Entire domain and every stage

Let X be the preprint's eight-point domain, and let T consist of binary sequences t=(t0,t1,...) with finite support. Define

    R(x,t) = (rho(x), j(t)),       j(t)=sum_i t_i*2^i.

Binary expansion makes j a bijection T -> N0, so R is a bijection onto the candidate's domain. At stage n it restricts to

    R_n: X x {0,1}^n -> D x {0,...,2^n-1}.

The preprint appends a zero bit when including stage n into n+1. The candidate includes the old domain as the first consecutive copy. Under R_(n+1), the appended bit 0 is the interval j<2^n; bit 1 is the interval 2^n<=j<2^(n+1). Thus these point embeddings commute exactly with R_n.

Base conjugacy has already been proved. Inductively, if R_n conjugates W_n to G_n, then R_(n+1) conjugates the two independent W_n factors to the corresponding G_n factors, and the top exchange of the last bit to the coordinate exchange of the two consecutive copies. These factors and exchange generate the full next stage. Therefore R_(n+1) W_(n+1) R_(n+1)^-1=G_(n+1). It also carries the group inclusion w -> (w,identity) to the candidate's same first-factor inclusion. Induction establishes these identities for every n, and compatibility gives R W R^-1=G on the whole infinite domain. This is an all-level proof, not extrapolation from computations.

As controls, the verifier exhaustively compares all 2048 elements of the first wreath extension, checks its bottom local action, and checks recursive generating actions through stage 6. The order recurrence is |W_n|=2^(6*2^n-1), consistent at every level.

Every w in W lies in a finite W_n and fixes every point outside X_n under all later inclusions. Hence W is finitary and locally finite 2. Finite-stage transitivity implies transitivity of the union because any two points lie together in a sufficiently large X_n. Higher stages preserve the partitions into canonical level-n blocks: independent base factors preserve their copies' internal partitions and the top exchange moves entire partition members. Thus every X_n is a W-block. The increasing finite blocks X_n cover the domain, directly satisfying the Notebook definition. Alternatively, a proper block of a transitive finitary action is finite: an element moving one of its points outside moves every point of that block. Any such finite block lies in some strictly larger X_n. The preprint's phrasing by absence of maximal proper blocks causes no gap for this example.

## The unrestricted subgroup quantifier and Sylow hypothesis

The bottom fibres are invariant as a block system at every stage, and restriction of a setwise fibre stabilizer is always in Q. The initial copy realizes Q on its fibre, giving W_B^B=Q exactly.

For an arbitrary transitive H<=W and x,y in B, choose h in H with h(x)=y. Since B is a W-block, h(B) meets B and must equal B. Thus the induced group H_B^B is a transitive subgroup of Q and equals Q by minimal transitivity. Choose lifts in H_B of the two crossing witnesses. A witness cycle contained in B is a whole cycle on the infinite domain because its lift preserves B. The other lift maps its support to a distinct intersecting support. This contradicts the H-block condition regardless of the lifts' actions outside B. No finite-generation condition, specified support, containment of a base copy, or regularity assumption on H is used. The conclusion excludes the bare cycle-block condition for every transitive H, and therefore also excludes the stronger requested property including total imprimitivity.

Finally choose p=2 and G=S=W. A locally finite 2-group is its own maximal 2-subgroup; it is also Sylow at each finite stage. The original problem asks for Sylow subgroups of G, not of FSym(Omega). Thus there is no extra ambient maximality requirement. The infinite union and the all-H argument in the earlier written proof are sufficient as written; no mathematical repair is needed to make it a full counterexample.

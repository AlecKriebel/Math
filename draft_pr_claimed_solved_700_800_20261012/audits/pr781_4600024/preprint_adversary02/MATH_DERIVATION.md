# Independent mathematical reconstruction

Target: the exact prescribed onto self-map f of a nonempty two-sided irreducible sofic shift T. The containing SFT may equal T. The full selected public record and paper were read before the independent position was frozen; their historical open-status assertions are not taken as established present facts.

1. A complete L(T)-DFA plus at most ell-1 input letters gives a deterministic finite automaton for the finite output. Accept if that output is outside L. For input length below ell the output is epsilon and hence legal. This proves B regular. A bad finite output remains a factor under input padding, including with asymmetric memory/anticipation. Factoriality makes B a two-sided ideal. Any finite legal input word extends inside T, so its output is legal and L and B are disjoint.

2. Restrict a larger-alphabet containing SFT to A^Z, retaining an SFT containing T. Every forbidden word for it is absent from T. If n is at least the maximum forbidden-list length, each forbidden occurrence in a point of T[n] would lie in a legal length-n block, impossible. Thus T[n] lies in the containing SFT.

3. Write the fixed completion F and any hypothesized extension G on their common input interval. Their rules agree on every common-window word occurring in T. For sufficiently large n all such windows in T[n] are legal, so F=G there. Consequently existence for any radius/completion/alphabet is equivalent to the fixed F mapping some T[n] into T. No alternate-radius assumption is hidden.

4. F(T[n]) is contained in T exactly when no occurring finite input is in B: a bad occurrence produces a forbidden output; any forbidden output occurrence has a finite input preimage interval. Since T is contained in T[n] and f is onto, image inclusion implies equality. This is precisely the use of onto.

5. The graph with vertices L_(n-1) and edges L_n is strongly connected: irreducibility connects any two vertex words inside an allowed word. Its finite paths extend both ways, including for periodic irreducible graphs. Long words are readable exactly when their n-factors are allowed. A short readable word extends to a length-n path and is thus a factor of an allowed n-word; conversely any allowed short word extends inside T. Epsilon is readable. This justifies the short-word statement and locally testable membership using n-factors plus finitely many short exceptions.

6. For saturation, let an LT[k] separator C contain L and avoid B. Suppose bad w occurs in T[n], with n at least max(2,k). Irreducibility supplies legal U containing every allowed n-word, and U can be extended to length at least n+k. Connect w between two copies of U within the strongly connected overlap graph, giving W=UcwdU in L(T[n]). Actual concatenation gaps can be chosen by extending graph paths; no letters of U or w are discarded by an overlap identification. Idealness implies W is bad. Independently connect U to itself in T, obtaining V=UeU in L.

7. V and W both have exactly all allowed n-factors, so exactly L_k as their k-factors, because legal k-words extend to legal n-words. Their first/last k-1 letters agree. A full published k-profile is a k-factor split after floor(k/2) letters; full profile sets agree. Left-truncated profiles follow from the common prefix and right-truncated ones from the common suffix. Their lengths exclude truncation at both ends. Thus actual profile SETS agree. This sufficient direction from factors/endpoints to profiles is all the proof requires; no converse or multiplicity claim is used. C contains V and therefore W, contradiction.

8. PRZ 2014 Theorem 4.2, actual published PDF pp. 8-9, assumes one common recognizing monoid morphism and gives k=4(|M|+1), equating LT separation with LT[k] separation. Position/profile definitions are p. 4; indexed classes pp.5-6. The transformation monoid generated by letters on the disjoint union of the two complete DFA state sets, including identity, recognizes both languages under one morphism and is finite and enumerable. The published theorem applies directly. Its general algebraic proof is a cited dependency, not independently formalized here.

Conversely avoidance gives the prescribed extension and onto image equality. To decide avoidance, enumerate legal N-words, form overlap edges, label each by the appended letter, and take all vertices initial/final. This emits exactly every finite occurring word: a preceding (N-1)-block supplies its start, including for short words. Strong connectivity extends each finite path bi-infinitely. Product with the complete B-DFA reduces intersection to finite accepting-state reachability. Termination is mathematical finiteness, without an empirical cutoff. No practical complexity improvement or arbitrary-input solver implementation follows.

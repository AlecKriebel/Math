## Exact claim and independent falsification

The claim tested was: for every prime p and every characteristic-p ACFH model (K, theta), the parameter-free unique pth-root map rho is a field automorphism commuting with theta, and its restriction to K* is outside the image of Z[X] under X↦theta. The note additionally proves that adjoining rho gives a faithful copy of Z[1/p][X]. It does not prove equality with the whole definable endomorphism ring or a characteristic-zero classification.

The independent derivation succeeds without an endomorphism classification. In an algebraically closed characteristic-p field, Frobenius is onto and is injective by (u-v)^p=u^p-v^p. The graph y^p=x defines its inverse without parameters. Unique roots prove addition and multiplication are preserved. The calculation theta(rho(x))^p=theta(x) uses only multiplicativity of theta. Its zero case follows from theta(0)=0.

For a nonzero integral polynomial Q of degree d, adjoin algebraically independent t_0,...,t_d and take the algebraic closure L of the resulting rational-function field. The subgroup generated by K* and t_0,...,t_(d-1) is a direct product. Therefore prescribing t_i↦t_(i+1) together with the old theta is consistent. A divisible target permits extension of the subgroup homomorphism to all of L*. The manuscript's Zorn proof handles every possible relation: exponents whose powers lie in the previous domain form either {0} or nZ, and an nth root of the prescribed image enforces all relations. Neither the domain's torsion nor a kernel in theta obstructs this extension. Extending by zero gives a legitimate base-theory extension.

The Laurent monomial Q(theta_L)(t_0) is not 1. Splitting positive and negative coefficients gives a quantifier-free matrix involving only positive powers and an inequation. Existential closure transfers this witness to the given K. Hence evaluation is faithful. This includes nonzero constants, d=0, coefficients divisible by p, either sign, empty products, and the smallest prime p=2. It requires no saturation or change of completion. An extension satisfying the base theory T suffices; it need not already be an ACFH model.

In the group endomorphism ring, addition is multiplication of values, zero is x↦1, and integer n is x↦x^n. In particular [p] is Frobenius, not zero. If rho=P(theta), then composition with [p] gives (pP-1)(theta)=0. Faithfulness would force pP-1=0 in Z[X], contrary to its constant coefficient pa_0-1. The distinction between field characteristic and endomorphism-ring scalars is explicit and correct. This is a universal deduction, not an inference from numerical testing.

Every group endomorphism commutes with [p], so its inverse rho is central even if the ambient endomorphism ring is noncommutative. Clearing powers of p makes P/p^n↦rho^n∘P(theta) well-defined and compatible with both operations; faithfulness kills its kernel. This establishes precisely the stated localized inclusion.

The nonvacuity claim matches the cited model-companion embedding: start from (algebraic closure of F_p, identity), a T-model, and embed in ACFH. Characteristic is preserved. The archived independent countable-witness construction also gives a sound alternative existence deduction. No deeper independence-theoretic result is required by the submitted proof.

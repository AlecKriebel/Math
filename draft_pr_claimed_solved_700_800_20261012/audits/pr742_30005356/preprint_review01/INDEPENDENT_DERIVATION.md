## Exact claim
For every existentially closed algebraically closed field with a total unary operator theta preserving the nonzero multiplicative group and theta(0)=0, in characteristic p>0, inverse Frobenius is parameter-free definable, commutes with theta, and does not equal any integral polynomial in theta as an endomorphism of K^*. These integer coefficients live in the endomorphism ring, not in the field. The corollary asserts only an embedded central localization Z[1/p][X], not classification of the whole definable endomorphism ring.

## Fresh derivation
1. Let Q=sum b_i X^i be nonzero. In an algebraic closure L of K(t_0,...,t_d), each t_i is algebraically independent over K. The multiplicative subgroup generated by K^* and t_0,...,t_(d-1) is K^* times a free abelian group. Indeed c product t_i^m_i=1 becomes, after clearing negative exponents, a nonzero polynomial relation unless all m_i=0 and c=1.
2. Prescribe theta_L(c)=theta(c) and theta_L(t_i)=t_(i+1) for i<d. This is a homomorphism on that direct product. L^* is divisible since every nonzero element has an nth root. A homomorphism to a divisible abelian group extends across any subgroup by the following elementary maximal-extension argument: for a new element a, the exponents k with a^k in the old subgroup form either 0 or nZ; assign any d in the first case and an nth root of the specified h(a^n) in the second. Relations show the extension is well-defined. Zorn gives a global endomorphism. No roots of unity or surjectivity of theta are assumed. Define theta_L(0)=0.
3. Q(theta_L)(t_0)=product t_i^b_i differs from 1 by algebraic independence, also for constant Q and negative coefficients. The quantifier-free existential condition x!=0 and product over positive coefficients != product over negative coefficients uses only ring operations and finitely many theta iterates. Existential closedness gives such an x in K. Thus integral evaluation is injective.
4. In characteristic p, F(x)=x^p is bijective in an algebraically closed field, since (u-v)^p=u^p-v^p and every element has a root. The inverse rho has the unique-root graph y^p=x, without parameters; the characteristic-p binomial identity gives additivity, and multiplication of roots gives multiplicativity. Theta(rho(x))^p=theta(rho(x)^p)=theta(x), including 0; uniqueness gives commutation.
5. If rho=P(theta) on K^*, compose with F=[p]. Then [p]P(theta)-Id=(pP-1)(theta)=0 in the endomorphism ring. Polynomial faithfulness implies pP-1=0 in Z[X], impossible since its constant coefficient is -1 modulo p. This is independent of parameter allowances for E and independent of a classification theorem.
6. Integer scalars are central in every endomorphism ring of an abelian group. Since [p] is a unit with inverse rho, the unique ring map Z[1/p][X] -> E sending X to theta exists. Its kernel clears a p-power denominator to polynomial evaluation; faithfulness kills that kernel. Rho witnesses strictness.

## Boundary checks and exact gap
Degree zero and empty products work; negative integers remain exponentiation by inverses; characteristic p never identifies [p] with the zero map; K^* torsion has no p-primary torsion, but that fact is not required. At zero theta and rho both fix zero. A theta=rho algebraically closed field is not existentially closed, since pX-1 would contradict step 3. Finite fields are not models here; their inverse Frobenius is an integral power, so they cannot witness exclusion. Characteristic zero has no fixed prime inverse Frobenius and is outside the theorem. The group quotient by torsion is a rational vector space; rho there is scalar 1/p, so quotient classification cannot substitute for full-group integral exclusion.

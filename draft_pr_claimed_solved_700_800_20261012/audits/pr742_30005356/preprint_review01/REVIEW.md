## Independent proof and falsification attempts

The claim was treated as a hypothesis. Its success criterion is a nonvacuous admissible model and a definable multiplicative endomorphism unequal to every integral polynomial in theta. The manuscript establishes the stronger every-model assertion. The following checks were deductive, not inferred from code or earlier PASS labels.

**Group-extension step, paper.tex proof section.** Let H be a subgroup of an abelian group A and let the target D be divisible. Compatible partial extensions have chain unions. For a maximal extension on B and a outside B, the exponents k with a^k in B form either zero or nZ. In the latter case choose d with d^n=h(a^n); in the former assign any d. If ba^k=b'a^l in the relation case, k-l=mn and b'=b(a^n)^m, so h(b)d^k=h(b')d^l. This proves well-definedness and multiplication compatibility. Zorn forces B=A. No torsion-freeness, operator injectivity, surjectivity, or field additivity was assumed.

**Faithfulness step, Lemma2.** In L=acl(K(t_0,...,t_d)), integer Laurent monomials in the independent indeterminates have no relation with K*. Thus the subgroup generated by K* and t_0,...,t_(d-1) is the requisite direct product. Prescribing h(c product t_i^m_i)=theta(c) product t_(i+1)^m_i is consistent on that subgroup. Since L* is divisible, it extends to L*, then setting theta_L(0)=0 makes precisely a T-extension. It need not already satisfy ACFH. The first d iterates of t_0 are the independent t_i, so a nonzero Q yields product t_i^b_i unequal to1. Clearing negative exponents yields the manuscript's single existential inequation in positive-power terms. Existential closedness transfers it to K. Roots newly added in L do not create a relation among the original prescribed integer monomials. The proof is independent of a desired endomorphism classification and of the stronger surjectivity result in the source.

**Inverse map and exclusion, Theorem1.** Algebraic closedness supplies p-th roots, and (u-v)^p=u^p-v^p supplies uniqueness. The graph y^p=x therefore defines a function without parameters. Unique roots give multiplication and addition compatibility, including0; the inverse is Frobenius. Multiplicativity alone gives theta(rho(x))^p=theta(x), hence commutation. In the group endomorphism ring [p] is the nonzero Frobenius map; field characteristic does not turn it into the constant-one ring-zero map. If rho=P(theta), composing with [p] gives (pP-1)(theta)=0_E. Faithfulness gives the impossible integral equality pP-1=0; its constant coefficient is -1 modulo p. Composition order causes no problem because integer scalars are central.

**Localized subring, Corollary3.** A unit which is central has a central inverse. Fractions P/p^n map to rho^n composed with P(theta). Multiplying by a common p-power proves well-definedness, ring compatibility, and injectivity by faithful polynomial evaluation. Every element in the generated subring has such a presentation. This proves the corollary exactly, without a claim that the entire ring E is commutative or generated this way.

**Nonvacuity and boundaries.** An identity operator on acl(F_p) is a T-model and has an ACFH extension preserving characteristic. The historical independent countable existential-witness construction was also checked: chain unions remain T-models, and a witness in an extension of the final union was possible at its processing stage. Degree0, empty products, all negative signs, leading zero coefficients, integer coefficients divisible by p, p=2, arbitrary torsion action, noninjective/nonadditive theta, and absence of saturation all behave as claimed. On a nongeneric algebraically closed model with theta=rho, pX-1 vanishes, proving that existential closedness is essential.

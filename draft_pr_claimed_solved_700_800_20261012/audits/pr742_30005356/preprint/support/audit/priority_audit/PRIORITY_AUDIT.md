## The claim that was compared

The candidate proves, for every positive-characteristic existentially closed algebraically closed field with a multiplicative operator θ, that the parameter-free map ρ defined by ρ(x)^p=x is a multiplicative and field automorphism, commutes with θ, lies outside the faithful integral operator ring Z[θ], and generates with θ a faithful subring Z[1/p][θ]. It does not classify all definable endomorphisms. Neither characteristic-zero equality nor positive-characteristic equality over Z[1/p] is resolved.

The priority target is the **explicit application to the unrestricted ACFH question**, including the exclusion from Z[θ]. The following are prior ingredients: the unique-root definition of inverse Frobenius, its algebraic properties, divisible-group extension, and polynomial faithfulness. The localized embedding follows from those ingredients by central localization. The elementary direct existential-closure proof in the candidate is a supporting derivation; this audit does not establish priority for that proof presentation.

In particular, the decisive step is a short deduction: if ρ=P(θ), then composing with Frobenius gives `(pP−1)(θ)=0`; polynomial faithfulness makes this impossible. Shortness or derivability from known results does not itself prove that the observation has already been published, but it sharply limits what can be represented as a new contribution.

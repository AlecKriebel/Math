## Exact hypothesis and success criterion

Fix a prime p. Let K be algebraically closed of characteristic p and let theta be a total multiplicative map: theta(0)=0, theta(1)=1, theta maps K* into K*, and theta(xy)=theta(x)theta(y). Assume (K,theta) is existentially closed among algebraically closed fields with such a map. A successful proof must produce a definable group endomorphism rho:K*->K* and prove inequality from every map

\[
 P(\theta)(x)=\prod_{i=0}^{d}\theta^i(x)^{a_i},\qquad P\in\mathbb Z[X].
\]

The endomorphism ring uses pointwise multiplication as addition and composition as multiplication. Its zero is the constant-one map, its identity is x->x, and the integer n represents x->x^n. The ambient field's characteristic never changes these integer coefficients into elements of F_p.

## Independent derivation

### Positive characteristic models exist

For each p, take the algebraic closure of F_p with theta equal to the identity. This is a T-model. The extension assertion of Corollary 3.16 supplies an ACFH extension, whose characteristic remains p. Thus the theorem is nonvacuous for every prime. The submitted proof uses the correct base theory and the correct notion of existential closedness.

### Divisible target extension, without hidden hypotheses

Let H<=A be abelian groups and h:H->D a homomorphism into divisible D. Order extensions by their domains. The union of a chain is a compatible extension, so Zorn supplies a maximal h_B:B->D. For a outside B, the set

\[
 S=\{k\in\mathbb Z:a^k\in B\}
\]

is a subgroup of Z. If S={0}, the representation ba^k is unique and h_B(ba^k)=h_B(b)d^k extends the map for any d in D. If S=nZ with n>0, choose d with d^n=h_B(a^n). When ba^k=b'a^l, k-l=mn and b'=b(a^n)^m. Hence h_B(b)d^k=h_B(b')d^l. The same formula is well-defined and multiplicative. Both cases contradict maximality unless B=A. Domain divisibility, injectivity of h, and torsion-freeness are unnecessary.

### Faithful evaluation follows directly from EC

Let Q(X)=sum b_i X^i be nonzero with degree d>=0. Form

\[
 L=\overline{K(t_0,\ldots,t_d)},
\]

where the t_i are algebraically independent over K. Inside L*, the subgroup generated by K* and t_0,...,t_(d-1) is the direct product of K* with a free abelian group on these indeterminates. A Laurent-monomial relation over K would, after clearing denominators, contradict algebraic independence. Define on this subgroup

\[
 h\left(c\prod_{i<d}t_i^{m_i}\right)
   =\theta(c)\prod_{i<d}t_{i+1}^{m_i}.
\]

For d=0 the subgroup is just K*. This is a well-defined homomorphism extending theta. L* is divisible because L is algebraically closed. Apply the preceding extension proof with target D=L*, and set the value at zero to zero. The result is a T-extension (L,theta_L) of (K,theta), with theta_L^i(t_0)=t_i for i<=d. It satisfies Q(theta_L)(t_0)!=1, since the nonzero exponent vector (b_0,...,b_d) cannot give the unit Laurent monomial.

The exact formula transferred to K can avoid all negative powers. Put

\[
 A_Q(x)=\prod_{b_i>0}\theta^i(x)^{b_i},\qquad
 B_Q(x)=\prod_{b_i<0}\theta^i(x)^{-b_i}.
\]

Empty products are 1. Then

\[
 \exists x\ (x\ne0\ \land\ A_Q(x)\ne B_Q(x))
\]

is existential in even the ring language plus theta: its matrix contains only positive integer powers and an inequation. On nonzero x it is equivalent to Q(theta)(x)!=1. It holds in L, so EC gives a witness in K. Therefore evaluation Z[X]->End_def(K*) is injective in every ACFH model, with no saturation assumption. This proves the needed input independently of Corollary 5.7 and of the stronger surjectivity argument preceding it in the source.

### Inverse Frobenius and the obstruction

F(x)=x^p is surjective by algebraic closedness and injective because u^p-v^p=(u-v)^p. Its inverse rho has the parameter-free graph y^p=x. Nonzero inputs have nonzero outputs. Uniqueness of roots gives

\[
 \rho(xz)=\rho(x)\rho(z),\qquad
 \rho(x+z)=\rho(x)+\rho(z).
\]

Thus rho is both a multiplicative endomorphism and a field automorphism. Multiplicativity of theta alone gives theta(rho(x))^p=theta(x), so theta(rho(x))=rho(theta(x)); the zero case follows from totalization.

If rho=P(theta), then for all x in K*,

\[
 P(\theta)(x)^p=x,
 \quad\text{hence}\quad (pP(X)-1)(\theta)(x)=1.
\]

Faithfulness forces pP(X)-1=0 in Z[X]. Its constant coefficient is pa_0-1, which is nonzero modulo p. Contradiction. The ring identity has not been confused with the field unit or the ring zero. The endomorphism [p] commutes with every group endomorphism, because f(x^p)=f(x)^p, so there is no order-of-composition issue even if the full ring is noncommutative.

The localization claim also checks: assign P(X)/p^n to rho^n composed with P(theta). Clearing common powers of p proves well-definedness; composing with the corresponding Frobenius power proves injectivity. Finite compositions of definable functions are definable. This yields a strict embedded copy of Z[1/p][X], and yields only a subring of the full definable endomorphism ring.

## Adversarial controls and exact limits

| Attack or boundary | Check and outcome |
| --- | --- |
| Characteristic p kills integer p | False in this ring: [p] is x->x^p and is invertible, not constant one. |
| Negative coefficients invalidate EC | Denominator-cleared A_Q!=B_Q is an existential witness without inverse symbols. |
| Theta must be additive or injective | Neither is used in defining h or in the extension theorem. |
| Roots in L create an unwanted monomial relation | Roots enlarge L*, but the subgroup on which h is prescribed still has unique integer-monomial expressions. |
| Extension must already satisfy ACFH | EC requires a T-extension; L is algebraically closed and theta_L is multiplicative. This is exactly sufficient. |
| Saturation or fixed completion is hidden | The transfer applies directly to the given EC K. The extension preserves its existing operator, torsion action, and characteristic. |
| Nonzero constant Q or negative leading coefficient | The construction includes degree zero, and the inequation handles either sign. |
| Smallest prime p=2 | pa_0-1 is odd, so the same obstruction applies. |
| Remove EC | Falsification control: take any algebraically closed characteristic-p field with theta=rho. Then rho is in Z[theta] and pX-1 annihilates theta. It is a T-model, but the constructed extension witnesses that it is not EC. |
| Finite-field testing | In F_(p^m), rho is the power x->x^(p^(m-1)); this is compatible with the theorem because these finite structures are outside T and ACFH. |
| Characteristic zero | The unique-root argument fails; this proof gives no characteristic-zero classification. |

No inspected source directly states the inverse-Frobenius obstruction or supplies a previously recorded answer to the exact unrestricted question. That observation covers this source audit only; it is not a literature priority certificate. The remaining questions of a characteristic-zero classification, equality with Z[1/p][theta] in characteristic p, and novelty are unassessed or unresolved here. They are not missing steps in the submitted literal counterexample theorem.

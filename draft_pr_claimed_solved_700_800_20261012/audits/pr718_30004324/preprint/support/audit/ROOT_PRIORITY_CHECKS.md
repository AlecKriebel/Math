Checked 2026-10-12 PDT. This supplements the separate independent priority

## Exact comparison target

Smooth integral projective X over an algebraically closed field of arbitrary
characteristic; epsilon(TX;x)>0 at one arbitrary specified closed point;
conclusion X is projective space. Neither Fano, global tangent nefness, a
general point, nor characteristic zero is part of the hypothesis. Equivalent
mechanism searched: every rational normalization through one point very free,
absolute minimum of ample degree, proper pointed family with contracted
section, numerical Picard rank one, Fano reduction.

## Provisional conclusion and framing

No earlier unrestricted resolution has been identified in these checked
sources or searches. The individual ingredients and the method of studying
proper rational-curve families are standard and must be credited accordingly.
The claimed contribution is the fixed-point, characteristic-free reduction to
Picard number one and Fano, followed by the existing FM Fano clause.

This supports continuing preparation subject to the independent priority
report and final manuscript reviews. It does not certify global absence of a
prior proof, an unpublished result, or a proof under another formulation.
Suitable publication wording is that the note proves the stated conjecture,
credits the established special cases and tools, and reports that a dated
bounded audit located no earlier full resolution without asserting first
priority. Discovery of an earlier full theorem would require reassessment
before publication.

## Standard numerical-generation precursor checked during the audit

Andreatta--Occhetta, *Fano manifolds with long extremal rays*, Asian J.
Math.9 (2005),523--544, DOI10.4310/AJM.2005.v9.n4.a5, is an additional
direct precursor. Its arXivmath/0504265v1 §2.3 Proposition2.18(b)
(final-journal Proposition2.20(b)) gives numerical generation of the
locus of a proper pointed rational-curve family by that family's class.
The root inspected the arXiv statement directly; the priority reviewer
inspected the final numbering and traced its earlier references.
The manuscript must describe the contracted-section numerical lemma as
a proof of this standard principle in the present setup, rather than
claiming the principle itself as a new discovery. The journal DOI open
returned an error for the root; the accessible primary preprint supplies
the actual statement.

# Root independent priority position for PR765

Recorded after the mathematical gate and before reading the new priority agent's findings. This is a bounded primary-source audit, not a certificate of first historical priority or exhaustive literature coverage.

The exact target is an existential connected open quasi-conical Dirichlet tower in each d>=2, with both expanding cube sizes and shrinking positive zero-thickness windows selected. The distinguishing conclusion is a complete eigenbasis, hence absence of singular as well as absolutely continuous spectral subspaces. Dense eigenvalues alone, arbitrary abstract diagonalization, bounded Neumann examples, disconnected Euclidean domains, and graph/fractal Laplacians do not constitute that same result.

## Direct primary comparisons

1. Krejcirik--Lotoreichik, DOI10.1112/blms.13113: the final publisher full text was publicly read, including Theorem1, Sections2.2/3 and Section5. The accepted author v2 was also read. The theorem supplies a connected quasi-conical domain with an embedded positive eigenvalue and no absolutely continuous spectrum. Section5 explicitly leaves singular continuous absence open and mentions any finite number of embedded eigenvalues. Neither observation proves completeness. The geometry, parameter freedom, finite-window convergence, and original perturbative method require substantive credit. No claim that this note invented those inputs is permissible.

2. Krejcirik, arxiv2609.28602v1, submitted23Sep2026: latest public abstract-page history still lists v1. The root freshly downloaded the hash-matched PDF and visually inspected page16. OpenProblem1 repeats the singular continuous question. Both displayed point-spectrum identities correctly have closure bars; an erroneous new spectral-review criticism has been separately withdrawn. This dated question supports exact scope, not current or historical priority certification.

3. Behrndt--Khrabustovskyi, Construction of self-adjoint differential operators with prescribed spectral properties, DOI10.1002/mana.201900491, final publisher full text, especially introduction, Theorem2.4 and its proof/discussion: the relevant rooms/passages examples concern bounded Neumann domains and prescribed essential spectral sets. The no-absolutely-continuous argument compares trace-class resolvent powers with a decoupled pure point operator. It does not establish the full connected quasi-conical Dirichlet spectral type. This comparison is based on the stated operator and conclusions, not an assumption that no related technique can apply.

Hempel--Seco--Simon, The essential spectrum of Neumann Laplacians on some bounded singular domains, DOI10.1016/0022-1236(91)90130-W: CaltechAUTHORS primary metadata and abstract identify bounded Neumann rooms/passages and combs with prescribed essential sets.

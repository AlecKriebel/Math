The candidate does not create the geometry, finite-window convergence, finite-cluster perturbation theory, or the abstract fact that an exhaustive family of finite reducing subspaces yields pure point type. The potentially new increment is their realization with nonresonant future cube widths and simultaneous summable transport of a growing exhaustive family of finite spectral clusters. An earlier theorem accomplishing that realization would defeat the narrow novelty assessment; none was located in these bounded checks.

The abstract route is closed as an anticipation argument unless a materially new geometric-realization theorem is produced. A compact perturbation furnished by a Hilbert-space theorem is not automatically the difference of resolvents for allowable Dirichlet face windows. Requiring that realization simply restates the central difficulty.

## Claim being tested

For each integer d >= 2, some connected open quasi-conical chain of expanding cubes, with both cube widths and positive shrinking zero-thickness face windows chosen inductively, has a form-defined Dirichlet Laplacian with complete orthonormal eigenbasis and spectrum = essential spectrum = [0,infinity), dense positive eigenvalues, no zero eigenfunction, and neither continuous spectral subspace. Neither arbitrary preassigned widths nor smooth boundaries nor positive-length passages is claimed.

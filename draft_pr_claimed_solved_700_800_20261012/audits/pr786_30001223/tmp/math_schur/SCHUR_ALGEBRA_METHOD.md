# Checkable tensor-orbit computation

At p=3 use E^(tensor5) with its 32binary-word basis. For every 2x2 nonnegative contingency table of total5, put a 1 at a matrix entry exactly when the input/output words have that table. The 56matrices have disjoint supports and form the full centralizer of the place-permutation action: matrix entries are constant on exactly these orbits. Their independence and all 3136multiplication products are verified modulo 3.

Let E,F act as sums of single-place raising/lowering operators, and H count zeros minus ones. C=EF+FE+2H^2 is verified central against every basis element. z=C^3 is verified idempotent. For weight a+b=5 let e_(a,b) select tensor words of that weight. The faithful algebra left ideals A e_(a,b)(1-z) have dimensions 4 at(4,1) and 6 at(3,2). For e=e_(3,2)(1-z), the corner eAe has basis e,N=eCe with N nonzero and N^2=0. This is a local dual-number algebra over F_3 and every field extension, so Ae is an absolutely indecomposable projective. For the (4,1) weight, the corner is scalar.

The 3 simple highest-weight labels are(5),(4,1),(3,2). L(5)=Sym^5(E) is simple and has C-eigenvalue1. L(4,1) has weights(4,1),(1,4); L(3,2) has weights(3,2),(2,3), and both have C-eigenvalue0. Therefore e annihilates the first 2 simples and has rank 1 on L(3,2). This identifies Ae as the projective cover P(3,2), of dimension 6, without assuming a decomposition matrix.

The module det(E) tensor Sym^3(E) is constructed from the tensor space as the quotient of the antisymmetric first 2 factors by place-permutation coinvariants on the last 3 factors. Taking a wedge representative and summing coordinates with equal symmetric monomial yields each of the 56 action matrices. Every product is checked against the tensor-algebra structure constants. The cube submodule and 2-dimensional quotient each have full 4-dimensional matrix-algebra image, hence remain simple over the algebraic closure. Exact intertwiner equations give Hom(M,socle)=0, Hom(M,quotient)=1, End(M)=1. Thus M has head L(3,2) and dimension 4. Since its projective cover has dimension 6, it is not projective.

This is a finite exact calculation for rank 2, p=3. It supports the analytic reciprocity identification M=I_2(4,1) and the universal derivation. It does not enumerate all ranks or prove arbitrary truncation preserves projectivity. The formal-root family checks at 10 odd primes are additional controls, not the proof for all odd primes.

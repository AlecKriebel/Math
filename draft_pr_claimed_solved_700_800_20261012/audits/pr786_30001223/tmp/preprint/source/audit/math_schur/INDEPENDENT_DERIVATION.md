# Independent Schur/highest-weight derivation before historical audits

This worker previously performed unrelated PR781 administrative review. No PR786 peer/root mathematical conclusions were supplied. The initial position was frozen before author proof; the following was derived after reading that proof and primary bodies, before any historical audits.

Fix an algebraically closed field k of odd characteristic p, q=1, r=2p-1, lambda=(r-1,1). The Young permutation module is the natural permutation representation k^r. Since r is invertible, it is the direct sum of constants and the augmentation A. Given any nonzero submodule U of A, a nonconstant vector v produces a nonzero coordinate difference v-(ij)v. Its permutation conjugates span A. Thus A is simple. The Specht polytabloids of shape (r-1,1) are those differences, so Y^lambda=A with this exact Young label.

At rank two put m=2p-3. In Sym^m E, the highest-weight cyclic submodule has monomial indices J={0,...,p-3} union {p,...,2p-3}, because binomial(m,j) is nonzero mod p exactly on J. Any monomial of J reaches the highest monomial under the opposite divided powers; hence this submodule W is simple. The missing indices p-2,p-1 form a simple quotient. Their respective highest weights are (m,0) and (p-1,p-2). A complement must consist exactly of the two missing torus-weight spaces, but the coefficient taking index p-2 to zero is 1; hence no complement exists. Determinant twisting gives socle L_2(2p-2,1) and head L_2(p,p-1), distinct.

For Sym^(2p-1) E every binomial coefficient is nonzero mod p. The same highest-monomial argument makes it simple. Rank-two costandard reciprocity then identifies I_2(lambda)=nabla_2(lambda): the only strictly dominating partition is (r), whose costandard module has no lambda composition factor. Consequently I_2(lambda) is not contravariantly self-dual, so it is not projective. This also admits a direct Schur-algebra test at p=3, developed independently in schur_tensor_check.py; the six-dimensional projective cover of its head cannot be the four-dimensional I_2(4,1).

For the all-ranks quantifier, assume I_N(lambda) projective, N>=2. Polynomial projective-injective modules are indecomposable tiltings, so I_N(lambda)=T_N(mu). A tilting's highest label dominates every composition-factor label, in particular lambda; since lambda=(r-1,1), mu is either (r) or lambda. These both fit rank two. Exact polynomial weight truncation therefore sends both sides to I_2(lambda)=T_2(mu). An injective tilting is projective, contradicting the explicit rank-two obstruction. No assertion that truncation preserves arbitrary projectives or heads is made.

Independent boundary probes: the initial r=p candidate is not adopted: its augmentation does not split from constants, so the r=2p-1 construction matters. At p=2 the displayed m=2p-3 and submodule formula collapse, lambda=(2,1) is column-2-regular, and the odd-prime theorem makes no assertion. Rank one is excluded because lambda has length two. Char0 and q generic are semisimple and outside the counterexample's hypothesis. Empty partition gives the trivial degree-zero category and no contradiction. A stronger restriction to characteristic zero with a nontrivial root parameter is not refuted.

Its printed 9 explicitly includes q=1 in positive characteristic. These are established structural facts, not a use of its classification conjecture.

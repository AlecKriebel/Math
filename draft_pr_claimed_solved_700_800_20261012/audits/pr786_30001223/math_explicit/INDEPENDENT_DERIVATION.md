# Independent explicit-family derivation, frozen before historical/peer conclusions

Claim tested: for each odd prime p, algebraically closed k of characteristic p, q=1, r=2p-1 and lambda=(2p-2,1), the classical Young module is simple while I_n(lambda) is not projective for every n>=2. This negates the broad simple-head-to-existential-projectivity implication. It is not a classification of projective injectives or a characteristic-zero quantum-root claim.

The Young side is direct. M^(r-1,1)=k^r is the point permutation module. r=-1 modp is invertible, so k^r=k1 plus augmentation A. A nonzero submodule vector cannot have all coordinates equal, since it has sum zero and r is invertible. For some i,j its difference with its (ij)-translate is a nonzero multiple of e_i-e_j. Conjugation supplies all differences, spanning A. Thus A is simple over k, not merely over Fp. The Specht polytabloids for shape(r-1,1) span exactly A. The Young summand containing that Specht module is therefore A, not the trivial summand. Hence Y^lambda is simple of dimension2p-2, with correct labeling.

For GL2, diagonal torus characters on Sym^m(E) are the distinct integer weights(m-i,i), i=0,...,m. A rational torus-invariant subspace decomposes into these one-dimensional weight spaces, even though characters would coincide after restriction to finite diagonal matrices. Invariance under all parameters of an upper/lower unipotent implies invariance under each formal coefficient operator by infinite-field Vandermonde extraction. These two facts are the explicit proof mechanism.

For m=2p-1, its base-p digits are(p-1,1), so all coefficients binom(m,j),0<=j<=m, are nonzero modulo p. Every monomial can be sent to x^m by a formal upper coefficient1, and x^m generates every monomial under lower coefficients. Sym^(2p-1)(E) is simple with highest weight(2p-1,0). This also follows directly from (1+t)^m=(1+t^p)(1+t)^(p-1).

For m=2p-3 the nonzero binomial support from x^m is S=[0,p-3] union[p,2p-3], omitting precisely p-2,p-1. W=span(v_i:i in S) is the image E^(F) tensorSym^(p-3)(E) by multiplication, with disjoint monomial supports, hence invariant. The same monomial generation argument makes W simple. The two quotient weights are(p-1,p-2),(p-2,p-1). Opposite coefficient operators connect them with nonzero scalar p-1, so the quotient is simple. Any invariant complement to W must be its unique torus complement span(v_(p-2),v_(p-1)). The upper coefficient of degreep-2 sends v_(p-2) to x^m, so that complement fails. Thus the extension is nonsplit; W is the full simple socle and the quotient is the simple head. At p=3 both have dimension2, but distinct highest weights, so dimension alone cannot distinguish them.

After determinant twist, nabla2(lambda)=det tensorSym^(2p-3)(E) has socle label lambda=(2p-2,1) and head label(p,p-1), which differ for every odd p. Only(r) strictly dominates lambda among degree-r partitions. The standard injective costandard-filtration reciprocity formula makes I2(lambda)=nabla2(lambda), because the dominating row costandard is simple and has no L(lambda) constituent. This is an actual identification of the injective hull, not an unverified summand assertion.

An indecomposable polynomial projective-injective is self-dual under contravariant duality and tilting. This can be rederived from the highest-weight category: injectives have costandard filtrations; duality fixes simples and preserves Grothendieck classes. If I(lambda) is projective, its dual is another injective. Distinct injective characters are independent by the unitriangular costandard multiplicity matrix, so the dual is the same injective. It then has both standard and costandard filtrations. Conversely an injective tilting is self-dual and hence projective. Different head and socle labels therefore obstruct rank2 projectivity.

For arbitrary N>=2, suppose I_N(lambda)=T_N(mu) is projective. Its tilting highest weight mu dominates the simple socle label lambda, so mu_1>=r-1. The only degree-r possibilities are(r) and(r-1,1), both length<=2. Exact rank truncation therefore sends both sides to I2(lambda)=T2(mu), with no possibility that a long tilting label disappears. This would make I2(lambda) an injective tilting and projective, contradiction. No assertion that an arbitrary idempotent functor preserves projectivity or heads is used. This proves failure of every permitted existential rank, including N arbitrarily larger thanr.

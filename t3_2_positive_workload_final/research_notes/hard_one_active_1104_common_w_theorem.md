# A common-potential theorem for all 1,104 hard-family one-active rows

**Proof-first scoped theorem, repaired and audited 2026-08-12 PDT.**  A
hostile replay of the first frozen version passed the generalized 951 rows,
the exact 44 rows, the four frozen alternatives, the reflected-debt scope,
and the finite partition, but correctly rejected the direct 99 and open six
interfaces for an unproved arbitrary-subpower entropy step.  The frozen
multi-service theorem pinned in Section 8 repairs exactly those 105 rows and
has received a separate strict independent PASS.  The resulting 1,104-row
local composition is therefore audited at its stated scope.  It is not a
pair-recurrence theorem: the two-active and all-active rows still have to be
composed under the same pair-fixed potential.  Every pair and global flag
remains false.

The finite companion
`src/hard_one_active_1104_common_w.py` has only two roles: it verifies the
exact partition of the 1,104 incidences and checks the support predicates of
the analytic lemmas used below.  It does not enumerate orientations,
histories, population boxes, or reaction words.

There is one necessary repair to the inherited labels.  Of the eight rows
called `family_ii_axis_no_history`, only four have a genuinely frozen
zero-inactive face.  The other four admit positive historical debt for a
permitted strong orientation.  They are nevertheless covered: after the
correct coordinate change, all nonfrozen exact-Family-II rows satisfy the
abstract hypotheses of the already audited mixed fast-Schur theorem.

## 1. Fixed-class scope and common potential

Fix one of the 333 hard support pairs, arbitrary strongly connected directed
graphs on its two linkage supports, arbitrary fixed positive rate constants,
and a closed irreducible physical class \(\Gamma\).  Fix **any** linear
correction \(\ell\) selected for that pair by the other dimensional charts,
and put

\[
 G_\ell(x)=K_\ell+\sum_{j=1}^3\log(x_j!)+\ell\cdot x\ge1,
 \qquad W_\ell=G_\ell^4 .                                  \tag{1.1}
\]

The factorial term dominates the fixed linear term, so \(W_\ell\) is
proper on \(\Gamma\).  A bounded reaction changes \(\ell\cdot x\) by
\(O_\ell(1)\); this observation will be used only after the actual
factorial endpoint has been retained.

Relabel a one-active entrance as

\[
                 (U,V,I)=(u,n,0),\qquad u=n^{o(1)},            \tag{1.2}
\]

where \(V\) is the old active species and \(I\) is the missing cofactor of
the distinguished top source \(V+I\).  Work on the all-species reflected
lift from a fixed reference \((x^\circ,0)\), and invoke a service block only
when the incoming selected mark satisfies \(D_V>0\).  Until the first
crossing \(V<n\), reflection is inactive and

\[
                     D_V(t)=D_V(0)+V(t)-n.                     \tag{1.3}
\]

Thus the first net service, which first reaches \(V<n\), services actual old
debt.  A repaired direct or open block may continue beyond that first
service: once reflection reaches zero, every later service is a valid
surplus service and decreases the historical residual \(H_V=V-D_V\).
If \(D_V=0\) at the entrance, then \(V=V-D_V\le x_V^\circ\); a divergent
sequence cannot remain in this one-active chart.  This is the fixed-class
alternative which replaces any claim about arbitrary unmarked physical
starts.

Choose the moving cutoff used by the imported local theorem.  At every
local stop use the following path labels, in priority order.

1. \(D\): the actual clean completion endpoint of the selected service
   block.  For the single-service imports this is the included first
   crossing \(V<n\); for the repaired direct/open mechanisms it is the
   included \(K\)-th net service, while the internal first net service is
   the one which lowers incoming old debt.
2. \(P\): the included cutoff-causing reaction lands at an **outer no-fast
   return**, with the active excursion closed.
3. \(B\): every cutoff first crossed while an excursion is open, including
   a path which would later clean up to the same population face.
4. \(E\): every other paid or competing local endpoint.

Only \(P\) is the exact outer-chart handoff.  The distinction is pathwise,
not inferred from a later endpoint.  Every causing reaction and its actual
post-jump \(W_\ell\)-cost is included.  Splitting a previously estimated
boundary union into the disjoint subsets \(P\) and \(B\) can only decrease
each endpoint-weighted bound.

Its normalized supports split analytically into 17 exact-cloud, 111 mixed,

In the common notation of (1.2), for every fixed \(p\) the combined theorem

 \mathbb P(D^c)&\le n^{-1+o(1)},\\

## 4. The 99 direct physical-source rows

After relabelling the active species as \(C\), every direct row has a mixed
linkage containing the physically enabled complex \(C\) and at least one
active-free complex.  This predicate is the only finite input.

Put \(u=A_0+B_0\) and run

\[
                              K=1+u                              \tag{4.1}
\]

net services, rather than attempting to pay an arbitrary subpower cloud
with one \(\log n\) decrement.  Strip the common active molecule from every
top complex and use active time \(s=\int_0^tC_r\,dr\).  Top-to-top reactions
are immigration, death, or conversion of independently labelled inactive
particles; they never branch a particle.

Choose a simple directed path in the \(C\)-linkage from \(C\) to its first
active-free vertex.  If its first edge is already a service, its
constant-rate active-time clock gives the required event directly.
Otherwise reserve one stripped-zero birth, tag its product, and reserve the
successive clocks of that tag along the path.  This event has a fixed
positive probability in a fixed active-time interval, independently of
every background particle.  A background service before the tag finishes
is already a success.  The \(K\)-service active-time horizon
\(S_K\) is therefore dominated by a sum of \(K\) geometric blocks, so for
every fixed \(r\),

\[
 \mathbb E S_K^r\le C_rK^r,
 \qquad
 \mathbb P\{S_K>CK+y\}\le Ce^{-cy}.                            \tag{4.2}
\]

If \(M_s=A_s+B_s\) and \(N_s\) is the service count, then before a competing
active-free-source reaction

\[
                         M_s\le u+\Pi_s+2N_s,                    \tag{4.3}
\]

where \(\Pi\) is a fixed-rate Poisson process in active time.  Every
active-free source has total physical propensity at most
\(C(1+M_s)^2\); since \(C_s\ge n-K\), its active-time hazard is at most
\(C(1+M_s)^2/(n-K)\).  The compensator formula and (4.2)--(4.3), including
the bounded causing jump, imply for every fixed polynomial endpoint weight
\(F\)

\[
                       \mathbb E[F;E]
       \le \frac{C_F}{n}(1+u+K)^{d_F}.                           \tag{4.4}
\]

Stop also at the included first inactive-mass crossing of
\(L_n=n^{1/16}\).  Because \(u+K=n^{o(1)}\), the Poisson and
negative-binomial tails make this boundary, with every fixed polynomial
endpoint bias, \(O(n^{-M})\) for every fixed \(M\).  On clean completion,

\[
\begin{aligned}
 C_\sigma&=n-K\quad\hbox{on }D,\\
 \mathbb E\sigma^r&\le C_rK^rn^{-r},\\
 \mathbb E\!\left[\sum_{i\ne C}\log(X_{\sigma,i}!);D\right]
       &\le C(1+u+K)\log(2+u+K).
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{4.5}
\]

The last estimate is precisely the step missing from the first frozen
version.  For arbitrary fixed \(\ell\), the entire inactive
factorial-linear term is an endpoint difference, while

\[
 \log\frac{(n-K)!}{n!}=-K\log n+O(K^2/n).
\]

Since \(K=1+u\) and \(\log K=o(\log n)\), the clean active reward amortizes
the whole possible inactive endpoint entropy:

\[
                     \mathbb E\Delta G_\ell\le-cK\log n.       \tag{4.6}
\]

The endpoint-weighted defect and boundary estimates give
\(\mathbb E|\Delta G_\ell|^r\le n^{o(1)}(\log n)^r\).  With
\(G_0=G_\ell(X_0)\asymp n\log n\), the last three terms in the exact identity

\[
 \Delta W_\ell
 =4G_0^3\Delta G_\ell+6G_0^2(\Delta G_\ell)^2
   +4G_0(\Delta G_\ell)^3+(\Delta G_\ell)^4                   \tag{4.7}
\]

are \(n^{-1+o(1)}\) relative to the leading
\(G_\ell(X_0)^3K\log n\) margin.  Thus the actual completing, defect, or
boundary endpoint satisfies

\[
 \mathbb E[W_\ell(X_\sigma)-W_\ell(X_0)+\sigma]
       \le-cG_\ell(X_0)^3(1+u)\log n.                           \tag{4.8}
\]

The first service reduces incoming reflected debt.  Continuing after that
mark reaches zero changes only the auxiliary reflected mark; later physical
services are legitimate surplus services.  This is the frozen audited
multi-service theorem, not a bounded-start extension of the killed-phase
estimate rejected by the first hostile replay.

## 5. The 48 exact-Family-II-labelled rows

The old label is misleading in the present hard table.  These are not the
five invariant-axis supports of the earlier exact Family-II lemma.  We now
show that the stronger abstract mixed theorem applies.

Keep normalized active species \(V=C\).  Let \(q=V+I\) be the unique
active-bearing source, let \(I\) be its inactive cofactor, and let \(U\) be
the other inactive species.  Every one of the 48 rows then has both linkage
supports inside

\[
 \mathcal B\cup\mathcal C\cup\{q\},\qquad
 \mathcal B=\{0,U,2U\},\qquad
 \mathcal C=\{I,2I,U+I\}.                                  \tag{5.1}
\]

Up to the two choices of cofactor and deletion of optional lower vertices,
there are eight translated support pairs.  In every one:

1. both linkage supports have at least two complexes;
2. the cofactor cap is zero;
3. at least one linkage, after deleting \(q\), contains both a member of
   \(\mathcal B\) and a member of \(\mathcal C\); and
4. the linkage containing \(q\) is not an exact base-open pair
   \(\{aU,q\}\).

These are exactly the abstract hypotheses in Section 1 of the mixed
fast-Schur proof.  That proof explicitly uses the finite 111-template table
only to verify these two structural conditions.  Its Schur complement,
strong-cut diagonal inverse, maximal-source killed Green estimate, actual
terminal entropy majorant, and physical-duration recursion therefore prove
the following corollary independently of whether the support appeared in
the original 111-row table.

> **Lemma 5.1 (mixed-Schur extension).**  Every historically reachable
> positive-debt entrance (1.2) in any of the 48 exact-Family-II-labelled
> rows which is not on a frozen face has a physical stopped block satisfying
> (3.1).  The block works for every strong orientation, positive rate
> vector, and fixed \(\ell\); it has every fixed endpoint and duration
> moment, retains the actual endpoint, and uses the path labels of Section
> 1.

Indeed, the ideal Schur macro has at most two \(q\)-sourced cleanup windows.
The strong cut bounds exact returns away from one.  A first nonfast
insertion has sourcewise probability \(n^{-1+o(1)}\), and the maximal base
degree gives the killed factorial Green function.  At service the active
factorial change is exactly \(-\log n\); the one-sided spectator entropy
majorant is \(o(\log n)\) because \(U=n^{o(1)}\).  All correction increments
are bounded.  Thus the proof gives

\[
 \mathbb E\sigma^p=n^{o(1)},\qquad
 \mathbb E[W_\ell(X_\sigma)-W_\ell(X_0)+\sigma]
       \le-cG_\ell(X_0)^3\log n.                              \tag{5.2}
\]

No resistance word or orientation enumeration is substituted for this
aggregate kernel.

### 5.1 Repair of the eight no-history labels

Four of the eight labelled rows have \((U,I)=(0,0)\), and neither linkage
contains the zero complex.  Every source then requires \(U\) or \(I\),
while \(q\) also requires \(I\).  No reaction is enabled.  If this state is
in a closed irreducible class, that class is the singleton, its marked lift
starts with zero debt, and a positive-\(D_V\) entrance is impossible.  These
are the four genuine frozen alternatives.

The claimed exclusion is false for the other four rows.  One translated
support pair is

\[
             \{I,U+I\},\qquad \{U,2I,2U,q\}.                   \tag{5.3}
\]

Give both supports their complete directed graphs, a permitted strong
orientation.  Starting from the no-fast base \((U,V,I)=(1,n,0)\), the
physical word

\[
 U\to2I,\quad 2I\to q,\quad I\to U+I,\quad
 U\to q,\quad 2I\to U                                      \tag{5.4}
\]

has successive relative states

\[
 (1,0,0),(0,0,2),(0,1,1),(1,1,1),(0,2,2),(1,2,0),             \tag{5.5}
\]

where the coordinates are \((U,V-n,I)\).  It returns to the same no-fast
base with two units of positive reflected \(V\)-debt.  Extra initial
\(U\)-molecules are inert slack, so the same construction works for every
\(U\ge1\).  Because the chosen linkage graphs are complete, the reverse
word is physical as well; these two states lie in the same closed reversible
communication class.  Hence four positive-spectator rows must not be removed
by a no-history declaration.  Lemma 5.1 supplies their correct analytic
route.

This counterhistory repairs a proof label; it is not a counterexample to
the local theorem or to T3-2.

## 6. The six open wholly-top rows

All six normalized rows have the exact supports

\[
                 \{C,A+C\},\qquad \{0,B,2B,B+C\}.              \tag{6.1}
\]

The first linkage is necessarily bidirected and has exact dynamics

\[
 C\longrightarrow A+C\quad\hbox{at rate }\alpha C,
 \qquad
 A+C\longrightarrow C\quad\hbox{at rate }\beta CA.             \tag{6.2}
\]

It preserves \((B,C)\).  Construct the unlocalized stopping rule solely
from the second-linkage clocks and the autonomous \((B,C)\)-path: stop at
the clean completion of \(K=1+A_0\) net services or at the included first
competing active-free-source reaction.  Conditional on that entire path and
on \(S^\circ=\int_0^{\tau^\circ}C_tdt\), the independent-particle formula
is exact:

\[
 A_{\tau^\circ}\ \stackrel d=
 \operatorname{Bin}(A_0,e^{-\beta S^\circ})
 +\operatorname{Pois}\!\left(\frac{\alpha}{\beta}
                    (1-e^{-\beta S^\circ})\right).              \tag{6.3}
\]

Thus every fixed endpoint moment is polynomial in \(1+A_0\), and the
expected \(A\)-factorial entropy is
\(O(1+A_0\log(2+A_0))\), uniformly in the stopped \((B,C)\)-path.

Write \(T=B+C\).  At \(B=b>0\), the total \(T\)-exit rate is at least
\(cmb\), whereas every active-free-source propensity sums to at most
\(C(1+b^2)\).  The probability that a competing lower clock wins the next
relevant race is at most \(C(1+b)/m\).  At \(b=0\), aggregate all outgoing
zero-complex channels.  The designated zero-source reaction which launches
the attempt is **allowed**; it is not a defect.  Here and in the endpoint
label \(E\), “defect” means a competing active-free-source reaction outside
that designated \(b=0\) launch.

If a launch lands in \(B\) or \(2B\), the next clean \(T\)-exit is a net
service.  If it lands in \(T\), its first clean exit is neutral when paired
with the launch; landing in positive \(B\) then enables the following net
service, while landing at zero restarts the attempt.  The probability

\[
 p_*=\mathbb P_0\{0\to B\hbox{ or }2B\}
 +\mathbb P_0\{0\to T\}\mathbb P_T\{T\to B\hbox{ or }2B\}      \tag{6.4}
\]

is positive for every strong orientation.  Otherwise \(\{0,T\}\) would be
a closed proper subset of the four-vertex linkage.  Neutral attempts are
therefore geometric.  Iterating the all-clock kernel gives

\[
\begin{aligned}
 B_j&\le j+3,\\
 \mathbb E\tau^r&\le C_rK^r,\\
 \mathbb E[(1+A+B+|C-n|+\tau)^r;E]
       &\le \frac{C_r}{n}(1+A_0+K)^{d_r}.
\end{aligned}                                                    \tag{6.5}
\]

Every neutral launch/exit pair has net active reward zero and every clean
macrocycle has one final unpaired \(T\)-exit, so \(C_\sigma=n-K\) on clean
completion.

Only after \(\tau^\circ\) is constructed do we impose the moving cutoff
\(L_n=n^{1/16}\) on \(A\).  Conditional on its independent operational
horizon, an upcrossing requires the immigrant subsystem to contain at least
\(L_n-A_0-1\) particles immediately before a birth.  Its stationary Poisson
factorial tail beats the polynomial bound
\(\mathbb E(1+S^\circ)^r\le C_rn^r(1+K)^r\).  Hence the included
upcrossing endpoint, with every fixed polynomial size bias, is
\(O(n^{-M})\) for all fixed \(M\).  This order of construction avoids
conditioning (6.3) on an \(A\)-dependent stopping time.

The active factorial reward is \(-K\log n+O(K^2/n)\); equations
(6.3)--(6.5) bound the whole inactive factorial-linear endpoint cost by
\(O_\ell(K\log(2+K))\).  Write
\(\Delta_\sigma W_\ell=W_\ell(X_\sigma)-W_\ell(X_0)\).  The
subpower-cloud amortization and the exact fourth-power calculation of
Section 4 give

\[
 \mathbb E[\Delta_\sigma W_\ell+\sigma]
       \le-cG_\ell(X_0)^3(1+A_0)\log n.                         \tag{6.6}
\]

Every completing, competing, and cutoff-causing reaction is included.
Refining the controlled boundary union into the path-labelled \(P/B\)
partition of Section 1 preserves the estimate.  The first net service
reduces incoming debt; later services after reflection reaches zero are
surplus.

## 7. Unified local theorem

> **Theorem 7.1 (hard one-active 1,104 common-\(W\) theorem).**  Fix a hard
> support pair, arbitrary strong orientations, arbitrary positive rates, a
> closed irreducible class, and any fixed pair correction \(\ell\).  Every
> divergent historically reachable positive-debt one-active failed entrance
> has an all-reaction physical stopped block, selected from Sections 3--6,
> such that for every fixed \(p\),
> \[
> \begin{aligned}
>  \mathbb E(1+U_\sigma+I_\sigma+|V_\sigma-n|)^p&=n^{o(1)},\\
>  \mathbb E\sigma^p&\le n^{2p+o(1)},\\
>  \mathbb E[W_\ell(X_\sigma)-W_\ell(X_0)+\sigma]
>    &\le-cG_\ell(X_0)^3\log n .
> \end{aligned}                                                    \tag{7.1}
> \]
> In particular, every required endpoint moment of some order \(q>8\) is
> available.  The causing reaction is included, \(P/B\) are path-labelled,
> and the actual endpoint is reclassified under the identical
> \(W_\ell\).  A row whose positive-debt entrance is impossible is covered
> by the marked finite/frozen alternative, not by a fictitious service
> probability.

### Proof

The support partition (2.1) is disjoint and exhaustive.  The generalized
rows use (3.1), the direct rows use Section 4, the nonfrozen exact rows use
Lemma 5.1, the four frozen exact rows cannot meet the positive-debt
hypothesis, and the open rows use (6.6).  All four stochastic blocks use the
same state function (1.1), so endpoint reclassification introduces no
comparison constant or continuation toll.  Taking the worst duration and
endpoint bounds gives (7.1).  This proves the local theorem. \(\square\)

The theorem deliberately stops here.  The old hostile audit and the
separately frozen strict repair audit together certify this one-active
premise, but nothing beyond it.  It proves the one-active premise
needed by the hard-family fixed-class composition, but it does not certify
any of the 333 pairs until the two-active, all-active, passing-descriptor,
and marked-gluing obligations are independently composed.

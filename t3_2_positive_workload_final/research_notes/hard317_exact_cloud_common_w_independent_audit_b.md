## 1. Exact contraction and nonstatic scope

For a lower edge with source \(cU+bI\), the ordered-Green atom correctly
separates its direct level-zero clock from its opened-excursion occupation:

\[
 A_e=d_e+\lambda_0\mathbb E_1J_e,
 \qquad
 \widehat A_e=d_e+\lambda_0p_e.                                \tag{1.1}
\]

The excursion occupation measure is exactly

\[
 g_i={\pi_i\over\pi_0\lambda_0},
 \qquad
 {\pi_i\over\pi_0}
 =\rho^i{(u)_{\underline{ai}}
            \over i!(v+1)^{\overline i}}.                       \tag{1.2}
\]

Consequently

\[
 A_e={\kappa_e\rho^b(u)_{\underline{ab+c}}
          \over(v+1)^{\overline b}}
 \sum_{j\ge0}{\rho^j(u-ab-c)_{\underline{aj}}
          \over j!(v+b+1)^{\overline j}}.                       \tag{1.3}
\]

The reversible ordered-double-Green calculation gives the sourcewise
relative estimate

\[
 0\le A_e-\widehat A_e
       \le {C(1+u)^2\over n}A_e.                                \tag{1.4}
\]

This remains valid for \(b=0\): the direct term \(d_e\) cancels from the
killing error.  The audit independently replayed the occupation identity,
the reversibility of the two temporal orderings, the edge-size-biased
factorial law, and the downward-carrier supersolution.

Every proper-enabled base in the seventeen supports has a feasible lower
source.  Structurally:

- every source-zero support has a source with \(w_0\le u\);
- the source-one support without \(0\) contains \(I\), with \(w_1(I)=1\);
- the source-two support without \(0\) contains \(U\), with \(w_2(U)=1\);
- every other source-one or source-two support contains \(0\).

If \(u<a\), a feasible lower source with \(b>0\) would have
\(w_a\ge a>u\), so every feasible source is a direct \(I\)-free source.
If none exists, both linkages are disabled and the face is static.  Starting
the reflected lift from the fixed-class reference cannot create positive
\(V\)-debt on that face, so the historical hypothesis excludes it.

At every nonstatic base, (1.3) has a feasible term with \(b\le2\), a
positive falling factorial, and a series whose first term is one.  Since
\(v=n+O(L_n)\), (1.4) therefore implies

\[
                         \sum_e\widehat A_e\ge c n^{-2}.         \tag{1.5}
\]

This is the exact lower-clock input used by the duration proof.

## 2. Shifted cleanup and defect endpoint

If \(y\to z\) fires at carrier level \(i=b_y+j\), the post-firing cleanup
has the exact parameters

\[
 s=u-w_a(y)+w_a(z),qquad
 w'=v+b_y-b_z,qquad I'=j+b_z.                                  \tag{2.1}
\]

For a direct branch, \(b_y=j=0\), so \(w'=v-b_z\), as required.  The
ordered-Green atom correctly splits this direct branch from the opened
edge-size-biased branch.  Its explicit polynomial supersolution has
downward increments

\[
 D_p(i)={C_p\over ni}(1+s+i)^{p+2}.                             \tag{2.2}
\]

The death contribution dominates the endpoint-weighted lower hazard, while
the birth/death error is \(O((1+s)^a/n)=o(1)\).  Stopped Dynkin and the
factorial opened-level law yield

\[
 \mathbb E[(1+U_E+I_E+|V_E-v|)^p;
       \hbox{second lower firing}\mid e]
 \le {C_p(1+u)^{p+3}\over n}.                                  \tag{2.3}
\]

The second firing is included in the endpoint.  Thus a defect that also
services \(V\) or crosses a cutoff is charged to the defect kernel, not
silently cleaned or relabelled.

## 4. Physical-time recursion

By (1.5), level-zero local time to the next lower macro is exponential with
all fixed moments at most \(C_pn^{2p}\).  Conditional on an opening, a
proper excursion has moments \(O(n^{-p}(1+u)^{c_p})\); openings form a
Poisson process of polynomial intensity in level-zero local time.  The
compound-Poisson moment formula therefore gives, for one contracted macro
of physical duration \(\eta\),

\[
                         b_p(u):=\mathbb E_u\eta^p
       \le C_pn^{2p}(1+u)^{c_p}.                                \tag{4.1}
\]

The audit checked the required time-marked recursion rather than relying
only on a first-moment occupation estimate.  Define

\[
 M_jf(u)=\mathbb E_u[\eta^j f(U');\Delta H_a=0]
\]

and let \(m_p\) be the \(p\)-th duration moment until the next strict drop.
Then

\[
 (I-Q_n)m_p=b_p+
       \sum_{j=1}^{p-1}{p\choose j}M_jm_{p-j}.                  \tag{4.2}
\]

The carrier factorial tail and bounded macro displacement give

\[
 M_j(1+U)^r(u)
 \le C_{j,r}n^{2j}(1+u)^{r+c_{j,r}}.                            \tag{4.3}
\]

Induction in \(p\), followed by the polynomial Green operator, proves

\[
                   m_p(u)\le C_pn^{2p}(1+u)^{c_p}.              \tag{4.4}
\]

The strong Markov property repeats (4.4) for at most three strict-drop
episodes.  This proves physical, rather than reaction-count, duration

\[
                  \mathbb E\sigma^p
       \le C_pn^{2p}(1+u)^{c_p}=n^{2p+o(1)}.                    \tag{4.5}
\]

## 5. Actual-endpoint entropy

Put \(B_\ell(u)=\log(u!)+\ell_Uu\).  For one equality episode let \(Q\)
be continuation and \(S\) the strict-drop kernel retaining its actual
spectator endpoint.  With

\[
                         h_C=B_\ell+C\log(u+e),                  \tag{5.1}
\]

the maximal-degree alternatives give

\[
                         Qh_C+SB_\ell-h_C<0                     \tag{5.2}
\]

outside a finite set.  If a maximal-degree strict-drop edge exists, its
bounded endpoint costs at most \(j_*\log(u+e)+O(1)\), while termination
omits the \(C\log(u+e)\) proof term.  If none exists, a maximal-degree
descending continuation pays \(-c\log(u+e)+O(1)\); positive edges have one
lower degree and contribute only \(O(u^{-1}\log u)\).  The degree-zero case
has no positive lower-degree term.

Let

\[
 g=[Qh_C+SB_\ell-h_C]_+,
 \qquad \chi=(I-Q)^{-1}g.                                     \tag{5.3}
\]

The support of \(g\) is finite and compact minorization makes \(\chi\)
bounded.  Thus

\[
 Q(h_C+\chi)+SB_\ell-(h_C+\chi)\le0.                           \tag{5.4}
\]

This keeps \(SB_\ell\), not a cemetery value.  Iterating one episode and
then restarting at its actual strict-drop endpoint gives a logarithmic
spectator cost.  Telescoping across at most three episodes yields

\[
 \mathbb E[B_\ell(U_D)-B_\ell(u);D]
       \le C\log(u+e)+C+o(\log n).                              \tag{5.5}
\]

The logarithm and \(\chi\) are proof correctors only; they are not added to
the common physical potential.

## 6. Rare-event uniform integrability and Taylor expansion

The defect estimate is endpoint-weighted at arbitrary polynomial order:

\[
 \mathbb E[(1+U_E+I_E+|V_E-n|)^p;E]
       \le {C_p(1+u)^{c_p}\over n}.                             \tag{6.1}
\]

At a first included boundary, bounded reaction vectors give

\[
                         U_B+I_B+|V_B-n|\le3L_n+C.              \tag{6.2}
\]

The factorial Green/carrier tail makes \(\mathbb P(B)\) superpolynomial.
Consequently, for every fixed \(r\),

\[
 \mathbb E[|\Delta G_\ell|^r;E]\le n^{-1+o(1)},
 \qquad
 \mathbb E[|\Delta G_\ell|^r;B]=O(n^{-M})                     \tag{6.3}
\]

for arbitrary prescribed \(M\).  The second estimate uses the deterministic
cap (6.2), not boundary probability alone.  On \(D\), the active factorial
increment is exactly \(-\log n\); the actual spectator cost is (5.5), and
the carrier endpoint has the edge-size-biased factorial law.  Hence

\[
 \mathbb E\Delta G_\ell\le-\log n+o(\log n),
 \qquad
 \mathbb E|\Delta G_\ell|^r=n^{o(1)}.                           \tag{6.4}
\]

At the base, \(G_\ell=\Theta(n\log n)\).  In the exact identity

\[
 \Delta(G_\ell^4)=4G_\ell^3\Delta G_\ell
  +6G_\ell^2(\Delta G_\ell)^2
  +4G_\ell(\Delta G_\ell)^3+(\Delta G_\ell)^4,                \tag{6.5}
\]

the last three expectations are \(o(G_\ell^3\log n)\) by (6.4).  The
mean duration is \(n^{2+o(1)}\), also lower order.  Therefore the common
potential \(W_\ell=G_\ell^4\) satisfies the claimed negative stopped drift.

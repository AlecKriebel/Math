# Status

## Outcome T3-2-CERT

This release certifies:

> Every finite bimolecular weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network
> with at most three dynamically active species and at most two active
> linkage classes is positive recurrent on every closed communicating class,
> for every positive rate vector.

The proof does not condition on a future reaction and does not use a
genealogical or obligation stack. Its only unbounded excursion variable is
the physical scalar workload excess above the excursion baseline.

The inherited candidate T3-2 release is preserved under `inherited/`; it is
not itself the certified proof. The present release replaces its stochastic
interfaces by the aggregate-debt, current physical carrier, and
reaction-count source-layer theorems.

No result with three active linkage classes, four active species, or
molecularity greater than two is asserted.

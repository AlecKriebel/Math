# Forbidden conditioning

Two exact examples invalidate future-activation conditioning.

For `0->2A->A->0`, conditioning on `0->2A` and appending its designated
return gives a positive payoff tending to zero:
\[
J_n=(7\log n+1)n^{-2}+O(\log n\,n^{-3}).
\]

For `A<->2A` together with `0->A+B->B->0`, conditioning on the second
linkage's birth and appending only its generated target episode gives
\[
J_n=\log n+o(\log n)>0.
\]

The certified proof starts every target path from a target already produced
by the unconditioned physical chain. Each activation remains in its natural
source-layer trace.

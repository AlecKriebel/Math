# Mandatory regressions

1. **Conditional activation**
   \[
   0\to2A\to A\to0.
   \]
   The conditioned block is eventually positive. The proof never uses it.

2. **Activation plus generated target**
   \[
   A\rightleftarrows2A,\qquad 0\to A+B\to B\to0.
   \]
   The conditioned pair is asymptotic to `+log n`, while the honest episode
   begun at the already carried `2A` target is negative.

3. **Pure cancellation**
   \[
   0\rightleftarrows A+B.
   \]
   Positive workload is cancelled but no strict descent is claimed. The
   token difference `A-B` is invariant.

4. **Multiple arrivals**
   Repeated slower events are represented by the scalar debt recursion, not
   by a stack of reaction obligations.

5. **Weighted zero layer**
   \[
   H\rightleftarrows2L,\qquad0\rightleftarrows L.
   \]
   The fast layer preserves `2H+L`; the slower death is strict.

6. **Delayed drain**
   \[
   0\to A+B\to B\to0.
   \]
   The persistent `B` token permits unpaired service of existing `A`.

7. **Critical queue**
   A symmetric reflected random walk has zero mean and nonzero variance. It
   is not accepted by the strict-capacity theorem and is never called an
   invariant.

8. **Extreme rates**
   Exact positive rates spanning 24 orders of magnitude change only the
   chart threshold and constants, not the finite sign alternatives.

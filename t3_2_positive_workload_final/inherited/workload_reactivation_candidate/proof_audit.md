# Fresh adversarial proof audit

## Inherited gates

## Aggregate debt

Pass. For positive integer debt,

\[
(D-S)^++A-D\le-\mathbf1_{\{S\ge1\}}+A.
\]

A conditional service probability `p` and conditional mean arrival `a<p`
give finite mean clearing. No independence or history is assumed.

## Current physical reactivation

Pass. The finite phase is exact in bounded coordinates and actual target.
Every positive edge carries an actual return certificate. A closed
positive/no-negative component is impossible. Loss of a service source is a
recorded support exit. Debt remaining after service is handled from the new
physical state.

## Source-layer hierarchy

Pass. Raw positive events are retained at natural probability and are
cleared through faster actual targets. Same/slower interruptions form the
arrival variable. Zero effective classes pass to slower physical traces.
The flag is finite.

## Zero debt and variance

Pass. Cancellation is not strict descent. A strict effective edge supplies
unpaired service. A zero recurrent class is edgewise zero after complete
faster relaxation, so its corrected variance is zero. Abstract zero-mean
nonzero-variance examples are rejected rather than called invariants.

## One active coordinate

Pass. Exact polynomial rates, `2A` quadratic descent, finite degree-one
Poisson correction, bounded-source birth service, and the token invariant are
all included. No unbounded service phase is introduced.

## Occupation localization

Pass. The proof localizes a probability occupation rather than choosing a
representative state sequence. Active sets, bounded boxes, supports and
source flags are separated by a finite flow decomposition. All omitted
transitions are retained as exits.

## Two-active atlas interface

Pass. The atlas is unchanged and replayed three ways. Available linkages are
handled stochastically; only closed shielded phases invoke the invariant,
deficiency-zero or service classifications.

## Global return

Pass. Every terminal occupation has negative workload flux, structural exit,
an affine invariant, product-form stationarity, service descent, or one-active
descent. Embedded return, physical return, nonexplosion and uniqueness are
proved separately.

## Regression discipline

The proof never conditions on `0->2A` or `0->A+B`; both known conditioned
blocks remain positive. Pure cancellation remains zero. Multiple arrivals
are recorded only by physical scalar debt.

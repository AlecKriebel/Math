Every finite bimolecular weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network
with at most three dynamically active species and at most two active linkage
classes is positive recurrent on every closed communicating class, for every
positive rate vector.

## Central mechanism

The proof fixes an integer workload `H=h.X` on each terminal source-rate
chart and uses only the physical scalar excess

\[
D=(H(X)-H(X_0))^+.
\]

No reaction genealogy or unbounded obligation stack is introduced.

A positive event creates an actual target in a faster source layer. The
creator linkage supplies a sequentially enabled return prefix. The finite
physical carrier phase either delivers service, exits the chart, or enters an
edgewise-zero component. Unprocessed slower positive workload has mean
`O(epsilon_R)`, while strict service has a uniform positive probability.

Therefore the reflected debt recursion has strict negative drift and reaches
zero in finite mean. At zero debt, an unpaired strict service gives genuine
descent below the baseline. Zero layers are transferred to the next physical
reaction-count normalization; if every layer is zero, a bounded phase
corrector produces an affine invariant.

## Global proof

An infinite expected embedded return time would produce an escaping labelled
reaction-count Green occupation. Compactification localizes it to a terminal
active-set/source-rate chart with nonnegative outward workload balance.

- Three active coordinates give direct first-changing-layer negative flux.
- Two active coordinates use aggregate debt and the certified finite atlas.
- One active coordinate reduces to a finite-phase polynomial-rate chain.
- Zero active coordinates give a finite chart.

Every terminal chart contradicts escape through strict negative flux,
structural exit, affine invariance, a deficiency-zero stationary measure, or
one of the two certified service descents.

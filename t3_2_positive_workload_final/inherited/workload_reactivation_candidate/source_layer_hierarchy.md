# Finite physical source-layer hierarchy

Let

\[
E_1\gg E_2\gg\cdots\gg E_m
\]

be the terminal chart's complete physical source-rate flag.

At a raw layer, retain each event at its natural probability. If its workload
increment is positive, its actual target starts a strictly faster carrier
relaxation. Same-layer or slower interruptions are retained as arrivals; the
carrier-race estimate makes their mean workload `O(epsilon_R)`. The aggregate
debt theorem therefore completes the faster relaxation in finite mean and
turns the raw event into a nonpositive effective transition or a structural
exit.

Proceed from fastest to slowest. At each layer all faster recurrent motion
has already been replaced by finite-mean nonpositive effective transitions.
For the resulting finite phase class:

- one strict effective edge gives strictly negative stationary corrected
  reward;
- zero stationary reward forces every positive-probability effective edge to
  have reward exactly zero;
- an outgoing phase edge is retained as structural exit.

In a zero class, path sums give a bounded rational phase correction `psi`.
The layer is contracted and passed to the next slower **physical**
reaction-count trace. A source or linkage whose fraction vanishes at one
normalization is not discarded.

The hierarchy terminates after at most the number of enabled source layers.
If every layer is zero, then

\[
H(X)+\psi(\phi)
\]

is invariant on the terminal recurrent support. The phase correction is
bounded and `h` is positive on every active coordinate, so the class cannot
carry an escaping occupation.

Because zero effective increments are edgewise zero after complete faster
relaxation, their corrected variance is zero. A critical nonzero-variance
random walk is not hidden in the zero branch.

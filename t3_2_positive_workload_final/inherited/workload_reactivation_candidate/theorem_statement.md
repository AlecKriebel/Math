# Certified theorem

Let a finite stochastic mass-action reaction network have at most three
dynamically active species and at most two active linkage classes. Assume:

1. every complex has total molecularity at most two;
2. every active linkage class is strongly connected;
3. every labelled reaction channel has a positive rate constant.

Then every closed communicating class of the population continuous-time
Markov chain is positive recurrent. Equivalently, each closed irreducible
class admits a unique stationary probability distribution.

The theorem includes coordinate faces, siphons, signed stoichiometric
invariants, parity and proper-lattice restrictions, arbitrary deficiency and
stoichiometric rank, and arbitrarily separated fixed positive rates.

The theorem does not cover three or more active linkage classes, four or more
dynamically active species, or complexes of molecularity greater than two.

# Boundary-face induction and finite-phase interfaces

## Terminal charts

An escaping normalized Green occupation is refined until the active
coordinate set, bounded coordinate box, capped availability phase, target
marks, lattice data and complete source-rate flag are fixed.  Positive flux
out of the bounded box promotes that coordinate.  Positive lower-shell or
lower-rank flux is retained as a structural exit.  Hence a terminal chart has
a fixed active set.

## Three active coordinates

All sources are physically enabled.  The first changing source layer has
strict negative physical workload flux unless the scalar workload is a global
invariant.

## Two active coordinates

The remaining coordinate is a finite phase.  If either linkage has an
available rare terminal, use `finite_shell_activation.md`: wait for an actual
channel of that linkage, follow its actual target path, and cancel finite
waiting reward by a shell Dirichlet correction.  A closed waiting component
with no such channel is a one-linkage reduction.

If both linkages are shielded, the exact workload-cone atlas gives a common
affine invariant, deficiency zero, or one of the two service systems.  The
service systems descend in `B-C` or promote the bounded coordinate.

## One active coordinate

The two bounded coordinates and all marks form a finite phase.  The exact
degree hierarchy is stated in `one_active_phase_theorem.md`:

- quadratic sources strictly drain;
- linear sources never increase the active coordinate once the quadratic
  target is absent;
- constant-rate births retain an actual linear carrier clock until service,
  lower-order phase interruption, or structural exit;
- exact finite Green elimination makes complete rewards nonpositive;
- all-zero recurrent classes are affine phase invariants.

## Edges, corners, and movement among faces

A zero-active chart is finite.  Repeated movement among faces appears as
positive chart-exit flux and contradicts terminality; it is not replaced by a
continuity argument.  Every designated path remains in the original closed
class, so boundary and lattice restrictions are preserved.

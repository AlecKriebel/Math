# Green duality and recurrence closure

Fix a state `o` in an infinite closed irreducible class.  Infinite mean
positive return would yield normalized killed Green occupations escaping
every finite set.  Workload-cut averaging gives a terminal physical flow

\[
 \sum_e J_e(y'_e-y_e)=q,
 \qquad h\cdot q\ge0.
\]

The local chart alternatives are:

- strict physical source-layer flux in the fully active chart;
- an actual-target rare-terminal episode with a finite shell correction;
- a common affine invariant;
- a deficiency-zero product-form probability;
- shellwise descent in one of the service workloads;
- finite-phase one-active descent or invariant;
- positive structural-exit flux.

A common invariant is constant on the fixed class and positive on the escape
cone.  Product form already gives positive recurrence.  Structural exit
contradicts terminality.  Strict physical flux contradicts the displayed
balance.  A strict marked episode contradicts the inherited shell-adapted
Green/Foster alternative after its finite Dirichlet waiting correction.

Therefore no escaping Green occupation exists.  The inherited finite
trace-chain theorem gives finite expected positive return.  The direct
linear-growth comparison for population-increasing reactions proves
nonexplosion, and a return-cycle occupation measure yields the unique
stationary probability.

# Certified theorem statement

## Three-species, two-linkage positive-recurrence theorem

Let a finite stochastic mass-action reaction network have at most three dynamically active species. Assume:

1. every complex has molecularity at most two;
2. the network is weakly reversible; and
3. after inert singleton components are removed, the reaction graph has at most two linkage classes.

For every assignment of positive reaction-rate constants, every closed communicating class of the population CTMC is positive recurrent.

Equivalently, every closed irreducible class admits a unique stationary probability distribution.

The theorem includes arbitrary deficiency and stoichiometric rank, coordinate faces, siphons, signed intermediate invariants, parity and lattice restrictions, and arbitrary separation of the positive rates.

No assertion is made here for three active species with three or more linkage classes, or for complexes of molecularity greater than two.

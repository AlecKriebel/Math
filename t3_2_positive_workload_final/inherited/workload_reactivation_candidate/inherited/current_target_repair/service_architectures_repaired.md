# Repaired service architectures

The certified two-active atlas leaves, up to symmetry, exactly

\[
 \{C,2C\}\ \&\ \{0,A,2A,B+C\}
\]

and

\[
 \{0,C,2C\}\ \&\ \{A,2A,B+C\}.
\]

The mixed linkage preserves \(W=B-C\).  The other linkage changes
\(W\) by \(-\Delta C\).

A shell trial starts at \(C=c_0\) and follows actual strongly connected
channels until the first time

\[
 C_\tau\ge c_0+1,
\]

or a declared chart/shell exit.  The endpoint may be \(C=c_0+1\) or
\(C=c_0+2\), so the correct statement is

\[
 W_\tau\le W_0-1,                              \tag{1}
\]

not equality.  Intervening decreases of \(C\) do not invalidate (1), because
the stopping rule uses the first **net** increase relative to the trial
start.

In a finite padded shell, every nonabsorbing state has an actual channel path
to the trial endpoint or a declared exit.  Hence the killed finite chain has
no closed nonabsorbing class and has finite mean absorption.  The inherited
service-support enumeration checks every strongly connected directed support
of the two exceptional complex sets.

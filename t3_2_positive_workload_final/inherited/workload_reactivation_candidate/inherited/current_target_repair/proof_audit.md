# Proof audit

## Certified

1. Embedded labelled reaction-count Green compactness, at its inherited
   scope.
2. The three independent workload-atlas implementations and their exact
   counts.
3. Actual-current-target residual factorial identity.
4. Nonoverlapping finite target episodes begun from an already carried
   target.
5. The original conditional-activation counterexample.
6. The stronger activation-plus-target counterexample in a genuine
   two-linkage network.
7. First-changing-source finite graph sign.
8. Exact one-active polynomial-rate decomposition and elementary source
   signs.
9. Finite Bellman/Poisson calculations for a supplied finite kernel.

## Not certified

1. Passage from the original escaping occupation to a finite unconditioned
   current-target kernel at every slower reaction-count layer.
2. A universal sign theorem for that kernel after faster zero classes are
   contracted.
3. The complete one-active mean-return theorem under arbitrary repeated
   cross-linkage interruptions.
4. The terminal-chart global charging theorem.
5. T3-2 positive recurrence.

## Exact failure mode

A future activation cannot be conditioned upon.  Moreover, the activation
jump cannot simply be paired with the target episode it creates: the combined
conditional block can have positive reward of order `log n`.  Any valid proof
must charge it against the **preceding** actual-current-target phase and the
full physical source-layer occupation.

# Classification of closed nonservice carrier classes

The finite carrier absorption lemma reduces the stochastic question to a
finite graph question: can the retained faster carrier graph have a closed
class with positive workload credit but no service and no structural exit?
For three active species and at most two linkage classes the answer is exactly
classified as follows.

## Three active coordinates

Every bimolecular complex is physically enabled beyond the chart threshold.
If the creator linkage has positive workload credit, its directed return path
contains a first crossing back to the pre-activation workload whose source is
strictly faster.  Every source on that prefix remains physically enabled.  Therefore
the finite carrier graph has a positive-rate path to service from every state
that carries the creator target.

A reaction in the other linkage cannot form a closed nonservice trap: all
creator-prefix sources remain enabled after bounded population changes until
a lower-shell exit.  Hence its strict service channel continues to have
positive physical hazard independently of the current target mark.  A closed
nonservice carrier class is impossible.

## Two active coordinates

The inactive coordinate is retained exactly in the finite carrier phase.  If
a linkage is in the available branch of the certified top-complex split,
every actual target produced by that linkage has a lifted same-linkage path
to the selected terminal or to a declared inactive-box/support exit.  Thus a
closed carrier class containing positive reaction flux from an available
linkage has a service/negative-episode edge.

Consequently, in a closed nonservice class every linkage carrying positive
fast flux is shielded.  A linkage whose fast flux is zero is passed to the
next slower trace and is not part of the closed fast class.  With at most two
active linkages, a terminal closed class is therefore one of the shielded
assignments in the certified exhaustive atlas.  That atlas gives exactly:

1. a common rational affine workload positive on both active directions;
2. a deficiency-zero network;
3. one of the two service architectures.

There is no fourth carrier trap.  The three independent atlas
implementations certify this finite alternative.

## One active coordinate

Without a `2A` source, every fast one-`A` transition is neutral or removes one
`A`.  A zero fast carrier class can avoid all deaths only when each one-`A`
complex consists of `A+D`, no unary `A` occurs, and no zero-`A` complex
contains any service species `D`.  In that case

\[
 A-\sum_D D
\]

is an exact invariant of every reaction in both linkages.  Otherwise a unary
or unpaired service edge leaves the zero class.  This is verified edge by
edge by `src/one_active_current_target.py`.

## Consequence

Every limiting finite carrier graph has uniform finite absorption into
service or structural exit, unless the original physical chart is already in
an invariant, deficiency-zero, or certified service branch.  This is the
network-specific hypothesis needed by the finite carrier absorption lemma.

# Independent reconstruction: exact endpoint

The reconstruction confirms the finite atlas and the embedded labelled
reaction-count Green theorem, but it does not reconstruct a proof of T3-2.

The precise break occurs when the candidate proof tries to turn a
conditionally rare terminal source into unconditional linkage drift.  The
single-linkage scalar envelope begins with an already carried target and
couples continuation probability to that target's source probability.  A
multi-linkage argument that first conditions on an unrelated activation
channel loses this coupling.

The exact counterexample in
`failed_approaches/conditional_activation_counterexample.md` verifies the
loss.  The needed replacement is a nonoverlapping decomposition of actual
linkage-switch jumps, current-target episodes and shielded-linkage waiting,
followed by a rate-weighted source-layer Green identity.  No such complete
identity is contained in the candidate release or in this repair.

Accordingly, the independent reconstruction stops at the certified partial
results listed in `STATUS.md`.

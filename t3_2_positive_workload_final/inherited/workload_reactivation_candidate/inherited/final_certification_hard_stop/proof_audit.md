# Adversarial proof audit

## Gate G1 — embedded reaction-count Green theorem

**Pass.**  The killed embedded construction uses normalized expected labelled
reaction counts.  It proves divergence of killed mean counts, escape from
every fixed finite set, exact bounded-test telescoping, bounded endpoint
workload displacement per expected jump, and a nonzero compactified
occupation.  It never assumes bounded integrated mass-action intensities.

## Gate G2 — terminal chart localization

**Partial.**  One-point compactification, active-set separation, capped
availability and finite source-order types are valid.  Inactive coordinates
are only tight, not confined to a fixed finite box.  Epsilon-box localization
is valid for bounded jump rewards, but the complete source-layer trace and
strict-margin passage required by T3-2 depends on Gate G3.

## Gate G3 — unconditional linkage activation

**Fail.**  The proposed uniform conditional terminal-payoff assertion is
false.  The exact directed cycle

\[
0\to2A\to A\to0
\]

with all rates one gives a conditioned activation/path payoff `J_n` satisfying

\[
\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac{n^2J_n}{\log n}=7.
\]

Thus `J_n` tends to zero from above, not to minus infinity.  Conditioning has
removed the source-probability factor used by the scalar envelope.

A current-target episode begun after an actual linkage reaction remains
valid, but its preceding activation jump and any shielded-linkage waiting
must be charged without overlap.  The needed rate-weighted charging theorem
has not been proved.

## Gate G4 — one-active finite-phase theorem

**Conditional.**  The polynomial generator decomposition and elementary
quadratic/linear sign facts are valid.  The structural service-token
alternative is valid.  The claimed universal macrochain closure imports the
unproved Gate G3 charging interface when service is created through another
linkage.  It therefore cannot presently certify the one-active branch.

## Finite atlas and service systems

**Pass.**  Three independent implementations agree on all counts.  The
common affine nullspace and deficiency-zero branches are exact.  Both service
architectures and all strongly connected directed supports are reproduced.

## Workload-cone gluing

**Not load-bearingly certified.**  Exact nullspace intersection is valid for
a supplied finite transition set.  The project has not yet constructed the
complete positive-flow transition set at infinity without Gate G3.  A local
phase coboundary is therefore not promoted to a global invariant.

## Gate G5 — global recurrence closure

**Fail by dependency.**  The final Green contradiction uses G3 and G4.  Since
G3 is false as stated and its replacement is unresolved, finite mean return,
CTMC positive recurrence and uniqueness are not established by this package.

## Gate G6 — two-species dependency

**Interface removed in the proposed proof, but immaterial to status.**  The
candidate did not need the unreproducible arbitrary-linkage two-species
archive once the local two-active atlas and one-active theorem were supplied.
Because G4 remains conditional, this does not close T3-2.

\subsection{1. Statement}

Fix one of the final 333 support pairs, give each linkage an arbitrary
strongly connected orientation and arbitrary positive rate constants,
and let \(X\) be its stochastic mass-action chain. For every closed
irreducible population class \(\Gamma\), there is a vector \(\ell\),
fixed for the whole pair, such that

\[
 G_\ell(x)=K_\ell+\sum_{i=1}^3\log(x_i!)+\ell\cdot x\ge1,
 \qquad W_\ell(x)=G_\ell(x)^4                         \tag{1.1}
\]

is a common population potential for every active-dimensional chart. The
choice menu is

\[
\begin{array}{c|r}
\text{correction family}&\text{pairs}\\ \hline
\text{arbitrary fixed }\ell&291\\
\text{reversible-top detailed balance}&34\\
\text{directed-triple correction}&8.
\end{array}                                            \tag{1.2}
\]

For an arbitrary-\(\ell\) pair we choose any fixed admissible value, and
in particular choose \(\ell=0\) on the four \(H_w\) pairs. The constant
\(K_\ell\) is chosen once, independently of invariant shells and
stopping episodes.

\begin{quote}
\textbf{Theorem 1.1.} Every closed irreducible class \(\Gamma\) of every
hard support pair is positive recurrent.
\end{quote}

The proof occupies Sections 2--7. Section 8 records exactly where the
finite atlas enters.

\subsection{2. Properness, nonexplosion, and the marked
lift}

The potential is proper. If \(N=|x|_1\), one coordinate is at least
\(N/3\), so Stirling's formula gives a factorial contribution of order
\(N\log N\), whereas the negative part of \(\ell\cdot x\) is only
linear.

The chain is nonexplosive independently of recurrence. Any reaction
which increases total population has source molecularity at most one,
since every target complex has molecularity at most two. The sum of all
population-increasing propensities is therefore bounded by
\(C(1+|x|_1)\). Localization and Gronwall bound the total population on
finite time intervals. Within a fixed population sublevel there are only
finitely many states and all total rates are bounded. Hence infinitely
many jumps cannot accumulate in finite physical time.

Fix \(x^\circ\in\Gamma\). On the reachable lift starting from
\((x^\circ,0)\), update three reflected debt marks after a physical jump
\(x\mapsto x+\zeta\) by

\[
 D_i^+=(D_i+\zeta_i)^+,
 \qquad H_i=X_i-D_i.                                  \tag{2.1}
\]

Then, pathwise,

\[
 0\le D_i\le X_i,
 \qquad H_i(t)\le H_i(0)=x_i^\circ.                  \tag{2.2}
\]

Indeed, \(H_i\) is unchanged when reflection is inactive and decreases
when reflection occurs. The set with all debts zero is consequently
finite. The lifted potential \(\widehat W(x,d)=W_\ell(x)\) is proper
because a fixed population admits only finitely many marks.

The mark is used only to establish eligibility of a one-active
old-species service block. If a divergent one-active sequence selects
active species \(V\) but has \(D_V=0\), then \(V=H_V\le x_V^\circ\), a
contradiction. Thus every genuinely divergent reachable one-active bad
tube has positive selected debt after descriptor reclassification. A
structural ``no-history'\,' face, on which \(V\) cannot have changed,
can never carry that positive debt. This replaces every false assertion
that an arbitrary unmarked one-active start must admit service.

\subsection{3. The local common-potential
contract}

Every stopped lemma used below is a physical strong-Markov rule. It
retains all enabled clocks, includes the reaction causing its terminal
event, evolves the marks along the actual path, and stops at the actual
endpoint. For a chart scale \(s\to\infty\) it supplies some
\(h_s\to\infty\) and some fixed moment order \(q>8\),

\[
 \begin{aligned}
 \mathbb E_x[W_\ell(X_\tau)-W_\ell(x)+\tau]
    &\le-cG_\ell(x)^3h_s,\\
 \mathbb E_x|G_\ell(X_\tau)-G_\ell(x)|^q
    &<\infty,\qquad
 \mathbb E_x\tau^q<\infty .
 \end{aligned}                                                \tag{3.1}
\]

The exact displayed orders differ between charts, but all dominate a
fixed negative constant outside a finite class-dependent set.
Moving-boundary events are path-labelled. Only a cutoff reached at an
outer no-fast return is a promotion endpoint \(P\); a cutoff first
reached while an excursion is open remains an auxiliary endpoint \(B\),
even if later cleanup reaches the same population face. Both are already
charged in (3.1). Reclassification at \(X_\tau\) therefore has zero
comparison toll because the next rule uses the identical \(W_\ell\). The
inputs often prove every fixed moment, but the composition below uses
only the existence of one fixed \(q>8\).

\subsection{4. Passing descriptors}

It remains to treat descriptors which pass the actual oriented
source-tier criterion. Let \(A_s\) be the largest enabled source
propensity and \(g_s\to\infty\) the logarithmic gap of a certified top
source to its lower target. For \(d_r=G_\ell(x+\zeta_r)-G_\ell(x)\), the
quantitative descending-source estimate is

\[
 \mathcal LG_\ell\le-cA_sg_s,
 \quad \sum_r a_r\le CA_s,
 \quad |d_r|\le C\log(1+|x|_1).                     \tag{4.1}
\]

The exact binomial identity

\[
 \mathcal LW_\ell
 =4G_\ell^3\mathcal LG_\ell
  +6G_\ell^2\sum_ra_rd_r^2
  +4G_\ell\sum_ra_rd_r^3+
    \sum_ra_rd_r^4                                  \tag{4.2}
\]

and \(G_\ell\asymp |x|_1\log(1+|x|_1)\) show that every remainder in
(4.2) is lower order than \(-G_\ell^3A_sg_s\). Hence

\[
                         \mathcal LW_\ell\longrightarrow-\infty          \tag{4.3}
\]

along every divergent passing descriptor.

\subsection{6. Random-time Foster
theorem}

For completeness we prove the gluing statement used here. Let \(K\) be
the finite target from Section 5. From a generator-good state, run until
first entrance into the episode-good set or \(K\). Localized Dynkin
applied to (5.2), followed by Fatou, gives

\[
 \mathbb E[W_\ell(X_\sigma)-W_\ell(x)+
                 \eta\sigma]\le0.                  \tag{6.1}
\]

At an episode-good state append its physical stopping time from (5.3),
then repeat from the actual endpoint. Stop the construction when \(K\)
is first visited, although a containing local episode may be completed
for drift accounting. If \(N\) is the number of completed exceptional
episodes and \(S_m\) accumulated physical time, conditional iteration
gives

\[
 \delta\,\mathbb E(N\wedge m)
 +\eta\,\mathbb E S_{N\wedge m}
 \le W_\ell(x)+\delta.                              \tag{6.2}
\]

Monotone convergence yields finite mean time to \(K\) from every
reachable marked state. Exact physical self returns may have been erased
inside a local resolvent only analytically: their holding times,
internal cutoffs, and actual mark evolution were already restored in
(3.1), so (6.2) is a physical time statement.

\subsection{7. From finite mean hitting to positive
recurrence}

If \(\Gamma\) is an absorbing singleton there is nothing to prove.
Otherwise take one ordinary physical jump from the finite marked target
and apply the hitting estimate to each of its finitely many successors.
The successive positive returns to the target define a finite trace
chain. Pick a recurrent state of that trace. Its discrete return count
has finite mean, and each physical target-to-target segment has
uniformly finite mean, so the marked process has a finite mean positive
return to that state. Projecting to the physical population can only
shorten the return. Irreducibility of \(\Gamma\) therefore makes every
state positive recurrent. This proves Theorem 1.1.

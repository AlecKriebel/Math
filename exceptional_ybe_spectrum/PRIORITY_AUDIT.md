# Priority and novelty audit

No novelty claim beyond the already published \(d=4\) result is made at
project initialization.

Any candidate theorem or witness must be audited separately for:

1. correctness;
2. novelty of the local matrix;
3. novelty of the dimension-spectrum theorem;
4. novelty of the proof mechanism;
5. novelty of the categorical or localization interpretation.

The audit must search for equivalence under:

- local unitary conjugacy;
- tensor-factor permutation;
- flip and complementary projection;
- scalar normalization and inversion;
- ancilla stabilization and blocking;
- generalized Yang--Baxter formulations;
- quaternionic, Clifford, and extraspecial-group constructions;
- subfactor, planar-algebra, module-category, and lattice-model realizations.

Use “apparently new” until both the literature audit and mathematical
equivalence checks are complete.  No external researcher will be contacted by
the assistant; possible outside questions may only be recorded for the human
researcher.

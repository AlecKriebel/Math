# Priority and novelty audit

No novelty claim beyond the already published \(d=4\) result is made at
project initialization.

Any candidate theorem or witness must be audited separately for:

1. correctness;
2. novelty of the local matrix;
3. novelty of the dimension-spectrum theorem;
4. novelty of the proof mechanism;
5. novelty of the categorical or localization interpretation.

The audit must search for equivalence under:

- local unitary conjugacy;
- tensor-factor permutation;
- flip and complementary projection;
- scalar normalization and inversion;
- ancilla stabilization and blocking;
- generalized Yang--Baxter formulations;
- quaternionic, Clifford, and extraspecial-group constructions;
- subfactor, planar-algebra, module-category, and lattice-model realizations.

Use “apparently new” until both the literature audit and mathematical
equivalence checks are complete.  No external researcher will be contacted by
the assistant; possible outside questions may only be recorded for the human
researcher.

## 2026-07-28 interim audit

### \(D^{(6)}\) Ocneanu cells

Evans--Pugh's published \(\mathcal D^{(6)}\) data were reconstructed exactly.
They do realize the same exceptional Hecke parameter, with balanced
multiplicity \(10+10\), but on a 20-dimensional composable-edge path space.
The six graph vertices do not provide an ordinary
\(\mathbb C^6\otimes\mathbb C^6\) local space. Direct zero and scalar Hecke
completions fail exactly.

Priority consequence: the path/face realization is prior art and must be
cited in any \(d=6\) construction paper. An ordinary tensor-power witness or
a new all-strand vertex--face conversion would still be a distinct claim.
No novelty is claimed for the connection itself.

### Controlled-leg divisibility

The new invariant restriction argument proves
\[
8\mid r d^2
\]
for every rank-\(r\) projection in either one-leg commutant. It subsumes the
new face and group-relative no-go calculations and exactly reconstructs a
\(V_4\) skew-conference calibration.

Priority status: **apparently new proof mechanism; literature audit
incomplete**. Before publication, search specifically for the same
common-sector divisibility statement in two-projection algebras, subfactor
commuting squares, biunitary connections, and controlled Yang--Baxter
operators.

### Canonical-channel and cubic countermodels

The exact \(d=6\) Weyl channel and the non-involutive cubic point \(H_0\)
are discovery tools and assumption audits. Their novelty has not been
searched, and they do not solve the matrix-class problem. In particular,
the affine involution associated with \(H_0\) is an involutive braid
operator with unbalanced multiplicities, a broad class with extensive prior
literature. No novelty claim should be attached without an equivalence
search.

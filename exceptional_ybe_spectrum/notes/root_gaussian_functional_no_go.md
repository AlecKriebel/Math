# Exact no-go in the cyclic Gaussian functional ansatz

Status: `PROVED` within the precisely defined ansatz below.

This result is not a nonexistence theorem for arbitrary \(d=6\) matrices.

## Ansatz

Let \(U\in\operatorname{End}(\mathbb C^6\otimes\mathbb C^6)\) be the
standard even-dimensional Gaussian Weyl generator.  It satisfies

\[
U^6=I
\]

and, on three sites,

\[
u v=q\,v u,\qquad
u=U_{12},\quad v=U_{23},\quad q=e^{i\pi/3}.
\]

Seek a Hermitian trace-zero involution in the cyclic algebra generated by
\(U\):

\[
H=f(U).
\]

The six eigenvalues of \(U\) are the sixth roots of unity, each with
multiplicity six.  Consequently every Hermitian involution in
\(\mathbb C[U]\) is obtained by assigning a sign \(h_j\in\{+1,-1\}\) to
each spectral root.  The trace-zero condition is exactly

\[
\#\{j:h_j=+1\}=\#\{j:h_j=-1\}=3.
\]

There are therefore exactly

\[
\binom63=20
\]

candidates.  The inverse discrete Fourier transform gives the coefficients
of \(f\) exactly in \(\mathbb Q(i\sqrt3)\).

## Exhaustive calculation

The verifier expands

\[
H_1H_2H_1-H_2H_1H_2-\frac13(H_1-H_2)
\]

in the exact twisted group-algebra basis

\[
\{u^a v^b:0\le a,b<6\}.
\]

Multiplication uses

\[
(u^a v^b)(u^c v^d)
=q^{-bc}u^{a+c}v^{b+d}.
\]

The twisted group algebra is faithful for these Weyl generators, so the
residual vanishes as a matrix if and only if all 36 basis coefficients
vanish.

All 20 sign assignments have a nonzero exact coefficient.  Hence:

> No trace-zero Hermitian involution \(H=f(U)\) in the standard cyclic
> Gaussian algebra gives an exceptional \(d=6\) solution.

The smallest residual support occurs for the alternating sign patterns
\(\{0,2,4\}\) and \(\{1,3,5\}\).  Even there the residual contains the
nonzero coefficient \(\mp2/3\) on \(v^3\).

# Version 1.0.1 correction audit

**Date:** 29 July 2026

**Scope:** independent scrutiny of proposed corrections to version 1.0.0 of
the exceptional Yang--Baxter structural-frontier paper.

## Verdict

The proposed review identified real release, cross-reference, and scope
problems but did not identify a false central theorem. The required
corrections and the recommended editorial changes were independently
accepted for version 1.0.1.

## Confirmed defects

1. **Release metadata.** The version 1.0.0 tag, PDF, and TeX source printed
   `Version 0.1.0` on the title page while the release and project page
   advertised `1.0.0`. The archived tag and assets remain unchanged.
2. **Equation labels.** The labels `eq:open-dims`, `eq:projection`,
   `eq:standardness`, `eq:multiplicity`, `eq:leg-divisibility`,
   `eq:general-cubic`, and `eq:four-local` occurred inside `\[...\]`.
   LaTeX consequently attached unrelated inherited counters to them without
   emitting a warning. The four referenced formulas now use numbered
   `equation` environments, and the three unused labels were removed.
3. **Rank-four public summary.** The landing-page fact omitted the hypothesis
   \(\operatorname{OSR}(H)=4\). The full theorem in the manuscript was
   already correctly scoped.
4. **Numerical-search wording.** The deterministic central verifier runner
   contains no numerical search, but the broader research record does.
   Public wording now says that the 10 verifiers are exact and that no
   theorem relies on numerical evidence.
5. **Frontier wording.** “The exact structural frontier” was too definitive
   given the open high-rank, simultaneous-degeneracy, and one-sided branches.
   It was replaced by “exact structural constraints.”

## Mathematical cross-check

The automatic-standardness input was rechecked directly against
[Lechner, Propositions 2.3--2.4 and Lemma 3.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.20158v1).
For a non-involutive Hecke matrix, absence of an opposite spectral pair makes
the associated inclusion irreducible and the character Markov; the Markov
property is equivalent to scalar normalized partial trace. This supports the
manuscript's use of those results without an added irreducibility hypothesis.

The theorem scopes retained in version 1.0.1 are unchanged:

- faithfulness is only after passage to the Markov-trace quotient;
- low-Schmidt rigidity is an existence theorem, not a classification of all
  low-rank matrices;
- rank-four sandwich degeneracy assumes
  \(\operatorname{OSR}(H)=4\);
- nonrestrictability is the defined two-sided condition;
- only the intersection of the two \(d=6\) leg commutants is proved scalar.

## Editorial decision

The low-Schmidt section was moved ahead of the unrestricted rank-four
section because the latter uses the invariant-leg projection theorem proved
in the former. Conditional Bell and Clifford mechanisms now follow the
universal tensor-local results. The one-sided \(4+2\) reduction and secondary
\(d=4\) orbit remain fully documented in the supplement but no longer
interrupt the main theorem sequence.

This audit is model-assisted review, not independent specialist peer review.

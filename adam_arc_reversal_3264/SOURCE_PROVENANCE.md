## Scholarly source

Carsten Thomassen, *Ádám’s conjecture*, Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series B **161** (2023), 15–20.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jctb.2023.01.005
- Publisher record: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0095895623000060
- Institutional publication record: https://orbit.dtu.dk/en/publications/%C3%A1d%C3%A1ms-conjecture/
- Accepted manuscript: https://backend.orbit.dtu.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/308267535/Adam_final_.pdf

The source-recovery assessment located Theorem 1 on manuscript printed page 4 (PDF page 5 including repository cover), with its proof on printed pages 4–6. The theorem supplies infinitely many strongly 2-connected, self-reverse oriented counterexamples to the unrestricted arc-reversal conjecture. The accepted manuscript is dated January 29, 2023. Its introductory discussion distinguishes the tournament restriction and older parallel-arc examples.

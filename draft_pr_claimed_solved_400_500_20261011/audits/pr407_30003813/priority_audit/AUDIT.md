## Material priority finding

The frozen source gate's statement that no publication was located explicitly closing the named OWR question is compatible with this audit. The missing literature was a general theorem covering the full target: **Benedetti–Knauer–Valencia-Porras, arXiv2303.10458v1 (18 March 2023), Theorem 2.6, p.7**. It gives h-star coefficients as incoming-degree counts in the rooted dual graph of the alcove triangulation for **every** type-A alcoved polytope. Its definitions use the ordinary integer lattice and hyperplanes y_i-y_j=l with y_0=0. The cumulative-coordinate substitution y_i=x_1+...+x_i has determinant 1 and converts every signed target inequality to exactly this form. There is no unresolved lemma or restriction to a special sign word in the application.

This is positive prior evidence satisfying the same literal coefficient-counting criterion under which the PR was judged complete. It is stronger than a finding that the candidate is merely reminiscent of classical theory. The 2023 theorem's full statement, definitions and shelling argument were read, and its theorem page was visually inspected. Its prior longer version is cited explicitly: the September 2026 v2 changes title and removes the longer Ehrhart section, while acknowledging that the earlier version contains that analysis. **Bullock–Jiang arXiv2412.02787v2 (2025), Theorem 5.4** independently supplies the corresponding general root-system graph formula. The exact-coordinate 2023 theorem suffices without transferring a nonstandard lattice.

## Reproduction and adversarial checks

The independently authored alcove script never imports the candidate or its natural labels. For every fraction-order simplex it constructs the exact integer vertices, verifies determinant +/-1 and closed signed constraints, joins cells by shared facets, computes BFS orientation, and compares its coefficient counts with original-coordinate integer-point transfer. Both normal and `-O` runs pass 255 patterns, 46,233 cells, 813 roots and 2,813 Ehrhart values. Source hashes before and after each run agree; stdout, stderr, exit codes and result hashes are retained. No assertion disabled by `-O` is used for these checks.

Checks reject three false generalizations: raw descents of the fraction-order word, direct centered type-B lattice transfer, and identification with the 2026 period-two upper-bound family. All equality boundaries and m=0 enter the direct counts. The general theorem and exact substitution establish all-dimension coverage; finite reproduction alone is not promoted to a proof.

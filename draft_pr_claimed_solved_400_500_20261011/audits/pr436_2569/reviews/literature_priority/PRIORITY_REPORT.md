## Full relevant earlier result and comparison

The selected primary section is §3.40, printed pp55–56. It treats exactly Problem21.60 with G=C3⋊C4, p2. Its argument classifies both F2 simple types through the central involution quotient S3 and exhibits rational lifts. Thus every finite-dimensional F2G-module, hence every projective indecomposable, has the required positive class. For the obstruction it constructs a quaternion division algebra quotient order O of RG with O/2O=M2(F2), proves J(O)=2O, and uses the impossibility of lifting nontrivial idempotents inside a division ring. The selected section was read in full; neither an abstract nor a search snippet is the basis of this conclusion.

This has the same central difficulty as the saved construction: reductions and composition classes can conceal the rational realization obstruction. The prior example uses a central involution and a globally nonsplit quaternion algebra with split reduction. The saved example uses the central double cover SL(2,5)→A5, doubles PIM composition classes, and forces one quaternionic character in a projective character. These are different concrete realizations of an already exhibited gap between positive decomposition classes and projective rational lifting. The earlier section does not give the saved SL(2,5) group, its24/32/8PIM dimensions, its three positive witnesses, or its particular Frobenius–Schur calculation.

The same selected earlier section, p56, also explicitly challenges Johnston–Rumynin Proposition3.3 in the retained v2. It gives trivial C2 actions with Ext^1_RC2(R,R)=0 and Ext^1_F2C2(F2,F2)=F2, and a generator-matrix explanation. Therefore, the saved supplemental C2 obstruction to unrestricted extension lifting is directly anticipated, including its version-specific qualification. The prior counterexample also contradicts Proposition3.3 as written. This is stronger than merely observing that a proof step looks unproved.

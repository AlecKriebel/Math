# Primary-source and prior-tools audit

The primary documents were independently downloaded from arXiv to private storage with default TLS. PRIMARY_DOWNLOADS.json records their exact hashes; CURRENT_DOWNLOADS.json and EXTRA_DOWNLOADS.json record later sources and failures. Foreign bodies are excluded from the public allowlist. Current arXiv histories were checked, including corrected Minasyan–Osin v6, Hagen–Wise v3, Bongiovanni–Ghosh–Gültepe–Hagen v3, and Wu–Ye v1.

The quotient/cover convention is supported by [Leary v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1540v3), Appendix C. Theorem C.9 applies to general cube complexes with arbitrary vertex links and establishes local CAT(0) exactly for flag simplicial links. Its proof establishes CAT(0) of the cover using the second subdivision. Theorem C.4 establishes strictness of a CAT(0) cube complex. C.11 gives a strict second-subdivided quotient, with changed counts. The dimension-three strict cover has no ascending chain of cells and hence is complete by A.6. The paper uses these theorems in the correct order; it does not silently apply only the strict version to the quotient.

The alternative existence deduction was checked independently against the named hypotheses. In H=<x,y,b | [x,y]=1,bxb^-1=y>, Britton normal form embeds A0=<x,y>=Z^2. Tree vertices are left cosets hA0. The edge A0->bA0 is stabilized by <y>; the incoming bx-axis edge at A0 is stabilized by (bx)^-1<y>(bx)=<x>. The intersection is trivial. Height from epsilon_b makes every (bx)^n e increase by one and ensures a bi-infinite nonbacktracking axis. [Minasyan–Osin v6](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.6289v6), Corollary 4.3, applies to this very loxodromic element with finite segment stabilizer, yielding WPD. No finite-extension transfer is assumed.

[Dahmani–Guirardel–Osin v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.7048v5), Corollary 2.9 and Theorem 6.8, produce a virtually cyclic hyperbolically embedded E(bx). The single-element application needs no pairwise comparison; bx has infinite order since epsilon_b(bx)=1. H is finitely generated, so [Sisto v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.7753v1), Theorem 1, gives the Morse property. In particular <bx> is quasiconvex in the ordinary finite-generator word metric, and the cyclic orbit is quasi-isometrically embedded.

[Ben-Zvi–Kropholler–Lyman v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05374v1), Theorems 1.1 and 5.1, apply to a finite graph realizing this exact subgroup, for the maximal-tree generating set of K. K has one vertex, so the maximal tree is empty and the generating set is x,y,b; its lifted 1-skeleton is exactly that Cayley graph. The lifted two-edge bx circle contains <bx> and is within one edge of it. Connecting geodesic endpoints to the cyclic orbit by paths of length at most one gives a uniform additive quasigeodesic, so Morse quasiconvexity covers the lifted circle as required. Completion is finite and its pointed image is exactly <bx>, not a supergroup, a power, or finite-index substitute. Local-isometry injectivity identifies its fundamental group with that infinite cyclic subgroup. The original bx circle is not itself a local isometry, since b-/x+ span an edge in the base link.

Postcomposition of the completed map by the explicit involution provides two local isometries on the same domain, with generator images bx and By. Their mapping cylinder has finite simplicial flag links by the same full-cone argument. Its fundamental group is the same HNN presentation already reversibly identified with full G. This proves qualitative geometric existence in dimension at most three; it does not furnish the explicit (1,7,8,2) counts. [Hagen–Wise v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07879v3), Remark 3.7, explicitly describes the local-isometry mapping-cylinder gluing. That passage is not used as a substitute for the paper's direct link proof.

Hagen–Wise Corollary 7.2 gives free cubulation for fast automorphisms. Its introduction expressly leaves metric properness, local finiteness and finite dimension outside that definition; Questions 1.2/1.3 and Remark 6.10 discuss cocompact upgrades. [Bongiovanni–Ghosh–Gültepe–Hagen v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15738) Theorem A yields HHG/coarse-median and quasi-isometry conclusions, not the equivariant geometric action required here. [Wu–Ye v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14192) treats a linear tubular family with both fiber generators modified by a fixed generator; its stated cocompact obstruction is not an obstruction to this quadratic b-dependent monodromy.

The report preserves correct author names and the coset notation hA0. Secondary review notation/name slips were reported to this reviewer by root only after the own reconstruction seal; they were not proof premises. The actual paper uses Minasyan–Osin, Ben-Zvi–Kropholler–Lyman, and hA0 correctly.

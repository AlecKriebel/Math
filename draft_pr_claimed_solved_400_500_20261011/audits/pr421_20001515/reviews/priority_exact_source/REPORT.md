## Current primary results and exact applicability

The [current author bibliography](https://robbielyman.com/math/) uses Robbie Lyman, lists work through June 2026, and states that older work was published as Rylee Alanza Lyman. It does not list an exact-target cubulation resolution. I inspected the relevant full current manuscripts, rather than treating this absence as a proof.

[Lyman's CAT(0) paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/1909.03097), latest v3 October 4, 2020 and published in 2023, constructs geometric CAT(0) 2-complex actions; Question 1 asks for cocompact cubulation. Its worked palindromic example is related to the workshop family. The [thesis's current v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.00164), August 20, 2024, retains the distinction between the author's CAT(0) 2-complexes and cubical structures. These sources do not answer the cubical question. Publisher full/PDF access for the CAT(0) article returned block pages, so I do not certify the version of record's byte equality with arXiv.

[Hagen–Wise v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.07879), August 13, 2025, records a correction to the earlier unrestricted claim. Its definition on page 2 explicitly permits failure of metric properness and finite dimension. Corollary 7.2 applies to this fast rank-three quadratic monodromy and gives a free action. Questions 1.2–1.3 and Remark 6.10 retain the geometric upgrade as an unresolved strategy.

[BGGH v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15738), May 15, 2026, gives coarse/HHG characterizations in Theorem A. Example 9.1 uses \(ba,cb\), with torsion-free abelianization, and does not identify the target. A quasi-isometry to a cube complex does not supply the requested equivariant action.

[Wu–Ye v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.14192), April 19, 2025, answers related CAT(0)/virtual-cubulation questions for a linear one-vertex tubular family. In rank three that displayed family has free abelianization rank at least three, unlike the target's rank two. I used its general family and Example 4.11 for scope; the introduction has an apparent \(c/b\) typographical error which cannot silently define the intended automorphism.

The published [Ben-Zvi–Kropholler–Lyman folding theorem](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0305004121000645) supplies a subgroup completion inside an already NPC cube complex, with finite termination under convex-cocompactness. It is an important precursor to the compact cyclic local-isometry construction; it does not automatically establish that hypothesis or build this entire mapping torus. [Leary's foundational cubical results](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.1540) likewise provide standard verification tools, not an exact-group identification. The primary Samuelson CAT(0)-space construction and Leary–Niblo–Wise nearby rank-three examples were checked; neither supplies this exact cubical conclusion. The latter's inverse signs and first homology prevent mistaken identification.

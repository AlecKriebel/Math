## Exact contribution

The research note proves an explicit counterexample to the geometric implication from regular negative-mass zero-area singularities on the natural static foliation to the homogeneous weak Bianchi identity, using the boundary-reaching smooth vector-test convention of Burtscher–Kiessling–Tahvildar-Zadeh (BKTZ), arXiv:1901.00813v3. It gives a full C1 approaching-surface ZAS check, mass −2a, an invariantly singular timelike inner end, absolute integrability of the complete contraction for every specified test, and the exact boundary functional. A normalized radial test gives −8π.

The source report is informal about arbitrary spacetime/slice admissibility. The note states the interpretation explicitly and does not certify every unstated author-intended strengthening. Regularity on every arbitrary tilted hypersurface, tests smooth after collapsing the boundary sphere, an added energy condition, prescribed electromagnetic matter, and the wider evolution program are outside the theorem. The example violates NEC. A short uniformly spacelike-tilt calculation and a nearly null control make the slice boundary concrete.

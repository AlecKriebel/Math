## Exact claim and endpoints

For every finite abstract simplicial S with |S| TOP homeomorphic to S^(2d), d >= 0, a missing d-simplex T on actual vertices, with every proper face present, makes |S^(d) union {T}| nonembeddable TOP in S^(2d). Ghost vertices are excluded. d=0 has no missing singleton; d=1 gives a simple graph with 3n-5 edges, above 3n-6. For positive d an embedded finite d-polyhedron cannot surject onto the 2d-sphere; puncturing yields an R^(2d) embedding.

## Algebra reconstructed

Local manifold homology identifies every link's integral homology with that of its corresponding sphere, including the empty-face global case. The resulting homology sphere is Cohen--Macaulay with symmetric h-vector. With r=2d+1 regular generic parameters on A=F[S], C=F[S^(d)] agrees with A, including multiplication, through degree d+1. The first Koszul homology in that total degree uses only C_(d-1), C_d, C_(d+1), so equals zero. In 0 -> M -> B -> C -> 0, M has no pieces through d and M_(d+1)=F x_T. The Koszul long exact sequence gives an actual injection of this one-dimensional term into B/(Theta), not just an unreduced monomial count. Thus reduced degrees d and d+1 have dimensions h_d and h_d+1.

If a PL embedding in the standard sphere existed, AP Theorem 2 retains the original abstract L in an ambient triangulation P, with the embedding allowed to change. KX Theorems 1.2--1.3 apply to its F2 homology sphere over Q(a_ij), with n=2d+1,m=d and ell=sum x_j. Anisotropy of ell u^2 forces injectivity of ell on degree d. Ambient symmetry gives surjectivity to degree d+1; a graded quotient preserves that surjectivity, contradicting the jump. The restricted r parameters are deliberately overlong on the d-complex, and no incorrect l.s.o.p. assertion on L occurs.

For d>=3 the normalized difference of a TOP embedding is an equivariant map on Weber's full deleted product. Original printed p.2 states 2m >= 3(n+1), inclusive: n=d,m=2d gives d>=3, including d=3. His semilinear embedding means PL after subdivision. Existence, rather than approximation/isotopy of the original embedding, is all that is used.

## Complete dimension-four reconstruction

A triangulated TOP 4-manifold is combinatorial: positive-dimensional links have dimensions <=2 and are spheres; vertex links are closed homology 3-manifolds. Given a loop in a vertex link, put a radial copy in a small cone neighborhood inside a punctured coordinate ball contained in the punctured star. It contracts in that punctured 4-ball and retracts to the link. Poincare plus 3-dimensional PL uniqueness makes the link a PL sphere. DFL printed p.797 gives exactly this dimension-specific conclusion. It supplies the actual PL structure, not its global standardness.

For a legal move support B0=alpha*boundary(beta), its boundary is locally flat: at vertices the link is a PL S2 in a PL S3, to which dimension-three PL Schoenflies applies. Hence the complement C is a compact PL manifold with collar. In the collared S4=B0 union C decomposition, connectedness and van Kampen give pi1(C)=0. The fundamental-class map H4(S4)->H4(S4,C)=H4(B0,boundary B0) has degree +/-1, so the exact sequence gives acyclicity, including H3=0. Hurewicz and Whitehead give contractibility. No assertion C is a standard PL ball is needed.

For a common missing triangle M, a triangle wholly on support vertices would have to equal beta by minimality; beta becomes a face. M therefore preserves inducedness and cannot contain removed alpha. Its entire boundary survives outside the interior support. Contractibility extends it as a disk map, collaring pushes its interior into C, and relative PL general position avoids the old 1-skeleton since 2+1<4; no embedded disk is presumed. Puncture B0 away from the old 2-skeleton, map the support naturally, and transplant B1 to B0 fixing the common boundary. The two maps on the abstract union agree on the common complex. Every disjoint triangle pair with a support factor has disjoint compact images, so a sufficiently close simultaneous general-position approximation keeps the finitely many separations and agreement. A TOP puncturing homeomorphism is allowed because it is then approximated jointly.

For every common-boundary face rho, W=old support vertices minus rho has Q[W]=(alpha minus rho)*(boundary beta minus rho). The first factor is a nonempty simplex. Thus Q[W] is a nonempty cone. Alexander duality makes the complement acyclic, and deleting W deformation retracts it by normalizing barycentric coordinates outside W. Its 2-skeleton has H0,H1 unchanged; adding M preserves H0 and can only quotient H1. Outside vertices exist because the induced ball cannot equal the closed sphere. These are precisely NW Section 5 structural conditions and (I.a),(I.b),(II.a),(II.b).

The first removes alpha-containing pairs; first patches use beta chains whose boundaries repair the missing portions; the remaining partner boundaries are patched with the support-avoiding homological cones of Lemma 5.2. The cone identities and its support control give both cycle closure and disjointness. All deleted/added pairs have a support factor, so their intersection contribution is zero under the hypotheses. This theorem itself is for arbitrary d-complexes, with no standard PL sphere hypothesis hidden in its conclusion.

Vertex insertion (4,0) produces no missing triangle with all edges present, and deletion (0,4)/(1,3) cannot produce the missing triangle without losing a proper face. These exhaust cases.

In (3,1), let U be the six support vertices and V=U union {v}. The new support contains every triangle on U. In the old unaugmented skeleton, homological cones for support vertices and old support edges obey boundary and support avoidance. Define B on the formal full 2-skeleton on seven vertices, fixing support faces and vertices, taking va and ve to cones, and v*beta to M. Its boundary identity on M holds because the edges from v to beta's endpoints were already old, and cones there equal those actual edges. The sum Omega of unordered disjoint triangle pairs is a 4-cycle: every disjoint edge/triangle pair leaves two possible extensions. At most one factor contains v, so support avoidance makes B on both factors a deleted-product chain map, giving cycle omega. Cones are old and cannot contain M.

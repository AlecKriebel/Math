## Exact claim and comparison rules

Let S be a finite simplicial complex with |S| homeomorphic to the topological sphere S^{2d}. Let T be a missing d-simplex on actual vertices of S: T has d+1 vertices, T is not a face, and every proper subset is a face. The target is that |S^{(d)} ∪ {T}| admits no arbitrary topological embedding in S^{2d}. The source and the prospective embedding both have unrestricted topological quantifiers.

An earlier theorem would defeat substantive full-result priority if it explicitly stated this result or supplied it as a direct consequence with both quantifiers covered. The following do not suffice by themselves: a PL-source restriction; a PL-embedding restriction; failure to be an entire d-skeleton; nonembedding of the d-skeleton of a (2d+1)-sphere; a numerical g-vector theorem; or a coarse face-number inequality with no sharp missing-face calculation. The vacuous d=0 and elementary d=1 cases add no new contribution. A potentially exotic PL structure in dimension four is allowed; no existence claim about such a structure is made.

## Established Lefschetz and ambient-completion lineage

[Adiprasito, Combinatorial Lefschetz theorems beyond positivity, 1812.10454v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.10454v4) was first submitted 26 December 2018; v4 is fixed at 2 July 2019. Observation 4.3, pp. 22–23, already transfers an ambient middle Lefschetz isomorphism to a graded quotient. Corollary 4.8, p. 25, uses the resulting inequality dim A^d(Δ) ≥ dim A^{d+1}(Δ) for subcomplexes. Remark 1.1, p. 8, explicitly limits the application to complexes realized as subcomplexes of triangulated homology spheres/manifolds and leaves arbitrary topological embeddings outside the proved broad face bound. The missing-face target is not stated there. Remark 4.9 also records Kalai's stronger shifted-face exclusion under the same ambient hypotheses. This is decisive prior mechanism overlap, not merely similar vocabulary.

[Adiprasito–Patáková, A higher dimensional version of Fáry's theorem](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.70036), first published 27 February 2025, Theorem 2, retains the original finite complex in an ambient triangulation after changing a PL embedding. The authors explicitly identify folklore antecedents. Lee–Nevo's 2026 revision, Theorem 1.1, independently uses this completion and credits a November 2022 personal communication as well. That private antecedent cannot be independently dated or checked here and is not claimed as a public theorem; it nevertheless limits any originality claim about the completion idea.

The current primary streamlining is [Adiprasito, The Lefschetz property for face rings, HAL hal-05575100v2](https://hal.science/hal-05575100v2), deposited **27 April 2026, 04:10:18**, with matching PDF frontmatter. Author provenance is confirmed by the author's [11 June 2026 blog post](https://kaguprasetya.com/2026/06/11/pipe-dreams-in-commutative-algebra-purity-of-frobenius-and-gorenstein-domains-the-lefschetz-property-is-not-so-ubiquitous/) linking this exact HAL record; the institutional author homepage links that blog. Corollary 5.6 and Lemma 5.7, p. 16, use PL embeddings and the folklore/AP25 completion. Definition 5.8 and Corollary 5.9, p. 17, extend to explicitly defined k-embeddings. Remark 5.11 still separates topological embeddings outside these methods. No missing-face theorem appears. This establishes strong current overlap while preserving the extra topological gap.

[Oba's FPSAC 2025 extended abstract](https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~slc/wpapers/FPSAC2025/56.pdf), Conjecture 1.2 / Theorem 1.3, likewise separates the balanced embedding face bound from the ambient simplicial-sphere subcomplex case. These are broader-lineage checks, not evidence that the exact target remains open. ## What the candidate adds, and what it cannot claim

The candidate's high-dimensional route has an established architecture: middle-degree quotient obstruction plus retained-complex completion, combined with the classical metastable existence theorem. Its missing-face Koszul calculation sharpens the coarse dimension inequality to a one-dimensional jump. For d≥3, the conclusion is a natural deduction from this existing toolkit; a new algebraic embedding principle should not be claimed. This audit did not find an earlier source explicitly packaging that deduction as the exact full target. The candidate's use of Karu–Xiao gives a precisely cited anisotropy theorem; it does not erase the earlier Adiprasito quotient mechanism.

Dimension four is the exact residual distinction. Nevo–Wagner's general transfer theorem supplies the obstruction machinery. Their sphere-specific observation invokes a PL-standard complementary ball, and their induction starts from the simplex boundary. The candidate instead uses a contractible complementary PL manifold, permits a self-intersecting filling map, and starts from a flag subdivision in the source's own PL class. That avoids needing standardness of the global source or its complementary four-ball. None of the checked earlier texts explicitly states this extension. The extension is close to the original proof and should be presented as such, not as new obstruction theory or a resolution of four-dimensional PL Schoenflies/smooth Poincaré.

Accurate preprint framing, subject to the separate mathematical reviews: **a proof of the missing-middle-face conjecture of Nevo–Wagner, using known Lefschetz/embedding results in metastable dimensions and an explicit extension of their bistellar argument in dimension four**. Credit Nevo 2006, the joint PL-source theorem, Adiprasito 2018/2019, and the cited later inputs. The existing candidate already says it does not assert novelty of the combination; retain that restraint. A claim of first proof, a new general embedding method, or a certified previously open problem is not supported by this audit.

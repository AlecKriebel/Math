The literal target has an arbitrary triangulation of a topological even sphere
as source and prohibits an arbitrary topological embedding of its middle
skeleton augmented by a missing middle-dimensional simplex. Removing either
qualification would weaken the problem.

The original catalogue locator is Nevo's contribution to OWR4/2007, printed
p238, Conjecture4. The later Nevo–Wagner paper repeats the full statement as
Conjecture1.3 and proves the PL-standard-source subcase in Theorem1.2. The
independent lineage and mechanism families both checked an earlier exact
formulation in Nevo's fixed arXivmath/0602531v1, Conjecture3.8, posted
23February2006; the journal version renumbers it3.7. This earlier attribution
must appear in the current manuscript.

I independently inspected Adiprasito's fixed arXiv1812.10454v4, Observation4.3
and Corollary4.8. Surjectivity of a Lefschetz map passing through a subcomplex
quotient is already explicit there. The fixed record dates v1 to26December2018
and v4 to2July2019; the mechanism family subsequently checked that the argument
is already in v1. The candidate must credit this mechanism and must not claim a
new Lefschetz obstruction method. Remark1.1 expressly limits the broad face
bound's treatment of arbitrary topological embeddings. That limitation is not
evidence that this narrower missing-face target was still open in2026.

The prospective PL embedding argument combines a missing-face-specific Koszul
dimension calculation, unsubdivided ambient completion, and generic ambient
middle Lefschetz surjectivity. Its d>=3 topological conclusion then uses Weber's
existence theorem. These are credited deductions from established inputs.
The published Karu–Xiao record is ALCO6(2023), no5,1313–1330, online7November2023;
the mathematical text independently checked is Theorems1.2–1.3. AP's fixed
versions and publisher record date the completion statement to2024/2025 and
describe it as folklore, precluding a claim that its first availability is
established by that date.

For d=2, Weber's required bound fails, and a theorem beginning with a PL
embedding cannot by itself prohibit all topological embeddings. The candidate
explicitly adapts the original Nevo–Wagner transfer to the actual PL class of
the triangulated topological4sphere, with a contractible complementary
manifold and a flag-subdivision base. The complete topology review verifies
this adaptation. The original proof starts at the standard simplex boundary;
its text does not supply the flag-base extension as a stated general result.

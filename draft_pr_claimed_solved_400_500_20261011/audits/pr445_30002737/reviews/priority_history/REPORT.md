## Affirmative earlier central resolution: the spin example

The fuller example-section check materially changed the initial bounded non-finding. Frank–Mañibo's arXiv:2108.08642v1, Example5.4 pp32–33, gives Q=2, D={0,1}, G=C4, W=[[1,i],[1,−1]]. The scalar character χ1(g)=g is faithful; its sector is singular by Proposition4.20 and the displayed cocycle bound. Its square character χ2(g)=g² has normalized unitary character matrix, so Corollary4.5 gives Lebesgue diffraction. The source was submitted19 August2021; the official publisher confirms online publication8 August2022, DCDS42(11),5399–5435, DOI10.3934/dcds.2022105. The arXiv pages32–33 were read visually as well as in text. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.08642v1), [publisher](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2022105).

The exact physical-hull equivalence check is recorded in `SPIN_PRIOR_RESOLUTION.md`. The χ1 comb has nonzero coefficients in {1,i,−1,−i}, so replacing each scalar coefficient z by z² is a continuous finite-alphabet local factor on its **actual** comb hull, preserving the support and commuting with all translations. No invisible colour or digit state is appended. This factor is the χ2 comb with Lebesgue diffraction; its diffraction subspace embeds into its real-line translation representation, which pulls back into the χ1 hull representation. Meanwhile Theorem4.1 and Example4.4 identify χ1 diffraction with a singular χ1-sector measure. Its periodic real-line lift is singular. Thus the earlier χ1 comb meets the original absence-of-AC antecedent and violates its consequent. Full digit-forgetting conjugacy is unnecessary because the square observable depends only on retained scalar coefficients.

Independent finite checks in `check_spin_antecedent.py` verify primitive transition power4, the faithful spin encoding, the square map, orthogonality of χ2(W)'s rows, and the displayed inequality margins. Aperiodicity is consistent with the known aperiodic RS character factor; the cited example and theorem are used within their stated primitive/aperiodic setting. The full real-line factor/diffraction mechanism avoids confusing a torus spectral measure with an ambient real-line measure.

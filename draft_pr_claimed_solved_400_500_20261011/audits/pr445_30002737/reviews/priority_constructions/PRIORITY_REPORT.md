## Recoverable published earlier closure

[Frank–Mañibo, *Spectral theory of spin substitutions*](https://www.aimsciences.org/article/doi/10.3934/dcds.2022105), DCDS 42 (2022), 5399–5435, was online August 8, 2022; [arXiv:2108.08642v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.08642v1) was submitted August 19, 2021. Published Example 10, p.5430 / PDF p.32 (v1 Example 5.4, pp.32–33), has G=C4, D={0,1} and W=[[1,i],[1,−1]]. It reports H^χ1 singular and H^χ2 Lebesgue. Proposition 14 (pp.5426–5427), Theorem A.1 and Corollary 2 (pp.5432–5433) relate these to scalar comb diffraction. Table 1 gives 2f(12)=0.688005 and error <10^−3, below log 2. Complete relevant pages and the example/table were visually checked in both versions. The bound/error are published assertions, not an independently reproduced quadrature certificate.

The following is the reviewer's reconstruction of the consequence, rather than a claim that the paper expressly names van Enter's conjecture. Put u_n=χ1(g_n)∈{1,i,−1,−i}, and use the actual scalar comb ω=Σu_n δ_n with its own translation hull. Its diffraction is singular by the cited result. This recorded alphabet retains g_n injectively. More crucially, u_n²=χ2(g_n) is a pointwise function of the recorded physical weights, so the square observable exists on this actual hull even if digit labels are not recovered. Corollary 2 gives Lebesgue diffraction for the squared comb, and Theorem A.1 gives Lebesgue spectral measure to this observable on the scalar subshift. Passing to the unit suspension gives a nonzero absolutely continuous R-action component; the support lattice and nonzero weights recover the suspension phase. Thus the needed dynamical component survives in the actual weighted-comb hull. No unproved full digit-hull conjugacy is being inserted.

This contrasts with assigning both colours the same scalar weight: that operation loses the very information needed for the proposed hidden observable. Here squaring uses information still present in the original measure.

Primitivity/frequency use the source’s substitution rule and Proposition 2. Aperiodicity is used as published input from Example 10, not independently reproved. All weights are nonzero and all integer sites occur; support Z+t determines phase modulo Z. The scalar image is a uniquely ergodic factor and its square lookup is measurable on that factor. Checks also verify χ2(W)χ2(W)*=2I and the error-margin arithmetic: 0.688005+0.001=0.689005 < log 2 by 0.00414218.

## Primary target

Aernout C.D. van Enter, Aperiodicity in statistical mechanics, Conjecture 1, in Mini-Workshop: Dynamical versus Diffraction Spectra in the Theory of Quasicrystals, Oberwolfach Report 53/2014, pp.2987–3013; relevant contribution pp.2996–2997. The question asks whether absence of an absolutely continuous diffraction component forces absence of one in the dynamical spectrum. No dimension-one, simplicity, irreducibility or all-factor-diffraction restriction is printed. The preceding context describes translation-bounded configurations and the unitary translation representation on L² of an invariant measure.

## Credited ingredients and comparison

- M. Baake and U. Grimm, Kinematic diffraction is insufficient to distinguish order from disorder, Phys. Rev. B 79 (2009), 020203(R), erratum 80 (2009), 029903(E). Preprint title Can Kinematic Diffraction Distinguish Order from Disorder?, https://arxiv.org/abs/0810.5750v2. Strict ergodicity, balanced signs and delta two-point correlations on pp.2–3 are credited inputs.
- M. Baake, D. Lenz and A. van Enter, Dynamical versus diffraction spectrum for structures with finite local complexity, Ergodic Theory Dynam. Systems 35 (2015), 2017–2043, https://arxiv.org/abs/1307.7518, https://doi.org/10.1017/etds.2014.28. Its reconstruction uses a collection of factor diffractions and does not identify the full dynamical spectral type with one physical diffraction measure.
- Y. Nagai, Absence of absolutely continuous diffraction spectrum for certain S-adic tilings, https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.14375. This gives sufficient diffraction criteria; it is not a proof of the unrestricted implication.
- Current comparison: Renormalization techniques for inflation systems and some of their applications, IUCr 2026, https://journals.iucr.org/a/issues/2026/04/00/gau5007/. Its cocycle criterion concerns absence of absolutely continuous diffraction under additional hypotheses.

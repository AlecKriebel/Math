The proposition under test is that the unrestricted positive-definite Hessian property on a compact affine manifold need not survive arbitrary small smooth affine deformations. A valid negative resolution needs one compact central Hessian manifold and arbitrarily small genuine flat torsion-free affine structures on the same marked smooth manifold for which no positive-definite Hessian metric exists. Failure of one selected metric, or failure only of invariant metrics, is insufficient.

Candidate to check independently: on the fixed standard smooth torus R²/Z², D_t = D_0 + t dx⊗dx⊗∂_y. We must verify global descent, completeness, flatness, torsion-freeness, smooth smallness, and genuine change under affine gauge. The developing map F_t(x,y)=(x,y+t x²/2) should conjugate standard deck translations to an affine representation. Its failure to descend as an ordinary torus diffeomorphism would not invalidate an equivariant developing pair, but requires care.

# Root primary-source and analytic reconstruction

This is verification of the frozen eligible PR463 head, not a new original
proof attempt or a final priority classification.

The root independently visually inspected the complete original problem page
(Furuhata–Matsuzoe–Urakawa, printed126), Baues–Goldman v2 printed8 and20,
and Shima1995 printed735. The original target is a compact Riemannian local
Hessian metric under small affine-structure deformations. The source imposes
no fixed holonomy or excluded dimension and separately identifies the
hyperbolic special case. The whole-plane torus example lies outside that case.
The family on Baues–Goldman p.20 is exactly F_t(x,y)=(x,y+t x²/2), and p.8
uses compact-open C∞ developing maps. Every derivative of F_t−F_0 on a fixed
compact set tends to zero. The cube-root period parameter is continuous;
t=s³ also makes it smooth. The global torus connection difference t dx⊗dx⊗Y
is smooth. F_t descends equivariantly to the varying quotient, not to a map
of the original standard quotient. Its free proper deck action is conjugate
by the developing diffeomorphism to translations.

The root reconstructs the decisive all-metric step directly. For any smooth
positive tensor g, the global torus frame gives periodic A=g(X,X), B=g(X,Y),
C=g(Y,Y). Hessian local potentials imply symmetry of Dg. Because D_X X=tY
and all other frame derivatives vanish, (D_Xg)(Y,X)=B_x−tC and
(D_Yg)(X,X)=A_y. Therefore d(g(X,.))=tC dx∧dy. On the closed torus Stokes
forces t∫C=0, and C>0 forces t=0. This uses arbitrary candidate metrics,
without invariance, parallelism, a global potential or continuous dependence
on t. The second Codazzi condition C_x=B_y is consistent and unneeded for
the contradiction. Indefinite 2dxdy and semidefinite dx² evade the argument;
the cover metric F_t*(du²+dv²) is positive Hessian but B=tx fails periodicity.

The parallel area dx∧dy follows from zero connection trace, equivalently
det J_t=det L_t=1. Developing linear holonomy for the x generator is
S_t=[[1,0],[t,1]], whereas tangent parallel transport can use its inverse.
Either sign is nontrivial, so no gauge conjugacy makes it the central identity.
If a separate older theorem supplies a D-parallel positive metric, its matrix
H is constant in global developing coordinates and S_tᵀHS_t=H. The (1,2)
entry gives B+tC=B, which is impossible for C>0 and t≠0. Crucially this last
argument alone excludes only parallel metrics. A verified older general
theorem implying their existence from *any* Hessian metric is required for
the proposed classical-priority classification.

The root also visually inspected Delanoë1989 Théorème3, printed67, and
Jörgens1954 printed130 from an official two-page preview. Delanoë's compact
local-Hessian theorem supplies a cohomologous positive Hessian metric of
determinant C times a prescribed density squared. With the parallel density
on this shear torus, the lifted determinant in global developing coordinates
would be a positive constant. On simply connected R², twice integrating the
Codazzi identities gives a global smooth strictly convex cover potential.
Rescaling it makes its Hessian determinant1. The Jörgens p.130 derivative
estimate, applied at any fixed point in balls of arbitrary radius R, forces
the gradients of its two diagonal Hessian entries to vanish as R→∞; the
mixed derivatives then force its remaining Hessian entry constant. This
yields a D-parallel positive metric and the same shear contradiction.
This is a provisional independent application of older theorems, to be
tested by the priority family and a fresh whole-result adversary. The full
Jörgens paper was not obtained, and no claim of directly auditing its full
proof is made. No exact earlier printed answer to item5(b) is established
by these observations.

A concrete control distinguishes existence of a replacement metric from the
false stronger assertion that the given metric is already D-parallel. On the
standard affine torus, g=(1+ε cos(2πx))dx²+dy² for 0<ε<1 is positive, periodic,
and locally Hessian with potential (x²+y²)/2−ε cos(2πx)/(4π²). D_0 preserves
dx∧dy, but D_0g has nonzero xxx component −2πε sin(2πx). Its Levi-Civita
connection is therefore not D_0. A modern search excerpt asserting equality
for the given metric cannot be used without additional hypotheses. An older
theorem supplying another positive metric is consistent with this control.
The root passed this caution to the internal priority and candidate reviewers.

## Author's chosen precise claim

    integral product_i |L(1/2+it,chi_i)|^(2a_i) dt
       ~ T A product_psi G(1+M_psi)^2/G(1+2M_psi) (log T)^E,

where psi ranges over distinct primitive inducers, M_psi=sum_{psi_i=psi}a_i and E=sum M_psi^2. Primitive equality is the equivalence relation; conjugate but unequal characters remain distinct. The local coefficient generating function is B_p(z)=product_i(1-chi_i(p)z)^(-a_i), with the analytic-at-zero branch. J_p=sum_r |b_p(r)|^2 p^(-r), equivalently a single common-phase local integral. A is the increasing-prime limit of product_p(1-1/p)^E J_p. This includes arbitrary fixed different moduli, repeated primitive inducers, and finite Euler deletions. It asserts a rigorous positive convergence regularization into an absolutely convergent residual product and finitely many |L^S(1,psi*conjugate(phi))| powers. ## Claims to separate

1. Literal squared product of possibly repeated factors. Even common outer powers with integer constituent multiplicities may already cover this; a new heuristic requirement is not a theorem requirement.
2. Independently chosen nonnegative real weights. Integer tuples and a single common real outer weight are separate quantifiers; a rational approximation or formal continuation is not a proved L-function asymptotic.
3. Arbitrary fixed different moduli and all correlated finite deletions. Common modulus can be arranged by Q=lcm(q_i), but restorations are reciprocal Euler factors and averaging must retain each shared phase.
4. Repeated primitive characters versus distinct nonreal conjugates. Exponent and Barnes factors must aggregate equal primitives only.
5. Ordered Euler convergence. Printed convergence assertions in predecessors need independent scrutiny; O(1/p) nonprincipal cross terms are not absolutely summable merely because their ordered sum converges.

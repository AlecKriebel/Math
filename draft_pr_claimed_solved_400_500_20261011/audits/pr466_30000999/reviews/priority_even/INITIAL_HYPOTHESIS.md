# Initial hypothesis and independence record

Created 2026-10-12 UTC, before reading the frozen proof, source gate, prior audits, or peer verdicts.

The target is a hypothesis to be checked: in sphere dimension at least two (ambient n >= 3), every fixed finite real p >= 1 admits no uniform same-order W_p Lipschitz inverse for the Funk transform even on smooth even probability densities bounded above and uniformly away from zero. The proposed quantitative obstruction is a harmonic localization family with lower divergence k^((n-2)/(2p)). A circle-even isometry is a claimed boundary case.

Independent prior: the unrestricted probability-measure inverse is likely obstructed immediately by the odd nullspace of the classical Funk transform. This does not by itself settle an inverse question restricted to even measures, and it does not establish the proposed W_p lower-divergence mechanism or its novelty. Classical spectral smoothing may already imply some even inverse instability, but an exact same-order W_p statement requires checking the transport norm and the localization estimates.

Success criteria: obtain the original Stephens formulation from a primary source; distinguish original and strengthened claims; locate primary classical spectral/smoothing statements and pertinent transport linearization/instability statements; check whether the proposed mechanism merely rephrases known conclusions or contains an independently verified strengthening. Absence of an exact printing in bounded searches is not evidence of an open problem or priority.

Falsification targets: parity convention and normalization; whether n denotes ambient or sphere dimension; sign and vanishing of eigenvalues; all finite p versus integer/even p; genuine density lower and upper bounds independent of harmonic degree; sharp harmonic mass localization; nonlinear W_p-to-negative-norm comparisons with constants uniform in degree; circle transport commutation and equality; original source's qualification by antipodal symmetry.

No external communication will be undertaken. Only public sources and own calculations will be used. Outputs are confined to this audit directory.

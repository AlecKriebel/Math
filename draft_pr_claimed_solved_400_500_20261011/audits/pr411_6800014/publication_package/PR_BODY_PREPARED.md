## Exact result and preprint

Problem 6800014 / AMR-067-0014, Morgan-Pansu Question 14: whether every local maximum of Scal²/||Ric||² among left-invariant metrics on a fixed solvable Lie group satisfies Ric = cI + D with D a derivation of that group’s Lie algebra.

The research note proves a negative answer for the known five-dimensional family A_u = J ⊕ uE₁₂, for every 0 < u < 1/2. Its standard metric is a non-strict local maximum with F = 1/3 among **all** nearby metrics on the fixed group, fails its derivation equation, and has an explicit same-group comparison with F = 9/17. The proof includes the full metric Gram matrix, an unrestricted orthogonal cross-block, and a uniform cubic remainder along the flat family. A semidefinite Hessian alone is not used as a neighborhood proof. Neither endpoint is included.

The metric is a geometric Ricci soliton, isometric to R² × H₃; a solvsoliton presentation on another transitive group does not satisfy the assertion about the original fixed group.

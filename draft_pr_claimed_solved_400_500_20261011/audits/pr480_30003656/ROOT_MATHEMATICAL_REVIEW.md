## Classifier and variance

Finite pointed sets are all free on their nonpoint elements. Their opposite
has finite products and is the Lawvere theory of an object with one distinguished
global element. It also has finite limits: finite colimits of finite pointed sets
remain finite. Equations between variables and the constant present finite pointed
quotients, so the finite-presentation site is Cᵒᵖ with the trivial topology.
The evaluation functor U:C→Set is the generic model. Presheaves on Cᵒᵖ are
covariant on C; successors in forcing are pointed maps out of the current stage.
This matches the analogous bi-pointed classifier in BBC Example 6.2, but the
candidate needs only one constant.

## Unbounded forcing calculations

Given any successor A→B and b in B, the collapse B→A₀ identifies b with c.
Thus no stage can force ¬(b=c), and every stage forces ¬¬(b=c). Including
all successor elements proves β in U. β implies ¬¬β=¬α intuitionistically.
Nondegeneracy and the nonpoint in A₁ keep this from asserting U is a singleton.

For V, any current nonpoint stays a nonpoint under every pointed injection,
so its equality to c is forced false and its double-negated equality fails.
Every finite pointed stage admits a successor with a fresh nonpoint. Hence no
stage forces β, including every successor of any stage, which is exactly the
negation clause for α. A₀ nonetheless has no current nonpoint witness.

Logical cross-check: β is equivalent to ¬∃x¬(x=c), so α is equivalent to
¬¬∃x¬(x=c). It is not equivalent to ∃x¬(x=c). In classical singleton and
two-element pointed Set models α is false and true, respectively.

## Geometric reflection

Restriction i*: [C,Set]→[D,Set] is identity on object components. It preserves
all objectwise limits/colimits and has a right Kan extension as right adjoint.
It is therefore an inverse image functor; identity-on-objects makes it conservative.
Interpretations of equality, finite conjunction, joins, and images all commute
with restriction. A sequent φ⊢ψ is the statement that φ∩ψ→φ is an isomorphism.
Validity in V reflects to U. Soundness in V and generic-model completeness
then imply redundancy. Neither implication nor universal quantification is
claimed preserved; their different behavior is the counterexample's mechanism.

## Independent syntactic mechanism

Inductively write each geometric antecedent as a small disjunction of branches
∃z E(x,z), each E a finite conjunction of equalities. An empty disjunction has
no branches. Finite conjunction uses finite products of branch sets; existential
quantification appends its witness to the finite context. No infinite conjunction
is used. Disjunction and existential elimination reduce derivability to each
branch with its witnesses free.

For such a branch quotient the finite list c,x,z by the equivalence relation
generated by E. Its valuation satisfies E, and V-validity supplies ψ at this
finite stage. Structural induction on a witness of pointwise geometric truth
extracts a derivation: equality chains follow E; conjunction combines; disjunction
selects its witnessed index; existential witnesses have representatives in the
finite list (take the first representative to avoid any arbitrary choice).
Truth is immediate; falsity has no such witness. Reassemble by eliminations.
This covers infinite branch sets through the small infinitary disjunction rule;
it does not assert a finite decision procedure for arbitrary geometric formulas.

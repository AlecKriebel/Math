# Exact target and falsifiable acceptance criteria

Selected source: Buchholtz, contribution to OWR 53/2017, and Bezem,
Buchholtz, Coquand, *Syntactic forcing models for coherent logic*, Section 7.
Problem 30003656 / OWR-15958-003 asks whether every T-redundant sentence
holds in the generic model when T is algebraic (purely equational).

Redundancy means: for every geometric implication σ in the original language,
intuitionistic derivability T+α ⊢ σ implies T ⊢ σ. Equality is included;
disjunctions may be indexed by arbitrary small sets and contexts are finite.

Candidate: T is one sort with constant c and no nonlogical axioms. Define
β = ∀x ¬¬(x=c), α = ¬β. For C finite pointed sets and all pointed maps,
E=[C,Set] has generic model U(A)=A. For D the same objects with pointed
injections, F=[D,Set] has model V(A)=A. U validates β and ¬α; V validates α.
Restriction from E to F is conservative and preserves geometric interpretation.
Thus α is redundant yet fails in the genuine generic T-model.

Acceptance requires all of the following, independently challenged:

1. C really presents the classifier and U its generic model, with correct variance.
2. Forcing calculations hold for the full unbounded categories, including A₀.
3. Geometric reflection includes equality antecedents, false antecedents,
   existential witnesses, and arbitrary small disjunctions.
4. The finite-quotient alternative supplies actual intuitionistic derivations,
   without importing classical completeness or an unproved choice principle.
5. The exact original algebraic question admits the candidate's signature and α.
6. Finite computations reproduce, and their truncation limits are accurately stated.
7. Priority comparison reads relevant primary full texts, especially 2019 follow-up.
8. No first-resolution or novelty claim exceeds available evidence; no paper
   claiming a newly solved open problem is published if the target was already solved.

Verification is not a new proof search; original 2/5 proof-attempt count remains.
Stronger unstated refinements of redundancy are outside this exact claim.

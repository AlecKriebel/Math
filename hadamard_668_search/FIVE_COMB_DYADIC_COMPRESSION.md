# Dyadic compression of the five-comb packing

## Status

This is a dependency-free exact reformulation of every root-of-unity norm
condition through order 16. It is a filter and construction architecture, not
an `H(668)` candidate.

## 1. Compression theorem

For completed rows `X_r`, define their length-16 integer compressions

```text
rho[r,a] = sum X_r(p),  over p = a mod 16.
```

Roots of orders `1,2,4,8,16` together are all sixteenth roots. Fourier
inversion therefore gives the equivalence

```text
sum_r PAF(rho_r)(t) = 334 if t=0,
                      0   otherwise,       t in Z/16.
```

Equivalently,

```text
sum_r Circ(rho_r) Circ(rho_r)^T = 334 I_16.
```

If `C_k` is the total positive-lag aperiodic correlation, define

```text
b0 = sum C_k, k=0 mod 16
b8 = sum C_k, k=8 mod 16
bt = sum C_k, k=+t or -t mod 16,  1<=t<=7.
```

Then the dyadic layer is simply

```text
b0=b1=...=b8=0.
```

The transformation from these buckets to the usual equations at
`1,-1,i,zeta_8,zeta_16` has determinant `-256`, so the two zero systems are
equivalent over the integers.

## 2. Component polarization

For a residue vector `z` containing `N` actual coefficients, define

```text
q0(z) = (||z||^2-N)/2
qt(z) = sum z[r,a] z[r,a+t],       1<=t<=7
q8(z) = (1/2) sum z[r,a] z[r,a+8].
```

For disjoint components `u,v`, the bilinear polarization `B` is

```text
B0(u,v) = u dot v
Bt(u,v) = sum (u[r,a]v[r,a+t] + v[r,a]u[r,a+t]), 1<=t<=7
B8(u,v) = sum u[r,a]v[r,a+8].
```

Exactly,

```text
q(sum z_i) = sum q(z_i) + sum_{i<j} B(z_i,z_j).
```

This turns the nonlinear root conditions into one nine-coordinate quadratic
target on five natural supercomponents: four paired carrier groups and the
hole vector.

## 3. Universal energy shell

The two length-84 rows contribute six signs to residues `0,1,2,3`; the two
length-83 rows contribute six signs to residues `0,1,2`. Thus 14 compression
cells are even and 50 are odd.

Let `e_j` count even cells of absolute value `2j`, and let `o_1,o_2` count
odd cells of absolute value 3 and 5. The energy equation is

```text
e1 + 4 e2 + 9 e3 + 2 o1 + 6 o2 = 71.
```

There are exactly 1,589 possible magnitude-count patterns, and every one
satisfies

```text
e1+e3 = 1 mod 2.
```

## 4. Memory-safe exact architecture

Fix one of the 31 surviving projective cores (core zero is excluded by
`verify_five_comb_core0_obstruction.py`) and group slots as

```text
(0,4), (1,5), (2,6), (3,7).
```

Each group owns disjoint free projective bits and emits one 64-coordinate
residue vector. Session sizing of a direct four-stage design gave an upper
bound of 330,301,440 carrier assignments per core and more than 21 GiB before
hash-table overhead. Those engineering figures are not regenerated by the
advertised arithmetic verifier, so they should be treated as design
estimates; in any case, that table must not be materialized on this host.

Two safe alternatives are:

1. meet in the middle, with session-estimated halves of at most 53,760 and
   430,080 states;
2. staged cyclotomic filtering through
   `Phi_1,Phi_2,Phi_4,Phi_8,Phi_16`, whose per-row additive dimensions are
   `1,1,2,4,8`.

The first two roots each admit only 672 ordered signed row profiles in twelve
absolute-value classes, making them the natural first filters.

A separate exploratory session found bare affine or reachability tests
modulo 3, 5, and 7 too weak: all 704 tested spectral-class/core units reached
full nine-dimensional affine rank after cross catalogs were included, and
every tested class had a modular zero. That generating audit is not retained
by the verifier below, so this is a recorded search observation rather than a
certified theorem. A useful new relaxation should retain component
consistency or combine modulo 8 with the physical high-lag table.

# Primitive-unit reduction for the shell-two `LP(333)` profiles

## Status

This result does **not** construct a Legendre pair or a Hadamard
matrix of order 668.  It gives one new exact structural reduction for the
five shell-two profile orbits and one exact negative audit of the next two
characteristic-37 diagonal coefficients.

The positive result is:

> Every physical phase placement over every one of the 84 labelled
> shell-two profile images has both recombined channel words nonzero in
> every primitive factor modulo 167.

Equivalently, each channel word is a unit in the six-factor primitive
algebra.  This removes every degenerate branch from the prime-167 norm cone
and permits a global ratio-torus normalization.  It does not exclude any of
the five profile orbits.

The negative result is:

> The exact physical margins together with the next two diagonal
> characteristic-37 coefficients exclude none of the 405 margin targets.

They reduce the aggregate candidate count by almost exactly `37^2`, leaving
`1,123,966,766,238,638,605` assignments.

## 1. Setup

For one channel, let the three physical row-residue words be

```text
U_0, U_1, U_2
```

and let `alpha` be the pinned ninth root of unity in the repository's
prime-167 field.  Recombine them as

```text
W = U_0 + alpha U_1 + alpha^2 U_2.
```

Writing

```text
q=167^6 mod 37=11,
H=<q^2>={1,10,26},
E=F_(167^12),
```

the primitive part of the `H`-invariant algebra is

```text
A_prim = E^6.
```

Its coordinates can be taken as

```text
w_r = W(zeta_37^(167^r)),       r=0,...,5.
```

The involution pairs factors `r` and `r+3`.  The three primitive norm
equations for two channels are

```text
w_(A,r) w_(A,r+3)^(167^3)
  + w_(B,r) w_(B,r+3)^(167^3) = 0,   r=0,1,2.          (1)
```

The previously known factorwise parameterization of (1) has a degenerate
branch whenever

```text
(w_(A,r),w_(B,r))=(0,0).
```

The audit below proves the stronger statement that neither coordinate can
vanish individually on the physical shell-two alphabets.

## 2. Primitive-unit theorem

Let `P` be any of the 84 formal symmetry images represented by the five
exact shell-two profile orbits.  Fix either channel `X`.  In every one of
the twelve nonzero column classes, choose any row support realizing the
physical residue-count triple, including the repository's alternating
complement convention.  Include the fixed normalized zero column.

Then, for every such physical choice and every `r=0,...,5`,

```text
w_(X,r) != 0 in E.                                      (2)
```

Consequently,

```text
W_A,W_B in A_prim^x
```

for every physical phase placement over every shell-two profile image.

### Exact finite audit

For a class whose physical residue profile has `a` active fibers, its
complete local alphabet has exactly `3^a` values in `E`.  Fibers of count
one choose one of three row phases; fibers of count two equivalently choose
the missing phase; counts zero and three are fixed.  Thus the product of
the twelve class alphabets is the complete physical placement space, with
no relaxation.

For a fixed profile, channel, and primitive factor, the verifier writes

```text
w_(X,r) = c_X + sum_(j=0)^11 eta_(r,j) W_(X,j)
```

in all 36 prime-field coordinates of the ambient exact field
representation.  It partitions whole class alphabets into two halves,
enumerates the two exact sumsets, sorts the full 36-byte keys, and counts
all labelled pairs satisfying

```text
left + right + c_X = 0 in F_167^36.
```

The largest case has 30 active trits and two lists of

```text
3^15 = 14,348,907
```

entries.  All 90 required channel/factor counts are exactly zero.  Every
case also checks:

1. the period formula against direct polynomial evaluation through the
   repository's CRT coordinates;
2. the meet-in-the-middle count against direct Cartesian enumeration on a
   deterministic small prefix;
3. a guaranteed-positive synthetic control.

The maximal `3^15` by `3^15` instance has an independent replay using a
pinned linear map to `Z/(2^64)`.  Exact key equality implies equality of
the linear hashes.  The physical left and target hash sets are disjoint,
so the replay is an exact nonintersection proof and does not assume that
the hash is collision-free.  Its positive control succeeds.

### Closure over all 84 images

The exact profile classification uses the order-24 action

```text
C6 rotations x A-star x B-star.
```

The normalized zero column retains only the order-12 labelled phase action
generated by the rotations and `B-star`.  On rows and columns, `B-star` is
realized by

```text
row r -> 3-r mod 9,       column c -> -c mod 37.
```

The normalized B zero word is fixed by this reflection.  The normalized A
zero word has trivial affine row stabilizer, so `A-star` is not a physical
symmetry of the normalized lift.

Exact enumeration partitions the 84 formal images into ten physical
orbits, represented by the five canonical profiles and their five
`A-star` images.  Their sizes, in that seed order, are

```text
12,6,6,6,12, 12,6,6,6,12,
```

and they are disjoint with union size 84.

For the five canonical seeds, both channels and all six factors require 60
audits.  For the five `A-star` seeds, only the A channel is new; B is
unchanged.  Those add 30 audits.  In the repository's pullback convention,
rotations act on evaluation exponents by `4^(-s)`.  The row reflection in
`B-star` applies the coefficient Frobenius `167^3`, so its source exponent
is multiplied by

```text
-167^(-3) 4^(-s) mod 37.
```

These nonzero multipliers permute the six primitive `q`-orbits.  Auditing
all six factors therefore closes (2) over each physical orbit.

## 3. Ratio-torus corollary

Because both primitive channel words are units, every physical solution of
the primitive complementarity equation can be normalized by

```text
R = W_B / W_A in A_prim^x.
```

Dividing the norm equation by `W_A W_A^*` gives the exact unitary equation

```text
R R^* = -1.                                             (3)
```

In paired coordinates, (3) is

```text
1 + R_r R_(r+3)^(167^3) = 0,
```

hence

```text
R_(r+3) = (-R_r^(-1))^(167^9),       r=0,1,2.           (4)
```

Thus three arbitrary nonzero coordinates of `R` determine the other three.
If `M=167^12`, the primitive ratio torus has `(M-1)^3` points.  The entire
all-coordinate-nonzero part of the three paired norm equations has
`(M-1)^9` points: choose the six coordinates of `W_A` and the first three
coordinates of `R`.

This replaces the degenerate/nondegenerate case split by a single
multiplicative search space.  The origin factor, the inverse-CRT sparse
alphabet intersection, exact margins, and integral verification remain.

physical margin fixes `A_0`, the first two nonconstant coefficients of its
diagonal norm are the additive summaries

```text
t1 = A_1 conjugate(A_0) - A_0 conjugate(A_1),

t2 = A_2 conjugate(A_0) + A_0 conjugate(A_2)
     - A_1 conjugate(A_1).
```

For each sequence and each exact augmentation, the verifier enumerates its
complete local phase alphabet and forms the exact distribution of
`(t1,t2)` in `F_37^2`.  It convolves the six conditioned distributions and
extracts the zero sum.

The convolution is exact.  It uses 2D transforms modulo

```text
1000037, 1000333, 1000777, 1000999, 1001369,
```

```text
1,003,519,385,826,631,335,762,098,865,127 > 3^54.
```

## 5. Assessment

The primitive-unit theorem is a genuine new exact lemma within this
repository.  It improves the algebraic architecture: all divisions needed
for the ratio-torus form are now certified on the complete shell-two
profile class, and no degenerate cone branch needs to be searched.

It is not by itself a publishable H(668) result.  It excludes zero profile
or margin targets, and the `T1/T2` layer still leaves about `1.124e18`
physical assignments.  Even an unrealistic billion complete checks per
second would require more than 35 years.  The unit theorem changes the
coordinate system, not that candidate count.

The mathematically sensible continuation is therefore a multiplicative
inverse-CRT sieve on the ratio `R`, for example a provable character,
resultant, or local-coset restriction derived from (3)-(4).  Continuing
with one-candidate-at-a-time higher-digit lifting would not use the new
reduction and is not justified by these counts.  If no nontrivial
multiplicative restriction emerges quickly, this lane should be preserved
as a strengthening of the five-orbit classification rather than treated
as near-term convergence to H(668).

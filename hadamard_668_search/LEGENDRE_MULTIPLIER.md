# Exact order-three multiplier sublane

`search_legendre_333_multiplier.py` restricts the fixed-compression LP(333)
model to pairs invariant under multiplication by an order-three unit modulo
333.  This is an exact search inside a motivated but non-necessary subfamily.
Failure here does not rule out the prescribed compression or a general
Legendre pair.

There are four order-three subgroups compatible with the prescribed
length-37 compression.  Representatives and their nonidentity squares are

```text
<10>  = {1,10,100}       <112> = {1,112,223}
<121> = {1,121,322}      <211> = {1,211,232}.
```

For multiplier 121 or 211, 333 coordinates collapse to 113 orbits.  Because
periodic correlations are constant on multiplier orbits of lags, the 166
independent LP equations collapse to 56 representative equations.  XOR
literals are cached by pairs of sign orbits, producing about 13,000 variables
before optional symmetry breaking instead of more than 111,000.

The model retains the fixed column margins, modulo-3 table, modulo-9 PAF
equations, and sharp distance bounds from the unrestricted exact model.  It
also quotients by every translation/reflection that preserves both the
multiplier-invariant subspace and the fixed compression.

## One subgroup is impossible without search

The subgroup generated by 112 (equivalently 223) is ruled out at lag 111.
Since

```text
112*i = i + 111*i  (mod 333),
```

invariance makes the signs at `i` and `i+111` equal whenever `i` is not
divisible by 3.  At least 222 of the 333 comparisons therefore agree, so the
cyclic Hamming distance at lag 111 is at most 111.

The fixed 9-bit column weights independently force the distance of each
sequence at lag 111 to be at least 112.  This contradiction rules out the
entire `<112>` subfamily.  The test suite mechanically checks the orbit-edge
count 111 and the fixed-column lower bound; CP-SAT also reports the model
infeasible during initial constraint loading.

## Reproduction

```sh
../tmp/hadamard-env/bin/python -m unittest -v test_legendre_multiplier.py

../tmp/hadamard-env/bin/python search_legendre_333_multiplier.py \
  --multiplier 112 --workers 1 --time-limit 2

../tmp/hadamard-env/bin/python search_legendre_333_multiplier.py \
  --multiplier 121 --workers 1 --max-memory-mb 2048 --time-limit 3600 \
  --output output/legendre_pair_333_multiplier_121.json
```

The `<10>`, `<121>`, and `<211>` searches remain open.  No multiplier-invariant
LP(333) candidate has been found.

# Exact symmetry action on the LP(333) profile zero gate

## Status

The 24-profile equation

```text
D_t=0
```

has a certified natural symmetry group

```text
G_profile = C6 x C2_A x C2_B,             |G_profile|=24.       (1)
```

The order-six factor is common even rotation of the twelve cyclotomic
classes.  Each involution is coefficient star on one channel:

```text
p_(X,j)  ->  kappa(p_(X,j+6)),

kappa(p0,p1,p2)=(p0,p2,p1).                              (2)
```

Star leaves the autocorrelation of that one channel exactly unchanged, so
the two channel involutions are independent.  The three factors commute.

On the 22 aggregate row-sum targets, (1) gives exactly seven orbits, of
sizes

```text
4,2,4,4,4,2,2.                                           (3)
```

Thus an exact profile-zero constructor may solve seven target
representatives, then generate the other target profiles by (2).  The
common `C6` action also gives canonicalization within every fixed target.

There is an important lift caveat.  With the **complete canonical
zero-column words** fixed, only the `B` star has an affine realization.
The `A` zero word has trivial affine stabilizer.  Therefore

```text
G_transport = C6 x C2_B,                  |G_transport|=12,       (4)
```

and the 22 targets form twelve lift-compatible orbits under this certified
transport subgroup.  The `A` star is
still a valid and useful symmetry of the 24-profile equations, but it must
not be used to transport a labelled lift.  Profiles generated by `A` star
have to be lifted independently.

Here “transport subgroup” means the natural affine/decimation action on the
canonical order-three quotient.  It is not a classification of every
possible automorphism of an unknown labelled solution.

This theorem is a finite search reduction, not a profile survivor, an
`LP(333)`, or an `H(668)`.

## 1. Coefficient model

For a composition `p=(p0,p1,p2)` of three, put

```text
z(p)=p0+p1 omega+p2 omega^2,
omega^2+omega+1=0.
```

Writing `epsilon_j=(-1)^j`, the actual nonzero-class coefficients are

```text
a_j=-epsilon_j z(p_(A,j)),
b_j= epsilon_j z(p_(B,j)),                 j=0,...,11,   (5)
```

and the distinguished zero column is

```text
a(0)=-1,                 b(0)=2.                           (6)
```

For either channel `f`, define

```text
C_f(t)=sum_c f(c+t) conjugate(f(c)).
```

Then, apart from the fixed origin subtraction,

```text
D_t=C_a(t)+C_b(t).                                        (7)
```

The local-global theorem in
`LP333_ORDER3_PROFILE_CRT.md` proves that on the energy-167 profile space
the exact equation `D_t=0` is equivalent to the finite fingerprint

```text
D_t in (1-omega)^3 Z[omega],
D_t=0 modulo 37.
```

The symmetry result here preserves `D_t` itself, up to a permutation of
the column classes.  It therefore preserves both sides of that exact CRT
equivalence without requiring a second integer-correlation representation.

## 2. Common even class rotation

Multiplication of the column coordinate by `4^s`, `s=0,...,5`, sends

```text
C_j -> C_(j+2s).
```

In source-index convention the profile action is

```text
(R_s p)_(X,j)=p_(X,j+2s).                                (8)
```

The shift is even, so it preserves both signs in (5).  Direct substitution
gives

```text
D_(R_s p)(C_j)=D_p(C_(j+2s)).                            (9)
```

This is the residual `C6` quotient action.  Physically it is realized by
the already audited length-333 decimation `226`, which is `1 modulo 9`
and `4 modulo 37`.  It fixes the complete zero column, not merely its
profile.

An odd class shift reverses the signs in (5).  Swapping the two channels
would repair the nonzero-class parity pattern, but it swaps the unequal
fixed coefficients `(-1,2)` in (6).  No signed channel permutation except
the identity fixes that vector.  Hence neither an odd shift nor odd shift
plus channel swap is a symmetry of the fixed-zero system.

## 3. Independent coefficient stars

For one channel define

```text
(Jf)(c)=conjugate(f(-c)).                                 (10)
```

Then

```text
C_(Jf)(t)
 =sum_c conjugate(f(-c-t)) f(-c)
 =C_f(t).                                                 (11)
```

Thus `J` can be applied to `A`, to `B`, or to both, independently.
Negation sends `C_j` to `C_(j+6)`, and coefficient conjugation swaps
residues one and two.  Since `j` and `j+6` have the same parity, (10)
becomes exactly the profile action (2).

The aggregate of a channel is an Eisenstein integer `u+v omega`.  Its star
image is

```text
conjugate(u+v omega)=(u-v)-v omega.                       (12)
```

Therefore on an aggregate target `(u,v,r,s)`,

```text
J_A: (u,v,r,s) -> (u-v,-v,r,s),
J_B: (u,v,r,s) -> (u,v,r-s,-s).                          (13)
```

The common residue conjugation without a class shift is not an extra
generator: it is

```text
R_3 J_A J_B.
```

Conjugating only one channel without also taking its opposite class does
not satisfy (11) and is not a symmetry.

## 4. Seven exact target orbits

The 22 catalog targets are closed under (13).  Their complete orbit
partition is:

| representative | orbit size | target-fixed profile symmetry |
|---|---:|---|
| `(-3,-3,-4,-2)` | 4 | `C6` |
| `(-3,0,-3,-3)` | 2 | `C6 x C2_A` |
| `(-1,-2,-5,-1)` | 4 | `C6` |
| `(1,-1,2,-2)` | 4 | `C6` |
| `(2,-2,-4,-2)` | 4 | `C6` |
| `(3,0,0,-3)` | 2 | `C6 x C2_A` |
| `(4,-1,0,0)` | 2 | `C6 x C2_B` |

For example, the first orbit is

```text
(-3,-3,-4,-2),  (-3,-3,-2, 2),
( 0, 3,-4,-2),  ( 0, 3,-2, 2).
```

The verifier pins every element of all seven orbits, not merely their
sizes.  A constructor can use the displayed representative list as its
seven formal target keys.

The correct workflow after such a profile construction is:

1. solve and canonicalize the profile equations on the seven
   representatives;
2. generate all target images under `J_A,J_B`;
3. retain every distinct profile image;
4. when entering the labelled phase lift, use only `C6 x C2_B` as an
   equivalence and lift `J_A` images independently.

Skipping step 3 could lose a labelled solution even though no profile-zero
solution is lost.

## 5. Why the labelled group is smaller

In plus-bit notation the canonical zero-column words are

```text
A0=(1,1,1,0,0,1,0,1,0),
B0=(1,1,1,1,0,0,1,0,0).                                (14)
```

Their three residue counts are

```text
A0: (1,2,2),             B0: (3,1,1),                   (15)
```

which explains why residue conjugation preserves the **profile** zero
coefficients in (6).

The full words are more rigid.  Exhausting all affine row maps

```text
r -> u r+t,             u in (Z/9)^*, t in Z/9,
```

gives the exact stabilizers

```text
Stab(A0)={(1,0)},
Stab(B0)={(1,0),(8,3)}.                                  (16)
```

The nontrivial `B` map is `r -> 3-r`.  Together with multiplication by
`27` on `F_37`, which sends `C_j` to `C_(j+6)`, it realizes `J_B`
physically and fixes `B0` exactly.  There is no corresponding affine map
for `A0`.  Equations (14)--(16) prove the distinction between (1) and (4).

In the full `Z/333` coordinate the `B` transport is

```text
n -> 323 n+111.                                          (17)
```

Its square has multiplier `100`, not literal multiplier one.  Likewise the
sixth power of the `C6` decimation `226` has multiplier `100`.  This is the
order-three subgroup action (`100=1 mod 9`, `100=26 mod 37`), hence it is
trivial on the order-three invariant quotient.  Thus (4) is an exact group
on the quotient under study; neither generator is being overclaimed as
having the same order on arbitrary length-333 sequences.

Under the lift-compatible subgroup, the 22 target set has twelve orbits:
ten pairs and the two fixed targets

```text
(4,-1,0,0),             (5,1,0,0).
```

## 6. Rejected extra symmetries

The audit explicitly rejects five tempting enlargements on a separating
24-profile fixture:

```text
plain A conjugation without opposite-class shift    10 bad parts,
channel swap with fixed zero coefficients           12 bad parts,
cyclic residue rotation with fixed zero profiles    12 bad parts,
odd class shift                                     12 bad parts,
odd class shift plus channel swap                   12 bad parts.
```

These are counterexamples to the expected covariance identities.  The
structural reasons are:

- cyclic residue rotation multiplies every nonzero-class coefficient by an
  Eisenstein unit but leaves the fixed real zero coefficients (6) unchanged,
  so their cross terms change;
- the only residue permutations preserving both profiles in (15) are the
  identity and residue conjugation;
- the unequal zero vector `(-1,2)` has trivial stabilizer among signed
  channel permutations;
- only even class shifts preserve the actual alternating signs (5);
- independent conjugation preserves one autocorrelation only in the
  star form (10).

The result does not claim to classify arbitrary exotic automorphisms of a
24-variable polynomial ideal.  It completely audits the natural
decimation, channel, residue, phase, and star transformations relevant to
the quotient construction.

# H(668) pause decision - 25 July 2026

## Decision

Pause the headline search for a Hadamard matrix of order 668 and publish the
paper-quality mathematics already obtained.

No Hadamard matrix of order 668, Legendre pair of length 333, exact base
sequence in `BS(84,83)`, or conference graph on 333 vertices has been found.
The pause is a resource-allocation decision, not a nonexistence claim.

## Final continuation gate

The theory-first restart did produce the new global principle required by
the previous gate. On every physical shell-two phase placement, all six
primitive coordinates of both channels are units. For each star-paired
factor define

```text
nu_(X,r) =
  Norm_(F_(167^12)/F_(167^3))
       (W_(X,r) W_(X,r+3)),       r=0,1,2.
```

Every complementary solution must satisfy

```text
nu_(A,r) = nu_(B,r),              r=0,1,2.
```

The abstract key space has exactly

```text
(167^3-1)^3 = 101,029,443,456,638,735,128
```

points. Equality is equivalent to nine character gates of orders `2`, `83`,
and `28,057`.

This is a genuine exact theorem and a useful search architecture. It did
not pass the operational continuation gate:

- all nine one-coordinate character images are full on every one of the
  fifteen seed/channel alphabets needed for the 84-image physical closure;
- every triple image has affine rank three;
- exact `3^9 x 3^9` physical slice joins contract almost exactly at the
  random rates `2^3` and `83^3`; simultaneous order-`28,057` intersections
  are empty on those tiny slices for the ordinary scale reason, not because
  a profile is excluded;
- a complete margin-conditioned channel pass still requires approximately
  `5.092e12` invariant evaluations and about 81.5 TB of naive exact-key
  storage;
- an independent algebraic red-team found no compression beyond the three
  pair keys;
- a bounded exact 54-trit full-phase model remained `UNKNOWN` after 300
  seconds and 3,270,456 branches. This is a solver diagnostic only.

If the three norm keys were independent of the prior exact margin and
`T1/T2` gates, the heuristic residual would be approximately `0.011`.
That makes the theorem mathematically interesting, but it does not make the
complete join mechanically feasible on the present architecture.

## Publication set

The valuable results divide naturally into three scoped papers:

1. **Eliahou repair obstructions.** Fixed-`q` reduction to empty `TU(41)`,
   the distance-80 and special-distance-41 frontier, the certified case-0
   exclusion, the complete nine-short-case census, and the long-case
   endpoint-orientation cascade.
2. **Fixed-compression `LP(333)` classification.** Exact shell descent,
   five shell-two and eighteen dense profile orbits, physical-margin and
   quadratic lift structure, prime-167 phase algebra, primitive units, and
   the pair-resultant norm gate.
3. **Semiregular `C37` conference quotients.** The 625-class quotient
   census, parity and trace laws, exact characteristic-two supports, and
   the proved constant and first-nonconstant generator obstructions.

Each paper must state prominently that it neither constructs nor excludes
`H(668)` and that novelty and correctness remain provisional pending
independent expert review.

## Restart triggers

Resume headline research only if at least one of the following becomes
available:

1. an algebraic or implicit character-sum method that computes the complete
   three-key join without enumerating the `5.092e12` channel assignments;
2. a proof-producing decomposition that closes at least one complete
   shell-two profile or one complete Eliahou long case;
3. a construction theorem that parametrizes a materially larger family
   than the multiplier, low-degree character, and local phase families
   already exhausted;
4. hardware plus an exact external-memory design that changes the complete
   join from a multi-terabyte experiment into a reproducible bounded
   computation.

Until then, further isolated character witnesses, stochastic solver runs,
and larger raw slices are not progress gates.

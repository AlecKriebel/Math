# Research log

## 2026-07-24

- Recast the alternative conference route to `H(668)` as a group-ring
  core of order 333.
- Observed that every quotient `G -> C9` pushes the full conference
  equation to the fixed nine-coordinate norm
  `S S^(-1)=333e-37J`.
- Used the forced `even,odd,...,odd` coset parities and the further
  `C9 -> C3` quotient to reduce the possible cyclic coset-sum vectors to
  37,224.  Exact integer enumeration leaves no survivor.
- Classified the `C3 x C3` quotient by its four affine-plane Radon
  directions.  Of 256 assignments, exactly 96 give integral perfect
  profiles.  They form one `GL(2,3)`-plus-sign orbit and none is invariant
  under inversion.
- Expanded the group-ring equation with `F=G-e-2D`.  Its reduction modulo
  two forces `D=D^(-1)`, so inversion symmetry is automatic rather than an
  extra construction assumption.
- Since every group of order 333 has a normal subgroup of order 37 and a
  quotient isomorphic to either `C9` or `C3 x C3`, this excludes all
  group-developed conference matrices of order 334 over every possible
  regular core group.  It does not exclude nongroup-developed conference
  matrices or arbitrary `H(668)`.
- Replaced the finite quotient census as the primary proof by a
  one-character obstruction.  The order-nine quotient always maps onto
  `C3`.  For its nonprincipal character, inversion makes `chi(F)` a real
  Eisenstein integer, hence an integer, while the conference equation
  requires `chi(F)^2=333`.
- Generalized the argument: if a group of nonsquare order `v = 1 mod 4`
  has a prime-order quotient `Cr` and supports a Paley-type PDS, then the
  squarefree part of `v` is `r` and `r = 1 mod 4`.  In particular, for
  distinct odd primes `p > q^2`, no group of order `p q^2` supports a
  Paley-type PDS.
- Priority audit: the needed linear-character eigenvalue identity is
  explicitly standard in Nelson--Swartz (arXiv:2507.23039), and Wang
  (arXiv:1908.07055) already excludes the abelian order-333 case.  The
  checked sources did not state the order-333 nonabelian corollary, but it
  is too immediate to advertise as major standalone novelty without a
  broader review.  The quotient enumerations remain useful independent
  anatomy rather than the primary nonexistence proof.

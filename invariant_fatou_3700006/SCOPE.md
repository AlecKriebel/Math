# Scope and acceptance boundary

## Accepted partial conclusions

1. Conditional on two completely invariant Fatou components of a transcendental entire function, each is attracting or parabolic, unbounded and inaccessible from infinity. Its dynamical disk model and all iterates have no interior asymptotic curves, are indestructible infinite Blaschke products, have infinite fibers for every interior value, and have internal critical values not relatively compact in the disk. The corresponding entire-map iterates have no finite asymptotic values in either component.
2. For f(z)=z+P(exp(lambda z)), where lambda is any nonzero complex number and P is a nonconstant finite Laurent polynomial, there is no completely invariant attracting or parabolic Fatou component and hence at most one completely invariant Fatou component. The proof also states the explicit conditional finite-singular-orbit obstruction for translation-equivariant entire maps. It does not prove an unrestricted result for such maps or for arbitrary holomorphic functions on the punctured plane.
3. Under the two-component hypothesis, J(f) is connected in the plane, and J(f) union {infinity} has no cut point. The connectedness condition is known, credited to Bergweiler–Eremenko and their Baker–Kotus–Lü attribution; this package supplies an independent Janiszewski-based argument. No claim that planar J(f) has no finite cut point follows.
4. An independently normalized disk associate of the corrected Rempe–Sixsmith exp(z)+z example satisfies the full individual attracting disk package, including iterated forms. This demonstrates consistency of those abstract properties, not realization by two completely invariant basins of one entire function.

## Not established

- The unrestricted at-most-one conjecture or a counterexample to it.
- A construction of two completely invariant Fatou components; forward invariance, connected full preimages, and chosen normal subdomains do not substitute for full component equality.
- A dynamical conjugacy from independently chosen source and target Riemann maps. Iterating the associate inserts a coordinate change and does not represent iteration of the entire map.
- An implication from unboundedness or common connected boundary to accessibility; or from infinite valence to an asymptotic curve; or from absence of asymptotic values to finite degree.
- A general exclusion for all translation-equivariant entire functions, an infinite-singular-orbit construction, or a novelty claim.

## Proof and audit status

The proofs are analytic. No numerical verification is required or claimed, and no formal proof-assistant certification is claimed. AUDIT.md checks the stated arguments and imported hypotheses, not every proof in the source literature. Its radial-limit exposition improvement is present in the publication copy of DYNAMICS.md.

Baker's failed general topological argument is not reused. The valid 2019 correction literature supplies the accessibility and compact-singular-set criteria. Baker's original 1970 chapter was not retrieved or independently inspected; no such inspection is claimed. The entire-function problem is distinct from the meromorphic at-most-two question and from Makienko's rational conjecture.

# Public sources and inspection provenance

Prepared 2026-10-20 UTC. This summary carries public-source facts and inspection metadata, not copies of third-party source text.

## Original question and status

- Alexandre Eremenko, [problem list](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/uns1.html), and [Two questions on completely invariant domains](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/conjectures.pdf), PDF pages 3–4. The second question is the transcendental-entire at-most-one problem; the rational Makienko question is separate. The live list still linked this question without a solved annotation on the inspection date. That is supporting status evidence, not a proof that no later or unpublished solution exists.
- I. N. Baker, Completely invariant domains of entire functions, in Mathematical Essays Dedicated to A. J. Macintyre, Ohio University Press, Athens, Ohio (1970), pp. 33–35. Bibliography verified through the Eremenko note and the 2019 correction paper. The original chapter was not retrieved or inspected.
- Das and Nayak, [Sum of the exponential and a polynomial: Singular values and Baker wandering domains](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.14835v1), 2024 preprint, Introduction. Its discussion treats the entire bound as a conjecture reopened by the flaw. Targeted searches including 2025 and 2026 located no later primary resolution; the search is not exhaustive.

## Main mathematical inputs

- Lasse Rempe-Gillen and Dave Sixsmith, [On Connected Preimages of Simply-Connected Domains Under Entire Functions](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00039-019-00488-2), Geometric and Functional Analysis 29 (2019), 1579–1615; [author preprint, arXiv:1801.06359](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1801.06359), inspected version 3. Relevant material: Theorems 1.2–1.7, Theorem 1.11, Remark 1.13, Observation 4.5, Section 5, Section 7 (failed Baker step), Section 9 (other affected arguments), and Proposition 8.6. The connected-preimage examples are not completely invariant dynamical components. Bounded singular values alone do not rescue the failed topological argument. The later paper also addresses the proposed Domínguez–Sienra proof, so its abstract is not resolution evidence.
- Vasiliki Evdoridou, Lasse Rempe and Dave Sixsmith, [Fatou's Associates](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-020-00148-6), 2020; [arXiv:2002.03320](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.03320), inspected version 4. Proposition 1.1 and the introduction supply the associate framework and the distinction between independent coordinates and dynamical conjugacy. Its compact-singular-set correspondence is not invoked without compactness.
- Lasse Rempe-Gillen, [On prime ends and local connectivity](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0309022), inspected version 6, PDF p. 6. Source of the exact pair-separation form of Janiszewski's theorem used in BOUNDARY.md.
- Walter Bergweiler and Alexandre Eremenko, [Meromorphic functions with two completely invariant domains](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0310495), inspected version 2, Lemma 1, PDF p. 2. Credits planar connectedness to Baker–Kotus–Lü. Its outdated introductory assertion about the general entire theorem is not used.

## Additional scope checks

- [Boundaries of Multiply Connected Fatou Components: A Unified Approach](https://doi.org/10.1007/s12220-026-02564-0), 2026, Introduction: periodic components of transcendental entire functions are simply connected. This is not a claim about all Fatou components or an unchanged meromorphic assertion.
- Bergweiler–Eremenko, [2008 chapter](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511735233.005), Introduction: the separate meromorphic conjecture has proposed bound two. This cannot prove the sharper entire bound.

## Retrieval and verification limits

It checked the 2024 discussion, 2026 periodic-component statement, and meromorphic chapter summary. The mathematical audit additionally checked the associate and Janiszewski primary preprints and the known connectedness statement. These are source-inspection records, not independent proofs of all source results.

A Liverpool PDF opening failed; the primary arXiv route succeeded. An AMS survey opening returned 403 and was not used. A DOI opening for the associate paper failed once, while primary arXiv inspection succeeded. None of these failures is treated as missing evidence for a claimed result.

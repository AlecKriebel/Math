# Independent initial attack

Recorded before reading the candidate argument, its executable files, or prior audit results. The task assignment itself mentioned finite quotient/dual-number tests, the Poincare leading term, and rank factor n(n^2-1); those hints are disclosed rather than claimed as independent discoveries.

Exact task: assess whether the rank-specialization invariant exists in the specified inverse limit, conditional on its precursor conjecture. A finite incompatible quotient suffices for nonexistence; compatible finite samples never prove existence. The coefficient ring and interpretation of the formal variable q must be recovered from the actual mathematical assumptions, not guessed from notation.

Mathematical attack: map q to 1+epsilon with epsilon^2=0, if this is a legitimate quotient of R1. Then q^n=1+n epsilon. Modulo the stage-N product, evaluate any representative at these points; consistency requires the images at ranks 1..N to fit the same finite-dimensional polynomial constraints. Over the dual numbers the formal Taylor rule gives A+(B+nC)epsilon for every polynomial in t, independent of its degree. Constant terms must agree and the epsilon coefficient must be affine in n. Check this over a general finite quotient first, including finite characteristic, nilpotents, N=1,2,3, nonunit slopes, and the fact that q-1 may be a zero-divisor. Check that the quotient map on the inverse limit is actually defined and does not assume arbitrary completion elements are polynomials. Three incompatible ranks would refute the literal inverse limit interpolation.

If the intended obstruction uses a first quantum coefficient proportional to n(n^2-1), derive the second finite difference and isolate all scalar/normalization/degree assumptions. Test n=1,2,3 and higher ranks, zero scalar/Casson invariant, sign reversal, characteristic 2/3, and changing q to exp(h) or to a fractional power. A cubic rank dependence alone has no force until a nonzero scalar is rigorously exhibited in an actual integral homology sphere and the coefficient is in the same expansion variable.

For the actual example, reconstruct its first coefficient directly from a complete series expression. A finite truncation is insufficient unless every omitted summand has divisibility order >=2 in q-1, uniformly for the tail. Verify that denominators and fractional powers do not invalidate the map, and do not conflate Taylor expansion at q=1 with a root-of-unity specialization.

Execution/trust attack: independently recompute all file hashes, modes and Git blobs against the immutable head. Treat checker output and historical receipts as data. Read all code and manifests before execution; preserve external pins before creating attack copies. Use only our own isolated tree/cwd/tmp. Try cold launches under normal Python and -O/-OO; passing assertion-only controls under optimization is vacuous. Mutate data, code and modes, add import shadows and symlinks, malformed/duplicate-key JSON, files outside declared namespaces, and hostile environment/path settings. Distinguish payload authentication from semantic validation. Do not infer external truth from a self-sealed package. A Linux-only full-FS read-only bwrap test must remain unreplicated on macOS unless that exact environment is present. chmod is not a read-only filesystem and permission-denied is not EROFS. Document the supported subset and exact prerequisites for a separately authored portable profile; never rewrite old receipts into new successes.

Success criteria: route-level mathematical obstruction independently reproduced with exact ring/variable conditions and a proved all-tail first coefficient; original bytes unchanged at end; supported original checks reproduced with genuine negative controls; unsupported full replay stated explicitly. No full-paper acceptance, priority, or literature status conclusion.

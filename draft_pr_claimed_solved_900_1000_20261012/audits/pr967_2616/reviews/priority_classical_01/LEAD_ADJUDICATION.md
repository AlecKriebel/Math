## Rejected short cuts

The following implications were not assumed: omega-resolvable implies expansive; non-H-separable implies no groupable dense set; failure of weak Fréchet on one subset implies no full-space blocks; no convergent point sequence implies no finite-block sequence; an arbitrary countable subspace of a compact group has its obstruction inherited by its group hull; strongest left-invariant topology implies topological group.

The last two are particularly important here. The direct group-hull route for the Cohen example is defeated by the explicit precompact-group construction in the mathematical appendix. An ordinary ultrafilter space has isolated integers and cannot itself satisfy the dense-partition hypothesis.

## Bibliographic versus mathematical conclusion

The complete specialization is checkable using source theorems that were publicly available by 2016. That is sufficient to prevent the priority clearance requested under the audit's explicit specialization criterion. The date and author of an earlier *explicit combined presentation* have not been established. No claim is made that a particular older author solved or announced Kourovka21.107, which was newly numbered in2026.

The unresolved book/paper access gaps require maintaining the historical uncertainty even after the classical specialization is checked. They do not weaken the mathematical deduction and they do not authorize an affirmative novelty claim. External library input could improve the bibliographic coverage; no outreach was drafted or initiated.

The attribution to Mathias/free Boolean **linear** topology is correct. Here is a direct identity check independent of trusting that label. The filter-relative Mathias basic set [s,A] requires every coordinate of s to be below every coordinate of A and consists of finite extensions of the stem s by subsets of A. Under that requirement A is disjoint from s, so [s,A] equals s+H_A exactly. Hence every such Mathias basic set is open in the candidate topology. Conversely, let x belong to a candidate coset s+H_A. Remove the finite initial interval through max(x) from A, taking that interval empty if x=0, and call the remainder B. Cofinite intersection gives B in U. Then the Mathias set [x,B]=x+H_B is contained in s+H_A and contains x. Thus every candidate coset is Mathias-open. The topologies coincide for any filter containing the cofinite filter; no ultrafilter selectivity enters this equality.

Sipacheva's adjacent discussion distinguishes this linear topology from the generally finer unrestricted free Boolean and Laver topologies. Its selectivity equivalences concern equality with those other topologies and group continuity for the Laver topology. They do not make the subgroup-generated Mathias topology a group only in the selective case. The candidate uses the linear topology and independently verifies joint continuity, so it does not silently import the special-filter hypothesis. Keep the word linear when making the free-group identification.

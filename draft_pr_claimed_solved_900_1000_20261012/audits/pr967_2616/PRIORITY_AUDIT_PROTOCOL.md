# Post-mathematical priority audit protocol

The claim under audit is a negative answer to Kourovka21.107: a countably infinite Hausdorff topological group that has a countably infinite dense partition and has no sequence of pairwise disjoint finite sets eventually meeting every nonempty open set. The present proof uses the standard Mathias/free Boolean linear topology from an arbitrary free ultrafilter; no claim to invent that topology is permitted.

Priority failure criterion: ANY earlier accessible complete proof or fully justified specialization yielding such a countable counterexample, in ANY group and under equivalent terminology, whether or not it names21.107, the present construction, or the current title. A mathematically valid unrefereed preprint counts as earlier work. Merely mentioning the question, supplying an uncountable example, or proving resolvability without failure of expansive blocks is insufficient. Verify all hypotheses and deductions of a potentially qualifying source before deciding.

Two independent post-proof approach families must investigate materially different literature: (1) classical topological-group/Mathias/filter/selection/groupability literature, including primary predecessor references, exact implications and filter characterizations; (2) recent official question status, version history, solution repository, recent indexed and author preprints, variant wording/number/title and any posted solutions. Each records exact searches, inspected source passages, publication/submission dates, coverage and access gaps, complete reasoning, matched/nonmatched hypotheses, whether earlier independent exact resolution exists and limits of any negative search. No absence-of-hit is a firstness certificate.

Use primary sources. Retain fetched bodies, extracts and renders privately; publicly record URLs, hashes, exact passage locations and authored conclusions. Search can return irrelevant problems but must not intake other PR bodies or source packages/status-excluded problems for processing. No communication, prepared outreach, contacting editors/authors, installations, Git/index/ref modifications or publication. External input needs, if any, are logged only.

Gate: paper preparation follows complete root intake of valid infinite proof and fresh bounded execution; priority audit follows mathematical confirmation and precedes promotion. No priority clean claim until all live leads are adjudicated, with explicit unresolved gaps. No specialized family is an entire-package clean review.

# Independent reconstruction before reading prior review verdicts

Prepared 2026-10-15 UTC from the paper, assigned raw target identity and primary-source retrieval. Earlier full-package verdicts were not read.

The claim to falsify is an existential example in dimension three: free kinetic energy on all of R3, interaction restricted to one ball B4, one fixed nonnegative smooth compactly supported radial V equal to one on B8, and one fixed unit test mode f. The ordered-pair density convention gives L² and the normal-ordered convention gives L(L−1). The flag is an ordering choice, not a cutoff regularization. The proposed failure is pointwise operator-norm continuity of the Heisenberg orbit, not strong operator continuity on fixed vectors.

## Reconstruction

1. On each antisymmetric n-sector, the potential is bounded, real and permutation symmetric, with bound n². The bounded perturbation theorem therefore gives self-adjointness on H²∩wedge^n L². The maximal direct sum has square-summable images H_n η_n; a uniform bound in n is unnecessary. This yields a strongly continuous Fock-space unitary group. No CAR-invariance statement follows from it.
2. The smooth b-ratios have positive denominators: their two zero sets do not meet. The radial potential is exactly one for |x|≤8 and exactly zero for |x|≥9. χ is one on B3 and supported in the closed B3.5, compactly inside B4. The packed cube's farthest corner has norm below 3 and its first coordinate exceeds 1.75, so it is disjoint from f in B1/3. The scaled φ supports lie strictly inside disjoint subcubes. They are orthonormal; a(f)Ψ_N=0 and Ξ_N=a*(f)Ψ_N has norm one.
3. With m=ceil(N^(1/3)), ℓ=1/(2m), the total kinetic expectation is Nℓ^-2||∇φ||²≤16N^(5/3)||∇φ||². Adding the fixed f and the n mass terms preserves C0 n^(5/3) for BOTH n=N and n=N+1. N≥1 avoids the κ=1 vacuum denominator.
4. On H², [h,χ]u=−(Δχ)u−2∇χ·∇u. Cauchy–Schwarz in the pair (||u||,||∇u||) yields the stated Cχ H¹ bound. Integrating the conjugated smooth χ is legitimate on H². Free evolution preserves H¹. Since χg=g and qχ=0, ||q e^-ish g||≤Cχ |s| ||g||H¹. No derivative of the discontinuous indicator occurs.
5. For a freely evolved rank-n Slater projection P and projection q, the determinantal two-particle formula gives E M²=(Tr Pq)²+Tr Pq−Tr(PqPq). The final trace equals the sum of squared occupied q matrix elements and is nonnegative; Var(M)≤E M. Thus ||MΦ_n(s)||≤β|s| n^(5/6)+β²s²n^(5/3). The restriction to free Slaters is essential. A superposition of all-inside and all-outside configurations has variance n²/4, exceeding mean n/2 for n>2.
6. Let K_n=T_n+n(n−κ). Then H_n−K_n=−M(2n−κ−M), whose absolute multiplier is at most 2nM for all integer 0≤M≤n and both flags. The sector perturbation is bounded, so Duhamel on the free reference Slater gives E_n(t)≤βn^(11/6)t²+(2/3)β²n^(8/3)|t|³. At t=π/(2N+1−κ), both n=N,N+1 errors are O(N^-1/6+N^-1/3). These exponents depend on d>2 for this packing argument; d=1,2 do not follow.
7. For A=a*(f), compare separately evolved bra Ξ_N and ket Ψ_N to the K evolutions. The total scalar matrix-element error is ≤E_N+E_N+1. Free covariance gives A(e^ith f); moving a(f) through A(g) using a(f)Ψ_N=0 gives <f,g>, even when g has occupied components. The phase is exp(it[(N+1)(N+1−κ)−N(N−κ)])=exp(it(2N+1−κ)). It is −1 at the chosen time. Since f is fixed and free evolution strongly continuous, its scalar overlap tends to one.
8. Taking adjoints conjugates the creation matrix element and yields <Ψ_N,D_NΞ_N>→−2, not +2. Bounded CAR norms imply ||D_N||≤2. This proves the scalar and operator norms tend to 2. Expanding ||D_NΞ_N+2Ψ_N||²≤8+4Re<Ψ_N,D_NΞ_N> proves the directional limit. The changing normal state evaluates D*D as ||DΞ_N||²→4. The creation norm is identical to the annihilation norm by adjunction; no creation vector-direction claim is needed.
9. For each fixed vector η, split U(t)BU(t)*η−Bη into the two strong-continuity terms. Uniform boundedness gives fixed normal-state continuity by spectral decomposition and dominated summation. These facts are compatible with changing witnesses escaping to high particle number and energy.

## Exact unproved extensions

This proof does not imply a fixed density/energy bound, a thermodynamic statement, all V/regions, dimensions one or two, or CAR non-invariance. Finite matrix checks cannot supply the continuum leakage or Duhamel proof. Novelty/first priority is separate from validity and must not be inferred from unsuccessful searches.

## Exact claim tested

P is failure of upper semicontinuity of the *sum* of rough values K(K+D), over every singular point in one fixed representative, for reduced multibranch plane-curve deformations with constant total delta. The candidate has an ordinary ninefold central point and twelve ordinary triples nearby, finite simultaneous normalization into nine disks, delta 36 on both sides and rough 7<12. The optional classical Fermat extension has excess n²−4 for n>=3.

Q is the distinct explicitly parametric published conjecture, whose central fiber is unibranched. P does not settle Q. Nor does P settle a sectionwise delta-constant problem. The ordinary-point case has N=0, so a fine-sign discrepancy in historical sources cannot change these computations; this observation is not used to enlarge the asserted theorem.

## Why the implication has no remaining mathematical gap

The full argument in the companion bridge starts with the *already published* specific arrangement. Choose infinity away from all pair intersections, so affine directions are pairwise distinct. The standard family prod(a_i x+b_i y−tc_i) scales that configuration to an ordinary ninefold point. Nonvertical plane factors give analytic flatness. Their disjoint union gives a finite simultaneous normalization on every fiber, including zero. All intersections are O(t), while affine-line distances from the origin are O(t), giving nine disks in one transverse ball with disjoint boundary circles. Pair counting gives constant total delta. The known ordinary-point formula gives 7 and 12.

Nothing is assumed about a hypothetical arbitrary singularity deformation, a generic nine-tangent modulus, independence of incidence conditions, or a new simultaneous-resolution theorem. A warning in de Jong–van Straten p. 501 about moduli restrictions on line configurations is respected: existence for the arrangement's particular central directions is all that is required. The central normalization has nine components throughout; there is no inference of unibranchedness from rationality. These checks recover precisely the candidate's qualitative theorem, including its stronger local conditions. They are direct routine verification of the prior mechanism.

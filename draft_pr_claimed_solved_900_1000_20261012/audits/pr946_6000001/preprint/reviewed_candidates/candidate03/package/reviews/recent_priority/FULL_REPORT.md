## Comparison rule for tensors and ordered connections

Fix the candidate's convention `C = nabla g`. Then

```
nabla      = L - (1/2) C-sharp,
nabla-star = L + (1/2) C-sharp,
```

where `L` is the Levi-Civita connection. Thus an isostatistical embedding preserving the metric and the correctly normalized cubic tensor into a positive dually flat statistical model preserves both ordered connections by orthogonal projection. Indeed, the tangential projection of the ambient Levi-Civita connection is `L`, and projection of the cubic difference gives the displayed formulas. If a source uses the opposite cubic sign or interchanges exponential and mixture order, that convention must be matched explicitly. Such a sign convention is not a substantive escape from an older universal theorem.

In particular, a valid unrestricted smooth finite-simplex embedding theorem would answer the source target. The audit does not demand its properness or the candidate's dimension. Conversely, preservation of the metric alone leaves the cubic data undetermined.

### Marugame: theorem, proof, publication and globalization

The [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.10102v1) was submitted 2021-03-18. The [publisher page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s41884-021-00056-4) records online publication 2021-09-29, Information Geometry4,363-376. The [UEC body](https://uec.repo.nii.ac.jp/record/10333/files/Bonnet_thm_statistical_mfd.pdf) is an accepted manuscript: its [repository record](https://uec.repo.nii.ac.jp/records/10333) explicitly says it is not the version of record. I read the complete ten-page arXiv proof and its corresponding accepted text. The official publisher PDF request returned HTML preview, so final post-acceptance changes were not authenticated.

Theorem2.2 assumes a local pair satisfying

```
g_ij = f_i^A phi_A,j,
Gamma_ijk = f_ij^A phi_A,k.
```

Its proof obtains a local primitive of `omega = phi_A df^A`, extends a first jet, and adds squared local defining functions to obtain positivity. The induced connection calculation agrees with the candidate's convention. This is substantial construction ancestry; the extension idea itself should not be presented as unprecedented. The candidate's claimed extra content is the universal finite global pair and its global nonuniform extension.

For the source's weaker ambient category, **a global potential is not necessary**. Nonzero periods alone therefore do not rule out an older global Hessian-manifold realization. This audit's decisive missing premise is a global pair/realization for arbitrary input, not a demand that every earlier theorem produce a globally exact potential.

As a comparison control, set on a circle `f(theta)=(cos(theta),sin(theta))`, `Jf=(-sin(theta),cos(theta))`, and `phi=f+a Jf`. Then `f' dot phi'=1`, `f'' dot phi'=a`, but `phi dot df=a dtheta`, whose period is `2 pi a`. These are global Lauritzen identities for the metric and constant lowered coefficient, while the same pair cannot be the restriction of `dPsi` for one globally defined `Psi` when `a != 0`. This only disproves a careless upgrade of the local proof to the candidate's global-potential conclusion. It is **not** an obstruction to the source target, another pair, or an ambient metric described by local potentials.

The actual Marugame references lead to Lauritzen's Lancaster workshop item, Lê's Journal of Geometry article, and AJLS2017. A forward citation index yielded Fox2023; direct title queries through 2026 also led to Gauvin's September2026 preprint. None of the inspected recent bodies upgrades Marugame to an unrestricted global theorem. An incomplete citation index cannot prove that no other extension exists.

### Free/jet constructions and fixed-model obstructions

The [Loi–Matta v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2110.06145v1) and [2023 accepted manuscript](https://iris.unica.it/retrieve/6dbf3c46-1ccf-403f-bb6f-a3757afe194a/TOPOL%20108438_merged.pdf) were read through their linearization, freedom, right-inverse and transversality arguments. The accepted cover links the publication DOI `10.1016/j.topol.2023.108438`, Topology and its Applications327,108438, issue2023-03-15. Its printedp2/PDFp3 still assumes already induced data. Its minimum-norm right inverse and finite-jet freedom deserve citation as closely related tools. Neither Proposition6 nor generic freedom solves an arbitrary nonlinear prescribed-data problem merely because the linearized operator has a right inverse.

A simple check clarifies why the fixed linear model differs from a custom Hessian target. For `g0=sum dx_i^2` and `T0=sum dx_i^3`, every Euclidean unit vector satisfies

```
|T0(v,v,v)| <= sum |v_i|^3 <= 1.
```

Consequently, any metric-and-cubic preserving immersion into `(R^N,g0,A T0)` forces the input's cubic comass to be bounded by `|A|`. A smooth statistical line with `g=dt^2`, `T=t dt^3` violates this fixed bound. This is a theorem-comparison control, not a new realization attempt. It does not obstruct a custom Hessian domain with unbounded cubic data. It also does not, by itself, refute arbitrary noncompact embedding into a finite probability simplex, whose cubic comass is unbounded near its boundary.

The candidate's scalar3-jet construction is not the same theorem as a map preserving a cubic tensor alone, a statistically free map, or perturbative openness. No source inspected here supplies its complete global exact Lauritzen pair by those facts alone. An unsupported implication from a free map to the desired arbitrary pair would transfer the central existence problem rather than settle it.

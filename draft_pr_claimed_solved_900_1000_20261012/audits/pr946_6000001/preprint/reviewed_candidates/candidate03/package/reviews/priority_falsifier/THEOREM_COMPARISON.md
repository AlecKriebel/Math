# Adversarial theorem comparison: PR946 / 6000001

This records implication checks on saved results. It supplies no new universal realization mechanism and adds no proof-attempt response. The original declaration remains four of five; native historical events are absent.

## The object a predecessor must imply

The source asks for a finite-dimensional positive dually flat ambient **manifold**, inducing the prescribed ordered torsion-free dual connections on the whole smooth boundaryless input. Local potentials and local affine charts suffice. The candidate adds a proper embedding into a single Euclidean open domain with one potential and dimension `binomial(2n+4,3)`. Those additions cannot be made requirements on the old question.

A valid unrestricted smooth finite-simplex embedding is therefore decisive for the old question. A positive global Hessian-manifold realization without a global chart can also be decisive. Mere local realizability, tensor realization into an infinite-dimensional sample model, or an already induced hypothesis does not have that implication by itself.

## Complete finite-simplex implication and the ordered sign

On a positive simplex use independent coordinates `p_1,...,p_d` and `p_0=1-sum_i p_i`. Put

`Psi = sum_(a=0)^d p_a log p_a`.

Its Hessian satisfies

`G_ij = delta_ij/p_i + 1/p_0`,

`G(v,v) = sum_i v_i^2/p_i + (sum_i v_i)^2/p_0 > 0` for `v != 0`.

The standard mixture connection `D` and its exponential dual are flat and torsion-free. Direct differentiation gives

`(DG)_ijk = -delta_ij delta_ik/p_i^2 + 1/p_0^2 = -T_AC,ijk`.

For the candidate convention `C=nabla g`, dual torsion-free connections obey

`nabla = L - C-sharp/2`, `nabla-star = L + C-sharp/2`.

If a smooth statistical embedding preserves `(g,-C)` into the simplex with cubic `T_AC`, the orthogonally induced first connection from `D` has cubic `C`. Indeed, differentiating the pullback metric and subtracting the induced connection terms gives the pullback of `DG`; normal terms have zero tangent inner product. Equivalently, projecting ambient Levi-Civita gives intrinsic `L` and projecting its symmetric difference tensor gives `-C-sharp/2`. Uniqueness of the metric dual supplies the second ordered connection. This checks both connections, rather than only isometry. Changing a source's cubic sign or exchanging exponential/mixture order does not avoid the implication.

A smooth **immersion** gives an immersed realization, but does not automatically give the embedding topology in the checked source interpretation. A `C1` map preserving first-derivative tensors does not by itself justify the smooth second-derivative projection formula. Those are genuine regularity/map distinctions; the smooth embedding branch of a universal theorem would remove both.

If a predecessor's ambient has dimension equal to `n`, adjoining a Euclidean flat line with potential `z^2/2` and using the zero section raises its dimension while preserving all induced data. The source's strict `m>n` requirement is therefore no priority escape either.

## Properness and global potentials are not priority shields

Let an earlier smooth embedding have image `S` in a Hessian domain `V`. Embedded-submanifold charts make `S` relatively closed near every point of `S`; hence `S` is open in `closure_V(S)`. Thus `B=closure_V(S)\S` is closed in `V`. On the open domain `U=V\B`, the image is closed. For a compact `K` in `U`, `S intersect K` is compact, and its inverse image under the homeomorphism `M -> S` is compact. Restriction preserves the statistical/Hessian structure. Thus that embedding is proper into an allowed smaller ambient domain.

The argument does not apply to an arbitrary immersion. It also does not imply completeness, convexity or global dual-coordinate injectivity, none of which is required here.

Nonzero periods of a particular closed form `phi_A df^A` obstruct that pair being the differential of one globally defined potential. They do **not** rule out a general Hessian ambient with local potentials, or a different pair. No predecessor is rejected here for failing the candidate's stronger exactness or chart requirements.

## Two scope controls

For the fixed linear model `(R^N,g0,A sum dz_a^3)`, `|A sum v_a^3| <= |A|` when `sum v_a^2=1`. A statistical line with `g=dt^2` and `T=t dt^3` has unbounded cubic comass. Thus an unrestricted fixed-linear-model reading is false. It does not refute finite-simplex realization, whose cubic comass can diverge near its boundary, or custom Hessian domains. This control already appears among the candidate's declared earlier approaches.

In dimension one, curvature of any torsion-free connection vanishes, and a statistical line already has a flat dual pair. The same line is therefore no counterexample to the source question. Zero-dimensional countable inputs are trivial separately. Compact priors, low-dimensional facts and fixed-model obstructions do not settle unrestricted noncompact input of dimension at least two.

Disconnectedness must not become a technical priority shield. A prior universal connected theorem with a uniform dimension bound depending on `n` extends to countably many components by taking a disjoint union of same-dimensional ambients in the source's weaker category. A nonuniform bound per component would require separate checking. No such unresolved distinction is used to dismiss Lê's explicitly dimension-controlled old claims.

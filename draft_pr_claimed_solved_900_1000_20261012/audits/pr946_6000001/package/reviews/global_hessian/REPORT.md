## Independently reconstructed proof chain

1. Let L be Levi-Civita and A=nabla-L. Torsion-freeness makes A symmetric in its two arguments. Duality identifies nabla-star-L with minus the g-adjoint of A; its torsion-freeness supplies the additional symmetry, so g(A_X Y,Z) is totally symmetric. Consequently C=nabla g=-2g(A_X Y,Z), and the lowered coefficients equal L_ijk-C_ijk/2. Thus Q_ijk=partial_k g_ij+Gamma_ijk is symmetric and equals (partial_i g_jk+partial_j g_ik+partial_k g_ij-C_ijk)/2.

2. Choose a proper smooth embedding e:M->R^(2n+1). Include all monomials through degree three in f. The linear-coordinate projection pi satisfies pi f=e. Therefore f is injective, immersive, and a homeomorphism onto its image: its inverse is e^{-1} pi on the image. For compact B in R^N, f^{-1}(B) is closed in the compact set e^{-1}(pi B). Hence f is proper. If f(p_j) converges in R^N, the convergent sequence together with its limit is compact, so a subsequence of p_j converges; its f-image is the asserted limit. Thus S=f(M) is closed. No properness of the polynomial map on all of R^(2n+1) is assumed.

3. At any point p, n linear combinations of e-coordinates have independent differentials and give local coordinates. Every scalar 3-jet is a polynomial through degree three in their translated values. Expanding gives a member of the same fixed global polynomial feature space. Jet evaluation E is therefore onto at every p, not just on finitely many charts. Since scalar jets form a finite-rank vector bundle, a bundle metric makes E E^dagger positive definite, and R=E^dagger(E E^dagger)^{-1} is a smooth global right inverse. Its norm can diverge at infinity without impairing smoothness.

4. In Levi-Civita normal coordinates, the prescribed scalar jet has value and first derivative zero, second derivative g, and third derivative -C/2. The local exponential germ provides that jet smoothly even for an incomplete metric. On changing coordinates, its ordinary third derivative adds the three Levi-Civita/Hessian terms, whose sum is half the sum of the three metric derivatives. This gives precisely Q above. The construction asks for a smooth section of the jet bundle, not a holonomic section arising from one global scalar function; there is no invalid differentiation of q_p(p)=0 into a vanishing second derivative.

5. Applying R gives a smooth coefficient field a with a f_i=0, a f_ij=g_ij, and a f_ijk=partial_k g_ij+Gamma_ijk (and a f=0). Set phi=-a. Differentiation of the first and second identities gives f_i partial_j phi=g_ij and f_ij partial_k phi=Gamma_ijk. In particular phi df=0, eliminating the period obstruction for the ambient potential's value on S. These differentiations distinguish the coefficient held fixed in jet evaluation from the smooth coefficient field varying with p.

6. Obtain a Euclidean normal-addition tube F(s,v)=s+v, v in N_s S, on an open neighborhood V of the zero section. Its inverse is smooth on T=F(V), yielding r and nu=z-r(z). At S, dr=P_T and dnu=P_N. The function Psi_0(z)=phi_r(z)(nu(z)) is smooth and has value zero and differential phi along S. Its differential in an arbitrary ambient direction h is phi(P_N h)=phi(h). Differentiating that identity along S gives Hess(Psi_0)(f_i,h)=partial_i phi(h). In particular its tangent restriction is g. Along a straight normal fiber through s it is linear, so its normal-normal Hessian is zero. Its mixed block B is generally nonzero; it must not be discarded.

7. With Euclidean tangent/normal metrics the Hessian on S is [A B; B^t 0], where A represents g and is positive definite. Define t=tr(A^{-1})||B||_HS^2 and lambda=1+t. Under local orthonormal changes Q on TS and R on NS, A becomes Q^t A Q and B becomes Q^t B R. Both factors in t are invariant. Thus t and lambda are smooth globally defined scalars even when the normal bundle is nontrivial; no orientation or global frame has been used.

8. The correction h=lambda(r)|nu|^2 has value and differential zero on S. Because nu=0 there, differentiating twice leaves only 2 lambda <P_N(-),P_N(-)>: derivatives of lambda, curvature of the projection, and second derivatives of nu are multiplied by a vanishing nu. Thus the corrected Hessian is exactly [A B; B^t 2lambda I]. Completing the square on a tangent vector v and normal vector w gives

       H(v,w)=<A(v+A^{-1}Bw),v+A^{-1}Bw>
              +<w,(2lambda I-B^t A^{-1}B)w>.

   Since A^{-1}>0, its operator norm is at most its trace. Therefore <w,B^t A^{-1}B w><=t|w|^2, and the last block is at least (2+t)I. This proves strict positivity at every point of S with no global estimate.

9. U={z in T:Hess(Psi)_z>0} is open in R^N and contains every point of S. A possible shrinking radius at infinity, divergent lambda, or tangent eigenvalues tending to zero does not remove any point of S. A compact subset of U is compact under its continuous inclusion in R^N, so restricting the target preserves properness of f. There is no assumption that a fixed-radius neighborhood of S is positive or tubular.

10. For G=Hess(Psi), standard D is flat and torsion-free, and its dual has coefficients (Gamma-star)^C_AB=G^{CD}partial_A G_BD. Symmetry of third derivatives gives torsion-freeness. The global map eta=dPsi has Jacobian G, so is a local diffeomorphism at every point. Direct substitution gives partial_A partial_B eta_E-(Gamma-star)^C_AB partial_C eta_E=0. Each local eta chart is therefore affine for D-star, proving flatness everywhere. Noninjectivity or topology of U does not affect this local tensor identity. Equivalently Gamma_i=G^{-1}partial_i G is a pure-gauge matrix connection and its curvature cancels identically.

11. Along S, G f_k=partial_k phi, whence f^*G=g and G(f_ij,f_k)=Gamma_ijk. The orthogonal tangential projection of D is the specified nabla. Restricting ambient duality proves that the projection of D-star is its unique g-dual, the specified nabla-star, with the correct ordering. Orthogonal projection of either torsion-free ambient connection is torsion-free, establishing the stated necessity too. This verifies the theorem's full construction rather than only a local Hessian germ.

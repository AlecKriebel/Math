# Independent reconstruction before earlier verdicts

The complete original PROOF.md and current TeX were read after the live START gate and before any earlier family/full verdict. I reconstructed the following argument independently. Target: every smooth Hausdorff second-countable fixed finite dimensional n>=1 manifold without boundary, positive g and prescribed mutually dual torsion-free ordered connections, admits a proper smooth embedding into an open U in R^binomial(2n+4,3), one smooth positive ordinary Hessian potential, and the stated ordered induced pair.

Let L be Levi-Civita, K=nabla-L and K*=nabla*-L. Duality and both torsion identities make g(K_XY,Z) totally symmetric and K*=-K. Thus C=nabla g=-2g(K,.) and nabla=L-Csharp/2. The array Q=partial_k g_ij+Gamma_ijk is symmetric and transforms as the ordinary third derivative of a scalar whose value/gradient vanish and whose Hessian is g. The scalar germ g_p(v,v)/2-C_p(v,v,v)/12 in L-exponential coordinates produces exactly this jet. In particular a field of scalar germs is not one scalar function on M; no integration of an arbitrary positive Hessian on a compact M is being assumed.

Choose proper e:M->R^(2n+1). All degree <=3 polynomial features f retain e in their linear coordinates, so f is a proper embedding with closed image S. At p, n restricted linear coordinates from e have independent differentials. Their cubic polynomials span every scalar 3-jet. Hence E:R^N->J^3 is fiberwise onto. Bundle metrics give the globally smooth algebraic right inverse E^dagger(EE^dagger)^-1 without uniform estimates. Applying it to the nonholonomic jet section gives a with a.f=a.f_i=0, a.f_ij=g_ij, a.f_ijk=Q_ijk. With phi=-a, differentiation gives f_i.phi_j=g_ij, f_ij.phi_k=Gamma_ijk and phi.df=0. The last equality removes period/gluing obstructions exactly; no patching of separately induced geometries appears.

A variable Euclidean normal tube of the closed S supplies r,nu. Psi0=phi(r).nu has dPsi0|S=phi. Differentiating this equality in tangent directions yields H0(f_i,.)=phi_i and the tangent block g. On each normal fiber it is linear, so the normal block vanishes. In the Euclidean orthogonal splitting write H0=[A B;B^t 0]. A>0, t=tr(A^-1)||B||HS^2 is a globally smooth scalar independent of frames, and lambda=1+t is pointwise finite. Adding lambda(r)|nu|^2 preserves the gradient on S and changes only the normal Hessian block to 2lambda I. Since B^t A^-1 B<=tI, the Schur complement is >=(2+t)I. The positive-Hessian locus is therefore one open neighborhood U containing all of S. This needs no uniform tubular radius, inverse norm or positivity bound, even for infinitely many components. Compact subsets of U remain compact in Euclidean space, proving f remains proper.

G f_k=phi_k implies f*G=g and G(f_ij,f_k)=Gamma_ijk, so the projection of D is the prescribed nabla, with the sign and order fixed. The dual connection has coefficients G^-1 partial G. Its local affine coordinates are eta=dPsi: their Jacobian G is invertible, and their covariant Hessians vanish. Thus it is flat and torsion-free without global injectivity of eta. Projected duality and uniqueness give the prescribed nabla*. Conversely projection of either ambient torsion-free connection has zero torsion, proving necessity separately for both.

Boundary checks: disconnected and noncompact cases use pointwise constructions; countability and paracompactness follow from the hypotheses. Fixed dimension is needed for the stated N. n=0 gives a countable discrete manifold embedded as a subset of the nonnegative integers with Psi=z^2/2; finite and empty sets cause no difficulty. No assertion extends to boundary, indefinite metrics, complete geometry, convex U, a fixed probability model, optimal dimension or a globally injective dual chart. The compact-manifold obstruction to globally strictly convex functions on M is inapplicable because the ambient potential is zero along a curved S and only its ambient Hessian is positive.

I found no mathematical gap in the stated theorem. The only external existence inputs are proper smooth Whitney embedding, smooth bundle metrics and a variable normal tube; actual decisive primary pages were personally viewed. Finite checks corroborate identities rather than establish this universal theorem. Historical priority remains a separate bounded-source question.

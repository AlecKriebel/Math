# Independent derivation before earlier verdicts

2026-10-16T05:42Z. This derivation follows my successful own actual postmerge gate. I read the original public/PROOF.md and current TeX, but no prior mathematical review or verdict before writing this document.

The precise hypothesis is a smooth fixed finite dimensional Hausdorff second-countable boundaryless manifold with positive metric and mutually dual torsion-free connections. The claim is a proper embedding into one open Euclidean Hessian domain of dimension binomial(2n+4,3) inducing the prescribed ordered pair. It does not require an affine complete, convex, finite-probability, or globally injective dual-coordinate target. A historical novelty statement is a different claim, requiring separate evidence.

Let L be Levi-Civita and lower the difference K=∇−L with g. Torsion of ∇ gives symmetry in its first two slots. Duality makes K* the negative last-slot adjoint. Torsion of ∇* then supplies the other transpositions, giving full symmetry and K*=−K. Thus C=∇g=−2g(K·,·) is symmetric, with ∇=L−C#/2 and ∇*=L+C#/2. This fixes the sign and ordered pair.

Choose a proper embedding e into R^(2n+1) and take every monomial of degree at most three, including the constant, as coordinates f. Projection onto linear features recovers e, proving immersion, injectivity, embedding topology, and properness. At a point n linear restrictions of e are coordinates. Their degree-three Taylor polynomials are restrictions of the same global polynomial space. The bundle map from coefficients to scalar 3-jets is therefore surjective everywhere. Positive bundle metrics give E†(EE†)^−1 smoothly, with no bounded singular value assumption or compactness. This finite construction does not transfer the problem to a jet-extension theorem.

The jet specified through the germ q_p(exp_p v)=g_p(v,v)/2−C_p(v,v,v)/12 has value and first derivative zero, second derivative g, and third derivative Q=(∂_ig_jk+∂_jg_ik+∂_kg_ij−C_ijk)/2. The derivative conversion adds the three lowered Levi-Civita terms. Hence Q=∂_kg_ij+Γ_ijk is symmetric and transforms as scalar third-jet data with the three Hessian/second-coordinate-derivative terms, not as a tensor. Smoothness of the exponential germ near the zero section suffices; neither completeness nor a common injectivity radius enters. These are separately varying germs, so their zero values do not force their Hessians to vanish.

Lift this jet with a=RJ, holding a fixed in the germ variable. Then af=afi=0, afij=gij, afijk=Qijk. Differentiation in the base point does differentiate a. For φ=−a one obtains fi·∂jφ=gij and fij·∂kφ=Qijk−∂kgij=Γijk. The one-form φdf vanishes identically, removing all period obstructions. Positive g makes dφ immersive but supplies no global injectivity; none is used.

Properness makes S=f(M) closed. In a variable Euclidean normal tube, z=r(z)+ν(z). Since φ annihilates TS, Ψ0(z)=φ_r(z)(ν(z)) has first jet dΨ0|S=φ and value zero. Differentiating that first jet gives H0(fi,·)=∂iφ, with positive tangent block A=g relative to Euclidean frames, cross block B, and zero normal block. There is no requirement to trivialize NS. Define t=tr(A^−1)||B||²_HS and λ=1+t smoothly pointwise. Adding λ(r)|ν|² changes only the normal block at S to 2λI. Its Schur complement is ≥(2+t)I because BᵀA^−1B≤tI. Consequently the Hessian is positive at every point of S. Its positive locus in the tube is open and contains the whole image even with no uniform radius, bound, or eigenvalue. Restriction to that locus preserves properness.

G=HessΨ has D-dual Γ*^C_AB=G^CE∂AG_BE. Third-derivative symmetry gives zero torsion. η=dΨ is a local coordinate map because its Jacobian is G. Its covariant second derivative vanishes for Γ*, proving flatness locally everywhere; its possible global multiple covering is irrelevant. The gradient equality on S gives G_AB fk^B=∂kφ_A, hence f*G=g and G(fij,fk)=Γijk. Orthogonal projection of D therefore induces ∇ first. Restricting duality and cancelling normal terms gives ∇* second by uniqueness of the metric dual. Projecting the torsion-free ambient identities separately proves necessity of both torsions.

No connectedness assumption was used. Second countability permits at most countably many components and supplies paracompact bundle metrics; noncompact or incomplete components and unbounded g,C,R,λ are allowed. For dimension zero, countable discrete M embeds properly as a subset of the nonnegative integers, and Ψ=z²/2 realizes zero tangent data. Empty M is vacuous. Boundary, indefinite metrics, nonsmooth data, varying dimension, convexity, completeness, global injectivity of dΨ, and minimal ambient dimension are outside the claim.

Potential falsifiers considered: the zero germ value does not constrain its moving Hessian; coordinate changes require Q's inhomogeneous terms; differentiating coefficients with respect to the germ variable would be erroneous; positivity only on tangent vectors is insufficient but the Schur term supplies full positivity; a uniform tube is unnecessary; flatness of D* does not assert Levi-Civita flatness; injectivity of φ is unnecessary; neither a local realization theorem nor a probability theorem is being assumed. I find no mathematical gap for the stated theorem at this stage. The differential-topology inputs and historical category still require primary-page examination.

An unrestricted finite-simplex embedding theorem would already give the historical realization: on the simplex, Ψ=Σp log p gives Fisher Hessian and third derivative −Σuvw/p², the negative Amari–Chentsov cubic. Select the cubic sign to match the ordered pair. Stronger properness cannot establish priority because an embedded image is locally closed and removing its closed frontier makes it proper in an allowed smaller open target. Neither this fact nor the displayed dimension proves novelty. Earlier full realization claims must therefore be examined directly rather than dismissed for lacking these refinements.

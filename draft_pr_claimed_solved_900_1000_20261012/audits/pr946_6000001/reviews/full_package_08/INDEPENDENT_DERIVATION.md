## Claim and falsifiers

For a smooth positive metric and ordered mutually dual torsion-free pair on a Hausdorff second-countable boundaryless fixed finite-dimensional n-manifold, n>=1, construct a proper embedding into one open Euclidean domain of dimension binomial(2n+4,3) with positive Hessian and the prescribed ordered induced connections. No convexity, completeness, finite sample model, globally injective gradient chart, optimal dimension, historical priority or human/formal certification is included. Potential falsifiers: failure of global jet compatibility or finite surjectivity; hidden compactness/uniform estimate; incorrect cubic sign; failure of the extension to preserve its first jet or positivity; confusion of induced dual ordering; stronger historical definitions.

## Reconstructed proof

Let L be Levi-Civita and K=nabla-L. Torsion-freeness gives K_XY=K_YX; duality says g(K_XY,Z)=-g(Y,K*_XZ). Combining the analogous symmetry of K* with this identity makes g(K_XY,Z) symmetric in X,Y,Z; thus K*=-K and C=nabla g=-2g(K_.,.,.). Consequently nabla=L-C^sharp/2 and the ordered dual is L+C^sharp/2. The array Q_ijk=partial_k g_ij+Gamma_ijk=(partial_i g_jk+partial_j g_ik+partial_k g_ij-C_ijk)/2 is symmetric, but is not a tensor.

Choose proper smooth Whitney e:M->R^(2n+1); include every monomial of degree <=3, including the constant, in f. Projection onto linear features recovers e, proving embedding and properness (preimages of compact sets sit as closed subsets in the compact e-preimage of their linear projection). S=f(M) is closed. The feature coefficient-to-scalar-3-jet map is onto at each p: select n ambient linear forms with independent differentials on T_pM and use their restrictions as coordinates. Every scalar 3-jet is represented by a polynomial of degree <=3 in those forms minus their values at p. The global feature space is fixed and finite, even for infinitely many components/noncompact M. Smooth bundle metrics give R=E^dagger(EE^dagger)^-1. Surjectivity, not a uniform singular value estimate, proves smooth invertibility pointwise.

Prescribe J_p by q_p(exp_p v)=g_p(v,v)/2-C_p(v,v,v)/12. This is a smooth section of the jet bundle, not j^3 of one scalar function. Normal coordinates give value 0, first derivative 0, second g, third -C/2. Converting ordinary third derivatives adds L_ij^a g_ak+L_ik^a g_aj+L_jk^a g_ai, giving Q. Under x=x(y), the cubic transforms by the three second-coordinate-derivative/Hessian terms; first derivative zero kills the third-coordinate-derivative term. Thus the data are globally compatible even though Q alone is not tensorial. Completeness of exp is irrelevant to its local germ.

Set a=RJ and phi=-a. Holding a(p) fixed in the scalar germ gives a f=0, a f_i=0, a f_ij=g_ij, a f_ijk=Q_ijk. Base differentiation then gives f_i partial_j phi=g_ij and f_ij partial_k phi=Q_ijk-partial_k g_ij=Gamma_ijk. Also phi df=0 identically, removing period obstructions. Positivity of g makes dphi injective on tangent spaces, without implying global injectivity.

On a variable Euclidean normal tube of closed S, z=r(z)+nu(z), define Psi0=phi_r(nu). On S, dr=P_T and dnu=P_N, so dPsi0=phi and Psi0=0. Differentiating its gradient along S gives Hess(Psi0)(f_i,.)=partial_i phi. Its tangent block A equals g; its normal-normal block is zero because Psi0 is linear on a fixed normal fiber. Its off-diagonal B need not vanish. A and B are smooth bundle maps and do not require global frames. With t=tr(A^-1)||B||_HS^2 and lambda=1+t, correction lambda(r)|nu|^2 preserves value/gradient and adds exactly 2lambda P_N to the Hessian on S. The Schur complement is >=(2+t)I since ||A^-1||_op<=tr(A^-1). Therefore the full Hessian is positive on S. Its positive locus in the tube is open and contains all S. No uniform radius, bound on R, bound on B or global minimum eigenvalue is used. Properness persists under restriction of the ambient open set.

For G=Hess Psi and D standard, the dual coefficients are (Gamma*)^C_AB=G^CE partial_A G_BE. Third derivative symmetry implies zero torsion, and eta_E=partial_E Psi satisfies Hess_(D*) eta_E=0 with invertible Jacobian G; these are local affine coordinates proving D* flat, regardless of global injectivity. On S, G(f_i,.)=partial_i phi. Hence pullback G=g and G(f_ij,f_k)=Gamma_ijk, proving the prescribed D-induced connection. Orthogonal projection of ambient duality then gives the unique metric-dual connection in the other slot, preserving ordering. Conversely projection of each torsion-free ambient connection is torsion-free.

## Boundaries and priority implication

Empty M is vacuous. A second-countable zero-manifold is countable discrete and embeds properly as a closed subset of nonnegative integers, with z^2/2 ambient potential. Disconnected/nonorientable manifolds and nontrivial normal bundles introduce no extra frame assumptions. Boundary/indefinite/nonsmooth cases are excluded, not proved. Unbounded coefficient fields and shrinking tubes are explicitly permitted.

A valid older unrestricted finite probability-simplex theorem would imply historical existence: Hess(sum p log p) is Fisher in mixture coordinates and its derivative cubic is minus the Amari-Chentsov cubic; choosing input cubic sign recovers either ordered pair. Further, an embedded manifold image is locally closed and open in its closure, so deleting its closed frontier makes it proper in an allowed smaller open domain.

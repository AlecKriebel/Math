## Embedded groups and abstract isomorphism

The derivative image is the matrix Veech group in the 2014 and 2017 sources.
Replacing the witness by an abstract copy of Z loses its rotational property.
ARSVW's introductory discussion refers to abstract groups; its positive Veech
theorem statements typically promise a group isomorphic to G and retain their
topological hypotheses. Their detailed constructions use prescribed matrix
actions in positive regimes, but no theorem there asserts arbitrary embedded
matrix realization on a nondisplaceable topology.

The broad favorable conjectural language in ARSVW's introduction cannot be used
to certify current openness of the embedded universal assertion: its own explicit
finite-Isom theorem and definition already imply a negative instance. This audit
does not claim to settle what its authors intended by every informal conjectural
sentence. It also does not infer an abstract-group obstruction from the infinite
cyclic rotational witness. The n = 1,2 boundary cases, finite rotations, incomplete
flat metrics, and the tame/non-tame distinction are preserved in the bridge.

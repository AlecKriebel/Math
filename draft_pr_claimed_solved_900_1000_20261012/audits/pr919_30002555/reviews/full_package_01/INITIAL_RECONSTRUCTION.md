The proposed stronger obstruction is finite rotational derivative image for infinite genus, finitely many n >= 3 ends all carrying genus.

1. Topology. Classification gives a compact n-holed sphere and n one-ended infinite-genus arms. If a disjoint image of the core were in arm i, the connected union of the original core and all other closed arms would lie in one complementary component of the image and contain n-1 ambient ends. Every image complementary component contains only one ambient end. This contradicts n-1 > 1. The distinction between ambient ends and the extra intrinsic collar end is essential. End permutations do not evade the contradiction.
2. Complex analysis. Infinite genus implies nonabelian fundamental group, excluding universal covers sphere and plane. For H/Lambda, the deck group has noncommuting a,b. A normalizer sequence approaching identity eventually centralizes a,b by discreteness. A nonidentity element of PSL(2,R) has abelian centralizer; thus this sequence cannot exist. Discreteness is upgraded to closedness by taking pairwise quotients of a convergent sequence. Properness on H and finite local compact lifts over a compact K imply only finitely many normalizer cosets can bring K to intersect itself. Hence Aut(S) is finite as soon as K is nondisplaceable.
3. Translation model.

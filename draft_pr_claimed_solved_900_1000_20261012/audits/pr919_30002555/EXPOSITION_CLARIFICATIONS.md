**Topology O1: ambient ends.** For an open component U of S minus a compact set define U^* as the ambient ends of S whose sufficiently far representatives lie in U. The assertion that each original arm carries one end means |U^*|=1. It does not assert that the open arm, considered as a separate surface, has exactly one intrinsic end. Removing its compact cuff can create an intrinsic collar end, which is not an ambient escaping end of S. A homeomorphism h is proper and sends U^* bijectively onto h(U)^*.

**Topology O2: connectedness.** If h(K) is disjoint from K and lies in arm U_i, then each closure cl_S(U_j), j!=i, is connected, disjoint from h(K), and meets K along its boundary cuff. Thus K union all these other closed arms is connected, lies in one component of S minus h(K), and carries at least n-1 distinct ambient ends. Every component h(U_k) must instead carry exactly one ambient end, giving the contradiction for n>=3. No contradiction results for n=1 or2.

**Conformal CF-O1: closedness.** The proved isolation of identity makes the normalizer a discrete subgroup of PSL(2,R). Such a subgroup is also closed: pairwise quotients of a convergent sequence eventually lie in an identity neighborhood meeting the subgroup only at identity, so the sequence is eventually constant. Therefore a compact transporter intersects that subgroup in only finitely many elements.

**Conformal CF-O2: compact covering lift.** Given compact K downstairs, choose finitely many open W_j covering K with closure(W_j) contained in an evenly covered coordinate neighborhood V_j. Pick one local section s_j on each V_j. The finite union L of s_j(closure(W_j)) is compact and projects onto a set containing K. This is a finite compact covering lift, not a global section and not the full inverse image of K. The coset-transporter argument then works exactly as in the original proof.

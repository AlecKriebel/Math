## Checkable bridges and blocked routes

**Quotient route: blocked.** Let q:C^m→C^m/<<P_m>>. For every p in P_m, q(p)=1; consequently q(P_m^(k))={1} for all k. A nontrivial commutator in that quotient cannot be the image of a target pure braid. Quotient nonsolvability supplies no obstruction on those elements. Even algebraically, C=P×H with H nonsolvable and F=C has a nonsolvable pure-factor quotient while P^(3)⊂F. Additional geometric input is required; the inspected quotient proofs supply none.

**Otto route: blocked at the extra derived step.** Published Theorem 5.3 already exhibits a pure 3-braid outside F1: L=[BR,σ1BRσ1^-1], detected by a length-6 Milnor invariant. BR has zero linking, so belongs to P3'. Conjugation by σ1 preserves P3' because P3 is normal in B3 and its derived subgroup is characteristic. Thus L belongs to P3''. This does not establish L in P3'''. Replacing its inputs by commutators could destroy the obstruction. No inspected statement supplies that upgrade. This family neither establishes a complete priority defeat nor rules out every classical implication of Otto; a materially new bridge belongs in the separate classical analysis.

**Whitney/grope route: blocked.** The source's height-2 tower containment supplies neither a negative answer nor the required shift at n=1. A proposed grope containment is not a theorem. Tower order, symmetric height and grope class are distinct.

**Unknotted-component route: blocked.** Unknotted components are necessary for pure-braid closure, not a sufficient representability criterion. The inspected boundary-link and weak-cobordism constructions do not control exact P_m^(n+2) membership. Independently applying a source ingredient would be a new application, not evidence that its author already answered the question.

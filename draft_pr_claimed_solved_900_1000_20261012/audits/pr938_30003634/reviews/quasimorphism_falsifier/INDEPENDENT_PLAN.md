## Target and success criteria
Let s(beta) be the ordinary signature of the oriented standard closure of beta in B3, calibrated so the positive trefoil has signature -2. Establish a uniform-defect bound for s and existence of its homogenization q; establish that q restricted to P3 is not a homomorphism. For every finite k>=1, prove or refute that q cannot vanish identically on the normal derived term P3^(k), using a complete descent argument and amenability of the quotient. Then test whether hypothetical containment P3^(k) in F1 forces q to vanish there, including powers, algebraic splitting, link characters and conventions. If all arrows pass, obtain an existential braid outside F1 at every derived depth and use filtration nesting for the source n+2 question. Boundary k=0 and P1/P2 must be treated explicitly.

Check the inferred homogeneous quasimorphism on a solvable quotient, using an original primary theorem and, if feasible, a direct invariant-mean proof.

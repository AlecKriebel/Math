## Universal obstruction checked independently

Here is the check of the candidate's central topological bridge, with the imported theorem confined to the final step. It is a verification of the candidate's mechanism, not a new proof-search route.

Let W satisfy Otto's displayed 1-solution conditions and let `rank H2(W;Z)=2r`. Every meridian is an integral H1 generator of the zero surgery. The character sending each to -1 defines a homomorphism from `H1(M;Z)=Z^m` to `{+1,-1}`. Composing the inverse of the inclusion-induced H1 isomorphism with this character extends it over W. This addresses extension at the boundary itself, rather than just extension over a link exterior.

A compact smooth W has a finite CW model. After choosing lifts, signed and ordinary cellular boundary matrices have the same cell counts. For each boundary map, reduction of the signed integer matrix modulo 2 agrees with the ordinary matrix because -1 equals 1 in characteristic 2. Any nonzero mod-2 minor is odd and hence nonzero over Q. Therefore the signed ranks are at least the mod-2 ranks, and

`dim_Q H2(W;Q_alpha) <= dim_F2 H2(W;F2) = 2r`.

The last equality uses both clauses that are easy to omit: H2 has an integral basis, so has no torsion, and H1 is free, so the UCT Tor contribution is zero. The inequality does not require an assumption about Alexander-polynomial roots, twisted H1, or a nonsingular boundary pairing. Complexification is flat and does not alter the rank or the real symmetric signature of this real sign local system.

For each embedded solution surface S, the restriction of the character is trivial, since every commutator maps to 1 in the abelian image. Thus its oriented fundamental cycle, equipped with a constant local coefficient after restriction, pushes to a class in twisted H2. This is the precise local-coefficient cycle underlying the shorthand that S lifts to the double cover. Different surfaces have no intersections except each dual pair's single point. Local parallel transport at that point is +/-1; change a coefficient sign to make it +1. A normal framing supplies a disjoint push-off, giving zero self-intersection. The two-dimensional block is consequently `[[0,1],[1,0]]`, with one positive and one negative direction.

If a linear combination of these 2r classes vanished, pairing it with each dual class would make each coefficient vanish. They are therefore independent even before any nonsingularity of the complete twisted pairing is assumed. The rank bound makes them a basis. Consequently the entire twisted form is hyperbolic, with no radical; the ordinary integral basis likewise gives ordinary signature zero. This argument includes r=0, when the rank bound makes twisted H2 zero. It also explains why the presence of a boundary causes no hidden radical problem.

Toffoli (1.1) now gives rho zero. In the same source's one-color formula (4.21), take `theta=1/2`, `omega=-1`, zero surgery, and the actual algebraically split link. Its framed linking matrix is zero, so both additional terms vanish and `rho=-sigma_L(-1)`. Thus ordinary signature is zero. The published journal formula and the early-view formula agree on these material hypotheses and signs. Their domain permits roots at -1 if any occur; no continuity, averaging or nearby-value argument is used.

Hence expect a signed cycle on N=sum|B| bounded vertices, with a weighted spoke to the deleted outer vertex at each sigma1 block. At a positive sigma1 crossing the local Goeritz contribution should be +1 and that crossing should be exceptional; at a positive sigma2 crossing it should be -1 and non-exceptional. The predicted reduced matrix is the sum of A_j e_t e_t^T minus sgn(B_j)(e_i-e_next)(e_i-e_next)^T for every sigma2 crossing. Its signature correction is sum A_j. Checks must include closure arcs, cyclic wrap, all crossing signs, a one-vertex loop and two-vertex parallel edges, components and orientation, mixed signs, exact inertia and determinant, and source-index ambiguity. A global mirror negates matrix and correction. Reversing all component orientations leaves the signature unchanged; reversing individual component orientations need not. Positive/negative Hopf stabilizations sigma1^(+/-2) sigma2^(+/-1) should have signature -/+1. These controls calibrate but do not prove the universal construction.

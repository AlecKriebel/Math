## Result and scope

The construction gives a negative answer to the unrestricted
Draghici--Li--Zhang anti-invariant integrability conjecture. On a genus-two
curve times an elliptic curve it produces smooth nonintegrable almost complex
structures with at least three independent real closed anti-invariant de Rham
classes. They converge smoothly to the product complex structure and agree
with it outside one coordinate ball. The paper does not compute the exact
anti-invariant dimension or claim compatibility with a fixed symplectic form.

Generic vanishing is the earlier theorem of Tan, Wang, Zhang and Zhu. The
research note credits Li--Zhang/DLZ cohomology, Donaldson positive planes,
Bande--Kotschick closed pairs, Hind--Tomassini examples and Chen--Zhang

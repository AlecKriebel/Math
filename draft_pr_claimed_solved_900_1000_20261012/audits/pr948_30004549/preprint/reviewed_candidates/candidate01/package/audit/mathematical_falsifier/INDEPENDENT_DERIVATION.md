## Compact starting manifold and classes
The degree-six polynomial z^6-1 has six distinct roots. Its function-field square-root extension is nontrivial, so the double cover is connected. The smooth compactification has two unramified points at infinity: in s=1/z and q=y s^3, q^2=1-s^6, so q=+/-1 at s=0 and 2q is nonzero. At a finite simple root r one may use y as a coordinate, since z-r is a nonzero constant times y^2 plus higher terms; dz/y therefore extends with nonzero leading coefficient. At infinity dz/y=-s ds/q and z dz/y=-ds/q. There are no other poles. Riemann-Hurwitz gives 2g-2=2(-2)+6=2, hence g=2. The forms eta0 and eta1 are independent because their ratio is the nonconstant meromorphic function z on the open set eta0 nonzero.

The complex torus E has a nonzero global holomorphic form dw. The product is compact, smooth, connected and Kähler. Omega0 and Omega1 are linearly independent holomorphic top forms; in complex dimension two their exterior derivatives vanish. For independent verification of the claimed real classes, a real combination F=a Re Omega0+b Im Omega0+c Re Omega1 equals Re H with H=(a-i b)Omega0+c Omega1. For a nonzero holomorphic top form H, F squared is one-half H wedge conjugate H, a nonnegative volume density, positive on a nonempty open set. If F were exact, the integral of F squared would be zero by Stokes, a contradiction. Thus exact F forces H=0 and a=b=c=0. This agrees with the author harmonic-form argument and does not require assuming deformation-stable independence.

Set B=(beta_e-(R_t/2)alpha)/sqrt(D). Then alpha wedge B=0 and B squared=alpha squared. The complex form Psi=alpha+i B has square zero and Psi wedge conjugate Psi=4V. ## Attempted obstructions

Harmonic unique continuation cannot prohibit the example merely because the forms or N vanish on open subsets: N is not a harmonic solution for this arbitrary smooth J, and each closed anti-invariant form is globally nonzero, with no open zero set. Exact perturbation preserves wedge pairings integrally: derivatives R_x,R_t integrate to zero on the compactly supported chart, consistent with cohomology.

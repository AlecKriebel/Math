# Scope and limits of the mathematical and priority evidence

The verified theorem is the explicit smooth compact real-four-dimensional
construction in the note: on a genus-two curve times an elliptic curve,
nonintegrable J_e approach the product J_0 and retain three fixed independent
closed real anti-invariant classes. No exact h-minus value, fixed symplectic
compatibility, real analyticity, new general positive-plane theory or new
generic-vanishing theorem is claimed. Three global classes occupy a pointwise
real plane with variable coefficients. The normalized imaginary form is not
closed. The displayed common-metric obstruction concerns the specified product
J_0, and is not asserted for every integrable almost complex structure.

compactification, Stokes independence, full-cutoff third-class identity,

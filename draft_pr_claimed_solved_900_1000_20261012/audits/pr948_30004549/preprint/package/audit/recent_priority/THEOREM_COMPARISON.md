Audit cutoff: 2026-10-16. The mathematical gate is an input, not a new proof attempt. “Qualifying prior result” means a checkable assertion furnishing a closed connected real four-manifold with smooth nonintegrable J and at least three independent real classes represented by closed J-anti-invariant two-forms. Merely stronger framing of PR948 (fixed classes, support in a ball, C-infinity closeness) cannot rescue priority if this weaker conclusion is already known.

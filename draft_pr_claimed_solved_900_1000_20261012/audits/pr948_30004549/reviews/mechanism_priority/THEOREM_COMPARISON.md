# Theorem comparison: general mechanism priority

Audited target: an earlier theorem or construction, together with an explicit elementary bridge, must yield a smooth nonintegrable almost complex structure on a closed connected real four-manifold with at least three independent real anti-invariant de Rham classes. It need not name the conjecture or use compact support. The mathematical gate is an input, not a priority certificate.

Checkable bridge tests (audit deductions, not newly proposed constructions):

1. **Normalized closed complex volume.** If a decomposable nonvanishing complex two-form Psi is closed, its kernel distribution is involutive: for kernel fields X,Y, contraction of dPsi gives contraction of Psi with [X,Y] equal to zero. This gives integrability. The candidate normalizes a closed pair by variable coefficients; its normalized imaginary part need not remain closed. Citing a theorem for closed normalized pairs would therefore change the hypothesis.
2. **Six to four dimensional restriction.** In the Hind–Tomassini product X times T², the retained forms come from holomorphic two-forms of the complex surface X. Restricting to a complex curve times T² annihilates them, because a complex curve has no (2,0)-forms. Lowering the base dimension therefore removes the stated lower bound. Inventing mixed representatives would require the missing construction, not an easy bridge.
3. **Definite triple.** At a point the candidate satisfies gamma_epsilon = u alpha − v beta_epsilon. Thus its three-form Gram matrix has rank at most two. A positive definite three-by-three wedge matrix has rank three. The two inputs cannot be identified.
4. **Diffeomorphism route.** Nijenhuis tensors are natural under pullback; N_(phi*J0) = phi*(N_J0) = 0. Pullback preserves the classes' independence but also preserves integrability.
5. **Metric route.** The original audit trail gives direct common-metric obstructions for the candidate. Those obstructions prevent misuse of the older bound; they do not establish novelty.

Routes 1–5 are BLOCKED AS PRIORITY BRIDGES on the checked evidence. Reopening requires an actual earlier theorem supplying the missing hypothesis/conclusion, not an unsupported preservation assertion.

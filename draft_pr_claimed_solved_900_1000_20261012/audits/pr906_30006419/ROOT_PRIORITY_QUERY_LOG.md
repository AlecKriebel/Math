The author-hosted 11September2015 preprint of Lytchak–Wenger, *Energy and area
minimizers in metric spaces*, was retrieved and selected theorem/lemma passages
read. Its bytes/hash and precise inspected locators are in
`ROOT_PRIORITY_SOURCE_RECORD.json`. Lemma4.1 tests arbitrary biLipschitz
reparametrizations, including changes to boundary parametrization. Plateau
minimizers have a larger allowable boundary-reparametrization class than the
source's exact fixed-trace neighborhoodwise competitors. The sharper known KS
seminorm constant is therefore nearby evidence requiring attribution and
hypothesis comparison, not by itself an earlier resolution of this question.

The primary current arXiv text of Lytchak–Wenger, *Regularity of harmonic discs
in spaces with quadratic isoperimetric inequality*, arXiv1512.01060, was read at
Definition1.1 and its stated regularity results. It defines fixed-trace
Reshetnyak harmonic/quasi-harmonic discs and establishes Hölder regularity.
Those inspected statements do not assert every such sphere is quasiconformal.
Current manuscript and publication dates must not be conflated.

The DML-CZ primary proceedings text of Jani Onninen, *Harmonic and energy-minimal

definitions, printedpp145–147. Its conformal energy uses the Hilbert–Schmidt
norm and its opening setting is bounded Euclidean domains. These inspected
claims are not an operator-norm-squared smooth closed-sphere counterexample.
This is a scope comparison, not a claim of reading all later references.

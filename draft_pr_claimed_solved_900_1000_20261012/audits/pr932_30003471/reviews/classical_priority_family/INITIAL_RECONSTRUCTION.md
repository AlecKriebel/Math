The exact target is an endpoint statement for a fixed combinatorial correspondence between arbitrary full-dimensional bounded convex Euclidean 3-polytopes: not every corresponding interior dihedral angle can be strictly larger in the first. Its equality alternative adds nothing. The selected candidate asserts more: weak domination at every edge forces equality of every pairwise outward-normal inner product, including nonadjacent facets. Priority must therefore be tested at two levels. A theorem that forbids strict all-edge endpoint domination settles the OWR problem; a weak-domination theorem with equality of the complete normal Gram matrix supersedes the candidate's mathematical conclusion. Equal-angle rigidity alone, infinitesimal/local rigidity alone, rigidity with fixed face metrics, or a theorem restricted to simplices/simple or acute polytopes does not automatically do either job.

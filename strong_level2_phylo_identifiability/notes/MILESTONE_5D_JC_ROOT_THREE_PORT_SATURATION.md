# Milestone 5D: every reticulate three-port root blob is JC-equivalent

## Scope and move

Consider a binary strongly tree-child level-2 blob containing the global root
and having exactly three outgoing cut-edge ports.  Ports are ordered by their
labelled descendant blocks.  The root blob may be a level-1 cycle or a
level-2 theta, and it may be triangle-free or contain one triangle.

Define move `R3` to replace any such **reticulate** root blob by any other
reticulate three-port root blob, carrying the same three labelled descendant
components in any prescribed correspondence.

**PROVED.** Under JC, every instance of `R3` preserves a common regular
four-dimensional stochastic region.  The move may change:

- the generator from cycle to theta;
- the reticulation count from one to two;
- the semi-directed topology;
- the placement of labelled descendant blocks; and
- whether the root blob contains a triangle.

This is not generated by `T`, `Theta`, `Psi`, `Omega`, or `C_root`; those
moves never replace a one-reticulation cycle by a two-reticulation theta.

## The four observable coordinates

For three JC ports, write the four nontrivial Fourier-orbit coordinates as

\[
r_{12},\quad r_{13},\quad r_{23},\quad u_{123}.
\]

The first three use a repeated nonzero character on one leaf pair.  The last
uses the three distinct nonzero characters.

**PROVED.** These four values determine the complete three-port JC tensor;
all nonzero-total coordinates vanish and every remaining coordinate is in one
of these four character orbits.

An ordinary three-port tree has effective arm multipliers `a,b,c` and obeys

\[
r_{12}=ab,\qquad r_{13}=ac,\qquad r_{23}=bc,
\qquad u_{123}=abc.
\]

Hence

\[
\boxed{r_{12}r_{13}r_{23}-u_{123}^2=0.}
\]

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** Its exact generic Jacobian rank is three.  Every
reticulate census model has an exact nonzero rank-four minor, so its
irreducible Zariski closure is the complete four-dimensional affine orbit
space.

Equal closures alone do not establish stochastic overlap.  The next
construction supplies one common interior regular point.

## A shared one-variable subfamily

In any of the seven reticulate unlabelled models, put every internal JC edge
multiplier equal to `h`, put every inheritance probability equal to `1/2`,
and leave the three pendant-port multipliers `a_1,a_2,a_3` free.  Then

\[
\begin{aligned}
r_{12}&=a_1a_2c_{12}(h),\\
r_{13}&=a_1a_3c_{13}(h),\\
r_{23}&=a_2a_3c_{23}(h),\\
u_{123}&=a_1a_2a_3d(h),
\end{aligned}
\]

where every `c_ij(h)` and `d(h)` is strictly positive on `(0,1)`.  Define the
pendant-scale-free ratio

\[
\kappa(h)=\frac{d(h)^2}{c_{12}(h)c_{13}(h)c_{23}(h)}.
\]

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** The seven exact functions, in census order up to the
stated core symmetries, are

\[
\begin{aligned}
&\frac{4h^2}{(h^2+1)^2},
&&\frac{(h+1)^2}{2(h^2+1)},\\
&\frac{2(2h^2+h+1)^2}
 {(h+1)(h+3)(h^4+h^3+2)},
&&\frac{8h^3(h+1)}{(h^4+h^3+2)^2},\\
&\frac{h(2h^2+h+1)^2}
 {(h+1)^2(h^2+1)(h^2-h+1)(h^2-h+2)},\\
&\frac{(2h^2+h+1)^2}{(h^2+1)^2(h^2+3)},
&&\frac{(2h^2+h+1)^2}
 {2(h+1)^3(h^2-h+1)}.
\end{aligned}
\]

The first two are the balanced and unbalanced root cycles; the last five are
the theta cases.

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** For each function, exact Sturm counting proves that

\[
\kappa(h)=\frac{16}{25}
\]

has exactly one root in

\[
\frac18<h<\frac78.
\]

Every root is simple.  The balanced and unbalanced cycle roots are rational,
`h=1/2` and `h=1/7`; the verifier records exact defining polynomials for the
five theta roots.

## Exact common algebraic point

Let

\[
\delta=2^{-30}.
\]

For the isolated root belonging to a given topology, set

\[
\begin{aligned}
a_1^2&=\delta^2\frac{c_{23}}{c_{12}c_{13}},\\
a_2^2&=\delta^2\frac{c_{13}}{c_{12}c_{23}},\\
a_3^2&=\delta^2\frac{c_{12}}{c_{13}c_{23}},
\end{aligned}
\]

using the unique positive square roots.

**PROVED.** Every constructed parameter is strictly in `(0,1)`.  Each pair
factor is a convex average of products of at most seven internal multipliers,
so

\[
h^7\le c_{ij}\le1.
\]

Since `h>1/8`, every pendant square is bounded by

\[
\delta^2 8^{14}=2^{-18}<1.
\]

**PROVED.** Direct substitution gives the same exact Fourier vector for all
seven unlabelled models:

\[
\boxed{
(r_{12},r_{13},r_{23},u_{123})
=
(\delta^2,\delta^2,\delta^2,\tfrac45\delta^3).
}
\]

The first three identities follow after squaring from the definitions of the
positive `a_i`; the fourth follows from `kappa=16/25`.  Positivity fixes every
square-root sign.  The vector is symmetric in the three port labels, so the
same point works for all 39 labelled rooted presentations simultaneously.

## Exact regularity

Use logarithmic output coordinates and variables
`(log a_1,log a_2,log a_3,h)`.  Their Jacobian is

\[
\begin{pmatrix}
1&1&0&(\log c_{12})'\\
1&0&1&(\log c_{13})'\\
0&1&1&(\log c_{23})'\\
1&1&1&(\log d)'
\end{pmatrix}.
\]

**PROVED.** Its determinant is

\[
(\log c_{12})'+(\log c_{13})'+(\log c_{23})'-2(\log d)'
=-\frac{\kappa'(h)}{\kappa(h)}.
\]

The isolated roots are simple, so this determinant is nonzero.  Passing back
from logarithmic coordinates only multiplies it by nonzero positive output
and parameter factors.

**PROVED.** Every one of the 39 reticulate labelled models is therefore a
local diffeomorphism onto a neighborhood of the same four-dimensional
target.  The finite intersection of those neighborhoods is again a nonempty
open neighborhood.  Thus all 39 models have one common full-dimensional
regular stochastic intersection.

## Classification theorem and propagation

**PROVED.** The complete `bowtie_JC` classification for three-port root
factors has exactly two classes:

1. the ordinary-tree class of dimension three, including its reversible root
   placements;
2. one dimension-four class containing every reticulate cycle and theta

The two classes cannot be `bowtie_JC` because their local dimensions differ.

**PROVED.** Tensor equality is preserved when arbitrary identical rooted
tree or network components are attached at corresponding ports.  The
inherited analytic gluing inverse makes the local and component ranks add, so
the `R3` overlap propagates as a full-dimensional regular global overlap.

**PROVED.** Restricting to the one-triangle class `L_1` still leaves 33

classified by Milestone 3D.

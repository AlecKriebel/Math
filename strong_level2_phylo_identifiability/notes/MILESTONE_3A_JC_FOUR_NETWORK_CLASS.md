# Milestone 3A: an exact four-network JC equivalence class

## Scope and conventions

All networks here are root-spanning, simple, four-leaf expansions of a theta
orientation core.  The four leaves are attached directly at the four outgoing
ports.  Edge indices refer to the deterministic order in
`certificates/jc_four_network_class.json` and in the census generator.

The semi-directed topology is obtained by deleting the root `S`, replacing its
two incident arcs by one edge, forgetting every tree-edge direction, and
retaining the arrowhead on every edge entering a reticulation.  If one child of
`S` is a reticulation, the replacement edge retains an arrowhead at that
reticulation.

This note uses the status labels required by the research program.

## Root relocation lemma

**PROVED.** For a stationary reversible group-based model, insert the root in
a semi-directed edge and give its two halves Fourier multipliers `u_g,v_g`.
In every displayed tree containing both halves, suppression of the degree-two
root replaces them by the product `u_g v_g`.  If the incoming reticulation
half is not selected, the other half ends at a degree-one uniform root and
integrates out.  Thus the distribution depends only on the effective
semi-directed multiplier `z_g=u_g v_g`.

For JC, every `z in (0,1)` has the rational open split

\[
u=\frac{1+z}{2},\qquad v=\frac{2z}{1+z},\qquad uv=z.
\]

Consequently, any two admissible root placements of the same semi-directed JC
network have equal complete open stochastic images.  This proves the exact
model equality for `0` versus `4` and for `13` versus `22`; it is stronger than
one full-dimensional overlap point.

## Triangle-redirection identity used at the common point

Let `A-B-C` be a triangle whose old reticulation is `C`.  Write

\[
x=x_{AB},\qquad u=x_{AC},\qquad v=x_{BC},
\]

and let `lambda` be the probability of selecting `A -> C`.  The four internal
JC factors seen at the three external arms are

\[
\begin{aligned}
X&=x,\\
Y&=\lambda u+(1-\lambda)xv,\\
Z&=\lambda xu+(1-\lambda)v,\\
W&=x\bigl(\lambda u+(1-\lambda)v\bigr).
\end{aligned}
\]

Redirect the reticulation to `B`, set the new opposite, `A-B`, and `C-B`
edge multipliers to `x,u,v`, respectively, and retain `lambda`.  The new
pair factors are `Y,X,Z` and the triple factor remains `W`.  Therefore the
external-arm rescaling

\[
r'_A=r_A,\qquad r'_B=r_B X/Y,\qquad r'_C=r_C Y/X
\]

preserves the complete three-port Fourier tensor.  Tensor contraction then
preserves the whole network distribution.

**PROVED.** This is an exact rational triangle-redirection correspondence on
the nonempty open region where the two rescaled arms remain in `(0,1)`.

# Milestone 3C: the JC root path-reversal move Omega

## Scope and convention

This note certifies a new collision in the root-spanning four-leaf census.  As
before, the semi-directed topology is obtained by suppressing only the
degree-two artifact created by forgetting the global root, forgetting the
directions of tree edges, and retaining every arrowhead entering a
reticulation.

The four rooted models are

| name | census index | leaf labels in port order |
|---|---:|---|
| `N16_source` | 16 | `(1,2,3,4)` |
| `N16_target` | 16 | `(2,1,4,3)` |
| `N26_source` | 26 | `(1,2,3,4)` |
| `N26_target` | 26 | `(2,1,4,3)` |

The two census indices are reversible placements of the global root within

## Primary theorem

**PROVED.** Under JC, the four rooted networks above are binary, strongly
tree-child, and level 2.  After suppressing the global-root artifact, they
have one nontrivial triangle-free blob whose simple cycles have lengths
`4,4,6`.

**PROVED.** They form four rooted isomorphism classes and exactly two
semi-directed isomorphism classes,

\[
\{N16_{\rm source},N26_{\rm source}\},\qquad
\{N16_{\rm target},N26_{\rm target}\}.
\]

The two semi-directed classes are not triangle-equivalent: neither contains a
triangle.  Nevertheless, their JC images have equal irreducible
nine-dimensional Zariski closures and contain a common nine-dimensional
regular relatively open stochastic region.  Call this root-adjacent
observational ambiguity the **path-reversal move** `Omega`.

**PROVED.** Restoring an incoming state port and a positive outgroup edge
destroys `Omega`.  The lifted source satisfies a quartet invariant
identically, whereas the lifted target makes it strictly positive throughout
the complete open JC parameter cube.  Thus the two lifted stochastic
interiors are disjoint.  This occurrence of `Omega` is root-local and does
not provide a stackable non-triangle ambiguity bit.

## Why the two semi-directed topologies differ

After suppressing `S`, there is exactly one label-preserving isomorphism
between the source and target underlying undirected graphs.  On internal
vertices it is

\[
U\leftrightarrow V,\qquad
P1_0\leftrightarrow P1_1,\qquad
X0\leftrightarrow P4_0.
\]

It simultaneously exchanges `L0,L1` and `L2,L3`; the changed port labelling
makes the induced map label preserving.

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** The source reticulation set `{V,X0}` is carried to
`{U,P4_0}`, disjoint from the target reticulation set `{V,X0}`.  Therefore
the unique underlying-graph reflection does not preserve the reticulation
arrowheads.  No semi-directed isomorphism exists.

This gives the combinatorial description of `Omega`: at the globally
suppressed root, reflect the two opposed subdivided paths and transfer the
two reticulation designations to the reflected alternating pair, while
preserving the labelled descendant blocks.  It changes reticulation
placement in a triangle-free semi-directed blob, not merely root placement
or triangle orientation.

## Propagation and current classification status

**PROVED.** By the inherited analytic leaf-substitution theorem, any or all
of the four pendant leaves can be replaced by corresponding arbitrary rooted
binary trees.  The resulting larger root-adjacent networks retain a
full-dimensional regular `Omega` overlap; every substituted edge parameter
is analytically recoverable.

**PROVED.** The strict incoming-port invariant prevents this natural gadget
from occupying a nonroot blob.  Consequently `Omega` gives arbitrarily large
one-root-blob pairs, but no independent product of `Omega` bits along a blob
tree.

**UNRESOLVED.** This result does not yet prove that every occurrence of
`Omega` in every possible strong level-2 generator is root-local.  It also
does not complete the JC local atlas, exclude other root or nonroot moves, or
prove the desired if-and-only-if theorem for `L_1`, `L_*`, or `S_2`.

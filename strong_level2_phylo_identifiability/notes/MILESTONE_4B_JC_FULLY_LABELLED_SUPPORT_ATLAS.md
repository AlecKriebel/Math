# Milestone 4B: fully relatively labelled bounded-support JC atlas

## Result

**PROVED.** In every strongly tree-child nonroot theta tensor obtained from a
minimal core-preserving support by retaining at most two additional outgoing
ports, full-dimensional regular JC overlap occurs exactly under labelled
isomorphism or ordinary triangle redirection `T`.

This statement includes arbitrary relative assignments of the descendant
block labels.  The incoming state port is distinguished and fixed.  It is not
the earlier, weaker calculation in which the labels were normalized by their
roles as sink, repair, or probe ports.

The new exact atlas handles the three large cases:

| minimal support | probes | outgoing ports |
|---:|---:|---:|

| 4 | 2 | 6 |

Milestones 3H and 3E respectively handle the remaining three- and

One-sided generic containment in the new five- and six-outgoing atlas is
**UNRESOLVED**.  Arbitrary-blob and global-network completeness are also

For each of the four oriented theta cores, choose every minimal strong repair.
Place the required one or two extra labelled probes on every directed core
segment, enumerate every order when several labels occupy one segment, and
quotient by exact colour-preserving rooted isomorphism.

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** This gives:

- 656 raw role presentations;
- 520 canonical role-labelled rooted candidates;
- 304 candidates with five outgoing ports;
- 216 candidates with six outgoing ports.

Apply every permutation of the outgoing labels while fixing the incoming
port.  There are exactly

\[
304\cdot 5!+216\cdot 6!=192{,}000
\]

relative-label presentations.

For a triangle-free semi-directed graph, the structural canonical code keeps
all vertex colours and directed reticulation edges.  For a graph with its
unique triangle, the code forgets only the reticulation designation and arrow
directions internal to that triangle, while retaining every direction outside
it.  Exact individualization and colour refinement then gives the quotient by
labelled isomorphism and `T`.

**PROVED.** This normalization is exactly the equivalence generated by `T`:
the unique triangle must map to the unique triangle; every external directed
edge is preserved; and all forgotten data are precisely the local triangle
orientation data.  No pendant placement, path order, external reticulation,
or underlying edge is forgotten.

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** The 192,000 presentations reduce to 19,500 structural
classes:

## Exact quartet tensor compression

Fix one selected quartet and write

\[
\sigma\in\{0,1\}^2
\]

for the two reticulation-parent choices.  For every original edge `e`, let

\[
\delta_e=(A_{e,\sigma})_{\sigma\in\{0,1\}^2}
\]

be its four displayed-tree descendant masks on the quartet.  Use the empty
mask when `e` is absent from that displayed tree or has no selected
descendant.

For a zero-sum character assignment `g`, the exact coordinate is

\[
\widehat p(g)=
\sum_\sigma w_\sigma(\lambda_1,\lambda_2)
\prod_e x_e^{
  \mathbf 1\{\bigoplus_{i\in A_{e,\sigma}}g_i\ne0\}}.
\]

If `e` and `f` have the same signature, their exponents agree in every term.
They therefore occur only through `x_e x_f`.  Replacing every signature class
by

\[
y_\delta=\prod_{e:\delta_e=\delta}x_e
\]

is an exact reparameterization.  The groups are disjoint, and the product map
from each open cube to `y_delta in (0,1)` is surjective, for example by taking
all factors to the appropriate positive root.  Thus this preserves the
complete open quartet tensor image, not merely its Zariski closure.

Permuting the two reticulations or swapping either pair of incoming parents
acts on the four displayed trees by a signed coordinate permutation and sends
an inheritance parameter to either another inheritance parameter or its
complement.  These eight cube automorphisms also preserve the complete open
image.

**EXACTLY COMPUTED.** The 7,360 quartet restrictions in the atlas reduce under
these exact operations to only 90 tensor types.

## Observational theorem

Let `H` and `H'` be two tensors in the bounded-support atlas.

**PROVED.** If they are in different structural classes, one selected
polynomial vanishes identically on one model and not on the other.  Their
irreducible model closures are therefore different.  A full-dimensional
regular overlap of equal-dimensional images would contain a relatively open
subset of each irreducible closure and force those closures to be equal.
Hence `H` and `H'` cannot satisfy `bowtie_JC`.

**PROVED.** If they are in the same structural class, they differ by labelled
isomorphism and a finite sequence of local triangle redirections.  The exact
`T` port-tensor correspondence leaves all nontriangle ports unchanged and is
regular on a nonempty open stochastic region.  All relevant redirections can
be composed on a common open region by choosing the external arm multipliers
sufficiently inside `(0,1)`.  Thus the two tensors do satisfy `bowtie_JC`.

Therefore

\[
\boxed{
H\bowtie_{\rm JC}H'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
H,H'\text{ are related by labelled isomorphism and }T.
}
\]

## Remaining logical gap

This milestone does **not** yet prove the arbitrary nonroot-theta theorem.
Given two large strong blobs `B,B'`, a labelled support chosen for `B` need not
induce a strong or even core-preserving restriction of `B'`.  The finite atlas
above classifies every restriction that is itself a strong support deck, but
does not yet separate those cross-support nonstrong targets.  Closing that
case requires either:

1. an exact atlas of all nonstrong restrictions induced by a strong blob; or
2. invariants showing that observational overlap forces a support of one blob
   to be a support of the other.

Recovery of the blob tree and exclusion of coordinated changes in different
blobs also remain **UNRESOLVED**.  No `L_1`, `L_*`, or `S_2` if-and-only-if
theorem is claimed here.

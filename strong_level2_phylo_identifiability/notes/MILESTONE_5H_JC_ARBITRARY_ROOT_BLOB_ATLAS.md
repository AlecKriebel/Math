# Milestone 5H: the arbitrary root-blob JC atlas

## Scope

This note completes the full-dimensional regular-overlap classification for
one **ported root blob** in the strongly tree-child level-2 class, subject to
the standing restriction of at most one triangle in the blob.  The ports are
distinct labelled descendant blocks; arbitrary identical rooted components
may be substituted at them.

The result classifies `bowtie_JC`.  It does not yet classify every one-sided
generic containment or every boundary intersection.

As throughout the project, the semi-directed convention suppresses only the
degree-two global-root artifact, forgets tree-edge directions, and retains
all arrowheads entering reticulations.  Ordinary redirection of the unique
triangle is quotiented by move `T` while forming structural census classes.

## Root move system

Let

\[
\mathcal R_{\rm root}^{\rm JC}
=\{T,C_{\rm root},R_3,\Theta,\Omega_{\rm chain}\}.
\]

The moves have the following certified meanings.

1. `T` redirects an admissible triangle reticulation and has an exact local
   port-tensor correspondence.
2. `C_root` inserts or removes the two-port root cycle.  Its complete open
   image is unchanged under JC, K2P, and K3P, even inside a reconnecting
   two-input context.
3. `R3` replaces any open reticulate three-port root cycle/theta tensor by
   any other.  Under JC all 39 rooted presentations, or 21 semi-directed
   topologies, share one regular four-dimensional neighborhood.
4. `Theta` is the inherited four-port theta pendant transfer.
5. `Omega_chain` reverses an arbitrary ordered long path and swaps its
   opposed short-path pair, as proved in Milestone 5G.

The former four-port symbol `Psi` is absent: Milestone 5F proved that it is
generated by contextual `C_root` and reversible root placement.

## Primary local theorem

**PROVED.** Let \(B,B'\) be leaf-labelled ported root blobs that are binary,
strongly tree-child, level 2, and have at most one triangle.  Give every port
an arbitrary positive JC arm multiplier.  Then

\[
B\bowtie_{\rm JC}B'
\]

if and only if their semi-directed ported topologies are related by a finite
sequence of moves in
\(\mathcal R_{\rm root}^{\rm JC}\) and port-preserving graph isomorphisms.

**PROVED.** Every move may carry arbitrary corresponding rooted components
at all ports.  The inherited Fourier gluing inverse preserves equality,
strict positivity, full local dimension, and regularity.

This theorem includes arbitrary finite edge subdivisions and arbitrarily many
ports.  It is not restricted to the four- and five-port examples used to
discover the moves.

## Finite support reduction

The first-principles level-2 generator theorem leaves one cycle core and the
finite theta orientation cores.  Every binary subdivision is an ordered word
of port labels on each directed core segment.

The support reduction proved in Milestone 4A applies with `S` interpreted as
the global root artifact as well as with `S` interpreted as an incoming
attachment vertex:

- a core-preserving strong support uses at most four outgoing ports;
- one additional port identifies its core segment;
- two additional ports identify relative order on that segment;
- restrictions on at most six outgoing ports reconstruct every finite set of
  segment words.

**PROVED.** A finite linear order is determined by its ternary betweenness
relation up to global reversal.  Thus two probes per segment suffice
uniformly; no bound on the total number of ports is needed.

For a cycle, the same argument reconstructs the two root-to-reticulation
words.  Inserting `C_root` above its two root sides embeds this comparison in
the theta support census.  Collapsing the inserted root triangle returns the
unique cycle code.

## Why different signatures separate models

**PROVED.** A signature bit records whether a concrete polynomial invariant
vanishes identically on a selected four-port marginal.  If two signatures
differ, one exact marginal invariant belongs to one model ideal and not the
other.  Products of unselected positive edge multipliers are independent
effective arm parameters, so a nonzero reduced pullback remains nonzero on
the complete subdivided model.

Therefore the two irreducible model closures differ.  Equal-dimensional
full regular stochastic overlap would force equal closures, so different
signatures rule out `bowtie_JC` exactly.  This argument does not infer
stochastic separation merely from a numerical sample.

## From the bounded table to arbitrary subdivisions

Normalize every triangle by `T` and collapse every visible `C_root` first.
Suppose two arbitrary port-word blobs survive all separating support tests.

If no support uses an `Omega_chain` collision, the finite support-reduction
theorem reconstructs the same core, the same segment for every port, and the
same order on every segment.  The normalized blobs are isomorphic.

If one support uses an `Omega_chain` collision, the exact census forces:

1. the root core is the opposed-path `Omega` core;
2. all three selected probe ports lie on its one long path;
3. the two opposed short-path ports exchange;
4. their long-path order reverses.

Choose overlapping triples of long-path ports.  Their ternary betweenness
relations force the same reversal on every overlap.  Hence all long-path
ports reverse in one global order, not in independently chosen subintervals.
Any port on another core segment would appear with two long-path probes in a
singleton support signature and separate the models.  Thus the complete
blobs differ by exactly one global `Omega_chain` move.

This also rules out a coordinated collection of inconsistent finite
reversals: the overlap of probe triples enforces one global reflection.

Low port counts are supplied by the prior exact atlases:

- two ports: ordinary root versus `C_root`;
- three ports: the tree is strictly separated from the reticulate `R3`
  class;
- four ports: only `T`, `Theta`, `Omega`, and the derived `Psi/C_root`
  classes survive.

Together these cases prove the primary theorem.

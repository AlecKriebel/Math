## Accepted scope

1. The expected-query minimax identity is pn, not n, for measurable almost-surely terminating algorithms in the exact matching-field full-output model, uniform success p, finite approximation factor at least one, and spectral or Frobenius relative-best-rank error. The stopping-time review supplies the full-seed filtration, adaptive Gaussian posterior induction, non-atomic residual argument on every finite stopping event, countable union, Tonelli step, and matching mixture upper bound. Joint measurability and ordinary conditional-distribution assumptions are part of this model.
2. A known d-dimensional row subspace yields the corresponding pd minimax cost. Its approximate version has the stated squared-additive Frobenius and spectral error bounds. The prior tail is not inferred or certified by those observations alone.
3. A square rank-r matrix with equal range and co-range is recovered exactly with r matching-field Gaussian queries. No optimality over the EP/normal subclass or numerical stability is claimed.
4. The stated known-u rank-one model has the exact candidate-ball radius and diameter. Its correspondence to the source's literal orthogonal-group near-symmetry parameter is restricted to the real case; the complex principal-angle convention is explicitly independent.
5. The one-dimensional constant-real-drift Dirichlet operator has the stated Fourier approximation bounds for all drift amplitudes. This is a special case, not a resolution of the general elliptic perturbation question. Function-valued oracle responses and exact Hilbert-space computations are assumed.

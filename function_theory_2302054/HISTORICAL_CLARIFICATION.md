# Clarification of the 2018 update

The frozen README describes the 2018 Update 2.54 as “inconsistent” with the known 1983 resolution. The precise historical statement should be narrower: the update fails to reflect Toppila's published negative answer.

An assertion that no progress was reported to the editors can literally coexist with an earlier publication. We make no claim that the editors knew of the result, that the reporting assertion is false, or that the two publications are logically contradictory. This clarification supersedes that wording in the preserved README without altering its audited mathematical content.

The independent audit found the exact problem match, counterexample, witness estimates and rigidity proof sound. The result is credited to Sakari Toppila, *Solutions of problems of Miller and Rubel*, Theorem 2 (1983), [DOI 10.5186/aasfm.1983.0826](https://doi.org/10.5186/aasfm.1983.0826). This packet asserts no new resolution or priority.

## Decisive primary resolution

Sakari Toppila, *Solutions of problems of Miller and Rubel*, Annales Academiae Scientiarum Fennicae, Series A. I. Mathematica **8** (1983), 369–370. [Primary PDF](https://www.acadsci.fi/mathematica/Vol08/vol08pp369-370.pdf), [DOI 10.5186/aasfm.1983.0826](https://doi.org/10.5186/aasfm.1983.0826), [journal volume index](https://www.acadsci.fi/mathematica/Vol08/vol08.html).

Theorem 2 on p.369 states the negative answer for the ray-plus-unit-disks set. Section 3 on p.370 provides both explicit witnesses and the annular rigidity argument. Both pages were read and visually inspected. The article explicitly cites the 1977 Problem 2.54, so this is an exact-match resolution rather than a result with an inferred connection. Its Theorem 1 concerns a different problem of Miller and is not used here.

The primary proof abbreviates the infinite-product lower bound and uses repeated Schottky estimates. PROOF.md checks the lower bound explicitly and supplies a uniform Harnack proof of the same rigidity conclusion. Thus the classification does not rest on an abstract alone.

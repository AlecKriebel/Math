## Exact accepted target

Let p be any prime, k a field with characteristic different from p containing all pth roots of unity, and G an arbitrary finite constant group over k. For CH^*(BG_k)/p, the least degree detecting all classes by the multiplication maps from elementary abelian p-subgroups and their centralizers, truncating only the centralizer factor, equals the greatest degree of a nonzero suspended unstable submodule. Degrees and suspensions are measured in **Chow codimension**; a suspension by d corresponds to topological shift 2d.

## Critical positive-word proof interfaces

1. **Arc order and all admissible twists.** HKM Definition 2.1 orders proper arcs from a fixed boundary lift. Our <= notation means weakly right, opposite to HKM's displayed inequality sign but with identical geometry. Boundary fixing preserves endpoints. A boundary-fixing orientation-preserving map preserves the lifted order, and right-veering maps are closed under composition. HKM Lemma 2.5's positive monoid explicitly uses homotopically nontrivial curves; its proof includes separating twists and thus is broader than BMVHM's nonseparating length function. There is no hidden homological-essentiality substitution.

2. **Faithfulness and strict motion.** A finite disjoint proper arc system cuts the surface into a disk. A mapping class fixing every proper arc class can be isotoped relative to the boundary to fix that system pointwise, and then is trivial by the disk argument. Non-null-homotopic Dehn twists are nontrivial, including separating and peripheral twists, as detected by annular winding of proper crossing arcs. A nontrivial right-veering twist strictly moves some arc; its inverse sends that image left. A nonempty positive identity would make the inverse of its first twist a product of right-veering maps, contradiction. The empty residual product, annulus and disk cases are covered. BMVHM Proposition 5 confirms the same inverse obstruction, but the submitted proof appropriately supplies the faithfulness detail rather than importing a narrower length convention.

3. **Noncommutative deletion.** With u=s_1...s_m a retained subsequence of v=b_0s_1b_1...s_mb_m, P_j=s_1...s_j and P_0=1,

       v u^{-1} = product_{j=0}^{m} P_j b_j P_j^{-1}.

   The prefixes are those of u, not of v. Adjacent prefix cancellations prove the identity. Each block splits into individual conjugated letters. The set of all positive non-null-homotopic twists is conjugation invariant, and a proper subsequence inserts at least one letter. Equal values would therefore yield a forbidden nonempty positive identity. The empty retained word is included. This does not assume commutativity or a faithful Artin representation.

4. **Finite-alphabet Higman step.** The submitted minimal-bad-sequence proof chooses each word by least possible length among bad continuations. Extracting a common final letter permits replacing the tail by shortened words. Any good pair in the new sequence contradicts original badness: either it lies in the old prefix, goes from that prefix to a shortened tail, or lies wholly in the shortened tail and extends by the common last letter. An infinite fiber enumerated without duplicates must therefore have a proper comparable pair. Repeated letters, empty shortened words and the empty alphabet cause no failure. The generic target h need not be central. No numerical bound or stopping certificate is extracted from this well-quasi-order argument.

5. **Surface application.** The coarse 2^(2g)(3g-2)+1 bound counts distinct support classes only. Same mod-2 homology and intersection <=1 imply zero intersection, including the zero class and separating curves. The ribbon-graph/complement record bounds support orbits and remembers cyclic orders, crossing pairings, cycle labels, complementary genera, attachment partitions and the distinguished boundary. Complementary annuli do not introduce a residual integer under ambient homeomorphism; prescribed circle maps extend through collars. Support-normalizing conjugation preserves the specific target because t_delta is central in this boundary-fixing group. This centrality is needed in the surface normalization, not in the generic group lemma. A finite union of finite fibers then proves the ordered-factorization theorem.

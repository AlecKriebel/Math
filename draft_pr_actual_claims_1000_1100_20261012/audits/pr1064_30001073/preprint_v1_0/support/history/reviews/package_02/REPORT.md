## Complete analytic claim and attacks

The claim covers every positive integer n and nonnegative row-stochastic square matrix A, including sparse inputs and permanent zero. With the unnormalized S=sum a_ij², it gives

    per(A) <= exp(-n + e^4 sum a_ij² log(e/a_ij))

The original Barvinok–Samorodnitsky question, OWR 44/2008 Problem 9, printed pp.2549–2550 / PDF pp.73–74, fixes gamma>=1 and assumes doubly stochastic A with S<=gamma. The all-n consequence is exp(e^4 gamma)n^(e^4 gamma)e^-n. Only n>=2 gets the pure-power n^(3e^4 gamma)e^-n display. The source's prior van der Waerden lower bound supplies the other direction in the doubly stochastic case. No row-stochastic lower bound, stronger entrywise gamma/n hypothesis, normalized S/n substitution, growing-gamma fixed-polynomial conclusion, or sharp exponent is asserted.

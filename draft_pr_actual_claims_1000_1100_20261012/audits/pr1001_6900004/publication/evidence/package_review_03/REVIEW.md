## Independent proof audit and attempted counterexamples

The existence of this entire sign pattern is invariant under the prescribed fibre homeomorphism. Surjectivity matters: its inverse preserves the same signs, so invariance is in both directions. Consequently, a class cannot have both a collapsed and a separated placement of an actual edge. Every component is either inside or outside its collision diagonal.

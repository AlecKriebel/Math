## Modular banks: independent reconstruction

Let h be the periodic indicator of A modulo p>=2. The two translated sums of h in c_a=1+h(-a-1)-h(-a) are equal, so the total multiplicity is p. Since h takes values 0 or 1, every c_a is 0, 1 or 2. The p counters must be distinct physical vertices; multiplicity two describes two vertices with equal initial offsets.

At integer k, storage is the sum of their residues. Advancing k by one adds p chips across the bank before subtracting p for each counter wrapping at offset -k-1. Thus the increment is p-p c_{-k-1}=p(h(k+1)-h(k)). Storage minus p h is constant. Each counter visits every residue once per period, so the storage's period average is p(p-1)/2; the average of p h is |A|. This proves the exact constant p(p-1)/2-|A| without induction assumptions about an initial firing phase.

For nonempty A, the attainable maximum is K+p, attained exactly at allowed residues. Full A gives c_a=1 and constant storage, with every residue allowed. At p=2 and full A, K=-1 is allowed because K is an algebraic constant, not an initial chip count. Empty A also satisfies the identity but never attains the falsely advertised value K+p; it is explicitly excluded by the theorem's bank hypothesis and never appears in a nontrivial SAT reduction. Negative k is merely a periodic identity parameter, while the firing interpretation uses k>=0. Fresh exhaustive controls covered empty and full boundaries as well as all 0/1/2 multiplicities.

## Boolean encoding and polynomial output

Normalize clauses by removing repeated literals and deleting a clause containing both signs of a variable. An empty clause is a recognized NO case; no remaining clauses is a recognized YES case. Relabel the occurring variable names and discard unused declarations. Therefore the number n of actual variables and the number H of banks are O(L) even if variable identifiers themselves are large binary integers. The inherited audit makes this ordinary encoding convention explicit; the written reduction does not depend on expanding numerical identifiers.

The first n distinct primes starting at 3 distinguish Boolean residues 0 and 1 from other residues. A non-tautological t-variable clause, 1<=t<=3, has exactly 2^t-1 satisfying Boolean assignments. CRT sends each local assignment injectively to the product modulus q of those distinct primes. Consequently every clause predicate is nonempty and excludes non-Boolean residues. Variable predicates determine a global Boolean assignment; a successful clause predicate says precisely that the corresponding clause is true. Conversely, CRT lifts a satisfying global assignment to a nonnegative integer satisfying all predicates. Both directions are independent of the sign, order or numerical naming of literals.

The reduction enumerates only p_i and products of at most three primes. The global product M exists in the correctness proof; the constructor never enumerates it, emits M vertices, or simulates M checkpoints. Arithmetic on a local CRT assignment has polynomial bit length. A standard prime bound, freshly checked at its primary interface, gives p_(n+1)<=e(n+1) log(n+1)<=3(n+1)^2. Thus each expanded modulus is at most 27(n+1)^6. With P=max q and Q=sum q, N<=27(H+1)(n+1)^6 and E=O(H P^2), so even an explicit adjacency matrix is polynomial-size. Each bank can be built by a polynomial offset loop. Tight O(n log(n+1)) prime growth is valid, but the looser explicit polynomial bound already suffices.

For a bank, B_b=q_b(q_b-1)/2-|A_b|+q_b. Initial counter chips are their offsets and all relay chips are zero. Root chips R=Q+B-1-S(0) are at least Q-1. The total C=Q+B-1 is nonnegative and at most Q+sum q_b(q_b+1)/2, polynomial in L. Every individual coordinate is at most C. A further exact check is (E-N)-C=sum_b[q_b(q_b-3)/2+|A_b|]>=0, since every nontrivial modulus is at least 3. Thus the construction does not manufacture immediate NO instances by violating the stable-capacity obstruction.

## Graph implementation and asynchronous evolution

There are Q distinct counters, root r and P-1 returns in the main graph. Each root-to-counter, counter-to-root, counter-to-return and return-to-root arc occupies a unique ordered pair. Different copies of the same offset are different vertices, so they create no parallel arc. Counter outdegrees are their bank moduli, return outdegrees are one and root outdegree is Q. Any counter in a largest-modulus bank reaches all returns, while r reaches every counter and every vertex returns to r. This proves strong connectivity, including the last return. Removing r leaves only counter-to-return arcs, so depth is at most one. The exact counts are N=Q+P and E=Q+sum q_b^2+P-1.

At every checkpoint all counters are stable and returns empty. One legal root firing adds exactly one chip to every counter. A counter can then fire at most once because it has no other incoming arc; its firing distributes exactly its outdegree chips. After handling counters, each return can legally fire once per chip it holds. That phase is finite and returns every emitted chip to r. Holding an active root while processing another legal vertex is permitted; no sink or altered game rule is introduced.

Induction now gives each offset-a counter the residue (a+k) mod q after k root firings and every return zero. Conservation gives root count Q+B-1-S(k). The root is stable iff S(k)>B-1; storage is integral and at most B, so this is iff S(k)=B. A failed bank has deficit exactly its positive modulus. Nonnegative deficits cannot cancel, hence total equality holds iff every predicate is true. The offset phase, the strict stability inequality and the minus one in C are all essential and correct.

A satisfying assignment supplies a finite nonnegative CRT checkpoint. The canonical legal word either terminates earlier or reaches that checkpoint and terminates there. No claim is made that this legal word has polynomial length. For an unsatisfiable formula every checkpoint permits another root firing and finite return phase, producing an infinite legal word. To exclude termination in another order, let f be any nonnegative stabilizing vector. At the first proposed firing beyond f_v, the count at v is f_v and every other coordinate count is at most its f-coordinate. Nonnegative incoming coefficients put the current chips at v at most its stable final value under f, contradicting legality. Every legal prefix is therefore bounded by f. This proves the required dichotomy without fairness or the assumption that a stabilizing vector itself is a legal word.

## NP certificate, orientation and the N=2 boundary

Given a candidate nonnegative integer firing vector f, compute z=x-Delta f and check 0<=z_v<d_v. Binary arithmetic is polynomial in the graph, input and certificate bit lengths. The preceding first-excess argument shows that a valid certificate bounds every legal word, so repeated legal firing must eventually stop. Conversely an actual terminating word supplies such f and z.

A strongly connected graph has a primitive strictly positive integer kernel vector pi, obtained from rooted in-arborescence counts divided by their common gcd. A rooted tree chooses at most one of N-1 outgoing arcs at each of N-1 nonroot vertices, so pi_v<=(N-1)^(N-1). If a terminating f were coordinatewise at least pi, then f-pi would be nonnegative with the same stable final configuration. Applying least action to the actual terminating word would give f<=f-pi, impossible. Therefore choose w with f_w<pi_w.

For an arc u->v, final chip balance is sum_(a->v) f_a=d_v f_v+z_v-x_v. All incoming firing counts and x_v are nonnegative and z_v<=d_v-1. Keeping only f_u gives f_u+1<=d_v(f_v+1). Iterating along a simple directed path from u to w of length at most N-1 yields f_u+1<=(N-1)^(N-1)(f_w+1)<=(N-1)^(2N-2). Hence coordinate bit lengths are O(N log N), without asserting polynomial stabilization time or assuming small binary input chips. The additive one is necessary: in the complete loopless three-vertex digraph, (2,0,0) stabilizes after one firing at vertex zero and none at the others. The inequality without slack would fail on its outgoing arcs. The candidate retains the correct slack.

For N=2 the only strongly connected loopless simple graph is a directed two-cycle with outdegrees one. Its only nonnegative stable configuration is zero. Conservation implies that only the all-zero input halts, with f=0, agreeing with the displayed bound. No logarithmic or zero-degree exceptional convention is left implicit.

## Oriented appendix and preprocessing

The appendix removes counter-to-root arcs and uses P returns, sending each modulus-q counter to q distinct returns. All arcs follow root -> counters -> returns -> root. Thus loops, repeated pairs and antiparallel arcs are absent. A largest-modulus counter reaches every return, and every counter reaches the root through a return. Counter, return and root outdegrees are unchanged, so the storage and checkpoint proof remains exact. N and E each increase by one and C does not change. Root deletion again leaves only counter-to-return arcs.

The main fixed preprocessing instances are the directed two-cycle with configurations (0,0) and (1,0). The appendix uses the directed three-cycle with configurations (0,0,0) and (1,0,0). The first cases are stable, and the second have explicit chip-circulation legal lassos. Both conventions, strong connectivity, simplicity, nonnegativity and root-deletion depth hold. This separately verifies the entire strengthened theorem rather than assuming that removal of antiparallel arcs is cosmetic.

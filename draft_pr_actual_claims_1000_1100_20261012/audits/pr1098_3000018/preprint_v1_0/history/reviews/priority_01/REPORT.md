this question from their binary directed-multigraph hardness theorem. The
historical Egres page is evidence for the question's formulation and attribution;

## Exact antecedents that must be carried into the paper

**P2: attribute the literal modular-bank mechanism.** Holroyd--Levine--Winkler,
*Abelian logic gates*, arXiv:1511.00422v2, Lemma 4.1, PDF/printed page 15,
construct a unary linear-plus-periodic function using parallel primed topplers.
For a nonempty periodic indicator h modulo q, substitute

    f(k) = k + h(0) - h(k).

The source's increment multiplicity at i is

    f(i)-f(i-1) = 1+h(i-1)-h(i).

Its primed state is a=q-i, giving **exactly**

    c_a = 1+h(-a-1)-h(-a).

The cumulative topplings F(k)=f(k), together with q physical chips returned
per counter toppling, then yield

    S(k)=S(0)+qk-qF(k)=q(q-1)/2-|A|+q h(k).

This is an exact mechanism antecedent, not a vague counter analogy. A
self-contained elementary derivation remains useful, but the formula and
primed-toppler method cannot be described as priority-cleared inventions.
The source's Section 8.3, PDF page 32, expressly separates conservative gates
from its splitters and one-output topplers. Its lemma does not by itself
establish the complete candidate's simple conservative HALTING language.

**P2: attribute the literal CRT/SAT encoding.** Stockmeyer--Meyer,

## Why the closest full-theorem routes do not yet imply the result

These are comparisons of exact read interfaces, not blanket certification of
every proof in every source.

1. **Generalized rotor reachability.** The actual Auger--Coucheney--Etse
   journal PDF, Electronic Journal of Combinatorics 33(2) (2026), P2.5,
   DOI 10.37236/13580, was read through the complete Theorem 21 hardness proof
   and Lemmas 22--23, PDF pages 39--43. Its variable gadgets have branching
   arc-state transitions, two sign routes, literal-sink outputs and a specified
   final arc/particle target. An ordinary chip vertex has one fixed output
   vector, and any reachable stable endpoint is schedule independent. Erasing
   the branching arc mechanisms erases the assignment choice. The source
   proves exact-target reachability in a more general model, rather than
   ordinary halting. A harder containing VAS class does not imply hardness of
   this subclass. No source reduction supplying the missing bridge was found
   or established. The journal and arXiv v2 PDFs have distinct bytes; they are
   not called publisher-identical.

2. **Sandpilization and conservative universality.** Bond--Levine,
   *Abelian networks II*, Definition 6.5 and Corollary 6.6, PDF pages 17--18,
   preserve halting **on all inputs**. They do not give equality of the
   outcome of an arbitrary fixed input and reset internal state. A fresh
   two-state self-feedback example in the derivations gives different outcomes
   for that unsupported same-input substitution while preserving the all-input
   property. This rejects the particular claimed shortcut; it does not rule
   out every possible state-aware reduction. Holroyd--Levine--Winkler's gate
   universality also uses nonconservative gates; its page-32 discussion does
   not supply a finished conservative ordinary-chip compiler.

3. **Petri-net classes.** Directly encoding a chip vertex gives one place and
   one consuming transition, input weight equal to the outdegree, unit
   outputs and exact token conservation. Both the choice-free and join-free
   restrictions hold; the weighted input threshold matters. The inspected
   Cheng--Esparza--Palsberg source uses a set-valued unit flow relation
   (PDF page 4), and the inspected Esparza survey treats unit/1-safe interfaces.
   A threshold-one replacement already changes a degree-three counter's
   outcome. Hardness for a containing communication-free weighted-net class
   does not imply hardness with one consumer, balanced mass, unit outputs,
   simplicity, strong connectivity and a given marking. The full
   Mayr--Weihmann construction and complete Hujsa--Devillers weighted-net
   proofs were unavailable in the attempted routes. Their additional
   consequences are **UNKNOWN**, not dismissed from titles or abstracts.

4. **Different chip-firing quantifiers.** Perrot--Pham's MINREC proof
   optimizes over recurrent configurations with a dissipative sink.
   Kiss--Tothmeresz optimize an additional chip distribution. Schulz's DIST
   proof chooses extra grains in a boundary-dissipative grid. These are not
   supplied-configuration ordinary halting, and a polynomial result on simple
   Eulerian halting prevents silently identifying the languages. Tothmeresz's
   Claim 2.4 is a recurrence-to-reachability implication; Proposition 2.8
   guesses a recurrent representative rather than constructing a particular
   halting input. Moore--Nilsson predict a final state; Cairns's undecidable
   models use infinite backgrounds. Their inspected hypotheses do not give
   the complete finite conservative simple result.

5. **Timing and graph expansion.** Ji--Li--Wang's inspected arXiv v3
   Lemma 4.3 and full Theorem 4.6 proof realize firing strings in synchronous
   parallel dynamics. Huang--Tian's October 2026 Theorem 4.1 and full proof
   (PDF pages 8--10) fix the period of an already active fixed-priority
   sequential orbit and explicitly leave the transient question. Neither
   read result supplies the SAT halting equivalence. Numerically subdividing
   multiplicity 2^k requires 2^k vertices; its k+1-bit description does not
   become a polynomial simple-graph reduction by that operation.

The complete conservative graph realization, its oriented variant, and the

## Thesis and lambda-zero normalization

[Sportiello thesis](https://pcteserver.mi.infn.it/~caraccio/PhD/Sportiello.pdf), full printed pp. 195-197 and 207-213 = PDF pages 203-205 and 215-221, read as text and visually inspected. Theorem 10.3 states the Catalan/noncrossing basis, and equation (10.75) reduces crossings with polynomial coefficients. The selected monomial coefficients in Lemma 10.6 are lambda-independent +/-1. Thus the normalized f-family is supported at lambda=0; directly specializing raw scalar products would destroy it. There is a harmless reversal of triangular-order direction between the lemma statement and the dipole proof: reversing the order gives the same unit triangular minor. The submitted proof's separate invariant argument needs neither the external basis theorem nor this editorial direction. Printed p. 195 discusses nonplanar transfer.

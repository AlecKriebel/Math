## Strongest direct predecessor comparisons

**The question and rank-one geometry.** The complete [OWR 38/2018 contribution](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46760), printed p.2332, formulates the rank-one uniqueness coNP-hardness conjecture under von Stengel's name, explicitly as joint work with Adsul, Garg, Mehta and Sohoni. The volume year is 2018; [EMS metadata](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/16413) records posting in August 2019. The [Waterloo abstract](https://uwaterloo.ca/combinatorics-and-optimization/events/tutte-colloquium-bernhard-von-stengel) already records the conjecture in July 2018. Credit should identify this historical conjecture without converting its date into proof that no later solution exists.

The controlling [Adsul et al. journal body](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10317978) proves the exact auxiliary-zero-sum/hyperplane characterization, with degeneracy allowed. This is established structure, not a project theorem whose independent reproving resets priority. The journal §10, p.630, actually leaves second-equilibrium hardness conceivable and output-efficient enumeration open; I inspected the full discussion and its preceding paragraph. It does not answer the decision problem. The matching discussion is p.36 of [arXiv v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.04611v4). Exponential equilibrium multiplicity and traversal of all parameter segments do not alone prove decision hardness.

Version numbering is material. In arXiv v4 dated 22 December 2019, rank reduction is Theorem 7, one-equilibrium polynomial time is Theorem 19, maximal Nash subsets is Theorem 20, and the exponential family is Theorem 21. In Operations Research 69(2) (2021), 613–631, those are Theorems 1, 4, 5 and 6 respectively. Journal Theorem 7 is a different structure/homeomorphism result. The article was published online 4 June 2020, DOI 10.1287/opre.2020.1981. Citation labels must name the version or use the journal numbering.

**The reduction's hard source event.** The complete [Disser–Skutella journal Lemmas 3.1–3.2](https://www2.mathematik.tu-darmstadt.de/~disser/pdfs/DisserSkutella18.pdf), pp.5:7–5:10 and Figures 1–3, already contain the paired signed counting gadgets and bridge. The complete proof of Corollary 1.7, p.5:17, already establishes parametric minimum-cost-flow designated-arc activity hardness and its parametric-LP consequence. The candidate's source network follows these formulas; the hard event cannot be advertised as newly created here. The source supplies an SSP diagnostic event, whereas the candidate needs exclusion of bridge use by all optimal flows at all real parameters and a full equilibrium-pair embedding. Those are additional obligations, not automatic consequences of the corollary's short statement.

The candidate's two upstream arithmetic clarifications are compatible with the actual arc formulas: the second base-case path cost is `1-v1`, and the post-adjustment envelope needs the base-cost perturbation. The root mathematical review discharges them. They neither transfer authorship of the source gadget nor warrant a claim of a new general DS theorem. Historical credit should mention the [SODA 2015 predecessor](https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/1.9781611973730.59), 858–872, online 22 December 2014, and the 2013/2014 arXiv history, while using the inspected 2018 journal body for controlling lemmas. The journal footer says November 2018; issue bibliographies sometimes use 2019. The retrieved arXiv v2's 2014 sidebar and 2018 internal title date prevent certifying it as the unchanged SODA body.

**General uniqueness and the default pair.** [Gilboa–Zemel (1989)](https://itzhakgilboa.weebly.com/uploads/8/3/6/3/8363317/gilboa_zemel_nash_complexity.pdf), complete §§3.1–3.2 and 3.13, already proves unrestricted bimatrix uniqueness coNP-completeness, uses a known default pair, and supplies polynomial-size rational countercertificates from support systems. The current compact closed-cell proof and explicit determinant bit bound make degeneracy details checkable, but the membership conclusion is prior art. [Conitzer–Sandholm (2008)](https://www.cs.cmu.edu/~conitzer/nashGEB08.pdf), Definition 4, complete Theorem 1 proof and Corollary 2, similarly has a strict default pure pair and additional assignment witnesses. Strictness follows directly when its default payoff is positive. A default-plus-witness reduction pattern is therefore also prior art.

These are not excluded merely by their abstracts. In Gilboa–Zemel's old-strategy principal block, two diagonal entries of `A+B` are `2(1+epsilon)` and the off-diagonal is either 2 or 0. Its determinant is positive for epsilon>0, so the displayed family has rank at least two. In Conitzer–Sandholm's literal/negated-literal block, the diagonals are `2(n-1)` and off-diagonals `2(n-4)`; the determinant is `12(2n-5)`, nonzero for integral n. Their actual constructions do not already impose exact rank one. No blanket strategic-equivalence reduction to rank one is inferred from those results.

## Later work and potentially equivalent mechanisms

The source catalogue gives exact inspected editions and levels. The most material comparisons are:

- Theobald's actual 2009 §4 parametric LP, complete Theorem 4.5/proof and §5 conclusion enumerate rank-one equilibria with a bound polynomial in best-response polytope vertex counts. Those counts can be exponential in input size, and need not equal equilibrium output size. Its degeneracy discussion and vertex-bound theorem do not certify global uniqueness in polynomial time or supply its hardness reduction.
- Garg–Jiang–Mehta (WINE 2011) actually generalizes the parameterized polytope/hyperplane method to bilinear games with compact polytopal strategy spaces and proves a one-equilibrium algorithm. A general compact polytope already appears in the literature; simply pointing to it cannot establish the candidate's simplex penalty embedding or exhaust all no-instance pairs. Matsui's complete 1995/1996 product-objective reduction operates over general linear constraints; its feasible sets are not the particular Nash best-response product constraints. Its NP-hardness is not an automatic rank-one uniqueness theorem.
- Heyman/Gupta's actual rank-reduction formulas minimize rank within positive affine strategic equivalence. The relevant theorem has a residual-rank formula, not a universal value of one. Heyman's manifold theorem expressly assumes a rank-one reduced pencil. The 2019 thesis body is retrieved, with its scope and corresponding papers inspected; not every thesis proof was read. None of these inspected formulas translates the unrestricted default reductions to exact rank one without further premises.
- Patris–Panageas ICLR 2024, full main-result proof (Appendix A.5), computes an approximate equilibrium using scalar consistency and learning iterations. It does not prove absence of other supports or crossings. Its 2026 thesis remains a distinct unread body.
- Overman 2026, actual Theorem 5.1 and §5.2 construction, optimizes reward against replicator dynamics. More strongly than a title distinction, its displayed game satisfies `A+B=1 v^T`. Subtracting `1 v^T` from the row payoffs makes the game `(-B,B)`, preserving best responses. Its equilibrium set is a compact product of LP optimal-strategy polytopes, so uniqueness can be checked by coordinate ranges. This specific reduction therefore cannot already yield the candidate target hardness under ordinary complexity assumptions. Later reward-approximation proof details were not needed to deduce this scope exclusion.
- Golden 2026, actual definitions and complete Theorem 3.9/Corollary 3.11 proof context, uses rank one of each player's own payoff matrix, supermodularity and global-game limit selection. Those assumptions and that conclusion differ from exact sum rank one and uniqueness of all complete-information equilibrium pairs.
- Mehta's rank>=3 PPAD theorem, symmetric-equilibrium work, recent common-payoff CLS work, semidefinite strategy games, the fixed 5×5 nondegenerate maximal-count theorem, and strongly monotone near-zero-sum games are scoped separately. Some were screened only at model/target level; their entire proofs are not represented as independently excluded. The symmetric nonuniqueness-to-nonsymmetry block construction was inspected in actual formulas; its rank restriction is not the candidate target.

Searches and forward citation discovery extend through 13 October 2026, but the OpenAlex 2021 list has only eight entries and omits retrieved later papers. Its DS lists and the 34-entry STOC list include duplicates and only title-screened records. Recent parametric-flow, pivot-rule, game-equivalence and algebraic-game leads can motivate follow-up; absence from a sparse citation index cannot establish absence from the literature.

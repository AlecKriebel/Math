## Complete mathematical reconstruction and adversarial checks

The frozen EARLY_DERIVATION.md supplies the independent derivation. The following details survived adversarial examination.

1. **Normalization and signs.** D_H is positive and finite exactly for 0<H<1; K_t has distance squared |t−s|^(2H), so independent real isonormal processes give standard fBm without a complex-Gaussian factor two. The real Hilbert inner product is indeed real by conjugate symmetry. The conjugation in u_h is essential: it yields h(t)−h(0), whereas omitting it would yield h(−t)−h(0) and destroy the positive-time cell pairing. The (2π) Fourier factor is correct.
2. **Cell regularity and constant.** φ=x⁴(1−x)⁴ has its first three derivatives zero at both endpoints. The zero extensions of f=φ′ and g=φ have r,r′,r″ traces zero, so their weak third derivatives have no boundary delta masses. A jump of f‴ is permitted. Three integrations by parts are valid. Exact polynomial calculation gives a=∫(φ′)²=4/45045, ‖f‖₁=1/128, ‖g‖₁=1/630, and ‖g‴‖₁=113/196. The norm ‖f‴‖₁ is the explicit positive radical expression recorded in FINITE_CHECKS.json. Defined polynomial integrals already suffice for the claimed explicit H-only constant.
3. **Uniform Gram estimate for all H.** α=2H+1 lies in (3/2,3); the weighted Fourier tail is O(|η|^(α−6)), and the periodized tail is bounded by the odd-power series in the proof. Its integral bound is exactly 1+1/[2(5−α)], valid throughout α<3. The Δ power cancels as 2H+2−α−1=0. The Parseval normalization is D_H/(2π). The bound applies to every finite vector and every Δ>0. It uses integer cell translation, so it does not silently cover nonuniform grids.
4. **Complete observation independence.** Every witness coordinate has zero covariance with every grid observation of both components. The complete combined vector is jointly Gaussian; zero cross blocks imply independence of whole vectors. Therefore their product sum Y is independent of the entire observation sigma-field and centered. This establishes E[FY]=0 for every nonlinear F∈L²(G), not just linear estimators or an Euler scheme.
5. **Signed covariances.** A,C are real symmetric PSD with A≤M_fI and C≤M_gI. Component independence gives E[Y²]=tr(AC). The upper bound follows from tr(C^(1/2)(M_fI−A)C^(1/2))≥0, allowing noncommuting matrices and negative entries. Fresh finite rational matrices with a negative off-diagonal entry confirm the algebraic normalization; the proof itself needs no numerical evidence.
6. **Finite-to-limit covariance.** For fixed n,T,j, component independence gives the exact finite identity E[L_mU_jV_j]=Σf_j(t_k)(g_j(t_(k+1))−g_j(t_k)). There is no missing Gaussian contraction. Its deterministic limit is ∫f_jg′_j, with error bounded by the modulus of continuity of f_j times ∫|g′_j|. Euler L² convergence passes through the expectation by Cauchy–Schwarz since U_jV_j is L². No simultaneous exchange of m and n limits is made. The resulting covariance is Δ^(2H)a per cell, hence naΔ^(2H) for Y.
7. **Canonical convention.** The polygonal correction is exactly Q_m=(1/2)ΣΔB¹ΔB². Independence gives the exact double covariance variance, which groups into m+2Σ(m−r)ρ_H(r)². The second-difference formula yields |ρ_H(r)|≤C_Hr^(2H−2); squared sums give m^(1−4H), m^(−2)(1+log m), or m^(−2) at the three regimes. All vanish throughout H>1/4, and the canonical dyadic limit is included. This elementary bridge removes a construction-by-name assumption.
8. **External witness law transfer.** The represented Euler sums and finite observations have identical joint laws to the original ones; taking their L² limit preserves that joint law. Every L²(G) variable is a Borel function of the finite observation vector up to null sets. Proving the inequality for those functions on the enlarged Gaussian space therefore proves it on the original model. Unobserved Gaussian directions can add witness variance but cannot invalidate Cauchy–Schwarz. A minimal path-space representation is unnecessary.
9. **Quantifiers and scaling.** Cauchy–Schwarz gives naΔ^(2H)/√(nM_fM_g)=c_HT^(2H)n^(1/2−2H). This proof includes n=1 and arbitrary positive T directly. It never interpolates a liminf over finitely many n or applies an unsupported self-similarity transfer. The n=1 witness occupies the whole cell and vanishes at both endpoints, which the observations include.
10. **Endpoints and residual scope.** At H=1/4 the Q variance is at least T/4 from its diagonal, so the bridge cannot identify an L² canonical limit there. At H=1, Q still vanishes, but D_H diverges and the path is linear, making the iterated area endpoint-measurable. These distinct failure mechanisms prevent extrapolation. No positive constant uniform up to H=1 is claimed. The all-H corollary keeps α<3 and every other hypothesis needed by the witness proof.

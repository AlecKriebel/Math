## Independent mechanism checks and manuscript slips

The SAT gadget's variable choices are s_i and s_i+1. The omitted value 2s_i+1 forbids choosing both. Clause points t_k can only be represented by a variable choice plus its corresponding complementary literal point; separation of the variable/clause ranges and Sidon uniqueness prevent other representations. Taking all clause complements plus the satisfying variable choice gives the forward witness. For the reverse direction, variables unused by a witness may be assigned 0; each represented clause already contains a satisfied literal. The check script independently tested all 230 two-variable formulas with one or two size-three clauses drawn as literal multisets, including repeats and tautologies. It enumerated every A subset U and compared with truth assignments. It also verified the two exclusion properties, nonnegative numerical bounds, and Sidon sets for sizes 1..100. This finite evidence supplements the proof, not replaces it.

In Lemma 5.1, the displayed all-p-point graph is not literally size q and its stated numerical estimate does not follow for every prime allowed by Bertrand. Taking only the first q graph points repairs this without changing 4q^2: choose odd p with q<=p<2q (q=1 fixed; q=2 choose p=3); then x<=q-1 and 2px+(x^2 mod p)<=p(2q-1)-1<4q^2. Sidon uniqueness follows by reducing the two coordinates mod odd p; integer equality implies coordinate equality because low-coordinate sum differences have absolute value <2p. This also avoids the characteristic-two diagonal failure. The source lemma's stated bound is attainable by this elementary truncation; no larger constant is needed in the final chain.

The skeleton is I=I' union I'', with a=5^5,u=5^6,s=5^7 and maximum 4*5^8=1562500.

This authored note supplements, rather than replaces, the complete frozen primary-family derivations and report in `history/reviews/primary_encoding_01/`. For q=v+c>=1 the literal final interface is a polynomially constructed S in [0,2^57 q^4] with formula satisfiability iff an unrestricted integer R has R+R=S. Its chain is SAT sandwich [0,160q^2] within [0,2^8q^2] (Lemma 5.3, pp21-23), masking [0,2^17h^2] (Lemma 1.5, pp15-18), and positioning [0,2^24h'] (Lemma 1.4, pp8-11). Thus h'=2^17(2^8q^2)^2=2^33q^4 and 2^24h'=2^57q^4. No final target anchor is assumed. Both directions of all three relevant integer mechanisms were reconstructed by the AI-assisted source family. Finite tests corroborate selected pieces, not the whole asymptotic construction.

The reconstruction avoids promoting inaccurate literal intermediates:

- The all-p-point Sidon graph need not have q points, its printed range estimate is not valid for every allowed prime, and p=2 has diagonal collisions. For q>=3 choose an odd prime q<=p<2q and use only the first q points f(x)=2px+(x^2 mod p), 0<=x<q. Handle q=1 by {0}, q=2 with p=3. Then f(x)<=p(2q-1)-1<4q^2. Equal integer pair sums imply equal low-coordinate sums (their difference has magnitude <2p), hence equal x+y and equal x^2+y^2 modulo p; odd p determines the unordered pair. Distinct unordered integer pairs therefore have distinct sums. The familiar finite-field fact supplies this Sidon proof. At q=6,p=11, the full graph has 11 points and maximum221>144; the first six have maximum113<144.
- The positioning skeleton has maximum4*5^8=1562500. Its doubled support requires coefficient8*max(I)+2=12500002<2^24, rather than the smaller inaccurate inline estimate. Endpoint and half-stride support cases are checked in the complete reconstruction. The relevant forward/reverse block arguments, not just a numerical fit, establish the interface.
- The masking translation places target and allowed root in [0,512N^2] with N=16h. Consequently 512(16h)^2=2^17h^2. The missing square in the inline v2 display is inconsistent with its formal lemma and final calculation; the quadratic bound is the one actually used. The complete reconstruction also records ancillary scale/symbol slips and the intended affine inverse, singleton pair sums, and [0,N] bounds.

These repairs preserve the final bound without creating a new hardness-gadget claim. The inspected integer-root quantifier is unrestricted R subset Z.

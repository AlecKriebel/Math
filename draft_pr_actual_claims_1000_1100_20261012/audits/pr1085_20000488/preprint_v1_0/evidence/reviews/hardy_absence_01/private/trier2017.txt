Summary

Motivated by the open problem
σd (Hα,Σ ) 6= ∅ for any Σ 6= Π and all α > 0?

The positive answer was known for su. large α > 0 (Exner-Kondej-03).



Main results
     We prove σd 6= ∅ for any α > 0 xed and a small local deformation.

     For suciently small local deformation #σd = 1.

     The lowest eigenvalue is asymptotically expanded in terms of the
     Fourier transform of the prole function.


Key features of the proof
     Involves applying BS-principle several times.

     Its by-product is an implicit scalar equation on the lowest eigenvalue.

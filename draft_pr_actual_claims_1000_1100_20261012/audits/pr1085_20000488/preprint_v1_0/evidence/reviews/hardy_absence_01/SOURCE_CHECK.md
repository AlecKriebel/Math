[Exner–Minakov](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.7624), v1, has 31 pages in the fetched preprint. I read the geometric assumptions and all pages required for the same-line case of Theorems 5.1 and 5.2; I visually inspected pages 16, 17, 20, 21, 22. Section 5 assumes one C4 infinite boundary curve that is straight outside a compact region. Its same-line nontrivial case binds for each positive attractive parameter. The proof uses the flat exponential transverse mode and a tangential cutoff; its negative geometric defect is the planar version of the report's boundary defect. Thus credit for the standard planar claim is correct. The concave-wedge absence statement has different asymptotic geometry and does not provide a compact same-line exception. This inspection is not an independent audit of the whole paper or a journal-byte comparison.

[Pankrashkin–Popoff](https://arxiv.org/abs/1502.00877), v2, has 29 pages. I read and visually inspected Definition 1.1, the curvature-gap hypothesis, Corollary 1.6, and its Section 7 proof (pages 2, 3, 4, 23). Corollary 1.6 requires admissibility plus a strict gap between the curvature supremum and its value at infinity. For any prescribed finite eigenvalue count, existence follows only above a sufficiently large attractive parameter. That quantifier does not contradict absence for all sufficiently small parameters on fixed compact graphs. The full proofs of effective-Hamiltonian Theorems 1.2/1.3 were not independently audited here; only their role and quoted consequence were checked.

every finite projected Hermitian SDP lift of normalized complex Sep(3,3).

identified with the standard fermionic projector hull on exterior-square C^6.
The map is real and injective on all 225 Hermitian coordinates, not merely on
pure projectors. Its normalization is one quarter in the unnormalized
complex coordinate vectors. The product-projector identity has positive
orientation; the full rank and identities are justified symbolically.

Split C^6 as orthogonal C^3 + C^3. A PSD zero-trace support equation selects
mixed-block generators; positivity excludes cancellation. The exact prior
Vrana–Lévay tensor-to-wedge lemma identifies their decomposability with
rank-one product tensors. Unit norm gives normalized, complex, trace-one
Sep(3,3). The two equations Phi=1,Q=0 are imposed **inside** the mass ball.
Q is nonnegative on the Kähler face and can be negative elsewhere. The
equations alone admit entangled projectors and are insufficient without
mass-ball membership. An ambient left inverse extends linearly, so finite
lifts transfer through the section and inverse, contradicting Fawzi.

The full degree-two part of the real vanishing ideal defines a 496-square
first-moment lift. Sphere moments fix its trace to 2, making the spectrahedron
and its projection compact. An affine SOS support would be nonnegative on
that projection. If every support had such a representation, the projection
would equal the mass ball, contradicting no-lift. A strict separating gap
persists under rational perturbation, giving rational **unnormalized**
coefficients. The exact maximum remains unknown; normalized coefficients
are not claimed rational. Signed coordinate wedges ensure positive maximum.
The elementary norm-completion-to-affine-SOS identity then excludes local
completion at every point for the resulting constant calibration.

The proof uses no nonexposed-face inference, unsupported polar representation,

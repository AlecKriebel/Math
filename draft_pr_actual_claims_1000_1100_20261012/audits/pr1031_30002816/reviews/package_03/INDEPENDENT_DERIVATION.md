# Independent derivation before prior reviewer conclusions

Written from the current paper alone. Target hypothesis: the real oriented mass ball C(4,12), convex hull of all decomposable unit real 4-vectors, has no finite projected SDP lift; therefore universal affine-SOS support exactness fails and a constant closed calibration has no pointwise or local finite norm completion. The external input to verify separately is Fawzi's no-lift theorem for normalized complex Sep(3,3).

1. Let J be the orthogonal complex structure on real R^12 and Phi = omega^2/2. The restriction of omega to an oriented real 4-plane has a skew matrix with two singular values in [0,1]. Phi on its unit volume is its Pfaffian, a signed product bounded by 1. Equality forces the compressed J to be isometric: the normal component of J vanishes by the Pythagorean identity, so the plane is J invariant. The positive sign fixes complex orientation. Since every generator has Phi<=1, a convex combination at Phi=1 consists entirely of positive complex 2-planes. Reversing orientation goes to Phi=-1, not this face.
2. For u_j=(e_j-i f_j)/sqrt(2), the exterior decomposition of V_C has a direct (2,2) summand with basis u_I wedge conjugate(u_J). A(H) is real for H=H* since conjugation changes H_IJ to H_JI and swapping degree-two blocks is positive. Independence on this complex basis gives real injectivity for all Hermitian H, real dimension 15^2=225, not just projectors. Replacing u by w=e-if supplies 1/4 and rational real Hermitian-basis coordinates. No hidden i or scale remains.
3. For complex a,b, a wedge Ja=-i a_hat wedge conjugate(a_hat). The two -i factors and moving conjugate(a_hat) past b_hat give net +1. Consequently A((a wedge b)(a wedge b)*)=a wedge Ja wedge b wedge Jb. The complex phase cancels in zz*, and a unit decomposable z has a complex orthonormal factorization with phase absorbed. Thus the full exposed face is A(K), K the convex hull of normalized decomposable fermionic projectors. On every Hermitian basis coordinate Phi(A(H))=tr(H).
4. With E=U direct-sum Z and dimensions 3,3, W splits into pure D and mixed B=U wedge Z. Every element of K is PSD and tr(P_D H) is the sum of nonnegative weights times ||P_D z_t||^2. Zero trace forces every active generator into B; PSD prevents cancellation. If nonzero a wedge b lies in B, a_U,b_U are linearly dependent, and likewise a_Z,b_Z; neither projection span can vanish. Hence a wedge b is a nonzero scalar x wedge y with x in U,y in Z. Conversely all such mixed products are decomposable. T(x tensor y)=x wedge y is unitary on orthogonal blocks, so unit norm corresponds to normalized complex product projectors, giving exactly Sep(3,3), not all PSD supported matrices.
5. The form Q=(omega_U^2+omega_Z^2)/2 satisfies Q(A(H))=tr(P_D H). Therefore C(4,12) intersect {Phi=1,Q=0}=A(Sep(3,3)) as a full convex-hull equality. Q need only be nonnegative on the Phi face; it is negative on oppositely oriented pure complex planes. Membership in C is indispensable. A mixed entangled projector satisfies the two equations in ambient space without belonging to the section. No global-Q supporting-face assertion is required.
6. An affine section of a projected finite PSD feasible set is still a projected finite PSD feasible set after imposing its affine equations on lift variables. A left inverse to injective A exists on its image and extends real-linearly to the 495-dimensional exterior space. Thus a lift of C induces a lift of normalized complex Sep(3,3); Fawzi rules it out. Real-symmetric feasible sets are a subclass of the admitted Hermitian lifts, so the comparison is in the required direction.
7. The full real degree<=2 vanishing ideal J_2 is finite dimensional, regardless of a finite explicit presentation. On a (N+1)x(N+1) moment matrix M>=0, impose M_00=1 and annihilation of J_2. Since X lies on the unit sphere, sum M_ii=1, hence trace M=2. All entries are bounded by PSD 2x2 minors and the diagonal sum; the affine slice is closed, so compact. Its first-moment projection T is compact, a finite lift and contains conv(X). Here N=binomial(12,4)=495 and matrix size 496; X is in S^494.
8. A supporting affine SOS discrepancy is itself degree<=2 and vanishes on X, hence belongs to J_2. Its moment evaluation yields delta-c.x=sum a_j^T M a_j>=0 for all x in T. If every support were affine SOS then every supporting halfspace contains T, whence T=conv(X). This contradicts the no-lift conclusion. Equivalently T strictly contains C and any strict separator of a point x in T\C gives a failing support. This uses compactness and support separation only, not duality or shadow closure.
9. The separation gap varies continuously in c: the support function of X is 1-Lipschitz and c.x is ||x||-Lipschitz. A rational c' in a sufficiently small open neighborhood preserves the positive gap. It is nonzero; signed coordinate wedges imply delta=max_X c'.p>0. The rational claim is only for the unnormalized functional, since delta need not be rational. Phi here is unrelated to the existential failing functional.
10. Define phi=c'/delta as a constant differential 4-form. Central symmetry gives absolute comass 1, and constant coefficients give closedness. A pointwise norm completion by finitely many linear 4-forms psi_j would imply 1-phi=(1/2)(1-phi)^2+(1/2)sum psi_j^2 on X, contradicting failure after multiplying by delta. Allowing local varying differential forms cannot help: evaluation at any single point gives such a forbidden identity.
11. The optional converse is sound. If 1-phi=||a+Bp||^2 on symmetric spanning X, comparison at +/-p gives B^T a=-phi/2 and ||a||^2+||Bp||^2=1. At a maximizer, Bp_0=-a and evaluation at -p_0 gives 4||a||^2=2. Then 2||P_(a-perp) Bp||^2=1-phi^2. Homogeneity extends the completion to all simple vectors. Smooth local selection is not inferred.

Strongest derived result: all elementary implications are valid, conditional only on the correctly scoped established Fawzi theorem. No explicit failing coefficient vector, exact comass, normalized rational calibration, minimal dimension or worldwide-priority claim follows from this argument. The six-page paper uses this scope.

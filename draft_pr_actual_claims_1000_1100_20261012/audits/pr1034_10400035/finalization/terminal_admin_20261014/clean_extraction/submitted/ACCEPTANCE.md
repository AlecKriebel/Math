# Mathematical acceptance: Stanford Question 2.13

Date: 2026-10-09 UTC. Problem 10400035 / AMR-103-0035.

## Exact accepted results

The following three statements have each passed an independent AI mathematical audit in the ordinary unframed smooth ordered fixed-endpoint string-link category over \(\mathbb Q\). “Free exterior” refers to the actual three-dimensional abstract fundamental group, without requiring the bottom meridians to form a chosen free basis.

1. At two strands and order at most two,
   \[
   M_2(2)=\operatorname{span}_{\mathbb Q}\{1,\ell,\ell^2\},\qquad
   a_1\notin M_2(2)+N_2(2).
   \]
   The Milnor algebra includes all lengths and repeated indices. The component Conway invariant therefore disproves the proposed spanning statement at this parameter pair.
2. For every integer \(k\ge2\),
   \[
   N_2(k)=0,
   \quad\text{hence}\quad N_n(k)=0\quad(0\le n\le2).
   \]
3. There exists a nonzero ordinary rational invariant of **exact order seven** that vanishes on every free-exterior two-string link. In particular,
   \[
   N_7(2)\ne0,\qquad N_n(2)\ne0\quad(n\ge7).
   \]
   The marked supporting theorem is universal: each free-exterior two-string link is isotopic, relative to the boundary and with both labels preserved, to its simultaneous reversal
   \[
   (\rho\gamma)_i(s)=r(\gamma_i(1-s)),\qquad
   r(x,y,t)=(x,-y,1-t).
   \]

## Mathematical basis and attribution

For the first result, Meilhan's complete ordinary order-two classification is applied to the resulting invariant function, even if it was originally expressed as a polynomial in arbitrarily high-length Milnor invariants. All-length repeated-index Milnor concordance invariance and three explicit null-concordant tests eliminate the non-linking terms. Nogueira's published free-tangle example, converted carefully to the marked smooth category, supplies a trefoil component in an actual handlebody exterior. Three independent pure-braid evaluations then establish the obstruction. No equality of a full Milnor vector with a braid is assumed.

For the second result, the complete all-strand Meilhan coordinates are eliminated using arbitrary-linking pure braids, isolated triple commutators with explicit Magnus expansions, the Kauffman–Lambropoulou rational witness with trefoil plat, and the two orderings of Nogueira's trefoil/\(T(3,-4)\) example. Willerton's normalization gives component values \((1,5)\). The proof establishes freeness and every needed mixed plat value for the precise split insertion of trivial strands. It does not assume closure of free exteriors under arbitrary stacking, gluing, or deletion.

For the third result, actual freeness first gives a genus-two handlebody. Classical genus-two hyperelliptic centrality and extension, an exact collar correction, explicit tube maps, and Hatcher's relative-ball diffeomorphism theorem give the required marked reversal symmetry. The full proof of this marked step is included. Duzhin–Karev arXiv v5 supplies the nonzero reversal-odd seven-wheel, its ordinary unframed survival in Section 6, and the rational weight-system integration framework. Antisymmetrization gives a nonzero exact-degree-seven symbol and universal vanishing on free exteriors. These established source results, rather than an unpublished computer experiment, are the stated theoretical inputs.

The complete public citations and precise source-edition limits are in SOURCES.json and the reports. The bounded context checks identify classical adjacent results and do not establish earliest priority. No ingredient is claimed as newly invented where the proof cites it from published work.

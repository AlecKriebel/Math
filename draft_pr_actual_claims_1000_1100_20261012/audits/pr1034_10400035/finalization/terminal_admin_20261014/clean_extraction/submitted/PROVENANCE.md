No theorem, lemma, mathematical hypothesis, proof argument, displayed calculation, mathematical warning, or literature-search limitation was deleted.

- Historical “audit requested,” approach counts, and residual claims in successive reports are retained with their chronology explained in README.md. The combined current result is stated in ACCEPTANCE.md and STATUS.json.

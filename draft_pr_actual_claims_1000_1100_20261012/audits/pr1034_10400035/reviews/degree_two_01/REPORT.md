## Core finite and universal calculations

The full Milnor-algebra intersection follows from null-concordant component/plat rows `(2,0,0)`, `(0,2,0)`, `(2,2,-4)`. Their determinant is -16. The resulting equations `2D=2E=2D+2E-4F=0` remove all non-linking coordinates without any weighted-degree argument.

The free-spanning obstruction uses pure braid linking 0,+1,-1 with a_1=0, followed by the source-backed free witness with a_1=1. Its four-row determinant is 2 for every unknown witness linking t. This excludes a_1 from the sum itself.

For the all-k kernel, pure braids first eliminate every distinct-index triple and every degree≤2 linking polynomial. The rational 3/2 witness has `(a_1,a_2,w)=(0,0,1)` and isolates every e_ij after separated insertion. The two labelings of the Nogueira witness have component rows `(1,5)` and `(5,1)` and eliminate each b pair with determinant -24. Pairs (1,j) cover every component for k≥2. This is a universal coefficient proof, not extrapolation from tested small k.

At k=2 the full six-row matrix has a linking block of determinant 2 and a component/plat block of determinant -24, giving -48 independently of all unknown linking and Nogueira plat values. The new checker verifies rational instances; the block argument proves the symbolic identity. The independent torus polynomial multiplication identity gives `Δ_sym=t³-t²+1-t^-²+t^-³` and Conway coefficient 5.

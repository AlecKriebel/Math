## Same-dimensional embedding and global support

The boundary of its tubular disk bundle is therefore the embedded hypersurface T^(d-1) x S^1 = T^d in R^(d+1). The circle base supplies all dimensions.

An explicit parametrization F(p,phi)=(f(p)+epsilon cos(phi)nu(p),epsilon sin(phi)) also verifies injectivity, immersion and a next global unit normal (cos(phi)nu(p),sin(phi)).

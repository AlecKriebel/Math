## Collar torsion, independently recomputed
Let E_i angular (i < n), T = partial_t, all commuting. Put L E_i=A E_i and L T=b+lambda T, with all coefficients depending only on t. Then [A E_i,b+lambda T] = -lambda A' E_i because angular fields differentiate no coefficients. [A E_i,T]=-A' E_i and [E_i,b+lambda T]=0. Therefore N(E_i,T)= -lambda A'E_i + A A'E_i=(A-lambda I)A'E_i. N(E_i,E_j)=0; N(T,T)=0 by skew symmetry. In particular no b' term can appear, since it would require two distinct transverse directions; there is just one. This is necessary and sufficient by tensoriality.

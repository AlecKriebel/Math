Every prescribed numerical real eigenvalue, equality pattern, multiplicity and block size is allowed. The chosen matrix is attained in a commuting local frame. The type, rank and eigenvalues may change elsewhere; global gl-regularity, analytic compact support and a fixed ambient Cartesian matrix are not claimed.

The affirmative mechanism is a same-dimensional torus hypersurface collar and an operator with matrix `[[A(t), b(t)], [0, λ(t)]]`. The torsion condition is the **ordered** identity `(A−λI) A′ = 0`. It permits detaching b, aligning λ with one real block, deleting Jordan arrows first to last, matching the resulting scalar drift to λ, and repeating over all blocks. Smooth endpoint plateaus, an even transverse reparametrization, and exact zero end bands give the compactly supported extension. This transition is materially different from merely citing a local normal form.

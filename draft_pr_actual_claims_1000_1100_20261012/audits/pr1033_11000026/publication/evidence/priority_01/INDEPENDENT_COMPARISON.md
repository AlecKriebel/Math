I independently identify Lenzhen--Rafi's uniform radial inequality (JDG 2011,
Theorems C/17, including the full proof) as the substantive prior theorem.
It already has the required topology-only, all-center, all-radius quantifiers.
For R >= c, move each endpoint inward by min(c, its radial distance). The
new endpoints have radius at most R-c. Their minimizing segment has radius
at most R. The radial pieces and triangle inequality give the exact path
bound d(x,y)+4c. For R<c, the path through the center suffices. This is a
straightforward implicit corollary of the known theorem, requiring no new
Teichmuller geometry, radial Lipschitz bound, or thin-part analysis.

This is a mathematical implication, not evidence that a historical author
explicitly stated it. I have not identified an explicit prior theorem in the
read primary bodies that states the full submitted conclusion or directly
answers Farb 3.5. Therefore I do not classify it as an explicitly documented
prior solution. Nor do I infer novelty from the missing explicit statement.

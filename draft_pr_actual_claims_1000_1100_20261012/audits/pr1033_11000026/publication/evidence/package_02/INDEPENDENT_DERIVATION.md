# Independent mathematical derivation before prior/root verdicts

Target: for each fixed closed oriented surface of genus g>=2, there is c_g>=0 independent of center o, R>=0, and endpoints x,y such that every x,y in the original closed Teichmuller ball B(o,R) admit an actual continuous rectifiable path inside that same ball of length at most d_T(x,y)+4c_g. Convention d_T=(1/2)log of minimal quasiconformal dilatation.

Conditional input to verify against primary literature: for each fixed topology a finite K_g>=1 gives Ext_z(alpha)<=K_g max(Ext_u(alpha),Ext_v(alpha)) for every essential simple closed curve alpha and every z on any Teichmuller geodesic [u,v]. Also exact ratio formula d_T(o,z)=(1/2)log sup_alpha Ext_z(alpha)/Ext_o(alpha), and existence of genuine length-realizing geodesics.

Set c_g=(1/2)log K_g. If d_T(o,u),d_T(o,v)<=r, the ratio formula gives Ext_u(alpha),Ext_v(alpha)<=exp(2r)Ext_o(alpha), so the displayed input implies the supremum ratio for z is <=K_g exp(2r). Consequently [u,v] is contained in the original B(o,r+c_g). This conclusion is uniform in alpha and in o,r,u,v; no change of metric, thick-part assumption, or compactness argument is needed.

If 0<=R<c_g, the concatenation [x,o] then [o,y] is in B(o,R), since a radial point at distance s from o has s<=d(o,x) or d(o,y). Its length is d(o,x)+d(o,y)<=2R<=4c_g<=d(x,y)+4c_g. R=0 means x=y=o and the constant path. If R>=c_g, take the unique point x' of a chosen geodesic [o,x] at distance max(d(o,x)-c_g,0) from o; similarly y'. Write a=d(x,x')=min(c_g,d(o,x)) and b=min(c_g,d(o,y)). Both x',y' are in B(o,R-c_g), so the input gives [x',y'] contained in B(o,R). The radial tails [x,x'] and [y',y] stay in B(o,R). Triangle inequality yields d(x',y')<=a+d(x,y)+b; total length a+d(x',y')+b<=d(x,y)+2(a+b)<=d(x,y)+4c_g.

Each of the three segments has an isometric arclength parameterization on a closed finite interval; concatenate them after translating their parameter intervals, omitting zero-length intervals. Matching endpoints imply continuity at the finitely many joins. Additivity of rectifiable length under interval concatenation gives exactly the sum of the three lengths. For a total length zero use a constant path on [0,1]; otherwise optional affine reparameterization of the total arclength interval gives a path on [0,1] with unchanged length. Thus this is a single continuous rectifiable path and not a chain or a limit of paths. No properness, nearest-point projection, completeness, or infimum attainment for a constrained intrinsic metric is assumed.

Boundary tests: R=c_g is allowed; x'=y'=o when each radial distance<=c_g. K_g=1 gives c_g=0 and direct geodesic containment for every R, including R=0. A source constant below one can be replaced by max(1,K_g). x=o, y=o, x=y, radial distance exactly c_g, and endpoints on the closed boundary cause no discontinuity or strict-inequality failure. Constants depend only on the fixed genus; no numeric or optimal constant is claimed.

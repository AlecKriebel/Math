`d_T(o,z) <= max(d_T(o,a), d_T(o,b)) + c_g`.

Their radii are max(d_T(o,x)-c_g,0) and max(d_T(o,y)-c_g,0), each at most R-c_g. The radial estimate puts the entire middle minimizing segment [a,b] in radius R.

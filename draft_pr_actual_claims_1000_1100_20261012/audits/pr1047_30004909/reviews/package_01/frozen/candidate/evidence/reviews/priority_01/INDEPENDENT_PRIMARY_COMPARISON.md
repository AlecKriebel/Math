## Transfer algebra: scaled and weighted ranks are material leads

If a positive scaling c*r has (c*r)/a <= f <= a*c*r and f(singleton)=1, nonloops and 1/a<=c<=a follow. Consequently r/a^2<=f<=a^2*r. A uniform constant existence theorem for scaled ranks would contradict the candidate ordinary-rank obstruction after squaring its constant. Conversely an ordinary rank is a special scaled rank, so a no-constant obstruction for scaled ranks would imply the candidate. Rates do not transfer unchanged.

For a standard nonnegative weighted matroid rank q(S)=max_{I subset S independent} sum_{i in I}w_i, singleton comparison forces nonloops and w_i in[1/a,a]. Thus r(S)/a<=q(S)<=a*r(S), and the two-sided comparison to f yields ordinary factor a^2. A uniform constant existence theorem for single weighted ranks on normalized inputs would therefore be material. A structural impossibility for single weighted ranks would be stronger than the candidate, hence must not be dismissed from its abstract. Weighted rank SUMS admit a different mechanism; the rational family is exactly in this larger class.

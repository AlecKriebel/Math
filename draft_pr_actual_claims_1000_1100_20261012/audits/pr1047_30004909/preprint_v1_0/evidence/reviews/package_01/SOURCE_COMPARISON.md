## Quantitative scaled and weighted transfer

The note restricts its theorem to one ordinary unscaled same-V rank and makes no dismissal of all weighted or scaled literature. Those larger output classes are mathematically material. Independently, if q=c*r satisfies q/a<=f<=a*q with normalized singleton values, each singleton forces nonloops and 1/a<=c<=a. Therefore r/a^2<=f<=a^2*r. For nonnegative weighted rank q(S)=max_{I subset S independent}sum_{i in I}w_i, the same singleton comparison forces w_i in [1/a,a], hence r(S)/a<=q(S)<=a*r(S), and again the ordinary factor is a^2. Rates must consequently be squared under this symmetric convention.

Under one-sided q<=f<=theta*q, normalization gives 1/theta<=w_i<=1 (or that interval for c). Thus r/theta<=f<=theta*r. A uniform constant theorem for a single scaled or weighted rank on the exact normalized input class would be relevant. These deductions do not assert a prior theorem, exclude unread bodies, or claim the ordinary lower-bound rates unchanged for a larger class. Nonnegative **sums** of ranks and scaled ranks on expanded copy domains are distinct mechanisms; the displayed rational function is already a classical rank sum.

# A characteristic-two counterexample to devissage for local deformation functors

Alec Kriebel, independent researcher; ORCID
[0009-0001-9320-500X](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X).

The five-page draft gives a negative answer to Byszewski's **unrestricted**
fibre-product question. For the weakly ramified additive F4 action and its F2
subgroup, no prorepresentable recipient permits a formally smooth
restriction–induction factorization, regardless of its tangent dimension.

The variant imposing prorepresentability of the subgroup and quotient
deformation functors remains unaddressed. Nilpotent constant coordinate changes
are permitted by the original category. A category fixing the zero section
would be a different question.

The additive action, involution pathology, parameter-shift conjugacy and
tangent-cohomology framework are established prior ingredients. In particular,
the recipient-collapse lemma follows from Byszewski–Cornelissen (2009),
Proposition 6.1; it is not advertised as a new pathology. The assembled
restriction–induction obstruction is the contribution under consideration.

AI tools were used extensively in solving, verifying, auditing sources and
preparing the note. It is unrefereed; the independent adversarial reviews are
AI reviews, with no conventional human peer review or journal acceptance
claimed. The bounded priority search does not certify exhaustive originality.

## Exact source scope

The original [Conforti report, OWR51/2008](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46195), printed pp.2904–2905 (PDF pages30–31), defines precisely the bipartite cover system with arbitrary rational edge demands and any subset of integer coordinates. Its ambient space is unrestricted real space. Problem1 asks membership in the convex hull and the surrounding paragraph discusses linear optimization. The displayed set has no nonnegativity hypothesis. The pages were inspected as full rendered images as well as extracted text.

The source writes the query point as real and leaves an encoding convention implicit. Thus publication should say **“polynomial-time exact membership/separation and optimization for rational data and rational query points in the standard total binary-input model.”** It should not advertise an arbitrary-real-oracle result. This qualification does not reduce graph size, integrality dimension, demand denominators, or permissible signs.

Conjecture2 is a different assertion: equality with an intersection of special subtree hulls. It is not proved by the claimed optimization/separation algorithm. Historical tree tractability and the half-integral hull theorem are credited in the original report, and must remain distinguishable from the general result.

## What is already classical, and what may be the contribution

There is a short independent way to recognize the inherited mechanism. After the bipartite sign change, the feasible relaxation is closed under meet and join. Minimizing a modular objective over continuous completions yields a submodular integer-coordinate value function. The classical mixed proximity theorem bounds relevant coordinate label ranges by O(n), so Birkhoff/threshold encoding produces a polynomial-size ring-family ground set; classical SFM then minimizes the exact LP oracle. The explicit recession/attainment and hull-description size checks allow classical GLS to give exact strong separation.

This explanation is a synthesis/inference from inspected ingredients and the current proof. It is **not** a finding that one earlier paper published the exact target solution. No earliest full-resolution source can responsibly be assigned. Earliest directly checked central proximity credit is Cook et al.1986; the ring-family optimization dependency is Schrijver2000 §6, and the hybrid partial-minimization result is Takamatsu–Hara–Murota2004 Proposition2. Murota2002/2003 documents the n² discrete-convex ring lineage (its cited Iwata1999 antecedent is an oral presentation, so this review does not certify an unpublished theorem body).

The likely publishable unit is the **explicit exact rational application and complete bit-model reduction**, including the LP oracle, quantitative localization, domain interface, and closed-hull separation accounting. Novelty of each generic ingredient is disproved or unsupported. Novelty of this assembly remains a plausible bounded-search conclusion.

# Frozen independent derivation before inherited audit labels

## Model and exact target
A legal firing at v requires x_v >= outgoing degree, counting every parallel arc and any loop. Its net update is incoming adjacency minus outgoing degree on the fired coordinate; loops cancel in the update but remain in the threshold. Reachability permits zero firings. The target subclass is loopless strongly connected finite directed multigraphs, explicit binary adjacency matrix, and nonnegative binary configurations. Dimension varies.

## Independent proof reconstruction
For a loopless source with n>=2 and C total chips, all legal source states belong to the finite nonnegative composition simplex. Its cardinality is at most (C+1)^n=N. A length-N legal walk repeats a state and hence contains a legal directed cycle, which can be repeated forever. An infinite walk has a length-N prefix. The conventional halting dichotomy is still needed to identify existence of such a walk with the source complement, rather than a game-strategy decision.

Scale old transfers by M=N+1. At every pre-gate prefix, H_v=M z_v+N-t_v. Before firing number N, t_v<=N-1. The original legal threshold d_v maps to M d_v+1; legal source vertices have at least M d_v+1 chips. An illegal source vertex has at most M(d_v-1)+N=M d_v-1 chips. Thus the first N old firings are exactly the original legal firings, for every ordering. Gate chips begin nB+1-N and gain one per old firing. The gate cannot fire before N old firings. The marker cannot fire before the gate. These two facts hold independently of any prescribed final count.

With D=max degree and B=N(MD+1), after exactly N simulated firings the old residuals M z_v+N-t_v are nonnegative, so the gate supplies at least B chips to every old vertex. Each needs at most N further firings of cost M d_v+1; B covers this worst case without using future receipts. Completing every old count to N, with gate count one and marker zero, gives Y exactly. Y_v=M x_v+MN(in_v-d_v)+B >= B-MND=N, Y_g=(n-1)N, Y_m=1. The initial gate is nonnegative and graph is loopless and strongly connected.

Conversely, any sequence reaching Y has marker balance k_g-k_m=1, so at least one gate firing, even if final counts differ from the prescribed vector. The prefix before its first firing has at least N old firings, none at the marker; its first N old firings project legally to the original source. The argument needs neither uniqueness of firing counts nor prohibition of subsequent gate/marker firings.

One-vertex source preprocessing must account for degree zero: with ell loops it is nonhalting iff x>=ell, including ell=0 and x=0. Compare binary integers and replace with two-cycle (1,0) or (0,0). For each loop packet, a relay initially zero preserves the threshold at the old vertex, delays only its own loop returns, and can fire at most as often as its parent. Lift each original firing with immediate relay return; project any expanded firing sequence by returning pending loop packets immediately. The original projected state differs by nonnegative pending packets at their respective parents, so all projected old firings are legal. Infinite expanded play implies infinite projected old firings. Binary expansion has at most twice as many vertices.

Signed normalization adds k_v=max(0,-x_v) loops and k_v chips, keeping the net Laplacian unchanged and adding k_v to both current count and threshold at v at every step. It preserves the entire legal sequence language. This is an optional interface, not an assertion that loop deletion without relays preserves legality.

N may be exponentially large numerically but log N=n log(C+1), log M=O(log N), log B=O(log N+log M+log D). All matrix entries and target coordinates have polynomial bit length. Integer exponentiation and n^2 adjacency operations give a polynomial Karp map. A formal language reduction should parse malformed/non-strongly-connected/negative source strings and send them to a fixed NO reachability instance; the source hardness restriction itself suffices on promised valid encodings.

## Independent coNP certificate route to authenticate
For strongly connected loopless n>=2, kernel over integers is generated by the positive primitive period p. If y-x is not in the integer image of L, image nonmembership can be certified through polynomial-time integer linear algebra (Smith or Hermite form). Otherwise select the nonnegative reduced solution g, with at least one coordinate below p: subtract the minimum floor(h_v/p_v) from any integer solution. Both p and g have polynomial bit length by determinant bounds and polynomial integer linear algebra. The cited reduced-firing cycle-removal theorem must show x->y implies a legal realization of g, not just of g+kp.

Assuming that exact theorem, a succinct negative certificate is any integer u with 0<=u<=g, u!=g, such that z=x+Lu has z_v<d_v for all v with u_v<g_v. No legality witness for u is necessary: in a purported legal firing sequence with count g, consider the first firing exceeding u. At v, own count then equals u_v and all other counts are <=u; incoming nonnegative entries imply current chips <=z_v<d_v, contradiction. Conversely a maximal greedy g-bounded legal execution provides such a u when no g execution exists. Need authenticate the source theorem and source encoding, rather than assume this removal step. The n=1 loopless case is directly decidable by x=y.

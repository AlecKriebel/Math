The theorem is a deterministic Karp coNP-completeness theorem for sequential conservative legal reachability on strongly connected, loopless, binary-adjacency directed multigraphs and nonnegative binary configurations. Invalid strings are outside the target language. General signed or simple/unary variants are different conclusions.

1. Conservation supplies at most (C+1)^n source states. N legal moves force a repeated state, whose closed word is repeatable indefinitely. The standard halting dichotomy then excludes termination. This is a finite witness cutoff, never a polynomial trajectory bound.
2. Before any gate, marker count is zero and h_v=M z_v+N-t_v. For each of the first N old moves its offset lies in [1,N], whereas M=N+1. Source legal implies h_v at least Md_v+1; source illegal implies at most Md_v-1. This is exact for every prefix/order; a projected illegal state is never assumed valid in deriving the induction.
3. Gate count starts nB+1-N; before its first firing every received chip is from an old move. Earliest possible gate is after N such moves. Marker endpoint 1 forces at least one gate in ANY target word, since k_g-k_m=1. Therefore arbitrary extra periods and late gates cannot create false positives; the first N old moves alone certify nonhalting.
4. A nonhalting source supplies N legal old moves, then one gate. Every old coordinate receives B, paying all its remaining at most N copies of cost Md_v+1 regardless of cleanup order. Counts N at all old vertices, gate 1, marker 0 give Y explicitly. Gate and marker may remain legal; ordinary reachability does not require a stable endpoint.
5. Y_v >= B-MND=N, X_g>=1, both totals MC+(n-1)N+nB+1. H is loopless strongly connected by its bidirectional gate links. N, M, B and all output entries have polynomial bits even though their numerical magnitudes and the witnessing word may be exponential.
6. Signed normalization adds equally to chips and loop-dependent firing thresholds, with net Laplacian unchanged: it conjugates every finite legal word. Loop replacement delays only nonnegative own returns; each relay count is bounded by its parent's count. Projection of any expanded word is legal in the original, while immediate relay return lifts every original word. Infinite expanded words necessarily have infinitely many old moves. The singleton zero-loop case really has an endlessly legal zero-cost firing and must be classified as nonhalting even with zero chips.
7. A positive primitive period p spans the integer kernel. Given an integer image solution h, subtract min floor(h_v/p_v)*p to obtain the unique reduced nonnegative g. Every nonnegative solution is g+kp, k>=0. Cofactor/minor bounds give polynomial bit length, independent of word length. Delete the first p_v occurrences at each vertex from a word with counts >=p. At a retained v firing, all p_v own deletions have occurred and other deleted counts <=p, hence (Lr)_v<=0. Repeated deletions yield count g.
8. Negative certificate u is only an integer vector 0<=u<=g, u!=g, with all still-required vertices below their firing thresholds in x+Lu. It need not itself be legal. The first exceeding coordinate in a hypothetical legal g-word has own preceding count u_v and all other counts <=u, so it has at most (x+Lu)_v chips, a contradiction. A maximal g-bounded legal word supplies u whenever g is unreachable. Looplessness makes the sign comparison immediate. n=1 is a separate equality test.
9. Source hardness, dichotomy, period machinery and coNP membership are prior results, not new discoveries. Tothmeresz's recurrence conversion is already deterministic; its existential recurrent representative cannot silently become a deterministic Karp output.

# Result: unresolved after five bounded routes

The full pointwise upper-bound question is not solved or disproved by this packet. Five distinct substantive routes were completed, and proof search stopped without a full candidate. No originality claim is made for the partial observations.

## Exact findings

1. Stability gives the ball mass bound and a liminf upper bound. A uniform thickness estimate for potential superlevel sets would give the desired pointwise estimate. The radial upper bound is established prior work by Villegas.
2. An explicit smooth-off-origin potential has a strict stability margin, two-sided critical mass at every scale, and all subcritical L^q bounds, while |x|^2V diverges. This is an obstruction to a stability-only implication, not a semilinear extremal counterexample.
3. Smooth positive increasing convex nonlinearities can have negative f''' or unbounded f''f/(f')^2. Consequently, a differential/Harnack proof cannot silently assume the signs or coefficient bounds it needs.
4. For n>=10, w(x)=-log(1+|x|^2) is an entire stable solution for G(t)=2(n-2)exp(t)+4exp(2t). It remains a valid nonconstant example after normalization g(0)=g'(0)=1, with g'(v)<=1. Thus a blanket high-dimensional stable-entire Liouville step is false. The example does not have the bounded-domain extremal boundary conditions.
5. Prescribing a stable potential does not enforce one common semilinearity or the extremal branch. Smooth separated exponential angular profiles already satisfy the target, while singular angular profiles violate isolation. The exact cylindrical equation and stability form expose the remaining construction constraints.

## Remaining gap

For arbitrary source-admissible f and domains, one must prove enough spatial nonconcentration of lambda* f'(u*) near the isolated singularity, or construct a compatible positive zero-Dirichlet extremal solution violating it. The five routes do neither.

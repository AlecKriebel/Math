## Exact source and theorem

- Original target: Vera Serganova’s contribution “Green correspondence for supergroups” to [OWR 52/2021](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46930), printed pp. 2840–2841, PDF pages 14–15. The target is the containment conjecture following Theorem 1.
- Resolution: Serganova–Sherman–Vaintrob, [Sylow theorems for supergroups, arXiv:2404.11077v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.11077v1). The [certificate](HYPOTHESIS_CERTIFICATE.md) gives exact definitions, theorem locators, the standard block subgroup/global-form calculation, the inside-K minimality argument, and connectedness checks.
- Group-level corroboration: Entova-Aizenbud–Serganova–Sherman, [Towards the Green correspondence for supergroups, arXiv:2507.09912v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2507.09912v1#page=13), section 4.1, printed/PDF p. 13.
- Historical source: Serganova–Sherman, [arXiv:2206.07693v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.07693v2), 13 July 2023. Its then-open GL minimality/conjugacy qualification predates the 2024 theorem.

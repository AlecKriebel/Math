Only references already identified by the recovery assignment were checked. This was source verification for the reconstructed findings, not a new research direction or a comprehensive search for current progress on O7b. No third-party source text is included in this packet.

## Inspected primary material

1. Gregory D. Kahanamoku-Meyer, Seyoon Ragavan, Vinod Vaikuntanathan, and Katherine Van Kirk, *The Jacobi Factoring Circuit: Quantum Factoring with Near-Linear Gates and Sublinear Space and Depth*, arXiv:2412.12558v4. The versioned HTML was inspected, including Section 5, Definition 5.1, Theorem 5.3, and its proof. It supports the distinct-exponent attribution in the turn-1 report. The relevant input restriction does not include squarefree composites. [Versioned article](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.12558v4).

2. Daniel J. Bernstein, *Factoring into coprimes in essentially linear time*, Journal of Algorithms 54(1), 1–30 (2005), DOI 10.1016/j.jalgor.2004.04.009. The author's institutional research record and abstract were inspected. They identify the earlier quadratic algorithm of Bach, Driscoll, and Shallit and the later near-linear coprime-basis algorithm. The recovery report proves its own elementary polynomial refinement bound rather than relying on an uninspected implementation. [Institutional record](https://research.tue.nl/en/publications/factoring-into-coprimes-in-essentially-linear-time/).

3. Eric Bach, James Driscoll, and Jeffrey Shallit, *Factor refinement*, Journal of Algorithms 15 (1993), 199–222. Bibliographic data were checked on Shallit's own publication list. The full article was not inspected. [Author's publication list](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~shallit/papers.html).

# Scoped acceptance report

Problem 30005233 / OWR-11101922-005. 10 October 2026 (UTC).

## Accepted result

The proof of Theorem 1.1 of Bérard–Dembin–Marêché, *Sharpness for monotone absorbing Interacting Particle Systems*, arXiv:2510.11424v2 (24 November 2025), is independently accepted **with explicit local repairs**, relative to the specified published Poisson OSSS inequality of Last–Peccati–Yogeshwaran and standard probability/analysis foundations.

The accepted model is an absorbing, attractive, finite-range, translation-invariant binary IPS with single-site updates and finite rates. If the occupied-origin probability from all one tends to zero for L, it decays exponentially for L+εD for every ε>0. Constants may depend on ε. The exact generator identity and time change also prove the strict-phase upper-density dichotomy along G_p=pG_1+(1-p)D.

This audit verifies the graphical construction, localization, finite-box limits, Russo derivative, absolute point-influence/mark-pivotality comparison, every published continuous-OSSS hypothesis, revealment estimate, differential inequality, analytic lemma and parameter conversion. A complete all-finite-configuration CTDT proof is supplied as a companion to the full integrated audit.

The publisher's publicly accessible page establishes publication but did not expose the full article text in this check.

This is mathematical audit acceptance, not journal acceptance or machine formalization. It does not close general finite-seed survival sharpness, arbitrary parameter curves, critical behavior, nonabsorbing sharpness, Corollary 1.2's topological assertions or the broader cleaned record.

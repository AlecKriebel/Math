# Additive clarifications after independent review

These notes clarify wording in the frozen author files. All original 24 files remain byte-identical; no proof-attempt count or mathematical conclusion changes.

1. **Asphericity citation.** Pierre Deligne, *Les immeubles des groupes de tresses généralisés*, Invent. Math. 17 (1972), 273–302, proves the required asphericity for complexified simplicial real arrangements. Finite real reflection arrangements, including noncrystallographic H4, satisfy that hypothesis. Primary source: https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/Number18.pdf . Selecting one hyperplane gives M(A) homeomorphic to C* times its decone; the latter is the projective complement and is therefore also aspherical. Its group is P/Z(P).
2. **Finite models.** The phrase “finite aspherical complexes” in turn 1 should be read as “aspherical complements having finite CW homotopy type,” hence finite aspherical CW models. The noncompact complements are not literally finite cell complexes in their natural topology.
3. **Transfer direction.** For a finite-index subgroup U of K, the exact identity with trivial rational coefficients is cor_U^K composed with res_U^K = [K:U] id on H^1(K; Q). It follows that restriction is injective. This is the identity used in turns 2 and 3.

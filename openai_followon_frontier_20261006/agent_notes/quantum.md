## Recommendation within this domain: the Coulomb Laughlin phase

**Exact initial target.** At fermionic filling 1/3 on expanding round spheres with Q=3(N-1), fixed magnetic length and fixed physical Coulomb energy units, prove a uniform spectral gap along a specified interpolation

H_N(s)=(1-s)c H_V1,N+s P_LLL(sum_{i<j}1/|x_i-x_j| + neutralizing-background energy)P_LLL, 0<=s<=1.

Here c>0 is fixed independently of N and chosen to match a declared pseudopotential normalization. The background on a round sphere contributes constants within each fixed-N sector; retain it for thermodynamic energy normalization. The intended inequality is inf_{N>=N0,s in[0,1]}(E1(N,s)-E0(N,s))>0 with a unique ground state throughout. A different explicitly specified physically natural path is acceptable if the straight interpolation fails. This is a finite-N Hamiltonian theorem uniform in the thermodynamic limit, not an assertion about realistic Landau-level mixing or arbitrary disorder.

**The exact obstruction.** Its proven perturbation is a weak bounded scalar one-body potential. Coulomb is a long-range two-body perturbation of order one, and the stability norm demands a sufficiently large polynomial decay exponent ell. Unscreened 1/r in 2D does not meet this norm. Ordinary small-perturbation stability cannot be iterated to s=1 without controlling shrinking gaps and changing local estimates. It is invalid to say the existing stability theorem proves Coulomb. Neutrality, screening and multipole cancellations are the candidate new mechanism; establishing these uniformly on low-energy states is central, not a technical appendix. A proof that simply assumes screening or assumes a gap throughout its spectral flow is circular.

**Research path and first falsifiable gate.** First derive a quantitative, N-uniform stability theorem for a genuine open set of rapidly decaying two-body pseudopotential perturbations around V1, using the companion's neutral-operator relative bound; give an explicit norm and explicit nonzero interval. Independently analyze the long-distance Coulomb remainder on neutral low-energy fluctuations and ask whether cancellations yield a summable relative-energy estimate. A finite-volume norm that grows with N, a bound paid only by extensive ground-state energy, or an assumed uniform continuation gap fails the gate. Certified finite-size spectra can falsify a proposed path but cannot prove the thermodynamic lower bound alone.

## Quantum-information runner-up: exact thermal-loss quantum capacity

**Precise target.** Determine for all transmissivity eta, positive bath photon number nu, and input energy N_S the unassisted capacity

Q(Phi_eta,nu,N_S)=lim_{n->infty}(1/n) sup_{Tr rho sum n_j<=nN_S} [S(B^n)-S(E'^n F^n)],

where F purifies the thermal bath E and E' is the complementary beam-splitter output. Arbitrary non-Gaussian inputs entangled across uses must be allowed. This would rate roughly9.3–9.7 in quantum Shannon theory. Exact full dynamic/private capacity is a larger separate program, not an assumed consequence.

**New leverage and obstruction.** Source #273 proves a sharp unconditional output entropy inequality for independent beam-splitter inputs, including arbitrary entanglement within either multimode input. The published local first-batch manuscript `openai_followon_bosonic_capacity/manuscript/main.tex` correctly uses vacuum complement/degradation to close pure-loss resource regions. Thermal noise changes the complementary system to E'F. EPnI controls S(E'), not S(E'F), and ordinary weak degradability is not degradability to that purification. The remaining obstacle is a genuinely new joint-entropy / regularized coherent-information inequality compatible with non-Gaussian optimizers. No sharp theorem of that kind was located in the new source; therefore this has weaker actionable leverage than the Coulomb route.

**Crucial current falsification result.** Mele–Catalano–Fanizza–Giovannetti–Lami, *Bosonic quantum communication beyond the thermal threshold*, submitted29July2026, https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.27449. Its abstract explicitly proves that the known thermal-input bound is the exact single-mode Gaussian supremum and then constructs a non-Gaussian input beating it. At eta=.8, nu=1 all single-mode Gaussian inputs have nonpositive coherent information, while a rank-two input on only six Fock levels has certified coherent information >=4.7e-4 qubits/use. They certify positivity down to eta=.7841, while eta<=.75 is antidegradable. Thus “EPnI proves thermal Gaussian coherent information is the capacity” is false, not just unproved.

Earlier independent warning: Noh–Pirandola–Jiang, *Enhanced energy-constrained quantum communication over bosonic Gaussian channels*, https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-020-14329-6, proves multimode Gaussian achievable improvements under an energy budget. Existing upper-bound framework: Rosati–Mari–Giovannetti, https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.04731. Conditional entropy-power inequality already exists and is asymptotically sharp at fixed conditional entropies: De Palma–Trevisan, https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.00440. A naive stronger photon-number formula with quantum conditioning is not justified, and g^{-1} cannot even take arbitrary negative conditional entropy.

**First gate if chosen.** Reproduce the certified six-Fock-level witness and compare every candidate converse to it before any optimization theorem is promoted. Identify exactly which joint entropy term the new inequality bounds. Derive a nontrivial strict improvement to a current energy-constrained converse on a parameter rectangle while retaining that witness. A reduction to an equally-hard regularized unknown with no usable bound fails the gate.

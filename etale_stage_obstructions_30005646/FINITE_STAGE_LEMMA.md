# Finite stage-3 matching lemma and limitations

Let K=k(C), with k a finite extension of Q_p and C smooth projective geometrically integral. Use closed-point valuations of C. Let E_3 -> E_2 be a continuous G_K-equivariant Postnikov fibration whose geometric fibre is K(B,3), where B is a finite discrete continuous G_K-module. Assume the action and twisting have been fixed so this is an abelian local coefficient system over the chosen section. Write S_i(F)=Map_{BG_F}(BG_F,(E_i)_{hG_F}) for the section space.

Fix an actual global section s in S_2(K), not just its component. The homotopy fibre L_s of S_3(K)->S_2(K) over s is the space of lifts WITH a chosen homotopy from their projection to s. The obstruction to nonemptiness is s^*(k_3) in H^4(K,B_s). Since cd(K)=3 and B_s is finite discrete torsion, it vanishes. After a chosen lift t, pi_0(L_s) is a torsor for H^3(K,B_s). The same holds over every K_v, since cd(K_v)=3. The twists B_s may be suppressed for geometrically simply connected E_2 with its fixed Galois module, but not in arbitrary non-simply-connected models.

Suppose prescribed local section components z_v in pi_0 S_3(K_v) project to the components of s_v. Pick projection homotopies p_v; these rigidify z_v as points of L_{s_v}. Relative to t_v their differences define delta_v in H^3(K_v,B_s). With these choices fixed, a global rigidified lift realizing them exists exactly when (delta_v)_v lies in the image of H^3(K,B_s)->product_v H^3(K_v,B_s).

This is NOT yet the test for unrigidified components. The group pi_1(S_2(K_v),s_v) acts by monodromy on pi_0(L_{s_v}). All possible p_v must be allowed; consequently one must ask whether the product of the local monodromy orbits meets the localization image of the global lift torsor. In a based split E_2=K(A,2) model, pi_1 S_2(F)=H^1(F,A). Its action on the H^3 lift torsor is induced by the k-invariant and must be calculated, not assumed zero. In general use the actual pi_1 of the section space. Quotienting the source by its global monodromy action only identifies global representatives; it does not replace the local orbit test.

Finally an adelic point surviving stage 2 can have MANY matching global s components. Nonexistence of a compatible stage-3 lift must hold for EVERY such component. Failure over a single chosen s is insufficient. Conversely failure already at any correctly constructed finite Postnikov quotient proves failure for the full homotopy type by functoriality.

Proof of assertions: Pull back the K(B,3)-fibration along s. Its class is the indicated degree-4 obstruction. If null, choices of nullhomotopy form the indicated degree-3 cohomology torsor. Localization is functorial. A fibration's long exact homotopy sequence identifies the unrigidified fibre on components as monodromy orbits of the rigidified fibre. This proves the stated image/orbit criterion without a spurious linearization.

## Exact limitations

1. This is a finite-coefficient statement. Finite quotients of pi_3 do not automatically form a compatible quotient Postnikov tower unless their k-invariants and transition maps are constructed.
2. For a countable tower of FINITE G_K-modules B_m, the standard derived-limit exact sequence is 0 -> lim^1 H^3(K,B_m) -> H^4_cont(K,lim B_m) -> lim H^4(K,B_m) -> 0. In this precise setting BOTH outer terms vanish. Indeed H^3 carries a surjection of finite modules to a surjection, by H^4(kernel)=0. For arbitrary transitions, the images I_nm=im(B_n->B_m) stabilize for fixed m, and H^3(B_n)->H^3(I_nm) is surjective. Hence the images of H^3(B_n) in H^3(B_m) stabilize too, giving Mittag-Leffler and lim^1=0. Thus this tower is NOT a source of a nonzero global H^4 obstruction.
3. The groups H^3(K,B_m) need not themselves be finite; the preceding argument, not their cardinality, proves Mittag-Leffler. Other derived-limit/model issues remain: e.g. lim^1 H^2 can occur in degree-3 continuous cohomology, and compatibility of local matching conditions and paths is a separate question. The vanishing above is asserted under its precise countable finite-module and continuous-cohomology hypotheses, not for arbitrary pro-objects without a justified comparison.
4. For actual adelic points one must impose appropriate restricted-product/unramified conditions. The raw product formulation above is a formal local-matching statement, not a claim of an arithmetic duality theorem for unrestricted products.
5. The lemma does not construct an algebraic X or a local point producing delta_v. The point-evaluation image can be much smaller than the abstract local section space.
6. A conclusion that the full obstruction is exhausted by stage 3 needs the relevant profinite comparison/convergence theorem. It does not follow solely from the finite-module calculation. For proving strictness, a genuine finite stage-3 failure suffices and avoids needing that converse.

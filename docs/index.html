Quantum graph theory · Finite-dimensional projector colorings

The Quantum Chromatic Number of the G19 Join Family

Resolves Lalonde’s proposed finite-witness family in the standard finite-dimensional model, allowing arbitrary finite projector rank. The first new unrestricted case is the one-apex graph \(G_{19}\vee K_1\); no commuting-operator extension is claimed.

Quantum information · Fixed-qubit Bell behaviors

Minimum Bell-Setting Complexity for Qubit POVM–PVM Separation

Proves equality of the shared-randomness-convexified fixed-qubit behavior sets when each party has two inputs, then gives an exact rational \(3\times2\) separation and minimum-setting classification. The qualitative \(3\times2\) separation is prior art; no raw-set, same-state, or operator-level POVM/PVM equality is claimed.

Quantum information · Cyclic Bell inequalities

Exact Quantum Values and Permutation-Blind Maximizers in Cyclic Bell Inequalities

Determines exact all-dimensional values, including the commuting-operator strengthening, and identifies phase-permutation freedom producing nonuniform exact maximizers. The result limits what the maximum scalar Bell value alone certifies; it does not contradict randomness conclusions conditioned on the full canonical behavior.

Stochastic reaction networks · Positive recurrence

Positive Recurrence for Single-Linkage Bimolecular Weakly Reversible Stochastic Reaction Networks

Proves that weak reversibility makes every population reachability set a closed communicating class. Under the additional one-linkage and bimolecular hypotheses, each nonabsorbing reachable class is positive recurrent for every positive rate vector. This removes the prior pure-species-complex condition; multiple linkage classes and higher molecularity remain open for recurrence.

Unrefereed manuscript · Version 1.2.3 · 57 tests, four deterministic PDFs, complete manifest and replay

Manuscript Overview Source & Verification

Evolutionary graph theory · Simultaneous amplification

Simultaneous Amplification Beyond Fitness Three Halves

Constructs one fitness-independent graph family that simultaneously amplifies Bd and dB fixation for every fixed fitness below an exact algebraic threshold \(1.5028569\ldots>3/2\). This is a strict lower-bound improvement; the unrestricted exact value of \(R_{\mathrm{sim}}\) remains open.

Unrefereed manuscript · Version 1.0.0 · Exact asymptotics and certificate package

Reaction-network dynamics · Reversible mass action

A Four-Parameter Family of Reversible Three-Species Mass-Action Continua Without a Common Factor

Determines a four-parameter positive rate family on one fixed reversible graph with a common compact positive equilibrium ellipse, generic geometric coprimality, and exact rate-cone and stability information. It is a fine-tuned family, not a universal realization theorem.

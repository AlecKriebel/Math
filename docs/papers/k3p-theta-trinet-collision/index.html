Abstract

The K3P analogue of the high-level trinet inequality fails already at strict level two: a binary semi-directed strict level-two theta trinet with a genuine nontrivial 3-blob has an exact interior K3P collision with a three-leaf tree. At the explicit witness, in the quartic number field \(\mathbb{Q}(h)\) with \(h=5^{-1/4}\), every network and tree edge lies strictly inside the stochastic parameter space \(\Theta_0\), every transition probability is strictly positive, and both inheritance probabilities equal \(1/2\). A sixteen-entry Fourier factorization gives equality of all sixty-four Fourier coordinates and hence all sixty-four leaf-pattern probabilities. A specified \(15\times15\) Jacobian minor has nonzero determinant, so the fixed theta-trinet image is locally full-dimensional in the 15-dimensional affine group-based Fourier space \(q_{AAA}=1\). A real-analytic implicit-function argument, with its Jacobian and tangent data verified exactly, further moves the realization into the strict continuous-time K3P cone where every substitution rate on every edge is positive, while preserving the same tree distribution.

1. Context and main theorem

Brits, Holtgrefe, van Iersel, and Martin proved the level-one K3P trinet inequality, proved arbitrary-level trinet inequalities for JC and K2P, and used those results to obtain a global JC/K2P tree–network distinguishability corollary. They asked whether the high-level trinet inequality and quartet distinguishability extend to K3P, noting that the K3P level-one proof needs several invariants and does not directly support the one-invariant induction used for JC and K2P.

Theorem (exact K3P tree–theta collision). There is a binary semi-directed strict level-two trinet \(N_\theta\) with a nontrivial 3-blob and a three-leaf tree \(T\) such that

\[ \mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{K3P}}(N_\theta;\Theta_0)\;\cap\;\mathcal{M}_{\mathrm{K3P}}(T;\Theta_0) \]

contains a point that is interior to \(\Theta_0\) for every edge of both models.

This answers the high-level K3P trinet question negatively at strict level two and rules out a direct K3P extension of the JC/K2P distinguishability corollary on the same parameter space. The JC and K2P results of the source paper are unaffected.

2. Topology, witness, and strengthenings

The displayed-tree calculation uses a rooted binary network with two reticulations \(r_2,r_3\); suppressing its degree-two root composes the two root-adjacent edges into an effective pendant edge \(K\odot K=(1,\tfrac14,\tfrac14,\tfrac14)\). The remaining blob is a theta graph with three internally disjoint paths between the two theta cut vertices — a maximal nontrivial blob with three incident leaf components and two reticulations, i.e. a strict level-two nontrivial 3-blob.

Let \(h=5^{-1/4}\), the positive root of \(5h^4=1\) isolated by \(2/3<h<7/10\). All exact calculations take place in \(\mathbb{Q}(h)\), represented in the basis \(1,h,h^2,h^3\). The rooted edge vectors \(K,U,V,S,T\) and the comparison-tree vectors are recorded exactly in certificate.json; for consistent Fourier labels \((x,y,z)\) with \(x=y+z\), the core displayed-tree mixture factors exactly as

\[ M_{y,z}=P_{y+z}\,B_y\,B_z \]

for all sixteen pairs, which extends to equality of all sixty-four Fourier coordinates and all sixty-four leaf-pattern probabilities with the stated three-leaf tree.

Differentiating the fifteen nonconstant consistent Fourier coordinates against the fifteen free edge and inheritance parameters gives, by exact Gaussian elimination in the quartic field,

\[ \det J_*=\frac{h(10h^2+1)}{2^{61}\,3^4\,5^{14}}>0, \]

so the fixed theta-trinet map has full rank at the collision and its image contains an ordinary open neighborhood of the common distribution in the 15-dimensional affine Fourier space \(q_{AAA}=1\). The explicit witness lies on two boundary faces \(U_C=U_GU_T\), \(V_G=V_CV_T\) of the strict positive-rate continuous-time K3P cone, while remaining strictly interior to \(\Theta_0\); a real-analytic implicit-function argument, using the verified Jacobian, exact fixed-output tangent identity, and positive margin derivatives \(\tfrac{10h^2-1}{1+10h^2}>0\) and \(1>0\), produces a nearby theta-network realization of the same tree distribution with every edge in the strict positive-rate cone.

4. Scope, priority, and limitations

The result settles the high-level K3P trinet extension negatively and blocks the corresponding direct K3P local-to-global argument at strict level two. It does not settle K3P quartet distinguishability, classify every tree/theta intersection, establish generic tree-equivalence of network parameters, refute every possible level-two K3P identifiability theorem, or classify arbitrary network observational equivalence. Full-rank local overlap is openness in distribution space; it is not a claim that a generic point of the larger network parameter space maps to a tree distribution.

A narrow post-discovery literature search found the source paper on tree–network distinguishability, prior K3P 3-sunlet work, and general group-based phylogenetic-invariant literature, but no exact match for this topology, quartic relation, factorization, or full-rank collision. This is not an exhaustive historical search, so the paper makes no absolute priority claim and requests an expert literature and terminology audit, particularly of the semi-directed blob conventions used here.

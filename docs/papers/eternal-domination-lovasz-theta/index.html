Abstract

The proposed inequality \(\gamma^\infty(G)\geq\vartheta(G)\) is false for the standard one-guard eternal domination number.

For the ten-vertex graph with graph6 record IEhbtj{ro, an exact rational feasible matrix for the standard Lovász-theta semidefinite program has objective \(7593/2500=3.0372\), while the eternal domination number is exactly three.

Main theorem

Theorem. Let \(G\) have graph6 record IEhbtj{ro. Then

\[ \gamma^\infty(G)=3 <\frac{7593}{2500} \leq\vartheta(G). \]

Therefore the eternal domination number is not universally bounded below by the Lovász theta function.

The graph itself and the equality \(\gamma^\infty(G)=3\) were published by MacGillivray, Mynhardt, and Virgile in 2022 in their work on a different parameter: clique-cover number. The new observation is the exact Lovász-theta separation.

Minimum order

MacGillivray, Mynhardt, and Virgile computationally verified that every graph on at most nine vertices satisfies \(\gamma^\infty(G)=\operatorname{cc}(G)\). The theta sandwich inequality gives

\[ \vartheta(G)\leq\chi(\overline G)=\operatorname{cc}(G). \]

Consequently no graph of order at most nine can violate the proposed inequality, and this ten-vertex graph has minimum possible order. This corollary relies on their published exhaustive computation; the local package does not re-enumerate every smaller graph.

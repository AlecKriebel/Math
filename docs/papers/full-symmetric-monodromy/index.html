Abstract

Gallagher's weighted-lift construction produces a three-dimensional Keller map of every generic degree n ≥ 3. This note isolates a simple uniform subfamily and claims that the Galois closure of its function-field extension has full symmetric group Sn. It also gives a uniform rational collision and claims the maps have no nonidentity rational deck transformations.

FamilyOne explicit map for every n ≥ 3
Claimed monodromyFull symmetric group Sn
CertificateUniform rational two-point collision

Scope and priority

This is a structural refinement of the newly posted Alpoge-Gallagher examples, not an independent construction of the underlying Jacobian counterexamples. The narrow residual contribution is the elementary subfamily, its uniform collision, and the claimed all-degree Sn monodromy with trivial rational deck group. No priority is asserted beyond the timestamped public record; literature and expert review may change this assessment.

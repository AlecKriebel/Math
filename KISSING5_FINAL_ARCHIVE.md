# Math-kissing5 final research archive

Date: 2026-08-20

This branch is the durable closeout of the former `Math-kissing5` worktree.
It preserves human-readable mathematics and the source needed to reproduce
the local computational investigations while deliberately excluding bulky,
regenerable products.

## Preserved

- mathematical notes, proof drafts, research logs, and hostile reviews;
- Python, C++, PARI/GP, and shell derivation or verification programs;
- compact JSON metadata, manifests, and result summaries;
- the seven previously modified Werner-NPT source and note files.

## Omitted as regenerable

- virtual environments, Python caches, temporary trees, downloaded tool
  builds, and compiled helper binaries;
- numerical `.pkl` and `.npz` checkpoints;
- CNF, DRAT, LRAT, BDRAT, solver-model, transcript, lock, and LaTeX auxiliary
  files;
- ignored matrix caches and private replay working directories.

The gamma-theta generators and verifier sources retain the production logic;
compact outcome JSON records retain run parameters and hashes. The Werner-NPT
source programs and notes retain the numerical algorithms, parameters,
reported observations, and selected checkpoint hashes. These omissions save
space without changing any mathematical claim.

Archival handoff completion: **100%**. The individual research programs retain
the incomplete or discovery-only status stated in their own research logs.

\section{Global all-fitness slices at small order}\label{sec:small}

Local curvature at $r=2$ does not imply global maximality.  At order three,
however, the full all-fitness comparison admits an exact centered
sum-of-squares certificate.

Let an undirected triangle have positive edge weights $(a,b,c)$.  If $F_i$
is fixation when $i$ is the unique mutant and $G_i$ when $i$ is the unique
resident, deleting holding probabilities gives six linear equations.  For
distinct $i,j,k$, put
\[
 u_{ij}=\frac{rw_{ij}}{rw_{ij}+w_{jk}},\qquad
 v_{ij}=\frac{w_{ij}}{rw_{jk}+w_{ij}}.                       \tag{6.1}\label{6.1}
\]
Then
\[
 (1+u_{ij}+u_{ik})F_i=u_{ij}G_k+u_{ik}G_j,                   \tag{6.2}\label{6.2}
\]
\[
 (1+v_{ij}+v_{ik})G_i=1+v_{ij}F_k+v_{ik}F_j.                 \tag{6.3}\label{6.3}
\]
The coefficient matrix is a strictly diagonally dominant $M$-matrix, so its
determinant is positive.

Put $s_1=a+b+c$, $s_2=ab+ac+bc$, and $s_3=abc$, and define
\[
\begin{aligned}
 A&=3s_3(s_1s_2-9s_3),\\
 D&=12s_1^3s_3-45s_1s_2s_3+4s_2^3-27s_3^2,\\
 E&=4s_2(3s_1^2s_2-3s_1s_3-8s_2^2),\\
 \mathcal H_r&=A(r-1)^4+Dr(r-1)^2+Er^2.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{6.4}\label{6.4}
\]
Exact elimination of \eqref{6.2}--\eqref{6.3} gives
\[
 \rho_{\dB}(W,r)-\rho_{\dB}(J_3,r)
 =-\frac{r(r-1)\mathcal H_r}{3(r+1)\mathcal P_r},           \tag{6.5}\label{6.5}
\]
where $\mathcal P_r>0$ is the cleared determinant.
More explicitly, if $M$ is the coefficient matrix of
\eqref{6.2}--\eqref{6.3} and
\[
 L=\prod_{\substack{p,q\in\{a,b,c\}\\p\ne q}}(rp+q)>0,
\]
then $\mathcal P_r=L\det(M)/3>0$.

To sign the numerator, set
\[
 U=s_1^2-3s_2,\quad V=s_2^2-3s_1s_3,\quad
 W=s_1s_2-9s_3,\quad Z=s_2^3-27s_3^2.                       \tag{6.6}\label{6.6}
\]
Writing $X=ab$, $Y=ac$, $Z_0=bc$ gives
\[
\begin{aligned}
2U&=(a-b)^2+(a-c)^2+(b-c)^2,\\
2V&=(X-Y)^2+(X-Z_0)^2+(Y-Z_0)^2,\\
W&=c(a-b)^2+b(a-c)^2+a(b-c)^2,\\
Z&=s_2V+3\{Z_0(X-Y)^2+Y(X-Z_0)^2+X(Y-Z_0)^2\}.
\end{aligned}                                                \tag{6.7}\label{6.7}
\]
The coefficient identities
\[
 A=3s_3W,\qquad D=12s_1s_3U+3s_2V+Z,\qquad
 E=4s_2(3s_2U+V)                                           \tag{6.8}\label{6.8}
\]
show that $A,D,E\ge0$.  If the weights are nonuniform then $U>0$, hence
$E>0$ and $\mathcal H_r>0$ for $r>1$.  Equation \eqref{6.5} proves
Theorem~\ref{thm:triangle}, including its equality class.

Appendix~\ref{app:k4} gives analogous exact certificates for the $1+3$ and
$2+2$ invariant positive weighted $K_4$ families.  Those families are
included because they test the first genuinely higher-dimensional subset
chains while retaining exact orbit reductions.  We make no claim that they
classify all weighted four-vertex graphs.

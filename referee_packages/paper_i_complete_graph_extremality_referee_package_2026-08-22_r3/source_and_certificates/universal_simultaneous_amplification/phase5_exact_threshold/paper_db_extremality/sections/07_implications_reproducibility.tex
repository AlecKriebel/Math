\section{Implications, limitations, and reproducibility}

\subsection{A necessary condition for asymptotic universal amplification}

The fixed-graph result does not interchange $n$ and $r$.  We first record the
monotonicity needed for a useful support condition on graph sequences.

\begin{lemma}[fitness monotonicity]\label{lem:fitness-monotone}
For every loopless row-stochastic replacement kernel $P$ and every initial
mutant set, the dB fixation probability is nondecreasing in mutant fitness
$r>0$.
\end{lemma}

\begin{proof}
For $0\le x\le1$,
\[
 \frac{\partial f_r(x)}{\partial r}
 =\frac{x(1-x)}{[1+(r-1)x]^2}\ge0,
 \qquad
 \frac{\partial f_r(x)}{\partial x}
 =\frac{r}{[1+(r-1)x]^2}>0.
\]
Couple chains with $r\le r'$ by using the same dead target $v$ and the same
uniform threshold $U$ at every update, declaring the replacement mutant when
$U\le f_r(P_{vS})$.  If their current mutant sets satisfy $S\subseteq T$,
then $P_{vS}\le P_{vT}$ and the two displayed monotonicities preserve the
inclusion after the update.  Starting both chains from the same set, fixation
of the $r$-chain therefore implies fixation of the $r'$-chain.
\end{proof}

Lemma~\ref{lem:fitness-monotone} and
Proposition~\ref{prop:support-limit} give, for every finite connected
undirected graph,
\[
 \rho_{\dB}(W,r)\le1-\frac1n\sum_i\frac1{s_i+1}.              \tag{7.1}\label{7.1}
\]

\begin{proposition}[support degree must diverge]\label{prop:degree}
Let $W_n$ be connected undirected weighted graphs of order $n\to\infty$.
If, for every fixed $r>1$, $W_n$ is eventually a dB amplifier, then
\[
 \frac1n\sum_i\frac1{s_i+1}\longrightarrow0.                \tag{7.2}\label{7.2}
\]
Consequently the support degree of a uniformly chosen vertex tends to
infinity in probability.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proof}
At fixed $R>1$, eventual amplification and \eqref{7.1} give
\[
 \limsup_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\sum_i\frac1{s_i+1}
 \le\lim_{n\to\infty}\{1-\rho_{\dB}(J_n,R)\}=\frac1R.
\]
Let $R\to\infty$.  For fixed $K$, the fraction with $s_i\le K$ is at most
$(K+1)$ times the average in \eqref{7.2}.
\end{proof}

This excludes uniformly bounded-support families, but not supports completed
by arbitrarily weak positive edges, diffuse perturbations, mesoscopic
modules, or singular population-dependent kernels.

\subsection{What the local theorem does and does not say}

Theorem~\ref{thm:local} proves strict negative fixation curvature in every
nonzero normalized directed direction at fixed order.  In particular,
neither column imbalance nor directed circulation escapes the local maximum.
The theorem does not supply a radius uniform in $n$, global concavity along
rays, or a path from an arbitrary kernel to $J_n$ on which fixation is monotone.  The
global conjecture
\[
 \rho_{\dB}(P,2)\le\rho_{\dB}(J_n,2)                         \tag{7.3}\label{7.3}
\]
for every kernel $P$ induced by an undirected weighting remains open.

\subsection{Exact verification and proof status}

The archive contains independent exact implementations for the two theorem
axes.  The strong-selection suite constructs literal subset-state chains,
checks row normalization and complete-graph lumpability, differentiates the
absorption equations, verifies the incoming-column sum of squares, and
replays the triangle and $K_4$ certificates.  The fitness-two suite
independently builds the marked and active chains, verifies the collision
normalization, reconstructs the rank Poisson equation, checks the tangent
decomposition, and replays all three irreducible-sector certificates.

The standard sector has an analytic tail certificate for $N\ge10$ and exact
rational closure for $2\le N\le9$.  The symmetric sector uses exact solves
for $3\le N\le39$, exact rational phase margins for $40\le N\le287$, and a
coefficient/discriminant certificate for $N\ge288$.  All arithmetic in these
finite ranges is rational.  The verifiers discharge the finite
computer-assisted portions; sampled numerical values are never used to
extend a quantifier.
The universal directed strong-selection conclusion and the all-order
antisymmetric-sector conclusion are proved analytically in Section~5 and
Appendix~A, respectively; their literal-chain executable checks are finite
consistency tests.

The sole certified end-to-end replay, run from the root of the enclosing
reproducibility package, is
\begin{verbatim}
./run_all_referee_checks.sh
\end{verbatim}
It verifies the exact delivered file set and hashes, rejects links, special
nodes, extra files or directories, and bytecode/cache entries, safely extracts
the verified source into a fresh disposable tree, provisions the pinned
environment and private empty cache, invokes the bootstrap and verifier runner
as internal stages, rebuilds the manuscript, and compares it byte-for-byte

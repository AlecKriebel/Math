# Post-release feedback audit

## Verdict

All mathematical and presentation criticisms in the external re-review were
checked against the frozen source and rendered PDF.  The reconstruction and
figure criticisms were confirmed; the principal identifiability and automatic
triangle theorems were unaffected.

## Corrections applied

1. The reconstruction procedure now returns a canonical **structural** class
   modulo ordinary triangle redirection.  It no longer calls every redirected
   endpoint compatible with a fixed input distribution.
2. Lemma 2.4's converse no longer assumes that a reticulation's outgoing edge
   is ordinary.  The proof uses the retained-edge local criterion directly.
3. Statements describing triangle redirection as the only uncertainty were
   replaced by the precise full-dimensional observational-compatibility claim.
4. The acknowledgements describe AI-assisted adversarial processes and
   separate automated implementations; they do not imply human reviewers.
5. Ardiyansyah's 2021 Fourier-invariant study of simple and semisimple
   level-2 network varieties was added to the introduction, prior-work table,
   bibliography, and novelty audit.
6. Figures 1, 6, and 7 were redrawn to eliminate the reported overlaps.  The
   full-page audit also found and repaired an additional overlap in Figure 8.
7. The false earlier visual-inspection report was replaced by a new report
   tied to the corrected PDF hash and two independent renderers.

## Human-controlled pre-submission items

The package does not fabricate a persistent repository identifier or a human
specialist endorsement.  The human checklist leaves both items open for the
author: deposit the final bytes and insert the resulting identifier, and obtain
an external specialist proof review if desired before journal submission.

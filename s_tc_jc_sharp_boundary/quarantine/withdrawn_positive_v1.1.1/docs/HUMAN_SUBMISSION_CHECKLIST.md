# Human submission checklist

Before uploading to a journal, the author should personally confirm:

- [ ] Name, correspondence address, independent-researcher designation, and date.
- [ ] The theorem scope in the title, abstract, Theorems 3.1--3.5, cover
      letter, repository description, and metadata is identical: all binary
      standard semi-directed `S_TC` level-2 networks under open JC, modulo `T`.
- [ ] Every use of "all level-2" remains explicitly qualified by `S_TC`,
      binary, standard semi-directed, and JC.
- [ ] The reconstruction theorem returns a canonical structural topology modulo
      `T`; it does not claim that the fixed input distribution lies in every
      redirected stochastic model.
- [ ] No affiliation with UCI or another institution is stated unless formally current.
- [ ] Every citation has been opened and its title, authors, version, theorem or
      proposition number, and DOI checked against the cited version.
- [ ] Ardiyansyah's 2021 level-2 distinguishability paper is retained in the
      introduction, prior-work table, bibliography, and novelty audit.
- [ ] The phrase "Proposition 2.26 in the April 2025 version" is retained unless
      the citation is updated to a version with a newly checked number.
- [ ] The automatic triangle theorem and its independent Python/C++ rooting
      censuses are included in the deposited package.
- [ ] AI-use disclosure and human responsibility statement are acceptable under
      the selected journal's current policy.
- [ ] An independent human specialist has reviewed the theorem-level proof and
      any resulting corrections have been incorporated or disclosed. This local
      release does not claim that such a review has already occurred.
- [ ] Competing interests and funding statements remain accurate.
- [ ] The final checksum-matched archive is deposited in a persistent public
      repository, and its DOI or other identifier is inserted in the manuscript
      data/code statement, cover letter, journal metadata, and `CITATION.cff`.
- [ ] `bash reproducibility/verify_quick.sh` and
      `bash reproducibility/verify_full.sh` both pass on the deposited bytes.
- [ ] The deposited archive SHA-256 matches the distributed checksum file.
- [ ] The final PDF was visually inspected page by page, including every figure,
      table, equation break, bibliography entry, and hyperlink.
- [ ] The cover letter does not overstate novelty and does not claim level
      greater than two, richer models, the full weakly tree-child class,
      complete stochastic-image equality, or input-specific compatibility of
      every redirected topology.
- [ ] Journal formatting, word limits, file naming, ORCID, suggested reviewers,
      and data/code policy are checked at submission time.

# Submission-safe fallback scope

Status: **VERIFIED — promoted as release 2.0.0**

If the finite positive-classification gates do not all close, the repaired
release will contain only the following theorem and its direct prerequisites.

## Verified primary theorem

For every `n >= 4`, there are two leaf-labelled binary standard
semi-directed level-2 topologies `N_n` and `N'_n` such that:

1. each topology has a rooted tree-child presentation and therefore belongs
   to `W_TC`;
2. neither topology belongs to `S_TC`, because each has an admissible rooting
   that is not tree-child;
3. the two topologies are nonisomorphic and are not related by ordinary
   triangle redirection `T`;
4. their open Jukes--Cantor model images in `Theta_0` share a regular
   relatively open subset of full dimension `2n`.

For `n=4`, the theorem is certified by an exact quadratic-algebraic common
point, equality of all 256 Fourier coordinates, a common irreducible
eight-dimensional model variety, and nonzero rank-eight Jacobian minors.  The
all-`n` statement is obtained by identical cherry substitution, whose
positive analytic inverse recovers the two new arm multipliers and the base
tensor and therefore adds exactly two model dimensions per new leaf.

## Claims deliberately excluded

The fallback manuscript will not assert:

- generic identifiability of `S_TC` level-2 networks;
- completeness of any root or nonroot finite atlas;
- one-sided-containment classification;
- projective bridge reconstruction or identifiable physical bridge
  parameters;
- automatic exclusion of multiple triangles;
- K2P or K3P results;
- Theta or Omega as counterexamples inside `S_TC`;
- any priority claim stronger than a comparison with explicitly read,
  version-locked sources.

## Release requirements

Promotion was conditioned on all of the following, and each item has now been
completed:

1. an independent implementation of the four-leaf graph and Fourier checks;
2. exact rational interval certification of every algebraic target parameter;
3. independent upper and lower dimension certificates;
4. a proof, not a finite regression, of the all-`n` substitution statement;
5. a convention review of `R_TC`, `W_TC`, `S_TC`, standard reduction, and
   ordinary `T`;
6. a current, narrow prior-work comparison;
7. one canonical source tree, clean-clone verification, and a visually
   inspected PDF.

## Mixed group proof checked step by step

**Invariant interpretation (`34–55`).** Lefschetz/Kunneth operators for the product category, Hodge–Riemann, and numerical semisimplicity yield the indicated semisimple cohomological tensor category. The symmetry modification is necessary for an ordinary fiber functor. Its weight-zero invariants are cycle tensors: the weight torus is trivial on weight zero and surjects onto the Tate action up to isogeny, so fixing Tate objects introduces no additional weight-zero invariants. The proof reduces to equality of the images of algebraic and Hodge tensor groups on the original transcendental summands. It does not need group equality on every auxiliary object.

**Individual action (`57–95`).** Self-power HC and algebraic projectors identify the individual image with `Hg(V_i)`. Exact KS and the algebraic right even-Clifford multiplications then determine the simultaneous action on `W_i` up to the scalar kernel. Removing a chosen spin lift leaves left multiplication by an even element commuting with every vector. The even full-Clifford center is scalar; the polarization forces scalar `+/-1`, supplied by the spin kernel. The joint group adds only finite spin-cover kernels and hence no Lie algebra factor.

**Shared ideals (`97–203`).** The standard Zarhin groups are `product SO_d` in the totally real case (`d >= 3`) and `product GL_d` in the CM case (`d >= 1`). Active-place circle eigenvalues exclude the SO6/SL4 coincidence and D4 triality, leaving matched standard modules or the SO3/SO4/U2 shared sl2 cases. The field identification uses equality of Galois sets carrying a shared ideal to an active place; transporting to the active place identifies the stabilizers and hence the embedded real fields. The active standard/dual traceless eigenvalue determines the connected center character for unitary rank >= 3; rank two is correctly left exceptional. Internal identification of both SO4 simple ideals would contradict surjectivity onto the individual SO4 group, so no loop edge is silently required in the graph.

**Weight-one intertwiners (`205–252`).** Full even-Clifford regular representations provide every needed spin word, including both half-spins for even blocks and exterior choices for unitary blocks. Choosing the transported shared choices at every place gives equal joint Lie representations; connectedness gives equal group representations. Extending coefficients does not posit new rational varieties: Hodge endomorphism spaces commute with scalar extension, and weight-one morphisms are algebraic abelian homomorphisms. Thus both intertwiners and multiplicity projectors are legitimate algebraic correspondences with complex coefficients.

**Normalization (`265–344`).** Equality of invertible tensor-product operators implies equality of individual factors up to scalars, and nontrivial simple representations determine adjoint actions projectively. Derived and connected-center corrections can independently remove compatible adjoint action and the CM action, since the derived group is trivial on the latter. For unitary blocks, comparing all-torus words gives `r_(i,p)^d_i=1`; rank one is removed. In ranks >= 3 the choice-one/choice-zero ratios agree at shared simple ideals and are supplied by the SL_d center. Analogously the half-spin ratios remove even orthogonal signs outside SO4. The residual list is exactly SO4 and U2 signs, as asserted; no general connectedness argument alone dismisses these finite relations.

**Graph and boxes (`346–503`).** Edges represent original SO4 blocks and U2 blocks; ordinary vertices are shared sl2 ideals and torus vertices are private endpoints. Every simple cycle and torus-to-torus path is used, including parallel-edge cycles. All its factors share the embedded totally real field and place; Galois transport gives a finite grid over every place. Passing to a totally real Galois closure and taking the orthogonal sum of one transported block per place gives signature `(2,d'-2)` at every real embedding. Extra negative directions can make `d' = 5 mod 8` arbitrarily large. The nonarchimedean anisotropic dimension bound plus Hasse principle successively provide two hyperbolic planes, since after one removal the real signature is still indefinite. Thus the box actually meets every auxiliary proposition hypothesis.

The explicit rational scalar-extension group homomorphisms (`461–480`) show that adjoining all finitely many box varieties changes the Hodge group only by finite spin covers. This avoids assuming auxiliary periods add no new factors merely because they look similar. Matching endpoint words have the same Lie representations, hence connected group representations, including their torus differentials. All varieties are chosen before the element `c`, avoiding element-dependent algebraicity.

**Local spin pairing and transport (`505–674`).** Initial endpoint signs in an original `W_i` give edge scalar epsilon on a local Hom space. The projected box tensor pairs the upper local Hom space with the *reverse* lower Hom space. Only the changed block contributes; spectator Clifford signs become fixed scalars on chosen half-spin words. The two-dimensional skew-form formula at `648–665` gives a perfect Hom pairing, including a chosen torus character line. The minimal-support lemma (`544–560`) first proves those subspaces are invariant and only then transports the scalar. It does not assume the local Hom subspace itself algebraic.

An exact rational calculation independently checks the subtle rank assertion: `checks/mixed_k3_audit/verify_local_spin_pairing.py` uses the two standard skew forms, their tensor-product quadratic metric, and the displayed Clifford maps. Its JSON certificate has rank 4 on full Hom and rank 2 after restriction to a torus character line. This is a local linear-algebra certificate, not a certificate that the geometric tensor P is algebraic. An independent finite graph enumeration (`verify_graph_signs.py`) also passed all 11,894 simple-graph sign cases on up to four vertices and 16 parallel-edge cases; the general proof remains the path-product deduction, not the finite enumeration.

**Cycle/endpoints and completion (`676–760`).** A scalar propagates across each edge because the images of all local maps span the next endpoint representation. Around a cycle the word is the same; for a torus-to-torus path both endpoint words are CM constituents and normalized scalars are one. The resulting product constraints are exactly sufficient: path products define signs at vertices, with sign one at all torus vertices. These vertex signs lie in centers of the shared simply connected derived factors and remove the original V actions. SO3 standard blocks correctly see trivial central action. Equality of algebraic/Hodge group images over C descends over Q, and the algebraic Kunneth decomposition then completes all original product cohomology components.

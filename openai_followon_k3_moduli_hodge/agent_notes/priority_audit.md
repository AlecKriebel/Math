## Verdict

The proposed theorem has **very low incremental research originality**. The reduction from powers of a K3 surface to Hilbert schemes and stable-sheaf moduli is longstanding, and the exact twisted/Bridgeland motive-theoretic machinery is Bülles' 2018 result. The OpenAI quadratic-locus companion already prints this exact moduli template, including all self-powers and birational smooth projective hyperkähler models, for its restricted K3 locus. Its abstract general criterion plus the universal KS companion already gives the corresponding unrestricted-base self-power conclusion, if those upstream inputs are correct.

The arbitrary-different-base **mixed-moduli** formulation is not explicitly stated in the three inspected companion manuscripts. It is an immediate newly assembled consequence of OpenAI's mixed-K3 theorem and Bülles' splitting, obtained by tensoring existing split correspondences. This is a genuine distinction in quantifiers, because separate self-power HC does not itself settle Hodge classes coupling different base transcendental structures. However, the new quantifier is supplied entirely by the already public mixed-K3 theorem. There is no new mechanism for that difficulty in the proposed follow-on.

A concise consequence note can accurately record this assembly, provided the upstream mathematics passes independent scrutiny. It should not advertise an independent solution of the K3 Hodge conjecture, a new motivic reduction, a first proof, or a new KS construction. A safer title is “A consequence for mixed products of K3 moduli spaces,” with an abstract beginning “Combining [OpenAI's theorem] with Bülles' motive decomposition, we record …”. Priority searching does not establish that this wording has never been stated elsewhere. There is insufficient basis here to claim substantial independent novelty. The difference between an explicit mixed corollary and an obvious already available implication must remain visible.

3. **Bülles, Motives of moduli spaces on K3 surfaces and of special cubic fourfolds**, [arXiv:1806.08284v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.08284v1), publicly submitted 21 June 2018 15:03:39 UTC; published Manuscripta Math. 161 (2020), 109–124, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00229-018-1086-0). The arXiv primary page has only v1. Theorem 0.1 states: for a complex projective K3 or abelian surface S, Brauer class α, and a smooth projective moduli space M of Gieseker-stable α-twisted sheaves, or of σ-stable objects in Dᵇ(S,α) for generic σ, the rational Chow motive h(M) is a direct summand of a finite direct sum of h(S^{k_i})(n_i), with 1 ≤ k_i ≤ dim M and integer n_i. The direct-summand conclusion is unconditional; finite-dimensionality is a separate corollary conditional on h(S). Theorem 0.1 plus tensor products already encodes the conditional mixed-moduli reduction. It does not resolve HC on arbitrary products of different base K3 surfaces. The dimension-zero edge must be handled separately, as the literal bound 1 ≤ k_i ≤ dim M has no integers when dim M = 0.

## Current literature search and limits

Searches performed 6 October 2026 included combinations of rational/Hodge conjecture, K3, moduli spaces, products/mixed products, Hilbert schemes, Bülles, Arapura, and exact repository/title searches. Primary source chains from Bülles and Arapura were read. The source corpus was searched for Bülles and moduli HC mentions, with the quadratic-locus companion as the exact relevant hit. Search absence is not evidence of novelty. These observations only delimit this audit's inspected sources.

Relevant established special-case use: Floccari, *The Hodge and Tate conjectures for hyper-Kähler sixfolds of generalized Kummer type*, [author PDF](https://www.math.uni-bielefeld.de/~sfloccari/HodgeKum3.pdf), uses Bülles to transfer HC on powers of a specially associated K3 S_K to a smooth projective stable-sheaf moduli Y_K (proof near PDF p. 28). Thus the transfer is used in existing literature as routine. [Floccari–Varesco](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-024-03027-z) proves special generalized-Kummer results and algebraicity within the H²-generated subalgebra; it is not universal mixed K3-moduli HC.

Search also surfaced a broader claimed proof: Bhattacharjee–Bhattacharya, [*Relative Secant Cycles and the Hodge Conjecture*, v3](https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202602.0462/v3), posted 23 July 2026, unrefereed; its Theorem 18 and Corollary 1 claim all hyperkähler HC and all self-products, and its main theorem claims HC for every smooth complex projective variety. It must not be silently omitted from a claim of “first.” Inspection does **not** certify it as an established result: Theorem 18's displayed deduction says H²(M,Q) consists of divisor classes by Lefschetz (1,1). This is false for K3s and hyperkähler varieties with H^{2,0} ≠ 0; Lefschetz only algebraizes rational classes already of type (1,1), not all H². It also asserts every hyperkähler variety is deformation-equivalent to a K3 Hilbert scheme, which its cited Beauville classification does not say. These specific broken deductions mean this paper cannot be used to certify the target or negate the need for the current base-product proof. No full audit of its separate universal-HC argument was conducted. The primary HTML was inspected via web tools; direct download returned HTTP 403, recorded in the source manifest. Record it as an earlier broader **claim**, not a verified solution.

# Author scope audit

This is an author-performed verification record, not independent peer review.

1. The full Oberwolfach PDF was retrieved. Printed pages 2690 and 2691 were visually inspected. “Minimal dominating” is defined there by nonempty complete general-point fibers. No Picard-number-one, Fano, or degree-minimization assumption is imposed in that definition.
2. The normality question concerns the VMRT at general ambient $x\in X$. It is not a claim merely at a general point of the tangent image, which would be much weaker.
3. Casagrande–Druel's full article was retrieved. Its construction, component/properness proof, normalization statement, Theorem 1.10, Remark 5.4, and Example 5.8 were inspected. The relevant component is irreducible and locally unsplit, even though it is not degree-minimal for any ample polarization. The normalized general-point fiber is smooth and connected, hence irreducible. Its reduced tangent image is expressly irreducible.
4. The plane-sextic/genus-seven example satisfies the codimension-one dimension condition. The three-unit genus defect proves failure of normality, not merely singularity. No claim of exactly three nodes is made.
5. Hwang–Kim's full journal PDF was retrieved. Theorem 1.3 and the proof on page 191 were visually inspected; the relevant construction, general-point argument, and Propositions 6.3–6.7 were read. The specialization $n=6,d=3$ obeys all numerical hypotheses. Its family is genuinely degree-minimal, and $n>d$ gives a single family and irreducible normalized fiber. This example is not described as codimension one.
6. Hwang–Mok's full author-hosted paper was retrieved. Theorem 1 and Corollary 1, including the finiteness and smoothness discussion preceding the corollary, were inspected. The normalization implication is proved in the report, so no inference from singularity alone is used in higher dimension.
7. The checks in `verify_packet.py` concern integer arithmetic, parameter admissibility, metadata shape, and content hashes. They do not certify a formal proof of the cited theorems. Their arithmetic calculations are recalculated from parameters, not copied as expected outputs.
8. Search-based prior-attempt checks found no substantive inherited attempt for this problem. This is a bounded retrieval finding, not a proof that no unindexed attempt exists. No current packet is based on counting an earlier mathematical attempt as new.
9. Only authored exposition and public verification metadata are included in the packet. No PDF, page image, extracted source text, corpus record, or coordination record is included.
10. No remote repository changes were performed while preparing this packet. Publication and independent acceptance remain separate steps.

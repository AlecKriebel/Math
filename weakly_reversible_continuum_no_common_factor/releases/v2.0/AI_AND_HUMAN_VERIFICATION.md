# AI contribution and human-verification statement

OpenAI Codex was used as a computational research assistant for structured
search, exact symbolic algebra, code generation, adversarial proof review,
manuscript drafting, independent verifier construction, and a post-solution
priority audit.  Isolated agent runs independently reconstructed the reaction
field and challenged the family, radical, optimality, stability, and
minimality arguments.

Every numerical datum used in the main theorem is exposed in machine-readable
form.  The included programs reconstruct the mass-action field from the raw
reaction table and use exact integer or rational arithmetic for graph,
stoichiometric-rank, ideal-membership, parametrization, gcd, Jacobian,
radical-decomposition, finite-enumeration, and Sturm-sequence checks.  Decimal
approximations in the prose are orientation only and are not used as proof.

The verification layers are intentionally redundant and algorithmically
different.  Executable success does not certify the prose arguments that are
not finite symbolic identities, and it does not establish novelty.  The named
human author is responsible for deciding whether to endorse, submit, or rely
on the claims.  Public timestamps and DOI metadata, once they exist, provide
disclosure and citability; they are not substitutes for human peer review and
do not themselves establish correctness or priority.

# AI contribution and human-verification statement

OpenAI Codex was used as a computational research assistant for structured
search, exact symbolic algebra, code generation, adversarial proof review,
manuscript drafting, and the post-solution priority audit.  Multiple isolated
agent runs independently reconstructed the reaction field and challenged the
proof.  The final archive contains two executable exact verifiers with
substantially different implementations.

Every numerical datum in the theorem is exposed in machine-readable form.
The verifiers reconstruct the mass-action field from the raw reaction table
and use exact integer or rational arithmetic for graph, rank, ideal-membership,
parametrization, gcd, Jacobian, and radical-decomposition checks.  Floating
point output is not used as evidence.

The named human author is responsible for deciding whether to endorse,
submit, or rely on the mathematical claims.  Public release and exact replay
provide transparency and a citable disclosure date; they are not substitutes
for independent human peer review and do not themselves establish priority or
correctness.

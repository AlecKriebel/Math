# Wreath-product and planar triangle consequences

Author: Alec Kriebel, ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X .

This package documents two separate research consequences and the audits of
their substantive inputs. It claims no mathematical connection between them.
The preprint and theorem/dependency ledgers state the mathematical claims.
Publication clearance and fresh review decisions are retained separately in
the research repository and bind this immutable kit identity. The kit itself
is not a publication receipt.

The original group question asks whether the **standard restricted regular**
wreath product of two finitely generated Hopfian groups is Hopfian. The
construction uses the finite cyclic lamp group of order two and a finitely
generated Hopfian acting group. It does not require or establish that the
original OpenAI acting group is Hopfian, or that the embedded acting group is
finitely presented. The exact orientation is right multiplication by the
one-sided inverse element in the group-ring model of finite-support lamps.

The second target is exactly the planar equality of the normalized two-point
infimum and the triangle-program infimum, with Fourier phase
`exp(-2 pi i <x,xi>)`. It concerns the three lengths `|x|`, `|y|`, `|x-y|`.
It does not resolve all-dimensional equality, characterize optimizers,
prove triangle attainment or give a universal objective-preserving primal
conversion. Number density and covered-area density are distinguished in
the preprint and proofs.

OpenAI's imported input theorems, Bradford–Fournier-Facio's equivalence and
embedding framework, Miller–Schupp/Bridson–Short's embedding literature,
and Cohn–de Laat–Salmon's product-program theorem receive explicit credit.
The short consequences and standard Poisson arguments are not described as
independently discovered breakthroughs. Source-version and priority records
identify the actual retrieved versions and limitations.

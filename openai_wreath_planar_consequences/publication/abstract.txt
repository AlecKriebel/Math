We derive two separately motivated consequences of precisely identified input theorems in OpenAI's mathematics collection. The torsion-free group-algebra counterexample, combined with the established Hopfian embedding framework, yields a finitely generated Hopfian group H for which the standard restricted regular wreath product C2 wr H is non-Hopfian. We give its split epimorphism and kernel explicitly. The sharp planar two-point Fourier certificate, combined with the Cohn-de Laat-Salmon product-program comparison and triangular-lattice Poisson bounds, yields L2=2/sqrt(3) and the exact planar triangle infimum P2=4/3. We reconstruct the deductions, audit the substantive inputs, repair product-proof bookkeeping, and provide independently implemented validated computations and source records. These consequences use credited literature; no first-priority or independent-breakthrough claim is made. The planar equality does not settle the all-dimensional triangle problem or assert attainment of its infimum.

# Conditional planar advance for draft PR #487

Comparison at OpenAI commit `adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a` and our draft PR #487 head `d33ec2f43aa7c59519f0b004202b8cd653ce4fd8`.

This is a checkable deduction from the two stated inputs, not an independent verification of the new OpenAI certificate.

## Claim

If Theorem 1.1 of [OpenAI, A sharp Fourier certificate for planar circle packing](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-sharp-Fourier-certificate-for-planar-circle-packing-September-23-2026/paper.pdf) is correct, then the exact triangle-program question in [draft PR #487](https://github.com/AlecKriebel/Math/pull/487) has equality in dimension two:

\[
P(C_\triangle)=\frac43,\qquad \sqrt{P(C_\triangle)}=L_2=\frac2{\sqrt3}.
\]

## Definitions and assumptions

Use the Fourier phase `exp(-2πi<x,ξ>)`, exactly as both manuscripts do. The raw Cohn–Elkies infimum is

\[
L_n=\inf\{g(0):g\in\mathcal S(\mathbb R^n;\mathbb R),\widehat g(0)=1,\widehat g\ge0,g(x)\le0\text{ for }|x|\ge1\}.
\]

Let `A={0}∪[1,∞)` and

\[
C_\triangle=\{(x,y):|x|,|y|,|x-y|\in A\}\setminus\{(0,0)\}.
\]

The triangle infimum is the infimum of `f(0,0)` over real Schwartz functions on `R^(2n)` with `fhat(0,0)=1`, `fhat≥0`, and `f≤0` on this exact set. The third side is `|x-y|`; no `|x+y|` condition is added.

## Derivation

1. OpenAI Theorem 1.1 supplies a real radial Schwartz `g` with `ghat(0)=1`, `g(0)=2/√3`, Fourier nonnegativity everywhere, and physical nonpositivity for `|x|≥1`. Thus `L_2≤2/√3`.
2. The triangular lattice generated by `(1,0)` and `(1/2,√3/2)` has minimum length one and covolume `√3/2`. Poisson summation on every Cohn–Elkies admissible function gives `g(0)≥2/√3`. Hence `L_2=2/√3`.
3. The exact product-program comparison recorded in our PR #487, proof §3, gives `P(C_△)≤L_2²=4/3`. This comparison is imported there from the Cohn–de Laat–Salmon product theorem; a naïve product of sign-changing one-point primal functions is not assumed feasible on the triangle set.
4. For any triangle-feasible `f`, the support of the square of this triangular lattice lies in `C_△∪{(0,0)}`. Poisson summation, Fourier nonnegativity and the sign constraint give `covol(Λ)^(-2)≤f(0,0)`. Therefore `P(C_△)≥4/3`.
5. The matching inequalities prove the claim. No attainment of the triangle infimum is needed.

## Remaining gap

Our draft currently identifies sharp dimensions `n=1,8,24`. The OpenAI result would add `n=2`. It does not settle equality for every dimension, characterize every optimizer, explain independent-rotation observations universally, or construct an objective-preserving conversion from arbitrary triangle optimizers to one-point optimizers. Those remain the original draft's gaps.

The deduction is elementary once the stated inputs hold. The OpenAI analytic/interval certificate and the imported general product theorem were not independently reconstructed in this comparison.

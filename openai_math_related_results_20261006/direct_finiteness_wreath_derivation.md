# Conditional wreath-product consequence of a group-ring defect

The external premise is OpenAI family 197, [Theorem 1.1](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a/preprints/A-Torsion-Free-Group-Algebra-That-Is-Not-Directly-Finite-October-4-2026/direct-finiteness.pdf): a finitely generated group G and a,b∈F₂[G] satisfy ab=1 and ba≠1. Its original proof is not validated here.

The published bridge is Bradford–Fournier-Facio, [Theorems 1.3/4.11](https://d-nb.info/1355447615/34). Their proof imports an embedding of any finitely generated group into a finitely generated Hopfian group H. The induced group-algebra map is injective: distinct basis elements of G remain distinct in H. Therefore the same one-sided inverse defect holds in R=F₂[H].

The additive group of R is the finitely supported C₂ lamp group on H. The regular restricted wreath product is W=R⋊H with multiplication

\[
(r,h)(s,k)=(r+hs,hk),
\]

where hs is left multiplication by the group element h. Define

\[
\Phi(r,h)=(rb,h).
\]

Right multiplication by b commutes with the left action, since (hs)b=h(sb). Hence

\[
\Phi((r,h)(s,k))=((r+hs)b,hk)
=(rb+h(sb),hk)=\Phi(r,h)\Phi(s,k).
\]

This map is surjective: for any (r,h), the element (ra,h) maps to it because (ra)b=r(ab)=r. It is not injective: e=1−ba is nonzero, and

\[
eb=b-bab=b-b(ab)=0.
\]

Consequently (e,1_H) is a nonidentity kernel element. W is non-Hopfian, whereas C₂ and H are finitely generated and Hopfian. W is finitely generated by H's finite generators and one lamp at the identity. No finitely presented or residually finite acting-group hypothesis occurs in our original PR #389 target.

This is a negative answer to that target **conditional on the external group-ring theorem and the published embedding input**. It does not address a separately constrained nonabelian free lamp group. The specific original G is not assumed Hopfian; replacing it by H is essential. Independent adversarial review checked the group action, ring orientation, finite support, surjectivity, and nonzero kernel.

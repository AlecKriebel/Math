# Statement on the use of artificial intelligence tools

OpenAI ChatGPT, model **GPT-5.6 Pro**, accessed through the ChatGPT interface on **5-6 August 2026**, was used extensively in this project. Its uses included exploratory proof search, attempts to construct counterexamples, generation and testing of verification code, drafting, mathematical criticism, literature-search assistance, and editorial revision.

Several AI-proposed approaches were rejected during the project, including universal one-step Lyapunov claims, an unjustified rate-changing cycle comparison, and incomplete fast-slow hierarchy arguments. The released manuscript relies only on the marked-reaction-channel, residual-factorial, target-following-path proof reconstructed and audited in the manuscript and supplement.

The human author reviewed the released manuscript and verification outputs and assumes responsibility for all mathematical claims, citations, code, disclosures, and conclusions. The automated checks are not peer review, and this statement does not claim that every argument has already been independently verified by another human expert. The AI system is not listed as an author.

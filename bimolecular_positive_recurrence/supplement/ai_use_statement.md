# Statement on the use of artificial intelligence tools

Generative-AI systems were used substantively throughout the research workflow, including mathematical exploration and derivation, adversarial proof analysis, symbolic and software work, literature support, manuscript preparation, and publication-readiness validation.

## Systems and uses

- **OpenAI ChatGPT, GPT-5.6 Pro**, accessed through the ChatGPT interface at <https://chatgpt.com> on **5-6 August 2026**, was used for exploratory proof search, attempted counterexample construction, mathematical derivation and criticism, generation and testing of verification code, literature-search assistance, drafting, and editorial revision.

- **Anthropic Claude, Opus 5 with Max effort**, accessed through <https://claude.ai> on **6 August 2026**, was used for an adversarial review of the proof and manuscript.

- **Anthropic Claude Code using Opus 5**, accessed through the Claude Code command-line application on **6 August 2026**, was used to publish the manuscript on the project website and perform related site formatting. It did not supply an additional mathematical argument.

- **OpenAI Codex desktop**, accessed on **8-9 August 2026**, was used for a further publication-readiness adversarial review, manuscript and verification-package repair, and validation. It used an **OpenAI GPT-5-family coding/research model**; the exact backend deployment identifier was not exposed in the session.

These systems are proprietary services developed by OpenAI or Anthropic. They were used without model modification, fine-tuning, or training on a project-specific dataset. AI-generated literature leads were not treated as scholarly authority; the release identifies the public or scholarly sources on which its literature claims rely. No third-party AI-generated text or result is cited as an original scholarly source.

Several proposed approaches were rejected during the project, including universal one-step Lyapunov claims, an unjustified rate-changing cycle comparison, and incomplete fast-slow hierarchy arguments. The released manuscript relies only on the marked-reaction-channel, residual-factorial, target-following-path proof reconstructed in the manuscript and tested through the accompanying exact artifacts.

The work was developed through an iterative, human-directed research process. The author determined the scope and claims released here, curated the resulting package, and assumes responsibility for the submitted manuscript and public verification materials. The proof and exact artifacts are supplied to make the AI-mediated research independently checkable; no claim of prior independent expert human verification is made. No AI system is listed as an author.

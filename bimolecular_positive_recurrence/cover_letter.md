Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Positive Recurrence of Bimolecular Weakly Reversible Stochastic Reaction Networks with a Single Linkage Class”** for publication in the *Journal of Applied Probability*.

The paper proves that every finite weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network with one linkage class and molecularity at most two is positive recurrent on every closed communicating class, for arbitrary positive rate constants. It removes the pure-species-complex hypothesis from the binary one-linkage theorem of Anderson, Cappelletti, and Kim, *Journal of Applied Probability* 57 (2020), 792-810. The paper does not claim the multiple-linkage, higher-molecularity, or full positive-recurrence conjecture.

The proof uses a marked embedded chain that records the target of the actual reaction channel most recently fired. A residual log-factorial potential then has an exact target/source falling-factorial increment. Fixed target-following paths propagate terminal negative drift through a scalar recursion, while a normalized-log compactification and an exhaustive bimolecular top-complex argument supply the needed rare terminal source along every divergent sequence unless a linear invariant already rules out divergence. A finite random-time Foster argument and trace-chain construction complete the recurrence proof, including a direct nonexplosion argument.

A self-contained verification package accompanies the manuscript. Its computations are adversarial checks of finite identities and examples; no computation is used as a substitute for the universal proof. A detailed AI-use declaration is included in the manuscript and supplement. OpenAI ChatGPT was used extensively for proof exploration, code generation, drafting, and critique; I have reviewed the released manuscript and assume responsibility for all claims, citations, code, and conclusions.

The manuscript has not been submitted or sent by this package. I declare no competing interests and received no specific funding for this work.

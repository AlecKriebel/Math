# Acceptance report

Status: claimed_solved5 for the explicitly separated immersed and unrestricted interpretations, subject to ordinary independent scholarly review rather than a claim of publication priority.

The finite integer-cochain construction supplies an elementary proof for primitive immersions. The strict vertex-count increase bounds the iteration, rank-one termination is a circle, nonrepetition forces covering degree one, and explicit small height perturbations descend the embedding. The proof does not use Howie's theorem.

The unrestricted nonrepetition formulation fails for the actual ababa path walk. The same-leaf interval lemma follows from order preservation of distinct continuous strands and yields an analytic contradiction. It allows shared endpoint equality when the domain point is the same and requires neither affine height functions nor a planar-drawing heuristic. The walk is combinatorially nonperiodic, including after rotations and inversion, but null-homotopic. Neither result refutes the primitive-immersion theorem of Louder–Wilton.

Tests are finite corroboration, not a substitute for the general proofs.

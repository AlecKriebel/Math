# Independent adversarial audit: elementary immersed stacking proof

Audit target: `immersed_elementary_proof.md`, read 2026-10-21. Verdict: the argument is complete for a nonempty combinatorial immersion of a circle whose cyclic edge word is not a proper repetition. I found no mathematical gap. This verdict does not extend the theorem to arbitrary backtracking walks and makes no claim of novelty.

## Checks

1. **Integral cochain exists.** For a connected current image with E edges and V vertices, gradients form a rational vector subspace of dimension V−1. Evaluation on the closed walk vanishes on that entire subspace. Its kernel has dimension at least E−1. Rank E−V+1≥2 implies E−1>V−1, so there is a rational element of the kernel outside the gradient subspace. Multiplication by a common nonzero denominator preserves both zero evaluation and non-gradientness. This works whether the walk has zero or nonzero homology.

2. **Lift and orientation are well-defined.** For an oriented edge e:u→v with voltage c(e), the cover has edges (e,k):(u,k)→(v,k+c(e)), for every integer k. A reverse traversal subtracts c(e). Total walk voltage zero closes the lifted circle. Take only the finite subgraph consisting of traversed edges and their endpoints. It is connected and edge-surjective for the lifted walk. The cover can be disconnected; its relevant component suffices, so no surjectivity-to-Z assumption is needed.

3. **Strict complexity increase.** The new image maps onto every old image vertex. If no fiber splits, assign each old vertex its unique visited integer level s(v). Every old edge is traversed, so its endpoints force c(e)=s(v)−s(u), including edges traversed only backwards. This contradicts non-gradientness. Thus the vertex count strictly increases, while it is at most n, the number of domain vertex occurrences. No claim that graph rank decreases is needed or made. Passing to the finite image at each step is essential.

4. **Rank and termination cases.** Covering maps preserve local immersion, and restriction to the image does not identify half-edges. At every visited image vertex the immersed cyclic walk uses two distinct incident half-edges, so degree is at least two; loop edges count twice. A finite connected image therefore has rank at least one. Since a rank-at-least-two graph always permits another strict vertex increase, the process reaches rank one in at most n−V_initial steps. A finite connected rank-one graph of minimum degree two is a subdivided circle. The case n=1 and an initial circle is included.

5. **Terminal covering degree.** A nonempty combinatorial immersion between circles is a covering with absolute degree d≥1. If d>1, its domain cyclic word traverses a terminal circuit d times. Composing that circuit with the projection through the intermediate graphs gives a closed path in the original graph, and its d-fold repetition is exactly the original parametrized edge walk. Hence the original no-proper-repetition hypothesis forces d=1. Intermediate preservation of group-theoretic primitivity is not needed. Equivalently, a purported proper power at any stage descends to a proper repetition downstairs.

6. **Height descent and separation.** The voltage height on (e,k) over oriented base coordinate t∈[0,1] is k+t c(e). At a fixed old edge interior point, distinct lifted projections therefore have heights differing by a nonzero integer, including when the domain traverses that edge with opposite orientations. The same is true over old vertices. If b is a stacking height for the lifted walk, compactness bounds |b| by M. For 2εM<1, h=a+εb separates pairs with different lifted projections by the triangle inequality; pairs with the same lifted projection have equal a and distinct b. Continuity follows from the voltage endpoint formula. Injectivity of the continuous map from a compact circle into the Hausdorff product gives an embedding. Starting with b=0 at the terminal embedded lift is legitimate. The construction remains piecewise linear on the fixed finite subdivision.

7. **Excluded topological imports.** The proof uses the explicit integer-voltage graph construction, rank/degree counting, rational linear algebra, and compactness. It does not invoke Howie's theorem, a maximal-cyclic-tower theorem, subgroup separability, or a one-relator result. Calling the sequence a tower would not itself create a dependency, but the explicit finite construction is clearer.

## Suggested clarifications, not repairs

- Say “absolute covering degree” at the final circle step.
- Include the explicit edge formula k+t c(e) to make reverse-edge comparisons transparent.
- Say that “embedding” follows from compactness after proving injectivity.
- Do not claim unrestricted closed-walk sufficiency, and do not mistake lack of immersion by itself for a counterexample to such sufficiency.

The necessity argument for a literal proper-repetition factorization also checks: h(τt)−h(t) is continuous and nowhere zero under a hypothetical embedding, has constant sign on the circle, and its orbit sum is zero. That is a contradiction.

## Construction-checker addendum

The final proof clarifications were reread and accepted. The original `check_cochain.py` implements the construction correctly: a spanning-tree gauge sets tree voltages to zero, two chord coordinates produce a nonzero homology-annihilating cochain, reverse edges use the oriented initial level, and heights use exact `Fraction` arithmetic. Its output certificates are checked for distinct heights over each vertex and same-sign endpoint differences for pairs of oriented affine strands. The latter is necessary and sufficient for disjointness of distinct affine strands over an edge interior. The original solve routine assumes a nonempty consistent immersed walk, as supplied by its enumerator; it is not a general malformed-input validator.

Ordinary replay exactly matched `cochain_results.json` (3,228 primitive and 64 proper-power rose words through length seven, plus 372 subdivided words). The original script used ten assertions and was optimization-dependent: `python -O` removed checks while still printing PASS. This was a test-artifact defect, not a defect in the mathematical proof. The publication-copy checker replaces each Assert AST node by an explicit `if not condition: raise AssertionError(...)`. An independent AST comparison verified that this is the entire semantic transformation, with all conditions and control flow preserved. The hardened normal and optimized executions both exactly matched the original saved result.

A separately authored, assertion-free validator is in `check_cochain_independent.py`. It independently checks all 3,292 rose cases, all 372 subdivisions, six additional barbell (including a bridge) and theta-graph primitive/power cases, exact terminal covering degrees, vertex-count growth, and every successful affine embedding certificate. Four corrupted certificates are rejected: repeated vertex heights, an edge crossing despite distinct vertex heights, a missing height, and inexact float values. Four invalid input classes are rejected before entering the construction: empty input, invalid orientation, inconsistent endpoints, and immediate reversal/nonimmersion. Normal and optimized executions agree. These finite checks corroborate the theorem but are not a substitute for its proof.

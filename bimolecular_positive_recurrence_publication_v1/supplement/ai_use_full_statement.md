# Full statement on the use of generative-AI systems

## Purpose of this record

This supplement expands the concise declaration in the Version 1.0
manuscript. Generative-AI systems were used substantively throughout the
research workflow, including mathematical exploration and derivation,
attempted counterexample construction, adversarial proof analysis, symbolic
and software work, literature-search support, manuscript preparation, and
publication-readiness critique. The dates below are the project dates on which
each system was materially used.

  attempts to construct counterexamples and identify missing boundary cases;

- **Dates:** 8–9 August 2026.

The listed systems are proprietary services developed by OpenAI or Anthropic.
They were used without model modification, fine-tuning, or training on a
project-specific dataset.

## Treatment of outputs

AI output was treated as untrusted working material, not as scholarly
authority or external expert validation. Literature leads, bibliographic
metadata, policy statements, and priority claims were checked against the
primary publisher, preprint, conference, or institutional sources identified
in the manuscript and supplements. Exact mathematical identities and finite
computations used for calibration are exposed in the proof or deterministic
verification package. No third-party text, dataset, or scholarly result was
incorporated from an AI output without the ordinary citation to its underlying
source.

Several explored approaches were rejected and are not part of the final
proof, including universal one-step Lyapunov claims, rate-changing cycle
comparisons without a valid uniform bound, and incomplete fast–slow hierarchy
arguments. The released argument is the marked-reaction-channel,
carried-target, residual-factorial, finite-path proof presented in the
manuscript.

The Version 1.0 manuscript contains no AI-generated raster figure. The
decorative proof-flow schematic from Version 0.3 was removed during the
publication revision.

## Direction, authorship, and responsibility

The work was developed through an iterative, human-directed research process.
The author determined the scope and claims released here, curated the
resulting package, and assumes responsibility for the submitted manuscript
and verification materials.

No AI system is listed as an author. The proof and exact artifacts are
provided to make the AI-mediated research independently checkable; no claim of
prior independent expert human validation is made.

## Policy alignment

This record was reviewed on 9 August 2026 against the Cambridge University
Press instructions for the *Journal of Applied Probability*. Those
instructions require disclosure of materially used AI tools, known model or
version, dates, access method, and a full description of use, and they do not
permit AI tools to be listed as authors. The manuscript gives a concise
reader-facing declaration; this supplement retains the fuller chronology.

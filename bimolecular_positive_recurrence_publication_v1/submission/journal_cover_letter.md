Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Positive Recurrence for Single-Linkage
Bimolecular Weakly Reversible Stochastic Reaction Networks”** for publication
in the Applied Probability journals.

The paper proves that, for every positive rate vector, the minimal CTMC of
every finite weakly reversible stochastic mass-action network with one linkage
class and molecularity at most two is nonexplosive on every closed
communicating class. Every nonabsorbing such class is positive recurrent, and
each absorbing singleton has its point-mass stationary law; consequently each
closed class has a unique stationary probability distribution. The theorem
does not claim finite
expected entry into a closed class from an arbitrary nonclosed initial state,
and it does not address multiple linkage classes, higher molecularity, or the
full weakly reversible positive-recurrence conjecture.

The result removes the additional pure-species-complex hypothesis from the
binary one-linkage theorem of Anderson, Cappelletti, and Kim, *Journal of
Applied Probability* 57 (2020), 792–810, and therefore contains that theorem
as a special case. The proof retains the target of the reaction channel most
recently fired and subtracts it from the population before applying a residual
log-factorial potential. This yields an exact target/source falling-factorial
increment. A scalar recursion propagates terminal negative drift along finite
target-following paths, while a normalized-log compactification and a
bimolecular top-complex alternative provide the required terminal source.
Random-time Foster and finite trace-chain arguments complete the class-wise
recurrence proof.

The manuscript positions its entropy growth relative to the classical
pseudo-Helmholtz/Horn–Jackson family without identifying the two potentials.
It also explains the systems-biology motivation: a stationary molecule-count
law supplies long-run state frequencies and averages of bounded observables in
low-copy-number biochemical CTMC models. Finite moments of unbounded count
observables require separate integrability. A rate-degeneration example makes
the theorem's qualitative limitation explicit; no tail, mixing, or useful
uniform bound on the exceptional set is claimed.

A self-contained deterministic verification package accompanies the paper.
It checks exact identities, boundary cases, finite calibration examples, and
stable output, but no finite computation is used in place of the universal
proof. The package includes a targeted proof-audit record, complete checksums,
and a clean-clone reproduction transcript.

Generative-AI systems were used substantively for mathematical exploration,
counterexample attempts, proof criticism, software work, literature support,
and manuscript preparation. The manuscript and supplement identify the known
tools and models, dates, access routes, and uses in accordance with the current
Cambridge disclosure instructions. I directed the research process, determined
the released scope and claims, curated the package, and assume responsibility
for the manuscript and verification materials. No independent expert human
validation is claimed.

The work has not been published as a peer-reviewed article and is not under

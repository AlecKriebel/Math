## Active, historical, and excluded material

- **Active mathematical evidence:** the entries in `ACTIVE_MANIFEST.json`, the
  component producers/verifiers named in the portable 44-command plan, and the
  active integrated gate. Their status is assessed row-by-row above rather
  than accepted from stored labels.
- **Historical/provenance-only:** the preserved historical H21 verifier and its
  reproduced rooted-DAG automorphism failure; the original cloud verifiers
  with broken absolute paths/missing dependencies; the K2P algebra; and the
  earlier universal pointwise K3P cut-rank equivalence. The article correctly
  marks the universal pointwise claim withdrawn and unused.
- **Model-independent frozen inputs:** graph parentage, restoration, topology,
  and transport ledgers imported from the companion work. They may be useful
  inputs, but a file hash or stored category is not independent regeneration.
- **Excluded release-engineering suite:**
  `reproducibility/test_release_engineering_mutations.py` audits Git-index,
  deterministic archive, source-build, and packaging behavior. Its source was
  inspected, but it is nonmathematical and was not reproduced because the
  extracted referee package is not the exact clean Git checkout it requires.

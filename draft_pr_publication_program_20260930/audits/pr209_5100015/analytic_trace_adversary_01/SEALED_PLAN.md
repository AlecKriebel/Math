## Exact hypothesis
For a nondegenerate ellipse with a>b>0 and proper confocal elliptic caustic with parameter 0<lambda<b^2, take a least primitive closed billiard of N>=3, gcd(tau,N)=1, 0<tau<N/2, and N not divisible by 4. The product of signed shoelace areas of the polygon of intersections of successive boundary tangent lines and the polygon of orthogonal projections of the center onto those same boundary tangent lines is phase independent. Star order and reversal are included. A fixed displayed repeat scales each area by repeat count, hence the product by its square. Degenerate/hyperbolic/focal caustics and unsigned lobe areas are outside the claim.

## Independent approach family and falsification targets
1. Derive area formulas directly using P(t)=(a cos t,b sin t), tangent normals, and affine pullback to the circle. Do not assume area product invariance.
2. Uniformize the elliptic billiard by Jacobi functions and express both edge area summands as meromorphic elliptic functions shifted by the billiard increment. Analyze traces over a finite torsion orbit, including half-period parity, divisors, residues, and possible poles at real/complex phases.
3. Seek a counterexample with a closed admissible torsion step, including odd N, N=2 mod 4, star winding, reversal, and approach to excluded parity. Independently implement geometry from explicit parameterization and tangent/foot formulas; avoid inherited scripts.
4. Audit exact factors and dimensions (areas degree 2, product degree 4), repeat behavior, circular and eccentric/degenerate limiting behavior. Compare direct shoelace with derived sums.
5. After recording an initial independent derivation, read the existing paper and its scientific files. Test every universal trace/product assertion against the independent derivation. If the claimed proof transfers the key issue to an unsupported theta/trace identity, call it an exact gap rather than accepting it.

## Success and stopping criteria
A refutation requires an exact counterexample or a reproducible numerical counterexample with precision/stability evidence and unambiguous hypotheses. A successful family audit requires a checkable analytic identity argument or full identification of its remaining gap, plus independent direct geometric checks. Numeric evidence is not proof. This is not whole-publication clearance, human peer review, formal verification, or novelty certification.

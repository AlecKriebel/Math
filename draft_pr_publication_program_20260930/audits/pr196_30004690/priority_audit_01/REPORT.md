## Exact comparison target

There exist $f\in C^\infty(\mathbb P^1\times\partial D)$ with $\omega_{FS}+dd^c_z f(\cdot,\tau)>0$ for every boundary parameter, such that the bounded weak Bedford–Taylor solution $\Phi$ of the homogeneous equation on $\mathbb P^1\times D$ lacks a second-order real Taylor expansion at some point with $0<|\tau|<1$.

For an exact predecessor, ambient space, smoothness, positivity, interior location, and strength of regularity failure all matter. A theorem that only rules out global $C^2$ regularity up to the boundary need not identify any interior defect. Discontinuous Hessian is also logically weaker than identifying a point without a quadratic Taylor expansion.

The candidate assumes the particular smooth lifted pre-contact family in the credited construction, a positive-length contact segment, and a contact time strictly between zero and total area. It excludes an isolated contact point, arc endpoints, the injection point, a terminal time-one slit, and arguments based on rank loss alone. Mathematical success is already recorded by Root; historical success would require an explicit, accurately credited account of what is added and what earlier sources establish.

## What is additional in the current proof

The analytic claim added to the credited flow construction is precise. At a fixed interior point of the time-$T$ fluid domain, enlarge the Green domain by filling a nonpolar, zero-area contact arc. The current identity makes the obstacle potential harmonic across this arc. The negative subharmonic forward difference quotients are therefore bounded above by the enlarged-domain Green function, strictly below the old-domain Green function. This produces a right-time slope gap.

On the smooth lifted pre-contact family, the usual Green-function time derivative has a finite, strictly negative derivative. Write $a(t)=\psi_t(w_*)$, $\kappa_0=a'_-(T)<0$, and $L=a''_-(T)\in(-\infty,0)$. With some $b<\kappa_0$, the new inequality is $a'_+(T)\le b$. Global concavity—not merely a local maximizer calculation—makes $t=T$ the optimizer for $b<\kappa<\kappa_0$. The conjugate is affine there. To the right of $\kappa_0$, the pre-contact branch has quadratic coefficient determined by $F''\to-1/L>0$. Hence there is no common quadratic expansion. The twist transfers this to a genuine interior point.

This is an explicit checkable proof contribution under mathematical gate PASS. It is not a new construction of the flow, a new duality theorem, a general theorem for all tangencies, or a certification that the OWR terminal-slit proposal fails. It is also not a mere rank-loss inference. A source-specific note may accurately say it derives an interior defect from the published contact-arc example and places this alongside the prior announced interior route. Historical firstness of that deduction is unestablished.

## Direct-corollary challenges

1. **Read boundary as interior:** The actual theorem parameter restriction is $|\tau|=1$. Reparametrizing an already defined disc does not move a boundary point into its interior while preserving the available domain and smooth boundary trace.
2. **Treat rank loss as irregularity:** This does not follow; the new proof needs a curvature comparison.
3. **Use terminal slit and assert strict positivity:** The terminal inverse-flow endpoint conditions are a substantive issue. The current finite-time route uses a different supported mechanism.
4. **Call all analytic steps novel:** They are established potential/convex analysis. At most their precise use here and the written resulting deduction are candidate new proof/application content.
5. **Use OWR announcement as complete predecessor proof:** The report does not supply the tentative smoothness step. The audit therefore cannot mark the exact theorem already completely proved on that basis.
6. **Use missing proof as world-openness:** Failure to locate a proof in this bounded audit cannot establish worldwide novelty, first disclosure, or independent discovery.

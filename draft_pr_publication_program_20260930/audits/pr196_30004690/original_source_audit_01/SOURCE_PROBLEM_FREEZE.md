## Source formulas and construction clues

The report uses a planar total potential phi in SH(C) with phi-log(1+|z|^2) bounded; phi_t is its obstacle envelope with Lelong number at least t at 0. The local HMAE potential is u_loc(z,tau)=sup_{0<=t<=1}(phi_t(tau*z)-t*log|tau|^2), with local boundary data phi(tau*z). Passage to the global potential subtracts the Fubini-Study local potential. Distinguish this phi from the globally smooth P^1 Kähler potential used in the later AIF paper.

The proposed harmonic-disc family is generated by simply connected Hele-Shaw domains. The report refers to the terminal conformal map f_1:D->Omega_1; its asserted occupied slice at tau is f_1(tau D)/tau. The suggested density is dd^c phi=|grad p_t|/|V_t| times planar area. Smooth extension across singular terminal-complement points is explicitly the problem left in the construction sketch, with vanishing density permitted there. These clues are not assumptions that may be silently asserted as proved.

## Success criteria and adversarial risks fixed in advance

1. Construct a globally smooth boundary potential and verify fiberwise semipositivity (or strict positivity, if a cited theorem needs it), including at infinity and any terminal singular set.
2. Establish that the actual envelope has the proposed flow, with correct time/area normalization, Lelong normalization, and operator sign.
3. Establish failure of C^2 at a specified point with |tau|<1; failure only on |tau|=1 does not suffice. Rank loss, failure of smoothness, absence of a global foliation, or failure to be a regular solution does not alone prove this.
4. Separate local harmonicity from global relative harmonicity. A merely C^2 solution need not have a transverse foliation through rank-zero points; any transport contradiction must use a region where fiber positivity/nonzero rank is justified.
5. Inverse flow density formulas require nonzero smooth normal velocity and smooth parameter dependence. The terminal singular set cannot be covered by a regular-boundary theorem without a separate argument.
6. Proper harmonic-disc classification, universal-cover versions, and Green-function time derivatives may have distinct hypotheses. A Riemann-map theorem cannot automatically cover multiply connected domains.
7. Distinguish weak-envelope existence from the prescribed flow construction, and the compact-P^1 problem from local C x D formulations.
8. Determine what existing cited theorems literally establish before treating a construction as new. This is source-contract checking only; historical novelty remains outside this audit.

## Exact hypothesis

This geometry, combined with smooth positive-velocity pre-contact motion on a cover, would create a strict right time-slope drop in the obstacle potential and hence a failure of second differentiability at an interior punctured-disc point under the published Legendre duality.

## Independent mechanism check
The proposed local mechanism is mathematically coherent at first inspection. A strictly subharmonic obstacle can touch a harmonic minorant on a smooth arc with fluid on both sides (local quadratic normal contact). Filling that zero-area arc removes a nonpolar boundary barrier for the Green function while adding no area measure to the obstacle current. The sign v_h <= G_D is compatible with the negative-Green extremal convention, and filling the arc should make the Green function strictly more negative. A cover that is injective on the limiting open interior but identifies boundary arcs can have a smooth positive-velocity lifted family.

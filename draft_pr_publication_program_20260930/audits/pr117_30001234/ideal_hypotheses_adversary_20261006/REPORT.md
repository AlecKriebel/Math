## Attempts to falsify the algebraic hypotheses

**Global monomial membership.** The all-one evaluation kills all three generators but sends every nonzero scalar monomial to its nonzero coefficient. Every polynomial combination in I is killed, regardless of degree or cancellation. Therefore I is proper and contains no nonzero monomial. This proof also covers nonzero constants. It does not rely on an assumption that a Gröbner basis is monomial-free.

**Homogeneous-local monomial membership.** Evaluation at the all-one point cannot be applied directly to S_m, where m is the origin's homogeneous maximal ideal. Nevertheless, if a monomial M entered I_m, some h outside m would have h*M in I. Its homogeneous component of degree deg(M) is h(0)*M, with h(0) nonzero. Homogeneity would force M into I, contradicting the global proof. This avoids depending on optional primeness for the local conclusion.

**Global and local minimal generation.** The six displayed monomials are distinct, so I_2 has dimension three. Every term of mI has degree at least three. Every homogeneous element of I in degree above two is already in mI. Thus dim_k(I/mI)=3. Localizing outside m leaves this quotient unchanged because each denominator acts by a nonzero constant on it. Every generating system of I_m has at least three members, so the three displayed generators are minimal locally and globally, even among arbitrary polynomial generators. Nonconstant coefficients and local denominator cancellations do not reduce that dimension.

**Irredundant versus minimum-cardinality binomial systems.** A two-term element of I_2 is a scalar multiple of one displayed minor, since supports of the three independent pairs are disjoint. Every binomial in this homogeneous monomial-free ideal must itself be homogeneous: otherwise its two homogeneous components would be monomials in I. Consequently any binomial generating system must supply all three degree-two pairs. Once it does, every further member is redundant. Thus every irredundant binomial system for this I is these same three minors up to scalar, orientation, and order. The candidate passes both meanings of minimality; no assumption about a particular favored generating system is needed for its ideal hypotheses.

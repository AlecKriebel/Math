## Independent reconstruction and falsification attempts

Let X_m be the link domain specified in the manuscript, V_m its unital finite-order invariant algebra, g = sum_(i<j) lk_ij^2, f = g(4g-1), and A = f V_m.

1. **Membership in the entire algebra, including unordered links.** Label the components temporarily on a fixed singular-link resolution cube. Crossing changes preserve the component set, so these temporary labels can be kept through every resolution. Every lk_ij on the cube is an affine integer-valued function of its binary resolution coordinates; changes between other components and self-crossings have coefficient zero. Squaring gives degree at most two, and summing gives degree at most two independently of the number of components. Thus all third differences of g vanish. The sum is invariant under permutation of labels and every component orientation reversal, so g descends to the stated unordered domain. The coefficient-bound argument for g does not assume that the individual indexed lk_ij are globally unordered invariants.

2. **Product rule without a hidden completion or common order cutoff.** On an r-crossing cube a finite-order invariant of order d has a unique multilinear representative of degree at most d. Indeed each coefficient of support size d+1 or greater is a face difference which vanishes: resolve the remaining coordinates, then use the finite-type condition, iterating when necessary. For u of order d and v of order e, multiply their representatives and reduce powers of each binary coordinate by x_i^2=x_i. Reduction cannot increase total degree. Hence uv has order at most d+e, uniformly over cubes and over all component counts. In particular f has order at most four. V is a filtered union over finite orders, not one fixed truncation; every product remains inside it.

3. **Actual zero set and its boundary.** Actual linking numbers are integers, so g is a nonnegative integer even though the invariant algebra has complex coefficients. The scalar equation g(4g-1)=0 has possible roots 0 and 1/4, but the latter cannot occur on an actual link. The remaining root occurs exactly when all pairwise linking numbers vanish. This includes the unlink and also nonsplit algebraically split links. No use of geometric splitness is made. The global zero set includes knots (and the empty link if allowed) vacuously. They do not invalidate nonemptiness or the witness on two components.

4. **Singular cubes and even component count.** For every n >= 0 and r >= 0, close a braid made of 2n ordinary positive crossings and r two-crossing blocks, each with its second crossing designated singular. The resolutions are sigma^(2n+2j) with coefficients (-1)^(r-j) binom(r,j). These are all even-exponent closures, with two coherently oriented braid components, and the m-2 added split unknots remain fixed. The identity sigma^(2n)(sigma^2-1)^r = sigma^(2n+r)(sigma-sigma^-1)^r is consequently the resolution of a genuine r-singular link, not merely a formal identity for sampled invariant formulas. Thus Delta^(d+1) v(L_n)=0 for every v of order d in the entire algebra.

5. **Whole-algebra polynomial restriction and character.** Characteristic zero gives the finite Newton polynomial P_v(t) = sum_(r=0)^d binom(t,r) Delta^r v(L_0), with agreement at every nonnegative integer by the difference recurrence. There are infinitely many distinct integers in C, so uniqueness follows. The defining restrictions imply P_(u+v)=P_u+P_v, P_(uv)=P_u P_v, P_(c)=c, and P_1=1 by polynomial uniqueness. The product rule already guarantees uv is finite order. Therefore chi(v)=P_v(1/2) is genuinely a unital complex character on the whole V_m, without assuming extension from C[g], injectivity, surjectivity, generation of V_m by linking numbers, continuity, or a common bound on the orders.

6. **Every positive radical power, not a bounded computation.** P_g=t^2, so chi(g)=1/4 and chi(f)=0. For arbitrary N >= 1 and arbitrary finite-order h, g^N=fh would imply 4^(-N)=0. Hence no positive power belongs to A. The universal N argument is the proof; checking finitely many powers is only a diagnostic. Equivalently restriction would force t^(2N)=t^2(4t^2-1)P_h(t), contradicted at t=1/2.

7. **Global and orientation-forgotten versions.** Restrict a globally finite-order invariant to one fixed component class, for example the two-component family. Its order remains finite, and the same polynomial construction is a character on the whole global algebra. The order-two definition of g is uniform on that global domain. For orientation-forgotten finite-type invariants, pullback to oriented links preserves the vanishing crossing-difference condition and gives the same obstruction, since g already descends. The argument equally works in the ordered/labelled oriented theory. For m=1 this g is identically zero, so the submitted construction makes no knot-only claim. Positive characteristic, arbitrary completed algebras, homogeneous ideals, and replacing links by all characters are excluded explicitly and are not silently invoked.

# Source receipt: focal-inversion area product

The original target is the formula in arXiv2004.12497v11 Table 9, printed p.11, k804,a. It is an orbit-area/focal-inverse-area product for primitive N divisible by4. Section2 fixes signed area; Section3.9 fixes unit-circle focal inversion of original orbit vertices. https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12497v11

The published companion Table 9, printed p.350, changes the labels: its k804 is a cosine-sum assertion. This edition does not provide an unchanged area-product row. https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0174.pdf

The later Garcia–Reznik paper Table 1, printed p.7, uses k805,a for the matching area product and lists low-N derivations4,4i,8. Its Proposition 4.9 gives the simple N4 value4. Those prior results are credited. The later paper also discusses hyperbolic caustics, which do not expand the original reviewed theorem. https://ami.uni-eszterhazy.hu/uploads/papers/finalpdf/AMI_online_1216.pdf

All three tables were read and visually inspected. Source-file hashes were checked against the provided source manifest. The canonical parametrization is Stachel Theorem 4.3/equation 4.9, European Journal of Mathematics8(2022), p1614, DOI10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2. The modulus is caustic eccentricity. DLMF22.4,22.8,22.13 were checked for periods, divisor locations, quarter shifts, and additions, including the sign in the reflected inverse-area summand.

This is a mathematical/source audit, not a comprehensive novelty search. No source-provided software was executed and no external communication or remote mutation was undertaken as part of the review.

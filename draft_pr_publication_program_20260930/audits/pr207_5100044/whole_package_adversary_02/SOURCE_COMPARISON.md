The table's 4i case is outside the strictly elliptical-caustic theorem. The current note credits the N=4 value. |
| Ferudun, Focal Inversion Area Products in Elliptic Billiards, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23093706 | Actual main.tex and matching PDF; Theorem 1, complete proof, Section 6.1, attribution section | The positive theorem already includes genuine least period divisible by four, all coprime elliptical stars, both foci, positive constant, reversal and repeated-traversal scaling. Section 6.1 already supplies a repeated-length obstruction. This is exact result overlap, not merely a neighboring billiard invariant. The present package consistently calls itself an attributed verification, and explicitly gives Ferudun the theorem and repetition-limitation credit. No novelty or causal-independence inference is warranted. |
| Stachel, On the motion of billiards in ellipses | Theorem 4.3 and Eq.(4.9), printed p.1614, surrounding parametrization | Supplies precisely the canonical coordinates, modulus c/alpha, step 2v and coprime closure used here. The surrounding printed dn real-shift sign is indeed erroneous; the current proof uses the correct period 2K. |
| Khare–Lakshminarayan–Sukhatme, Local Identities Involving Jacobi Elliptic Functions | Eq.(42) and its surrounding pole/principal-part argument | Supplies the classical local decomposition mechanism into shifted dn functions and their derivatives. The reciprocal-dn form here is a quarter-shifted specialization. The current note works out coefficients rather than claiming this mechanism as new. |
| Khare–Lakshminarayan–Sukhatme, Cyclic Identities II, arXiv:math-ph/0207019v2 | Eq.(20), the first master identity and its Fourier/archetypal-sum context | Covers the cyclic reduction of a function's principal parts to derivatives of the uniform-grid dn sum. This is direct methodological overlap, correctly credited. |
| Khare–Sukhatme, Generalized Landen Transformation Formulas | Eq.(13) with its normalization (14)-(15) and subsequent derivation | States the all-integer-order dn grid transformation. Its alpha is the reciprocal of this note's lambda. Current use of a nome raised to m and lambda=m K_m/K agrees with the classical normalization. |
| Chavez-Caliz, More About Areas and Centers of Poncelet Polygons | Theorem 6 and proof, printed pp.104-105 | The related even-period invariant uses an original Poncelet polygon and its outer tangent polygon. It does not alone identify focal-inverse area. The package supplies that reduction and avoids attributing the focal result to this different polygon theorem. |
| DLMF | Sections 22.2, 22.4, 22.8 and Eq.22.11.3; live 22.11 page checked | The dn Fourier coefficient is pi/(2K) plus (2pi/K) times the cosine series with q^r/(1+q^(2r)); the note's coefficients and frequencies agree. Quarter shifts, real periods and imaginary anti-periodicity are consistent with the proof. |

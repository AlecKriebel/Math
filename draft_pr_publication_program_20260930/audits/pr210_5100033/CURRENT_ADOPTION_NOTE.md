## Exact result and audit scope

For an ellipse with semiaxes a>b>0 and a proper confocal elliptical caustic with 0<lambda<b^2, fix an odd least billiard period N>=3 and any admitted coprime winding number. Project each of the original ellipse's two foci onto the ordered supporting side lines of the polygon formed by the boundary tangents. The product of the two resulting signed shoelace areas is constant along the family. Ordered star trajectories are included. Reversing traversal preserves the product; repeating a trajectory a fixed number s of times multiplies it by s^2. The circular case is treated separately. Hyperbolic or collapsed caustics, grazing trajectories, filled-star areas and projections clipped to segments are outside the statement.

This is row k605,a in Table 7 of the specified 2020 author version 11 of Reznik-Garcia-Koiller, [arXiv:2004.12497v11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12497v11); the corresponding published 2021 Table 7 labels it k606. It is distinct from the original chord-polygon focal-pedal product.

The direct meromorphic proof accounts for all possible poles, opposite residues, reflection, imaginary antiperiod and complementary zeros on the reduced torus, including the pole-free case. The supporting Landen/contact-area and opposite-focus inversion routes were also audited.

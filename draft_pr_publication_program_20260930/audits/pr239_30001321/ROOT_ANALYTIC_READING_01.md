# Root analytic check of PR239

The exact claim is fixed-law iid nearest-neighbor space-time RWRE, with 0<p<1 almost surely, deterministic interval lengths M_N tending to infinity and deterministic alignments: each fixed-epsilon coarse endpoint L1 discrepancy has environmental probability O(N^{-K}) for every fixed K. No uniformity as the law degenerates, or over the rate M_N, is assumed. Root read the complete PROOF, TURN_1, TURN_2, exact source/credit addenda and then the preserved full historical review. The following deductions were checked before relying on the historical review.

1. Two same-environment replicas have the exact half-difference birth-death kernel: off zero both neighbor probabilities a=q(1-q), at zero both are delta=E[p(1-p)]. Strict ellipticity gives 0<delta<=a<=1/4. Its reversible weight at zero is a/delta. Laziness at least one half makes its self-adjoint spectrum nonnegative; hence the return g_n is decreasing. The explicitly derived resolvent bound, together with z=n/(n+1), yields g_n=O((n+1)^{-1/2}) pointwise, rather than an unsupported coefficient estimate.

2. The backward return profile is maximal at zero. Direct subtraction gives origin difference recurrence v_{n+1}(0)=(1-2delta-a)v_n(0)+a v_n(1), with all coefficients nonnegative. Conditional future replica averaging therefore yields E[I_{s+t}|F_s]<=g_t for each deterministic s,t. Ordered time-index expansion, conditioning successively at the penultimate indices, proves E[V_{s,L}^k|F_s]<=k!(sum_{j<L}g_j)^k. Repeated indices are measurable and I_s<=1. This neither presumes independent overlaps nor all moments of instantaneous I_N.

3. Whole-layer martingale increments are precisely the sum of mu_s(x)(p_{s,x}-q) times a deterministic annealed semigroup gradient. Conditional independent centering gives the stated variance identity. The biased binomial parity-lattice peak/adjacent gradient/total variation bounds are respectively O(t^{-1/2}),O(t^{-1}),O(t^{-1/2}); Fourier modulus and unimodality justify them. Interval telescoping and disjointness bound the sum of squared test gradients. The resulting convolution, central-window Cauchy-Schwarz and annealed tails establish the qualitative all-diverging-scale expectation bound F, including real half-open intervals. It imposes no polynomial growth on M_N.

4. The scalar exponential supermartingale proves a joint tail for a large martingale and small predictable variance. It is not conditioned on a future good event. In TURN_2 the deterministic dyadic occupation event controls every bounded terminal test; its complement is charged once before the sign-test union. Writing d=gamma-1/4>0, the entropy margin is 5d/4 and the boundary-mass concentration margin is 3d/4. Residue masses on the parity lattice are bounded by 2/M plus twice its peak, also for odd M. The Lipschitz periodic boundary majorant and two endpoint couplings legitimately restore the last segment. Thus G applies at fixed auxiliary gamma=5/16.

5. The final argument is acyclic. Its mesoscopic choices ell=N^{3/4},H=N^{5/16},r=N^{3/8},R=ceil(N^{3/8}logN),eta=kappa=1/32 yield old fixed-interval expectations O(N^{-1/8}logN), predictable variance O(N^{-7/32}), threshold N^{-1/32} and joint exponent N^{5/32}. The O(N) influence-interval union is harmless. No uniform control over environment-selected intervals is used.

6. In a fixed fresh environment, independent coins until meeting and shared coins afterward preserve both marginals. Same-parity nearest-neighbor paths cannot cross without meeting. After environmental averaging, the premeeting half-gap is the homogeneous symmetric lazy walk, independent of drift bias. Reflection including holding steps gives the survival identity sum_{j=1-d}^d p_ell(j), and hence averaged quenched kernel L1 difference O(|x-y|/sqrt(ell)). Replacing each old H-cell conditional law by that of alpha_m preserves its mass and yields exact L1 cell discrepancy to alpha_m. Kernel contraction and F then give the conditional future norm mean bounded by L_{m,H}+O(H/sqrt(ell))+rho(ell,M_N). Only rho involves the arbitrarily slow target scale.

7. The killed kernel reads only transition variables in its deterministic tube around x+vt. Disjoint tilted strips are genuinely independent finite inputs, even for irrational v. Altering a strip affects only starting positions in its prescribed J_k; endpoint killing consults no variable at the killed destination. At most six J_k contain a starting point, so sum w_k^2<=6 max w_k. Subprobability row oscillation is at most two; reverse triangle inequality gives norm oscillation at most 2w_k. Conditional bounded differences is valid given the old environment. The old interval bound produces exponent N^{1/32}.

8. Restoring killed mass uses a maximal annealed centered bounded-increment inequality across the entire segment, not an endpoint-only bound. Its cost exp(-c log^2N) stays superpolynomial after fixed-threshold Markov. All errors vanish or are superpolynomial with constants depending only on the fixed law, epsilon,K; the eventual threshold may depend on M_N. Opposite parity, static environments and delta=0 arrow laws are excluded explicitly and unused.

# Source and credit addendum at the full turn-3 candidate

The original full OWR report and the two earlier primary sources remain as
listed in source_manifest.json. The source says elliptic; the candidate
allows every fixed iid law with 0<p<1 almost surely. The model retains the
nearest-neighbor parity. PROOF.md Section 6 covers both the lattice-cell and
real geometric readings of interval length, so no integer wording omitted
from the source is used to weaken its quantifier.

A further full primary source was accessed during the third turn:
Joseph, Rassoul-Agha and Seppäläinen, *Independent Particles in a Dynamical
Random Environment*, in *Probability and Analysis in Interacting Physical
Systems*, Springer Proceedings in Mathematics & Statistics 283 (2019),
pp.75–121, DOI 10.1007/978-3-030-15338-0_4.
Full final PDF: https://www.math.utah.edu/~firas/Papers/walkers.pdf

Its introduction, model and Theorem 1.1 were inspected. It gives equilibrium
and fluctuation results for a broader finite-range dynamic model and uses
coupling ideas in recurrent dimensions. It is relevant prior context, not
an exact source-strength all-diverging-scale tail theorem used in this
proof. Its aperiodicity condition is not silently transferred to the
nearest-neighbor parity lattice here. The elementary same-parity
coalescing coupling used in the candidate is proved in full.

Replica, exposure, coalescing-coupling and bounded-differences methods are
classical. The searched sources do not certify novelty or current priority
for the combined theorem. No claim that the conjecture remained open until
this work, and no new-discovery assertion, is made. A rigorous independent
source and mathematical audit is required before promoting this candidate.

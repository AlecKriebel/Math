## Target and assumptions

For a single fixed i.i.d. space-time law supported on (0,1), each deterministic real length M_N→∞ and each deterministic alignment b_N, establish P(full coarse L1>epsilon)=O(N^-K), for each fixed epsilon,K>0. Thresholds may depend on the law, sequence and epsilon,K; the bound is uniform in alignment at each N. No law-uniform estimate, arrow-law estimate, static-law estimate, environment-selected partition, rate for M_N, or simultaneous supremum over all sequences is claimed.

## Independent reconstruction and falsification checks

1. Replica: average two quenched-independent copies. At unequal sites their half-gap jumps ±1 with probability a=q(1-q), holds otherwise; at zero it jumps ±1 with probability delta=E[p(1-p)]. Since 0<delta≤a≤1/4, the chain is sticky and at least half-lazy. Reversibility measure pi(0)=a/delta, pi(d)=1; P=(Id+Q)/2 with Q a Markov contraction, hence spectrum(P)⊂[0,1]. Thus g_n=P_0(D_n=0) decreases. Solving the killed hitting recurrence gives rho, and G(z)=[(1-delta/a)(1-z)+(delta/a)sqrt((1-z)(1-(1-4a)z))]^-1≤sqrt(a)/(2delta sqrt(1-z)). Monotonicity and z=n/(n+1) yield g_n≤C/sqrt(n+1). This step uses spectral monotonicity, not an invalid coefficientwise Tauberian inference.

2. The backward profile b_t(d)=P_d(D_t=0)-P_{d+1}(D_t=0) propagates with first-row coefficients 1-2delta-a and a, and interior a,1-2a,a. All are nonnegative. Therefore u_t(d)≤u_t(0), giving E[I_{s+t}|F_s]≤g_t for arbitrary old probability masses on one parity. For ordered times j_1≤...≤j_k, conditioning on the penultimate selected layer yields E[product I]≤g_(j_k-j_(k-1)) times the earlier product, since I_t is F_t-measurable. Repeated times use I_t^r≤I_t. Summing the nonnegative ordered convolution gives k!(sum_(j<L)g_j)^k≤k! C^k L^(k/2). This provides arbitrary inverse powers at an N^eta sqrt(L) threshold with k fixed after the desired power.

3. Expose a complete environmental layer. Exact increment is sum_x mu_s(x)(p_sx-q)[T_(n-s-1)f(x+1)-T_(n-s-1)f(x-1)]. Distinct x within the layer are independent. Consequently the conditional variance is sigma² sum mu_s² h², and the crude absolute increment is sup|h|. This remains valid for biased q and for sigma²=0. Physical shift 2 is an adjacent binomial-index shift; replacing it by physical shift 1 would fail parity, but the manuscript does not do that.

4. The binomial Fourier characteristic satisfies |phi|²=1-4a sin²(theta/2). Peak ≤C/sqrt(t+1), adjacent mass derivative ≤C/(t+1), and total adjacent variation equals twice the peak by unimodality. A contiguous real interval has contiguous reachable parity indices. Thus max_I|h_I|≤C min(t^-1/2,(M+1)t^-1), sum_I|h_I|≤C t^-1/2, hence sum_I h_I²≤C min(t^-1,(M+1)t^-3/2), with t+1 throughout. Variance summed over cells ≤C n^-1/2[1+log(1+min(M+1,sqrt n))]. For s≤n/2 the t^-1 bound sums to O(n^-1/2); for late s split at (M+1)². The number of cells intersecting a B sqrt n window is ≤2B sqrt n/M+2. Cauchy-Schwarz plus the averaged Chebyshev tail gives an alignment-uniform expectation tending to zero for every n,M→∞, even arbitrarily slowly and nonmonotonically. Fix B first, then send n,M to infinity, then B to infinity; no interchange needing a quantitative target-scale rate is used.

5. Auxiliary polynomial concentration: gamma=1/4+d, beta=1/4+d/2, eta=d/4. Average the last h=floor(n^(2beta)/log²n) layers. Dyadic overlap blocks give Q≤C n^eta/sqrt(h) on one common good event. Freedman for a fixed bounded sign test has exponent n^(beta-eta)/log n, while the number of window cells has exponent n^(1/2-gamma)log n. Their power gap is 5d/4>0, so union over all signs is valid. Cell signs are deterministic. Averaged tail of a sqrt n log n window is exp(-c log²n), also for the smoothed measure after taking expectation. Boundary restoration uses a deterministic Lipschitz periodic majorant with mean O((r+1)/M+(r+1)/sqrt n), tending to zero. Its variation exponent is 2beta-1/2-eta=3d/4>0. Drift is incorporated as x+vh; equality at a half-open boundary is included in the exceptional boundary set. Integer M is needed only here and is supplied by H later.

6. Coalescence: same parity nearest-neighbor paths cannot swap order without meeting. Use independent transition coins until meeting, common coins thereafter, preserving quenched marginals. Before meeting, the annealed positive half-gap is homogeneous lazy with steps a,a; survival from d is sum_(j=1-d)^d p_ell(j)≤C d/sqrt(ell+1) by reflection and a Fourier peak bound. Thus expected full L1 row distance ≤C|x-y|/sqrt(ell+1), capped by 2. Opposite parity is excluded from this use; both old laws and their within-cell replacement are supported on the same reachable parity.

7. Replace old conditional distribution within each H-cell by alpha_m conditional distribution, keeping mu_m's H-cell masses. Exact L1 distance from alpha_m equals coarse L1 at H. Couple same-cell starts, use coalescence at cost H/sqrt(ell), then use contraction. For alpha_m K_ell, convexity over deterministic alpha_m weights and translation of each partition gives conditional future expectation ≤rho(ell,M_N). With ell~N^(3/4), H~N^(5/16), the coalescence cost is N^-1/16; auxiliary concentration applies since H≥m^(5/16). This does not assume the all-scale conclusion in order to prove it.

8. Old local masses: deterministic intervals of length O(R), R~N^(3/8)log N, have smooth majorants of Lipschitz constant N^-3/8, mean O(N^-1/8 log N). Common overlap control gives variation O(N^-7/32); threshold N^-1/32 has Freedman exponent N^5/32. Union over O(N) specified intervals remains superpolynomial. No random center or interval chosen from the old law is used.

9. Kill paths on first full-segment exit from |X_(m+t)-x-vt|≤R. Averaged lost mass is ≤2 exp(-R²/(2ell)), a full-path maximum estimate. Independent input vectors are deterministic strips kR≤y-vt<(k+1)R in fresh layers, even for irrational v. Alive paths from start x only consult coordinates with y-vt in [x-R,x+R]. Thus changing strip k affects only x in deterministic J_k=[kR-R-1,(k+1)R+R+1]. This remains true in both environments; killed destinations use no new environment variables. Each x lies in at most six J_k. Consequently a strip sensitivity is ≤2 mu_m(J_k), sum sensitivities²≤24 max_k mu_m(J_k). Bounded differences conditional on F_m gives exp(-t²/(12 max weight)). Kernels from nearby starts need not be independent; no such independence is invoked.

10. Surviving subprobability kernel differs positively from the true kernel, hence |F-Fdagger|≤lost mass D. Conditional mean D≤d_N and Markov P(D>fixed t|F_m)≤d_N/t. This controls both the center and restoration. Combining fixed-threshold concentration with the qualitative target-scale expectation yields all K without imposing any quantitative divergence on M_N. The strip and coarse bounds are uniform in deterministic alignment, while the final eventual threshold depends on rho(ell,M_N).

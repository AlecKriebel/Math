## Mathematical and operational reconstruction

The exact resource is the symmetric single-excitation W ket in A1,A2,B1,B2 order. Both transmitted qubits cost one physical qubit per copy; after routing the quantum laboratories are L=A1B1 and R=A2B2. The sources define asymptotic local-unitary encoding with independent sender information, not a common pair-index encoder or an obligatory one-copy orthogonal alphabet.

1. **Independent senders:** each sender chooses only its own Pauli codeword. The random ensemble prior is the product of two uniform four-letter priors. Winter's codes are separate deterministic maps from independently uniform message sets. A joint codebook for the ordered pair has not been substituted.
2. **Paired ket and complete instrument:** reordering gives `(psi+_L|00>_R + |00>_L psi+_R)/sqrt(2)`. Bell residual branches have weights `(1/4,1/4,1/2,0)`. PauliA1 merely permutes Bell projectors, including the zero branch; PauliA2 acts locally on the retained R ket. All four outcomes are defined and retained. There is no conditioning on successful postselection and no renormalized success-only rate.
3. **Channel and receiver cut:** after receiver1's complete Bell measurement and classical forwarding, the entire J⊗R state is physically local to receiver2. For n copies this is a finite memoryless cqMAC with output dimension16 per copy. No quantum object crosses L:R after routing. Arbitrary joint operations on R^n are local laboratory operations allowed by the original model.
4. **Spectra and entropies:** conditional spectrum is `(1/2,1/4,1/4,0×13)`, entropy3/2. Averaging y gives `(1/4×2,1/16×8,0×6)`, entropy3. Averaging x gives `(1/8×8,0×8)`, entropy3. Full average has `(3/32×8,1/32×8)`, entropy `5−(3/4)log2(3)=3+h2(1/4)`. Traces are one. Independent determinant reconstruction verifies all multiplicities, not just entropy values.
5. **cqMAC quantities:** both conditional Holevo quantities are3/2; joint quantity is `3/2+h2(1/4)`. These are channel information quantities, not an assertion of one-copy accessible information or a general LOCC formula.
6. **Primary coding theorem:** quant-ph/9807019v3, SectionII, defines finite alphabets, memoryless channel, separate encoders and average error for the uniform Cartesian message set. Theorem9, pp4–5, includes every nonnegative tuple obeying the subset conditional-information constraints, with arbitrary small error/slack for all sufficiently large n. This supplies precisely the needed independent-message achievability. The article's coding theorem is established prior work; it is not proved anew here.
7. **Local implementation of its POVM:** outputs are diagonal in J^n. Pinching every POVM element by classical J projectors preserves positivity, completeness and the Born probabilities. For each received J string, the diagonal block is a complete POVM on R^n. Receiver1 sends the classical Bell string, receiver2 uses the conditional POVM, and any reporting of decoded labels remains receiver-to-receiver classical communication.
8. **Exact rate definition and zero components:** to convert Winter's rates-at-least-with-slack definition to codebook sizes converging to a specified interior pair, choose a slightly larger positive pair still inside the constraints. Restrict the two codebooks to Cartesian subsets of the desired sizes. Averaging over such subsets shows some product subcode has error no larger than the full uniform average error. Merge unused decoder labels into a retained label to keep a complete POVM. For a zero component this permits a singleton sender codebook; for `(0,0)` use the constant decoder. Thus zero components introduce no unsupported asymptotic assumption. This argument is recorded in `PROOF_RECONSTRUCTION.md`.
9. **Average error/Fano and closure:** for at least two message pairs, the displayed Fano inequality is correct. Dividing by n, the binary-entropy term vanishes, while the error times the finite asymptotic sum rate also vanishes. The fixed `(9/8,9/8)` code has sum9/4. Rate pairs approaching the dominant sum face establish a supremum lower bound; the paper does not claim an attained code on the face. F02 concerns only the degenerate formula domain.
10. **Exact strict inequality:** `h2(1/4)>3/4` is equivalent to `27<32`; consequently both9/8 individual rates and their sum are strictly inside the region. The displayed decimal2.311278124459133 is consistent with the exact expression and serves only as an approximation.
11. **Historical LO shell:** every single-qubit marginal has eigenvalues3/4,1/4; every two-qubit marginal has1/2,1/2,0,0. The source convention sums the two marginal unitary dense-coding capacities, giving `2h2(1/4)<2`. Its classical benchmark/fallback has value2 and no strict LO advantage. This is the cited historical shell convention; the proof does not assert a universal impossibility for every later formulation involving joint pooling of local outcomes.
12. **One-copy and optimality limits:** both-Bell decoding on this uniform16-letter ensemble gives four equiprobable conditional outcome pairs and a uniform16-outcome average, hence mutual information2. This concerns that measurement only. The claimed2.311... lower bound needs collective asymptotic decoding. No exact LOCC capacity, one-copy advantage, zero-error, maximal-error or explicit finite-blocklength error guarantee is certified.

No mathematical circularity or transfer of the central achievability problem to an unsupported stronger theorem was found. The necessary outside theorem is directly stated in the primary source with matching assumptions.

The first Bell measurement is substantively the same old ingredient. Combining the old *complete both-Bell classical channel* with a general coding theorem does not yield the present >2 uniform-product quantity: that channel's mutual information is exactly2. Removing the second measurement and calculating the retained cq output is a different reduction requiring the verified channel calculation here. No explicit equivalent W4 strict-rate theorem was identified in the inspected passages.
- **Winter2001 and Huang–Zhang–Hou2000:** the cqMAC coding theorem and general multiple-access dense coding are old and credited. Huang SecVI pp9–11 routes both independent senders' systems to one common receiverCharlie. A theorem for that different receiver ownership does not itself furnish an allowed LOCC decoder across the present cut. - **Das2015 and Muhuri2024:** the inspected arXiv model/body passages have the same split-receiver topology but explicitly bound capacity from above. - **Hayashi–Wang final2022:** the actual final publisher PDF, pp6,8,12 (Assumption1, Figure2/model, Theorem2), was read. Encoding acts on one subsystem A, sent as a whole to Bob, with a fixed helper F. Taking A=A1A2 sends both original qubits into one quantum lab and changes the receiver cut. Taking A=A2B2 and F=A1B1 leaves PauliA2⊗I_B2 with a multiplicity-two representation, outside the direct multiplicity-free assumption, and does not absorb sender1's independent actions into a fixed helper.

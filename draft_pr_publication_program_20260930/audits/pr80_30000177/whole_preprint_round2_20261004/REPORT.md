## Proof reconstructed and adversarial checks

**Complete Bell instrument and normalization, lines 81–108.** With `L=A1B1`, `R=A2B2`, W4 is `(ψ+_L 00_R + 00_L ψ+_R)/sqrt(2)`. Bell contraction gives residual vectors `ψ+/2, ψ+/2, 00/sqrt(2), 0` for `Φ+,Φ−,Ψ+,Ψ−`. Their squared norms are `1/4,1/4,1/2,0` and sum to one. Sender one's Pauli alphabet permutes the Bell branches, with irrelevant vector signs; sender two acts on `A2`. Every branch, including its zero-probability case, remains in the classical register. The density blocks are unnormalized branch operators, not normalized selected outcomes. No postselection or heralded-success division occurs.

**Exact spectra and entropy, lines 118–155.** The 16 sender-letter states have positive spectrum `(1/2,1/4,1/4)`, entropy `3/2`. Averaging sender two with sender one fixed gives `(1/4)×2,(1/16)×8`, entropy 3; averaging sender one with sender two fixed gives `(1/8)×8`, entropy 3. The full average has `(3/32)×8,(1/32)×8`, entropy `3+h2(1/4)`. Zero eigenvalues complete each 16-dimensional list. The marginal/twirl identities and full average block diagonal `diag(3,1,3,1)/32` agree with direct construction. Thus the independent-product-prior cqMAC constraints are `R1<3/2`, `R2<3/2`, `R1+R2<3/2+h2(1/4)`. These constraints include the conditioning on the other sender; a single-message Holevo calculation was not substituted for a MAC theorem.

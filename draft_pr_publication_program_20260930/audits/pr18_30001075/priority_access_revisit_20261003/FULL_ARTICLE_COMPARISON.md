## A concrete boundary control

The topological result cannot be used as a spatial-nullness shortcut. Let the three sets be the open radius1/4 balls centered at(0,0,0),(0,0,3),(0,0,6). They are pairwise disjoint, and every vertical line with horizontal intercept in the open radius1/4 disk intersects all three. These transversals sweep an open infinite cylinder, which has positive volume even in a bounded slice. Theorem1.3 remains applicable. Acyclicity therefore permits positive-volume sweeps of ordinary transversals. The supporting-tangency condition in PR18 is indispensable, and its covering/rank argument is additional mathematics.

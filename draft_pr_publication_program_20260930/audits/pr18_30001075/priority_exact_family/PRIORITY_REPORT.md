## Bound claim and independent seal

Original head: `99e403e85d38d92b021198c4a57bbad3cd8775ba`. Reviewed proof: `../reviewed_candidate/CANDIDATE.md`, SHA256 `8ac19b70bd9081107e903ca47bb9dd6ad05274f604d03000a7fd18e3cfb3bf12`. The claim is that for three pairwise disjoint convex subsets of R3, the union of **entire affine lines** which meet all three sets and which, for each set, lie in one of its supporting planes has Lebesgue outer measure zero. No smoothness, closedness, boundedness, full dimension, positive curvature, definability, or general position is assumed. Empty inputs give an empty locus. OWR printed p.2552 gives the meeting-plus-support-plane definition and Conjecture 4. Its stronger Conjecture 3 asks for a countable cover by 2-manifolds and is excluded from the candidate claim. [Original EMS report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46191).

## Positive prior-subsumption test

A prior counts as an earlier solution when a dated, checkable primary theorem or derivation yields the entire swept null set for every compact pairwise disjoint convex triple, including all affine dimensions, or yields an explicitly stronger statement with a verified adapter. Restrictive hypotheses require a universal adapter, not a claim that approximation is routine. A two-dimensional family in four-dimensional line space can sweep three-dimensional volume; line-space dimension alone is insufficient.

Here is the compact-to-arbitrary adapter. For any original tangent line choose contacts `a_i in K_i`. These are distinct because the sets are pairwise disjoint. Choose pairwise disjoint closed balls `B_i` with rational centers and rational positive radii, containing `a_i` in their interiors. Set `C_i = closure(K_i) intersect B_i`. Each `C_i` is nonempty compact and convex, and the triple is pairwise disjoint. A closed supporting halfspace for `K_i` contains its closure; its boundary plane still contains the line and contact `a_i`. Thus the **same entire line** is tangent to all `C_i`. There are only countably many rational ball triples, so

`S(K_1,K_2,K_3) subset union_B S(closure(K_1) intersect B_1, closure(K_2) intersect B_2, closure(K_3) intersect B_3)`.

Countable subadditivity of outer measure finishes the adapter once the compact universal conclusion is known. This does not assume measurability of the original sets. It does not remove a dimension-at-least-two, smoothness, or algebraicity restriction: those remaining restrictions must be separately discharged. Ray-origin theorems must also be checked against the entire-line conclusion; no ray-to-line adapter is being silently assumed.

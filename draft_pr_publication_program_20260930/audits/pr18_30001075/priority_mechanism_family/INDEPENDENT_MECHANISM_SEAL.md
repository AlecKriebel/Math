# Independent mechanism/search-map seal

This mechanism-family priority audit is validation of the reviewed candidate, not a new proof charge. The prior original research charge remains 1/5. No priority literature or sibling results were read before this seal; input was the current candidate and the original EMS source only.

## Exact target

For every triple of pairwise disjoint convex subsets of R^3, including nonclosed, unbounded and lower-dimensional sets, the union of all affine lines that meet each set and lie in a supporting plane of each set is contained in a Lebesgue-null set. The outer-null formulation matters. The separate countable-union-of-2-manifolds conjecture is outside the target.

The source inspected is EMS serial article 46191, OWR 44/2008, PDF page 75, printed p.2552, section 13, Conjecture 4. Its original text distinguishes the manifold claim (Conjecture 3) from the null claim (Conjecture 4), and motivates them by visual-event surfaces. Tangency requires intersection plus a supporting plane.

## Equivalent mechanism routes to test independently

1. **Line-space incidence / integral geometry.** Use the incidence manifold {(line,x):x in line} and a measure/area theorem for multiple support incidence. A theorem about a set of lines having zero invariant 4-dimensional measure is insufficient: a 3-parameter family of lines can sweep 3-space. Need the *swept-point* null conclusion or a proven rank bound on incidence projection. Search common-support-line parameterizations, kinematic measure, exceptional viewpoints, and integral geometry of convex incidence.
2. **Rectifiable bitangent locus / generalized implicit function.** A countable Lipschitz 2-cover of all bitangents of disjoint compact arbitrary convex bodies would supply the main cover ingredient. Need no smoothness, strict convexity, generic position, unique support normal, or semialgebraicity. DC zero-set rectifiability needs a verifiable regularity/transversality condition; arbitrary intersections of Lipschitz hypersurfaces can have excess dimension. Search nonsmooth implicit functions, d.c. geometry, convex level sets, semiconcavity, and normal cycles.
3. **Legendrian / normal-cycle intersection.** Convex normal cycles are rectifiable; their projections parameterize tangent lines with an extra direction variable. Need justify dimension reduction at two *distinct* contacts and preserve singular contacts. A generic intersection assertion does not cover a prescribed pair of bodies. Search contact geometry of line space and characteristic/focal sets.
4. **Focal polynomial / developable sweep.** For a 2-parameter line congruence, transverse derivative at height z is A+zB; at each tangency contact its rank is at most one, and det(A+zB) has degree at most 2. Three distinct focal/contact heights force identically zero swept Jacobian. Search focal points, caustics of line congruences, tangent developables, triple tangency, and rectifiable ruled sets. A smooth congruence theorem is an established ingredient unless it has measurable/density-point quantifiers for arbitrary convex contacts and the global bitangent cover.
5. **Visual-event and visibility arrangements.** Statements that tritangent events are surfaces may encode the whole claim; inspect hypotheses and proofs. Generic smooth, polyhedral, algebraic/semialgebraic arrangements are special-case credit, not exact prior resolution.

## Whole-dependency adapter required for an already-solved verdict

An older general theorem is exact-equivalent only if its inputs are verified to include arbitrary disjoint compact convex sets, every supporting-plane tangent including corners/flat contacts, and lower-dimensional cases (or rigorous independent reductions). Its conclusion must give outer nullness of the *entire swept union*. If it instead supplies the bitangent cover, it must combine with an older verified multiple-contact rank/sweep theorem with density-point and noninjective area-formula quantifiers. Finally a countable rational contact-ball localization must extend arbitrary convex sets: closure alone cannot preserve disjointness.

## Falsifiable checks / boundary controls

- A sphere-only, positive-curvature, generic-position, or finite-complexity theorem fails the exact target.
- Nonzero measure can coexist with nowhere density; empty interior is insufficient.
- Rectifiability of each tangent hypersurface does not by itself imply rectifiability of their intersection.
- Hausdorff dimension 2 of a line set does not imply nullness of its 3-dimensional sweep without rank information.
- A closedness theorem is only needed after compact reduction and cannot be asserted globally for nonclosed sets.
- A 2-rectifiable cover up to line-space H^2-null error is insufficient unless the exceptional error's whole sweep is controlled.

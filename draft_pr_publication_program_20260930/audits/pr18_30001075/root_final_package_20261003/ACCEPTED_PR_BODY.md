This result resolves Conjecture 4 in OWR 44/2008 for three pairwise disjoint convex subsets of R³: the spatial union of their complete common tangent lines has Lebesgue outer measure zero. Tangency means meeting the set while lying in a supporting plane. The proof covers arbitrary affine dimensions, nonclosed and unbounded sets. The stronger Conjecture 3 is outside this result's scope.

The accepted research note is **The spatial locus of common tangents to three disjoint convex sets is null**, by Alec Kriebel, published with its verification package at [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23127955](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23127955).

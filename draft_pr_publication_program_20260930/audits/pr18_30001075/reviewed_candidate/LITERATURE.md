## Scope of the proposed result

The candidate proves only Conjecture4, through a null swept Jacobian on Lipschitz two-parameter line covers. This does not yield a countable cover of the locus by smooth 2-manifolds. Its reduction extends from compact convex sets to arbitrary convex sets, including nonclosed ones, without presupposing their measurability.

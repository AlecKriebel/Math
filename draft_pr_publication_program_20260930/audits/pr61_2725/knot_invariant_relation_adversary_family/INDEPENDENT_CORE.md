# Independent topological and exact-relation mechanism

Recorded before candidate or historical/fresh reviewer bodies. The direct primary target is unrestricted exact Lagrangian cobordism between Legendrian links in standard contact R3. The argument below is independent of a particular symplectic construction.

## Minimum logical import

Suppose A and B are Legendrian knots of unequal underlying smooth knot type and properly embedded cylindrical Lagrangian annuli exist from A to B and from B to A. Then their Legendrian isotopy classes are unequal: a Legendrian isotopy is in particular a smooth knot isotopy. Each annulus is a cobordism admitted by the literal relation, so [A] precedes [B] and [B] precedes [A]. This contradicts antisymmetry. No classification of all cobordisms, invariant completeness, non-regularity or higher-dimensional spinning is needed.

This argument uses only existence of one such pair. A single directed non-symmetric example does not violate antisymmetry; the direction of the required quantifiers matters. A quotient that identifies mutually cobordant objects changes the object equality and answers a different question.

## Exactness of cylindrical annuli

Let lambda=e^t alpha_std and i:L->R x R3 be a Lagrangian annulus with cylindrical Legendrian ends. Its pulled-back1-form eta=i*lambda is closed, because d eta=i*d lambda=0. On either cylindrical end it vanishes as a1-form: alpha_std vanishes on the Legendrian tangent and lambda has no dt component. Every H1 class of an annulus is a multiple of an end circle, so every period of eta is zero. Therefore eta=dF for a smooth global F. F is constant on each connected cylindrical end; the two constants may differ.

Thus for one-component concordances the conventional exactness requirement follows from the cylindrical Lagrangian topology. This is not an exactness theorem for arbitrary positive-genus cobordisms. On a surface with a handle, a smooth closed1-form supported near a nonseparating interior loop can have zero restriction to every boundary circle and nonzero period along a transverse handle loop. Vanishing boundary periods alone does not eliminate handle periods.

An additive shift changes both end constants together and cannot force unequal constants to equal zero simultaneously. Exactness conventionally requires constancy separately on the two ends, not one common prescribed value. For composing connected one-knot concordances, shift the second primitive by the difference of constants on the glued end. The primitives then match on a cylindrical collar. Translation of t scales lambda and its primitive by a positive constant and preserves exactness, allowing the compact pieces to be placed at different levels. Gluing along the genuine cylindrical collars is smooth and embedded. For links, a definition permitting distinct constants on different components can require further compatibility; none is needed for a one-knot witness.

The cylinder over a Legendrian link has primitive0 and establishes reflexivity. For equality modulo Legendrian isotopy, the correct exact concordance is obtained by the Hamiltonian lift of a contact isotopy, with a cutoff in the symplectization coordinate so it acts at the positive end and is the identity at the negative end. A naive graph of an arbitrary moving Legendrian is not automatically Lagrangian. The usual exact end-constant convention permits concatenation and makes the relation a preorder. Only its failed antisymmetry is needed for a negative answer.

## Stabilization and classical necessary conditions

A Legendrian stabilization inserts a local front zigzag in a ball. As a smooth embedded arc this is isotopic to the original arc relative its endpoints, so it preserves the underlying smooth knot type. Its two new cusps lower tb by1; the oriented cusp difference changes rotation by +1 or -1. With a positive and b negative stabilizations,

    tb(S_+^a S_-^b K)=tb(K)-a-b,
    r(S_+^a S_-^b K)=r(K)+a-b.

These local formulas never identify two unequal smooth knot types. Matching tb and rotation supplies necessary consistency only; neither number is a complete isotopy invariant.

For a connected orientable cobordism with precisely one knot at each end, chi=2-2g-2=-2g. The classical Lagrangian identities tb(positive)-tb(negative)=-chi and equality of coherent rotation imply tb increases by2g. In particular, an annulus preserves both numbers. If two such orientable connected cobordisms exist in opposite directions, adding their tb equations gives 2(g_forward+g_reverse)=0, hence both have genus0. This implication cannot be extended without checking connectedness, orientability and the number of boundary components.

For a disc-filled knot with tb=-1 and rotation0 and the standard unknot with the same numbers, k stabilizations of each sign give tb=-1-2k and rotation0 at BOTH ends. This is fully consistent with annular concordance. Smooth concordance also does not imply smooth isotopy; a nontrivial slice knot and the unknot can be smoothly concordant. A nontrivial knot paired with the unknot remains topologically distinct after every finite number of Legendrian stabilizations.

## Boundary of this mechanism

The universal implication, automatic exactness for annuli and classical compatibility do not construct the required Lagrangian annuli. The actual existence pair must be read from a primary theorem with the correct standard R3 ambient manifold, both directions, stabilization quantifiers and properly embedded cylindrical boundary convention. Numerical invariant matching and an abstract finite relation table cannot replace that theorem. No unstabilized, fillable, decomposable, regular or fixed-smooth-knot-type variant is settled merely by this implication.

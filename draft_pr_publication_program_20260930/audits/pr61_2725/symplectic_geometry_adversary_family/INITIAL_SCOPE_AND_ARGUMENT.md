## Independent success/falsification analysis

1. Equality convention. A literal relation on positioned embedded links already identifies Legendrian-isotopic copies through isotopy traces, but the mathematical question is sensibly about Legendrian isotopy classes. Do not use trivial translated copies as a purported substantive solution. A pair with different underlying smooth knot types defeats antisymmetry under the stronger class interpretation as well. Knot examples suffice to refute the relation on links.

2. Needed witness. Verify two specific Legendrian endpoints A,B, each in (R³,ker(dz-y dx)), with A not Legendrian-isotopic to B. Show existence of a properly embedded exact Lagrangian with negative end A and positive end B, and another with the same endpoints reversed. A one-way cobordism, nonsymmetry, a higher-dimensional result, an immersed Lagrangian, or a different contact ambient manifold is insufficient. Time reversal of one concordance is not automatically Lagrangian/exact in d(exp(t)(dz-y dx)).

3. Exactness and ends. For every imported construction, check cylindrical ends outside a compact t-interval, primitive of exp(t)(dz-y dx) restricted to the Lagrangian, and constant values on each end. Establish whether a paper requires equal constants at the two different ends or merely constant separately, and compare the target definition. A connected annulus has only one circle on each end, simplifying end matching; do not infer link gluing from this without checking componentwise constants. Exactness of an immersion/formal solution is weaker than an embedded exact cobordism.

4. Topology and classical invariants. A concordance means a cylinder/annulus, hence orientable and compact-cut Euler characteristic zero. The classical exact/orientable-cobordism constraint forces equal tb and compatible rotation. Distinct smooth knot types must nonetheless be smoothly concordant. Stabilizing a Legendrian representative adds local zigzags and preserves underlying smooth knot type, while changing tb by minus1 and rotation by a sign. Confirm that both construction directions terminate at one fixed stabilized pair, rather than incompatible further stabilizations or alternating choices that never yield a two-cycle.

5. Ambient dimension and theorem quantifiers. Audit the original precise theorem, its proof and any stabilization/h-principle import: dimension3 versus2n+1>3, all versus some Legendrians, positive/negative stabilization counts, smooth/ribbon/general concordance, regular/decomposable versus unrestricted cobordism, and exact versus nonexact. A claimed flexible existence theorem may still carry formal, Maslov, primitive, or boundary assumptions. Prove every application hypothesis actually available.

6. Construction evidence and citation gap. Read the complete relevant original proof and definitions through primary author/publisher sources. Track which external ingredients are imported, which proof steps are independently checked, and which details remain unverified. Published theorem attribution can support already_solved, but does not mean a new constructive proof or a comprehensive recertification of all foundational h-principles. A finite calculation of compatible invariants is not existence proof.

7. Order logic. Reflexivity is the exact product cylinder. Transitivity uses compatible cylindrical gluing with primitive constants; on isotopy classes include exact isotopy traces. A mutual pair of distinct classes disproves antisymmetry regardless of the other axioms. Quotienting instead by mutual cobordism would force a poset for a preorder, but that is a different equivalence relation from Legendrian isotopy and does not answer the literal inquiry affirmatively.

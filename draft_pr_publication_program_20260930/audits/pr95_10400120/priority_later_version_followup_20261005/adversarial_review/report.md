## Checkable interpretation boundary

At b=1, subtracting the two displayed SU(N) exponents gives the following termwise ratio, with the global framing factors excluded:

$$
\frac{I_{\mathrm{loc}}(\phi,h)}{I_{\mathrm{WZNW}}(\phi,h)}
=\exp\!\left[-i\pi k\sum_s h_s^2\right].
$$

This is an algebraic deduction from Eq(3.2) and the comparison footnote. It equals one for even shifted k. It also equals one for even n on the allowed SU(N) sectors, since sum h_s is divisible by n and sum h_s^2 has the same parity as sum h_s. Thus the later caveat must not be inflated into a claim that every magnitude differs. Generally, relative phases can affect a sum's magnitude; an overall unit phase cannot.

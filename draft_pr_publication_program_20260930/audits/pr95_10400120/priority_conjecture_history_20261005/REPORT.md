# PR95 priority audit: conjecture history and subsequent claims

Closed 2026-10-05. This is a private bounded literature audit, not a preprint or publication authorization. It performs no new central proof search. The original author budget remains 2/5.

## Verdict

**The printed target scope is confirmed. This family did not authenticate an earlier finite-level full SU(5) magnitude counterexample in the inspected literature. Novelty is not cleared.** The most consequential remaining gap is the full text of Takahito Kuriya's directly relevant preprint, *The LMO invariant and the Guadagnini–Pilo conjecture for lens spaces*. Its existence and a February 2003 talk are authenticated by primary institutional/proceedings sources. Its complete theorem statements and hypotheses were not obtained. An aggregator abstract cannot settle this gap.

This verdict is deliberately narrower than “the conjecture was open” or “the result is new.” Search no-hits, sparse citation indexes, an old solutions page, and a title do not prove worldwide absence. The other priority family is separately examining explicit higher-rank lens-space formulas; this family has not treated its work as evidence of clearance.

## Mathematical input and exact priority question

The independently completed parent gate supplies ordinary full SU(5), WZW k = 5 and shifted r = 10. For L(5,1) and L(5,2), both with fundamental group Z/5, the S3-normalized squared magnitudes are respectively 3475 + 1550 sqrt(5) and 4025 + 1800 sqrt(5). Both are positive and their difference is 550 + 250 sqrt(5). This audit accepts that gate; it does not re-prove or extend it.

The historical question is whether this answers the broad nonzero-magnitude claim printed as Ohtsuki Conjecture 7.5 and whether that target was already answered. The latter is not settled merely by confirming that the displayed calculation violates the printed claim. A general earlier theorem or formula may already imply a counterexample without naming the conjecture or using these particular spaces.

## Original source, dates and scope

Guadagnini and Pilo's [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/hep-th/9612090) was submitted 9 December 1996; its cover says November 1996. The [publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s002200050290) dates the journal article to March 1998, *Communications in Mathematical Physics* 192, 47–65. The original preprint introduces the nonzero-magnitude dependence on the fundamental group for closed connected oriented 3-manifolds in the compact simple gauge-group setting, including SU(N). The paper then proves the lens-space assertion for SU(2) and reports SU(3) computations. The broad conjecture is not thereby restricted to SU(2). Its k is the renormalized coupling, and its invariant has I(S3) = 1. Read scope was the introductory/normalization passages, the end of the SU(2) argument, SU(3) section and conclusion, not every proof line. The journal PDF attempt returned publisher HTML; only its metadata/abstract were read, so journal-versus-preprint identity is not claimed.

The [MSP printed source](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf) places Conjecture 7.5 on p. 474, following the definitions on pp. 471–474. It uses quantum G invariants and r = k + h dual, rather than a fixed SU(2) invariant. Reference [158] is Guadagnini–Pilo. The relevant four pages of the independently obtained arXiv printed copy and MSP copy agree after whitespace removal; p. 474 was also visually inspected. The volume is labeled 4 (2002), while the source records publication on 1 June 2004 and revisions through 8 April 2004. The [February 2003 draft](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~bigelow/publications/03.14problem.pdf), p. 103, already contains the same broad conjecture. These facts establish the printed target's scope and chronology, not its current status.

The inherited full SU(5) calculation falls inside that broad compact simple group setting and the nonzero condition. Translating WZW k = 5 to r = 10 matters: the original GP notation should not be used to call the inherited computation a renormalized-level-five result. These are consequences of the authenticated definitions plus the parent gate.

## The directly relevant Kuriya gap

The [Kyushu institutional author record](https://www2.math.kyushu-u.ac.jp/coe/staff/staff_sub/kuriya.html) lists the exact-title work as a preprint. The [2004 Topology Symposium proceedings](https://www.mathsoc.jp/~topology/topsymp/kouenshu/ts2004all.pdf), printed p. 54, reference [12], independently lists it. The [Kyushu COE report](https://www2.math.kyushu-u.ac.jp/coe/report/pdf3/report-6.pdf), physical PDF p. 32, printed pp. 120–122, was visually read after OCR located the author section. Printed p. 121 records the exact-title talk at the 10th Japan–Korea School of Knots and Links, 21 February 2003. This later report is retrospective primary evidence for that talk; the 2003 program itself was not obtained.

The [ResearchGate publication page](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265356017_THE_LMO_INVARIANT_AND_THE_GUADAGNINI-PILO_CONJECTURE_FOR_LENS_SPACES) explicitly says no full text is available and the author has not claimed the record. Its abstract describes lens-space LMO calculations, positive GP cases and a conditional perturbative PG version for simply connected simple groups. This is an **unverified aggregator abstract only**, retained as a locator and warning against conflating perturbative statements with the finite full invariant. No mathematical or priority conclusion is based on its correctness. The full-text gap remains even if its description is accurate, since “some cases” is not specified there.

Searches by exact title, surname, conjecture, Japanese author name, repository terms and PDF terms found institutional/bibliographic records and the later works, not the preprint bytes. The institutional record lists no homepage. A public Wayback CDX lookup of an explicitly inferred Kyushu user path returned an empty array; this does not establish that no author page or archived preprint ever existed. The old CiNII endpoint redirected to its generic homepage, so that attempt was uninformative. An API title search was rate-limited. No request-for-full-text, login, or outside outreach was attempted.

## Exact remaining gaps and permitted claim

The missing Kuriya preprint is a **directly relevant full-text gap**, because its title and later citation explicitly concern this conjecture and lens spaces. In contrast, the closed 2000 6j article and a thesis entry surfaced by an index, plus the unread Yamada 1995 text, are background read-scope limitations. They are not silently treated as inspected or as disproved leads. The journal GP PDF gap is a version-comparison limitation; the original v1 and the printed Ohtsuki target were authenticated independently.

No earlier counterexample was authenticated in this inspected history family. No absence theorem follows, and no claim that the exact SU(5) target was previously unanswered is warranted. A publishable novelty statement would require integrating the other independent priority family and resolving or sharply bounding the Kuriya scope. This audit confers no publication authority, does not alter the parent mathematical gate, and does not spend an additional central proof-search turn.

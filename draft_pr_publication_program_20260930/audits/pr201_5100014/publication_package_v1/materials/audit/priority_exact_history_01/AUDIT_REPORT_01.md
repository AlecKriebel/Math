## Exact target and translation

For fixed a>b>0, 0<lambda<b², let E have semiaxes a,b and C have squared semiaxes a²-lambda,b²-lambda. The Poncelet translation has **least** order N=2 modulo 4, N>=6. Write P_i for an orbit in cyclic traversal order, T_i for the intersections of consecutive E-tangents, and Q_i(M) for the perpendicular feet from a fixed finite real M to those E-tangents. A' and B_M are the signed shoelace areas of T and Q(M). The claim is phase invariance of A' B_M for every M, all admitted coprime star windings, and reversal. Zero or negative B_M is allowed. Hyperbolic and degenerate caustics and unsigned filled-face areas are outside the claim.

Ferudun v1.0 Section 1 uses V_i in place of T_i and the same Q_i(M), A',B_M. Its hypotheses, quantifiers, signed areas and primitive winding convention coincide exactly with this target. Section 5 explicitly treats reversal and zero pedal area without division. There is no change of pedal construction, focus restriction, restriction to convex trajectories, or unproved limiting translation needed. v1.1 Section 1/Theorem 1 retains these conventions. This is exact theorem overlap, not merely an adjacent invariant or special case.

The original source notation A'_m translates to B_M, not to the pedal of the original chord polygon. In particular, the original and outer polygon areas A and A' are different factors; the credited A A' theorem alone is not k303,a.

## Contemporary manuscript: statement, proof and ancillary overlap

Version 1.0 has eight PDF pages and a complete Section 1–7 argument. The exact theorem is already on p.1. Its Sections 2–5 use the canonical Jacobi parameter, an affine formula for the actual tangent-intersection polygon at a half-step shift, central pairing of the foot map, disjoint possible simple-pole sets, and two reversal-induced complementary zero identities. The product is then entire and elliptic, hence constant. The relevant denominator is the bilinear squared boundary normal; adjacent singular feet have parallel residues. This is substantive mathematical text, not a claim of a proof with a missing central lemma. [Archived v1.0 PDF](https://zenodo.org/api/records/23087221/files/main.pdf/content).

The candidate's distinct presentation is: genus-one flag curve; quotient by the exact Poncelet translation; at most two simple poles for original area A; location of all isotropic common-tangent pedal poles in those orbits using odd half-period; parallel residues remove double poles; Riemann-Roch and reversal give B_M=c_M A; the credited Chavez-Caliz A A'=K then finishes. Ferudun instead proves constancy of the product directly on a Jacobi torus. Both use central symmetry, meromorphic continuation, isotropic normal poles, parallel-residue cancellation and signed reversal. The two-pole formulation is a legitimate presentation difference; it is not evidence that the broad meromorphic method or the theorem is new.

Moreover, B_M=c_M A cannot be presented as a new theorem solely because the other paper phrases its conclusion as a product: on the nonzero real family, combining the same credited A A'=K with the exact product theorem gives that proportionality, and meromorphic continuation supplies its usual extension. The prospective contribution must lie in the proof or organization, not in a renamed equivalent conclusion.

Ancillary overlap also matters. v1.0 Section 6 already derives radial quadratic M-dependence and Proposition 4 gives, for lambda=a²b²/(a+b)²,

    A' B_M = ab(a+2b)(2a+b) (1 + |M|²/(a+b)²).

At a=2,b=1 this is 40(1+|M|²/9), matching the candidate's six-periodic evaluation. Section 7 gives exact same-caustic triangles traversed twice whose arbitrary-point products differ, so the need to exclude arbitrary six-entry lists is also already explicit. Different exact examples would not make the underlying qualification novel.

v1.1 adds Theorem 2 and Section 6 for center pedals and all traversal lengths not divisible by four, including repetitions. A source-text diff confirms that Theorem 1 and its Sections 2–5 proof are preserved; theorem/lemma numbers later shift. Its new center theorem is outside this audit's target and should not be confused with a new arbitrary-M assertion. The current website's PDF and source ZIP exactly match the corresponding v1.1 Zenodo files by SHA256.

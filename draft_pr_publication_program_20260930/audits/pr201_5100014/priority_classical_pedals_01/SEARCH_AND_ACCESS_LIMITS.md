## Access paths and limits

1. Primary Steiner pages were fetched as individual ETH e-rara IIIF images. The entire two-volume221MB scan was deliberately not downloaded. Two bounded institutional OCR packs (about139KB and37KB) were searched; only the named relevant sections were promoted as source evidence. Individual page images verified the formulas. The main1881 volume manifest is about493KB and contains public page metadata, not an authored discovery.
2. Numdam primary Sturm, Jacobi and Thébault articles were obtained directly from the institutional PDF links. Local text extraction served navigation; complete relevant formula pages were inspected as images. Each article is under1MB. Two initially selected Sturm articles, pp.13–16 and17–23 of vol.14, proved unrelated; their bytes/receipts are excluded as supporting evidence.
3. Several web opens to Numdam and e-rara initially failed or timed out. Direct institutional access succeeded. These transient failures are not evidence against any theorem's existence. No login, paywall bypass, author request, or noninstitutional mirror was used.
4. Jacobi's1828 German source was located bibliographically and through a publisher preview. The inspected primary fulltext is the explicitly shortened1845 French translation by Terquem. No claim of a complete1828 memoir audit is made.
5. The official Tohoku index locates Thébault1920, vol.17 pp.73–83. Fulltext was not recovered in the bounded search. This is a remaining source-access gap. We do not claim the1914 paper exhausts Thébault's work.
6. Stachel2021 was read through the primary publisher fulltext at named results, including Cor4.2, Thm4.7 and Cor4.9. The absence of an area bridge in those results is a scoped content finding, not a statement about all Stachel papers.
7. The KLS2003 cyclic-identity manuscript pp.3–8 and local-identity paper§1pp.2–4 were inspected through arXiv. Their many appendices were not exhaustively matched against the target rational metric traces. No explicit specialization was recovered. A full symbolic reduction to a listed identity is an open task, not silently assumed impossible.
8. NIST DLMF22.8 was used as an official statement of standard addition formulas. Its cited Whittaker–Watson, Lawden and Walker pages were not inspected; those references cannot support an absence or priority claim here.
9. Modern secondary pages, search snippets, ResearchGate entries, Scribd/Dokumen search hits and community reference requests were discovery aids. No unauthorized fulltext from a shadow-library source was used. Search results for other current project papers were not opened or used as priority verdicts. No sibling new priority-family report was read.

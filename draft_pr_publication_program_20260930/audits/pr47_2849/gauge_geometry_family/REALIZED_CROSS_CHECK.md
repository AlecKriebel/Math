# Independent cross-verification of a genuine degenerate example

This later cross-verification was undertaken only after my source-first baseline and original candidate audit were preserved. The mechanism and example were supplied by the cover algebra family, then independently tested here. It reconstructs known mathematics rather than claiming novelty. The primary [Sivek–Zentner menagerie paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.13270), presentation (2.1), Theorem 1.2 and Proposition 6.1, gives the relevant Seifert family. Author-PDF identity is freshly authenticated separately in `REALIZED_CROSS_RESULTS.json`.

Let Y have Seifert data S²((3,1),(3,1),(3,2)). In the stated source convention its group is generated by c1,c2,c3,h, with h central, c1³h=c2³h=c3³h²=1 and c1c2c3=1. Abelianization has matrix

    [ 3 0 0 1 ]
    [ 0 3 0 1 ]
    [ 0 0 3 2 ]
    [ 1 1 1 0 ].

The gcds of every minor of each size are 1,1,3,36, and the determinant is −36. Its Smith factors are therefore 1,1,3,12. Thus H1 is C3⊕C12. This closed oriented manifold is a rational homology sphere by Poincaré duality; no simply connected or finite fundamental-group assumption is needed.

Here is an independent all-representations check. If rho(h) is noncentral, its centralizer is a circle, and every generator lies in that circle. If rho(h)=1, each rho(ci) has cube 1, so is either 1 or has quaternion angle 2π/3. A central generator forces the other two to be inverses, hence commuting. In the remaining case write q1=cos(theta1)+v1 sin(theta1) and q2=cos(theta2)+v2 sin(theta2). The relation q1q2=q3^-1 gives real part −1/2. With theta1=theta2=2π/3, this says −1/2=1/4−3(v1·v2)/4. It forces v1·v2=1 and hence v1=v2, so all three commute.

If rho(h)=−1, the first two cubes are −1 and the third cube is 1. A central first/second generator is −1, or a central third is 1; each endpoint again makes the other two commute by the product relation. Otherwise the first two angles are π/3 and the third angle is 2π/3. The same real-part equation forces v1=v2. This exhausts the group relation cases and proves every SU(2) representation is abelian.

Set omega=e^(2πi/3) and chi(ci)=omega, chi(h)=1. The character satisfies every relation and is noncentral. For the normal coefficient a=chi²=omega², let a cocycle have generator values u1,u2,u3,u_h. Applying the crossed-homomorphism rule u(gh)=u(g)+lambda(g)u(h) to each h commutator gives (1−a)u_h=0. Since a≠1, u_h=0. The cube relations then contribute (1+a+a²)ui=0 and no further restriction. The product relation is u1+a u2+a²u3=0. These are two independent constraints on four complex variables, so Z1 has dimension 2. The nonzero coboundary vector is (a−1,a−1,a−1,0); it satisfies the product constraint because 1+a+a²=0. Thus H1 has complex dimension 1. The conjugate system a=omega has the same result. Degree-one group cohomology agrees with manifold local-system cohomology without an asphericity assumption.

The trivial real adjoint summand has no H1 because Y is a rational homology sphere. The complex normal summand therefore gives real adjoint H1 dimension 2. Its orbit is a 2-sphere, while its original Zariski tangent and framed Hessian kernel have real dimension 4. Hence the framed critical component is genuinely degenerate under the exact target assumptions. This already falsifies a universal normal-vanishing route independently of the cover geometry.

The source's degree-three cyclic cover is consistent with the computation: the torus orbifold cover of S²(3,3,3) lifts Y to a circle bundle over T². Euler number is −4/3 downstairs and −4 upstairs. Abelianizing the bundle relation leaves two free generators and fiber torsion of order 4, hence b1=2. The chosen adjoint character's kernel is that cover, and the two nontrivial deck eigensystems each contribute complex dimension 1. The cover's invariant eigensystem has dimension 0, matching base b1=0.

**Consequence:** the original candidate's proposed universal vanishing lemma cannot be established. Correct the exact-gap statement to control actual degenerate reducible Floer data or use another mechanism. **No framed instanton dimension was computed here, and this is not a counterexample to KP-3.51.**

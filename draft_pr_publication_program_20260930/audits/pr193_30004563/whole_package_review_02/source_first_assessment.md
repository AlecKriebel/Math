# Sealed source-first assessment and attack plan

Written before reading manuscript, code, certificates, archive, or earlier audit reports.

## Exact source target
MRT, published Definitions 2.1, 2.4, 2.5 and 2.7 (pp. 788–792), permits crossings in alpha-quads and alpha-realizations. Proper embeddings impose extra conditions which are not part of this target. For six distinct boundary points with both alternating triples noncollinear, the incoming quads are A1 A2 A3 A0, A3 A4 A5 A0, A5 A6 A1 A0. Outgoing quads are A2 A3 A4 A7, A4 A5 A6 A7, A6 A1 A2 A7. The alpha-quad equation is AB^alpha+CD^alpha=AD^alpha+BC^alpha.

Published Remark 4.8 p. 811 expects at most three intersections of two construction curves sharing a focus when alpha>1. OWR 35/2020 p. 1785 explicitly conjectures three as maximal potential centers in the immersion setting and expressly allows edge intersections. Therefore a single fixed six-point algebraic boundary admitting an incoming realization and five distinct outgoing centers at alpha=16 refutes both of those numeric expectations in precisely this setting. It does not itself refute a proper-embedding bound or address all alpha.

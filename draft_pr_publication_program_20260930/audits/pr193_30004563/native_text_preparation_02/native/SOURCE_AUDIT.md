## Established earlier curve families

Math. 74 (2014), 1315--1337, [doi:10.1137/130922732](https://doi.org/10.1137/130922732), [author manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.6540), Definition 4.12. - D. P. Bourne and S. M. Roper, *Centroidal power diagrams, Lloyd's algorithm and applications to optimal location problems*, SIAM J. Numer. Anal. 53 (2015), 2545--2569, [doi:10.1137/141000993](https://doi.org/10.1137/141000993), Section 5, p.2568. This repeats the p-power family.

exactly the common-focus equations used here. The curves are members of that established family; the result does not introduce a new family or claim discovery of p-power bisectors.

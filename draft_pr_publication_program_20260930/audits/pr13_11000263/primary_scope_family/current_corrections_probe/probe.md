## Extension: original AMS 2025 abstract and BIRS 2004 report

The following records materially qualify what the initial negative correction search can support: there are primary public announcements of finite-dimensional zipper algebras, both before and after the printed 2005 question. They are not explicit corrigenda of that question.

**AMS Paper48822.** The original [meeting-app route](https://meetings.ams.org/math/spring2025w/meetingapp.cgi/Paper/48822) loads a JavaScript shell. Its own `BaseURL` identifies the original [public meeting API record](https://meetings.ams.org/math/spring2025w/meetingapi.cgi/Paper/48822), fetched directly with HTTP 200 on 2026-10-01. The title is *The zipper algebra*, author Stephen Bigelow, in the 2025 Spring Western Sectional Meeting at San Luis Obispo. The `RawAbstract` proposes a large finite-dimensional algebra having Temperley-Lieb, Iwahori-Hecke, and Birman-Murakami-Wenzl as quotients. It announces an algorithm expressing any braid as a linear combination of finitely many standard forms, using additional terms and zipper relations. The abstract characterizes this as current work with Choomno Moos. Its public text supplies no defining equations, parameter domain, proof, `X_3`, Question 6 reference, or identification with arXiv:math/0505064. Those are limitations of this abstract, not claims about the underlying work.

The [public slot record](https://meetings.ams.org/math/spring2025w/meetingapi.cgi/SlotData/Paper48822_Slot8120) gives `Slot_Date = 2025-05-04`, `Slot_StartTime = 10:30:00`, `Slot_EndTime = 11:00:00`, and `TimeZoneOffset = -07:00:00`. The paper record's `ParentList_Day` and `SortableTimeString` agree. Autogenerated Chicago/MLA citation fields in the same paper record instead say May 3. Thus May 4 is supported as the **scheduled talk date**, not a claimed journal-publication date. Paper-record metadata says `_originated = 2025-03-05T03:03:21` and `_lastchanged = 2026-02-19T22:46:05`; these operational fields do not independently date mathematical changes.

**BIRS 04w5526.** The [official event page](https://www.birs.ca/events/2004/5-day-workshops/04w5526) links the original [final report](https://www.birs.ca/workshops/2004/04w5526/report04w5526.pdf). The report is by Joan Birman, Patrick Dehornoy, and Dale Rolfsen. Its physical/PDF page 4, under Section 2, was both text-extracted and visually rendered. It reports Bigelow's announcement of finite-dimensional quotients of `C B_n`, called `Z_n(q,r)`, depending on two nonzero complex parameters. It describes `Box_k` with `k+1` inputs and outputs, a q-twisted opposite-crossing combination identified with `Box_2`, and inductive skein relations involving the next box. It attributes finite dimension and proper extension of BMW to Bigelow, while reporting that exact dimensions and behavior at `q=1` were unresolved then. Page 4 provides prose descriptions rather than a complete defining presentation or proof. These reported box indices are not enough to reconstruct equations. HTTP `Last-Modified` is 2005-06-14; the report is linked under the official **2004 workshop**, and server metadata is not its scientific-publication date.

**Presentation boundary.** Neither source supplies a relation-by-relation identification or explicit quotient map linking its announced algebra to the exact 2005 printed algebra, or to a later repair of that presentation. BIRS explicitly uses a two-parameter notation, whereas the AMS abstract does not specify its parameters. Consequently these sources support the existence of public finite-dimensional-algebra announcements and their stated scope; they do not independently settle `X_3 = 0` in a specified presentation. Transferring either announcement to the target printed/repaired algebra would require a separate checked algebraic identification. No global openness, novelty, or priority conclusion follows from this probe.

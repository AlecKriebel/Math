# A nonzero Fano-family reproduction

Checked 2026-10-01 UTC. This is an independent concrete test of the integral,
restriction, endpoint and local/global conventions, not evidence replacing the
general proof. Resolution stress examples are derived separately in
`../lct_family/LCT_AUDIT.md`.

Let X be the blowup of P³ along a line L, H the pullback hyperplane class and E
the exceptional divisor. A plane through L has strict transform S≅P²; these
planes give the morphism X→P¹ and S is a fiber. Write h for its line class.
Then H|S=h and E|S is an actual reduced line C. The canonical blowup formula
gives −K_X=4H−E=3H+(H−E). The nef cone is generated by H and H−E: one
checks the contracted exceptional fiber and a strict transform of a line
meeting L. Both corresponding morphisms together distinguish points, so a
positive combination is ample and X is smooth Fano.

The exceptional divisor is P(N_{L/P³})≅P¹×P¹, with H|E=O(1,0) and
O_E(E)=O(1,−1). Thus H³=1, H²E=0, HE²=−1, E³=−2, and (−K_X)³=54.
The effective cone is generated by E and H−E. Indeed every nonexceptional
prime is the strict transform of a degree-d hypersurface of multiplicity m
along L, with 0≤m≤d, giving dH−mE=(d−m)E+d(H−E). Therefore

    D(u)=−K_X−uS=(4−u)H+(u−1)E,
    τ=4,
    N(u)=0 for 0≤u≤1,
    N(u)=(u−1)E for 1≤u≤4.

For the second piece E is forced with that coefficient: its intersection with
an exceptional fiber is negative, and sections of a multiple with positive
E coefficient have precisely the corresponding exceptional fixed component;
the residual multiple of H is basepoint free. The first piece is nef,
D(u)=3H+(1−u)(H−E), and has no fixed component. At τ the effective
representative is 3E, which avoids S as required.

The positive part restricted to S is 3h for u≤1 and (4−u)h for u≥1. Thus

    w(u)=1/2 on [0,1],
    w(u)=(4−u)²/18 on [1,4],
    N(u)|S=(u−1)C on [1,4],
    B=aC,  a=∫₁⁴ (4−u)²(u−1)/18 du=3/8.

Because C is a smooth line, lct(S;C)=1 and lct(S;B)=8/3. Hence both
K₀ and Kopt equal 3/8, attained by F=C with A_S(C)=1. Blowing up a point
on C gives order a and log discrepancy 2, consistent with the inequality.
At a distinguished point outside C, all admitted centers have valuation
order zero and the local optimal constant is zero. The global constant
remains 3/8; using that local constant for F=C would fail. At u=1 the
positive pieces agree, and at τ the weight vanishes while N remains finite.

`check_exact_examples.py` uses standard-library rational arithmetic to
integrate the polynomial and verify these values, the wall/endpoint values,
and the finite resolution thresholds 2, 3/4, 1/2 for transverse curves,
tangent curves, and a nonreduced Cartier section on a Du Val A1 surface.
The resolution derivations, not arithmetic alone, justify those inputs.

Run from any directory:

    python3 check_exact_examples.py

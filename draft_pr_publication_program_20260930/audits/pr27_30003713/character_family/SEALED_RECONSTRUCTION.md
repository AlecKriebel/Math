## Universal mechanism reconstructed independently
Let I=g tensor E. The extension is the split semidirect product g semidirect I, with I abelian and action adjoint on g and trivial on E. In CE chains, identify exterior^n(g direct-sum I) as direct-sum over p+r=n of exterior^p(g) tensor exterior^r(I), placing g entries before I entries. The differential's I-I terms vanish, g-g terms reduce p, and g-I terms reduce p while preserving r; the cross-term signs are exactly the coefficient action signs in CE(g;exterior^r I). Consequently H_n(g tensor A) is direct-sum_r H_(n-r)(g;exterior^r(g tensor E)); this is an actual direct decomposition by E-degree, without a spectral-sequence extension issue.

U(g)=T(V). The augmentation ideal has free resolution 0 -> T(V) tensor V -> T(V) -> C ->0, multiplication as differential. Hence coefficient homology has only p=0,1, computed by delta: V tensor M -> M, delta(v tensor m)=v.m (an overall sign is irrelevant). For M=g^tensor r, use diagonal adjoint action: delta_r(v tensor x_1 tensor ... tensor x_r)=sum_i x_1 tensor ... tensor [v,x_i] tensor ... tensor x_r. S_r permutes only the r coefficient positions, by ordinary place permutations. Delta_r is equivariant and its kernel K_r and cokernel Q_r are analytic functors of V.

Exteriorization requires the sign character: exterior^r(g tensor E) = (g^tensor r tensor E^tensor r tensor sgn_r)_(S_r), with diagonal ordinary place permutations before applying the sign twist. In characteristic zero this coinvariant functor is exact and commutes with kernel/cokernel. Therefore H_(r+1) in E-degree r equals (K_r tensor E^tensor r tensor sgn_r)_(S_r), and H_r in E-degree r uses Q_r instead. A trivial S_r summand in K_r produces exterior^r E; a sign S_r summand produces Sym^r E. There is no free choice of this twist.

The finite-degree identity [Q_r]=[g^tensor r]-[V tensor g^tensor r]+[K_r] is a Grothendieck/character identity, with semisimplicity giving existence of a noncanonical decomposition. It is not itself a computation of K_r and cannot be promoted from Euler information to full homology.

## Primary source facts and exact translations
Powell arXiv:2507.03453v4 (16 Dec 2025), pp.1-2 and pp.5-6: S_r acts by ordinary coefficient-place permutations. Proposition 3.3 gives K_r[d]=0 for d<=r, K_r[r+1]=Sym^(r+1)V tensor triv_r (divided powers equal symmetric powers over C). Theorem 1 gives for r>1, K_r[r+2]=(exterior^(r+2)V tensor sgn_r) direct-sum (S_(r,1,1)V tensor triv_r); r=1 has exterior^3 V only; r=0 has zero in d=2. Thus after exteriorization the d=r+1 band is exterior^r E tensor Sym^(r+1)V. The d=r+2 band for r>1 is Sym^r E tensor exterior^(r+2)V direct-sum exterior^r E tensor S_(r,1,1)V. At r=1 its sole term is E tensor exterior^3 V; adding the formally coinciding second term doubles the answer and is false.

Powell Example 3.1 identifies K_1=CycLie(V), with exact sequence 0 -> CycLie(V) -> V tensor Lie(V) -> Lie(V) -> V ->0. In homogeneous d>=2, bracket is onto: every Lie monomial is generated by brackets with a generator (Jacobi moves a generator to the outer bracket). Thus its multilinear S_d character is p_1*l_(d-1)-l_d, with l_d=(1/d)*sum_(k|d) mu(k)*p_k^(d/k). This is the cyclic Lie/Whitehouse kernel, not an arbitrary sign variant. d=2 gives s_(2), d=3 gives s_(1,1,1), d=4 gives s_(2,2). E-degree one corresponds to E tensor CycLie(V) in H_2 and E tensor V in H_1, for every dimension of E. It does not describe all homology when dim(E)=1: for r>=2 exterior powers of g tensor E still survive.

Powell's Theorem 1 encodes S_(1^(r+2)) tensor S_(1^r) plus S_(r,1,1) tensor S_(r). Gadish-Hainaut's Phi_1[r+2,r] has conjugate partitions on both group actions, from two sign twists; their convention must not be copied untransformed.

## Literal defect and universal corrected intersection

Write F=ker(Λ³⊗Γn−1→Λ⁴⊗Γn−2)=S(n,1,1). The source's first embedded face F⊗V inside Λ³⊗Tn is invariant under the singleton subgroup fixing the last position. The swapped face is invariant under the subgroup fixing the penultimate position. For n≥3 these subgroups overlap in n−2≥1 positions. Their transposition graph is connected; specifically the missing last-two transposition is (last−1,first)(first,last)(last−1,first). Consequently their intersection is in Λ³⊗Γn.

On that symmetric subspace the first face's wedge-to-Λ⁴ condition is exactly the de Rham kernel. To see both directions without a dimension shortcut: full singleton symmetry places an element in Γn−1⊗V for either selected last factor; its wedge with any singleton is zero if and only if the first-singleton wedge is zero. Thus ker(d:Λ³⊗Γn→Λ⁴⊗Γn−1) lies in both faces, while every element of the face intersection lies in this kernel. Pieri and nonzero d identify it as S(n+1,1,1).

# Append-only qualification of the early source chronology

The early reconstruction remains sealed and unchanged. Its sentence “The source
is 2018, not 2019” is too categorical. The [EMS article metadata](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/15987)
assigns the report to *Oberwolfach Reports* 15 (2018), no. 1, pp. 43–123 and
DOI 10.4171/OWR/2018/2, but states that it was published on 5 January 2019.
The workshop took place 14–20 January 2018. Consequently a dataset citation
using 2019 can refer to the publication date; it is not by itself a factual error.
The target and the mathematical criteria do not change.

The newly retrieved EMS PDF also differs in bytes from the historical checksum
record. The historical record gives 654,519 bytes and SHA256
`9d4c4b3ece921051f15c24c23b911839ed968607225fe9244b409960658065be`;
the current URL returned 734,914 bytes and SHA256
`91efb3f45550efd99cbae32c72001ea6b8d40fd0a9c1301c2f524398f9530de5`.
The original historical PDF was not available in the frozen attempt packet, so
its bytes were not reconstructed or visually compared. The current literal
Question 13 on printed p. 118 was read with surrounding context and visually
inspected. This supports the target now, while leaving the historical PDF
identity as an attestation limit. Gadish–Hainaut and Powell v4 fresh PDF hashes
match their original checksum entries exactly.

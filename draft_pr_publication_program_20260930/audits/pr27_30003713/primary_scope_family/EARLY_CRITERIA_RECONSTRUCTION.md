## Independent deduction: exact two-term reduction and remaining difficulty

Write L=Lie(V) and I=L⊗E. The current algebra h=L⊗(C⊕E) is the split semidirect product L⋉I; I is abelian because E²=0, and L acts by the adjoint action on its L factor. In the ordinary CE chain complex, C_n(h)=Λ^n(L⊕I)=⊕_{p+q=n}Λ^pL⊗Λ^qI. The bracket differential preserves q: brackets of two I factors vanish, L-L brackets reduce p, and L-I brackets replace an I factor with an I factor while consuming one L factor. Thus each q-component is CE(L;M_q), M_q=Λ^q(L⊗E), shifted in chain degree by q. This direct-sum grading produces H_n(h)=⊕_{p+q=n}H_p(L;M_q), without assuming collapse of an unspecified spectral sequence.

The enveloping algebra of the free Lie algebra is T(V). The augmentation ideal of T(V) is free as a right T(V)-module V⊗T(V), via concatenating the first letter of any nonempty tensor word. Hence 0→V⊗T(V)→T(V)→C→0 is a free right resolution of trivial C. Tensoring with any left L-module M gives the two-term complex V⊗M→M, v⊗m↦v·m. Therefore H_p(L;M)=0for p>1, H_1=kerδ, H_0=cokerδ. For M_q define δ_q as the diagonal adjoint action. Then H_n(h)=cokerδ_n ⊕ kerδ_{n-1}, with the negative-index summand omitted and E-degrees n,n−1 respectively. This is universal, but the kernels and cokernels still require evaluation as polynomial functors.

For q≥0 the Cauchy rule gives Λ^q(E⊗L)=⊕_{lambda⊢q}S_lambda(E)⊗S_lambda'(L), where lambda' is the conjugate partition. Thus evaluating H_*(L;S_lambda'(L)) is the precise remaining family. Swapping lambda and lambda' without also swapping factors, or using the symmetric Cauchy rule in this exterior problem, changes the answer. No general semidirect spectral-sequence collapse principle is needed; the preserved E-degree proves the decomposition here.

Boundary falsifiers: V=0gives only H_0=C; E=0recovers H_0(L;C)=C,H_1=V, higher0; dimV=1makes L=V abelian and h=V⊗(C⊕E) abelian, so H_n=V^{⊗n}⊗[Λ^nE⊕Λ^{n−1}E]. The abelianization gives H_1(h)=V⊗(C⊕E) for arbitrary E,V. Ordinary exterior powers, not symmetric powers, are required in these checks. These derivations validate scope and conventions only; they are not a new solution attempt.

## Independent universal reconstruction / falsifiable obligations

1. The infinitesimal rigid kernel must be proved on connected open domains: Eu=0 implies all second distributional derivatives of u vanish, and Du is a constant skew matrix. Disconnected domains get independent constants.
2. Parallel nonzero case: normalize the line to e odot e. In orthogonal coordinates (s,t), transverse strain zero gives u_T=A(s)t+b(s), A(s) skew. The mixed equations require grad_t u_1=-A'(s)t-b'(s). Symmetry of a gradient forces A'=0. Removing A and skew-affine freedom leaves u=e[H(s)+t dot P'(s)]-sum_j v_j P_j(s), with strain H'(s)+sum_j t_j P_j''(s). At distributional level, L1 displacement and Radon strain must yield the claimed BV / derivative-BV profile conditions; arbitrary distributions are insufficient.
3. Independent case: a nonsingular linear coordinate/displacement congruence can reduce the fixed symmetric line to e1 odot e2, but it must transform both x and u. Saint-Venant compatibility for the scalar coefficient lambda should force partial_12 lambda=0, partial_1k lambda=partial_2k lambda=0, and partial_kl lambda=0 for k,l transverse. On the entire product space these imply lambda=f(x1)+g(x2)+2c dot z, with c constant. Integrating f,g and matching strains gives (H1(x2)+x2 c dot z, H2(x1)+x1 c dot z, -c x1x2), modulo the kernel. Necessity for signed measures requires distributional separation and BV primitives. For smooth u the profiles can be chosen smooth by coefficient extraction, rather than assuming every BV representative is smooth.
4. Reconcile nonorthogonal/scaled a,b with the actual geometric formula and the published normalization. The printed condition a != +/- b is insufficient for arbitrary unnormalized parallel vectors. A robust split is linear independence versus nonzero parallelism. Test b=2a, b=-2a, a dot b !=0, signs and negative scaling.
5. Verify full published theorem and proof rather than substituting later positive tangent theorems; positivity can eliminate transverse terms on all R^d but signedness cannot. Countercontrols must exercise jump profiles and sign-changing measures.

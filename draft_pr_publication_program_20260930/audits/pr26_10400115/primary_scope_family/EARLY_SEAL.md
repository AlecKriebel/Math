## Exact success criterion
For each fixed strand count n in the unresolved historical range n >= 4, exhibit a finite d (which may depend on n) and a homomorphism B_n -> GL_d(Qbar) with trivial kernel; or prove nonexistence for a specified n across every finite d. A Lawrence-Krammer specialization is only one sufficient approach. Failure or obstruction of one family is not nonexistence for arbitrary representations. Infinite-dimensional, function-field, transcendental-coefficient, finite-set-separating, and discrete-image results do not by themselves meet this criterion.

## Independent attacks and falsifiable controls, defined before result exposure
1. Discrete-image leakage: a map B_n->GL_1(Q), sigma_i->2, has discrete image but kills sigma1 sigma2^-1 for n>=3. A trivial representation also satisfies the theorem's hypotheses with J=1. Therefore discreteness cannot stand in for injectivity.
2. Quantifier leakage: generic symbolic faithfulness can separate any fixed finite set at some algebraic parameter without yielding one algebraic parameter faithful for every element. Control: finitely generated G=Z wr Z represented by matrices [[t^k,p(t)],[0,1]], p in Z[t±1]. Generic representation is faithful; any nonzero algebraic a kills the nonzero unipotent with p the integer minimal polynomial of a. Any finite set of nonidentity matrices survives away from finitely many roots. This refutes a general specialization implication, not the braid-specific target.
3. Family/parameter leakage: after l=s^k1, alpha=s^k2 a nonzero Laurent relation l^k2-alpha^k1 becomes zero (when both exponents zero, l-1 is enough). A noninjective coefficient specialization is not itself a nonfaithful group representation. A claimed group kernel must be a nonidentity braid with identity specialized matrix, checked in the exact normalization.
4. Rational restriction of scalars: finite generators with algebraic entries lie in a finite number field K. Replacing K multiplication by rational matrices gives GL_d(K)->GL_(d[K:Q])(Q) injectively. Thus existence over Qbar and over Q with unbounded finite dimension are equivalent for B_n. This does not preserve dimension, integrality, or arbitrary chosen parameter families.
5. Historical/current leakage: source open statements establish the printed historical claim. A bounded fresh literature search can find a positive matching theorem; its negative result cannot prove worldwide absence, earliest priority, or continued openness.
6. Type/provenance leakage: UNSOLVED 1/5 readiness with checked obstructions is an obstruction/audit artifact, not a solved theorem or paper. Original attempts and raw corpus attribution must be distinguished from later checkpoints, model names, and cached search summaries.

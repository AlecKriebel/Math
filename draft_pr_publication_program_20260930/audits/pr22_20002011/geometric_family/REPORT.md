- **Correct hypothesis and class.** `S=(Delta |P|^2)dV` is a parity-even polynomial natural density with two curvature factors and two derivatives, of scalar weight -6 in dimension six. The integral vanishes on every closed oriented six-manifold by Stokes' theorem. It therefore satisfies the hypothesis even more strongly than requested. It uses no noncompactness, boundary, nonpolynomial curvature expression or noncritical-weight loophole.
- **Density rather than scalar.** Conformal variation includes the volume term. In dimension six this gives `D=-4 div(B grad)-2 Delta T`, where `B=|P|^2` and `T=P^{ij} Hess_ij`. All zeroth-order conformal-weight terms cancel. The first summand and the scalar Laplacian are symmetric; the skew part is `-2(Delta T-T*Delta)` with `T*h=nabla_j nabla_i(P^{ij}h)`. All integrations by parts are on a closed manifold.
- **Necessity of the test.** Fixed affine directions in a conformal class give symmetric mixed derivatives for any allowed local-density functional. Standard critical Q has a symmetric linearization, while pointwise invariant densities have zero variation. The required sum must therefore have symmetric linearization. The integral of `S` being identically zero proves only `D*1=0`; it does not give `D=D*`.
- **Actual global geometry.** A smooth radial cutoff in a coordinate ball of the flat six-torus makes the cubic `x1 x2 x3` a smooth global function. Exponentiating it gives a positive smooth metric globally conformal to the flat metric. Choosing the same cutoff cubic as a conformal direction is allowed. The polynomial is never assumed periodic without its cutoff.
- **No hidden connection terms.** At the center, the conformal factor and its first and second derivatives vanish. Christoffel symbols and their first derivatives vanish, as does curvature. Consequently `P=0`, `nabla P=-partial^3 f`, and the covariant third derivatives of the test function equal its partial third derivatives. Every potentially troublesome connection term is multiplied by an explicitly vanished lower jet. No unjustified interchange of general fourth covariant derivatives is used.

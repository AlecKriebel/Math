# Primary proof and scope adapters

Complete relevant source bodies were independently retrieved after the first-pass derivation was sealed, except the original target, which was retrieved and read before that seal. Exact bytes, dates, versions, URLs and proof locators are in `ORIGINAL_SOURCE_RECEIPT.json` and `PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json`. All foreign PDFs and rendered pages remain in ignored `tmp/`; they are not redistributed with this audit.

1. **AIM, 15 October 2003.** Printed pp. 16–18 and 28 were read completely. Printed pp. 17 and 28 were also rendered and visually checked. The local-density primitive condition is separate from the two-metric cocycle discussion, and separate from the arbitrary-divergence conditions. Both target occurrences lack a formal-self-adjointness assumption. The discussion on p. 18 describes the critical polynomial contraction class; the candidate's two curvatures and two derivatives belong to it. Original Q density has a symmetric critical linearization. This family does not need a converse inverse-variational theorem.

2. **Branson, published 2005.** This is the 46-page DML copy, comprising a bibliographic cover plus printed pp. 11–55. Definition 2, Remarks 6–12, Proposition 13 and Conjecture 14 on printed pp. 32–34 were read as complete arguments; printed p. 34 was visually inspected. They explain the difference between invariant integrals and symmetric conformal linearizations. The curvature and connection formulas (44)–(46), the basis (47), and the six-dimensional calculation on printed pp. 35–40 were read. Printed p. 40 was visually inspected, including the complete equations (53)–(54) and the subsequent divergence representative. Remark 18 on p. 41 provides the conformally flat specialization. The digitized cover and article header establish publication in 2005, despite the 2004 workshop filename.

   The relevant published density is the double contraction `nabla^c(P_ab|c P^ab)`. By metric compatibility it equals half of `nabla^c nabla_c |P|^2`. Thus the candidate uses twice that published representative with its stated Laplacian convention. Branson's positive-Laplacian notation can be read from `B1=-J Delta J`; converting to the candidate's `Delta=nabla^c nabla_c` may change how the Laplacian is named but does not change this identity or nonvanishing. At the harmonic cubic jet, the third-order term in (54) is -12 for the initially chosen representative. The subsequent divergence has the opposite skew part, giving 12, and doubling gives the candidate's 24. This checks the exact representative rather than relying on a dimension-count statement alone.

   Branson's repaired Conjecture 14 uses a space of generalized Q-type densities and a symmetric-linearization hypothesis. It is a different assertion from the literal AIM sentence. The present record does not certify its current worldwide status.

3. **Case–Lin–Yuan, arXiv:1711.05579v1, 15 November 2017, 36 pages.** Section 2, printed pp. 6–7, was read for the natural-scalar, conformal-variation and gradient definitions, including (2.3)–(2.4). Its scalar linearization differs from the full density linearization by multiplication by the scalar invariant times the dimension. That additional multiplication is symmetric, so the necessary skew test is equivalent. Section 8.4, printed pp. 30–32, was read through the complete proofs of Lemmas 8.1–8.2 and Proposition 8.3. In particular the conformal gradient of the integral of `J^3` is `-3 Delta J^2` in dimension six, agreeing with the candidate's Ricci conversion. This source is supporting structure, not the decisive priority source.

4. **Alexakis, SIGMA 7 (2011), 019, 41 pages, DOI 10.3842/SIGMA.2011.019.** The complete Theorem 1.1 and its hypotheses were read on printed pp. 1–2. Its conclusion includes an arbitrary natural divergence and does not assert that every such divergence is a conformal gradient. For the candidate, its own displayed divergence supplies that component. The theorem is therefore compatible with the counterexample. This audit did not independently reproduce the entire multi-paper general decomposition proof; the boundary comparison needs only the exact theorem statement.

All source comparisons support a known published negative answer to the literal target. No inference about novelty is made from missing search results, and no communication with outside individuals was initiated.

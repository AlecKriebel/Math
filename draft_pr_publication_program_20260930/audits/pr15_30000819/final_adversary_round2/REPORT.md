## Independently verified theorem

Let A be a finite set of distinct selected lattice points, with homogenized
columns a_i=(v_i,1), S=N A, generated group G=Z A, and saturation
Sbar=cone(A) intersect G. Measure the normalized volume V of P=conv(A) in
the affine lattice generated by A. If H=Sbar minus S is finite and nonempty,
its last height satisfies

`h <= 2 V^2 (V-1)^2 - 2`.

The full mathematical reconstruction is in the sealed first pass. Its critical
checks and implications are as follows.

1. If a column is outside the span of the others, its real coefficient is
   unique; group membership makes it an integer and cone membership makes it
   nonnegative. The semigroup, group, and saturation split along that column.
   Thus holes split as H' times N. Any hole propagates to infinitely many heights.
   Finite nonempty holes exclude every free column. A normal pyramid is allowed
   to have no holes; it is the simultaneous finiteness and nonemptiness that
   matters.
2. Circuits span the rational relation space by induction on support. For a
   primitive homogeneous circuit, its signed coefficient sums agree and equal
   its degree; nonzero integer entries bound support by twice that degree.
   The primitive degree is at most intrinsic V. A circuit basis of c=n-d-1
   vectors covers every coordinate because an uncovered coordinate would be
   free. Therefore `n<=2Vc`.
3. A triangulation using every selected point exists by successive insertion
   into the containing simplex face and its incident simplices. The dual graph
   of top simplices is connected inside a convex polytope. A parent-first
   spanning-tree order introduces at most one vertex per later simplex.
   Consequently `n<=d+t`. Positive integral normalized simplex volumes sum
   to V, so `t<=V` and `c<=V-1`. Unselected lattice points are never inserted
   by fiat.
4. The toric regularity bound is `reg(I_A)<=nVc`, with regularity of the
   ideal. Primitive Graver degree is at most Vc; reduced toric Groebner bases
   lie in the Graver basis. Every Taylor least common multiple has each
   exponent at most Vc and total degree at most nVc. Degeneration supplies
   the same bound for the original homogeneous ideal. This requires neither
   affine/projective normality nor a complete lattice-point configuration.
5. Normalization is finite graded over R=C[S]. It need not be generated in
   degree one. Every positive saturation monomial has a positive power in S;
   hence the radical of the extended irrelevant ideal is Rbar_+. Hochster
   Cohen-Macaulayness, dimension at least two, and domain H0 vanishing give
   `H0(Rbar)=H1(Rbar)=0` for that support. Finite holes make C=Rbar/R finite
   length with end C=h. The exact sequence identifies `C=H1(R)`, so
   `reg(R)>=h+1`. Since I_A is nonzero, its resolution is the positive part
   of the quotient resolution, giving `reg(I_A)=reg(R)+1`.

It follows that

`h <= reg(I_A)-2 <= nVc-2 <= 2V^2 c^2-2 <= 2V^2(V-1)^2-2`.

## Exact claims and initial verdict

The theorem to test is distance-two exclusion for the explicitly defined class C: edge paths, together with non-edge vertex-to-first-vertex paths having an even number N of face pieces and transported endpoint-angle difference 2pi/5. Length is unbounded and self-intersections allowed. A separate proposition asserts that the imported bare angle condition has exactly the two first-face diagonals as additional solutions for each fixed starting face and vertex. These are distinct statements. The available primary printing does not force the adopted disambiguation at exceptional directions; claiming to have authenticated Fuchs's intent would be unwarranted. The manuscript explicitly avoids that claim.

The unbounded proof currently appears sound. Its potentially fatal steps are addressed as follows. Every normalized odd face is P and every even face is Q up to translation, since reflecting in any pentagon edge changes orientation while its rotational factors are pentagonal symmetries. For even N, the supplementary terminal-angle convention identifies the *corner sector* q1, not just the common singular point. h(q1)=p0 and Dh=-I therefore send the reversed terminal germ to the same specified initial P-sector germ. Unique continuation until the first singularity is legitimate despite the 6pi cone angle of the double pentagon. The midpoint is regular and fixed by h; h swaps polygon interiors and reverses the parameter on each glued edge, hence the midpoint lies at an open-edge midpoint. A polygon-respecting translation projection cannot send an ordinary interior point to that edge or an ordinary edge point to the singularity. Pulling back the edge parameter gives a physical midpoint. The solid's actual edge-axis half-turn has intrinsic derivative -I there and reverses the parameterized physical geodesic, so it exchanges the endpoints. A distance-two pair has a unique common neighbor by girth five; the half-turn would fix that neighbor, impossible because the rotational vertex stabilizer has order three. No finite computation is a premise.

I also derived a quotient-free attack: write the last face as c+Q and use its terminal corner c+q1=B. Since Q=1-P, the last face is B-P. Point reflection z -> B-z then identifies the reversed terminal face and sector with the initial face and sector. Unique sequential development forces symmetry of the whole face chain. Even N puts the middle on the common edge of the two middle face occurrences, whose endpoints are exchanged. This independently tests the projection/stratum step and produces the same physical midpoint, with no assumption that *all* double-pentagon saddle connections are symmetric.

Odd N plus the bare difference relation forces alpha to be an integer multiple of pi/5. All four permitted rays already hit a first-face vertex; two are edges and two are proper diagonals. The beta values are 2pi/5,3pi/5,4pi/5,pi. There is no room for an unbounded odd-N exception, repeated vertex continuation, or a boundary-edge trajectory of length beyond the adjacent vertex.

The claimed 16-face witness must still be recomputed: validity and its squared length are logically separate from its claimed type and displayed A+B eta formula. The manuscript claims the transported terminal ray 2->16 gives 144 degrees/type A1; the outward physical ray 2->10 is a different convention. The literal coordinate norm discrepancy follows directly from eta+conj(eta)=phi and is independent of all unfolding computations.

## Exact target and source distinction

The target is a square Dirichlet-Poisson load f in H^-1 outside L2 whose solution lies in A^(1/2) for one compatible labeled shape-regular newest-vertex-bisection family, while E_N(f), the infimum of global H^-1 distance over all budget-N admissible meshes and every real P0 coefficient, is bounded below by c log(N)/sqrt(N).

The original OWR contribution includes s=1/2, asks for existence of P0 dual-norm approximants, and separately discusses their construction. CDN's B^s class is instead defined using the localized data norm D(f,T), not E_N(f). An assertion that a large D(f,T) alone implies a large best P0 error would be false. The decisive later source applies D to f-g with both mesh and arbitrary-P0 infima, eliminating that mismatch.

## Direct prior-art overlap

Lemma21 minimizes the local H^-1 distance of f-g over every P0(M) and every allowed budget mesh. Lemma4(ii) says D_M(ell)<=sqrt(3)||ell||_global for any functional ell. Taking ell=f-g and the two infima immediately gives E_N(f)>=L_N N^-1/2/sqrt(3), with L_N tending to infinity. This is the actual global endpoint disproof, not merely localized estimator failure.

In dimension2, KV(3.19b) is (6/|e|)lambda_A lambda_B(1-5lambda_C) on either incident triangle. Multiplying it by2|e|/3 gives exactly the PR polynomial4lambda_A lambda_B-20lambda_A lambda_B lambda_C. The audited record does not explicitly display a matching lower bound L_N>=c log N. Nevertheless the same old allocation sum gives n^2*4^-n by weighted Cauchy, and the identical old bubbles give E_(4^n)>=(cn-C)2^-n. Interpolation yields the claimed logarithmic lower bound. ## Adversarial findings and limits

The localization inequality has the correct direction and requires no Galerkin orthogonality. Finite-mesh P0(M) has no a priori coefficient cap: the L-infinity definition merely means that any chosen finite collection of real coefficients is allowed. One labeled forest suffices and the overlays stay in it. The exact-zero tail observation can improve presentation or constants, but the old reverse-triangle tail estimate already proves the same asymptotic rate.

A compressed step in KV's proof uses a best-mesh energy estimate after fixing an arbitrary mesh. A naive subtraction of infima would fail. Overlay with a good solution mesh supplies a fixed budget factor and repairs that quantifier detail. The independent jump/bubble deduction also bypasses it.

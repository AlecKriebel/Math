## Exact prior implication about centers

If X_n/n converges in distribution to (delta_A+delta_B)/2, with A!=B, take disjoint fixed neighborhoods of A,B of radius |A-B|/8. Each receives probability tending to 1/2. For every fixed M, an interval [c_n-M sqrt(n),c_n+M sqrt(n)] divided by n has length 2M/sqrt(n), so eventually intersects at most one neighborhood. Thus its probability is at most 1/2+o(1), uniformly in c_n. This proves non-tightness for every deterministic center from the prior boundary mixture. In the candidate's source, the boundary t=F(01 000...)=7/8 has endpoint strings 00 111... and 01 000..., whose m-values are 9/4 and 7/4. Proposition 2.5 is an exact applicable special case for this boundary. Arbitrary deterministic centering at a boundary is therefore not a new conclusion.

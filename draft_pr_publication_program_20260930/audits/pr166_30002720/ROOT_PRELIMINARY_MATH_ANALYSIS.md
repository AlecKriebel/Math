## Independent rederivation and attempts to falsify

The ordered cylinder intervals have exact probability lengths and refine by the conditional transition probabilities. Their maximum lengths tend uniformly to zero. Uniform coding consequently has the stated Markov law, is order preserving away from the countable endpoints, and transports sample order statistics to the source's ordered strings. The source's exact count formula includes the root bucket and discards counts at most one. This is the actual singleton-stopping convention, directly verified against the primary definitions.

For any two words sharing a nonempty prefix w, the remaining profile sums each lie between zero and 3 pi(w): the total conditional tail mass is bounded by q/(1-q)=3. Thus their profile values differ by at most 3 pi(w), not twice that bound. The square-position word has infinitely many zeros and ones, so it is interior to every prefix cylinder and its rank is a continuity point of the profile.

Counting the isolated ones before position j^2 gives 2j-4 changes and j^2-2j+2 stays in W_j. Hence P_j=(1/2)(1/4)^(2j-4)q^(j^2-2j+2). At the adjacent endpoint words W_j0 followed by ones and W_j1 followed by zeros, the profile additions after the common prefix are respectively 2q P_j and 2(1-q)P_j. Their difference is P_j precisely for q=3/4. This identity would fail for other persistence probabilities; a stationarity-preserving mutation to q=4/5 detects that dependence.

The left local cylinder W_j0 1^K has length P_j q^K/4, and the right W_j1 0^K has length P_j q^K/12, for K>=1. The latter coefficient contains two change probabilities and K-1 stays; missing that first transition would be a factor-three error. The construction has 2j zeros before the next square, so 0<t-tau_j<P_j q^(2j)/12. Summing lexicographic left-child masses proves tau_j=sum_(i=2)^j q P_i and the infinite rank series. Its positivity and t<1/2 follow already from the first zero and the later ones; it is not any boundary.

With w_j=P_j q^j, K_j=floor(j/2) and n_j=ceil(w_j^(-2)), the windows fit strictly inside both local cylinders eventually, sqrt(n_j)w_j tends to one, sqrt(n_j)P_j>=q^(-j), and log n_j=O(j^2). The ceiling error is bounded by one and does not change any limit. Profile errors in both windows are at most P_j q^K_j, which are negligible relative to the jump.

For the empirical CDF, centered Bernoulli moment-generating functions have second log derivative at most1/4 under every exponential tilt. The elementary two-tail bound at the n^2+1 grid points, followed by monotonicity, gives failure probability at most4 n^(-2), including the mesh correction n^(-2). Every cylinder count is bounded by the two-endpoint discrepancy2 nD_n. A count and its singleton-truncated count differ by at most one. The union bound on colliding pairs at depth H_n uses sum pi(w)^2<=max pi(w), giving n^2 q^(H_n-1)/4. On the complement, all deeper costs vanish simultaneously for every string. The remaining deterministic profile tail is at most2 q^(H_n-1). Combining these facts yields the asserted uniform cost error O_P(sqrt(n)(log n)^(3/2)). No independence between this high-probability event and the selected order statistic is required.

The order-statistic CLT follows from the exact binomial tail identity with parameter x=t+z/sqrt(n). Uniform boundedness of Bernoulli summands gives the triangular-array CLT, since x tends to the interior t and the variance converges to t(1-t)>0. The threshold's floor-plus-one error divided by sqrt(n) vanishes. Along n_j both endpoints of each rank window converge after scaling to -1,0 or0,1, because (t-tau_j)/w_j tends to zero. The two probabilities therefore tend to the same strictly positive rho.

The cost error divided by n_j P_j tends to zero, bounded by a constant times j^3 q^j. Intersecting either rank event with the common high-probability cost event loses at most its vanishing failure probability. Two deterministic cost bands of half-width n_j P_j/8 each retain probability rho-o(1), and their separation is3 n_j P_j/4. Every bounded square-root-scale interval, regardless of its deterministic center, misses one complete band eventually. This supplies a uniform positive escape probability along the subsequence and defeats tightness for each centering sequence. It establishes failure for the full n-sequence, rather than only failure of convergence along a chosen subsequence.

For the claimed example of expectation centering, expectations are finite: if L_n is the largest shared-prefix length of any pair, Y_n(k)<=n(L_n+1), and P(L_n>=d)<=n^2 q^(d-1)/4 for d>=1. Summing this geometric bound, capped at one, gives E L_n=O(1+log n). This small supporting observation makes the expectation-centering example fully explicit.

## Object distinctions
A quenched scalar LDP fixes/conditions on a direction sequence and studies the scalar draw; an annealed scalar LDP averages the direction as well; a direction-law LDP studies the direction or its sorted absolute coordinates; the target studies the distribution of the **conditional probability law** as a point inP(R). An existing directionLDP closes the target only after a proven continuous map and well-defined contracted rate. A theorem for fixed projection dimensionk does not automatically cover growingk(N); neither the stated target norJKP requires that extension. The adjective 'weak' in weak topology does not mean weakLDP:JKP TheoremA asserts a fullLDP in weak topology with a goodrate.

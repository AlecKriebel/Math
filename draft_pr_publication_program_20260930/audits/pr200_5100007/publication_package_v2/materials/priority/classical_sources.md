## Leads not promoted to evidence

- Poncelet's 1822 treatise and Darboux 1870/1872 papers were located through bibliographies. The entire original works were not exhaustively inspected. Fouché's attribution to Poncelet is reported as Fouché's attribution; an exact original Poncelet page for the polar-chain statement was not independently verified.
- G. Loria, *I poligoni di Poncelet* (1889), 50 pages, was found in contemporary/modern catalogue references but no complete primary copy was obtained. It remains an unclosed bibliographic lead, not evidence for or against exact coverage.
- Hamilton's *Lectures on Quaternions* (1853), §§379–393, was referenced by C5. Its full focal relations were not independently read. C5 explicitly identifies the double-contact setting; no k115 implication was established from it.
- Search results for modern Darboux–Blaschke, elliptic-billiard, Poncelet-grid, and numerical-range papers were treated only as routing/bibliographic leads. This family did not duplicate the separate modern-history audit.

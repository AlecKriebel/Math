Closing without merging or publishing a preprint: **no novel research contribution has been established**.

The ordinary smooth conditional tightness theorem is a classical corollary of Dathe–Khoule's 2012 affine-deformation criterion (Definition 2.1/Theorem 2.2, pp.102–103), the Eliashberg–Thurston tightness neighborhood theorem, and finite smooth Gray stability. Under `d alpha = alpha wedge omega`, the mixed coefficient is zero, so the prior criterion gives the same contact-plane pencil; a finite endpoint near the taut foliation is tight, and Gray stability transfers tightness to the given smooth contact form. The finite-interval approximation for C1 alpha is a routine regularization of this argument.

Our longer supplement provides explicit C1-form/C2-disk smoothing details and verification artifacts. Those assertions were checked, but their historical novelty has not been established. They do not provide a verified novel resolution warranting publication under this project's research goal.

This is a novelty-based closure, not a finding that the corrected mathematics is invalid. The ordinary smooth conditional result is already implied by established results.

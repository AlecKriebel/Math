## Exact theorem and physical step

Let a>b>0, 0<lambda<b², alpha²=a²-lambda, beta²=b²-lambda. For a physical billiard of least period N>=3, signed original area A times signed center-pedal area B, feet taken to the complete ORIGINAL side lines, is phase independent when N is odd or divisible4. Every coprime star winding and either direction is included.

RGK Table3 k203,b gives N not congruent2mod4 and M=O but does NOT literally state least period. This report preserves the least-period qualification as an explicit interpretation, not a quotation of the table. Its necessity is proved below.

Put k²=(a²-b²)/alpha² and k'=beta/alpha. The strict hypotheses imply 0<k,k'<1. Choose unique v in(0,K) with cn(v)=beta/b. Then sn²(v)=lambda/b² and alpha*dn(v)/cn(v)=a. Consequently the canonical coordinates and step are
P(w)=(-a sn(w),b cn(w)), delta=2v in(0,2K).
For the caustic tangent n(u).X=1, n(u)=(-sn(u)/alpha,cn(u)/beta), substitution with the addition formula gives n(u).P(u-v)=n(u).P(u+v)=1. The numerator reduces to sn²(u)dn²(v)+cn²(u)=1-k²sn²(u)sn²(v). These are exactly the two intersections of that tangent with the real outer ellipse.

At P(w), the incoming and outgoing contacts are w-v and w+v. They are distinct, and these are all two real tangents to the strictly interior caustic. Confocal tangents obey physical reflection: explicitly, writing P=(a cos t,b sin t), rho²=a²sin²t+b²cos²t, T=(-a sin t,b cos t), U=(b cos t,a sin t), the two directed unit velocities are
[r*T +/- sqrt(lambda)*U]/rho², r=sqrt(rho²-lambda)>0.
They have equal tangential and opposite normal components. They are tangent to the caustic since, for unit V, the identity
a² V_y²+b² V_x²-det(P,V)²=(U.V)²
reduces the tangency condition to (U.V)²=lambda. Thus the algebraic Poncelet step is the physical reflection step on each orientation component.

The real map P is one-to-one modulo4K (the Jacobi amplitude is strictly increasing). Therefore physical least period N is equivalent to N*delta=4K*tau with gcd(N,tau)=1 and 0<tau<N/2. This proves coverage of arbitrary primitive stars and excludes an unsupported torsion/physical-period identification. These formulas also check Stachel Theorem4.3/equation4.9 directly. The extracted p1613 shift paragraph has an apparent dn-sign typo; the correct dn(u+2K)=dn(u), separately checked in DLMF, is used here.

## Original area trace and its complete divisor

Let S(w)=sum(j=0..N-1) dn(w+j*delta).
The addition identities give
det(P(u-v),P(u+v))=2ab sn(v)cn(v)dn(u)/(1-k²sn²(v)sn²(u)),
dn(u-v)+dn(u+v)=2dn(v)dn(u)/(1-k²sn²(v)sn²(u)).
Summing the signed edge determinants yields A(w)=C_A*S(w), C_A=ab sn(v)cn(v)/dn(v)>0.

Set m=N for oddN and m=N/2 for evenN, ell=2K/m, p=iK'. The subgroup generated by delta modulo2K has orderm: delta/ell=2tau for oddN, tau for evenN, with the appropriate integer coprime tom. Hence S is even, ell-periodic, and anti-periodic under2iK'. It is meromorphic on X=C/(ell Z+4iK' Z).

Dn has simple poles at 2rK+(2s+1)iK'. Atp exactly N/m summands contribute, all with identical residue -i because their arguments differ by real periods2K. The total residue is -(N/m)i, not zero. At3p the residue is opposite. No other poles exist onX. Therefore S has precisely two simple poles.

At p+ell/2, the relation -(p+ell/2)+ell+2iK'=p+ell/2 and the even/anti characters force S=0. This point is not a pole; the same is true at3p+ell/2. The zero/pole degree theorem on a compact torus shows these are the entire zero divisor, each simple. Thus S(w)S(w+ell/2)=H is constant. It is positive on the real line since dn>0 there. No special geometry can merge the two poles or the listed zeros because ell,K'>0 and their real/imaginary differences are nonzero in the lattice.

## Pedal trace, all poles, multiplicities and explicit residue

The real Euclidean center foot is
Q(u)=n(u)/(n(u).n(u))
=(-alpha*k'²*sn(u)/dn²(u), beta*cn(u)/dn²(u)).
This follows from n.n=dn²(u)/beta². Complex continuation uses the bilinear dot product, not conjugation. It is a globally meromorphic expression; no square-root branch has been added.

Let T(u)=1/2 sum(j=0..N-1)det(Q(u+jdelta),Q(u+(j+1)delta)).
The physical pedal area is B(w)=T(w+v).

All possible poles can be checked explicitly. Near p=iK', shift identities give
sn(p+t)=1/(k sn t), cn(p+t)=-i dn t/(k sn t), dn(p+t)=-i cn t/sn t.
Therefore Q(p+t)=(alpha*k'²*sn t/(k cn²t), i*beta*dn t*sn t/(k cn²t)), which is holomorphic and O(t). Thus common sn/cn/dn poles are REMOVABLE for Q.

At r=K+iK', the exact transformed expression is
Q(r+t)=alpha/k*(cn t*dn t/sn²t, i*cn t/sn²t).
It is even int, has order2, and leading coefficient U0=alpha/k*(1,i). All its translates have the same orders/characters. These are the only remaining poles, because the only zeros ofdn are translates ofr, and they are simple.

If u+jdelta=r+t, the two incident area terms combine as
1/2 det(Q(r+t),Q(r+delta+t)-Q(r-delta+t)).
The neighbors are holomorphic: delta is strictly between0 and2K, so no real pole-to-pole shift occurs. Evenness about r makes the neighbor difference O(t); specifically it is2t Q'(r+delta)+O(t³). Thus the combined area pole has order at most1, with residue det(U0,Q'(r+delta)). This includes N3. For evenN there are N/m=2 pole indices, separated bym>=2; none are adjacent, and their contributions are equal. N4 hasm=2, delta=K: the neighbors are the removable zeros at common Jacobi poles, and the same expansion still applies. No generic-position or nonzero-neighbor assumption enters.

The shift characters are Q(u+2K)=-Q(u), Q(u+2iK')=diag(1,-1)Q(u). Hence T is2K-periodic and2iK'-anti-periodic. Cyclic reindexing makes itdelta-periodic, thereforeell-periodic. OnX its only possible poles are simple, atr andr+2iK'. Matching residues with S(u+K) and using compactness/anti-periodicity proves T(u)=C_T*S(u+K).

The coefficient is explicit. Write q_z=Q_x+iQ_y. The transformed expression simplifies exactly to
q_z(r+t)=-alpha*k*cn(t)/(1+dn(t)).
Using sn'=cn*dn, cn'=-sn*dn, dn'=-k²sn*cn,
q_z'(r+t)=alpha*k*sn(t)*(dn(t)+k'²)/(1+dn(t))².
The residue of T atr is (N/m)det(U0,Q'(r+delta)); the residue of S(u+K) is -(N/m)i. Their ratio is
C_T=i det(U0,Q'(r+delta))=(alpha/k)q_z'(r+delta)
=alpha² sn(delta)*(dn(delta)+k'²)/(1+dn(delta))²>0.
Here sn(delta)>0 for0<delta<2K and dn(delta)>0. Multiplicity N/m cancels exactly. This derivative algebra is also a symbolic polynomial control. In particular there is no actual zero-pedal-area exceptional case in this proper real domain, although allowing C_T=0 in the submitted proof was harmless.

## Parity, boundaries and exact repeated-list counterexample

A(w)B(w)=C_A*C_T*S(w)*S(w+K+v).
For oddN, (K+v)/ell=N/2+tau is a half-integer.
For N=0mod4, m=N/2 is even andtau odd; (K+v)/ell=(m+tau)/2 is a half-integer.
For N=2mod4, m,tau are odd, so that expression is an integer.
Thus the eligible products equal C_A*C_T*H, universally. For excluded primitive parity the product equals C_A*C_T*S(w)² and is NONCONSTANT. If it were constant over any real interval, the meromorphic identity theorem would make S² constant onX, contradicting its poles. Reversal changes both signed areas, preserving the product. There are no divisions by A orB in this proof.

The proper hypotheses exclude k=0, k=1 and v=0,K; no assertion about circular, collapsed, hyperbolic, nonconfocal, moving-point or segment-projection extensions is used.

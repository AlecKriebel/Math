## Inputs used in the proof

The canonical parametrization is [Stachel, On the motion of billiards in ellipses](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2), European Journal of Mathematics8(2022),1602-1622, Theorem4.3/equation4.9, printedp1614. The published page was read and visually checked while recovering the neighboring k115 target; this result uses the same verified primary source, not the k115 theorem.

A complete published PDF is available at the author's university repository:
https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/136087/1/Stachel-2022-European%20Journal%20of%20Applied%20Mathematics-vor.pdf . The catalogue/filename mislabels the journal; the PDF and DOI supply the correct European Journal of Mathematics attribution.

Jacobi facts are checked against [DLMF22.4](https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.4), [addition formulas22.8](https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.8), and [derivatives22.13](https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.13). The meromorphic dot product used for analytic continuation is bilinear, agreeing with real orthogonal projection on the real line; it is not a Hermitian expression. The compact-torus pole argument is standard complex analysis. Earlier complex approaches to billiard invariants are credited to [Akopyan-Schwartz-Tabachnikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02934).

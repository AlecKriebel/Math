## Proof comparison and actual mathematical increment

PR204's preserved proof §§3–6 reduces original area to the cyclic Jacobi dn trace S. It passes to the reduced torus with real period ell=2K/m, counts two simple poles and two half-period zeros, and proves S(w)S(w+ell/2) constant. At a double pole of the central-foot parametrization Q, it groups the two incident signed-area terms. The reflected neighboring feet agree at the limiting phase, eliminating the apparent double part. Two-pole uniqueness plus imaginary anti-periodicity then identifies the pedal trace T(u)=C_T S(u+K). A parity calculation finishes.

The exact centered preprint pp2–4 first derives a velocity-product telescope and an explicit central-pedal scalar trace sum(dn/W). It then identifies two disjoint pole sets precisely for the allowed parity, uses odd pairing at vertex poles and reversed-edge pairing at scalar-trace poles, and makes the area product entire and elliptic. These are different organizations of a shared meromorphic strategy, not evidence of different or independent discovery.

The pointwise identity

    dn(u+K-v)+dn(u+K+v)=2ab dn(u)/W(u)

and closed cyclic reindexing convert its scalar trace exactly to PR204's shifted dn trace. This gives the identical positive C_T (TRACE_COMPARISON.md), so an explicit coefficient is not a verified new formula. Sixteen pointwise and nine closed-orbit diagnostics, including stars and excluded parity, support the algebra; they are not interval proof certificates.

The later arbitrary-point preprint DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23400541 , pp9–11, also uses the same adjacent-foot double-pole cancellation (Lemma4.3). Its TheoremA at M=O overlaps the4k portion; its text explicitly credits the earlier centered note for odd/4k origin cases. Thus isolating the adjacent-term cancellation does not presently justify a new-method claim either.

Older primary lineage also matters: Chavez-Caliz's Theorems3/6 prove the even original/outer tangent-polygon area product by complementary poles/zeros on an elliptic curve, while AST cancels neighboring singular contributions and applies Liouville to different invariants.

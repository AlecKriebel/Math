## Additional related coefficient criteria

[Noor–Khan–Piejko, Alpha Convex Functions Associated with Conic Domains, IJAA 11(2) (2016), 70–80](https://www.etamaths.com/index.php/ijaa/article/view/738/210): the primary PDF's indexed text supplies Definition 3 and Theorem 1. Its parameter range is 0<=alpha<=1, and the ordinary class is included by choosing the half-plane case. It gives coefficient sufficient conditions, so this package makes no claim to introduce coefficient criteria to this literature. The direct PDF download returned HTTP403, while the primary PDF was readable through indexed web access. No unverified formula from this paper is needed for the proof.

## Exact use of prior work

Kumar–Ravichandran explicitly display the two-denominator majorant used here, and apply it to radius questions with prescribed coefficient envelopes. Their published estimate and majorization mechanism are credited; the candidate's scalar reduction and all-real-alpha criterion are proved directly. The 2026 preprint was read for its definition and theorem statements as current context, not used as a proof dependency or independently recertified. The source credits the endpoint tests to Clunie–Keogh, *On starlike and convex schlicht functions*, JLMS 35 (1960), 229–233; the present proof independently recovers those endpoints.

The 2018 note that no progress had been reported to the editors is historical and does not establish present openness or priority. The candidate does not claim a necessary-and-sufficient coefficient region, nor optimality outside its explicitly stated family and parameter range.

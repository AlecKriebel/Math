## Full-scope older mathematical consequence

Its title does not limit this sufficient theorem to negative coefficients. The negative restriction is needed for its converse.

The full Mocanu–Reade1975 paper, The Radius of Alpha-Convexity for the Class of Starlike Univalent Functions, Alpha Real, Proc.AMS51(2),395–400, DOI10.1090/S0002-9939-1975-0374404-5, printed397 gives the alpha-convexity radius of the entire starlike class:

R_alpha=(1+alpha)-sqrt((1+alpha)²-1) for alpha>=0;
R_alpha=sqrt[(2-sqrt(-alpha))/(2+sqrt(-alpha))] for -3<=alpha<=0;
R_alpha=-(1+alpha)-sqrt((1+alpha)²-1) for alpha<=-3.

The essential leading minus in the last line was checked visually after OCR omitted it. These formulas agree at -3, R_0=1, and 0<R_alpha<1 for every nonzero real alpha.

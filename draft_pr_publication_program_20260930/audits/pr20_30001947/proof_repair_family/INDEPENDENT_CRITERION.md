# Independent proof-repair criterion

Sealed before reading sibling-family reports. This is validation of existing published source, not a fresh attempt at the target theorem.

Target: compact PL pseudomanifold without boundary/codimension-one strata, integral orientation of regular stratum, mod-two Witt vanishing at every odd-codimension link, dimension 4j+2. The requested conclusion is zero Witt class of the nonsingular mod-two middle form. A stronger alternating-form conclusion is a sufficient route, not the definition of success.

A valid repair must supply a checkable existing proof of numerical odd-middle-square vanishing in precisely that category, with every coefficient/perversity operation typed. A theorem restatement or its dependent bordism corollary is evidence of published status, not an independent repair. Integral orientation gives a top-degree orientation/Bockstein fact; it must not silently supply a lower-middle Bockstein. Mod-two middle duality does not imply integral coefficient base change.

For the printed lift route, verify (1) integral representative lifting in the claimed perversity, (2) d(c)=2b with b remaining in the needed complex, (3) all cup-i/commutativity identities in their destination perversities, (4) valid passage to the top evaluation. For a modified upper-perversity lift, verify the type adapter independently: an integral upper lift and its divided boundary need not return to integral lower-middle. An F2 lower/upper comparison may permit reduction but cannot be assumed to solve the integral step.

Falsification control: the suspension of RP3 times S2 is an integrally oriented six-dimensional mod-two Witt space with only codimension-four singularities. Its middle mod-two pairing is expected hyperbolic, so it cannot falsify the target conclusion. Any claim of universal same-middle integral lifting must survive its torsion-sensitive coefficient comparison. A chainwise short exact sequence would force universal-coefficient reduction behavior; incompatible group dimensions falsify that premise.

Search boundary: inspect GP1989 relevant full proof pages and bounded primary literature on intersection Steenrod/Adem/Wu relations, coefficient adapters, and errata. Do not import ordinary Steenrod relations without intersection-cohomology hypotheses. Do not conclude absence of a repair in this bounded audit proves global openness or theorem falsity.

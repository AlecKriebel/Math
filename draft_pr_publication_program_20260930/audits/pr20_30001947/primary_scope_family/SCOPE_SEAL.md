## Immediate context and notation

- F is a field. “F-Witt” means PL stratified pseudomanifold with lower-middle-perversity middle link homology zero for even-dimensional links at odd-codimension strata. The report distinguishes lower and upper middle perversity, explaining the resulting self-duality.
- F2 is the field, rather than integers modulo two as a coefficient ring with different theory. The requested W(F2) is the Witt group of nonsingular symmetric bilinear forms, with its nontrivial class.
- The question is preceded by the failure of a singular-surgery argument in characteristic two: antisymmetry alone does not make self-intersections zero. A claimed injection from the 4j+2 F2-Witt bordism group into W(F2) leaves a geometric realization question.
- The source explicitly distinguishes integral orientation from F2 orientation. Real projective spaces supply examples under merely F2 orientation, and are excluded by Question2. Integral orientation means a compatible ordinary orientation on the top manifold stratum, reducing to the F2 fundamental class; it is not a request for integral Witt link vanishings.
- The source states compactness and PL stratification literally. It does not separately print “without boundary” or “no codimension-one strata” in Question2. These are inherited from its pseudomanifold/bordism and absolute nonsingular Poincare-duality context, not extra narrowing to be silently added. Their standard precise definition needs checking in the cited contemporary sources.
- j has no explicit quantifier/range in the printed question. Dimension4j+2 forces j>=0 for nonnegative dimensions. The special j=0 surface case and all j>=1 must be included in an exact negative answer.
- Question1 concerns topological production of isolated singularities for rational Witt spaces. It is a separate question and is not being marked resolved here. The paragraph following Q2 explains why isolated-singularity integrally oriented examples cannot realize the nonzero class. Thus any hypothetical Q2 example would need positive-dimensional singular set.
- The report contains notation slips: pairings in the introductory paragraph use L where the enclosing sentence has X, and the Siegel reference year is printed1934. These are not changes of Q2 scope.

## Original target and exact density

The complete [Bandeira 2013 post](https://afonsobandeira.wordpress.com/2013/11/01/a-conjecture-on-the-singular-values-of-a-gaussian-matrix/)
and complete [MIT 2015 Open Problem 1.2 handout](https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-s096-topics-in-mathematics-of-data-science-fall-2015/a9963e8f7bd9c10b4d48df8115f63116_MIT18_S096F15_Open1.2.pdf)
were read literally, including their quantifiers and field directions.
They ask for every positive integer square size: real increase and complex
decrease. The handout uses non-strict inequalities. A strict result covers
that target. The complex convention has component variances 1/2, so
E|X_ij|^2=1 and each standard complex entry has density pi^-1 exp(-|z|^2).

For G=X/sqrt(n), the exact statistic is
`alpha_K(n)=n^-1 E||G||_*=n^-3/2 E Tr(XX*)^(1/2)`.
The real adjoint is transpose. The complex Wishart weight is exp(-x),
while the real LOE weight is exp(-x/2); mixing those conventions creates
a factor sqrt(2). Square shape is alpha=0, not alpha=1. A positive fixed
shape means a fixed excess of columns, not a fixed total number of columns.
Noninteger shape defines a density, not a noninteger-sized matrix.

The square expected counting density is
`rho_n(x)=exp(-x) sum_{j=0}^{n-1} L_j(x)^2`; its mass is n.
The target moment is `integral sqrt(x)rho_n(x) dx`, with no extra 1/n
inside this counting-density expression. Fresh exact controls check mass
n and first moment n^2 separately. They agree with the complex entry
variance and catch a hidden density normalization error.

## Universal complex proof and recurrence validity

The complete three-page [Baslingker-Dan v1 paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2608.27532v1)
was read, and its cited recurrence was inspected in the actual published
[Cunden-Mezzadri-O'Connell-Simm paper](https://maths.ucd.ie/~noconnell/pubs/cmos19.pdf),
pp. 1107-1108, Theorem 4.4, equation (4.11), and the remark after (4.16).
The actual [Gupta-Ismail-Masson equation (2.2)](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/S0008414X00008889)
was read and visually checked at printed p. 1266. These were full proof
inputs, not an abstract-only source check.

The independent square route is stronger than checking the citation:
finite Laguerre connection and orthogonality produce
`H(z)=Gamma(s+1)z(1-z)^(-s-2)2F1(-s,-s;1;z)` coefficientwise.
The coefficient ratio of this 2F1 gives an ODE. Substitution and coefficient
extraction give, for EVERY n>=1,

`Q_(n+1)(s)=(2+s(s+1)/n^2)Q_n(s)-Q_(n-1)(s)`, Q_0=0.

Thus at s=1/2 the cited coefficient is exactly 2+3/(4n^2), and the sign
of the previous-moment term is minus. This direct density reconstruction
does not depend on Carlson analytic continuation, an unproved equivalence,
or a finite sample of dimensions. The seal supplies all equations and the
formal-series justification. Each coefficient uses finite sums.

The separate finite 3F2 telescoping certificate validates the cited
dimension recurrence algebra for all n>=2 and real nonnegative shape.
It confirms the matching prefactor and all signs. At n=1, direct n=1,2
integration supplies the boundary; it is incorrect to assign meaning to
0 times a divergent nonterminating square-boundary hypergeometric series.
This subtle boundary does not invalidate the proof because its induction
starts from the separately computed initial increment.

Normalizing C_n=Q_n/n^(3/2) gives the decrement propagation in the seal.
Convexity of t^-1/2 proves
`(1+x)^(3/2)+(1-x)^(3/2)>2+3x^2/4` for 0<x<=1.
The initial values Q_1=sqrt(pi)/2 and Q_2=11sqrt(pi)/8 give C_2<C_1
by the exact integer comparison 121<128. Positivity and induction then
prove C_(n+1)<C_n for every n>=1. The full original complex direction is
independently verified under the exact original Gaussian convention.

The paper's printed phrase x>=0 overstates the real domain of that scalar
inequality; x=1/n always lies in [0,1]. No repair to the candidate's
operative claims is required because the candidate already states this
correct domain and separates finite diagnostics from universal proof.

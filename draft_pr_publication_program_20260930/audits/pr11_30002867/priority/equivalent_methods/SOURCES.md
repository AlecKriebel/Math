## Decisive sources

**S01 — Original target.** Boris Shekhtman contribution to *Multivariate Splines and Algebraic Geometry*, OWR 2015/21, DOI [10.4171/OWR/2015/21](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2015/21). [Publisher full PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46567), local `owr2015_21.pdf`. Cover title and workshop dates 19–25 April 2015 verified. Checked printed p. 1182 equation (1), p. 1183 Definition 5 and Problem 2. Definition is global `d`-generator affine finite-colength CI, not strict CI. This is the question, not priority evidence.

**S02 — Hartmut Wiebe.** *Über homologische Invarianten lokaler Ringe*, *Math. Ann.* **179** (1969), 257–274, DOI [10.1007/BF01350771](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01350771). Publisher metadata: received 18 June 1968, issue December 1969. [Original GDZ scan](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN235181684_0179/LOG_0075.pdf), local `wiebe_original.pdf`. Printed p. 258 defines `Fitt_0` through determinants of kernel columns and states presentation independence; printed p. 260 Satz 3 is the nonzero Fitting criterion for zero-dimensional local CI, with equality to the socle. The scan of p. 260 was visually inspected, as well as OCR. GDZ volume PPN235181684_0179, article LOG_0075, IIIF physical canvases 279–296 correspond to printed 257–274. This supplies the decisive original criterion.

**S03 — N. Mohan Kumar.** *On two conjectures about polynomial rings*, *Invent. Math.* **46** (1978), 225–236, DOI [10.1007/BF01390276](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF01390276). Publisher metadata: received 24 May 1977, revised 2 September 1977, issue October 1978. [Original GDZ scan](https://gdz.sub.uni-goettingen.de/download/pdf/PPN356556735_0046/LOG_0020.pdf), local `mohan_kumar1978.pdf`; downloaded URL checked byte-identical with original core-audit copy. Printed p. 234 visually checked. Theorem 4 directly bounds a locally CI polynomial ideal by the ambient variable count. Theorem 5 gives the stable conormal/global generator equality. Theorem 4, plus height, suffices for the Boolean original question; no later general conjectural statement is needed.

**S04 — Martin Kreuzer, Le Ngoc Long, Lorenzo Robbiano.** *Algorithms for Checking Zero-Dimensional Complete Intersections*. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.09563): v1 22 March 2019 15:42:45 UTC; v2 6 August 2019 05:57:50 UTC. Full [v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.09563v1) and [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1903.09563v2), local `klr_v1.pdf`, `klr_v2.pdf`; both full 24 pages. Verified Definition 3.1 p. 7, Proposition 3.2 p. 8, Proposition 3.3 pp. 8–10, Algorithm 3.4 p. 10, Algorithm 3.6 pp. 10–11, Proposition 3.7 p. 11, Remark 5.6 p. 22, references pp. 23–24. These locators are version-paper page numbers. Both versions have the decisive local affine algorithm. Journal: *J. Commut. Algebra* **14** (2022), no. 1, 61–76, DOI [10.1216/jca.2022.14.61](https://doi.org/10.1216/jca.2022.14.61). [University-hosted journal extract](https://csdlkhoahoc.hueuni.edu.vn/data/2022/11/2022-1-2_Algorithms_for_checking_zero-dimensional_complete_Intersections.pdf), local `klr_journal.pdf`, contains only seven pages and omits central Section 3. Its first page verifies received 22 March 2019, revised 5 August 2019. Official Project Euclid fulltext was not retrieved. No central algorithm's exact journal page is claimed verified.

**S05 — Anne-Marie Simon, Jan R. Strooker.** *Complete intersections of dimension zero: variations on a theme of Wiebe*. Manuscript dated 7 September 2006; [arXiv math/0703880](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0703880) v1 29 March 2007 13:17:11 UTC; [full PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0703880), local `simon_strooker.pdf`. ## Checked classical infrastructure and adjacent fulltexts

**S06 — Martin Kreuzer, Lorenzo Robbiano.** *Deformations of Border Bases*, *Collect. Math.* **59**(3) (2008), 275–297; [arXiv:0710.2641](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.2641), v1 14 October 2007. [Author PDF](https://www.uni-passau.de/fileadmin/dokumente/fakultaeten/fim/lehrstuhl/kreuzer/AlgebraicOil/Publications/073_Kreuzer_Robbiano_Deformations_of_Border_Bases.pdf), dated 18 January 2008, local `kr_border.pdf`. Definition 3.1 pp. 7–8: generic formal multiplication matrices and commutation/border-basis condition, citing their 2005 book Theorem 6.4.30. Theorem 3.4 pp. 8–10: universal border family free on the order ideal, quotient construction. Theorem 2.4 concerns degree-form deformation, so it is not mis-cited as the commuting criterion.

**S07 — Le Ngoc Long.** *Various Differents for 0-Dimensional Schemes and Applications*, dissertation title page June 2015. [German National Library full PDF](https://d-nb.info/1081246545/34), local `long_dissertation.pdf`. Checked printed pp. 89–91, Definition 3.3.10 and Lemma 3.3.11. These characterize projective CI through relative local CI/Artinian reduction; they are not asserted to directly classify arbitrary affine original ideals. Bibliography was inspected for earlier different sources.

**S08 — Kreuzer, Long, Robbiano.** *Computing subschemes of the border basis scheme*, [arXiv:1910.09426](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.09426), v1 21 October 2019, v2 26 August 2020; [v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1910.09426v2), local `klr_subschemes.pdf`. Published *Int. J. Algebra Comput.* **30** (2020), 1671–1716, DOI [10.1142/S0218196720500599](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218196720500599). Checked v2 Definition 8.1 pp. 24–25 and Algorithm 8.3 pp. 25–26: strict complete intersection locus. Version-specific numbering matters; in this v2 Algorithm 7.10 is a strict Cayley–Bacharach calculation, not the CI algorithm.

**S09 — J. L. Taylor.** *The analytic-functional calculus for several commuting operators*, *Acta Math.* **125** (1970), 1–38, DOI [10.1007/BF02392329](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02392329). [Full original article PDF at mathematical archive](https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/117/6094-11511_2006_Article_BF02392329.pdf), local `taylor1970.pdf`. Definition 1.2, printed pp. 4–5, is the Koszul cochain complex of commuting endomorphisms; discussion relates its degree-reversal to the earlier chain complex. This establishes classical operator-complex provenance, not an independent CI criterion. Related *A joint spectrum for several commuting operators*, *J. Funct. Anal.* **6** (1970), 172–191, DOI 10.1016/0022-1236(70)90055-8 was metadata-only; publisher access was forbidden.

**S10 — B. de Smit, H. W. Lenstra Jr.** *Finite complete intersection algebras and the completeness radical*, *J. Algebra* **196** (1997), 520–531. [Author full PDF](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~smitbde/papers/1997-j-algebra-desmit-lenstra.pdf), manuscript dated 3 February 1997, local `desmit_lenstra1997.pdf`. Checked introduction, Proposition 1.1, and Theorem 1.3. Finite-CI presentation existence and the Jacobian/completeness radical are adjacent; no exact fixed-tuple detector is attributed to them.

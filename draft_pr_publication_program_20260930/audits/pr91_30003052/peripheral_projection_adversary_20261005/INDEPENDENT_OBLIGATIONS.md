## Exact hypotheses and claims to falsify

Let X=C^k, k>=1, with any complex norm, let U be its closed unit ball,
let A be complex linear with ||A||<=1, and let Kf=f composed with A on complex C(U).
Let S be the eigenvalues of A on the unit circle, and let Gamma be the
multiplicative subgroup generated by S (Gamma={1} when S is empty).

The local claims are:

1. The spectral projection P onto the generalized peripheral subspace is a
   contractive projection, obtainable as an operator-norm limit of powers A^n.
2. If Kf=lambda f, f nonzero, and |lambda|=1, then f=f composed with P.
3. The unimodular point spectrum of K is exactly Gamma, including when S is
   empty or has repeated eigenvalues.
4. On the peripheral ball V=U intersect ran(P), the induced operator has full
   spectrum closure(Gamma). For the J-empty case only, adjoining a nonzero
   nilpotent stable part contributes exactly the spectral point 0.

Success means a checkable proof under the exact hypotheses, or a concrete
counterexample; numerical agreement alone cannot certify the claims.
The whole stable-open-disc classification is outside this family's scope.

## Adversarial obligations

- Power boundedness must exclude every nontrivial Jordan block at modulus 1.
- Recurrence must furnish unbounded indices with A_E^n tending to identity;
  an unbounded full sequence of powers need not converge.
- P must be contractive for the supplied norm, even if nonorthogonal in a
  chosen Euclidean coordinate system. Do not assume orthogonality.
- Orbit comparisons must stay inside U and be uniform in x. Compactness and
  uniform continuity, not unproved pointwise convergence, must justify them.
- A unimodular eigenfunction cannot be supported only off the peripheral ball.
- Necessity lambda in Gamma must use actual Gamma, not only its closure.
- Every polynomial used for approximation must be continuous on V, and
  negative exponents must be represented by conjugates, without division at 0.
- Dense Gamma and lambda in closure(Gamma) minus Gamma is a critical boundary:
  approximate eigenvalues need not be true eigenvalues.
- No invariant measure, ergodicity, or orbit-transitivity assumption is allowed.
- Inspect E={0}, A=0, A=I, roots of unity, irrational phases, repeated phases,
  stable Jordan blocks, nilpotent parts, and singular/invertible A.

## Independent mathematical conclusion recorded before source read

No counterexample to these local claims has been found. The following complete
mechanism appears to prove them: finite-dimensional power boundedness excludes
peripheral Jordan blocks; compact torus recurrence produces P as a subsequence
limit; stable orbit differences and uniform continuity give f=fP directly;
Stone-Weierstrass coordinate polynomials and Cesaro averages exclude every
unimodular lambda outside Gamma. The detailed proof is recorded separately.

This is an audit-family conclusion, not a conclusion about the whole PR,
source identity, originality, publication readiness, or human peer review.

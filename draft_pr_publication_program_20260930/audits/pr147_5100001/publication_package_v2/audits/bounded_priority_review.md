## Prior construction and what is newly combined

[Garcia–Reznik](https://doi.org/10.33039/ami.2022.02.001), published pp58,68–69, gives the simple four-periodic vertices, fixed caustic, area extrema and AA'=8a²b². The same explicit vertices occur in [their January 2021 v3 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06640v3), p18. Substitution x1=a cos t, y1=b sin t yields exactly PR147’s general P,Q,-P,-Q construction. Thus the construction and all-parameter pair are not new geometry.

Table1 p55 omits k107/k108. Its explicit counterexamples on pp58,62 and Section5 concern k804, a different focus-inversive quantity. Neither omission nor those failures constitutes a k107 correction. All 27 final-paper text pages were read; target pages were also viewed.

Our comparison deduction, rather than a quoted earlier theorem, is as follows. Set S=a²+b² and D=a²b². On the known family let h,k be the two perpendicular support distances. Reflection and elementary internal-angle geometry give half-angle product h²k²/S². With A=2D/(hk) and AA'=8D, it follows that

K = 2h⁴k⁴/(DS²) = 32D³/(S²A⁴).

Consequently the already known variation of A yields variation of K once this half-angle identity is supplied. This limits the correction’s mathematical novelty to a specific algebraic combination and its source-table diagnosis. We found no earlier explicit statement of that combination in the sources read. A new deduction from existing building blocks must not be represented as discovery of those blocks.

Its condition-3 paragraph initially treated a coherent embedded tree as enough to justify a simple representative of an equal-image quotient. That assessment is withdrawn. A geometric-family challenge arrived only after the early seal, original-file reading, and independent action reconstruction; it prompted the following separately checked correction.

Let x,y be coherent inner peripheral generators of the essential pants neighborhood of two independent handle meridians joined by a fixed tree arc. The third simple boundary represents (xy)^±1. The quotient xy^-1 is not conjugate to any of the essential simple-loop classes x^±1, y^±1, (xy)^±1 of these pants. Its abelianization (1,-1) distinguishes it from those classes. Since the pants is essential, give its boundary geodesic representatives in a hyperbolic metric; the geodesic representative of a class carried by the pants lies in its convex lift. Thus xy^-1 cannot acquire an ambient simple representative while retaining that free homotopy class. Reversing one oriented input does not prove the asserted fixed-tree quotient word is simple.

The C3 diagnostic is exact: x,y each have image 1 in additive C3, so xy^-1 has image 0 while the actual simple third boundary xy has image 2. With three coherent meridians in a genus-three block, all pair images can be equal and this issue persists. A cyclic genus-six surface cover can realize these images on the six handle meridians, with all dual generators killed. This is a counterexample to the literal construction step, not a counterexample to the sufficient-condition theorem or OWR target.

The ordered-prefix repair independently checks out. A planar neighborhood of m independent meridians joined by a tree has m+1 boundary circles. Choose a coherent, standard ordered peripheral basis x1,...,xm. Among m+1 images of ordered prefixes there is a repeated pair, and the corresponding nonempty contiguous product x_(i+1)...x_j is killed. Its simple representative encloses precisely that consecutive block of holes; its homology is a nonzero sum of distinct signed independent meridians. This proves the required nonseparating killed curve in the same genus-m block, without a quotient-band assumption or any nonabelian commutation.

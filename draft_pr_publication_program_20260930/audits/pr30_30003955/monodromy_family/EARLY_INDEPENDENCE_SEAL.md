## Independently reconstructed target

Problem 12, Dmitri Gekhtman, printed page 2528 asks for an embedded sphere with four disk interiors removed and essential boundary in every finite cover S_h -> S_g with h>g>=2 such that the interior of its inverse image is disconnected; it separately asks the three-boundary variant and permits restricting to regular covers. I interpret finite cover as a connected unbranched cover of closed oriented surfaces, as in the candidate. Euler characteristic gives h=1+d(g-1), so d>1. Restricting to regular covers or a numerical sufficient condition does not prove the general target.

## Mechanism and proofs fixed before computation

Let Gamma=pi_1(S_g), cover subgroup K<=Gamma, core C=intersection_gamma gamma K gamma^-1, G=Gamma/C, H=K/C. Use the left coset action Omega=G/H (an opposite-group identification gives the usual right-coset path convention). For a connected subsurface Y, lifted components are precisely the orbits of A=rho(pi_1(Y)) on Omega: points in one orbit lift a based loop to a connecting path; a path in a lifted component projects to a based loop realizing the orbit relation. Paths from the global to local base point conjugate A. A conjugate has the same number of orbits, by the fiber bijection x -> gx. The component count is |A\G/H|. Proper A is insufficient for an irregular action. If H=1 (regular action), count=[G:A]; left versus right multiplication and opposite identification do not alter that count.

An intermediate connected regular Q-cover corresponds to a normal subgroup N of G containing H, with Q=G/N. The equivariant fiber quotient gH -> gN is well-defined exactly when H<=N. Thus a cyclic prime quotient of nonperfect G provides an intermediate cover only if a nonzero character G -> C_p kills H. Nonperfectness alone does not ensure this for an irregular cover. Conversely every cyclic intermediate cover descends to G, since the core C is contained in K and hence in the kernel of the character. Disconnectedness in the intermediate cover implies disconnectedness upstairs: the quotient of fibers sends each A-orbit into a single quotient orbit and is surjective. Disconnectedness upstairs need not descend through a refinement.

Condition 1: a nonzero character chi on H_1(S_g;F_p) can be scaled to have a coordinate equal to 1 and lifted to a primitive integral class. Its Poincare dual is primitive and can be realized by a nonseparating simple curve c (via a symplectic basis/handle slides). A cut system containing c has planar complement F with 2g boundary circles; every loop in F has algebraic intersection zero with c, hence chi=0 there. Partition the 2g circles into four nonempty sets to obtain H with essential boundary (g=2: use F itself). The p-fold cyclic cover restricts to p disjoint copies of H. Every nonperfect finite regular group has nontrivial abelianization with a prime cyclic quotient. Every nontrivial finite solvable group is nonperfect by its derived series. These statements require regularity for the implication from group nonperfectness to factoring.

Condition 2: pi_1(H) is free of rank 3, so its image has at most 3 generators. The universal action hypothesis "every at-most-3-generated subgroup is intransitive" immediately works for any chosen essential H. For a regular G, d(G)>3 guarantees every such subgroup is proper. For pants replace 3 by 2; d(G)>2 suffices for regular covers. The hypotheses are sufficient, not necessary. They cannot be established by counting sampled subgroups or sampled subsurfaces. The assumption entails G itself is not 3-generated, but that weaker statement does not suffice in arbitrary actions, since proper subgroups can be transitive.

Condition 3: pigeonhole equalities on based handle generators can produce a band sum with trivial image. Conjugating a killed loop keeps it killed, but equality of images of freely represented loops without a coherent tree is inadequate. The candidate explicitly supplies a coherent tree, so this potential flaw is addressed. Two disjoint genus-|G| blocks produce two killed disjoint nonseparating curves; the planar cut-open genus-2-with-boundary region is killed. Its essential H construction relies on an independent topological verification of connected complement and essential new boundary. The stated g>=2|G| implies g>=4 and excludes a disk complement. Bounds |G|<=d! and |G|=d for regular actions give the advertised corollaries. I will not promote a finite group computation as proof of this topological universal result.

If H has essential boundary it is incompressible in S_g. A separating curve inside H cutting its four boundary circles into two pairs is nontrivial in H and hence essential in S_g. Pants on either side have image contained in A after a common base-point conjugacy. Restricting an intransitive action to a subgroup cannot merge orbits; thus four-boundary sufficient results imply pants sufficient results. Degree 1 would fail every disconnectedness conclusion and is correctly excluded by h>g.

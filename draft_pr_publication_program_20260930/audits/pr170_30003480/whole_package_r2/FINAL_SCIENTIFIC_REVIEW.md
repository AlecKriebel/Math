## Exact mathematical target and adversarial findings

For a real tensor T in (R^2) tensor n, mode i has a two-row flattening M_i. The coordinates are d_i=det(M_iM_i^T), with ||T||_F<=1. The universal assertion excludes every real polynomial P in these coordinates, with unrestricted degree, satisfying G_n={d in C_n:P(d)>=0}; it excludes the analogous equality relative to H_n. C_n is [0,1/4]^n and H_n is its intersection with d_i<=sum_{j!=i}d_j. Success requires a universal deduction, rather than finitely many examples, a failure of the particular Q1-Q2, or a statement in another coordinate system.

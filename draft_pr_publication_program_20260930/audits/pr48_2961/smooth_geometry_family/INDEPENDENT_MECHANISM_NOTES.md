Extending the four compactly supported commutator factors and their compact isotopies by the identity gives the ambient bound four only on stabilized elements.

5. A coordinate ball can be shrunk and moved, with a compact chart-supported smooth isotopy, into each of N pairwise disjoint sub-balls of a larger chart. A finite coordinate-ball cover excludes an invariant probability for the full positive-dimensional closed connected group, including atomic and singular measures. A connected zero-dimensional manifold is a point and has an invariant Dirac probability; the dimension condition is essential.
6. Compact manifolds have finitely many connected components. Identity-component diffeomorphisms preserve them. For perfect factors the commutator length in the finite product is exactly the maximum of component lengths: coordinate projections give the lower bound, padded simultaneous commutator factorizations give the upper bound.

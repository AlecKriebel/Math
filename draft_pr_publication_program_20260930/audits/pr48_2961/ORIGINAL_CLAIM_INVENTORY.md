## Original mathematical claims and exact gaps

1. **Imported perfectness and non-uniformity distinction.** Mather–Thurston perfectness gives finite ordinary lengths in the stipulated groups. The problem asks for unbounded finite lengths. These imported infinite-dimensional theorems have not been proved or source-verified by this preservation stage. The finite differentiability exception r=dimX+1 must not be used to answer the C-infinity target.
2. **Published no-middle-handle bound.** Tsuboi2012 Theorem1.1(2), printed p142, is claimed to bound width by4 for a compact even-dimensional manifold admitting a handle decomposition without middle-index handles, including four-dimensional no-2-handle manifolds. The new general theorem1.2 is stated to start in dimension6. The package claims S1xS3 has Morse/handle indices0,1,3,4 and #_k(S1xS3) has counts(1,k,0,k,1). This is a literature-credit correction to the K3 remark that only S4 is understood. No implication H2=0 => no2-handles is assumed. Fresh source-hypothesis and handle-construction verification remains required.
3. **Lemma1, displacement compression.** For H<=G and conjugates H,FHF^-1,...,F^mHF^-m commuting pairwise, h=c1...cm with ci=[ai,bi] and ai,bi in H is claimed to have ambient length<=2. T=conjugation byF; A=product T^i(ai), B=product T^i(bi) for i1..m; suffix si=c_(i+1)...cm for i0..m-1; C=product T^i(si). Claimed identity h=[C,F][A,B], using s_i s_(i-1)^-1=c_i^-1 and only inter-copy commutation. Increasing product order, arbitrary noncommuting ci, m1, empty identity case, non-injective commuting conjugate copies and reversed/prefix negative controls matter. Written proof and old controls are preserved; no identity was executed here.
4. **Lemma2, compact-isotopy portable product.** For closed connected smooth M, every element of Diff^infinity_(c,0)(MxR2), with isotopy having compact total support, is claimed to have length<=2. Imported perfectness first supplies a finite factorization. All map supports fit in MxB_R; choose L>2R and a compactly supported vector field equal to L partial_x on the full finite corridor through m translated disks. Its time-one map displaces the relevant copies, with compact support/isotopy, and Lemma1 applies. Compactness of M, finitely many factors, full trajectories inside the constant corridor, and extension in the same isotopy group are substantive hypotheses. The package does not claim the closed product MxS2 is portable.
5. **Proposition3, stabilized-element ambient bound.** For closed connected M and f in Diff^infinity_0(M), cl_Diff0(MxS2)(f xid)<=4. A smooth isotopy f_t and two open disks U,V with compactly contained cutoff supports give a(x,y)=(f_chi(y)(x),y), b(x,y)=(f composed f_chi(y)^-1(x),y), b composed a=f xid. The claimed isotopies a_s=f_(s chi), b_s=f_s composed f_(s chi)^-1 have compact total supports in MxU and MxV. Lemma2 supplies two commutators each, extended by identity into the full ambient identity component. No one-parameter-subgroup property is assumed. Smooth inverse dependence, compact support, extension, composition order and identity endpoints remain independent proof obligations. The bound concerns these stabilized elements only, all dimensions of M as stated; for surface M it is four-dimensional.
6. **Stable length and Corollary4.** The proposition applied to all powers is claimed to imply zero ambient stable commutator length on the stabilized subgroup. A homogeneous quasimorphism q with defectD is claimed conjugation invariant and bounded byD on one commutator, hence by(2k-1)D on k commutators. For k4, |n q(f xid)|<=7D implies q vanishes there. This proves no general absence of quasimorphisms on the full group. Defect conventions, inversion, homogeneity and n->infinity require review.
7. **Corollary5, no retraction for positive genus.** An abstract retraction of the full Diff0(Sigma_gxS2) onto Diff0(Sigma_g) restricting to the identity on the product inclusion would send ambient four-commutator factorizations to a uniform surface bound4. The contradiction imports BHW Corollary1.3 for closed orientable positive-genus surfaces. The claim does not require continuity of the retraction. Fresh BHW scope verification is required; genus0 is outside this consequence.
8. **General cutoff failure.** The same point-dependent-time idea need not make a diffeomorphism for a general ambient isotopy. The original example rotates S2 by angle t about the third axis and uses tau=(1+2xy)/2 in[0,1]. At w=(0,1,0), v=(-1,0,0), d tau(v)=-1, so the derivative kills v before an invertible rotation. The original independent receipt claims tangent rank1 with surviving z direction. This is a negative control, not an obstruction to every possible ambient factorization.
9. **Cocycle averaging defect.** For c(fg,x)=c(f,gx)c(g,x), target quasimorphism psi and integrability of all displayed terms, Q(f)=integral psi(c(f,x))dmu gives Q(fg)-Q(f)-Q(g)=integral psi(c(f,y))d(g_*mu-mu)(y)+E, |E|<=Dpsi. Invariance of probabilitymu removes the extra term; without it the extra term has no established uniform bound. The package blocks only the direct invariant-measure route, not all averaging constructions or all quasimorphisms. Change-of-variable conventions and integrability are explicit assumptions.
10. **No full-group invariant probability.** For positive-dimensional closed connected smooth X, the package claims the full Diff0 action admits no invariant Borel probability. A chart ball with closure in a larger chart can be shrunk/moved by local smooth isotopies into arbitrarily many pairwise disjoint small balls; invariance forces mass0; a finite chart-ball cover contradicts mass1. Atomic and singular measures are included by the claimed argument. Independent verification must justify all local isotopies and packings. Dimension0 and restricted conservative groups are outside the stated assertion.
11. **Subgroup inequality and extension.** For H<=G, cl_G(h)<=cl_H(h); unbounded subgroup lengths do not supply ambient lower bounds. Surface stabilization is the concrete claimed collapse. A subgroup quasimorphism needs a justified extension to G.
12. **Central-kernel descent criterion.** For 1->Z->Gtilde->G->1 central, a homogeneous quasimorphism on Gtilde is claimed to descend iff it vanishes on Z. Sufficiency uses additivity on commuting elements by homogenizing (az)^n=a^n z^n. Nonzero kernel values obstruct descent; a universal-cover construction requires this check. Kernel centrality and precise group/quasimorphism definitions matter.
13. **Fragmentation direction.** In the usual isotopy-fragmentation setting, the original imports cl_G(f)<=2 frag(f) from the local two-commutator bound. Unbounded fragmentation yields no ambient lower bound; zero stable length on a subgroup does not prove a uniform ordinary bound on G. The package does not assume every ball-supported mapping class is locally isotopic to identity.

The two original ledger mechanisms are surface-product transfer and cocycle averaging beyond conservative subgroups, both partial_stalled at the original dated5% estimate. Their exact gaps are no ambient lower-bound sequence/uniform factorization for arbitrary diffeomorphisms, and no replacement bounded-defect construction on the full group.

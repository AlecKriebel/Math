The substantive target is a PL homeomorphism of the entire B(i,d) to this product for every d>=2 and 0<=i<=d-2. The whole-boundary endpoint i=d-1 is immediate and does not establish the remaining range. Boundary classification, homology, homotopy type, existence of some disk bundle, and low-dimensional cases alone do not establish the target.

The candidate realizes the complex as a closed spherical cone of bounded zero-deleted sign variation. A strict totally positive tridiagonal flow traps it around a linear attracting sphere. Algebraic spectral coordinates give a global cross-section and a continuous hitting graph; straightening that graph while fixing a neighborhood of the core yields a semialgebraic product homeomorphism. The PL upgrade invokes the compact semialgebraic Hauptvermutung. These are comparison mechanisms, not assumed novel ingredients.

For a positive priority finding, an earlier accessible primary theorem or proof must supply the entire target with an explicit adapter covering all parameters. In particular: (a) a disk-bundle or collapse result must actually establish a trivial bundle or the requisite regular neighborhood of a standard embedded sphere; (b) projective homotopy results require an actual full-space product/disk-bundle classification and a justified lift; (c) a contractive-flow theorem must match a sphere attractor and justify extension at that sphere, not merely contraction to a point; (d) strict sign-variation invariance alone does not identify the topology. Missing these premises blocks that subsumption route rather than turning it into a completed answer.

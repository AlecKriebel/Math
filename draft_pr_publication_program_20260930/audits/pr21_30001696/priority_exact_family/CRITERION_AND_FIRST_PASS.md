# Independent exact-target priority criterion

Frozen before reading the historical source audit, historical review, root reconstruction, or sibling reports. The task statement and candidate bibliography were seen; those are leads, not evidence of novelty.

## Target

For each integer d >= 2 and 0 <= i <= d-2, the entire pure facet-generated cross-polytope subcomplex whose NONCYCLIC word switches occur only between positions j and j+1, 1 <= j <= d-1, is PL homeomorphic to S^i x D^(d-i-1). The whole-boundary endpoint i=d-1 is elementary and is not a novelty criterion. A theorem for the antipodal projective quotient may subsume the target only with a verified lift and actual product identification.

The exact original full source is OWR 08/2011, Klee contribution printed pp. 370-372, Question 4 p. 372. It asks the product for the entire B(i,d). Its Theorem 2 separately states homology, manifold structure and a boundary product. Those weaker conclusions do not answer Question 4. The switch definition at printed p. 371 explicitly uses 1 <= j <= d-1; cyclic variation is a different space.

## Positive prior criterion

A full checked pre-existing source proves this PL product for all parameters, or proves a clearly equivalent statement with a complete parameter/space/category adapter. An actual theorem identifying a trivial disk bundle would qualify. A merely asserted disk bundle, collapse to an abstract sphere, homotopy type, product boundary, low-dimensional cases, or a different triangulation of the same desired product does not alone qualify. A general prior theorem may subsume the candidate only if all hypotheses are independently checked and no new central geometry or bundle triviality is imported.

## Novelty boundaries

The candidate does not claim to originate the complex, its manifold/homology/boundary results, strict total positivity, Krawtchouk eigenvectors, classical zero-sensitive variation diminution, semialgebraic triangulation, or the semialgebraic Hauptvermutung. Its putatively new contribution is the universal whole-space product identification through a spectral cross-section, continuous hitting graph and identity-near-core extension. Priority of the answer and priority of that mechanism are different questions.

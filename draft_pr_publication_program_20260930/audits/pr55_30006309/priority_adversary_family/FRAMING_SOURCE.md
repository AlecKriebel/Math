Proposed title: **A product-triangulation proof of the Hurwitz polytope formula**.

The endpoint is a known theorem. The proposed note presents a direct alternative comparison of the two combinatorial models for smooth toric varieties embedded by complete very ample systems, with every lattice point of the Delzant polytope included and degree at least two. The proof uses product face volumes, cancellation of lower-dimensional contributions, and established GKZ fan results. It generalizes the surface product calculation of Ogusu–Sano and replaces the analytic route used by Sano for the comparison. It should also explain explicitly how the endpoint follows from Esterov's earlier, more general mixed-fiber formula.

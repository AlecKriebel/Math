locators, source URL/byte/hash/mode receipts, actual operator sources, captured

full primary PDF, bulk extracted primary text or primary page PNG.

All twelve complete primary reading objects remain privately retained at
`../private_primary_reading_cache/geometric_topology_adversary_family/`. They
are excluded from the fixed public handoff and must also be excluded from any
future Git/publication package. Retain them as private reading evidence; no
deletion is authorized. Their current paths, full bytes/SHA256/07777 modes and
actual download/render argv are preserved in PRIMARY_SOURCE_RECEIPTS.json and
owned captures. No relocation was performed. Historical original-source modes
in that timestamped receipt remain the values measured then; current originals
are independently pinned in INPLACE_SOURCE_BINDINGS.json.

ROOT helper execution requires no private cache and performs no download,
extraction, rendering, scientific-operator import/replay, SQL, Git or native
mutation. The readback helper imports only definitions from the custody helper
with bytecode writing disabled. ROOT must own/capture each actual execution,
write its receipts outside this immutable family and make its own mathematical
acceptance decision. Presence of READY or authenticated preparer files does not
establish ROOT closure, readback, native acceptance or publication authority.

All indexed files are frozen at full 07777 mode 0444; all indexed directories
are full 0755. SOURCE.json is mode 0444 and absent from its own file inventory.
The collector seal is an administrative handoff; the two ROOT helper sources
remain unexecuted by this family. Older collector versions are preserved per
actual run; their hashes are compared within the run, not falsely equated to
the final collector body.

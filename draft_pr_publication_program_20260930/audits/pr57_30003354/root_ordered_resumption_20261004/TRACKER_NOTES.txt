Integer endpoint discontinuity of normalized uniformization for positively curved surfaces. Research note v1.0 with manuscript PDF and verification archive: https://zenodo.org/records/23131374. PR57, problem 30003354 / OWR-15208-008. For every fixed finite integer r>=0 on the plane and sphere, smooth complete strictly positively curved metrics converge in ordinary compact-open C^r while their unique point-normalized uniformizing diffeomorphisms fail compact-open C^(r+1) convergence; the nonlinear r=1 correction is included. Resolves the specified finite-integer endpoint question for this normalized parametrization; no RP2, noninteger/smooth topology or abstract-homeomorphism claim. Classical logarithmic and twisting mechanisms are credited; the bounded primary-source search through 3 October 2026 found no verified earlier full geometric resolution, with unindexed-priority risk disclosed. Extensive AI assistance and two independent adversarial publication-package reviews; unrefereed, no conventional human peer review or formal proof certification.

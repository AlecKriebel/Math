# Exact scope, prior work and verification qualifications

The theorem resolves the finite-integer endpoint question for the specified point-normalized parametrization of smooth complete nonnegatively curved metrics on the plane and sphere. The counterexamples have strictly positive curvature and preserve smoothness, completeness and the source's point normalization. The spaces contain smooth objects equipped with ordinary compact-open `C^r` and `C^(r+1)` topologies; they are not classes of merely finitely differentiable objects. The manuscript states all restrictions and proves the all-finite-integer construction.

The question was recorded on printed page 149 of the 2017 Oberwolfach report *Spaces and Moduli Spaces of Riemannian Metrics*, [DOI 10.4171/OWR/2017/3](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2017/3). The workshop ran 8–14 January 2017; the report was published on 3 January 2018. The published expectation of failure is credited. The 2016 Belegradek–Hu erratum ([DOI 10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1)) and Banakh–Belegradek's 2018 *Spaces of nonnegatively curved surfaces* ([DOI 10.2969/jmsj/07027344](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/07027344)), especially Sections 2 and 8–9, supply the topology/parametrization context. Existing continuity in noninteger Hölder and smooth topologies is not contradicted by this finite-integer theorem.

General logarithmic and twisting endpoint mechanisms are classical. The directly examined endpoint passage in Mateljević–Salimov–Sevost'yanov's 2022 preprint ([arXiv:2208.02551](https://arxiv.org/abs/2208.02551), Section 7, Example 5) attributes a logarithmic Beltrami example to Astala–Iwaniec–Martin (2009) and Bojarski–Gutlyanskii–Martio–Ryazanov (2013). Those books were credited indirectly through that passage; their complete texts were not directly examined. This note does not claim invention of the general analytic obstruction.

A bounded primary-source search through 3 October 2026 did not locate an earlier full geometric resolution in the precise smooth, complete, positive-curvature setting at every finite endpoint. Earlier unindexed realizations, particularly of the `r=1` curvature correction, remain possible. The search is not an exhaustive priority certificate or a blanket claim that no prior solution exists. The proposed contribution is the explicit full geometric realization, including the critical nonlinear correction.

The official workshop passage, complete erratum and relevant published topology/uniformization sections were examined in the review program. The complete cited endpoint section of the 2022 preprint was examined. Not every cited dependency or historical book was read in full. Independent AI analytic and geometric reviews checked the universal construction; manuscript assembly and portable diagnostic adaptation add zero independent-review credit. No human peer review is claimed.

The exact finite diagnostic mechanisms are credited in `VERIFICATION_PROVENANCE.json`. Algebraic tests supplement the proof and do not certify all orders, global curvature, completeness or novelty. No third-party publication text, PDF or primary image is included in this package.

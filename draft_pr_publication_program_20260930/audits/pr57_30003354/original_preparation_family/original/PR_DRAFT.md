## Result and scope

This draft contains a complete candidate negative answer to the specified conformal-metric parametrization question for every finite integer r>=0, on both the plane and the sphere.

The counterexample sequences consist of smooth complete strictly positive-curvature metrics converging in C^r, while their uniquely normalized conformal diffeomorphisms fail to converge in C^(r+1). The r=1 endpoint includes an exact nonlinear curvature-preserving correction; curvature continuity in C^1 is not assumed.

The proof concerns this particular parametrization. It does not rule out other product homeomorphisms, and it does not contradict the known Hölder or C-infinity statements. The source's 2016 erratum and exact derivative shift are documented.

The [official OWR PDF](https://ems.press/doi/pdf/10.4171/OWR/2017/3) was
available to the web reader as a 34-page PDF. Belegradek's contribution on
printed pp.148–151, especially p.149 (zero-based PDF page 16), was read in
extracted text. It concerns smooth complete nonnegatively curved metrics on
the plane and sphere, with orientation-preserving diffeomorphisms fixing 0,1
or 0,1,infinity. The conformal parametrization is a bijection; its integer
endpoint was expected to fail. The action notation abbreviates its regularity.
The [publisher metadata](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/15208)
identifies OWR 14 (2017), no.1, 133–166; the workshop was 8–14 January 2017,
while publication was 3 January 2018. The imported year variation is therefore
explained. No fresh page screenshot or historical PDF checksum is certified.

The [full two-page 2016 publisher erratum](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1.pdf)
was read in extracted text, including the second-page references. It corrects
the earlier integer-topology assertion to the Hölder setting: metric and
function regularity k+alpha, diffeomorphism regularity k+1+alpha, with
0<alpha<1. It attributes the error to use of a Hölder continuity theorem,
expresses suspicion of integer-endpoint failure without counterexamples at
that time, and says other results are unaffected. The document identifies
Math. Ann. 364 (2016), 711–712, publication 4 January 2016, DOI
10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1. The [publisher article page](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1)
also resolved. This does not newly audit the original 2015 paper's full proof.

## Nongeneric placements and every face

For an actual affine embedding, every pair of disjoint nonempty faces has compact disjoint images and hence strictly positive distance. Finiteness gives a positive minimum. Uniformly small changes in vertex positions change each convex hull in Hausdorff distance by at most the maximum vertex movement, so all these disjoint-face distances remain positive. Suppose an overlapping pair of faces acquired an impermissible intersection, or a simplex became degenerate. The equality of two distinct convex representations gives equal coefficient sums. Cancel the minimum of the coefficients at each common vertex. Unless the representations coincide, positive equal total mass remains on two disjoint supports; after normalization these are disjoint subfaces with intersecting images. That is impossible. If the residual mass is zero, the representations coincide and describe only the permitted common face. Therefore preservation of disjoint-face separation really preserves every affine-embedding condition, including vertex-edge and edge-triangle cases. Generic positions exist arbitrarily near the perturbed labeled coordinates because the finitely many determinant vanishing loci have empty interior. Unused labels can subsequently be added avoiding these loci. Thus the obstruction covers all affine placements, not merely generic ones.

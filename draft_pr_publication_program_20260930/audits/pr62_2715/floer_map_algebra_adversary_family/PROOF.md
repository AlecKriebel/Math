# Independent universal deduction and exact obstruction

This final derivation supplements, without rewriting, the pre-candidate INITIAL_SCOPE.md and INDEPENDENT_CORE.md. It proves a conditional algebraic reduction, not KP-1.56.

Let J≤K mean a smooth annulus in S³×[0,1], bottom J and top K, whose height has only critical indices 0 and 1. Use finite, absolutely Maslov/Alexander-bigraded hat HFK over F₂. In this convention the primary ribbon map f:HFK(J)→HFK(K) has degree(0,0); with compatible decorations the reversed ordinary-concordance map g satisfies gf=id. The needed theorem and selected full proof are [Zemke, Theorem 1.1 and §3](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v190-n3-p05-s.pdf).

The normalization can be checked directly against its §2 grading formulas(7)–(8). S³×I has χ=σ=0 and H²=0, so the Chern-square terms vanish. Two dividing arcs cut the annulus into w/z rectangles. Each has χ=1 and one corresponding basepoint at each end; reduced χ=1−(1+1)/2=0. Both gr_w and gr_z shifts are zero, hence Maslov and Alexander shifts are zero. Endpoint decoration ambiguities are graded automorphisms. Choices therefore do not spoil injectivity or the isomorphism conclusion.

For any field and finite bigraded V,W, gf=id implies f injective. Thus every deficit d_b=dim W_b−dim V_b is nonnegative. If total dimensions agree, the finite sum ∑d_b=0 forces every d_b=0. Consequently f is a graded isomorphism. Multiplying gf=id by f⁻¹ shows g=f⁻¹ and fg=id. This argument requires the actual injection; two abstract isomorphic groups can carry a singular map.

It follows that KP-1.56 is equivalent to strict total-rank increase on strict ribbon-comparable knot types. In one direction, an affirmative equality case implies a nonisotopic comparable pair cannot have equal total ranks, since equality gives equal graded groups. In the other, strict rank increase excludes every nonisotopic comparable equal-HFK pair. This is a reformulation; strictness has not been proved.

Reverse height sends a surface Morse index i to 2−i. Births of C become deaths of its reversal. Thus g is geometrically induced by an ordinary concordance without providing a reverse RIBBON arrow. Agol's antisymmetry theorem requires actual ribbon concordances both ways. A linear inverse cannot discharge that hypothesis.

A precise independent logical countermodel is the category a<b, with only identities and a→b. Send both objects to F₂ in grade(0,0) and every arrow to id. Images are split isomorphisms, and the source is already a partial order; nevertheless there is no reverse arrow and a≠b. The functor is faithful because every hom-set has at most one element. Faithfulness therefore still fails to lift inverses; fullness or a separate geometric rigidity mechanism is missing. This is not a knot realization.

Finite-support cancellation also proves the band-twist exclusion conditional on the two cited primary theorems. If graded tables B+T_nv and B+T_mv agree, coordinatewise integer subtraction gives T_nv=T_mv. A nonempty finite support cannot be invariant under nonzero translation: an orbit s+j(n−m)v would contain infinitely many distinct support points. For v=(2,4), n≠m and a nonzero tail H, Wang's family tables are distinct although total ranks agree. A degree-zero ribbon-induced Khovanov injection would therefore contradict the same finite-dimensional lemma. A zero tail would defeat the distinctness argument; the primary theorem supplies H≠0 for the specified nontrivial band. No model table in our code is presented as an actual knot table.

The normalized Euler characteristic gives rank(HFK(K))≡Δ_K(1)=1 mod2. If strictness were proved, each strict downward ribbon step would decrease rank by at least 2, yielding at most(R−1)/2 strict steps from total rank R. Without the missing equality case, only the nonincreasing integer ranks eventually stabilize; the knots need not be shown to stabilize. This conditional consequence is not a termination proof.

Necessary exclusions and diagnostic limits:
- Infinite countable vector spaces admit a split shift with equal infinite dimensions that is not onto. Equal free ranks over Z leave torsion or nonsplit-index obstructions. Ungraded equality cannot silently fix grading shifts. These controls do not apply to the stated finite F₂ theorem.
- Our abstract filtered complexes have equal associated-graded and total homology but different induced homology filtrations. Exact enumeration finds NO filtered chain map between them inducing an associated-graded isomorphism. Hence they illustrate insufficiency of abstract group equality only; they do not refute any conclusion from an actual ribbon map. Zemke Theorem 1.7 supplies a stronger filtered CFK∞ left inverse. This audit neither denies its implications nor classifies knots by filtered complexes.
- Mutation is not universally HFK-preserving: the selected Ozsváth–Szabó family with parameters r=2, n=1 has top Alexander grading 2 on the Kinoshita–Terasaka side and 3 on the Conway-mutant side. That pair fails the equal-HFK premise and is not a KP-1.56 counterexample.

Exact remaining gap: prove endpoint isotopy for every ribbon-comparable equal-HFK pair outside the imported rigidity classes, or construct and verify one actual nonisotopic pair with BOTH required premises. Neither has been achieved. Algebraic inverse-lifting transfers the central difficulty to an unsupported geometric assertion, so this route is blocked absent a materially new mechanism.

The question asks whether ribbon-concordant knots with isomorphic hat knot Floer homology must be isotopic. Both remarks were read. Mechanism: finite bigraded vector spaces over the SAME field, a degree-zero injection with a left inverse, and equality of total dimensions force the actual forward map to be a bigraded isomorphism. This yields an algebraic inverse. It does not construct a reverse RIBBON concordance. The time-reversed annulus is an ordinary concordance, and its index-zero critical points become index-two maxima. Geometric reversal being ribbon is precisely an additional claim.

## Primary source and conventions

The live problem page was unavailable to the web reader, so the complete pinned record was used, followed by the full K3 author-source PDF. Problem 1.56 and the surrounding discussion on printed pp. 55–56 were read; p. 55 was visually checked. The PDF metadata dates this copy to April 2026. It is a modern question proposed and scribed by Jennifer Hom, not a question extracted from an older Kirby edition with the same number.

The formulation uses hat knot Floer homology. The standard primary-theorem setting is the bigraded vector space over F2. Because a ribbon map is grading-preserving and injective, equality of total dimensions already forces an isomorphism in each grading; the package proves that finite-dimensional observation explicitly.

The direction of the ribbon relation is fixed operationally by a no-maxima movie and the direction of the induced injection. Historical papers sometimes reverse the verbal “from/to” convention. All references are reconciled through J<=K and injection HFK(J) into HFK(K), rather than verbal conventions.

## Sources actually read and their limits

1. [Zemke, Knot Floer homology obstructs ribbon concordance](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/wp-content/uploads/annals-v190-n3-p05-s.pdf), Ann. Math.190(2019),931–947. The complete article was retrieved. The main statements, fibered discussion, and the split-injectivity proof in Section 3 were read; the full-complex extension was checked for scope. The proof explicitly distinguishes a homological identity map from a product concordance. No geometric inverse is inferred.
2. [Boninger, Positive knots and ribbon concordance](https://msp.org/pjm/2025/335-1/pjm-v335-n1-p04-p.pdf), Pacific J.Math.335(2025),81–93, DOI10.2140/pjm.2025.335.81. Section 4 was read, including the exact Gordon rigidity lemma, its coefficient/group conventions and the Alexander-polynomial Euler characteristic; printed p. 88 was visually checked. Gordon's 1981 full article was not obtained from the attempted publisher endpoint, which returned HTML. The package imports the precise published restatement and does not claim to reconstruct Gordon's proof.
3. [Wang, arXiv:2006.01070v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.01070), published as Geom.Topol.26(2022),2941–3053. The complete accessible preprint was retrieved; the relevant introductory theorem statements and grading formula were read. Its equal-HFK result is Theorem 1.3, while K3 cites Theorem 1.2 in the published numbering. Theorem 1.8 gives the exact shifted Khovanov decomposition and nonzero summand. The journal typeset copy was not independently obtained, so no line-by-line edition comparison is claimed. Khovanov split injectivity is explicitly recalled there and attributed to Levine–Zemke.
4. [Agol, Ribbon concordance of knots is a partial ordering](https://doi.org/10.1090/cams/15), Comm.AMS2(2022),374–379. The official publication record and exact scope were checked. Its theorem is not promoted to a conclusion from a reverse ordinary concordance or an inverse Floer map.
5. [Baldwin–Hanselman–Sivek, arXiv:2602.21109v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.21109), February24,2026. The full PDF was retrieved; Introduction, Theorems 1.2/1.4/1.7 and Question 1.8 were read in context. Finiteness of fibered predecessors is not uniqueness or a theorem for every nonfibered predecessor. This package does not independently reprove the immersed-curve argument.
6. [Hom–Park, arXiv:2608.06625v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06625), August6,2026. The complete PDF was retrieved and the introduction/current theorem scopes checked. Rigidity of cables of a fixed companion does not address an arbitrary pair in KP-1.56.
7. [Dunkerley, arXiv:2606.20802v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20802), June18,2026. The complete PDF was retrieved and the introduction/minimality statements checked. Its particular non-nilpotent minimal knots show that the classical sufficient class is not a complete classification; they are not counterexamples to this question. No full independent proof audit of this preprint is claimed.

The arXiv records checked for the three2026 preprints currently show a single version. A bounded current-source search found no complete general answer. This absence is not an exhaustive literature proof; the status recommendation is that this campaign attempt did not solve the question.

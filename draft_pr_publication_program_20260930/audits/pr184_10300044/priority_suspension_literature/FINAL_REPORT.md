## Misleading near matches and bounds

[Hurder's September 5, 2000 survey draft](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~hurder/papers/54manuscript.pdf), pp. 1–3 and portions of §§8–9, was read. Its vanishing discussion restricts holonomy/dynamics. It does not establish a volume-only bound for all taut foliations. [Hurder arXiv:0804.1240v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.1240v2), pp. 17–19, describes Rasmussen's hyperbolic 3-manifold input in a codimension-two construction whose secondary classes lie in degree five. That is not ordinary codimension-one GV on a hyperbolic 3-manifold.

[Ghys 1989](https://www.numdam.org/book-part/SB_1988-1989__31__155_0/), §7 pp. 175–178, discusses circle suspensions and extensions of GV below C2 regularity. I checked p. 176 visually. Lower-regularity extended invariants cannot simply be substituted for the target smooth ordinary invariant.

[Walczak's July 1, 2012 slides](https://webhome.auburn.edu/~kuperkm/dynamicsKrakow2012/Krakow_01_07_2012_PWalczak.pdf), all 14 content slides, concern taut pairs and minimal normal distributions. The visually checked slide 11 vanishing hypothesis is minimality of the complementary normal distribution, with a reversed taut pair in its second corollary. It is not a theorem that ordinary GV vanishes for every taut codimension-one foliation.

The [Hurder problem-set version](https://homepages.math.uic.edu/~hurder/papers/58manuscript.pdf) is dated September 1, 2003, updated December 6, 2003, with hyperref added July 7, 2013. Read pp. 1,3,15–16: it expressly omits taut 3-manifold topics in favor of Calegari's list. Its omission of the target has no priority force.

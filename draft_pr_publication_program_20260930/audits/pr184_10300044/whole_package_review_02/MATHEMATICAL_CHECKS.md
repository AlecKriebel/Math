# Independent mathematical falsification checks

This review uses accepted classical theorems, with their relevant primary statements directly read. It does not recursively reprove Mather–Thurston or hyperbolization. The entire current four-page manuscript was checked; the initial own proof/claim assessment was frozen before prior opinions.

## Differential identities and boundary conditions
In positively oriented local coordinates x,y,s, write alpha=ds+p dx+q dy. Then eta=i_(partial_s)d alpha=p_s dx+q_s dy. Direct exterior differentiation gives

    alpha wedge d alpha = (q_x-p_y+q p_s-p q_s) dx wedge dy wedge ds,
    eta wedge d eta = (q_s p_ss-p_s q_ss) dx wedge dy wedge ds.

Consequently d alpha=alpha wedge eta holds exactly when the Frobenius coefficient vanishes. A test on arbitrary polynomial p,q checks algebraic identities; it does not make every tested p,q a foliation. The current docs say this explicitly. The normalized exact example Phi=s+x s^2+y s^3 has J=1+2xs+3ys^2. For nonnegative x,y,s, J>=1 analytically, so the particular example is nonsingular there independent of sampling. Its local nonzero density is compatible with alpha=dPhi/J and does not give a nonzero closed integral. The separate modified-Phi grid counterexample attacks an invalid sampling inference, not this example or the theorem.

For a positive function f, replacing alpha by f alpha permits eta'=eta-d log f, and changes the GV form by -d(log f d eta). Replacing eta by eta+h alpha changes it by d(h alpha wedge eta), using alpha wedge d eta=0. Thus the closed integral is independent of choices. For a block with boundary the proof does not assert unconditional invariance: it fixes eta=0 on open collars and closes the block by an actual product interval. The classical circle-suspension integral then equals the block integral with zero additional contribution. This is the essential boundary justification.

## Attempted obstructions and their disposition
- Abstract surjectivity without geometric compatibility would be inadequate. The Tsuboi1981 printed p.3 compactification/suspension diagram supplies the exact compatibility, and pp.1 and6 give the positive class and its surface representative. The candidate does not substitute non-taut S3 examples.
- A finitely generated surface-group image lies in one common compact-support interval because generators and inverses fix its complement. Conjugating R into an interval therefore gives actual open identity collars, not merely infinite-order boundary tangency.
- Flat interval holonomy need not be trivial. The weighted positive local coordinates trivialize the smooth bundle relative collars while leaving the horizontal foliation intact. The positive vertical derivative, prescribed endpoint values, and inverse-function theorem give a global fiberwise smooth inverse.
- An arbitrary suspension leaf can be noncompact or recurrent. Its abstract leaf is Sigma_tilde/Stab(u), and the projection is a covering of the entire connected base. Every such leaf meets the fixed fiber. Product collar leaves prevent transitions between interior block leaves and adjacent slabs; all remaining leaves are entire product fibers. This accounts for all leaves, including interfaces and accumulation phenomena.
- Compact leaves inside a block correspond to finite interval orbits. An increasing permutation of a finite ordered set fixes every point, so these are degree-one copies of Sigma, never tori when g>=2. The fibration injects pi1 Sigma into pi1 M, consistent with all leaf injections; no Reeb/ball-plug mechanism is hidden.
- A disk-fixed smooth representative can have a pseudo-Anosov mapping class. Fathi's actual printed p.468 theorem applies to the filling positive/negative twists; thin annuli leave a disk untouched. Hyperbolization depends on that mapping class, and the closed selected surface has negative Euler characteristic. A smooth representative is sufficient.
- The optional archived every-genus construction also checks out: h=(12),v=(13) give a transitive degree-three punctured-torus cover with one degree-three peripheral end, hence genus two after capping. A power of [[2,1],[1,1]] lifts because the index-three subgroup set is finite. Pulled-back measured foliations have six prongs at the branch point. Degree g-1 unbranched cyclic covers and lifted powers give every genus g>=2. The subsequent disk-isotopy modification is independent of foliation flexibility. This extra argument is not needed in the current manuscript proof.
- No rho–phi extension is required. Every insertion is in an interior product slab and all seam forms are dt,0. The fixed circle gamma over a disk point is smooth/embedded, with tangent partial_t, and alpha_N(partial_t)=1. The same circle is positive and meets every leaf for each N.
- Under h(x,t)=(x,(t-a)/ell), ell>0, the coefficients of alpha_j=ell h*alpha are ell p(x,(t-a)/ell), ell q(x,(t-a)/ell),1. Thus eta_j=h*eta and the GV coefficient is h*(old coefficient)/ell. The orientation-preserving Jacobian cancels compression in the integral. Actual open collar equality glues all derivatives and introduces no interface terms.
- For any finite N, separated positive-width slabs exist, e.g. [(2j-1)/(2N+1),2j/(2N+1)] for j=1..N. Every derivative remains smooth at that N. No infinite stack, uniform derivative bound, or limiting foliation is claimed. The fixed volume V0 does not change. Since N a grows without bound, any finite B(V0) fails.

## Scope and remaining mathematical dependencies
The verified result is precisely one fixed closed connected oriented hyperbolic M, one a>0, one embedded positive circle, and smooth cooriented foliations with ordinary GV=N a and that all-leaf transversal for every finite positive integer N. It directly refutes the literal 2002v1 Q10.6. It neither extends to all hyperbolic manifolds nor answers a minimal-foliation variant. The ordinary fiber foliation, with eta=0, separately refutes the imported positive-volume lower bound. No explicit numerical holonomy, metric certificate, full classical reproof, formal proof-assistant certificate, historical firstness, or present-day-open-status certificate is supplied or required for the authorized credited consequence note.

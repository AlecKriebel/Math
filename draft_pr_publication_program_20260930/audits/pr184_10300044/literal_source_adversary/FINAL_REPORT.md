## Adversarial mechanism checks

1. **Finite support and genuine collars.** A closed surface group is finitely generated. A compact interval contains the supports of all generator images and inverses, so every word fixes the same complement. Compactification therefore creates actual identity endpoint collars, not just asymptotic flatness or finite-order tangency. Smoothness at the endpoints follows because the maps equal the identity on neighborhoods.

2. **Relative bundle trivialization.** In compatible local interval charts let s_i be increasing fiber coordinates, equal to the common coordinate near endpoints. If psi_i is a base partition of unity, s=Σpsi_i s_i has strictly positive derivative on every positively oriented vertical vector, equals0 and1 at the endpoints, and agrees with the prescribed coordinate on collars. Each fiber map is a diffeomorphism onto I. The inverse-function theorem gives a smooth global inverse. This trivializes the bundle while allowing its flat foliation to remain nontrivial.

3. **No arbitrary boundary-invariant assumption.** Choose alpha annihilating the foliation, alpha(V)=1. It equals ds on collars. Integrability gives i_V(alpha wedge d alpha)=d alpha-alpha wedge i_Vd alpha=0; eta=i_Vd alpha consequently satisfies d alpha=alpha wedge eta and vanishes on collars. Adding the identity complementary circle interval produces the actual compactified suspension in the classical diagram. Its GV integral equals the block integral because eta=0 on the added region. This justifies the relative value without asserting that arbitrary primitives have invariant boundary integrals.

4. **One surface and one manifold suffice.** Pick one positive nonzero suspension class. A positive component of a disconnected surface representative still has positive value. Trivial added handles are implemented by a degree-one collapse and do not change that represented class. Arrange genus≥2 once, choose a pseudo-Anosov mapping class once, choose a smooth disk-fixing representative once, then form its mapping torus once. None depends on N. No uniform genus over all real GV values is required.

5. **No monodromy extension condition is missing.** The suspension blocks occupy interior product slabs. Near the mapping-torus seam the foliation and forms are the ordinary fiber product. Therefore rho need not intertwine the pseudo-Anosov monodromy. Such an intertwining condition would be needed for a different construction attempting to descend a single global flat representation, which is not what is used here.

6. **Exact source tautness.** A suspension leaf projects over the connected surface by complete path lifting; all holonomy maps are diffeomorphisms of the entire interval. It meets the fiber over the chosen disk point p. The vertical circle over p closes smoothly because the monodromy fixes p. It is transverse throughout every block and complementary product slab, and it meets every block leaf, product leaf, and interface leaf. Compact leaves do not violate this argument and no Reebless-to-taut implication is substituted.

7. **Smoothness and additive GV.** On a slab of length ell>0 put alpha_j=ell h_j^*alpha and eta_j=h_j^*eta. Then alpha_j=dt and eta_j=0 on the collars; the defining equation and coorientation persist. Extend by dt,0 on the complement and seam. No interface cross terms occur. Orientation-preserving change of variables makes each integral a, so the total is Na. Each N is finite. Increasing derivatives as ell shrinks create no loss of regularity for an individual foliation; the target has no uniform derivative constraint.

8. **Volume and diagnostic boundary cases.** The chosen manifold and metric remain fixed, hence volume cannot scale with N. N=0 gives the smooth fiber foliation with alpha=dt and GV=0 on the same positive-volume manifold; this disproves source_record's imported positive-volume lower-bound assertion. That diagnostic alone would not answer the requested upper-bound question. Allowing signs or reversing orientation does not obstruct the positive unbounded family. The construction makes no minimality or real-analyticity claim.

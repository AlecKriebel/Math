## Assessment and independent deductions
The argument appears mathematically sound, conditional only on the cited classical theorems in their stated standard forms. Tsuboi p.3 explicitly provides compact-support-to-circle suspension compatibility; his p.6 supplies surface representatives. The positive class can be chosen by surjectivity of GV (p.1) and connected component selection. Raising genus by trivially mapped handles preserves the homology class. There is no dependence on uniform genus across changing GV values: a single positive block suffices.

Finite generator support gives a common compact interval fixed pointwise outside. Smooth endpoint extension is legitimate because every generator is identity on a common neighborhood. The local-coordinate partition-of-unity average has strictly positive derivative on every fiber and prescribed endpoints, so it defines a smooth fiberwise trivialization relative to collars. Normalization alpha(V)=1 is permissible because V is transverse and positively cooriented. Contracting Frobenius gives d alpha=alpha wedge i_V(d alpha), with eta=0 in product collars. Compactifying by a product interval produces the same circle suspension and contributes zero.

The disk-fixed smooth monodromy is in a pseudo-Anosov mapping class; the map itself need not be pseudo-Anosov. Fathi p.468 supports the twist construction with a complementary disk. Thurston identifies the mapping torus of that isotopy class as hyperbolic. The same disk point defines a smooth embedded seam-compatible circle and a globally defined vertical vector field. The disk is used to close the transverse orbit, not to constrain horizontal holonomy. Interior slab insertion needs no holonomy-monodromy intertwining.

The slab rescaling is constant: alpha_j=ell h*alpha, eta_j=h*eta, so d alpha_j=alpha_j wedge eta_j and alpha_j(partial_t)=1. Product open collars ensure smooth gluing to dt,0, including all jets and the mapping-torus seam. Slab leaves project as coverings of the connected surface and meet its fiber at p; product leaves meet that same circle. A horizontal leaf cannot cross a product collar leaf, so these cases exhaust global leaves. Integrals add without interface terms and are invariant under orientation-preserving slab changes. Each N is finite, so unbounded derivative growth is no smoothness obstruction. No global explicit numerical representation is claimed or needed for this existential theorem.

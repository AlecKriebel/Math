## Exact claim and original prior-open formulation

The target is Fuchs's Conjecture 2.7, printed p. 512: for regular n>=3, two source-parallel short trajectories of canonical source types A_k,A_l have length ratio sin((k+1)pi/n)/sin((l+1)pi/n). Short means from one vertex to the first subsequent vertex, with sides included; it is not a length cutoff. The type indices are residues modulo n-2 with canonical representatives. Definition 2.1 must retain the preceding segment-parity convention. Parallelism is the broader source dihedral, unoriented equivalence from section 1.1. These details match the repaired theorem. [Full original publisher PDF](https://amj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/020-0170.pdf).

The original final article labels this exact statement a conjecture. Its introduction credits Veech/Ward and suggests that their framework can prove the conjectures, with pentagon work as a model; that is methodological credit, not a supplied all-n labelled proof. This establishes what the author put forward in January 2021, rather than independently proving that no earlier equivalent theorem existed. The [publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40598-020-00170-8) confirms online/version-of-record 7 January 2021, received 24 August 2020, revised 9 October 2020, accepted 4 November 2020; issue date December 2021. ## What the earlier original candidate already contributes

The original candidate contains the same all-n theorem, the double-polygon surface (including the even double cover), cone-germ angular residue, hyperelliptic centrality argument, credited classical cusp reduction, model-chord weights, and shared-direction derivative cancellation. In particular it already writes the identity w_n(-k)=w_n(k) and explicitly frames the quantity transported in the length comparison as the type **weight**. These are substantive proof components recorded before the competing deposit.

Its unconditional statement that physical source labels are affine-invariant and always negated by reversal is false. An even-segment physical reversal can preserve the source label. The corrected interpretation is k=sigma K for the starting folding chart, where intrinsic K is affine-preserved and sigma is the chart orientation; reversal has k_rev=(-1)^N k. This missing distinction is a real obligation. Nevertheless it does not change the sine weight: for 1<=k<=n-3 the canonical representative of -k is n-2-k, and

sin(((n-2-k)+1)pi/n)=sin((n-1-k)pi/n)=sin((k+1)pi/n),

with k=0 immediate. Thus the original's intended weaker weight invariant survives the sign correction. The repaired proof supplies the Q-chart folding argument needed to justify applying it after an affine map. This is a local but substantive correction to an already recorded central argument; it would be inaccurate either to call the original a fully verified proof or to claim the proof mechanism first appeared after October 9.

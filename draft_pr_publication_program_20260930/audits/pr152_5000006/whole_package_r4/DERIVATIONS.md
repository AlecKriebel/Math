## 2. Exact surface and all-affine germ index

The gluing P_j~Q_{j+1} and Q_j~P_{j+1} produces the step-one alternating corner cycle. It has one orbit of size2n for odd n and two of size n for even n. For n>=5, g>=2 (including n6, though its ratio is separately proved). For any real affine automorphism f, the conjugate j=f iota f^-1 has derivative A(-I)A^-1=-I in translation coordinates. This uses the standard uniqueness theorem for genus>=2, not a false centrality theorem for every marked torus.

For A in GL+(2,R), the argument map on the plane has increasing continuous lift F. Its winding number is1. ## 3. Physical chart dictionary, orientation and sine

Normalize the physical initial corner to P0; its fold preserves orientation. A P-terminal backward germ has offset pi-beta from its lower boundary. A Q-terminal corner folds by a reflection, so its offset is d*pi-(pi-beta)=beta-2pi/n. Subtract initial alpha. For even endpoints use Q0=iota(P0) to base the other cone, so terminal corner numbers are t=2a+e for P and t=2a+1-e for Q. Equating the resulting difference with (1-e)pi+2pi*m gives:

P: mn=aM+e(n-1)-s, where s=n(alpha+beta)/(2pi).
Q: mn=aM+s+e-2, where s=n(beta-alpha)/(2pi).

Modulo M, n=2 and n-1=1. The source dictionary s=1-k (sum) and s=k+1 (difference) yields k=2m+1-e in both cases. For odd n use e0 and t2a/2a+1, giving k=2m+1. Changing a full-cone lift adds nr to these integer equations: nM for odd n, nM/2 for even n. Both are multiples of M, the latter precisely because n is even. This addresses the marked even cone winding explicitly.

A Q-starting physical fold is H composed with iota, H a polygon reflection in a midpoint axis. Iota preserves K. H sends alpha to d*pi-alpha and beta to pi+2pi/n-beta. Thus sum s goes to n-s and difference s to2-s, so k becomes -k for either parity. Therefore general physical k=sigma*K, sigma=fold orientation; it is not intrinsically affine invariant.

Physical path reversal instead sends alpha to pi-beta and beta to pi-alpha. Sum s becomes n-s; difference s is unchanged. Hence k_reverse=(-1)^N*k. A cusp is an orbit of a parabolic fixed direction under the derivative group. Consequently one affine map sends the whole original direction to a model direction. No interior ray can exit at a side interior first in a model direction.

**First-vertex unfolding and actual surface.** Reflection in a side equals its midpoint half-turn composed with the perpendicular-bisector reflection preserving the regular polygon. Reflected polygon sets therefore alternate translates of P and -P. Physical vertex orientation still reverses at each reflection; the manuscript retains it in the terminal-Q angle offset. Each developed segment projects by a local isometry to the two-polygon translation surface, retaining its length and excluding intermediate marked vertices. **Hyperelliptic centrality and lift offsets.** The polygon-exchanging half-turn has no polygon-interior fixed point. Across a zero, input/output flat coordinates z=w^r lift -z to a fixed root of -1 times w, so the conjugate extends holomorphically. Its quotient is topologically a sphere. Genus>=2 hyperelliptic uniqueness proves commutation.

For a positive real-linear derivative the increasing angle lift has degree one.

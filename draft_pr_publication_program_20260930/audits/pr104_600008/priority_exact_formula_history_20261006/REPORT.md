## Target and comparison standard

Here \(a,b,c\) are **squared** semi-axes, all positive. The inspected candidate defines
\[
f(t)^2=a\sin^2t+b\cos^2t,\qquad
M=\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}\sqrt{\frac{f(t)^2}{c+f(t)^2}}\,dt,\qquad L=2\pi M.
\]
Its actual positively advancing null-path lift of GKT's equator return has
\[
\rho=\frac{1-M}{2M}=\frac{\pi}{L}-\frac12.
\]
Thus \(T^n\) closes with winding \(r>0\) precisely when \(M=n/(n+2r)\). A chain counted as individual full tropic-to-tropic arcs additionally needs even \(n\). Least period of \(T\) requires \(\gcd(n,r)=1\); if \(\rho=p/q\) is reduced, a full chain has least arc count \(\operatorname{lcm}(2,q)\).

A prior density, Poncelet theorem, unspecified rotation, closure iff rational rotation, integrability, ambient interior billiard criterion, or an arithmetic existence assertion is not by itself this parameter-only formula with these counts. A finite algebraic Cayley condition would be a distinct and potentially stronger output.

## Exact comparison of the two substantive priority obstacles

**DR2019/2020.** Let
\[
F_\gamma(x)=\sqrt{(a_1-x)(a_2-x)(a_3+x)(\gamma-x)}.
\]
Their even-period interior null-billiard rank uses Taylor coefficients \(A_j(\gamma)\) of \(F_\gamma\) at \(x=0\); \(a_1>a_2>0,\ a_3>0\). Their \(n\) counts billiard reflections, including the conventions for tropic encounters. Their Remark4.4 explicitly takes the remaining caustic parameter \(\gamma\to0\) and asserts a solution of the source problem. At zero the radicand has a simple factor \(x\), so this is not an ordinary Taylor expansion. For fixed axes and integer \(j\ge1\),
\[
A_j(\gamma)\sim\sqrt{a_1a_2a_3}\,(-1)^j{1/2\choose j}\gamma^{1/2-j}\quad(\gamma\downarrow0).
\]
For example, the \(n=6\) condition is \(A_4=0\), whereas its leading coefficient is nonzero and diverges. This check **does not refute every possible properly rescaled or varying-period limit**. It shows why a mere \(\gamma=0\) substitution cannot authenticate the asserted equivalence. Remaining work is to recover a valid limiting condition, the corresponding two real cycles, actual winding, and the relation between finite reflections and full arcs/equator returns. This audit supplies no new central solution and makes no “paper is wrong” conclusion.

**Tejada2018 special case.** His parameters \(A,C\) are ordinary semi-axis lengths. Set \(Q=\sqrt{A^2+C^2}/A>1\), so the candidate gives \(M=1/Q\), \(\rho_T=(Q-1)/2\), and actual same-northern-tropic angular increment \(2\pi(Q-1)\). The thesis parametrization makes \(u\) the longitude; Example4.9 prints \(\pi(u)=u+\pi/Q\). At \(A=C\), these increments are respectively \(2\pi(\sqrt2-1)\approx2.603\) and \(\pi/\sqrt2\approx2.221\). The normalization disagreement should be recorded and resolved if citing its numeric shift. Nevertheless, its rational-rotation closure condition is equivalent to \(Q\in\mathbb Q\), which is also equivalent to the candidate's rotational closure condition. Thus a general “no explicit previous special case” claim fails, while this source is not the same exact normalized triaxial formula.

## What could remain as a substantive increment

The most defensible candidate increment is the **explicit application and normalization**: replacing the unknown GKT translation by a complete nonsingular mean depending only on the axes, proving the actual path lift, and organizing the criterion for all positive real axes with complete counting conventions. Even that increment has only bounded search support until DR2019/2020 and Wüstholz2021 are reconciled.

- Classical pseudo-confocal separation, flat null coordinates and the GKT invariant density are established background.
- The period identity must not be advertised as a newly discovered elliptic-integral identity. After this family's independent searches, ROOT supplied the classical DLMF family identification. I checked [DLMF19.7.8](https://dlmf.nist.gov/19.7.E8), which connects two third-kind integrals with reciprocal parameters \(\alpha^2\omega^2=k^2\) and an inverse circular term. The exact substitution identifying the candidate's periods was supplied by ROOT's independent work and was not rederived as a new central argument here. The application to the specific real cycles and path lift can be useful even when the special-function relation is classical.
- All-real axes, interchange \(a,b\), the limit \(a=b\), full-arc parity, least-period arithmetic and winding-lift choice are useful completeness and correction of conventions. The axisymmetric rationality classification itself has prior coverage.
- Monotonicity/unique-height consequences are elementary calculus once \(M\) is known. For fixed \(a,b\), \(\partial_cM=-(4\pi)^{-1}\int f(t)(c+f(t)^2)^{-3/2}dt<0\), with \(M\to1\) at \(c\downarrow0\) and \(M\to0\) at \(c\to\infty\). Thus \(\rho\) increases from0 to infinity, giving a unique \(c\) for each prescribed positive \(r/n\). This is a useful corollary, not independent evidence of major novelty.
- A finite algebraic criterion in the axes is not established by the candidate. Ordinary elliptic-group torsion does not follow from integrating a third-kind density. This audit neither proves such a criterion impossible nor clears an algebraic “Cayley theorem” claim.

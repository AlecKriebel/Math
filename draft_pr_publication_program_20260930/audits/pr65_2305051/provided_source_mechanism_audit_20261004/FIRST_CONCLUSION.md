# Independent first conclusion

Saved before reading any ROOT or sibling review-opinion body. The source scope at this checkpoint is the complete Piranian 1966 and Duren--Shapiro--Shields 1966 text extractions; visual inspection of every Piranian page (printed 255--262) and Duren printed 247--250; the original submitted CANDIDATE.md; and the current note.tex treated only as claims to test.

Piranian, printed pp. 260--261, supplies Kahane's exact deterministic four-adic recursion: start at height one; freeze zero; replace a positive height a by (a-1,a+1,a+1,a-1). No fast-growth sequence or free coefficient is needed for this second construction. Piranian explicitly excludes every finite nonzero ordinary derivative for its limiting distribution function (p. 261). This is the exact real-variable mechanism that the package presently dates to Kahane 1969. Its fully specified printed antecedent is already in Piranian 1966.

Duren--Shapiro--Shields, pp. 248--250, defines the affine-adjusted periodic primitive and proves that its Zygmund condition is equivalent to F'(z)=O((1-|z|)^(-1)) for the Herglotz transform F. Thus the Bloch bridge used in PR65 is itself printed in the companion 1966 paper. Neither complete 1966 body prints the normalized pure Blaschke Cayley transform B=(F-1)/(F+1) as an answer to Holland's problem. Its derivation from the old fixed rule plus the classical singular-factor/converse-Fatou inputs is an easy subsequent deduction, not evidence that a full Holland answer was printed in these papers.

PR65's cyclic neighbor-directed signs give a different deterministic ordering of the same absorbed unit-step walk. Already its first list (2,0,2,0) differs from the fixed rule's (0,2,2,0), including under circular translation or reflection. The signs are not a literal copy of that fixed recursion, and the cubic construction of Piranian pp. 256--260 is a separate mechanism. But PR65 supplies no new measure mechanism or needed mathematical improvement: the old fixed rule has mass consistency, atomlessness, singularity, all-point finite-positive-density obstruction, a neighbor bound of two, and a seam made flat by the first-stage zero outer quarters. Those suffice for the same normalized target and the same effective midpoint approximants.

This defeats any novelty assertion for the construction mechanism. It does not prove first priority of the final Cayley articulation, settle every intended meaning of the historical word explicit, or independently verify the analytic converse-Fatou theorem. An effective recursive answer with algebraic stages and a compact convergence modulus is distinct from a closed-form zero enumeration. The two 1966 papers provide the former's old premises; neither supplies a closed-form zero list.

Required package corrections: credit Kahane's construction as published by Piranian in 1966, add Duren--Shapiro--Shields pp. 248--250 as the direct old Bloch bridge, and remove the now-false statements that these full bodies were not read. Do not upgrade historical priority or original-discovery clearance from these findings.

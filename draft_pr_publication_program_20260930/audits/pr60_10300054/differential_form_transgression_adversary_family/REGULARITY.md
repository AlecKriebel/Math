# Independent regularity extension and qualification

The smooth form calculation has an honest weak regularity extension. Assume alpha is a global C1 nowhere-zero1-form, omega is continuous with continuous weak exterior derivative d omega, and d alpha=alpha wedge omega. Let f,g be C1 functions. Then omega'=omega-df+g alpha is continuous with continuous weak exterior derivative d omega'=d omega+dg wedge alpha+g d alpha, since d2f=0 distributionally. Applying d to the defining equation (products with alpha C1 are legitimate) gives alpha wedge d omega=0. The pointwise algebra in INDEPENDENT_CORE.md therefore remains valid.

The primitive T'=df wedge omega+g alpha wedge(omega-df) is continuous. Its weak derivative is the continuous3-form GV'-GV. One can justify d(df wedge omega)=-df wedge d omega by C1 approximation of f: for smooth f_k the product rule is valid with omega and its continuous weak derivative, and uniform convergence of df_k on compact charts passes it to distributions. The other terms follow by the same argument with C1 coefficients and d alpha continuous. Thus no nonexistent classical second derivatives of C1 scalar f are invoked. Weak Stokes follows by smoothing/partition of unity or the distributional identity on a closed manifold. Boundary restrictions require the appropriate Stokes trace conditions; no arbitrary boundary integral is asserted to vanish.

A cooriented C2 foliation atlas gives a global C1 defining alpha by patching positively oriented local differentials dt_i with a smooth partition of unity. Locally alpha=A_i dt_i, where A_i>0 is C1. Set omega_i=-d log A_i, a continuous weakly closed1-form. Then d alpha=alpha wedge omega_i. Patch omega=sum psi_i omega_i with a smooth partition; its weak derivative sum d psi_i wedge omega_i is continuous, and it still satisfies the defining equation. This constructs an admissible continuous pair without assuming the foliation became smooth. It also shows why requiring ordinary C1 omega or C2 alpha is stronger than the atlas premise unless separately established.

This does not classify every low-regularity auxiliary omega: the difference g alpha may have only continuous g with suitable leafwise derivatives. Smooth/C1 gauge formulas give a valid class of changes and cohomological invariance, but an equivalence reducing the entire original pointwise existence question to smooth gauges requires an additional justification. Nonnegative densities with zeros are not automatically preserved by approximation. Any candidate claim must state its regularity domain accurately.

Fresh primary regularity sources were read through official author/journal PDFs: Hurder–Langevin2016 discusses C2 invariant versus smooth expository formulas and lower-regularity leafwise/distributional complexes; Hurder–Katok1990 §7 discusses transverse C1,alpha coefficients and the continuous/distributional split. These support treating the smooth algebra and C2 invariant carefully. They are not used to infer a smooth-foliation reduction or a solution of monotone wobble.

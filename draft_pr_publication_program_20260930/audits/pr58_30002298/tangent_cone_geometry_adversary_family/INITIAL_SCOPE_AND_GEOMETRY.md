Independent geometric route: triangulate the actual union into interior-disjoint
full-dimensional simplices, rather than summing overlapping convex pieces.
Tangent indicators at a nonvertex point of a simplex are line-cones, so every
algebraic vertex must occur in every triangulation. This proves A(P)⊆V(P)
without equating source triangulations with another dissection convention.

For the complete local test, flip simplicial-cone generators into one strict
η-positive halfspace. One flip satisfies [C]+[C_flip]=[D] a.e., where D is
invariant along the flipped generator. Iteration gives a signed cone function
g+ congruent to the original tangent indicator modulo line-cones. Its support
lies in a common pointed cone K, with η·x≥c|x| on K. The actual Laplace
integral of g+ converges near -η. If its rational valuation vanishes, the
Fourier transform of e^{-η·x}g+ vanishes; L1 Fourier uniqueness forces g+=0
a.e. Conversely a zero g+ provides an explicit finite signed line-cone
decomposition. Thus signed chamber nonvanishing is a complete geometric test,
not a transferred unsupported geometric-realization problem.

A simplex's rational transform is squarefree in its vertex affine factors.
Triangulation partial fractions associate each vertex factor with the homogeneous
tangent-cone valuation. On a nonzero vertex's affine hyperplane, homogeneity
prevents a nonzero cone valuation from vanishing identically. Distinct affine
vertex factors have distinct hyperplanes, so exactly nonzero algebraic vertices
survive, each once. This gives the candidate's full reduced denominator, hence
the exact requested iff characterization by A(P)⊆V(P).

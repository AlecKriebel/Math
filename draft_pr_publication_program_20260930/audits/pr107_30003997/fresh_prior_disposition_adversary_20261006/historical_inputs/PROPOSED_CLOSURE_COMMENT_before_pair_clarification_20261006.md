Closing without merging: the mathematics is sound after minor verification and preprocessing repairs, but this is an `already_solved` target as an elementary consequence of published work. The audit did not establish a substantive new mathematical contribution suitable for publication as a novel resolution.

The source target is Kaibel's fixed-root, destination-dependent path-cost arborescence optimization (Problem 2, OWR50/2018, printed pp.3014–3015). Independent mathematical audits verified the submitted 3SAT reduction, including all feasible trees, binary coefficients, depth-three consecutive layers, nonroot indegree at most three, and the strictly positive {1,2} offset. Normal and optimized Python reproduction exposed an assertion-based guard defect; the effective audit package fixes it without changing the reduction. Empty-clause/trivial-input preprocessing is now explicit. The immutable original proof and original 1/5 effort remain archived.

The decisive priority comparison is Chapoullié–Szigeti, *On packing time-respecting arborescences*, Discrete Optimization 45 (2022), 100702, [DOI 10.1016/j.disopt.2022.100702](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disopt.2022.100702), Theorem 13 and its full proof on printed pp.11–12 ([author-hosted full text](https://pagesperso.g-scop.grenoble-inp.fr/~szigetiz/OCG/13.C-Szigeti.pdf)). Its exact-cover construction has two colored parallel root arcs per hyperedge, black membership arcs, and gray overlap arcs. Subdivide each root option through its own forced selector vertex. Charge each element destination one on gray first arcs and each overlap destination one on black first arcs, with every other coefficient zero. Cost zero is then equivalent to the published monochromatic spanning-arborescence condition. The resulting graph is simple, reachable, a four-layer depth-three DAG, and has nonroot indegree at most three. Adding one to every coefficient shifts every tree's cost by the same 4h+3m, giving the claimed {1,2} variant too. Thus the published construction already implies the entire claimed hardness bundle.

This is an explicitly checked corollary: the earlier paper does not literally print this destination-cost formulation. We do not claim earliest priority or that every possible refinement is known. The candidate's exact minimum-unsatisfied-clause identity and reproducible direct SAT presentation are retained as verified explanatory refinements; their exact earlier wording has not been established, but they do not establish a new resolution or an additional substantive theorem in this PR. This closure concerns priority, not a counterexample to the proof.

## Decisive evidence and independent checks

Chapoullié–Szigeti, *On packing time-respecting arborescences*, Theorem 13, Discrete Optimization 45 (2022), 100702, DOI [10.1016/j.disopt.2022.100702](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disopt.2022.100702). Its full primary theorem/proof, spanning-arborescence convention, and Figure 2 were inspected. [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.01096) was submitted **2 March 2022, 13:32:07 UTC** and contains the same theorem; the journal issue is August 2022. No earliest-priority claim is made.

The exact translation separately subdivides each of the source's two colored parallel root-arc options. Both new vertices are spanned, while their shared child selects exactly one parent. For each source sink, its own cost vector assigns one to the incompatible first root arc and zero everywhere else. Every other destination is free. A source root path is monochromatic exactly when its matched destination path has zero price. Thus one fixed-root spanning tree is shared across destinations; the aggregation is exactly the sum of their individual arc-price vectors. No capacity, minimax, changeover/reload, or independent-path replacement appears.

This produces a **simple, root-reachable, depth-three DAG with four consecutive layers**, all costs in **{0,1}**, and every nonroot indegree at most **three**. Adding one to every cost increases every tree's value by the same total depth, **4n+3m**, giving the **{1, 2}** version at that threshold. These are every advertised hardness restriction of the repaired candidate. The source's exact-cover correctness mechanism also was independently verified: chosen black hyperedge parents cover every element; pair sinks prohibit two overlapping choices. Conversely, any exact cover supplies all zero-cost parents. The classical RXC3 premise was checked in Gonzalez's full 1985 primary article, Appendix A, Theorem A.1, with the precise frequency-three definition.

Source notation defects were not ignored. The displayed pair set omits i<j; its figure, counting bound and converse explicitly identify distinct unordered pairs. Literal self-pairs would destroy its yes-construction. We state the intended convention explicitly. The Z-set/common-root shorthand also has an omission. Our direct completeness and soundness arguments use neither shorthand, and the source figure was rendered for visual confirmation.

[verify_prior_translation.py](verify_prior_translation.py) constructs the actual simple graph and dense destination table, reconstructs paths, and evaluates the sum. It checked **82,944 complete arborescences** for the four-element no-cover case, plus **2,688 local parent choices under all 64 root assignments** of a six-element yes-cover case, and **3,072 local parent choices** of a six-element no-cover case. The four-element case is a boundary instance outside the usual universe-size-multiple-of-three RXC3 promise; both six-element cases meet that promise. Both graph restrictions and the positive offset passed; a corrupted destination-cost negative control was detected. [VALIDATION.json](VALIDATION.json) records the results. The universal inference is proved in the translation artifact; finite computation is supporting evidence.

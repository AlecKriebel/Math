## Exact claims and meanings

The target is Colazzo, Oberwolfach Report 9/2023, printed p.543 Question 1: for an arbitrary skew brace B, do all property-(s) elements form an ideal? It is unrestricted; global property (S), finiteness, and two-sidedness are not assumptions of the question. The accepted PR constructs a left brace with additive group Z×Z4, circle group D_infinity×C2, finite lambda image V4 and S=Z×{0,1}. Its additive double witness fails property (s). The target contribution is this explicit negative example and exact infinite-index calculation.

The property-(s) definition uses finite indices of Fix^r(x)∩C_x^+ and Fix^l(x)∩C_x^∘. Each index is finite exactly when both factor indices are finite. The right star map g↦λ_x(g)-g is constant on additive left cosets of Fix(λ_x), and equality of two star values is equivalent to their representatives differing by an element of Fix(λ_x). The other star orbit is the lambda orbit translated by -x. Group conjugacy classes correspond to centralizer cosets. Therefore the four finite-orbit formulation used in the July paper is **exactly equivalent**, not merely similar, to the original definition.

The distinction between B globally θ_f (all additive conjugacy/lambda orbits finite) and B globally (S) (every element property (s)) matters. PR156 is globally θ_f but is not globally (S): its multiplicative reflections have infinite conjugacy classes. Its finite lambda image and abelian additive group make the theta conditions automatic, leaving multiplicativeFC precisely S. This equality already follows for PR156 from CPV's March Proposition 5.17, without the July strengthening.

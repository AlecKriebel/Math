# Independent pre-exposure mathematical control notes

Saved 2026-10-02T21:36:57.446760+00:00. Still before candidate/current/family/root-review contents have been opened. These notes formulate proof checks and preserve the unresolved exterior claim; they are not a new unconditional target resolution.

Let Q_L be a square of side L. Let F_n(Q_L) denote expected length of the all-pair route union from n iid points in Q_L, either total length or intersection with Q_L as stated. Coupling every n to the same iid point sequence makes F_n monotone. Finite E D1 and scale/rotation invariance give the elementary expectation bound F_n(total) <= (n choose2) sqrt(2)L E D1. A successful Poisson comparison must use this finite bound to control count-error events. The count variable is independent of the point sequence and route process; route lengths themselves need not be independent of the points or one another.

For a fixed Poisson intensity lambda, the finite source-window union is a subset of S(lambda), so interior mean <= ell sqrt(lambda)L^2. On a fixed unit observation cell, the union over source points in expanding centered windows increases to S(lambda), by its definition as a countable all-pair union. Monotone convergence for length measure gives the local expectation limit. To move the local limit to the whole source square, fix a source radius R, tile the part of Q_L farther than R from its boundary, and translate that fixed local estimate to every tile. The boundary area is O(RL); take L -> infinity before R -> infinity. This supplies a checkable expected-interior argument without requiring ergodicity.

For de-Poissonization, couple independent N_-~Poisson((1-epsilon)k) and N_+~Poisson((1+epsilon)k) to iid samples in Q_sqrt(k). On the respective good count events N_-<=k and N_+>=k, monotonicity supplies the comparisons. The omitted expectation terms are at most constants times sqrt(k) E[N_-^2 1_{N_->k}] for the lower comparison and sqrt(k)k^2 P(N_+<k) for the upper comparison. Chernoff bounds with fixed epsilon force these errors to o(k). Send epsilon ->0 only after the k limit. Any proof that drops these count terms or sends epsilon to zero prematurely needs repair.

The result thereby checked is an interior law, not the full target. Given that law, the full indexed expectation asymptotic is equivalent to E len(route union outside Q_sqrt(k))/k ->0. All length measures are nonnegative, so no cancellation can hide exterior escape. Claiming this equivalence alone is an exact reformulation, not a mechanism that resolves the central difficulty.

For boundary length, a stationary integrable network has E length(S(lambda) intersect boundary Q_L)=ell sqrt(lambda) area(boundary Q_L)=0, hence zero boundary length almost surely. The finite Poisson union is a subset. Conditioning its count to n, an event of positive probability, transfers zero expected boundary length to an n-iid sample. This justifies additive inside/outside decomposition when endpoints are sampled as required. It does not assert the same fact for adversarial fixed endpoints or arbitrary deterministic networks.

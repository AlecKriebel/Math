## Exact scope

Records an affirmative answer to the literal per-element additive-distortion condition in Calegari's Question 8.2. This is a credited consequence of known line-action conjugacy results, not a new discovery.

Deroin–Kleptsyn–Navas–Parwani, Ann. Probab. 41 (2013), Theorem 8.5, gives a stronger conclusion for finitely generated orientation-preserving actions. The package includes self-contained deterministic proofs of the needed weaker conclusion, including a countable-family, orientation-reversing extension. All maps use one common coordinate change.

The historical toroidal-obstruction remark is explicitly retained as a mismatch with the displayed quantifiers. No bound uniform across group elements, geometric control of the conjugacy, or ambient leaf-distance conclusion is asserted.

## Independent circle-action mechanism

Let F be the chosen positively oriented Poncelet/billiard vertex map for a nested confocal elliptical caustic. It is an orientation-preserving circle homeomorphism. Classical Poncelet porism gives F^N=id when the caustic admits an N-periodic orbit; if the least period is N, F has order N. Central inversion S is a nonidentity orientation-preserving involution and commutes with F, since it preserves the ellipse, caustic, and chosen orientation.

The group generated by commuting F and S is finite, of size at most2N. A nonidentity finite-order orientation-preserving circle homeomorphism cannot have a fixed point: cutting at such a point gives an increasing interval map, whose nontrivial displacement is incompatible with finite order. Consequently the finite group acts freely. Its action on any finite orbit preserves cyclic order, so it embeds in the cyclic group of rotations of that finite orbit and is cyclic. A finite cyclic group has a unique involution. For even least period N, F^(N/2) is an involution, hence F^(N/2)=S. This works without assuming winding1 or a simple polygon. It does not hold merely because an even number of entries was written down.

The circle-homeomorphism/porism premise is a classical input, not a derived new theorem. The proof above independently establishes the primitive-even consequence once that premise is present.

## Independent focal-pair algebra

Write A=(a cos u,b sin u), B=(a cos v,b sin v), t=(u+v)/2, d=(v-u)/2, and g(t)=a² sin²t+b² cos²t. Reverse the orbit if needed so every edge uses an unwrapped advance0<v-u<π. A nested ellipse containing O guarantees this choice; stars may accumulate multiple turns globally.

The chord's line is x cos t/a+y sin t/b=cos d. Tangency to the confocal ellipse gives

sin²d=λ[cos²t/a²+sin²t/b²]=λg/(a²b²).

Since0<λ<b², both sin d and cos d are positive, and the edge is not an antipodal chord. Let f=(c,0), c²=a²-b², D=b²-λ, K=a²b²-λc². Its antipedal intersection Q satisfies

(A-f)·Q=|A|²-f·A, (B-f)·Q=|B|²-f·B.

The determinant is2b sin d(a cos d-c cos t). For the opposite edge it is2b sin d(a cos d+c cos t), and

a²cos²d-c²cos²t=gD/b²>0.

Thus these lines genuinely meet throughout the stated open parameter range. For O the determinant2ab sin d cos d is likewise nonzero.

Solving the x coordinate gives

Q_x=[cos t(a²+c²sin²t-c²sin²d)-ca cos d]/(a cos d-c cos t).

Replacing t by t+π for the opposite edge and adding simplifies to

[Q_x(A,B;f)+Q_x(-A,-B;f)]/2
= c[-1+K cos²t/(a²D)].

Subtracting the two original line equations shows
(B-A)·[Q(A,B;f)+Q(-A,-B;f)+2f]=0.
Since B-A is parallel to(-a sin t,b cos t), the y coordinate of that pair mean is

cK sin t cos t/(abD).

Reflection across the y axis supplies the opposite focus, with the overall factor c replaced by−c and no need to change the underlying orbit orientation. For O, the two intersections are negatives, giving centroid zero.

## Independent variational cancellation

The length of an individual edge is
ell(t,d)=2 sin d sqrt(g).
At a stationary specular billiard polygon, the first variation of total length under arbitrary tangential vertex motions is zero. In particular all u_i may be increased by the same infinitesimal epsilon. This holds d_i fixed while varying t_i; the perturbed polygon is NOT assumed to stay tangent to the original caustic.

Differentiate the unrestricted length first, then evaluate the caustic relation:

partial_t ell=sin d*g'/sqrt(g)
=2c²sqrt(λ) sin t cos t/(ab).

Summing gives sum_i sin t_i cos t_i=0. Differentiating the already-substituted formula at fixed λ would be logically wrong because the tangential variation leaves the caustic family; this is a specific proof/code audit trap.

The original caustic edge lengths themselves equal

ell_i=2sqrt(λ)g_i/(ab)
=2a sqrt(λ)/b *[1−(c²/a²)cos²t_i].

Therefore H:=mean_i cos²t_i equals
(a²/c²)[1−bL/(2a sqrt(λ)N)].
Combining this with opposite-edge pairing proves the proposed focal formula and zero vertical centroid, provided the classical family and period premises above are correct. Along any smooth connected billiard/Poncelet family, the same stationarity gives dL/ds=0, hence L and this centroid remain constant.

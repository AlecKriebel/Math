# The vertex centroid of focal antipedals of even elliptic billiards

Alec Kriebel, ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X.

This research note proves k405 for the original-polygon, full-line antipedals of periodic elliptic billiards about the center and the two foci. The centroid is the unweighted mean of the antipedal vertices. The hypotheses are a strictly nested nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic and even **least** period. Both primitive star polygons and either orientation are included. The circle is a separate zero-centroid case; the noncircular focal coefficient is not substituted into the circular case.

The source table did not formally define least period. Its opposite-vertex symmetry discussion supports the primitive interpretation adopted explicitly here. An even number of entries in a repeated odd orbit is insufficient. Hyperbolic and degenerate caustics are outside this result.

The mathematical argument is in `antipedal_centroids.tex` and its compiled PDF. The verification directory provides portable exact checks and explains their limitations. Those finite checks do not replace the finite-action and perimeter-stationarity proofs of the all-period result.

The companion `priority_and_provenance.md` records the original source, classical ingredients, the exact later overlapping theorem and the documented chronology. No absolute priority, exclusive novelty or independent-discovery claim is made. The question was posed without proof in the 2020/2021 source; this package does not claim it remains unsolved at publication.

AI tools were used extensively to develop, draft, reproduce and adversarially audit the result. Independent AI review is not conventional human peer review. This preprint is unrefereed and has not undergone conventional human peer review.

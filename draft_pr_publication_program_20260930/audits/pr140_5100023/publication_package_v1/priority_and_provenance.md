## Target

Invariant k405 in Reznik–Garcia–Koiller, *Fifty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard*, Arnold Mathematical Journal 7 (2021), 341–355, Table 5, p.348, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y. The arXiv companion's printed title is *Eighty New Invariants in the Elliptic Billiard*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12497v11, Table 5, printed p.7 (the arXiv metadata uses the longer title *Eighty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard*). The source asks for the constant antipedal vertex centroid at even N for the center and foci. Its initial setting is a pair of confocal ellipses. Its Section 3.7 uses central inversion of opposite vertices, supporting the explicit least-period parity convention here; the table itself does not formally define least period.

## Earlier mathematical ingredients

The primary-source review included the source conjecture; Bialy–Tabachnikov's *Dan Reznik's identities and more*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-020-00428-7, published online in 2020; Gutkin–Tabachnikov's *Billiards in Finsler and Minkowski geometries*, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/S0393-0440(01)00039-0; Stachel's 2021 geometry of elliptic billiards, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00022-021-00606-2; Weill's 1878 original on Poncelet polygons; Schwartz–Tabachnikov centroid results; discrete-conic and Poncelet-grid work; related circummass/lamina results; and Garcia–Koiller–Reznik's 2022 spatial-integral study, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s10883-022-09608-y, supplied by the user.

Central symmetry and perimeter stationarity are classical. The earlier boundary-tangent pedal centroid and harmonic theorems are different from the original-polygon antipedal statement. Under the classical skew-hodograph map, those earlier results imply the focal antipedal formula **after** insertion of the target-specific opposite-edge antipedal identity. The audit did not locate an earlier published complete bridge with that identity. Reconstructing this deduction during the audit is not evidence that the complete target theorem had been published previously.

The spatial-integral paper concerns spatial/aperiodic averages and does not state this finite-period k405 theorem. Other inspected centroid results concern ordinary vertices, contact polygons, pedal feet, laminas or circumcenters; object distinctions matter.

The original Veselov and M'Clelland texts were not read in full; the relevant classical skew map was checked in the accessible Gutkin–Tabachnikov primary text. No concrete earlier complete k405 theorem was located in these unread originals. This access limit and the finite search coverage prevent an absolute-priority claim. They are not presented as proof of either presence or absence of the target theorem.

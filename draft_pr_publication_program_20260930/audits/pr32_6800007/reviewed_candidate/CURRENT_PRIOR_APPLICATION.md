## 2. Integral low characteristic spaces suffice

The map

The elementary homogeneous sphere fibration U(n)→U(n+1)→S^(2n+1) shows that U(3) already has those stable groups in degrees ≤5. The determinant gives π₁(U(3))≅Z and SU(3) has the same groups in degrees ≥2. Consequently BU(3) has π₂=Z, π₄=Z and vanishing π₁,π₃,π₅; BSU(3) has π₄=Z and vanishing groups in degrees 1,2,3,5. The first Chern class detects π₂. The second detects π₄ with coefficient ±1: the rank-two quaternionic Hopf bundle on S⁴ has c₂ a generator and its stabilization generates this group. Equivalently its S³ sphere bundle has total space S⁷ and the Gysin sequence makes the Euler class, equal to c₂, a generator. No rational Chern character or division by a factorial is used.

The map induces isomorphisms through π₅ and is surjective on π₆, since the target's π₆ is zero. Its homotopy fiber is therefore 5-connected. This is sufficient for the component and loop classification over M. Relative problems for maps, loops and comparisons of loops involve M, M×I, M×S¹ and M×S¹×I, of dimensions at most 3,4,4,5. All relevant relative obstruction groups in the homotopy fiber vanish. No phantom-map or compact-source inference is needed. In particular, φ induces the needed π₀ and π₁ isomorphisms on the unbased mapping spaces and preserves the components of their homotopy fibers.

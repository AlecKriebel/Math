Required attribution: Gromov/Lees h-principle; Duchamp/Forstnerič/Borrelli Euclidean formal classification; Derdzinski–Januszkiewicz determinant/Maslov mechanism and explicit three-dimensional existence extension; Koshkin primary/secondary homogeneous-map framework; Falbel–Veloso sphere result. Exact old citations alone do not replace these precise statements.

The initial survey main-body gap was closed by independently reading the complete raw HAL manuscript supplied after a separate successful primary retrieval. Its Theorem 3.5 gives local null-curvature equivalence; §7 gives a scalar invariant constant on homotopy classes, not injective classification. Algebraic K3/Bloch representation invariants in §§4/6 address a different input. This is another inspected predecessor, not a new exact-target theorem.

Material remaining limits: several publisher PDF versions were not obtained, despite complete author/arXiv/HAL alternatives; incoming citation indexes and bounded searches are not exhaustive. A genuinely equivalent stronger classification of the non-simply-connected homogeneous frame target or twisted section problem counts as prior even without a printed flag formula. This flag family establishes no such all-source equivalence and cannot rule out every general theorem elsewhere; that dependency is explicitly assigned to the independent topology family. Novelty cannot be inferred from the lack of specialized search matches.

## Source pitfalls that affect comparisons

K09's de Rham Corollary 1, pp 344–345, says ordinary integral H³ vanishes for closed nonorientable three-manifolds. This is false: H³(RP²×S¹;Z)=Z/2. Thus its integral Theorem 3 and its de Rham corollary must be separated. A vanishing conclusion about top-degree de Rham cohomology does not discard integral torsion. This source defect does not certify novelty of the candidate.

AK03's topological Proposition 1 is stated for closed 3-manifolds, but the displayed proof invokes a Heegaard splitting without addressing nonorientability; later p 6 claims torsion-freeness of H²(M;Z), which also fails on RP²×S¹. Use its stated integral map-to-group coordinates as prior context, not as the sole broad nonorientable proof certificate. AS06 separately labels its new main theorems orientable and warns that Proposition 1 describes a set rather than the full group structure.

DJ's arXiv version was mislabeled v4 in INITIAL_CONCLUSION_SEAL.md. VERSION_CORRECTION.md preserves and corrects that error: the record lists v1, submitted 29 December 2006. The relevant n=3 note is present both there and in the journal-formatted author copy. Dpre's “Date:1984” is not a secure publication date, since the same file cites 1986 works and the author's bibliography labels it a preprint.

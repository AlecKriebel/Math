The literal question concerns unrestricted couplings of complete nearest-neighbour SRW path laws on Z^3 or Z^4 with starts ten graph steps apart, and positive probability that their whole ranges do not meet. The source does not add co-adaptation or joint Markovianity. Treating every displacement of l1-norm ten is a stated stronger interpretation, not a silent source quantifier. A complete resolution would have to decide the infinite probability, not short finite horizons.

Fix any integer d>=1, x,y and N>=0. Alphabet size q=2d; each word w of length N has probability q^-N and determines a unique ordered path (even when its range repeats). There are L=q^N labelled words on each side. Let E contain exactly the pairs whose vertex ranges, including starts, are disjoint. Every joint finite law is an L by L nonnegative matrix P with row and column sums 1/L. Thus Q=LP is doubly stochastic. Its finite decomposition into permutations can be proved without a generic LP assumption: for every set of rows A, positive-support column neighbours have size >=|A| by summing row masses and bounded column masses, so support has a perfect matching. Subtract the smallest matching entry times that permutation. The residual has common row and column sum 1-epsilon; repeat on its normalization, reducing the number of positive entries, until zero. The subtracted coefficients sum to one. The objective mass on E is a convex combination of numbers of E-edges in permutations, divided by L. Each such subset is a matching. A maximum matching of size M can be completed by any bijection between its unused rows and unused columns. An E-edge created by this completion would increase M, impossible; hence the optimum is exactly M/L.

Hall deficiency is max_A(|A|-|N_E(A)|)>=0 (empty A admissible), and M=L-deficiency. An elementary proof is to take a maximum matching, begin alternating paths at its unmatched left vertices, and obtain reachable sets S (left) and T (right). There are no unmatched right endpoints. Each reachable matched right partner is in S, and every neighbour of S is T. Therefore |S|-|T| equals the number of unmatched left vertices. Conversely every matching loses at least |A|-|N(A)| left vertices for each A. Crucially N(A) is the union of the neighbours of individual A-words: disjoint from AT LEAST ONE word. Replacing union with intersection would ask a different question.

The infinite increment space Omega is a countable product of a finite discrete alphabet, compact metrizable. The set C of couplings of its two fixed iid product marginals is nonempty, closed under weak convergence (test bounded continuous one-coordinate functions), and compact. R_N depends on only two finite prefixes and is clopen; R_N decreases to R, the full all-times disjointness event. a_N is nonincreasing by restriction. Extend an optimal prefix joint law by independent iid tails, independent of the joint prefix and of one another, to an element pi_N of C. A subsequence pi_Nj converges weakly. For every FIXED k and all Nj>=k, pi_Nj(R_k)>=a_Nj>=inf a_N. Clopen weak continuity yields pi(R_k)>=inf a_N. Continuity of probabilities from above yields pi(R)>=inf a_N. Every coupling has pi(R)<=a_k for each k. Thus sup_C pi(R)=inf a_N and the supremum is attained by the constructed limit. This does not imply the optimizers are consistent, that moving R_N events have weakly continuous probabilities, or that the positive a_N have positive infimum. No exchange of inf and sup is asserted without the above proof.

Boundary cases: N=0 has L=1 and optimum 1 for unequal starts, 0 for equal starts. Equal starts force all a_N and the infinite optimum to zero. All dimensions d>=1 and all x,y are covered by the reformulation, but no dimension's infinite optimum is evaluated by it. In d=1 recurrence supplies a control: the first walk hits the second initial vertex almost surely, so alpha=0 at unequal finite starts. This must coexist with positive finite optima; positive finite estimates do not imply the target.

Write v=y-x with l1-norm ten. Under translation coupling Y_n=X_n+v, an intersection entails X_i-X_j=v. Since any two points on a length-N path have graph distance <=|i-j|<=N, this cannot occur for N<=9. For an infinite iid increment path choose any ten-letter geodesic word of displacement v. Independent consecutive ten-letter blocks have this word with fixed positive probability, hence one occurs almost surely (the probability of no occurrence in K blocks is (1-q^-10)^K). At an occurrence starting at time 10k, X_10k+10=Y_10k; thus the same coupling has infinite avoidance probability zero. This validates only finite a_N=1 for N<=9.

In every coupling, the event that X hits y is a subset of range intersection because Y_0=y. Hence alpha<=1-P_x(T_y<infinity). At time <=10 a first hit of y must occur exactly at 10 and along a geodesic. With a_i=|v_i|, the number of geodesic words is 10!/product(a_i!) and its probability is that number/q^10>0. This yields a strict upper bound below one, not a zero result. Time zero and signed displacements must remain included.

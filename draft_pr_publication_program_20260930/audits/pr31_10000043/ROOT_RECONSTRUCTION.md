## Universal nonanticipating coupling and completed product law

For fixed root and finite cap M, each accepted vertex has finite degree and finitely many earlier vertices in the FIFO queue. Processing all its edges and never querying twice gives a sequential predictable exploration. Each queried horizontal edge above base e={u,v} has its height in one of the accepted fiber-height sets at u or v. Their union has at most2M elements, so at most2M distinct such edges are queried, independently of interleaving, closed trials and rejected proposals. M1 has a smaller actual bound because all accepted heights equal the starting height, but the stated2M bound remains correct. The original sharpness control is M2, not a sharpness claim for every M.

Give every base edge a disjoint independent reservoir of2M Bernoulli(p) variables, plus independent variables for vertical edges. A query uses the next never-read entry of its reservoir or its never-read vertical variable. The choice of variable label is measurable from earlier revealed states; its value is independent of that past. Finite transcript probabilities therefore match the ordinary product-edge exploration by induction, even when queries are chosen using outcomes from many other reservoirs.

Here is an explicit countable completion argument beyond a finite simulation. Introduce independent fallback variables U_f for every physical product edge f, independent of all reservoirs/vertical inputs. At exploration step n define Wn by the assigned values on edges already queried and U_f on the others. Each Wn has the full independent Bernoulli product law by finite predictable exposure and independent completion. For any fixed finite set of physical edges, Wn eventually equals the final W: queried edges have finite query times, and edges never queried keep their fallback values forever. Thus their joint law converges to the same product law, and all finite cylinders of W have the required independent law. This proves legitimate completed-product-law coupling for a possibly infinite exploration. There is no conditioning on the ultimate fiber-size event.

Define Ye as the maximum of all2M entries of reservoir e, including unused entries. Disjoint reservoirs give independent homogeneous Bernoulli(qM) base edges with qM=1-(1-p)^(2M)<1 for p<1. Each accepted vertex has an ancestry path from the root; its horizontal steps used open reservoir entries, hence its projected path is Y-open. By pc(G)=1 this projected component is finite a.s.

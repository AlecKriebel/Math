## Exact result and evidence

Fix a noncircular ellipse and a strictly nested, nondegenerate confocal elliptical billiard caustic. For every admissible odd least period N>=3, including coprime star turns, intersect consecutive tangents at the billiard vertices. Unit-invert these OUTER vertices separately about the two ORIGINAL foci. Join each inverse vertex list by straight edges in its inherited order. The product of the two signed shoelace areas is independent of phase.

Signed area is essential for stars. Original-vertex inversions, outer-locus foci, inverse circular arcs and pedal polygons are different constructions. The theorem does not extend without qualification to even periods, collapsed or grazing caustics, or hyperbolic caustics. Circle families are handled separately by rotation. Reversal changes both area signs; r-fold traversal multiplies the product by r^2. Unit inversion under spatial dilation by lambda gives the product factor lambda^-4.

## Exact definitions, scope, and mechanism

Its outer vertices V_i are intersections of the tangents to the original E at consecutive original P_i. The inversion is in the unit circle at each original boundary focus; it is applied to the V_i. Areas are ordered shoelace sums, hence areas of straight joined inverse vertices. The standing domain on p. 3 is least N>=3 and every admitted coprime winding number. Theorem B specializes to odd N, includes stars, and states a positive phase-independent product. Reversing orientation changes each factor's sign and preserves the product. Theorem A on p. 3 also proves, for every N, that each outer-inverse area is kappa>0 times the corresponding original-vertex inverse area, with one kappa common to both foci. There is also a direct proof of B on p. 16: the two focal outer-area functions have possible simple poles on disjoint cosets S1/S2 for odd N, and folding forces the complementary zeros; their product is holomorphic and doubly periodic. The delicate outer-specific content is in Lemma 5.2, including the additional isotropic outer vertices, cancellation of terms of apparent order two, and the triangular adjacency exception.

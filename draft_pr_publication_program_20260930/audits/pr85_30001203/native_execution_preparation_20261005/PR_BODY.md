## Result

A compact connected two-dimensional counterexample to Krener's printed manifold conjecture: completeness and negative curvature of the actual finite-time observation Gramian do not force global observability.

Take a connected two-sheet cover of a closed genus-two hyperbolic surface, a smooth Nash isometric embedding of the base into R17, observation through the cover, and zero dynamics. The state surface has genus three. For every fixed T>0 the exact Gramian is P_T=Tπ*g, complete with sectional curvature −1/T. It is uniformly positive and bounded relative to a fixed smooth background metric, with explicit bounds in a fixed finite atlas. The observation is locally injective, yet every attained output history has exactly two distinct initial states.

The original local-coordinate manifold formulation does not require simple connectivity, a global Euclidean state domain, nonzero dynamics, or a restricted output dimension. This result addresses that formulation; no strengthened Euclidean or simply connected claim is made.

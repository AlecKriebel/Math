# Current audited result — OWR-3394-020 / 30001203

PR85 addresses Krener's printed manifold conjecture, “Open Problem: Global Observability of Spaces of Negative Curvature,” *Oberwolfach Reports* 11/2009, printed pp. 674–675, DOI <https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2009/11>. The printed formulation uses local coordinates on a smooth manifold. It does not impose simple connectivity, a global Euclidean state domain, nonzero dynamics, or a smaller output dimension.

The verified construction is two-dimensional. Let `(S,g)` be a closed oriented genus-two hyperbolic surface with curvature `−1`, let `π:M→S` be a connected unramified double cover, and let `e:(S,g)→R17` be a smooth isometric embedding. The cover is a closed connected genus-three surface. For zero dynamics and observation `h=e∘π`, the **actual finite-time observation Gramian** is

`P_T = T π* g`, for every fixed `T>0`.

Its sectional curvature is `−1/T`; compactness gives completeness. Relative to any fixed smooth background metric `q`, compactness supplies constants `0<c≤C<∞` with `Tc q≤P_T≤TC q`. In a fixed finite atlas of sufficiently small centered hyperbolic charts, `4T I≤P_T≤(64T/9) I`. These are bounds for a fixed normalization and positive `T`; no uniform statement under arbitrary coordinate rescaling or as `T→0` is asserted.

The observation is locally injective, but every attained history has **exactly two distinct initial states**. Since the dynamics are stationary, the same ambiguity persists for all real times. Thus completeness and genuine negative sectional curvature of this observation Gramian do not imply global observability in the printed manifold formulation. The result is an application of classical hyperbolic geometry, covering theory, and Nash's smooth compact embedding theorem (1956, Theorem 2, p. 59), with the classical local/global observability ambiguity credited to Hermann–Krener (1977, Example 3.3). It does not settle a strengthened Euclidean or simply connected formulation.

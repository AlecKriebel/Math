## Actual mathematical content and comparison

Printed661–663 develop scaling-group actions on proper rational transfer functions and associated orbits, quotient parameter spaces and principal bundles. Theorem1 classifies connected components of Rat(n); Theorem2 concerns free scaling actions on transfer-function spaces and associated fibre bundles. These are spaces of input-output models and their parameters. They are not the compact hyperbolic state manifold, output observation map, or finite-time differential Gramian of Krener's conjecture.

Printed663–665 discuss identification of discrete-time linear systems with Gaussian noise, normalized support/likelihood functions, common factors, pole/zero strata, and the Fisher–Riemannian metric derived from a likelihood Hessian. The paper distinguishes unidentifiability and degeneracy in the rational-function model space. It does not construct two distinct initial states with identical output histories and a complete negatively curved finite-forward-time observation Gramian.

Printed665–667 treat the Bezout resultant as a relative invariant, Hamiltonian/symplectic structures and induced volume forms under scaling. A symplectic two-form or invariant volume density is not the positive Riemannian Gramian in the target problem. None of these statements supplies its curvature, completeness or uniform positive bounds.

Printed668–669 give invariant integral geometry, probability densities, a Laplace–Beltrami operator, and a Riemannian quadratic form on minimal linear realizations. The realization metric uses state-space matrices and controllability/observability matrices. It is invariant under changes of realization basis; it is a metric on a space of realizations, not the target state's exact unweighted finite-time Gramian. The final discussion explicitly identifies a displayed coefficient-space quadratic form as flat. That statement does not establish flatness of every metric mentioned in the paper, and no such extrapolation is used here.

Printed670 concludes and lists references. Reference6 points to Sussmann's1974 quotient announcement; reference4 is Krishnaprasad's1977 Harvard dissertation, *Geometry of Minimal Systems and the Identification Problem*. Those references are not treated as full-text evidence simply because their titles appear here. The older scaling/quotient/metric ingredients warrant historical context, but this complete inspected paper does not exhibit the full PR85 negatively curved exact-Gramian construction or state a theorem directly implying it with the target's hypotheses.

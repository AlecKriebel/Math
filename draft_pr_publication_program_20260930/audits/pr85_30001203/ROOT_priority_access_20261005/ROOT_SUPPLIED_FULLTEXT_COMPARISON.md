## ECC2009: precise exclusion of overlap

The original Theorem1.2 has assumptionsA1–A4. A3 requires different omega-limit sets to have different output-image sets. For the submitted systemf=0, omega(x)={x}. A distinct deck pair has different singleton limit sets and the same singleton output image. It failsA3. This premise failure suffices independently of whether the smooth Nash output is analytic. The source's complete background Riemannian metric is arbitrary and used topologically; it is not prescribed to be the differential observation Gramian.

Lemma2.1 assumes injectivity on its compact setA; Lemma2.2 assumes observability on the invariant setA. They cannot supply global injectivity by dropping those premises. Corollary2.1 separates critical points/output images; Corollary2.2 retainsA3. The cascade application requires finitely many limiting sets and their output separation. The perspective/matrix applications impose strongly regular dynamics and additional rank or equilibrium-output separation conditions. No application gives the compact zero-dynamics hyperbolic-cover example or a curvature-only criterion. All five pages contain no asserted disposition of Krener's OWR conjecture, Nash realization, or complete negatively curved exact-Gramian counterexample.

The paper is comparison evidence, not a dependency of our counterexample proof. Its own global-convergence proof is not being certified here; for example, a printed reference toA2 in the different-limit-set separation step points to the condition stated asA3. ## Survey2013: precise exclusion of overlap

Sections1.1–1.2 distinguish point separation/global observability from local rank. Printedp5 expressly restricts the empirical-Gramian interpretation to a neighborhood of a nominal trajectory. It supplies a numerical local linear approximation, not global injection from negative sectional curvature. Printedp10 assumes the output-derivative coordinate map is a diffeomorphism on its region before constructing a high-gain observer. The subsequent global/semi-global observer discussion is tied to additional system structure and referenced observer designs, not a theorem about arbitrary Gramian curvature.

Sections1.3–1.4 concern local linearization, Lyapunov/high-gain designs, noisy Kalman/H-infinity/minimum-energy filters; their covariance, Riccati and optimization objects are not the exact target Gramian. Section1.5 concerns linear Hilbert-space observability or PDE reductions via inertial manifolds, rather than the compact finite-cover state/output system. Section1.6 and the entire bibliography contain no explicit negative-curvature conjecture disposition or hyperbolic-cover/Nash construction. The chapter does cite Hermann–Krener1977, Krener–Ide2009 and Krener–Kang2003, but not the OWR conjecture. Absence of an OWR citation does not prove the problem's continued worldwide openness.

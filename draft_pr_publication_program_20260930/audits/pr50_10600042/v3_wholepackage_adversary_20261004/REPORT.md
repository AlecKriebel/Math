## Universal proof and edge/boundary audit

Soundness is independent of completeness. C is conjugation at the displayed even count. BC applies conjugation to the prefix on N−1 strands and positively stabilizes each end. T compares the positive stabilization with either signed classical or virtual stabilization of the same prefix on N−1 strands. D applies an allowed stabilization and then a positive one. R/L are genuine virtual exchanges at N strands. BR/BL are genuine exchanges at N−1 followed by positive stabilization. The listed support restrictions are exactly sufficient for each operation.

For completeness, P(m,w)=(m,w) at even m and (m+1,w sigma_m) at odd m fixes the original even endpoints. Every fixed-level relation lifts in its entire old context with its tail retained, so group equality is handled by the explicit presentation without a word-problem oracle. Even/odd conjugation becomes C/BC. Even/odd stabilization becomes D/T, including both signs and the virtual generator; reversals cover every destabilization. Even/odd right exchange becomes R/BR, and even/odd left exchange becomes L/BL. The shifted blocks in odd left exchange use at most N−2, while the extra positive tail is sigma_(N−1). There is no missing unrestricted edge family and no appeal to the desired restricted theorem to justify completeness.

The one-strand empty word pads to the two-strand single positive crossing, whose closure is the unknot. At N=2, BC has empty blocks, T is a valid comparison of one-strand stabilizations, and the R/L exchanges are identities up to defining relations. BR/BL first occur at N=4. An isolated zero-strand state handles the empty link if desired; no positive-strand closure is empty. Empty words on different tags remain different unlinks. Arbitrarily long blocks are covered symbolically rather than through bounded testing. The height bound follows directly from the nondecreasing function m+(m mod2), and applies to an already supplied certificate.

Only defining relations are authorized in arbitrary surrounding word contexts. The schemes are replacements of whole tagged states subject to their stated support conditions. Extending T/R/BR/BL freely inside arbitrary unsupported words would be a different and generally false theorem; the manuscript does not do this. Classical chains use only classical letters; the virtual presentation does not include a welded forbidden relation.

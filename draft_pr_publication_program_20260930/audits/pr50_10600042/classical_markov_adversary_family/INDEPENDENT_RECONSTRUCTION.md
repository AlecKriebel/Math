Let B_m be the ordinary Artin braid group with oriented ordinary closure. Assume the classical Alexander theorem and ordinary unframed Markov theorem, with both signs of right stabilization, as imported theorems. Define P on a tagged braid (m,b): identity for even m and (m+1,b sigma_m) for odd m. Embedding preserves every defining relation, so this is well defined on group elements as well as on words. Adding a positive terminal stabilization preserves oriented closure.

The even object category consists of positive even N>=2 and words using indices1..N-1, modulo ordinary Artin relations. The replacement schemes C,BC,T,D in the original text have explicit word data; neither closure equivalence nor existence of an odd path is a move-applicability oracle. Auxiliary odd braids occur in the proof, not among move states.

Every classical Markov edge has the following exact image. At even m, conjugation becomes C and stabilization becomes D:(m,b)<->(m+2,b sigma_m^e sigma_(m+1)). At odd m, conjugation becomes BC:(m+1,b sigma_m)<->(m+1,a b a^-1 sigma_m); stabilization becomes T:(m+1,b sigma_m)<->(m+1,b sigma_m^e). Here e=+1 or-1 and, crucially, a,b lie in the embedded B_m using indices at mostm-1. Relations at odd m retain the positive terminal letter and remain valid relations in the larger even group. Reverse edges are reverse replacements. Images of even endpoints are fixed, so lifting a finite ordinary Markov certificate proves completeness with only even states.

Soundness is independently checked: C is conjugation; BC is closure-preserving conjugation before the same positive stabilization; T compares the two signed stabilizations of the same lower braid; D is two stabilizations. This reasoning uses the imported unrestricted theorems only to justify closure preservation and completeness, not as an implicit definition of the move relation. Support restrictions are mathematically essential, not cosmetic. For T, dropping the lower-word support permits sigma_1^3<->sigma_1 at N=2, connecting trefoil and unknot. Component count alone cannot detect that error.

Every nonempty oriented link has a positive-strand Alexander representative; padding once makes its count even. The one-strand identity becomes sigma_1 on two strands, representing the unknot. The two-strand identity remains the two-component unlink; tags cannot be dropped. Zero-strand empty link is isolated if included, and no assertion about positive strand counts covers it. Framed/transverse versions need different stabilization hypotheses and are not implied.

For any certificate with maximum strand count M, P gives maximum 2 ceil(M/2); this is exact for that pointwise lift and is achieved whenever an odd maximum is attained. No intermediate move state exceeds that bound. Each conjugation/stabilization edge maps to at most one displayed scheme (positive odd stabilization may become identity); a relation edge is a retained ordinary relation. This does not bound the length or height of a certificate that must first be found, does not prove decidability, and does not assert bounded geometric support independent of block words.

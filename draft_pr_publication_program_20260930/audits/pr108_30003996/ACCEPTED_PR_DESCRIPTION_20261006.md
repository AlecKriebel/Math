Resolve Volker Kaibel’s Problem 1 in the Oberwolfach report “Combinatorial Optimization” (workshop 2018; published 2019): choose one undirected spanning tree minimizing the sum of the whole-tree orientation cost over all vertex roots, with explicitly supplied root-dependent arc costs.

The decision problem is strongly NP-complete even with costs in {0,1,2}, and also with strictly positive costs in {1,2,3}.

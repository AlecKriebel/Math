## Exact target and claim

The target is Kaibel Problem 1: choose one undirected spanning tree; for each vertex root orient all tree edges consistently away from that root; minimize the sum of arbitrary root-specific linear costs on every oriented tree arc. Inward orientation is equivalent by a global transpose of each cost vector. The candidate establishes strong NP-completeness of the finite, explicitly encoded nonnegative integral decision subclass and optimization NP-hardness. Problem 2 has a fixed-root arborescence and one path cost per destination; this is a different objective.

## Published prior coverage (falsification criterion)

A concrete antecedent establishes prior coverage if a legitimately published primary theorem predating this work either (a) expressly proves NP-hardness of the exact all-root/full-orientation objective or its precisely equivalent all-integral Martin lift, or (b) gives a theorem and polynomial parameter-preserving instance transformation from its model into this exact model, with each feasible object and objective accounted for. A simpler restricted subclass is sufficient if it is actually contained in this model. The publication, theorem scope, transformation, and proof assumptions must be inspectable. A historical assertion may be evidence, but not by itself a checkable transfer. Projected spanning-tree integrality, hardness of an arbitrary superclass, path multiplicities, predecessor-edge charges, and color constraints do not establish coverage unless an exact transfer is verified.

## Substantive residual novelty and permitted disposition

A independently checked reduction deciding the literal 2018 question is a substantive resolution contribution if no concrete covering antecedent survives an adequately scoped adversarial search. This does not establish first-in-history novelty. The source authorship and classical Martin formulation must be attributed; dated audit language may state no earlier proof was located. A narrower numerical bound, transpose, or uniform shift adds no independent novelty if an earlier theorem already covers the exact hardness claim. Mathematical validity alone is not sufficient for priority clearance. Publication may proceed only with claims matched to evidence and with a later whole-package review; this review cannot certify an unprepared publication package.

## Material obstruction / HOLD criterion

HOLD is required for (i) an identified concrete covering prior result, (ii) an identified plausibly covering primary theorem whose missing full proof or unavailable exact statement is necessary to determine coverage, (iii) a failed essential transfer/equivalence needed for the intended claims, or (iv) an unqualified novelty/open-status assertion unsupported by history evidence. Generic possible unindexed literature, an unread original used only for accurately limited classical attribution, and inability to prove an exhaustive negative are historical uncertainty, not automatically material obstructions. Whether a missing source is material depends on its mechanism and exact objective, never only its age or search popularity.

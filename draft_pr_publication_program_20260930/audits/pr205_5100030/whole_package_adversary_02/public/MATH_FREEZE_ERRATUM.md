In the geometric reconstruction paragraph, if C=cn(x)cn(y), D=dn(x)dn(y), S=sn(x)sn(y), and Delta=1-mS², the two subtraction equations are

    C+S D = cn(y-x) Delta,
    D+mS C = dn(y-x) Delta.

The frozen note omitted the factor Delta from both right-hand sides in its shorthand sentence. Solving this system gives

    C=cn(y-x)-S dn(y-x),
    D=dn(y-x)-m S cn(y-x).

These are the identities used by the packet and by the contact-incidence check. The determinant remains Delta>=1-m>0. This corrects the reviewer's transcription, with no change to the packet, the geometric incidence, or the mathematical assessment. The final report uses the fully written equations.

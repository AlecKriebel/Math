# Geometry and exact implication boundaries

Let the caustic have semiaxes alpha>beta>0 with alpha²−beta²=c², sharing the outer ellipse's foci (±c,0). Write an original side line as n·X=h with n=(cos phi,sin phi) a unit outward normal and h>0. Its support value is h=sqrt(alpha²cos²phi+beta²sin²phi). Since h²−c²cos²phi=beta²>0 and h>0, both h±c cos phi are positive ordinary distances, regardless of where their perpendicular feet lie on the bounded chord. Thus

q+=h−c cos phi, q−=h+c cos phi, q+q−=beta²,

and, with H=sum h and C=sum cos phi,

(sum q+)(sum q−)=H²−c²C².

This is the same support-function geometry directly used in published Bialy–Tabachnikov Lemma4.2, with their caustic (a,b)=(alpha,beta). The printed use of segments there is interpreted by its explicit support formula as supporting-line distances. It supplies no clipped-segment extension.

Pointwise fixed products do not imply a fixed product of sums: positive lists (1,1,1) and their reciprocals have product of sums9; lists (2,1/2,1) and their reciprocals have49/4. Both have q+i q−i=1. These are a logical nonimplication control, not billiard counterexamples.

In the canonical notation already verified in the sealed source audit, v is the contact parameter and k=c/alpha, k'=beta/alpha. One has q±=beta(1±k sn v)/dn v. Put t=v+K. The normal coordinates become, up to fixed signs/interchange, (cn t,sn t), while h=alpha dn t. Consequently the target is alpha²[(sum dn t_i)²−k²(sum cn t_i)²]. Reversal and a coprime winding permute the entire odd grid; an odd r-fold repetition multiplies each sum by r and the product by r².

Akopyan–Schwartz–Tabachnikov Thm6.2 applies to the real unit-direction circle obtained by their dual Minkowski construction. Summing its offset correlations gives constancy of (sum cn t_i)²+(sum sn t_i)². That is a different quadratic expression. The pointwise Jacobi square relation alone does not turn all cross dn products into that expression. This prevents a bare claim that Thm6.2 already prints or immediately proves k601.

A tempting map (dn t/k', i k cn t/k') lies on a complex unit circle and represents the target through a bilinear norm. This suggests extending the published argument to a different complex conic and its offset correspondences. This audit has not established the full Poncelet-caustic correspondence, closure components, nondegeneracy for all offsets, or continuation of the published real-domain theorem for that map. It remains a partial route; no priority conclusion rests on it. The other family owns the direct generalized-Landen implication.

Corvaja–Zannier's Eq48/proof gives a reusable residue-cancellation mechanism on the elliptic phase curve. To infer k601 one must still identify the appropriate trace/product as a meromorphic function on a valid cover and verify every pole and cancellation. Their specific h on the outer ellipse and rational single-function Prop5.1 do not by themselves make that identification. Established mechanism credit is warranted; an exact all-odd theorem implication has not been completed here.

Likewise a projective mapping of conic pairs does not preserve Euclidean perpendicular distances. The projective diagonal/concurrence theorems were not promoted into metric statements by assertion. Contact-to-vertex distances r_i,l_i, focus-inversive vertex distances, limiting-point pedal perimeters and original-side focal perpendicular spokes remain distinct quantities throughout this audit.

# Independent derivation before reading the earlier families

Target: actual compact exteriors X of smooth or locally flat PL S² in S⁴; the unmarked full triple includes the action and k. Ordinary exterior homology is that of S¹, so μ has infinite order and ∂X=S²×S¹ injects as <μ>. Use the left regular coefficient module R=Z[G]; Hom over left R gives a right R cohomology module. Only finite formal sums are allowed. Nonzero H-invariants would contain the entire infinite orbit of a support point, impossible; H⁰X=H⁰∂X=0. Thus the pair LES injects H¹(X,∂X;R) onto ker(H¹(G;R)→H¹(H;R)). Equivariant PL duality converts right to left by inversion and identifies this relative cohomology with H₃ of the cover. This formula needs the inclusion H→G and does not extract H from an unmarked triple.

For a degree-one oriented PAIR map, the boundary degree is one by naturality of the boundary fundamental class. Write m,n for its H¹,H² multipliers on S²×S¹; cup product gives mn=1, so m=±1 and the boundary π₁ map is an isomorphism. The isomorphism of ambient π₁ then carries the meridional inclusion square to an isomorphism. The kernel formula makes f* an isomorphism on relative H¹. Naturality of cap product with f*[X]=[Y] makes f* on third cover HOMOLOGY an isomorphism (cohomology and homology arrows have opposite directions). π₂ isomorphism gives cover H₂ by Hurewicz; cover H₁=0 and connected H₀=Z. Cover H₄ vanishes by noncompactness/relative duality and H>4 vanishes. A simply connected homology equivalence of CW-type spaces is a homotopy equivalence. No construction of the pair map follows from this argument.

For A=C₇⋊₂C₃, C=C₃, B=C₃⋊₋₁Z, the presentation is A *_C B by its universal presentation and injective factor normal form. All H¹ for finite vertex/edge groups with restricted R vanish since regular finite modules are induced and the groups are finite. Rᴮ=Rᴳ=0. Mayer–Vietoris yields 0→Rᴬ→Rᶜ→H¹G→H¹B→0. The normal finite C in B gives H¹B≅H¹(Z;Rᶜ), a direct sum of Z over right B-cosets. Restriction to <t> is injective: corestriction after restriction is 3 and the domain is torsion free. Therefore the restriction kernel is Rᶜ/Rᴬ. Each right A-coset gives seven disjoint C-norm vectors; its A-norm is their primitive diagonal sum. The quotient is free rank six. Right C\A cosets become left A/C by inversion, with inverted group element converting right to left action. a acts by j→2j, b by j→j+1. This arithmetic verifies a block, not its geometric occurrence.

Integral caution: the quotient Q=Z⁷/Zd and the augmentation lattice I=ker(sum:Z⁷→Z) are different constructions. The natural sum-zero map Q→I is [x]↦7x−sum(x)d; relative to [e₀],…,[e₅] and eᵢ−e₆, its matrix is 7I₆−J₆, determinant 7⁵, hence not an integral isomorphism. The inclusion I→Q has index 7. Rank equality and rational equivalence do not certify an integral permutation-module equivalence.

Two gaps stay blocked: (1) obtain a relative-degree-one meridian-compatible pair map from an unmarked full triple; (2) construct two source-category exteriors with equal full triples and different homotopy types, with actual geometric identification of t. Weight one only kills the group after imposing t=1; it does not produce the missing geometric pair.

# Finite multispecies relativistic Vlasov–Maxwell

**Signed impulse estimates and global smooth solutions for finite multispecies relativistic Vlasov–Maxwell**

Alec Kriebel — ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X. No affiliation or coauthor is claimed. Preprint date: October 6, 2026.

The theorem gives global classical existence and uniqueness in three space dimensions for any fixed finite species list with positive masses and arbitrary real charges, nonnegative smooth compact phase data, and compatible Maxwell fields in C_b^∞∩L². Every finite interval retains compact particle phase support. Bounded joint momentum is a continuation criterion. Neither smallness, symmetry nor neutrality is required.

The one-species breakthrough, universal geometric cancellation, dyadic angular selection machinery and broad-field localization are inherited from OpenAI family 362 at commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. Mass normalization and finite-species continuation are established kinetic theory, including Glassey–Schaeffer 1988. This note proves the coefficient transfer for each signed source/receiver pair and closes a simultaneous common-field bootstrap for distinct charge-to-mass ratios. It does not apply separate scalar global theorems to a coupled system. No firstness claim is made.

Constants may depend on all fixed species parameters. Zero charges, opposite signs and repeated species are included. There is no uniform massless limit, infinite-species theorem, collision model, noncompact momentum-tail extension or curved-spacetime extension.

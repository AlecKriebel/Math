# Independent priority audit: strict Yang inequality and gap nonattainment

Checkpoint: 2026-09-22 (America/Los_Angeles). Scoped audit completion estimate: **100%**. This is a completed, bounded literature check, not a certification of novelty or an exhaustive search. No external person was contacted. No source PDF was added by this audit to the public package; temporary working copies are outside the repository.

## Exact candidate and finding

For a bounded, nonempty Euclidean open set with Dirichlet eigenvalues (E_j), counted with multiplicity, the candidate is

\[
D_J=\left(\frac{n+2}{n}\frac1J\sum_{j\le J}E_j\right)^2
-\frac{n+4}{n}\frac1J\sum_{j\le J}E_j^2
>\frac{(E_{J+1}-E_J)^2}{4}
\]

at every finite (J\). A stronger proposed intermediate statement is

\[
\sum_{E_j<z}(z-E_j)\bigl(z-(1+4/n)E_j\bigr)<0
\qquad (z>E_1).
\]

**Finding:** I did not locate a publication explicitly proving either this all-index nonattainment statement or this strict threshold inequality in the sources and searches below. The trace identities, quadratic remainder, non-strict inequality, root enclosure, gap bound, and relevant commutator technology are established prior art. The potentially additional contribution is the bounded-domain equality obstruction and its application to the recorded saturation question. It should be described that narrowly, with priority still unverified.

Ashbaugh 2002 explicitly leaves strict Yang1 undecided, and the 2017 chapter still distinguishes strict Yang2/HP/PPW from a non-strict Yang1 starting point. This historical evidence cannot establish absence of informal knowledge or a later result.

## Primary sources and exact locations

1. **MFO problem statement (2009).** [Oberwolfach Report 06/2009](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3107/OWR_2009_06.pdf), printed p. 415, PDF page index 60, under “Problem - universal inequalities”. The earlier context fixes bounded Euclidean Dirichlet domains and (n\ge2). The saturation question is explicit. Formula (2) defines (M_p) as a root mean; hence the displayed difference must be interpreted as (M_1^2-M_2^2), not the dimensionally inconsistent printed (M_1^2-M_2). The [AIM workshop problem list, Version 6, April 2009](https://aimath.org/WWN/loweigenvalues/loweigenvalues.pdf), pp. 3–4, items (7)–(8), independently reproduces the definitions, typo, and question. The corrected expression agrees with the original Harrell–Stubbe discriminant. These records establish that the question was asked in 2009; they do not establish its present open status.

2. **Harrell–Stubbe (1997), foundational prior art.** [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-97-01846-1); [1995 author preprint source](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/mp_arc/e/95-431.amstex). *On trace identities and universal eigenvalue estimates for some partial differential operators*, Trans. AMS 349, 1797–1809. In the accessible preprint, **Proposition 6(i)–(iii)** gives the non-strict quadratic inequality, discriminant, interval containment, and gap bound. Its subsequent Discussion says the Dirichlet result is sharp at leading Weyl order and supplies exact saturation for the one-dimensional harmonic oscillator with the appropriate kinetic-energy parameter. That oscillator has a potential and is not a bounded Euclidean Dirichlet counterexample. I found no bounded-domain Yang equality obstruction in this preprint. Final published text was not independently checked in full.

3. **Levitin–Parnovski (2002), directly inspected full paper.** [Author-hosted published PDF](https://www.homepages.ucl.ac.uk/~ucahlep/Papers/commutators.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1006/jfan.2001.3913). *Commutators, spectral trace identities, and universal estimates for eigenvalues*, JFA 192, 425–445. **Theorem 2.2 and Remark 2.4, pp. 428–430**, provide the spectral identities and weakened domain assumptions. **Corollary 2.8, pp. 431–432, (2.14)–(2.16)** derives non-strict Yang via the spectral tail; **Example 4.2, p. 438, (4.8)** specializes it to Euclidean Dirichlet domains. No all-index strictness claim or equality-impossibility proof was found. The proof is close prior art for the proposed reduction of equality to finite spectral support; that reduction alone should not be portrayed as a new sum rule.

4. **Ashbaugh (1999; arXiv 2000).** [Full author preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0008087). *Isoperimetric and universal inequalities for eigenvalues*, in *Spectral Theory and Geometry*, pp. 95–139. **Preprint §3, equations (3.31)–(3.34)** gives the explicit quadratic Yang upper bound and Yang2; the paragraph after **(3.39), preprint p. 27**, directs readers to the longer paper for strictness questions. Inspection of the universal-inequality discussion did not locate the candidate assertion. The full-text check of the 2002 paper was subsequently completed; see the next entry.

5. **Ashbaugh (2002), full text recovered and inspected.** [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02829638); [Internet Archive snapshot of the IAS-hosted published PDF](https://web.archive.org/web/20070101113227id_/http://www.ias.ac.in:80/mathsci/vol112/feb2002/Pm1970.pdf). *The universal eigenvalue bounds of Payne–Pólya–Weinberger, Hile–Protter, and H C Yang*, Proc. Indian Acad. Sci. (Math. Sci.) 112, 3–30. **Printed p. 12, final paragraph (PDF index 9)** explicitly excludes Yang1 from the strict inequalities proved: “except perhaps Yang’s first inequality, which we leave undecided.” This passage was verified visually in the PDF. **§3, pp. 12–14**, proves strict Yang2/HP/PPW; **(3.12), p. 14**, retains a non-strict first relation. **P. 19, Remark 1 after Theorem 4.1**, again excludes the Yang1 forms in the constant-density, zero-potential case. This closes the named access gap and confirms that this key predecessor does not settle the candidate. It gives positive evidence that strict Yang1 was unresolved in this paper, not proof of present-day novelty.

6. **Ashbaugh (2017), most directly relevant survey evidence.** *Universal inequalities for the eigenvalues of the Dirichlet Laplacian*, Chapter 8 of *Shape optimization and spectral theory*, pp. 282–324; [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/9783110550887-008), [published chapter text mirror](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/316735044_8_Universal_Inequalities_for_the_Eigenvalues_of_the_Dirichlet_Laplacian), [Library of Congress book copy](https://tile.loc.gov/storage-services/master/gdc/gdcebookspublic/20/20/71/98/95/2020719895/2020719895.pdf). **P. 286** explicitly states strict Yang2, HP, PPW at every index and strict Yang1 in the (m=1) case. **(8.88)–(8.89), p. 302**, writes the hierarchy with the first relation non-strict and the three comparisons strict. **(8.31), p. 287**, is the same root bound. The chapter discusses strictness arising from a nonzero nonnegative potential, which does not settle the zero-potential candidate. Published chapter text was inspected through its indexed reproduction; direct publisher/LOC download attempts failed.

7. **Harrell–Stubbe (2010; preprint 2008).** [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.1133); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1137/090763743). *Universal bounds and semiclassical estimates for eigenvalues of abstract Schrödinger operators*, SIAM J. Math. Anal. 42(5), 2261–2274 (2010), published online September 16, 2010. **Corollary 2.3, equation (2.9), preprint p. 5**, gives an exact quadratic trace remainder. **Theorem 2.5, (2.10), p. 6**, and **§5, pp. 11–13**, supply refinements involving the gap and eigenvalue means. These are close prior art, not an identified all-index strictness result for bounded domains. The introduction distinguishes oscillator/sphere saturation from Weyl sharpness. Searches of the complete extracted text found no direct candidate statement.

8. **Harrell–Stubbe (2011; preprint 2009).** [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0903.0563); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-2011-05252-9). *Trace identities for commutators, with applications to the distribution of eigenvalues*. **§2** develops the general trace identities; **(3.11)–(3.13), pp. 11–12**, specializes to the Yang-type quadratic and notes recovery of the Euclidean Dirichlet inequality. **Theorem 4.1 and Remarks 4.3**, and **§7**, discuss monotonicity, improvements, and exact special examples. No proof of the requested bounded-domain nonattainment was found. Equality examples for compact manifolds or periodic problems do not contradict the candidate.

9. **Harrell–Hermi (2011), refinement check.** [Author-hosted published PDF](https://faculty.fiu.edu/~lhermi/HarrellHermi2011.pdf); [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1080/03605302.2011.595865). *On Riesz means of eigenvalues*, CPDE 36, 1521–1543. **§2.3, p. 1528, Theorems 2.3–2.4**, states monotonicity and optimality of the Riesz-mean constant. Its use of “strict bounds” concerns finite bounds with Weyl growth, followed by an asymptotic proof that the constant cannot improve. I did not find the all-index strict Yang statement in this paper. Optimality of a coefficient as the threshold tends to infinity does not imply finite-threshold attainment.

10. **Recent scope checks.** [Provenzano–Stubbe, arXiv:2503.02716](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.02716), *Semiclassical eigenvalue bounds for compact homogeneous irreducible Riemannian manifolds*: **Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.3**, **§2, (2.8)–(2.10)**, and **§3** concern quadratic sum rules, compact-manifold equality cases, and equality-generating sequences. This is relevant mechanism-level prior art but does not state the bounded Euclidean candidate. [Funano, arXiv:2604.11114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.11114), *Some universal inequalities for Dirichlet eigenvalues of the Laplacian on a Euclidean convex domain*: **Theorems 1.1–1.2** concern ratios of arbitrary-index eigenvalues on convex domains; **§4** reviews older inequalities. It does not supply an additive positive remainder for the candidate discriminant. Neither paper licenses claiming an exhaustive review of 2025–2026 literature.

## Search record and unresolved priority gap

Web searches included the exact paper titles and combinations of: `Yang first inequality strict equality bounded domain`; `Yang1 strict`; `Yang eigenvalues strictness`; `Yang inequality remainder`; `Yang Dirichlet remainder term`; `Harrell Stubbe gap bound saturation`; `Harrell Stubbe saturated Dirichlet`; `Harrell–Stubbe strict`; `Yang eigenvalue nonattainment`; and `Dirichlet finite spectral coordinate eigenfunction`. Several broad searches were noisy and returned unrelated Yang–Yau or Hardy inequalities. Those were excluded. Sources were followed from the foundational papers and the Ashbaugh surveys. Downloaded texts were searched for strictness, equality, saturation, remainders, and the relevant formulas, with local inspection around hits.

Access limitations: no MathSciNet/zbMATH citation-network audit; no systematic non-English search; no complete inspection of every cited paper; no guarantee that newer preprints are indexed. A 2023 author-hosted *Topics in Spectral Geometry* PDF was located, with search excerpts at **§5.4, pp. 188–195**, still presenting non-strict Yang, but downloads failed (HTTP 406); it is supplementary evidence only.

The clean wording justified by this audit is: **“We prove nonattainment of the corrected gap bound for bounded Euclidean Dirichlet domains, answering the saturation question recorded in 2009. The underlying trace identities and non-strict bounds are classical. We have not located an earlier explicit nonattainment proof in the literature checked; priority remains subject to further review.”** Avoid “first proof,” “new trace identity,” or an unqualified assertion that the question remains open today.

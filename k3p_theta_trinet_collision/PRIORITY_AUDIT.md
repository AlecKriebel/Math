# Narrow post-discovery priority audit

This audit was conducted only after the exact collision, Jacobian, and continuous-time perturbation had been completed. Its purpose was to calibrate wording, not to establish priority conclusively.

## Search target

The search was restricted to:

- the exact three-leaf strict level-two theta topology with two reticulation leaves;
- the algebraic relation `5h^4=1` and the displayed edge vectors;
- the factorization `M[y,z]=P[y+z]B[y]B[z]`;
- exact K3P tree/network collisions on three leaves;
- full-dimensional stochastic overlap between this fixed theta topology and the tree model.

Exact-expression and descriptive searches were run across arXiv-indexed and general scholarly web results. The audit also checked the cited source paper and nearby work on K3P invariants and 3-sunlet models.

## Results

The search located:

1. J. Brits, N. Holtgrefe, L. van Iersel, and S. Martin, *On Tree–Network Distinguishability and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic Networks*, arXiv:2607.12919v2 (2026), which proves the level-one K3P trinet inequality, arbitrary-level JC/K2P trinet inequalities, and the resulting JC/K2P blob-distinguishability corollary, while discussing the high-level K3P extension as open.
2. S. Cox, E. Gross, and S. Martin, *Group-Based Phylogenetic Models on 3-Sunlet Networks*, Bulletin of Mathematical Biology 87 (2025), 132.
3. General work on group-based phylogenetic invariants and K3P tree models.

No search result reproduced the present theta topology together with the quartic witness, exact tree factorization, interior stochastic collision, or full-rank 15x15 Jacobian.

## Limitation and wording adopted

This was a narrow search and may miss differently described, unpublished, or non-indexed constructions. The package therefore uses the statement:

> A narrow post-discovery search did not locate an earlier exact interior tree/theta-trinet collision with this topology and factorization.

It does not claim absolute priority. An expert terminology and literature audit is explicitly requested in the author handoff and email draft.

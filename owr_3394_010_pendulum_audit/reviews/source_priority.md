## Candidate-to-source checks and necessary precision

These are independent checks of the supplied candidate, using the source locations above as the theorem interface.

- **Equation:** exact match after choosing a=b=1 in the published family. The circle is essential: theta is an angle modulo 2 pi, not an unwrapped real coordinate.
- **Quantifier order:** exact match. Nothing warrants a single exceptional set valid for every disturbance. The cited result concerns a fixed open-loop input, not arbitrary state feedback.
- **Local stability:** the candidate verifies the downward equilibrium's eigenvalues, so the additional local stability clause in the journal definition is met.
- **Regularity:** A0 literally describes a C^1-Lipschitz vector field. Any summary should retain the published requirement or explicitly state that the present vector field is smooth, and therefore satisfies the needed local derivative regularity. Merely rewriting A0 as an arbitrary C^1 state dependence is unnecessary and risks weakening the cited theorem.
- **Ultimate boundedness:** the journal's Definition 2 is eventual confinement to a common input-dependent radius, not just a limsup inequality. The candidate's explicit energy estimate proves this after adding positive slack: for each initial state, eventually H(t) <= 3+D^2, hence rho(t) <= sqrt(pi^2+6+2D^2) <= sqrt(pi^2+6)+sqrt(2)D. This supplies an actual eventual bound with a constant plus a class-K function. For locally bounded inputs without a finite global norm, forward completeness follows by applying the energy estimate separately on each finite interval.
- **Source arithmetic caution:** the unnumbered energy display on journal p. 1588 has a factor-of-two equality error after Young's inequality when W=omega^2/2-a cos(theta). Replacing that equality by a less-than-or-equal sign makes the displayed upper bound valid. The source's unshifted W and V also require an additive constant to satisfy the nonnegative convention. These are repairable presentation points: the candidate's shifted H and direct inequalities avoid them.
- **Imported proof versus new proof:** the candidate's explicit coefficient 1/2 and elementary square bounds are a transparent specialization of the published perturbed-energy construction. They do not establish novel priority for the theorem. Accepting the application means accepting the cited theorem and its dependencies; an algebra-checking script cannot certify the measure-theoretic theorem by itself.

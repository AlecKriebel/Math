## Exact theorem scope assessed

The objects are binary, standard one-step-root-suppressed semi-directed
networks on one fixed finite labelled leaf set, with blobs of level at most two
and with the strongly tree-child property (an admissible rooting exists and
every admissible rooting is tree-child).  Isomorphisms preserve leaf labels,
ordinary edges, arrowheads, vertex roles, and the observable `C,G,T` Fourier
sectors.  Inheritance probabilities are in `(0,1)`.

The principal edge domain is the strict positive K3P stochastic domain
`D3,+`.  The continuous-time restriction additionally has
`c>g*t`, `g>c*t`, and `t>c*g` on every edge.  Boundary spectra, zero/one
inheritance, nonstandard suppression, higher-level blobs, and arbitrary weak
tree-child networks are outside the principal classification.

For two networks in this class, the article asserts equivalence among:

1. regular full-dimensional source-germ containment through a physical
   real-analytic target section;
2. equality of the labelled reduced trees of blobs, mixed-graph isomorphism
   of corresponding nontriangle factors, and only coherent ordinary-triangle
   redirection in the remaining factors; and
3. a common regular full-dimensional analytic germ with physical sections on
   both sides.

Hence no proper one-sided containment of this specified kind occurs in the
strong class.  The claim is not equality of complete stochastic images and is
not numerical parameter identifiability.  The same equivalence is asserted in
strict continuous time.

For each fixed topology, removal of a proper complex Zariski-closed exceptional
set gives generic structural identification modulo ordinary triangles.  The
reconstruction theorem is an exact-real termination statement using field
operations, exact sign decisions, and real-closed-field quantifier
elimination; it makes no bit-complexity, conditioning, finite-sample, or
statistical claim.

For every `n >= 3`, the sharpness theorem constructs a weak-but-not-strong
tree-child pair that is neither isomorphic nor triangle-equivalent but shares
a strict-CT regular full-dimensional germ of dimension `6n-3`.  The certified
three-leaf equality-slice zero is unique only in the selected rational box, not
globally.  The separate tree/double-theta containment example lies outside
even the weak class.

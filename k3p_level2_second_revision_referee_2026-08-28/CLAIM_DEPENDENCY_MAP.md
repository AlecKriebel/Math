## Active/historical boundary inconsistency

The current paper replaces the former JC cut premise correctly.  The active
global-transfer subsystem does not.  In particular:

- `cut_recovery/global_logic/CUT_GLOBAL_LOGIC_REPORT.json` says its overall
  theorem status is blocked and binds the JC manuscript.
- `strong_crossbridge/global_transfer/build_global_transfer.py` nevertheless
  labels that object a load-bearing directed-inclusion audit and states D1 by
  “isotropic-JC generic recovery.”
- `verify_global_transfer.py` checks only the stored inclusion orientation,
  the stored `reverse_inclusion_proved=false`, and the target-regularity flag;
  it does not validate the report's status or rederive the K3P implication.
- The independent adversarial transfer verifier also binds/text-mines the JC
  manuscript and trusts the same booleans.
- `ACTIVE_MANIFEST.json` simultaneously promotes the transfer theorem and
  labels the same legacy report historical.
- The integrated verifier executes the new word/minor programs, but validates
  them separately from the old transfer gate.  No dependency edge makes those
  programs the premise of D1.

This is repairable without changing the paper theorem: replace the load-bearing
object with a K3P D1 certificate bound to the current displayed-tree lemma and
minor/palette evidence; make both transfer implementations verify that
implication; remove the JC manuscript/report from active inputs; add a coherent
premise-substitution mutation; and rebuild all downstream seals.

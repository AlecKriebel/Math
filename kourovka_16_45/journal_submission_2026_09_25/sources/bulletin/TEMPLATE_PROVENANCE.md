The downloads are untouched publisher/society originals. The class identifies itself as `2010/12/03 v2.1b`; the example template is dated 2010-12-07. These are still the files linked by the current author instructions, not substitutes from an unofficial mirror.

`baustms.cls` is distributed under LPPL 1.3c or later; `srtnumbered.bst` under LPPL 1 or later. Their full notices remain in their headers. The example template has no separate license notice. Do not assign the project's own software license to these third-party files.

The current instructions require MSC2020, while the class defaults to 2010. Its documented optional argument permits the non-invasive correction `\classification[2020]{primary 20B05; secondary 20D30, 20D60}`. The template text says a 150-word abstract; current instructions allow 200. Use no more than 150 words to satisfy both.

The class handles publisher typography (30pc × 45pc text, 10.5pt body), author addresses and running titles. Use the published class, not personal font/margin changes. Printed pagination can still differ at production.

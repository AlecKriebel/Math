\section{Scope and explicit identifications}

The paper proves structurally that the explicitly specified group
$G=\F_{29}^2\rtimes H$ has
\[
 |G|=100920,\qquad \bfth(G)=b(G)=3<4=\mup(G).
\]
Here $\mup$ is the maximum size of an independent subset; $b$ is the maximum
size of an inclusion-minimal base over all permutation representations,
with bases taken for the permutation image; and $\bfth$ restricts this
maximum to faithful representations. This supplement describes optional
exact checks of the finite inputs and witnesses. These computations are not
premises of the structural upper-bound proof. In particular, no subgroup
enumeration of the group of order $100920$ is asserted.

Over $\F_{29}$ the complement generators are
\[
 A=\begin{pmatrix}0&28\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 B=\begin{pmatrix}2&7\\12&28\end{pmatrix},\qquad H=\langle A,B\rangle.
\]
The verifiers obtain exactly $120$ determinant-one matrices and check
$A^2=B^3=(AB)^5=-I$. An explicit isomorphism $\SL_2(5)\to H$ is specified by
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}0&4\\1&0\end{pmatrix}\longmapsto A,
 \qquad
 \begin{pmatrix}0&4\\1&1\end{pmatrix}\longmapsto B.
\]
These pairs generate a subgroup of $\SL_2(5)\times H$ of order $120$,
with both coordinate projections bijective, certifying the isomorphism.
The additional checker verifies all $120^2=14400$ multiplication identities.
Similarly, $A\mapsto(1\,2)(3\,4)$ and $B\mapsto(1\,3\,5)$ define
a quotient onto $A_5$ with kernel $Z=\{I,-I\}$; all $14400$
multiplication identities for this quotient are checked as well.

The independent triple is
\[
 R_1=A,\qquad R_2=\begin{pmatrix}12&24\\0&17\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 R_3=\begin{pmatrix}25&3\\4&4\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The words $R_2=BAB^2$ and $R_3=AR_2(AB)^2$, the identities $R_i^2=-I$,
and the product orders $|R_2R_3|=4$, $|R_1R_3|=3$, $|R_1R_2|=10$
are checked. Their omission subgroups have orders $8,12,20$, intersect
in $Z$, and have core $Z$ in $H$.

\section{Coverage of the subgroup and affine checks}

The complement subgroups are enumerated by starting with the identity
subgroup and, from each discovered subgroup, adjoining every element outside
it. Every subgroup has a generating sequence, and each successive prefix
occurs along a path in this procedure. The resulting list therefore contains
all $76$ actual subgroups, not just conjugacy representatives. As a further
check, their order distribution is
\[
\begin{array}{c|rrrrrrrrrrrr}
 \text{order}&1&2&3&4&5&6&8&10&12&20&24&120\\\hline
 \text{number}&1&1&10&15&6&10&5&6&10&6&5&1.
\end{array}
\]
A subgroup family is \emph{meet-irredundant} when removing any member
strictly enlarges its total intersection. Each of the
$\binom{75}{4}=1215450$ four-families of proper subgroups is tested;
none is meet-irredundant. Since every subfamily of a meet-irredundant family
is meet-irredundant, larger ranks are excluded as well. Of the
$\binom{75}{3}=67525$ three-families, $900$ are meet-irredundant, but none
has trivial intersection. Explicit pairs with trivial intersection give
the lower bound for $\bfth(H)$; the displayed triple gives the other lower
bound. Thus the finite results corroborate $\mup(H)=3$ and $\bfth(H)=2$.

All $30$ lines of $\F_{29}^2$ are tested, and each stabilizer is cyclic of
order $4$. The additional checker independently enumerates the $62$ subgroups
of the scalar group $\F_{29}\rtimes C_4$ and finds no meet-irredundant triple.
This is a finite check of the line-subgroup bound used in the proof; the
paper supplies the general elementary argument.

Using $(v,h)(w,k)=(v+hw,hk)$, the affine generators
\[
 (e_1,I),\quad (0,R_1),\quad (0,R_2),\quad (0,R_3)
\]
generate $100920$ elements. Their omission subgroups have respective orders
$120,6728,10092,16820$, and every omitted generator is absent from its
omission subgroup. The total intersection is
$Z_0=\{(0,I),(0,-I)\}$. Nonnormality is checked directly from
\[
 (e_1,I)(0,-I)(-e_1,I)=(2e_1,-I)\notin Z_0.
\]
The three subgroups
\[
\begin{split}
 L_1&=\langle(e_1,I),(0,-I)\rangle,\qquad
 L_2=\langle(e_2,I),(0,-I)\rangle,\\
 L_3&=\langle(e_1+e_2,I),(2e_1,-I)\rangle
\end{split}
\]
have order $58$. Their pairwise intersections have order $2$ and their total
intersection is trivial; the additional checker verifies the displayed
intersection elements in the paper. Their disjoint coset action has degree
$3(100920/58)=5220$ and a faithful minimal base of size $3$.

\section{Why the characteristic matters}

The conclusion does not extend to every characteristic in which such a
complement exists. Over $\F_{11}$, put
\[
 A_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}0&10\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
 B_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}0&2\\5&1\end{pmatrix},\quad
 C=A_{11}B_{11}=\begin{pmatrix}6&10\\0&2\end{pmatrix}.
\]
The first two matrices generate a group of order $120$. The element $C$
has order $10$, with $C^5=-I$, and stabilizes the distinct lines
$W_1=\langle(1,0)\rangle$ and $W_2=\langle(1,4)\rangle$; its eigenvalues
on them are $6$ and $2$, respectively. Write $D=\langle C\rangle$,
$D_2=\langle C^5\rangle$ and $D_5=\langle C^2\rangle$.
Inside $V\rtimes\langle A_{11},B_{11}\rangle$, where $V=\F_{11}^2$, take
\[
 Q_1=W_1\rtimes D,\quad Q_2=W_2\rtimes D,\quad
 Q_3=V\rtimes D_2,\quad Q_4=V\rtimes D_5.
\]
These have orders $110,110,242,605$. Since $W_1\cap W_2=0$ and
$D_2\cap D_5=1$, their total intersection is $1$. The deletion intersections
are, in order, $W_2,W_1,D_5,D_2$, of orders $11,11,5,2$.
Every member is therefore essential, giving a faithful minimal base of
size $4$. This exact witness tests the limit of the argument: the
characteristic-$29$ proof uses $\gcd(120,28)=4$ and the resulting cyclic
2-group line stabilizers, whereas $\gcd(120,10)=10$ permits this obstruction.

\section{Reproduction and limits}

The README gives ordinary Python and C++17 commands and the expected outputs.
Python uses only its standard library; all mathematical arithmetic is exact.
The reconstruction programs read no precomputed certificate. The two supplied
implementations and a newly written checker agree on all $76$ subgroups as
sets of actual matrices. The two Python implementations additionally agree
on the digest of the complete sorted affine element set. Saved expected
outputs are used only for subsequent comparison; elapsed runtime is not a
mathematical assertion.

These programs check explicit finite claims and provide reproducibility;
they do not replace the structural proof of the universal bounds, establish
bibliographic novelty, or imply external human peer review. The paper and
checks were prepared with AI assistance. No proof-assistant formalization is
claimed.

# Specialist journal comparison

Checkpoint: 2026-09-26 04:33:44 UTC. Scope: compare four specialist alternatives for the present seven-page structural counterexample to Kourovka Problem 16.45. Completion estimate: 100% of bounded comparison; World Scientific primary-page access remains unresolved. No contact, manuscript changes, or submission occurred.

## Conclusions

Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society is a defensible short-paper/quick-decision choice, but this audit supplies no evidence that its acceptance probability is highest. Specialist subject fit and likelihood of acceptance are different judgments. No manuscript-specific acceptance probabilities can be inferred from the material inspected.

1. **Journal of Algebra is a credible specialist alternative.** It publishes closely related permutation-base work and an exact seven-page precedent for a negative answer to a Kourovka question. Length and the fact that our main result is a counterexample do not exclude it. Its stated standard stresses a substantial contribution with lasting effect, so editorial interest remains uncertain. Current Elsevier policy is more permissive than the old readability-only rule and does not categorically exclude this work, but the human author must satisfy substantive oversight/original-contribution requirements; appropriate research-method and manuscript-preparation disclosures are needed.
2. **Journal of Group Theory is arguably the closest pure subject match**, but De Gruyter Brill explicitly says AI-generated papers are not accepted. Its simultaneous allowance for disclosed AI contributions/design-methodology does not establish that our substantially AI-developed/written manuscript is eligible. Do not describe this as verified compatible or silently treat it as a mere no-AI-authors rule.
3. **Communications in Algebra is in scope but presently a poor policy choice.** Taylor & Francis's detailed author guidance explicitly prohibits AI creating a manuscript first draft or sections and AI creating/concluding the argument. Disclosure alone does not cure that restriction for this package.
4. **Journal of Algebra and Its Applications remains an unverified alternative in this audit.** Primary World Scientific scope and AI-policy pages were inaccessible (HTTP 403 and browser security-verification screen). Secondary quotations point to disclosure and editor discretion, but those do not meet this audit's primary-source verification standard. No claim of verified eligibility or higher acceptance is warranted.

## Primary evidence

### Journal of Algebra

- Official scope: <https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-algebra/0021-8693>. Substantial lasting results and innovative methods are emphasized. This is a research-quality criterion, not an acceptance-rate statement.
- **Manuel Staiger, “On the almost-palindromic width of free groups,” Journal of Algebra 659 (2024), 475–481**: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S002186932400382X>, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2024.07.008>. Seven-page negative answer to Kourovka Problem 19.8; abstract confirms the problem and negative answer. This verifies the suitability of concise Kourovka solutions as a format, not equivalence of novelty/significance with our result.
- **Nick Gill and Bianca Lodà, “Statistics for S_n acting on k-sets,” Journal of Algebra 607 (2022), 286–299**: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0021869321005366>, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2021.10.037>. Calculates maximum minimal-base size, height, and maximum irredundant-base length. This directly supports topic fit.
- **Ballester-Bolinches, Cossey, Kamornikov, Meng, “On two questions from the Kourovka Notebook,” Journal of Algebra 499 (2018), 438–449**: <https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0021869317306725>, DOI <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2017.12.014>. Finite soluble-group intersections, regular-orbit methods, and Kourovka answers provide another relevant precedent.
- Current policy, updated June 2026: <https://www.elsevier.com/about/policies-and-standards/generative-ai-policies-for-journals>. Inspected current publisher HTML directly as well as indexed page. Allows tools including agents/deep research for literature synthesis, identifying gaps, ideas, organization and writing support. Requires author critical thinking, control, verification, thorough adaptation reflecting authentic author contributions, transparency, and a manuscript-preparation declaration; research-process uses belong in Methods. Policy is not a categorical ban on substantive AI assistance, but does not authorize replacing human judgment or original intellectual contribution. Journal-specific guide could not be fetched in this audit, so no stronger journal-specific exemption or restriction is claimed.

### Journal of Group Theory

- Official journal scope: <https://www.degruyterbrill.com/journal/key/JGTH/html>; all aspects of group theory.
- **Coen del Valle, “Greedy base sizes for sporadic simple groups,” Journal of Group Theory 28 (2025), 1079–1094**: <https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/jgth-2024-0187/html>. Explicit permutation-base/Cameron-problem research supports close readership fit, although greedy bases differ from the present invariant.
- Publisher policy: <https://www.degruyterbrill.com/publishing/for-authors/author-policies/publishing-ethics> and <https://brill.com/page/509467>. Short exact excerpt: “we do not accept papers generated by Artificial Intelligence (AI) or Machine Learning Tools”. The policy also requires disclosure of significant AI contributions and research-method use. Given both provisions, eligibility of this substantially AI-written manuscript is not established.

### Communications in Algebra

- Official scope: <https://www.tandfonline.com/journals/lagb20>; includes original algebra research and group theory. Subject fit is straightforward.
- Detailed policy: <https://authorservices.taylorandfrancis.com/editorial-policies/using-ai-in-your-research-and-manuscript-preparations/>. Table expressly disallows AI drafting the manuscript or sections, and creating/concluding its argument. This is stronger and more relevant than its general welcome for responsible assistance. General policy: <https://taylorandfrancis.com/our-policies/ai-policy/>.

### Journal of Algebra and Its Applications

- Primary locations attempted: <https://www.worldscientific.com/page/jaa/aims-scope> and <https://www.worldscientific.com/authors/position-statements/ai-tools>.
- Both unavailable to direct retrieval; policy also stopped at a browser security-verification page. No challenge was bypassed. Secondary policy summaries were treated only as leads, not authoritative verification.

## Recommended wording for author advice

“Bulletin was selected for this paper's concise format, explicit policy compatibility, and rapid initial decisions. It is not uniquely best by subject, nor have we established that it offers the highest acceptance chance. Journal of Algebra is a serious specialist alternative with directly relevant publication precedents. Journal of Group Theory would be especially natural by topic, but its current AI policy creates a material eligibility concern for this manuscript.”

This report does not evaluate Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society, which is covered by the coordinating review.

## Steps

1. Read the final PDF, particularly the proof, attribution, and AI-use disclosure, and approve it as the responsible author. Confirm that the paper is not currently under consideration elsewhere and that its author list is complete. The recorded checks are internal AI-assisted reviews, not external peer review.
2. Open the [official submission page](https://journal.austms.org.au/ojs/index.php/Bulletin/about/submissions). Register or log in, with the author role enabled. Begin a new submission in **Articles**.
3. Read the live preparation checklist. Use your confirmed name, email and affiliation. Enter any additional postal-address details directly in the journal system if it requests them; no street address has been invented or placed in the public package.
4. Upload `01_Manuscript.pdf`. Copy the fields from `05_Submission_Metadata.txt`. Include your ORCID, primary **20B05**, secondary **20D30, 20D60**, and keywords. The abstract is under both the current 200-word rule and the old template's 150-word rule.
5. If a cover-letter/comments field is offered, use `04_Cover_Letter.txt`. Retain the preprint and substantive AI-use disclosures. If asked for a preferred subject editor, **John Cossey (group theory)** is a suitable current board member, provided you have no relevant conflict. No editor preference is known to be mandatory.
6. If a suitable additional-file field is offered, you may provide `02_Reproducibility.zip` for referee access. Do not request formal publisher-hosted supplementary publication as though it has been approved: the journal asks for consultation before that route. The cited public repository already supplies the checks, and the article's proof is self-contained. Source ZIP is ready if requested.
7. Review the journal's generated PDF/preview and metadata. Confirm the title, all equations, seven pages, abstract, author details, references and declarations. Only you should accept the author declarations and press the final submission button.
8. Save the submission number, confirmation, and exact submitted PDF. Record the submission date. Do not submit the same manuscript to another journal while this submission is under consideration.

## Cost and existing preprint

Use the ordinary **Green Open Access** route, which the journal currently lists as having no publication charges. Paid Gold Open Access is optional. The existing Zenodo preprint is allowed under the publisher's preprint policy and is disclosed. Do not create another Zenodo release just to submit this paper.

## Why the target changed

The live Archiv der Mathematik author agreement bars articles largely written by AI. The current manuscript's provenance makes that target unsuitable as prepared. The Bulletin's own instructions expressly address disclosed AI-generated content, with human approval and accountability. The package therefore targets the Bulletin and does not conceal or recast the assistance as mere copy editing.

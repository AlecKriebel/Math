Dear Editors,

Please consider “A counterexample to Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.45” for publication in the Bulletin of the Australian Mathematical Society.

The paper gives a negative answer to P. J. Cameron's question comparing the largest size of an inclusion-minimal permutation base with the largest size of an independent subset of a finite group. For an explicit affine group of order 100920, it proves that the two invariants are respectively three and four. The upper bound covers all permutation representations, including nonfaithful and intransitive actions.

The seven-page manuscript gives a structural proof with explicit witnesses. Its key distinction is the normality of the intersection in an irredundant subgroup family: the independent four-set has a nonnormal intersection, while a separate argument excludes every four-member family with normal intersection. The result answers a question posed in Cameron's 2024 published account and as Problem 16.45 in the Kourovka Notebook. I believe its focused statement and self-contained proof suit the Bulletin's short-paper format.

An earlier unrefereed version is available at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22929486 and is also hosted in the Kourovka Notebook's online repository (https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/repository/).

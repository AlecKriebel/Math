We construct an explicit affine group G = F_29^2 semidirect H, with H isomorphic to SL_2(5), of order 100920 for which b_f(G) = b(G) = 3 < 4 = mu′(G). Here b(G) is the largest size of an inclusion-minimal base over all permutation representations, including nonfaithful and intransitive actions, and mu′(G) is the largest size of an independent subset, whether or not it generates G. This gives a negative answer to P. J. Cameron's Kourovka Notebook Problem 16.45.

The upper bound is structural and does not enumerate the full subgroup lattice of the affine group. Every subgroup missing the translation subgroup has faithful base invariant at most two, and every nontrivial normal subgroup contains the translations. Explicit witnesses attain both bounds. The intersection associated with the independent four-set is a nonnormal subgroup of order two, exposing the normal-bottom obstruction.

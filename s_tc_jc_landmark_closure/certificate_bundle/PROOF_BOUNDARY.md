# Proof boundary

The article supplies human proofs of the structural reductions: the fixed
mixed-graph convention, primitive cycle/four-theta exhaustiveness, cut and
bridge localization, marginal submersion, restoration, coherent probes, root
reduction, contextual triangle gluing, and the local-to-global theorem.

The finite computation proves exactly the bounded directed-relation statement
used in Theorem 6.3.  For each canonical directed relation it:

1. reconstructs both decorated rooted graphs;
2. enumerates displayed switchings and descendant masks;
3. derives the complete exact JC Fourier tensors;
4. regenerates the attached polynomial, flattening minor, or equality test;
5. proves strict sign on the open cube or verifies labelled isomorphism / one
   ordinary triangle redirection;
6. verifies every raw-to-canonical transport and restoration-parent edge;
7. checks one- and two-port probe coherence.

The primary and clean-room programs do not share graph canonicalization,
displayed-tree enumeration, descendant-mask construction, separator
selection, or rank code at the load-bearing gates.  Frozen outputs and
regeneration programs are both included: neither is treated as sufficient by
itself.

The package also contains exact component checks for the cut/bridge theorem,
ordinary triangle germ, and the Omega and Theta sharpness families.  Referee
reports and historical failure narratives are not mathematical inputs and are
excluded.

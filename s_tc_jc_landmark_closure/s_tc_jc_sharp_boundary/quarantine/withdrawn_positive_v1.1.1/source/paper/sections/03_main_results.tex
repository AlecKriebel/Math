\section{Main results}\label{sec:main-results}

We first record the structural fact that removes the triangle-count hypothesis from the statistical theorem.

\begin{theorem}[Automatic triangle bound]\label{thm:automatic-triangle-bound}
Every binary standard semi-directed level-2 topology in $\TCw$ has at most one triangle in each blob.  In particular, the conclusion holds for every topology in $\TCs$.
\end{theorem}

The proof is purely graph theoretic and appears in \cref{sec:automatic-triangle}.  It reduces a hypothetical two-triangle blob to the $(1,2,2)$ theta core and excludes every possible tree-child rooting.

We now state the observational classification.  All networks in \cref{thm:main-classification,cor:generic-identifiability} are binary standard semi-directed $\TCs$ level-2 topologies on the same fixed leaf set.

\begin{theorem}[One-sided observational classification]\label{thm:main-classification}
Let $N$ and $N'$ be networks in the stated class.  Then
\[
N\preceqJC N'
\]
if and only if the following conditions hold.
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\alph*)}]
\item Their labelled homeomorphism-reduced bridge trees are isomorphic.
\item Under the induced correspondence of ports, every pair of corresponding local factors is port-labelled isomorphic or related by ordinary triangle redirection $\Tmove$.
\end{enumerate}
Every surviving one-sided relation is a symmetric full-dimensional regular overlap.  In particular, there are no proper one-sided generic containments between distinct local generator types or port placements.
\end{theorem}

The necessity is proved in \cref{sec:global}.  The pointwise cut theorem forces the same bridge tree; analytic peeling localizes containment to each blob; the local atlas then leaves only isomorphism and $\Tmove$.  Sufficiency follows from the common regular triangle tensor germ and positive contextual gluing.

\begin{corollary}[Generic identifiability modulo triangles]\label{cor:generic-identifiability}
For every fixed topology $N$ in the stated class, there is a proper algebraic subset $Z_N\subsetneq\ThetaOpen(N)$ such that the exact JC distribution at every $\theta\in\ThetaOpen(N)\setminus Z_N$ determines the standard semi-directed topology up to labelled isomorphism and valid ordinary triangle redirection.
\end{corollary}

\begin{corollary}[Recoverable structure]\label{cor:recoverable}
At a generic parameter point the exact JC distribution determines:
\begin{enumerate}[label=\textup{(\roman*)}]
\item the labelled homeomorphism-reduced bridge tree;
\item whether each blob is a cycle or a theta blob;
\item the oriented theta core modulo the local triangle move;
\item the labelled descendant block attached at every port;
\item the directed core segment containing every port and the total order of ports along each segment.
\end{enumerate}
Ordinary triangle redirection is the only topology change that can remain observationally compatible on a full-dimensional model region.  Equivalently, the generic topology is determined modulo ordinary triangle redirection.
\end{corollary}

The conclusion concerns semi-directed topology.  Rooted presentations remain nonidentifiable under the stationary reversible JC model.  Moreover, $\Tmove$-related endpoints are proved to share a full-dimensional regular stochastic germ, not necessarily the same complete open stochastic image.

The positive theorem is sharp with respect to the tree-child convention.

\begin{theorem}[Sharpness of strong tree-childness]\label{thm:sharpness}
For every $n\ge4$ there are two nonisomorphic, non-$\Tmove$-equivalent binary level-2 standard semi-directed networks $N_n,N_n'$ such that
\[
N_n,N_n'\in\TCw\setminus\TCs,
\qquad
N_n\bowtieJC N_n'.
\]
The common local model dimension is $2n$.  Both topologies have rooted tree-child representatives, but each has admissible rootings that are not tree-child.
\end{theorem}

The explicit four-leaf pair and its all-$n$ leaf-substitution extension are given in \cref{sec:sharpness}.  Together with \cref{thm:automatic-triangle-bound}, this establishes the sharp boundary statement
\[
\boxed{
\TCs\text{ is generically JC-identifiable modulo }\Tmove,
\qquad
\TCw\text{ is not.}
}
\]
for all binary standard semi-directed level-2 networks.

\begin{remark}[Scope]
The theorem does not address level greater than two, substitution models richer than JC, a positive classification of the full weakly tree-child class, or equality of complete stochastic images under $\Tmove$.  Blobs with multiple triangles are not an omitted level-2 case: \cref{thm:automatic-triangle-bound} proves that no binary topology in $\TCw$ can contain one.
\end{remark}

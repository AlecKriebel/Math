# Sharpness theorem under the cleanup convention

The two four-leaf Theta networks in the baseline manuscript are already-simple presentations. Their `sd0` and exhaustive-cleanup reductions are identical.

Primary and independent rooting censuses give, for each topology:

* five admissible already-simple rootings;
* exactly two tree-child rootings;
* at least one non-tree-child rooting.

Thus both topologies remain in

\[
W_{TC}(clean)\setminus S_{TC}(clean).
\]

They remain nonisomorphic and non-`T`-equivalent because leaf 1 is adjacent to a triangle vertex in one labelled graph and to a nontriangle vertex in the other. The exact common JC point, rank-eight minors, and leaf-substitution inverse are unaffected because no cleanup operation is applied to either graph.

Therefore the all-`n` sharpness boundary holds under `sd0`, Holtgrefe's simple mixed-graph convention, and the Englander/Brits cleanup convention.

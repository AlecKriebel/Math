# Independent adversarial counterexample search

Date: 2026-08-09  
Scope: standard simple `sd_0` semi-directed binary level-2 topologies with
three, four, or five labelled leaves and at most two reticulations.

## Verdict

**NUMERICALLY OBSERVED — no standard-strong non-`T` counterexample was
found in the bounded search.**

This is useful falsification evidence, not a proof of the global positive
classification.  In particular, failed nonlinear fits do not certify
separation, and only a sample of the five-leaf one-sided directions was fitted.
No claim in this report closes arbitrary subdivisions, bridge localization,
or the global one-sided-containment theorem.

Two exact conclusions were obtained:

1. **PROVED:** the locked standard weakly tree-child level-2 class has at
   most one triangle in each blob.
2. **PROVED:** on three leaves the open JC tree and 3-sunlet loci are strictly
   separated, while the three labelled sunlet orientations form one ordinary
   `T` class.

The frozen weak counterexample was independently replayed exactly and was
correctly classified as `W_TC \ S_TC`; it never entered the strong census.

## Clean-room implementation

The implementation in this directory does not import historical project graph,
rooting, Fourier, canonicalization, atlas, or separator code.

- `clean_graph.py` implements the narrow `sd_0` rooting universe, LSA tests,
  tree-child tests, exact role/label/arrowhead canonicalization, ordinary `T`,
  and blob calculations.
- `enumerate_census.py` generates graphs from all internal edge subsets,
  labelled leaf allocations, reticulation sets, and incoming-incidence choices.
- `fourier_engine.py` derives all displayed switchings, descendant masks, JC
  orbit coordinates, values, and Jacobians from primitive arcs.
- `atlas_core_crosscheck.py` obtains the internal cores independently from the
  NetworkX graph atlas.
- `networkx_isomorphism_crosscheck.py` uses a coloured incidence-gadget
  encoding and GraphMatcher rather than the brute-force canonicalizer.
- `regression_checks.py` independently reconstructs the frozen pair and checks
  its exact quadratic-field Fourier equality.

## Why the bounded graph census is exhaustive

For `n` leaves and `r` reticulations, a rooted binary network has

    t = n+r-2

tree vertices other than the root.  After the one-step `sd_0` root
suppression, the mixed graph therefore has

    m = n+2r-2

unlabelled internal vertices and

    E = 2n+3r-3

edges.  Deleting the `n` labelled degree-one leaves leaves a connected simple
internal graph with

    E_internal = n+3r-3,

maximum degree three, and total degree deficit `n`.  Conversely, every such
internal graph, every allocation of the labelled leaves to its degree
deficits, every `r`-vertex reticulation set, and every choice of one outgoing
incidence at each reticulation produces every possible binary mixed graph
exactly before quotienting.

The generator loops over precisely those finite sets.  Isomorphism is quotiented
by all internal permutations preserving reticulation role, fixed leaf labels,
and incidence arrowheads.  For every remaining graph, every edge is tried as a
root site and every residual ordinary-edge orientation satisfying the exact
indegree requirements is generated by a complete forced-or-branch recursion.
A candidate is retained as `S_TC` only when it has at least one admissible
LSA-valid rooting and every such rooting is tree-child.

This proves exhaustiveness only for the stated bound `3 <= n <= 5`, `0 <= r <=
2`, and the locked simple `sd_0` convention.

## Exact three-leaf separator

Order the four nonconstant JC orbit coordinates as

    A=q_011, B=q_101, C=q_110, D=q_123.

Primitive displayed-tree expansion gives

    ABC-D^2 = 0

for the three-leaf tree.  For the independently generated 3-sunlet, with the
script's seven edge multipliers `p0,...,p6` and inheritance probability `p7`,
the pullback factors as

    p0^2*p1*p2*p3^2*p4*p5^2*p6^2*p7*(1-p2)^2*(1-p7).

It is strictly positive when every parameter lies in `(0,1)`.  Thus neither
open model is generically contained in the other.  This is an exact
open-domain certificate, not a numerical observation.

## Four-leaf search

There are 60 standard-strong ordinary-`T` representatives.  At two exact
finite-field evaluation points, all admissible rootings of a topology gave the
same observed Jacobian rank.  The representative counts by observed rank are:

- trees: three classes of rank 5;
- one reticulation: six classes of rank 6 and twelve of rank 7;
- two reticulations: three classes of rank 7, twelve of rank 8, and twenty-four
  of rank 9.

Among 3,540 ordered nonidentity class pairs, modular dimension screening
discarded 1,305 directions and flattening-rank screening discarded another
102.  Every remaining 2,133 directed relation was fitted at two independent
interior source points with multiple target starts.  No residual reached the
candidate threshold `1e-8`.

**Status: NUMERICALLY OBSERVED.**  The modular ranks and failed fits nominate no
counterexample, but they are not saturated-ideal or inequality certificates
for all pairs.

## Five-leaf search

The 1,605 ordinary-`T` representatives were profiled at two exact prime-field
points using full Jacobian rank, every four- and three-leaf marginal rank,
three-leaf separator pullbacks, and 24 deterministic coordinate-subset ranks.
Observed ranks were:

- 15 tree classes of rank 7;
- 45 one-reticulation classes of rank 8 and 180 of rank 9;
- 45 two-reticulation classes of rank 9, 360 of rank 10, and 960 of rank 11.

The profiles produced 3,032 unordered non-`T` pairs with identical complete
screening signatures.  Every one was numerically fitted.  In addition, 1,000
directed one-sided relations were sampled from 199,227 profile-dominant
directions.  No fit reached `1e-8`.

The twenty closest sampled one-sided near misses and twenty closest
equal-profile near misses were then rerun at five fresh interior source points,
20 starts per point, and 240 iterations.  No relation fitted all five points to
`1e-10`.  The very small one-sided residuals drove at least one target
parameter near `0` or `1`, consistent with boundary approximation rather than
an open `Theta_0` containment.  The equal-profile near misses remained between
roughly `10^-3` and `10^-2`.

**Status: NUMERICALLY OBSERVED.**  The equal-profile screen is exhaustive as a
finite computation, but the inference from two modular profiles to equality of
all generic algebraic data is not a proof.  The one-sided fitting covers a
sample, not all 199,227 profile-dominant directions.

## Definition and triangle attacks

`DEFINITIONS_AND_TRIANGLES_REVIEW.md` gives the proof that a level-2
weakly tree-child blob cannot contain two triangles.  The only possible
double-triangle theta has path lengths `(1,2,2)`, hence underlying `K4-e`.
Adjacent reticulations violate tree-childness; the only nonadjacent choice
forces either an omnian or a directed four-cycle.  No tree-child rooting
exists.

The lock now explicitly requires an `S_TC` mixed graph to have at least one
admissible rooting, preventing vacuous membership.  Its SHA-256 is recorded in
`INPUT_LOCK.json`.  The implementation enforced this convention from its
first run, so the correction changes no count or conclusion.

The lock's local tail-incidence criterion for `S_TC` was also compared with
complete rooting enumeration on every rootable graph through four leaves;
there were no mismatches.

## Bottom line for the landmark program

This adversarial search found no Outcome-C candidate.  It gives positive
bounded evidence for Outcome P and removes the three-leaf containment case
exactly.  It does **not** prove Outcome P: the missing load-bearing work remains
an exact arbitrary-subdivision local atlas, one-sided cut preservation, and
projective local-to-global localization.  Promoting this bounded negative
search to the global theorem would repeat the methodological error identified
in the earlier audit.

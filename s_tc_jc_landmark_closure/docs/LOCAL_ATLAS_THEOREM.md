# Directed local atlas theorem

Status: **CANDIDATE — promotion awaits the completed relation stream and its
independent normalized-record comparison.**

## Statement to be certified

Let `H` and `H'` be corresponding nontrivial ported factors of standard
semi-directed `S_TC` level-2 networks, with one distinguished incoming
boundary.  If a source-relative full-dimensional regular projective JC germ
of `H` is contained in the selected projective tensor image induced by `H'`,
then the complete labelled port words of `H` and `H'` are isomorphic modulo
ordinary triangle redirection `T`.

The selected tensor of a full strong target need not retain the target's
primitive core after marginalization.  Such a record is not called weak or
strong.  It is retained as a non-core-retaining completion until omitted
support roles are restored.

## 1. Finite source and target objects

Every nontrivial factor contracts to the cycle core or one of four directed
theta cores.  A rigid source support `Q` consists of every path-sink child and
one labelled port on every segment of a minimum repair.  Its outgoing size is
at most four.  The source universe contains `Q`, `Q+p`, and `Q+p+q` with every
segment assignment and every within-segment order.

For a selected target set, distribute its ordinary labels by a weak
composition over directed segments and select any subset of path-sink ports.
Choose a minimum repair of the full target.  Restore one zero-character dummy
on every chosen repair segment that has no selected port and restore every
omitted sink child by a zero-character dummy.  Additional omitted ports have
the same displayed descendant-mask row as an adjacent retained edge and are
absorbed into a serial product.  This generates every selected tensor induced
by every full strong target.

The target record separately carries:

1. the full dummy-restored realization graph used for its Fourier tensor; and
2. only when all sinks and some minimum repair are selected, the intrinsic
   selected graph that retains the original primitive core strongly.

Thus topology is never read from a dummy-bearing completion graph.

Source labels are anchored once.  Every target port permutation is retained.
For an arbitrary labelled directed relation, simultaneously relabel by the
inverse source assignment.  This produces an anchored-source target
assignment in the enumerated relative-permutation orbit, so the convention is
exhaustive.  Setwise source automorphisms can create duplicate anchored
representatives; they omit none and are removed only after the complete
decorated relation is canonicalized.  Pointwise rigidity is used later for a
unique fixed-label core map.

## 2. Exact tensor descriptors

For every displayed switching and physical edge, record the descendant mask
of the selected ports.  An edge is discarded only when every recorded mask is
zero.  Edges with the same complete switching-mask row enter every JC Fourier
coordinate only through the product of their multipliers; replace the class
by one effective variable.  The product map from its physical open cube onto
`(0,1)` is surjective and has nonzero differential everywhere.  Reticulation
choice permutations and parent flips merely permute inheritance coordinates
or replace `lambda` by `1-lambda`, preserving the open cube.

The resulting descriptor therefore parameterizes exactly the selected
positive tensor image, and the physical-to-descriptor parameter map is an
onto semialgebraic submersion.  Descriptor coordinates need not be minimal
coordinates on that image: a collapsed selected core can make switching
columns or inheritance coordinates tensor-redundant.  The promotion proof
uses only the onto parameter-cube statement for a core-retaining source.

## 3. Algebraic hard cover

Every ordered restriction of every four-port subset is compiled.  On each
quartet the atlas evaluates the 84-element leaf-permutation orbit of seven JC
invariants.  The orbit is multihomogeneous in every port arm, so vanishing and
strict sign descend to the projective quotient.

For a descriptor, modular nonzero evaluation is used only as an exact witness
that an integer pullback polynomial is not the zero polynomial.  A modular
zero is always expanded over the integers.  If a target invariant vanishes
identically while its source pullback is nonzero, a source-full-dimensional
regular germ cannot lie in the target zero set.  This discards the relation
outside a proper source algebraic set.

Every remaining directed signature pair is expanded presentation by
presentation.  A strict relation must carry a regenerated invariant whose
source pullback is zero and whose target pullback has a factor/Bernstein
certificate of one strict sign on the entire open cube (or the reverse
zero/nonzero orientation).  Such a relation has no open stochastic common
point.  A core-retaining equal relation must have one identical labelled
`T`-quotient code.  A non-core-retaining equality is not promoted and is sent
to support completion.

The minimal two-outgoing cycle case is separate and exact:

```text
F = q011*q101*q110 - q123^2.
```

Its tree pullback is zero and its 3-sunlet pullback is a strictly positive
product throughout the open cube.  The three sunlet orientations are exactly
ordinary `T`.

## 4. Completion of nonretaining equalities

The finite atlas must establish that every nonretaining equality requiring
further labels that can arise in a complete equal-boundary factor comparison
is a cycle-source/theta-target relation on a four-outgoing cycle probe.
Restore every omitted theta sink/repair role (at most three) and insert the
same actual labels in every ordered cycle-source position.  Each resulting
five-, six-, or seven-outgoing decorated relation is regenerated from graphs
and separated by the arm-multihomogeneous trinet polynomial `F` or by one of
the 84 quartet invariants.

There is also a three-outgoing cycle/theta deck equality in the selected
completion census.  It is not a missing bounded relation.  If the source
factor has only those three outgoing boundaries, equality of the already
recovered labelled bridge trees forces the target factor to have the same
complete boundary set.  A full standard-strong target with no omitted real
sink or repair boundary is core-retaining, so the nonretaining completion is
inapplicable.  If the source factor has another boundary, choose it together
with the cycle's two-port rigid support and one further boundary when
available; this is the four-outgoing support-plus-two gate above.  In the
unique four-boundary case it is the complete factor, and in every larger case
the marginal-submersion and probe-coherence lemmas promote its conclusion.
Thus no three-outgoing dummy completion is promoted to a competing complete
factor.

No historical target-only identifier or frozen separator assignment is an
input to this step.

## 5. Promotion to arbitrary words

The selected-parameter map is a product of disjoint path-product maps and
identity inheritance coordinates.  For every nonempty serial class,

```text
d(x_1...x_k) = sum_i (product_{j != i} x_j) dx_i
```

is nonzero throughout the open cube.  Hence a full-dimensional arbitrary-word
containment projects to a full-dimensional bounded decorated relation.

Fix the pointwise-rigid support `Q`.  Every `Q+p` identification uses the same
core map and identifies the segment of `p`.  Every `Q+p+q` identification
agrees on overlaps and determines the pairwise order on a common segment.
These comparisons assemble uniquely into one total word on each segment.  If
some extra port subdivides a support-triangle edge, that probe has no triangle
and fixes the literal orientation.  Otherwise the same unique triangle
persists and all probe-level `T` choices are one coherent global redirection.

When the completed relation and mutation certificates have no failure, these
five steps prove the stated directed local theorem.  Until then this document
is a proof ledger, not a promoted theorem.

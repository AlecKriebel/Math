# Candidate global theorem and proof ledger

Status: **CANDIDATE — no theorem is promoted until every referenced gate is
independently verified.**

## Candidate theorem

Let `N,N'` be binary standard semi-directed `S_TC` level-2 topologies on the
same labelled taxa, under the locked open JC domain.  Then

    N preceq_JC N'

if and only if their labelled bridge trees agree and each corresponding
nontrivial blob is labelled-isomorphic or differs by ordinary triangle
redirection `T`.  Consequently there is no proper one-sided generic
containment, and symmetric full-dimensional regular overlap is exactly the
equivalence relation generated by `T`.

Together with the frozen all-`n` theorem in `W_TC \ S_TC`, this would make
strong tree-childness the sharp boundary for level-2 JC topology
identifiability modulo `T`.

## Proof ledger

### 1. Standard structure

For a binary rooted network, deleting one parent edge at each reticulation
gives a tree.  Cyclomatic number is additive over blobs and equals the number
of reticulations blob by blob.  Thus a nontrivial level-2 blob has cycle rank
one or two.  After suppressing unported ordinary bivalent vertices it is,
respectively, a cycle or a theta core.

The independent orientation compiler derives one cycle and four theta event
cores.  Binary subdivisions are finite words of port labels on their directed
segments, together with the obligatory reticulation-sink ports.  The exact
arrow-tail criterion translates `S_TC` into the enumerated repair conditions.

The only theta core with two graph-theoretic triangles has path lengths
`(1,2,2)`.  Its graph is `K4-e` and has no tree-child admissible rooting.
Hence every standard `W_TC`, and therefore every `S_TC`, level-2 blob already
has at most one triangle.

Dependencies: definitions lock, primary core/support universe, independent
primitive compiler, independent triangle proof.

### 2. Pointwise cut theorem

For a true cut split, each of the four Fourier character blocks of the
flattening has rank one.  For a noncut split, the independent compiler gives
strict nonzero minors in the one-active-component case.  In the omitted
two-active crossing, a nontrivial endpoint satisfies the exact dichotomy

    F=abc-t^2>0,

or `F=0` and `G=a-bc>0`; the ordinary trivalent endpoint has `F=G=0`.
Thus every endpoint with `F=0` satisfies `a>=bc`.  Rank one would force
`F=F'=0` and

    aA=z^2bcBC,

contradicting `aA>=bcBC` and `0<z<1`.  Word compression lifts the finite
endpoint calculation to every subdivision.

Therefore a split is a cut exactly when its Fourier flattening has rank at
most four, at every open JC point.  If `N preceq_JC N'`, a cut of either
network must be a cut of the other: use the rank equation on the shared source
germ and the pointwise strict inequality on the network for which the split
is noncut.  The labelled bridge trees are equal.

This first recovers an unmarked reduced component tree.  A degree-three node
could still be either an ordinary trivalent median or a nontrivial
three-sunlet.  The arm-multihomogeneous polynomial

    F = abc-t^2

vanishes on the median and is strictly positive on the open three-sunlet
model.  It therefore excludes ordinary-to-blob and blob-to-ordinary
source-relative containment in both directions.  A nontrivial theta factor
has at least four boundaries in the standard-strong class, while a binary
ordinary component has degree three.  Hence the two networks have the same
ordinary/nontrivial decoration of the recovered component tree.

Dependency: independent bridge/cut package, three-sunlet marker, and their
adversarial reviews.

### 3. Correct bridge quotient and localization

The complete positive factorization fiber on a leaf-supported bridge tree is
the incidence action

    P_u -> a_{u,e}P_u,
    P_v -> a_{v,e}P_v,
    x_e -> x_e/(a_{u,e}a_{v,e}).

The old reciprocal-only chart is false.  The reduced standard bridge tree has
no unmarked bivalent component, so the local incidence actions admit explicit
positive analytic slices.  The intrinsic bridge coordinates are normalized
effective scales, not physical edge multipliers.

After the bridge tree is known, intrinsic projective extraction sends a
global source-relative containment to a source-relative containment of every
corresponding local factor.  Equivalently, select one taxon in every branch
incident to a focal blob.  Outside branches collapse to positive JC arm
scalars, and independent adjacent bridge multipliers make this marginal map a
submersion.  No continuous choice of target parameters is needed and distant
blobs cannot compensate for a local projective separator.

When finitely many target incoming-role/completion types are possible, the
focal source box lies in their union.  Semialgebraic dimension guarantees that
at least one member contains a nonempty source-relative full-dimensional open
subgerm.  It does **not** imply that one member contains the entire focal
source germ; the local relation `preceq_JC` needs only the former statement.

Dependency: independent bridge theorem, exact local stabilizer exclusion, and
multihomogeneity certificate for every atlas invariant.

### 4. Bounded local theorem

Choose a rigid source support: every reticulation-sink port and one port on
each segment of a minimum strong repair.  Theta support sizes are at most four
and cycle support size is two.  Support-plus-one locates an additional port's
segment; support-plus-two orders every pair on a segment.

Every selected marginal of a full strong target is one of the generated
completion records: selected ports are distributed in order on primitive
segments, while omitted sink and repair roles are restored by zero-character
dummy ports.  Serial omitted edges occur only through their product.  The
record separately states whether the selected ports retain the original
primitive core; a nonretaining marginal may reduce to a smaller strong
topology and is never called intrinsically weak.

The graph-bound exact atlas must certify all directed cases:

- the minimal two-outgoing cycle factor: the independently regenerated
  three-port polynomial `q011*q101*q110-q123^2` vanishes on the ordinary tree
  and is strictly positive on every open 3-sunlet, while the three sunlet
  orientations are ordinary `T`;
- theta to theta at five and six outgoing ports;
- theta to cycle at five and six outgoing ports;
- cycle to cycle at four outgoing ports;
- cycle to theta, beginning with four-port equalities and completing the
  union by every one, two, or three missing theta support role, for at most
  seven outgoing ports.

The selected three-outgoing cycle/theta equality uses an omitted target
sink/repair boundary.  It cannot represent two complete factors once cut
equality has fixed their identical boundary set.  A cycle factor with another
boundary is tested on its four-outgoing support-plus-two restriction, and a
cycle factor without one admits no nonretaining full strong target of the
same bridge-tree degree.  Hence it creates no residual local direction.

Every non-equal decorated relation must carry an exact graph-derived strict
separator or noncontainment witness.  Every equal relation must reconstruct
as labelled isomorphism or ordinary `T`.  No signature count alone is a proof.

Dependency: corrected all-four-subset 84-invariant atlas, cycle/theta union
compiler, independent compiler/reviewer, exact rank bounds, mutations.

### 5. Arbitrary-subdivision promotion

For a suppressed serial path with effective parameter

    z=x_1...x_k,

the differential

    dz=sum_i (product_{j != i} x_j) dx_i

is nonzero throughout the open cube.  The physical-to-effective
descriptor-parameter map is therefore an onto semialgebraic submersion.  It
need not be a minimal chart on a core-collapsed target tensor image; the proof
uses the core-retaining source map.  A full-dimensional arbitrary containment
therefore projects to a full-dimensional bounded decorated relation.

Fixing one pointwise-rigid support forces all one-port probes to use the same
fixed-label core identification.  Two-port probes agree on overlaps and
recover one total order on each segment.  If a probe subdivides a support
triangle edge, it has no `T` ambiguity and fixes literal orientation;
otherwise the unique triangle persists and one global `T` choice is coherent.
Setwise support symmetries may duplicate anchored atlas representatives but
cannot mix fixed-label probe transports.

Dependencies: rigid-support audit, bounded atlas, independent probe-coherence
review.

### 6. Root reduction

In a tree-child admissible rooting, repeatedly choose a tree or leaf child.
This reaches a labelled leaf along an all-ordinary path.  Reroot on the real
boundary cut edge on that path and reverse only ordinary arcs.  Tree vertices
on the path have indegree one in the original rooting, so the reversal creates
no directed cycle; retained reticulation arrowheads are unchanged.  The new
rooting is admissible and is tree-child by `S_TC`.

Uniform-root JC reversibility preserves the complete tensor.  Splitting or
combining the real boundary arm remains inside `(0,1)` and changes only its
incidence scale.  Thus every root-containing factor is covered by the
incoming-port theorem without introducing an unobservable artificial port.

Dependency: independent adversarial root-site review.

### 7. Synthesis and converse

Cut equality identifies the bridge tree.  Localization plus the arbitrary
local theorem forces corresponding factors to be isomorphic or `T`-related.
Thus no proper one-sided containment survives.

Conversely, the independently verified local `T` correspondence supplies a
common full-dimensional regular projective germ.  Fix positive representatives
of the finitely many incident arm orbits.  On each bridge choose a common
effective scale `z` in a sufficiently small nonempty open interval, so that
the physical multiplier `z/(a_{u,e}a_{v,e})` lies in `(0,1)` in both network
realizations.  The bridge tree has no cycle, so these choices are independent
and glue all local germs simultaneously.  Hence every coherent combination
of ordinary `T` moves gives a common global full-dimensional regular germ.

Dependencies: all preceding nodes and independent `T` verifier.

## Reconstruction consequence

Outside the union of the finite atlas's nonzero witness zero sets, Jacobian
singular loci, and anchor denominators:

1. Fourier-transform the exact distribution.
2. Recover cuts by rank-at-most-four flattenings and build the labelled bridge
   tree.
3. Select bounded taxon representatives around each component.
4. Match their graph-derived invariant deck to the certified local theorem.
5. Use one- and two-port probes to recover every segment word.
6. Put each triangle into the lexicographically least `T` orientation and
   assemble the components.

The largest corrected anchor-plus-two certificate has ten tensor ports, so a
direct implementation uses `O(n^10)` bounded marginals.  It recovers no
physical bridge multiplier.  It returns one canonical topology modulo `T`.
To list the orientations realizing one particular distribution, each finite
`T` variant must be tested for open-model membership separately; the common
germ theorem does not assert equality of their complete stochastic images.

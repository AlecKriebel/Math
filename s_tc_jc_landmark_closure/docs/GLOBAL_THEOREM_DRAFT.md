# Candidate global theorem and proof ledger

Status: **CANDIDATE — no theorem is promoted until every referenced gate is
independently verified.**

## Candidate theorem

Let `N,N'` be binary standard semi-directed `S_TC` level-2 topologies on the
same labelled taxa, under the locked open JC domain.  Then

    N preceq_JC N'

if and only if their labelled bridge trees agree and each corresponding
nontrivial blob is labelled-isomorphic or differs by ordinary triangle
redirection `T`.  Consequently there is no proper one-sided generic
containment, and symmetric full-dimensional regular overlap is exactly the
equivalence relation generated by `T`.

Together with the frozen all-`n` theorem in `W_TC \ S_TC`, this would make
strong tree-childness the sharp boundary for level-2 JC topology
identifiability modulo `T`.

## Proof ledger

### 1. Standard structure

For a binary rooted network, deleting one parent edge at each reticulation
gives a tree.  Cyclomatic number is additive over blobs and equals the number
of reticulations blob by blob.  Thus a nontrivial level-2 blob has cycle rank
one or two.  After suppressing unported ordinary bivalent vertices it is,
respectively, a cycle or a theta core.

The independent orientation compiler derives one cycle and four theta event
cores.  Binary subdivisions are finite words of port labels on their directed
segments, together with the obligatory reticulation-sink ports.  The exact
arrow-tail criterion translates `S_TC` into the enumerated repair conditions.

The only theta core with two graph-theoretic triangles has path lengths
`(1,2,2)`.  Its graph is `K4-e` and has no tree-child admissible rooting.
Hence every standard `W_TC`, and therefore every `S_TC`, level-2 blob already
has at most one triangle.

Dependencies: definitions lock, primary core/support universe, independent
primitive compiler, independent triangle proof.

### 2. Pointwise cut theorem

For a true cut split, each of the four Fourier character blocks of the
flattening has rank one.  For a noncut split, the independent compiler gives
strict nonzero minors in the one-active-component case.  In the omitted
two-active crossing, a nontrivial endpoint satisfies the exact dichotomy

    F=abc-t^2>0,

or `F=0` and `G=a-bc>0`; the ordinary trivalent endpoint has `F=G=0`.
Thus every endpoint with `F=0` satisfies `a>=bc`.  Rank one would force
`F=F'=0` and

    aA=z^2bcBC,

contradicting `aA>=bcBC` and `0<z<1`.  Word compression lifts the finite
endpoint calculation to every subdivision.

Therefore a split is a cut exactly when its Fourier flattening has rank at
most four, at every open JC point.  If `N preceq_JC N'`, a cut of either
network must be a cut of the other: use the rank equation on the shared source
germ and the pointwise strict inequality on the network for which the split
is noncut.  The labelled bridge trees are equal.

Dependency: independent bridge/cut package and its adversarial review.

### 4. Bounded local theorem

Choose a rigid source support: every reticulation-sink port and one port on
each segment of a minimum strong repair.  Theta support sizes are at most four

Every selected marginal of a full strong target is one of the generated weak
completions: selected ports are distributed in order on primitive segments,

The graph-bound exact atlas must certify all directed cases:

Every non-equal decorated relation must carry an exact graph-derived strict

Dependency: corrected all-four-subset 84-invariant atlas, cycle/theta union

### 5. Arbitrary-subdivision promotion

For a suppressed serial path with effective parameter

    z=x_1...x_k,

the differential

    dz=sum_i (product_{j != i} x_j) dx_i

Dependencies: rigid-support audit, bounded atlas, independent probe-coherence

### 6. Root reduction

In a tree-child admissible rooting, repeatedly choose a tree or leaf child.
This reaches a labelled leaf along an all-ordinary path.  Reroot on the real
boundary cut edge on that path and reverse only ordinary arcs.  Tree vertices
on the path have indegree one in the original rooting, so the reversal creates
no directed cycle; retained reticulation arrowheads are unchanged.  The new
rooting is admissible and is tree-child by `S_TC`.

Uniform-root JC reversibility preserves the complete tensor.  Splitting or
combining the real boundary arm remains inside `(0,1)` and changes only its
incidence scale.  Thus every root-containing factor is covered by the
incoming-port theorem without introducing an unobservable artificial port.

Dependency: independent adversarial root-site review.

### 7. Synthesis and converse

Cut equality identifies the bridge tree.  Localization plus the arbitrary
local theorem forces corresponding factors to be isomorphic or `T`-related.
Thus no proper one-sided containment survives.

Conversely, the independently verified local `T` correspondence supplies a
common full-dimensional regular projective germ.  Products of these local
germs and arbitrary positive effective bridge scales glue through the bridge
tree.  Hence every coherent combination of ordinary `T` moves gives a common
global full-dimensional regular germ.

Dependencies: all preceding nodes and independent `T` verifier.

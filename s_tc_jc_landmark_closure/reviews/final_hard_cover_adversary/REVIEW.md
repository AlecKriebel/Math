**UNRESOLVED for the global arbitrary-subdivision theorem.**

**VERIFIED for the completed schema-3 theta-2 n=4 hard-cover and probe
packages.**  These are substantial repaired components, but they are not the

## Findings

### 1. Quarantined schema 2

**FALSE AS A FIXED-ROOT DECORATED-RELATION CERTIFICATE.**

The old n=4 theta-2 stream keyed states only by standard semi-directed mixed
codes and remaining roles.  Independent replay found:

- 1,518 states and 2,106 raw coverages;
- 276 states merging multiple fixed root cases;
- the same 276 states merging multiple exact target rooted graph IDs;
- 1,452 rooted-binding mismatch events.

The colliding rooted graphs do have equal standard mixed codes.  That is why
the bug evaded the old key and why mixed-code equality cannot repair it.

### 2. Schema-3 theta-2 hard cover

**VERIFIED.**  The replacement state key binds the fixed-full root-case ID and
both exact rooted graph IDs.  The clean-room replay imports no primary graph,
Fourier, canonicalization, or sign code and verifies:

- all 132 independently regenerated fixed roots;
- all 2,106 states and 2,106 exact raw paths;
- all 606 graph bodies and all 21 polynomial bodies;
- every content hash and producer-summary commitment;
- class membership for every graph, with tree-childness quantified over
  internal vertices only and leaves explicitly excluded;
- all source/target graphs rebuilt from raw provenance;
- all 1,860 graph-derived generic separator pullbacks;
- all 114 per-path refinement states and their independently regenerated child
  sets;
- all 132 terminal labelled isomorphisms;
- zero ordinary-T terminals and zero unresolved terminals.

No path crosses a fixed root case or exact rooted graph ID.  There are zero
per-path, merged-provenance, or state-level child-set mismatches.

The hard-cover mutation suite rejects all 13 attacks, including deletion,
duplication, cross-root merge, equal-mixed-code rooted-graph substitution,
port-map alteration, source/target reversal, map corruption, separator swap,
valid polynomial attached to the wrong graph, missing key, incoherent parent,
child deletion, and a forged strict-sign proof.

### 3. Actual theta-2 probe streams

**VERIFIED.**  The independently reconstructed package contains:

- 23,400 standard-strong level-2 rooted graph bodies;
- 29 content-addressed polynomial bodies;
- 168,582 state rows;
- 168,582 one-to-one root/path bindings;
- 12,906 complete `A+p` relations;
- 155,676 complete conditional `A+p+q` relations.

For every row, the reviewer independently checks exact deletion to the
recorded parent, the permitted internal-blob insertion arc, physical port
labels, base root/path and role bindings, state/graph IDs, rigid quotient
transport, transport restriction to the parent, and the regenerated JC
separator body.  Expected and actual p/q Cartesian cover sets are identical.
No state is multiply bound across roots or paths.

The probe mutation suite rejects all 9 attacks: deletion, duplication,
port-map alteration, source/target reversal, polynomial swap, wrong transport,
fabricated Jacobian metadata, wrong q parent, and cross-root state merge.

Jacobian checking is not applicable to this package because it makes no rank
or Jacobian claim and its closed schema contains no Jacobian field.

### 4. Remaining artifact gaps

**UNRESOLVED / FALSE AS A COMPLETENESS CERTIFICATE.**

- The full schema-3 n=3 replacement is still being generated and is not yet
  included in this verdict.  Historical schema-2 n=3 streams cannot be used
  under the strengthened rooted identity.
- The independently rebuilt bounded signature universes contain 110 unequal
  necessary directed n=3 pairs and 776 unequal necessary directed n=4 pairs.
  No pair-level `bounded_relations_n{3,4}` streams or exact associated sign
  libraries exist.  Equal-deck hard-cover streams do not certify these 886
  directions.
- The verified theta-2 terminals have no triangle.  Their probe package cannot
  establish the requested T-edge subdivision case; that must be bound to a
  valid triangle-bearing base package.

These are artifact/theorem-proof gaps, not counterexamples to the desired JC
identifiability theorem.  The correct global status remains **UNRESOLVED**
until they are closed.

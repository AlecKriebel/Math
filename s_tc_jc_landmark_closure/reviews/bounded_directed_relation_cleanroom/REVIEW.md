## Directed retention theorem

Let `S(M)` be the indices of the audited invariant deck whose pullbacks are not
identically zero on a model `M`.  If a relatively open part of the source model
lies in the target model, every target identity is a source identity.  Hence

```
source <= target  =>  S(source) subseteq S(target),
```

implemented exactly as

```
source_nonzero & ~target_nonzero == 0.
```

This is only a necessary prefilter.  A retained unequal relation is closed by
either a target pullback having a strict sign throughout the entire open cube
(so the stochastic interiors are disjoint) or its bound fixed-full hard-cover
root.  A common signature alone is never accepted as overlap or containment.

To prevent a graph from borrowing another graph's signature, every distinct
graph/port use is assigned an independently regenerated effective descriptor
deck.  The full invariant signature is recomputed once for every exact
descriptor class, and every graph use must agree with that class.

## Zero-sum and bounded root-side convention

For a quartet assignment with total character zero, the character sum on a
split equals that on its complement.  Therefore replacing a mask `m` by
`min(m, 15 xor m)` is exact.  If several physical arcs have the same normalized
row through all switchings, every Fourier monomial contains their multipliers
with equal exponents and the tensor depends on them only through their product.

For every `k >= 1`,

```
(x_1,...,x_k) -> x_1...x_k
```

maps the open cube onto `(0,1)` and has nonzero differential there.  Thus the
product quotient is the exact effective positive JC image, not a diagonal or
boundary specialization.  Noncomplementary split rows remain distinct.

Consequently the bounded-atlas convention that retains rooted selected-side
masks and two root arcs as separate physical variables is sound for
zero/nonzero/strict-sign classification.  Its graph-specific pullback is the
pullback of the same effective tensor along a positive surjective submersion.
No atlas regeneration is required for that convention.

## Limits of this verdict

This is a finite bounded directed-relation gate.  It does not by itself prove:

- arbitrary-subdivision promotion;
- bridge peeling or cut preservation;

in this directory promotes them.

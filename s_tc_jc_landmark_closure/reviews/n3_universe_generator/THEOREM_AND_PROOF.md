Status: **VERIFIED**

## Theorem

three-outgoing rigid support of a standard-strong cycle or theta factor, with
one independently distinguished incoming boundary.  Let the target range
over every selected tensor induced by a full standard-strong cycle or theta
completion on the same four physical boundaries, allowing its structural
incoming boundary either to be selected or marginalized.  Retain a directed
source-to-target presentation exactly when the necessary JC invariant-deck
condition

```text

```

holds.  Here `S` is the exact 84-invariant nonvanishing signature on the
ordered four-port tensor.

Then the complete theorem-forced universe consists of:

- 8 independently generated source supports;
- 831 selected-incoming target completions;
- 1,983 marginalized-incoming target completions;
- 10,826 raw necessary decorated directed presentations; and
- 10,466 merged decorated directed relations.

The normalized raw and merged multisets are exactly the multisets classified
by the primary graph-to-algebra stream.  Consequently the existing
recordwise clean-room algebra applies to an exhaustive relation universe; it
is no longer conditional on a primary-supplied census.

## Proof

The primitive data are the structurally proved cycle and four theta arc
templates and their minimum repairs.  They are written explicitly in the
independent theta-2 algebra engine and are not loaded from the primary
completion compiler.

For a source core, choose one minimum repair, select every reticulation-sink
child, and add exactly enough ordinary segment ports to obtain three outgoing
boundaries.  Distribute the extra labels over all directed segments and take
every order on each segment.  Exact labelled mixed-graph quotienting leaves
eight supports.  This construction is the rigid-support theorem specialized
to three outgoing boundaries, so it omits no source and introduces no
arbitrary topology search.

For a target, independently choose:

1. one of the five primitive cores;
2. selected or marginalized incoming mode;
3. the subset of selected reticulation-sink children;
4. one minimum repair; and
5. a weak composition and order of the remaining selected ordinary ports on
   the directed segments.

One zero-character dummy restores each omitted sink, each empty segment of
the chosen repair, and the structural incoming boundary in marginalized
mode.  Conversely, contracting every unselected ordinary subdivision in a
full standard-strong target produces exactly these choices.  Thus the
grammar is onto.  It gives 831 and 1,983 completions in the two modes.

switchings, computes descendant masks, quotients only true zero-sum


Enumerating these theorem-forced choices gives 10,826 necessary raw

## Independence boundary

already-independent switching/mask/invariant engine in
`reviews/theta2_signature_gate/verify_gate.py`; that engine itself imports no
primary graph, completion, Fourier, invariant-evaluation, or atlas code.  The
only primary objects used are inert coefficient files and the final relation
stream being tested.  Primitive generation, target completion generation,
relative port assignments, exact signatures, and normalization are all
performed before the claim stream is opened.

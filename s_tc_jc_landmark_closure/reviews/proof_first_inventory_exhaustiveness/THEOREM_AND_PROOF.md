## Theorem

Work under `docs/DEFINITIONS_LOCK.md` and the primitive-core and support
theorems in `docs/GENERATOR_AND_SUPPORT_THEOREM.md`.  Let a *fixed-full root
relation* record:

1. one anchored rigid source support, including its actual incoming boundary;
2. one full standard-strong target completion;
3. whether the target's incoming boundary is one of the selected tensor ports
   or has character zero and is restored as a dummy boundary;
4. the complete relative permutation of source and target tensor ports;
5. the ordered restoration roles needed when the selected target marginal
   does not retain its full primitive core; and
6. the source-to-target direction.

Then the following statements hold.

1. The complete anchored source-support universe at three selected outgoing
   ports consists of eight presentations: three cycle presentations, two
   `theta-0` presentations, one `theta-1` presentation, and two `theta-3`
   presentations.
2. `theta-2` has no three-outgoing rigid support.  Its complete anchored
   source-support universe at four selected outgoing ports consists of three
   presentations.
3. Every selected target completion is generated by the finite grammar:
   choose a primitive core, a selected/marginalized incoming boundary, a
   subset of selected path-sink children, a minimum repair, and a weak
   composition of the remaining ordinary selected ports among the directed
   core segments; then insert exactly the missing repair and sink dummy ports.
   Finally apply every relative tensor-port permutation.
4. The regenerated source supports are exactly the source supports appearing
   in the frozen root streams.  Every target completion appearing there lies
   in the independently regenerated raw target grammar, every relative port
   transport is a permutation, and every selected/marginalized incoming role
   and dummy-role multiset is forced by that grammar.
5. Regenerating all derived fields gives every frozen root-case object
   exactly, including its exact SHA-256 object identifier.  No two frozen
   records have the same normalized decorated directed relation key.
6. The 5,344 three-outgoing root cases and 132 `theta-2` four-outgoing root
   cases are exactly the roots of the respective frozen hard-cover refinement
   forests.  There is no omitted, duplicated, unknown, or dangling root key.

## Proof

### 1. Primitive cores and ports

The locked generator theorem reduces every factor to one of the five entries
in `core_universe.json`: `cycle`, `theta-0`, `theta-1`, `theta-2`, or
`theta-3`.  Every ordinary side vertex deleted by core reduction lies on one
directed segment and carries one outgoing port.  Conversely, an ordered word
of ports on each directed segment uniquely reconstructs all such vertices.
Thus a completion is combinatorially a finite tuple of segment words plus the
distinguished incoming boundary and path-sink children.

A strong completion must occupy one of the listed minimum repairs and must
give every path-sink reticulation its outgoing child port.  A rigid source
support therefore has

```text
minimum repair size + number of path sinks
```

outgoing labels.  The core data give:

| core | minimum repair | path sinks | rigid size |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| cycle | 1 | 1 | 2 |
| theta-0 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| theta-1 | 2 | 1 | 3 |
| theta-2 | 2 | 2 | 4 |
| theta-3 | 1 | 2 | 3 |

This proves immediately why the three-outgoing stream covers precisely
`cycle`, `theta-0`, `theta-1`, and `theta-3`, whereas `theta-2` first occurs in
the four-outgoing stream.  The cycle receives one additional probe port to
make the fixed-full three-outgoing source.

### 2. Independent source-support quotient

The verifier independently computes each tiny source automorphism group as a
role-preserving automorphism of the directed segment multigraph.  It fixes the
incoming source and fixes each distinctly labelled path-sink child.  It does
not read an automorphism table from the primary implementation.

The resulting quotient is transparent:

- the cycle swaps its two parallel source-to-sink segments; inserting the one
  probe before or after the repair anchor, or on the other segment, gives
  three orbits;
- `theta-0` has no nontrivial fixed-sink source automorphism, so its two
  repairs give two supports;
- `theta-1` swaps its two parallel final segments, so its two repairs give one
  support orbit;
- `theta-3` has no nontrivial fixed-sink source automorphism, so its two
  repairs give two supports;
- in `theta-2`, the branch swap fixes the two sink labels and identifies
  repairs 1 and 2, while repairs 0 and 3 remain distinct, giving representatives
  0, 1, and 3.

Hence the independent counts are `3+2+1+2=8` and `3`.  The exact regenerated
provenance lists equal the exact provenance sets in the frozen streams.

### 3. Exhaustive target-completion grammar

Fix a core with `m` directed segments and `r` path sinks, and let `p` be the
number of selected outgoing source ports.  Choose:

- `i in {0,1}`, according as the target incoming boundary is selected;
- a sink mask `M` in `{0,...,2^r-1}`;
- one listed minimum repair `R`; and
- a weak composition of

  ```text
  p + 1 - i - |M|
  ```

  ordinary selected ports into the `m` segment words.

The formula counts tensor ports: source and target both have `p+1` selected
ports; a selected target incoming boundary and selected path-sink children
consume the indicated positions.  Along each segment, ordinary ports have a
unique linear order.  If a repair segment receives no selected ordinary port,
insert one `D_REPAIR` port there.  If a path sink is not selected, insert its
`D_SINK` child.  If the incoming boundary is not selected, insert the
`INCOMING` dummy boundary.  This produces a full strong completion.

Conversely, contract every unselected ordinary port-free path in any full
completion.  The surviving core, incoming mode, selected sink mask, occupied
minimum repair, and ordered segment words are exactly the data above.  Every
missing required repair or sink child is exactly one of the inserted dummy
roles.  Thus no completion is omitted.  Applying the full symmetric group on
the `p+1` tensor positions supplies every relative port matching.

Before graph-isomorphism quotienting, the independent normalized provenance
grammar has 2,822 keys for `p=3` and 6,146 keys for `p=4`.  The slight collapse
from the raw weak-composition count occurs only when two cycle repair choices
describe the same already-repaired completion; the cycle provenance correctly
stores no artificial repair index.  Every target provenance used by the
hard-cover roots belongs to this larger grammar.

### 4. Decorated directed keys

The mathematical key used here contains both source and target provenances,
the source-to-target direction, incoming-boundary mode, complete relative port
transport, and ordered target restoration roles.  The order of restoration
roles is not replaced by a target-only hash.  This is necessary: a
nonretaining selected marginal can have the same target topology under
different directed restorations.

For each frozen root, the verifier derives from the grammar:

- the selected source labels and identity source anchoring;
- the selected target labels;
- the complete dummy-role multiset;
- the incoming-boundary mode; and
- the arity constraints.

Keeping the frozen restoration order as part of the decorated key, it rebuilds
the complete root-case dictionary.  Its canonical JSON hash is exactly the
stored `root_case_id`.  Provenance-to-primitive-id,
primitive-id-to-provenance, and source-id-to-signature maps are all
one-to-one or functional as required.  The normalized decorated keys are
pairwise distinct.

### 5. Exact use by the hard-cover refinements

Independently stream the hard-cover relation files.  For each fixed-full root
key, collect all states and all child references.  The forest entries are the
states that are never referenced as a child.  Their set equals the stored
`entry_state_ids` for every root.  Every relation points to a known root, every
child is present in the same root forest, and every root has a forest.  The
counts are:

| stream | roots | relation states |
|---|---:|---:|
| n3 | 5,344 | 68,584 |
| theta2-n4 | 132 | 2,106 |

This proves that the hard-cover streams consume precisely the certified root
inventory.

## Scope boundary

This verifier intentionally does not regenerate Fourier tensors or invariant
decks.  Consequently it cannot independently derive the exact subset selected
by the opaque `selected_signature_sha256` field and does **not**, by itself,
prove that the upstream Fourier-signature filter selected every and only
algebraic collision.  Under the input restrictions of this audit, that claim
is **UNRESOLVED**, not verified by the crosswalk.

What is proved independently is the combinatorial statement: the primitive
and support grammar is exhaustive; every frozen selected root is a fully
specified valid decorated relation in that grammar; the frozen inventory has
fixed exact normalized content; and the downstream hard-cover forests cover
exactly that inventory.  Algebraic selection exhaustiveness remains the
responsibility of a separate Fourier/pullback verifier.

## Literal versus operational 2-sub-blobs

The four-sunlet

```text
v0--v1--v2--v3--v0,
vi--Li,
v1->v0<-v3
```

is simple, binary, and strongly tree-child.  For `W={v0,v1}`, the induced
subgraph is connected, contains no globally cut edge, and has exactly two
vertices adjacent outside.  It therefore satisfies the literal three-clause
Brits definition.  Four external edges leave `W`, so contraction creates a
degree-four vertex and ordinary degree-two suppression is undefined.

By contrast, an operational factor with exactly two external incident edges
in a simple binary level-2 graph has the `K4-e` whole-blob core; the census
above excludes it from `S_TC`.  The paper may use this operational lemma, but
must not identify it with every literal Brits 2-sub-blob.

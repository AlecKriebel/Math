# Compact-only probe-extension certificate

## Exact statement

**VERIFIED.** For the eight committed compact path-bound probe shards—four
for the three-outgoing (`n3`) family and four for the theta-2 four-outgoing
(`theta2_n4`) family—the compact bytes alone, together with their committed
hard-cover parent graphs and frozen JC invariant coefficients, determine and
certify every decorated `A+p` and conditional `A+p+q` relation.

The exact totals are:

| `n3` | 144 | 9,316 | 91,832 | 101,148 | 9 ports |
| `theta2_n4` | 132 | 12,906 | 155,676 | 168,582 | 10 ports |
| **total** | **276** | **22,222** | **247,508** | **269,730** | **10 ports** |

For `n3`, all four terminal classes occur and are verified exactly:

- 90,008 generic polynomial separations;
- 624 strict open-cube separations;
- 9,676 labelled isomorphisms;
- 840 ordinary triangle redirections `T`.

separations and 15,510 labelled isomorphisms.

No verbose `probe_extension_*` binding, state, graph, polynomial, summary, or
replay stream is an input to this theorem.

## Proof

Each compact summary binds one half-open range of a common ordered path
inventory.  The verifier reconstructs that inventory directly from the
committed hard-cover state and graph streams.  It validates the historical
graph content addresses, normalizes each rooted graph independently, and
checks that the four ranges in each family are disjoint, ordered, gapless,
and exhaustive.

For a path parent pair, admissible internal blob arcs are recomputed from the
rooted graph after independent rooted validation and standard semi-directed
reduction.  If the source arcs are `s_i` and target arcs are `t_j`, packed
`A+p` word `ic+j` is interpreted only as insertion on `(s_i,t_j)`.  The child
graphs are rebuilt by subdividing those exact arcs; the clean-room insertion
routine also deletes the inserted port and proves exact recovery of the
parent.  Thus a target hash cannot erase source embedding, direction, or port
matching.

Only an `A+p` cell independently certified as labelled-isomorphic or
ordinary `T` may have an `A+p+q` block.  For each such cell the admissible
child arcs are recomputed and their Cartesian product fixes the next block's
length and order.  The stored allowed-parent list and block shapes are then
checked against these regenerated values.  Exact exhaustion of each packed
array proves that no cell is omitted, duplicated, shifted, or appended.

For every separated relation, the verifier enumerates displayed reticulation
switchings from the rebuilt graph, computes descendant masks, restricts to
the selected quartet, applies the zero-sum complement quotient, and constructs
the complete JC Fourier coordinate polynomials.  It regenerates the stated
invariant pullback and compares the exact sparse polynomial—not merely its
hash—with the compact polynomial body.  A generic separator is accepted only
when the source pullback is nonzero and the target pullback is zero.  A strict
separator is accepted only in the reverse zero/nonzero orientation and after
independent exact factorization proves its stated sign throughout `(0,1)`.

For every allowed relation, labelled mixed-graph isomorphism is solved again.
For ordinary `T`, only arrowheads internal to the unique labelled triangle
are forgotten.  The induced vertex, edge, reticulation, and physical-port
transport must be unique and must equal the compact transport body.  Every
`q` transport is required to restrict to its exact parent `p` transport.  This
is the needed probe-coherence check; inconsistent per-probe isomorphisms or
triangle choices are rejected.

Every witness, transport, and polynomial library record is used by at least
one regenerated relation, and the independently counted class totals equal
the committed shard totals and the locked family totals.  The maximum
selected support is eight ports, so the two inserted probes never exceed ten
ports.  This proves the statement.

## Input closure

`TRACKED_INPUTS.json` is generated by resolving every external file reached
from the eight compact summaries and their two hard-cover summaries, then
requiring `git ls-files --error-unmatch` for each one.  It locks 50 exact
files and records their byte lengths and SHA-256 digests.  The verifier can
recheck this lock without a `.git` directory.  The package's own files are
covered by `MANIFEST.sha256` and by the archive-clean replay.

The shard summaries bind an earlier byte version of the compact schema.  Its
exact committed historical bytes are preserved here as
`COMPACT_PROBE_SCHEMA_LOCKED.md` and have the required digest
`febd49cb8d483fa528d744c782b82d90e9f5597e3c3eb75c1c25e0cd9b6c323e`.
The later upstream edit was prose clarification only; the gate does not
pretend that the two byte versions are equal.

## Scope

This theorem removes the active release's dependency on untracked verbose
probe-extension artifacts.  It certifies the two finite arbitrary-subdivision
probe families and their exact compact evidence.  It does not independently
assert the global level-2 identifiability theorem; that theorem uses this gate
as one finite computational premise.

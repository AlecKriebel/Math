# Required bibliographic repair

The marked integrated proof's final Basu–Karisani entry links arXiv:2207.10497 while naming the simplicial-replacement paper. The correct primary identifier for that named paper is [2009.13365v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13365v3). [2207.10497](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.10497) is instead their paper on computing the homology functor on semialgebraic maps and diagrams.

Repair the identifier and title/edition consistently before any manuscript promotion. Do not substitute an algorithm computing only rational homology for D4: its proof requires integral chains, induced maps, cross products and an integral Chern class. The original D4 argument enumerates actual semialgebraic triangulation/homeomorphism and simplicial-approximation witnesses and checks them by real quantifier elimination. Integral Smith normal form then supplies the required groups and matrices. The correct publisher introduction credits classical triangulation, and this witness argument does not depend on the efficient rational-only algorithm. This is a repairable citation defect, not a counterexample to the theorem.

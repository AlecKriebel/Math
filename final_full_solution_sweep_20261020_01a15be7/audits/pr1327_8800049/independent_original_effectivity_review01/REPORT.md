## Family1: finite complete count and model enumeration

**Mechanism.** The imported ideal-class-monoid and polarized Deligne classification compute an unweighted finite number of polarized finite-field isomorphism classes. Exact theta-equipped projective models are enumerated, filtered by their full Frobenius polynomial, and deduplicated by a finite projective-linear test. The exact count is the stopping certificate.

**Arithmetic checks.** Ordinarity of a simple class forces its Honda–Tate division algebra to be its CM field: the ordinary local slopes yield integral local invariants and there are no real Weil roots. Thus the degree-six polynomial is irreducible and square-free under the promise. The arbitrary-order weak-class enumeration is supported by the actual generality of Marseglia Proposition5.1 and Theorem4.6; it is not the Bass-only disjoint union of Picard groups. With the maximal order as auxiliary over-order, the conductor-bounded representatives cover every weak class. Filtering by the actual multiplicator ring before applying its Picard group is essential and is present. [Primary monoid paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.09671v3)

For a scalar isomorphism between I and H=conjugate(I)^t, its colon is (H:I)=a(I:I). Hence any true isomorphism passes invertibility and principality of the colon; any returned principal generator differs from a by a unit and also sends I onto H. Conversely the final exact equality x0I=H is sufficient. This guard avoids the false general inference from a principal colon alone. It is a valid repair of that possible shortcut, including for noninvertible ideals.

After such an isomorphism exists, S=(I:I) is conjugation-stable. Unit norms are then positive at all complex embeddings and form a subgroup of S*. The quotient S*/N(S*) is finite: u/conjugate(u) is an algebraic-integer unit with all conjugates of absolute value1, hence a root of unity; some even power of u is a unit norm. Finite-generation plus the exact unit computation makes the orbit enumeration finite. The imaginary/positivity filters are invariant on norm classes. These checks agree with the actual polarized classification and conjugated unit action. [Primary polarized paper, §§5–6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1805.10223v3)

Unconditional effectivity is a legitimate import. Lenstra Theorem5.4 and the following bounded-height S-unit construction explicitly produce full units and the class group; they do not rely on the tentative complexity statement5.5. **Model checks and deduplication.** Smooth projective dimension3, global functions equal to the ground field, and checked scheme-level group identities give an abelian variety. The supplied Cartier divisor and cube identity imply an ample theta line bundle. The Hilbert polynomial27t^3 then gives Euler characteristic1 and a principal polarization; the27 independent ambient linear forms give the complete3Theta embedding. Lang's theta-torsor result over a finite field ensures every polarized class has a presentation of this kind.

For two presentations, a true polarized group isomorphism can be rationally translated so that it pulls the actual second theta divisor onto the first. The principal polarization identifies the required Picard-zero difference with a rational point, and the theta section space has dimension1. Complete3Theta systems make the translated map projectively linear over the ground field. Conversely, a projective-linear theta-preserving map, translated back at the origin and checked as a group homomorphism, is a polarized isomorphism. This proves both directions of the finite PGL test. It does not cancel multiplication by3 on polarization maps, so characteristic3 causes no gap.

The Frobenius fingerprint uses prime-to-p M-torsion, with M>40q^3. All M^6 points eventually split over a finite extension; observing that exact cardinality proves completeness. **Stopping proof.** Every accepted retained model injects into the classes counted by N. Every counted class has a finite presentation somewhere in the enumeration. There are finitely many such classes and every preceding verification terminates. Therefore the list eventually reaches N, and equality of the two finite cardinalities proves completeness. ## Family2: marked canonical-lift inverse

**Mechanism.** Jointly enumerate a finite-field ppav, a number-field DVR lift, a lift of the original q-Frobenius satisfying h(F)=0, topology witnesses, and an integral unimodular matching matrix. Each whole tuple receives finite checks. An incorrect tuple cannot monopolize the computation by an infinite search for its missing marking.

**Fixed convention.** A computable nested tower of normal number fields with compatible chosen primes and residue embeddings defines a computable p-adic place on complex algebraic numbers. The locally unramified algebraic subfield H and its valuation ring D have finite-data membership and arithmetic tests. D is the chosen algebraic strict henselization, whose completion is the Witt ring with algebraically closed residue field. Only algebraic coefficients enter the verifier. The complex embedding of a candidate is its predetermined inclusion, not a successful-candidate-dependent choice. Deligne's full original §3 supplies algebraic canonical-lift descent and §8 ties the complex structure to the positive-valued Frobenius eigenvalues. [Deligne original](https://publications.ias.edu/sites/default/files/Number6.pdf)

**Independent canonicity derivation.** For ordinary h, write h=h_et h_mult over Z_p, with coprime reductions, h_et(0) a unit and h_mult congruent x^3 modulo p. Bezout/CRT yields complementary idempotents in Z_p[x]/h. If h(F_B)=0, these act at every finite p-power level and split the lifted p-divisible group into direct factors. Their special fibers are exactly the étale and multiplicative ordinary factors because the lifted endomorphism reduces to the specified q-Frobenius. A finite flat factor with étale special fiber is étale over the local base: its finite differential module vanishes modulo the maximal ideal and therefore vanishes by Nakayama. Applying this to the Cartier dual of the other factor proves it is of multiplicative type. Henselian uniqueness of the two factors gives the canonical split lift. Serre–Tate full faithfulness identifies the abelian deformation and its endomorphism with the canonical ones. There is no p>2 assumption or discarded torsion Serre–Tate parameter. Katz Theorems1.2.1 and2.1(1),(4) have the required deformation and unique-homomorphism content, checked at the Artin quotients and then passed to the complete base. [Katz primary source](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/serretatelocmod.pdf)

The reduction check really retains q-Frobenius as a base-linear endomorphism after a residue-field extension. Replacing it with q^m-Frobenius or with a semilinear coordinate map would be insufficient; neither replacement appears in the stated check.

**Certificate existence.** The critical descent argument is supported, rather than merely asserted for arbitrary extension fields. If an element of the completed fraction field is algebraic over H, it is algebraic over Q. Its finite local extension of Q_p has value group contained in Z and hence ramification index1. With perfect finite residue fields it is unramified, so the element belongs to H. Thus H is relatively algebraically closed in its completion; characteristic0 makes each finitely generated intermediate coefficient extension regular. Milne Corollary15.4(b), including its graph proof, descends the required generic-fiber homomorphisms over those regular extensions. BLR §1.2 Proposition8 and proof on printedp15 supply extension to the abelian schemes over the DVR. Both exact source locations were freshly inspected. [Milne](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AV110.pdf), [BLR original chapter scan](https://math.arizona.edu/~cais/scans/BLR-Neron_Models/neron1.pdf)

Over the strictly henselian base, the smooth Picard torsor for the polarization has a lifted point; rigidification provides the actual line bundle. The ample degree-one theta bundle has a relative section and its cube gives the embedding. The equations, group maps, Frobenius, polarization, divisor and special-fiber identification involve finitely many elements of D, so they descend to a single enumerated locally unramified number-field localization. This is the needed finite successful tuple. Merely knowing an arbitrary CM lift exists would not suffice, but the manuscript proves more.

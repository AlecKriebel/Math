## Independent reconstruction and falsification of the essential chain

**Minimum and field passage.** A closed topological 3-manifold has a finite simplicial triangulation by the standard3-dimensional triangulation theorem. The nonempty set of integer g2-values is bounded below by Bagchi, so its minimum is attained. A minimizer with Gamma=10beta1 satisfies the triangulation-level equality theorem. **Morse equality.** The new mu definition gives mu0=sum_v 1/(m_v+1) and mu1=sum_v(1+s(link(v)))/(m_v+1). It is valid without neighborliness. In a uniform vertex ordering, the earlier-neighbor subset A has probability 1/((m_v+1)binom(m_v,|A|)). A one-vertex pair has relative beta0=1 exactly for an empty earlier-neighbor set, and relative beta1=c(A)-1 for nonempty A. This independently reproduces Bagchi's averaging formula, including the empty-set convention.

The exact sequence for a pair A⊂B yields

    beta1(B,A)-beta0(B,A)
      = beta1(B)-beta1(A)-beta0(B)+beta0(A)
        + dim ker[H1(A)->H1(B)].

Summing over an ordering and averaging gives beta1(K)-1<=mu1-mu0. Equality holds precisely when every induced H1(Y)->H1(K) is injective: all nonnegative step-kernel dimensions vanish for every ordering, and every induced subcomplex can occur. This reconstruction uses ordinary exact-sequence linear algebra over the fixed field; it imports neither orientability nor the older neighborly-only mu formula.

The separation theorem gives mu1-mu0<=(f1-4f0)/10=g2/10-1, with equality exactly when every vertex link is stacked. Thus g2=10beta1 forces both local stackedness and induced-H1 injectivity. Local stackedness alone would not suffice.

## Universal argument, independently checked

Each coordinate of an F-flow is a binary even edge set. Thus every cycle-continuous function pulls an F-flow back to an F-flow. Any target flow nonzero on the image would yield a nowhere-zero source flow. Every permutation of the three nonzero F values is linear and preserves conservation.

First suppose a cycle-continuous map sends K to L, where K and L belong to `{A,B}`. Missing a target edge permits its edge-deletion flow to pull back to a nowhere-zero flow, impossible. The map is onto and, by equal 27-edge sizes, bijective. Images of a source vertex star are orthogonal to every target binary cycle, hence form a target cut by incidence-matrix row-space/kernel orthogonality. Every size-three target cut is a vertex star. The edge bijection therefore induces an injective vertex map, hence a vertex bijection, and consequently a graph isomorphism. In particular, maps between A and B are impossible, and a self-map of B is an automorphism.

For every integer n≥2, place A at path indices 0,n and B at the n−1 internal indices. At every internal block use a on the left and b on the right. Delete attachment vertices and join their three exposed neighbors across each interface by any fixed matching. Independence of a,b prevents an edge being incident to both deleted vertices. The result $H_n$ is simple, cubic and connected, with $16n+18$ vertices and $24n+27$ edges. Connectedness follows directly from the connected attachment-deleted cores.

Each restored block has a 27-edge image (E_i\subset E$H_n$). Adjacent images intersect in exactly their three joining edges; other pairs are disjoint. Their private subsets $I_i$ are disjoint, with 24 edges for endpoints and 21 for internal blocks. A binary cycle of $H_n$, restricted to $E_i$ and with the attachment vertices restored, is a block cycle: its degree at a restored attachment is even because the joining triple is a cut. Thus the natural block edge injection is cycle-continuous.

Conversely every block cycle extends to a binary cycle of $H_n$ with exactly its original restriction to $E_i$. At each attachment, it uses zero or two edges. In the latter case connect their exterior endpoints by a path through the connected exterior branch. The two branches of an internal block are disjoint. Taking the union or symmetric difference yields even degree everywhere and changes no edge in $E_i$. Since every block edge lies on a block cycle, each edge of $H_n$ lies on a cycle; $H_n$ is bridgeless.

Now let a cycle-continuous map send K in `{A,B}` to $H_n$, with image R of size at most 27. If R contains an $E_i$, size equality gives R=$E_i$. Testing against extended block cycles makes the induced restored-block map cycle-continuous, hence the isomorphism already proved.

Otherwise every $E_i$ misses an edge. At most one private set $I_i$ can lie wholly in R, since two disjoint such sets have at least 42 edges. If every block misses a private edge, choose one such edge per block and put a critical F-flow, zero exactly at that edge, on the restored block. Every attachment star has three distinct nonzero values. Sequentially apply a linear permutation to each next block's whole flow to agree with the previous block on their prescribed matching. A path creates no compatibility cycle. The flows glue to a target flow whose zeros all lie outside R, a contradiction on pullback.

If one $I_i\subset R$, choose a missed joining edge z of its $E_i$, with adjacent block j. Use the critical flow zero at z in both these blocks. Every other block has a missed private edge and receives its corresponding critical flow. At the special interface the values have the form `(0,c,c)` on both sides, with the zero on the same joining edge; a linear permutation can match c. All other interfaces have three distinct nonzero values. The attachments are independent, so z affects no other attachment star. Sequential matching again gives a global flow nonzero on R, the same contradiction. Therefore every K-image is exactly one restored block, via an isomorphism.

Finally let a cycle-continuous map send $H_m$ to $H_n$. Restricting it along every natural source block injection gives target indices f(i) and local block isomorphisms. The types A/B are preserved. Adjacent source blocks share a triple, whose three distinct images force their targets to be equal or adjacent. Equality is impossible for an A/B pair. For a B/B pair, equality would identify the image of the right-attachment star b in the left block with that of the left-attachment star a in the right block. Composing the two local automorphisms would give an automorphism sending a to b, excluded above. There is no collapse.

At an internal source block the left and right attachment triples are disjoint, and its local map is an edge bijection. They cannot both map onto the same target joining triple. Thus f(i−1)≠f(i+1): there is no fold. All steps on the target path consequently have the same sign, ±1. The distinct source endpoints have target endpoint type A, so their images are exactly 0 and n. It follows that m=n. Hence the infinite family `{H_n:n≥2}` is pairwise incomparable in both directions and answers OPG323 completely.

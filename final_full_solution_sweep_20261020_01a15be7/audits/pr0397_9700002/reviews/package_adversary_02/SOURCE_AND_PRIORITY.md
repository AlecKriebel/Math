## Direct primary-body comparisons

- [Hassin–Zemel1988 final author-hosted paper](https://www.tau.ac.il/~hassin/transportation_88.pdf): the body was read from the actual scanned copy with OCR; printed81–82 were independently rendered and visually checked. The stated sufficient condition gamma>alpha+beta+chi, chi=max(0,2epsilon/3,(beta+epsilon)/2), fails for balanced requirements k, total k² and k rows/columns: natural exponents give2>3. Properness holds for capacities bounded below by1/2, so failure of properness is not the original obstruction. Size reparameterization preserves the deficit; reducing capacities by a growing factor loses uniform properness. The old theorem’s max-flow application uses different bounded terminal edges. This rejects these direct theorem transfers, not all conceivable older derivations.
- [Karp–Motwani–Nisan actual1988 report](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/1988/CSD-88-392.pdf): I read the formulation, Section4 directed theorem and Section5 further work, and visually checked printed15. Its proved directed theorem fixes bounded integer support and positive constant mean surplus. The scaled candidate mean is1+delta_k/2 with continuous unbounded support and vanishing surplus. Fixed truncation at K has limiting mean1-exp(-(2K-1))/2<1. The report explicitly separates growing support and vanishing surplus as future work. The1993 final journal body remained inaccessible in my fresh publisher attempt; no edition equivalence or global nonapplication is asserted.

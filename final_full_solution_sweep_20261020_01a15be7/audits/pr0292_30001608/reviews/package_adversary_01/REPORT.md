## Mathematical attempts to falsify the theorem

This review does not transfer the result to a total-population denominator, a fixed aggregate seed-upload capacity, another selection rule, zero arrival rate, a uniform-in-load bound, or stationary moments.

**Generator and rounding.** Every arrival adds two to workload `W=2a+2b+x+y`; every internal event subtracts one. First downloads conserve `Z=a+x-b-y`; departures give `LZ=(y-x)/D`. The total rate of unit jumps changing Z is exactly `lambda+d`, with `d=(2xy+x+y)/D`. The rounded function has globally `1/m`-Lipschitz derivative, including both noninteger junction crossings. Therefore `c/(2m)=1/8` gives precisely `beta=17lambda/8` and `q=7/8`. Neither an omitted arrival variance term nor an incorrect favorable sign is present.

**Finite Poisson terminal state.** The auxiliary chain has invariant weights `gamma^k`; adding a new upper state increases the truncated mean, which tends to `theta+1`. Consequently a finite R with the two strict margins exists for every positive real load. Prefix centering makes each corrector increment negative. At the top, the essential equation is `R g(R-1)=R-mu`; it is included. At R the upward increment vanishes, and at R+1 a downward jump reaches `g(R)=0`. Birth-rate increases multiply nonpositive differences and death-rate decreases multiply nonnegative differences, giving the required actual-generator upper comparison, including the terminal state. No stationary mean of the evolving original process is substituted.

**Postjump interfaces and unbounded rooms.** The exact product identity uses `g` at the successor state to multiply the cutoff difference. Every first download raises D while reducing or preserving each waiting-room numerator, so both cutoff increments are nonpositive for arbitrarily large a,b. There is no hidden factor proportional to these potentially unbounded download rates in the positive error estimate. At a departure, a ratio already at or above eta is saturated; otherwise its numerator is below eta D, so the cutoff increase is at most `2 ell/D`. Two cutoffs give the stated `4 ell G d/D` error. **Region coverage and coercivity.** If both visible counts are at least R+1, every current and successor correction vanishes and `d >= min(x,y)`.

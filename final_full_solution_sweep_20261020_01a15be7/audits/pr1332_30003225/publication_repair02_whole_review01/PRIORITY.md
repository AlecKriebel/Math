# New independent priority and provenance challenge

Verdict: no material prior-publication objection was identified in the inspected primary sources. This is a bounded search finding, not a worldwide firstness or continuous-openness certificate. Caution in wording alone was not treated as evidence of originality.

The proposed contribution is the exact complete moving free-center intersection formula and its five-blowup obstruction to a **specified reference multiple**. The mechanisms—blowup charts, toric valuations, contact cylinders and density—are standard and credited. I specifically looked for an earlier equivalent calculation rather than asking whether the method was new. I found no authenticated earlier statement giving this negative application in the sources below. The intersections being other divisorial Nash sets is established toric background, not a new general result.

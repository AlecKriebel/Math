## Reconstruction
Fix c1=0 and c2 the tangent y=0. Write D_j for the exceptional divisor at the jth center. Inductively, after j zero free choices, y=x^j u_j. On D_j, the only intersection with its predecessor is u_j=infinity; each allowed free continuation is finite u_j=a, followed by u_j=a+x u_(j+1). Hence the complete length-n free family with first k centers fixed is parametrized by P(x)=sum_(e=k)^(n-1) a_e x^e, y=P(x)+x^n w. The first differing a_e changes the next center; all coefficients are allowed. A branch of blowups on an older component is not a minimal predecessor sequence for this final prime divisor, and a satellite choice changes proximity type.

Set z=y-P(x). The cylinder S_P defined by z_1=...=z_(n-1)=0 in centered arcs is irreducible: its coefficient algebra is a polynomial algebra after the triangular coordinate change z=y-P(x). Its open subset x_1!=0 is dense and lifts to (x,w=z/x^n) with transverse contact to the last exceptional component. Conversely in the two charts adjacent to that component the maps are (x,w)->(x,x^n w) and (v,h)->(vh,v^(n-1)h^n). Centering the lift on it forces respectively ord x>=1 or ord h>=1, and thus ord z>=n. This proves that the closed Nash set is S_P, including x=0 and the constant arc as limits. The explicit algebraic family x_eps=x_0+eps*t, y_eps=P(x_eps)+y_0-P(x_0) verifies density for every degenerate arc. No single simultaneous family over P is needed for an intersection of closures.

For the full family, P=0 and P=x^k imply ord y>=n and ord x^k>=n, hence ord x>=ceil(n/k). These bounds conversely imply ord P(x)>=n for every coefficient tuple, so they are sufficient. For exactly k common centers one excludes a_k=0. The two allowed choices x^k and 2x^k imply the same bounds by subtraction. This uses characteristic zero, as stated C. Quantification over all complex coefficient tuples is resolved analytically, not by finite testing.

For D_j and q>=1, the same two charts show every exact-q or at-least-q lift has ord x>=q and ord y>=jq. The cylinder with those bounds is irreducible, and the dense open locus ord x=q lifts to exact contact q. Thus both closed contact conventions give threshold pair (q,jq), including j=1 by the usual two first-blowup charts. Arcs x=0 can lie in the closure despite their actual lift landing on an older divisor; this does not falsify the equality because the sets are closures.

Order cylinders have unique independent finite thresholds, witnessed by setting one coordinate to its lowest allowed monomial and the other to a sufficiently high monomial.

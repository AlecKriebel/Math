# Reviewed preprint: free spanning-forest degree under squaring

Alec Kriebel, ORCID [0009-0001-9320-500X](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X).
*Free spanning-forest degree can decrease under squaring*,22October2026,
preprint version1.0.0.

The [seven-page paper](publication/graph-square-forest-degree.pdf) gives an
infinite connected locally finite simple unweighted vertex-transitive
counterexample. For the binary grandparent graph G, the expected FUSF root
degree is `(24-sqrt(2))/7>161/50`; for its simple square it is at most
`3208526010913748780/10^18<321/100`. With the explicitly stated convention
`b_F=Edeg_FUSF/2-1`, the decrease exceeds1/200. Both infinite free-exhaustion
arguments and the finite-support-to-free-limit transfer are written out.

This addresses the arbitrary-vertex-transitive assertion in Abért's2010
Question24. That source does not print a normalization formula. G's full
automorphism group is nonunimodular; no negative result for the unimodular
subclass, another nonunimodular Betti invariant or the known Cayley case is
claimed. The square bound is an upper bound, not an exact expected value.

value sets without multiplicity. TheoremH on p503 assumes a nonconstant entire
f, k>=2, constants a,b with b!=0, CM sharing of a between f and f', and
f'=b implies f^(k)=b. The question replaces CM sharing by IM. There is no
printed a!=0 assumption in that theorem. Its conclusion is
f=d exp(cz)+((c-1)/c)a, c,d nonzero and c^(k-1)=1.

their derivatives*, Annales Polonici Mathematici95(2009),67–75,
DOI10.4064/ap95-1-5, Example1 p68, explicitly gives g=(B/4)z^2 in the
context of replacing CM sharing by IM. Root read its publisher PDF introduction

The example's extra predicate is g'=B implies g=B, a DIFFERENT predicate.

Set B=2b with b!=0, and check the actual target afresh:

    g=b z^2/2,  g'=b z,  g''=b,  a=0,  k=2.

The b-fiber of g' is the nonempty singleton{1}, and g'' is b everywhere. Thus
the target implication is true and nonvacuous. The conclusion at a=0 would be
a nonzero exponential, which is zero-free, whereas g(0)=0. Also b!=a. This is
exactly the PR's smallest obstruction at b=1. No predicate is imported from

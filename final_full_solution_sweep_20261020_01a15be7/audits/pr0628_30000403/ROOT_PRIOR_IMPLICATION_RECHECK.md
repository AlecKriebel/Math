## Independent geometric reconstruction

Let H=PGL2 over k and let F be the geometric orbit in the ordinary coarse
fiber. The quotient point is rational, so the orbit descends to k. Its
geometric stabilizer E is finite and faithful in H: an element stabilizing the
cover point acts on the distinct branch divisor, and one fixing three distinct
points is identity. The cover-point stabilizer can be strictly smaller than the
branch-divisor stabilizer. Use E, not the latter. Work with the reduced smooth
orbit, not an assumed scheme-theoretically reduced fiber. In characteristic
zero the relevant finite inertia is étale and tame.

The quotient gerbe G=[F/H] is geometrically BE. The associated family
U=[(F x P1)/H] over G is a projective-line family. Its coarse space Y is a
smooth projective genus-zero curve: geometrically it is P1/E. The complement
of the nontrivial stabilizers in P1 is a nonempty Galois-stable open, since E
acts faithfully and every nonidentity element has finitely many fixed points.
Over its quotient Y°, U has trivial inertia, hence coincides with the coarse
space Y°. This yields Y rationally mapped to G, with geometrically irreducible
genus-zero fibers.

A K-point of Y° is therefore an actual object of U(K). Such an object is an
H-torsor T with an equivariant map to F x P1. The P1 component is a rational
point of the associated conic T x^H P1. It splits that conic and therefore
trivializes T, by the usual H1(K,PGL2)/conic correspondence. A trivialization
then gives F(K). This is the essential fixed-split-line bridge; mere neutrality
of the residual gerbe, or descent to some twisted line, would be insufficient.
It does not choose a compatible actual marked-cover model and makes no claim
that a central cover-stack obstruction has disappeared.

Every nonempty open of a genus-zero curve over the infinite field k has a point
of degree at most2. For a split curve, rational points are dense. For a
nonsplit plane conic choose a k-line avoiding the finitely many excluded points
and tangencies; its intersection is a degree2 closed point. Thus F acquires a
point over a degree<=2 extension, uniformly for every finite E and all the
source's characteristic-zero fields.

If E is noncyclic, Bresciani2022 Proposition2 forces Y to split: a nonsplit
genus-zero curve mapping to a finite étale gerbe with genus-zero geometric
fibers can have only cyclic geometric band. Then Y° has a k-point, giving F(k).
This includes V4 and is stronger than its isolated improvement. The general
structure framework is Bresciani2023 Theorem2 and Lemma6; its hypotheses do
not require the original extra structure to be only a branch divisor. The
explicit quotient construction above checks the faithful-inertia hypothesis
for this particular coarse orbit.

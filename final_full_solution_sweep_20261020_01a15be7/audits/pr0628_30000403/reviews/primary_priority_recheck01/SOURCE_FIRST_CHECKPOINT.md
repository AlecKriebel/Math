## Independent checkable implication to pursue

For a reduced coarse Hurwitz point, take its finite-stabilizer PGL2 orbit and quotient gerbe R. The associated universal projective line over R has faithful geometric stabilizer E (the stabilizer of the cover point, not necessarily the full branch stabilizer). Its coarse total space should be a smooth genus-zero curve C/k, with rational map C -> R and geometric fiber P1. A degree <=2 extension splits C. A rational point in the open where coarse space and total stack agree gives a neutral gerbe together with a rational point on its descended projective line; the latter therefore splits. This yields an ordinary coarse Hurwitz point on fixed split P1. Prop.2 additionally forces C split when E is noncyclic, hence E=V4 implies m=1.

## Approach 1: Circle quotient with sheet-fixing isotropy

Question tested: can the free-circle quotient argument be replaced by local section neighborhoods of circle orbits, even when the circle action has finite stabilizers?

Retained argument:
1. A fixed-point-free Hessian-group element has three distinct projective eigenvalues. This is proved algebraically from the affine semidirect product and the weighted 3-cycle characteristic polynomial.
2. Every stabilizer of the circle H={diag(1,z,z)} is cyclic, and its generator has a repeated eigenvalue. Thus the entire stabilizer fixes a flex.
3. A stabilizer-fixed sheet yields a section on its circle orbit. Equivariant tubular neighborhoods and covering homotopy lifting extend the section to a saturated open neighborhood.
4. The 7-dimensional orbit space has an open refinement organized into eight colors, giving eight section domains upstairs.
5. Pullback to the cubic coefficient space, together with the Chen–Wan lower bound, yields genus 8.

## Complete review of the replacement argument

**Projected vertices.** For a finite-order affine isometry preserving the base alcove, averaging its iterates is orthogonal projection onto the fixed apartment. The average of the orbit of a vertex lies in the relative interior of its orbit face. Elements of H=W_aff^theta carry this face to another face of the ambient Coxeter complex and preserve ambient vertex types. If two projected images agree, their minimal faces agree. Each face contains at most one vertex of the distinguished type, so the original two vertex images agree. This proves injection on H*p, exactly the set needed for right-coset comparison; no injection on the whole apartment, fixedness of p, or theta-stability of K is assumed.

**Finite translation separation.** The theta-fixed coroot lattice has full rank in the direction space D, because the finite linear action is integral. Each distinct pair of required right cosets has a nonzero projected vertex difference. Its orthogonality condition is a proper hyperplane in D. Any open rational chamber cone contains an invariant lattice direction avoiding finitely many such hyperplanes after scaling. Equality of the two K-double cosets would force equal distances to p, since W_K fixes p. The squared-distance difference is an affine function of the large integer N with nonzero slope. Each pair excludes at most one value; a single sufficiently large N separates every required pair. Distance equality is used only as a necessary condition for coset equality. No claim that distance classifies cosets, nor an all-wall escape, is made.

# Independent review of the existing preprint's main theorem

I read all ten pages of Eric Hou's arXiv2607.20816v5, including the complete
existence proof. Theorem1.2's full cofinite/transcendental conclusion is valid.
This is a mathematical review of the body, not acceptance of metadata or Lean.

I checked the derivative series, uniform compact convergence, product-space
measurability, nonzero-coefficient event, Cauchy–Schwarz growth bound and coefficient
formula for order. Both saddle estimates follow from the written factorial and
binomial-series inequalities. The isolated saddle coefficient is genuinely
independent of its remainder; disk area gives the claimed small-ball estimate.
The circle-average tail uses layer-cake bounds on intersections with one event,
without spatial independence. The strict norm-mean gap is positive. On a zero-free
disk, harmonic mean value with a strict inner-radius margin forces a logarithmic
deficit of order sqrt(n). Its tail is summable. Compact minimum-modulus events are
measurable. Countable disks, Borel–Cantelli and deterministic subsequence
equivalence give one transcendental witness for every open set.

I found no main-theorem gap. The separate Boas–Reddy source diagnosis remains
unadopted because those original bodies were inaccessible. No Lean build or
journal acceptance is certified. The bounded-coefficient construction is distinct
from the Gaussian almost-sure statement verified for PR1308.

## Independent proof checks: product theorem and completeness

\[
 \Delta u=u_{xx}-(f_x/f)u_x+f^2u_{zz}+ff_zu_z.
\]

The sign and the Riemannian density are essential; changing to Lebesgue measure would change the problem. The distance to the singular set is |x| because any admissible curve pays at least its change in x and a horizontal segment attains it. \[
 \int m|w'|^2=\int m|w'-bw/t|^2+

\]

The cross-term derivative is ((mb/t)'=m(-ab+tb'-b)/t^2\); this checks the sign on negative as well as positive half-lines. \[
 \Delta W=[\phi''-(F_x/F)\phi']/z+

\]

Thus U,W and both Laplacians are in L2; the transition terms have compact support. Testing against arbitrary compact test functions puts U,W in the adjoint domain, with no invented boundary conditions. Divergence form and integration over z<Z give

\[
 \langle\Delta^*U,W\rangle-\langle U,\Delta^*W\rangle

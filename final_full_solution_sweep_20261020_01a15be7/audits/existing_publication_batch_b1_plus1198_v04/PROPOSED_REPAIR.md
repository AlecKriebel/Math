The proposed parser recognizes only explicitly labelled references or this repository's full PR URLs. A label is `PR` or `pull request`, with case normalization, arbitrary whitespace around the number/hash, and one or more whitespace characters between `pull` and `request`. The number may adjoin `PR` or `request`. One optional colon and then one optional `#` are accepted. A positive ASCII decimal token is consumed completely and compared by integer identity, including leading-zero spellings. The label cannot start inside a Unicode word; a following word character or `.digit` continuation prevents a partial numeric match. Thus `PR10070` identifies 10070, never 1007; `XPR1007`, `PR1007suffix`, fractional continuations and bare 1007 are excluded.

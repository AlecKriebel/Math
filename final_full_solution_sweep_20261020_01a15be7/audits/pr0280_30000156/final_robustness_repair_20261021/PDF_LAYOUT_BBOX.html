A ﬁxed birational map without a prime-ﬁeld cycle-distribution limit Alec Kriebel Independent researcher ORCID: 0009-0001-9320-500X Version 1.1 21 October 2026 (UTC) Abstract For the ﬁxed birational map L(u, v) = (u, (u 2 + 1)v), reduction at every prime p ≡ 3 (mod 4) permutes all of F 2 p . Its point-weighted distribution of periods divided by p has no limit. At every ﬁxed 1/2 ≤ x < 1, the distribution function is 1−φ(p−1)/(p−1)+o(1), with limsup 1 and liminf at most 23/24. Moreover, neither these distribution-function values nor the period measures converge on any further prime subset of positive upper relative density. The latter statement uses the classical continuous empirical law for φ(p − 1)/(p − 1). A supplementary proposition treats all odd primes on the invariant regular domain, and under uniform-point deletion or arbitrary exceptional-period completion. These conventions cannot restore convergence on any positive- upper-density prime selection. The contribution is a precisely delimited application to the broad existence assertion in the 2004 Oberwolfach problem on ﬁnite-ﬁeld maps. The triangular family, primitive-value estimates, totient mean and scalar distribution law are established ingredients; no new arithmetic theorem or historical ﬁrstness is claimed. 1 Statement and scope  Let  v .

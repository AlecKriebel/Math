Abstract
For the ﬁxed birational map L(u, v ) = ( u, (u2 + 1)v), reduction at every prime p ≡ 3 (mod 4)
permutes all of F2
p. Its point-weighted distribution of periods divided by p has no limit. At
every ﬁxed 1/ 2 ≤ x < 1, the distribution function is 1 − φ(p − 1)/ (p − 1) + o(1), with limsup 1
and liminf at most 23/ 24. Moreover, neither these distribution-function values nor the period
measures converge on any further prime subset of positive upper relative density. The latter
statement uses the classical continuous empirical law for φ(p − 1)/ (p − 1). The contribution is a
precisely delimited application to the broad existence assertion in the 2004 Oberwolfach problem
on ﬁnite-ﬁeld maps. The triangular family, primitive-value estimates, totient mean and scalar
distribution law are established ingredients; no new arithmetic theorem or historical ﬁrstness is
claimed.

## Other sources and failures

No core primary-source download or page inspection failed. Aivazidis-Sofos arXiv:1308.5701 was located by title/abstract but its body was not read or relied on; DH is enough for the scalar law. Kátai (1968) and Stephens (1969) were not independently retrieved, so claims of a direct full-text read of those originals would be false.

The search returned primary-body links for Roberts-Jogia-Vivaldi's 2003 Hasse-Weil paper and related finite-field dynamics. Search excerpts were considered only as leads; no conclusion of exact priority was drawn from excerpts, metadata or inaccessible material. No identified unavailable manuscript was used against novelty.

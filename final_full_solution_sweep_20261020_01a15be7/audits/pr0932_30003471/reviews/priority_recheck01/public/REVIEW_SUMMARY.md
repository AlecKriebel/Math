# Dihedral comparison: priority recheck

The exact theorem is already stated in Martin Winter's
[Stoker's theorem from polytopal tensegrities](https://martinwintermath.github.io/pdf/publications/Stokers_conjecture.pdf),
Theorem 1. For combinatorially equivalent bounded full-dimensional convex
polytopes, weak comparison of every corresponding interior dihedral angle
forces one orthogonal map between all corresponding unit facet normals.
This is equivalent to equality of their entire labeled normal Gram matrices.
The statement covers nonsimple vertices and allows different facet supports.

The polar argument is valid despite the rescaled target normals not generally
realizing the source polar. The earlier graph-embedding rigidity lemma allows
this, and a facewise positive-half-space homotopy establishes its required
origin condition. DERIVATION.md supplies the complete implication, including
the equality-to-orthogonality step. The original OWR 2017 p.1198 problem 4
asks only for one weakly compared corresponding edge, so it is a consequence.

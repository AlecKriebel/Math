## Genus two: independently reconstructed ordinary generation

View V_2 as a regular neighborhood of a theta graph and take its three edge co-core disks D_0,D_1,D_2. They are disjoint, nonseparating, and essential. Any two cut the handlebody to a ball. Their surface complements are two pairs of pants, so orientations can be chosen with integral meridian classes a,b,a+b. The first two give a basis of the full meridian lattice, not an index-two sublattice. Let t_i denote the three right disk twists. Disjointness implies that the t_i commute.

Let T_2 be the kernel of H_2→Out(F_2). Luft's closed-model theorem says that all meridian disk twists ordinarily generate T_2. The natural Out(F_2)→GL_2(Z) is an isomorphism, so H_2∩I_2⊂T_2. In the original Luft article, Theorem 2.2 and Corollaries 2.5–2.6 prove the unmarked outer-kernel statement; Theorem 3.2 and Corollary 3.2 independently identify the genus-two body-homology kernel with this twist group. Body homology and boundary homology are distinct here. The natural Nielsen comparison, not just an abstract isomorphism between two groups, is the required fact.

Order a symplectic boundary basis as (a,b,x,y). For a disk with boundary class pa+qb the twist has shear matrix U(Q_D), where

Q_D = [[p²,pq],[pq,q²]],  U(Q)=[[I,Q],[0,I]].

All these shears multiply additively. Thus χ:T_2→Sym_2(Z), recording Q, has kernel T_2∩I_2. The reference images Q_0,Q_1,Q_2 are an integral basis: their column determinant in coordinates (Q_11,Q_12,Q_22) is −1. For Q=[[r,s],[s,t]] set

σ(Q)=t_0^(r−s) t_1^(t−s) t_2^s.

Commutativity makes σ a homomorphism, and χσ=id. Therefore A=⟨t_0,t_1,t_2⟩≅Z³ and χ is injective on A. For every essential disk D define

B_D=T_∂D t_0^(pq−p²) t_1^(pq−q²) t_2^(−pq)=T_∂D σ(Q_D)^−1.

This gives χ(B_D)=0, independently of the orientation of the disk boundary. A common reversal of twist/shear sign also leaves the exponents and the conclusion valid. For separating D, p=q=0, and for each reference disk the word is the identity.

The critical generation point is not merely that each original twist can be corrected. Put W=⟨B_D⟩. Every s∈A fixes the meridian lattice pointwise, preserves the disk set, and commutes with σ(Q). Consequently s B_D s^−1=B_s(D). Thus A normalizes W, WA is a subgroup, and every original disk twist lies in WA. Luft gives T_2=WA. If f∈kerχ and f=ws, injectivity of χ|A forces s=1. Hence W=kerχ=H_2∩I_2. The standard closed equality I_2=K_2 then gives the second target as well.

This argument establishes a genuine split extension of the twist group using these commuting reference twists. It does not assume that some arbitrary handlebody-to-homology extension splits. It also does not use Pitsch's or Omori's g≥3 inputs in genus two.

## Higher genus: topology imports, explicit image, and full kernel

### Transfer and the first generating family

[Omori's Theorem 1.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06553v1) is a normal-generation theorem in the ambient disk-fixed handlebody group, for g≥3, by a particular genus-one homotopical bounding-pair map h. I read the proof through its Magnus, Luft–Torelli/CBP, and conjugacy steps, and inspected the figures. It is not a statement that a single map ordinarily generates HI or that all genus-one HBP maps form one orbit; Proposition 2.5 explicitly cautions against the latter shortcut.

The disk-fixed group surjects onto the closed handlebody group: isotope an extending representative near the boundary to fix the chosen disk, and extend that boundary isotopy through a collar. Capping identifies first homology, so the full preimage of closed HI is the disk-fixed HI. This proves that Omori's normal closure descends, and that

HI_g=⟨w h^ε w^−1 : w∈H_g, ε=±1⟩

is an ordinary generating family in the closed model. The ambient conjugating group is H_g. The closed seed remains a genuine bounding pair for g≥3; its degeneration in g=2 is why a separate argument was necessary.

Fix meridian classes a_i, dual b_i, and ω=Σa_i∧b_i. The standard bounding-pair formula, with a single fixed choice of twist order, gives τ(h)=[a_1∧b_1∧b_2]. Put

E=Λ³H_1(S_g;Z), Q={x∧ω:x∈H_1(S_g;Z)},
W=ker(E→Λ³(H_1/A)), J=E/Q.

The closed Johnson target is J, not the bordered E. Additivity, equivariance, and kerτ=K_g are established Johnson facts; Putman's actual kernel proof and the needed formulas were inspected.

### Ambient lifts are computable and do not hide a kernel oracle

Wajnryb's five closed-handlebody generators provide a finite signed alphabet X. The construction uses his generation conclusion, not every relation in the original presentation. The known corrections in Ishida–Sato's actual paper were checked, including their explicit repetition of the five-generator conclusion.

The integral homology image is the full stabilizer of the meridian lattice in Sp(2g,Z). With block matrix [[P,R],[0,P^−T]], the correct condition is that P^−1 R is symmetric; R itself need not be symmetric. Handle permutations and Nielsen handle slides give the GL action, and disk twists along e_i and e_i+e_j span the symmetric shear lattice integrally. Thus one obtains the pure permutation and pure elementary diagonal-block matrices as well. The required matrix lifts can alternatively be specified by first matching word in shortlex order over X^±. Matrix equality is an exact terminating test because the target matrix is in this known image. It is not a test for membership in HI or HK and does not assume an algorithm for decomposing an arbitrary HK element.

Let L_ij act by a_j↦a_j+a_i, b_i↦b_i−b_j and fix the other basis vectors. Let M_i send b_i↦b_i+a_i. Let D(i,j) be the specified permutation conjugate of h, so τD(i,j)=[a_i b_i b_j]. For any ambient f and z∈HI put Δ_f(z)=f z f^−1 z^−1. Its image is (Λ³ρ(f)−I)τ(z). No assertion that f itself is Torelli is required.

The PR's exhaustive repeated-index cases are essential and correct:

### Primitive quotient and the actual ordinary HK family

For x=a_i or b_i, R_x=x∧ω has g−1 terms with coefficients ±1. Each term has exactly one complete pair a_j,b_j and one extra vector x on a different handle. Hence the supports of the 2g relations R_x are pairwise disjoint. For ν(i)=i+1 cyclically, choose the coefficient-±1 pivot underlying x∧a_ν(i)∧b_ν(i). The pivots are distinct. Removing these pivots from the monomial basis of W gives an integral basis of W/Q. Explicitly,

red(v)=v−Σ_x (v[p_x]/ε_x)R_x,   ε_x=±1.

No division other than by a unit occurs; this proves primitivity and excludes a hidden 2-torsion or finite-index issue. The rank is C(2g,3)−C(g,3)−2g. It is 13 in genus three; using the bordered rank here would be wrong.

For each surviving monomial choose its Z/Y/F lift p_l, in fixed order, and define t_n=p_1^n_1⋯p_r^n_r. Its Johnson image is n and t_0=1. The p_l need not commute; t is only a set section. For every ambient word w and ε=±1 let s=w h^ε w^−1, and compute c from red(ερ(w)(a_1 b_1 b_2)). Then

G(n,w,ε)=t_n s t_(n+c)^−1,  n∈Z^r,

has image zero and lies in the full closed HK. Its indices are unrestricted words, signs, and integer vectors, not elements selected by a kernel test.

For f=s_1⋯s_m∈HK put n_0=0, n_j=Σ_(i≤j)c_i. Since τf=0, n_m=0. The product of the displayed generators with successive indices n_(j−1) cancels the adjacent t_n^−1 t_n factors exactly and equals f. Thus they ordinarily generate the entire kernel. Neither a prefix-closed transversal nor a homomorphic section is needed. This proves the actual target, not just an image lattice or the intersection of HK with the Luft kernel.

The optional normal-family version is also sound: kill all s t_c^−1 and all basis-lift commutators inside HI. The quotient is generated by commuting lifts, whose Johnson images form an integral basis. Its induced map to Z^r has a right inverse from those generators, so the quotient is exactly Z^r. This alternative still does not prove finite normal generation.

## Separate meridian obstruction and Holden

This result is separate from the generating-family proof. For g≥3 take IA automorphisms A:x_1↦x_2x_1x_2^−1 and B:x_2↦x_3x_2x_3^−1, fixing the other relevant basis elements. Their commutator fixes x_i for i≥2 and conjugates x_1 by

w=x_3 x_2^−1 x_3^−1 x_2.

For n≠0 its nth power is not inner: an inner automorphism fixing x_2 and x_3 has conjugator in ⟨x_2⟩∩⟨x_3⟩={1}, whereas w^n x_1 w^−n is a nontrivial reduced word of length 8|n|+1. The IA lifts can be chosen in the disk-fixed HI by Omori's surjectivity step, or by explicit Nielsen lifts with symmetric shear correction; capping gives the required outer actions. Their commutator lies in HK because τ is abelian. It therefore has infinite order in Out(F_g).

All meridian disk twists have trivial outer action. Thus the subgroup generated by separating meridian twists has infinite index in the full HK; the same finite-index-subgroup variant follows by taking a nonzero power lying in the subgroup. The actual [Holden v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07587v1), Definition/Conjecture 7.1, uses the full closed intersection, so that proposal conflicts with this obstruction in g≥3. Her Theorem D is conditional on a finite-index hypothesis; this is not a refutation of an unconditional theorem, nor evidence of any later correction. No such conditional theorem is an input to the main construction.

## Ingredient synthesis versus exact application

Conductor duality identifies the relevant degree(d−2) forms with sections of the normalization's dualizing sheaf twisted by2H. It does **not**, by that identity alone, establish a nonzero section. Likewise, normality of a surface makes its local rings Cohen–Macaulay but does not make its homogeneous section ring ACM; replacing the graded extension hypothesis in Eisenbud–Ulrich by surface normality would be a hidden assumption.

There are short routes to the required positivity from classical ingredients. In characteristic zero, adjoint regularity/vanishing and duality give it. In arbitrary characteristic, one must control irregularity without identifying tangent dimension with Picard dimension unconditionally; the special case `p_g=0` permits that identification, and Picard restriction or Albanese generation then supplies the needed genus bound. This explains why the candidate's characteristic-free replacement is substantive and why the checked char0 sources alone do not refute its contribution.

I found no pre-candidate paper stating the full exact application. A combination of old ingredients is not evidence that somebody previously published that application. The later publication, however, actually carries out the full characteristic-free adjoint-to-line-classification deduction, so there is current theorem-level overlap even if the candidate's resolution/Hodge/Albanese implementation is a different proof.

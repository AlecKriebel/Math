## Correctness and adversarial findings

The complete independent derivation is DERIVATION.md. Its essential checks are:

1. A connected image of rank at least two has an integral edge cochain of zero walk sum outside the gradient space. The dimension gap remains valid for homologically zero walks. No torsion, positivity, or surjectivity assumption is hidden.
2. The voltage graph is generally infinite and possibly disconnected, but its visited image is a finite connected graph. A failure to split any vertex would force the cochain to be a gradient. Visited vertex count strictly increases and is bounded by domain length. The PR does not assume rank decreases.
3. Local immersion gives minimum image degree two, including loops and bridges. Finite rank-one termination is a circle; its absolute covering degree is one for a nonperiodic input. This proves the central existence/termination step instead of transferring it to an unsupported theorem.
4. Over a fixed old edge coordinate, lifted height is k+t c(e), even for reverse traversals. Distinct lifted projections over the same base point differ by a nonzero integer. Bounded perturbation a+εb, 2ε||b||∞<1, separates all fibers; equal lifted projections are separated by b. Continuity and compactness finish descent. The final constant-height lift is legitimate.
5. A proper repetition cannot stack by a continuous difference of heights along a finite-order deck rotation and a telescoping sum. This direction does not need immersion.
6. The ababa path walk is a cyclic-itinerary nonpower by coprime directed counts 3 and 2, yet every continuous stacking would force disjoint interiors of same-leaf excursion height intervals. Its a-intervals separate two height blocks, and both b-intervals cross their gap. The contradiction covers arbitrary continuous heights. It is not an affine-only search artifact.

No fatal mathematical gap was found, and no proof repair was required. The ambient graph can be arbitrary because only the finite image is used. A one-edge oval positive control prevents incorrectly treating every backtrack as an obstruction. The empty/constant walk convention is not defined by the short source and is not silently included in the PR's explicitly nonempty immersed theorem. Free-basis primitivity is a different property from the nonpower criterion.

## Load-bearing repairs incorporated in the manuscript

1. **Paired-sector uniqueness.**  The manuscript proves \(C/T\)-scale
   equality separately for a marked component and for an unmarked component
   of degree at least three.  It displays the genuine bivalent stabilizer
   \((t,t^{-1})\) and excludes it graph-theoretically rather than assuming it
   away.  The \(K_4-e\) check is bound to the exact census of 25 acyclic
   rootings and zero tree-child rootings.
2. **Physical local product.**  Saturation is constructed around an interior
   serial split \((r,r),(s/r^2,g/r^2),(r,r)\).  The proof does not claim that
   every ambient normalized tensor is physical.
3. **Paired marginal openness.**  The section uses
   \(\max\{S,G,2S-G,0\}<r^{m-1}<1\); it is not an unsupported coordinatewise
   root assertion.
4. **Restoration implication.**  The full relation, omitted label, source
   insertion, target attachment, and transport are fixed before
   marginalization.  No abstract selected relation is lifted, and no target
   deletion map is inverted.
5. **Root movement.**  The proof includes the case where the suppressed old
   root lies on an arc entering a reticulation and checks both switchings.
6. **Probe completeness.**  One-port segment membership plus two-port order
   uses the completed exact ledger with parent-bound transports.  The
   independent replay reconstructs all 176 anchors, all 29,964 one-port rows,
   all 2,107 parents, and all 544,571 two-port rows; a one-port checkpoint was
   not used for promotion.
7. **Triangle context.**  The common rank-nine triangle germ is composed with
   an arbitrary external contraction \(\mathcal H:Q\times C\to Y\), and rank
   is checked at a generic maximal-rank context point.  The argument does not
   assume that an isolated submersion automatically stays full-dimensional
   after embedding.
8. **Genericity.**  The physical incidence correspondence is stratified, so a
   full-dimensional competing intersection yields the analytic physical
   target section required by the definition of containment.
9. **Reconstruction output.**  The output is the structural triangle quotient.
   The text expressly does not assert that a particular tensor lies in every
   redirected representative.
10. **Continuous-time gluing.**  Both sections are handled simultaneously by
    the interval in equation (11.2), rather than by a one-sided smallness
    argument.
11. **Sharpness dimension.**  The lower bound uses the four-observable local
    inverse; the upper bound uses factorization through the old tensor plus
    four new edge eigenvalues.  Hence each cherry adds exactly four, not only
    at least four.

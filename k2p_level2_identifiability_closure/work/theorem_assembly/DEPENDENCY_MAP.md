## Why the two hard ledgers are non-substitutable

The retained-class sweep cannot substitute for the raw ledger: it begins from
precomputed descriptor/rank pickles and does not prove that every raw graph,
incoming role, and port presentation was generated and correctly excluded or
retained.  A one-point nonzero Jacobian minor is a lower bound on image
dimension, not an upper bound suitable for exclusion.

The 36 direct obstructions cannot substitute for restoration: the current
manifests mark 997 canonical classes as restoration parents and encode 2,962
requests for direct five-port children.  None of those child records is in the
project.  The abstract statement "separating children block a restoration
path" has no force until the actual child, parent transport, and marginal link
are bound.

## Promotion rule

`K2P-SAME` is promotable only if every required gate is replayable and both
status ledgers have `complete=true` and `gap_count=0`.  In addition:

- the raw ledger must verify its partition identity, graph provenance,
  dimension upper bounds, and raw-to-canonical maps; and
- the restoration ledger must bind and close all 2,962 requests, replay every
  implication, and report zero incoherent or unresolved probe survivors.

Continuous-time and weak-class sharpness are separate theorem labels.  Their
failure does not change the logical truth of a principal-\(\mathcal D_+\)
classification, but neither may be advertised without its own gates.

The referee found no counterexample, theorem-level defect, census mismatch, or
failed production replay.  The mathematical result therefore remains
unchanged.  The HOLD is accepted as a release-evidence HOLD: several mutation

## Finding-by-finding disposition

1. **Parameter-transport mutation evidence — accepted.**  Four cases were
   counted as rejected solely from clean-versus-mutant row-hash inequality.
   That is tautological and does not exercise the complete certificate replay.
   The replacement must build disposable, coherently rebound full ledgers and
   certificates, invoke the untouched production verifier, and require the
   intended semantic or regenerated-byte diagnostic.  Byte inequality is not
   a rejection criterion.

2. **Canonicalizer wrapper — accepted.**  The reported missing-`networkx`
   control reproduces a false wrapper PASS.  The wrapper must run a clean
   baseline, require the exact case-specific diagnostic, reject tracebacks,
   import failures, timeouts, and signals, and ensure that no success artifact
   was emitted.

3. **Restoration and probe wrappers — accepted.**  The submitted mutants
   appear to have reached plausible semantic gates, but generic nonzero exit
   status is insufficient evidence.  Both wrappers and the outer release
   binder must enforce clean baselines, exact case-specific diagnostics,
   crash/timeout rejection, and absence of success output.

4. **Atlas-free all-family partition — recorded as a nonblocking evidence
   boundary.**  The report identifies no split, merge, omitted record, or
   counterexample.  The theorem uses convergent primitive regeneration,
   independent predicates, exact ledgers, and replay.  A new atlas-free global
   partition would be new research and is not required to repair the observed
   release defects.

5. **Stale C11/C12 reconstruction authority — accepted.**  The current
   article contains the correct retain-all-candidates and exact-membership
   reconstruction.  The historical promotion narrative must not be named as
   current reconstruction authority unless brought into exact agreement.  The
   crosswalk will designate the current article source and demote historical
   prose.

6. **C05 rank replay omission — accepted.**  The executable symbolic-rank
   verifier and `syzygy_upper.py` module are present but omitted from the
   reader crosswalk.  Both will be listed with exact roles and hashes.  In
   addition, a complete copied-project mutation must replace a symbolic upper
   certificate by sampled-point evidence and demonstrate rejection by the
   untouched production verifier.

7. **Optimized-mode documentation mismatch — accepted.**  The portable bundle
   checker is part of the distributed executable surface and will receive the
   same explicit optimized-Python guard as the other entry points.  This is a
   packaging contract repair, not a theorem change.

8. **Brits et al. version — accepted after checking the primary source.**
   arXiv version 3 is dated 25 August 2026.  Its Theorem 4.9 still supplies the
   level-1 JC/K2P/K3P identifiability result used here.  The narrower change to
   a separate arbitrary-level statement is not used by this paper.  The
   bibliography and supplement will cite version 3 precisely.

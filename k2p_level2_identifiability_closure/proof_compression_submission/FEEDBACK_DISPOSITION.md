# Disposition of the adversarial proof-compression review

## Verdict

Three independent audits found no new load-bearing mathematical defect in the
promoted principal-domain theorem.  The review is nevertheless substantially
correct about presentation: the 14-page promotion manuscript is a certified
release report, not yet a self-contained submission article.  Its recommended
compression phase is therefore accepted with corrections and a bounded
`PC-PARTIAL` target.

## Accepted as high priority

1. Measure the mathematical proof surface separately from hashing, release,
   and mutation infrastructure.
2. Derive the finite target counts and raw universes mathematically.
3. Replace record-level quadratic exposition by normalized formula tables.
4. Present the 36 higher-degree direct records by proved orbit families while
   retaining the exact record-to-transport ledger.
5. State the rank argument as its universal polynomial-vector-field mechanism
   plus the exact finite exceptional orbit residue.
6. Attempt one exact restoration-archetype quotient and retain the forest if a
   smaller theorem is not proved.
7. Expand the existing segment/order word theorem and make its finite anchor
   residue explicit.
8. Rewrite the paper with self-contained topology, K2P, bridge, localization,
   finite-universe, genericity, continuous-time, and sharpness sections.
9. Move detailed hashes and replay commands to a reader-oriented supplement.
10. Compare every compressed family assignment against the frozen classifier
    and run independent mutations.

## Corrected before use

- Every authoritative sign family is a direct whole-map
  `full_map_Ti_strict_sign` identity.  The historical rooted tree/sunlet oracle
  is revoked and cannot appear in a compressed classifier.
- Ordinary-triangle redirection proves a common physical regular germ; it is
  not a free certificate-body symmetry.
- Inheritance complements and pole or sink exchanges are used only when an
  exact direction-preserving mixed-graph and parameter transport is supplied.
- Probe completeness includes the ordinary-tree anchor as well as the five
  non-tree primitive kernels.
- The 997 restoration obligations do not currently have a certified small
  archetype quotient.  Finding one is new bounded analysis, not assumed input.
- The 36 direct residuals form three proposition-level families, but the
  frozen data retain 27 transported polynomial bodies.  Family-level
  exposition must not erase those transport obligations.

## Already mathematically established; exposition only

The feedback sometimes describes existing arguments as absent.  The frozen
proof already establishes root movement through a reticulation-incoming edge,
fixed-full restoration quantifiers, the complete two-sector bridge fibre and
physical local saturation, analytic target sections in the genericity proof,
continuous-time gluing, and both the lower and upper dimension bounds in the
weak-sharpness induction.  These arguments require fuller presentation, not
new theorem gates.

## Policy decisions

- Work remains on `main`; the reviewer-suggested branch is not adopted.
- The frozen release remains unchanged and authoritative until a new package
  independently passes old/new equivalence.
- `PC-FULL` is not a submission prerequisite.
- A DOI release is deferred until a genuinely final submission package exists.
- Corresponding email and code/data/manuscript licenses are human metadata
  choices and will not be invented by the compression program.

## Quantitative evidence supporting compression

- The 839 four-port quadratic terminal classes use 13 stored certificate
  payloads and only eight literal normalized polynomial bodies before further
  graph symmetry.
- The 96 theta2 quadratic classes use four literal bodies.
- The 54 cycle quadratic classes use six literal bodies.
- Restoration algebra uses six quadratic classes with five literal bodies and
  ten exact transports of the inherited quartic.
- The four-port rank layer has one universal mechanism for 3,515 descriptors
  and 75 certified exceptional orbit representatives covering the other 864.
- The large raw target counts follow one stars-and-bars formula from the core
  segment, sink, and repair data.

These reductions are large enough to justify one serious bounded attempt, but
not enough to claim a hand proof without the exact coverage computation.

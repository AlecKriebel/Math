table, and arbitrary-word induction are genuine proof compression.  The
restoration pass also finds 297 deterministic structural/outcome archetypes,
but these are descriptive fingerprints only.  No labelled cross-parent
transport theorem was proved, so they cannot replace the 997 exact parent
assignments.

The irreducible finite residue retained by this submission is therefore:

- 75 exceptional rank representatives and every direction-sensitive whole-map
  \(T_i\) row;
- 27 direction-specific high-degree polynomial bodies;
- 997 restoration assignments, 2,540 roots, 36,824 exact edges, and 16
  presentation-specific algebra transport classes; and
- 176 probe anchors, 29,964 one-port rows, 544,571 two-port rows, 67,741 exact
  transports, and 4,379 parent restrictions.

The compression verdict is **`PC-PARTIAL`**, while the mathematical closure
verdict of the frozen release remains **`K2P-SAME`**.

## Safety clauses

No step uses source--target reversal, treats an ordinary triangle as a
polynomial symmetry, replaces whole-map \(T_i\) by a rooted restriction, or
uses an uncertified inheritance, pole, sink, or core symmetry.  Algebraic
transports are used only where their exact coordinate and multihomogeneous
certificates are present.

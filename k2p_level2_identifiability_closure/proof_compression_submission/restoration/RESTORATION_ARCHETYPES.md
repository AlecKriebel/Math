# Restoration archetypes: bounded compression result

## Outcome

The corrected restoration forest admits a useful but deliberately limited
compression.  Its 997 canonical parents fall into **297 deterministic
structural/outcome archetypes**.  These archetypes compress the exposition of
the repeated child mechanisms.  They do **not** form a proved quotient of the
exact labelled parent--child transport forest.

Accordingly, the honest result of this bounded attempt is:

- 297 descriptive archetypes;
- four terminal proof mechanisms and one continuation mechanism;
- exact coverage of all 2,540 member roots and 36,824 forest edges; and
- an irreducible load-bearing residue consisting of the exact 997-parent
  assignment, every concrete transport, and all presentation-specific algebra
  certificates.

This is a `PC-PARTIAL` compression result.  It improves presentation without
changing the frozen theorem or its proof authority.

## Definition of an archetype

For a member root, the analyzer records the following fingerprint:

1. target kernel, incoming-boundary mode, repair, selected-sink mask, and the
   label-free occupancy word on every core segment;
2. the physical role being restored;
3. every source insertion site's core role and endpoints;
4. the ordered proof/status result for each insertion;
5. the exact stored algebra-certificate identifier whenever the separator is
   a quadratic or transported quartic; and
6. the complete relative second-restoration profile, if present.

A canonical parent fingerprint consists of its source kernel and repair,
together with the multiset of its member-root fingerprints.  Parents with the
same fingerprint form one descriptive archetype.  This conservative rule
produces 297 groups with parent multiplicities

## Why 297 is not a theorem-representative count

Matching fingerprints prove that parents have the same *recorded pattern* of
roles, sites, outcomes, and exact algebra templates.  They do not by
themselves construct a labelled mixed-graph isomorphism between the parents,
nor do they prove that one parent's concrete port and marginal transports can
be transported to every other member.

The bounded pass therefore does not discard any of the following:

- 997 canonical-parent assignments;
- 2,540 physical member presentations;
- 36,824 exact parent--child edges;
- source and target marginal transports on every edge; or
- 16 presentation-specific algebra transport certificate classes.

A smaller exact archetype theorem would require an additional, independently
checked cross-parent transport construction.  No such construction was found
or assumed here.  The residue is consequently declared irreducible for this
submission pass.

## Item-by-item disposition

1. **Stop proof compression — accepted.**  `PC-PARTIAL` remains the final
   compression status.  Literal equality of polynomial bodies is not promoted
   to a graph orbit, and the exact restoration and probe ledgers remain
   load-bearing.

2. **Topology attribution — accepted after checking the cited sources.**
   Huber et al. supply the two underlying simple strict level-2
   semi-directed generator shapes.  Englander et al. use those generators and
   supply the displayed-quartet/tree-of-blobs results reused here.  The four
   incoming-source event placements and fixed-port repair grammar are the
   directed ported refinement proved in this paper and used in the released JC
   companion.  The article and bibliography now say exactly this.  The local
   K2P/K3P collision draft is not cited because it has no verified public
   identifier.

3. **Triangle theorem invocation — accepted.**  Rank nine is a submersion
   statement, not invertibility of the full parameter map.  The proof now
   prints the exact (4\times4) and (5\times5) blocks, applies the analytic
   submersion theorem, and uses the constant-rank theorem plus an explicit
   section composition in context.

4. **Complete K2P marginal descriptor — accepted with safeguards.**  The new
   proposition records both Fourier-sector signatures, paired serial products,
   invisible parameters, exact coordinate transports, and parent complements
   only when a certified graph transport reverses the stored parent order.  It
   expressly does not introduce a free inheritance-complement quotient.

5. **Completion-count semantics — accepted; no recount.**  Primitive replay
   confirms that the formula counts repair-tagged directed descriptors.  The
   compiler distributes selected physical labels and then inserts a named
   zero-character completion role on a required empty repair segment.  Those
   roles do not consume selected labels.  Distinct repair witnesses remain
   distinct raw records until the complete ledger is classified.

6. **Expose the compressed algebra — accepted without a new search.**  A
   generated, sealed appendix prints the 8+4+6+5 quadratic bodies, the five
   stored higher-degree bases, coordinate dictionaries, coverage and transport
   scope, and three worked certificate paths.  The appendix distinguishes
   literal coordinate-body equality from the three separately certified
   directional high-degree proposition families.

7. **Name weak-sharpness rank columns — accepted as a derived crosswalk.**
   The frozen certificate did not store edge names, so it has not been edited.
   A separate producer and an independently implemented verifier reconstruct
   the edge-class order from the primitive graph descriptors, bind the frozen
   hashes, replay both exact determinants, and reject column reorderings.

8. **Generic-dimension sentence — accepted.**  The article now proves that a
   generic complex-rank minor is nonzero on the Euclidean-open physical domain
   and cites the standard semialgebraic-dimension/Zariski-closure fact.  This is
   a hardening of the existing argument, not a changed theorem premise.

9. **Reproducibility and metadata — accepted; human-only residue retained.**  The
   package already had field-rich machine crosswalks and source/PDF hash
   bindings, so the claim that they were absent was overstated.  The revision
   nevertheless prints the requested schema, producer, replay, mutation, and

   The clean detached full replay passed all 35 layers in 5,172.89 seconds,

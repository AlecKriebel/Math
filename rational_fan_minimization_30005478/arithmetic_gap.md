# Strict arithmetic gap in the fan-span minimization

Let W = H ⊕ E ⊕ F = Q^4 ⊕ Q^2 ⊕ Q, with H=X⊕Y and X=Y=Q^2. Set

- P1=X;
- P2=Y;
- P3=graph(I);
- P4=graph(T), T=[[0,2],[1,0]].

Take the six input subspaces H, E, and S_i=P_i⊕F (i=1,...,4). For any field extension K/Q, define

f(U)=2 max_V dim(U+V)−dim U,

where V runs over these six spaces. Then

min over rational U f(U)=7, whereas min over real U f(U)=6.

## Proof

Write s=dim U, a=dim(U∩H), b=dim(U∩E). The individual objective terms for H and E are 8+s−2a and 4+s−2b. Their sum is 12+2(s−a−b)≥12 because H∩E=0. Thus f(U)≥6 over every field.

If f(U)≤6, then a≥(s+2)/2 and b≥(s−2)/2. Since a+b≤s, both inequalities are equalities, s is even, and U=A⊕B with A⊂H, B⊂E, dim A=(s+2)/2 and dim B=(s−2)/2. In particular s∈{2,4,6} and U avoids F.

The condition for S_i is 6+s−2dim(A∩P_i)≤6, hence dim(A∩P_i)≥s/2.

For s=6 this is impossible since dim P_i=2. For s=4 it would force A to contain P1 and P2, whose sum is H of dimension4, whereas dim A=3. For s=2, A is a 2plane in H meeting every P_i in a line.

Meeting P1 and P2 forces A=L_X⊕L_Y. Meeting graph(I) identifies L_X and L_Y with a common line L⊂K². Meeting graph(T) then forces TL=L. This is impossible over Q, because T has characteristic polynomial t²−2; it is possible over R or Q(sqrt2), for example with L spanned by (sqrt2,1).

The resulting real A has f(A)=6. There is no rational U with f(U)≤6, and the full rational ambient W has f(W)=7, proving the claims.

## Explicit rational simplicial fan realization

Use standard basis e1,...,e7. Let H span e1,...,e4, E span e5,e6, and F span e7. Choose the following basis pairs (p_i,q_i) for P_i:

1. (e1,e2)
2. (e3,e4)
3. (e1+e3,e2+e4)
4. (e1+e4,e2+2e3).

For each i take the 3-dimensional simplicial cone generated by p_i+e7, q_i+e7, p_i+q_i+e7. It spans S_i, meets H only at0, and meets F only at0. Distinct P_i intersect only at0, so distinct S_i intersect in F; consequently these cones pairwise meet only at0.

For H take the cone generated by

(5,0,4,4), (4,1,4,4), (4,0,5,4), (4,0,4,5),

with trailing zero coordinates. These vectors are linearly independent. If a nonzero point is their nonnegative combination with coefficient sum t>0, then x1≥4t, x2≤t, y1≥4t, and y2≥4t. This excludes membership in X or Y, in graph(I) (y2>x2), or in graph(T) (y1>2x2). Hence this cone meets each S_i only at0. Finally take cone(e5,e6) for E. The six cones and all their faces form a finite rational pointed simplicial fan with the required maximal cone spans. Face spans cannot increase max_V dim(U+V), so they do not alter f.

This is an arithmetic gap, not an undecidability proof. It rigorously rules out replacing rational feasibility with real or algebraically closed feasibility when computing this minimum.

## Scope caveat

The explicit fan above is nonpure: its maximal cones have dimensions4,3,2. This is enough for the stated arbitrary rational fan problem, but does not establish the same arithmetic gap for pure fans, balanced fans, or fans arising from irreducible tropical varieties. Neither this example nor the reduction below proves impossibility, undecidability, or absence of an algorithm over Q.

## Exact reduction from rational Grassmann intersection feasibility

Input: rational subspaces P_i⊂H=Q^h with p_i=dim P_i, an integer0≤r≤h, and requirements dim(A∩P_i)≥k_i for an unknown rational r-dimensional A⊂H. Reject immediately if k_i>min(p_i,r); constraints below the automatic lower bound max(0,p_i+r−h) can be replaced by that bound or discarded. Thus assume max(0,p_i+r−h)≤k_i≤min(p_i,r).

Take W=H⊕E⊕F where dim E=dim F=h−r. Define t_i=h−p_i−r+k_i. The assumptions imply0≤t_i≤h−r. Choose a rational t_i-dimensional coordinate subspace F_i⊂F. Set V_i=P_i⊕F_i. Use H,E,F,V_1,...,V_m as input spaces and set threshold M=2h−r.

Claim: min over rational U⊂W f(U)≤M if and only if the original incidence instance has a rational solution.

Proof: Set s=dim U, a=dim(U∩H), b=dim(U∩E). The H and E inequalities at threshold M give 2a≥s+r and 2b≥s−r. Since a+b≤s, these force equality and U=A⊕B⊂H⊕E, a−b=r, s=2a−r. In particular U∩F=0. The F inequality is then 2(h−r)+s≤2h−r, so s≤r. Combined with a≥r and s=2a−r, it follows s=a=r,b=0, and U=A⊂H. Finally dim V_i=h−r+k_i, and its objective inequality becomes

2(h−r+k_i)+r−2dim(A∩P_i)≤2h−r,

which is exactly dim(A∩P_i)≥k_i. Conversely any feasible A has all three anchor objective terms equal M and satisfies every V_i inequality.

This reduction is constructive rational linear algebra. It shows that an exact general algorithm for the objective over Q would decide these rational incidence instances. It does not independently establish any undecidability result.

If actual fan input is required, first discard input spaces contained in another (their objective inequalities are redundant). For the remaining finite antichain of positive-dimensional rational subspaces, choose a rational ray in each outside every other subspace and a sufficiently narrow full-dimensional rational pointed simplicial cone around that ray in its span. The cones can be chosen to avoid all pairwise span intersections. Their pairwise intersections are then {0}, and adding all faces produces a rational simplicial fan with exactly the same objective. This argument makes no purity or balancing claim.

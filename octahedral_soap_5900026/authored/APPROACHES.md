# Five substantive approaches and stopping points

The count is five mathematical approaches, not five searches or tool calls. Both sublevels of the affine-dual investigation count together as one approach. Literature/readiness checks, coding, reruns, and independent audit are not added turns.

1. Primal polyhedral construction and parameter optimization. Derived A(a)=6√2 a+6√(6a²−4a+1), proved strict convexity, and located the unique family minimum a=1/6. This establishes an upper bound and a family result, not an unrestricted result.
2. Adjacency-restricted paired calibration. Constructed eight reflected-tetrahedron vectors and proved an exact global lower bound only for a specified adjacency graph. Ten omitted pair types violate the full-pair constraints.
3. Full-pair dual optimization. Solved all constant and all affine divergence-free paired-field classes by symmetry averaging and exact vertex norm constraints. The affine certificate improves the constant bound but still has a strict gap to the candidate.
4. Convex real-current relaxation. Constructed the half-density superposition of the two parity candidates, checked the incidence/trace constraints and exact mass, and separated it from ordinary films and unsupported mod-v identifications. This mixture gives no improvement and establishes no integrality gap.
5. Central rigidity/no-go argument. Used simultaneous calibration of the equal-area orientations to force two translated tetrahedra of central field values, then obtained the exact contradiction 4|d|²+6≤4. This rules out sharp continuous-at-center paired fields, while leaving singular fields and other proof methods open.

Final disposition: UNSOLVED; 5/5 approaches completed. The restricted theorem and explicit lower bounds are partial mathematical results subject to independent review. No external repository or queue changes were made by this work.

## 1. Explicit geometry and the nontrivial first central variety

On H, the second quadric cuts a smooth curve C: dependent gradients force all nonzero coordinates to have the same coefficient among 1,2,3,4, so at most one coordinate could be nonzero, contradicting the first quadratic equation. The class is (2,2). The exact sequence for O_H(-2,-2), whose H^1 vanishes by Kunneth, gives connectedness; adjunction gives genus one. Thus the stated example belongs to Miao-Wang family 2.23(a), without a genericity gap.

The blowup formula gives -K_V=3h-F. The ideal I_C(2) is generated by the second quadratic equation and u times all five homogeneous coordinates. Its graph realizes the blowup as a closed subvariety of Q times a projective space, and the restriction of O(1,1) is 3h-F. This proves ampleness. Since the center is smooth, V is smooth. The strict transform E is H because blowing up a Cartier divisor on H changes no underlying H.

The family t^2 u^2 + sum x_i^2=0, blown up along C times the base, is exactly the ordinary degeneration induced by ord_E after the coordinate change to Miao-Wang's model. Their actual Section 3.1 identifies this degeneration and Theorem 3.2 supplies the weighted K-polystability input. Near the center the ambient family is smooth and the two center parameters form a regular sequence; the two blowup charts are flat over the base and identify the central fiber with Bl_C Q_0. ## 3. Universal resultant proof of irrationality

If b^2=2, the second rewrite would leave 8+4b=0, impossible for b>0. If b were algebraic, its nonzero algebraic denominator b^2-2 would make exp(2b) algebraic. The classical Hermite-Lindemann theorem for a nonzero algebraic exponent contradicts this. Thus b is transcendental. This theorem is an explicit classical input, not a numerical test.

Assume a/b=p/q with arbitrary positive integers p,q and r=p/q. The draft's A(T,U), B(T,U) are polynomials in Q[T,U] of U degrees m=3p and n=2q, respectively. Substitution U=exp(b/q), T=b gives a common root, since 3p b/q=3a, 2p b/q=2a, and 2q b/q=2b.

For a direct proof of the leading coefficient, every determinant term of the Sylvester resultant contains n coefficients from A and m coefficients from B. Their T degrees are bounded by 3 and 2, so degree_T R <=3n+2m=6(p+q). The coefficient of this bound is the resultant of the leading T parts:

    Res_U(r^3 U^(3p), U^(2q)+3).

These polynomials retain their full U degrees and have no common root: the first has only U=0 and the second has value 3 there. The monomial resultant formula gives

    r^(6q) * 3^(3p),

which is nonzero for every p,q>0. Hence R has exactly degree 6(p+q), not merely a possible upper bound, and is a nonzero polynomial over Q. There is no common factor over Q(T), no cancellation of the highest determinant degree, no leading-coefficient root at infinity, and no coprimality restriction on p,q. A common finite root after T=b forces the Sylvester determinant R(b)=0, whether or not a specialized leading coefficient were to vanish. This contradicts transcendence of b. The irrationality proof is therefore complete and universal.

## 4. The valuation is on the function field and has rational rank two

Take a rational uniformizer h of E in C(V) and toric characters z,w on S with ord_D(z)=0 and ord_D(w)=1. For a Laurent polynomial over C(V), the displayed Gauss formula follows from the local monomial valuation at the generic point of E times D. With irrational a/b, a leading weight specifies a unique pair (ord_E(f_ij),j). For that pair, the leading residue is a nonzero Laurent polynomial in z over C(E); distinct z powers cannot cancel. Leading residues multiply in a domain, which proves multiplicativity and the extension to the fraction field C(V)(z,w)=C(X).

All nonzero rational-function values lie in aZ+bZ. Conversely h and w attain a and b. The exact group is therefore aZ+bZ, of Q-rank two by irrationality. This excludes every real divisorial valuation c ord_F, whose group has Q-rank one. The group is an Archimedean subgroup of R, so its ordered rank remains one. The draft makes the right distinction; irrational rescaling alone is not being used.

## 5. Associated graded ring and the real-filtration issue

Using Kunneth, an E-adapted basis of A_m tensored with toric character lines of B_m has raw weights ak+b(j+m). The residue calculation above prevents new cancellations. Separating the (k,j) parts and preserving multiplication identifies

    gr_v R(X) = (gr_ord_E R(V)) # R(S).

Miao-Wang's special ord_E degeneration identifies the first graded factor with the anticanonical section ring of Z. The Segre product is the complete anticanonical section ring of Z times S; in particular it is finitely generated, has the correct polarization, and its Proj is Z times S. Passing to a common Veronese does not change this Proj or the normalized weights.

It would be incorrect to prove finite generation of a literal Rees algebra as a C-algebra by claiming otherwise. The draft expressly avoids that claim. Its finite generation is the associated-graded notion stated exactly in BLXZ v2 Definition 2.2, and its finite real diagonal embedding is Han-Li's Geometric R-TC I immediately after Definition 2.8.

The exact locators are BLXZ arXiv:2103.15278v2, Section 2.1, Definition 2.2, printed/PDF p. 5; and Han-Li arXiv:2009.01010v1, Section 2.2, printed/PDF p. 8, immediately after Definition 2.8 and Remark 2.9, under “(Geometric R-TC I:)”. The geometric assertion has a concrete justification here. Choose finitely many homogeneous generators of the associated graded algebra and lifts in R(X). In each fixed total degree m the filtration has only finitely many jumps, since A_m and B_m are finite dimensional. Subtracting a polynomial in the lifted generators to kill a leading term strictly raises the value and must terminate in that degree. Thus the lifts generate the filtration in the finite weighted-embedding sense. After a common Veronese, this supplies an adapted finite anticanonical embedding. Equivalently, the ordinary E degeneration commutes with the toric S action, and adding the two real diagonal weight directions gives the required degeneration with the same central ring. Rational directions approximate it with the same underlying central variety. This does not require the dense positive cone to be finitely generated and does not claim any approximating rational direction is H-minimizing.

    (omega_Z+omega_S)^5/5!

because binomial(5,3)/5!=1/(3!2!). The Hamiltonian for the canonically lifted product action is the sum, and the canonical local volume density is the product. Hence the factor equations multiply exactly to the soliton equation for the required sum parameter.

## Disposition

**No verified complete prior example matching both parts of the source question was located in this bounded recent-source audit. Priority and continued openness remain unproved.** The original package already declines research-novelty claims, which is appropriate. Its literature note can be strengthened substantially by acknowledging the earlier group-compactification example below and by separating arXiv editions from journal editions.

The most serious adverse lead is **Li–Li, arXiv:2103.06439v6, Section 6, p. 36, case (2)**. It computes a nontrivial first global H-optimal degeneration of an explicit smooth Fano sixfold (a compactification of SO4(C)) using an interior two-coordinate minimizer. The inspected body does not prove that this vector lies on no rational ray. Thus I cannot classify its valuation as divisorial or nondivisorial from the located argument. It is incorrect to discard it merely because it is not the candidate's fivefold, and equally incorrect to call its two coordinates a proof of rational rank two. A later primary source certifying its irrational direction would defeat a broad first-example claim. No such certification was located here, and the subscription journal body was not obtained.

The strongest verified recent result closest to the candidate remains Miao–Wang's explicit factor degeneration, already stated in their **25 January 2024 v1**, not first in the 13 April 2026 revision. Both displayed H-minimizers in v1 and v2 are positive real multiples of prime-divisor valuations. The candidate adds a product and a proof of irrational *relative* weights; its claims must be attributed as an authored deduction from those inputs. This audit found no recent primary body already stating that exact product construction or its irrationality certificate. That sentence is a search result, not a theorem of novelty.

### Miao–Wang, Optimal Degenerations of K-unstable Fano threefolds

Bodies: [2401.13999v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.13999v1), 25 January 2024; pinned [v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2401.13999v2), 13 April 2026. Current arXiv history has two versions and describes v2 as adding Section 2.3 plus minor changes. Read introductions, Corollaries 1.4/1.8, relevant construction statements, and reference list; the product candidate is not an example asserted by these corollaries.

On pp. 2–3 in both editions, family 2.23(a) has H-minimizer `a0 ord_Htilde`, while family 2.23(b) has H-minimizer `b0 ord_Eo`, with positive scalars. Their central varieties are explicitly described and weighted K-polystable. In 2.23(b), the intermediate ord_H degeneration is weighted unstable (Proposition 1.6); the correct H-minimizer is the different divisor in Corollary 1.8. Thus this is also a warning against treating any displayed special degeneration as the first canonical H-degeneration.

Journal metadata: **Advances in Mathematics 503, Part A (October 2026), article 111194**, DOI [10.1016/j.aim.2026.111194](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0001870826004159). Crossref's record was created 20 August 2026 and gives print month October; this is not proof of the actual online-publication day. Elsevier's metadata API returns cover date 31 October 2026, an issue date later than this audit's local date. Do not relabel arXiv v2 as a journal PDF. The publisher preview agrees on the divisorial claims; the full journal body was unavailable. Crossmark marks the document current with no listed substantive update.

The v2 introduction points backwards to Li–Li, Tian–Zhu and Wang–Zhu for prior nontrivial examples; its reference list was actually inspected. Its broad scarcity statement is neither a nondivisorial-example theorem nor a proof of this problem's continued openness.

### Miao–Wang, Kähler-Ricci solitons on Fano threefolds with non-trivial moduli

Body: [2309.14212v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2309.14212v4), 22 April 2025. Read pp. 1–4 and searched the full body for irrational/nondivisorial/product-formula assertions. Theorem 1.1 gives KRS metrics for smooth threefold families 2.28 and 3.14; Theorem 1.3 describes weighted stability through cubic-curve GIT; Theorem 1.4 treats projective cones. The rank-one torus examples do not assert a nondivisorial first H-minimizer with changed underlying first central variety. Nontrivial moduli and a nonzero soliton field cannot substitute for that condition. Publication details and correction caveats are above. This is stronger than the native triage's bare journal label but does not establish the native triage's assertion of unresolved status.

### Wang, Generalized optimal degenerations of Fano varieties

Body: [2409.15718v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.15718v3), 12 October 2025; first submitted 24 September 2024. Read pp. 1–4, Section 5 (pp. 27–28) and references. Theorem 1.1 establishes a general H^g theory under monotonicity/log-convexity assumptions. Remark 1.2 identifies `g(x)=exp(x)` as ordinary H. Theorem 5.2 retains X itself for families 2.28 and 3.14. Theorem 5.3 retains the same 2.23(a) central variety for all allowed g; its proof explicitly reduces minimization to a positive scalar times `ord_H`. These are not prior certified higher-rational-rank ordinary-H examples. They materially broaden the factor's prior theorem coverage.

The official [Springer record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s42543-025-00112-3) gives first publication **8 January 2026**, DOI 10.1007/s42543-025-00112-3. A second journal host displays 2 February 2026; use the maintained Springer/Crossmark date and identify the discrepancy. Full journal body not obtained.

### Wang, A valuative criterion of K-polystability

Body: [2406.06176v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2406.06176v2), 12 October 2025; first submitted 10 June 2024. Read introduction and Section 5 examples (pp. 22–25). Theorem 1.8 and Examples 5.5/5.7 strengthen arbitrary-weight polystability for families 2.28, 3.14 and the 2.23(a) limit. Example 5.9 considers the torus-rank-two 2.23(b) *central variety*, but its KRS choice is still the one-coordinate `eta0` and cites the divisorial Miao–Wang corollary for the original variety's optimal degeneration. An automorphism torus of rank two is not a proof that the H-minimizing valuation on the original variety has rational rank two. The paper's general Theorem 6.1 about realizing higher-rank toric valuations does not single out the required minimizer.

### Delcroix, Weight sensitivity in K-stability of Fano varieties

Body: [2411.07864v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.07864v1), 12 November 2024; only arXiv version in the observed history (body also dated 13 November). Read pp. 1–7 and the explicit theorem/construction discussion. Theorems 1.2/1.4 give explicit non-isomorphic weighted optimal limits of quadrics and their blowups, including higher dimensions. On p. 5 the author explicitly notes that those manifolds are not ordinarily K-unstable, and that the weights do not satisfy the extra hypotheses in Wang's original conjecture. Theorem 1.3 is instead a log-pair example with boundary. These are serious neighboring constructions, but they do not by themselves give the ordinary global H example on a boundary-free Fano variety required here. Higher dimension alone is no reason to exclude them; the functional and first-step conditions are the actual distinctions.

### Li–Li, Equivariant R-test configurations and semistable limits of Q-Fano group compactifications

Body: [2103.06439v6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.06439v6), 1 June 2022 (first submitted 11 March 2021). Read Theorems 1.3/1.4, Section 5's minimization/semistability framework and Section 6, pp. 35–36. Journal: [Peking Mathematical Journal 6 (2023), 559–607](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42543-022-00054-0), online 7 November 2022. The publisher PDF endpoint returned subscription preview HTML, not a PDF; the final journal example text was not independently compared.

Theorem 1.3 identifies their equivariant minimizer with the global first semistable degeneration; Theorem 1.4 identifies the first central variety with the flow limit in two explicit cases. On p. 36, case (1) is `Lambda0=s(1,-1)`, hence has rational direction despite any irrational scalar. Case (2) uses the polygon cut out by `y>-x`, `x>y`, `x<2`, `y>-2`, `x-y<3`, `2x-y<5`, density `(x-y)^2(x+y)^2`, and a minimizer in the chamber interior, yielding a horospherical central variety. This is an explicit prior nontrivial first degeneration. The printed argument uses computer checks to locate the critical point/chamber; it neither gives an exact irrational-ratio proof nor asserts nondivisoriality. Interior membership excludes certain root walls, not all rational rays. Its exact unresolved audit gap is arithmetic rational rank, plus unavailable journal-edition comparison; it is not a verified rank-one example.

## What can and cannot be promoted

The recent audit supports accurate attribution of the factor to Miao–Wang (already 2024), supports the distinction between the candidate's irrational relative weights and an irrational scalar, and finds no verified recent source already supplying the exact product certificate. It also identifies a material prior near-example that the short original literature note omitted.

It does **not** establish that the source problem was still open in August/October 2026, that this is the earliest such example, that products are an unanticipated mechanism, or that Li–Li case (2) has rank one. It does not certify journal/arXiv identity where a journal body was inaccessible. No statement here changes the parent's mathematical disposition or grants whole-package clearance.

Suggested factual scope for the parent's own note: an explicit product construction with a proved nondivisorial first global H-minimizer, deduced from specified prior inputs; no research-priority claim. The literature discussion should mention the SO4 case (2) and its unverified arithmetic rank rather than saying all known explicit examples are divisorial. A continued-openness sentence should be deleted or expressly qualified as unverified dated triage. Parent retains the final disposition and authors any publication note.

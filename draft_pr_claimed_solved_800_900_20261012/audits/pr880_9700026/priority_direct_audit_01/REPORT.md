## Exact claim and falsification criteria

The candidate fixes finite `n>=2` distinct interior sites in the unit square and uses hard maximum cells for `z_i^α |y-x_i|^-β`, with positive probability-vector initial data and `z_i'=area(S_i)-z_i`. Its claim is global all-coordinate persistence when `β>2α`, including `α>1`; it does not claim convergence, uniqueness of a limiting equilibrium, stochastic consequences, or collapse in any region. An earlier exact all-`n` theorem with the same allocation/time law, or a checkable general theorem whose assumptions include this law, would defeat novelty of this theorem. An earlier explicit generic counterexample to Conjecture 32(a) defeats a first-counterexample claim but need not subsume the stronger theorem. Different arrival, site-founding, payoff-share, moving-site, or additive/power-diagram laws do not transfer without an exact map.

I read the frozen author proof, author source audit and metadata, and completed source-scope report to identify the claim. Earlier negative searches were not treated as evidence. Searches and primary reading were then independent of the other priority family; no other-family report was consulted.

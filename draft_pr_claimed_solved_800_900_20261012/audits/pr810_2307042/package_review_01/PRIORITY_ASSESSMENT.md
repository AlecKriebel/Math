# Fresh source and priority assessment

This review independently reopened and downloaded the versioned Hayman–Lingham source, the complete Herron, Shiga and Leutwiler publisher PDFs, and Bear–Smith final-title author manuscript. RETRIEVAL.json records exact measured identities. All downloaded primary sources, extracted texts and rendered source pages are reviewer working materials only, excluded from the intended public authored supplement.

The complete original Problem 7.42 and update were checked in fresh text and rendered printed pp.173–174. The disc example specifies equality along a pole-directed radius, then reformulates that property for each Martin kernel along a Green line issuing from the base. The note's explicitly qualified equality-along-line interpretation is supported. The typo in the normalization and repeated definition do not create the result. The theorem does not decide a separately weakened remote-contact question. The source update describes the editors' knowledge in 2018, and the paper correctly avoids treating it as worldwide 2026 openness certification.

Herron (1987) was read in full extracted text; the key Poisson ratio and annular extrema formulas were also checked in original printed pp.13–14. Theorem 4 gives the symmetric distance through maxima and minima of the boundary Poisson ratio. Theorem 5 computes distance for two antipodal interior points on an annular symmetry circle. It does not give the local gradient of a prescribed antipodal boundary kernel; confusing those antipodal objects would be a priority error, and the submitted paper does not do so. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kodaimath1978/10/1/10_1_9/_pdf/-char/en

Bear–Smith's eight-page, final-title author manuscript dated 23 May 2005 was checked against its actual title, definitions, Theorems 1.1/3.3 and proof mechanism. Its rigidity compares symmetric Harnack and hyperbolic distances; its existential normalized cone extrema do not imply a supporting direction for every prescribed kernel. Publisher metadata supports the 2006 journal citation, but this does not certify full typeset/manuscript identity. https://math.hawaii.edu/~wayne/mathpapers/harnack.pdf ; https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0022247X05005251

Shiga's primary PDF was checked at definitions/Martin representation, positive-harmonic distance and density equality theorems (3.1–3.4), and the complete Martin/Harnack section including Proposition 5.1. Its annulus bounded-harmonic invariant is a different function class. Supremum formulas over kernels decide existence of extrema for a pair of points, not contact for every prescribed kernel. No direct resolution of the chosen fixed-kernel contact claim was found in the inspected results. https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/jmath/68/1/68_441/_pdf/-char/en

Leutwiler's full primary OCR text was read. Its one-sided envelope (3.1), two symmetrizations (4.1)/(5.1), and directional differential metric (6.14) are particularly relevant context. They provide a framework but not the selected annular kernel's strict interiorness in a competitor gradient hull. No direct annular fixed-kernel contact result was identified there. Some scanned formula text is poorly extracted; no conclusion about an unreadable formula is used to certify historical absence. https://www.acadsci.fi/mathematica/Vol12/vol12pp003-017.pdf

The earlier dated audit's wider source ledger, including Köhn, Armitage, Kargar, Svetlik and publisher January2021 extras, was read as authored inspection history; those additional full sources were not all independently reinspected by this reviewer.

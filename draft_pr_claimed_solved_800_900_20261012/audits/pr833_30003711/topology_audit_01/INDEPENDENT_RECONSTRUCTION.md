# Independent reconstruction before prior-review exposure

This record was saved before opening any earlier independent_audit or second_review verdict. It uses the whole supplied author proof, the complete queue record, and the primary source statements encountered during direct source retrieval. It does not defer to prior reviewer conclusions.

Exact object: X=CP^9 minus the cubic discriminant, Y its nine-flex incidence cover. The target is the least number of open section domains, with disconnected domains allowed. The stronger minimum of connected domains with the whole cover trivial is a separate target.

1. Let P=PGL3(C), G=PU3, T=P1 minus the four singular Hesse parameters, identified with C minus {1,omega,omega^2}. The point at infinity is singular (xyz=0), not a missing smooth member. Every smooth plane cubic is projectively equivalent to a finite Hesse member. A projective equivalence of two smooth pencil members maps their common nine-flex set S to itself. The pencil is the projectivization of the vector space of cubics through S, so such an equivalence preserves the pencil. Thus every fiber of (g,lambda)->gF_lambda is one Gamma orbit for (g,lambda).gamma=(g gamma,gamma^{-1}lambda). Exceptional parameter stabilizers do not invalidate freeness: g gamma=g forces gamma=1. The induced bijection of real 18-manifolds is a homeomorphism by invariance of domain. The marked-flex version replaces Gamma by Gamma_0.

2. Right-unitary equivariance of polar projection is exact. If U(M)=M(M*M)^(-1/2), then U(cM)=(c/|c|)U(M) and U(MV)=U(M)V for V unitary. This descends to P and gives r(g gamma)=r(g)gamma. The maps [g,lambda]_{Gamma}->r(g)Gamma and [g,lambda]_{Gamma_0}->r(g)Gamma_0 yield a square of genuine covers. Above [g,lambda] the nine classes are indexed by gamma Gamma_0 and map to r(g)gamma Gamma_0; this is a bijection even at exceptional Hesse parameters. A fiberwise-bijective morphism of covers is an isomorphism onto the pullback. Hence the compact homogeneous cover E=G/Gamma_0->M=G/Gamma, dim_R M=8, controls the upper bound; no coarse cubic moduli quotient has been substituted.

3. Conditional interface: for H={[diag(1,z,z)]}, every finite subgroup of any conjugate of H is cyclic. A nonidentity element has one simple and one double eigenvalue, which is invariant under scalar representatives and conjugation. The author's spectral lemma would therefore supply a fixed sheet for a generator, hence a common sheet for the whole stabilizer. A statement merely giving individual fixed sheets for arbitrary finite groups would be insufficient. The separate algebra/group audit owns the full spectral proof.

4. At m=g Gamma the stabilizer is H intersect g Gamma g^{-1}; it is finite, so Hm is a compact embedded circle. Its action on the nine points is conjugate to Gamma intersect g^{-1}Hg acting on Gamma/Gamma_0. If e is stabilizer-fixed, H/L_m->E, hL_m->he, is continuous and defines an orbit section. An invariant tubular neighborhood W retracts to the orbit. For R(w,t) with R(w,0)=w and R(w,1)=rho(w), use F(w,t)=R(w,1-t), with initial lift s_m(rho(w)); the covering homotopy property gives a section at t=1. No quotient of E by H is assumed to be a cover. The section need not be equivariant. Isotropy jumps cannot obstruct this extension because it uses an ordinary covering over a neighborhood retracting onto the entire orbit.

5. B=H\G/Gamma is compact metrizable. H\G is a compact smooth real 7-manifold, with a smooth finite Gamma action by right multiplication; equivariant triangulation, followed by a no-inversions subdivision, yields a finite quotient polyhedron of dimension at most 7. The finite action need not be effective or free. This is not the false unrestricted assertion that arbitrary finite-to-one maps never increase dimension.

6. Given a finite open cover of B by saturated section neighborhoods, covering dimension <=7 supplies a finite open refinement of multiplicity <=8. A subordinate partition of unity maps B to its <=7-dimensional nerve. In its barycentric subdivision, color a vertex indexed by a simplex sigma by dim sigma. Equal-color open stars are disjoint because they cannot occur together in a strictly increasing chain. In the open star of b_sigma every original coordinate indexed by a vertex of sigma is positive. Thus each preimage lies in one original section neighborhood. The eight unions of disjoint open pieces have continuous sections, and cover M. Finite cardinality already eliminates any concern about infinitely accumulating pieces; even for an arbitrary disjoint open family continuity is local on the pieces. Empty colors may be padded by empty domains.

This mechanism proves g(Y/X)<=8 conditionally on the shared-fixed-sheet lemma, and, combined with the stated Chen-Wan lower bound in precisely this coefficient-space, section-based convention, proves g=8 (normalized 7). It says nothing about an eight-cover by connected full-nine-sheet trivialization domains or equality of branching complexity. Orbit monodromy may fix one sheet and move the others; such neighborhoods are deliberately permitted here.

8. An eight-domain cover yields a section of the eightfold fiberwise join using a subordinate partition of unity. Its fiber S^{*8} is 6-connected, has first reduced homology H_7=I^{tensor8} where I=ker(Z[S]->Z), rank 8^8, and the first obstruction lies in H^8 with the diagonal permutation monodromy on I^{tensor8}. A global section forces this primary obstruction to vanish. This does not show every arbitrary skeleton-dependent higher obstruction is zero.

# Independent reconstruction: degree-seven characteristic map

Saved before consulting any other audit or challenge, 2026-10-15T06:00:34Z.

## Exact claim under audit

Chen-Wan arXiv:2306.17303v2, Proposition 4.2(1), asserts that the characteristic map

`cl*: H^7(BK;F3) -> H^7(PU(3)/K;F3)`

is nonzero, for the projective subgroup K generated by the cyclic permutation A and diagonal B=diag(1,w,w^2). This is the lower-bound input cited in the frozen candidate PROOF.md. The claim is treated as a hypothesis. The journal text DOI 10.1090/proc/17184 has not been read, and no claim about its proof follows from this version-specific audit.

## Direct falsification by Poincare duality

Let H be the full inverse image of K in SU(3). The displayed A and B have determinant one, satisfy A^3=B^3=I and BA=w AB, and generate H together with wI. Every element is uniquely `w^c A^a B^b`, with a,b,c in F3. Therefore H has order 27, its center and derived group are `<wI>`, and H_ab is K=C3^2. There is a natural diffeomorphism

`M=PU(3)/K = SU(3)/H`.

As SU(3) is simply connected, pi_1(M)=H, and the characteristic map cl induces H -> H_ab=K. Consequently cl*:H^1(BK;F3)->H^1(M;F3) is an isomorphism.

Here is an explicit check that the product of the two resulting degree-one classes vanishes. In the above normal form, the group multiplication is

`(a,b,c)(a',b',c')=(a+a',b+b',c+c'+b a')`.

Let y(a,b,c)=a, y'(a,b,c)=b, t(a,b,c)=c. For inhomogeneous group cochains with trivial coefficients,

`(delta t)(g,h)=t(h)-t(gh)+t(g)=-b a' = -(y' cup y)(g,h)`.

Thus y' y=0 and hence y y'=0 in H^2(BH;F3), and in H^2(M;F3). Also y^2=y'^2=0 by graded commutativity over F3.

The ring of BK is `Lambda(y,y') tensor F3[x,x']`, where deg(y)=deg(y')=1 and deg(x)=deg(x')=2. Every degree-seven class is a sum `y P_3(x,x')+y' Q_3(x,x')`. Multiplication of its pullback by either element of the basis y,y' of H^1(M;F3) is zero. But M is a closed connected orientable real eight-manifold: it is the quotient of SU(3) by a finite right-translation group, and each right translation is isotopic to the identity. Poincare duality over F3 makes

`H^7(M;F3) x H^1(M;F3) -> H^8(M;F3)=F3`

nondegenerate. Therefore every pulled-back degree-seven class is zero.

**Conclusion: for these actual embeddings, cl*:H^7(BK;F3)->H^7(PU(3)/K;F3) is the zero map.** In particular the displayed class y x x'^2 cannot survive as a nonzero characteristic class. This is a falsification of Proposition 4.2(1) in the inspected preprint, not merely an objection to an omitted spectral-sequence justification.

## Initial spectral-sequence diagnosis

The pullback fibration U(3)->M->B(Ptilde) has E2=H*(B(Ptilde);F3) tensor Lambda(a1,a3,a5), with primary transgressions d2(a1)=c1, d4(a3)=c2, d6(a5)=c3. These statements alone do not prove that the edge kernel equals the ideal (c1,c2,c3). The c1=+-yy' zero divisor creates classes in row one; products with a3 create possible row-four sources for d5 into row zero. In degree seven this is exactly a potentially relevant differential.

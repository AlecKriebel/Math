# Working falsification of the Chen–Wan v2 ordinary-coefficient lower-bound map

Status: root derivation under independent adversarial verification. This challenges v2 Proposition4.2(1); it does not yet refute genus≥8 or assert anything about the inaccessible 2025 final proof.

Let K=<A,B>≅C3×C3 in PU3, M=PU3/K and c:M→BK classify the principal K-cover PU3→M. The preimage Ktilde in simply connected SU3 is generated by determinant-one A,B and central ωI, with exponent3, order27 and nontrivial commutator ωI. Thus π1(M)=Ktilde and its abelianization is K; c* is an isomorphism on H1(-;F3).

The extension C3→Ktilde→K has class ±yy′ in H2(BK;F3), where y,y′ are the degree-one basis. Indeed its restrictions to each coordinate cyclic subgroup split, removing the two Bockstein components, while its commutator is nontrivial. Inflation kills this class. Since c factors through B Ktilde, c*(yy′)=0. The same vanishing is implicit in v2's c1=yy′ and its claimed Chern-class edge kernel.

H*(BK;F3)=Λ(y,y′)⊗F3[x,x′], |y|=|y′|=1, |x|=|x′|=2. Every degree-seven element is yP3(x,x′)+y′Q3(x,x′). Its product with ay+by′ is yy′(aQ3−bP3), and therefore pulls back to zero on M.

M is a closed connected oriented eight-manifold: PU3 is connected oriented of dimension8 and finite right translations preserve orientation. Poincaré duality makes H1(M;F3)×H7(M;F3)→H8(M;F3) nondegenerate. Since c*H1(BK) is all H1(M), every element of c*H7(BK) pairs trivially with the entire H1 and hence is zero. This appears to contradict v2's explicit nonzero map in Proposition4.2(1), rather than merely a missed optimization.

Potential failure points to falsify: actual lift/presentation or abelianization; extension class and inflation; classifying-map factorization; orientation/dimension; H1 surjectivity; cup-product convention or local coefficients. If these stand, the v2 claim that the spectral-sequence edge kernel is exactly the Chern ideal cannot support its degree-seven conclusion; secondary differentials or an earlier ring relation must be examined. A different lower-bound argument, twisted coefficients or a corrected journal version could still establish the theorem. No such substitute is assumed.

Separate issue: v2 §5 calls Fubini–Study distance PGL3-invariant. That is not used by the proposed note, which excludes exact algorithmic complexity. It must not be confused with the topological map challenge above.

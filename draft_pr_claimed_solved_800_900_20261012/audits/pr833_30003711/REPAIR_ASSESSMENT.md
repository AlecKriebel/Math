# PR833: repair assessment for the lower-bound dependency

2026-10-15 UTC. Status: verification and reasonable repair assessment in progress;
no closure or publication decision is recorded by this file.

The exact frozen claim is ordinary unnormalized section genus **equal to 8**
for the nine-flex cover over the smooth cubic coefficient space. The independently
verified submitted construction establishes **at most 8**. The full equality
depends on Chen--Wan arXiv:2306.17303v2, Theorem4.1, whose proof invokes its
Proposition4.2(1). A direct structural proof, independently checked and supported
by exact cochains, shows that this proposition's constant-F3 degree-seven map is
zero. See `lower_bound_audit_01/REPORT.md` and `ROOT_LOWER_BOUND_REPLAY.json`.

The following repairs were investigated as verification of the existing claim,
without concealing a new central proof search or charging fictitious proof turns.
The original effort remains 1/5.

1. **Correct a convention, group lift, coefficient, or classifying map.** The
   lift in SU3 is the exponent-three Heisenberg group of order27; its
   abelianization is K. The exact cochain identity uses the actual determinant-one
   matrices. The inspected proposition expressly uses constant F3. Changing
   a right-action or cup-product sign leaves the vanishing argument intact.
   No convention correction repairs the proposition.
2. **Replace the single displayed detector by another constant-F3 degree-seven
   class for the same cover.** Blocked by the proof that the *entire* map in
   that degree is zero. Searching a different polynomial representative cannot
   fix this route. The map's target has dimension2, so this is not a vacuous
   target-cohomology argument.
3. **Correct the transcription of Leary's ring or Chern labels.** The independent
   lower-bound audit checked the actual primary ring, corrected the printed
   x/x-prime inconsistency, and identified Chern classes up to harmless sign.
   Its degree-seven Chern-ideal quotient still has the proposed residual class,
   whose image is nevertheless zero by duality. Thus a transcription correction
   alone does not justify the asserted edge-kernel equality. A second fresh
   audit is reconstructing this spectral-sequence route before an integrated
   disposition is made.
4. **Use the actual final journal proof or a documented replacement.** Standard
   public publisher, author, arXiv, reference and correction routes were checked
   by independent source and lower-bound auditors. The final article exists and
   has later 2024 revision dates, but its proof body was not obtained through
   ordinary access. A normal PDF route leads to institutional login; no access
   restriction was bypassed. No inspected primary replacement was recovered.
   This is a bounded access/search result, not a claim that the final article
   contains the preprint's error or that no correction exists.
5. **Treat a possible nonconstant-coefficient lower bound as a proof.** Blocked:
   a correct such argument could establish genus at least8, but no explicit
   module, nonzero class and pullback proof has been supplied or verified.
   Merely moving to twisted coefficients transfers the central unresolved
   lower-bound difficulty to another unsupported claim. Reopening this route
   requires materially new checkable evidence or an explicitly budgeted new
   proof attempt; it is not a routine repair completed by this audit.

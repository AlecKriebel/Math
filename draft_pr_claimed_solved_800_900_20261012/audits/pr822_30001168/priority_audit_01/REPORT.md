## Exact hypothesis and source gate

The target is Carlo Morpurgo's Conjecture 1, section 9 item (16), printed pages 421–422 in [*Low Eigenvalues of Laplace and Schrödinger Operators*, OWR 6 (2009), 355–428](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2009/06). On the unit round sphere, `Y=Delta+n(n-2)/4`, `Y_W=W^-1/2 Y W^-1/2` on round `L2`, with smooth strictly positive `W` satisfying `integral W^(n/2)=round volume`. The conjecture compares the maximum over **all** positive heat times of `t^(n/2)Tr exp(-tY_W)` against the round maximum, universally for `n>=3`.

The full supplied catalog record was read directly; its `open` label and dated literature triage are not source-authority proofs of current openness. The exact official target pages and the AIM version 6, April 2009 duplicate were inspected in the independent direct lane. The source observation that scaled traces initially increase in dimension 3 refutes a monotonicity claim but does not settle this maximum comparison. The solved critical half-line result belongs to item (15), not this question.

The author proof was independently read for mechanism and scope. Its fixed finite `L=10000` capped cylinder is smooth in the standard round conformal class of `S^3`; at normalized time `4a`, the accepted lower value is `>13/20`, while the accepted round expression is `<129/200` at every positive time. A fresh spectral-family audit strengthens the round bound to `<321/500`. These finite inequalities, rather than numerical sampling or a singular limiting metric, are the mathematical acceptance basis.

## The material classical-construction deduction

[Ammann–Jammes](https://doi.org/10.1017/CBO9780511863219.002) introduced the relevant Pinocchio conformal blowups before the 2009 report; arXiv first submission is 2007, the inspected author text is dated 8 December 2008, the chapter is 2011, and current arXiv v2 is 2015. The chapter title includes *first eigenvalues*; the earlier preprint title differs.

A chosen representative in the round sphere conformal class can be Euclidean near one point. A-J's inverse-radius annulus then becomes an exact unit `S^2` cylinder. Choosing a fixed smooth rescaled cap within their permitted construction makes its cap volume independent of length. Their condition `F_L<=1/r` alone is not a uniform cap-volume claim for every permitted choice; this specialization is explicitly required.

The cylinder Yamabe potential is `1/4`. Standard zero-extended longitudinal Dirichlet trial modes give `lambda_k<=1/4+(pi*k/ell)^2` for all indices, while `V_L=4pi*ell+O(1)`. Volume normalization and Gaussian integration yield normalized heat mass with limit inferior `sqrt(pi)/e`. The accepted strict round bound lies below that constant. A sufficiently long **finite smooth** member therefore violates the exact comparison without any fixed-index spectral convergence theorem. Details and quantifier caveats are checkable in `CLASSICAL_CONSTRUCTION_DEDUCTION.md` and the adversarial report.

This is an independently assembled corollary of an earlier published construction plus elementary additions; it is not evidence that A-J explicitly stated the Morpurgo refutation. The author's symmetric cap formula, finite length and rational certificate make the application explicit, but that difference does not justify claiming new conceptual geometry.

## Tested exclusions and blocked transfer routes

1. **Morpurgo trace/zeta ordering → time maximum:** the 2002 manuscript's relevant transform integrates `exp(-t lambda)` against a convex continuous function subject to integrability restrictions. A point mass at a specified time is outside that class, and maximizing over time is nonlinear. A sign for an inverse moment or regularized Mellin transform does not imply an integrand sign without a further valid order theorem. Reopening by merely saying “Mellin inversion” transfers the central gap and is blocked.
2. **CLR upper counts → sharp round maximum:** Frank 2014 Theorem 3.2 and Lemma 3.4 take local heat/spectral bounds as inputs to counting bounds. HKRV and Corso–Ried improve counting/weak-ideal constants. Reversing these implications, or identifying a different sharp constant with this round maximum, requires an unsupported additional theorem. Blocked as a direct resolution route.
3. **GGM sphere count examples → nonround target:** Frank's §4.6 reconstructs potentials mapping to constant sphere potentials. Under the target positive-weight normalization a constant weight is 1. Its higher-dimensional counterexamples concern another sharp-count conjecture and supply no nonround dimension-three heat witness.
4. **Fixed-index bubbling → strict heat excess:** fixed eigenvalue infima or convergence do not by themselves estimate `O(L)` modes. A disjoint round-bubble scaled trace is a volume-weighted sum of round values and cannot exceed the round maximum. Extra neck estimates are needed; a shared geometry keyword does not fill that gap.
5. **Other sphere conformal spectra → target:** Sire–Xu and Pérez-Ayala use a first-power weight normalization; current HPP nonround sphere minimizer claims impose, for Yamabe, `n>=7`. These are not dimension-three volume-normalized maximum results.
6. **Other Morpurgo heat conjectures/determinants → target:** Neumann heat-diagonal radial monotonicity on disks/balls, local `S^2` heat extrema, and local determinant/zeta extrema concern distinct operators, functionals or quantifiers. Available complete statements were separated from this question; the unavailable 1996 local-extremum text was screened only by metadata/abstract, not treated as theorem-level clearance.

The cylindrical **lower** heat-mass route is not blocked by items 2–4: explicit trial spaces and normalization supply its decisive estimate directly.

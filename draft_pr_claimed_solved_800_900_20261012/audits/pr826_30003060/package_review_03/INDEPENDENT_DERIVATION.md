# Derivation before prior verdicts

The unrestricted target record states iff; title/background emphasize the converse. The complete Solotar OWR contribution pp. 479–481 gives no field restriction. BLS original v1 and final §2 allow R=0 over any field and use weight as internal grading, without supercommutator signs. §5.1 imposes char≠2 for arbitrary derivations, while §5.2 explicitly removes this assumption for e_A. §6.4 assumes char0, and §6.5 adds H0=k and is motivated as a converse after discussing possible removal of the characteristic assumption. Unique intended scope is not determined.

For A=T(V), multiplication A⊗V→A is an injective free left map onto the augmentation ideal, yielding a linear resolution of k. Independently, the bimodule cut resolution is exact: in weight n each fixed word gives the incidence complex of a path with n edges and n+1 cut vertices, followed by coefficient sum; hence it is exact over every field. R=0 makes Wj=0 for j≥2. Coefficient-A differential in degree 1 is av−va, not multiplication. Fundamental LEFT cap has sign (+1) at q=p=1 and is va. On cycles the sum of va equals the sum of av; thus higher homology is kernel/cokernel of the natural invariants-to-coinvariants map for unsigned last-letter rotation.

For each distinct orbit of size d, invariants are k·sum(distinct words), and coinvariants are k·[w] (nonzero by coefficient sum). No averaging is needed. The natural map is multiplication by d, giving one kernel and cokernel exactly when char(k)=p divides d. At n=0 only H0=k; all higher ordinary groups in degrees≥2 vanish, so no incoming higher degree-one boundary. At each prime p, x^(p−1)y has p distinct rotations, yielding nonzero cycle z_p, cap p[w]=0, including p=2. Extension fields do not change the scalar test. Zero letters: A=k; one letter: singleton orbits and no positive-weight higher groups, including x^p. Repeated words may have d<n and must use d, not n. Composite residue rings are outside the field claim.

Loday–Quillen §5 Lemmas 5.1–5.2 give exactly the unsigned tensor-algebra two-term complex, and Lemma 5.3 gives B as the n-term norm in the opposite direction. Thus B([w])=(n/d)s_O, Euler cap(s_O)=d[w]; both composites are n. Goodwillie II.4.8 is the cap formula, and II.4.2 gives the commutator identity on homology. For primitive n=p, B is invertible in char p and Euler cap vanishes. The mechanism is a classical assembly; it is not an independently new mechanism.

The exact strongest theorem is the full orbit formula for finite-rank tensor algebras over every field, and its two-generator positive-characteristic counterexample to the unrestricted FORWARD implication, including the H0 conjunct version. It leaves the characteristic-zero and general vanishing-to-Koszulness converse unresolved. It is deduced algebraically and does not depend on finite checking. No novelty/firstness/current-openness certification follows.

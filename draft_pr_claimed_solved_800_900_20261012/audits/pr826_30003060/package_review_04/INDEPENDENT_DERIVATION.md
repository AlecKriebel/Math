# Independent derivation — before previous verdicts

Recorded 2026-10-15 UTC. Target read in full: literal field-unrestricted iff, with converse-emphasizing title and background. OWR printed pp.479–481 read in full. Canonical note read in full. Primary BLS definitions §§2.1–2.2, cap definition 4.1, §§5.1–5.3 and theorem/conjecture 6.4–6.5 consulted before prior reports.

Let A=T_k(V), R=0, k a field and V with finite letter basis. Zero is a permitted subspace of V⊗². W0=k, W1=V, and Wj=0 for j≥2. Left resolution 0→A⊗V→A→k→0 maps a⊗v↦av: each nonempty word has one last-letter decomposition, proving injectivity and image augmentation ideal, with generators of homological degree one in internal degree one. This proves usual left Koszulness. In BLS's bimodule convention, 0→A⊗V⊗A→A⊗A→A→0 has the difference map. In total word weight n each fixed word gives an interval incidence complex on its n+1 cut positions: n edges map to successive vertex differences, which are independent over every field and generate the multiplication kernel. Hence bimodule Koszulness independently follows without relying on equivalence conventions.

Coefficient-A ordinary complex is instead 0→A⊗V→A→0 with b(a⊗v)=av−va. No coefficient boundary enters degree one. BLS left cap on q=p=1 has sign (−1)^0 and value va; on a sum cycle, sum va=sum av. Thus higher δ is inclusion of invariants followed by coinvariant projection, not the norm map. No higher incoming differential exists because HK2=0.

Fix weight n>0, identify each a⊗v with the word av, and move final letter to front by ρ. Then b=1−ρ. On a DISTINCT cyclic orbit of size d, invariance forces all coefficients equal, giving line s=sum distinct words; quotient by neighboring differences is a line [w], nonzero by coefficient-sum functional. δ(s)=d[w]. This is a field computation valid without averaging or division: δ is invertible iff d≠0 in k. Therefore HKhi_1 and HKhi_0 have one line for each orbit whose cardinality is divisible by characteristic p. All higher homological degrees vanish, independent of weight; weight zero has k only in degree zero. For r=0, no positive words; r=1, only d=1, so no positive-weight higher groups in any characteristic. Composite rings are outside the asserted scope, and replacing 'divisible by characteristic' by 'zero scalar' is not enough to claim the same dimensions over them.

For r≥2, w=x^(p−1)y has exactly p distinct rotations by the position of its unique y. Splitting each rotation at its final letter yields p distinct basis tensors, a nonzero chain z. b(z)=0 by telescoping; cap image is p[w]=0. Neither ordinary nor higher incoming degree-one boundaries exist. This works in characteristic two (x⊗y+y⊗x), all primes, and every extension field, because independence and scalar-zero arguments are over the same field. Repeated word x^p has d=1, not p, hence is NOT a witness. More general repeated words have d dividing n; Euler scalar is d, not n.

Connes B, in the reverse direction on this two-term Hochschild model, sends [w] to sum of n rotations WITH repetition, namely (n/d)s. Thus δB=Bδ=n on respective one-dimensional orbit lines, agreeing with the Euler/Connes homotopy identity. For primitive weight p, d=p, Euler zero and B scalar one, so confusing the operators would reverse the conclusion. At repeated x^p, Euler one and B zero.

Conclusion: universal algebraic proof is sound and refutes only the unrestricted Koszul⇒higher-positive-vanishing direction (also when a degree-zero k conjunct is added). It settles neither characteristic-zero converse nor general vanishing⇒Koszulness. No numerical enumeration can replace this argument. Its mechanism is elementary assembly of classical tensor-algebra Hochschild calculations and fundamental Euler cap; this supports no claim of new mechanism or historical priority. This derivation is independent of all earlier review verdicts.

## Attacks on immediate prior implication

These checks deliberately go beyond searching for the exact theorem's wording. The detailed deductions appear in `INDEPENDENT_PRIORITY_MEMO.md`.

**Changing arbitrary factors to a hyperbolic source is possible but does not finish the priority attack.** One may lift each non-fixed inverse pair of alphabet elements to an infinite cyclic generator and each self-inverse letter to an order-two generator, preserving factor colors. This gives a virtually free group mapping onto the original free product with exactly the same constrained word distribution. What remains is a proper language restriction; it is not generally a word-metric annulus or a maximal component of the source group's geodesic combing. Thus “arbitrary factors need not be hyperbolic” is not, on its own, an adequate exclusion argument. Conversely, the lift alone does not establish property (T).

**Ashcroft's 2022 general-language results are strong antecedents, but their hypotheses must actually be proved.** I read [arXiv:2202.12318v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.12318v2), Theorems A-B, the deterministic Theorem 3.5 / Corollary 3.6, the maximal-component and Parry-measure setup, the weighted graph definitions, and Theorems 6.2 / 6.9. A primitive finite transition matrix alone does not invoke these probabilistic theorems. In particular, their positive vertex-weight graph distributions do not automatically accommodate the present factor-forbidden corners. Annular random relations do not imply the same assertion with only one fixed length retained: property (T) passes in the direction of adding relations. The exact all-residue link analysis therefore cannot simply be omitted.

**The standard free-group law is genuinely different.** For three infinite cyclic factors with radius-one alphabets, the cyclic syllable and ordinary free-group counts are respectively 4^l + 2(-2)^l and 5^l + 3 + 2(-1)^l. The former support has exponentially vanishing relative size. Conditioning a high-probability theorem on such a support is unsupported without a new estimate. The witness does not assert that every possible reduction is impossible; it refutes the unqualified distribution identification.

**Unequal alphabets and length types matter.** For valid part sizes (6,1,1), realized for example by the nonidentity alphabets of C7, C2 and C2, the Perron eigenvalue is 4 and the Perron walk has eigenvalue -3/4. Its tensor product with the unequal-length type eigenvalue -1/2 has positive eigenvalue 3/8; the resulting limiting gap is 5/8. More generally sizes (m,1,1) give gap 1/2 + 1/(2 lambda), approaching 1/2. One cannot import a universal 3/4 gap from a balanced-alphabet predecessor. This is consistent with, and explains the need for, the candidate's parameter-dependent margin.

**Replacement is not a novelty gap.** Occupancy plus density slack couples at least |S_l|^{d'} distinct uniform relators inside replacement samples for any fixed d' < d, with probability tending to one. A known same-support monotone quotient theorem above one third would transfer by taking 1/3 < d' < d. I have not used differing replacement conventions to reject such a hypothetical antecedent.

The [Bassino--Nicaud--Weil Markovian model](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.01088v2) is a real general-language precedent, including a free-product normal-form example. Its inspected main phase-transition theorem gives small cancellation / degeneration conclusions, not the needed property-(T) conclusion at one third. The Drutu--Mackay multipartite theorems inspected use balanced classes and do not, as written, identify the present weighted two-length link. Gromov's density construction and expander-labeled quotient constructions likewise supply context and mechanisms; no law-preserving extraction proving this target was located. Treating such an extraction as automatic would transfer the central link problem to an unsupported assertion. These routes are blocked **as uncompleted priority implications**, not disproved research programs.

# Independent target reconstruction (before PR proof / earlier audits)

The 2026 Oberwolfach problem asks for property (T) with overwhelming probability in the free-product density model for at least three factors and fixed density strictly greater than one third. This is not the unrestricted two-factor question. The actual model is on finitely many nontrivial finitely generated factors and a fixed positive integer ball radius in specified finite generating sets. Each factor contributes its nonidentity ball elements as distinct syllable symbols. Relators are rooted cyclically reduced normal-form syllable strings of exact integer length; both neighboring and wrap-around factors must differ. The sample consists of independent uniform words with replacement. The density exponent is the cardinality of that exact word set, with an immaterial integer rounding convention absent in the source.

The current arXiv v2, dated 28 September 2026, retains Question 1.9, but its formal scope includes two factors. This wider question has an immediate odd-length obstruction: its own Remark 1.3 states the relator universe is empty for odd lengths with two factors. No all-integer-length theorem for two factors should be inferred from the OWR question. At least three nonempty factor alphabets admit cyclic normal words at every sufficiently large integer length. Neither the boundary density one third nor a variable-radius, variable-factor, or growing-alphabet limit is included. A proof using only a multiple-of-three subsequence must add a valid all-length transfer, and a proof using unrooted/conjugacy or inverse classes needs a model comparison. Property (T) passing to a quotient does not alone identify random word distributions on quotient factors.

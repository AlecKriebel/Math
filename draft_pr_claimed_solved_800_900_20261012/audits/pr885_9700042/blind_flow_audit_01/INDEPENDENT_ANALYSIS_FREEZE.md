The two forced source-to-sink boundary edges contribute c_L+c_B. In the remaining face graph, an interface runs from the top-left face to bottom-right face; reflection gives a Q_(n-2) corner path. Outward directed crossings contribute primal capacity, inward crossings zero. Other interface components have nonnegative cost. Conversely a simple face path separates the boundary arcs and induces a directed cut with exactly that cost. Thus V_n=c_L+c_B+T_(n-2).

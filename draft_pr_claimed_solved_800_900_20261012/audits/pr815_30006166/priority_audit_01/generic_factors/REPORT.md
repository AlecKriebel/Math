## Primary references and exact scope

1. Michal Doucha, Julien Melleray, and Todor Tsankov, *Dense and comeager conjugacy classes in zero-dimensional dynamics*, [arXiv:2507.05474v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05474v1), submitted 7 July 2025, 42 pages; [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05474v2), 10 April 2026, 46 pages; [author PDF](https://math.univ-lyon1.fr/~tsankov/papers/comeager-cantor-actions.pdf) also inspected. Key statements were independently cross-checked; whole-file typesetting equivalence is not claimed. Proposition 3.2 (p.11 both versions) places **all** Cantor group actions in an admissible Boolean-algebra coding. Definition A.3 (v1 p.37; v2 p.41) specifies realization of finite extension constraints. Proposition A.7 (v1 p.38; v2 p.42) makes existentially closed models dense G_delta. Proposition 2.2 (p.6) perturbs extensions through finite clopen data. Corollary 3.9 (p.13) concerns profinite subspaces; Theorem 6.5 (v1 p.24; v2 pp.24–25) concerns minimal-pmp finitely generated free-group actions. Neither is an all-actions wreath theorem. Proposition 7.9 (v2 p.28) prevents transferring minimal genericity to all W-actions.

   **Specialization check, not a quoted theorem:** write the old fibers as `C_i` and the lift fibers as `D_j`. The finite Boolean formula requires a nonempty partition, `a(D_j)=D_j`, `t(D_j)=D_(j+1 mod n)`, and `C_i=union_(j=i mod m) D_j`. The frozen fiber product realizes this formula in an extension. Proposition A.7 realizes it internally on a dense G_delta. Successive factorial lifts then give the frozen profinite height. Finite generation is essential to this formula comparison; no new hyperfiniteness conclusion follows from it.

## Adversarial scope checks and remaining gap

- Having a finite factor differs from the original action factoring through a finite group. The source comparison concerns factors, not finite-order actions.
- A dense G_delta of actions with extension properties does not require a comeager conjugacy class. DMT Proposition A.7 applies without the additional atomicity conditions used later in Theorem A.9.
- A generic result inside a proper profinite/minimal/pmp subspace cannot establish a generic result in the full action space. W is finitely generated, amenable, and not locally finite; the minimal scope cannot be silently promoted.
- A hyperfinite factor alone does not make the extension orbit relation hyperfinite. The frozen slabs, compatible metric union, and entire exceptional preimage remain separate ingredients. Their novelty/correctness was not certified in this lane.
- Full-text scans of both DMT versions found no `hyperfinite` or `wreath` occurrence. The inspected generic-dynamics sources supply no direct exact-target resolution. This bounded finding does not establish worldwide current openness.

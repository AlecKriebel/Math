# Generative-AI provenance

## Scope of use

Generative-AI systems were used substantively in mathematical exploration and
derivation, adversarial proof analysis, symbolic-code generation and
debugging, literature support, verification design, and manuscript and
release preparation. OpenAI ChatGPT and Codex were the principal systems.
Anthropic Claude was used during a separate generative-AI review stage.

Exact backend model identifiers were not exposed or retained for every
session. The available records identify the product or interface, but they do
not support a complete version-by-version reconstruction; no unrecorded model
version is inferred here.

## Human direction and responsibility

Alec Kriebel selected the research problem, directed the iterative research
program, supplied source material and local computation, determined the
claims and publication scope, reviewed the resulting package, and accepts
responsibility for every released statement and artifact. Generative-AI
systems are not listed as authors.

## Verification boundary

AI output was not treated as evidence by itself. The release includes exact
rational and algebraic checks, separately organized verification scripts,
frozen hashes, a theorem-
to-artifact map, and adversarial manuscript audits. Those executable checks
verify finite identities and explicit constructions. They do not formally
verify the universal convexity, extremality, physical-reconstruction,
multiplier, or rank-exhaustion arguments; those remain manuscript proofs and
require expert mathematical scrutiny.

No claim of independent expert peer review is made.

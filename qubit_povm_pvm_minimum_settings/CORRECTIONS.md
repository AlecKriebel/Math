# Corrections and revision status

Version 1.2.0 is a bibliographic, positioning, disclosure, notation, and
submission-production revision. It does not alter any pre-existing theorem
hypothesis, proof mechanism, Bell functional, exact witness, certified bound,
or mathematical conclusion.

The revision adds a restrained comparison with related contemporaneous work
that was obtained independently and appeared after the public 1.1.0 deposit,
clarifies which exact claims overlap, and records the distinct two-input
equality and minimum-setting theorem more prominently. It also clarifies
notation, local physical reconstruction, literature context, and submission
presentation without changing a mathematical conclusion.

No known erratum applies to the theorem, proof, Bell coefficients, explicit
strategies, analytic PVM bound, or verification artifacts. If expert review
identifies a substantive issue, it will be handled in a separately preserved
version rather than by replacing a public file in place.

# PRA upload checklist

- [ ] Insert the new version-specific paper and software DOIs everywhere.
- [ ] Recheck PRA article type, section names, AI policy, and file limits.
- [ ] Confirm the final PDF and Supplemental Material PDF open and render.
- [ ] Confirm the uploaded manuscript is the one-column REVTeX 4.2f PRA PDF,
      not the archival article-layout PDF.
- [ ] Confirm `run_all.sh` and all manifests pass from the frozen archive.
- [ ] Confirm author name, email, Independent Researcher affiliation, and
      authenticated ORCID.
- [ ] Confirm the visible title block has no street, city, state, postal code,
      raw ORCID number, personal website, or version label; the email must
      appear through the corresponding-author footnote.
- [ ] Upload the author PDF as a Regular Article.
- [ ] Upload Supplemental Material as a separate file.
- [ ] Supply `supplemental_material_README.txt` as the Supplemental Material
      description if requested by the portal.
- [ ] Upload the verifier ZIP only if the portal accepts code; otherwise cite
      the Zenodo software DOI.
- [ ] Paste the exact Data Availability Statement and AI disclosure.
- [ ] Disclose the public Zenodo preprint and ZCW as related work.
- [ ] Confirm no simultaneous journal consideration.
- [ ] Privately screen every suggested referee and editor for conflicts.
- [ ] Do not describe the manuscript as peer reviewed or formally verified.
- [ ] Do not alter the July 30 Zenodo record in place.

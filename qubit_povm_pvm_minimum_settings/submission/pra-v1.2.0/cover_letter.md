# Cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the Regular Article “Minimum Bell-Setting Complexity for
Qubit POVM–PVM Separation” for publication in *Physical Review A*.

The central theorem proves that, for every bipartite Bell architecture with two
inputs per party and arbitrary finite, input-dependent outputs, the shared-
randomness convex hull of fixed-qubit POVM behaviors equals the corresponding
PVM hull. It also supplies an exact rational 3×2 separator, with an explicit
algebraic qubit-POVM strategy strictly above a global analytic bound for every
fixed-qubit PVM strategy. Together these results establish that 3×2, up to
party exchange, is the minimum input architecture for a linear fixed-qubit
POVM–PVM separation.

This is a natural fit for PRA’s Fundamental Concepts and Quantum Information
Science sections: it gives a sharp operational resource threshold for
generalized quantum measurements, while the proof develops exact convex and
Lorentz-geometric tools for dimension-bounded Bell correlations. The article
is self-contained. Its accompanying exact programs check the finite algebraic
certificate layers, and the manuscript explicitly identifies the universal
steps that remain mathematical rather than executable proofs.

Related contemporaneous work by Lin Zhu, Ranyiliu Chen, and Xin Wang
(arXiv:2608.01317v1) directly overlaps on an analytic arbitrary-state 3×2
fixed-qubit separation. The first public version of the present preprint was
deposited on Zenodo on 30 July 2026 under the version-specific DOI
10.5281/zenodo.21699161. This is public chronology only, and the manuscript
describes the works as independently obtained contemporaneous results. The
present-work-only result is
the universal two-input convexified equality and resulting minimum-setting
classification. ZCW additionally determine the unrestricted finite-
dimensional optimum of their different functional using an exact
noncommutative SOS certificate and provide a Lean 4 formalization of their
fixed-qubit theorem. The present paper claims neither of those additional

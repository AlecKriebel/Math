# Finite-certificate descriptions

## Exact 3×2 separation

The separation verifier checks the Bell coefficient tables, state and effect
constraints, the simple and strengthened attained values, discrimination-dual
factorizations, determinant and discriminant identities, robust scalar
inequalities, all six ternary-PVM rank patterns, and both strict exact gaps.

## Two-input closure algebra

The closure verifier checks the Lorentz metric and coefficient rays, the
quadratic null-ray map, its generic inverse, exceptional direct/cross
branches and resultants, one exact conformal-Lorentz regression instance,
the weighted Hessian square completion, coefficient-matrix independence, and
bounded-flow identities.

## Rank-zero simulator

The simulator gives and checks one exact deterministic decomposition for the
symmetric interior instance. Universality comes from the manuscript's
bounded-transportation interval proof, not from enumeration by this script.

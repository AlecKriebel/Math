# Verification boundary

The executable checks use exact rational and algebraic arithmetic and verify
the finite identities and constructions encoded in the package. They do not
formally prove compactness, nearest-point separation, the arbitrary-output
extremal reduction, one-binary-party circuit simulation, local physical
reconstruction, strict multiplier positivity, rank exhaustion, or the
universal equality theorem. Those are mathematical arguments in the main
article and must be assessed directly.

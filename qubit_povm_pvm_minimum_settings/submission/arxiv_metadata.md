Minimum Bell-Setting Complexity for Qubit POVM--PVM Separation

## Abstract

For every bipartite Bell architecture with two inputs per party and arbitrary
finite, input-dependent output alphabets, we prove that the shared-randomness
convex hull of fixed-qubit positive-operator-valued-measurement (POVM)
behaviors equals the corresponding convex hull of projection-valued
measurement (PVM) behaviors. Consequently, no two-input linear Bell
functional separates these convexified fixed-qubit models. We complement this
impossibility theorem with a rational \(3\times2\) Bell functional and an
exact qubit-POVM strategy whose value lies strictly above a global analytic
qubit-PVM upper bound. Thus, up to exchanging the parties, \(3\times2\) is the
minimum input architecture for a linear Bell-functional separation of these
shared-randomness-convexified fixed-qubit models. The equality proof combines
a one-binary-party
Lorentz-cone simulation, an extremal binary--ternary reduction, a physically
exact Lorentz incidence manifold, finite-POVM duality, and a rank trichotomy
based on the incidence second variation. The equality concerns convexified
behavior sets; it does not assert equality of raw nonconvex strategy images,
same-state simulation, or operator-level projective simulability.

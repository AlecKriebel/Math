## Description

This unrefereed preprint proves that, for every two-input-per-party bipartite
Bell architecture with arbitrary finite, input-dependent outputs, the shared-
randomness convex hull of fixed-qubit POVM behaviors equals the corresponding
PVM hull. It gives a distinct exact rational 3×2 separator and thereby proves
that 3×2, up to party exchange, is the minimum input architecture. The
equality is not raw-set equality, same-state simulation, or operator-level
POVM simulability.

Version 1.2.0 adds a restrained comparison with related contemporaneous work
by Zhu, Chen, and Wang (arXiv:2608.01317v1), whose construction was obtained
independently, and foregrounds the two-input closure theorem. No pre-existing
theorem statement, proof, Bell functional, exact witness, certified bound, or
conclusion changed.

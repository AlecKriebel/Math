# Journal cover letter

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript **“Minimum Bell-Setting Complexity for Qubit
POVM–PVM Separation.”**

The manuscript determines the minimum number of Bell inputs needed to
separate generalized from projective measurements at fixed local dimension
two. It proves that, for arbitrary finite declared outputs, every bipartite
two-input qubit POVM behavior belongs to the shared-randomness convex hull of
fixed-qubit PVM behaviors. It also supplies an explicit rational `3 x 2` Bell
functional with an exact algebraic POVM strategy strictly above a global
analytic qubit-PVM upper bound. Together these results establish `3 x 2` as
the minimum input architecture, up to party exchange.

The broad existence of qubit POVM advantages in `3 x 2` scenarios is prior
art and is not claimed as new. The contribution is the complete two-input
equality and resulting minimum-input classification, together with a
dependency-complete analytic proof and exact reproducibility package.

The proof combines a simultaneous Lorentz-cone circuit simulation, an
extreme-behavior reduction excluding four-outcome POVMs, and an exact Lorentz
incidence analysis whose rank trichotomy eliminates every residual strict
maximizer. Exact symbolic certificates and machine-readable strategy data are
included, while all convex-geometric and physical-completeness arguments are
presented explicitly in the manuscript.

Generative-AI systems were used substantively throughout exploration,
derivation, adversarial proof analysis, software work, literature support,
and manuscript preparation. This use is disclosed explicitly in the
manuscript and submission statements. I assume responsibility for the
submitted claims and do not represent the work as previously verified by
independent experts.

The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere.

## Description

This unreviewed preprint establishes a minimum-setting classification for
fixed-qubit POVM–PVM separation in bipartite Bell experiments. For every
two-input-per-party architecture with arbitrary finite, input-dependent output
alphabets, the shared-randomness convex hull of qubit POVM behaviors equals the
shared-randomness convex hull of fixed-qubit PVM behaviors. The paper also
gives an explicit rational \(3\times2\) Bell functional with an exact
qubit-POVM strategy whose value exceeds a global analytic qubit-PVM upper
bound. Consequently, \(3\times2\), up to exchanging the parties, is the
minimum input architecture for such a linear Bell-functional separation.

The equality concerns convexified behavior sets. It does not assert equality
of the raw nonconvex strategy images, same-state simulation, or operator-level
projective simulability of individual POVMs.

Generative-AI systems were used substantively throughout the mathematical
exploration, derivation, adversarial proof analysis, software development,
literature support, and manuscript preparation. The human author accepts
responsibility for all claims, citations, and released artifacts. At the time
of this version's initial release, the manuscript had not undergone
independent expert peer review.

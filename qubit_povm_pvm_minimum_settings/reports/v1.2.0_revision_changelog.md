## Mathematical content unchanged

- The fixed-qubit model, shared-randomness convexification, theorem
  hypotheses, Bell functional, exact POVM witnesses, analytic PVM bound,
  certified gaps, proof dependencies, and conclusions are unchanged.
- Neither displayed lower bound nor the PVM upper certificate is relabeled as
  an exact global optimum.
- The universal result remains convexified behavior-set equality; no raw-set,
  same-state, operator-level, unrestricted-dimensional, self-testing, or
  private-randomness claim was added.
- One prior combined residual-domain definition was split into Definitions
  5.1 and 5.2. Theorem 6.1 now displays normalization and steering-recovery
  equations already used by its proof. These are clarification changes, not a
  new hypothesis or proof mechanism.

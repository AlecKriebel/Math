## Narrow literature finding

The dated primary-source search found no earlier public source stating the
present arbitrary-output two-input convexified equality or the resulting
minimum-setting classification. Closest results have materially different
scopes:

- Gisin posed the fixed-dimension measurement question and noted the binary-
  outcome obstruction.
- Vértesi–Bene supplied the historical 3×2 architecture and a genuine ternary
  POVM advantage, with the unrestricted-state comparison for their displayed
  choice supported numerically.
- Oszmaniec and collaborators concern operator-level simulation of individual
  POVMs, not behavior-level equality after the state and full strategy may
  change.
- Panahi and collaborators prove an adjacent Hilbert-space dimension
  reduction when one party has two binary measurements, not projectivization
  of arbitrary-output measurements.
- Cerf–Ollivier develop a perturbative two-setting program but do not prove
  the universal closure theorem.
- Zhu–Chen–Wang independently prove a directly overlapping exact analytic
  3×2 separation and additional unrestricted-optimum/formal-certificate
  results, but explicitly do not settle the two-settings-per-party program.

ZCW version 1 uses a qualified “To our knowledge” first-analytic claim for
its arbitrary-two-qubit-state separation and contrasts its analytic global
argument with the fixed-state/numerical treatment in the earlier
V\'ertesi--Bene line. Recording that public wording does not convert the
chronology above into a claim about private development, dependence,
misconduct, correctness, or final mathematical priority.

## Defensible claim

> To our knowledge, the universal shared-randomness-convexified equality for
> arbitrary finite two-input architectures, and the resulting 3×2
> minimum-input classification, were not previously known.

Do not claim that the broad fixed-qubit POVM–PVM phenomenon, the 3×2
architecture, or an analytic arbitrary-state 3×2 separator is uniquely first.

## Mathematical-status boundary

The chronology audit is not a correctness audit. The manuscript and exact
checks support the stated theorem, but the work remains an unrefereed
preprint. The universal proof is a manuscript proof; executable artifacts
check finite exact identities and constructions rather than formally proving
the quantified closure theorem.

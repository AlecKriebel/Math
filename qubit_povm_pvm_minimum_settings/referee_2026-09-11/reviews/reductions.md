## Verdict for this proof family

I found no mathematical or semantic blocker in the reduction chain inspected. These files construct actual complex two-qubit states and complete labelled POVMs/PVMs, rather than inserting the desired simulation as a structure field. The hypotheses needed by the residual construction are proved from a strict extreme separator. In particular, purity, full Schmidt rank, nonzero effects, binary/ternary support counts, and invertibility of the local effect frames are not silently postulated at the assembly boundary.

This is nevertheless **not a line-by-line formalization of all mathematics in the manuscript**. Several manuscript lemmas are replaced with sufficient specialized results or a different proof. The distinction is material to the user's request, even though the identified coverage differences do not obstruct the final convex-hull equality.

## Exact manuscript-coverage qualifications

1. **General pointed-cone lemma is specialized.** The manuscript's `paper/main.tex:668` lemma quantifies over every pointed convex cone of dimension at most three and support-minimal positive relations. `Bell/ConeCircuits.lean:157`, `:229`, and `:319` instead prove support bounds for extreme points of a normalized finite balance polytope of future-null vectors in the specific four-dimensional Lorentz space, constrained to a subspace of dimension at most three. This specialization is enough for the binary-party reduction. It is not a formal theorem about arbitrary pointed cones, nor a formalization of the manuscript's terminating circuit-extraction algorithm.

2. **Standalone one-binary-party proof has a narrower interface.** The manuscript theorem at `paper/main.tex:703` starts with arbitrary binary POVMs. `Bell/BinaryParty.lean:83` starts with Bob's two actual PVMs, and `:157` handles extreme full-rank behaviors with two active outcomes on each Bob input. `Bell/Convexity.lean:48` only proves nonnegativity and normalization of the scalar spectral weights; it does not construct the spectral projectors, prove the operator decomposition, or take the product of both input decompositions. The manuscript theorem follows mathematically from the stronger final universal equality, and the assembly independently reaches the needed binary case via extremality. It would be inaccurate to describe `binary_bob_simulation` alone as the complete standalone binary-POVM theorem.

3. **General extremal-POVM rank-square bound is absent.** The manuscript's Appendix `paper/appendices.tex:200` proves the supported-Hermitian perturbation criterion and `sum rank(M_a)^2 <= 4` for arbitrary extremal qubit POVMs. `Bell/ExtremeMeasurement.lean:109` and `:138` instead derive active-effect independence and the implication “a full-rank effect forces a deterministic measurement” directly from extremality of the complete behavior and full Schmidt rank. These suffice to prove all nondeterministic effects have determinant zero (`:186`) and the two/three support reduction (`Bell/MeasurementSpans.lean:139`, `:152`). They are not a formal statement or proof of the general POVM extremality criterion or rank-square inequality.

4. **General semidefinite POVM duality is not formalized.** The manuscript Appendix at `paper/appendices.tex:220` establishes dual attainment and complementary slackness for arbitrary finite Hermitian score families. `Bell/Discrimination.lean:74`–`:123` checks one explicit discrimination dual certificate and its bound. In the main equality chain, `Bell/IncidenceScores.lean:110` deliberately obtains positive multipliers from deterministic replacement gaps, and its comment at `:109` explicitly states that no local-POVM duality theorem is used. Thus the general duality theorem and the manuscript's duality-based proof of multiplier positivity are not certified merely because the alternative final theorem is certified.

5. **Lorentz representation is sufficient but does not include every displayed conformal identity.** `Bell/ResidualEncoding.lean:215` defines the exact complex steering transform, proves injectivity at `:228`, and proves steered-frame invertibility at `:256`. `Bell/ResidualStrategy.lean:68` proves the full Born table from the frames, and `:87` builds all physical residual data. The inspected source does not state the manuscript identities `L_Xi^T J L_Xi = |det Xi|^2 J` and `P^T g^-1 P = 4 |det Xi|^2 h` from `paper/main.tex:1053`. Instead the proof uses steered null effects, congruence determinant preservation, and invertible frames directly. This is a sound alternative route for the incidences, but leaves those exact displayed identities outside the explicit formal theorem inventory.

6. **Common-span filtering is specialized to the required full-rank pure realization.** `Bell/CommonSpanFiltering.lean:50`–`:104` constructs valid normalized branch tables through an unnormalized assemblage, and `:107` gives their unequal positive mixture weights and derives scalar coefficients. The manuscript filtering lemma at `paper/main.tex:837` is stated for a general mixed density matrix, with explicit filtered density matrices. The formal proof establishes the necessary full-pure-state instance after the independently proved purification reduction. It does not literally formalize that lemma at its original full mixed-state generality or reproduce its exact branch-density formula.

## Adversarial checks and strongest verified source facts

- **Purification does not restrict the raw state class.** `Bell/SteeringRepresentation.lean:24`–`:82` constructs the steered assemblage of an arbitrary positive normalized four-by-four density matrix using its Gram rows, proves positivity/common sums, and verifies the complete Born table. `Bell/Purification.lean:109` uses the transpose of the inverse-root congruence as the new Bob effect; `:129` checks every probability against one common pure two-qubit state. This is the finite assemblage reconstruction argument and can change Bob's measurements. It does not claim to purify the original mixed density matrix on a second qubit while keeping the original measurements unchanged. `Bell/SteeringRepresentation.lean:105` uses the singular-product branch to justify invertibility whenever the behavior is outside the PVM hull.

- **Compactness concerns the actual model and ordinary finite mixtures.** `Bell/QuantumCompactness.lean:54`–`:100` proves both directions of the Gram-factor parameterization for arbitrary raw POVM and PVM strategies. The Gram constraints are polynomial and their factors bounded (`:135`, `:165`, `:173`); the raw strategy images are compact (`:193`, `:198`). `Bell/FiniteConvexCompactness.lean:59` bounds finite mixture size using Carathéodory, including empty sets, and `:101` proves compactness of the ordinary convex hull. Consequently the closed-hull argument used in the cone proof does not enlarge the target set to infinite or limiting mixtures.

- **The extreme maximizer is derived, not assumed globally.** `Bell/QuantumCompactness.lean:215` separates an actual counterexample from the closed PVM hull, maximizes over the compact POVM hull, chooses an extreme point of the exposed face, and uses the imported `extremePoints_convexHull_subset` theorem to recover one raw strategy. The local extremality hypotheses in the reduction files are therefore supplied at `Bell/Assembly.lean:41`–`:82`.

- **Common randomization acts on complete behaviors.** `Bell/ConeCircuits.lean:44` imposes one normalized nonnegative vector of weights with a balance constraint for both inputs. `Bell/CircuitRealization.lean:37` maps it linearly to the complete input/output table. The refinement/coarsening map at `:115`–`:149` is a physical `StrategyMap`. The circuit realization at `:193` handles singular reduced sums through product locality and invertible sums through explicit purification and projectivity. `:216` transfers the whole compact cone section into the actual PVM hull. No step replaces this by separate decompositions for individual input pairs.

- **Zero effects, repeated rays, and degeneracies remain covered.** `Bell/BinaryParty.lean:101` uses a subtype to remove only zero split pieces; `:49` proves exact regrouping at the original labels. Equal geometric vectors retain distinct finite indices. `Bell/ConeCircuits.lean:229` excludes a one-versus-many extreme balance using exposed null rays even when indexed rays repeat. Compression chooses a three-dimensional ambient hyperplane containing both PVM spans, so parallel, antiparallel and scalar observables do not require an independence assumption. `Bell/ConeCompression.lean:145` explicitly chooses a fallback spatial direction when the spatial radius vanishes.

- **Rank-one binary projectivity is an operator theorem.** `Bell/MeasurementGeometry.lean:123` proves both idempotence and mutual annihilation from positivity, null determinants and a sum equal to the identity. `:202` restores all declared labels and proves PVM structure when at most two nonzero null effects exist. A deterministic identity effect is not wrongly forced into this null-effect constructor; deterministic measurements are handled separately.

- **Residual encoding does not discard alphabet information or assume generic frames.** `Bell/SmallOutputEncoding.lean:120` proves exact coarsening after active-outcome enumeration and padding. `Bell/ResidualEncoding.lean:163` proves the complete behavior is recovered by a deterministic map, and `:181` derives frame invertibility from the 2+3 active counts, independence and the common-span bound. `Bell/ResidualStrategy.lean:133` supplies all nullness/nonzero/invertibility inputs and transfers the original Bell functional through that same complete-table map before invoking residual closure.

Accept these reductions as a substantive proof chain subject to the coordinating clean build and transitive axiom audit. Do not say it formalizes every theorem, lemma, proof, or displayed identity of the primary paper without adding the missing general statements above and auditing their correspondence.

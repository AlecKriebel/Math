# Proof boundary

## What is derived

The producer exhausts the primitive completion grammar for the distinguished
incoming presentation:

- one elementary rooted three-port tree;
- two minimum-repaired cycle sources against 289 selected-incoming plus 831
  marginalized-incoming three-port targets and all six port permutations;
- four minimum-repaired theta2 sources against 1,983 selected-incoming plus
  4,155 marginalized-incoming five-port targets and all 120 permutations;
- every fixed-full source insertion and target dummy-role promotion required
  by the cycle and theta2 parent grammars.

At every physical terminal it applies exact labelled semi-directed
mixed-graph isomorphism or the ordinary-triangle quotient.  The theta2
incoming boundary flag is retained: deleting a marginalized incoming role can
make an abstract selected mixed graph isomorphic, but the primitive anchor
grammar does not admit it as a theta2 fixed-full repair parent.

The separate verifier reconstructs the primitive cores, completions,
restrictions, mixed graphs, equality relations, and restoration paths from
literal code.  It does not import the producer, the K3P atlas, the historical
K2P atlas, or the frozen anchor/closure inputs.

## What is not used or claimed

No K2P polynomial, Fourier compiler, rank certificate, sign certificate,
quadratic separator, or model descriptor is active evidence here.  The
producer's topology filters are used only as necessary graph invariants before
an exact equality test; their non-equality categories are not promoted as K3P
algebraic separation claims.

This directory establishes completeness of the model-independent anchor-seed
starting universe conditional on that incoming-boundary convention.  It does
not replace the active K3P
algebraic separation, restoration, probe, or global-classification gates.
The frozen contract supplies the canonical serialization of the 43 four-port
rows; the existing exhaustive full four-port route independently verifies
every one of their locators and relations.  The strengthened final crosswalk
does not assume that those 43 rows exhaust raw rooted equality presentations:
it derives and transports every equality row in the exhaustive raw ledger as
described below.

The frozen 176-anchor contract, theta2 closure, and cycle physical-anchor list
are used only by the final crosswalk.  They cannot add a row to or remove a row
from the independently fixed 133-row non-four set.

## Four-port parent and descendant quotient

The final crosswalk imports only the literal graph portion of the independent
full-replay core.  It invokes the primitive source/completion grammar,
restriction, root suppression, labelled incidence isomorphism, and exact
mixed-graph relation.  It never invokes a Fourier descriptor, Jacobian rank,
sign oracle, separator, or K2P artifact.

The generating relation is explicit.  Two ordered four-port equality parents
are in the same presentation class when the source graphs are exactly
labelled, arrowhead-preserving semi-directed isomorphic and, independently,
the target graphs are so isomorphic.  The exhaustive ledger contains 144
equality presentations in 93 active map classes (30 raw/24 map classes
isomorphic and 114 raw/69 map classes ordinary-triangle).  Under the ordered
graph-pair relation they have nine classes.  Every class is represented among
the 26 direct contract rows; each of the 144 parents has two, three, or four
contract presentation hits, with multiplicities 44, 12, and 88 respectively.

For each of the 114 dummy parents the verifier then promotes every remaining
target dummy role and inserts the same new label on every nonroot source arc
not ending at a leaf.  Exact parent vertex transports carry each source site
to a unique mixed edge of its direct seed.  The target site is the unique
canonical child compatible with the parent transport.  This gives 1,260
first-restoration requests and 161 distinct existing one-port ledger rows,
with zero unmatched.  Their graph relations are 15 isomorphic, 24 triangle,
and 1,221 none; the ledger independently records the none cases as 1,080
displayed-quartet mismatches and 141 K3P tree/sunlet SOS separations.

There are 27 physical five-port equality terminals, in 15 ordered graph-pair
classes.  The 17 designated restored contract rows represent 11 of those
classes and 19 of the raw terminal presentations.  The eight remaining raw
presentations form four triangle classes.  They are not missing seeds: they
transport exactly to four existing triangle one-port descendants of the
direct seed `four:raw154873`, each twice:

- raw 202225 and 269761 map to `P1:four:raw154873:0:0`;
- raw 202231 and 269767 map to `P1:four:raw154873:5:6`;
- raw 202465 and 270001 map to `P1:four:raw154873:1:1`;

The other 12 equality children retain one dummy role.  Their 96 second
restorations transport to 64 existing two-port ledger rows, all with exact

and 12 by the K3P tree/sunlet SOS certificate.  Consequently all 1,356
fixed-full restoration requests are bound to active descendants and there are
zero uncovered obligations.  The 43 contract rows remain the designated

## Marginalized-incoming transport certificate

The separate implementation finds exactly 176 topology-compatible theta2
presentations (44 per source) whose selected mixed graphs become abstractly
isomorphic only after a marginalized incoming role is deleted.  Their dummy
multiplicity is 56 with one role, 88 with two roles, and 32 with three roles.
If one deliberately ignores the anchor-parent convention and promotes every
role while inserting every source leaf, these presentations have 424 fully
physical graph-isomorphic paths: 56 at depth one, 176 at depth two, and 192 at
depth three.  They are downstream incoming/root-movement one-port
presentations, not new rows in the bounded 133-anchor seed list.

The independent verifier certifies that statement path by path.  For every
one of the 424 fully physical paths it removes the restored label occupying
the target's distinguished incoming boundary, compacts the surviving labels,
and finds exactly one canonical graph-pair class among the 96 theta seeds.
The result is 56 transports to five-port seeds, 176 to six-port seeds, and 192
to seven-port seeds, with zero unmatched paths.  It also checks exact equality
at all 984 nonempty restoration prefixes and transports the removed one-port
attachment site onto an actual mixed edge on both sides of the canonical
seed.  The 424 explicit mapping rows and their canonical transport hashes are
stored in `INDEPENDENT_NON_FOUR_VERIFICATION.json`.

The terminal structural reconciliation then maps the 424 rows to 66 distinct
transported site pairs and verifies that every pair is already present as an
`isomorphic` row in `probes/one_port_ledger.jsonl.gz` and as a canonical
one-port relation class in `probes/two_port_parent_inventory.jsonl.gz`.  Its
full path crosswalk is stored in
`MARGINALIZED_THETA_ONE_PORT_RECONCILIATION.json`.

This certificate does not assert K3P algebraic separation.  It proves the
narrow combinatorial point needed here: the 424 cases are one-port descendants
of already enumerated theta seeds rather than omitted seed obligations.  No
K2P algebra is used.

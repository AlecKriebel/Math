## 2. Colored reduced-tree dichotomy

Assume that a source bridge split `S = A | B` is absent from the target.
Singleton sides cannot be lost because every leaf edge is a bridge, so both
colors occur at least twice.

In the target reduced bridge tree, let `H_A` and `H_B` be the convex hulls of
the two leaf-color classes.

- The hulls cannot be disjoint: an edge on the path between them would
  separate all `A` leaves from all `B` leaves and would make `S` a target
  bridge split.
- If their intersection contains an edge `R`, both colors occur on both
  sides of `R`.  Thus `R` crosses `S`.
- Otherwise two connected subtrees of a tree intersect in exactly one vertex
  `v`.  Every branch at `v` is monochromatic; a mixed branch would put its
  incident edge in both hulls.  Each color occupies at least two branches;
  one branch would make its incident edge realize `S`.

The crossing-edge alternative is impossible.  The already-proved direction
makes `R` a source bridge, while `S` is a source bridge by assumption.  Edge
splits of one bridge tree are compatible, but `R` and `S` have all four
intersections nonempty.

We are therefore in the unique-central-component alternative.  Its degree
is at least four, so it cannot be an ordinary binary trivalent component.
It is a nontrivial target blob.  The ordinary tree record in the 204-row
finite universe is a harmless exhaustive superset; it is not needed for this
central-blob handoff.

As a falsification check, the independent verifier exhausts every labelled
tree through seven vertices, every two-coloring of its leaves with at least
two leaves of each color (modulo color swap), and every noncut coloring.  It
finds 9,000 crossing-edge cases, 10,270 unique-central cases, and zero
counterexamples.

## 4. Side blobs really do compress to strict K3P arms

This is the gap that had to be repaired explicitly.

Fix one selected branch at the central blob and condition on its boundary
state.  Reticulations cannot straddle a bridge, so side choices are disjoint
from central choices and from choices in other branches.  After all other
side leaves are marginalized, every displayed switching contributes the K3P
transition matrix along its unique boundary-to-selected-leaf path; off-path
row-stochastic factors sum to one.  The switching weights are independent of
states, strictly positive, and sum to one.  Thus the arm kernel is

```
K_eff = sum_omega w_omega K_path(omega).
```

For `G = Z2 x Z2`, a strict K3P edge has spectrum `(1,c,g,t)` and a strictly
positive inverse-Fourier probability vector.  Serial composition convolves
those probability vectors and multiplies the three nontrivial spectra.
Consequently every nonempty displayed path is strict K3P.  Convex mixing is
linear in probability coordinates and in the common Hadamard eigenbasis, so
all four probabilities remain strictly positive and each of `c,g,t` remains
strictly between zero and one.  Hence `K_eff` lies in strict `D3,+`.

The verifier checks the 16 Hadamard orthogonality identities, all 64 character
homomorphism identities, exact rational serial and mixture examples, and the
switching-weight polynomial identity for 0, 1, and 2 reticulations (seven
switching terms total).  The general strictness is the elementary closure
argument above, not an inference from the examples.

Multiple arms factor conditionally over their distinct bridge boundaries.
Their effective triples need not vary independently: the 204 local theorem
is pointwise for every strict tuple, so any correlations inherited from the
full target section are irrelevant.

Central inheritance probabilities are retained as `lambda`, complemented to
`1-lambda` under parent-order transport, or cancel when two switchings become
identical after marginalization.  Side inheritances occur only inside the
positive arm mixture.  Every retained value remains in `(0,1)`.

## 5. Pointwise rank contradiction

Marginalizing the original containment identity to the four actual taxa sets
all omitted Fourier characters to zero.  It is a direct linear identity for
every source point in the contained germ and requires no target-open marginal
image.

On the source, `S` is a bridge.  Its four character blocks have rank one, so
the selected quartet flattening has rank at most four.  On the target, the
central factor and strict effective arms give one of the 204 normalized
wrong-split directions.  The aggregate K3P certificate is pointwise on the
entire strict principal domain, so the same flattening has rank at least five.
This is impossible.  Therefore no source bridge can be lost, giving the
reverse inclusion and hence equality of cut sets.

## 6. Circularity audit

The argument uses only the target reduced bridge tree as an object attached
to `Nprime`; it never assumes that it equals the source bridge tree.  It does
not invoke corresponding source/target factors, the bridge-fibre localization
theorem, restoration, probes, or the fourteen-orbit classification.  The
target parameter section need not be open or regular because the local
wrong-split certificate is pointwise.

No universal pointwise K3P cut theorem is used or claimed.

The mutation suite coherently reseals and then rejects replacement of the
K3P displayed-tree premise by the legacy report, deletion of the exact minor,
and deletion of the `K0 -> D1` proof dependency.  These checks prevent a
hash-consistent regression to the former parallel-only evidence graph.

Dear Editors,

Please consider “Triangle Hypersurfaces and a Sharp Identifiability Boundary
for Level-2 K3P Networks” as a Research Article in *Systematic Biology*.

The article gives a necessary-and-sufficient classification of regular
full-dimensional stochastic containment for binary standard semi-directed
strongly tree-child level-2 phylogenetic networks under the Kimura
three-parameter model. It proves generic structural identifiability and exact
reconstruction modulo ordinary triangle redirection on both the principal
positive domain and the strict continuous-time domain. The K3P result is not a
formal extension of reduced-symmetry models: ordinary triangle orientations
have rank 14 and share an irreducible quartic hypersurface, while a certified
weak-not-strong construction shows that the strong tree-child hypothesis is
sharp.

The work is relevant to a broad systematics audience because it identifies an
exact information-theoretic boundary for reticulate evolutionary models and
separates robust stochastic ambiguity from singular or boundary collisions.
The main article states the biological interpretation and limitations; a
reader supplement maps every computer-assisted step to exact certificates,
independent replays, and fail-closed mutation tests.

No empirical biological dataset was used. Before submission, the exact code,

Please consider “Triangle Hypersurfaces and a Sharp Identifiability Boundary
for Level-2 K3P Networks” for publication in the *Journal of Mathematical
Biology*.

This article gives a necessary-and-sufficient classification of regular
full-dimensional stochastic containment for binary standard semi-directed
strongly tree-child level-2 phylogenetic networks under the Kimura
three-parameter model. It proves generic structural identifiability and exact
reconstruction modulo ordinary triangle redirection on the principal positive
and strict continuous-time domains. The local K3P geometry is genuinely new:
the three ordinary triangle orientations have rank 14 and share an
irreducible quartic hypersurface. A rigorous interval certificate also shows
that strong tree-childness is sharp by constructing full-dimensional
ambiguity immediately in the weak-not-strong class.

The paper links algebraic statistics, real algebraic geometry, graph
structure, and evolutionary modelling. A dedicated biological-interpretation
section explains the infinite-data meaning of the theorem, why independent
K3P substitution sectors matter, and why the result is not yet a
finite-sample inference guarantee. Online Resource 1 gives a reader-facing
crosswalk from every computer-assisted claim to exact certificates,
independent implementations, interval bounds, and mutation tests.

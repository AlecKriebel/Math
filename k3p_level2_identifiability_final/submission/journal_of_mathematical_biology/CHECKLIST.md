# Journal of Mathematical Biology submission checklist

Official instructions:
<https://link.springer.com/journal/285/submission-guidelines>
(re-checked 2026-08-25).

- [x] Named, single-blind title page includes author, affiliation, ORCID, and
  explicit contact placeholders.
- [x] Journal abstract is between 150 and 250 words.
- [x] Six keywords are supplied (allowed range: four to six).
- [x] MSC 2020 codes are supplied.
- [x] The article contains a biological-interpretation and limitation section.
- [x] Statements and Declarations includes funding, competing interests,
  author contributions, data availability, code availability, ethics, and AI.
- [x] Online Resource 1 is cited and its expected filename is `ESM_1.pdf`.
- [x] The source manifest includes editable article and supplement sources.
- [ ] Resolve every `@@TOKEN@@` from author-confirmed information.
- [x] Active corresponding email is bound to `me@aleckriebel.com` from the
  established local publication metadata.
- [ ] Supply the corresponding city/country.
- [ ] Confirm funding, competing interests, data, and code statements.
- [ ] Deposit the exact archive and verify the reviewer-access link or DOI.
- [ ] Decide and record the code/archive license; no license is inferred.
- [ ] Confirm originality, authorship, and that the paper is not under
  simultaneous consideration elsewhere.
- [ ] Decide whether to retain the portable article class or convert to the
  recommended current Springer Nature LaTeX template, then validate the
  complete editable source set.
- [ ] Compile and inspect the manuscript PDF page by page.
- [ ] Compile and inspect `ESM_1.pdf` page by page; it will be published as
  received without copyediting.
- [ ] Re-check the live portal and official instructions immediately before the
  human author submits.

Exact PDF/TeX Section 7.2 defines #FugacityWeightedPM with nonnegative rational weights/fugacities given as binary numerator/denominator pairs. Lemma 25 explicitly constructs a unit-edge zero-fugacity two-external-edge circuit with signature diag(p,q) in time polynomial in the binary p,q. The displayed binary path construction gives polynomial, visibly O(log² W), size for G_(W,1). Its 00 state has W full matchings; its 11 state matches terminals externally and leaves exactly one internal matching; parity forces mixed states zero. Thus (W,1,0,0) is established, not a novel signature. Lemma 27 explicitly constructs a **simple** graph with #PM=C times the original weighted partition, C a product of edge denominators. It removes parallel edges by length-three subdivision. This is an exact compact rational reduction, stronger evidence than general #P hardness or a statement about explicit multigraph edges.

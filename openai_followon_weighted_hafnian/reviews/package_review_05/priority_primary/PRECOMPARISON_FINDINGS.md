- Rudelson–Samorodnitsky–Zeitouni, arXiv:1409.3905: downloaded and inspected v1 (2014-09-13) TeX/PDF and current v2 (2016-09-02) TeX/PDF. Theorem 1.2 in v2 treats dense strongly expanding d-regular graphs, with errors in log hafnian of C n^(1-epsilon), improved to C sqrt(n log n) under a spectral gap. - Barvinok, arXiv:1601.07518: downloaded all five TeX/PDF versions; inspected exact v1/v5 approximation theorem. It is deterministic quasipolynomial and excludes arbitrary zero/small weights from that theorem. V5 also has near-one complex zero-free/approximation statements, which do not give unrestricted signed/complex approximation.

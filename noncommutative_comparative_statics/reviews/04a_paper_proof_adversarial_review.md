   Gram operator
   \[
   \widetilde D_A^\ast\widetilde D_A,\qquad
   \widetilde D_A=W_C^{1/2}D_AW_E^{-1/2},
   \]
   not raw \(D_A^\ast D_A\).
2. **Response-order domains.** A partial continuation may delete a cofinal
   subsequence and change a liminf. Define the order on a cofinal
   common-success scale set and require continuations to be defined on both
   route outcomes on the same eventual tail.
3. **Extended-metric filling.** The product of an infimal zero Lipschitz
   modulus and an infinite local distance is undefined. Use declared positive
   valid suffix bounds (or restrict to finite metrics).
4. **Allocation typing and calibration.** Write the final fiber as a singleton
   state
   \[
   X_{AB}=\{(B\to1,A\to2)\}.
   \]
   Treat compatibility and nonpreemption as assumptions or calibrate them
   using forced-unavailability and sequential trials, not isolated jobs.
5. **Wording.** Do not call the boundary-exposure result “sharp” without an
   optimality theorem.

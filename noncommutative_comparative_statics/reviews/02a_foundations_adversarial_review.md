# Adversarial Review 02a — Rejected Foundations Draft

**Artifact:** `checkpoints/02a_foundations_rejected.md`

**Verdict:** no-go as an accepted checkpoint; 7/14.

## What was correct

- Partial nonexpansive maps form a category.
- The failure-completed exact-descent proposition was correct for separated
  extended metrics.
- The Moore–Penrose formulas and path-graph singular values were algebraically
  correct.
- The smooth bracket computation and active-set projection arithmetic were
  correct.
- The restricted claim that no canonical additive stratified decomposition
  exists was appropriately cautious.

## Fatal or material issues

1. A unique cost argmin need not be nonexpansive; generic horizontal and seam
   transports also need not be nonexpansive. The declared target category did
   not contain the claimed sectors.
2. The continued-memory inequality omitted matched continuation domains.
3. The ordinary filling area ignored whether intermediate partial routes were
   defined.
4. The proposed morphism was not a precise relation-preserving functor plus
   natural transformation.
5. Common failure received zero defect and was not a required output.
6. Theorem 7.1 was the standard Hilbert Hyers–Ulam error bound
   \(\operatorname{dist}(a,\ker T)\le\|T^\dagger\|\|Ta\|\), not a new
   theorem. The constant-map corollary erased every prefix and was
   composition-degenerate.
7. The raw singular-value constant could be changed by duplicating relations
   or changing generators while leaving the quotient category unchanged.
8. Translation responses were not closed under the advertised full gauge
   group.
9. The long-chain example changed the source presentation; it was a
   conditioning example, not fixed-source Ulam instability.
10. Smooth square typing, amplitude calibration, and uniform regularity
    hypotheses were incomplete.
11. Computational checks did not test the categorical or rectification
    claims.
12. Two native applications and held-out predictions were missing.

## Direct prior-art collision added

Huang and Moslehian, “Relationship between the Hyers–Ulam stability and the
Moore–Penrose inverse,” *Electronic Journal of Linear Algebra* 23 (2012), DOI
`10.13001/1081-3810.1564`, arXiv `1211.0192`, identifies the optimal stability
constant with the Moore–Penrose inverse norm.

## Disposition

Accepted. The old draft was frozen rather than silently overwritten. The new
Checkpoint 2:

- uses partial Lipschitz maps;
- reports asymmetric failure, common failure, and finite value defect
  separately;
- restricts filling bounds to admissible derivations;
- imports rather than claims the Hyers–Ulam result;
- generalizes the linear baseline to affine contractions with a
  gauge-covariant relation operator;
- makes seam-margin failure conversion and amplitude-calibrated response order
  the central proposed additions;
- includes two native, falsifiable applications.

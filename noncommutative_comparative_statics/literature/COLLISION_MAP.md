# Literature Collision Map

This map records what the proposed theory must cite as antecedent. It is not
evidence that any cited field is incomplete; it is a boundary document for
preventing novelty inflation.

The map was last checked on 27 July 2026. Searches included the exact proposed
name and combinations of comparative statics, noncommutativity, stateful
response, partial maps, recourse, guard robustness, and path dependence.
Failure to find the exact package is not proof of priority.

## Closest formal translations

### Quantitative rewriting and concurrency

A response square is a quantitative diamond, and a square-swap derivation is
a rewriting derivation. Nonexpansive/Lipschitz propagation of local error is
already developed in quantitative rewriting. A directed 2-complex whose
squares declare independence is standard trace / higher-dimensional-automata
machinery.

Primary leads:

- Gavazzo and Di Florio, “Elements of Quantitative Rewriting,” DOI
  `10.1145/3571256`, arXiv `2206.13610`.
- Mazurkiewicz, “Theory of Traces,” DOI
  `10.1016/0304-3975(88)90051-5`.
- Earnshaw, Nester, and Román, “Resourceful Traces for Commuting Processes,”
  DOI `10.4230/LIPIcs.CSL.2026.28`.
- Kahl, “Topological Abstraction of Higher-Dimensional Automata,” DOI
  `10.1016/j.tcs.2016.04.018`.

Consequence for NCS: the elementary filling/telescoping bound is a baseline,
not a novelty theorem.

### Metric-enriched categories and approximate representations

Assigning objects to vertices and maps to edges is a functor from a free path
category. Exact response means factorization through the quotient by the
declared relations. Cell defects give an approximately commuting diagram.
The question “almost satisfies the relations, therefore near an exact
representation?” is Ulam stability.

Primary leads:

- Kubiś, “Metric-enriched categories and approximate Fraïssé limits,” arXiv
  `1210.6506`.
- Gowers and Hatami, approximate representations of finite groups, arXiv
  `1510.04085`.
- Burger, Ozawa, and Thom, “On Ulam stability,” arXiv `1010.0565`.
- Voiculescu, “Asymptotically commuting finite rank unitary operators without
  commuting approximants” (1983).
- Becker and Chapman, stability of approximate group actions, DOI
  `10.4171/JEMS/1267`.
- Arzhantseva and Păunescu, almost commuting permutations, DOI
  `10.1016/j.jfa.2015.02.013`.

Consequence for NCS: rectification must be presented as a
presentation/target-specific stability problem. Heterogeneous fibers,
partiality, and noninvertibility are extensions, not proof that stability
itself is new.

### Discrete connections and lattice gauge theory

Invertible edge transport, plaquette defect, gauge conjugation, and continuum
curvature limits are standard.

Primary leads:

- Leok, Marsden, and Weinstein, “A Discrete Theory of Connections on
  Principal Bundles,” arXiv `math/0508338`.
- Fernández, Juchani, and Zuccalli, discrete Atiyah sequence, DOI
  `10.1016/j.geomphys.2021.104417`.
- Wilson, “Confinement of Quarks,” DOI `10.1103/PhysRevD.10.2445`.

Consequence for NCS: it cannot claim edge transport, cell curvature, gauge
invariance, or the smooth limit. The distinctive target is directed
partial/noninvertible adjustment with physical response semantics.

### Smooth constrained transport

Metric-minimum infinitesimal feasible adjustment is the orthogonal Ehresmann
connection / weighted-pseudoinverse rule. Curvature and holonomy are
classical.

Primary leads:

- Ehresmann (1952), infinitesimal connections.
- Ambrose and Singer, DOI
  `10.1090/S0002-9947-1953-0063739-1`.
- O’Neill, Riemannian submersions, DOI `10.1307/mmj/1028999604`.
- Altafini, redundant robotic chains, DOI `10.1109/TRA.2004.824636`.
- Shamir and Yomdin, repeatability of redundant manipulators, DOI
  `10.1109/9.14412`.

Consequence for NCS: the smooth sector is a reduction and empirical benchmark.

### Parametric optimization and moving constraints

Solution-map sensitivity, active-set changes, moving feasible sets,
incremental projection, jumps, and rate-independent hysteresis are mature.

Primary leads:

- Robinson, strong regularity, DOI `10.1287/moor.5.1.43`.
- Bonnans and Shapiro, *Perturbation Analysis of Optimization Problems*, DOI
  `10.1007/978-1-4612-1394-9`.
- Moreau, moving convex sets, DOI `10.1016/0022-0396(77)90085-7`.
- Mielke and Theil, rate-independent hysteresis, DOI
  `10.1007/s00030-003-1052-7`.
- Bauschke and Borwein, projection algorithms, DOI
  `10.1137/S0036144593251710`.

Consequence for NCS: ordered projection is a baseline example. Holonomy and
hysteresis must not be used as synonyms.

### Stratified and hybrid transport

Exit-path categories are directed and noninvertible; hybrid systems have
guards, resets, saltation matrices, and order-dependent products at multiple
events. Scattering diagrams have wall maps, ordered products, and
codimension-two consistency.

Primary leads:

- Treumann, exit paths and constructible stacks, DOI
  `10.1112/S0010437X09004229`.
- Woolf, fundamental category of a stratified space, arXiv `0811.2580`.
- Burden et al., event-selected vector fields, DOI
  `10.1137/15M1016588`.
- Kong et al., saltation matrices, DOI `10.1109/JPROC.2024.3440211`.
- Gross, Hacking, Keel, and Kontsevich, scattering diagrams / canonical
  bases, DOI `10.1090/jams/890`.

Consequence for NCS: seam and junction response require explicit protocol
data. No canonical additive “curvature + seam + junction” formula exists in
the general noninvertible metric setting.

### Predicate robustness, guard margins, and boundary risk

The membership margin of a continuation domain is the absolute signed-distance
robustness radius of its Boolean indicator. Preserving membership under a
perturbation smaller than that radius, and decomposing robust error into
ordinary error plus boundary-band mass, are established.

Primary leads:

- Fainekos and Pappas, “Robustness of Temporal Logic Specifications for
  Continuous-Time Signals,” DOI `10.1016/j.tcs.2009.06.021`.
- Zhang et al., “Theoretically Principled Trade-off between Robustness and
  Accuracy,” PMLR 97 (2019), arXiv `1901.08573`.
- Beer, Caserta, Di Maio, and Lucchetti, “Convergence of Partial Maps,” DOI
  `10.1016/j.jmaa.2014.05.040`.

Consequence for NCS: the signed-distance guard lemma and its one-step
boundary-exposure bound are imported baselines. The domain/value split is
useful operational reporting, but it is not claimed as a new mathematical
object.

### Comparative statics and sequential recourse

Adjustment costs, sequential intervention, action order, and recourse
invalidation are already studied in economics and machine learning.

Primary leads:

- Milgrom and Shannon, monotone comparative statics, DOI `10.2307/2951479`.
- Dekel, comparative statics with adjustment costs, DOI
  `10.3982/ECTA22841`.
- Rasouli and Yu, ordered coherent recourse, DOI
  `10.1007/s41060-022-00365-6`.
- Verma, Hines, and Dickerson, sequential algorithmic recourse, DOI
  `10.1609/aaai.v36i8.20828`.

Consequence for NCS: “order matters for recourse” is not a novelty claim. The
field proposal is a shared defect/rectification calculus.

## Safe novelty statement

No source found in this mapped primary literature packages the entire
following combination:

1. comparative-statics semantics with a declared intervention presentation;
2. heterogeneous feasible-state fibers and partial/noninvertible response
   maps;
3. directional failure-aware reporting and intrinsic directed filling cost;
4. presentation-conditioned response rectification;
5. comparison of smooth, active-set, and jump scaling in one protocol theory.

This is an absence-of-mapped-source statement, not proof of global priority.
The paper must claim a proposed synthesis/research program, not that humans
previously lacked mathematics of order dependence or guard robustness.

# Primary bibliography and credits

1. Bruno Colbois, survey contribution in *Geometric Aspects of Spectral Theory*, Oberwolfach Reports 9 (2012), report 33, printed pp. 2034–2036; problem-session variant p. 2064. DOI [10.4171/OWR/2012/33](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2012/33). [Primary report PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46403).
2. Bruno Colbois, *The spectrum of the Laplacian: A geometric approach*, in *Geometric and Computational Spectral Theory*, Contemporary Mathematics 700 (2017), pp. 1–40. DOI [10.1090/conm/700/14181](https://doi.org/10.1090/conm/700/14181). [Author repository record](https://libra.unine.ch/entities/publication/7ee0a8c4-17f2-45ea-874c-999ad547e1a3). Remark 49 records the exact negative answer and attributes it to Pétiard.
3. Luc Pétiard, *Counter-example to a Kröger type spectral inequality*, [arXiv:1706.09189v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1706.09189v1), submitted 2017-06-28. Complete proof, Theorems 2–3 and Proposition 7; dimension at least three in the stated main result.
4. Luc Pétiard, *Isoperimetric inequalities for Laplace and Steklov problems on Riemannian manifolds*, doctoral thesis, Université de Neuchâtel, 2019; defended 2019-02-05. DOI [10.35662/unine-thesis-2749](https://doi.org/10.35662/unine-thesis-2749). [Current repository record](https://libra.unine.ch/entities/publication/335eca35-b0d2-483b-b843-9a8cee1e04c8). [Primary PDF](https://libra.unine.ch/bitstreams/cb35ac23-61a8-402a-9fdb-a1eb4d47c041/download). Chapter 2 includes every dimension at least two.
5. Bruno Colbois and Luigi Provenzano, *Conformal upper bounds for the eigenvalues of the p-Laplacian*, Journal of the London Mathematical Society 104 (2021), pp. 2128–2147. DOI [10.1112/jlms.12493](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.12493). [Journal-format repository PDF](https://libra.unine.ch/server/api/core/bitstreams/737fba8f-d6c7-49bb-be7a-7ff977dc4b4d/content). Appendix A, Remark 4 and reference 37 acknowledge Pétiard's result.
6. Bruno Colbois, Emily B. Dryden, and Ahmad El Soufi, *Bounding the eigenvalues of the Laplace–Beltrami operator on compact submanifolds*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 42 (2010), pp. 96–108. DOI [10.1112/blms/bdp100](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/bdp100). [arXiv:0909.5346v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.5346v1), 2009-09-29. Theorem 1.4 and §5 provide the high-gap construction and embedding/smoothing argument.
7. Bruno Colbois, Ahmad El Soufi, and Alexandre Girouard, *Isoperimetric control of the spectrum of a compact hypersurface*, Journal für die reine und angewandte Mathematik 683 (2013), pp. 49–65; first online 2012-04-03. DOI [10.1515/crelle-2012-0008](https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2012-0008). [arXiv:1007.0826v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1007.0826v2), 2011-10-02. Multiplicative estimate and related ambient examples.
8. Colette Anné, *Spectre du laplacien et écrasement d'anses*, Annales scientifiques de l'École Normale Supérieure, series 4, 20 (1987), pp. 271–280. [Primary archive](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASENS_1987_4_20_2_271_0/). Supplies the thin-handle spectral convergence used by Pétiard, with a dimension-two-compatible setting.

The earlier large-gap metrics, isometric embedding, and smoothing work credited in the inspected sources includes Colbois–Dodziuk (1994), Buser–Burger–Dodziuk (1988), Nash, Kuiper, and Hirsch. Their original bodies were not independently read in this priority audit; CDE's explicit derivation and Pétiard's complete target-level argument were read. This distinction is part of the read scope, not a transfer of credit to the candidate.

# A simple left brace outside every two-trivial-factor asymmetric product

Problem 30004311 / OWR-17294-011: Ferran Cedó’s Problem 9 in Oberwolfach Report 51/2019.

## Accepted complete negative answer

There is an explicit nontrivial simple left brace B of order 20,000 with additive group C₅⁴ × C₂⁵ and multiplicative group

    (C₅³ ⋊ C₂) × (C₂⁴ ⋊ C₅).

The stated reflection and five-cycle actions make the multiplicative group metabelian with abelian Sylow subgroups. Nevertheless B admits no asymmetric-product presentation with both factors trivial. This answers the exact original question negatively.

The proof gives direct operations, an explicit multiplicative group isomorphism, additive lambda automorphisms and a complete all-ideal simplicity argument. For every two-trivial-factor product with abelian multiplicative Sylow subgroups, triple star products within each additive Sylow subbrace vanish. This covers the full admissible symmetric-2-cocycle definition, because the brace laws force biadditivity when both factors are trivial. In B, the Sylow-5 element x instead satisfies (x*x)*x=x³≠0.

Redundant intrinsic witnesses are also retained: |Fix(B)|=10 differs from the multiplicative abelianization order 50; the multiplicative center has order five; and an explicit derived-subgroup star witness fails [G,G]*B⊆Fix(B). Their requisite two-trivial-factor properties are independently proved in the audit. No separate recognition theorem is needed.

## Focused new audit derivations

The independent prior-implication reviewer supplied two routes that the parent
checked algebraically. These are **new deductions during this audit**, not claims
that an old paper published these exclusion theorems.

In a finite metabelian A-group N semidirect S with N abelian, every action of an
element a of S' on N has U=alpha_a-id satisfying U^2=0, since a and [a,N] lie in
the abelian derived group. For p-primary a, abelian Sylows kill its action on N_p.
On N_q for q different from p, alpha_a has p-power order, so
(id+U)^(p^e)=id+p^e U=id; multiplication by p^e is invertible on N_q, hence U=0.
Thus alpha(S') is trivial.

For the finite Theorem4.5 family, invertibility of t-id puts A in S' and hence
in G'. The same argument makes alpha(A) trivial. The A-subgroup has identity
lambda action within A1; its embedded elements then lie in the final socle,
contradicting finite nontrivial simplicity. This rules out that specific family
as a source of the full target under both group restrictions.

For iteration with a trivial N and simple two-trivial S=U asymmetric-product V,
the preceding argument gives alpha(U)=1, using S'=U. Equivariance forces the
new cocycle values into the elements fixed by all lambda_U; nondegeneracy of
the old cocycle makes that set exactly V. In coordinates (n,u,v), addition
therefore has cocycle beta_N(n,m)+b0(u,u'), while multiplication acts only
through v via alpha_v direct-sum alpha0_v. This flattens to an asymmetric product
of the two trivial braces N direct-sum U and V. It eliminates this iteration
route, not every possible older construction with nontrivial factors.

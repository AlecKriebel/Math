# Discrete initial surreal groups admit initial omnific models

Problem 30003321. Publication edition, 10 October 2026.

Project author: Alec Kriebel, [ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X).

Every discrete initial ordered additive subgroup of the surreal numbers No is order-group-isomorphic onto an initial subgroup of the omnific integers Oz. The theorem covers sets and proper classes in NBG with global choice, including the trivial group. Initiality is closure under sign-sequence prefixes, not order convexity. Neither multiplication nor preservation of the original simplicity order is required.

## Sources and scope

The proof uses Philip Ehrlich and Elliot Kaplan's canonical Hahn-image characterization of initial additive groups, alongside their real coefficient-group classification and the normal-form criterion for Oz: *Number systems with simplicity hierarchies: a generalization of Conway’s theory of surreal numbers II*, The Journal of Symbolic Logic 83 (2018), 617–633, [DOI 10.1017/jsl.2017.9](https://doi.org/10.1017/jsl.2017.9). The exact target appears as Question 9.1 in the [authenticated author manuscript](https://elliotakaplan.github.io/Number_systems_with_simplicity_hierarchies_II.pdf) and as Question 1 in the Kaplan–Ehrlich contribution to [Oberwolfach Report 60/2016](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46663).

This is an additive-group theorem. The known discrete initial subdomain theorem is not substituted for it. The image transports precisely the source series; it is not a silently enlarged full Hahn product. Every individual support is a reverse-well-ordered set, even for a proper-class group.

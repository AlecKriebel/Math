## Mechanism-specific findings

The candidate combines four steps: discreteness gives bottom exponent−α and bottom coefficient group2^(-n)Z; a special sign transformation moves that exponent to0; only that coefficient is multiplied by2^n; and the actual supports already realized in the group are transported term by term. The crucial target check is that a right prefix in the image pulls back to a right prefix in the source. Without it, a new dyadic divisibility requirement could make the image fail Theorem5.1.

The least-positive shape is already explicit in II Proposition5.2. The auxiliary observation that an initial exponent set with a minimum has minimum−α is also explicitly recorded in the universal-H-field Lemma3.2. These are partial antecedents, not witnesses against full-result priority.

The checked substructure and cut machinery preserves both numerical order and simplicity throughout its source. The candidate deliberately changes the role of the minimum and need not preserve the original simplicity relation there. Its use of an initial nonnegative exponent image together with the one-way right-prefix implication and bottom-only scaling is therefore a further verification obligation, not a conclusion already stated in the checked generic theorems. This is a comparison, not a claim that any of those elementary operations is individually new.

Ordinary ordered-class universality, Hahn embeddings, or a newly supplied combination of generic tree lemmas cannot be labeled PRIOR_FULL under the audit criterion. That route is blocked as a priority witness until an earlier explicit full theorem or recorded consequence is located. Similarly, replacing the realized supports by the entire Hahn product would change the object being represented; no such replacement was accepted as a source match.

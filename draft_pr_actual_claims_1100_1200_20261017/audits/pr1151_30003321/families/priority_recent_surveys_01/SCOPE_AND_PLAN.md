## Target reconstructed before candidate and verdict access

OWR 60/2016, Kaplan (joint with Ehrlich), pp. 3354–3356, Question 1; and Ehrlich–Kaplan, *Number systems with simplicity hierarchies II*, §9, Question 9.1, ask the same question:

> Is every discrete initial subgroup of No isomorphic to an initial subgroup of Oz?

The source subgroup is an ordered additive subgroup of No closed under surreal simplicity predecessors. Discrete means having a least positive element. The conclusion seeks an order-preserving additive isomorphism onto some subgroup of Oz which is itself initial in No. It does not require the original subgroup to lie in Oz, or the isomorphism to preserve simplicity. The nontrivial hypothesis is implicit in the discrete question; the zero group can be handled separately.

EKII explicitly works in NBG with global choice, and explicitly says classes include sets and proper classes (§1, p. 2). Audit the set case and proper-class case separately. A set-only solution is a partial priority hit, not full coverage of that source convention.

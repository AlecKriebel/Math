# Review independence and evidentiary limits

The root saw the current PR body and its outline before reconstructing the original primary-source target. ROOT_TARGET_PREPROOF.md was sealed before reading the candidate. ROOT_CORE_PREVERDICTS.md was sealed after candidate inspection and before reading old audit/acceptance or fresh-family verdicts. This is independent checking, not blind rediscovery.

All three new initial families were started with no inherited parent history. Each was supplied the mathematical target, sources, assigned distinct attack family and candidate location, with instructions to seal its own primary-source reconstruction before candidate access, and not read old/root/sibling verdicts. The candidate itself contains historical-acceptance prose, so reviewers encountered those assertions while reading the candidate and explicitly did not use them as proof evidence. Each family's own seal, scope and receipt document the actual order of access. No perfect-blindness or human-peer-review claim is made.

Root directly authenticates evidence receipts and reads complete reports, replays available material diagnostics only in modes that preserve their checks, and distinguishes old claims about unavailable diagnostic programs from new reproducible evidence. Primary proof dependencies were directly read in the byte-pinned author manuscript; no publisher-PDF identity or recursive reproof of all literature foundations is implied. New publication package reviewers must also start without parent history and seal paper/target cores before older verdict access. No family/agent was asked to contact another shard or any outside person.

The following is my deduction from those facts, independently reconstructed before the priority review's full report:

Take the connected double cover of the disk branched at all three marked points. Its Euler characteristic is2-3=-1; odd boundary monodromy gives one boundary component, so its genus is1. Any base map permuting the points preserves the mod2 covering character. Its two lifts differ by the deck involution. Since that involution acts nontrivially on the connected double-cover boundary, exactly one lift is identity on the external collar. Uniqueness makes these chosen lifts a homomorphism; no central cocycle remains.

In a branch chart p(w)=z_i+w², a base translation from z_i to z_j is p(w) mapped to z_j+w². The chosen lift is therefore w mapped to either w or -w, both smooth with smooth inverse. Away from branch points ordinary covering coordinates apply. This does not assert smooth lifting for arbitrary smooth base germs.

Adjacent Artin half-twist classes lift to the two right Dehn twists about the preimages of adjacent branch arcs, which meet once. This is a mapping-class identification in the boundary-fixed cover, not merely homology. Its central disk full twist lifts to the square root of the boundary twist, consistent with (uv)^6=delta. Exact base relations remain exact because of the unique collar-fixed lift.

Conjugate the resulting one-holed-torus action into a compact regular neighborhood of any prescribed oriented-surface pair, then extend by identity. This yields all requested regularities, prescribed classes, compact support and collar identity. No extra ambient relation or full higher-genus section is inferred.

Thus, as confirmed by the completed independent source/version audit, the full target is already a consequence of the earlier theorem plus standard covering facts. The direct flow proof may be a different exposition, but does not create a new full-resolution theorem.

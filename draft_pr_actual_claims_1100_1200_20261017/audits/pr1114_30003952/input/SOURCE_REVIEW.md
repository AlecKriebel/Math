# Source dependencies, prior realization and scope

Problem 30003952 / OWR-16415-015 asks for actual representatives of two
prescribed right Dehn twists, satisfying the braid relation as maps.
The complete construction answers the homeomorphism, C1 and every finite
Cr question affirmatively, and also provides C∞ maps with C∞ inverses.

## Directly relevant positive prior work

Mann–Tshishiku, Theorem 4.1, already proves smooth realization of B3 on
a disk with three marked points. Its proof supplies identity near the
external boundary and translation behavior near the marked points.
This earlier positive result is central to the attribution. No novelty
or priority is claimed. The direct one-holed-torus construction here
does not assume that an arbitrary smooth map lifts smoothly through a
branched cover.

presented as publisher-edition verification. The source supplies the

The inverse matrices on printed page 68 matter: the draft calls its

## Negative results and exact accepted scope

Salter–Tshishiku's inspected Theorem 1.1 and Remarks 1.3/1.5 concern C1
non-realization from n≥5 strands with boundary and n≥6 without. Those
ranges do not preclude the positive B3 case. Chen's opening theorem and
corollary were inspected by the audit for their high-strand scope. Their
full negative proofs were not re-audited.
[Salter–Tshishiku](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.00941),
[Chen](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.08248).

The accepted result lifts the specified homomorphism B3→Mod(S). It need
not be a section of its image if the ambient surface adds relations;
for instance, the model boundary twist can become trivial after inclusion
in a once-punctured torus. This corrects an earlier overly broad subgroup
realization statement. The actual braid relation and prescribed twist
classes are proved in full. No realization of a full higher-genus mapping
class group and no contradiction of its non-realization theorems is claimed.

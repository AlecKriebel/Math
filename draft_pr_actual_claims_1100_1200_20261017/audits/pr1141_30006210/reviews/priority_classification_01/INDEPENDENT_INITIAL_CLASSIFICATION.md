## Exact claim and classification criterion

For each integer d>=1, there is an inverse polynomial delta(d)>0 such that any two nonzero real multilinear functions f,g on the uniform {-1,1}^n cube of degree at most d, with every relative influence at most delta(d), have overlapping supports. Relative influence is Fourier L2 mass containing coordinate i divided by **full** Fourier L2 mass, including the constant coefficient. Scaling either polynomial does not change it. d=0 is immediate. The claim is not an assertion about influence divided by variance, total Boolean functions, only nonnegative polynomials, or two supports whose union is the cube.

For an `already_solved` priority classification I require a primary publication/version that records this exact theorem or an explicitly instantiated equivalent result whose hypotheses cover every such pair. A new derivation from generic prior tools can make the mathematical consequence easy or nonnovel in a broad sense, but does not by itself establish that the exact open problem was already recorded as resolved. Conversely, failure to locate such a record cannot establish firstness.

## Independent findings so far

1. Austrin's OWR 9/2025 p.445 gives the exact full-norm influence problem and exponential sufficient scale 2^(-d)/d. This is the target, not an inverse-variance version.
2. Li, Li, Li, Liu arXiv:2608.03824v1 (4 August 2026), Lemma 3.5, already gives a depth O(d^4) separator for **arbitrary real** disjoint-support degree-d pairs, with the promise that one polynomial is nonzero. Its proof explicitly labels empty-promise subcubes arbitrarily, so the underlying tree yields a total Boolean extension distinguishing the two supports. The full 10-page body was searched for influence, compatibility, and every theorem/lemma/corollary. It does not state an inverse-polynomial influence incompatibility bound. Removing the Polynomial Compatibility Conjecture as a dependency for a cryptographic attack is not a proof that that conjecture itself is true.
3. OSSS arXiv:cs/0508071v1 Section 3.3 already gives the crucial two-function decision-tree covariance machinery, including the arbitrary-real-g extension. The original source must receive priority for that inequality. Lee's 2010 paper states a Boolean/Boolean covariance version, so attributing the stronger real-input tool solely to Lee would undercredit OSSS and would obscure the range assumption.
4. Kothari et al. arXiv:2601.08727v3 defines rational representations with denominator nowhere zero, and its nondeterministic representations of h and 1-h have complementary supports covering the entire cube. Its Theorem 4 and Theorem 10 therefore do not directly cover an arbitrary disjoint-support pair with a hole. If h is defined as 1 on supp(f), 0 elsewhere, g is not a nondeterministic representation of 1-h on that hole. The rational candidate f/(f+g), or f^2/(f^2+g^2), has zero denominator there. A Boolean extension supplied by Li repairs the support separation, but converting that to the target influence bound still requires a weighted analytic application; the total rational-degree theorem alone is not the exact target theorem.
5. CRYPTO 2022 Conjectures 1.2/5.5 are stronger distribution-average/SOS-like compatibility questions; its Theorem 4.4 establishes exponential influence only. Point-mass distributions normalize arbitrary nonzero real f,g and yield the exact deterministic target as a special case of the conjecture. A conjectured stronger theorem is no earlier proof.

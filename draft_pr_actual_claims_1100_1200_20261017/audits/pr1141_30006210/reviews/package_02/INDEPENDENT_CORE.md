## Adaptive covariance reconstruction
Run a deterministic nonrepeating tree on independent X. Let Y be independent uniform. Hybrid U_t replaces the first t queried coordinates of X by those of Y; after stopping, leave U unchanged. Conditional on the complete pre-query history, replaced coordinates use fresh Y and untouched coordinates use fresh independent X. Hence the pair at the next coordinate has exactly the uniform single-coordinate resampling distribution. Conditional on the final leaf transcript, U_tau is uniform independent of that transcript: replaced positions are independent Y, unread X positions are independent of all queried values. Therefore Cov(h(X),phi(X))=E[h(X)(phi(U_0)-phi(U_tau))]. Since |h|<=1, telescoping and conditional expectation give absolute covariance<=sum_i delta_i E|phi(X)-phi(X^resamp_i)|. Stop time may vary and can be padded to n without extra changes. Conditioning after observing the next X_i would fail and is deliberately avoided. This is exactly the arbitrary-real L1 two-function OSSS Eq6 interface, not the squared metric variant.

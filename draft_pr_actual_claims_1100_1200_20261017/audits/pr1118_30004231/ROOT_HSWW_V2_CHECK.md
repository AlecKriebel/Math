## Independent universality check

## Controlled test of a normal-fan-only transfer

The priority adversary proposed a simple control, which root checked independently. Put Q=D4−D4. The pair (D4,−D4) has sum Q and the 50-vertex exact mixed-face graph proved in the note. The pair (Q,Q) has sum 2Q, with the identical complete normal fan. For every canonical face of the latter pair the two summands are F,F and their sum is 2F. If dim F>0, then dim(2F)=dim F<2 dim F, so full additivity fails; if dim F=0, positivity fails. Its qualifying poset is empty. Thus the complete sum normal fan alone does not determine the printed exact mixed-face poset.

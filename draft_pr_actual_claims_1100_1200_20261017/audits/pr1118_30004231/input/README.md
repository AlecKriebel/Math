# Totally mixed face posets: complete negative resolution

Problem 30004231 / OWR-17135-028. Both universal assertions printed by Sam Payne
in Oberwolfach Report 39/2019 have a negative answer under the standard
order-complex convention. The source retains faces with positive-dimensional
summands and additive face dimension. No extra general-position hypothesis is
present in the printed question.

Let D be the standard four-simplex and I=[0,1]. The full-dimensional lattice
polytopes D×I and (−D)×I in R5 have a totally mixed order complex consisting of
two disjoint connected pure graphs, each with 50 vertices and 60 edges. Its
ordinary rational Betti numbers are (2,22,0,0,…). It is not shellable and cannot
have the rational homology of a wedge of three-spheres. A separate R3 example
has six isolated points and refutes only the homology assertion; it is a
shellable zero-dimensional complex.

# Building, local-curvature and spectral priority audit

Decision: **bounded no-find**. This independent audit did not find an earlier explicitly recorded theorem or construction with the full target conclusion. It did find close, substantial precedents for the construction and both principal mechanisms. This is not a certificate of novelty or current global openness.

The target was fixed from Nevo’s original OWR17/2005 Definition 1, Theorem 7 and Problem 8, printed pp. 960–961 (PDF pp. 36–37), and arXiv:math/0505334v2 Definition 3.1, Theorem 3.4 and Problem 3.7. The target/plan were sealed before reading the candidate or performing the search. Both OWR pages were subsequently rendered and visually confirmed. No other priority reports or root/old audit verdicts were read.

## Exact target and priority gate

The candidate concerns a finite two-dimensional original abstract simplicial complex, 2-CM over Q (hence over characteristic-zero extensions), with no two-dimensional homology-sphere subcomplex over any field. The sphere condition includes every face link, including the empty face. Such an example prevents even an unordered sphere cover, before the overlap and reordering conditions in Nevo’s question arise.

An earlier explicit full solution must record the matching conclusion or a theorem with all the needed hypotheses and conclusion in one stated construction. Existing theorems that permit a new combination are substantial partial precedents, not by themselves a recorded full solution. Subdivision, a sphere immersion, a minimal-cycle support, global homology alone, or a finite CW/Δ-complex with unresolved face identifications is not silently substituted for the target.

## Closest precedents found

**Lubotzky–Meshulam is particularly close to the quotient mechanism.** The complete 10-page author manuscript of *A Moore Bound for Simplicial Complexes* was read. Its Proposition 3.3 (p.7) uses conjugation matrices linear in 1/y, degree bounds, congruence divisibility, normality and vertex transitivity to bound displacement in finite affine-building quotients. This is essentially the same established degree-versus-congruence mechanism used by the candidate to preserve local structure. Theorems A_d/B_d concern top homology and acyclicity radius. They do not assert double CM, all vertex deletions, or a negative sphere-cover conclusion. A top homology cycle is not an all-face-link homology sphere. [Author manuscript](https://math.huji.ac.il/~alexlub/PAPERS/a%20Moore%20Bound%20for%20simplicial%20complexes/a%20Moore%20Bound%20for%20simplicial%20complexes.pdf)

**LSV supplies the actual building/lattice infrastructure.** In arXiv:math/0406217v2, Section 2 describes lattice flags; the p.9 conjugation embedding, Corollary 4.6, Equation (9) and Proposition 4.8 establish the polynomial representation and simply vertex-transitive action. For PGL_3, the building is two-dimensional and vertex links are incidence graphs of a projective plane. These are strong pre-existing construction inputs. No inspected theorem states all-deletions 2-CM together with absence of sphere subcomplexes. The candidate needs none of the Section 6 global Ramanujan assertion. The residue finite field and homology coefficient field are separate parameters. [LSV v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0406217v2)

**The spectral vanishing mechanism is classical.** Ballmann–Świątkowski’s 1996 preprint Theorem 1 gives the dimension-two normalized-link-gap threshold above 1/2 for first L2-cohomology vanishing; its trivial-group case covers finite complexes. Theorem 2/Corollary 2 already put high-girth links and property T in a common construction. Section 3.1 records the projective-plane normalized gap. These facts prevent presenting the spectral criterion or the broad high-girth/property-T combination as a new mechanism. The specific deletion robustness needed for 2-CM, and the full negative answer, were not recorded in the inspected passages. [Primary preprint](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/2255/)

**The curvature obstruction is also established.** Januszkiewicz–Świątkowski Example 1.8(5), pp.11–12, explicitly invokes combinatorial Gauss–Bonnet to rule out 6-large triangulations of the two-sphere. Example 1.8(4) includes affine A2 buildings among systolic complexes. Fact 1.2(2) states inheritance for full subcomplexes; that restriction cannot be removed without an argument. The candidate’s local incidence/Euler argument deals with arbitrary allowed sphere subcomplexes and every coefficient field. These source passages do not supply a finite doubly CM counterexample to Nevo. [Published paper](https://www.numdam.org/article/PMIHES_2006__104__1_0.pdf)

## Other checked families and exclusions

Swartz’s *g-elements, finite buildings and higher Cohen-Macaulay connectivity*, Theorem 2.3 and Theorem 4.1, gives convex ear decompositions and double CM for finite **spherical** buildings. The apartments are spheres of a finite Coxeter complex. This is a positive decomposition family, not a theorem that arbitrary finite quotients of affine buildings remain doubly CM. Its homology-sphere convention includes every face link. [Swartz v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512086v1)

Oppenheim v5’s Theorem 1.1/Corollary 1.2 refines weighted spectral vanishing. Papikian v1’s Theorem 1.1 and Theorem 4.4 state vanishing for finite building quotients, with exact action and thickness conditions. First v3’s Theorem 7.22 gives completely Ramanujan affine-building quotients. Babson–Welker **v4, 4 May 2026**, Theorem 2.2 and Section 6/Corollary 6.3 give current spectral exactness and random simplicial/cubical examples. None of the inspected statements records the conjunction of all vertex-deletion CM conditions with the full sphere exclusion. The spectral conclusions cannot be promoted to that conjunction without checking deletion, purity, connectedness, all face links and the original complex. [Oppenheim](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5724v5), [Papikian](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.07904v1), [First](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.02664v3), [Babson–Welker](https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.00972v4)

Fløystad v2’s l-CM characterization and girth results were checked. His simplicial girth measures minimum support of top enriched homology, not short cycles in vertex links. Hoàng–Minh–Trung v3’s high-girth CM graphs concern independence complexes, another distinct use of the terminology. LSV’s *Division Algebras* arXiv v1 concerns the real/complex manifold version; a search return from a later author-hosted version is not silently assigned to that older acquired version. [Fløystad](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0411570v2), [Hoàng–Minh–Trung](https://arxiv.org/abs/1204.5561v3), [LSV inspected version](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0501064v1)

## Scope and remaining gap

There were **45 exact dated query inputs**, spanning building quotients, classical/current spectral vanishing, higher CM/deletion, systolic/nonpositive curvature, local girth and Nevo’s decomposition terminology. QUERY_INPUTS.json records every string and private raw-return location. EVIDENCE_LEDGER.json lists unique primary works with exact inspected versions, theorem locations, read scope and target mapping.

The strongest verified priority finding is that the candidate combines established infrastructure, an established quotient degree argument, established spectral vanishing, and an established local-curvature obstruction. The exact unfilled priority gap is an **earlier recorded full construction/conclusion**: all-deletions 2-CM over Q plus absence of all allowed two-dimensional homology-sphere pieces, or an explicit full negative theorem for Nevo’s question. No such record was found within this bounded reading/search.

Garland’s original 1973 full paper was not acquired; its publisher page was metadata only, and exact usable vanishing statements were read in later primary work. Wachs 1986 was a search-excerpt lead, not an independently verified full paper. Other current decomposition papers found by search were forwarded as leads to the assigning parent, without reading another audit. Thus no global absence claim follows. No external person was contacted. The candidate and repository publication state were left untouched.

# Target and independence seal

UTC: 2026-10-18T01:21:18.204172+00:00

For every finite d-dimensional doubly Cohen–Macaulay simplicial complex K over a fixed field k, d>=1, can K be written as a union of d-dimensional homology k-sphere subcomplexes S_i with successive intersections containing a d-face (and reorderability beginning at any prescribed S_i), as in Nevo Theorem 7? A negative solution needs a finite 2-CM K over an explicitly stated k with no such decomposition. Stronger absence of any homology sphere is sufficient. Homology sphere means all face links, including the empty face, have the reduced k-homology of the sphere in their dimension; merely global sphere homology is weaker and is not silently substituted.

Approach: Independently reconstruct finite congruence quotients of affine A_2 buildings; verify algebra-to-graph displacement, every adjacency and triangle type and faithful unchanged local links. Then independently check CM, each single vertex deletion, spectral/chain arguments and topological sphere obstruction. Seek counterexamples to any implicit quotient or homological inference before evaluating the candidate.

The original coefficient field is fixed for 2-CM and for the proposed sphere pieces. The local-link requirement is included. I have not read candidate PROOF.md or any prior/new reviewer verdict. Source pins and read scope are in TARGET_SEAL.json.

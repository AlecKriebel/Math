## Exact target and independence

The independent plan was sealed at **2026-10-18T01:24:20.540826+00:00**, before reading the candidate proof, old audits, root verdicts, or other new priority audits. The original Nevo manuscript was read first. The OWR source pages 36–37 were rendered and visually checked against printed 960–961.

The target is the possibility of choosing homology-sphere pieces in the minimal-cycle decomposition of every finite fixed-field 2-CM complex of dimension at least 1, preserving its facet overlaps and reorderability. Homology spheres carry the conditions on all face links, including the empty face. A two-dimensional Q-2-CM example containing no two-dimensional homology-sphere subcomplex would fully negate this universal statement, even before ordering constraints. It need not work in each positive characteristic. The further question about CM intersections is distinct.

The source trail is unusually clear. [Nevo OWR 17/2005](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/45990) gives Definition 1, Theorem 7 and Problem 8 on printed 960–961. In [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0505334v1) the question is Problem 3.5; after two corollaries were added it becomes Problem 3.7 in [v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0505334v2). The [journal PDF](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-007-9017-y) retains Problem 3.7 on printed 417. It was published online 5 September 2007 and appeared in DCG 39 (2008), 411–418. Thus an erroneous numbering search or the generated 2018 front-date on the v2 PDF could obscure the trail; neither establishes a later problem date.

## Results that might look like a prior answer

The strongest later general results inspected settle an algebraic motivation for the question. [Adiprasito–Papadakis–Petrotou, arXiv:2101.07245v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2101.07245v2), Section 3.1/Corollary 3.2, obtains top-heavy Lefschetz and g-vector conclusions for 2-CM complexes by mapping into Gorenstein cycle quotients. This is not a cover by homology-sphere subcomplexes. The distinction persists in [Oba, ALCO 2025](https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/alco.419/), Theorems 7.6 and 7.8: weighted simplex-boundary expressions after introducing a cone vertex are algebraic representations of cycles. They do not place those simplex boundaries inside the original complex. The field restrictions also matter: Oba's stated multigraded result here is in characteristic 2.

[Oba's balanced minimal-cycle rigidity paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.05005v1), Theorem 4.1/Corollary 4.4, proves rigidity and balanced lower bounds, retaining minimal-cycle pieces. Its Example 7.6 obstructs a constrained sparse realization of an existing sphere. In the later ALCO paper, Theorem 8.3 gives a more general negative example for weak Lefschetz maps under colored parameter restrictions. Those examples start with sphere complexes; they cannot be read as absence of sphere pieces.

The recent [Larson–Stapledon paper](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2026.10288), authenticated in both its September 2026 arXiv v2 and published form, classifies complementary vectors of 2-CM complexes. Its Examples 3.2–3.3 obstruct certain Gorenstein quotient or determinant extensions. Their complexes are graphs, where Nevo's original cycle decomposition already supplies the sphere pieces. The negative answer in Example 4.9 concerns numerical inequalities. None inspected is a recorded answer to the sphere-cover problem.

[Swartz's 2006 work](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512086v1), Theorem 4.1, gives 2-CM as a consequence of convex-ear constructions. This supports a special class, not the converse for all 2-CM complexes. His [2014 survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.0987v1), Section 5, records negative examples for weak Lefschetz expectations on 2-CM complexes. The nonunimodal glued example explicitly contains a sphere; the matroid example belongs to a class with convex ears. Failure of WLP therefore cannot substitute for failure of sphere covers.

The local-condition distinction was checked against [Athanasiadis–Welker](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1931v3). Example 2.11(i) has an articulation vertex and is not 2-CM. Example 2.11(iii), a torus with an added triangle, retains nonzero first homology and is not CM. Their Theorem 4.1 extends Nevo's *minimal-cycle* decomposition to connected Buchsbaum* complexes. Neither the weaker local examples nor that extension supplies a full earlier answer.

Other inspected primary bodies include Babson–Nevo sphere constructions, Kubitzke–Nevo subdivisions, Klee–Novik balanced lower bounds, balanced manifold canonical-module arguments, poset fiber methods, and Park's polynomial decompositions for flag complexes. Their exact scopes are in the ledger. Each supplies a special class, changes the complex, concerns algebraic maps, or studies another decomposition notion. No full prior sphere-cover theorem or counterexample was located there. The Adiprasito–Yashfe partition-complex survey and relevant OWR 2023 contribution likewise expose algebraic/local-to-global tools, without the demanded sphere-piece statement in the inspected passages.

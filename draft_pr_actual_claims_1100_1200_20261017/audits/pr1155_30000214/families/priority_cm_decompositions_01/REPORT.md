Nevo’s original setting is a finite d-dimensional 2-CM complex, d≥1, over a fixed field. His sphere replacement must cover the complex and obey additional overlap/reordering properties. Thus a two-dimensional example over Q with no permitted sphere subcomplex already defeats the original statement. The candidate’s stronger exclusion over every sphere coefficient field does not require 2-CM over every positive characteristic.

The all-face-link meaning of homology sphere is supported directly by [Swartz’s primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512086v1), Section 4, PDF pp. 12–13, and independently by [Babson–Nevo](https://arxiv.org/abs/0802.1058v1), Introduction, PDF p. 1. Athanasiadis’s flag-complex paper gives the same fixed-field definition. Consequently, replacing the required local homology spheres with complexes having only global spherical homology would change the question.

The strongest leads resolved as follows:

- **Convex ears and CM connectivity.** Swartz’s Theorem 4.1 and its sphere/ball extension are sufficient constructions of 2-CM complexes. Schweig’s 2006 and 2011 work and Woodroofe’s 2009 work provide further ears and numerical results. The inspected failures of related inequalities or interval constructions do not record a full no-sphere-cover conclusion.
- **Buchsbaum* gluing.** Athanasiadis–Welker identify CM Buchsbaum* complexes with 2-CM complexes, and give generalized gluing tools. Their inspected counterexamples distinguish weaker Buchsbaum properties. All three arXiv versions were retrieved; the earlier gluing statement and the revised one were compared. None of those inspected statements supplies the required complete negative example.
- **Almost Gorenstein* examples.** [Matsuoka–Murai](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2016.02.005), Example 3.12, is a particularly close finite two-dimensional example under a weaker CM condition. A reproducible face calculation shows that deleting vertex 6 leaves a disconnected link at vertex 1. It therefore fails 2-CM over Q and cannot answer the original question. The calculation and its source facets are private evidence.
- **Aspherical examples.** [Atanasov](https://doi.org/10.4171/GGD/217) uses a local CM convention in the inspected group-theoretic examples. The two-torus example has nonzero rational first homology, so it does not satisfy Nevo’s global CM condition. Local CM and asphericity alone cannot close that gap.
- **Recent h-vector counterexamples.** [Larson–Stapledon](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2026.10288), published online 2026-10-02, gives algebraic graph counterexamples and a 2-CM numerical counterexample explicitly built by gluing spheres. Those examples do not obstruct the sphere cover in question. The Cambridge final source was inspected; the indexed author-copy URL failed and was not treated as another inspected version.
- **Björner/Wachs/Welker background.** Wedge-of-spheres homotopy statements, nonpure counterexamples, and CM/homotopy-CM distinctions concern different conditions. They were not promoted into embedded local-sphere decompositions or their impossibility.

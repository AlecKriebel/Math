## Accepted result and published preprint

Accepted as a full affirmative answer to Tunçel’s original 2017 existential problem (OWR14/2017, p.799, Open Problem1). The one-sided algebraic regular cone K={x,y,z≥0:xy²≥z³} has the parameter-100 normal barrier F=-25 log(z(xy²-z³)), with D³F(a)[h,u,u]≤0 for every interior base point, cone direction h and real test vector u. No homogeneous polynomial in the original R³ defines K as its hyperbolicity cone.

Published research note and source/verification package: **[10.5281/zenodo.23440119](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23440119)**. The exact reviewed files and metadata were verified on Zenodo, and the DOI resolves. Google Workspace tracker entry `'Math Puzzles'!A151:D151` was appended and read back exactly.

The cone is credited to [Hildebrand, Theorem3.2 family5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.4814); [Roy–Xiao, Theorem2](https://optimization-online.org/2018/01/6441/) already supplies a different parameter-3 self-concordant barrier on this same cone. The parameter100 is deliberately nonoptimal. The accepted contribution is the explicit negative-curvature certificate together with the arbitrary-polynomial hyperbolicity obstruction. The broader every-regular-cone barrier question is outside the theorem. No worldwide novelty or priority certification is claimed.

## Exact claim and falsifiable success criterion

For every prime p, characteristic-p field k and compact Lie G with non-p-nilpotent finite component group, set X=(BG)^p-completed and A=C_*(ΩX;k). The canonical k-cellular approximation of A must induce zero in every homotopy degree. Completion precedes loops. The target is Greenlees OWR9/2026 Q2.2, printed p594, without substituting ordinary G-chains. No claim of derived nullity is permitted.

## Independently checked deduction

1. A nonzero finite-dimensional ordinary right ideal I has a finite coproduct-coefficient space J containing I. Coassociativity makes J a right coideal. The antipode-cancellation formula in the report proves J is a right ideal. The Hopf-module conclusion is valid without a bijective antipode. Independently, a nonzero right coideal J has ε(J) nonzero, and then multiplication of its restricted coproduct with the antipode puts1 in J, proving J=A. Opposite-and-coopposite treats left ideals with the same antipode.
2. In odd characteristic parity bosonization doubles both dimensions, preserves graded one-sided ideals, and gives the displayed sign. In characteristic2 forgetting grading is legitimate. Thus infinite-dimensional graded Hopf algebras have no nonzero finite-dimensional graded one-sided ideal.
3. A compact proxy K for k gives Loc(K)=Loc(k). Compact Morita theory and the filtered finite-semi-free presentation factor each homogeneous class of any cellular M through P in thick(K). The dependency closure is finite by finite branching plus absence of infinite decreasing well-ordered labels. Since K is finitely built from k, P has finite TOTAL homology, not merely degreewise finite homology.
4. For each A-linear P→A its homology image is a finite-dimensional graded left ideal. Hence it is zero. Applying class by class proves the asserted homotopy vanishing. No detection of morphisms by homology or compactness of k is assumed.
5. DGI publisher-version §5.7, printed p382, explicitly gives proxy-smallness of completed loop chains for any compact Lie G over Fp. Scalar extension of the compact proxy works for any field extension Fp→k; chains commute with this flat extension. No cochain-dual interchange is used.
6. Loop homology has the graded Hopf structure even when the loop space is disconnected. Ishiguro Prop1.3(a), printed p199, gives p-compactness⇒p-nilpotent π0G; its contrapositive gives infinite total homology dimension. No invalid converse is used.

For Karpenkov's historical eight-sail Klein model, this unrefereed AI-assisted manuscript proves countably infinitely many GL(3,Z)-inequivalent continued fractions in every fixed totally real cubic field. Its independent internal AI mathematical, source, and computational audit found no required mathematical correction.

- The actual eight-sail union recovers the defining hyperplanes, their rational multiplication algebra, and an integral multiplier order. The recovered-order trace discriminant is geometric and must not be confused with an operator-polynomial discriminant.
- The lattices Z + f O_K have invariant f⁴ disc(K), proving pairwise inequivalence for all positive integers f. Every full lattice has an irreducible positive-eigenvalue SL(3,Z) realization.
- The exact lattice criterion is J = a tau(I), for a nonzero field scalar and a genuine field automorphism. It includes all full lattices and orders, without assuming invertibility over a nonmaximal order.

The finite conductor quotient is a structural reduction, not a supplied turnkey algorithm for general class-group and fundamental-unit computations.

## Accepted PARTIAL result

Problem 30000515 / OWR-1276-008 remains unresolved by this work. The complete authored partial proof and full substantive independent internal AI-assisted mathematical audit are included. No full-resolution, counterexample, novelty or priority claim is made.

For a numerical Godeaux surface, every bicanonical member is 2-connected if and only if Picard torsion is zero. The equivalent conditions imply integral H_1=0 and a perfect topological fundamental group. Noether's inequality bounds every connected finite unramified cover of any numerical Godeaux surface by degree six. Perfectness then rules out every nontrivial finite quotient, and hence every connected finite cover. The profinite completion and algebraic fundamental group are trivial.

The exact residual is an infinite, finitely presented, perfect topological fundamental group with no nontrivial finite quotient or proper finite-index subgroup, under all the original hypotheses including four distinct simple bicanonical base points. Such a group is neither excluded nor constructed. Finiteness, residual finiteness, a universal vanishing-cycle calculation and exhaustive smooth-deformation classification are not established. The four-base-point hypothesis is unnecessary for the accepted partial deductions.

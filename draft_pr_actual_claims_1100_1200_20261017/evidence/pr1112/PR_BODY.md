## Scoped counterexample, original question unresolved

The normal singular quadric threefold X = V(x0*x3 − x1*x2) in complex P^4
has numerical surface space R^2, but its positive cone of integral
Ottem-ample surfaces is exactly R_{>0}(1,1), which is not open in that
full space. The same ray occurs with only smooth or lci generators.
Requiring smooth generators does not make the ambient variety smooth.
The counterexample has codimension one.

The complete proof and mathematical audit also establish the normal
codimension-one criterion: for a normal integral projective T over C of
dimension at least two, the cone generated by all integral ample
codimension-one centers is the image of Amp(T) in the numerical divisor
cycle space, and is open there exactly when the Cartier-to-Weil map is
surjective. This general criterion does not impose smooth generators.

The original OWR introduction uses smooth subvarieties of complex
projective manifolds. This example refutes a broader singular-projective
formulation, and does not solve the original smooth-ambient problem or
a version separately requiring codimension at least two. No novelty,
priority or worldwide current-status claim is made.
Original problem, OWR 43/2017, Problem 1(1), printed p. 2633:
https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2017/43

## Accepted partial theorem; original question remains open

For every k>=2, an explicit polynomial-size finite simple directed graph
with k distinct ordered terminals and unit edge costs has integral optimum
C_k-k^2 and an original edge path-distance LP vector of cost C_k/3, where
C_k = sum_{l=1}^k (k-l+1) floor(k/l). The lower bounds 3(1-k^2/C_k)
tend to 3; the actual gaps have liminf at least 3. Convergence of the actual
gaps to 3, exact LP optima, unbounded gaps and a universal constant upper bound
are not proved. The original constant-versus-unbounded question remains OPEN.

The full proof and mathematical audit preserve the characterization of every
retained interval family, the exact auxiliary optimum, internal terminal visits,
all arbitrary final edge cuts, finite protected bundles, LP feasibility,
harmonic asymptotics and polynomial size. No new mathematics is introduced
by this edition. A prior packaging-only checker correction was authored by
the independent audit, not the original proof author; mathematical proof,
source records and mathematical certificates were unchanged.

Original question: Chandra Chekuri, Oberwolfach Report 50/2018, p. 3013:
https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46772
Bérczi, Chandrasekaran, Király and Madan report general-k bounds ceil(log2 k)
above and 2(1-1/k) below; the tight sqrt(2) result concerns three terminals:
https://doi.org/10.1007/s10107-019-01417-9
Aprile, Drescher, Fiorini and Huynh's earlier gap-3 context for the general
induced-P3 covering LP is credited:
https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-73879-2_24
No novelty or priority is claimed.

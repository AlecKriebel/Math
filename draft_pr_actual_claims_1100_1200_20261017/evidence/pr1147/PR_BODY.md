## Accepted partial result: exact all-order homometric flowers

Adds the complete authored mathematical report and substantive independent audit for problem 30002326 / OWR-12481-016. For every integer n >= 20736, the explicit connected flower G_n satisfies

h(G_n) = floor((n - floor(log_2(log_12 n)) + 3)/4).

The proof retains all construction details, odd-clique domination, both cone root states, the j=0 boundary and exact integer rounding. The entire flower class has asymptotic minimum ratio 1/4. This is PARTIAL: neither direction of the global sublinearity question is resolved, and no global positive linear lower bound is claimed. Necessary degree, diameter and two-distance restrictions are retained.

The operational quantity is the minimum over connected n-vertex graphs of the maximum common size of disjoint ambient-distance-homometric pairs. Equivalence with a universal exact-size convention is not assumed. The cone calculation requires k >= 2. Localization is for pairs of size at least four, with the sharp size-three exception preserved. The revised [Axenovich–Özkahya paper](https://web.cs.hacettepe.edu.tr/~ozkahya/pub/homsets.pdf) already states threshold four; no new correction to that revised paper is claimed.

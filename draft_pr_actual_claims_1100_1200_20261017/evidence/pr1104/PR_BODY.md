## Accepted partial; general linear-size question unresolved

Preserves the explicit Boolean O(n log n) rank-parity construction, full gate
accounting and O(log^2 n) depth, linear-overhead all-terminal fan-out
conversion, threshold/count gadgets, ordinary and balanced bit-shift
restrictions, and balanced-promise equivalence with the required output mask.
Unconditionally the gap remains Omega(n) versus O(n log n).

Theta(n log n) is claimed ONLY under the undirected k-pairs conjecture in the
precise form used by AFKL, Lower Bounds for Multiplication via Network Coding,
Theorem 2: https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.ICALP.2019.10
The prior conditional stable-compaction barrier is credited to Asharov–Lin–Shi:
https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.09884
No novelty or priority is claimed.

The explicit three-wire example disproves Regan's actual Theorem 1 stability
assertion for 0,1<2. The comparator lower bound is retained with its separate
0–1-principle/counting proof; it is not a general Boolean-circuit lower bound.

## Accepted partial; universal per-set target unresolved

For a planar point set S in general position, let E(p) be the set of
original hull vertices whose removal makes interior point p extreme. Under
the explicit hypothesis that all these sets are nonempty, the complete
fixed-edge injection proves
ppt(S) >= (product over p of |E(p)|) t(S).
The inverse recovers both the input triangulation and witness choices.

A six-point coordinate wheel has no contained pointed pseudotriangulation.
It refutes universal containment-only maps and Hall arguments on that
relation. It does not refute the requested counting inequality: the audit
independently proves t=11 and ppt=25 analytically.
The one-interior fiber identity and general weighted-refinement identity
remain in full. The necessary global compensation inequality is unproved.

Known results are credited precisely: the survey characterization and
Streinu attribution; Aichholzer–Orden–Santos–Speckmann family results;
Randall–Rote–Santos–Snoeyink's Catalan wheel difference; and the prior
containment obstruction recorded by TOPP 50 and O'Rourke's 2002 column.

## Accepted bounded partial result

For reduced degrees at most three over F_p and p>=11, the complete
magnitude classification includes constant/constant, nonconstant
linear/linear, quadratic/quadratic, cubics related by g=f(uX+v)+w,
and the linear/pure-cubic overlap exactly when p=2 mod 3.
One nonzero-frequency equality is equivalent to all nonzero frequencies.
All zero-sum, degenerate, cross-degree and negative-sign cases are retained.

For rational polynomials of degree at most three and all sufficiently large
primes, the first four classes remain, with rational u,v,w for cubics.
Chebotarev is used only for pure-cubic rational descent. One prime or merely
infinitely many primes does not imply that characteristic-zero conclusion.

The full arbitrary-degree target remains OPEN in both prime regimes.
X^2 versus X^6 obstructs universal affine rigidity at arbitrarily large
primes 2 modulo 3, but not at all sufficiently large primes. X^4 versus
-4X^4 agrees at every odd prime and obstructs rational input-affine rigidity
with output sign restricted to +/-1, while remaining complex-affine.
The p=5 complementary-fiber example is a boundary control; no sharpness
of the sufficient p>=11 bound is claimed.

The complete proof is byte-for-byte unchanged. The full substantive audit
is preserved. The required source-only correction is applied in the source
review: arXiv-v1 Morse Definition 1.6 also requires f to have simple roots
and deg(f')=d-1, beside squarefree f' and distinct critical values. Its
tension with arbitrary output translation is documented, unresolved and
not used in the accepted proof. The symmetric Sidon-Morse option's
odd-polynomial condition and Theorem 6.3 characteristic restrictions remain.

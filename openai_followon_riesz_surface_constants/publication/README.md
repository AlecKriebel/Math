# Hypersingular Riesz-energy constants on surfaces from triangular-lattice universal optimality

Author: Alec Kriebel. ORCID: https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X.
Date: October 6, 2026. Research note / preprint.

For every real s>2, the ordered-pair minimal Riesz-energy constant C_(s,2) equals the nonzero-site Epstein sum of the covolume-one triangular lattice. On every smooth compact embedded surface, including smooth boundary, with positive area A and ambient Euclidean distance, the leading coefficient is zeta_Lambda(s) A^(-s/2). For the unit sphere the coefficient is zeta_Lambda(s)/(4*pi)^(s/2).

This is a consequence note. OpenAI's recently released planar universal-energy theorem is the substantive new input. Kuijlaars–Saff posed the sphere coefficient conjecture (unordered pairs); Brauchart–Hardin–Saff recorded the general lattice-constant formula. Hardin–Saff proved area universality, with the corrected argument used here. Hardin–Saff–Simanek and Hardin–Leblé–Saff–Serfaty established the finite/periodic/thermodynamic reductions. We present a self-contained gap-periodization proof in exact centered-disk, ordered-pair and covolume conventions. No independent base breakthrough or first-public-priority claim is made.

No microscopic crystallization, uniqueness of finite minimizers, new s<=2 conclusion, other-dimensional constant, or subleading expansion is claimed.

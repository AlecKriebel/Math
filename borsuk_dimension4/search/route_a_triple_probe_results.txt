Activation pattern
------------------
One internal maximum I_A and all cross maxima C_AB,C_AC,C_BC are tied.
Radii are 1,t,u.  With n_i the squared orbit norms, M_i the within-orbit
diameter squares, and c_ij the cross minimum inner products:

  M_A = n_A+n_B t^2-2c_AB t
      = n_A+n_C u^2-2c_AC u
      = n_B t^2+n_C u^2-2c_BC tu.

  delta_B = c_AB^2-n_B(n_A-M_A)
  delta_C = c_AC^2-n_C(n_A-M_A)
  t = (c_AB +/- sqrt(delta_B))/n_B
  u = (c_AC +/- sqrt(delta_C))/n_C

After substitution, the last equality is

  M_A-2n_A+2c_AB t+2c_AC u-2c_BC tu = 0.

Exact method
------------
Squarefree-decompose delta_B and delta_C.  Expand the last expression in the
Q-basis terms 1, sqrt(p), sqrt(q), sqrt(squarefree(pq)); combine coincident
terms and require every rational coefficient to vanish.  Determine signs of
A+B sqrt(p) by direct signs when A,B agree and by comparing A^2 with pB^2
when they oppose.  This certifies t,u>0 and M_B t^2<=M_A,
M_C u^2<=M_A exactly.

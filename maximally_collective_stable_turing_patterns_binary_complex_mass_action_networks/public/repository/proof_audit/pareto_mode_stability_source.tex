\section*{All-mode stability for $rL^2\ge1/3$}
For a mode with scalar damping $t$, row scaling gives
\[
 \det(\lambda I-H_m(L)(A_m-t\Delta_m))
 =\det H_m(L)\,\{Q_m(\lambda,t)F(\lambda,t)-G(\lambda,t)\}.
\]
The fixed boundary factors are
\[
 g_1=\lambda+2+\frac{23}{63}t,
 \quad g_m=\lambda+5+\frac17t,
 \quad g_Z=\lambda+4+\frac{16}{45}t,
\]
\[
 F=g_1g_mg_Z-4g_1-4g_m+g_Z,
 \qquad G=g_Z(4g_1+g_m)-36.
\]
For $2\le i\le m-1$,
\[
 g_i=\frac{\lambda}{h_i}+1+\frac{t}{K_i}
 =\frac{K_{i-1}}{K_i}(1+L\lambda)+\frac{t-1}{K_i}.
\]
If $t\ge1$ and $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\ge0$, then
\[
 |Q_m|^2\ge\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2|1+L\lambda|^{2r}
 \ge\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2
 \left(1+2rLx+rL^2y^2\right),
\]
where $x=\operatorname{Re}\lambda$.  If $rL^2\ge1/3$, this dominates the
lower bound with coefficient $1/3$ in $y^2$.

The exact polynomial
\[
 \left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2
 \left(1+A x+\frac13y^2\right)|F|^2-|G|^2,
 \qquad A>0,
\]
has 84 nonzero monomials in $(x,y^2,t-1)$.  Every coefficient is a
polynomial in $A$ with nonnegative rational coefficients.  Its coefficients
of $x$, $y^2$, and $t-1$ are strictly positive, so equality occurs only at
$(\lambda,t)=(0,1)$.  This proves a simple isolated first-mode zero and
Hurwitz stability for every $t>1$.

For the homogeneous mode, each interior factor satisfies
\[
 \left|1+L\frac{K_{i-1}}{K_i}\lambda\right|
 \ge|1+L\lambda|.
\]
The corresponding fixed boundary polynomials are
\[
 P=\lambda^4+12\lambda^3+42\lambda^2+47\lambda+16,
 \qquad R=5\lambda^2+33\lambda+16.
\]
The polynomial
\[
 \left(1+A x+\frac13y^2\right)|P|^2-|R|^2
\]
has 34 nonzero monomials, again with nonnegative coefficient polynomials in
$A$ and strict equality only at the origin.  Hence the only closed-right-
half-plane homogeneous eigenvalue is the conservation zero.

Both coefficient tables are in `pareto_all_m_certificate.json` and are
regenerated independently.

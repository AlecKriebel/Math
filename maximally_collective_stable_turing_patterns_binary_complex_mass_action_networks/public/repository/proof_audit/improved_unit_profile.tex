\section*{Improved exact critical mode}
Define
\[
K_i=91m-181-i.
\]
Choose
\[
r_1=1,
\qquad
r_i=-\frac{K_i}{63(m-2)}\quad(2\le i\le m-1),
\qquad
r_m=-\frac29,
\qquad
r_Z=\frac5{14},
\]
and
\[
d_1=\frac{23}{63},
\qquad
d_i=\frac1{K_i}\quad(2\le i\le m-1),
\qquad
d_m=\frac17,
\qquad
d_Z=\frac{16}{45}.
\]
Since $K_{m-1}=90(m-2)>0$, every diffusivity is positive.  The chain identities
$K_{i-1}=K_i+1$ give the interior rows of $(A_m-D_m)r=0$; the four boundary
rows reduce to exact rational identities.

Moreover,
\[
\Theta(I,D_m)=\frac{16}{45}-8\sum_{j=2}^{m-1}\frac1{K_j}
\ge \frac{16}{45}-\frac{8(m-2)}{90(m-2)}=\frac4{15}>0.
\]
Thus the one-bad-minor theorem gives a unique positive stationary threshold;
the displayed nullvector fixes it at $s_*=1$.

The minimum and maximum diffusivities are
\[
d_{\min}=d_2=\frac1{91m-183},
\qquad
d_{\max}=d_1=\frac{23}{63},
\]
so
\[
\boxed{\chi_D(D_m)=\frac{23(91m-183)}{63}.}
\]

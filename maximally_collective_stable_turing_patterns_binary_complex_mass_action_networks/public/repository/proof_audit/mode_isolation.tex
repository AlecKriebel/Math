\section*{Exact first-mode isolation}
For $\lambda\in\mathbb C$ and scalar damping $t$, define
\[
g_1=\lambda+2+\frac{23}{63}t,
\quad
g_i=\lambda+1+\frac{t}{K_i}\ (2\le i\le m-1),
\]
\[
g_m=\lambda+5+\frac17t,
\qquad
g_Z=\lambda+4+\frac{16}{45}t,
\qquad
Q_m=\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}g_i.
\]
Sparse chain elimination gives
\[
\det(\lambda I-A_m+tD_m)=Q_mF-G,
\]
\[
F=g_1g_mg_Z-4g_1-4g_m+g_Z,
\qquad
G=g_Z(4g_1+g_m)-36.
\]
At $t=1$,
\[
\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}(1+d_i)=\frac{K_1}{K_{m-1}}=\frac{91}{90}.
\]
Write $\lambda=x+iy$, $z=y^2$, and $t=1+s$ with $x,z,s\ge0$.  Since
$1+d_{m-1}\le91/90$,
\[
|Q_m|^2\ge\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2+z.
\]
The exact 77-term source polynomial is
\[
E_{77}(x,z,s)=
\left[\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2+z\right]
|F(x+i\sqrt z,1+s)|^2-|G(x+i\sqrt z,1+s)|^2.
\]
It expands into 77 strictly positive monomials in $(x,z,s)$ and has no constant
term.  In particular its coefficients of $x,z,s$ are respectively
\[
\frac{1086412028}{10333575},
\quad
\frac{32638690751329}{65101522500},
\quad
\frac{148103696}{10333575}.
\]
Thus equality is possible only at $(\lambda,t)=(0,1)$.  Therefore
there is no nonzero closed-right-half-plane eigenvalue at $t=1$.  Multiplicity
at the equality point follows from the exact identity
\[
 \mathfrak h_m=\sum_{j=1}^{m-2}K_j^{-1},\qquad
 \left.\frac{d}{d\lambda}\det(\lambda I-A_m+D_m)\right|_{\lambda=0}
 =\frac{7043400m-13600927-7043400\mathfrak h_m}{255150}
 =-\frac{163}{45}\ell_m^Tr_m>0.
\]
Hence $A_m-D_m$ has one algebraically simple zero with one-dimensional kernel
and cokernel, and $A_m-tD_m$ is Hurwitz for every $t>1$.  The complete 77 coefficients are
stored in `modulus_certificate.json` and regenerated independently.

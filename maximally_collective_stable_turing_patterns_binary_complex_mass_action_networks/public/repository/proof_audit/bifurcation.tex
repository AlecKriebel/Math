\section*{Primary crossing summary}
The homogeneous Jacobian is stable on $c^\perp$ by the inherited 35-term
closed-right-half-plane certificate.  The improved 77-term certificate proves
that the first nonzero Neumann mode has exactly one simple zero at $t=1$ and
all larger scalar dampings are Hurwitz.  The exact left and right vectors give
nonzero transversality.  The one-bad-minor theorem gives a positive real
eigenvalue for every $0<t<1$.  Hence the crossing is primary, simple,
stationary, transverse, and isolated from all higher modes.

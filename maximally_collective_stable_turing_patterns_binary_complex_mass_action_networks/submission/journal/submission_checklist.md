- [ ] Verify ORCID `0009-0001-9320-500X`, the immutable version 1.0.9 release URL, the Zenodo concept DOI, and the release-record version DOI in the portal.
- [ ] Approve funding, competing-interest, exclusivity, and AI-assistance wording.

      continuous line numbering, visible keywords, and MSC codes.

Supplementary Materials Index

Article: Exact Diffusion Design for Maximally Collective Stable Turing Patterns
in Binary-Complex Mass-Action Networks
Author: Alec Kriebel

1. supplement.pdf
   Description: Mathematical supplement S1-S11 containing the reaction family,
   exact flux and rank calculations, generalized diffusion-ray theorem,
   omission-minor formulas, nonlinear normal-form calculations, signed and
   coefficientwise symbolic certificates, near-threshold construction, and
   robustness argument.
   Justification: Supplies detailed derivations and exact certificates that
   support the theorem statements while keeping the main article readable.

2. final_release_data.zip
   Description: Open data archive containing exact finite-instance data,
   generated coefficient tables, simulation outputs, and figure-source data.
   Justification: Allows readers to regenerate and audit all displayed finite
   values and numerical illustrations. Numerical illustrations are not used as
   proof of any theorem.

3. source_package.zip
   Description: Self-contained LaTeX source for the line-numbered SIADS review
   manuscript and supplement, including the cited bibliography, generated
   tables, and included figures.
   Justification: Enables independent compilation and checks that the submitted
   PDFs agree with the maintained source.

The exact proof verifiers and one-command replay are also preserved in the
immutable version 1.0.10 repository release. Archived versions share
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21753404; the exact preceding version 1.0.9 is
https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22478273.

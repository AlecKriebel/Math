# bioRxiv author checklist

- [ ] Approve title, author name, email, and affiliation.
- [ ] Approve the AI-assistance disclosure.
- [ ] Confirm funding and competing-interest statements.
- [ ] Verify that ORCID `0009-0001-9320-500X` is entered correctly in the portal.
- [ ] Verify that the immutable version 1.0.6 release, tagged-source URL, and Zenodo concept DOI resolve.
- [ ] Confirm Systems Biology / New Results selection.
- [ ] Upload `manuscript.pdf`, `supplement.pdf`, and `source_package.zip`.
- [ ] Verify equations and figures in the bioRxiv proof.
- [ ] Do not imply experimental validation or natural biochemical occurrence.

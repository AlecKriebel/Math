# Terminology crosswalk

| Term | Meaning in flagship | Excluded ambiguity |
|---|---|---|
| binary-complex | both source and target complexes have total molecularity at most two | not merely source-bimolecular |
| principal subsystem | numerical principal Jacobian block on a species set | not a projected reaction network |
| all-spectrum localization | every smaller principal block is Hurwitz | stronger than absence of positive real roots |
| stationary diffusion threshold | a simple zero eigenvalue on a positive diagonal diffusion ray | separate from wave/Turing--Hopf instability |
| semipositive conservation law | a nonzero nonnegative left-kernel vector with at least one zero entry | not a positive conservation law or conservative CRNT network |
| stable pattern | local exponential asymptotic stability in the fixed-mass $H^1$ phase space | not global attraction or far-from-onset persistence |
| exponent-optimal trade-off | both contrasts achieve $\Theta(\sqrt n)$ and no simultaneous exponent below $1/2$ is possible | no claim of optimal constants or full Pareto frontier |

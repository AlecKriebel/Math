\section*{Uniform supercritical sign along the scaling family}
Let
\[
 \mathcal H_m=\sum_{j=1}^{m-2}\frac1{K_j}.
\]
Then
\[
 \frac1{91}\le\mathcal H_m<\frac1{90}.
\]
Let $w_0^{\rm ref}$ denote the reference zero-mode correction satisfying
$c^Tw_0^{\rm ref}=0$, and let $\rho$ generate $\ker A_m$.  The corrected
zero mode for the physical fixed-mass class is
\[
 w_0(L)=w_0^{\rm ref}+\tau_m(L)\rho,
\]
\[
 \tau_m(L)=-\frac{(H_m(L)^{-1}c)^Tw_0^{\rm ref}}
 {(H_m(L)^{-1}c)^T\rho}.
\]
The second harmonic is unchanged, because the stationary equation inside the
invertible row factor $H_m(L)$ is unchanged.

With the inherited normalization of $r_m$ and $\ell_m$,
\[
 \ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m
 =-\frac{485873}{924210}-\frac{11180}{1467}L(m-2)<0.
\]
The cubic numerator has the exact decomposition
\[
 N_m(L)=N_m^{\rm ref}+\tau_m(L)S_m,
\]
where
\[
 S_m=-\frac4{462105}(1760850\mathcal H_m-10253),
 \qquad -\frac1{10}<S_m<0.
\]
The reference certificate gives
\[
 N_m^{\rm ref}>\frac1{100}.
\]
Indeed $N_m^{\rm ref}=R_m+C_m\mathcal H_m$, with $C_m<0$; substituting the
upper harmonic bound and writing $m-2=u+1$ produces the positive coefficient
list
\[
\begin{split}
&(3790502986637265684840,
17978298402717728137711,
33955060177057334483573,\\
&31899843595051464379292,
14895188986348368035728,
2762610207555043720056).
\end{split}
\]

Put $r=m-2$.  The exact rational expression for the gauge is
\[
 \tau=-\frac{
1494249120\mathcal H Lr^2-69786990\mathcal H Lr
+108738630Lr^2+1214388Lr-8521L-125249670r^2+1031940r}
{15876(8r-1)(32760\mathcal H Lr+32760Lr^2+4L-4095r)}.
\]
On $L\ge1/\sqrt{3r}$ its derivatives with respect to both $\mathcal H$ and
$L$ are negative.  Hence its maximum occurs at
$\mathcal H=1/91$ and $L=1/\sqrt{3r}$.  After writing
$y=\sqrt{3r}$, the inequality $\tau<1/20$ reduces to two negative
polynomials.  For $r=1$ use $\sqrt3<7/4$; for $r\ge2$ use
$\sqrt{3r}\le5r/4$.  The independent verifier checks the exact resulting
integer inequalities.

If $\tau\le0$, then $\tau S_m\ge0$.  If $0<\tau<1/20$, then
$\tau S_m>-1/200$.  Therefore
\[
 N_m(L)>\frac1{200}>0.
\]
The normal-form coefficients are
\[
 \eta_m(L)=\frac{\ell_m^T\Delta_m r_m}
 {\ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m}>0,
\]
\[
 c_m(L)=\frac{N_m(L)}{\ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m}<0.
\]
Thus every member with $L\ge1/\sqrt{3r}$ is supercritical.

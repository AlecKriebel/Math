\section*{Homogeneous relative stability}
At $a=b=1$,
\[
\det(\lambda I-A_m)=\lambda q_m(\lambda),
\qquad
q_m=(\lambda+1)q_{m-1}+5\lambda^2+33\lambda+16,
\]
with $q_3=(\lambda+7)(\lambda^2+5\lambda+2)$.  Equivalently,
\[
\lambda q_m=(\lambda+1)^{m-3}P(\lambda)-R(\lambda),
\]
\[
P=\lambda^4+12\lambda^3+42\lambda^2+47\lambda+16,
\qquad
R=5\lambda^2+33\lambda+16.
\]
For $\lambda=x+iy$ with $x\ge0$, the polynomial
\[
|1+\lambda|^2|P(\lambda)|^2-|R(\lambda)|^2
\]
is a sum of 35 nonnegative monomials in $x$ and $y^2$, with equality only at
$\lambda=0$.  The case $m=3$ is immediate from the displayed factorization
of $q_3$.  If $m\ge4$ and a nonzero closed-right-half-plane root of $q_m$
existed, then
\[
 |1+\lambda|^{2(m-3)}|P(\lambda)|^2=|R(\lambda)|^2.
\]
But $|1+\lambda|\ge1$, so the left side is at least
$|1+\lambda|^2|P(\lambda)|^2$, strictly larger than the right side by the
35-term certificate.  This is a contradiction.  Finally,
$q_m(0)=16m-34>0$, so the conservation zero is simple and
$A_m|_{c^\perp}$ is Hurwitz for every $m\ge3$.

\section*{Reaction family reconstructed from the indexed list}
For every integer $m\geq3$, let $\widehat N_m$ have species
$X_1,\ldots,X_m,Z$ and the $m+2$ indexed reactions
\[
0\to X_1,
\qquad
X_1+X_i\to X_1+X_{i+1}\quad(2\leq i\leq m-2),
\]
\[
X_1+X_{m-1}\to2X_m,
\qquad
2X_m\to X_2,
\qquad
2Z\rightleftarrows X_1+X_m.
\]
The middle chain is empty when $m=3$.  Every source and target has total
molecularity at most two.

With long-circuit flux $a>0$, reversible-pair flux $b>0$, and unit
equilibrium, direct summation of reaction vectors times source monomials gives
\begin{align*}
\dot x_1={}&a-a x_1x_{m-1}+b z^2-bx_1x_m,\\
\dot x_2={}&-a x_1x_2+a x_m^2,\\
\dot x_i={}&a x_1x_{i-1}-a x_1x_i\quad(3\leq i\leq m-1),\\
\dot x_m={}&2a x_1x_{m-1}-2a x_m^2+bz^2-bx_1x_m,\\
\dot z={}&-2bz^2+2bx_1x_m.
\end{align*}
Substitution of $x_1=\cdots=x_m=z=1$ makes every right-hand side zero.
No earlier generated vector field is used in this derivation.

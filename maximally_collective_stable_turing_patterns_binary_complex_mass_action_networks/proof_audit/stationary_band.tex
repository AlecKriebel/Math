\section*{Critical scale}
The unique threshold $s_*$ is the unique positive root of the strictly
increasing polynomial
\[
q_D(s)=\beta_1+\beta_2s+\cdots+\beta_{m+1}s^m.
\]
It obeys the scaling law
\[
s_*(\gamma D)=\frac{s_*(D)}{\gamma}\qquad(\gamma>0)
\]
and the exact upper bounds
\[
s_*\le \min_{2\le k\le m+1}
\left(\frac{-\beta_1}{\beta_k}\right)^{1/(k-1)}.
\]
The monotone polynomial gives certified rational upper and lower enclosures by
bisection.  At the selected improved profile, $s_*=1$ exactly and a separate
77-term certificate proves stability for all $s>1$.

\section*{All-dimensional coefficient signs}
Let
\[
H_m=\sum_{j=2}^{m-2}\frac1{227m-451-3j}.
\]
Direct contraction yields closed formulas for $\ell^Tr$ and $\ell^TD_mr$.  The bound
\[
0\le H_m\le\frac{m-3}{224m-445}
\]
reduces their signs to shifted polynomials with positive coefficients, giving $\ell^Tr<0$, $\ell^TD_mr<0$, and hence $\eta_m>0$.

The cubic numerator telescopes to $N_m=R_m+C_mH_m$.  The rational functions $R_m$ and $-C_m$ have positive denominators and numerators that become coefficientwise positive after $m=u+3$.  Because $C_m<0$, substitution of the upper bound on $H_m$ gives
\[
N_m\ge\frac{G_6(m)}{566562816000(m-2)(8m-17)(224m-445)Q(m)},
\]
where $G_6(u+3)$ has seven strictly positive coefficients.  Thus $N_m>0$ while $\ell^Tr<0$, so $c_m<0$ for every $m\ge3$.  The complete coefficients are supplied in the supplement and machine-readable certificate.

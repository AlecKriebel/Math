\section*{Principal-minor diffusion-ray theorem}
For a real $n\times n$ matrix $J$, set
\[
 a_I=(-1)^{|I|}\det J_{I,I},\qquad a_\varnothing=1.
\]
Assume
\[
 \det J=0,\qquad a_I>0\ (|I|\le n-2),\qquad
 \sum_{|I|=n-1}a_I>0.
\]
The last condition follows, in particular, when zero is algebraically simple
and every nonzero eigenvalue of $J$ has negative real part: it is the
coefficient of $\lambda$ in $\det(\lambda I-J)$.

For $D=\operatorname{diag}(d_1,\ldots,d_n)\succ0$, principal-minor expansion
gives
\[
 p_D(s)=\det(sD-J)
 =s\{\beta_1(D)+\beta_2(D)s+\cdots+\beta_n(D)s^{n-1}\},
\]
where
\[
 \beta_k(D)=\sum_{|I|=n-k}a_I\prod_{j\notin I}d_j.
\]
For $k\ge2$, all summands have $|I|\le n-2$, hence $\beta_k(D)>0$.
Therefore the bracket is strictly increasing on $s\ge0$ and has a unique
positive zero exactly when $\beta_1(D)<0$.  That zero is simple.

For the real-eigenvalue band, fix $s>0$ and expand
\[
 \chi_s(\lambda)=\det(\lambda I+sD-J)
 =\sum_Ia_I\prod_{j\notin I}(\lambda+sd_j).
\]
In $\chi_s'(\lambda)$, every term with $|I|\le n-2$ is positive for
$\lambda\ge0$.  The total contribution from $|I|=n-1$ is the positive number
$\sum_{|I|=n-1}a_I$, and the full-order term vanishes.  Thus
$\chi_s'(\lambda)>0$ for $\lambda\ge0$.  It follows that
\[
 J-sD\text{ has a positive real eigenvalue}
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 p_D(s)<0
 \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
 0<s<s_*.
\]
The theorem concerns positive real eigenvalues; it does not exclude an
unstable nonreal pair.

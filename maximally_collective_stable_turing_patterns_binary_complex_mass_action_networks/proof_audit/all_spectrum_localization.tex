\section*{Topology-wide all-spectrum localization}
\begin{theorem}
For every $m\geq3$, $a,b>0$, $H\succ0$, and principal species set $I$ with
$0<|I|<m$, the matrix $J_m(a,b,H)_{I,I}$ is Hurwitz.  The block on
$C_m=\{X_1,\ldots,X_m\}$ has a positive real eigenvalue.  Hence, for every
positive realization of the topology,
\[
\min\left\{|I|:\varnothing\ne I\subseteq[n],\
\alpha(J_{I,I})>0\right\}=m=n-1.
\]
\end{theorem}

\paragraph{Strongly connected blocks.}
In the directed graph $j\to i$ when $J_{ij}\neq0$, every nontrivial strongly
connected component of an induced subgraph on fewer than $m$ vertices is one
of
\[
\{X_1,\ldots,X_{m-1}\},\qquad
\{X_2,\ldots,X_m\},
\]
or a principal subset of $\{X_1,X_m,Z\}$.  All remaining diagonal blocks are
negative singletons.  A chain vertex can return to the boundary only after a
complete long cycle has been included; joining that cycle to an additional
boundary vertex requires at least $m$ vertices.

For the base case $m=3$, direct inspection gives the two complete long cycles
$\{X_1,X_2\}$ and $\{X_2,X_3\}$; every other nontrivial strongly connected
component is a principal subgraph of $\{X_1,X_3,Z\}$.  The chain argument
below is therefore needed only for $m\ge4$.

For the first long cycle, a root with $\Re\lambda\geq0$ would satisfy
\[
\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}|\lambda+d_i h_i|
 =a^{m-1}\prod_{i=1}^{m-1}h_i,
\quad d_1=a+b,\,d_i=a\;(i\ge2).
\]
The left side is at least $(a+b)a^{m-2}\prod h_i$, strictly larger than the
right side.  For the second cycle, the diagonal product is
$(4a+b)a^{m-2}\prod h_i$, while the cycle product is
$4a^{m-1}\prod h_i$; the same contradiction applies.

On $\{X_1,X_m,Z\}$ the characteristic polynomial is
$\lambda^3+c_1\lambda^2+c_2\lambda+c_3$, where
\begin{align*}
c_1={}&ah_1+4ah_m+bh_1+bh_m+4bh_Z,\\
c_2={}&a(4ah_1h_m+7bh_1h_m+4bh_1h_Z+16bh_mh_Z),\\
c_3={}&16a^2bh_1h_mh_Z.
\end{align*}
All are positive.  Direct expansion gives $c_1c_2-c_3=a$ times
\begin{align*}
&4a^2h_1^2h_m+16a^2h_1h_m^2+11abh_1^2h_m+4abh_1^2h_Z
+32abh_1h_m^2\\
&+32abh_1h_mh_Z+64abh_m^2h_Z+7b^2h_1^2h_m+4b^2h_1^2h_Z
+7b^2h_1h_m^2\\
&+48b^2h_1h_mh_Z+16b^2h_1h_Z^2+16b^2h_m^2h_Z
+64b^2h_mh_Z^2,
\end{align*}
so the cubic is Hurwitz.  Its one- and two-species principal blocks are
Hurwitz by negative trace and positive determinant.  Frobenius block
triangularization proves the claim for every smaller principal set.

Finally, sparse determinant expansion along the chain gives
\[
(-1)^m\det J_{C_m,C_m}
=-2a^{m-1}b\prod_{i=1}^m h_i<0.
\]
The characteristic polynomial of this block is negative at $\lambda=0$ and
positive for large positive $\lambda$, so it has a positive real root.

Put $\nu=m-2$.  At unit equilibrium the exact linear diffusion infimum is
$8\nu$, while the improved stable construction has contrast
$23(91m-183)/63=\Theta(m)$.  For the physical trade-off family,
\[
 \chi_D(L)=\frac{23}{63}91\nu L,
 \qquad
 \chi_H(L)=\frac{91\nu-1}{91\nu L}.
\]
At the certified lower endpoint, $L_0(1)=1/\sqrt3$ and
$L_0(\nu)=\sqrt5/(2\sqrt\nu)$ for $\nu\ge2$, so both contrasts are
$\Theta(\sqrt m)$.  The topology-specific necessary bound for stationary
crossings implies $\max(\chi_D,\chi_H)>\sqrt{8\nu}$, proving exponent
optimality only among stationary-crossing realizations of $\widehat N_m$.

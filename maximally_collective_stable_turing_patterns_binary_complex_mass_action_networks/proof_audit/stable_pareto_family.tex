\section*{Stable Pareto theorem}
For every integer $m\ge3$, write $r=m-2$ and let
\[
 L_0=\frac1{\sqrt{3r}},
 \qquad
 L_1=\frac{90r}{90r+1}.
\]
For every $L\in[L_0,L_1]$, the frozen binary-complex topology admits positive
rates, a positive equilibrium, and positive diagonal diffusion coefficients
such that:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the homogeneous equilibrium is stable on its stoichiometric class;
\item the first Neumann mode has one simple transverse stationary zero and all
other eigenvalues stable;
\item all higher modes are stable;
\item the conservation-compatible cubic normal form has $\eta_m(L)>0$ and
$c_m(L)<0$;
\item the two nearby positive patterned equilibria are locally exponentially
asymptotically stable in the fixed-mass $H^1$ phase space;
\item the physical contrasts are
\[
 \chi_D(L)=\frac{23}{63}(91rL),
 \qquad
 \chi_H(L)=\frac{91r-1}{91rL}.
\]
\end{enumerate}
Consequently
\[
 \chi_D(L)\chi_H(L)=\frac{23(91r-1)}{63},
\]
and at $L=L_0$ both contrasts are $\Theta(\sqrt r)$.  Since every stationary
crossing in the topology satisfies $\chi_D\chi_H>8r$, the simultaneous
square-root exponents are optimal.


\section*{An exact equilibrium-scaling family}
Let $r=m-2$ and $K_i=91m-181-i$.  Retain the unit-flux effective reaction
operator $A_m$, its critical vector $r_m$, and the effective diffusion matrix
\[
 \Delta_m=\operatorname{diag}\left(
 \frac{23}{63},\frac1{K_2},\ldots,\frac1{K_{m-1}},
 \frac17,\frac{16}{45}\right).
\]
For a scalar $L>0$ define
\[
 H_m(L)=\operatorname{diag}(h_1,\ldots,h_m,h_Z),
\]
with
\[
 h_1=h_m=h_Z=1,
 \qquad
 h_i=\frac{K_i}{L K_{i-1}}
 \quad(2\le i\le m-1),
\]
and take the physical diffusion matrix
\[
 D_m(L)=H_m(L)\Delta_m.
\]
The positive equilibrium is $x^*=H_m(L)^{-1}\mathbf1$, and choosing each
rate constant as the reciprocal of its source monomial at $x^*$ realizes
unit reaction flux.  Hence every parameter is positive and the physical
Jacobian is $H_m(L)A_m$ in normalized concentration coordinates.

Writing $z=H_m(L)x$, the reaction--diffusion equation becomes
\[
 z_t=H_m(L)\{f_m(z)+D_m^{\rm eff}z_{\xi\xi}\},
 \qquad D_m^{\rm eff}=\Delta_m.
\]
Thus the stationary bracket and its critical right vector are unchanged by
$H_m(L)$.  The dynamic left critical vector is
\[
 q_m(L)=H_m(L)^{-1}\ell_m.
\]
The fixed physical conservation class becomes the normalized constraint with
left vector $H_m(L)^{-1}c$.


\section*{All-mode stability for $rL^2\ge1/3$}
For a mode with scalar damping $t$, row scaling gives
\[
 \det(\lambda I-H_m(L)(A_m-t\Delta_m))
 =\det H_m(L)\,\{Q_m(\lambda,t)F(\lambda,t)-G(\lambda,t)\}.
\]
The fixed boundary factors are
\[
 g_1=\lambda+2+\frac{23}{63}t,
 \quad g_m=\lambda+5+\frac17t,
 \quad g_Z=\lambda+4+\frac{16}{45}t,
\]
\[
 F=g_1g_mg_Z-4g_1-4g_m+g_Z,
 \qquad G=g_Z(4g_1+g_m)-36.
\]
For $2\le i\le m-1$,
\[
 g_i=\frac{\lambda}{h_i}+1+\frac{t}{K_i}
 =\frac{K_{i-1}}{K_i}(1+L\lambda)+\frac{t-1}{K_i}.
\]
If $t\ge1$ and $\operatorname{Re}\lambda\ge0$, then
\[
 |Q_m|^2\ge\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2|1+L\lambda|^{2r}
 \ge\left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2
 \left(1+2rLx+rL^2y^2\right),
\]
where $x=\operatorname{Re}\lambda$.  If $rL^2\ge1/3$, this dominates the
lower bound with coefficient $1/3$ in $y^2$.

The exact polynomial
\[
 \left(\frac{91}{90}\right)^2
 \left(1+A x+\frac13y^2\right)|F|^2-|G|^2,
 \qquad A>0,
\]
has 84 nonzero monomials in $(x,y^2,t-1)$.  Every coefficient is a
polynomial in $A$ with nonnegative rational coefficients.  Its coefficients
of $x$, $y^2$, and $t-1$ are strictly positive, so equality occurs only at
$(\lambda,t)=(0,1)$.  This proves a simple isolated first-mode zero and
Hurwitz stability for every $t>1$.

For the homogeneous mode, each interior factor satisfies
\[
 \left|1+L\frac{K_{i-1}}{K_i}\lambda\right|
 \ge|1+L\lambda|.
\]
The corresponding fixed boundary polynomials are
\[
 P=\lambda^4+12\lambda^3+42\lambda^2+47\lambda+16,
 \qquad R=5\lambda^2+33\lambda+16.
\]
The polynomial
\[
 \left(1+A x+\frac13y^2\right)|P|^2-|R|^2
\]
has 34 nonzero monomials, again with nonnegative coefficient polynomials in
$A$ and strict equality only at the origin.  Hence the only closed-right-
half-plane homogeneous eigenvalue is the conservation zero.

Both coefficient tables are in `pareto_all_m_certificate.json` and are
regenerated independently.


\section*{Uniform supercritical sign along the scaling family}
Let
\[
 \mathcal H_m=\sum_{j=1}^{m-2}\frac1{K_j}.
\]
Then
\[
 \frac1{91}\le\mathcal H_m<\frac1{90}.
\]
Let $w_0^{\rm ref}$ denote the reference zero-mode correction satisfying
$c^Tw_0^{\rm ref}=0$, and let $\rho$ generate $\ker A_m$.  The corrected
zero mode for the physical fixed-mass class is
\[
 w_0(L)=w_0^{\rm ref}+\tau_m(L)\rho,
\]
\[
 \tau_m(L)=-\frac{(H_m(L)^{-1}c)^Tw_0^{\rm ref}}
 {(H_m(L)^{-1}c)^T\rho}.
\]
The second harmonic is unchanged, because the stationary equation inside the
invertible row factor $H_m(L)$ is unchanged.

With the inherited normalization of $r_m$ and $\ell_m$,
\[
 \ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m
 =-\frac{485873}{924210}-\frac{11180}{1467}L(m-2)<0.
\]
The cubic numerator has the exact decomposition
\[
 N_m(L)=N_m^{\rm ref}+\tau_m(L)S_m,
\]
where
\[
 S_m=-\frac4{462105}(1760850\mathcal H_m-10253),
 \qquad -\frac1{10}<S_m<0.
\]
The reference certificate gives
\[
 N_m^{\rm ref}>\frac1{100}.
\]
Indeed $N_m^{\rm ref}=R_m+C_m\mathcal H_m$, with $C_m<0$; substituting the
upper harmonic bound and writing $m-2=u+1$ produces the positive coefficient
list
\[
\begin{split}
&(3790502986637265684840,
17978298402717728137711,
33955060177057334483573,\\
&31899843595051464379292,
14895188986348368035728,
2762610207555043720056).
\end{split}
\]

Put $r=m-2$.  The exact rational expression for the gauge is
\[
 \tau=-\frac{
1494249120\mathcal H Lr^2-69786990\mathcal H Lr
+108738630Lr^2+1214388Lr-8521L-125249670r^2+1031940r}
{15876(8r-1)(32760\mathcal H Lr+32760Lr^2+4L-4095r)}.
\]
On $L\ge1/\sqrt{3r}$ its derivatives with respect to both $\mathcal H$ and
$L$ are negative.  Hence its maximum occurs at
$\mathcal H=1/91$ and $L=1/\sqrt{3r}$.  After writing
$y=\sqrt{3r}$, the inequality $\tau<1/20$ reduces to two negative
polynomials.  For $r=1$ use $\sqrt3<7/4$; for $r\ge2$ use
$\sqrt{3r}\le5r/4$.  The independent verifier checks the exact resulting
integer inequalities.

If $\tau\le0$, then $\tau S_m\ge0$.  If $0<\tau<1/20$, then
$\tau S_m>-1/200$.  Therefore
\[
 N_m(L)>\frac1{200}>0.
\]
The normal-form coefficients are
\[
 \eta_m(L)=\frac{\ell_m^T\Delta_m r_m}
 {\ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m}>0,
\]
\[
 c_m(L)=\frac{N_m(L)}{\ell_m^TH_m(L)^{-1}r_m}<0.
\]
Thus every member with $L\ge1/\sqrt{3r}$ is supercritical.


\section*{Certified stable Pareto curve}
Set
\[
 L_0(r)=\frac1{\sqrt{3r}},
 \qquad L_1(r)=\frac{90r}{90r+1}.
\]
The interval is nonempty for every $r\ge1$.  For
$L\in[L_0,L_1]$, every interior equilibrium scaling satisfies $h_i\ge1$.
The largest is
\[
 h_2=\frac{91r-1}{91rL},
\]
while the boundary scalings equal one.  Thus
\[
 \chi_H(L)=\frac{91r-1}{91rL}.
\]
The physical diffusivities are
\[
 d_1=\frac{23}{63},
 \qquad d_i=\frac1{LK_{i-1}}
 \ (2\le i\le m-1),
 \qquad d_m=\frac17,
 \qquad d_Z=\frac{16}{45}.
\]
Their minimum is $d_2=1/(91rL)$ and their maximum is $d_1$, so
\[
 \chi_D(L)=\frac{23}{63}(91rL).
\]
Consequently
\[
 \boxed{\chi_D(L)\chi_H(L)=\frac{23(91r-1)}{63}}
\]
throughout the certified stable curve.  Increasing $L$ increases diffusion
contrast and decreases equilibrium contrast, so no two points of this curve
dominate one another within the curve.

At the balanced-order endpoint $L=L_0$,
\[
 \chi_D=\frac{2093}{63\sqrt3}\sqrt r,
 \qquad
 \chi_H=\sqrt{3r}\frac{91r-1}{91r}.
\]
Hence both contrasts are $\Theta(\sqrt m)$.

For every stationary crossing, the exact diffusion law gives the universal
trade-off
\[
 \chi_D\chi_H>8r.
\]
In particular
\[
 \max(\chi_D,\chi_H)>\sqrt{8r}.
\]
The construction therefore attains the optimal square-root exponent in both
coordinates.  The global constant-optimal Pareto frontier is not determined:
the constructed product has asymptotic coefficient $2093/63$, while the
universal lower coefficient is $8$.

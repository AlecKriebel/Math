\section*{Adjoint critical vector and transversality}
Normalize the left nullvector by $\ell_Z=1$.  Then
\[
\ell_1=-\frac{266}{815},
\qquad
\ell_i=\frac{78260(m-2)}{163K_{i-1}}
\quad(2\le i\le m-1),
\]
\[
\ell_m=\frac{18368}{7335},
\qquad
\ell_Z=1.
\]
The relation $1+d_i=K_{i-1}/K_i$ telescopes the adjoint chain.  Put
\[
\mathcal H_m=\sum_{j=1}^{m-2}\frac1{K_j}.
\]
Direct contraction gives
\[
\ell^Tr=-\frac{7043400m-13600927-7043400\mathcal H_m}{924210},
\]
\[
\ell^TD_mr=-\frac{2(1760850\mathcal H_m+16559)}{462105}.
\]
Since
\[
0\le \mathcal H_m\le\frac{m-2}{90m-179},
\]
the second expression is negative and the first is negative because the
lower-bound numerator becomes, after clearing the positive denominator,
\[
633906000m^2-2491895430m+2448652733,
\]
whose shift $m=u+3$ has coefficients
$633906000,1311540570,678120443$.  Hence
\[
\eta_m=\frac{\ell^TD_mr}{\ell^Tr}>0.
\]

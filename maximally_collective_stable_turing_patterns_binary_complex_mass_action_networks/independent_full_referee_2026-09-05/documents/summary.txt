Most failure-prone points. External checking should focus on: (i) exhaustion of principal SCCs, including b = 2a; (ii) strict
equality cases in the 22/35/77/84-term certificates and the direct ν = 1 cubic; (iii) the principal-minor derivative monotonicity;
(iv) the equilibrium-scaled fixed-mass gauge; and (v) the cubic comparison Nm (L) > 1/200.

Scope. The family is synthetic, rank deficient with one semipositive conservation law, and all-mobile. Results are local for
each fixed dimension. No arbitrary-data global boundedness, global attraction, constant-optimal stable frontier, biochemical
realization, cross-diffusion, or immobile-species theorem is claimed.

S1      Reaction matrices, flux cone, and semipositive conservation
Order species as X1 , . . . , Xm , Z and reactions as R0 , R2 , . . . , Rm−2 , Ra , Rb , R+ , R− . For each indexed reaction, the
source and target vectors have total molecularity at most two. The source matrix Ym and stoichiometric matrix Γm
are obtained by placing these source vectors and reaction differences in columns.
   The balance equations Γm v = 0 read, successively, equality of every flux around the long circuit and equality of the
two boundary-pair fluxes. Thus
                                            ker Γm = span{(1m , 0, 0), (0m , 1, 1)}.
The balance equations show that this kernel is exactly two-dimensional. Since Γm has m + 2 columns, rank–
nullity gives rank Γm = m. As an independent explicit check, retain the rows X1 , X3 , . . . , Xm , Z and the columns
R2 , . . . , Rm−2 , Ra , Rb , R+ , with the chain range empty when m = 3. The resulting maximal minor has determinant
4(−1)m . The vector
                                                       c = (0, 4, . . . , 4, 2, 1)T
is orthogonal to every reaction vector, so im Γm = c⊥ . Notice that c is semipositive rather than positive: X1 is absent.
    For flux (a1m , b, b) and unit equilibrium, Am (a, b) = Γm diag(a1m , b, b)YmT has precisely the entries printed in
the main text. At an arbitrary positive equilibrium, right multiplication by H = diag(1/x∗i ) gives J = Am (a, b)H.
Conversely the rate assignment kr = vr /(x∗ )yr realizes every a, b, H > 0.


S2      All-spectrum principal-block classification
Use the directed convention Xj → Xi when the (i, j) Jacobian entry is nonzero. The interior arrows are the one-way
chain X2 → X3 → · · · → Xm−1 . The boundary arrows are those incident to X1 , Xm , Z and the returns Xm−1 → X1 ,
Xm → X2 . The following case analysis is exhaustive for an induced set with fewer than m vertices.
   For m = 3, inspect the principal sets of order at most two directly. The sets {X1 , X2 } and {X2 , X3 } are the two
complete long cycles; every other non-singleton strongly connected component is a principal subgraph of {X1 , X3 , Z}.
Hence assume m ≥ 4 in the chain argument below.

 2. If it contains a chain arrow and returns through Xm → X2 , then the forward path forces every vertex X2 , . . . , Xm .

S11      Numerical protocol and independent verification
Cosine–Galerkin simulations use exact modal convolution for the quadratic reaction terms and an adaptive stiff
integrator. Initial data are small perturbations of 1 in the critical direction. The first-mode amplitude is measured by
the adjoint projection. Spatial and temporal refinement tables, open-format profile data, exact parameters, and all
solver tolerances are included in the data archive. No numerical output is used in a proof.
    The release contains independent programs for the reaction family, flux cone, all-spectrum block theorem, omission
minors, principal-minor diffusion-ray theorem, diffusion criterion, contrast bounds, improved profile, Pareto family,
mode certificates, harmonic corrections, cubic sign, and branch stability. Mutation tests change signs, endpoints, the
factor eight, Fourier factors, and certificate coefficients and must be rejected.

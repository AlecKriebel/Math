## Conclusion

No publication-blocking defect was found in the topology-wide localization, principal-minor diffusion-ray theorem, omission-minor computation, sharp stationary contrast theorem, or claimed exponent optimality. These are substantial, inspectable finite-dimensional mathematical results; the strongest contribution is their conjunction in one highly constrained, all-dimensional mass-action topology. This is not a complete referee report or an independent literature priority determination. The nonlinear PDE theorem needs its own expert analytic scrutiny.

## What was actually checked

- **Theorem `thm:allspectrum` / Lemma `lem:scc`, main lines 279–381; Supplement S2.** The SCC argument covers the two long cycles and boundary triad; the edge cancellation at b=2a and the minimal case m=3 are handled. The diagonal-product modulus argument is strict for arbitrary positive diagonal equilibrium scaling. Independently reconstructed the reaction matrices and verified the boundary cubic Routh gap has exactly 14 positive monomials for symbolic positive a,b,h1,hm,hZ. Checked all proper boundary blocks symbolically.
- **Propositions `prop:flux`, `prop:realization`, `prop:omissions`.** Independently reconstructed Gamma and the reactant matrix directly from the printed reactions, without importing repository matrix builders. For symbolic a,b and m=3,...,7, checked binary complexes, rank m, the stated conservation and positive-flux vectors, and every omission minor. The uniform H factors follow from determinant column scaling, so these computations corroborate the printed all-m proof rather than replace it.
- **Theorem `thm:diffusionray`, main lines 398–447; Supplement S3.** The determinant expansion and monotone bracket proof are sound. Crucially, the derivative of the characteristic polynomial groups the possibly negative order-(n−1) minors into their positive unweighted sum. This proves a unique positive real eigenvalue inside the band, excludes positive real eigenvalues outside it, and proves an algebraically simple zero at the crossing. It does not exclude nonreal unstable eigenvalues, which the statement correctly says. Checked an independent n=2 example and exact network polynomials for m=3,4,5, with diffusion below, exactly at, and above beta1=0.
- **Theorems `thm:diffusionlaw` and `thm:contrast`, main lines 524–616; Supplement S4.** Restriction to the fixed stoichiometric class being Hurwitz implies an algebraically simple conservation zero and positive order-(n−1) minor sum. The iff threshold law and nonattained infimum T(H) follow. Equality beta1=0 yields no positive nonzero stationary threshold; strictness matters. The product lower bound is sharp over stationary crossings and is not claimed to be attained by a nonlinear stable branch.
- **Theorem `thm:pareto`, main lines 865–908.** Independently checked critical-vector and physical contrast formulas at both interval endpoints for m=3,...,7, including the exceptional nu=1 endpoint. The physical-to-normalized diagonal transformation is consistent: normalized dynamics are H[f+(1−mu)Delta x_xx], physical diffusion is H Delta. The paper explicitly limits optimality to exponent 1/2 for stationary crossings of this topology; it does not assert the full Pareto frontier or constant optimality.
- Exact SCC/Hurwitz checks covered 912 principal subsets over two rational realizations in each m=3,...,7, including b=2a. A separate reproducible numerical stress test checked 324 smaller principal blocks across m=3,4,5,8,12 with broad positive diagonal scalings. No unstable smaller block was found. These samples do not establish universal quantifiers.

The independent script, text log, and JSON result are in this folder. Runtime: `/usr/local/bin/python`, SymPy 1.14.0, NumPy 1.24.3. No repository verifier module is imported.

## Limits and exact remaining gaps

1. The all-dimensional SCC proof and determinant derivation were read and mathematically assessed, not encoded in a proof assistant. Finite tests are supporting evidence only.
2. I did not independently derive the complete cubic all-m recurrence or validate every sectorial/center-manifold hypothesis. The dynamic stability result cannot be inferred from the checked algebra alone.
3. The homogeneous stable region S_m remains unclassified. The necessary inequality T(H)>1 is not a sufficient classification, and the manuscript correctly avoids that claim.
4. No exact nonlinear stable contrast infimum is known. The asymptotic stable upper product is about 4.15 times the stationary lower product; the certified square-root family's largest contrast is about 13.1 times the topology-specific minimax lower bound. Therefore the exponent result is meaningful but not a near-optimal practical design claim.
5. Scope exclusions matter: synthetic topology, semipositive conservation, local positive branches, fixed dimension, fixed Neumann interval, stationary crossings. The unstable real-eigenvalue band extends down to arbitrarily small positive wavenumber. It should not be sold as intrinsic wavelength selection or a broadly validated biochemical mechanism.

## Lean recommendation

**Do not make full Lean formalization a prerequisite to specialist feedback or journal submission.** It could strengthen confidence in a selected algebraic theorem, but it does not establish novelty, biological relevance, or the PDE conclusions if those are left informal. The existing explicit proof and reproducible symbolic certificates already make the finite algebra unusually reviewable.

If a small formalization pilot is desired, the highest-value self-contained target is `thm:diffusionray`: the principal-minor expansion, coefficient positivity, monotonicity, existence/uniqueness of the positive threshold, and the characteristic-polynomial derivative argument under the exact stated hypotheses. A second tier is the symbolic boundary-triad and omission-minor identities, with mechanically checked positivity certificates. A formal m=3 example alone does not certify the paper's all-dimensional central claim. Formalizing every PDE conclusion would require substantially more analysis infrastructure and should be treated as a separate research/software project, with a precise list of imported or assumed theorems.

The best next mathematical review request is an expert assessment of novelty versus existing principal-subsystem localization results plus a separate check of the conservation-compatible bifurcation/stability argument. That input is more directly relevant to journal acceptance than a broad Lean conversion. The human user alone should decide and conduct any external communication.

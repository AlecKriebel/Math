\section*{All-dimensional supercritical sign}
After $m=u+3$, the descending coefficient lists of $\mathcal Q_m$, $P_R$,
and $P_C$ are, respectively,
\[
\begin{gathered}
(589180301,1802606769,1838302066,624878904),\\
(68605040480814208768,272378299600144474259,
405345143374782665711,\\
267974666768626234894,66402795166594173768),\\
(652054120726848,2672677467393709,4108255612276161,
2806739366867294,719107361052288).
\end{gathered}
\]
All entries are positive.  Hence $\mathcal Q_m>0$, $P_R(m)>0$, and
$P_C(m)>0$ for $m\ge3$.  There is no negative prefactor in $R_m$, while the
external prefactor $-215$ in $C_m$ reverses the sign; thus $R_m>0$ and
$C_m<0$.

Use
\[
0\le \mathfrak h_m\le\frac{m-2}{90m-179}.
\]
Because $C_m<0$,
\[
N_m=R_m+C_m\mathfrak h_m
\ge R_m+C_m\frac{m-2}{90m-179}.
\]
After clearing the positive denominator, the lower-bound numerator is
\[
\begin{aligned}
L_m={}&2729945147827667886720m^5
-27755132420474170999952m^4\\
&+112813395868533457497683m^3
-229153280695458887386228m^2\\
&+232620996871721820873517m
-94412163900120968220300.
\end{aligned}
\]
The exact clearing identity, including its positive denominator, is
\[
 R_m+C_m\frac{m-2}{90m-179}
 =\frac{L_m}{286118780220(8m-17)(90m-179)\mathcal Q_m}.
\]
Its coefficients after $m=u+3$ are
\[
\begin{gathered}
2729945147827667886720,
13194044796940847300848,
25446870127333515303059,\\
24475321329207325509911,
11736484608366875120374,
2243933770033305838488,
\end{gathered}
\]
all strictly positive.  Hence $N_m>0$.  The adjoint calculation gives
$\ell^Tr<0$, so
\[
\boxed{c_m<0\qquad(m\ge3).}
\]
Together with $\eta_m>0$, the bifurcation is supercritical for every member of
the family.

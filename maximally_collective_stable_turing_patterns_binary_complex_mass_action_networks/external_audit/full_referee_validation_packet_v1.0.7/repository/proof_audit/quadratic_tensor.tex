\section*{Quadratic reaction tensor}
At the unit equilibrium
\[
f_m(\mathbf1+u)=A_mu+\frac12B_m(u,u),
\]
with no cubic reaction tensor.  The symmetric bilinear map is
\[
(B(u,v))_1=-(u_1v_{m-1}+u_{m-1}v_1)+2u_Zv_Z-(u_1v_m+u_mv_1),
\]
\[
(B(u,v))_2=-(u_1v_2+u_2v_1)+2u_mv_m,
\]
\[
(B(u,v))_i=u_1v_{i-1}+u_{i-1}v_1-u_1v_i-u_iv_1
\quad(3\le i\le m-1),
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
(B(u,v))_m={}&2(u_1v_{m-1}+u_{m-1}v_1)-4u_mv_m+2u_Zv_Z\\
&-(u_1v_m+u_mv_1),
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
(B(u,v))_Z=-4u_Zv_Z+2(u_1v_m+u_mv_1).
\]
Reaction-wise conservation gives $c^TB_m(u,v)=0$.

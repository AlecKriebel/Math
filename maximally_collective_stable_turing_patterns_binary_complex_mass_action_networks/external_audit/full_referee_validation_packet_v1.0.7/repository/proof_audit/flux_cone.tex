\section*{Complete positive steady-flux cone}
Let the reaction coordinates be ordered as the $m$ long-circuit reactions,
followed by the two directions of the reversible pair.  Species balance gives
successively
\[
v_0=v_2=\cdots=v_{m-2}=v_a=v_b=:a
\]
and
\[
v_+=v_-=:b.
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\ker\Gamma_m=\operatorname{span}\{(\mathbf1_m,0,0),(0_m,1,1)\}.
\]
The balance equations show directly that this is the entire two-dimensional
kernel.  Since $\Gamma_m$ has $m+2$ columns, rank--nullity gives
$\operatorname{rank}\Gamma_m=m$.  Equivalently, the maximal minor on rows
$X_1,X_3,\ldots,X_m,Z$ and columns
$R_2,\ldots,R_{m-2},R_a,R_b,R_+$ has determinant $4(-1)^m$ (with an empty
chain range at $m=3$).  Therefore
\[
\{v>0:\Gamma_mv=0\}=\{(a\mathbf1_m,b,b):a,b>0\}.
\]
This equality, rather than one selected normalized flux, is used throughout.

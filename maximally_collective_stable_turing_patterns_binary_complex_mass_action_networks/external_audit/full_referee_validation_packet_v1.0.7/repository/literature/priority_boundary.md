# Priority and claim boundary

The manuscript claims novelty only for the conjunction of the following properties:

1. one indexed binary-complex classical mass-action topology in every dimension;
2. a complete two-parameter positive-flux image and all positive equilibrium scalings;
3. every principal block below order $n-1$ Hurwitz throughout that full realization family;
4. an unstable order-$n-1$ block;
5. an exact necessary-and-sufficient stationary diffusion-ray law;
6. sharp linear contrast infima and a topology-wide stationary heterogeneity product bound;
7. primary supercritical locally exponentially stable patterns;
8. a stable diffusion--equilibrium family matching the topology-specific stationary minimax exponent.

The paper does not claim that order $n-1$ is new for arbitrary matrices, that projected injectivity first fails at order $n-1$, that the stable constants are optimal, or that the family is biochemical.

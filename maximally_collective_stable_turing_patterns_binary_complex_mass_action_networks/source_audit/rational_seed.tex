\section*{Successful fixed rational realization}
No parameter tuning is required.  For every $m\ge3$ take
\[
a_m=b_m=1,
\qquad H_m=I,
\qquad x_m^*=\mathbf1,
\]
and
\[
(u,v,p,q)=\left(\frac73,\frac1{32},\frac{11}{16},\frac1{40}\right).
\]
Writing
\[
L_i=227m-451-3i,
\]
the right critical vector is
\[
r_1=1,
\qquad r_i=-\frac{L_i}{96(m-2)}\ (2\le i\le m-1),
\qquad r_m=-\frac{11}{16},
\qquad r_Z=\frac1{40}.
\]
All rates and equilibrium coordinates are one.  The diffusion entries are
listed separately in `diffusion_profile.tex`.

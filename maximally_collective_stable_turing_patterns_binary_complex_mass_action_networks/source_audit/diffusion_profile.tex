\section*{Positive diffusion profile}
For the rational seed set
\[
d_1=\frac{257}{240},
\qquad
d_2=\frac{m+1}{227m-457},
\qquad
d_i=\frac3{227m-451-3i}\quad(3\le i\le m-1),
\]
\[
d_m=\frac{43}{165},
\qquad d_Z=21.
\]
The middle range is empty for $m=3$.  Since
\[
227m-451-3i\ge227m-451-3(m-1)=224(m-2)>0,
\]
every entry is strictly positive for every $m\ge3$.  Substitution into the
rows of $A_m$ proves $(A_m-D_m)r_m=0$ exactly.

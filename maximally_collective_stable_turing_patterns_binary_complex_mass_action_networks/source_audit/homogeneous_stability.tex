\section*{Homogeneous relative stability}
At $a=b=1$ the sparse determinant recurrence gives
\[
\det(\lambda I-A_m)=\lambda q_m(\lambda),
\]
\[
q_m=(\lambda+1)q_{m-1}+R(\lambda),
\qquad
R(\lambda)=5\lambda^2+33\lambda+16,
\]
with
\[
q_3=(\lambda+7)(\lambda^2+5\lambda+2).
\]
Equivalently,
\[
\lambda q_m=(\lambda+1)^{m-3}P(\lambda)-R(\lambda),
\]
\[
P(\lambda)=\lambda^4+12\lambda^3+42\lambda^2+47\lambda+16.
\]
For $m=3$, $q_3$ is Hurwitz.  For $m\ge4$, a nonzero root in the closed right
half-plane would imply
$|1+\lambda|^{m-3}|P(\lambda)|=|R(\lambda)|$.  But the exact polynomial
\[
|1+\lambda|^2|P(\lambda)|^2-|R(\lambda)|^2
\]
becomes a sum of 35 strictly positive monomials in
$x=\Re\lambda$, $z=(\Im\lambda)^2$, with no constant term; its complete
coefficient certificate is `modulus_certificate.json`.  It vanishes only at
$\lambda=0$.  Since $|1+\lambda|\ge1$, no nonzero closed-right-half-plane root
exists.  Finally,
\[
q_m(0)=16m-34>0,
\]
so the conservation zero is simple and $A_m|_{c^\perp}$ is Hurwitz.

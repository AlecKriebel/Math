\section*{Exact adjoint kernel}
Normalize the left nullvector by $\ell_Z=1$.  Then
\[
\ell_1=-\frac{45880}{5123},
\qquad
\ell_2=\frac{783160}{15369},
\qquad
\ell_m=\frac{219835}{10246},
\qquad
\ell_Z=1,
\]
and, for $3\le i\le m-1$,
\[
\ell_i=\frac{59520160(m-2)}{5123(227m-448-3i)}.
\]
The interior adjoint recurrence is
\[
\ell_{i+1}=(1+d_i)\ell_i,
\]
which telescopes because
$1+d_i=(227m-448-3i)/(227m-451-3i)$.  The four boundary equations verify the
remaining components directly.  Thus $(A_m-D_m)^T\ell_m=0$.

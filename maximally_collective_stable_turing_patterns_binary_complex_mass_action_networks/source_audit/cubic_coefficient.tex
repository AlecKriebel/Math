\section*{Exact cubic coefficient}
With $\phi_1(\xi)=\cos\xi$,
\[
\phi_1^2=\frac12(1+\cos2\xi),
\qquad
\phi_1\cos2\xi=\frac12(\cos\xi+\cos3\xi).
\]
Consequently the critical-mode projection at cubic order is
\[
c_m=\frac{\ell^T\left(B(r,w_0)+\tfrac12B(r,w_2)\right)}{\ell^Tr}.
\]
The factors $1/4$ in the stable-mode equations arise from the $1/2$ in the
Taylor expansion and the $1/2$ in $\cos^2\xi$; the further $1/2$ multiplying
$B(r,w_2)$ is the critical Fourier coefficient of
$\cos\xi\cos2\xi$.

Let $H_m$ be as above.  Exact telescoping gives
\[
\ell^T\left(B(r,w_0)+\frac12B(r,w_2)\right)=R_m+C_mH_m,
\]
where
\[
R_m=\frac{A_5(m)}{566562816000(m-2)(8m-17)Q(m)},
\]
\[
C_m=-\frac{372001B_4(m)}{78689280(8m-17)Q(m)},
\]

\section*{Conservation-compatible homogeneous correction}
The equation
\[
A_mw_0=-\frac14B_m(r,r),
\qquad c^Tw_0=0
\]
has the unique solution
\begin{align*}
(w_0)_1&=\frac{721600m-1519799}{76800(8m-17)},\\
(w_0)_2&=\frac{4800m^2-43001m+68002}
 {76800(m-2)(8m-17)},\\
(w_0)_i&=(w_0)_2-\frac{i-2}{64(m-2)}
 \quad(3\le i\le m-1),\\
(w_0)_m&=-\frac{121}{1024},\\
(w_0)_Z&=\frac{109402m-229079}{38400(8m-17)}.
\end{align*}
The formulas are obtained by the chain recurrence, followed by the augmented
boundary system described in `conservation/reduced_inverse.tex`.  Direct
substitution verifies both the differential equation and the conservation
gauge for every $m\ge3$.

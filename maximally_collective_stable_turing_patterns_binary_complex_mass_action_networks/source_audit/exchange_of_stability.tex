\section*{Exchange of stability}
Along either branch,
\[
\partial_A(\eta_m\mu A+c_mA^3+\cdots)
=\eta_m\mu+3c_mA_\pm(\mu)^2+O(\mu^2)
=-2\eta_m\mu+O(\mu^2)<0.
\]
Thus the center eigenvalue enters the open left half-plane on the
supercritical side.  The patterned equilibria differ from the base state by
$O(\sqrt\mu)$ in a space embedded into $L^\infty$, so their reaction
linearizations are bounded $O(\sqrt\mu)$ perturbations of the onset operator;
the diffusion coefficient changes by $O(\mu)$.  Analytic perturbation of the
isolated center projection and continuity of the complementary spectral bound
preserve the negative complementary gap for sufficiently small $\mu>0$.
Consequently both reflection-related positive patterned equilibria have their
full linearized spectrum in the open left half-plane in the fixed-mass phase
space.
The Neumann interval has no continuous translation symmetry, only a discrete
reflection.  Therefore no symmetry-generated neutral eigenvalue remains.
Together with the fixed integrated-mass constraint, this accounts for every
possible zero direction near the branch.

# Mathematical typography audit

The final source was checked literally for:

- the empty chain and middle-diffusion ranges at `m=3`;
- every occurrence of `n=m+1` and `m=n-1`;
- species labels `X_1,...,X_m,Z`;
- principal set `C_m={X_1,...,X_m}`;
- all product and summation endpoints in the flux, determinant, harmonic-sum, and diffusion formulas;
- minus signs in the order-`m` determinant, critical vector, adjoint contractions, and cubic coefficient;
- the fixed integrated conservation condition;
- source and target molecularity in the binary-complex definition.

No unresolved placeholder, malformed set-builder expression, or obsolete stationary-only scope statement remains in the manuscript.

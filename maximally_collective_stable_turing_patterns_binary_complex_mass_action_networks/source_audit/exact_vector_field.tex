\section*{Exact quadratic reaction field}
At the selected unit-flux realization, write $x=\mathbf1+u$.  Because every
source complex has molecularity at most two,
\[
f_m(\mathbf1+u)=A_mu+\frac12B_m(u,u)
\]
with no cubic or higher reaction tensor.  Both $A_m$ and $B_m$ are reconstructed
reaction by reaction from the indexed list, not inferred from a fitted
polynomial vector field.

The flagship keeps the exact formulas and explicitly declines constant-optimal or complete-Pareto claims.

$\widehat x=Hx$, the parameterized physical system becomes
$\partial_t\widehat x=H\{f(\widehat x)+(1-\mu)\Delta
\partial_{xx}\widehat x\}$; at $\mu=0$ the critical right vector is
unchanged, the dynamic left vector is $H^{-1}\ell$, and only the homogeneous

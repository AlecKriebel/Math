\section*{Inverse on the homogeneous stoichiometric space}
The homogeneous Jacobian $A_m$ has one-dimensional kernel
$\operatorname{span}\{\rho\}$ with $c^T\rho\neq0$.  Hence its restriction
\[
A_m:c^\perp\longrightarrow c^\perp
\]
is bijective.  Equivalently, the augmented matrix
\[
\mathcal A_m=\begin{pmatrix}A_m&c\\c^T&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
is nonsingular.  Indeed, if $A_mw+\alpha c=0$ and $c^Tw=0$, left
multiplication by $c^T$ gives $\alpha c^Tc=0$, then $w\in\ker A_m\cap c^\perp$,
so $w=0$ and $\alpha=0$.  This augmented inverse is used for the zero harmonic;
no full-space pseudoinverse is invoked.

\section*{Transversality coefficient}
Define
\[
H_m=\sum_{j=2}^{m-2}\frac1{227m-451-3j},
\]
with the empty sum equal to zero.  Direct contraction gives
\[
\ell^Tr=-\frac{892802400m^2-3400424303m+3217891606}
{7377120(m-2)}+\frac{1860005}{5123}H_m,
\]
\[
\ell^TD r=-\frac{99148487m-186549574}{7377120(m-2)}
-\frac{1860005}{5123}H_m.
\]
Since
\[
0\le H_m\le\frac{m-3}{224m-445},
\]
one obtains
\[
\ell^Tr\le-rac{G_3(m)}{7377120(m-2)(224m-445)}<0,
\]
where, after $m=u+3$,
\[
G_3(u+3)=199987737600u^3+638219119328u^2
+677034371347u+238767747419.
\]
The second contraction is visibly negative.  Therefore
\[
\eta_m=\frac{\ell^TD_mr}{\ell^Tr}>0.
\]
For the parameterization $(1-\mu)D_m$, the critical eigenvalue satisfies
$\lambda'(0)=\eta_m\neq0$.  Since the left and right nullvectors pair
nontrivially, the zero eigenvalue is algebraically simple.

\section*{Scalar center reduction}
Normalize the spatial amplitude by writing the critical component exactly as
$A r_m\cos\xi$ with $r_1=1$; retain the adjoint normalization $\ell_Z=1$.
The reduced dynamic equation is
\[
\dot A=\eta_m\mu A+c_mA^3
+O(|A|^5+|A|^3|\mu|+|A||\mu|^2).
\]
Reflection $\xi\mapsto\pi-\xi$ sends $A\mapsto-A$, so the reduced field is odd.
The coefficients are
\[
\eta_m=\frac{\ell^TD_mr}{\ell^Tr}>0,
\qquad
c_m=\frac{\ell^T(B(r,w_0)+\tfrac12B(r,w_2))}{\ell^Tr}<0.
\]
Thus, for $\mu>0$ small,
\[
A_\pm(\mu)=\pm\sqrt{-\eta_m\mu/c_m}+O(\mu^{3/2}).
\]
The branches are supercritical in the chosen parameter orientation.

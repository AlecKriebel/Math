\section*{Adjoint contraction recurrence}
For any vector $w$, the reaction-wise contraction reduces to
\[
\ell^TB(r,w)=
\sum_{i=2}^{m-1}d_i\ell_i(w_i+r_iw_1)
-\frac{11}{8}\delta_b w_m
+\delta_+\left(\frac{11}{16}w_1-w_m+\frac1{20}w_Z\right),
\]
where
\[
\delta_b=\ell_2-2\ell_m=\frac{123655}{15369},
\qquad
\delta_+=\ell_1+\ell_m-2\ell_Z=\frac{21}{2}.
\]
The interior terms telescope after inserting the formulas for $\ell_i$, $r_i$,
$w_0$, and $w_2$.  All nonrational dependence on the chain length reduces to
the single finite sum
\[
H_m=\sum_{j=2}^{m-2}\frac1{227m-451-3j}.
\]
This reduction was derived symbolically and independently replayed by direct
reaction-tensor contraction at $m=3,4,5,6,8,10$.

More explicitly, write $\bar w=w_0+w_2/2$, let
\[
K=\frac{59520160(m-2)}{5123},
\quad
\mathcal D=L_{-1}L_0L_1L_2,
\quad
\mathcal C=W_2+\frac{\sigma L_2}{9},
\]
and set
\[
C_0=(w_0)_2-\frac{\sigma L_2}{3},
\qquad
C_1=\frac{5\sigma}{18}-\frac{\bar w_1}{96(m-2)}.
\]
For every $3\le i\le m-1$, the interior summand has the exact decomposition
\[
\frac{3K}{L_iL_{i-1}}(\bar w_i+r_i\bar w_1)
=
\frac{3K\mathcal C}{2\mathcal D}L_{i-2}L_{i-3}
+\frac{KC_0}{L_i}
+\frac{-KC_0+3KC_1}{L_{i-1}}.
\]
The polynomial part sums by
\[
\sum_{i=3}^{m-1}L_{i-2}L_{i-3}
=(m-3)(50851m^2-201376m+199366),
\]
while the two reciprocal sums combine to
\[
3KC_1H_m+KC_0\left(\frac1{L_{m-1}}-\frac1{L_2}\right).
\]
This is the summation certificate that yields $R_m+C_mH_m$ after adding the
$i=2$ term and the three boundary contractions.

\section*{Affine-chain critical-vector ansatz}
Let
\[
r_1=1,
\qquad
r_i=-\left(u+v\frac{m-1-i}{m-2}\right)\ (2\le i\le m-1),
\qquad
r_m=-p,
\qquad
r_Z=q.
\]
For $A_m=A_m(1,1)$ define $d_i=(A_mr)_i/r_i$.  Direct row substitution,
not matrix interpolation, gives
\begin{align*}
d_1&=-2+u+p+2q,\\
d_2&=\frac{1+2p-u-v(m-3)/(m-2)}{u+v(m-3)/(m-2)},\\
d_i&=\frac{v}{(m-2)u+v(m-1-i)}\quad(3\le i\le m-1),\\
d_m&=\frac{2u-5p-2q-1}{p},\\
d_Z&=\frac{2-2p-4q}{q}.
\end{align*}
Thus $(A_m-D)r=0$ by construction.  The sufficient positivity region used in
the search is
\[
u,v,p,q>0,
\quad u+p+2q>2,
\quad u+v<1+2p,
\quad2u>1+5p+2q,
\quad p+2q<1.
\]
The selected rational point lies strictly inside this region.

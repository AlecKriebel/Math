\section*{Second-harmonic correction}
Put $L_j=227m-451-3j$ and
\[
\sigma=\frac1{64(m-2)},
\quad s_1=-\frac{14503}{9600},
\quad s_2=-\frac{L_2}{192(m-2)}-\frac{121}{512},
\quad s_m=\frac{47269}{19200},
\quad s_Z=\frac{1101}{1600}.
\]
These are the boundary components of $-B(r,r)/4$; every interior component is
$\sigma$.  Define, for $i\ge2$,
\[
T_i=\frac{L_{i-3}L_{i-2}L_{i-1}L_i}{L_{-1}L_0L_1L_2},
\]
so $T_2=1$ and $T_i/T_{i-1}=L_i/L_{i-4}$.  Let
\[
P=T_{m-1},
\quad g_2=\frac{231m-453}{L_2},
\quad \alpha=\frac{P}{g_2},
\quad U=\frac{L_{m-1}-PL_2}{9},
\quad \beta=-\alpha s_2-\sigma U.
\]
The boundary values $(W_1,W_m,W_Z)$ are the unique solution of
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-377/60+\alpha&-2\alpha-1&2\\
1-2\alpha&4\alpha-997/165&2\\
2&2&-88
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}W_1\\W_m\\W_Z\end{pmatrix}
=
\begin{pmatrix}s_1+\beta\\s_m-2\beta\\s_Z\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Its determinant is
\[
-\frac{Q(m)}{561825(77m-151)(227m-451)(227m-448)},
\]
where
\[
Q(m)=1910521667596003m^3-11322779437089660m^2
+22368031913707929m-14729097938020928.
\]
After $m=u+3$, the coefficients of $Q$ are
\[
1910521667596003,
5871915571274367,
6015440316262050,
2054067894388000,
\]
so the determinant never vanishes for $m\ge3$.  Set
\[
W_2=\frac{-W_1+2W_m-s_2}{g_2},
\]
\[
W_i=T_i\left(W_2+\frac{\sigma L_2}{9}\right)
 -\frac{\sigma L_i}{9},
\qquad3\le i\le m-1.
\]
Then $w_2=(W_1,\ldots,W_m,W_Z)^T$ satisfies
\[
(A_m-4D_m)w_2=-\frac14B_m(r,r).
\]
The proof is a first-order recurrence; the displayed four-factor expression is
the telescoped product and is valid also in the $m=3$ empty-chain case.

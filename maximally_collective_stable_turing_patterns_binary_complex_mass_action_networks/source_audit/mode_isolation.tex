\section*{All-mode isolation}
For $t\ge0$ put
\[
g_1=\lambda+2+td_1,
\quad g_i=\lambda+1+td_i\ (2\le i\le m-1),
\quad g_m=\lambda+5+td_m,
\quad g_Z=\lambda+4+td_Z.
\]
Sparse elimination along the chain gives
\[
\det(\lambda I-A_m+tD_m)=Q_mF-G,
\]
where
\[
Q_m=\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}g_i,
\quad
F=g_1g_mg_Z-4g_1-4g_m+g_Z,
\quad
G=g_Z(4g_1+g_m)-36.
\]
At $(\lambda,t)=(0,1)$,
\[
Q_m=\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}(1+d_i)=\frac{57}{56},
\qquad
\frac{G}{F}=\frac{57}{56},
\]
so the determinant vanishes.

Let $x=\Re\lambda\ge0$, $z=(\Im\lambda)^2\ge0$, and $s=t-1\ge0$.  Exact
expansion shows
\[
\left(\frac{57}{56}\right)^2|F|^2-|G|^2
\]
is a sum of 49 positive monomials in $(x,z,s)$ with no constant term.  The
machine-readable expansion is `modulus_certificate.json`; equality occurs
only at $(x,z,s)=(0,0,0)$.  Moreover,
\[
|Q_m|\ge\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}(1+td_i)\ge\frac{57}{56}
\qquad(t\ge1,\ \Re\lambda\ge0).
\]
Thus $Q_mF=G$ has no closed-right-half-plane solution except
$(\lambda,t)=(0,1)$.  Therefore $A_m-D_m$ has exactly one simple zero and all
other eigenvalues stable, while $A_m-tD_m$ is Hurwitz for every $t>1$.
On $(0,\pi)$, all modes $k\ge2$ have $t=k^2>1$ and are stable.

\section*{Critical kernel and algebraic simplicity}
The interior equations of $(A_m-D_m)x=0$ are first-order recurrences.  Their
product satisfies
\[
\prod_{i=2}^{m-1}(1+d_i)=\frac{57}{56}.
\]
After eliminating $x_2,\ldots,x_{m-1}$, the equations for
$(x_1,x_m,x_Z)$ are
\[
\begin{pmatrix}
-9523/4560&-169/57&2\\
-55/57&-4172/3135&2\\
2&2&-25
\end{pmatrix}
\begin{pmatrix}x_1\\x_m\\x_Z\end{pmatrix}=0.
\]
This matrix has determinant zero and the upper-left $2\times2$ minor is
$-5123/62700\neq0$, so its kernel is one-dimensional.  It is generated by
$(40,-55/2,1)^T$, which reproduces the displayed $r_m$ after normalization
$r_1=1$.  Hence $\ker(A_m-D_m)=\operatorname{span}\{r_m\}$.

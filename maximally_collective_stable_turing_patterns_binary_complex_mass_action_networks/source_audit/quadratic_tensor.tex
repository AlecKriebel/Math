\section*{Quadratic Hessian tensor}
For a reaction with source $e_i+e_j$,
\[
D^2x^y[u,v]=u_iv_j+u_jv_i,
\]
and for source $2e_i$,
\[
D^2x^y[u,v]=2u_iv_i.
\]
Multiplication by the reaction vector and summation gives the symmetric map
$B_m$.  Its components are
\begin{align*}
(B(u,v))_1={}&-(u_1v_{m-1}+u_{m-1}v_1)+2u_Zv_Z
 -(u_1v_m+u_mv_1),\\
(B(u,v))_2={}&-(u_1v_2+u_2v_1)+2u_mv_m,\\
(B(u,v))_i={}&u_1v_{i-1}+u_{i-1}v_1-u_1v_i-u_iv_1,
\quad3\le i\le m-1,\\
(B(u,v))_m={}&2(u_1v_{m-1}+u_{m-1}v_1)-4u_mv_m+2u_Zv_Z
 -(u_1v_m+u_mv_1),\\
(B(u,v))_Z={}&-4u_Zv_Z+2(u_1v_m+u_mv_1).
\end{align*}
Every reaction vector is orthogonal to $c$, hence
\[
c^TB_m(u,v)=0
\]
identically.

## Scope and checkpoints

The question sought in each source was specifically whether integrability, for every center, of the distribution orthogonal to outgoing connection-geodesics forces the *dual statistical structure* to be 1-conformally flat. A result about metric conformal flatness, ambient Hessian level hypersurfaces, or statistical structures with torsion is not automatically an answer to that question.

## Additional primary texts actually read

### The 2002 continuation of the problem list

[Publisher PDF](https://www.impan.pl/shop/media/volume_intro/bc57-0-00.pdf), *PDEs, Submanifolds and Affine Differential Geometry*, Banach Center Publications 57 (Warsaw, 2002). All four PDF pages were read: introduction, printed pp. 7–8, and complete problem list, pp. 9–10. The two problem pages were also rendered and visually checked.

The list explicitly describes itself as a continuation of Binder–Simon (2000). Its ten questions concern polynomial surfaces, minimal equiaffine realizations, prescribed curvature, Weingarten operators, curve equations, realizations of affine connections, and affine/centroaffine hypersurface rigidity. It contains no radial orthogonality question, no statistical 1-conformal converse, and no acknowledgement of a counterexample to FMU 3(e). The omission cannot establish the status of a question outside its selection.

### The author-hosted 1996 workshop precursor

[Author-hosted PDF](https://www.math.sci.hokudai.ac.jp/~furuhata/research/doc/9612.pdf), Urakawa and Furuhata, “アファイン微分幾何学”小研究集会 問題集 (problem list from the December 16–18, 1996 workshop). All three PDF pages were text-inspected; the pages containing Kurose's section were visually checked.

Kurose's section 3 contains only (a), (b), and (c), addressing generalized conformal geometry/divergence, affine minimal surfaces, and algebraic curvature identities. It does not contain the later printed (d) or (e). This precursor therefore gives no answer to the target question. It also does not independently establish that the particular radial-integrability question was already on this preliminary 1996 list. The 1998 published item remains the definitive statement examined by the main review.

### Uohashi–Ohara–Fujii (2000)

Keiko Uohashi, Atsumi Ohara, and Takao Fujii, *1-conformally flat statistical submanifolds*, Osaka Journal of Mathematics 37(2) (2000), 501–507. [Institutional full text](https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/12027/ojm37_02_16.pdf), [repository DOI](https://doi.org/10.18910/12027). All seven article pages, including the complete bibliography, were read; printed pp. 501–502 were rendered and checked against extracted text.

Theorems 1 and 2 (p. 502, dimension at least two) concern statistical structures induced on level hypersurfaces of a potential in an ambient flat Hessian manifold, and realizations of 1-conformally flat structures in such an ambient manifold. Proposition 4.1 (p. 506) repeats Kurose's criterion using the projective flatness of the dual connection and symmetry of Ricci. None is an implication from the radial distributions in FMU 3(e). No explicit answer to FMU 3(e), the product-sphere counterexample, or citation of the FMU problem list appears in this paper. This is a full-text negative finding for this paper only.

### Related Matsuzoe 2010 lecture notes

[Author-hosted PDF](http://matsuzoe.web.nitech.ac.jp/infogeo/OCAMI2010/matsuzoe.pdf), *捩れをゆるす統計多様体とプレ・コントラスト関数* (Statistical manifolds admitting torsion and pre-contrast functions), lecture by Hiroshi Matsuzoe, notes recorded by Tomonori Noda. All five pages were read. The final page explicitly cites the ASPM survey as in press.

The notes discuss contrast functions, maximum likelihood, pre-contrast functions, and the geometric structures admitting torsion that these functions induce. They contain no explicit answer to the radial-integrability implication, no product-sphere counterexample, and no FMU citation. Their accessible content helps identify the author's contemporaneous research, but it cannot replace inspection of the 19-page ASPM chapter.

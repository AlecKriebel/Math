It reports necessary and sufficient conditions for certain two-point functions when the given connection is flat, without displaying those conditions. This is related background, not a located answer to the unrestricted fixed-connection question. |
| [2014 annual report for project 22540107](https://kaken.nii.ac.jp/report/KAKENHI-PROJECT-22540107/225401072014jisseki/) | Full results paragraph read from the primary HTML. It explicitly discusses geodesic-orthogonal level surfaces and a Gauss-lemma-type condition, with results for flat connections in dimensions at least three and extension to nonflat connections left as future work. No complete theorem statement or explicit counterexample appears. |

The two grant-report summaries above are contextual screening, not substitutes
for the underlying unpublished arguments. In particular, they do not show that
Kurose was unaware of the elementary Levi–Civita counterexample. The exact
property sought for a two-point function can be stronger than integrability of
the distribution in the printed question.

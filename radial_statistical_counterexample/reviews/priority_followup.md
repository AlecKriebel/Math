The independent routes converged on established ingredients, not on a located
earlier answer. In particular,
[Henmi–Kobayashi (2000)](https://doi.org/10.1017/S002776300000739X) and
[Felice–Ay's preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.11363) explicitly discuss
the Levi–Civita radial-gradient fact. We do not claim novelty for that fact,
Kurose's criterion, the standard cylinder curvature, or the known statistical
deformation. A number of papers citing FMU98 address its other questions about
Hessian realizations or tangent bundles; a citation to the list alone does not
identify an answer to 3(e).

## What would increase confidence further

The most useful next evidence would be legitimate full-text access to the
following concrete gaps, followed by a theorem-by-theorem comparison with the
printed hypothesis and conclusion:

1. Kurose (1999), *1-conformally flat statistical manifolds and their realization
   in affine space*, Fukuoka University Science Reports 29, 209–219.
2. Matsuzoe (2010), *Statistical manifolds and affine differential geometry*,
   [DOI 10.2969/aspm/05710303](https://doi.org/10.2969/aspm/05710303).
3. Binder–Simon, the older affine-geometry problems/bibliography chapter,
   [DOI 10.1142/9789812792051_0001](https://doi.org/10.1142/9789812792051_0001).
   Publisher metadata gives 2000; the earlier audit's 2001 label was an indexing
   discrepancy. The accessible 2002 continuation does not replace this chapter.
4. The actual content of Kurose's 2016 talk on Gauss-lemma-type propositions and
   generalized Hessian structures; the title and report summaries alone are
   insufficient to clear it.
5. Matsuzoe (1999), *Geometry of contrast functions and conformal geometry*,
   [DOI 10.32917/hmj/1206125160](https://doi.org/10.32917/hmj/1206125160).
   Its full text was also unavailable and the radial-geometry citation route
   makes this a relevant unresolved lead.
6. Kurose (2023/2024), *A certain ODE-system defining the geometric divergence*,
   [DOI 10.1007/s41884-023-00110-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s41884-023-00110-3).
   Only publisher abstract and references were inspected. Additional narrower
   gaps, including the full 2008 tangent-bundle paper, appear in the route reports.

Further subject-index/review coverage and specialist historical knowledge
could reduce uncertainty about unindexed, unpublished, or differently phrased
observations. No outside communication was prepared or initiated. There is no
finite web search that proves an observation has never previously been made.
The short, classical nature of this argument makes that limitation particularly
material.

A DOI or public repository timestamp would document this particular exposition's
availability; it would not certify that it is the first discovery. The defensible
claim remains: **a complete explicit negative answer, with no earlier explicit
answer found in the documented search.**

## Reliability check

The expanded background describes one-conformal flatness in terms of the
metric's conformal class, without the essential simultaneous connection change.
This is incomplete and potentially misleading for this problem. Kurose's actual
definition changes both the metric and the statistical connection; see
[Kurose (1994), p.428](https://doi.org/10.2748/tmj/1178225722).
Ordinary conformal flatness of the metric is insufficient. Indeed the round
cylinder is ordinarily conformally flat, while the statistical obstruction in
our paper is nonzero. The original papers, rather than the database's generated
explanation, control the interpretation.

For the ordinary conformal statement, set \(r=e^t\): the Euclidean metric in
polar coordinates satisfies \(dr^2+r^2g_{S^2}=e^{2t}(dt^2+g_{S^2})\).
This elementary identity does not flatten the statistical connection under
Kurose's prescribed 1-conformal transformation.

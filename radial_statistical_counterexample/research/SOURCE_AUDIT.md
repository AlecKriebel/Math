# Original-source audit

Audit date: 22 September 2026 (America/Los_Angeles; 23 September UTC).

## Exact target and conventions

H. Furuhata, H. Matsuzoe and H. Urakawa, *Open Problems in Affine Differential Geometry and Related Topics*, Interdisciplinary Information Sciences **4** (1998), 125–127, DOI [10.4036/iis.1998.125](https://doi.org/10.4036/iis.1998.125), item 3(e), p.126. The problem is attributed within the article to Takashi Kurose. The workshop was held 16–18 December 1996; 1998 is the publication year.

[Publisher PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/iis/4/2/4_2_125/_pdf/-char/en).

Paraphrase: for an arbitrary center p in an n-dimensional statistical manifold, define a rank n−1 distribution on U minus p, where U is a connection-convex neighborhood. At q its vectors are metric-orthogonal to the velocity of the connection-geodesic from p to q. Does integrability of every such distribution force the dual statistical manifold to be 1-conformally flat?

The puncture is explicit in the source. There is no nonzero cubic-tensor condition or exclusion of metric connections. Item 3(a), p.125, explicitly regards a Riemannian metric with its Levi–Civita connection as a statistical manifold. No hypothesis from another question in item 3 is imposed on 3(e).

T. Kurose, *On the divergences of 1-conformally flat statistical manifolds*, Tohoku Mathematical Journal **46** (1994), 427–433, DOI [10.2748/tmj/1178225722](https://doi.org/10.2748/tmj/1178225722).

[Publisher PDF](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/tmj1949/46/3/46_3_427/_pdf/-char/en).

Pages 427–428 define a statistical manifold by a torsion-free affine connection and a nondegenerate metric whose covariant derivative is symmetric. The duality identity is metric duality. Page 428's alpha-conformal formula specializes at alpha=1 to h-hat=exp(phi)h and connection-hat=connection−h(X,Y)grad_h(phi). Flatness refers to the affine connection. Proposition 1 relates this to projective flatness of the dual with symmetric Ricci tensor. The manuscript's primary proof does not use that proposition.

The source PDFs were downloaded and visually inspected, including formulas that text extraction omits. Third-party PDFs are not redistributed in the upload package. Their URLs, bibliographic information, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded in `sources.json`.

## Scope and unresolved historical question

The claim is a negative answer to the printed implication, in dimensions at least three. It is not a classification of all statistical manifolds with integrable radial distributions, and it makes no assertion about every individual dimension. It is not a counterexample to ordinary Riemannian conformal flatness; the round cylinder is ordinarily conformally flat.

The catalog identifier is a cross-reference, not proof of current open status. The public database page could not be inspected in this audit. Historical novelty is assessed separately in the priority report and cannot be proved by a finite search.

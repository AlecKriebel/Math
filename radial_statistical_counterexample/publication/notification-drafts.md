## Comment for AMR-059-0011

I've posted a short, unrefereed preprint giving an explicit negative answer to
Question 3(e) as printed in Furuhata–Matsuzoe–Urakawa (1998), p.126:

**Radial orthogonality does not imply dual 1-conformal flatness**

https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22983519

The counterexample is S²(1) × ℝ with its product metric and Levi–Civita
connection. For every center, the radial orthogonal distributions are tangent
to energy level sets by the first variation formula. The connection is
self-dual, but the sphere and mixed planes give incompatible curvature
conditions for a statistical 1-conformal flattening.

The four-page note also gives a non-self-dual example with nowhere zero cubic
tensor and the product extension to every dimension at least three. The deposit
includes the proof, source, exact verification scripts, and audit notes.

The ingredients are classical, and historical priority remains unresolved;
no first-resolution claim is made. AI assistance is disclosed in the paper
and deposit.

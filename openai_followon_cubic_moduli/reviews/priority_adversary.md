## Overlap with the public all-dimensional manuscript

KSZZ define the cubic component by dimension, volume `3(n−1)^n`, and Q-Gorenstein deformation to a smooth cubic. Theorem 1.1 states that every K-semistable variety in that component is a cubic, that K/GIT stability agree, and that the stacks and good moduli spaces are naturally isomorphic. Section 3 explicitly treats `n ≥ 5`; lower dimensions are inherited. Theorem 4.1 proves the boundary assertion; page 13 transfers it to moduli. This covers the algebraic content requested here and exceeds the user's restricted topological target.

Their new mechanism controls the limiting hyperplane system and uses slope semistability to rule out intermediate image dimensions. Thus it differs from the proposed gap route. The distinction between duplication of the theorem and duplication of the proof must be preserved.

The core GH topology follows from the established KE/K-moduli comparison if complex structures are retained, as in Spotti–Sun's convention. Specifying this category is necessary clarification, not by itself a new theorem. Bare metric-space GH moduli identify a variety with its complex conjugate, so cannot simply be equated to the complex GIT space without addressing that loss of information.

## What was already public about this proof route

Spotti–Sun's Theorem 1.3(2), public since April 30, 2017, gives the cubic KE/GIT conclusion assuming the metric ODP gap through dimension `n`. Section 5.2 proves it using cubic volume, the Cartier-root/Gorenstein theorem, Fujita's classification and moduli continuity. Remark 5.6(1) records the algebraic-to-metric implication when the required normalized-volume/density identification holds. Li–Liu's July 2017 version proves global minimization for the Reeb valuation of a smooth Sasaki–Einstein link, including irregular fields.

Once a valid all-dimensional gap is available, the proposed derivation is

`upstream algebraic gap + established Reeb minimization/density bridge + Spotti–Sun's conditional cubic theorem ⇒ requested compactification`.

This is an immediate consequence of a new upstream input through an old reduction. The direction from a bound on an infimum to an upper bound for the Reeb valuation must be checked carefully, but doing so does not invent the transfer. The correct attribution is OpenAI for the new gap, Li–Liu for the needed minimization, Spotti–Sun for the cubic reduction, and KSZZ for the currently disclosed stronger all-dimensional cubic result.

An assembled unconditional gap-based proof might not already exist as one document; I have not proved that it does. That uncertainty must not be converted into a literal assertion that every line of our intended note has already appeared together. Nevertheless, all its proposed mathematical mechanisms are disclosed, and the intended cubic conclusion is already publicly claimed. A bibliography, dependency diagram and verified concatenation do not establish a new theorem or substantive proof contribution.

Priority and validity are independent issues. KSZZ's existence does not validate the upstream gap. If the upstream input fails its audit, our derivation remains conditional. Nor does the mere existence of a public manuscript certify every step of KSZZ; this review records prior disclosure, without substituting it for proof verification.

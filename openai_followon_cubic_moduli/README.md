# Metric rigidity at the boundary of cubic compactifications

Current publication candidate: a sharp singular Cartier-index rigidity criterion and its application to the ordinary unmarked metric GH compactification of cubic n-folds, n>=5. The complex cubic K/GIT comparison is credited as prior work and reproduced as a consequence of OpenAI's cited gap theorem, Li-Liu and Spotti-Sun. The paper makes no first-solution or first-disclosure claim.

main.tex is standalone, with inline bibliography; paper.pdf is an actual exported eight-page PDF. The new criterion excludes product covers when an ample Cartier root satisfies -K=rL and r>dim(X)/2+1, proves stable tangent and parallel-complex-structure rigidity, and has a product counterexample at equality. DSII regularity, normal extension and SGA2 Picard uniqueness identify all cubic boundary metric fibers as conjugation orbits. No arbitrary nonsmoothable metric-completion theorem, scheme/stack upgrade or effective GH bound is claimed.

Author: Alec Kriebel, ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X. No affiliation or coauthors are invented. AI tools were used extensively in research, drafting and verification. The preprint has not undergone conventional human peer review. Scoped AI audits are evidence, not full formal certification of the external inputs.

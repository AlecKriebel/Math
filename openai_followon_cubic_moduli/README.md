# Metric rigidity at the boundary of cubic compactifications

Published version 1.0: a sharp singular Cartier-index rigidity criterion and its application to the ordinary unmarked metric GH compactification of cubic n-folds, n>=5. The complex cubic K/GIT comparison is credited as prior work and reproduced as a consequence of OpenAI's cited gap theorem, Li-Liu and Spotti-Sun. The paper makes no first-solution or first-disclosure claim.

main.tex is standalone, with inline bibliography; paper.pdf is an actual exported eight-page PDF. The new criterion excludes product covers when an ample Cartier root satisfies -K=rL and r>dim(X)/2+1, proves stable tangent and parallel-complex-structure rigidity, and has a product counterexample at equality. DSII regularity, normal extension and SGA2 Picard uniqueness identify all cubic boundary metric fibers as conjugation orbits. No arbitrary nonsmoothable metric-completion theorem, scheme/stack upgrade or effective GH bound is claimed.

## 047 ⇒ negative Costa retract conjecture: PASS, immediate corollary
A=C[X] is nonpolynomial of transcendence degree four, while A[t] is C[x1,...,x5]. Inclusion A→A[t] and t↦0 give a split algebra retraction. Transport through the supplied isomorphism. No extra smoothness/normality assumptions needed. This disproves the general characteristic-zero retract assertion in ambient dimension five; **does not settle ambient dimension four**. No Costa/retract mention found in 047 TeX. But Nagamine arXiv:1811.04153 Proposition1.4 explicitly states retract conjecture implies cancellation, so this is a known one-line implication, not a substantial new proof. A concrete polynomial idempotent endomorphism could make a useful checkable package.

## 091 ⇒ smooth even log-Minkowski uniqueness: PASS with precise scope
Primary source https://arxiv.org/html/2510.21530 Theorem4 states: for p∈[0,1), Lp Brunn–Minkowski inequality implies unique smooth even solution of h^(1−p)det(∇²h+h Id)=f for positive smooth even f on S^n. Thus 091 supplies the premise, including p=0. Here h is a positive support function of a smooth strictly convex origin-symmetric body. The evenness and positive smooth density restrictions are essential to this citation. At p=0, arbitrary singular even measures do NOT have unique solution: origin-symmetric boxes of equal volume and identical facet normals share cone-volume measure. Hence any all-measure log-Minkowski uniqueness claim is false. 091 itself does not assert uniqueness; its introduction explicitly distinguishes existence from the volume inequality. Theorem4 already gives the conditional bridge, so novelty is an unconditional corollary, not a new PDE method.

For 0<p<1, uniqueness among full-dimensional origin-symmetric bodies with equal Lp surface-area measure is plausibly a direct consequence even without smoothness. Proof checkpoint: log-Minkowski + Jensen yields Lp mixed-volume inequality, equality forces h_L/h_K constant dV_K-a.e.; applying the inequality in both directions for equal S_p gives equal volumes, hence ratio one. Classical mixed-volume equality then forces K,L homothetic translates, and origin symmetry/equal volume force equality. This uses the first-variation equivalence valid for arbitrary convex bodies, and should be spelled out; **do not drop origin symmetry**. Smooth theorem alone does not establish arbitrary nonsymmetric uniqueness.

## 032 ⇒ Hodge for K3 moduli and products: PASS, substantial duplication caveat
Primary https://arxiv.org/html/1806.08284 Theorem0.1 states exactly: S complex projective K3 (or abelian) surface, α∈Br(S), M smooth projective moduli of Gieseker-stable α-twisted sheaves, OR smooth projective moduli of σ-stable objects in Dᵇ(S,α) for generic σ. Then h(M) is a direct summand of a finite sum of Tate twists h(S^ki), ki≤dim M. This is unconditional and does NOT assume finite-dimensional motives (that is separate Cor0.2). Applying 032 to arbitrary mixed products of K3 surfaces and tensoring split correspondences proves rational HC in every codimension on any finite product of such moduli, including mixed Hilbert schemes. It extends to birational smooth projective hyperkähler varieties by known Chow-motive isomorphism. Exclude arbitrary deformations of K3^[n] type, singular compactifications and arbitrary abelian-surface bases.

Same paper ext:criterion (around lines 145–155) proves exact KS tensor ⇒ HC+GHC on all powers of ANY K3.

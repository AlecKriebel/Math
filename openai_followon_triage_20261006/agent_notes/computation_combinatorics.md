## 1. Binary-alphabet superpolynomial two-way automaton lower bounds

**Target.** An explicit family of binary languages with polynomial-size one-way NFAs for which every equivalent two-way DFA needs stretched-exponentially many states. Parallel target: superpolynomial complementation cost for binary 2NFAs. This removes the conspicuous growing-alphabet qualification in family 129. Do not advertise a linear-exponent 2^{Omega(n)} bound before optimizing the encoding, nor L != NL.

**Exact new input.** `/Users/alec/Desktop/math/preprints/An-exponential-two-way-deterministic-state-lower-bound-for-one-way-liveness-September-25-2026/build/sections/introduction.tex:100`: OWL_h uses the alphabet of every binary relation on h points (2^{h^2} symbols), has an h+3-state one-way NFA, and every deterministic two-way recognizer satisfies 4(s+2)^2 >= 2^{floor((h-2)/31)}. At line 192 the paper explicitly limits its conclusion to growing alphabets. `/Users/alec/Desktop/math/lean/docs/129.md` confirms the scope. The companion complementation theorem uses the same relational family and yields an exponential h lower bound for nondeterministic complement recognizers.

**Mechanism.** Encode each relation as its h^2 binary adjacency entries. A binary source NFA scans whole fixed-length blocks, remembers source state, guesses destination, checks the chosen matrix entry, and passes the destination to the next block. Conservative state bound O(h^4) suffices; count carefully to improve it. Conversely, an s-state binary 2DFA can be simulated over the relation alphabet by storing the virtual bit offset in a block, using O(sh^2) states. Its transitions can inspect the indexed matrix bit of the current giant-alphabet symbol. Therefore s must remain exp(Omega(h)), yielding a stretched-exponential lower bound in source state count. Malformed block lengths are checked with O(h^2) states; complement reductions restrict to well-formed encodings.

**Exact gap/checks.** Write complete finite-state encoders/decoders and count states, including empty input, endmarkers, stay moves, acceptance conventions, and nonhalting rejecting runs. Check that each reverse simulation transition crosses at most one real input symbol. The entire central lower bound comes from the new result; no fresh lower-bound mechanism is needed. Strong likelihood of a short proof, high theoretical impact, but very high race/independent-discovery risk because the transfer is standard.

**Primary comparator.** Adeogun–Kapoutsis, [A quadratic lower bound for 2DFAs against one-way liveness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.24279), 2026. Its abstract confirms the previous general one-way-liveness lower bound was quadratic. Pighizzini, [Two-Way Finite Automata: Old and Recent Results](https://arxiv.org/abs/1208.2755), supplies historical problem scope. Corpus-wide searches found no binary-alphabet transfer statement. Adversarial check requested from pde_geometry agent.

## 2. Uniform deterministic finite-field construction and factorization over arbitrary finite fields

**Target.** Given p, m, and an explicit representation of F_{p^m}, factor every dense degree-n polynomial over that field in deterministic time polynomial in n,m,log p, including multiplicities. Package with deterministic construction of irreducible polynomials of every prescribed degree over finite fields. This is a natural computational-algebra stream to combine with the arithmetic agent's root-extraction consequences.

**Exact new input.** `/Users/alec/Desktop/math/preprints/Deterministic-Polynomial-Factorization-over-Prime-Fields-October-4-2026/build/sections/00-introduction.tex:27` proves uniform deterministic factorization only over prime fields F_p, with enormous but fixed exponent 10^{12}. The theorem depends on the companion Hecke zero-free theorem. Do not suggest practical efficiency. No Lean doc for 142 was listed in the catalogue.

**Mechanism.** Build the Berlekamp Frobenius-fixed algebra of F_q[x]/f by linear algebra. Trace-coordinate functions Tr_{F_q/F_p}(alpha_j b_i), with alpha_j a field basis and b_i a basis of the fixed algebra, separate distinct component values. Their minimal polynomials lie in F_p and split into linear factors; invoke 142 and gcd splitting. Standard squarefree decomposition handles multiplicities. All vector-space dimensions are polynomial in n,m.

For irreducible construction, an existing direct black-box reduction is already published: Shoup, [New algorithms for finding irreducible polynomials over finite fields](https://www.shoup.net/papers/detirred.pdf), Theorem 3.1, reduces construction over F_p in deterministic polynomial time to polynomial factorization over F_p. This subtarget is especially safe: instantiate an existing theorem instead of redesigning the construction. The current literature comparator [Pseudo-Deterministic Construction of Irreducible Polynomials over Finite Fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.04071) explicitly uses Shoup's reduction and gives a weaker pseudo-deterministic approach before the supplied breakthrough.

**Exact gap/checks.** Track representation and trace computations, prove a polynomial bound on calls/degrees, specify how an initial field representation is given or constructed, and handle inseparability without factoring integers or enumerating F_q. Audit whether the original factorization proof already includes this through an internal lemma; keyword searches did not find an external-field theorem. Novelty is an algorithmic consequence, not a new factoring mechanism. Confidence high for irreducible construction; high but requires writeup for extension-field factorization. Adversarial check requested from pde_geometry agent.

## 3. FPRAS and sampling for arbitrary nonnegative weighted hafnians

**Target.** A uniform FPRAS for haf(A) of every even-order symmetric nonnegative rational matrix A, allowing arbitrary zeros and weights encoded in binary; exact detection of zero; polynomial runtime in full bit input and inverse relative error. Then weighted perfect-matching and weighted f-factor samplers via self-reduction. This extends the new count beyond its explicit unweighted input model.

**Exact new input and nonduplication.** `/Users/alec/Desktop/math/preprints/A-Fully-Polynomial-Randomized-Approximation-Scheme-for-Perfect-Matchings-in-General-Graphs-September-23-2026/build/main.tex:132` proves an FPRAS for unweighted simple graphs. Lines 3081–3284 already prove *unweighted* f-factor, fixed-cardinality matching counting, and TV sampling. Crucially lines 3126–3127 explicitly exclude edge weights and compressed multiplicities. `/Users/alec/Desktop/math/lean/docs/113.md` confirms unweighted formalized scope. The entropy companion's introduction lines 241–256 still describes weaker weighted hafnian algorithms, and its own weighted result is deterministic exponential-factor approximation, not FPRAS.

**Mechanism.** Clear rational denominators with a common multiplier D of polynomial bit length; haf scales by D^{n/2}. Compile each positive integer weight W into a two-terminal simple perfect-matching gadget of size O(log W), whose unmatched-terminal count is 1 and matched-terminal count W. Binary addition/doubling composition avoids numeric multiplicity expansion. Apply 113 to the resulting unweighted graph and divide by the known scale. Deletion conditioning gives weighted sampling; Tutte's factor reduction transfers to weighted factors if assigned weights reside only on original cross edges.

**Exact gap/checks.** Supply and verify the two-terminal gadget identity, including the absence of one-terminal contributions, simple-graph realization, O(log W) size, rational scaling, zero weights, empty graphs, and worst-case runtime. This is very likely standard rather than a deep new obstacle, but gadget details were not independently proved here. Since the source's internal chain uses positive weights, auditing a direct weighted extension is an alternative, although gadgets keep dependence on the base theorem minimal.

**Primary comparator.** Barvinok, [Approximating permanents and hafnians](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.07518), provides quasipolynomial approximation under fixed positive lower bounds on all entries. [On randomized estimators of the Hafnian of a nonnegative matrix](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.10143) explains that familiar estimators can need exponentially many samples even on simple examples. The proposed arbitrary-support binary-weight FPRAS removes these restrictions. Do not claim general Gaussian boson sampling becomes easy: complex signs/phases and output-distribution issues lie outside this target.

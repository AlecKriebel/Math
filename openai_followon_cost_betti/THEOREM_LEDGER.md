# Precise target and current strongest result

## Core target (not yet promoted)

Let A=F(a,b_1,...,b_99), w=ab_1ab_2...ab_99a, J=<b_1,...,b_99,w>, B=J x <t>, and Gamma=A *_J B. Let X be the conull free part of [0,1]^Gamma with the right Bernoulli action (xg)(h)=x(gh). Exhibit R=R_(Gamma acting on X) with beta_0^(2)(R)=beta_1^(2)(R)=0 and Cost(R)>1. Gamma must be infinite and finitely generated, and R must be free and p.m.p.

## Established conditional deduction

If the upstream cost claims Cost(Y_M)<=1+99/M for every integer M>100 and Cost(X)>=1+eta (eta>0) are valid, Gaboriau's inequality and free-action invariance give 0<=beta_1^(2)(Gamma)<=99/M for all such M. Hence beta_1^(2)(Gamma)=0, and beta_1^(2)(R_X)=0. Infinitude and freeness give beta_0^(2)=0. Thus Cost(R_X)-1>beta_1^(2)(R_X)-beta_0^(2)(R_X).

The full inequality is Cost(R)-1 >= beta_1^(2)(R)-beta_0^(2)(R). For infinite free orbits beta_0^(2)=0. For finite orbits the correction must be retained.

Group cost defined as the infimum over free p.m.p. actions satisfies Cost_*(Gamma)=1=1+beta_1^(2)(Gamma). This is not a counterexample to that group equality.

Mechanism: source theorem gives essentially free Y_M actions of costs ≤1+99/M; Gaboriau's inequality implies β₁⁽²⁾(Γ)=0. Free-action invariance transfers this to Bernoulli relation, whose cost is >1. Source: preprints/A-group-without-fixed-price-October-5-2026/build/introduction.tex and conclusion.tex. Primary background: https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/FAQ.pdf and https://perso.ens-lyon.fr/gaboriau/Travaux-Publi/ME-Cost-L2-Lectures/ME-Cost-L2-lectures.pdf. No Betti claim found in source intro/conclusion or relevant corpus search. No 259 Lean scope file found.

2. **Positive-characteristic Kaplansky idempotent counterexample and projective cancellation (197).** Let R=F₂[G], ab=1, ba≠1 with torsion-free finitely presented G. Then e=1−ba is idempotent, nonzero and not 1. Thus the positive-characteristic idempotent conjecture fails. Moreover R≅R⊕eR as right modules, so nonzero finitely generated projective eR has [eR]=0 in K₀(R). This gives a precise cancellation failure package. Source: preprints/A-Torsion-Free-Group-Algebra-That-Is-Not-Directly-Finite-October-4-2026/build/sections/introduction.tex, theorem main. Importance medium-high; tractability immediate; publication novelty low unless explicit coefficients and module maps are extracted and formalized. Important distinction: family 207 proves characteristic-zero idempotent triviality only. Lean docs/197.md covers older torsion constructions and determinant result, **not** October 4 torsion-free strengthening. Formalization must bridge that gap before advertised as Lean-verified.

3. **Single-solution Diophantine height bound impossibility (242).** Exact target: no total computable height bound for unique natural-number solutions to arbitrary integer polynomial equations; quantitative domination of every computable function by Tyszka's unique-solution bound ξ(n). Mechanism: source's single-fold representation of halting gives unique witnesses; a computable height bound would decide halting. Existing conditional consequences already established in https://arxiv.org/abs/1404.5975 and https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.3549 become unconditional. Tractability immediate, importance medium, novelty low. Source already proves undecidability under at-most-one promise, so do not repackage that as new; a genuinely new deliverable should give explicit universal polynomial/system and quantified dimension-degree budget, which source explicitly leaves existential (build/consequences.tex lines 35 onward). Such effectivity extraction may be substantial.

4. **Quenched dynamical Voronoi scaling limit (224), provisional higher-value stream.** Transfer the pivotal-measure/Poisson-clock construction of Garban–Pete–Schramm https://arxiv.org/abs/1305.5526 to the new quenched near-critical universality. New source gives joint near-critical threshold laws, not time dynamics. Gap: conditional pivotal-measure convergence and dynamic multi-time/tightness estimates; must inspect marked construction and ensure monotone threshold coupling can be replaced by repeated recolorings. Plausible but **not yet justified as straightforward**. Existing Vanneuville work already establishes exceptional times and identifies noise scale in terms of arm probabilities, so those must not be claimed new. No dynamical theorem identified in 224 source intro.

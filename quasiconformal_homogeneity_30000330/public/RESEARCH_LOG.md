# Research accounting

Five substantive attempts were carried out. Source lookup, packaging, arithmetic checks, and independent review are not additional proof attempts.

1. **Geometric compactness.** Tested the limit argument, identified loss of topology to the hyperbolic plane, and derived the explicit area-based systole bound for closed genus g. This rules out bounded-genus counterexample sequences but leaves topology escaping to infinity.
2. **Regular-cover construction.** Tested a negative-answer strategy. Proved the additive bound d <= ell + 2 diam(base) using the Lipschitz injectivity-radius function and deck transitivity. Residually finite towers show d/ell can tend to 1. This does not force the homogeneity constant to tend to 1, so the counterexample construction fails at its central analytic step.
3. **Conformal approximation.** Traced the rigidity argument through canonical boundary-fixing lifts and quotient diameter. It succeeds with the strong homotopy restriction. The missing unrestricted replacement theorem and the direction of the homogeneity-constant inequalities were isolated explicitly.
4. **Mapping-class counting.** Proved an elementary disk-cover bound with radius D0(K squared), including the finite-subgroup specialization. Deduced necessary superlinear class proliferation for a closed counterexample sequence. The needed genus-linear upper bound is unproved; pointwise finiteness and finite-index subgroup results do not supply it.
5. **Short-geodesic coverage.** Proved the tube-area lower bound on the orbit of a systole, then tested transfer of the planar topological argument. Curves meeting once on a handle invalidate that transfer. The required near-systole count and an infinite-area replacement remain absent.

These are five distinct proof or counterexample approaches with explicit mathematical derivations and failure points. The established inputs and the relation to existing work are credited in REPORT.md. No attempt yields a full resolution.

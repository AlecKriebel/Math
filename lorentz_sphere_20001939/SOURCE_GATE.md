## Exact question and assumptions

Burns–Matveev, *Open problems and questions about geodesics*, AIM version, printed page 29, Question 12.0.6:

> Can a 3-dimensional sphere admit two nonproportional projectively equivalent metrics of indefinite signature?

Primary source: https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/geodesicsproblems.pdf
Published paper: *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 41 (2021), 641–684, https://doi.org/10.1017/etds.2019.73

The setting is smooth global metrics on closed S³. In dimension three, indefinite signature is Lorentzian up to overall sign. Projective equivalence means equality of unparameterized geodesics; it is not affine equivalence or the existence of a projective vector field. The statement supplies no geodesic-completeness, invariance, analyticity, diagonalizability, or constant-Jordan-type assumption. The appended acknowledgements in the catalogue extraction are not part of the question.

The exact catalogue URL https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/20001939 was not readable through web extraction, and a direct cloud-browser inspection returned a 403 “This request was blocked” page. The problem ID, title and statement were therefore checked against the supplied pinned corpus, whose two SHA256 digests were verified. Live page freshness is not claimed. The primary AIM statement was independently read through web text extraction.

## Primary literature used

1. Bolsinov–Matveev, *Splitting and gluing lemmas for geodesically equivalent pseudo-Riemannian metrics*, *Transactions AMS* 363 (2011), 4081–4107. Theorem 3 supplies the local-product splitting for relatively prime characteristic factors. https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.0535 and https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-2011-05187-1
2. Bolsinov–Matveev, *Local normal forms for geodesically equivalent pseudo-Riemannian metrics*, *Transactions AMS* 367 (2015), 6719–6749. Equation (5), Theorem 1.4, Proposition 3.1 and Corollary 1.13 were inspected. The nonreal-spectrum corollary forces a finite torus cover in dimension three, so it excludes nonreal spectrum on S³. Local forms do not classify global transitions. https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.2492 and https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-2014-06416-7
3. Matveev–Mounoud, *Gallot–Tanno theorem for closed incomplete pseudo-Riemannian manifolds and applications*, *Annals of Global Analysis and Geometry* 38 (2010), 259–271. Corollary 5.2 gives mobility two for a non-affine closed indefinite pair, without assuming completeness. https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.5344 and https://doi.org/10.1007/s10455-010-9211-7
4. Bokan–Šukilović–Vukmirović, *Geodesically equivalent metrics on homogenous spaces*, *Czechoslovak Mathematical Journal* 69 (2019), 945–954. The published invariant-pair restriction is distinguished from the unrestricted-partner deduction in Attempt 3. https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.08240 and https://doi.org/10.21136/CMJ.2018.0557-17
5. Bolsinov–Konyaev–Matveev, *Applications of Nijenhuis Geometry V: Geodesic Equivalence and Finite-Dimensional Reductions of Integrable Quasilinear Systems*, *Journal of Nonlinear Science* 34 (2024), article 33. The local construction result is not a global S³ existence theorem. https://doi.org/10.1007/s00332-023-10008-0

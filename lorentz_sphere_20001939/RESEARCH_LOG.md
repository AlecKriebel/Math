# Five-attempt research log

All five attempts address the original global indefinite-metric existence question. Source retrieval, prior-attempt checks, verification runs, editing, review and packaging are not counted as proof attempts.

1. **Global spectral splitting.** Applied the splitting lemma and finite-cover compactness to exclude any globally simple real eigenvalue; obtained two mandatory collision sets. No argument joins those sets or controls the intervening signature/Jordan changes.
2. **Constant Jordan type.** Derived the canonical-frame Riccati identity and excluded an everywhere size-three block; combined the algebraic cases to force a type-change set. Canonical frames need not extend across that set.
3. **Symmetry inheritance.** Used the mobility-two representation to make compact connected isometries fix every compatible tensor; excluded compact-transitive candidates, including a left-invariant first metric with an unrestricted partner. Arbitrary candidate metrics need not have such symmetries.
4. **Cross-term torus ansatz.** Reduced the full invariant equations, proved constancy of the angular block, and used endpoint collapse to exclude all candidates with nondegenerate principal orbits. The inverse orbit matrix fails at null tori.
5. **Degenerate-orbit and collision boundary.** Constructed smooth global Lorentz metrics with null principal tori, proved a same-radical matching condition for partners there, and constructed a genuine local compatible Jordan transition. The examples test and delimit the attempted extension; neither gives a global pair on S³.

Final accounting: 5/5 substantive mathematical attempts; original question unresolved; partial obstruction results only. Four symbolic identity checks passed. Independent review remains necessary, and a passing identity check must not be interpreted as a formal verification of the global claims.

# Exact mathematical inputs and source-read limits

The written proof, rather than finite computations, establishes the theorem.
All statements concern a field of characteristic zero and nonzero h.
The basis and normalizer formulas are insensitive to scalar normalization:
Benkart–Lopes–Ondrus use a monic radical polynomial; pi=h/monic_gcd(h,h')
differs by the nonzero leading coefficient. Their spans and innerness tests
are unchanged. The algebra can also be rescaled to monic h.

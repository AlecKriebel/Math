# Bounded priority assessment, 22 October 2026

No authenticated prior full classification for all nonzero h in the ordinary
canonical HH*(A_h) was found in the inspected sources. This is a bounded
literature result, not a proof that no earlier work exists or a certificate
that the question remained open when the argument was developed.
The classification was already public in project PR 1391 before this note.
The present preprint is dated 22 October 2026 and is not backdated.
No publication DOI is assigned by this package.

Essential inheritance and distinctions:

* Lopes–Solotar 2021 and Benkart–Lopes–Ondrus 2015 supply the nontrivial
  Hochschild/resolution/derivation structure. The new note is a deduction from
  that structure plus the canonical cup comparison and obstruction, not a
  new structural cohomology computation. The later 2023 workshop records
  the concrete BV question. Source bodies and proof-read limits are in
  DEPENDENCIES.md.
* Herscovich–Li, Doc. Math. 27 (2022), 1773–1804,
  https://doi.org/10.4171/DM/X18 ,
  actual publisher PDF https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/29296 ,
  SHA256 dd1ebe978ced66bb48a25ee2b0c5a9ff405849e6e8f07e7c72d39e767877a92f:
  introduction, FK(3) definition/basis and complete Proposition 4.15 proof
  were read. It already rules out any degree-minus-one bracket generator
  without a square-zero assumption, using incompatible cup/bracket relations.
  This phenomenon and obstruction strategy are prior and must be credited.
  FK(3), a finite-dimensional algebra with three generators, is a different
  algebra; this DOI does not publish the all-h classification.
* Kowalzig–Krahmer, J. Reine Angew. Math. 697 (2014), 159–219,
  https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2012-0086 ,
  actual arXiv:1203.4984v2: Theorem 1.7 and Section 7.3/7.4 were read.
  Its ordinary-HH theorem requires a semisimple modular automorphism.
  Twisted coefficients in homological duality do not mean its conclusion
  concerns only twisted cohomology. It cannot be applied to the nonconstant
  h branch by discarding semisimplicity.
* Bian–Itagaki–Kou–Lyu–Zhou, arXiv:2603.04834v1 and current v2:
  actual introduction, truncated-cycle setup, Criterion 7.7, its relevant
  proof mechanism and Theorem 7.11 were read in v1; current v2 essential
  criterion and theorem were read and give the same family distinction.
  v2 PDF SHA256 9fd257db539beedae14756f62bb125ccf9091a40b9f2d14f4bc7cb757c7cc029.
  This is a positive theorem for KZ_e/J^N. Its criterion imposes commuting
  odd generators. It neither publishes the all-h classification nor
  contradicts our cup-zero noncommuting pair.
* Liu–Ma, arXiv:2009.05910v2 (2021), subsequently Front. Math. 17 (2022),
  DOI 10.1007/s11464-021-0978-6: actual introduction, GWA definition,
  semisimple Nakayama formula/Theorem 2.13 and Section 5.1/5.2
  BV formulas with their direct Connes/duality calculation were read.
  The quantum-GWA hypotheses and classical-type conclusion do not give
  an A_h theorem. The author-manuscript body is
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/2009.05910v2 ,
  SHA256 844da93b076ccdfb3ac17fa07a78255dce78b0a656779295390dc6113dee1d9f.

# Full Gerstenhaber BV classification candidate

## Scope and result
Let k be a field of characteristic zero and 0 != h in k[x]. Set A=k<x,Y>/(Yx-xY-h), d=monic gcd(h,h'), and pi=h/d. The candidate theorem is: the existing Hochschild Gerstenhaber algebra HH*(A) admits a square-zero degree -1 generator if and only if d=1. For d=1 the zero operator works. For d!=1 there is not even a degree -1 generator, regardless of square-zero.

This concerns the ordinary full Hochschild cohomology and its existing bracket, not a different zero bracket or a twisted/invariant replacement.

Public source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/30909 . Corollary 3.11 identifies HH2=A/dA; section 3 gives HH0=k and HHn=0 for n>=3; section 6.1 and Theorem 6.2 give the center of HH1 and D_g inner iff g in h k[x]. Nakayama automorphism at p1381: x maps to x, Y maps to Y+h'.
OWR46/2023 p2702 explicitly asks for a BV operator generating the existing Gerstenhaber bracket; question 2 concerns this A_h (not finite-dimensional Frobenius).

## Approach 1: recover graded and Lie structures
HH0=k, HH2=A/dA, higher zero. D_g(x)=0, D_g(Y)=g. Such D_g are inner iff h divides g. If d=1, Theorem 6.2 says HH1 is its center, hence abelian. If d!=1, HH1 is infinite-dimensional and contains a nonzero central class c=D_d. Theorem 6.2(b) applies since deg d<deg h.

## Approach 2: derive exactly the necessary cup formula
Use normalized bar notation [a|b]. The degree-two generator of the small resolution for r=Yx-xY-h maps into the bar resolution as
 [Y|x]-[x|Y]-sum_{s>=2} h_s sum_{i=1}^{s-1} [x^i|x] x^{s-1-i},
where h=sum h_s x^s. Its boundary equals the image of the relation differential: the internal terms telescope and cancel [h]. Thus for derivations D_g and E the cup class is represented in A/dA by
 (D_g cup E)(r)=g E(x).
All polynomial correction terms vanish because D_g(x^i)=0. In particular c cup E=0 for all E, since d E(x) belongs to dA. This establishes the needed products without assuming all degree-one products vanish.

## Approach 3: degree-one BV constraint
Write lambda(a)=Delta(a) in k for a in HH1. Since c is central and c cup a=0, the BV generating identity on degree-one elements gives
 0=[c,a]=lambda(c)a-lambda(a)c.
HH1 has dimension >1 and c!=0, so lambda(c)=0 and then lambda(a)=0 for every a. Therefore any generator must vanish on HH1. Consequently a cup b=0 implies [a,b]=0. This necessary condition is enough to contradict existence.

## Approach 4: explicit obstruction for every repeated irreducible factor
Choose an irreducible u dividing d and set g=d/u. In characteristic zero u is separable and u also divides pi. Define a=D_g and define b by
 b(x)=pi,  b(Y)=L Y,  L=pi h'/h-pi'=pi d'/d.
The expression L is polynomial since pi contains each distinct irreducible factor of d. The derivation relation is checked directly:
 [LY,x]+[Y,pi]-h'pi = Lh+h pi'-h'pi=0.
The cup formula gives a cup b=[g pi]=0 in A/dA, since u divides pi.
The commutator has zero x-component and Y-component
 q=L g-pi g'=pi(d'/d)(d/u)-pi(d/u)'=pi d u'/u^2=h u'/u^2.
This is a nonzero polynomial: u^2 divides h; u'!=0. It is not divisible by h, since deg(q)=deg(h)-2deg(u)+deg(u')<deg(h). Thus [a,b]=D_q is a nonzero HH1 class, contradicting Approach 3.
No square-zero hypothesis was used in this negative result.

## Approach 5: positive and boundary cases; Nakayama issue
If d=1, HH>=2=0, HH1 abelian, brackets with HH0=k vanish, and Delta=0 generates the entire bracket and squares to zero. This includes all nonconstant squarefree h, including h=x; the Nakayama translation is not semisimple whenever h'!=0, since the invariant span of Y and h' is a nontrivial size-two Jordan block. For nonzero constant h the algebra is Weyl, HH*=k, and nu=id. For h=x^n, n>=2, the obstruction becomes a=D_{x^{n-2}}, b(x)=x, b(Y)=(n-1)Y, [a,b]=D_{x^{n-2}}, while the cup is x^{n-1}=0 in HH2. The primary cited family assumes h!=0. If h=0 is allowed in the actual embedded-subalgebra description, the algebra generated by x and h(x)y is k[x], not the abstract two-generator presentation. The latter presentation at h=0 is instead the commutative polynomial plane. Both have standard divergence BV structures, but they must not be identified.

## Independent audit result and exact checks
An independent mathematical audit accepted the classification, verifying the bar-comparison boundary, cup formula, centrality, all field cases, and the noninner commutator. Auditor corrected an earlier exploratory ambient-Weyl identification: the explicitly defined b need not equal ad(pi y) when Y=yh. The proof above does not use that identification. More precisely ad(pi y)(Y)=L Y+pi h''-L h'.
The dimension >1 claim can be proved without invoking Witt: D_d and D_{d/u} have independent classes since every nonzero linear combination has polynomial degree below deg h and so is not inner.
Seven exact symbolic factor cases pass in symbolic_checks.json, generated by check_obstruction.py: x², x³, x⁶, mixed simple/double roots, two different repeated irreducible factors, nonsplit x²+1, and nonmonic h. These support but do not replace the symbolic general proof.

## Algebra and hypotheses recovered from the cited paper

Lopes–Solotar, *Lie structure on the Hochschild cohomology of a family of subalgebras of the Weyl algebra*, J. Noncommut. Geom. 15 (2021), 1373–1407; DOI 10.4171/JNCG/439; author manuscript https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.01226 (v1, 4 March 2019).

- The paper explicitly assumes nonzero h∈F[x]; F is a field, not necessarily algebraically closed. Although it also studies positive characteristic, that is not the OWR question's stated setting.
- A_h=F⟨x,ŷ⟩/(ŷx−xŷ−h(x)), a unital infinite-dimensional Ore extension. No Frobenius assumption is made.
- The paper embeds ŷ as yh rather than hy. These generate the same subalgebra because yh−hy=h′∈F[x].
- Page 10 gives the Nakayama automorphism x↦x, ŷ↦ŷ+h′, and says A_h is twisted Calabi–Yau. Its bimodule resolution has length two.
- Corollary 3.11 gives HH²(A_h)≅A_h/gcd(h,h′)A_h in characteristic zero. This cohomology calculation is not a BV construction.

Elementary inference (not a quoted theorem): in characteristic zero, this automorphism is nonsemisimple for every nonconstant h. Indeed, on span{ŷ,h′}, its difference from the identity is nonzero with square zero. If h is a nonzero constant, the automorphism is the identity. Thus the actual nonsemisimple branch excludes the Weyl-algebra constant case.

## The BV compatibility being requested

On HH^*(A_h,A_h), retain the usual cup product and Gerstenhaber bracket. The question requires an F-linear degree −1 operator Δ with Δ²=0 that generates that existing bracket, not merely some square-zero operator or some alternate bracket. In the OWR convention, for homogeneous a,b:

[a,b]=(−1)^|a| Δ(ab)−(−1)^|a| Δ(a)b−aΔ(b)+aΔ(1)b.

Ordinary Hochschild cohomology is nonnegatively graded, hence Δ(1)=0. Sign conventions must be matched rather than silently mixed with another presentation.

## Correct semisimple theorem

The reference is Kowalzig–Krähmer, *Batalin-Vilkovisky structures on Ext and Tor*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 697 (2014), 159–219; DOI 10.1515/crelle-2012-0086; https://arxiv.org/abs/1203.4984.

Theorem 1.7 proves BV on ordinary HH^*(A,A) for a twisted Calabi–Yau algebra with semisimple modular automorphism. Section 7.3 uses an algebra over a field and defines semisimple as a direct sum of eigenspaces over that field. Definition 7.5 requires a finite-length finitely generated projective A^e-resolution of A and Ext^i_{A^e}(A,A^e)=0 except in dimension d, where it is A_σ. This is homological smoothness and twisted duality, not finite-dimensionality of A.

The proof uses duality HH^*(A,A)≅HH_{d−*}(A,A_σ) and the eigenvalue-one cyclic subcomplex. Semisimplicity justifies the relevant quasi-isomorphism and permits transferring the Connes operator. The twisted coefficients occur in the homology used for construction; the conclusion is ordinary Hochschild cohomology. Dropping semisimplicity without replacing this argument is unjustified.

The separate Lambre–Zhou–Zimmermann Frobenius theorem (J. Algebra 446 (2016), 103–131, DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2015.09.018) is not OWR reference [3] and must not be substituted here.

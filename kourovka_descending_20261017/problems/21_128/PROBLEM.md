Originalquestion: Are the Artin groups A[F4] and A[H4] commensurable? The Notebookdefines commensurability as existence of isomorphic finite-index subgroups, whose indices need not be equal. It describes this as the most difficult remaining case in sphericalArtin commensurability classification.

The Coxeter graphs are rank-four chains with labels3,4,3 forF4 and5,3,3 forH4 (reversalofchainlabels gives the same diagram). The associated Artin presentations have alternating-word braid relations of those lengths on adjacent vertices and commuting relations on the nonedges. A positive answer needs checked isomorphic finite-index subgroups or an equallyrigorous existence proof; a negative answer needs an invariant of abstract commensurability that actually separates thispair.

Distinct whole-group profinitecompletions, ordinary Betti numbers, Coxeter orders, and Euler characteristic values alone do not establish noncommensurability. Quasi-isometry alone is also insufficient without solving21.129. Prior129research provides audited filters and exactfullscope reference but has not settledthispair.

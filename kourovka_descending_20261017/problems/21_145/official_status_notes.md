# Kourovka 21.145: independent primary-source status audit

Checkpoint: 2026-10-17 20:50 UTC. Audit completion estimate: 100% for the bounded current-source check; mathematical discovery completion estimate: 5% (no solution produced or verified here).

## Exact claims and separation

Let F be the standard Thompson group. Clause (a) asks whether F is quasi-isometric to F x Z; clause (b) asks whether F is quasi-isometric to F x F. Each requires a quasi-isometry, including uniform coarse surjectivity; quasi-isometric embeddings of these products into F do not settle either clause. This audit concerns their public status, rather than proving either answer.

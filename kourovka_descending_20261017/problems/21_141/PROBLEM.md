# Kourovka 21.141

Opened 2026-10-17 UTC. Status: researching. Initial discovery estimate **5%**.

Official 21st edition, 6 October 2026, printed p.198:

> Let G=G1 coproduct_H G2 be a free pro-p product of coherent pro-p groups
> with polycyclic amalgamation. Is G coherent?
>
> For abstract groups this is known to be true. A group is said to be
> coherent if each of its finitely generated subgroups is finitely
> presented, and in the question the coherency is used in the pro-p sense.
>
> P. A. Zalesskii.

Authoritative source:
https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt-1.pdf .
The cached source text is at the program's sources/21tkt_oct06_2026.txt;
its PDF and provenance are preserved separately.

Every subgroup, generation, presentation and amalgam must be interpreted
in the pro-p category, with closed subgroups and topological generation.
The intended meaning of polycyclic for pro-p groups and any properness
convention for amalgams require primary-source verification. An abstract
or locally compact combination theorem is not automatically a theorem in
this category. No answer is assumed.

Official 21st edition, 6 October 2026, printed p.198, credits
P. A. Zalesskii with this question: for fixed distinct primes p,q, does
every finite group embed into a finite group invariably generated by an
element of order p and one of order q? Invariable means that every pair
of independently chosen conjugates of those two elements generates the
whole group.

The official entry is starred with a negative answer for every pair of
distinct primes, citing Gong–Yang–Zeng, 1 August 2026. The primary paper's
Theorem 1.1 has exactly those quantifiers. More precisely, sufficiently
large alternating groups cannot embed into any such invariably generated
finite group. This is stronger than finding just one exceptional prime
pair, so it closes the whole original question.

Primary source: Ting Gong, Yong Yang, Michael Ruofan Zeng,
*Alternating Groups and Embeddings into Groups Invariably Generated by
Two Prime-Order Elements*, arXiv:2608.00703v1, 1 August 2026,
https://arxiv.org/html/2608.00703v1 .

The official notice also cites I. Capdeboscq and C. Parker,
*The structure of finite groups invariably generated by two elements of
prime order*, arXiv:2608.03935, 4 August 2026,
https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.03935 , for the p=2,q=3 examples.
Their special pair is not being substituted for the first paper's full
quantifiers. The official accepted solution notice and exact primary
Theorem 1.1 are the basis for exclusion; no independent reproof of the
complete classification-dependent argument is claimed.

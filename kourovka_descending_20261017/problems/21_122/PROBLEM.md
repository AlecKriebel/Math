# Kourovka21.122

Latestofficialsource:21stNotebook, October20,2026 revision, printed/PDFp197. Proposer:P.Shumyatsky.

Let w be a group word and G a profinite group. Is the cardinality of the SET of w-values in G either finite or at least continuum?

Precisely, for every ordinary word w in finitely many variables and every profinite G, prove |G_w|<2^aleph0 implies G_w finite, or construct one explicit counterexample with an infinite smaller value set. No finite-generation, metrizability, rank, solubility or pro-p hypothesis is permitted in a full claim. The identity word and finiteG are boundary cases. A conclusion only about w(w(G)) is insufficient; finiteness of the closed verbal subgroup is stronger than the question.

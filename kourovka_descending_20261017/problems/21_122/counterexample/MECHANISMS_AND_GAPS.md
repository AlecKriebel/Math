The strongest general new-to-this-pass reduction comes from the already
published Shumyatsky theorem: a small word image forces a nontrivial composed
word law on the group. The note includes the exact closed-subgroup assumptions
and a free-product proof that the composed law is nontrivial. This is not an
originality claim and does not settle the problem on groups satisfying laws.

The recent primary Conte--Vannacci preprint
[2609.25448v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.25448v1) was checked as an alternative
way around the obstacle. Its introduction and Theorem1.1 address Haar measure
of word images, with a free-pro-p and rank restriction. A null compact image
can still have continuum cardinality. No arbitrary-profinite cardinality
shortcut was found; the paper also distinguishes positive word images from
their inverse-symmetrizations. No extra prior-solution claim is made.

Further work on this family should resume only with a new mechanism for a
genuine fixed-word central cocycle, or new algebraic restrictions that rule
out all such cocycles. Naming an arbitrary continuous countable-image map,
assuming sparse activations, or demanding a theorem equivalent to21.122 is
not such a mechanism. The family is terminal for this pass rather than a

Question: If two Artin groups of spherical type are quasi-isometric, must they be commensurable? The Notebook notes this fails for right-angled Artin groups.

Spherical type means the associated Coxeter group is finite. No irreducibility restriction is stated; reducible finite Coxeter systems and their direct products are included. Commensurable means abstractly commensurable: some finite-index subgroups are isomorphic, as defined in immediately preceding21.128. Quasi-isometry concerns the finitely generated groups with word metrics. An affirmative resolution must cover all eligible types/products; a negative resolution requires a checked quasi-isometric noncommensurable pair of spherical Artin groups.

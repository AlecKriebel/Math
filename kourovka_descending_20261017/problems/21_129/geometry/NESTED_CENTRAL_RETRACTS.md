# Nested central elements give a maximal-rank abelian retract

Date: 2026-10-24. Status: proved deduction from the standard arrangement model and the pure central splitting; novelty is not claimed. It limits a proposed filling-function approach, and is not a QI classification theorem.

## Precise claim

Let `A=A[S]` be an irreducible spherical Artin group of rank `n>=2`. Let `P=ker(A->W)` be its pure subgroup, and let `Q=P/Z(P)`. Then `Q` has a group retract isomorphic to `Z^(n-1)`, generated by images of central full twists of a nested sequence of proper standard parabolics.

Consequently, for `n>=3`, both `Q` and `A/Z(A)` have ordinary Dehn function equivalent to `L^2`. In rank two they are virtually free and have linear Dehn function, so the quadratic assertion is deliberately restricted to `n>=3`.

## Winding coordinates and their normalizations

In the finite reflection-arrangement model, `P=pi_1(M)`, where

`M = V_C minus union_{H in arrangement} H_C`.

For each reflection hyperplane `H`, choose a nonzero complex linear form `alpha_H` with kernel `H_C`. The winding number of `alpha_H` along a loop defines a homomorphism

`lambda_H : P -> Z`.

Normalize a positive meridian to have winding one. The familiar pure-Artin abelianization statement is `P_ab = Z^{T}`, with `T` the reflection set and basis the meridians; the `lambda_H` are the dual coordinates. Only the existence of these homomorphisms and the following explicit values are needed here, so no assertion about the full abelianization is required to prove the retract.

For `X subset S`, let `z_X=Delta_X^2`, the central full twist of the standard parabolic on `X`. Let `T_X` be its set of reflections. Then

`lambda_H(z_X)=1` if `H` corresponds to `T_X`, and `0` otherwise. In particular the global full twist `z_S` has all winding coordinates equal to one.

One geometric verification of the indicator formula uses the local normal arrangement at the standard face corresponding to `X`. Choose a generic point `a` on that face and a generic sufficiently small normal vector `epsilon b`. The local Hopf loop is `a+epsilon exp(2 pi i t)b`. If `H` contains the face, then `alpha_H(a)=0`, and its value winds once. If it does not contain the face, choose `epsilon` so small that the circle centered at `alpha_H(a)` does not surround zero; its winding is zero. This local Hopf loop is the embedded parabolic full twist `Delta_X^2`. Changing the path to the basepoint conjugates the loop and does not change a winding homomorphism.

For the full irreducible arrangement, the global Hopf fiber generates `Z(P)`: the trivialized Hopf fibration gives `P=pi_1(projective complement) x Z`, and Proposition 3.1(1)-(2) of Cumplido–Paris proves that the whole pure center is exactly this cyclic factor. Thus every difference of two winding coordinates kills `Z(P)` and descends to `Q`.

## Explicit retraction

Order the standard generators as `s_1,...,s_n`, and put `X_j={s_1,...,s_j}`. Let `u_j` be the image in `Q` of `z_{X_j}`, for `1<=j<=n-1`.

The `u_j` commute. Indeed for `i<=j`, `z_{X_i}` belongs to `A[X_j]` and `z_{X_j}` is central in that group. Define

`j : Z^(n-1) -> Q`, by `j(e_i)=u_i`.

Let `H_i` be the simple reflection hyperplane corresponding to `s_i`. A simple reflection belongs to the standard parabolic reflection system `T_Xj` precisely when its generator belongs to `X_j`; equivalently, `i<=j`. Therefore

`lambda_{H_i}(z_{X_j}) = 1_{i<=j}`.

For `i=1,...,n-1`, define the descended homomorphism

`r_i = lambda_{H_i} - lambda_{H_{i+1}} : Q -> Z`.

The indicator formula gives

`r_i(u_j) = 1_{i<=j} - 1_{i+1<=j} = delta_ij`.

Hence `r=(r_1,...,r_{n-1})` satisfies `r o j = identity`. It follows simultaneously that `j` is injective, that its image `B` is free abelian of rank `n-1`, and that `j o r : Q -> B` is a group retraction. This is an integral splitting, not merely a rational homology argument.

For a concrete rank-four matrix, the values of the three winding differences on the three nested twists are exactly

```
                   z_{X1} z_{X2} z_{X3}
lambda_H1-H2          1      0      0
lambda_H2-H3          0      1      0
lambda_H3-H4          0      0      1.
```

The same computation works regardless of edge labels, including `F4/H4` and `D_n/E_n`. If a prefix `X_j` is reducible, `Delta_Xj^2` is the product of its components' full twists, which has exactly the same indicator values. Alternatively, connected prefixes can be chosen by ordering along a spanning tree.

## Standard parabolic intersection check

If `X` is proper, there is at least one hyperplane outside `T_X`. Winding homomorphisms of elements of the pure standard parabolic vanish outside `T_X`, while every nonzero power of the global pure central full twist has nonzero winding there. Thus `P_X intersect Z(P)=1`. If an element of `A[X]` lies in `Z(A)`, a positive power lies in `P_X intersect Z(P)`, so torsion-freeness gives `A[X] intersect Z(A)=1`. Therefore the standard parabolic embeds in the central quotient, as used in the alternative rank-two explanation of the filling lower bound.

This is an auxiliary check; the explicit abelian retraction above already supplies the required quadratic lower bound without invoking this intersection fact or a subquadratic isoperimetric-gap theorem.

## Dehn function consequence, including the finite-presentation point

Spherical Artin groups are biautomatic and have finite classifying complexes. Thus `P` is finitely presented and its ordinary Dehn function has a quadratic upper bound. By the pure central splitting `P=Q x Z`, `Q` is finitely presented: geometrically it is the projective arrangement complement group, with a finite model; algebraically, a finite presentation for `P` can be quotient by the single central generator to present `Q`. The splitting makes `Q` a group retract of `P`.

For finitely presented groups a homomorphic retract has Dehn function bounded above by that of the ambient group. To check this directly, choose finite generating sets and a homomorphic retraction. Apply it to every edge label in an ambient van Kampen diagram. Each of the finitely many ambient relators becomes a fixed null word in the retract; choose bounded-area fillings for those finitely many words. Replacing the cells gives a filling with area multiplied by at most a fixed constant, and generator substitutions change boundary length by at most a fixed constant. This proves the needed inequality.

It follows that `delta_Q(L) <=_equiv L^2`. For `n>=3`, the proved retract `Z^(n-1)` in turn retracts onto `Z^2`. The same retract inequality and the standard quadratic filling function of `Z^2` imply `L^2 <=_equiv delta_Q(L)`. Therefore `delta_Q(L) =_equiv L^2`.

By Cumplido–Paris Corollary 3.2, `Q` has finite index in `A/Z(A)`. Dehn functions of finitely presented finite-index groups are equivalent, so `delta_{A/Z(A)}(L)=_equiv L^2` as well.

For `n=2`, the projective reflection arrangement is `CP^1` minus at least three points, hence `Q` is nonabelian free. The constructed abelian retract has rank one and does not supply a quadratic lower bound. For `n=1`, the pure central quotient is trivial; it is outside the displayed theorem's `n>=2` scope.

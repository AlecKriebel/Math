1. Wilson, *Profinite groups with few conjugacy classes of p-elements*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 150 (2022), 3297–3305, arXiv:2204.09936v2 (September 29, 2022). Its subject counts all p-elements, including finite-order p-elements, rather than only the infinite-order p-elements in 21.136. The 2023 paper develops the genuinely different infinite-order question and notes corrected small errors in the arXiv proof of an earlier proposition.
2. Barnea–Camina–Ershov–Lewis, *On groups that can be covered by conjugates of finitely many cyclic or procyclic subgroups*, Mathematische Annalen (2025), DOI 10.1007/s00208-025-03119-4; current author-hosted PDF obtained. This classifies groups with finite NCC (number of cyclic/procyclic subgroups up to a covering by conjugates), and discusses countable NCC as a separate question. A small number of conjugacy classes of cyclic subgroups is not a small number of conjugacy classes of individual elements. The paper cites Wilson 2023 and does not state a resolution of 21.136.
3. Jaikin-Zapirain–Nikolov, *An infinite compact Hausdorff group has uncountably many conjugacy classes*, Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 147 (2019), 4083–4089, is cited by Wilson as background. It counts all element classes and does not by itself restrict that continuum lower bound to infinite-order elements. Its full proof was not independently re-audited here.
4. Wilson's April 16, 2024 MPI seminar abstract again states only the finitely generated conclusion. This is primary explanatory context, not a later theorem that closes the unrestricted case.

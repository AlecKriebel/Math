## Morse and projection countermodels

**Pass.** In Z²*Z with basis a,t,c, the subgroup S=<t,c> has no a coordinate. Thus the detour a^n t^n a^-n reaches distance n from S. For subpaths within any one side or crossing a single corner, the path is geodesic. If it crosses both corners, its intervening vertical displacement is n and its length is at most3 times its endpoint distance.

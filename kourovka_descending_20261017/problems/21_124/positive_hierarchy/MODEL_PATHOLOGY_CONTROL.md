# A concrete remaining specialness gap in the one-twist model

UTC: 2026-10-24. This is a scope control, not a group-theoretic nonembedding claim.

Use ONE_TWIST_CUBULATION.md with r=2 and w=ab. Then k=1,L=2. The free-graph factor is the ordinary two-loop rose; the z-circle has two edges. The square annulus has two transversal edges c_0,c_1. They are dual to the same annulus-midcircle hyperplane, because they are opposite edges of the annulus squares. At the w-side of the annulus, both their terminal endpoints are the rose basepoint, since a and b are loops. Both incoming transversal directions have the same coorientation, and they do not span a square corner: each annulus corner consists of a transversal and a longitudinal edge. Thus the annulus-midcircle hyperplane **directly self-osculates**.

The same feature remains in every cover of this particular model pulled back only from c mod n, n≥1, killing the whole vertex group. The vertex spaces are unchanged copies, and every annulus still has both w-side transversal endpoints at one rose basepoint. Such covers of the displayed model cannot be A-special. In particular, passing to the VH double cover does not by itself make this model special.

This is a geometric-model obstruction only. The word ab is primitive in F(a,b), so changing the free basis gives an isomorphic one-twist group with w as a basis element. It has a tubular decomposition with one loop at the <w,t> vertex and a bridge to the <b,t> vertex; the underlying graph is cycle-disjoint. The cited cycle-disjoint theorem also requires balancedness, which holds here: the established geometric CAT(0) action is proper and semisimple, so each nontrivial element g of this torsion-free group has positive translation length ℓ(g). If g^k is conjugate to g^l for nonzero integers k,l, invariance under conjugacy and the power formula give |k|ℓ(g)=|l|ℓ(g), hence |k|=|l|. Thus the group is balanced and is already virtually special by the prior balanced, cycle-disjoint tubular theorem of Merladet Urigüen–Minasyan2511.21293v1, Corollary1.4. Therefore the canonical self-osculation does not disprove virtual specialness of the group. A successful construction may alter the free basis, the vertex covers, and the cubulation.

The example makes the precise gap visible: the proof of compact NPC and VH models does not give the finite covers needed to control the relevant hyperplanes. The general positive mechanism must supply those covers or a separate injective RAAG homomorphism. Repeating the c-height cover operation alone cannot repair this already known positive example.

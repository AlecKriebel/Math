## Previously resolved subclasses and useful tools

1. **Hyperbolic groups.** Hagen–Wise, *Cubulating hyperbolic free-by-cyclic groups: the general case*, TheoremA, gives a free cocompact cubulation for finite-rank free fiber; Agol then gives virtual compact specialness. [Hagen–Wise1406.3292](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.3292). More recently, **Linton2510.10178v2,22Apr2026**, Theorem1.2 embeds any finitely generated free-by-cyclic group in a finite-rank-free-by-cyclic group as a retract. Corollary1.4 supplies a hyperbolic ambient group and quasiconvex embedding in the hyperbolic case, and **Corollary1.5 states that every hyperbolic free-by-cyclic group is virtually compact special**. This addresses possible infinite free-kernel rank in the hyperbolic subclass. The general embedding theorem does not assert the ambient group is CAT(0) when the given group is CAT(0), and it does not itself solve21.124. [Linton](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.10178v2).

2. **Rank at most2; 3-manifold cases.** The trivial and rank-one fibers are elementary as described in SOURCE_CONTRACT. Wu–Ye's introduction records virtual specialness of every F₂-by-Z group using the punctured-torus-bundle description and3-manifold cubulation results. Liu's Theorem1.1 concerns **compact aspherical graph manifolds**, and should not be quoted by itself as an all3-manifold theorem. Its author's update credits Agol and Przytycki–Wise for the other3-manifold cases. [Liu1110.1940](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.1940).

3. **Tubular VRC subclasses.** Merladet Urigüen–Minasyan, *Virtual retractions in free constructions*, **2505.18054v3,29Oct2025**, with its appendix by Merladet Urigüen, Minasyan, Wu and Ye, establishes tubular(VRC)⇒virtually special and virtually finite-rank-free-by-cyclic. Its general Euclidean-by-finite criterion is a criterion for(VRC), not a proof that every CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group has(VRC). [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18054v3). Their *Property(VRC) and virtual fibering for amalgamated free products*, **2511.21293v1,26Nov2025**, Corollary1.4 proves that balanced tubular groups whose underlying graph is **cycle-disjoint** have(VRC), are CAT(0), are virtually special, and are virtually finite-rank-free-by-Z. Here every graph vertex lies in at most one nontrivial simple cycle. The restriction is real; the paper's examples outside it do not supply the missing eligible CAT(0) counterexample. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2511.21293v1).

4. **Finite-dimensional tubular cubulations.** Woodhouse, *Classifying virtually special tubular groups*, Groups Geom. Dyn.12(2018),679–702, Theorem1.1 identifies virtual specialness for tubular groups with a free finite-dimensional CAT(0) cubulation (and an equivalent locally finite condition). Button's2017 theorem that all tubular free-by-cyclic groups act freely on some CAT(0) cube complex does not provide that finite-dimensional or locally finite hypothesis. Woodhouse's introduction attributes a finite-dimensional assertion to Button; that introductory phrase is not used as a theorem premise here, since it would incorrectly imply that Gersten's group is virtually special. [Woodhouse](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/29874), [Button accepted author manuscript](https://api.repository.cam.ac.uk/server/api/core/bitstreams/8a19b850-deb4-4cd8-afcd-ffefcc239426/content).

## Exclusions and corrections essential to a genuine research target

- **Gersten is not eligible.** The F₃-by-Z group with a↦a,b↦ba,c↦ca² has no finite-index RAAG embedding (Brady–Soroko Appendix10,Corollary10.3) but is not CAT(0). The positive-characteristic nonlinearity discussion of Button includes this same example, rather than furnishing a CAT(0) counterexample. [Brady–Soroko author paper](https://sites.math.unt.edu/~soroko/Dehn-functions-RAAGs.pdf). General nonlinearity over positive characteristic would also require care: it is not by itself a contradiction to characteristic-zero linearity.
- **CAT(0) does not mean virtually compact special here.** Wu–Ye Theorem1.4/Example4.11 takes a↦a,b↦a^m b a^m,c↦a^n c a^n. These are CAT(0) and virtually special for all integersm,n, while virtual free cocompact cubulation occurs iff|m|=|n|. For examplem=1,n=2 is a prior negative result for a *compact* strengthening, and a positive example for the actual noncompact target. Munro–Petyt, **2407.09275v3**, *Coarse obstructions to cocompact cubulation*, TheoremsC,E likewise concern coarse medians/cocompact cubulation. They cannot be used to infer nonembedding in a RAAG. [Primary paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.09275).
- **Samuelson's2006 CAT(0) criterion needs correction.** Delage, **2602.08759v3,26Mar2026**, *Which F₃-by-Zs are CAT(0)?*, explicitly corrects Samuelson's Theorem4.4. In the explicit normal form φ(a)=a, φ(b)=b, φ(c)=c w(a,b), the group is CAT(0) iff w=1 or w∉[F₂,F₂]. A nontrivial commutator twist cannot be certified CAT(0) by the old theorem. Delage's new CAT(0) constructions do not assert general virtual specialness. [Delage](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.08759v3).
- **Recent other consequences are not the required embedding.** Dahmani–Hughes–Kudlinska–Touikan **2509.22346v2,21Apr2026**, proves conjugacy separability for all finitely generated free-by-cyclic groups, resolving19.41; it does not resolve21.124. Gray–Linton **2607.07496v1,8Jul2026**, proves finite complete rewriting systems for all finitely generated virtually free-by-cyclic groups and their finitely generated subgroups; this is not an automaticity, CAT(0), or virtual-specialness theorem. [DHKT](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22346), [Gray–Linton](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07496).

## Accepted theta theorem

For a graph of groups consisting of two rank-two free abelian vertices and three edges between them, a geometric CAT(0) action together with a height homomorphism nonzero on every edge gives a homomorphism into the Euclidean-by-finite group R²⋊C₂ that embeds each vertex group as a discrete lattice. The cited prior criteria then imply virtual specialness and existence of a finite-index subgroup embedding in a finitely generated RAAG.

I checked the proof against the following possible failure modes.

1. **Height and edge normalization.** The edge generator must be oriented consistently at its two endpoints. Its two heights are then the same nonzero integer nᵢ, even if negative. Dividing in the rationalized vertex group is legitimate linear algebra, not an extraction of a group root. A height-kernel vector and u₀/n₀ form a rational basis because their heights are respectively zero and one. Neither the vertex height nor any edge inclusion must be primitive, and global surjectivity of h is unnecessary. A nonzero height assigned originally to a non-tree stable letter does not matter: the constructed representation may set that letter's H-coordinate to zero because no defining relation prescribes it.

2. **All degeneracies in the sign lemma.** I independently solved all four sign systems by exact row reduction. The only failure occurs when all three slopes on one side coincide and all three slopes on the other are different. The written proof covers repeated slopes, simultaneous zeros, one-sided zeros, and negative values; the (+,+) case cannot rescue either stated exception. Nonzero a,b make the vertex linear transformations invertible even when c=0.

3. **Use of CAT(0).** The geometric action supplies flat-torus norms on the actual rank-two vertex subgroups. Translation length is conjugacy invariant, so corresponding edge vectors have equal norms. Dividing by |nᵢ| gives equality for normalized vectors, including negative nᵢ. A positive-definite quadratic in a nonzero kernel direction cannot take one fixed value at three distinct inputs. Thus both exceptional configurations really are impossible. The proof does not replace a geometric action by a merely free, possibly infinite-dimensional cubical action.

4. **Relations and discreteness.** Reflection fixes the H-direction. The sign equations are precisely the non-tree edge relations, independently of which equivalent stable-letter orientation is used because the chosen linear maps are involutions. The tree edge is also satisfied. An invertible real linear map of the rank-two integral vertex lattice is injective and discrete. There is no assertion that the whole group representation is injective.

5. **Prior implication and virtual quantifier.** [Merladet Urigüen–Minasyan, Theorem 1.6](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18054v3) applies to finite graphs of finitely generated virtually abelian groups. Its vertex-injective discrete Euclidean-by-finite criterion applies exactly here. Their [Proposition 1.10 and Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.18054v3#A1) give a finite-index RAAG embedding for tubular groups with (VRC). This proves the needed existential virtual conclusion for this subclass; no statement about every finite-index subgroup is used. [Button, Theorem 2.1](https://arxiv.org/html/1510.05842) confirms the nonzero-edge-height criterion, but the theorem under audit explicitly assumes the height and does not depend on a hidden rank convention.

## Accepted Pell eligibility and limited obstruction

The presentation with pairs (3,2), (20,14), (119,84) is an eligible CAT(0) F₅-by-Z group.

- The map fixing a,b and sending each sᵢ to b⁻qᵢsᵢaᵖᵢ is a free-group automorphism: the displayed inverse composes to the identity on every free generator. Rearranging the original relations gives exactly this action of t. This proves the free-by-cyclic claim without relying on an imprecise kernel theorem. Independently, the Bass–Serre quotient for the stated height has two cyclic vertex groups, four trivial edge groups, and underlying first Betti number three, so its free rank is 2+3=5. All vertex and edge height images are Z in this particular example.
- The Gram determinants are 7/4 and 3 and their leading entries are positive. Their edge squared lengths agree by the exact Pell identities; the tree-edge length also agrees. The standard graph of flat tori and positive-width Euclidean cylinders has a tree of planes and strips as universal cover. Gluing along the complete geodesic boundary lines is gluing along convex subsets; equivalently, the finite graph-of-spaces is locally CAT(0). It supplies the required proper cocompact action. The edge vectors in this example are primitive, since the t-coordinate is one. This claim is consistent with the primary translation-length/gluing criterion discussed in [Wu–Ye, §2](https://arxiv.org/html/2504.14192v2#S2).
- For elementary abelian-2 linear holonomy, raise a,b,t to one common positive even exponent killing their linear parts. Commutation in the two vertex groups ensures that taking powers of each edge relation gives the claimed vector equation; translations of the stable letters disappear under conjugation of translations. The three stable-letter linear maps commute and are involutions, so simultaneous real sign eigenspaces exhaust the whole representation. On each of the seven nontrivial sign patterns the exact coefficient matrix is invertible; on the all-positive eigenspace its kernel is precisely the T-axis. Consequently A=B=0 everywhere. Injectivity on a vertex contradicts Φ(aᴹ)=1 (and also Φ(bᴹ)=1). Discreteness is not needed for this limited impossibility.

This excludes every dimension for **elementary abelian-2 holonomy only**. It does not exclude noncommuting finite holonomy, commuting elements of higher order, arbitrary finite covers, or virtual RAAG embeddings. If a subgroup with elementary abelian-2 quotient had an abelianization map injecting its vertex intersections, the induced representation of G would have such forbidden holonomy; this explains the precise finite-cover filter. A general RAAG-embeddable subgroup need not be detected by its own abelianization, so that filter cannot be promoted to a universal virtual obstruction.

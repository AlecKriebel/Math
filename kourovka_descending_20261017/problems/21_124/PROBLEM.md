# Kourovka21.124

Official21st edition, October20,2026 update, printedp198; I.Soroko. Authoritative statement and source custody: priority/SOURCE_CONTRACT.md.

(a) Is every CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group virtually special?
(b) Does every such group virtually embed in a right-angled Artin group?

The printed definition does not require compactness. For finitely generated groups the two literal virtual properties agree, including the original weaker HW special terminology after a finite parity cover; root's independently audited argument is in ../21_125/root_controls/TARGET_AND_HEIGHT_DELTA.md. The printed weaker/stronger wording is a convention discrepancy, not a license to assume compactness.

Do not silently restrict the free kernel to finite rank. The hyperbolic case, including the broader free-kernel reading, is prior solved work. A complete negative answer needs one genuine CAT(0) free-by-cyclic group with no RAAG-embedding finite-index subgroup. A complete positive answer must cover the full remaining class. Gersten, nonCAT(0) commutator twists, and failures of cocompact cubulation do not suffice.

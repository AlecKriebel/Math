## Strongest checked findings

1. **Necessary finite-cover condition.** Every finite-index subgroup that embeds in a RAAG avoids all conjugates of \(a_2C_{G_m}(t)\). This follows from the exact nonabelian two-Engel pairs \(a_2u,t^q\), with \(u\in C(t)\), and established Baudisch theory. In particular no finite abelian cover can embed in a RAAG for \(m\ge3\). Exact complete permutation enumeration, with an independent second implementation, excludes indices1–7 for all \(m\ge4\), and index8 for \(m\ge8\). These are bounds on a possible **RAAG-embedding** subgroup's index; they are not arbitrary-index nonexistence statements.
2. **The same obstruction is insufficient in every rank.** There is a uniform transitive permutation action of degree \(2N<4(m-1)\), with \(N\) the least power of2 at least \(m-1\), passing the full centralizer-coset condition. The proof checks the whole centralizer through fixed free-fiber abelianization, not a finite sample. The finite image has order \(2^{m-1}N\) and nilpotency class exactly \(m-1\); its normal kernel also passes the condition. Thus even the normal finite2-group-quotient version of this obstruction stops at a precise gap.
3. **A principal virtual-homology candidate is false in rank5.** The independent companion investigation constructs a specific index512 subgroup \(K\le G_5\) and an integer character \(\lambda:K\to\mathbb Z\) with \(\lambda(a_1^2)=1\). Hence \(K\) retracts onto \(\langle a_1^2\rangle\). The same cover detects powers of all five standard generators, all ten elementary fiber commutators, and four listed nested commutators (18distinct words in19labels). A fresh separate adversary reconstructs the graph, all Hydra relators,2880 integer cochain identities and target lifts, and rejects eight corrupted certificates. Thus these candidates cannot be persistently homologically invisible. The listed homology commutator words use xyx^-1y^-1 as explicitly encoded in their certificates; the parent Engel lemmas use x^-1y^-1xy. Neither virtual RFRS nor universal cyclic-retraction visibility follows. A separate fresh delta also verifies an index2048 subgroup of G6 detecting powers of all six standard generators, with character value14 on a1^2; this supplies visibility, without a retraction claim for that exact cyclic subgroup.
4. **A stronger metric criterion remains unfulfilled.** If \(G_m\) virtually embeds in a RAAG, one common exponent \(N\) makes every nonabelian subgroup \(\langle x^N,y^N\rangle\) undistorted in \(G_m\), with constants allowed to depend on the pair. This follows from Carr's two-generator quasi-isometry theorem and the upper Lipschitz bound of a finitely generated subgroup embedding. A negative solution would therefore follow from distorted such pairs for every \(N\) at a fixed unresolved rank. No family with that universal persistence was found. The existing distorted rank-two Hydra subgroup cannot suffice, since \(G_2\) is known virtually special.

## Exact gaps and reopen criteria

The ordinary Hydra question remains unresolved here. A finite-cover countermodel passing one necessary condition is not an embedding. A character detecting finitely many words does not provide a residual chain, nor address all words. Passing to arbitrary powers does not preserve the short Engel relation: the second Engel commutator of \(a_2^p,t^q\) is nontrivial for all \(p\ge2,q>0\). High distortion of the original free Hydra subgroup has no unproved implication for the intrinsic Dehn function or persistence in every finite-index subgroup.

The blocked negative routes should be reopened only with materially stronger information: a new finite-index-persistent algebraic/cube invariant; a proof of universal homological invisibility for another fixed word; or a uniformly persistent distorted powered-pair construction. Repeating the existing centralizer-coset filter, base abelianization, selected cover failures, or one highly distorted free subgroup cannot close the remaining quantifiers.

# Hydra virtual homology obstruction: exact finite-cover tests

Checkpoint: 2026-10-24 21:50 UTC. Discovery estimate for a negative answer to Hydra virtual specialness: **3%**. Completion of this particular homology-route audit: **85%**; independent adversarial review remains pending. These are partial research controls, not a solution of Kourovka 21.125(b).

## Exact obstruction being tested

Let
\[
G_m=F(a_1,\ldots,a_m)\rtimes_\theta\langle t\rangle,
\qquad t^{-1}a_it=\theta(a_i),
\]
where \(\theta(a_1)=a_1\) and \(\theta(a_i)=a_ia_{i-1}\) for \(i>1\).
The target is an element \(1\ne g\in G_m\) such that, for **every** finite-index subgroup \(K\le G_m\) and every nonzero power \(g^n\in K\), the class \([g^n]\) vanishes in \(H_1(K;\mathbb Q)\). Such an element would obstruct virtual RFRS, hence virtual specialness. Failure in finitely many covers cannot establish this universal statement.

For completeness, the needed RFRS implication follows directly from the definition. If \(R\) is RFRS, take its finite-index rational-solvable chain \(R_i\). For any \(1\ne h\in R\), choose the last \(i\) with \(h\in R_i\); then \(h\notin R_{i+1}\), while the kernel of \(R_i\to H_1(R_i;\mathbb Q)\) is contained in \(R_{i+1}\). Thus \(h\) is homologically nonzero in that finite-index subgroup. If only a finite-index subgroup of \(G_m\) is RFRS, first take a nonzero power of \(g\) in it. No persistent-invisibility conclusion follows from non-RFRS of the original group alone.

The base abelianization is \(H_1(G_m;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z^2\), generated by \(a_m,t\). Every \(a_i\) with \(i<m\) has zero base class. Elements with nonzero height exponent already have nonzero base homology, so a persistent-invisibility witness would have to lie in the free fiber.

## Finite covers and their exact homology

Let \(L\le F_m\) correspond to a connected finite covering graph \(B\) of the rose. Suppose \(\theta^r(L)=L\). Then
\[
K=\langle L,t^r\rangle\cong L\rtimes_{\theta^r}\mathbb Z,
\quad [G_m:K]=r[F_m:L],
\]
and
\[
H_1(K;\mathbb Q)\cong\mathbb Q\oplus
H_1(L;\mathbb Q)/(\theta^r_*-I)H_1(L;\mathbb Q).
\]
All computations use this intrinsic finite-cover coinvariant formula, with exact rational arithmetic.

Vertices of the initial cover are \((\mathbb Z/p)^m\); a positive \(a_i\)-edge adds the \(i\)-th basis vector. The monodromy on deck vertices is the unipotent Jordan matrix, so \(r=p^{\lceil\log_p m\rceil}\) makes its action trivial. A spanning tree gives an explicit fundamental-cycle basis of dimension \(1+(m-1)p^m\). The programs expand \(\theta^r\) as exact positive words, compute every induced cycle image, and reduce \(\theta^r_*-I\) over \(\mathbb Q\).

For each homologically visible target the output stores a rational linear functional annihilating every column of \(\theta^r_*-I\) and taking value one on the target. This is stronger evidence than a floating point rank comparison.

For the characteristic mod-2 fiber cover the results are:

| rank | subgroup index | rational first Betti number | visible standard powers |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | 2 | 2 | \(a_1^2\) |
| 2 | 8 | 5 | \(a_1^2,a_2^2\) |
| 3 | 32 | 13 | \(a_2^2,a_3^2\) |
| 4 | 64 | 29 | \(a_2^2,a_3^2,a_4^2\) |

The mod-3 control through rank4 also leaves \(a_1^3\) invisible for ranks3 and4; at rank4 it leaves \(a_2^3\) invisible too. None is evidence of invisibility in all finite-index subgroups. In particular, a further invariant-character double cover at rank3 detects \(a_1^2\). This agrees with the already announced virtual specialness in that rank.

## An explicit rank-five counterexample to the a1 obstruction

The simplest-looking candidate \(a_1\) does **not** have persistent rational homology invisibility in \(G_5\). There is a concrete subgroup \(K\) of index **1024** with an integral character \(\lambda:K\to\mathbb Z\) such that
\[
a_1^2\in K,\qquad\lambda(a_1^2)=1.
\]
Consequently \(K\) actually retracts onto \(\langle a_1^2\rangle\), by \(x\mapsto a_1^{2\lambda(x)}\). This does not assert that \(K\) embeds in a RAAG.

Here is a compact specification of its free-fiber graph. Write its64vertices as \((v,s)\) with \(0\le v<32\) in binary and \(s\in\mathbb F_2\). Index generators from zero within this paragraph: generator \(a_{i+1}\) sends
\[
(v,s)\longmapsto(v\mathbin{\mathrm{xor}}2^i,\ s+\epsilon(v,i)).
\]
The function \(\epsilon\) is1 at precisely
\[
(2,1),(3,1),(5,2),(6,2),(10,3),(12,3),
\]
and zero everywhere else. This is a connected graph cover of degree64. Direct endpoint checks show that \(\theta^{16}\) induces the identity on its vertices. Thus its subgroup \(L\le F_5\) is invariant under \(\theta^{16}\) (inclusion has equal finite index, hence equality), and \(K=\langle L,t^{16}\rangle\) has index \(64\cdot16=1024\).

The complete integer edge weights for \(\lambda\) are recorded in `rank5_cocycle_certificate.json`, along with the other detecting characters below. They are verified without a matrix solver by `verify_cocycle_certificate.py`. For each integer edge cochain \(\alpha\) it checks integer vertex potentials \(\beta\) satisfying, for every positive edge \(e:v\to w\),
\[
\alpha(\theta^{16}(e))-\alpha(e)=\beta(w)-\beta(v).
\]
For any loop the right hand side telescopes to zero. Hence \(\alpha\) defines a \(\theta^{16}\)-invariant homomorphism on \(L\), which extends to \(K\) by assigning zero to \(t^{16}\). The verifier then follows the signed target words and evaluates their actual closed lifts. It does not assume author-computed homology ranks.

This one subgroup detects powers of every standard generator; it also detects all ten elementary free-fiber commutators \([a_i,a_j]\), and the four left-associated commutators \([\cdots[[a_1,a_2],a_3],\ldots,a_j]\), \(2\le j\le5\). There are19 target entries, with the first nested commutator duplicating \([a_1,a_2]\). The standalone certificate verifies9 integral characters and2880 edge-cell identities. In particular none of these18distinct elements can be the required persistent-invisibility witness.

The separate single-character doubling experiment, repeatedly choosing one particular character detecting \(a_2^2\), leaves \(a_1^2\) invisible through three successive double covers. Choosing a different invariant character detects it immediately. This is an explicit falsification of the inference that a few failed iterated homology covers establish a virtual obstruction.

## What remains missing

No nontrivial word has been shown homologically trivial in **every** finite-index subgroup containing a nonzero power. No finite computation here is evidence for that universal quantifier. Conversely, seeing these18elements does not prove virtual RFRS or property(VRC): there are infinitely many other cyclic subgroups, and an RFRS conclusion additionally requires a single residual chain whose successive terms contain the rational homology kernels.

There is no mechanism presently proving persistent invisibility for a new word, nor a complete residual-chain construction. The base-abelianization route and the specific \(a_1\)/standard-commutator candidates at rank5 are therefore closed unless a genuinely new mechanism is introduced. The general higher-rank Hydra question remains unresolved. No novelty claim is made for these finite-cover observations.

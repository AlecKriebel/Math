# Kourovka 21.150 — already_solved

Source: official 21st edition, October 6 2026 update, printed p.199.

The proposed upper bound for the rank of a central commutator subgroup is false. The official solution uses the additive group of F_3[x,y]/(x,y)^3 and two multiplication automorphisms generated by 1+x and 1+y. The central derived subgroup has rank3 while the acting group has rank2. Attribution: P.Monticone, letter21March2026; see also vanDoorn–Judin–Monticone–Morrison, arXiv:2607.17477.

## Exact question and finding

The target is bounded *ordinary rational* second Betti numbers over **all** finite-index subgroups of a hyperbolic virtually compact special group, with conclusion virtually free or virtually a closed-surface group. A bounded normalized sequence, vanishing second L2-Betti number, a result restricted to HGFC or residually free groups, or a result assuming a surface profinite completion is weaker/different input.

**No full solution of this exact statement was found in the primary sources checked below.** The strongest newly located 2026 adjacent result concerns coherence and virtual free-by-cyclic structure in rational cohomological dimension at most two. ## Primary-paper checks

### Wilton, arXiv:2406.02121v3

[Current abstract/version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.02121); [full v3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2406.02121v3). First submitted 4 June 2024; v3 **22 January 2026**, accepted final version. Theorem A produces a one-ended quasiconvex infinite-index subgroup of a cubulated hyperbolic group unless it is free or a surface group. Corollary B extends an analogous subgroup conclusion to fundamental groups of compact virtually special cube complexes. This is a subgroup theorem. It does not turn bounded finite-cover H2 into freeness of all infinite-index subgroups. Its full text contains no second-Betti characterization (the occurrence of “Betti” is in a b0 connectivity lemma).

### Fruchter–Morales, arXiv:2209.14925v3

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14925); [full v3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2209.14925v3). Current revision **21 May 2024**. Theorem A treats finitely generated residually free groups and hyperbolic fundamental groups of finite graphs of finitely generated free groups with infinite cyclic edge groups (HGFC). Finite vb2 occurs in the free, closed-surface and applicable free-abelian cases. Section 5.3, Question 5.5, explicitly asks the broader hyperbolic cohomological-dimension-two finite-vb2 question. This source does not establish the characterization for all hyperbolic virtually compact special groups. The HGFC hypotheses cannot be silently replaced by specialness.

### Ascari–Fruchter, arXiv:2505.20960v1

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20960); [full v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.20960v1). Submitted **27 May 2025**. For hyperbolic graphs of free groups with cyclic edges, apart from free products of free and surface groups, every finite abelian group occurs as a direct summand of some finite-index subgroup's abelianization. This paper **does cite Fruchter–Morales**, reference [12] in the complete cached HTML, under the current title. Its invariant is H1 torsion, and its ambient class remains HGFC. Torsion abundance does not establish unbounded rational H2.

### Ascari–Fruchter, arXiv:2509.15075v1

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.15075); [full v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.15075v1). Submitted **18 September 2025**. For the same nonexceptional hyperbolic graphs of free groups with cyclic edges, constructs exhausting nested nonnormal finite-index chains with exponential homological torsion growth, simultaneously at every prime. This concerns torsion and chosen chains. It supplies no extension of the rational-vb2 classification to arbitrary hyperbolic virtually special groups. The complete cached text has no Morales/2209.14925 reference, so this was an author-list follow-up rather than a verified citation-graph edge.

### Fruchter, arXiv:2509.22052v1

[Version record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22052); [full v1 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.22052v1). Submitted **26 September 2025**. Establishes subexponential first-homology torsion growth along exhausting regular towers for books of I-bundles with negative-Euler-characteristic pages, contrasting the preceding nonnormal-cover results. This has neither the invariant nor the full ambient class required for 21.139.

### Kreuzer–Moldenhauer–Rosenberger, arXiv:2512.19645

[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19645); [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.19645); [journal record](https://gcc.episciences.org/17637), published **17 March 2026**. This is a verified newer citation of Wilton 2406.02121v2, reference [26]. It studies small-generator subgroup structure in cyclically amalgamated products, and revisits the “every infinite-index subgroup is free” surface characterization for specified cyclic amalgams and HNN extensions. It does not state the bounded virtual-second-Betti characterization.

### Jaikin-Zapirain–Morales, arXiv:2312.12293v2 / Selecta 2025

[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.12293); [v2 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.12293v2); [published record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00029-025-01082-1), published **18 September 2025**. A verified citation of 2209.14925 as [FM23]. Theorem B recognizes a surface group among finitely generated groups with cd=2 and b2^(2)=0, **assuming the same finite quotients as a surface group**, or the stated residually finite-solvable variant. Bounded rational virtual H2 alone does not provide that finite-quotient hypothesis. Thus the theorem cannot simply be substituted for the missing 21.139 implication.

### Fisher–Sánchez-Peralta, with Linton appendix, arXiv:2603.16763v1

[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.16763); [full HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/2603.16763v1). Submitted **17 March 2026**. Corollary 1.6 gives, for finitely generated virtually RFRS groups of rational cd≤2, equivalence of coherence, b2^(2)=0, virtual free-by-cyclic structure, and other conditions. The introduction explicitly says the free kernel **is not assumed finitely generated**. Linton's appendix gives virtual free-by-cyclic structure for cubulated locally quasiconvex hyperbolic groups. This is useful adjacent progress, but free-by-cyclic with a possibly infinite-rank free kernel does not imply virtually free/surface. The paper is not a verified citation of either target paper; it was discovered by topic search.

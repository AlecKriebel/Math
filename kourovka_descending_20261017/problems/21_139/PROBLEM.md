# Kourovka 21.139

Opened 2026-10-17T21:35:36.587327+00:00. Status: preliminary priority audit and independent research. Initial discovery estimate **5%**.

Official 21st edition, 6 October 2026, printed p.198:

> Let G be a hyperbolic group which is virtually compact special in the sense of Haglund–Wise. Suppose that the set of second Betti numbers of the finite-index subgroups of G is bounded. Must G be virtually either a free group or a surface group?
>
> H. Wilton.

Source: https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt-1.pdf . The source entry has no solution marker. Subsequent literature and proposer sources must be checked before any claim of genuine openness.

Interpret ordinary second Betti number as dim H_2(-;Q); record any source convention issues explicitly. The hypothesis bounds these dimensions over ALL finite-index subgroups, not only a selected tower or normalized Betti numbers. The conclusion is virtual; finite groups and virtually cyclic groups satisfy the virtually-free alternative (including trivial or rank-one free subgroups). Passing to a torsion-free compact-special finite-index subgroup is permitted only with an explicit boundedness/virtual-conclusion argument. A restricted class such as graphs of free groups with cyclic edge groups does not settle the full statement.

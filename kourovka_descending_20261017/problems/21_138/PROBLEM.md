Every infinite-index subgroup is quantified, including infinitely generated ones. The original conclusion is an isomorphism classification of G itself, not merely a virtual classification. Infinite finite-generation examples without finite presentation do not answer the question. A proof for cubulated hyperbolic, one-relator, or residually free groups is restricted unless every remaining G is proved to belong to that class. Surface convention issues must be checked against the proposer; closed aspherical surfaces are the substantive nonfree examples.

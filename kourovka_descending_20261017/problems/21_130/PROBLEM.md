Claim to resolve: for every finite additive abelian group G of odd order, and every subset A of cardinality n > 2, there is a cyclic enumeration (a_1,...,a_n) of all distinct elements of A such that the n group elements a_i+a_(i+1), with a_(n+1)=a_1, are pairwise distinct.

The claim includes arbitrary subsets and arbitrary odd-order abelian groups; a result only for the whole group, arithmetic intervals, cyclic groups, bounded n, or asymptotic density is partial. Repeated vertices and cycle covers with multiple cycles do not suffice. A negative resolution requires an explicit odd-order group and subset with an exact proof that every cyclic enumeration has repeated sums.

# Universal collision-cover reduction

This is an exact reformulation and finite-search mechanism, not a resolution. The unsupported all-n collision-cover assertion transfers the original central difficulty and is marked blocked without a new mechanism.

Fix n>2, formal vertices e_0,...,e_(n-1) in Z^n, and differences d_ij=e_i-e_j. For every four distinct indices there are three possible balanced collision relations r=e_i+e_j-e_k-e_l, up to sign. Let R be a subset of these relations and L their integer span. Call R a cycle cover if every undirected Hamilton cycle on these formal vertices has two disjoint edges whose collision relation belongs to R. More generally, it suffices for that relation to vanish after killing the 2-primary torsion of Z^n/L.

**Equivalence.** An odd finite abelian counterexample on n vertices exists if and only if some such cycle-cover system R has every d_ij nonzero in (Z^n/L) tensor Z[1/2].

Forward: translate one vertex to zero if desired, and take all actual equal-sum relations. Equal colors cannot share an endpoint by cancellation, so every repeated-color Hamilton cycle yields one of the allowed four-vertex relations. The homomorphism e_i -> a_i into the odd group annihilates L. Multiplication by 2 is invertible in that group, so every d_ij survives localization, since a_i differs from a_j.

Reverse: write the finitely generated abelian quotient as Z^f direct-sum T_2 direct-sum T_odd, separating its finite 2-primary and odd-order torsion parts. Localization survival means every d_ij has a nonzero image in W=Z^f direct-sum T_odd. Choose one odd integer M larger than the absolute values of all free coordinates of these finitely many differences. Reduce the free coordinates modulo M, retaining T_odd. Every difference with a nonzero free coordinate remains nonzero, and every remaining difference has nonzero odd torsion coordinate. Thus the images of all e_i in the finite odd group (Z/M)^f direct-sum T_odd are distinct. The asserted collisions hold, so every Hamilton cycle repeats a sum. If f=0, only T_odd is needed. Additional collisions introduced by the quotient do not damage the counterexample.

**Effective 2-primary test.** The rational rank r of L is at most n-1 because every relation has coordinate sum zero. The torsion order of Z^n/L is the greatest common divisor of the nonzero r-by-r minors of a relation matrix. A nonzero minor has absolute value at most 2^r by Hadamard (rows have norm 2), so that torsion order is at most 2^(n-1). Consequently d vanishes in the localization if and only if 2^(n-1)d belongs to L. The forward implication uses that the order of a 2-primary torsion class divides the torsion order; the converse uses invertibility of 2. For rank zero no nonzero difference belongs to L. The same test detects forced collision relations after localization.

The search branches on a Hamilton cycle not yet forced to repeat a color, adding each possible collision from its disjoint edge pairs in turn. A branch is impossible for odd groups as soon as some vertex difference is forced to vanish after localization. Integer column Hermite normal form supplies a canonical exact lattice representation; no floating arithmetic is used. A completed finite search for a specific n can certify that fixed cardinality only. A stopped or bounded search certifies no absence of counterexamples outside explored branches.

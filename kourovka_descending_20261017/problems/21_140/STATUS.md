## Strongest reviewed findings

- Every central extension of a group containing F contains F. An explicit mirrored interior self-embedding of F kills integral H_2; natural universal coefficients with H_1(F)=Z^2 give splitting after pullback for arbitrary central kernels. This is a partial obstruction only, with no novelty claim. The more general sufficient condition is that a quotient contains F and Out(kernel) contains no F.
- Every simplicial-tree action of F has a subcopy fixing a vertex. Consequently absence of F is closed under abstract graphs of groups, as well as subgroups, products, extensions and directed unions. This does not assert property FA for F or apply to pro-p trees.
- Kropholler–Mislin forces every target group outside the locally hierarchical class LHF. Ordinary constructions inside that hierarchy cannot finish the problem. No converse from nonmembership to containing F is established.
- An explicit three-generator HNN group is torsion-free, F-free and infinite-cd, but has infinite-rank H_2 and fails FP_2. The classical finitely presented Baumslag group meets these three conditions and residual finiteness, but has infinite-dimensional rational H_3 and fails FP_3. A classical matrix family reaches each fixed finite finiteness length, never all lengths in one group.
- Under the recorded uniform untwisted hypotheses, a graph-wreath counterexample would already have a vertex group or head that is a counterexample. Residually finite finitely presented graph-wreath products also admit a finite coloring of the graph. No new counterexample arises from simpler inputs by these routes.

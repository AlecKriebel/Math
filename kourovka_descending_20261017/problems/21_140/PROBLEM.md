# Kourovka 21.140

Opened 2026-10-17 UTC. Status: researching; initial discovery estimate **5%**.

Official 21st edition, 6 October 2026, printed p.198:

> Let G be a torsion-free group of type F_infinity of infinite
> cohomological dimension. Must G contain a copy of Thompson's group F?
>
> S. Witzel, M. C. B. Zaremsky.

Authoritative source:
https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt-1.pdf .

All hypotheses must be verified separately in any proposed counterexample:
torsion-free, geometric type F_infinity, infinite integral cohomological
dimension, and absence of any abstract subgroup isomorphic to standard
Thompson F. Merely FP_infinity, arbitrarily high finite finiteness lengths,
torsion-derived infinite cohomological dimension, or failure of one
particular embedding does not suffice. An affirmative proof must cover
every group in the stated class. No conclusion or priority is assumed.

## Exact proposed conclusion

For every prime p there is a Golod group G in the explicitly cited Notebook 9.76 sense containing a subgroup S isomorphic to a countable infinite locally finite AT p-group H in Rozhkov's original 1986 definition. The inverse isomorphism S→H supplies the epimorphism requested in 21.131. The problem asks for one such example, so this proposed conclusion is stronger in its prime quantifier.

## Independently developed mechanisms and their composition

The tree-family proof constructs H on the regular p-ary tree. Enumerate labels by the successive rows 1;1,2;1,2,3;… . A rooted p-cycle a and directed generators d_i have local p-cycles at precisely the incomparable vertices 0^(n−1)1 whose label is i. Each label recurs infinitely, so each selected longitudinal generator has infinite level support. Every accompanying group is a transitive cyclic p-group. For any finite set I of generators choose a depth N whose label is absent from I. All sections at depth N then lie in a finite elementary abelian tail group. This embeds the generated subgroup into a finite wreath product, proving local finiteness without replacing the original infinite-support generators by finitary ones. See `tree_targets/locally_finite_original_at_target.md` and its independent audit.

Every generator of H has order p. The diagonal inclusion H→K=H^p⋊C_p has image in K': for each order-p generator h, the diagonal tuple is an explicit commutator of the cyclic shift with the tuple of successive powers of h. This generatorwise assertion suffices for all of H; it does not assert that all elements of H have order p.

The augmentation ideal A of F_p[K] is countable dimensional and locally nilpotent, hence stable nil. Each finite collection of coefficients lies in the augmentation ideal of a finite p-subgroup. Alahmadi–Alsulami–Jain–Zelmanov, *Matrix wreath products of algebras and embedding theorems*, Theorem 4.3, embeds A into a finitely generated stable nil algebra B. Thus K embeds into U=1+B and the given H embeds into U'.

The algebra-family proof gives a finite generating set for L=E_3(F_p+B,B): take all conjugates, under the finite group E_3(F_p), of the elementary matrices associated to a finite algebra generating set of B. Elementary commutator and addition identities produce every elementary matrix over B. The displayed finite set is normal under the constants and generates the relative group.

For u∈U, elementary factorization puts D(u)=diag(u,u^−1,1) in L. The exact identity

    D(u) D(v) D(uv)^−1 = diag(1,u^−1 v^−1 u v,1)

embeds U' into L through h↦diag(1,h,1). D itself is not a homomorphism. Therefore H embeds in L.

Write the finite generators of L as 1+c_j and let C be the algebra generated by the differences c_j. It is a subalgebra of the nil matrix algebra M_3(B), so it is nil. It is finitely generated. Since L contains infinite H, C cannot be nilpotent: a finitely generated nilpotent algebra over F_p is finite. Hence L=⟨1+c_j⟩≤1+C is exactly a Notebook 9.76 Golod group. It cannot be cyclic, so its finite generating list has at least two nontrivial entries after deletion of repetitions.

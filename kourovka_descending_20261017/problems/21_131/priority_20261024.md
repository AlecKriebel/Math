## False-positive boundaries for subsequent work

- GeneralAT is not synonymous with all automaton groups, all branch groups, GAT, γAT, or Tarski groups.
- GeneralAT need not be periodic or finitely generated; target torsion-p/countability arise from the requested Golod subgroup/image.
- Original longitudinal support means infinitely many coordinate levels. Merely infinitely many moved vertices at finitely many levels is insufficient. A finitary tree automorphism cannot itself be a chosen original longitudinal generator.
- Graded nil, a nil multiplicative monomial semigroup, a nil Lie algebra, and Jacobson radical are each insufficient to establish a **nil associative algebra**.
- General Golod groups as9.76 are not interchangeable with all Golod–Shafarevich groups or all torsion p-groups without an embedding/realization theorem.
- A negative13.55claim would not by itself answer the weaker21.131request; a positive13.55example would.

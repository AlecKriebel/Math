# Candidate claim and gates

Status: candidate proof under adversarial review. No complete-solution or originality claim is certified.

For G = D_<2>([0,1]), construct an order-preserving automorphism alpha with the following property: for every standard Dlab group A, every embedding e:G->A (even without an order-preservation assumption), and every u in A, conjugation by u fails to implement alpha on some element of e(G).

Success requires all standard interval and extended-real Dlab families, every slope subgroup H of the positive reals, every permitted linear order, and every embedding. Testing only the natural inclusion or rank-one targets is insufficient.

Candidate method: perfect centralizers have disjoint supports; least local action components inherit a uniform orientation under finite-tuple transitivity; a normalizer with bump intervals approaching both source endpoints forces an infinite descending sequence of ambient bumps whichever orientation occurs.

Boundary cases: trivial slope group (no embedding); arbitrary higher-rank and non-Archimedean slope orders; infinite-left target action component; eventual-affine germs at negative infinity; source maps with infinitely many affine pieces accumulating from the left; nonstandard embeddings; compact, one-sided, full, and reflected target groups.

External dependencies: Dlab 1968 simplicity and group-order theorem for the compact source and its local groups; exact extended-real definitions from Zenkov--Medvedev 1999. All other proof steps should be reproduced in the note. September 2026 Gong--Yang--Zeng result is prior art for the rank-one ambient theorem and one-tail construction.

Publication gate remains closed pending full written proof, primary-definition reconciliation, independent fresh adversarial reviews, exhaustive priority audit, compiled/visually checked paper, and reproducibility package.

## Exact outcome

For the compact-support locally right powers-of-two affine Dlab group G on [0,1], the paper constructs an automorphism preserving both Dlab orders that cannot be induced by conjugation after any embedding into any standard real Dlab group, with arbitrary positive-real slope group and without requiring the embedding to preserve an order. This addresses the corrected Problem 21.149 in the official 21st edition, 6 October 2026 update, p. 199, by Kopytov and Medvedev, communicated by Zenkov.

The complete proof is `preprint/main.tex`. It uses Dlab’s source simplicity theorem, perfect-centralizer support separation, least action components, finite-tuple transitivity, and a two-tail normalizer. Standard real families lie in the explicit eventual-left-affine ambient class by Zenkov–Medvedev’s primary decomposition. The paper gives a countermodel showing why that hypothesis matters.

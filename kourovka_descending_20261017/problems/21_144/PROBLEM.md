21.144. (M.Brin,M.Sapir). Conjecture:Every subgroup of Thompson’s groupF is either elementary amenable or else contains a subgroup isomorphic toF. M.C.B.Zaremsky.

Source:https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt-1.pdf . Standard finite dyadic PL ThompsonF is intended. All subgroups, including infinitely generated ones, are quantified. Elementary amenability is the smallest class containing finite and abelian groups and closed under subgroups,quotients,extensions and directed unions. A proof restricted to solvable subgroups or a bounded computational family is insufficient. A negative answer requires H<=F not elementary amenable and containing no isomorphic copy ofF, with both properties rigorously established. Current-priority audit pending.

Elementary reduction:the full conjecture is equivalent to its restriction to finitely generated subgroups. Every H is the directed union of its finitely generated subgroups; if all of these were elementary amenable, so would beH. Thus a nonelementary-amenable H contains a finitely generated nonelementary-amenable K. A proof of the finitely generated dichotomy gives F<=K<=H. Conversely the full assertion immediately implies the finitely generated one. This reduction is not a solution or novelty claim.

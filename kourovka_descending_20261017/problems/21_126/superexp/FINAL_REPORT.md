# Kourovka 21.126: super-exponential construction family

**Outcome: unsolved.** No finitely presented RAAG subgroup with super-exponential intrinsic Dehn function was obtained. Publication gates remain closed. Discovery completion estimate: 5%, reflecting rigorous route filters rather than a candidate witness.

The exact original target, finite-presentation condition and growth convention are in ../priority/SOURCE_CONTRACT.md. This family independently attempted constructions after reading the primary status audit; it did not merely reclassify known literature examples.

## Strongest derived results

1. **Normal-double embedding.** For N◁G, D=G *_N G injects into G×((G/N)*(G/N)) by fold and quotient. If G and its quotient embed in RAAGs, this proves an actual RAAG embedding of D. If G is finitely presented and N finitely generated, D is finitely presented, even when N has no finite presentation.
2. **Intrinsic filling ceiling for that concrete route.** When G is finitely presented with polynomial Dehn function, N◁G is finitely generated, and G/N is itself a RAAG, the double has an at-most-exponential intrinsic Dehn function. The full proof controls the length and area of every rewriting step and fills the final null kernel word in G. It never assumes N finitely presented. The associated normal-kernel distortion is also at most exponential, even without a Dehn assumption on G. See NORMAL_DOUBLE_BARRIER.md.
3. **Parabolic double/centralizer injections.** For G≤A(Γ), H=G∩A(Λ), reduced amalgam/HNN normal forms inject the double and centralizer extension into explicitly described RAAGs. This is an exact potentially useful mechanism for a future construction; arbitrary huge distortion alone does not satisfy its intersection hypothesis. See PARABOLIC_DOUBLE_ROUTE.md.
4. **Canonical compact-special obstruction.** Under a specific compact cubical local isometry into a Salvetti, the intersection is represented by a compact locally convex inverse-image component, is undistorted, and its double is again compact special with a quadratic filling upper bound. Abstract specialness does not constrain every possible alternative embedding in the same way. The convex-intersection fact is prior; a complete local link proof of the double statement is included.
5. **Stronger diagonal obstruction for arbitrary embeddings.** If two commuting homomorphisms α,β:H→A have α(h)β(h) in a parabolic P for every h, then α(H′),β(H′) are contained in P. Thus for nonabelian H, no embedding H×H→A makes its diagonal exactly its intersection with any parabolic. For H not virtually abelian, this remains impossible up to finite index and commensurability. The proof derives the application from the precise DKR2007 centralizer and parabolic-intersection theorems; it makes no first-priority claim. See DIAGONAL_PARABOLIC_OBSTRUCTION.md. DIAGONAL_SHARPNESS_EXAMPLE.md checks why shared abelian twists prevent a stronger conclusion for the whole factor images.

These are partial construction filters. They do not prove a universal exponential bound for finitely presented RAAG subgroups, exclude every embedding of the Brady–Tran ambient group, or settle either branch of 21.126.

## Additional asymmetric-character mechanism

After the initial report, a distinct attempt began with the known exponential-Dehn subgroup G=(Σ×Σ)⋊diagφ Z and doubled along the kernel of a genuinely different character nonzero on the surface factors. The intent was to make two interacting height compressions amplify intrinsic area. The exact calculation instead proves that every character of this G extends to Γ×Γ. Hence, when the new kernel is finitely generated, the double is a finitely presented coabelian subgroup of automatic Γ×Γ×F₂ and has an exponential upper bound by the directly inspected Llosa Isenrich2404.12334v2 Corollary1.3.

ASYMMETRIC_CHARACTER_DOUBLE.md states and proves the general extendable-character kernel identity, its positive-multiple/rational-extension variant, and a finite-iteration version with the extension hypothesis stated at every step. This addresses an independent character, not merely a renamed original height. It leaves a precise escape gap: a genuinely nonextendable character in a later construction, together with a rigorous super-exponential intrinsic lower bound, is not excluded. None was constructed here. Fresh independent delta review is recorded separately in review/.

The finite-index escape for that *original* surface-product model was then investigated separately. FINITE_INDEX_CHARACTER_DOUBLE.md proves the same exponential ceiling for every finite-index G₀ of the original G and every primitive character on G₀ with finitely generated kernel. It constructs an appropriate product ambient beneath G₀, aligns its height images, and applies the coabelian theorem to a finite-index subgroup of the actual double image. It never assumes the vertexwise smaller double has finite index. The independent reviewer required the surface fibers to be explicitly finitely generated/compact; that hypothesis was repaired before the final input was frozen. This closes arbitrary finite-index passage for the first different-character step; later iterated groups need their own structural argument.

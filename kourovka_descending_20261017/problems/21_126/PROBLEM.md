# Kourovka 21.126

Official 21st edition, October20,2026 update, printed p.198. Attribution: N.Brady; entry signed I.Soroko. The primary source and full rate/quantifier contract are preserved in priority/SOURCE_CONTRACT.md.

Do there exist finitely presented subgroups of right-angled Artin groups with super-exponential Dehn function, or with sub-exponential but nonpolynomial Dehn function?

Both branches concern the subgroup's intrinsic Dehn function. Use the strong, explicitly disclosed interpretation: super-exponential means no coarse exponential upper bound; intermediate means superpolynomial with log(delta(n))/n tending to zero. The source itself does not formally define these adjectives. A full witness to either branch answers the disjunction, while the other branch's outcome remains separately recorded.

# A uniform polynomial exclusion for ambient RAAGs on at most five vertices

Prepared October24,2026 UTC. Proof draft under independent adversarial review. This is a partial reduction of21.126, not a complete answer, and no first-priority claim is made.

**Proposition.** Every finitely presented subgroup H of a RAAG with at most five defining vertices has δ_H≼n^{18}. In particular neither existence branch of21.126 can be witnessed in such an ambient RAAG.

The exponent is a convenient verified upper bound, not an asserted optimal exponent. Constants may depend on H and its presentation.

## Finite classification certificate

TWO_DIMENSIONAL_FILTER.md proves a polynomial exclusion of degree9 for the recursive graph class C+ generated by triangle-free graphs and complete multipartite graphs with at most three parts, under disjoint unions and adjoining universal vertices. The independently reproduced exact census includes all graphs with at most four vertices and29 of34 isomorphism classes on five vertices. The five outside canonical masks are59,185,187,221,254. Edge masks use the ordered pairs

01,02,03,04,12,13,14,23,24,34.

Their exact edge lists were reproduced independently in review/independent_dimension_two_census.json. It remains to exclude precisely these five graphs.

## Three chordal graphs

The graphs with masks59,185,187 have respectively the following perfect elimination orders:

- 59: 4,3,2,0,1;
- 185: 4,0,2,1,3;
- 187: 4,0,1,2,3.

At each deletion the remaining neighbors of the deleted vertex form a clique. These checks are finite and explicit. Thus all three graphs are chordal and their RAAGs are coherent. Casals-Ruiz–Duncan–Kazachkov, *Limit groups over coherent right-angled Artin groups*, [arXiv2009.01899v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.01899v2), Lemma9.5, proves their Salvetti complexes are geometrically coherent. Definition9.2 and the paragraph following it show that every finitely generated subgroup is the group of a compact locally CAT(0) core, hence a CAT(0) group with quadratic Dehn upper bound. This is an established stronger result, not an original consequence claimed here.

## Mask254: a product of two limit groups

The graph is the join of the independent pair {0,1} with the disjoint union of the edge {2,3} and the vertex4. Its RAAG is therefore

F_2 × (Z² * Z).

Both factors are limit groups over free groups. For completeness, here is a direct discrimination proof for Z²*Z. Write Z²=⟨u,v|[u,v]=1⟩ and the remaining Z=⟨w⟩. For an integer k map

u↦p^k,  v↦p^{k²},  w↦q

into F(p,q). Given finitely many nontrivial input words, put them in free-product normal form. Every nonzero abelian syllable u^a v^b maps to p^{ak+bk²}; finitely many nonzero polynomials have only finitely many integer zeros. Choose k avoiding those zeros. Every such syllable remains a nontrivial p-power, and every nontrivial w-syllable remains a nontrivial q-power, so the reduced alternating free-product normal form survives. Applying this to the finitely many pairwise differences of elements of an arbitrary finite set proves the required finite-set injectivity. Thus Z²*Z is fully residually free and finitely generated, i.e. a limit group. The free factor F_2 is a limit group by its identity map.

Ascari–Bertolotti–Italiano–Llosa Isenrich–Migliorini, [arXiv2406.19860v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.19860v2), Corollary4.8 (printed p24), explicitly states that every finitely presented subgroup of a direct product of at most three limit groups has Dehn function bounded above by N^{18}. Apply this to the two factors. The degree-nine free-factor result is not being incorrectly reused for arbitrary limit groups.

## Mask221: the house graph

The graph has square edges01,12,23,30 and roof edges04,14. Its RAAG has the clique-amalgam splitting

A(C4) *_{⟨0,1⟩≅Z²} ⟨0,1,4⟩≅Z³.

The common edge {0,1} is a clique; the two presentations share exactly its commutator relation and generators, so the amalgam presentation is exactly the RAAG presentation. Standard special-subgroup retractions give the necessary edge injections. All finitely presented subgroups of A(C4)=F_2×F_2 have a quadratic Dehn upper bound by Huang–Osajda (or by the standard two-free-factor subgroup theorem). Subgroups of Z³ are free-abelian and likewise have quadratic upper bounds.

The structural lemma in ABELIAN_EDGE_FILLING.md applies to any finitely presented H in this RAAG: the subgroup Bass–Serre graph has finite quotient, its edge groups are finitely generated free-abelian, its vertex groups are finitely presented subgroups of conjugates of the two ambient vertex groups, and all its edge groups are undistorted in the ambient RAAG. The stable-word-length interval argument gives δ_H≼n^{2·2+1}=n^5. If H fixes a vertex, the quadratic vertex bound applies directly. This case depends on the separately reviewed derived structural lemma; it is not claimed as a direct assertion of Huang–Osajda about the three-dimensional house RAAG.

The five remaining isomorphism classes now have polynomial upper bounds of degrees2,2,2,18,5 respectively. Combining them with C+ gives the proposition. □

## Remaining scope

This excludes only ambient RAAGs admitting a defining graph on at most five vertices. A hypothetical witness could embed only into larger RAAGs or have a presentation with few intrinsic generators but use many ambient generators; intrinsic generator count is not bounded by this proposition. No restriction of the original problem to small ambient graphs is permitted. No super-exponential example, no superpolynomial subexponential example, and no full negative answer is established.

## Exact finite integral matching

The equations are an incidence system separately for each coordinate. If an all-required connected component has nonzero total charge, summing its equations gives an immediate contradiction; a loop column contributes zero. If it has zero total charge, successively routing subtree charge over the parent edge produces integral flows. If there is a reservoir, root there and route every other vertex's charge to it. Its final winding is unrestricted. Thus there is no divisibility obstruction: the construction uses only additions and sign changes. Multiple edges and isolated vertices cause no exception. Combining these separately constructed coordinates is legitimate because each edge group is exactly the coordinate subgroup specified in the statement, with no extra coupling relation.

I checked the actual surface meaning rather than identifying this linear system with scl. A loop in a vertex torus is nullhomotopic exactly when its winding is zero. An annulus can carry an arbitrary outer loop and its inverse as the oriented inner loop. The latter is an actual extra boundary component. The note expressly retains this boundary, and therefore does not make the false assertion that adding genus fills nonzero vertex homology. Pairing two outer turn intervals through a rectangle changes Euler characteristic by minus one. Each capped inner circle changes it by plus one. This proves the stated ordinary Euler characteristic identity, including the pullback formula `M(|D|-|E|)-r`, when only the specified nonexceptional vertices over D are capped. It is not automatically a chi-minus or absolute-scl formula.

Independent finite checks enumerate **18,888** incidence systems: all undirected multigraphs on one through three vertices with at most three edges, including loops, every charge in {-1,0,1}, and every required-vertex subset. A separately written rational matrix-rank computation agrees with the component criterion and with the integer vectors returned by the author's construction. The general proof, not this finite enumeration, establishes integrality for arbitrary sizes and charges.

## The asymptotic finite-cover criterion

Necessity is exact. The inverse image of a connected tree in a degree-M graph cover has M connected components each isomorphic to that tree. If its charge sum is nonzero, each requires an exceptional vertex. This forces at least M exceptions. An isolated vertex is the one-vertex tree case.

For sufficiency, choose a nontrivial loop in every relevant non-tree coordinate component. A subgraph includes injectively on fundamental groups, so each loop and each of its positive powers remains nontrivial in the ambient free group. Residual finiteness separates the finite set of powers with exponent below N in one finite quotient. In its regular cover, the monodromy of each chosen loop has order at least N. At a fixed fiber, the image of the entire component's fundamental group contains this cyclic subgroup. Regularity makes each orbit a coset of the same finite subgroup, so every lift component has degree at least N. This justifies the bound M/N on the number of lift components; it would not follow merely from large order somewhere in an arbitrary nonregular permutation action.

One exception in each charged lift component then removes its all-required condition. Choices for different coordinates may be united: removing further required equations only helps. Zero-total-charge components stay zero in each lift because the charge is pulled back uniformly. Components containing an original reservoir have one in every connected lift. Theorem 1 therefore provides simultaneous integral flows. The finite union has size at most KM/N, and N may tend to infinity. Arbitrarily large reservoir winding is explicitly allowed and is essential. Disconnected finite covers are allowed as written; for disconnected base graphs, common-degree disjoint copies of component covers suffice.

No assertion that these graph covers repair the absolute-scl reservoir-filling gap appears in the notes. That distinction is necessary and has been preserved.

## Persistent P4 obstruction and actual boundary chain

The b-coordinate subgraph consists of the two disjoint paths `M0-L0-M1` and `M2-L1-M3`. Their charge sums are +1 and -1, despite the global sum being zero. Both are trees. Every degree-M cover therefore has 2M disjoint charged b-components, each demanding an exception. No exception can meet both of these components. Conversely, leaving the M copies of M0 and M2 uncapped suffices, so the lower bound is exact for these data. This invalidates unrestricted disk promotion for separately adjustable pieces. It does not invalidate every augmented surface program.

The parent cycle thickens to an annulus before inserting the retained inner holes. I independently checked its two ribbon boundary orbits. With the displayed arc labels, its forward boundary is `b a d b^-1 a^-1 d^-1`, equal to `[ab,d]` since a and b commute. Its reverse boundary is `[d,a]`. After the six zero-charge pieces are capped, the remaining surface is connected, has Euler characteristic -2 and four boundary components, hence genus zero. The two other boundary labels are b and b^-1 with suitable base paths and orientations. They are retained.

An annulus having the two prescribed outer oriented labels would require `[ab,d]` to be conjugate to `[a,d]`. Killing c gives the free product `Z^{a,b} * Z^d`. Both words are cyclically reduced with four syllables, and the abelian-factor vectors (1,1) and (1,0) cannot match under any cyclic rotation. The free-product conjugacy criterion therefore excludes the annulus. Killing a and c sends the boundary chain to `[b,d]` plus the trivial chain. Chain-scl monotonicity gives the stated lower bound 1/2. This is a chain test of a proposed mechanism, not an irrational element or an answer for arbitrary RAAG words.

The independent code also tested 400 P4 covers with independently randomized permutation voltages on all relevant edges, in degrees one through eight. All had exactly 2M charged b-components. Arbitrary-cover validity follows from the tree argument above, rather than from this sampling.

## Finite-congruence false capping

The free subgroup generated by b and d is a retract, since killing a and c respects every RAAG relation. Monotonicity applied to inclusion and retraction gives equality of scl there and in the P4 RAAG. The free commutator has scl 1/2, an established result explicitly recalled in [Heuer, Section 2.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1802.01107v3). The surface formula and chain monotonicity used here are stated in [Chen, Definitions 2.1–2.2 and following discussion](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1904.08360v2). Their general graph-of-groups rationality theorems are not used to claim rationality for P4.

The ordered product of the N conjugates of g is exactly `[b^N,d]` in the free group itself. After these N boundary terms, append `d b^N d^-1` and `b^-N`; the whole product reduces to the identity. Consequently an (N+2)-holed sphere already maps to the original group. Its two last boundaries are freely homotopic to b^N and b^-N. Replacing their exact nonzero winding by a zero congruence class and capping them without a defect cost increases Euler characteristic from -N to 2-N. The resulting N prescribed boundaries all have degree +1 over the same g loop. In the quotient imposing b^N=1 this is an actual N-holed sphere and gives the claimed upper bound `(N-2)/(2N)`. At N=2 it is an annulus and the bound is zero. The commutator remains nontrivial by the free-product normal form in `C_N * Z`.

This construction refutes a relaxation which includes genuine surfaces and permits precisely these false zero-cost caps. It does not refute a method that tracks additional exact winding constraints, transport conditions, or defect costs. The primary Chen theorem is not accused of allowing such caps. In the independent code, the telescoping and the (N+2)-boundary relation were checked directly in the free group for every N from 2 through 150, without invoking the author's torsion reducer.

## Limited addendum: the finite-endomorphism countermodel

At the author's request, I also audited the elementary mathematical construction in `FORMAL_LANGUAGE_GAP.md`. The substitutions A→ABA and B→AB have count matrix `[[2,1],[1,1]]`. Starting with A gives exactly `(F_{2n+1},F_{2n})` after n iterations. The finite control language consisting of one or more applications of this substitution followed by terminalization is an EDT0L description; this is not a claim that the terminal-word language is regular.

For positive ratio r, its update is `(2r+1)/(r+1)`. When r>phi, subtracting r gives `(-r^2+r+1)/(r+1)<0`; subtracting phi gives `(2-phi)(r-phi)/(r+1)>0`. Starting at 2, the ratios therefore decrease while bounded below by phi. Passing to the limit in the recurrence forces the positive root of `L^2-L-1`, which is phi and is irrational. A finite sum of the count vectors has a denominator-weighted average ratio, so allowing such sums preserves this infimum. The independent code checked the exact Fibonacci identities and strict ratio inequalities for n=1 through 80; the symbolic argument establishes the infinite claim.

This correctly refutes only a general inference from finite endomorphism descriptions and additive count objectives to rational optimal ratios.

## Primary mathematical inputs

1. Bestvina and Brady, *Morse theory and finiteness properties of groups*, Inventiones Mathematicae 129 (1997), 445–470. Their theorem is the positive finiteness input for the join-of-discrete-sets Bieri–Stallings kernels in turn 2. The explicit negative homology certificate is written out in these notes.
2. Schesler and Zaremsky, *Random subcomplexes of finite buildings, and fibering of commutator subgroups of right-angled Coxeter groups*, arXiv:2107.10958v2 (9 November 2022), Journal of Topology (2023): https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10958 . The paper supplies higher algebraic-fibering constructions in finite-dimensional ambient groups and restates the relevant finiteness and Morse-theory framework. Its varying ambient examples do not give the single group asked for here.
3. Bieri, Geoghegan and Kochloukova, *The Sigma invariants of Thompson's group F*, Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics 4 (2010), 263–273: https://ems.press/journals/ggd/articles/4038 and https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.5138 . Theorem A computes the invariants; Theorem B constructs arbitrary subgroups of all finite finiteness lengths; Corollary 1.2 states the integral-character kernel criterion. Their distinction is central to turn 4.
4. Bartholdi, de Cornulier and Kochloukova, *Homological finiteness properties of wreath products*, Quarterly Journal of Mathematics 66 (2015), 437–457: https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5261 and https://doi.org/10.1093/qmath/hau035 . Theorems A/B give the exact homological/homotopical finiteness criteria used in turn 5. The relevant lamp group is Z, so the required infinite abelianization hypothesis holds.
5. Bieri and Renz, *Valuations on free resolutions and higher geometric invariants of groups*, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici 63 (1988), 464–497: https://eudml.org/doc/140130 . General BNSR provenance; the exact kernel criterion used here was checked in item 3.

Schesler's current publication list, https://eduardschesler.de/publications.html , and focused author, problem-number, and virtual-fibering searches were also checked.

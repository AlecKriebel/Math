# Unbounded finiteness lengths of virtual algebraic fibers

KOU-21.119, catalogue ID 2628. Work in progress, 3 October 2026.

Status: unresolved; two of five substantive proof-attempt turns written. These AI-assisted notes are unrefereed, have not yet received independent adversarial review, and make no novelty claim.

The problem asks for one group whose finite-index subgroups admit maps to Z with kernels of type F_n but not F_{n+1}, for every n. A separate ambient group for each n does not suffice.

Current results: necessary F_∞ and virtual first-Betti-rank conditions; nested-normal witness reduction; exact virtual fiber spectra of finite products of nonabelian free groups, with explicit infinite-cyclic-cover homology certificates. No universal existence proof or impossibility theorem has been obtained.

Best-guess completion toward resolving the original question: 10%, a subjective research estimate, not a probability or a count of completed attempts.

Sources: the Kourovka Notebook, issue 21 (2026), p.195, https://alglog.org/21tkt.pdf ; Bestvina–Brady, Invent. Math. 129 (1997), 445–470; Schesler–Zaremsky, https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.10958 ; Bieri–Geoghegan–Kochloukova, https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.5138 .

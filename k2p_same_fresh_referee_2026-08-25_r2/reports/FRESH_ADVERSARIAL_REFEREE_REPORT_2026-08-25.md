**HOLD.**

the central finite classification. The prior quartet-mutation relocation

rank-nine germ, and both weak-sharpness tensors and Jacobians.

The package does not yet meet the stated ACCEPT standard because several
advertised mutation-evidence mechanisms are not fail-closed at the semantic
level:

1. the parameter-transport mutation runner counts four changed-row hashes as

3. the restoration and primary probe wrappers similarly do not bind the

These are load-bearing qualification defects because the review protocol
requires a mutation to be rejected for its intended semantic reason. They do
not establish that a corrupted authoritative ledger is accepted: the full
producer/verifier regeneration passed, complete corrected-composite mutants
were rejected, and no classification counterexample was found. The defects
have localized remedies within the existing architecture. The correct
recommendation is therefore HOLD, not REJECT.

**Classification:** computational-completeness-blocking; not theorem-fatal.

**Files and records.** In
`work/canonicalizer_completeness/inheritance_transport/run_parameter_transport_mutations.py:156--187`,

`893d1716040315a24191a1b05fee3aa5cfcf1900b780d3f283c0921de3e20634`
and payload
`4640b0015ec251e98cf392377025956def5bd828d41fb5c8412d937921d40722`.

2. `serial_product_factor_omitted`;
3. `root_suppressed_incoming_incidence_hidden`;
4. `source_target_reversal_without_inverse_transport`.

**Effect.** This does not show that such a complete corrupted ledger would be

content manifest, tag, checksum, and ZIP. No theorem-source change is implied.

### Finding 2 — canonicalizer mutation wrapper falsely PASSes unrelated dependency crashes

**Classification:** computational-completeness-blocking nested evidence defect;
not a demonstrated outer-harness fail-open.

**Files.** `work/canonicalizer_completeness/test_canonicalizer_mutations.py:71--99`
requires only a nonzero child exit. It does not require either intended marker:
`NONORDINARY_ATLAS_ACCEPTED` or `SELECTED_TRIANGLE_ATLAS_ACCEPTED`.
`work/final_theorem_release/release_common.py:4735--4752` binds names and

`f4bf1d4eb8f9ef48213ab05cf8de2f48a2f368e2809a2f62474b931e135d32c9`.

two intended diagnostics; its report SHA-256 is
`10b8eebaa739f3853434527bd6b55d90cdb28028345cb6285d687a8c3961dfdc`

**Effect.** The current frozen cases are semantically correct, and the outer
release harness separately validates the runtime environment and byte-binds

canonicalizer guard rejected each mutation.

**Smallest remedy.** Require an exact diagnostic for each case; reject

map in `release_common.py`, regenerate the report, and reseal downstream

## 7. Scope and literature audit

### What is proved

For binary standard semi-directed strongly tree-child level-2 networks on the
same labelled leaf set, strict inheritance probabilities, and all K2P edges in

`D_plus = {(s,g): 0<s<1, 0<g<1, g>2s-1}`,

the article proves equivalence of directed containment, structural equivalence
modulo coherently transported ordinary-triangle redirection, and sharing a
full-dimensional physical regular analytic germ. It also proves generic
topology identifiability modulo ordinary triangles, a terminating exact-input
reconstruction procedure, the same classification on the strict
continuous-time cone `0<s<1, s^2<g<1`, and a `4n-3`-dimensional
weak-but-not-strong tree-child sharpness family.

### What is not proved

The article does not claim mixed-sign, stochastic-boundary, singular-edge,
higher-level, weak-class-identifiability, numerical-stability, bit-complexity,
finite-sample inference, or statistical-consistency theorems. Reconstruction
is exact and semialgebraic; it is not a practical finite-precision estimator.
The PC-PARTIAL label is appropriate: literal polynomial-body equality is not
graph-orbit equivalence, and rank, restoration, probe, and exceptional ledgers
remain load-bearing.

### Attribution and novelty search

The checked primary records support the roles assigned to them. Huber et al.
prove that semi-directed binary level-2 networks are encoded by their quarnets
and that blob trees are encoded by quarnets; that is structural support, not
the present K2P analytic containment theorem. Brits et al. v3 prove full
level-1 semi-directed identifiability modulo triangles for JC/K2P/K3P, not the
present strongly tree-child level-2 classification. The current v3 change is
recorded in Finding 8. Englander et al. supply the stated quartet/tree-of-blobs
level-2 separation inputs; the article does not promote their JC level-2
classification to K2P.

The dated primary-source record, including Huber et al. Lemma 4.2/Figure 8,
Englander et al. Propositions 2.9--2.10, Theorem 2.11 and Corollary 2.12, and
Brits et al. Theorem 4.9, exact identifiers, retrieval dates, versions, and
source hashes, is preserved in `notes/literature_audit.md`.

A targeted search did not find the exact principal-domain level-2 K2P-SAME
equivalence elsewhere. That is search evidence only, not an exhaustive
priority guarantee. No unsupported load-bearing attribution was found.

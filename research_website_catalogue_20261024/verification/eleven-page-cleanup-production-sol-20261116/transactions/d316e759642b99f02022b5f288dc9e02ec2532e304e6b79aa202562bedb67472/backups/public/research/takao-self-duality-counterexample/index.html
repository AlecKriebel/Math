A counterexample to Takao's self-duality question

Alec Kriebel · Independent researcher · 4 October 2026 · Unrefereed preprint

ORCID: 0009-0001-9320-500X · DOI and Zenodo record · Zenodo version 1.0

Open PDF · Download PDF · Original verification ZIP · BibTeX

Original manuscript abstract

Takao asked whether a finite flat commutative group scheme over a Witt ring, killed by p and having quasi-supersingular special fiber, must be Cartier self-dual in ranks p2n with n ≥ 3. For every p > 3 and n ≥ 3, we give a counterexample over W(F̅p). A six-dimensional Dieudonné module has an explicit filtration by supersingular elliptic p-torsion modules, an explicit finite Honda complement, and an image-intersection invariant distinguishing it from its dual. Both the special fiber and its finite flat Witt-ring lift are non-self-dual. The construction is an application of classical Dieudonné and finite Honda theory.

Overview

Cartier duality is the group-scheme form of character duality. A group scheme is self-dual when it is isomorphic to its Cartier dual. Takao asked whether a filtration built from supersingular elliptic p-torsion forces self-duality under the stated finite flat hypotheses.

This note gives an explicit negative answer in every stated higher rank. The special fiber has the required filtration, yet a simple invariant distinguishes it from its dual. A finite Honda system realizes that same special fiber in a finite flat group scheme over a Witt ring; the lift also fails self-duality.

The exact result

For every prime p > 3 and every integer n ≥ 3, let k be the algebraic closure of Fp and let W = W(k) be its Witt ring. There is a finite flat commutative group scheme Gn/W, killed by p and of rank p2n, whose special fiber Hn is quasi-supersingular, with

Hn ≄ HnD, and Gn ≄ GnD.

Here quasi-supersingular means that the special fiber has a subgroup filtration whose successive quotients are Ei[p] for supersingular elliptic curves Ei. The theorem does not require this filtration to lift over W.

Takao's Proposition 2 gives self-duality for n ≤ 2. The question immediately following it, on p. 2479 of his Oberwolfach contribution, asks about n ≥ 3. The construction starts with n = 3, of rank p6, and extends to every larger n.

A six-dimensional module

Contravariant Dieudonné theory encodes the special fiber by a vector space M with Frobenius-semilinear F and inverse-Frobenius-semilinear V. On a basis e0, …, e5, the nonzero images are

F(e0) = e1, F(e1) = e2, F(e3) = e4;
V(e0) = e5, V(e3) = e2, V(e5) = e4.

These operators satisfy FV = VF = 0 and F3 = V3 = 0. The explicit basis change in the paper gives an F,V-stable flag of dimensions 2 and 4. Its three two-dimensional factors all have Fx = Vx = y and Fy = Vy = 0, the module of supersingular elliptic p-torsion over this algebraically closed field. Exact contravariance turns the module flag into the required subgroup filtration.

The obstruction to self-duality

The invariant is δ(T) = dimk(im FT2 ∩ im VT2). An isomorphism preserves both second-image subspaces and their intersection.

For M, the second images are the distinct lines ke2 and ke4, so δ(M) = 0. For the dual module, both second images are the line ke0*, so δ(MD) = 1. The different values rule out self-duality of the special fiber.

The dual operators carry the appropriate Frobenius twists. Their matrices are the swapped transposes here because the displayed coefficients lie in the prime field; the argument retains the semilinear convention.

An actual Witt-ring lift and all higher ranks

The finite Honda complement is L = spank(e0, e3, e5). It complements im F, and V sends its basis to the independent vectors e5, e2, e4. Thus V is injective on L and L/pL → M/im F is an isomorphism. Together with the displayed module identities, these are the finite Honda conditions used in the paper.

The classical finite Honda anti-equivalence produces a finite flat p-killed group scheme over W(k) whose special fiber has Dieudonné module M. Cartier duality commutes with base change: self-duality of the lift would force self-duality of the special fiber. The same obstruction therefore rules out both.

For general n ≥ 3, append n − 3 supersingular elliptic p-torsion blocks, with their Honda lines. Each added module and its dual has F2 = V2 = 0. The values δ = 0 and δ = 1 remain distinct while the rank becomes p2n. The filtration and finite Honda conditions extend block by block.

Classical inputs and credit

The module and its duality classification belong to the classical cyclic-word framework. In the Pries–Ulmer convention it is M(vvfvff); the note's forward-arrow contravariant string is ffvfvv. Oort's cyclic construction supplies a classical balanced supersingular completion over the residue field.

The mixed-characteristic lift uses Fontaine–Laffaille's finite Honda theory in Hoshi's p-torsion formulation. Hoshi also supplies the Dieudonné, duality, and supersingular elliptic-factor interfaces. Numbered statements in the note refer to Hoshi's March 2021 revised author manuscript.

The contribution is the explicit application to Takao's stated question, with direct calculations of the filtration, duality obstruction, and finite Honda complement. No first-discovery or first-priority claim is made.

Scope and review status

The examples use W(k) with k the algebraic closure of Fp. They disprove the universal assertion for perfect residue fields; descent to every perfect field is not asserted. Quasi-supersingularity is a condition on the special fiber. No lifted quasi-supersingular filtration over W is claimed.

The result concerns general finite group schemes. It produces no principally polarized Jacobian and does not decide the surrounding Coleman conjecture. The classical completion over the residue field does not supply an additional p-divisible object over W.

The proof is symbolic for every allowed p and n. The companion package includes finite checks of the basis, filtration, finite Honda conditions, and Frobenius-twist calculations. These support the written proof and do not replace the classical classification inputs or certify historical priority.

Original Zenodo record description

For every prime p greater than 3 and every n at least 3, this research note constructs a finite flat commutative group scheme over W(closure of F_p), killed by p and of rank p^(2n), with quasi-supersingular special fiber, such that both the special fiber and its Witt-ring lift fail Cartier self-duality. It gives an explicit negative answer to the higher-rank question following Proposition 2 on page 2479 of Naotake Takao's contribution to Oberwolfach Report 42/2023.

The proof uses an explicit six-dimensional Dieudonne module, a supersingular elliptic p-torsion filtration, an image-intersection invariant and a finite Honda complement. These are applications of established Dieudonne and finite Honda theory, with the classical cyclic-word framework credited. No first-discovery or first-priority claim is made.

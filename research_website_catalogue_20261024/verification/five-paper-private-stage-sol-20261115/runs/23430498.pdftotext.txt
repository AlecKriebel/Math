             low-degree restrictions and an order-seven example

                                                 Abstract
          We give applications of published ﬁnite-type classiﬁcation and classical genus-two symmetry
      to the locus of string links with free exterior group. The category is ordinary unframed smooth
      ordered string links with ﬁxed boundary, upward orientation, and rational invariants. Freeness
      means that the actual three-dimensional exterior group is abstractly Fk . Recalling a ﬁltered
      Habegger–Masbaum corollary, the intersection of the entire Milnor algebra with the two-strand
      order-at-most-two space is spanQ {1, ℓ, ℓ2 }. Meilhan’s classiﬁcation and known free tangles give
      a component Conway coeﬃcient outside the Milnor-plus-vanishing span at (k, n) = (2, 2), and
      give zero vanishing kernel for every k ≥ 2 and 0 ≤ n ≤ 2. An exact marked genus-two
      reversal argument, combined with the prior Duzhin–Karev ordinary orientation detector, gives
      a nonzero kernel invariant of exact order seven on two strands. The seven-wheel is credited
      to Bar-Natan following Duzhin–Karev’s journal proof. Historical priority of these combined
      free-exterior applications is undetermined; no ﬁrstness is claimed.

1    Category, question, and scope
Let C be the corner-rounded oriented cylinder D2 × [0, 1], with distinct marked points p1 , . . . , pk
in the interior of D2 . An element of SLk is an ordered smooth proper embedding of k intervals,
with strand i directed from (pi , 0) to (pi , 1), standard in product endpoint collars. Equivalence is
smooth ambient isotopy ﬁxing the boundary; isotopies can be taken ﬁxed on a smaller boundary
collar. Upward speciﬁes orientation, and imposes no height-monotonicity condition. No strand
framing is part of an object.

obligations made explicit.

higher-order kernels, or higher-order spanning and general classiﬁcation. Failure of spanning at
order two does not by itself imply failure at larger order bounds. No claim about the current
worldwide open status of those residual questions is made.

kills that diagram over Q. Framing independence kills the two self-struts. Through degree two the
surviving ordinary tree diagrams are therefore the empty diagram, the mixed strut, and its square.

two-braids attain every integer value of ℓ, proving independence. These three functions belong to
the Milnor algebra and have orders at most 0, 1, 2, respectively. The upper and lower inclusions
follow.

Lemma 5. Every L ∈ F2 is ambient isotopic to ρL relative to the boundary, with each label and
orientation preserved. The isotopy can ﬁx a suﬃciently small boundary collar.

Proof. We give the complete boundary-pattern and collar argument.
    Recovering a handlebody. Delete the relative interiors of two standard tubes Ni about the arcs.
The compact exterior H has closed connected genus-two boundary, formed from the outer four-holed
sphere P and the lateral annuli Ai . It is irreducible: a sphere in int H bounds its bounded-side
ball in C by smooth Schoenﬂies [20, Theorem 1.1]; every connected tube meets ∂C and misses the
sphere, so lies outside that ball. The ball is therefore contained in H.
    A positive-genus closed surface group is not free, whereas every subgroup of a free group is free.
Thus a positive-genus boundary component cannot inject into a free component group. The Loop
Theorem [20, Theorem 3.1] gives an essential compressing disk. Cutting on it retains irreducibility:
a sphere in a cut piece bounds a ball in the uncut manifold, and the connected cutting disk,
with boundary on the exterior boundary and disjoint from that sphere, cannot enter this ball.
Reconstruction by a one-handle gives a free-product decomposition; the cut component groups are
free factors and remain free. Repeat. The sum of 3g − 2 over positive-genus boundary components
strictly decreases under every essential compression (including torus and separating cases). The
ﬁnal pieces have only spherical boundary and are irreducible, hence are balls: a boundary sphere
pushed into its collar bounds a ball on the side away from that collar, and adjoining the collar gives
the original piece. Reverse the disk cuts to reconstruct a handlebody. Its boundary genus is two.
This uses actual group freeness, not a completion or special meridian generating set.

For the algebraic input we use the exact arXiv v5 body of Duzhin–Karev, dated 25 July 2005

    Let H7 be the connected seven-wheel with cyclic leg colors 1121222 and the vertex orientation
of [15, Proposition 2]. It has seven trivalent and seven univalent vertices, so degree (7 + 7)/2 = 7.
The colored Jacobi reversal changes it by (−1)7 = −1. Under symmetrization χ, write d = χ(H7 )
for the ordinary rational diagram class. The cited deframing result says d 6= 0 after imposing the
ordinary 1T relations that delete the two self-struts. Its qualiﬁcation is important: a connected
nonsingle-strut class is primitive, and the two framing generators are central degree-one primitives.
The symmetrized wheel belongs to the complementary ordinary subalgebra identiﬁed by Lemma 8.
Connectedness alone is not a general license to pass arbitrary framed classes through 1T.
    The operation in (14) agrees with this reversal. For the standard long lines (i, 0, t), reversing
strand parameters is restored to upward labeled lines by (x, y, z) 7→ (x, −y, −z); translation and
rescaling give exactly r. Ambient orientation is preserved, and reversing both tangents at a crossing
preserves its crossing sign. Thus ρ preserves ordinary ﬁnite-type order, with symbol involution τA
satisfying τA (d) = −d. It is neither a label-exchanging half-turn nor a mirror.

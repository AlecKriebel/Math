Let V be the ordinary, uncompleted complex algebra of all finite-order finite-type invariants of links. We give a principal ideal with a nonempty actual-link zero set whose vanishing ideal strictly contains its radical. The generator is g(4g-1), where g is the sum of squared pairwise linking numbers. The obstruction is a character on the entire algebra, obtained by evaluating the standard twist-restriction polynomial at a nonintegral parameter. This gives a negative answer to the arbitrary-ideal formulation of Bar-Natan's Problem 2.27, as recorded by Ohtsuki; the separate linking-number-generated ideal question is not decided.

    - heading "Abstract" [level=2]
    - paragraph: We construct an associative pointed free product system of separable complex Hilbert spaces whose maximal ordered tensor subsystem does not generate the whole free system. Its tensor core is the time-ordered finite-word Fock system, and the closed free subsystem generated by that core is precisely the full finite-word Fock system. The ambient system has, in addition, nonzero orthogonal sectors of words of ordinal length below ω². A recoverable Gaussian mixture supplies a sigma-finite measure class invariant under finite-prefix absorption. Finite monochromatic-run charts turn concatenation into the free product structure. We prove separability, an explicit continuous Hilbert-field structure including time zero, and continuity of the full structural maps and their inverses. This gives a negative answer, in the scalar specialization, to the universal tensor-core generation question stated by Skeide in 2009. It does not answer the separate adapted-cocycle or forward tensor-recovery questions. The argument is analytic; accompanying finite computations do not certify its infinite-dimensional assertions. This draft is unrefereed and extensively AI-assisted.
    - heading "The idea in plain English" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A product system describes how mathematical spaces for shorter time intervals combine into a space for a longer interval. There are different ways to combine them. Tensor products impose one kind of ordering; free products allow a richer collection of alternating combinations.
    - paragraph: The question is whether the largest tensor subsystem inside a free product system always contains enough information to rebuild the whole free system. This paper constructs an example where it does not. Its tensor core generates all the finite-word sectors, but the full system also contains orthogonal sectors made from infinite words. Taking limits of the finite-word sectors cannot reach those extra sectors.
    - paragraph: The construction retains separable Hilbert spaces and proves a specified continuous dependence on time, including at time zero. The obstruction therefore persists beyond a purely algebraic example.
    - heading "The precise result" [level=2]
    - paragraph: There exists an associative pointed free product system E of separable complex Hilbert spaces with the following structure at every positive time t. Its maximal ordered tensor subsystem F is the time-ordered finite-word Fock system. The closed free subsystem generated by F is the full finite-word Fock system K, and K_t is a proper subspace of E_t. A nonzero grade-ω sector remains orthogonal to K_t.
    - paragraph: The system has a continuous vacuum and norm-continuous support-subspace sections, is locally trivial at positive time, and has jointly continuous structural maps and inverses, including at time zero.
    - paragraph: This gives a negative answer in the scalar case to the universal tensor-core generation question stated by Michael Skeide in 2009.
    - heading "Why the result holds" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A recoverable Gaussian mixture gives a sigma-finite measure class on convergent infinite sequences that is preserved under finite-prefix absorption.
    - paragraph: Words of ordinal length below ω² split at a time cut into finitely many monochromatic runs, producing associative pointed free-product unitaries and the specified continuity structure.
    - paragraph: Intersecting ordered tensor ranges leaves decreasing finite words. Their free hull contains exactly finite words, while a positive finite-measure grade-ω set supplies a nonzero orthogonal remainder.
    - heading "Scope and limitations" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The continuity claim uses the explicit Hilbert-field convention stated in the paper. Stronger regularity requirements would need to be checked separately.
    - paragraph: The result concerns tensor-core generation. The adapted-cocycle and forward tensor-recovery questions remain separate, as does a classification of free product systems.

We prove that every finite central free arrangement over a field of characteristic zero whose nonzero exponents are 1 or 2, with at most one 3, is supersolvable. Factorization and linear-relation formality restrict the incidence graph of the multiple rank-two flats to a tree or a subdivision of K4 with trees attached. A direct modular-coatom argument then gives a maximal modular chain in every rank. This proves the full conjecture stated in Tohăneanu's Oberwolfach contribution. We credit the earlier all-2, tree, rank-three and modular-pruning results and acknowledge Ferudun's later matching full-target preprint. The accompanying package preserves the dated candidate, source audit and exact computational diagnostics. AI tools were used extensively in developing, writing and checking this work.

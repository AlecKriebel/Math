    - heading "Abstract" [level=2]
    - paragraph: We construct a finite locally CAT(0) unit cube complex with one vertex, seven edges, eight squares, and two three-dimensional cubes, whose fundamental group is the mapping torus of a→ a, b→ ab, c→ bcb. Its universal cover gives a proper cocompact cubical action of the exact group in AIM Question 5.2. All attaching maps, signed vertex links, and reversible presentation changes are given explicitly. We also explain a qualitative existence deduction from earlier WPD, Morse, and cubical completion theorems. The contribution presented here is the explicit small finite model; no historical firstness or minimality claim is made.
    - heading "The idea in plain English" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Some groups can be understood through the geometry of spaces assembled from squares and cubes. A useful arrangement has nonpositive curvature and repeats so that a finite quotient describes the entire infinite space.
    - paragraph: This paper gives an explicit construction for one group in the AIM problem list. Its finite model has one vertex, seven edges, eight squares and two three-dimensional cubes. The face identifications and the resulting fundamental group are specified. The universal cover is the infinite CAT(0) cube complex on which the group acts freely, properly and cocompactly.
    - paragraph: The compact description is the interesting feature. “Two cubes” counts the three-dimensional cells in a quotient that allows boundary identifications. It does not mean that every point on the boundaries of two ordinary solid cubes remains distinct.
    - heading "The precise result" [level=2]
    - paragraph: Let F be the free group on a, b and c, with automorphism φ(a)=a, φ(b)=ab and φ(c)=bcb. The mapping torus uses the convention tut⁻¹=φ(u); the final b in bcb is positive.
    - paragraph: There is a finite locally CAT(0) unit cube complex Z of dimension three, with cell counts (1,7,8,2) and fundamental group isomorphic to the full group F⋊φZ. Its universal cover carries a free, proper and cocompact action of that exact group by cubical isometries.
    - paragraph: The finite quotient uses the general cube-complex convention allowing isometric boundary-face identifications. The stated cell counts are before subdivision; the CAT(0) universal cover satisfies the stricter embedded-cube convention.
    - heading "Why the result holds" [level=2]
    - paragraph: A base built from two squares admits a cubical involution. A second two-square complex has infinite cyclic fundamental group and two compatible local-isometry maps into the base.
    - paragraph: Attaching the ends of its product with an interval gives the finite quotient. The links are checked with signed edge directions and all repeated corner occurrences retained; the unique vertex link is an actual flag simplicial complex.
    - paragraph: Reversible presentation changes identify the entire fundamental group, including the positive final b in the automorphism. Standard cubical geometry then gives the CAT(0) universal cover and the proper cocompact deck action.
    - paragraph: A separate argument explains qualitative existence using earlier WPD, Morse and cubical-completion theorems. Those prior tools do not supply the displayed small cell counts.
    - heading "Scope and limitations" [level=2]
    - paragraph: The result concerns this exact marked automorphism. The workshop summary also discusses a related example with b↦aba; that formula is distinct from the b↦ab target here.
    - paragraph: No minimum dimension, minimum cell count, virtual specialness or theorem for a whole family of free-by-cyclic groups is claimed.
    - paragraph: The original quotient permits boundary identifications. A stricter cubical quotient can be obtained by subdivision, but the counts (1,7,8,2) then change.
    - paragraph: The explicit construction is presented with credit to earlier geometric tools. The paper makes no historical-firstness or absolute-novelty claim, and an archived problem statement does not establish current worldwide openness.
    - heading "Status and AI use" [level=2]

We give an explicit affirmative answer to the two-polynomial question posed by Austrin in Oberwolfach Report 9/2025. Two nonzero real multilinear polynomials of degree at most d on the uniform Boolean cube have a common nonzero vertex whenever every relative coordinate influence is at most 1/(4096d^8). In fact, disjoint supports force a relative influence at least 1/(1024d^8). The result is a deduction from the disjoint-support separator of Li–Li–Li–Liu and the covariance inequality of O'Donnell–Saks–Schramm–Servedio, combined with a square-density estimate. We supply the complete deduction and an explicit separator constant. No novelty or first-priority claim is made for this consequence.

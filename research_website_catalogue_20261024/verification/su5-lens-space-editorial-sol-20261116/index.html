An explicit SU(5) lens-space counterexample to the printed Guadagnini-Pilo conjecture

Alec Kriebel · Manuscript dated 5 October 2026 · Zenodo version 1.0 · Unrefereed research note

The fundamental group records how loops behave in a space. A quantum invariant assigns the space a number using additional algebraic structure. This note gives two closed oriented three-manifolds with the same fundamental group but different nonzero magnitudes of one fixed quantum invariant.

The comparison concerns the ordinary full SU(5) theory at WZW level 5. It contradicts the broad assertion printed as Conjecture 7.5 in Ohtsuki’s collection. Historical priority remains unresolved because Takahito Kuriya’s directly relevant preprint has not been obtained for comparison.

Original abstract

For the ordinary full SU(5) Reshetikhin–Turaev invariant at WZW level k=5, or shifted level r=10, the lens spaces L(5,1) and L(5,2) have fundamental group ℤ/5 but unequal nonzero magnitudes. With τ̂(S³)=1, their squared magnitudes are 3475+1550√5 and 4025+1800√5. A short exact root-lattice reduction proves the calculation, conditional on the established modular-category and surgery formulas; finite cyclotomic programs provide reproducible checks. This contradicts Conjecture 7.5 as printed in Ohtsuki's collection. Historical priority is unresolved: Takahito Kuriya's directly relevant preprint could not be obtained, and no firstness or new historical resolution is claimed. This is an unrefereed research note with extensive AI assistance and no conventional human peer review.

Same group, different magnitudes

Fix the full ordinary SU(5) Reshetikhin–Turaev theory at WZW level k = 5, hence shifted level r = k + 5 = 10. Normalize its invariant by τ̂(M) = τ(M)/τ(S³), so τ̂(S³) = 1. Then the exact comparison is:

The two lens spaces in the same normalized theory
ManifoldFundamental group|τ̂|²
L(5,1)ℤ/53475 + 1550√5
L(5,2)ℤ/54025 + 1800√5

Both squared magnitudes are positive. Their difference is 550 + 250√5 > 0, so the nonzero magnitudes are unequal. Unknot filling gives each fundamental group the presentation ⟨μ | μ⁵ = 1⟩. This establishes equality of the actual groups, rather than just their first homology.

All 126 dominant labels (a₁,a₂,a₃,a₄), with aᵢ ≥ 0 and a₁+a₂+a₃+a₄ ≤ 5, belong to the category used here. The root-lattice quotient in the calculation is a computational part of the full-category formula. It imposes no root-label restriction, PSU quotient, spin or bundle sector, inserted link, or retained manifold framing.

The exact reduction

The proof specializes the established Hansen–Takata v2 lens-space formula, Theorem 5.1. For the A₄ root system, the root lattice Y has rank four. At surgery numerator 5 and shifted level 10, its quadratic phase is identically one. Character orthogonality reduces the sum over the 625 classes of Y/5Y to five Weyl terms for each target space.

The selection condition is qρ − wρ ∈ 5X, where X is the weight lattice, the dual of Y. Using 5Y instead would incorrectly discard four of the five terms. Their exact sums have squared norms |B₁|² = 11 + 2√5 and |B₂|² = 9 + 4√5.

The Weyl denominator and the S³ normalization give

|τ̂(L(5,q))|² = 625|Bq|² / (225 − 100√5).

The denominator is positive and rationalizes exactly to the two values above. The general formula used here permits the noncoprime pair 5 and 10; a later coprime simplification in Hansen–Takata is not used. Category existence and the RT surgery theorem remain established mathematical inputs, rather than foundations reconstructed by the finite computation.

Reversing orientation conjugates the ordinary invariant, preserving its magnitude. Inverse-denominator conventions exchange 2 and 3 within one magnitude class. A fixed nonzero rescaling of the theory cannot make the pair equal.

Printed target and historical limit

Ohtsuki’s collection, printed page 474, attributes Conjecture 7.5 to Guadagnini and Pilo: the magnitude of a nonzero quantum G invariant is determined by the fundamental group. The volume is nominally dated 2002 and was published on 1 June 2004. The target here is that literal broad statement. Guadagnini–Pilo’s proved SU(2) lens-space theorem is not contradicted.

The calculation credits Hansen–Takata’s full-category and surgery formulas, specifically arXiv:math/0209403v2. Its final journal edition was not compared in this work. No new general RT formula, all-level classification or present worldwide-open-status claim is made.

Takahito Kuriya’s The LMO invariant and the Guadagnini–Pilo conjecture for lens spaces is listed by his Kyushu institutional record and is associated with a 2003 talk. Its full text, hypotheses and conclusions could not be obtained. We cannot establish that it does not already contain or circumscribe this example. Historical priority therefore remains unresolved; this note claims neither a first counterexample nor a new historical resolution.

Supporting computation

The archive includes exact cyclotomic programs for the lattice and full-weight formulations. The current review includes a successful exact root-lattice check; a full-weight NumPy reproduction was not completed. The exact hand and lattice derivation supports this specialization. These calculations neither establish the general category/surgery theorems nor resolve historical priority.

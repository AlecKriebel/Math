OpenAI's explicit complex cancellation fourfold has a polynomial one-variable cylinder and is not affine four-space. We transport coefficient inclusion and evaluation at zero through its concrete cylinder coordinates. This gives a complex algebra retract of a five-variable polynomial ring, of transcendence degree four, that is not a polynomial algebra. We provide split maps and a five-component idempotent polynomial endomorphism, in factored form with no denominators, and exact symbolic certificates for the coordinate identities, including the fiber at the parameter zero. The implication from cancellation failure to a negative answer to Costa's retract question is classical; the nonpolynomiality input and cancellation breakthrough belong to OpenAI. This note makes that consequence explicit in ambient dimension five and makes no assertion about ambient dimension four.

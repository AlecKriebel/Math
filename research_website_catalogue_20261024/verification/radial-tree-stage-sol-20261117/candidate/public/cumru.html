About Cumru Township

Cumru Township is a township located in Berks County, Pennsylvania. It is home to a diverse population with a rich history. The township is home to many historical landmarks and sites.

Cumru Township History

Cumru Township was founded in 1713 and was named after the Welsh village of Cymru. The township was originally settled by Welsh immigrants and played a significant role in the Revolutionary War.

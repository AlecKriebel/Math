TapBall

TapBall is an iOS game based around a progressively speed increasing-ball that must be tapped.

You lose at the game of TapBall when the ball is not tapped once during a round.

TapBall is based upon the flash game: ClickBall, which you can play here >
 

As of 2015, TapBall has been open-sourced here.

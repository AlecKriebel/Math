The Coin Project

The Coin Project is a project to help bring awareness to the various cryptocurrencies that exist and are traded daily. 

In order to do this, a series of apps have been, or will be, released to the world on the iOS App Store. 

This is the support page for all of those apps. 

As of 2015, the Coin Project has been open-sourced here.

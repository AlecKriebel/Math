## 107 — nilpotent constants are a real category boundary

The contradiction therefore concerns actual functors in the full unframed category, not framed functors or arbitrary chosen hulls. The draft states the full category, actual fixed subfunctor, complete Noetherian local W(k) recipient and every tangent dimension; it leaves the component-prorepresentable variant untouched.

## 111 — why normalized Hilbert–Schmidt would change the formula

For a hypothetical normalized norm ||·||HS/√N, take N=4 and Φ(x)=diag(e^{i d(p,x)},1,1,1) on a unit-round sphere. The distance function is1-Lipschitz, so Φ is at most1/2-Lipschitz in that normalized metric. At an antipode W=diag(−1,1,1,1), however, I+W is singular, and the displayed fractional formula is undefined at t=1. Under the actual **unnormalized** Hilbert–Schmidt metric this map has Lipschitz constant1 and is outside the stated half-sublevel. This is a checkable warning against a normalization substitution, not a defect in the actual two-metric theorem. The draft keeps the unnormalized norm, intrinsic length metric, fixed basepoint, all closed times, nonsmooth maps and constant-map retraction.

## Exact mathematical and operational claim

The channel is the memoryless one-mode pure-loss attenuator L_eta, with an independent vacuum environment at each use and eta in [1/2,1]. Its complement is L_(1-eta) up to an entropy-irrelevant phase; its n-use input can be arbitrarily entangled across modes/uses. The optical constraint is Tr(G_n rho_bar)<=nN, G_n the sum of number operators and 0<=N<infinity. It averages over classical messages, consumed key and encoder randomness; quantum data use the maximally mixed logical source, equivalently the transmitted half of a maximally entangled reference. No maximum per-message, per-codeword, peak-photon or arbitrary-source quantum cost guarantee is proved.

All rates are real signed net rates: positive generation, negative consumption, with finite gross rates and exponentially sized finite resource registers. The normal form has a CPTP block encoder, forward noiseless resources and a receiver decoder. It provides no feedback, backward communication, receiver cooperation, or two-way-assisted capacity. The proof applies to this stated normal form and its usual forward-resource flattening, not an uncounted adaptive-feedback model. Side resources are charged by rate rather than noisy-channel photon cost. Uniform-source trace-norm reliability/secrecy errors vanish; the norm is unhalved, so a probability changes by at most half the norm.

For the private theorem the generated secret W=(M,T_A) is required uniform and independent of all public information X=(K,L) and Eve E^n after consumed V=(J,S_B) has been traced out. Generated communication labels are fresh independent uniform messages initially independent of the preshared key. Eve is not additionally handed the used key. Public common randomness is not counted as communication. The claim is neither semantic security for arbitrary priors nor secrecy after used-key disclosure.

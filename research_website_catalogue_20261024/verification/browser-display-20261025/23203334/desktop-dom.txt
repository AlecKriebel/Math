		28 container abstract
			29 heading Abstract, Value: 2
				30 text Abstract
			31 text We extend the signed-impulse proof of the one-species three-dimensional relativistic Vlasov–Maxwell theorem in OpenAI’s family 362 manuscript to a fixed finite collection of particle species. Mass normalization gives a common velocity law and constant species-dependent accelerations. The entire source–receiver cancellation has coefficient eₐe_b/mₐ; the source acceleration cancels before its sign is discarded. A positive mass-weighted energy controls a disjoint union of species label spaces, and the angular occupation and selected receiver coefficient use one simultaneous momentum bootstrap. We prove the resulting global classical theorem for nonnegative smooth compact phase data and compatible C_b^∞ ∩ L² Maxwell fields, without smallness, symmetry or neutrality. The universal geometric identity is inherited; the new estimates keep all source and receiver coefficients explicit and close the shared-field bootstrap.
		32 container plain-language
			33 heading The idea in plain English, Value: 2
				34 text The idea in plain English
			35 text The relativistic Vlasov–Maxwell equations describe a collisionless population of charged particles and the electromagnetic field they create. Several particle species share the same field, but their masses and charges make them respond differently. The question is whether initially smooth data can develop a singularity in finite time.
			36 text This paper claims global smooth evolution for a fixed finite collection of species under its stated regularity assumptions, without requiring the particles to be dilute, symmetric or electrically neutral overall. It extends the signed-impulse argument in a specific OpenAI one-species manuscript; that upstream analytical argument is an essential dependency.
			37 text The useful cancellation occurs before taking absolute values. Electric and magnetic contributions that look large separately can partly cancel in the accumulated change of a particle’s momentum. With several species, the signs and mass factors must stay attached to the correct source and receiving particle throughout that calculation.
			38 text The added problem is a shared-field estimate when charge-to-mass ratios differ. Simply adding all species into one density does not generally produce a one-species equation. The paper instead combines their positive energies and controls every species in one momentum argument.
		39 container result
			40 heading The precise result, Value: 2
				41 text The precise result
			42 text Fix a finite number of species in three space dimensions, each with a positive mass and a real charge, including zero charge. Their initial phase densities are nonnegative smooth functions with compact support in position and momentum.
			43 text The initial electric and magnetic fields lie in C_b^∞(R³) ∩ L²(R³): every spatial derivative is bounded, and the fields have finite L² norm. They satisfy the Maxwell divergence constraints for the initial charge density.
			44 text Under these assumptions, the published theorem asserts a unique global classical solution that is smooth on every finite time interval, with compact phase support on each such interval and fields continuous in time with values in L². No smallness, symmetry or neutrality condition is imposed.
			45 text Mass normalization gives all species the same velocity law u(v) = v/√(1 + |v|²), with acceleration coefficient eₐ/mₐ. The complete interaction between a receiver of species a and a source of species b carries eₐe_b/mₐ; positive mass-weighted energy controls all species together.
			46 text The argument imports the geometric cancellation and analytical framework of the pinned OpenAI family 362 manuscript. Its claimed addition is the explicit coefficient bookkeeping and simultaneous closure needed for a fixed finite multispecies system.
		47 container proof
			48 heading Why the result holds, Value: 2
				49 text Why the result holds
			50 text Normalize momentum by each species’ mass. This produces a common relativistic velocity law while leaving the distinct charge-to-mass ratios explicit in the acceleration.
			51 text Apply the inherited source–receiver identity to each pair of species before estimating magnitudes. The source acceleration cancels within the identity, leaving the correct receiver-dependent factor eₐe_b/mₐ.
			52 text Combine the separate species label spaces using their positive mass-weighted energy. One common momentum bound then controls angular occupation across the shared field without pretending that the sum of the densities solves a scalar transport equation.
			53 text The paper transfers the direct-force and selected-receiver estimates with this common bound. The selection gain offsets the angular loss, allowing the signed-impulse bootstrap to improve itself for every species simultaneously.
			54 text The resulting momentum-growth control rules out finite-time momentum blowup. A local theory compatible with nonneutral fields and the bounded-momentum continuation criterion then give the asserted global solution.
		55 container limits
			56 heading Scope and limitations, Value: 2
				57 text Scope and limitations
			58 text This is an extension of a specific upstream proof. Its conclusion depends on the analytical estimates in that proof; it is not an independent proof of the one-species theorem obtained from the headline existence statement alone.
			59 text The data assumptions include compact phase support and bounded derivatives of all orders for the initial fields. The theorem does not cover arbitrary smooth finite-energy data, noncompact momentum tails, collisions or a different spacetime model.
			60 text The number of species and their masses and charges are fixed. Constants may depend on these parameters, the initial data and the time horizon. No uniform infinite-species or zero-mass limit, or uniform-in-time momentum bound, is established.
			61 text When all charged species have the same nonzero charge-to-mass ratio, a weighted reduction already gives a one-species problem. The substantive extension concerns differing ratios, including mixtures of opposite charges.
			62 text Classical bounded-momentum continuation results, the retarded division identities and the upstream signed-impulse mechanism are prior inputs. This bounded literature review does not establish exhaustive historical priority.
			63 text The finite algebraic and rational checks in the archive do not verify the full analytical theorem. No independently reproduced proof-assistant certificate or completed external human peer review is claimed.

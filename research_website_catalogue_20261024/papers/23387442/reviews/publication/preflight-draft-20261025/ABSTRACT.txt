Let A_n(k) be the integral Andreadakis filtration of the automorphism group of a free group, and let D_n(k) be the kernel of its action on J/J^{k+1} in the rational SL(2,ℂ) matrix-entry algebra at the trivial representation. We construct elements of D_n(5) \ A_n(5) for every n≥4, and of D_n(6) \ A_n(6) for every n≥3. Thus the equality in all degrees conjectured in Satoh’s 2018 report is false at every rank in its stated range. The construction uses a classical degree-five Lie identity, elementary free-group automorphisms, and integral Magnus coefficients. The proof works with actual powers of J, rather than only values along one-parameter representations.

S1 Reaction matrices, flux cone, and semipositive conservation
Order species asX1,...,Xm,Zand reactions asR0,R2,...,Rm−2,Ra,Rb,R+,R−. For each indexed reaction, the
source and target vectors have total molecularity at most two. The source matrixYm and stoichiometric matrixΓm

two boundary-pair fluxes. Thus

S11 Numerical protocol and independent verification
Cosine–Galerkin simulations use exact modal convolution for the quadratic reaction terms and an adaptive stiff
integrator. Initial data are small perturbations of1 in the critical direction. The first-mode amplitude is measured by
the adjoint projection. Spatial and temporal refinement tables, open-format profile data, exact parameters, and all
solver tolerances are included in the data archive. No numerical output is used in a proof.
The release contains independent programs for the reaction family, flux cone, all-spectrum block theorem, omission
minors, principal-minor diffusion-ray theorem, diffusion criterion, contrast bounds, improved profile, Pareto family,
mode certificates, harmonic corrections, cubic sign, and branch stability. Mutation tests change signs, endpoints, the
factor eight, Fourier factors, and certificate coefficients and must be rejected.

\section*{Complete order-$m$ omission-minor table}
Let $J=A_m(a,b)H$ with $a,b>0$ and $H=\operatorname{diag}(h_1,\ldots,h_m,h_Z)
\succ0$.  For the $m\times m$ principal blocks obtained by omitting one
species, direct sparse expansion gives
\[
(-1)^m\det J_{\widehat Z,\widehat Z}
=-2a^{m-1}b\prod_{i=1}^{m}h_i,
\]
\[
(-1)^m\det J_{\widehat {X_j},\widehat {X_j}}
=16a^{m-1}b\,h_Z\!\prod_{\substack{1\le i\le m\\i\ne j}}h_i,
\qquad 2\le j\le m-1,
\]
and
\[
\det J_{\widehat {X_1},\widehat {X_1}}=0,
\qquad
\det J_{\widehat {X_m},\widehat {X_m}}=0.
\]
For an interior omission, the induced matrix is block triangular: every broken
chain component contributes a negative singleton factor $-a$, and the remaining
boundary triad contributes signed determinant $16a^2b$.  Omitting $X_1$ leaves
the restricted conservation vector as a left nullvector.  Omitting $X_m$ leaves
the restriction of the full right kernel vector, whose $X_m$ coordinate is
zero.  The $Z$-omission formula follows by the same sparse chain expansion and
contains the unique negative order-$m$ signed minor.

Multiplying each omission minor by the omitted diffusivity yields
\[
\boxed{
\beta_1(D)=2a^{m-1}b\left(\prod_{i=1}^{m}h_i\right)
\left[8h_Z\sum_{j=2}^{m-1}\frac{d_j}{h_j}-d_Z\right].}
\]

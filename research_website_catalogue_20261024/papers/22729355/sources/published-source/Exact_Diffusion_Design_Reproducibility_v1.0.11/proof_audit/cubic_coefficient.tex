\section*{Exact cubic coefficient}
On the one-dimensional fixed-mass center manifold, normalize the
adjoint-projection amplitude as $A r\cos\xi$.  The Fourier identities
\[
\cos^2\xi=\frac12(1+\cos2\xi),
\qquad
\cos\xi\cos2\xi=\frac12(\cos\xi+\cos3\xi)
\]
give
\[
\dot A=\eta_m\mu A+c_mA^3+
O(|A|^5+|A|^3|\mu|+|A||\mu|^2),
\]
where
\[
c_m=\frac{\ell^T\left[B_m(r,w_0)+\frac12B_m(r,w_2)\right]}{\ell^Tr}.
\]
No direct cubic reaction tensor occurs.
Setting the reduced vector field to zero gives the stationary
Lyapunov--Schmidt bifurcation equation.
Spatial reflection $\xi\mapsto\pi-\xi$ sends the critical mode to its
negative.  Equivariance therefore makes the reduced vector field odd in $A$,
which accounts for the absence of even powers and for the reflected pair of
nonzero branches.

Put
\[
\mathfrak h_m=\sum_{j=1}^{m-2}\frac1{K_j}.
\]
The numerator $N_m=\ell^T[B(r,w_0)+\tfrac12B(r,w_2)]$ reduces exactly to
\[
N_m=R_m+C_m\mathfrak h_m.
\]
Introduce
\begin{align*}
\mathcal Q_m={}&589180301m^3-3500015940m^2+6930529579m-4574434500,\\
P_R(m)={}&68605040480814208768m^4-550882186169626030957m^3\\
&+1658612632937449670852m^2-2219226476204103501323m
+1113379274975809565700,\\
P_C(m)={}&652054120726848m^4-5151971981328467m^3
+15265080924982572m^2\\
&-20102347725659113m+9927281930180400.
\end{align*}
Then
\[
R_m=\frac{P_R(m)}{286118780220(8m-17)\mathcal Q_m},
\]
and
\[
C_m=-\frac{215P_C(m)}{11645046(8m-17)\mathcal Q_m}.
\]
The reduction follows by using the stable-mode recurrence in the interior
contraction.  Specifically,
\[
\sum_{i=3}^{m-1}K_{i-3}K_{i-2}
=\frac{(m-3)(24571m^2-97470m+96662)}3,
\]
while every reciprocal remainder telescopes to $\mathfrak h_m$.

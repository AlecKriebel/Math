\section*{Conservation and fixed-mass space}
Let
\[
c=(0,\underbrace{4,\ldots,4}_{X_2,\ldots,X_{m-1}},2,1)^T.
\]
Each reaction vector is orthogonal to $c$, hence $c^T\Gamma_m=0$.  Since $\operatorname{rank}\Gamma_m=m$ in $m+1$ species,
\[
\operatorname{im}\Gamma_m=c^\perp.
\]
On $(0,\pi)$ define the tangent spaces
\[
X_c=\{u\in H_N^2:\int_0^\pi c^Tu\,d\xi=0\},\qquad
Y_c=\{g\in L^2:\int_0^\pi c^Tg\,d\xi=0\}.
\]
Pointwise reaction conservation and Neumann boundary conditions imply the stationary operator maps $X_c$ to $Y_c$.  The critical function $r\cos\xi$ is admissible because its spatial mean vanishes.  The homogeneous correction is fixed by the gauge $c^Tw_0=0$, and the augmented matrix
\[
\begin{pmatrix}A_m&c\\c^T&0\end{pmatrix}
\]
is nonsingular because $\ker A_m$ is generated by $\rho=(2,-2,\ldots,-2,0,1)^T$ and $c^T\rho=17-8m\ne0$.

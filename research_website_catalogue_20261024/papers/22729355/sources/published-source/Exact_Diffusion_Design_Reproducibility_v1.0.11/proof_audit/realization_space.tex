\section*{Complete positive-equilibrium realization space}
At unit equilibrium, differentiation gives $A_m(a,b)$ with nonzero entries
\begin{align*}
&A_{11}=-(a+b),\quad A_{1,m-1}=-a,\quad A_{1m}=-b,
  \quad A_{1Z}=2b,\\
&A_{21}=-a,\quad A_{22}=-a,\quad A_{2m}=2a,\\
&A_{i,i-1}=a,\quad A_{ii}=-a\quad(3\leq i\leq m-1),\\
&A_{m1}=2a-b,\quad A_{m,m-1}=2a,\quad A_{mm}=-(4a+b),
  \quad A_{mZ}=2b,\\
&A_{Z1}=2b,\quad A_{Zm}=2b,\quad A_{ZZ}=-4b.
\end{align*}
For a positive equilibrium $x^*$ put $H=\operatorname{diag}(1/x_i^*)$.
The mass-action Jacobian formula gives
\[
J_m(a,b,H)=A_m(a,b)H.
\]
Conversely, given arbitrary $a,b>0$ and $H\succ0$, set $x_i^*=H_{ii}^{-1}$,
use flux $(a\mathbf1_m,b,b)$, and define
\[
k_r=\frac{v_r}{(x^*)^{y_r}}>0.
\]
Then $x^*$ is an equilibrium and its Jacobian is exactly $A_m(a,b)H$.
Thus no positive equilibrium realization is omitted.

\section*{Flux cone and complete realization space}
The balance equations along the long circuit force its $m$ fluxes to a common value $a$, while the reversible pair has equal forward and reverse flux $b$.  Exact row reduction gives
\[
\ker\Gamma_m=\operatorname{span}\{(\mathbf1_m,0,0),(0_m,1,1)\},
\qquad \operatorname{rank}\Gamma_m=m.
\]
Here rank follows immediately from the displayed exact two-dimensional kernel
and the $m+2$ columns of $\Gamma_m$.  As a direct minor check, retaining rows
$X_1,X_3,\ldots,X_m,Z$ and columns
$R_2,\ldots,R_{m-2},R_a,R_b,R_+$ gives determinant $4(-1)^m$.
Hence the strictly positive flux cone is exactly
\[
\{v>0:\Gamma_mv=0\}=\{(a\mathbf1_m,b,b):a,b>0\}.
\]
At unit equilibrium the factor $A_m(a,b)=\Gamma_m\operatorname{diag}(v)Y_m^T$ has nonzero entries
\begin{align*}
&A_{11}=-(a+b),\ A_{1,m-1}=-a,\ A_{1m}=-b,\ A_{1Z}=2b,\\
&A_{21}=-a,\ A_{22}=-a,\ A_{2m}=2a,\\
&A_{i,i-1}=a,\ A_{ii}=-a\quad(3\le i\le m-1),\\
&A_{m1}=2a-b,\ A_{m,m-1}=2a,\ A_{mm}=-(4a+b),\ A_{mZ}=2b,\\
&A_{Z1}=2b,\ A_{Zm}=2b,\ A_{ZZ}=-4b.
\end{align*}
At a general positive equilibrium $x^*$, with $H=\operatorname{diag}(1/x_i^*)$, the Jacobian is $J_m=A_m(a,b)H$.  Conversely, for arbitrary $a,b>0$ and $H\succ0$, choose $x_i^*=H_{ii}^{-1}$ and $k_r=v_r/(x^*)^{y_r}$.  This realizes the prescribed triple, so the parametrization is onto the complete positive-equilibrium Jacobian family.

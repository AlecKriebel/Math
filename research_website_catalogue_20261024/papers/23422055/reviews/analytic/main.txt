                                                 Abstract
           We construct smooth nonintegrable almost complex structures on the product of a genus-two
       curve and an elliptic curve with at least three independent real anti-invariant de Rham classes.
       The structures converge smoothly to the product complex structure and equal it outside one
       coordinate ball. The construction gives a negative answer to the unrestricted integrability
       conjecture of Draghici–Li–Zhang. The separate generic-vanishing statement is a prior theorem
       of Tan–Wang–Zhang–Zhu.

3   Comparison and limits
The two-plane realization and closed-pair framework are established ingredients [6, 7]; the third-
class identity (3) is the specific step used here. Three independent cohomology classes do not mean
three pointwise independent two-forms: on B their representatives span a real plane, with the
variable coeﬀicients in (3).
    Two distinctions explain why familiar integrability criteria do not obstruct this construction.
First, on the core of B,
                                       √                               k′
               Bε = k −1/2 du ∧ dt +       k dv ∧ dx,       dBε = −          dx ∧ du ∧ dt ̸= 0.
                                                                      2k 3/2
A closed real pair need not remain closed after variable normalization. In particular, Ψε is not a
closed complex symplectic form; the closed normalized-pair criterion [7, Theorem 6] is inapplicable.
Second, on this core J0 ∂u = ∂v whereas Jε ∂u = k −1/2 ∂v . A positive metric compatible with both
would give guu = gvv = k −1 gvv , impossible where k ̸= 1. Thus no common compatible metric with
this product J0 exists. No compatibility with a fixed symplectic form, spatial analyticity or exact
value of h−
          Jε is asserted.
    Earlier compact four-dimensional nonintegrable examples attain h−      J = 2 [3, 8].       Hind–
Tomassini’s final arXiv version [8, Proposition 4.2] distinguishes this compact case from its
noncompact and real-six-dimensional larger examples; localized deformation alone is consequently
not a new ingredient. Chen–Zhang’s accepted manuscripts, I, §6.3 [9], and II, §5.1 [10], give closely
related nonintegrable products T 2 × S. Their computed plurigenera are different from the real
closed anti-invariant cohomology used here, and II explicitly leaves the latter calculation for those
models as an interesting question. We do not claim that no further argument about those older
models could supply such classes.
    A bounded literature audit through October 16, 2026 found no verified earlier qualifying coun-
terexample. It does not establish firstness, novelty, present openness or exhaustive absence. The
supplementary audit records actual reading and access limits: some journal-final comparisons were
unavailable; Chen–Zhang accepted versions were read; only selected author-uploaded passages of
the 2012 DLZ survey were accessible; and Geiges’s 1996 paper remained a bibliographic lead. With-
drawn claims [11, 12] are not used as theorems. These historical limits do not enter the explicit
proof.

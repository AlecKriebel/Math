For two independent standard fractional Brownian motions with the same fixed Hurst parameter $H\in(1/4,1)$, we give a localized Gaussian-witness proof that every square-integrable estimator of the canonical iterated integral $X_T=\int_0^T B^1_s\,dB^2_s$, based on both complete uniform grids of $n$ observations on $[0,T]$, has root mean-square error at least $c_HT^{2H}n^{1/2-2H}$. The positive constant is explicit and depends only on $H$; the inequality holds for every $T>0$ and integer $n\ge1$, including nonlinear estimators. Smooth compactly supported covariance paths, a Fourier periodization upper Gram bound, and an exact deterministic area pairing provide the witness. The Brownian optimum and the smooth asymptotic rate are prior results. Fourier localization and Gaussian projection have established antecedents; historical theorem and method priority, full novelty, and worldwide current-open status remain unverified. The note uses canonical-area existence and sufficient $L^2$ convergence, without endorsing exact Euler-error coefficients of the pinned arXiv v1.

   Having seen the (conjectured) asymptotic behavior of ideals in Sym-invariant
chains, it is a natural question to ask whether Lefschetz properties can be under-
stood from this point of view. More precisely, let (In )n≥1 be a Sym-invariant chain
of artinian ideals. Then decide whether either In has SLP/WLP for all n ≫ 0
or In has not SLP/WLP for all n ≫ 0. This question is studied in a working
group as part of the workshop “Lefschetz Properties in Algebra, Geometry and
Combinatorics”.

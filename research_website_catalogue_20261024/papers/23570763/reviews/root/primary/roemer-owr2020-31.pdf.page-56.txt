Example 10 (A wild form with non minimal border rank). Consider the forms
f ∈ C[x, y, z, u, v]288 , given by f = g 16 with
                                f = xu17 + yu16 v + zv 17 .
We know that f has vanishing Hessian. Indeed, by Gordan-Noether criteria, since
the partial derivatives of g satisfy gx16 gz = gy17 , they are algebraically dependent,
therefore, hess f = 0. Moreover, the choice of g was in such a way that its polar
image has degree d. If the polar degree was lower, then the f could be not 16-
concise. We checked the 16-conciseness of f which implies that its border rank is
non minimal. In this case cr(f ) > a16 = 4845 and rk(f ) ≤ 4640, hence f is wild.

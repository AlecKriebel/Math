Definition 4.15. Given a prime divisor E, its log-discrepancy aE (C2 ) is the positive integer
                                                   1 + νE (dx ∧ dy).
Here νE (dx ∧ dy) denotes the order of vanishing along E of the pull-back of the holomorphic 2-form

Consider power reinforcement with exponent α>1 on a finite simple loopless graph: vertices fire at positive rates and select an incident edge proportionally to its current count to the power α. We prove that the infinitely reinforced edges almost surely form a forest for α>4/3, for arbitrary positive vertex rates and positive integer initial counts. The known triangle obstruction shows that the constant 4/3 cannot be decreased; its equality case is excluded. For equal rates we prove that every surviving component has diameter at most three when α≥17/4. The main deterministic step excludes an arbitrary weighted odd support cycle of length m when α>sec²(π/(2m)); the corresponding uniform cycle threshold and the even-cycle method are known. We use the published finite WARM convergence and support theorems, and credit the earlier least-weight and champion arguments. The forest result answers the forest-bound improvement requested in Oberwolfach Reports 12/2023; the suggested sharp whisker bound remains open. AI tools were extensively involved. This is an unrefereed preprint.

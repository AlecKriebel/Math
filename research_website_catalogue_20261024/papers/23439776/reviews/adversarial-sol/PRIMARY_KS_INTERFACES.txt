L219: 
L220: We first define the notion of a seed.
L221: ###### Definition 5.
L222: 
L223: Let $M$ be a finite index set and $M_{0}\subset M$. We associate a quiver $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ to a datum $\Sigma=(\Lambda,\left<,\right>_{\Sigma},\{e_{k}\}_{k\in M})$, where
L224: 
L225:   * (i)
L226: 
L227: $\Lambda$ is a lattice,
L228: 
L229:   * (ii)
L230: 
L231: $\left<,\right>_{\Sigma}$ is a skew-symmetric $\mathbb{Z}$-valued bilinear form on $\Lambda$,
L232: 
L233:   * (iii)
L234: 
L235: $\{{e_{k}}\}_{k\in M}$ is a basis of $\Lambda$.
L236: The set of vertices $\{v_{k}\}_{k\in M}$ of the quiver $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ is indexed by the set $M$. The set $\{v_{k}\}_{k\in M_{0}}$ is called the subset of frozen vertices and the set $\{v_{k}\}_{k\in M\setminus M_{0}}$ is called the subset of mutable vertices. Two vertices $v_{k}$ and $v_{\ell}$ of $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ are connected by $\left<e_{k},e_{\ell}\right>_{\Sigma}$ arrows in $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ with source $v_{k}$ and target $v_{\ell}$ if and only if $\left<e_{k},e_{\ell}\right>\geq 0$.
L237: The datum $\Sigma$ is called a $\emph{seed}$.
L238: Let $\Sigma=(\Lambda,\left<,\right>_{\Sigma},\{e_{k}\}_{k\in M})$ be a seed. For each $k\in M\setminus M_{0}$ we define the seed $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$, called the mutation of $\Sigma$ at $k$, by replacing the basis $\{e_{k}\}_{k\in M}$ by the new basis $\{e^{\prime}_{k}\}_{k\in M}$ defined as
L239: 
L240:  | $$e^{\prime}_{j}=\begin{cases}e_{j}+\max\{0,\left<e_{j},e_{k}\right>_{\Sigma}\}e_{k}&\text{ if }j\neq k\\
L241: -e_{k}&\text{ if }j=k.\end{cases}$$  |
L242: The quiver $\Gamma_{\mu_{k}(\Sigma)}=\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ has the same vertex set as $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$. The mutable vertices of $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ equal the mutable vertices of $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$. The arrow set of $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ equals the arrow set of $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ with the following changes:
L243: 
L244:   * (i)
L245: 
L246: All arrows of $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ with source or target $v_{k}$ gets replaced in $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ by the reversed arrow.
L247: 
L248:   * (ii)
L249: For every pair of arrows $(h_{1},h_{2})\in\Gamma_{\Sigma}\times\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ with
L250: 
L251:  | $$v_{k}=\text{target of $h_{1}$}=\text{source of $h_{2}$}$$  |
L252: 
L253: we add to $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ an arrow with source the source of $h_{1}$ and target the target of $h_{2}$.
L254: 
L255:   * (iii)
L256: 
L257: If a $2$-cycles was obtained during (i) and (ii), the arrows of this $2$-cycle get erased in $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$.
L258: 
L259:   * (iv)
L260: 
L261: Finally we erase all arrows between frozen vertices.
L262: The quiver $\mu_{k}(\Gamma_{\Sigma})$ is called the mutation of $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ at $k$.
L263: 
L264: To each seed $\Sigma=(\Lambda,\left<,\right>_{\Sigma},\{e_{k}\}_{k\in M})$ we assign a collection of $\mathcal{A}$-cluster variables $\{A_{k}(\Sigma)\}_{k\in M}$ and $\mathcal{X}$-cluster variables $\{X_{k}(\Sigma)\}_{k\in M}$ and tori
L265: 
L266:  | $$\mathcal{A}_{\Sigma}:=\text{Spec}\mathbb{C}[A^{\pm 1}_{k}(\Sigma)\mid k\in M],\qquad{{\mathcal{X}}}_{\Sigma}:=\text{Spec}\mathbb{C}[X^{\pm 1}_{k}(\Sigma)\mid k\in M],$$  |
L267: called the $\mathcal{A}$-cluster torus and the $\mathcal{X}$-cluster torus associated to $\Sigma$, respectively. We call $\{A_{k}(\Sigma)\}_{k\in M_{0}}$ and $\{X_{k}(\Sigma)\}_{k\in M_{0}}$ the frozen $\mathcal{A}-$ and $\mathcal{X}$-cluster variables respectively.
L268: Assume that the quiver $\Gamma_{\Sigma^{\prime}}$ of the seed $\Sigma^{\prime}$ is obtained from the exchange graph $\Gamma_{\Sigma}$ of the seed $\Sigma$ by mutation at the vertex $k$. We define birational transition maps (see [【45†FG09】, Equations (13) and (14)])
L269:  | $\displaystyle{\mu_{k}^{*}}A_{i}(\Sigma^{\prime})$  | $\displaystyle=\begin{cases}{A_{k}^{-1}(\Sigma)\left(\displaystyle\prod_{j\,:\,\left<e_{j},e_{k}\right>_{\Sigma}>0}A_{j}(\Sigma)^{\left<e_{j},e_{k}\right>_{\Sigma}}+\displaystyle\prod_{j\,:\,\left<e_{j},e_{k}\right>_{\Sigma}<0}A_{j}(\Sigma)^{-\left<e_{j},e_{k}\right>_{\Sigma}}\right)}&\text{if $i=k$,}\\
L270: A_{i}(\Sigma)&\text{else,}\end{cases}$  |

L313: 
L314: Let $G$ be a simply connected complex semisimple Lie group with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{g}$. Let $H$ be a maximal torus with Lie algebra $\mathfrak{h}$. L315: 
L316:  | $$L^{e,w_{0}}=N\cap B_{-}w_{0}B_{-}.$$  |

L318: ###### Definition 8.

L320: Two vertices $v_{k},v_{\ell}$, $k<\ell$ are connected by an edge in $\Gamma_{{\bf i}}$ if and only if $\{k^{+},\ell^{+}\}\cup[N]\neq\emptyset$ and one of the two conditions are satisfied
L321:   * (i)
L322: 
L323: $\ell=k^{+}$,
L324: 
L325:   * (ii)
L326:

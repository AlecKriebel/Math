## Adversarial boundary checks

The mixed real example in current Damele–Loi §3, proof of Theorem 1.4 item 10, has target sl3(R) and source so3(R)⊕(a⋉n), from KAN. Take H=diag(1,0,-1) in a. Its eigenvalues on E12,E13,E23 are 1,2,1, and its adjoint action on the other source factors has trace zero. Thus tr(ad_g H)=4. The source is nonunimodular; its two-dimensional a quotient also shows it is not perfect. Complexification preserves that nonzero trace. This is not a counterexample to either audited claim. This computation is an independent deduction from the printed example.

The old solvable/unimodular obstruction is genuine partial overlap: [Burde–Dekimpe](https://arxiv.org/abs/1108.5950), Theorem 3.3, requires both properties. A nonzero perfect nonsemisimple source is not solvable, so that theorem does not settle the native problem.

Decomposition arguments need particular care. The current author-hosted 2019 Rota–Baxter PDF prints an unrestricted Proposition 3.6 claiming that a vector-space sum of two semisimple subalgebras is semisimple. [Burde–Moens 2022](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2022.04.015), Example 2.2, explicitly provides sl_n⋉C^n as a perfect nonsemisimple sum of two conjugate Levi subalgebras. The sum is not direct. The later semisimple-source rigidity proof uses a *direct* decomposition. This old unrestricted assertion is not a safe substitute for the draft's central step. Moreover, images of a perfect source are perfect, not automatically semisimple. Replacing the missing conclusion by a claim that every perfect complement to the diagonal is semisimple merely restates the central difficulty; that route is blocked without a new theorem.

## Recommended paper framing

Subject to the separate mathematical acceptance, describe the result directly as a theorem for finite-dimensional complex unimodular sources with a transverse pair of homomorphisms into a semisimple target. State the perfect-source nonexistence assertion as its corollary, attributing that assertion to OWR p.2689 and the 2023/2024 perfect-Lie paper. Describe the determinant argument as establishing the global transitivity needed for the homology comparison. Cite recent work as related rigidity/integration literature with its hypotheses.

Suitable wording is: “We prove a unimodular-source obstruction for transverse pairs of homomorphisms into a complex semisimple Lie algebra. As a consequence, we obtain the perfect-source nonexistence statement formulated as a conjecture by Burde and Dekimpe and studied by Burde, Dekimpe and Monadjem.” Avoid “first proof”, “previously unresolved as of October 2026”, or an exhaustive-search claim. The remaining historical gap is unlocated or inaccessible literature and deductions not covered by this bounded audit, not a verified competing theorem.

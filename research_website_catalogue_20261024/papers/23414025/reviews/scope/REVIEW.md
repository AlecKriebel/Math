## Claim and added contribution

The main theorem concerns equal-dimensional finite-dimensional complex Lie algebras: a unimodular source g, a semisimple target n, and two homomorphisms g → n whose difference is a vector-space isomorphism. Its conclusion is that g is semisimple. The post-Lie consequence follows from the established inner-derivation / transverse-pair construction. Perfectness implies unimodularity, giving the perfect nonsemisimple-source obstruction for every semisimple target.

The proposed contribution is the holomorphic-group adaptation of a determinant relative-invariant mechanism followed by a top-degree homology obstruction. An open orbit alone does not prove transitivity; the invariant holomorphic determinant is the step that propagates local transversality globally. The actual discrete stabilizer produces a covering, and simple connectedness gives a manifold diffeomorphism. Contractibility of the solvable factor then conflicts with the target compact form's top homology unless the radical vanishes.

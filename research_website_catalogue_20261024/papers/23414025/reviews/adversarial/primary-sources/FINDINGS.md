# Direct primary-import check

Downloaded source receipts bind the exact PDFs. No historical source-audit opinions were used.

**Etingof, MIT Spring 2024 notes:** https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-755-lie-groups-and-lie-algebras-ii-spring-2024/mit18_755_s24_lec_full.pdf

Printed p.55, Theorems 9.12–9.13 and Corollary 9.14 explicitly cover real and complex Lie groups and provide the required simply connected integration/uniqueness. Printed p.31, Proposition 4.12 and Corollary 4.16 support the transitive quotient and stabilizer description. Theorem 16.19 supplies nondegeneracy of the semisimple Killing form.

Printed pp.231–233, Corollary 43.5, Theorem 43.7, Corollary 43.8 and the covering discussion support compact real forms and polar decomposition beyond the adjoint group. In particular the compact real form for a simply connected complex semisimple group is compact; its real dimension equals the complex Lie algebra dimension. Printed pp.264–265 give the Levi split and contractible simply connected solvable integration (Theorems 48.9, 49.1). No extra linearity/algebraicity hypothesis is needed for the target proof. Page 233 was also inspected visually.

**Hatcher, Algebraic Topology:** https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf

Proposition 1.32, printed p.61, relates the sheet number of a path-connected covering to a fundamental-group index. Theorem 3.26(a),(c), printed p.236, gives integral top homology for a closed connected orientable manifold and vanishing above its dimension. Compact connected Lie groups satisfy these manifold conditions. Page 236 was also inspected visually. These are ordinary singular-homology statements, as required.

**Burde–Dekimpe–Monadjem, author manuscript dated 8 March 2024:** https://homepage.univie.ac.at/dietrich.burde/papers/burde_78_perfect.pdf

Proposition 2.6, manuscript p.5, states the transverse-pair formulation for a semisimple target. The target paper also derives the required implication explicitly. This import is not a substitute for its main obstruction. The introduction distinguishes the earlier special cases from the general perfect-source conjecture. No broader priority conclusion was inferred.

The affine predecessor and other contextual literature are credited in the paper; they are not load-bearing completeness premises in the argument reviewed here. No exhaustive novelty assessment was attempted.

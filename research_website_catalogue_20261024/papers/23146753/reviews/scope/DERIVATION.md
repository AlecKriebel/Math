# Checkable scope derivation and boundary map

The early reconstruction is immutable. This fuller derivation adds the inspected primary interfaces and the supplement's facial argument after the independence checkpoint. Finite diagnostics below are checks of mechanisms, not replacements for universal proofs.

For a fixed finite simple G=(V,E), define F2 as the normalized nonnegative unary/original-edge product masses that satisfy MTP2, and A as positive attractive Ising masses with arbitrary fields. The exact target is F2=closure A in the finite probability simplex. Both sides include all allowed zero-support boundaries through the closure; neither includes arbitrary globally Markov MTP2 masses automatically.

1. If p factors finitely and S={p>0}, then S equals its unary/edge projection-cylinder intersection. Every actual projected cell occurs at a positive mass and its local factor must be positive; a state meeting all those projections consequently has positive product. MTP2 gives closure under meet/join. Remove pinned coordinates. On active coordinates the support has bottom0 and top1, so each active edge projection is full, an implication, or equality. These original-edge relations and pins describe S exactly.

2. Contract the directed implication strongly connected classes. Their variables form a poset, and valid states are upsets. All supported logarithms of local factors are finite. Extending them arbitrarily to missing cells and substituting pinned/tied variables expresses log p=c+sum H_K y_K+sum B_KL y_K y_L. Comparable products reduce to one unary variable; only incomparable pairs remain. Each remaining B is the sum of original cross-edge coefficients, not a claim about any given potential separately.

3. For incomparable K,L the union of all strict successors is an upset omitting both. Adding either/both is allowed. The four logarithmic values have mixed difference B_KL, so MTP2 forces B_KL≥0. Place that aggregate on one existing original edge between its classes, and each field on one original coordinate. Such an edge exists whenever B is nonzero because B arose only from original edges. L agrees with log p-c on S. Put D=sum pin violations+sum original directed implication violations x_i(1-x_j). Then D=0 exactly on S and is an integer≥1 elsewhere. Its positive normalized exponential converges to p: the supported sum tends to e^-c and every other cell vanishes. This also treats all-pin and no-active-coordinate supports. For empty V, both classes contain just mass1.

4. For a positive attractive law let E=−h·x−sum Jx_ix_j. Capacities are finite and nonnegative; zero arcs may be omitted. A maximum flow exists by compactness. 6. The literal source MI uses an edge-only product, unlike F2. On a graph with E nonempty, every isolated coordinate in such a probability is uniform and jointly independent. That property is closed. Apply F2 closedness on the nonisolated subgraph; absorb each unary into an incident existing edge and the isolated uniform scalar into any existing edge. Signed real factors may be replaced by absolute values because the entire product is nonnegative. 7. The supplement's facial lemma is also checkable without importing naturality. Let the edge-configuration indicator design be A and let nonempty sublattice S be the zero set of H=c·A, with H≥1 outside S and H≥0 everywhere. Every Boolean sublattice is determined by its pins and all-pair implications: for active i, meet all S-states having i=1 to form m_i; any assignment satisfying the implied order is the join of the selected m_i and the bottom state. Equal-coordinate classes yield a quotient poset.

8. Each equality class must be connected in G. Otherwise choose support states differing exactly on that class at a poset cover; split the class into two nonempty components D,F with no cross edge. Holding the exterior fixed, pairwise locality gives H10+H01=H00+H11=0. Nonnegativity forces both split states into S, contradicting their partial equality. Each quotient cover likewise needs a joining original edge: a common exterior supplies allowed patterns00,10,11 (or their order-dual), while01 is forbidden. With no joining edge, H01=H00+H11−H10=0, contradiction. Original-edge projections then enforce connected ties, cover implications and nonisolated pins. H cannot see an isolated coordinate, so it cannot pin or tie one. Therefore S is edge-A-feasible. This handles the all-support step missing from the literal natural-support/clique-design theorem.

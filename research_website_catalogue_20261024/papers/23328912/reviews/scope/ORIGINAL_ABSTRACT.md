# Complete original abstract

We construct a finite locally CAT(0) unit cube complex with one vertex, seven edges, eight squares, and two three-dimensional cubes, whose fundamental group is the mapping torus of a→ a, b→ ab, c→ bcb. Its universal cover gives a proper cocompact cubical action of the exact group in AIM Question 5.2. All attaching maps, signed vertex links, and reversible presentation changes are given explicitly. We also explain a qualitative existence deduction from earlier WPD, Morse, and cubical completion theorems. The contribution presented here is the explicit small finite model; no historical firstness or minimality claim is made.

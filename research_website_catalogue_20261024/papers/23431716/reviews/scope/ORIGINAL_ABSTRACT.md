# Original published abstract

We give a self-contained proof that ordinary conservative chip-firing halting is NP-complete on finite loopless simple strongly connected digraphs. Hardness holds with polynomial numerical total initial chips and a specified vertex whose deletion leaves an acyclic graph of depth at most one. A separate construction forbids antiparallel arcs. The proof combines the primed-toppler mechanism of Holroyd–Levine–Winkler with the classical prime/Boolean/clause encoding of Stockmeyer–Meyer, also reconstructed by Gramlich. We prove the physical conservative realization, its legal checkpoint dynamics and the arbitrary-schedule implication in full; NP membership is credited to Farrell–Levine and reproved with an explicit certificate bound. This is an attributed proof of the historical simple-digraph question. Exact full-result and realization priority and preexisting worldwide openness remain unknown after a bounded audit; no firstness is claimed. AI tools were used extensively. The paper is unrefereed and has not received external human peer review.

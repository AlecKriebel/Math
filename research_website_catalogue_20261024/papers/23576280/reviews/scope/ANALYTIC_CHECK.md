# Independent reconstruction of the full decision theorem

The actual six-page PDF, all 390 lines of controlling TeX and six unaltered page renders were read. The first substantive reconstruction was sealed at 08:14:59 UTC before external primary bodies or archived/current scientific opinions. This is its expanded derivation, with subsequent primary-interface authentication distinguished from original deductions.

**Literal language.** Inputs explicitly encode a finite undirected loopless graph G (parallel edges permitted) with at least three vertices, remaining connected after deletion of any vertex, and a signed integer t in binary. Accept precisely when β(G)≥t. An open ear decomposition partitions all edges into an initial connected 2-regular circuit and subsequent paths with two distinct old endpoints and new internal vertices; one-edge ears are allowed. Odd means odd edge length. β is the largest initial consecutive odd prefix, counting the initial circuit and one-edge ears; any first even ear ends the prefix. No odd starting circuit means β=0. Hardness outputs simple graphs with a specified universal z and connected bipartite G−z. This is an input-dependent threshold theorem, not fixed-threshold hardness, total-odd-ear hardness, closed-ear hardness or P≠NP.

**Classical interface and the rank objective.** The supplied established Lovász theorem says that a 2-vertex-connected graph is factor-critical exactly when it has an all-odd open ear decomposition. The exact form is authenticated in the actual Benchetrit–Sebő v1 Theorem 3.2 together with its explicit open qualifier in Section 1.1; the original 1972 proof was not recovered or newly verified. An all-odd prefix has a 2-connected factor-critical union. Its initial circuit has rank |E|−|V|+1=1, and each further ear adds one rank, since a path of ℓ edges adds ℓ−1 vertices. Conversely an all-odd decomposition of a suitable H extends to G: each outside component has at least two distinct neighbors in the current union, since a sole neighbor would cut G. A path through it between two attachment vertices is a valid open ear with new interior vertices. Repeat, then add unused edges singly. Thus β is the maximum rank of a 2-connected factor-critical subgraph, or zero for an empty family. Induced completion preserves connectivity and every deletion matching, and only increases rank. These extension and completion arguments are supplied directly; they are also consistent with Benchetrit–Sebő Proposition 1.13 and Section 3.1.

For connected bipartite B with at least two vertices, every eligible factor-critical subgraph of its cone contains z: a connected bipartite graph of odd order cannot remain perfectly matchable after deletion from its smaller nonempty side. On induced vertices S∪{z}, deleting z forces B[S] to be connected and perfectly matchable. Conversely, given its perfect matching M, delete z and use M; after deleting u∈S, match its former mate to z and keep the other M edges. The cone is 2-connected. Its rank equals |E(B[S])|. Hence the relevant objective is **all induced edges on a connected perfectly matchable selection**, not the number or weight of matching edges. Nonempty feasible S has at least two vertices.

**Reduction and size for arbitrary inputs.** Explicit Set Cover supplies U={1,…,m}, n listed sets and binary budget s. Negative s gives NO even for empty U; for s≥0 empty U gives YES. Nonempty U with no sets, an uncovered element, or s=0 gives NO. Covered U with s≥n gives YES. Each trivial branch maps to the same triangle, with threshold 1 for YES and 2 for NO; the triangle has β=1 and is a cone over connected bipartite K₂. All outputs obey the promised graph class. The remaining m,n≥1,1≤s≤n instance may still contain empty or repeated sets.

Put T=Σ(1+|Aⱼ|), k=T+n+3, M=(m+1)k² and F=M−ks. Then k>T, k>s, k²>ks+T and F>0, since k−s≥T+3. Make m+1 disjoint Kₖ,ₖ blocks Qᵢ, each with distinguished right qᵢ. A left connector cⱼ meets q₀ and qᵢ for i∈Aⱼ; there are no other interblock edges. The base B is connected and bipartite, because all connectors meet the root and coverage attaches each element block. It has 2k(m+1)+n vertices and M+T edges. Output (z*B,F). For explicit input length L, after the coverage check m,n,T=O(L), so k=O(L), the graph has O(L²) vertices and O(L³) edges, and F has polynomial binary length. No enormous binary-encoded block is expanded. The construction does not solve Set Cover; it uses only the listed instance.

**Forward direction.** From any cover within s, remove redundant sets to get an inclusion-minimal J with r≤s. Each j has a private element p(j), else j was redundant; these elements are distinct. Choose all block vertices and these connectors, but omit one left vertex from each private element block. Match cⱼ to q_p(j). Within that block the remaining unmatched vertices form Kₖ₋₁,ₖ₋₁; all other blocks match internally. All selected vertices are covered by disjoint edges. Because k≥2, each retained block remains connected; all selected connectors meet the root, and coverage connects each element block. The induced-edge count is exactly M−kr+Σ_{j∈J}(1+|Aⱼ|)≥F. Finding a minimal subcover given a cover is polynomial; neither existence nor the reduction assumes access to an unknown optimal cover.

**Reverse direction without a block normal form.** Take any S with B[S] connected, perfectly matchable and at least F induced edges. If one block has no retained internal edge, its loss is k² and even all T connector edges yield at most M−k²+T<F. Thus every block, including root, retains an internal edge. For arbitrary omissions aᵢ left, bᵢ right (including qᵢ if omitted), its exact lost edge count is dᵢ=k(aᵢ+bᵢ)−aᵢbᵢ. If r connectors are selected, perfect matching necessarily balances the bipartition, giving Σ(aᵢ−bᵢ)=r. For every 0≤aᵢ,bᵢ≤k,

dᵢ−k(aᵢ−bᵢ)=bᵢ(2k−aᵢ)≥0.

Therefore Σdᵢ≥kr and M−ks≤|E(B[S])|≤M−kr+T. Integrality and k>T imply r≤s. Each element block and the distinct root block contain an edge. Connectivity gives a path between them, which must leave the element block through a selected connector whose set contains that element. Thus the selected sets cover U within budget. No whole-block assumption, retained-q assumption, equality-to-matching-size assumption or balanced-implies-matchable substitution occurs.

**NP membership.** For t>0 provide H⊆G and a perfect matching of H−v for each v∈V(H). Graph membership, at least three vertices, H/H−v connectivity, exact matching validity and full coverage, and rank≥t are polynomially checkable. There are O(n²) matching edges with polynomially many identity bits; explicit parallel-edge encoding is harmless. The rank characterization gives soundness and completeness. Valid inputs with t≤0 are immediate YES, invalid graphs are rejected polynomially, and arbitrarily large positive t simply cannot satisfy the rank bound. No full optimization oracle is used by the verifier.

**Boundary attacks and literature interface.** A bipartite G has no odd initial circuit and β=0; the empty factor-critical family agrees. Two-edge parallel circuits are even, while every hardness output is simple. Empty/repeated sets and all preprocessing branches are covered. Missing distinguished vertices and partial blocks are included in the reverse inequality rather than assumed absent; connectivity can force their retention only after the high-edge bound. Source finite tests compare the complete-ear objective with cone and factor-critical enumerations and deliberately change first-circuit, one-edge and first-even conventions. They supplement, not prove, the universal reduction.

Sharifov optimizes all induced edge weights without connectivity; the paper's three-K₃,₃ construction separates its 27-edge disconnected optimum from the connected 25 optimum. With both connectors, matching balance loses at least six internal edges and adds at most four; omitting an entire block gives at most 22. The 25-edge witness is explicit. Gomes et al. optimize the sum of **matching** edge weights, not all induced edges. Havet–Nisse allow closed cycle attachments and study whole-decomposition length constraints. Bai–Bérczi–Nalam study joins and total-even-lobe parameters. These statements were checked at exact actual-primary interfaces; none is substituted for the present reduction.

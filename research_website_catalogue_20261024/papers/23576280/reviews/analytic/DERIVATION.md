1. A nonempty all-odd prefix has an odd circuit of at least3 vertices initially (loops are excluded, so an odd circuit cannot be a two-edge parallel circuit). Adding open paths with distinct endpoints preserves2-vertex-connectivity. The classical structural theorem equates2-connected factor-critical graphs with all-odd open ear decompositions; its EXACT external statement/conventions remain to be checked. Each ear contributes one to cycle rank |E|−|V|+1: an added length-l path brings l edges and l−1 new vertices, while the initial circuit has equal vertex/edge counts. Thus any odd prefix supplies an eligible factor-critical H of that rank. Conversely, an eligible H's odd decomposition extends to all of G: every component outside the current union has at least two distinct old neighbors, or one neighbor would be a cut vertex of G. Join two such neighbors through that connected component by a simple path, adding an open ear, and iterate until spanning. Add all remaining edges separately as one-edge ears. Induced closure on a fixed vertex set preserves2-connectivity and existing deletion matchings and increases rank. Hence beta=max rank of eligible induced H, or0 if none. This proof covers arbitrary missing/partial edges and parallel edges.

2. For connected bipartite B with an edge, the cone is2-connected. A factor-critical graph has odd order and a2-connected bipartite graph cannot be factor-critical: both bipartition classes are nonempty and deleting a vertex of the smaller class leaves unequal sizes. Hence an eligible induced H in the cone must contain z and equal z*B[S]. Deleting z imposes connectivity and a perfect matching on B[S]. Conversely, a connected nonempty B[S] with perfect matching M has at least2 vertices; its cone is2-connected. Delete z and use M. Delete u∈S, remove its matching edge uv and match the now-unmatched v to z. Thus the cone is factor-critical. Its rank is exactly the number of ALL induced base edges, |E(B[S])|. It is not the weight/cardinality of matching edges alone.

Then k>T, k>s, k−s≥T+3, k²>ks+T, F>0. For binary-encoded labels, the successful union test ensures m is at most the number of listed incidences, even if an initial universe size were announced in binary. Each selected set has a private element, and those elements are distinct: otherwise it would not be private. Select every block vertex and the selected connectors, but omit one LEFT block vertex in each private element block. Match its connector to that block's distinguished RIGHT vertex.

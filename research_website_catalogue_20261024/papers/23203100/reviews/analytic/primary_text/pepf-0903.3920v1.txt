                                                              We consider using a secret key and a noisy quantum channel to generate noiseless public commu-
                                                            nication and noiseless private communication. The optimal protocol for this setting is the publicly-
                                                            enhanced private father protocol. This protocol exploits random coding techniques and “piggy-
                                                            backing” of public information along with secret-key-assisted private codes. The publicly-enhanced

                                                            private father protocol is a generalization of the secret-key-assisted protocol of Hsieh, Luo, and Brun
                                                            and a generelization of a protocol for simultaneous communication of public and private information
                                                            suggested by Devetak and Shor.

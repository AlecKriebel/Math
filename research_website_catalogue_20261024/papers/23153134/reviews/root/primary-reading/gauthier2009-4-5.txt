    1. the output injection method (the equivalent for observability of feedback

iﬀ:
    • 1. the accessibility set A(x0 ) of x0 ∈ X , i.e. the set of points that can
       be reached from x0 by some trajectory of Σ, in positive time, has open
       interior in X, whatever x0 ∈ X.
    • 2. The orbit O(x0 ) of x0 ∈ X , i.e. the set of points that can be joined to
       x0 by some continuous curve which is a concatenation of trajectories of Σ
       in positive or negative time, is equal to X, whatever x0 ∈ X.
    Moreover in 1, 2 above, it is enough to restrict to piecewise constant control
functions. Also, if Σ is symmetric, O(x0 ) = A(x0 ) ∀x0 ∈ X.

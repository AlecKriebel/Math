             An open problem about palindromic polynomials
                           Rodolphe Sepulchre
                    (joint work with Alexandre Mauroy)

The following is conjectured: Let N ≥ 2 an even integer. The polynomial
                   S(z) = z N + sN −1 z N −1 + · · · + s2 z 2 + s1 z + 1
possesses N distinct roots on the unit circle if the coefficients s1 = sN −1 , s2 =
sN −2 , . . . , sN/2 satisfy the ordering
                                                         N
                           1 > si > si+1 > 0, i = 1, . . . ,
                                                          2
   A positive answer to the conjecture would provide a generalization of the sta-
bility result proven in [1]. The linear function F (x) = S + γx in that paper (Leaky
                                                                            2
integrate-and-fire model) could be replaced by the exponential F (x) = ex , which
is a good approximation of the quadratic integrate-and-fire model.



   Note: At the time of publishing this report, it seems that the conjecture has
just been solved by Julien Hendrickx and Alexander Megretski (MIT).

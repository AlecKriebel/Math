## Exact claim and contribution

Distance sums D(u)D(v) over unordered edited pairs and permits additions and deletions; uniform distance is |ΔE|/n². The success convention is 2/3.

The classical result is an all-input-size, size-oblivious one-sided canonical tester with O(ε⁻⁶ log(1/ε)) samples and O(ε⁻¹² log²(1/ε)) adjacency calls. It may exhaustively decide sample membership: no polynomial runtime follows.

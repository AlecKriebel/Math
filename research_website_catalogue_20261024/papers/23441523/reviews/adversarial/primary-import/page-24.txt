To show that the result is optimal, we apply Yao’s principle [31]. We first prove the case of K
being an oriented tree. Applying Yao’s principle to our setting, we first have to define for every n,
two distributions of digraphs D,, D2, where all the digraphs in D, are e-far from being H-free, and
all the digraphs in D2 are H-free. In order to define the two distributions we use the digraph Gy

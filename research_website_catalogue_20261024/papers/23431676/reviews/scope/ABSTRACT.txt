Ordinary legal chip-firing reachability is coNP-complete under deterministic polynomial-time many-one reductions for finite loopless strongly connected directed multigraphs, with binary adjacency entries and nonnegative binary initial and target configurations. We give an explicit reduction from the complement of the halting problem of Farrell and Levine. A finite-state cutoff controls an exact simulation, a gate supplies a cleanup reserve, and a marker forces that gate to have fired in every target-reaching word. Together with the prior coNP-membership theorem of Hujter, Kiss and Tóthmérész, this answers the historical general-digraph hardness question in the stated model. We credit earlier period, recurrence and reserve-simulation arguments.

  Conjecture 1.5 consequence and the non-conflict with GHR Theorem 5.27.

  cubic converse basis argument explicit, and removed the unused Jones-index

  Frobenius residual table for GHR Equation (5.2) and the present active
  operator, while avoiding any claim of inequivalence as bare matrices.

    We consider a problem on the conditions of a compact Lie group G that the loop
    space of the p –completed classifying space be a p –compact group for a set of
    primes. In particular, we discuss the classifying spaces BG that are p –compact for
    all primes when the groups are certain subgroups of simple Lie groups. A survey of
    the p –compactness of BG for a single prime is included.

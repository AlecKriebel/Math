Abstract
Morpurgo’s volume-normalized weighted Yamabe heat trace comparison, stated for every dimension
at least three, is false in dimension three. We specify one smooth positive conformal weight on the
round three-sphere whose scaled heat trace exceeds 13/20 at a specified positive time, whereas the
round maximum is less than 321/500. The argument applies classical cylindrical conformal geometry
and Dirichlet min–max estimates. A finite cylinder length, explicit smooth caps, and exact rational
certificates give quantitative separation.

Long cylindrical conformal blowups are classical; Ammann and Jammes used Pinocchio metrics for

chosen fixed rescaled cap, ordinary Dirichlet trial modes, and a strict round maximum bound already give a
qualitative counterexample. We record a specified finite quantitative application to Morpurgo’s comparison.

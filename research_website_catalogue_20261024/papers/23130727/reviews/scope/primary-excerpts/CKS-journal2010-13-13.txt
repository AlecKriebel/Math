   Combining all results obtained in this section together, we have the extended Fine and Wilf theorem for an antimorphic
involution θ :
Corollary 32. Let u, v ∈ Σ ∗ be two words with |u| > |v|. If there exist two θ -powers α(u, θ (u)) ∈ u{u, θ (u)}∗ and
β(v, θ(v)) ∈ v{v, θ (v)}∗ having a common prefix of length 2|u| + |v| − gcd(|u|, |v|), then ρθ (u) = ρθ (v). Furthermore,
this bound is optimal.

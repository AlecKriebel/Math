                                               Product formulas for the 5-division
                                               points on the Tate normal form E5.




arXiv:1612.06268v1 [math.NT] 19 Dec 2016
                                                                           Patrick Morton
                                                                         December 17, 2016

                                                                                 Abstract
                                                     Explicit formulas are proved for the 5-torsion points on the Tate
                                                 normal form E5 of an elliptic curve having (X, Y ) = (0, 0) as a point
                                                 of order 5. These formulas express the coordinates of points in E5 [5] −
                                                 h(0, 0)i as products of linear fractional quantities in terms of 5-th roots
                                                 of unity and a parameter u, where the parameter b which defines     √ the
                                                 curve E5 is given as b = (ε5 u5 − ε−5 )/(u5 + 1) and ε = (−1 + 5)/2.

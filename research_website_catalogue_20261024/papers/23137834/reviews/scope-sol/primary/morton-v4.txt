                                                                                 Abstract
                                                    Explicit formulas are proved for the 5-torsion points on the Tate
                                                normal form E5 of an elliptic curve having (X, Y ) = (0, 0) as a point
                                                of order 5. These formulas express the coordinates of points in E5 [5] −
                                                h(0, 0)i as products of linear fractional quantities in terms of fifth roots
                                                of unity and a parameter u, where the parameter b which defines   √      the
                                                curve E5 is given as b = (ε5 u5 − ε−5 )/(u5 + 1) and ε = (−1 + 5)/2. If
                                                r(τ ) is the Rogers-Ramanujan continued fraction and b = r5 (τ ), then
                                                the coordinates of points of order 5 in E5 [5] − h(0, 0)i are shown to be
                                                products of linear fractional expressions in r(5τ ) with coefficients in
                                                Q(ζ5 ).

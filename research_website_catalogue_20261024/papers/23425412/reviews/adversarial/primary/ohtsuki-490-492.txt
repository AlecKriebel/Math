Problem 7.27 (J. Roberts) Explain the appearance of modular forms in the
Witten invariants.

Remark (J. Roberts) Lawrence and Zagier discovered in [243] that the per-
turbative series for the Poincaré homology sphere was close to a modular form.
Is this a random coincidence, or is there a more systematic explanation? Does
such a relation ever hold for a hyperbolic 3-manifold?

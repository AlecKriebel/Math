                            16. Concluding remarks
  In this section, we give some remarks and discussions.

16.1. The WRT invariants as limiting values of holomorphic functions.
As explained in the introduction, the invariant JM for an integral homology sphere
M unifies the WRT invariants τζ (M ) for all the roots of unity in an algebraic way.
It should be remarked that there is another analytic approach to “unify” the WRT
invariants by realizing the radial limiting values of a holomorphic function defined
on the unit disk {z ∈ C; |z| < 1}, studied in [44, 45, 46, 47, 24, 25, 26, 27].
   Unfortunately, the invariant JM ∈ Z[q]d does not immediately determine such a
holomorphic function, i.e., there is no natural homomorphism from the ring Z[q]   d
to the ring of holomorphic function on |q| < 1. However, some expressions for JM
may define well-defined holomorphic functions for on |q| < 1 as we explain below
for the Poincaré homology sphere Σ(2, 3, 5).

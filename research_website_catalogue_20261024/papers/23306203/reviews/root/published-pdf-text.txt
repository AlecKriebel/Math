                       An exact period criterion for focal-distance
                              products in elliptic billiards
                                                   Alec Kriebel∗

                                                11 October 2026


                                                       Abstract
              For a noncircular elliptic billiard with a strictly nested nondegenerate confocal elliptical
          caustic, we prove that the product of ordinary distances from all vertices to either ﬁxed focus is
          independent of phase if and only if the least period is 2 modulo 4. This proves the least-period
          interpretation of entry k114 in Reznik–Garcia–Koiller’s list of unproved invariants and classiﬁes
          every other primitive period and every repeated traversal. The proof identiﬁes the complete
          divisor of the focal-distance factor in the classical Jacobi parameter. An exact doubled-triangle
          example shows why a condition on return length alone is insuﬃcient. The parametrization,
          identities and divisor method are established tools. This AI-assisted research note is unrefereed.

5    Attribution, veriﬁcation and status
The theorem proves the least-period k114 observation and classiﬁes its broader return-period in-
terpretation, which is partly false. Stachel’s parametrization, classical Jacobi identities and pole-
removal method are prior machinery. Meromorphic Poncelet functions and pole-removal arguments
were used by Schwartz [6] and, for example, Akopyan–Schwartz–Tabachnikov [4] and Roitman–
Garcia–Reznik [5]. The target-speciﬁc step is the focal divisor and its coset arithmetic; no new
elliptic-function method is claimed. Ferudun’s related unrefereed note [7] treats the diﬀerent outer-
tangent-polygon product k115 .
    The accompanying priority note records the primary sources checked and search limits. No
explicit earlier proof of this exact target was located in the checked sources; the novelty of this
speciﬁc formulation and exclusive historical priority are not certiﬁed. The full text of a related
inertia-and-billiards paper by Dragović and Gajić [8] was unavailable; its uninspected billiard sec-
tions remain a disclosed bibliography coverage gap. Portable exact checks reproduce ﬁnite divisor
inventories and physical counterexample arithmetic. They support the written proof, and do not
replace its all-period analytic argument. No validated numerical certiﬁcation or proof-assistant
formalization is claimed.
    AI tools were used extensively in solving, drafting, comparing sources, reproducing computa-
tions and independent adversarial reviews. This preprint has not undergone conventional human
peer review or refereeing. The author is responsible for its claims.

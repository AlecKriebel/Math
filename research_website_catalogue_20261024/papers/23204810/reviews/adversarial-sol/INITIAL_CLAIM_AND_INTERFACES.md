## Conditional chain reconstructed by hand

Assume E(k;g), the elementary complete-intersection Hilbert series and graded
Gorenstein perfect pairings. Symmetric chains in the pure-power monomial box
give subspace matching dim W<=dim B_1 W when h_j<=h_(j+1), using leading
monomials. Apply E to AV to get dim J_j=dim V and dim J_(j+1)=dim A_1 V;
the ideal property gives dim V<=dim B_1 J_j<=dim J_(j+1)=dim A_1 V.
No single linear form with all these ranks is inferred.

For homogeneous I with least degree alpha<p, replacing I by its truncation
above alpha changes mu by dim A_1 I_alpha-dim I_alpha>=0. Iteration gives
mu(I)<=mu(I intersect m^p). For homogeneous I contained in m^p set
J=Ann(mI). Frobenius duality and mJ subset Ann(I) give mu(J)>=mu(I).
Since mI subset m^(p+1), J contains Ann(m^(p+1))=m^(c-p), which contains
m^p because c-p<=p for the last maximum. Truncation of J then gives
mu(I)<=mu(J)<=mu(m^p)=h_p. For arbitrary I, take lowest-degree initial
parts, not highest-degree parts. The finite intersection filtration yields
dim gr I=dim I and m(gr I) subset gr(mI), hence mu(I)<=mu(gr I)<=h_p.

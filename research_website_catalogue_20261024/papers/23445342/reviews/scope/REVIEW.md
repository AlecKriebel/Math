## Exact claim and public boundaries

The model is a fixed law of iid space-time right-step probabilities strictly between zero and one almost surely. The discrete-time walk starts at zero on Z and makes nearest-neighbor steps. Time layers are fresh even when a path revisits a location. Bias is permitted; uniform ellipticity and inverse-moment assumptions are absent.

For every deterministic real sequence M_N > 0 diverging to infinity, the endpoint laws are compared after partition into intervals of length M_N. For every fixed positive tolerance ε and power K, the environmental probability that the full coarse ℓ¹ discrepancy exceeds ε is bounded by C_{ε,K} N^(−K) eventually. The bound is uniform over each deterministic alignment. It is not simultaneous control of a partition selected from the observed environment. Full ℓ¹ distance is twice conventional total variation. Constants depend on the fixed law, and the eventual threshold may depend on the chosen scale sequence. M_N need not be monotone or have a prescribed growth rate.

No static or correlated environment theorem, law-uniform bound near the arrow limit, shrinking-tolerance rate, or microscopic pointwise Gaussian limit is asserted. An ordinary qualitative central/local limit does not by itself give the full environmental tail estimate. “Agree” in the original abstract is explained as a small-discrepancy asymptotic statement, not exact finite-time equality.

## Mechanism and strongest distinctive content

The paper supplies its analytic inputs rather than relegating them to an inaccessible earlier turn. The replica half-difference chain has neighbor rates a away from zero and δ at zero, yielding return and occupation estimates. Martingale exposure gives expectation and auxiliary-scale concentration. Same-parity coalescence smooths starting positions over a final segment. A deterministic collection of independent moving environment strips then permits bounded differences once their starting masses are small; a killed-path construction controls strip overlap.

The informative feature for the page is the quantifier over every diverging coarse scale, together with all fixed inverse-power environmental tails under ellipticity without a common bound. The auxiliary polynomial scale is not an additional hypothesis on the target scale. The familiar ingredients and final-layer idea are not claimed as new.

            A set of linear operators on a normed linear space is col-
       lectively compact if and only if the union of the images of
       the unit ball has compact closure. Bounded linear operators
       T and Tn, n = 1, 2,     , such that Tn-+T strongly and {Tn - T}
       is collectively compact are investigated. The theory somewhat
       resembles that for 11 Γ» - T \ \ -* 0. The spectrum of Tn is
       eventually contained in any neighborhood of the spectrum of
        T. If f(T) is defined by the operational calculus, then f(Tn)
       is eventually defined, f(Tn) -> f(T) strongly, and {f{Tn) - /(T)}
       is collectively compact. If f(Tn) and f(T) are spectral pro-
       jections, the corresponding spectral subspaces eventully have
       the same dimension. Other results compare eigenvalues and
       generalized eigenmanifolds of Tn and T.

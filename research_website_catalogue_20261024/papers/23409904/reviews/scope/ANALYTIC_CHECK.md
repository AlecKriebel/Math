# Whole-claim analytic reconstruction

2026-10-25 UTC. The initial reconstruction was sealed after reading the entire actual six-page PDF and TeX, before archived or sibling review bodies. The following records the completed check. Historical agreement is supplementary; it is not a proof premise.

Let M be any smooth Hausdorff second-countable manifold without boundary, of fixed finite dimension n>=1. Let g be smooth positive definite and nabla,nabla* a prescribed ordered mutually g-dual pair, both torsion-free. There is no connectedness, compactness, completeness, bounded geometry, or uniform injectivity-radius hypothesis.

1. Set L equal to Levi-Civita and K=nabla-L. Torsion-freeness makes K symmetric in its first two arguments. Duality identifies the other difference with the negative metric adjoint; its torsion-freeness supplies symmetry in the remaining arguments. Hence C=nabla g=-2g(K.,.) is a symmetric cubic, and nabla=L-Csharp/2, nabla*=L+Csharp/2. In coordinates Q_ijk=partial_k g_ij+Gamma_ijk=1/2(partial_i g_jk+partial_j g_ik+partial_k g_ij-C_ijk). The signs and the ordered connection convention are fixed by this identity.
2. The proper smooth Whitney input gives e:M->R^(2n+1). The actual original scan's countable and possibly disconnected conventions, Lemma19 and footnote32 were inspected. Define f using every monomial through degree3, including constant, in the e-coordinates. Its dimension is N=binomial(2n+4,3). Projection onto linear features recovers e, proving injectivity, immersion, embedding topology, and properness: the inverse image of a compact B is closed in compact e^-1(pi B). Thus S=f(M) is closed in R^N.
3. At each p, choose n ambient linear coordinate combinations with independent restricted differentials. Their restrictions y are local coordinates. Every scalar3jet at p is a polynomial through degree3 in y-y(p), hence an ambient polynomial in the fixed feature space. Therefore E_p:R^N->J^3_p(M,R) is onto for every p. The scalar jet space is a finite rank vector bundle, including value and lower derivatives; its coordinate changes are linear but mix derivative orders. Smooth bundle metrics exist by paracompactness. Eadjoint(EEadjoint)^-1 gives a smooth global right inverse. This is pointwise smooth inversion, not a bounded inverse assertion on a function space. No compactness or finite chart cover is used.
4. The local exponential inverse germ of L depends smoothly on its base point near the zero section, even for an incomplete metric. The germ with expression 1/2 g_p(v,v)-1/12 C_p(v,v,v) specifies a smooth global scalar-jet section J. It need not be holonomic: q_p is a different germ at each p, not one global scalar function with zero value everywhere. In normal coordinates its jet is (0,0,g,-C/2). Conversion to arbitrary coordinates adds L_ij^a g_ak+L_ik^a g_aj+L_jk^a g_ai, exactly half the sum of the three metric derivatives. This gives third ordinary derivative Q. Under x=x(y), the scalar chain rule has the three second-coordinate-change terms g_ij(X_ab^i X_c^j+X_ac^i X_b^j+X_bc^i X_a^j); no third-coordinate-change term remains because the scalar gradient vanishes. The manuscript's Q law is therefore correct and is not an erroneous tensor transformation.
5. Set a=RJ and phi=-a. Jet evaluation holds a(p) fixed in the germ variable, giving a f=0, a f_i=0, a f_ij=g_ij and a f_ijk=Q_ijk. These then become smooth basepoint identities, so differentiating includes derivatives of a. The results are f_i partial_j phi=g_ij and f_ij partial_k phi=Q_ijk-partial_k g_ij=Gamma_ijk. Also phi df=0 identically, eliminating period obstructions. The metric identity makes dphi injective, but does not prove phi is globally injective. The manuscript correctly says Lauritzen-type pair and does not need a global embedding of phi. Both prescribed connections will be realized by f itself.
6. The variable normal-addition tubular theorem applies to closed S; the actual author-hosted formulation and proof were read. Write z=r(z)+nu(z), with dr=P_T and dnu=P_N at S. No uniform radius, normal trivialization or orientation is used. The smooth function Psi0(z)=phi_r(z)(nu(z)) has value0 and differential phi along S, since phi annihilates TS. Differentiating this ambient covector equality along tangent directions gives Hess(Psi0)(f_i,.)=partial_i phi. Its tangent block A is the positive g, its normal-normal block is0 along each straight normal fiber, and its mixed block B is retained.
7. Regard A as a positive endomorphism of TS for its Euclidean induced inner product. The smooth invariant t=tr(A^-1)||B||HS^2 is finite at each point and may diverge at infinity. Set lambda=1+t. The added term lambda(r)|nu|^2 has zero first jet on S and Hessian 2lambda times the normal metric there; derivatives of lambda and higher derivatives of nu multiply vanishing nu terms. Thus the full Hessian block is [A B; B^T 2lambda I]. Since ||A^-1||op<=tr(A^-1), B^T A^-1 B<=tI. Completing the square gives a positive tangent square plus normal Schur complement >=(2+t)I. This proves positive definiteness at every point of S without a uniform lower bound. Define U to be the open positive-Hessian locus inside the tube. It contains all of S. The same single smooth Psi restricts to U, and f remains proper because compact subsets of U are compact in R^N. Psi need not have positive scalar values; the precise positivity conclusion is Hess(Psi)>0.
8. For G=Hess(Psi), standard D is flat and torsion-free. Its metric dual has coefficients G^{CE}partial_A G_BE, symmetric in A,B by third-derivative symmetry. The gradient coordinates eta_E=partial_E Psi have invertible Jacobian G and zero D*-covariant Hessian. Hence they provide local affine charts, proving dual flatness everywhere. No global injectivity of the gradient, convex U, or vanishing Levi-Civita curvature is inferred.
9. Along S, G f_k=partial_k phi. Therefore f*G=g and G(f_ij,f_k)=Gamma_ijk, so the orthogonal projection of D gives exactly nabla. Ambient duality restricted to tangent fields drops orthogonal normal terms; the other projection is the unique g-dual and therefore exactly the prescribed nabla*. This verifies both connections in their stated order, rather than only a metric, a cubic without sign, or an unordered pair.
10. Projecting an ambient torsion identity along any immersion shows its induced torsion vanishes. Applying this to both ambient connections proves necessity for the general dual-pair criterion; sufficiency is the construction above.

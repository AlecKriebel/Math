## Mathematical assessment

**Exact separation, Theorem 3.1 and appendices A–B.** The three displayed effects are positive rank one and sum to the identity. Their maximally entangled strategy gives the auxiliary value 16/25 and CHSH value 2√2. The discrimination dual certificate is exact. The PVM bound covers all six auxiliary support patterns, including the deterministic and zero-effect cases. The state can be made pure before Schmidt reduction. The two CHSH deficit bounds control both Schmidt imbalance and Bob's angular overlap. The robust auxiliary comparison is a polynomial nonnegativity identity on a larger box than the physically compatible triples; no omitted compatibility constraint is needed. Completing the square gives the stated global bound

`U = 20√2 + 3/5 + (4+3√2)/250`.

The attained simpler lower bound exceeds it by `3(2−√2)/250 > 0`. The strengthened attained value in appendix B is also reproduced, but neither lower bound is an exact global POVM optimum. The PVM upper bound likewise need not be sharp. Degenerate CHSH observables and local stochastic output maps are explicitly accounted for.

**One binary party, Theorem 4.2.** Binary qubit effects decompose into projective measurements including deterministic ones. The compressed steered-state cone is Lorentz in dimension three when Bob's Bloch axes are independent. A finite positive relation among its extreme rays reduces to support-minimal circuits. Pointedness and extremality exclude one-versus-many circuits; the dimension bound leaves two-versus-two. The unique planar rank-one lifts preserve their common operator sum, and purification converts both decompositions simultaneously into PVMs. The same classical variable chooses the state and every measurement, so this is a decomposition of the complete behavior. Repeated labels, collinear rays, scalar observables, and lower-dimensional cones are handled without deleting output probabilities. The construction does not preserve the original state and does not purport to show operator-level projective simulability.

**Residual reduction, Theorem 4.5.** Selecting an extreme point of the globally exposed maximizing face is legitimate because the finite-dimensional strategy hull is compact. Pure-state and extremal-measurement decompositions retain that behavior. Full-rank reduced states make all retained nonzero outcome marginals positive, which is essential in the common-span filtering contradiction. If both measurement spans shared a nonscalar operator, the filtering identity would give a nontrivial behavior decomposition. Dimension then forces one span to be commuting and hence a stochastic postprocessing of a binary PVM. After deterministic decomposition, the other measurement has at most three independent effects; extremality and the rank-square bound force the genuinely nonprojective case to be ternary rank one. The argument is valid separately for each declared finite output architecture, including reinserted zero labels.

The imported rank-square condition agrees with [D'Ariano, Lo Presti, and Perinotti, Theorem 2 and Corollary 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/quant-ph/0408115). The manuscript also supplies the support-perturbation proof it needs, rather than relying on an unexplained dimension assertion.

**Physical exactness, Theorem 6.1 and Proposition 6.2.** The 4×4 probability matrix is invertible because the pure-state steering map and both effect frames are invertible. The five incidence constraints have independent probability gradients; normalization is independently detected by radial variation. The dimension is therefore 14. Reconstructing Alice's frame from the Lorentz metric and the rank-one steered states from the probability columns gives a positive reduced state. Its canonical purification reconstructs Bob's effects. The trace-pairing factor 1/2, transposition, normalization, and full-rank assumptions are consistent. Positivity does not require strictly positive joint probabilities: a zero joint entry is still a trace of positive operators. Thus the regular-level-set tangent really corresponds to a two-sided physical curve locally, rather than a relaxation containing nonphysical directions.

**Multiplier positivity, Proposition 7.3.** The finite POVM dual exists and attains its value. Complementary slackness makes each rank-one effect's dual slack a nonnegative adjugate multiple. The normalization multiplier agrees with the POVM dual because their difference annihilates ranges spanning the qubit space. The positive factor `4|det Ξ|²` links those coefficients to the incidence multipliers. A zero multiplier would allow replacing that entire Bob input by a deterministic PVM without changing the score. With only one remaining nontrivial Bob input the complete behavior is local; hence it cannot support a strict advantage. This establishes strict positivity of all five multipliers at the hypothetical separating global maximum.

**Second variation and rank trichotomy, Sections 8–9.** I independently checked the sign: stationarity is `∇L−α∇n = −Σλ∇F`; along a feasible normalized curve this gives `L'' = +Σλ Hess(F)`, not the opposite sign. The square completion after `δP = PW+HQP` yields `2 Tr(SWΛWᵀ)`. The Lorentz signature of S and positivity of Λ give inertia (4,12). Compatibility has codimension `dim ker Dᵀ`, so rank D≥2 leaves at least 13 dimensions and necessarily a positive direction. Adding a multiple of the identity enforces normalization while preserving the quadratic value because the null-incidence and compatibility identities kill both extra terms.

For rank one, the quadratic map's generic cubic inverse is exact. I checked all four exceptional-divisor cases in appendix G, including the cross branches, base roots, zero target coordinates, and the strict inequalities excluding an identically vanishing residual factor. Thus distinct nonbase null rays cannot have the same projective image. At most one metric-differential row could be nonzero in rank one, preventing a strictly positive multiplier in the kernel. In rank zero, future orientation fixes positive ray scalings; the unique two-versus-three circuit forces a partition-preserving permutation and common scale. Normalization then reduces the table to `P=g`. The bounded transportation construction in appendix H supplies a single deterministic mixture reproducing all four input blocks. No rank case is omitted.

These pieces establish the claimed convexified equality and, together with the exact 3×2 witness and locality when one party has a single input, the minimum-setting conclusion. The reasoning does not establish equality of raw nonconvex strategy images, same-state simulation, higher-dimensional analogues, or a private-randomness entropy statement; the manuscript explicitly distinguishes these.

Independent controls import none of the paper's implementation:

- `independent_controls.py` separately derives the simple strategy's dual certificate, exact gap, generic projective inverse, and robust comparison polynomial. All symbolic identities pass.
- 150 random genuine residual strategies satisfy the five incidence equations, six-constraint Jacobian rank, physical reconstruction, and Hessian square completion. Maximum Born reconstruction error was `4.45e-16`; maximum relative Hessian discrepancy was `3.01e-12`.
- The same 150 independently generated strict metrics admit the rank-zero bounded-flow decomposition, with maximum complete-table reconstruction error `2.78e-16`.
- 1,800 PVM measurement samples cover all six auxiliary support patterns, generic directions, directions near the locking point, and degenerate CHSH observables. For every sample the state was optimized exactly numerically by the largest Bell-operator eigenvalue. The largest sampled score was `28.8804416177`, below analytic `U = 28.9172418102`. This is a falsification search, not a global optimization certificate.
- `second_variation_control.py` finds a physical stationary rank-four fixture with all five multipliers strictly positive, constructs a compatible direction with `q = 0.6793938370`, solves the incidence equations on both sides, and reconstructs actual positive qubit strategies. At steps ±0.0002 both Bell gains are positive, approximately `2.718e-8`, matching `q t²` within 0.019%. Constraint residuals are below `2.6e-16`; reconstructed Born probabilities agree within `9.1e-17`. This directly tests that the decisive uphill direction is physical and has the manuscript's Hessian sign. It is one numerical fixture, not a substitute for the universal tangent argument.

## Literature and remaining limits

The older existence result is correctly distinct from the claimed setting lower bound: [Vértesi and Bene 2010](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1007.2578) already supplied a fixed-qubit POVM advantage. The cited nearby [Cerf–Ollivier 2026 work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.26875) describes a perturbative method near deterministic points; [Panahi et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.20519) describes a Hilbert-space dimension reduction. These source checks support the stated distinctions but are not a systematic novelty search. The cited [Schwarz et al. corrigendum](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05253) exists; retaining zero effects and every PVM support pattern is essential.

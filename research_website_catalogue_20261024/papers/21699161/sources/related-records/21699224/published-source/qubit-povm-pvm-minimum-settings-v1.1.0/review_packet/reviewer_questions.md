# Focused questions for expert reviewers

The author especially welcomes scrutiny of the following points.

1. Does the shared-randomness-convexified formulation match the intended
   fixed-dimensional comparison?
2. Does the one-binary-party proof construct one simultaneous decomposition
   of the complete behavior, including both inputs and all declared outputs?
3. Does the extreme-point/common-span reduction exhaust four-outcome extremal
   qubit POVMs and all original finite output counts?
4. Does the Lorentz reconstruction prove that every incidence tangent
   integrates to a genuine two-sided physical curve?
5. Are the pullback convention and signs identifying determinant multipliers
   with positive POVM dual slacks correct for both local inputs?
6. Is the projective-fiber analysis complete on all four exceptional divisors
   and their zero-coordinate intersections?
7. Does the bounded transportation argument always satisfy row, column, and
   capacity constraints, including boundary capacities?
8. Does the rank-zero deterministic mixture reproduce all four setting
   blocks simultaneously?
9. Does the `3 x 2` PVM bound cover every state, degeneracy, support pattern,
   relabeling, and postprocessing?
10. Is the literature positioning narrow enough, especially relative to the
    earlier `I_CH3` family?

Therefore Exp(D)=F(U) exactly, with U the complement of the kernels of A_i^T and F(u)=sum A_i A_i^T u/||A_i^T u||. Each kernel has codimension at least two. A two-segment detour proves U is nonempty, open and path connected. Positive real radicands make F real analytic there, without a complex square-root branch or independence of radicals.

The ideal of complex polynomials vanishing on F(U) is prime by the real-analytic identity theorem: if P Q vanishes and P does not, Q composed with F vanishes on a nonempty open set and hence everywhere on connected U. The manuscript proves the analytic uniqueness step explicitly and then uses the standard prime-ideal irreducibility criterion.

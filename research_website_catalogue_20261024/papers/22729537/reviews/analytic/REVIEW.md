## Exact mathematical scope

The main result assumes a finite labelled temporal population DAG; contemporaneous labelled sampled lineages with a known taxon map; every terminal taxon sampled; independent loci with known nonrecombining locus boundaries; independent lineage routing at binary reticulations; positive finite coalescence rates and finite epoch durations; inheritance probabilities strictly between zero and one; and one common irreducible stationary reversible finite-state substitution generator on a labelled alphabet. At least two sampled lineages and two alphabet states are required. Some substitution conductances may vanish provided their support remains connected.

These are model assumptions, not facts about real genomes established by the paper. The observed mathematical input is the complete indexed collection of population alignment laws for every length L, not estimated gene trees, one arbitrarily long observed locus, pooled sites, or a finite alignment sample. “Determines” is exact population identifiability unless a later statistical theorem explicitly supplies an estimator and sampling regime.

The normalization is r(Q)=−tr(Q), Q0=Q/r(Q), scaled times r(Q)t and scaled coalescence rates λ/r(Q). The residual physical-unit action is (τ,λ,Q)↦(cτ,λ/c,Q/c), after a particular normalized realization is fixed. Non-scale equivalences between different graph or demographic realizations remain possible and are explicitly retained.

## Reconstructed load-bearing arguments

**The all-length bridge.** A length-L word is a mixed moment of the random conditional site-pattern tensor V. Lower moments follow by summing trailing states. Coordinate polynomials separate points of the compact probability simplex, so equality of all such moments fixes the probability measure ν. The full moment map is a continuous injection from a compact space into a Hausdorff product space, giving its continuous inverse on its image. This argument does not require a compact genealogy space.

The one-leaf marginals of every conditional tensor give the same stationary π. For any sampled pair, its conditional joint matrix is J=e^(Qᵀu)Dπe^(Qw), so det(Dπ⁻¹J)=exp(tr(Q)(u+w)). Thus δ=−log det(Dπ⁻¹J) is the trace-normalized pair distance. Under reversibility Dπ⁻¹J=exp(Q0δ). Symmetric similarity makes its eigenvalues strictly positive and returns Q0=Log(Dπ⁻¹J)/δ whenever δ>0. This direct one-matrix route independently verifies the paper's tail/two-matrix argument, including repeated eigenvalues. The matrices are conditional matrices recovered via ν, never the population average of J.

Contemporaneous genealogies can also be reconstructed directly from their pair coalescence heights δij/2: at each height the relation δij≤2t gives the nested clades. This supplies an independent ultrametric route to the rooted tree conclusion. Zero edges are contracted, and the zero-determinant limit corresponding to infinite distances has no probability mass. Finite rates and a finite event list ensure that the ancestral pair-survival tail is positive. The Borel constructions and reverse pruning operation therefore establish the normalized triple equivalence on the principal image.

**Canonical response.** At a fixed epoch and partition, there are finitely many population assignments. The no-merger operator is diagonal with the negative sum of pair hazards; each specified merger carries one λ factor and routing is a finite stochastic boundary map. Every complete history density is a finite chronological matrix product. Prefix and suffix evaluations factor the timed Hankel kernel through this finite raw space.

The potentially delicate step is that a prefix with a late merger need not exist at an earlier cut. The manuscript handles spans, not individual prefixes: after factoring out the common terminal no-mark flow, the remaining row is analytic in the free merger times. A linear functional vanishing on the earlier nonempty open simplex vanishes on the whole connected simplex. This proves span saturation. The matching analytic argument for suffixes proves backward propagation of nullity. Together they make interior residual transport invertible. Reachable quotient states identify literally with future functions, so the minimal intertwiner is forced on the spanning prefix family.

For merger-coordinate derivatives the operator is X↦A⁻X−XA⁺ on the raw rectangular matrix space. Cayley–Hamilton bounds each coordinate recurrence, and successive recurrence reductions determine every mixed Taylor coefficient from a finite box. This justifies both the breakpoint test and proposed-model equality test. The order is correctly based on raw dimensions, not the smaller rank at a fixed time. Invisible boundaries glue complete scalar history kernels and therefore residual transports; this does not imply a single autonomous stochastic population generator on the glued interval. The intrinsic positive cone is pointed and generating because suffix evaluations separate points and actual positive prefix functions span. It need not be simplicial or supply a biological realization.

**Finite determination.** For each fixed sequence word, the physical sequence map has an analytic extension near each strict chart point. Finite-time integrals use compact fixed simplices. Root integration uses the stable transient subspace, with positive coalescent absorption; it does not invert the terminal one-block zero mode. Reversible conductance coordinates permit signed ambient extensions at zero-conductance faces without making those extensions physical models. Repeated hazards and substitution spectra do not introduce a rate-difference singularity.

For a compact semianalytic chart pair C, Siu's real theorem applies to analytic germs along all of C. An analytic subvariety cut out near C intersects C in a compact semianalytic set with finitely many components. The ideal generated by the sequence-coordinate differences is consequently finitely generated. Each abstract ideal generator uses only finitely many original coordinate differences, so a finite subset of actual sequence words suffices. Equality at their maximum length implies all their lower-order equalities by marginalization. This is an existence theorem depending on the entire compact finite-presentation class, not a numerical formula for an effective length and not a theorem on an unrestricted noncompact domain.

**Epoch obstruction.** For two-taxon normalized JC, each specified pair-pattern probability is affine in X=e^(−βT), and X=4(d−o). Consequently the length-L law is equivalent to moments through degree L. For the step-hazard perturbation, differentiating the survival integral gives ∂Lj(s)=s exp(−(s+λ0)aj)/(s+λ0)². At s=kβ, removal of nonzero row/column factors leaves xj^k with distinct positive xj=e^(−βaj). Its determinant is a nonzero Vandermonde product. With L+1 step parameters, the first L moments have a one-dimensional analytic level curve, while the next moment varies along it. Each tangent coordinate is nonzero because every maximal minor of the first L rows is invertible. Hence two nearby nonzero points have genuinely nonzero demographic changes and remain strictly positive. This yields exact equality, not a numerical near-collision, and keeps taxa, reticulation count, alphabet and sampling fixed while epoch count grows.

## Graph claims, boundary cases and unsuccessful falsification attempts

The tree inverse works from pair support thresholds and logarithmic hazards, not the modal gene-tree topology. For singleton terminal samples, no pair visits that pendant population and its entire demographic schedule is invisible. With two samples, the pair hazard exposes it. Equal adjacent rates create no genuine rate breakpoint.

The sample-unary funnel obstruction is exact: there is at most one lineage, no coalescence, the same elapsed temporal interval on every route, and routing weights sum to one. Stationarity/shared substitution makes the unchanged metric genealogy sufficient for unchanged sequence laws. It is valid as a contextual replacement and is not presented as the first historical example of metric-genealogy nonidentifiability.

The intrinsic port table records every partition at one law-defined cut, including histories whose mergers all occur on one side. A raw reticulation cut cannot replace that common cut: the supplied early/late examples have different reachable partition ranks at those raw cuts. The published correction using all marked strips and all partitions is mathematically appropriate. The general completeness of this representation is behavioral; it alone is not a graph recovery theorem.

For the child decoder, the positive-rate moment matrix factors as H=V diag(wλ²)Vᵀ and v=V(wλ), so vᵀH⁻¹v=Σw. Its next determinant/rank certificate is exact for a finite positive mixture even at repeated rates after combining atoms. A reticulation creates the negative co-occupancy jump −2γ(1−γ)Sij(h) precisely for the child-pair clique; demographic changes preserve occupancy and deterministic tree transport only increases it. Unrelated simultaneous tree events cannot cancel a negative entry for a pair physically in the reticulation's child population. A singleton child does not meet this certificate and its routing time can remain movable.

I checked the strict posterior-balance example algebraically: (1/3)²e^(−log4)=(2/3)²e^(−2log4)=1/36. Swapping future rates 3 and 4 therefore preserves both survival and its marked-merger derivative. After a merger there is one side-free block; split survivors join at the common reconvergence. The whole local kernel agrees even though the linked parent words differ. The article correctly retains this ambiguity in its all-partition fiber.

For level one, the proper-cluster scalar-separator-faithfulness assumption is substantive. I did not reinterpret it as an automatic consequence of ordinary level-one topology. The full-S lemma has a separate argument: the frozen completion probability is analytic in elapsed time; equality with Kingman on an interval forces eventual completion probability one. Any positive posterior assignment in different frozen populations would prevent that. Strict route/survival support then forces one shared accessible population and hence a full-S bridge. This handles full-S unary galls without erasing them.

The occurrence order retains distinct time intervals for repeated identical clusters; replacing it by a set of clusters would lose unary blobs. The keyed multi-cut construction conditions on the joint feasible entry tuple, not a product of separately normalized entry factors. For local inheritance recovery, every arriving block remains a labelled lineage, and a port carrying several blocks is not collapsed to one synthetic sample. These details are necessary and are present in the reviewed edition.

I also examined the local-fiber moving-boundary issue: reference-visible times are held by separate coordinates; reference-invisible side differences vanish as entire germs, so moving evaluation points contribute no first-order chain-rule term at the reference parameter. Doubling the Sylvester operator represents parameter derivatives. The paper expressly distinguishes the linearized equation kernel from the real arc tangent cone, avoiding an unjustified inference that every infinitesimal null direction integrates to a physical fiber curve.

## Strongest checked conclusion and remaining limits

The strongest checked conclusion is the exact pointwise equivalence of the entire indexed locus-law family with (π,Q0,normalized metric-genealogy law), and therefore with (π,Q0,CCR), on the stated principal class. The same reading supports finite determination on each specified compact semianalytic finite-presentation class and exact arbitrarily-high-order collisions when demographic epoch count is not bounded.

No unresolved load-bearing proof gap or explicit counterexample was found in this analytic pass. The remaining limits are not concealed: this is not a general literal graph inverse; level-one recovery requires the named hypotheses; finite determining length is non-effective; exact comparison assumes a terminating zero-test domain; global reconstruction is Borel rather than uniformly stable; and no finite-data performance, empirical validity, universal novelty or expert peer-review certification has been established. The separate companion on strongly tree-child level-two networks is not used or reviewed here.

A website description may accurately present an exact identifiability theorem under a specified model, a finite observable response description and an epoch-complexity limitation. It should not say the method reconstructs arbitrary evolutionary networks from ordinary DNA samples, guarantees practical recovery with a short locus, or establishes broad biological conclusions independently of the model assumptions.

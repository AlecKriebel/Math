## Exact public claim boundaries

1. **Observation object.** The complete indexed family of exact population alignment laws, one for each locus length, rather than pooled sites, estimated gene trees, or one observed finite dataset. Within-locus sharing of a genealogy supplies nonlinear moments. The pointwise sequence–genealogy equivalence is therefore not a finite-data recovery guarantee.
2. **Principal assumptions.** A finite sampled-complete labelled temporal population presentation, at least two sampled lineages, positive finite rates and epoch durations, strict inheritance probabilities, independent lineage routing, independent loci, known nonrecombining locus boundaries, contemporaneous sampling, and one shared irreducible stationary reversible finite-state generator. Zero substitution conductances are allowed if irreducibility is retained. Repeated eigenvalues are included.
3. **Identified triple.** Stationary distribution, trace-normalized generator, and normalized rooted metric-genealogy law. Physical calibration is absent, and non-scale behavioral ambiguities remain. “Recovers the genealogy” would be misleading without saying that this is the genealogy *distribution* obtained from the full population-law hierarchy.
4. **CCR.** A finite analytic encoding of a time-indexed reachable–observable linear response, invariant up to typed isomorphism. Its cone does not give a stochastic occupancy basis or a biological graph. Fixed-time minimal rank also does not bound exponential complexity or derivative test orders; the manuscript deliberately uses raw dimensions for those tests.
5. **Graph statements.** Sampled-complete species-tree inversion recovers exposed rates, with terminal demographic schedules observable only when at least two lineages are sampled from that taxon. One-reticulation behavioral classification is general within that class; its incidence conclusion is restricted to incidence-separated presentations. Reduced level-1 additionally needs scalar-separator faithfulness and local incidence separation. No unrestricted level-1, level-2, or general-network graph claim belongs on the page. Parent demographic words can collide through posterior-balanced continuation switches.
6. **Finite determination.** The positive theorem concerns fixed finite unions of compact semianalytic normalized presentation charts. It is non-effective and supplies neither a numerical bound nor a general noncompact theorem. The negative result holds with unbounded demographic epoch count: exact two-taxon, zero-reticulation known-JC histories can match through L and differ at L + 1. It is not a claim that every fixed-complexity family is unidentifiable.
7. **Computation and statistics.** Proposed-model finite equality testing assumes an exact representation with terminating zero tests. Statistical consistency is for the observable equivalence class under the declared compact or increasing-sieve conditions, not universal stable raw-parameter inference. Finite example checks cannot establish moment determinacy, Borel inversion, general analytic continuation, or theorem completeness.

## Primary-literature assessment and credit

This was a focused comparison, not an exhaustive priority search. Searches covered direct sequence/network identifiability, NMSC genealogy distinguishability, timed-Hankel/coalescent overlap, demographic moment identifiability, and the cited analytic foundation. No conclusion of “first,” “solves all network identifiability,” or “earlier literature only used estimated gene trees” is justified.

- **Allman, Baños and Rhodes (2022), [logDet network identifiability](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00285-022-01734-2).** Primary published text/search extraction describes direct genomic-sequence recovery of substantial ultrametric level-1 topology using logDet, without needing loci partitioned by genes. This prevents describing direct-sequence identifiability itself as new. The manuscript’s distinct object is the all-length within-locus law and the full normalized genealogy distribution, under its different shared-generator model. Its breadth in one dimension does not dominate every broader substitution mixture covered by earlier work.
- **Zhu and Degnan (2017), [displayed trees and NMSC distinguishability](https://doi.org/10.1093/sysbio/syw097).** The primary article’s retrieved results distinguish displayed-tree combinatorics from stochastic gene-tree distributions and discuss the role of branch lengths and multiple samples. The public copy attributes the ambiguity precedent without asserting that the paper first discovered invisible networks. The manuscript itself explicitly disclaims priority for the first funnel phenomenon.
- **Yang and Flouri (2022), [full-likelihood introgression unidentifiability](https://doi.org/10.1093/molbev/msac083).** Read the primary PDF’s abstract and introductory comparison. It establishes exact bidirectional-introgression label-switching ambiguities in full-likelihood models. Direct alignment methods and intrinsic model ambiguity therefore predate this paper.
- **Allman, Baños, Garrote-López and Rhodes (2024), [level-1 identifiability from gene-tree quartets](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-024-01339-4).** Publisher/arXiv primary abstract states topological and numerical identifiability results with 3-cycle failures. The observation is a topology summary; its ambiguity mechanisms must not be transferred automatically to the finer metric-genealogy behavior.
- **Allman, Ané, Baños and Rhodes (2025), [galled tree-child networks](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01545-8).** Read primary author-deposited abstract and published description: restricted subclasses at arbitrary level already admit graph-identifiability theorems, with data and sampling conditions. Thus no “first beyond level 1” language is appropriate here; this paper explicitly defers its separate companion graph problem.
- **Bhaskar and Song (2014), [demographic identifiability from genomic variation](https://doi.org/10.1214/14-AOS1264).** Read primary author-deposited abstract and introduction. Piecewise demographic complexity already controls sufficient sample-size bounds for expected site-frequency spectra. This is important context not in the submitted bibliography. Its observable is the SFS, so it neither establishes nor refutes the paper’s exact finite linked-locus collision. The page credits the relationship without conflating the experiments.
- **Siu (1969), [Noetherian compact analytic rings](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9939-1969-0247135-7).** Visually read the author-hosted scan at printed pp. 488–489. Theorem 2 is explicitly real-analytic despite the article title, and the final remark invokes compact semianalytic finite connectedness. This supports the manuscript’s stated Frisch–Siu infrastructure and the necessity of preserving compact semianalytic assumptions. This inspection is not a reproof of Siu or a blanket verification of all subsequent applications.
- **Dai and Molloy (2026), [level-1 blob reconstruction](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.WABI.2026.1).** Read primary proceedings abstract (published 27 August 2026). It studies efficient reconstruction from quartet-based information once a tree of blobs is known. This recent neighboring result does not match the manuscript’s full indexed-law/CCR classification, but further cautions against portraying it as the first practical reconstruction method. Omitted from the short public background list to keep the page focused.

Compact moment determinacy, logDet/additive-tree recovery, minimal Hankel realization, confluence of divided differences, and Frisch–Siu Noetherianity are established mathematical ingredients. The claimed addition is their precise assembly for this NMSC observation experiment, the time- and type-sensitive response quotient, qualified graph interpretation, and exact linked-locus epoch-complexity obstruction. Establishing the originality of every component would require a broader specialist literature assessment.

The conceptually central result is the distinction between the information in all indexed alignment laws and the non-unique biological networks generating it.

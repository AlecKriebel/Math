## Exact question and contribution

b(G) maximizes the size of an **inclusion-minimal** base over all permutation representations, including nonfaithful and intransitive actions. A base has pointwise stabilizer equal to the action kernel, or trivial stabilizer in the permutation image. This is not minimum base size in one action or maximum ordered irredundant base size. μ′(G) maximizes independent subsets, not only independent generating sets.

For A=[[0,28],[1,0]], B=[[2,7],[12,28]] over F₂₉, H=⟨A,B⟩≅SL₂(5) acts on V=F₂₉². The claimed exact values for G=V⋊H are |G|=100920 and b_f(G)=b(G)=3<4=μ′(G). The actual matrix action is specified. The empty/trivial conventions are handled in the paper.

Cameron's subgroup and semilattice formulations are prior work. The addition is an explicit group realizing their nonnormal-bottom obstruction and upper bounds across all actions. H has b_f(H)=2 but b(H)=μ′(H)=3. Its unique involution limits trivial subgroup intersections; its F₂₉ line stabilizers have order dividing four. Every subgroup of G missing V then has faithful base invariant at most two. Restriction bounds b_f(G), while every nontrivial normal subgroup contains V, reducing nonfaithful actions to H. A parallel structural argument bounds μ′(G).

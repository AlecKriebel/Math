- Praeger's **Finding strong involutions in finite classical groups in odd characteristic**, Oberwolfach Report 37/2016, pp. 2160–2161, read in full: the explicit problem fixes one sign and seeks c/log n for the even Bray output. It reports the special-linear case solved, unitary work in progress, and symplectic/orthogonal cases open at that date. Source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46642 . This establishes the historical target, not current worldwide openness.
- Glasby–Praeger–Roney-Dougal, **Involution centralisers in finite unitary groups of odd characteristic**, arXiv:1812.01362v2: read introduction/Theorems 1–2 and the relevant Section 11 generation proof. It establishes the unitary marginal and linear/unitary generation results and uses disjoint sampling blocks with a final diagonal escape. The present paper credits these antecedents correctly. The earlier paper explicitly says the symplectic/orthogonal cases require different arguments. Source: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1812.01362v2 .

In the relevant large dimensions the stated non-subspace correction is at most 1/(e−2); small exceptional types are excluded by the manuscript's dimension hypothesis. Source: https://seis.bristol.ac.uk/~tb13602/docs/fpr1.pdf .

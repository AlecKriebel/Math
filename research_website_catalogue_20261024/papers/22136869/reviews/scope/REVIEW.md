## Exact claim and assumptions

The network likelihood is a **displayed-tree mixture substitution model** with uniform stationary root distribution and independent choices of one parent at each reticulation. It is not a network multispecies-coalescent theorem. The group is Z₂², ordered A,C,G,T, and the Fourier convention has q_AAA = 1, with only zero-sum labels potentially nonzero. K2P has equal C/T nontrivial eigenvalues. K3P allows all three to vary.

The explicit theta has three attachment edges, two reticulations, and a genuine strict level-two 3-blob. The rooted arcs are ρ→1,ρ→u,u→p,u→q,p→r₂,q→r₂,p→r₃,q→r₃,r₂→2,r₃→3. In symmetric witnesses the p-parent arcs both carry S and the q-parent arcs both carry T. Dangling unlabelled branches after a retained-parent choice contribute likelihood one and disappear under pruning. Suppressing the degree-two root composes two edge matrices and gives the effective pendant vector K⊙K.

The parameter chamber requires stochastic nonidentity symmetric matrices, nontrivial eigenvalues in (0,1), inheritance weights in (0,1), and, for all explicit witnesses, every matrix entry strictly positive. These are genuine interior collisions, not zero-weight, zero-length, or negative-probability degeneracies. The compact Q(√71) construction has exact minimum pattern probability 1188799/79626240. K2P inclusion in K3P already gives both Kimura non-disjointness statements; a second construction is not logically needed for the negative K3P answer.

The quartic K3P witness has an edge with three distinct nontrivial eigenvalues, excluding every **globally character-relabelled K2P parameter specialization**. Nevertheless its common tree distribution is globally relabelled K2P, not observably genuine K3P. Full rank and a local submersion then yield nearby common distributions outside all three global K2P symmetry strata. The global relabeling is the same across every edge and leaf; edge-dependent relabelings or gauge equivalences are not classified.

The unrestricted effective theta parameter dimensions are 20 (K2P) and 29 (K3P), not the lower dimensions of the symmetric closed-form ansatz. The effective affine Fourier dimensions are 9 and 15; tree dimensions are 6 and 9. Full ranks 9 and 15 give local collision loci of dimensions 17 and 23, with fixed-output theta fibers of dimensions 11 and 14. Local coverage is relative to a neighborhood of the specific tree distribution. It is not a claim that every parameterized tree distribution is realizable by this theta.

Dominance/Zariski density concerns the effective complexified Fourier spaces after normalization, inconsistent zeros and K2P character symmetry. It does not mean physical probabilities fill a simplex, eliminate positivity/continuous-time inequalities, or remove tree invariants. Tree equivalence remains a proper codimension-three/six condition on theta parameter space. Thus **full pointwise distinguishability fails while generic theta parameters are not tree-equivalent**. “Robust family” must not be read as an open subset of all theta parameters consisting of trees.

Edgewise strictly continuous-time means each edge separately has a symmetric positive-rate generator, with generators, rate ratios and edge times free to differ. For K2P it requires g>s²; for K3P each eigenvalue exceeds the product of the other two. The compact K2P witness is not asserted to satisfy this stronger condition; a different algebraic witness does. For every labelled unrooted binary **tree topology**, and a chosen internal vertex, the common-kernel argument gives parameter choices with one theta replacement and identical full 4ⁿ-pattern distribution.

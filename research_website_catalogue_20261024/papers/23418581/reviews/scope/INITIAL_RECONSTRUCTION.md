## Scope and exact target

Target theorem: for every odd prime p, algebraically closed k of characteristic p, r=2p-1, lambda=(2p-2,1), the degree-r Young module in the specified FI convention is simple of dimension2p-2, and the degree-r polynomial injective hull I_n(lambda) is nonprojective for every integer n>=2. This is not a result in characteristic zero at nontrivial q, not a theorem for n<2 where lambda is unavailable, and not a classification of all Young modules or all projective-injectives. The claimed counterexample to a specific 2009 printed proposal also depends on the exact original proposal's quantifiers and field/parameter conventions, which remain unverified until the foreign-source phase.

## Twelve materially distinct reconstructed claims

1. **Permutation augmentation.** For any r>=2 with p not dividing r, A=ker(sum:k^r->k) is a simple kS_r-module of dimensionr-1. A nonzero v in a submodule cannot have all coordinates equal, since rv_1=0 would then give v=0. Choose unequal entries; v-(ij)v is a nonzero multiple of e_i-e_j, and conjugation supplies every difference. These span A. This proof works over any characteristic-p field for the permutation module; no algebraic closure is needed. If p divides r, the constant line lies in A and the argument fails. Here r=2p-1 is -1 modulo p. Exact small boundary p=3,r=5 gives dimension4.

2. **Highest-weight dominance at the hook.** A partition mu of r dominating (r-1,1) must have first part at leastr-1, hence mu=(r) or (r-1,1). This is a direct partition calculation, valid at all ranks where labels exist. It supplies the two possible labels in both the symmetric-tensor argument and the all-rank contradiction; no exhaustive partition search is necessary.

3. **Injective decomposition and FI label.** Conditional on the claimed injective-weight multiplicity theorem for H=Sym^{r-1}E_N tensor E_N, N>=r, its only injective summands are I_N(r) and I_N(lambda). The lambda highest weight has multiplicity1. For L_N(r), the simple socle of Sym^r contains x_1^r; unipotent expansion has coefficient r x_1^{r-1}x_2, nonzero because p does not divide r. The ambient weight space is1-dimensional. Thus multiplicity1. This reasoning checks the nontrivial label bridge rather than importing an ordinary Young-module convention from memory. Exact source gap: existence/formula for the injective decomposition and the simple-socle assertion must be independently verified.

4. **Polarization splitting and multilinear kernel.** Multiplication m:H->Sym^r E_N has GL_N-equivariant polarization delta(f)=sum_i (partial_i f) tensor x_i. Euler's homogeneous identity gives m delta=r id in characteristicp; invertible r gives a splitting. With claim3 and I_N(r)=Sym^r, Krull-Schmidt cancellation gives ker m isomorphic to I_N(lambda). On weight(1^r,0...), H has exactly r monomial-tensor basis vectors; m sums their coefficients. Therefore exactness of the Schur weight functor gives FI_N(lambda)=A. Source gaps: costandard reciprocity establishing I_N(r)=Sym^r and Schur-functor exactness/convention, not the Euler identity. N>=r is essential for this weight-space realization.

5. **Rational torus/coefficient extraction.** For a rational GL2(k)-submodule of Sym^m(k^2), distinct monomial algebraic-torus characters allow weight-space projection. Over infinite k, a polynomial unipotent family of degree at mostm yields each coefficient by evaluation at m+1 distinct scalars and Vandermonde inversion. Thus finite-field sample invariance is insufficient, but algebraic closure of finite characteristic is infinite and validates extraction. From any monomial x^{m-i}y^i, y->y+tx has t^i coefficient x^m with coefficient1. This gives the highest monomial in any nonzero invariant submodule without claiming every monomial coefficient of an arbitrary translate is nonzero.

6. **Simplicity of Sym^{2p-1}.** Modulo p, (1+t)^{2p-1}=(1+t^p)(1+t)^{p-1}; the two degree ranges[0,p-1] and[p,2p-1] are disjoint and every coefficient is nonzero, since binomial(p-1,j)=(-1)^j modp. Claim5 sends any nonzero submodule to x^{2p-1}; the opposite unipotent sends that vector to every monomial coefficient, proving simplicity. Dimension is2p. This proof is all-prime, includingp=2 for this isolated assertion. It does not say Sym^{2p-1}(k^N) is simple at N>2, which is not required.

7. **The submodule W of B=Sym^{2p-3}.** For odd p, m=2p-3>=3. The multiplication E^{(1)} tensor Sym^{p-3}E -> B has disjoint monomial ranges i in[0,p-3] or[p,2p-3], so it is injective and equivariant. This gives W dimension2(p-2). Its monomial support is exactly the nonzero coefficient support of (1+t)^{2p-3}=(1+t^p)(1+t)^{p-3}; binomial(p-3,j) is nonzero for0<=j<=p-3 because all factorials involved are less thanp. Claim5 yields x^m from any nonzero W-submodule, and the opposite coefficients generate all W. Hence W is simple with highest weight(m,0). At p=3 the ranges are{0,3}; W=span(x^3,y^3), dimension2, the Frobenius twist of E.

8. **Two-dimensional simple quotient.** The only missing indices arep-2,p-1. The quotient weights are(m-(p-2),p-2)=(p-1,p-2) and(p-2,p-1). The y->y+tx coefficient sending indexp-1 to indexp-2 is p-1, and the opposite root's coefficient from indexp-2 to indexp-1 is also p-1. Both are nonzero, so neither line is invariant, and torus decomposition implies the quotient is simple. Its highest weight is(p-1,p-2), dimension2. This is not inferred solely from having dimension2.

9. **Nonsplitting and socle/head.** A rational invariant complement to W would be a torus-stable two-dimensional space with exactly the missing monomial weight lines, hence span(v_{p-2},v_{p-1}). Translating v_{p-2} by y->y+tx has t^{p-2} coefficientv_0, nonzero inW, so that space is not invariant. The extension is nonsplit. Since W and B/W are nonisomorphic simples, any other simple submodule maps either0 or isomorphically onto B/W; the latter would split. Therefore soc(B)=W. Dually any simple quotient besides B/W would force a split; head(B)=B/W. Atp=3 both dimensions equal2 but highest weights(3,0) and(2,1) differ, so self-duality is still excluded.

10. **Rank-two injective and obstruction.** Conditional on costandard reciprocity, only mu=(r),lambda can occur in I_2(lambda). The simple Sym^r has highest weight(r), so contributes no L_2(lambda); the diagonal multiplicity for nabla_2(lambda) is1. Therefore a costandard filtration of I_2(lambda) has just nabla_2(lambda)=det tensor B, hence equality up to isomorphism. Its socle label(2p-2,1) and head label(p,p-1) differ. Contravariant duality fixes simple labels but interchanges head/socle, so it is not contravariantly self-dual. Exact source-dependent gap: the asserted implication polynomial injective and projective => contravariantly self-dual is stronger than a general quasihereditary-algebra tautology and must be checked for Schur algebras.

11. **All-rank exclusion via labels.** If I_N(lambda) were projective at N>=2, the claimed Schur-algebra theorem makes it an indecomposable tilting T_N(mu). A highest-weight tilting has no composition factors outside weights dominated bymu, hence mu dominateslambda. By claim2, mu has at most2 parts. Exact labelled rank truncation to2 is then claimed to send both I_N(lambda) toI_2(lambda) and T_N(mu) toT_2(mu). This would make I_2(lambda) an injective tilting and thus projective, contradictingclaim10. Crucial non-generic source gaps: labelled injective truncation, labelled tilting truncation, projective-injective=>tilting and injective-tilting=>projective. Mere exactness of an idempotent functor does not establish preservation of arbitrary projectives, and the manuscript correctly does not rely on that shortcut. N=2 is covered directly; no limiting/stable-rank extrapolation alone suffices.

12. **Printed-proposal interpretation and stronger criterion.** If the actual conjecture has the printed quantifiers alleged by the target, a simple Y automatically has simple head, whereas claim11 excludes the existential rank n>=length(lambda)=2. Thus the broad equivalence is refuted. If membership of lambda in the constructed Lambda_n^i is sufficient for I_n(lambda) projective, the same example excludes membership for every allowed n and refutes a simple-head=>existential-membership proposal. Exact original-source gap: verify the conjecture's formulation, its q and k hypotheses, whether the characteristic-zero sentence in the subsequent subsection is local rather than global, and the proven sufficiency of this Lambda family.

                                                Abstract
         We give an aﬃrmative answer to Question 3 in Iyer’s contribution, joint with Shinko, to
     Oberwolfach Report 2/2025. A dense Gδ of continuous actions of the restricted wreath product
     ZoZ on Cantor space has hyperﬁnite orbit equivalence relation on the entire space. We specialize
     established Cantor-action lifting principles to obtain a continuous proﬁnite shift height. Finite-
     width lamp slabs then satisfy the compatible-metric theorem of Conley–Jackson–Marks–Seward–
     Tucker-Drob. The full preimage of the embedded integers is treated by a separate ﬁnite Borel
     exhaustion. The auxiliary Borel theorem requires the height factor and allows stabilizers.

Theorem 1. There is a dense Gδ subset of Act(W, 2N ) such that every action in it has hyperﬁnite
orbit equivalence relation on all of 2N .
    This is the exact whole-space question posed as open in [5, printed p. 115, Question 3]. The
locally ﬁnite-asymptotic-dimension theorem of Iyer–Shinko [6] does not cover W : it is ﬁnitely
generated and contains undistorted copies of Zd for arbitrarily large d. Our theorem makes no
restriction to a conull or comeager subset of the phase space, and does not assert hyperﬁniteness
for every Borel W -action.
    We use three published consequences of Conley–Jackson–Marks–Seward– Tucker-Drob [2], with
numbering from their author manuscript. Corollary 5.5 gives ﬁnite Borel asymptotic dimension for
any Borel action of each ﬁxed Zm . Lemmas 2.2 and 3.1 and the paragraph following Deﬁnition 3.2
give its ﬁnite-color characterization: for every ﬁnite set of group moves there is a Borel coloring
with a ﬁxed ﬁnite number of colors and uniformly ﬁnite monochromatic move components. The
number of colors depends on m, not on the move set. Finite Borel covers may be disjointized to
obtain colorings.
    Theorem 7.3 of [2] says that if proper Borel extended metrics ρn on a standard Borel space each
have ﬁnite Borel asymptotic dimension and

Proof. By [2, Corollary 5.5] and its ﬁnite-move characterization, there is a ﬁxed ﬁnite color count
Dm +1 such that, for each R ≥ 1, Bm has a Borel coloring whose monochromatic components under

need not themselves be monochromatic; only their endpoints are used. The lifted component has

4    Veriﬁcation and publication status
The accompanying package preserves the original proof and reviews, adds fresh independent math-
ematical and dated priority audits, and supplies portable ﬁnite diagnostics and integrity checks.
Finite computations check group identities, nonfree slab models, divisor cuts, projections, and
bounded-height exhaustions; they do not prove the inﬁnite theorem. The proof above relies on the
precise published inputs stated in Section 1.
    The bounded priority audit of October 13, 2026 found no exact earlier resolution in its inspected
corpus and identiﬁed the lifting predecessors credited above. It does not certify historical ﬁrstness
or worldwide current openness. This note answers the historically posed exact question.
    OpenAI tools were used extensively in developing and checking the argument, drafting the note,
writing diagnostics, and conducting independent adversarial AI reviews. This is an unrefereed
preprint. No completed external human peer review or formal proof-assistant veriﬁcation is claimed.

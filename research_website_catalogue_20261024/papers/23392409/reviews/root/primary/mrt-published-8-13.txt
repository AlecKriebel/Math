Definition 2.3. Let ˛ 2 R. An ˛-embedding of G is an embedding of G ˘ in the plane
such that every internal face of G ˘ is an ˛-quad.

    The fact that G ˘ is embedded in the plane implies that the ˛-quads corresponding
to internal faces are proper. In particular, for embedded graphs, edges cannot collapse
to a point, two distinct edges cannot meet outside of their endpoints, faces have non-
empty interiors and two distinct faces have disjoint interiors. This setting is the one
preferred for the study of statistical mechanical models such as spanning trees and
the Ising model. However, in the more general setting we consider, we need to allow
drawings of graphs that are not necessarily embeddings.

Definition 2.4. Let ˛ 2 R. An ˛-realization of G is defined to be any map from the
vertices of G ˘ to the plane such that every face of G ˘ is mapped to an ˛-quad.

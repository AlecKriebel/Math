                  REMARKS ON THE AREA THEOREM
              IN THE THEORY OF UNIVALENT FUNCTIONS

                            M. PAVLOVIĆ AND JOSÉ ÁNGEL PELÁEZ

                                   (Communicated by Nigel J. Kalton)


           Abstract. We prove an integral extension of the classical area theorem for
           univalent functions. We give an application ﬁnding geometric conditions on
           the image domain of a univalent function f which imply that f belongs to the
           Hardy space H p , 0 < p < ∞.

   Given a space X of analytic functions, one of the most interesting problems in
the theory of univalent functions is ﬁnding geometric conditions on a domain Ω
which imply that Ω is an X-domain; that is, any analytic function f deﬁned on D
with f (D) ⊂ Ω belongs to X. This problem has been solved for a good number
of spaces, such as the Bloch space, Besov spaces B p , 1 < p < ∞, . . . (see [1], [3]).
However, this is an open problem for H p spaces (see [4]). We shall use Theorem 3
to ﬁnd geometric conditions on the image domain of a function f ∈ U which imply
its membership in H p . For simplicity, we shall assume that 0 ∈ f (D).
   Given a domain Ω ⊂ C and a point w in Ω, we shall write dΩ (w) to denote the
(Euclidean) distance from w to the boundary ∂Ω. The following inequalities play
an essential role in the proof of our results (see, e.g., [9], Corollary 1.4).
   If Ω is a simply connected proper subdomain of C and if F is a conformal

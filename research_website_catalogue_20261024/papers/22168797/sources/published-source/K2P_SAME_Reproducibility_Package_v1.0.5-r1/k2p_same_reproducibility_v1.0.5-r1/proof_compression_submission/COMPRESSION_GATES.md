# Corrected compression gates

The compressed layer is accepted only when all gates below pass.  These gates
do not replace or weaken the frozen theorem release.

## C1. Theorem preservation

The theorem remains exactly the principal-domain `K2P-SAME` classification,
its generic structural-identifiability and exact-reconstruction statements,
the strict continuous-time corollary, and the weak-class \(4n-3\) sharpness
theorem.  No mixed-sign or full-image-equality claim is added.

## C2. Corrected raw coverage

Every corrected four-port, theta2, cycle, restoration, and probe record is
assigned exactly once to a compressed family.  Required counts are read from
the frozen release; missing, duplicate, direction-reversed, or unclassified
rows are fatal.

## C3. Exact representative proof

Every claimed polynomial, rank, whole-map \(T_i\), quartet, isomorphism, and
ordinary-triangle template has an exact representative proof.  A family
transport is accepted only when its direction-preserving mixed-graph,
parameter, Fourier-coordinate, and boundary-incidence maps are explicit.

## C4. Frozen/compressed agreement

The compressed family assignment must agree with the corrected frozen
classifier on every raw and canonical record.  The frozen classifier remains
the comparison target even when the new explanation is shorter.

## C5. Fixed-full restoration

Every one of the 997 canonical parents and 2,540 physical roots is covered;
every one of the 36,824 physical child edges is present; all 36,792 leaves are
terminal; and parent, marginal, and transport restrictions agree.  Any
archetype theorem supplements rather than erases these quantifiers.

## C6. Probe reconstruction

The hand word theorem plus its finite base residue reproduces all 29,964
one-port and 544,571 two-port outcomes, all 67,741 exact transports, all 4,379
parent restrictions, and every reverse-order and global-triangle gate.

## C7. Independent audit and mutations

A separately implemented consumer checks formulas, family coverage, and
old/new equivalence.  Mutations must reject at least a missing family, wrong
transport, wrong certificate family, missing restoration child, wrong parent,
changed probe exception, source-target reversal, corrupted polynomial,
restored rooted-oracle field, and changed sharpness inheritance assignment.

## C8. Honest residual statement

The final outcome is one of `PC-FULL`, `PC-PARTIAL`, or
`PC-NO-SAFE-COMPRESSION`.  Every remaining finite computation is identified
by its exact role.  Row enumeration used only for coverage is distinguished
from representative exact arithmetic and from release infrastructure.

## Explicitly forbidden shortcuts

- rooted tree/sunlet classification outside the proved ordinary three-port
  identity;
- ordinary-triangle redirection as a free polynomial symmetry;
- source-target reversal;
- inheritance complement without its exact parent-edge transport;
- graph automorphism without direction, label, role, and arrowhead checks;
- numerical rank in place of exact lower and symbolic upper certificates; and
- describing finite computer-assisted coverage as a hand proof.

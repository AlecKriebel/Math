## Literal target and quantifiers

Any completion F of its rule to the full shift defines F_*(w) by consecutive output windows, or epsilon when |w|<ell. L is the full finite-word language including epsilon, and B={w:F_*(w) notin L}. A common finite recognizing monoid M for L and B yields k=4(|M|+1), N=max(2,ell,k).

The complete Theorem1 claims equivalence of: (1) this same f has a block extension from some containing SFT, allowing non-strict containment and larger alphabet; (2) L admits a locally testable separator from B; (3) Lang(T[N]) avoids B; (4) F(T[N])=T. On failure, no alternative radius, completion or containing alphabet can help. It does not decide existence over all self-maps, arbitrary reducible sofic variants, or cellular-automaton stability. Its general LT-separation bound is imported and explicitly credited.

# Independent check of the full criterion

The input is a nonempty two-sided irreducible sofic shift T over a finite alphabet A and a specified onto block self-map f. Its memory/anticipation window has length ell≥1. Fix any completion F of its local rule to all ell-blocks on A. Let L=language(T), including epsilon, and let B be words whose consecutive F outputs are not in L. A common finite recognizing monoid M for L,B is effectively available. The theorem states equivalence of arbitrary containing-SFT extension, LT separation, language(T[N])∩B=∅ and F(T[N])=T, with k=4(|M|+1), N=max(2,ell,k). It excludes every alternative completion, radius or larger input alphabet on failure.

Regularity is explicit: a finite-state transducer buffers at most ell−1 letters and updates a complete allowed-output-language DFA whenever it emits a letter. Short words emit epsilon, so are not bad. If w is bad then its output remains a factor of the output of uwv; factoriality of L makes B a two-sided ideal. Every allowed input word occurs in a T point, whose output is f of that point, so L∩B=∅.

For any purported extension G:S→T, intersect its larger alphabet with A^Z. This remains an SFT containing T. Take n at least the lengths of its finite forbidden list; no such forbidden word can appear in T[n]. Enlarge n beyond a common local window for F,G. That window of any T[n] point occurs in T, so the rules agree there. Thus arbitrary extension is equivalent to F(T[n])⊂T for some n. A forbidden output has a finite input witness and conversely, proving inclusion iff language(T[n]) avoids B. Since T⊂T[n] and f is onto, inclusion gives image equality. This is the precise use of surjectivity.

The canonical overlap graph, formed from the *actual* L_{n−1} and L_n, is strongly connected by language irreducibility: connect any two words inside T. Consequently a long word whose n-factors are allowed extends in both directions along graph paths. Short words in the approximation are exactly L words of those lengths: each extends to an n-block, while T⊂T[n]. Its language is therefore LT by checking n-factors and finitely many short cases. This argument does not assume a raw input graph presentation is itself irreducible or correctly trimmed. The language automaton must represent actual bi-infinite shift words, not spurious finite paths of an arbitrary untrimmed graph.

Saturation supplies the crucial reduction from a separator to one fixed approximation. Suppose C is LT[k], contains L and avoids B, while a bad w occurs in T[n] with n≥max(2,k). Irreducibility supplies U∈L containing every allowed n-word, padded to length at least n+k. Strong connectivity of the overlap graph lets one place a finite occurrence of w between two U occurrences, yielding W=UcwdU in language(T[n]). Even if w is shorter than n, extend its occurring graph segment before making these connections. W is bad by the ideal property. A separate connection inside T gives V=UeU∈L.

Both V,W have exactly all allowed n-factors: U supplies every one and neither word has a forbidden n-factor. Two-sided extendability of T extends every allowed k-word to an n-word, so they have the same length-k factor set L_k. Both start and end with U, hence have identical (k−1)-prefix/suffix, and both have length at least 2k. Actual PRZ profiles at positions0,…,|w|−1 consist of at most floor(k/2) letters before the position and ceil(k/2) letters beginning there. Full profiles are the common k-factors split accordingly. Single-end truncated profiles are determined by the common boundaries; dual-end truncation cannot occur at these lengths. The profile *sets* coincide, without multiplicities. A union of profile-equivalence classes containing V must contain W, contradicting separation. This proves the uniform saturation lemma, not merely a finite example.

If T is SFT, take S=T; containment need not be strict. If an automorphism f were steady, complete its inverse g locally. The identity gF=id holding on T holds on T[n] for sufficiently large n. Image inclusion into T then gives x=gF(x)∈T for every x∈T[n], so T=T[n] is SFT. For the mixing even shift, for each n choose odd m with m+2>n. The periodic point (01^m)^infinity has no length-n block containing both zeros of an odd gap, hence all its n-blocks occur in T, but it is not in T.

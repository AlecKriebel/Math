## Main adversarial checks

1. **All complex competitors.** The substitution removing `Im a` gives the exact stable chart `x≥0`, `U=u+y²/4≥0`, `V²≤x²U` with `V=v−xy/2`. Equivalently the third inequality is `F=v²−xyv−x²u≤0`. The chart correctly includes its singular faces; discarding `U≥0` would be false. Near the smooth witness path, `∇F=2t³ n_t`, independently confirming the normal without using the author's root derivative. Local polynomial Taylor support and a small normal step beat all competitors in the ball; the triangle inequality excludes every stable point outside it. The proof never restricts minimization to real coefficients or to the path.
2. **Universal fixed metrics.** For fixed G, `||c_t||_G²≤3(trG)t⁴` when `t≤1`, while the limiting covector pairs to `t³`. For any normal stepτ, choosing `t<2τ/(3trG)` gives a strictly closer stable competitor. This proves nonproximity for arbitrary fixed G. Direct moving-normal support has ratio `1/[2t(2+t²)]→∞`, also violating the uniform support formulation. Confusing covectors with metric vectors fails in explicit mixed-SPD controls.
3. **Global projection implication.** The actual PRT Definition1.1, Proposition1.2 and complete Theorem1.3 were checked. The theorem explicitly concerns global P_C and includes(a)⇔(k) when local weak closedness holds, automatically in finite dimensions. Non-single-valuedness could mean nonattainment without a separate existence argument, so the monic bounded-minimizing-sequence argument and full-degree compact localization were checked separately. Both are valid.
4. **Roots, radicals and leading coefficient arithmetic.** The roots are exactly `−it` and `−t²+it`; one is on the imaginary axis and the other has negative real part. The radical formula has the correct factor1/2 and the nonnegative branch. The two leading coefficient directions independently give `(t³/2,−t²)`. Exact controls include both stable and unstable root-constructed quadratics, imaginary double roots and zero-face cases. Reversing a branch normal or replacing closed by strict stability is detected.
5. **Function versus epigraph.** At a negative discriminant the scalar subgradient interval is purely imaginary; at zero it is the half-plane `Re q≥0`. The diffeomorphism `(a,b)↦(a,a²−4b)` transports lower supports in both directions, justifying equality without an unsupported general chain rule. Since `v_b=−2q`, localizing a finite v0 bounds q, excludes off-ray gradients near zero, and gives the claimed support inequality. The remainder sign is correct. Explicit zero-discriminant comparisons exercise adverse curvature without positive scalar slack. Dropping subgradient localization fails: at discriminants `t²` and `4t²`, a valid gradient gives required curvature `8/(9t³)→∞`.

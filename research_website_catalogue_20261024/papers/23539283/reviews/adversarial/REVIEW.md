## Attacks and independent reconstruction

### 1. Recoverability, sigma-finiteness, and prefix absorption

A potential fatal error would be silently treating mutually singular fixed-parameter Gaussian laws as equivalent. The paper does not do this: from any x in C, the limit recovers a, the Cesaro limit recovers positive d, and the reconstructed z satisfies exactly the two defining Gamma conditions. Conversely Theta recovers these quantities. This gives an actual Borel bijection and transfers sigma-finiteness from bounded a and log-delta boxes.

Deleting k coordinates gives

`d(tail_k x)^2 = 4^(-k) d(x)^2`.

The finite removed terms divided by the new Cesaro length vanish; the ratio of averaging lengths tends to one. Prepending arbitrary finite real values reverses this exactly. The Gaussian tail and its first k coordinates split under the original product measure, while d-delta/delta is invariant under delta -> 2^(-k) delta. The displayed q_k is therefore the right finite-coordinate conditional density. Every factor is positive and finite. Tonelli is legitimate on the sigma-finite parameter product; no infinite-mass probability disintegration is needed.

The unitary multiplier must be q_k^(-1/2), since pulling output measure back yields q_k times product measure. The manuscript has that direction correct. The multiplier can be unbounded pointwise without invalidating the unitary: its squared factor cancels q_k exactly in the norm identity.

### 2. Ordinal concatenation and loss of finite coordinates

Ordinal addition alone loses finite suffix *length*, which would destroy a naive coordinate bijection. Here those suffix coordinates are retained as the prefix of the next infinite block. For fixed input grades the split position is specified, so deleting that prefix reconstructs the two inputs. This does not require ordinal cancellation when grades vary.

Regrouping three inputs retains all original infinite blocks and gives each intervening finite string to the next infinite block. It produces the same coordinate word for either parenthesization. The exact q-chain, together with positive square roots, also identifies the transported L2 maps. Common translations preserve q because all displacements from the recovered limit are unchanged.

Independent bounded controls compared full symbolic coordinate expressions (not only ordinal grades), tested inverses on forward images, and rejected deliberate coordinate loss and omitted scale-shift mutations. The general proof remains the analytic coordinate argument above.

### 3. Run charts, limit boundaries, and countability

At a fixed cut, the removed coordinate and block-limit equalities are null. Away from these equalities, each convergent infinite block has eventually constant color and only finitely many side changes. Finitely many blocks and a finite suffix imply finitely many maximal convex monochromatic runs. A boundary can occur at an ordinal limit without a predecessor; the proof accounts for it and does not infer that every boundary lies between consecutive coordinates.

A run starting in the middle of an infinite block uses its tail, which remains in C. A run spanning several limit positions contains the corresponding separate infinite blocks and, if necessary, a final finite prefix. Its order type remains below omega squared. A finite alternating list of such one-color words therefore reconstructs exactly its own maximal runs.

The chart index consists of finite color patterns and finite lists of grades from a countable grade set. Thus it is countable. Fixed-grade concatenation is a Borel isomorphism; restrictions to the countable coordinate inequalities are Borel and preserve measure classes. Maximal runs make images disjoint even where ordinal sums coincide. These facts justify the orthogonal sum and surjectivity of the free product map.

### 4. Associator and measure factors

For three fixed intervals, all three-color words have finite maximal runs after only finitely many cut-specific null sets are removed. The ordinary pointed free-product associator flattens grouped alternating words to exactly these reduced three-color words. Grouping maximal stretches that avoid the third color reproduces the internal concatenations. There is no requirement for one conull set working for all real cuts. Associativity follows on every countable chart and hence as an L2 operator identity.

### 5. Continuity, time zero, and inverses

For a fixed positive upper boundary, a word's coordinate closure is compact: it is the union of finitely many convergent sequence closures and a finite suffix. Its supremum is a coordinate or a block limit. The event that this supremum equals that boundary is null. This makes P_t strongly continuous by dominated convergence against |h| squared. At zero, each nonempty positive word has at least one fixed positive coordinate and is eventually excluded. No uniform control over all word lengths is needed.

In the source free-product ambient space, Q(s,t) is strongly continuous on each finite word summand and hence on their direct sum. For fixed input grades, the zero-extended structural map is a fixed concatenation unitary after support restriction and strongly continuous translations. This proves continuity on a dense family; the already established contraction norm extends it to the entire source. It avoids the invalid alternative of summing an unrestricted concatenation operator over potentially overlapping output grades.

When a centered input fiber approaches zero, its projected fixed vector tends to zero. Single surviving factors and the vacuum have the claimed canonical limits. Finally, continuity of V alone would not justify continuity of V*. The manuscript correctly also uses VV*=P_(s+t); the displayed norm expansion then proves strong continuity of V*. I found no zero-time or inverse-map gap.

Positive-time trivialization via D_t has exponent n+m (one factor per infinite block and finite letter), as follows from da scaling and invariant d-delta/delta. This exponent need not be additive under ordinal absorption: the nontrivial concatenation density already handles that change. No local triviality at zero is asserted.

### 6. Tensor core and exclusion of infinite sectors

At a fixed partition, the tensor range is the entire support subspace for weakly decreasing interval colors, not merely a span of selected pointwise words. The positive finite density transport preserves those support subspaces. Countably many rational two-cell cuts detect every inversion in the real labels. Their multiplication-projection intersection therefore equals the support on globally nonincreasing words.

In any infinite block the disjoint-pair differences have signs of independent symmetric nondegenerate Gaussians. Infinitely many positive signs occur almost surely, precluding globally nonincreasing labels. Tonelli and sigma-finiteness extend this null assertion to the mixed parameter measure and higher sectors. Restricting to an interval event cannot turn that null set into positive measure. Finite-grade equal-label diagonals are also null, leaving exactly the decreasing simplexes.

For the reverse inclusion into every real-endpoint partition, the proof fixes a partition at a time. It does not take an uncountable union of exceptional null sets. The tensor subsystem factorization is then the ordinary simplex split, with density one. Maximality follows because every inherited tensor subsystem lies in every partition tensor range.

### 7. Free hull and a genuinely nonzero orthogonal obstruction

K is itself a closed free subsystem: free products and run decompositions preserve finite total word length. For each fixed finite length, rational partitions separating distinct labels give countably many support rectangles. Their full L2 spaces occur in finite-partition free images of the singleton sectors of F. The rectangles cover the complement of the diagonal null set, proving density by orthogonality. Completion cannot add an infinite-grade component, since the finite-grade direct sum is already closed.

For the displayed parameter window the parameter mass is `(t/3) log 2`. The Gaussian-coordinate failure bound is `sum_j 9/(2500*4^j)=3/625`. Therefore the specified set has measure at least `(622/625)(t/3) log 2`, strictly positive and finite. The strict coordinate bounds put every coordinate inside (0,t). Its indicator gives the claimed nonzero grade-omega L2 vector. This construction succeeds for every t>0.

The Minimum Distance of Unshifted Spherical Fibonacci Lattices
                                                       Alper Ferudun∗

                                                      27 October 2026



                                                               Abstract
              For the unshifted spherical
                                   √      Fibonacci lattice with Fn ≥ 2 points, we prove that the least
          Euclidean distance is 2/ Fn . The unique closest pair consists of the north pole and the
          next point in the deﬁning order. This answers the separation question stated in Brauchart’s
          contribution to Oberwolfach Report 40/2012. The proof combines a global inequality for
          the Lambert equal-area map with Zaremba’s classical formula for the ﬁgure of merit of a
          Fibonacci lattice. More generally, a rational lattice with N points and Zaremba index at
          least N/4 has exactly the same minimum distance after the unshifted Lambert lift. No
          limiting argument or numerical hypothesis is used.

4    Scope and checks
The arithmetic input (6) is an established theorem, not a new claim of this paper. The ar-
gument above supplies the geometric implication needed for the spherical separation question.
It concerns the exact rational, unshifted construction in (1), not discrepancy, optimal packing
among all N -point sets, or the exact separation of shifted irrational Fibonacci conﬁgurations.
    A common height shift k 7→ k + η, 0 ≤ η ≤ 1, in the latitude coordinates preserves (3) and
the lower bound in Theorem 3, because height diﬀerences are unchanged. For 0 < η < 1, pole
attainment (5) is unavailable, so equality is not asserted.

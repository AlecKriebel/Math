 Spherical Fibonacci Points: Hyperuniformity, and more
                                          Johann S. Brauchart
                                      Graz University of Technology
                                        j.brauchart@tugraz.at


   One way of explicitly constructing point sets on the unit sphere in R3 is to map a suitable set in the unit
square to the sphere by means of an area-preserving Lambert transformation.
   Using the example of the Fibonacci lattice in the unit square, we study properties of its spherical analogue.
   In particular, we consider hyperuniformity aspects (cf. [1, 2]).

Joint work with: Josef Dick (UNSW, Yuan Xu (University of Oregon).

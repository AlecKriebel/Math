Reduced C*-algebras of the Thompson family: an attributed consequence of nonamenability

Authors/Creators

  • Kriebel, Alec

Description

An attributed consequence note applying OpenAI's nonamenability theorem for standard Thompson F to the published equivalences of Le Boudec and Matte Bon. The reduced group C*-algebras of F, T, the full automorphism group Aut(F), and the abstract commensurator Comm(F) are simple and have their canonical traces as their unique tracial states, by the discrete-group trace theorem of Breuillard, Kalantar, Kennedy and Ozawa. The countable standard circle-overgroup consequence is also recorded. All these simplicity reductions are inherited; T's unique trace was already unconditional. No new simplicity criterion, independent proof of nonamenability, or first-priority claim is made. The conclusions depending on the new input have exactly the same dependency on its validity. The source proof was manually audited; actual Lean source and semantics were inspected, but no fresh Lean kernel build was reproduced owing to insufficient disk space, and the follow-on theorem is not claimed to be formalized. AI tools were used extensively in research, drafting and verification. This preprint has not undergone conventional human peer review or refereeing; automated adversarial audits are not human peer review.

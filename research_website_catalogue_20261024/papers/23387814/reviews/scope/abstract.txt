We give an explicit family of counterexamples to the proposed lower bound ε(S,L;x) ≥ (2+|K_S²|^(1/4))⁻¹ for minimal complex projective surfaces. For every integer d ≥ 7, a classical plane-pencil construction followed by a double base change gives a smooth surface with ample canonical bundle, K_S² = 6(d−1)(d−3), and an ample integral line bundle L with L² = 6 and ε(S,L;x) ≤ 1/(d−1). Already d = 7 contradicts the bound. The pencil and covering ingredients are classical; historical priority has not been fully cleared.

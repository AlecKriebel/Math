# Functional-analytic interfaces checked

1. **Form realization, no smooth boundary.** H₀¹(Ω) is defined as the H¹ closure of C_c^∞(Ω). The form q is densely defined, closed and nonnegative on L². Its representation operator is self-adjoint. The exact operator domain is {u∈H₀¹:−Δu∈L² distributionally}. To check the last assertion, an L² distributional right side represents q(u,φ) for compactly supported smooth φ. Both sides are continuous in φ∈H₀¹, so density extends the identity to the whole form domain. The converse follows by testing the representing identity against C_c^∞. No regular boundary elliptic estimate is used.

2. **Compact resolvent and strict positive bottom.** Extend test functions by zero from Ω to an enclosing box B and pass to their H¹ closures. This gives an isometric closed supported subspace of H₀¹(B). The box Poincaré inequality bounds ||u||₂ by a constant times ||∇u||₂, giving E₁>0. Rellich compact embedding on the box restricts to a compact embedding on this subspace. The compact-resolvent spectral theorem yields an L² orthonormal eigenbasis, finite multiplicities and finitely many eigenvalues below each finite threshold. Nonempty open Ω contains a ball and infinitely many independent tests, so the sequence is infinite. Compactness does not require finitely many connected components.

3. **Coordinates and distributions.** On bounded Ω, xα and its first derivative are bounded; multiplying a convergent H¹ approximation by xα proves xαu∈H₀¹. The distribution product identity −Δ(xαu)=xα(−Δu)−2∂αu has L² right side for an eigenfunction, so interface1 puts xαu in Dom H. The derivative ∂αu need not initially belong to H₀¹ or Dom H. That additional domain membership is deduced only under the contradictory finite-support/Dom H² assumption. Distribution derivatives commute, so differentiating the eigen-equation requires no boundary extension or interior smoothness assertion.

4. **Reality and spectral series.** The real form commutes with conjugation, so each finite-dimensional eigenspace has a real orthonormal basis. For w=xαuⱼ∈Dom H, spectral calculus gives ∑(Eₖ−Eⱼ)²|⟨uₖ,w⟩|²=||(H−Eⱼ)w||². Also ∑|Eₖ−Eⱼ||aₖ|²≤[∑(Eₖ−Eⱼ)²|aₖ|²]¹ᐟ²[∑|aₖ|²]¹ᐟ²<∞. This is the required absolute convergence, including finitely many negative first-moment factors. Finite low-state sums can then be rearranged without conditional summation.

The closed-form representation theorem, compact self-adjoint spectral theorem and Rellich compact embedding on a box are standard foundational imports, not newly formalized or proved from axioms in this packet. The non-smooth-domain application and every additional domain inclusion used by this paper were checked as above. Harrell–Stubbe’s numerical inequality is not an unproved import here: the target supplies, and the reconstruction derives, its entire remainder identity.

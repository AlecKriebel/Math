                                                                                            Abstract
                                                     We prove that for all q > 211, there always exists a primitive root g in the finite field
                                                     Fq such that Q(g) is also a primitive root, where Q(x) = ax2 + bx + c is a quadratic
                                                     polynomial with a, b, c ∈ Fq such that b2 − 4ac 6= 0.

machinery. In §3 we use a sieve to prove that there are at most 1528 exceptions to Theorem

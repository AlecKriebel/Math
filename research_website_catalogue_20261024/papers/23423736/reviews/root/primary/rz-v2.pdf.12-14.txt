   We need one last ingredient for the proof of the main theorem. The following
result of Artin and Stafford shows that under certain conditions, a subring A of a
twisted ring B must be equal to B in large degree.
Lemma 4.3. [AS, Theorem 4.1] Suppose that B = B(X, L, σ) where L is σ-ample.
Let A be a graded subring of B, and suppose that for n ≫ 0 the sections in An ⊆
H0 (Ln ) generate the sheaf Ln , and that the map to projective space X → P(An )
determined by a k-basis for An is a closed embedding. Then An = Bn for n ≫ 0.
Proof. This is a slight restatement of [AS, Theorem 4.1], which requires in addition
to our hypotheses that L is also σ −1 -ample, and that A has subexponential growth.
But since L is σ-ample, L must also be σ −1 -ample [Ke1, Corollary 5.1], and B has
finite GK-dimension [Ke1, Theorem 6.1], so A does as well.                         

* **Question and category.** T. Stanford's Question 2.13 in T. Ohtsuki (ed.), *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4(2002), printed 408 / actual PDF 36, asks separately whether the rational restriction kernel on actual free complements is nontrivial and whether it spans together with Milnor products. The actual 200-page source was acquired; only the relevant full page was read. The target does not resolve the whole classification question. The rank in its F_k definition is automatic if the actual exterior group is free: Mayer–Vietoris for the exterior and k contractible arc tubes, with k annular overlaps in the ball, gives H_1(E)=Z^k.

* **Complete degree-two functions.** Jean-Baptiste Meilhan, arXiv:math/0402036v2,10 November 2004; journal J. Knot Theory Ramifications 14(2005),665–687. ActualPDF315045B, SHA-256 8b88fa5ea5df29a3b4a16934e5ade6bab37269462ace0d43bd15bff265f9a3b7. Definition 1.1 is smooth upward, ordered, fixed-boundary string links without height monotonicity or an extra framing. Definition 1.2 uses all double points. §2.1 defines precisely the component Conway/Casson coefficient and the plat correction V_2 used as w. Proposition 2.1 establishes its finite type. Theorem 2.4 is a formula for every function v(sigma), including all pair-linking quadratic products and distinct-index triple terms; it is stronger than merely a list of graded symbols. Its proof explicitly describes the same spanning family. The two-strand degree-at-most-two space therefore has the six coordinates used by the target.

* **Concordance and knot identities.** Meilhan and Akira Yasuhara, arXiv:0904.1527v1,9 April 2009, actualPDF §2.1.4 and §5.1 (PDF 4, 22), define all multi-indices including repetitions and relative string-link concordance, and explicitly state concordance invariance of Milnor invariants, crediting A. * **Actual free knotted-strand witness.** João Miguel Nogueira, Coimbra preprint14–09, received27February 2014, actual 16-page institutional PDF at https://www.mat.uc.pt/preprints/ps/p1409.pdf , SHA-256 455b0187bbf9b8214022236ac7a949ecdc5e3a13f7e92aec802ed6d9f9f6d98b. Personally read the complete appendix on printed 14–15 and visually inspected the complete Figures 9–10. It defines actual free tangles by free complement group/handlebody exterior, constructs a trefoil arc with unknotting tunnel, slides the tunnel endpoints to the ball boundary and identifies the other capped strand as T(3,−4). It expressly gives handlebody complements before and after the slide. The main essential-surface theorem and the later arXiv edition with missing artwork are not substituted for this input. Individually knotted capped strands are not the same as an isolated local-knot summand. The target's ordinary marking/smoothing argument is assessed independently.

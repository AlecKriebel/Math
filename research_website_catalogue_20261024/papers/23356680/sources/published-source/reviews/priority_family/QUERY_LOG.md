## Outcomes and followups

- Scope/current-source queries led to the full MVW arXiv v1 PDF and OWR report, the author bibliography, publisher endpoints, ETH repository record, and Crossref publication metadata. The current abstract lists only v1. Publisher final HTML, PDF, and XML each yielded an empty body. ETH yielded a metadata-only record.
- Operator-norm/local-minimizer queries led principally to LW Plateau/energy-area/regularity and calibration theory, plus older finite-distortion/extremal-integral literature. No earlier exact compact smooth-sphere counterexample was authenticated. The 1968 Reshetnyak extremal-integral article remains metadata-only; its title is not sufficient to identify its variational integrand or conclusions.
- KS/Hopf queries led to KS1993, Breiner-Mese, Sagman, and Markovic-Sagman primary texts. NPC or CAT(kappa) restrictions were explicitly checked. Finsler queries led to Li2014 and Nishikawa2010 fulltexts and a Nishikawa2015 publisher-preview discovery lead; these use other smooth/Finsler energies, so they are not interchangeable with KS on arbitrary complete targets.
- The exact-phrase searches also returned the UnsolvedMath dataset and notes reproducing the posed question. They are secondary collection/search material, not a new resolution or a contemporary openness certificate.
- Calibration followups retrieved Pankka2020 and Heikkila-Pankka-Prywes fulltexts. They authenticate established closed-form/comass comparison mechanisms, with different competitor assumptions; no unbounded-distortion sphere is supplied there.
- Full relevant portions of forward-citation candidates were inspected: LW canonical parameterizations; LW harmonic-disc regularity; Fitzi-Wenger Morrey lemma; Soultanis-Wenger homotopy; Creutz-Soultanis maximal surfaces; Ntalampekos-Romney polyhedral approximation. These concern parameterization minimization, regularity, area or uniformization, not the exact negative example or unrestricted KS theorem.

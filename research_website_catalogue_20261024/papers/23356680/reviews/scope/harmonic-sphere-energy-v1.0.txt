Abstract
We construct a compact smooth Riemannian sphere and a smooth homeomorphism from the
round sphere which locally minimizes Reshetnyak energy against every same-trace Sobolev com-
petitor, but has essentially unbounded inﬁnitesimal distortion. This gives a negative answer for
the Reshetnyak member of the general-energy harmonic-sphere question posed in the inspected
2025 texts of Meier–Vikman–Wenger. Scalar calibration on a ﬂat belt and an exact two-form
calibration on conformal caps prove the full local comparison. The map does not minimize en-
ergy in its homotopy class and is not Korevaar–Schoen harmonic. Separately, an angular-stress
argument gives the nonsharp bound 4 +
√
17 for locally Korevaar–Schoen-minimizing spheres
into complete metric spaces; the sharper classical SL(2)-minimal bound also applies. Classical
ingredients are credited; ﬁrst priority is not claimed.
1 Question, energies, and exact conclusions
Question 1 of Meier’s Oberwolfach contribution [ 2, p. 1625], also Question 1.9 of Meier–Vikman–
Wenger [1], asks whether harmonic spheres are inﬁnitesimally quasiconformal in its stated metric
setting. The energy is ﬁxed from a class including Reshetnyak and Korevaar–Schoen energies [ 1,
Deﬁnition 3.1]. We use the precise neighborhoodwise deﬁnition: each source point has an open
neighborhood U such that, for every Lipschitz domain Ω ⋐U and every v ∈W 1, 2(Ω,X ) with the
trace of u|Ω , the chosen energy of u|Ω is at most that of v [1, Deﬁnition 6.1]. Competitors may
leave the reference image and need not be continuous or homotopic to the reference map.

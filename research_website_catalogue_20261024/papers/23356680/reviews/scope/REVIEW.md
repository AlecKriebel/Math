## Exact claim and contribution

The main construction is a compact smooth Riemannian sphere X and a smooth homeomorphism u from round S² which locally minimizes **Reshetnyak** energy against every same-trace W¹,² competitor, yet has essentially unbounded infinitesimal distortion. Smooth homeomorphism does not mean diffeomorphism: the inverse need not be smooth at the compressed equator. The ratio 1/t² exceeds every finite bound on positive-area bands; this is not only a defect on the measure-zero equator.

The quantifier is neighborhoodwise: each point has U such that every Lipschitz Ω compactly contained in U permits comparison with all target-valued W¹,² maps of the same trace. Competitors may leave the image chart, be discontinuous, or have different topological behavior. The scalar McShane calibration and exact two-form trace-gluing comparison are the mechanisms that address this full class. Target compactness/smoothness supply geodesicity, local quadratic isoperimetry and null-homotopy of all sufficiently small-diameter continuous spheres.

This is a counterexample for the Reshetnyak member of the admissible general-energy question. It is not a counterexample for ordinary Dirichlet/Korevaar–Schoen energy or for global energy minimizers in a homotopy class. The paper explicitly exhibits failure of both stronger/different minimizing properties.

The separate complete-target KS theorem uses angular stress and vanishing of a holomorphic quadratic differential on S². It gives a self-contained nonsharp constant 4 + √17; stress-zero also implies SL(2)-minimality, so the older sharper 2√2 + √6 bound applies. Neither a new optimal distortion constant nor classical smooth-target conformality should be presented as the contribution. General metric tangent norms can be balanced without being Euclidean, so bounded distortion does not mean conformality in that setting.

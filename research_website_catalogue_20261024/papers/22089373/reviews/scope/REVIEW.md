## Exact scope that a page must preserve

1. **Fixed graph and rooting convention.** The positive class is binary, LSA-rootable and already-simple under one-step root deletion/suppression retaining reticulation arrowheads. It excludes silently choosing a hidden rooted refinement or changing the full graph by exhaustive cleanup. Leaf restrictions/displayed networks can have their separately defined cleanup. Strong tree-child means every admissible rooting is tree-child, with at least one admissible rooting. Weak means some admissible rooting. Rooted, weak and strong are distinct predicates.
2. **Model.** Uniform-root, four-state, displayed-tree mixture JC uses edge multipliers x in (0,1) and inheritance probabilities in (0,1). This corresponds to positive finite JC edge lengths; it does not cover negative Fourier eigenvalues, endpoints, general substitution models, NMSC/ILS, or cross-site rate mixtures. Root movement transports effective products; the theorem concerns semi-directed topology, not root location.
3. **Containment and identifiability.** The directed relation requires a source-relative full-dimensional regular local image neighborhood contained in the target image. It does not presuppose a target parameter section or equal target dimension. The positive theorem classifies that relation by equal labelled reduced bridge trees and blob isomorphism/ordinary triangle redirection; the conclusion rules out proper one-sided cases in the specified class. Generic topology identification is outside a proper algebraic exceptional set and within that class. The everywhere-positive assertion is the bridge-cut rank criterion, not the entire topology theorem.
4. **Physical quotient.** Positive cut factorization recovers projective local tensors modulo the full incidence-scaling group. The local quotient and physical image are different objects. A normalization slice need not itself have every physical arm in (0,1), and physical bridge multipliers are not identified. The full physical arm relation, not an omitted-arm internal map, is the relevant classification interface for independent math review.
5. **Triangle meaning.** Ordinary redirection changes reticulation placement/heads inside a triangle while preserving the labelled underlying graph, attachment locations and all outside heads. It is not pendant transfer. A common full-dimensional regular germ is weaker than equality of entire positive images; particular distributions can distinguish orientations.
6. **Sharpness.** The Omega construction supplies, for every n >= 4, a triangle-free weak-but-not-strong pair with regular full-dimensional overlap of dimension 2n+1. The Theta family has triangles and dimension 2n. This blocks replacing the whole strong class by the whole weak class. It does not characterize every individual ambiguous weak graph or establish strong tree-childness as logically necessary for every identifiable graph.
7. **Computational boundary.** The fixed finite atlas is load-bearing. First-principles graph construction, physical port arms, independent incoming roles, all relevant completions, honest coefficient-sign certificates, exact rank witnesses and restoration transports must match the theorem's relation. Bounded probes reduce arbitrary subdivisions to finite interfaces; a finite small-case check without this reduction would not establish the universal theorem. The reconstruction assumes a 4^n-entry exact distribution and disregards algebraic bit complexity; it is not an efficient finite-sample method in n.

                                                Abstract
          We construct a smooth Fano ﬁvefold with a nondivisorial global entropy-minimizing valu-
      ation and a nontrivial canonical ﬁrst degeneration. The example is the product of an explicit
      quadric blowup with the ﬁrst Hirzebruch surface. Its two positive valuation weights are
      speciﬁed by exact moment equations; an elementary resultant argument, using the classical
      Hermite–Lindemann theorem, proves that their ratio is irrational. The associated graded
      anticanonical ring identiﬁes the ﬁrst central variety, which is singular although the original
      ﬁvefold is smooth. Global optimality follows from the established optimal-degeneration and
      soliton theorems after checking the product ﬁltration and normalization. The threefold fac-
      tor and its optimal degeneration are due to Miao–Wang. No claim of novelty or historical
      ﬁrstness is made.


1    The construction and its target
Blum’s discussion of canonical Fano degenerations asks for explicit examples whose ﬁrst central
variety diﬀers from the original variety, and in particular for one with a nondivisorial minimizing
valuation [1, pp. 888–889]. The construction below satisﬁes both requirements. We work over
C. By a divisorial real valuation we mean c ordF , where F is a prime divisor over the variety
and c > 0 is real. An irrational rescaling alone therefore does not prove nondivisoriality.
    Earlier explicit nontrivial ﬁrst centers include Li–Li’s SO4 -compactiﬁcations [3, §6]. Their
second example has a two-component optimizer; we have not veriﬁed irrationality of its direction.
Miao–Wang’s divisorial threefold factor already appears in their January 2024 ﬁrst preprint.
We cite the revised edition for precise theorem numbering. The present claim is an explicit
construction, not a priority certiﬁcate.

2    Geometry and the established factor degeneration
The quadric Q and its hyperplane section H are smooth. Dependent gradients for the two
equations of C ⊂ P3 would give (i + 1 − λ)xi = 0 for all i = 0, . . . , 3. Distinct coeﬃcients permit

at most one nonzero coordinate, contradicting x2i = 0. Thus C is smooth. On H ≃ P1 × P1
it is a connected (2, 2)-curve of genus one: connectedness follows from H 1 (H, OH (−2, −2)) = 0,
and adjunction gives its genus.
     Write h for the pulled-back hyperplane class and F for the blowup exceptional divisor. The
codimension-two blowup formula gives −KV = 3h − F . The ideal IC (2) is globally generated
by the second quadric equation and the products of u with the ﬁve coordinates. The graph of
these generators embeds the blowup into Q × PN , with second hyperplane class 2h − F . Hence
3h − F is ample. Also E ≃ H, since C is Cartier in H.
     These data put V in family 2.23(a) of [2]. Miao–Wang identify the ordE special degeneration,
its central variety Z, and its canonical weight coordinate s = k/m−1 [2, §3.1, Theorems 3.1–3.2].

Lemma 2. The ratio a/b is irrational.

Proof. Elementary integration, for t > 0, rewrites the two moment equations as

                           (a3 + a2 − 2a − 6)e3a + 16ae2a + 4a + 6 = 0,                            (3)
                           (b2 − 2)e2b + 3b2 + 4b + 2 = 0.                                         (4)

Here b2 ̸= 2, since the remaining coeﬃcient would be 8 + 4b ̸= 0. If b were algebraic, (4) would
make e2b algebraic, contrary to Hermite–Lindemann. Thus b is transcendental.
   Suppose a/b = p/q, with positive integers p, q, and write r = p/q, z = eb/q . Then

                                         A(b, z) = B(b, z) = 0,

where

                 A(T, U ) = (r3 T 3 + r2 T 2 − 2rT − 6)U 3p + 16rT U 2p + 4rT + 6,
                 B(T, U ) = (T 2 − 2)U 2q + 3T 2 + 4T + 2.

The polynomial R(T ) = ResU (A, B) ∈ Q[T ] has degree at most 6(p+q), by separate homogeneity
of the resultant. Its coeﬃcient in that degree is

                                 ResU (r3 U 3p , U 2q + 3) = r6q 33p ̸= 0.

Indeed these are exactly the highest T -degree parts of the two polynomials in the Sylvester
determinant. Thus R is nonzero. The common root z implies R(b) = 0, contradicting the
transcendence of b.

4    The real ﬁltration and its normalization
Let Am = H 0 (V, −mKV ) and Bm = H 0 (S, −mKS ). Künneth gives R(X)m = Am ⊗ Bm . For a
ﬁrst-factor section of E-order k and a toric character (i, j) ∈ mP ∩ Z2 , the raw weight is
                                         ak + b(j + m).
An ordE -adapted basis tensored with character lines is adapted to v. Irrationality separates
distinct (k, j); diﬀerent i remain linearly independent in the residue ﬁeld. Leading coeﬃcients
multiply without an additional cancellation. Therefore, as degree-graded algebras,
                               grv R(X) ≃ (grordE R(V )) # R(S),
where # is the equal-degree Segre product. The factor degeneration identiﬁes grordE R(V ) with
the anticanonical section ring of Z, after a common Veronese if needed. The Segre algebra is
ﬁnitely generated and its Proj is Z × S with anticanonical polarization.
    This is ﬁnite generation in the geometric real-ﬁltration sense: ﬁnitely many homogeneous
section generators with real weights. It is not a claim that the positive cone of the dense group
aZ + bZ is a ﬁnitely generated semigroup [7, Deﬁnition 2.2]. The ordinary E family and the
toric action on S, or an adapted ﬁnite anticanonical embedding with these real diagonal weights,
realize the special real degeneration with this central ring [5, §2.2, Geometric R-TC I]. Rational
directions approximate it by ordinary special degenerations; no such rational direction is asserted
to minimize H.
    Both transverse divisors have discrepancy one on the smooth X, so AX (v) = a + b. The
canonical anticanonical weight is consequently
                           ak + b(j + m) − m(a + b) = a(k − m) + bj.                           (*)
Thus the induced central parameter is precisely (aξE , b(0, 1)). In particular no unaccounted
character twist or sign change appears when applying the optimality criterion.


5    Global optimality and the canonical ﬁrst step
We spell out the analytic product step to avoid assuming a tensorization theorem for H-
minimizers. The polystability of (Z, aξE ) from [2], together with [7, 6], gives its weak soliton.
The toric factor has the soliton speciﬁed above. Use the Han–Li convention
           e−θϕ (ξ) MAn (ϕ) = e−ϕ ,     Ric(ω) − ω = −ddc θϕ (ξ),   MAn (ϕ) = ω n /n!,
with harmless constant normalizations of the canonical volume measure [5, (110)–(111), Re-
mark 2.45]. For factor potentials ϕZ , ϕS , put ϕ = ϕZ + ϕS , θ = θZ + θS . The binomial expansion
gives exactly
                               MA5 (ϕ) = MA3 (ϕZ ) ⊗ MA2 (ϕS ).
The factor equations multiply to the same equation on Z × S for the sum parameter. The
potentials are bounded on a resolution [6, Corollary 31, Proposition 32]; the product Monge–
Ampère measure and klt canonical measure have no mass on the analytic singular set. Hence the
equality extends from the regular locus as a weak equation. This also follows in the ﬁnite-energy
formulation of [6].
    By [7, Theorem 1.3], the central pair is weighted K-polystable, in particular semistable. Han–
Li’s criterion [5, Theorem 5.3], with their comparison of inﬁma [5, Theorem 1.5], now proves
that v minimizes H among all valuations on the original X. The special real degeneration,
its klt anticanonical central ring, ﬁnite discrepancy, and exact induced parameter were checked
above; no restriction to product-form competitors is made. Blum–Liu–Xu–Zhuang’s absolute
uniqueness theorem [7, Theorem 1.2] identiﬁes this valuation and Proj grv R(X) with the canon-
ical ﬁrst step. The central pair is already polystable, so its second step changes no underlying
variety. The smooth/singular distinction and Lemma 2 complete Theorem 1.

6    Scope and veriﬁcation
This is an explicit construction from the cited general theorems and Miao–Wang’s established
threefold example. We do not claim historical ﬁrstness, a new general optimal-degeneration
theorem, a three-dimensional nondivisorial example, or an exhaustive priority determination.
The statement resolves the speciﬁed construction request in dimension ﬁve without a product
exclusion. The accompanying archive preserves the original proof and corrections, primary-
source ﬁngerprints, independent adversarial analyses, and exact auxiliary checks. Numerical
brackets and ﬁnitely many resultants do not prove irrationality; the universal argument above
does. Successful software checks do not formally verify the geometric or analytic theorem inputs.
   AI tools were used extensively in developing, checking, and preparing this work. The reported
adversarial reviews were conducted by AI agents. This preprint is unrefereed; independent
human peer review and formal veriﬁcation have not been completed.

2. **Local multiplicity without a classification.** Let m distinct normals lie in one rank-two span. Choose forms x,y,x+c_i y, with c_i nonzero and distinct. The first two coefficient rows then satisfy y·row_x−x·row_y=0 over the fraction field, impossible for a basis of the rank-r derivation module. Therefore m≤d+1. The pairwise coprimality and degree comparison are explicit; no enumeration or freeness of a localization/deletion is presumed.

5. **Terminal-pencil lemma in arbitrary rank.** Let L={a,p₁,…,p_{m−1}} be a rich line and each p_i belong to no other rich line. Let E'=E\{p_i}, U=spanL and W=spanE'.

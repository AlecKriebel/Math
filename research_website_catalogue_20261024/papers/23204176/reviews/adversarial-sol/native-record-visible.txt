Rational Hodge classes on mixed products of K3 moduli spaces
Authors/Creators
Kriebel, Alec
Description
Combining OpenAI's rational Hodge theorem for arbitrary mixed products of projective K3 surfaces with Bülles' Chow-motive decomposition, this note records rational Hodge in every codimension for finite mixed products of the smooth projective K3 moduli spaces covered by that decomposition. Bases may differ or repeat. The factors include Hilbert schemes, stable twisted-sheaf moduli, and eligible moduli of Bridgeland-stable objects, subject to the exact smoothness, projectivity and stability hypotheses stated in the paper. The explicit pull-push proof checks codimensions and the non-fine, empty and zero-dimensional cases. The contribution is an assembled corollary of the cited results, with prior conditional transfers by Arapura; it supplies no new proof of the base K3 theorem, no new motivic reduction, and no first or substantial independent novelty claim. The base input is the public OpenAI Math Release snapshot adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a, accessed October 6, 2026: https://github.com/openai/math/tree/adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. Manuscript cover dates do not establish earliest public priority. No assertion is made about arbitrary K3^[n] deformations, singular or semistable moduli, abelian-surface moduli, integral or generalized Hodge, or finite-dimensional motives. AI tools were used extensively in research, drafting and verification. This preprint has not undergone conventional human peer review or refereeing; automated adversarial reviews are not human peer review. The supplementary exact finite checks certify only the stated algebraic computations; neither they nor the upstream Clifford Lean module formalize the full Hodge theorem.

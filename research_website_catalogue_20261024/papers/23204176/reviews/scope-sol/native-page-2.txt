    Arapura had already proved conditional Hodge transfers to Hilbert schemes and K3 sheaf mod-

Theorems 7.4, 7.8]. Bülles supplies the precise unconditional Chow-motive splitting for twisted
sheaves and the eligible Bridgeland moduli [3, Theorem 0.1]. The earlier OpenAI quadratic-locus
companion already records the moduli and self-power application on its restricted locus [5, Corol-

theorem [6], also yields the unrestricted-base self-power application. We record the mixed-moduli
assembly: the extra mixed-base quantiﬁer is supplied by [7], and the reduction itself is inherited.

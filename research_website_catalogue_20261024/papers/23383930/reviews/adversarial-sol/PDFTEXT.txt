         Maximal norm discontinuity for a bounded-region
                    continuum fermion interaction
                                           Alec Kriebel

                         Research note, version 1.0 — 14 October 2026


                                              Abstract
         For continuum fermions in three dimensions, we give one ﬁxed smooth compactly supported
     pair potential and one ﬁxed bounded interaction region for which the Heisenberg orbit of a
     ﬁxed annihilation operator is discontinuous in operator norm. Explicit ﬁnite-particle Slater
     states and times tending to zero witness norm diﬀerences tending to the maximal value 2.
     The construction works both with diagonal density pairs and with normal ordering, without
     ultraviolet smearing. A ﬁxed-cutoﬀ commutator estimate, a Slater second-moment identity,
     and a two-sector comparison supply a self-contained proof. Continuity on every ﬁxed vector
     and in every ﬁxed normal state remains valid.

Question and scope. Oberwolfach Report 7/2025, printed pp. 365 and 371 [1], distinguishes
pointwise operator-norm continuity from strong-operator continuity and asks for a potential and
states violating the former for an unregularized interaction restricted to a bounded region. We
answer that existential question in dimension three, with kinetic energy on all of R3 . The Hartree
discussion preceding the fermionic question concerns a separate bosonic model. We impose no
ﬁxed-density or ﬁxed-energy restriction on the varying witnesses.
    Siebert’s earlier 2026 preprint [3] proves full-space norm discontinuity by a high-density cluster
and localized phase argument. That proof adapts directly to the bounded symmetric cutoﬀ kernel,
yielding the qualitative conclusion here. Its Proposition 2.6 also gives the maximal limit 2 for the
full-space constant-number reference. Our explicit free-Slater comparison transfers that limit to
the ﬁxed spatial cutoﬀ. The audit also records an additional energy-bootstrap derivation using
the earlier localized phase mechanism; neither cutoﬀ deduction is a theorem literally stated in
the cited preprint. Siebert’s 2025 construction [2] uses an extended algebra, with norm continuity
after time averaging. This note claims neither ﬁrst priority nor that the 2025 question remained
open when this proof was prepared. The accompanying veriﬁcation archive records the bounded
primary-source priority audit and the precise relation to earlier mechanisms.

Topology and boundaries.           For every ﬁxed vector η, any self-adjoint H and bounded B obey
                ∥(eitH Be−itH − B)η∥ ≤ ∥B∥∥(e−itH − 1)η∥ + ∥(eitH − 1)Bη∥ → 0.
For B = a(f ), this also implies Tr(ρDt∗ Dt ) → 0 for every ﬁxed positive trace-class state ρ of trace
one, by its spectral decomposition and the uniform bound 4. The witnesses in the theorem vary
with N . The result concerns the chosen ball and potential; it does not assert all potentials or
regions, ﬁxed-density thermodynamic behavior, or CAR non-invariance. The packing exponents
−1/2 + 1/d and −1 + 2/d do not settle d = 1, 2.

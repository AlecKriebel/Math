## Exact claim and boundaries

For each integer d ≥ 2, the paper constructs a connected open quasi-conical domain in Rᵈ. Here quasi-conical means containing Euclidean balls of arbitrarily large radius. The Dirichlet Laplacian is the operator of the closed energy form on H₀¹, without a smooth-boundary assumption. It has a complete orthonormal eigenbasis, zero kernel, and vanishing absolutely continuous and singular continuous subspaces. Its spectrum, essential spectrum and the closure of its positive point spectrum are all [0,∞).

The construction uses expanding cubes and positive shrinking windows in common faces. Both widths are chosen inductively. It is an existential result in this family, not a statement for arbitrary preassigned cube sizes, smooth boundary, positive-length passages or other boundary conditions. No quantitative aperture bounds are provided. Pure point spectral type must not be described as compact resolvent, discrete spectrum, or an eigenvalue at every nonnegative energy.

The essential extra step is completeness. At each finite stage, whole isolated finite spectral clusters are transported with summable projection-norm error. A growing family repeatedly approximates dense cube-supported probes. Finite limiting invariant spaces yield eigenvectors, and exhaustion of the probes rules out any orthogonal continuous part. Density of selected eigenvalues, preservation of a single mode, or trace-class removal of the absolutely continuous part alone would not establish this conclusion.

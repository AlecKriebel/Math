        Pure point Dirichlet spectrum on quasi-conical domains

                                       Independent researcher

                                                   Abstract
         For every dimension at least two, we construct a connected open quasi-conical domain whose
     Dirichlet Laplacian has a complete orthonormal eigenbasis, although its spectrum and essential
     spectrum are the entire nonnegative half-line. Its positive eigenvalues are dense, zero is not
     an eigenvalue, and both continuous spectral subspaces vanish. We use the expanding-cube and
     shrinking-window geometry of Krejčířík and Lotoreichik, choosing both cube sizes and positive
     apertures inductively. The additional construction transports a growing family of complete ﬁnite
     spectral clusters with summable norm errors. A repeated dense test family proves completeness
     and excludes singular continuous spectrum. This answers the existential spectral-type question
     in their discussion and the corresponding Oberwolfach contribution.

1    The question and the precise result
An open set is quasi-conical if it contains Euclidean balls of arbitrarily large radius. On any open
Ω ⊂ Rd , let AΩ be the nonnegative self-adjoint Dirichlet Laplacian deﬁned by the closed form
                                          Z
                               qΩ [u] =        |∇u|2 ,       dom qΩ = H01 (Ω).
                                           Ω

This deﬁnition imposes no regularity assumption on the boundary. Krejčířík and Lotoreichik [1] con-
structed connected quasi-conical domains with an embedded eigenvalue and with no absolutely con-
tinuous spectrum. Their Section 5 asks whether the singular continuous part can also be removed.
Lotoreichik’s Oberwolfach contribution [2, pp. 2077–2078] poses the same existential question; it is
repeated in [3, Open Problem 1, PDF p. 16].

Theorem 1. For every integer d ≥ 2 there is a connected open quasi-conical set Ω ⊂ Rd , consisting
of expanding cubes joined through positive shrinking windows in their common faces, such that
A = AΩ has a complete orthonormal eigenbasis and

         σ(A) = σess (A) = σp (A) = [0, ∞),              ker A = {0},     Hac (A) = Hsc (A) = {0}.

Both the cube widths and the window widths are chosen in the construction.

    Pure point spectral type means completeness of the eigenvectors. It does not assert that ev-
ery point of [0, ∞) is an eigenvalue. The theorem concerns zero-thickness interface screens with
open windows. It makes no assertion for arbitrary preassigned cube sizes, smooth boundaries, Neu-
mann conditions or positive-length connecting passages; no eﬀective numerical aperture bounds are
supplied.
    The geometry, freedom to choose future cube sizes and ﬁnite-window continuity below come
from [1, Sections 2.2 and 3]. Whole spectral families already handle multiplicities in [4, Proposi-
tion 1.4]; see also the explicit warning in [1, Remark 3.1]. The additional step here is the exhaustive
geometric transport and its completeness estimate. Neither ﬁnite-cluster perturbation theory nor
diagonalization on ﬁnite reducing subspaces is claimed as a new abstract principle.

6    Spectral support and the zero endpoint
Nonnegativity gives σ(AΩ ) ⊂ [0, ∞). For λ ≥ 0, choose ξ ∈ Rd with |ξ|2 = λ and χ ∈ Cc∞ (B1 (0))
of L2 norm one. With cube center cn and rn = an /2, put
                                 un (x) = rn−d/2 χ((x − cn )/rn )eiξ·x .
These unit functions belong to Cc∞ (Ω) ⊂ dom AΩ . The product rule gives
                       ∥(AΩ − λ)un ∥2 ≤ rn−2 ∥∆χ∥2 + 2|ξ|rn−1 ∥∇χ∥2 −→ 0.
Their supports lie in disjoint cubes, so they are orthonormal and weakly null. Weyl’s criterion puts
every λ ≥ 0 in the essential spectrum. Completeness identiﬁes the spectral set with the closure of
the eigenvalues. Finally, a zero eigenfunction has zero weak gradient and is constant on connected
Ω. Its inﬁnite volume excludes a nonzero L2 constant. This proves all assertions of Theorem 1.


Veriﬁcation, priority and availability
The accompanying source and veriﬁcation archive preserves the original proof and dated audits, in-
dependent geometric and spectral reconstructions, bounded primary-source priority searches, their
adversarial challenge, exact ﬁnite diagnostics and ﬁle-integrity checks. Those computations do not
formally verify the inﬁnite-dimensional theorem or compute admissible apertures. An explicitly
corrected source-reading error in one audit is preserved with its superseding addendum; it did not
change the theorem.
    The priority searches located no earlier full matching result. They do not certify historical
ﬁrstness or worldwide current openness. Some older full bodies, a restricted ﬁnite-dimensional gap-
transport note, unindexed work and nonpublic material remained outside the comparison. The
dated question and substantive antecedents are credited above; the claim is the complete construc-
tion proved here. The research record is repository PR 765. This note and its authored supporting
material are released under CC BY 4.0.

                                                        Abstract
          Starting from the recently announced three-dimensional noninjective Keller map, a rank-
      compressed homogenization of a 13-variable stable model gives a 22-variable cubic homogeneous
      noninjective Keller map. The de Bondt–van den Essen symmetric reduction then gives a 44-
      variable homogeneous quartic Hessian-nilpotent polynomial with 538 monomials and an exact
      collision. Expanded potentials and collisions are supplied as machine-readable exact certificates.
      For reuse we also record a normalized six-variable gradient lift. A post-release priority audit
      found that Cassidy had already executed the equivalent six-dimensional symmetric transport;
      no novelty is claimed for that construction. An appendix preserves the uniform rational collision
      from the archived first exploration while crediting the earlier, stronger monodromy theorem for
      the full weighted-lift family.

Verification disclaimer. The author is a complete amateur and cannot independently verify
these claims. This is an experiment in the limits of AI-assisted mathematics, not an established
result. Every argument, attribution, and novelty claim requires independent expert review. Exact
checks are evidence, not peer review.

2    Rank-compressed homogenization
Lemma 2. Let

                     Ψ(X) = X + H2 (X) + H3 (X) : Cn → Cn ,             det JΨ = 1,                (7)

where Hd is homogeneous of degree d. Suppose H3 = BK for a constant n × r matrix B and a
cubic homogeneous K : Cn → Cr . Define
                                       (                        )
                          h(X, U, t) = tH2 (X) + t2 BU, −K(X), 0 .                    (8)

Then h is cubic homogeneous and Jh is nilpotent. If p 6= q and Ψ(p) = Ψ(q), then (p, K(p), 1) and
(q, K(q), 1) collide under W 7→ W + h(W ).

Proof. For an indeterminate s, a Schur complement in I + sJh gives
                                             (                     )
                          det(I + sJh) = det I + stJH2 + s2 t2 BJK
                                            = det JΨ(stX) = 1.                                     (9)

Thus the characteristic polynomial of Jh is T n+r+1 . At t = 1,

                           (X, K(X), 1) + h(X, K(X), 1) = (Ψ(X), 0, 1),                           (10)

which proves the collision statement.

   The standard homogenization [1] takes r = n. Lemma 2 retains only the constant coeﬀicient
span of the cubic components.

Theorem 3. The polynomial P is a homogeneous quartic in 44 variables with 538 monomials and
nilpotent Hessian. The map Z − ∇P(Z) is noninjective. Moreover,

                              ∆m P m = 0 (m ≥ 1),          ∆m P m+1 6= 0                           (20)

for infinitely many m.

Proof. Only the last assertion remains. Zhao [6] proved that Hessian nilpotence is equivalent to
∆m P m = 0 for all m ≥ 1, and that the formal inverse of Z − t∇P is governed by

                                        m≥0

Eventual vanishing would make the inverse polynomial, contradicting the explicit collision.

The full hashes are in MANIFEST.md. The symbolic verifier checks the Hessian congruence, the
rank-eight factorization, identity (9), exact nilpotence guards, and both collisions. A dependency-
free checker reads only the JSON, differentiates both sparse potentials, and verifies the six-variable
identity linear part and both finite fibers. A separate Node.js checker independently implements
exact rational complex arithmetic with JavaScript BigInt and repeats the exported-certificate
checks. Neither certificate checker is a stand-alone proof of Hessian nilpotence.
    The six-variable result is an explicit instance of Meng’s known reduction, not a new reduction
theorem. Cassidy’s public repository commit [9], authored at 14:46:10 UTC on 20 July 2026, had
already executed the equivalent de Bondt–van den Essen/Meng transport in C6 , checked symmetry,
and transported the same three-point fiber. Our determinant-one, identity-linear formula is a
normalized presentation, not an independent discovery. The first version of this note failed to find
that artifact and overstated this part of its novelty claim.
    Zhang [7] left the Vanishing Conjecture consequence existential. Our earlier note [10] gave a
54-variable certificate. That note is now an archival derivation subsumed by the present paper.
Thompson [8] posted a 24-variable cubic homogeneous reduction at 03:29:42 UTC on 21 July 2026.
His work contains the same rank-compression principle in executed form and has priority for that

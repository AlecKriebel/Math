                             An Exact Full-Dimensional K3P
                              Tree–Theta-Trinet Collision
                                                 Alec Kriebel

                                                 August 2026


                                                   Abstract
          We give an exact negative answer to the high-level K3P trinet question: the K3P analogue of
      the high-level trinet inequality fails already for a binary semi-directed strict level-two theta trinet
      with a genuine nontrivial 3-blob. The comparison model is a three-leaf tree. At an explicit point
      in the quartic number field Q(h), where h = 5−1/4 , every network and tree edge lies strictly inside
      the stated stochastic parameter space Θ0 , every transition probability is strictly positive, and
      both inheritance probabilities equal 1/2. A sixteen-entry Fourier factorization proves equality
      of all sixty-four Fourier coordinates and hence all sixty-four leaf-pattern probabilities. The
      explicit closed-form witness lies on the boundary of the smaller cone in which every continuous-
      time K3P substitution rate is strictly positive, but this is not a boundary of Θ0 . We further
      compute an exact nonzero 15 × 15 Jacobian determinant, proving that the fixed theta-trinet
      image contains an ordinary open neighborhood in the 15-dimensional affine space of consistent
      three-leaf group-based Fourier coordinates, qAAA = 1. Finally, a real-analytic implicit-function
      argument, whose Jacobian and tangent data are verified exactly, shows that the collision persists
      in the strict continuous-time K3P rate cone. A dependency-free verifier reconstructs the topology,
      parameters, collision, Jacobian, and the algebraic hypotheses and tangent signs used in that
      analytic argument.


1    Context and statement of the result
Brits, Holtgrefe, van Iersel, and Martin proved the level-one K3P trinet inequality in their Lemma 4.2,
proved arbitrary-level trinet inequalities for JC and K2P in Lemmas 5.5 and 5.6, and used those
results to obtain their global JC/K2P tree–network distinguishability Corollary 5.8 [1]. In their
discussion, they asked whether the high-level trinet inequality and quartet distinguishability extend
to K3P, noting that the K3P level-one proof requires several invariants and does not directly support
the one-invariant induction used for JC and K2P.
    The construction below answers the high-level K3P trinet question negatively at strict level two.
It therefore also rules out a direct K3P extension of Corollary 5.8 on the same parameter space.
The JC and K2P results of [1] are unaffected.
Theorem 1 (Exact K3P tree–theta collision). There is a binary semi-directed strict level-two trinet
Nθ with a nontrivial 3-blob and a three-leaf tree T such that

                                   MK3P (Nθ ; Θ0 ) ∩ MK3P (T ; Θ0 ) ̸= ∅.

At the exhibited collision, every edge has all three nontrivial Fourier eigenvalues in (0, 1), every
transition probability is strictly positive, and both inheritance probabilities are 1/2.
    The result is stronger locally than the existence of one isolated collision.

Proposition 2 (Full-rank local overlap). At the explicit network parameters in Theorem 1, the
parameterization of the fixed theta-trinet topology has rank 15, equal to the dimension of the affine
group-based Fourier space qAAA = 1. Consequently its stochastic image contains an ordinary open
neighborhood of the common distribution within that affine space. Its intersection with the tree model
contains a relatively open neighborhood of the common point inside the tree model.
    The closed-form parameters have two zero continuous-time rate margins. Nevertheless, the
full-rank calculation permits an analytic perturbative strengthening with exactly verified algebraic
data.
Corollary 3 (Strict continuous-time collision). The same fixed tree distribution has a nearby preim-
age on the same theta-trinet topology for which every network edge is generated by a continuous-time
K3P process with all three instantaneous substitution rates strictly positive. The three comparison-tree
edges also have strictly positive K3P rates.

Scope. The construction shows that the K3P analogue of the high-level trinet inequality fails,
prevents a direct K3P extension of the JC/K2P global blob-detection corollary, and disproves
universal identification of a nontrivial 3-blob from three-leaf K3P distributions on Θ0 . It does not
by itself settle K3P quartet distinguishability, classify all tree/theta intersections, imply that generic
theta-network parameters are tree-equivalent, refute every possible level-two K3P identifiability
theorem, or classify observational equivalence for arbitrary networks.

8    Consequences, hypotheses, and reproducibility
The exact collision shows that a three-way reticulate blob is not universally detectable from three-leaf
K3P distributions on Θ0 . In particular, the local trinet step that proves the JC/K2P global theorem
in [1] cannot be extended verbatim to K3P. The full-rank result shows that this is not merely an
isolated singularity in distribution space: the network image is locally full-dimensional. The strict
continuous-time corollary further shows that the phenomenon is not confined to zero substitution
rates.

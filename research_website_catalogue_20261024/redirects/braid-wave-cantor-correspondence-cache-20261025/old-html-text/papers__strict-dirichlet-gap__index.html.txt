A short equality argument


The classical spectral identity expresses the Yang deficit as a sum of nonnegative terms.
Equality would make a coordinate times an eigenfunction a finite sum of eigenfunctions. Self-adjointness rules this out.
The strict Yang inequality and its quadratic roots give the strict gap bound. Repeated ground eigenvalues are handled directly.


The paper proves strict Yang inequality at 
every real threshold above the lowest eigenvalue
.




What was checked


Two fresh adversarial AI reviews checked the preprint; the final round found no actionable issue. Exact rational checks cover 1,312 certified box/index cases, including 600 zero gaps and 12 repeated-ground cases.


The 7,016 checks audit algebra and explicit spectra. They do not machine-prove the general analytic theorem.

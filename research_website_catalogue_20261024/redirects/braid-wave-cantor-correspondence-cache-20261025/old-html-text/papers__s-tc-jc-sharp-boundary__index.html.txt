Full-Dimensional Jukes–Cantor Ambiguity in Weakly Tree-Child Level-2 Networks

      
Alec Kriebel · Independent Researcher

      
An exact four-leaf construction and an all-taxa extension

    

  


  

    

      
Open paper (10 pages)

      
SHA-256

      
Source and exact verifiers

    


    

      

        
Abstract

        
We give two explicit four-leaf binary level-2 semi-directed phylogenetic networks whose labelled mixed graphs are nonisomorphic and cannot be related by redirecting their unique triangle, but whose open Jukes–Cantor model images share a regular relatively open subset of full dimension eight. Each has a tree-child rooting, while neither is strongly tree-child in the semi-directed sense that every admissible rooting is tree-child. Identical leaf substitution extends the construction to every number of taxa at least four, with common local model dimension 
2n
.

      


      

        
Main theorem

        

          
All-taxa full-dimensional ambiguity.
 For every 
n
 ≥ 4, there are two leaf-labelled binary standard semi-directed level-2 networks in 
W
TC
 but not 
S
TC
 whose topologies are nonisomorphic and not related by ordinary triangle redirection, but whose open JC model images both have dimension 
2n
 and share a regular relatively open subset of that dimension.

        

        
The four-leaf witness lies strictly inside the positive-definite stochastic parameter domain. Its two complete 256-coordinate Fourier tensors agree exactly in a quadratic number field, and rank-eight Jacobian minors are nonzero on both sides. A positive real-analytic inverse for identical cherry substitution proves the all-(n) extension.

      


      

        
Exact verification

        
Two separately implemented verifiers replay the graph, rooting, Fourier, algebraic-number, positivity, Jacobian, and substitution checks. The independent program uses only the Python standard library and shares no graph, Fourier, number-field, isomorphism, or rank code with the primary symbolic implementation. The repository includes deterministic source and reproducibility archives, a theorem-to-certificate crosswalk, and a SHA-256 manifest.

      


      

        
Scope

        

          
i

          

            
The strong-class theorem remains open here.

            
This manuscript does not claim a classification or counterexample inside the standard strongly tree-child class 
S
TC
. It proves failure over the full weakly tree-child level-2 class even modulo ordinary triangle redirection. Because the example also contains a triangle, it does not settle the triangle-free weakly tree-child subclass. Earlier draft claims of a global positive classification were withdrawn after adversarial audit and are not used.

          

        

        
The result concerns the displayed-tree JC model with uniform root distribution, edge Fourier multipliers and inheritance probabilities strictly between zero and one. It does not assert equality of complete stochastic images, a K2P/K3P extension, or an efficient inference algorithm.

Quantum information · Fixed-qubit Bell behaviors
Minimum Bell-Setting Complexity for Qubit POVM–PVM Separation
Proves equality of the shared-randomness-convexified fixed-qubit behavior sets when each party has two inputs, then gives an exact rational \(3\times2\) separation and minimum-setting classification. Exact Quantum Values and Permutation-Blind Maximizers in Cyclic Bell Inequalities

Stochastic reaction networks · Positive recurrence
Positive Recurrence for Single-Linkage Bimolecular Weakly Reversible Stochastic Reaction Networks
Proves that weak reversibility makes every population reachability set a closed communicating class. Under the additional one-linkage and bimolecular hypotheses, each nonabsorbing reachable class is positive recurrent for every positive rate vector. This removes the prior pure-species-complex condition; multiple linkage classes and higher molecularity remain open for recurrence.

Abstract
We construct a ﬁnite group G for which
p = 1009,

|G| = | Aut(G)| = 100952359 .

The group is the Baker–Campbell–Hausdorﬀ group of an explicitly speciﬁed rank-31 Lie ring
modulo 10091689 . Its nilpotency class is at most 846 < 1009. An asymmetric lattice forces
every automorphism of the ﬁnite Lie ring into an integral logarithm–exponential neighborhood.
This gives a bijection between the full automorphism set and the kernel of a linear derivation matrix. Exact independently implemented Python and C++ computations establish the
Smith valuations of that matrix. The resulting computer-assisted proof answers the existence
question aﬃrmatively. The construction and all exact certiﬁcates are supplied with separately
implemented veriﬁcation software.

Corollary 5.2. The set of parameters of N admitting a realization on N 0 contains a nonempty

Proof. The full parameterization has rank eight at the source point because the restricted gauge map

embedding. Its inverse is real analytic on its image. Consequently, substituting a cherry adds exactly
two model dimensions and preserves full-dimensional regular overlap.

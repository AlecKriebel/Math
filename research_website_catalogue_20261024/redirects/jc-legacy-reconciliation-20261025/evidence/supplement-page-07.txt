9     Interpretation limits

The theorem reconstructs topology, not all numerical parameters. In particular, bridge data de-
termine projective local tensor orbits and normalized eﬀective scales, not physical bridge lengths.
The ordinary triangle result is a common full-dimensional regular germ; it does not assert equality
of complete stochastic images or one orientation compatible with every distribution. No pointwise
classiﬁcation of the exceptional locus, richer substitution model, ﬁnite-sample inference result, or
complete weak-class classiﬁcation is claimed.

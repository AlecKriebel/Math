       Abstract 
       Gallagher's weighted-lift construction produces a three-dimensional Keller map of every generic degree  n  ≥ 3. This archived note studies one explicit seed and gives a uniform rational collision. Its monodromy proof is retained for provenance, but an earlier public artifact proves the stronger result for the entire family. 
       Family One explicit map for every n ≥ 3 Claimed monodromy Full symmetric group S n Certificate Uniform rational two-point collision 
     
     
       Scope and priority 
       Mikhail Szh's initial commit  predates this note and proves full S n  monodromy for the entire weighted-lift family. That also implies the trivial rational deck group in this specialization. The explicit seed and uniform rational collision are preserved in the archived Discovery 03 paper as a potentially new certificate, without an unconditional priority claim.

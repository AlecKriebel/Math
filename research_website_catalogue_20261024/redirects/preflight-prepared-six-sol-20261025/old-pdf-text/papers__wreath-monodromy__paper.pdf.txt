                                                  Abstract
         Let F : C → C be the newly announced noninjective polynomial map with constant Jaco-
                  3     3

     bian determinant −2 and geometric monodromy S3 . We determine the geometric monodromy
     of its second and third iterates:
                             Mon(F ◦2 ) = S3 o S3 ,      Mon(F ◦3 ) = S3 o S3 o S3 .
     The respective degrees are 9 and 27, and the group orders are 1296 and 13,060,694,016. On
     the target line (1, 2, s), exact primitive eliminants have one-edge Newton polygons at infinity,
     giving a 9-cycle and a 27-cycle. Each discriminant has a squarefree divisor of multiplicity one
     and hence gives a single deepest-block transposition; at level three, exact leading-coeﬀicient
     and reconstruction-denominator guards are included. Elementary group lemmas force the full
     wreath products. Postcomposition by diag(1/4, 1, 1) gives a noninjective unit-Jacobian map
     realizing the pair (9, S3 o S3 ). We also prove that every iterate F ◦m has a full 3m -cycle in its
     geometric inertia at infinity. Exact SymPy, dependency-free finite-field, PARI/GP, and GAP
     checks accompany the argument.

5   The group argument
Lemma 4. Let H ≤ S3 o S3 in its natural action on three blocks of size three. Suppose H contains a
9-cycle α, a single transposition β, and an element γ inducing a transposition on the blocks. Then
H = S3 o S3 .

Proof. The 9-cycle induces a 3-cycle on the blocks, and α3 acts as a 3-cycle inside each block. The
transposition β is supported in one block. Conjugating it by α3 gives a distinct transposition in
that block, so these elements generate the full S3 supported there. Conjugation by powers of α
gives the full S3 independently in all three blocks. Hence the base subgroup S33 lies in H.
    On blocks, α supplies a 3-cycle and γ a transposition, so the image of H is the top S3 . A
subgroup containing the full base and surjecting onto the top is the full semidirect product.

    The three inertia elements constructed above satisfy the lemma, so the slice monodromy is
S3 o S3 . Slice monodromy is a subgroup of generic monodromy; combining this with (5) proves the
level-two assertion of Theorem 1.

Lemma 5. Let H ≤ W3 contain a 27-cycle and a single transposition. If the image of H on the
nine bottom blocks is W2 , then H = W3 .
Proof. The 27-cycle α induces a 9-cycle on the bottom blocks, and α9 acts as a 3-cycle in each
such block. The elements τ and α9 τ α−9 generate the full S3 on one bottom block. Conjugation
by powers of α supplies independent S3 factors on all nine bottom blocks, so S39 ≤ H. This is the
complete kernel of W3 → W2 . Since H maps onto W2 , it follows that H = W3 .

   The image of Mon(F ◦3 ) on the bottom blocks is Mon(F ◦2 ) = W2 by the level-two argument in
Sections 3–5. Applying Lemma 5 gives

                       Mon(F ◦3 ) = W3 ,      |W3 | = 69+3+1 = 13,060,694,016.

This paper proves level three only. The accompanying bounded-memory level-four certificate was

sition and proves Mon(F ◦4 ) = W4 . Its localization and norm-to-inertia argument remain outside
this level-three paper.

     The closest public monodromy study states only Mon(F0 ◦F0 ) ≤ S3 oS3 and calls its exact group
a natural next computation [2]. A separate four-variable construction already claims a different
imprimitive degree-eight group of order 192 [3]. We therefore do not claim the first imprimitive or
first non-symmetric Keller monodromy. The claimed contribution is the exact second- and third-
iterate groups, the realizations (9, W2 ) and (27, W3 ), and the all-iterate full-cycle statement. A
timestamped 23-repository audit accompanies the artifact.
     The stronger arboreal assertion that every iterate has the full iterated wreath product remains
open: the present paper does not produce a new single transposition at every level. The separate
certificate in w4_search/RESULT.md settles W4 .

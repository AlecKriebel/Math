## Exact contents and hypotheses

Section 2 contains all three formal results.

- **Lemma 2.1.** For fixed \(n,d\), the subset \(A(n,d)\) of polynomial
  automorphisms \(F:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n\) satisfying
  \(\deg F\le d\) and \(\operatorname{Jac}(F)=1\) is Zariski closed.
  The proof first establishes constructibility, then uses the inverse
  degree bound \(\deg(F^{-1})\le d^{\,n-1}\) to prove Euclidean
  closedness.

- **Theorem 2.2.** Let \(X(n,d)\) be the affine algebraic variety of
  polynomial maps \(F:\mathbb C^n\to\mathbb C^n\) with
  \(\deg F\le d\) and \(\operatorname{Jac}(F)=1\). Every irreducible
  component \(Y\) either consists entirely of polynomial automorphisms,
  or its generic element is a counterexample to the Jacobian
  Conjecture. Moreover,
  \(\dim Y\ge n^2-1\). The dimension estimate comes from right
  composition by the determinant-one linear group.

- **Corollary 2.3.** If \(X(n,d)\) is irreducible, \(n\ge3\), and
  \(d\ge6\), then its generic element is a counterexample. The
  irreducibility of \(X(n,d)\) is a hypothesis, not a conclusion.

The abstract accidentally calls the ambient polynomial-map set
\(A(n,d)\) in one sentence; Section 2 consistently distinguishes the
closed automorphism locus \(A(n,d)\) from the Keller-map variety
\(X(n,d)\).

There is also a citation-level gap in the proof of Corollary 2.3 as
written. It cites only Alpöge's X post for the required counterexample,
whereas the announced displayed map has total degree seven, so that
reference alone does not justify the threshold \(d=6\). A known
dimension-three example of degree at most six repairs the existence
input, but the paper does not cite one. This issue does not create a
collision with the present program.

## Program relevance

Theorem 2.2 supplies a useful moduli-space observation: once an
irreducible component contains one counterexample, counterexamples form
a dense open subset of that component because the automorphism locus is
closed. It may motivate deformation searches, but it neither certifies
that two explicit maps lie on the same component nor replaces any
quartic exclusion or construction argument.

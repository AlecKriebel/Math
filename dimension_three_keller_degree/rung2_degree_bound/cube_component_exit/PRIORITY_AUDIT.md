# Priority audit

## Conclusion

**Worldwide novelty unresolved.**  This audit did not find the precise
statement
\[
\nabla(C+\ell^3+Q_2+L_1)\ne0
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
C+\ell^3+Q_2+L_1\text{ is a coordinate of }k^{[3]},
\]
nor the explicit inverse-degree-three consequence.  The search was not
exhaustive enough to support a novelty claim.

## Closest located literature

1. N. R. Ribeiro,
   “Classification at infinity of polynomials of degree 3 in 3
   variables,” [arXiv:2201.11026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.11026).
   The source text was inspected.  It classifies cubic polynomials at
   infinity in an isolated-singularity setting and includes triple-line
   leading forms, but no theorem located there states the present
   cube-leading submersion-to-coordinate implication or its inverse
   degree bound.
2. S. Kaliman,
   “Polynomials with general \(\mathbf C^2\)-fibers are variables. I,”
   [arXiv:math/9912058](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9912058).
   This is broad variable-polynomial prior art under general-fibre
   hypotheses, not an identified source for the elementary rank
   criterion used here.
3. T. T. Moh,
   “On the Jacobian conjecture and the configurations of roots,”
   J. Reine Angew. Math. 340 (1983), 140--212.
   This supplies the conservative plane low-degree fallback, not the
   coordinate lemma.
4. J. A. Guccione, J. J. Guccione, R. Horruitiner, C. Valqui,
   “Increasing the degree of a possible counterexample to the Jacobian
   Conjecture from 100 to 108,”
   [arXiv:2204.14178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14178).
   This supplies the plane floor used for \(d\le35\).
5. A. Vistoli, “The Jacobian conjecture in dimension 3 and degree 3,”
   *J. Pure Appl. Algebra* 142 (1999), 79--89.  This proves
   invertibility when the entire Keller map has degree at most three,
   not that one nonsingular cubic component is a coordinate.
6. J. Blanc and I. van Santen, “Automorphisms of the affine 3-space of
   degree 3,” *Indiana Univ. Math. J.* 71 (2022), 857--912.  This is
   adjacent classification work under automorphism or
   affine-plane-fibre hypotheses; no exact component criterion was
   located.
7. V. Shpilrain and J.-T. Yu, “Polynomial retracts and the Jacobian
   conjecture,” *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.* 352 (2000), 477--484.  This
   is a warning that a unimodular gradient alone must not be promoted
   to a coordinate theorem without the special cube-leading shape.

## 2026-07-26T08:09:09Z — full-text and current-source delta

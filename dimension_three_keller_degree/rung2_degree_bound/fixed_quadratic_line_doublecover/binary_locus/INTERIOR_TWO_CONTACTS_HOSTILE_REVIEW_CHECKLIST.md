# Hostile review checklist: squarefree-interior two-contact
\(\delta=2,\{1,1\}\) exclusion

1. Derive the contact cubic with independent coefficients \(A,D\).
   Verify that \(p\leftrightarrow q\) swaps them and that normalizing
   \(D=1\) does not discard an endpoint.
2. Recompute the stabilizer of the marked pairs
   \(\{0,\infty\}\), \(\{w,w^{-1}\}\).  Check the transformations
   \(a\leftrightarrow a^{-1}\), \(w\leftrightarrow w^{-1}\), and
   \((w,a)\mapsto(-w,-a)\), and confirm that \(u=w^2\) is the
   cross-ratio modulus only up to inversion.
3. Recompute the exact-open factors \(E_L,E_M,\kappa_\pm\) and the
   generic bracket gcd \(pq\).  Keep every incidence mutation and
   exceptional \(\{2,0\}\) mutation outside the theorem.
4. Rebuild the full \(E_7\) matrix.  Verify rank six and the exact
   internal pivot \(B\); do not specialize its rational basis at
   \(B=0\).
5. Reconstruct all six generic contact minors and the factorization
   through \(K_1,K_2,Q_0,\ldots,Q_5\).  Check numerator contents
   before computing resultants.
6. Independently recompute
   \(\operatorname{Res}_a(K_1,K_2)=-S_-S_+\), primitive contents,
   and irreducibility of both reciprocal sextics.  Over each sextic
   field, check fresh \(E_7\) rank, contact rank four, and the
   non-Veronese kernel.
7. Treat every reciprocal octic in
   \(\operatorname{Res}(K_1,Q_5)\) as an exact algebraic branch.
   Verify the two identities expressing \(E_L,E_M\) in terms of the
   octics; do not call them denominator artifacts.
8. Recompute the common-\(Q_i\) resultant gcd.  Over
   \(w^2+1\), verify the exact common polynomial \(a^2+1\).
   Over \(5w^4-6w^2+5\), verify the exact reciprocal quadratic in
   \(a\), split it in that field, and check that both roots are
   incidence boundaries.
9. Rebuild the alternate \(B=0\) basis from a pivot independent of
   \(B\).  Check its determinant and distinguish its genuine
   \(u=-1\) singularity from cleared-denominator factors.
10. Recompute the alternate-minor elimination and the primitive
    irreducible polynomial \(P_{16}\).  Verify the reduced rational
    formula for \(a\) modulo \(P_{16}\), all exact-open factors, and
    fresh contact rank five.
11. At \(u=-1\), derive \(B=-256a\) and hence \(a=0\).  Use a fresh
    \(E_7\) basis.  Check contact rank three and that the restricted
    Veronese conic is a nonzero square, giving one projective lift.
12. Recompute the explicit lift
    \((2p^2+q^2,q^2,0)\), \((x_5,y_5)=(-w,0)\), and verify both
    \(E_6\) and the vanishing top-only \(E_5\) coefficient.
13. Restore every lower coefficient.  Check the rank-six \(E_6\)
    solve, both coefficients of \([r]E_5\), the rank-three constant
    \(E_5\) subsystem, and especially the compatibility
    \(12v_0^2=0\).
14. Verify the complete \(E_4\) solve and then \(E_3=E_2=E_1=0\).
    Confirm structurally—not only by a determinant expansion—that
    the first column of the linear part is \(u_2\) times its third
    column, so the Keller constant is zero.
15. Audit geometric scope.  This result closes only the
    squarefree-interior two-ramification-contact
    exact-\(\delta=2,\{1,1\}\) leaf; it does not close the two
    doubled-nonbranch leaves or any \(\delta\ge3\) mutation.

This is a mathematical audit checklist.  Exact CAS output is not peer
review.

## Candidate result

For the frozen normalized leading data
\[
h=(p+q)^2,\qquad
P=hp^2,\qquad Q=hq^2,\qquad R=(p+q)^3,
\]
the complete \(E_7/E_6\) contact locus is the union of two conjugate
planes.  Both transverse plane charts are inconsistent at \(E_5\); their
punctured intersection forces \(\det L=0\); and the origin collapses to an
unconditional low-degree plane automorphism.  Consequently this one
canonical family contains no Keller counterexample.

This is a family-level exclusion only.  It is not a new total-degree lower
bound.

## Division-free reduction and chart denominator

The raw determinant gives
\[
[p^3r^3]E_6=4c^2.
\]
After \(c=0\), six displayed \(E_6\) pivots are checked individually to
have nonzero constant coefficients.  Their complete residual is
\[
16a(b+k)p^4qr+
\bigl(6au_3+(4b+3k)u_2\bigr)p^2q^4+
6u_3(b+k)pq^5.
\]
The next exact coefficients are
\[
-24(b+k)^3,\qquad -12a^2b,\qquad bu_2+6au_3.
\]
Over a characteristic-zero domain these force
\[
c=0,\quad k=-b,\quad a^2b=0,\quad bu_2+6au_3=0.
\]

The non-origin locus therefore has exactly two charts:

1. \(b=k=0,\ a\ne0,\ u_3=0\);
2. \(a=0,\ b\ne0,\ k=-b,\ u_2=0\).

The substitution \(r\mapsto r/s\) sends
\((a,b,k)\mapsto(a/s,b/s,k/s)\), leaves \(H_4\) and \(R\) unchanged,
and sends \(\det L\mapsto(\det L)/s\).  Hence normalizing the first
chart to \(a=1\) and the second to \(b=1\) loses no invertible linear
part and divides only by the chart-defining nonzero parameter.

## Chart checks

### Chart I

For \(a=1,b=k=0\), exact \(E_5\) coefficients force
\[
u_2=0,\qquad 2t_2=3v_3.
\]
The remaining \(E_5\) pivot block has full rank five and is replayed in
full.  Then
\[
[p^2r^2]E_4=12v_3.
\]
After \(v_3=0\), the remaining \(E_4\) pivot block has rank three and
forces column two of \(L\) to equal \(v_2/2\) times column three.
Thus \(\det L=0\), contrary to the Keller hypothesis.

### Chart II

For \(a=0,b=1,k=-1\), exact \(E_5\) coefficients force
\[
v_0=0,\qquad u_0=4t_1-2v_2,\qquad
u_3(t_1-v_2)=0.
\]
The remaining \(E_5\) block has full rank four and residual
\[
6u_3(\ell_8-t_0)pq^4+
3u_3(t_1-v_2)q^5.
\]

- If \(u_3\ne0\), the two factors vanish.  The rank-three \(E_4\)
  block then makes column one of \(L\) equal \(t_1\) times column
  three.
- If \(u_3=0\), put \(d=t_1-v_2\).  First
  \([p^3r]E_4=8(\ell_8-t_0)^2\), so \(\ell_8=t_0\).
  On \(d\ne0\), the localized \(E_4\) block has rank five; every
  displayed pivot is licensed by \(d\) or \(d^2\), and complete replay
  leaves
  \[
  [q^2r]E_3=12d^3,
  \]
  a contradiction.  On \(d=0\), the boundary \(E_4\) block has rank
  two and
  \[
  [p^3]E_3=-4(-\ell_6+t_0t_1)^2.
  \]
  The same linear factor kills the reduced determinant of \(L\).

These branches exhaust both normalized nonzero charts.

## The \(E_7\)-origin

At \(a=b=c=k=0\), the audit retains arbitrary binary
\(U_0,V_0,T_0\).  The full \(E_6\) block has six independent pivots and
forces
\[
A_r=0,\qquad B_r=2\ell_8q.
\]

If \(\ell_8=0\), all nonlinear terms are binary.  Since \(L\) is
invertible, a target-linear change isolates the nonzero \(r\)-column
and gives a triangular extension of a plane Keller map of degree at
most four; the unconditional low-degree plane theorem makes it an
automorphism.

If \(\ell_8\ne0\), the third component is
\[
\ell_8r+B_3(p,q),
\]
a coordinate whose inverse has degree at most three.  Straightening it
gives plane Keller fibres of degree at most \(3\cdot4=12<100\).
Moh's unconditional bounded-degree theorem, followed by the fibrewise
injectivity/Ax argument, again gives an automorphism.  Therefore the
origin contains no counterexample.

# AI assistance and verification disclosure

This project was produced with substantial assistance from frontier
language-model agents. The agents proposed case splits, derived symbolic
identities, wrote verification programs, searched public sources, and
drafted the exposition. A human researcher directed the program.

The work is not peer reviewed. Exact SymPy and PARI/GP checks are evidence
about the algebra encoded in the programs; they do not replace proof review,
do not guarantee that an encoded model matches every intended mathematical
hypothesis, and do not establish worldwide priority.

The theorem is intentionally narrow: it excludes the six frozen
exact-\(\delta=4\) families inside one quartic leading-shape row. It is not a
universal quartic exclusion or a new total-degree lower bound.

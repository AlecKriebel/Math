# Priority audit: vertical primitive cubic-pencil calculations

**Searched (UTC):** 2026-07-25T21:35:00Z.

This is source-specific evidence, not a guarantee of worldwide priority.
No person was contacted.

## Scope searched

The claims checked were the exact vertical-fibre reductions for a quartic
dimension-three Keller candidate with
\[
H_4=(z^4,zq,0)^T:
\]

- exclusion of vertical multiplicities one and two by homogeneous
  first-integral valuations;
- the two surviving cubic companions \(G_3=z^3,q\);
- exclusion of the complete nonvertical companion \(G_3=q\);
- exclusion of the \(G_3=z^3\), \(a\ne0\), \(\ell=0\), nontriple-\(q_0\)
  sublocus.

The last item is only a sublocus statement.  None of these calculations is
presented as an exclusion of the frozen row
`Q2-E1-A3-B1-D1-N1`.

## Queries and sources

Searches combined:

- `Keller map quartic fixed linear cubic pencil vertical companion`;
- `Jacobian quartic cubic pencil primitive vertical fibre`;
- `site:arxiv.org Keller map "cubic pencil"`;
- `site:mathoverflow.net Keller map quartic degree`;
- the displayed leading form `H4=(z^4,zq,0)`;
- the phrases `vertical primitive cubic pencil` and
  `fixed-linear cubic-pencil`.

The sweep covered arXiv and general scholarly indexing, with separate
site-restricted searches for MathOverflow and Terence Tao's blog.

The closest returned source was T. Shaska, *Graded Keller maps and the
Jacobian Conjecture*, arXiv:2607.20210.  It studies the announced
counterexample through a mixed-sign grading and a quotient surface.  It
does not classify ordinary quartic leading forms by a primitive cubic
pencil, derive the vertical-multiplicity congruences, or contain the
constant-minor exclusions encoded here.

Older results returned by the search concern homogeneous Keller maps,
rank-two Jacobian classifications, plane pencils, or general properness.
No checked source states the vertical companion lemmas in this directory.

One search result advertised a total-degree-three counterexample.  Reading
the source resolved the apparent conflict: that construction is in
dimension \(22\), not dimension three.  A separate page's “degree-four
map” refers to generic fibre degree four and explicitly has component
degrees \((12,11,4)\).  Neither changes the dimension-three total-degree
frontier.

## Result

No collision was found in the named sources.  This negative result is not
a priority guarantee.  The exact scripts certify only the encoded algebra,

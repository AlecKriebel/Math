## 2026-07-25T15:01:52Z

Searched the public web and arXiv for combinations of “Keller map”,
“dimension three”, “degree four”, “fixed linear divisor”, and
“Hilbert--Burch”.  No source located the marked-double fixed-linear
\(\delta=2\) exclusion proved here.

A potentially conflicting phrase, “explicit degree-four map”, was
checked against the primary public page:

- Alexis Gallagher, *The Jacobian counterexample, explained*,
  <https://jacobianfun.org/jacobian-explained>.  Its “degree four” is
  the **generic fibre degree**; the displayed component degrees are
  \((12,11,4)\), so it is not a total-degree-four Keller map.
- Christopher D. Long, arXiv:2607.18186,
  <https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18186>.  Its quartic in three variables
  is a Gaussian Moments Conjecture witness, not a Keller map.
- *An explicit degree-3 counterexample to the Jacobian Conjecture*,
  <https://rhicksrad.github.io/jacobian-degree3/>, is a
  Bass--Connell--Wright construction in dimension \(19\), not dimension
  three.

The false positive was retained here because it illustrates the mandatory
distinction among total degree, component degrees, and generic degree.

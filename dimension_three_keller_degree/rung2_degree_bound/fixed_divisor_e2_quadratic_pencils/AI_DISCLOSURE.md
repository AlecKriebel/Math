# AI assistance, verification, and review disclosure

This research package was produced with substantial assistance from
frontier language models.  AI systems contributed to mathematical
exploration, normal-form discovery, case organization, exact symbolic
programs, independent hostile replays, literature searches, and exposition.
The human researcher directed the program and is responsible for deciding
whether and how to disseminate the result.

The package is not peer reviewed.  Its exact computations establish facts
about the algebra and finite coverage ledgers encoded in the supplied
programs; they are evidence, not peer review and not a guarantee that the
mathematical encoding matches every intended hypothesis.

Independence was enforced at the internal-audit level.  In particular, the
clean-room bridge auditor was denied the candidate bridge, the root
exploration scripts, and the primary discrete-orbit and endpoint verifiers.
It independently reconstructed the frozen routing and the two discrete
`CO` quotients using a dependency-free exact sparse-polynomial engine.  It
found and required correction of two release-draft errors:

1. zero-companion branches prove that a Keller map is an automorphism; they
   do not prove that a constant-Jacobian map cannot exist there;
2. frozen coefficient pivots `C30`--`C44` are empty in the rank-two row,
   rather than entering the intrinsic-minor router.

Public-web priority searches were source specific.  They do not establish
worldwide priority, and no outside person was contacted on behalf of this
project.

# AI assistance, verification, and review disclosure

This research record was produced with substantial assistance from OpenAI
Codex agents.  The primary human researcher directed the program.  Agents
helped derive and audit the proof, search public sources, write exact
verification code, and prepare the manuscript.  In accordance with the
project's independent-research policy, no agent contacted any person.

The family-wide theorem passed a separate hostile-agent mathematical audit
and an independent source audit.  This is not peer review.  The exact
scripts are evidence about the algebra encoded in those scripts; they do not
verify the universal proof and do not replace expert review.

Gallagher has priority for the weighted-lift family and its generic-degree
theorem.  Brink has priority for the two-free-coefficient symmetric Galois
theorem.  MathOverflow answer 513470 and its linked Note 19 have priority
for the finite-range \(S_d\) computation in Gallagher's canonical tower for
\(3\le d\le13\).  The only candidate new contribution claimed here is the
uniform Brink--Gallagher corollary for every admissible seed and every
\(d\ge3\), together with its exact Keller root-field identification.

The work has not been peer reviewed.  Source-specific searches cannot
guarantee worldwide priority.

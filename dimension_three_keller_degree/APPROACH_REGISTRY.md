# Approach-Family Registry

Status labels: **active**, **blocked**, **banked**, **reopen condition**.

| Family | Mechanism | Status | Decisive gap or target |
|---|---|---|---|
| Quadratic Galois theorem | Apply Campbell--Razar--Wright to a separable degree-two extension | **banked; priority occupied** | Gives the universal exclusion immediately; not new |
| Quadratic singular-locus argument | Write \(L=K(\sqrt h)\), obtain \(g^2=h(F)\), and compare \(F(V(g))\) with \(\operatorname{Sing}V(h)\) | **banked; independently checked** | Supplies a short algebraic proof without compactification |
| Quadratic normalization/purity | Factor through the finite normalization and compare branch and missing divisors | **banked** | Valid only after using Galois transitivity; purity applies to the finite normalization, not directly to \(F\) |
| Naive deck involution | Extend the field involution to the affine source | **blocked** | The involution need not preserve the source open inside its normalization |
| Naive covering topology | Declare the complement of the nonproperness set simply connected | **blocked** | A hypersurface complement generally has meridian fundamental group |
| Homogeneous-piece Jacobian | Normalize \(F=L_0X+H_2+H_3+H_4\), expand \(\det JF\) into nine weighted identities, and constrain \(JH_4,JH_3\) | **active; several subcases banked** | \(H_2=0,\operatorname{rank}JH_4\le1\), invariant leading image line, primitive rank-one quotient pencils, and every degree-at-most-two target component are excluded |
| Global simultaneous triangularization for rank-one \(JH_4\) | Try to force one constant flag from the nine identities | **blocked by exact automorphism** | \((x+y^2,y+(x+y^2)^2,z)\) has rank-one \(JH_4\) but no common flag; reopen only with an extra invariant |
| Rank-one leading, projected cubic quotient | Project \(H_3\) modulo the constant image line of \(H_4\); use degree-seven first integrals and lower determinant identities | **primitive locus excluded; composite gcd-one locus excluded** | A counterexample requires either a fixed component in the projected cubic pencil or, in the fixed-component-free binary-composite case, a nonconstant gcd of \(J(P,Q),J(P,h),J(Q,h)\) |
| Vistoli compactification | Reconstruct the cubic proof and analyze the projective surface/pencil step for quartics | **stalled / reopen condition** | Full article proof was not accessible in the current sweep; reopen with the primary text or a new quartic compactification invariant |
| Generic-line curve geometry | Pull back a generic target line; combine complete-intersection genus, punctures, inertia, and the Jelonek locus | **proved working lemma; audited** | Turn the defect/genus identity into a quartic exclusion |
| Quartic conductor/adjunction | On a coprime-leading \((4,4)\) closure, combine \(\nu^*\omega_X=\omega_{\bar C}(A)\) with the Keller residue | **proved working lemma; audited** | Exploit local conductor semigroups beyond parity; generic degree three and \(s=2\) are the sharpest current profile |
| Quadratic-component exit | Straighten a quadratic submersion by a degree-two automorphism and apply the established plane bound fibrewise | **proved working theorem; audited** | Any quartic counterexample has no target-linear component of degree at most two |
| Primitive line-type leading net | Use relative algebraic closedness and vertical divisors to constrain the subleading third components | **proved conditional stratum; audited** | Only the forced \(L^4/L^3\) branch remains compatible with a counterexample |
| Line image implies primitive pencil | Infer relative algebraic closedness merely from projective line image | **blocked by exact counterexample** | \(H_4=(x^4,y^4,0)\); reopen only after splitting minimal pair degrees \(4,2,1\) |
| Genuine line image, type \((2,2)\) | Use primitive quadratic-pencil divisors, joint outer/companion moduli, and lower homogeneous determinant identities | **no-double-line locus, finite \(yz\)-chart, one infinity orbit, and rank-one open orbit banked; exceptional locus active** | For \(p=x^2,q=yz\), the finite mixed chart and \((a,c)=(0,0)\) are excluded.  For \(q=y^2+xz\), the unmarked finite mixed locus \(c(c^2-9)\ne0\) is excluded.  Remaining: companion infinity, the other outer-infinity moduli, three marked rank-one orbits, and rank-one \(c=0,c^2=9,\infty\) |
| Genuine line image, type \((1,4)\) | Use binary ramification minors, Hilbert--Burch splittings, and degree-six curvature | **ramification shapes banked; active** | Nonexceptional gcd degree is \(1\)–\(4\) with five exact splittings; degree \(5\) is exactly the \(L^4/L^3\) power fibre. | Conic image, type \((2,2)\) | Use the adjugate normal \(n=(q^2,-2pq,p^2)\), vertical divisors, Veronese syzygies, and canonical quadratic pencils | **entire genuine stratum excluded** | No- and unique-double-line pencils are impossible; two-double-line presentations are nonminimal and reclassify to \((1,4)\) |
| Conic double cover | Normalize the degree-two map of the minimal pencil, solve the ramified conic normal through \(E_8,\ldots,E_4\), and retain the affine relative-position moduli | **entire taxonomy row excluded; independently audited** | The zero tangent orbit is plane plus shear; the two-nonzero and both one-nonzero lower branches force a zero column pair or column in \(L_0\) |
| Birational rational-quartic leading curve | Use the gcd-one normal minors as a cubic Hilbert--Burch matrix | **unramified locus banked; ramified locus active** | A counterexample requires a nonconstant ramification divisor in the basepoint-free parametrization |
| Quartic curve-image taxonomy | Factor \(H_4=hA(p,q)\), with \(e+ab=4\) and outer image degree times parametrization degree \(b=\delta\nu\) | **complete raw taxonomy; audited** | Thirteen rows; the conic double cover is now closed, while most fixed-divisor \(e>0\) rows and ramified outer parametrizations remain |
| Nodal-cubic leading image | Use tangent Hilbert--Burch forms for \(H_3\), splitting according to whether the fixed linear divisor is transverse to or contained in the minimal pencil | **entire nodal row excluded; both branches audited** | The transverse branch reaches \(\det L_0=0\) in degrees six and five; in the scalar-aligned branch four division-free degree-seven certificates kill all complementary-variable nonlinear dependence, leaving a plane Keller block plus a shear |
| Cuspidal-cubic leading image | Use the ramified reduced normal and its degree-\((1,2)\) Hilbert--Burch syzygies, split by the position of the fixed linear divisor | **entire cuspidal row excluded; both branches audited** | The transverse branch forces \(\det L_0=0\). | Fixed-divisor conic | Put \(H_4=h\operatorname{Ver}(p,q)\), classify the quadratic divisor under the parabolic stabilizer of the minimal pencil, and solve the tangent-field branches | **entire taxonomy row excluded; audited** | The seven parabolic normal forms are exhaustive. | Fixed-cubic line image | Put \(H_4=h(p,q,r)(p,q,0)\), combine logarithmic valuations with the binary-cubic stabilizer and lower-syzygy trees | **entire taxonomy row excluded; both halves audited** | The nonbinary half reduces to two impossible \(h=pr^2\) orbits. | Fixed-linear line triple cover | Put \(H_4=r(A(p,q),B(p,q),0)\) with a completely general cubic cover and diagonalize the transverse Wronskian derivation | **transverse/nonbinary subrow excluded; audited; binary subrow active** | The eigenvalues \(4j-3\) and \(4j-2\) never vanish; both nonlinear normal components die and the plane-field exit applies |

| Symmetric-multiplication deformation | Vary Tao's slice and normalization in the \(\mathrm{Sym}^1\times\mathrm{Sym}^2\to\mathrm{Sym}^3\) model | **queued for Rung 3** | Determine whether the degree-seven presentation can cancel to total degree six or lower |

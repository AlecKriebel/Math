#### arXiv and primary literature

- Recent listings and keyword searches in
  [math.AG](https://arxiv.org/list/math.AG/recent),
  [math.AC](https://arxiv.org/list/math.AC/recent),
  [math.CO](https://arxiv.org/list/math.CO/recent), and
  [math.NT](https://arxiv.org/list/math.NT/recent) were checked for
  “Jacobian”, “Keller”, “polynomial automorphism”, “generic degree”,
  “monodromy”, and “Newton”.
- Trushin,
  [*Contracted divisors and Degree-Two Maps*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26390),
  posted 25 May 2026, explicitly treats degree as function-field degree,
  invokes the Galois theorem for degree two, and gives a separate
  branch-divisor obstruction in Theorem 4.3.3.  This conclusively occupies
  priority for a new degree-two exclusion.
- Shaska,
  [*Graded Keller maps and the Jacobian problem*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20210),
  was checked for low-total-degree classifications.  It studies grading
  structure; no universal total-degree-four exclusion was located.
- Long,
  [*Small counterexamples following the Jacobian counterexample*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.18186),
  was checked.  Its small degree/term counts concern the Gaussian-moment
  cascade or higher-dimensional reductions, not a total-degree-\(<7\)
  counterexample in dimension three.
- Migus,
  [*Generic degrees of real Keller maps with non-dense image*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.21572),
  explicitly invokes Campbell to exclude generic degree two.  Its real-image
  hypotheses do not yield a new complex total-degree bound.
- The characteristic-two preprint
  [arXiv:2607.20968](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.20968) was noted and excluded
  from the characteristic-zero program.
- The plane-bound sources
  [arXiv:2204.14178](https://arxiv.org/abs/2204.14178) and
  [arXiv:1605.09430](https://arxiv.org/abs/1605.09430), and
  Makar-Limanov's
  [arXiv:2106.06869](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.06869), were inspected as
  technique sources only.  No dimension-three total-degree table was found.
- Campbell,
  [*A condition for a polynomial map to be invertible*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01349234),
  Math. Ann. 205 (1973), 243--248, states on p. 248 that a nowhere-degenerate
  polynomial map cannot have degree two.
- Razar,
  [*Polynomial maps with constant Jacobian*](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02764906),
  Israel J. Math. 32 (1979), 97--106, Theorem 2, and Wright,
  [*On the Jacobian conjecture*](https://projecteuclid.org/journals/illinois-journal-of-mathematics/volume-25/issue-3/On-the-Jacobian-conjecture/10.1215/ijm/1256047158.full),
  Illinois J. Math. 25 (1981), 423--440, Theorem 3.7, establish the
  characteristic-zero Galois case in arbitrary dimension.
- Vistoli,
  [*The Jacobian conjecture in dimension 3 and degree 3*](https://ricerca.sns.it/handle/11384/2572),
  J. Pure Appl. Algebra 142 (1999), 79--89,
  [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0022-4049(98)00040-1), proves that a
  dimension-three Keller map of total degree at most three is an
  automorphism.  This already occupies the total-degree floor \(4\).

#### MathOverflow and current public constructions

- The newest Jacobian-conjecture questions and
  [the geometric-degree discussion](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/513440/geometric-degrees-of-counterexamples-to-the-jacobian-conjecture-in-dimension-thr/513470)
  were checked.  The latter records constructions for every generic degree
  \(d\ge3\).
- The 20 July
  [monodromy discussion](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/513387/galois-structure-of-the-new-counterexample-to-the-jacobian-conjecture-an-explic)
  already states that generic degree three is minimal by the Galois-case
  theorem.
- Gallagher's
  [public explainer](https://jacobianfun.org/jacobian-explained) was checked.
  Its advertised “degree-four” map has generic degree four, not total
  polynomial degree four; the displayed component degrees are
  \((12,11,4)\).  The weighted-lift construction realizes every generic
  degree at least three.

### Priority conclusions at this timestamp

1. Generic-degree-two exclusion and minimal generic degree three are
   conclusively prior.
2. The full generic-degree counterexample spectrum
   \(\{3,4,5,\ldots\}\) is already publicly realized.
3. The certified total-polynomial-degree lower bound \(4\) is already a
   corollary of Vistoli's 1999 theorem.
4. No checked source supplied a universal exclusion of total degree four, a
   dimension-three counterexample of total degree below seven, or a published
   dimension-three total-degree table.  This is not a guarantee of worldwide
   priority.

### 2026-07-24T23:00Z — line-section overlap audit

- Jelonek's 1993 paper
  [*The set of points at which a polynomial map is not proper*](https://doi.org/10.4064/ap-58-3-259-266)
  was checked at Proposition 12/Theorem 15.  It gives
  \[
  \deg S_F\le
  \frac{\prod_i\deg F_i-\mu(F)}{\min_i\deg F_i}.
  \]

  Arch. Math. 89 (2007), 385--389, was checked.  Its hypotheses apply when

  \(\deg S_F\ge2\) is prior.

  withdrawn.  Versions 4--5 are marked incorrect, and the withdrawn record
  must not be used as a theorem source.

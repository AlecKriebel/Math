# Five-attempt research record

All five substantive attempts were written on 3 October 2026. Source retrieval, prior-work checking, regression tests, review and packaging are not counted as research attempts.

1. **Negative-literal elimination on lattices:** proved the density-zero exceptional-set estimate and the exact joint-avoidance criterion; obtained a decision procedure with no positive relation atoms.
2. **One positive relation with negations:** derived exact fiber-identity tests, proved effective rational-function power tails, and combined these with the credited affine-gcd decision argument.
3. **Undecidability by interpretation:** proved the two-shift synchronization lemma and named-power multiplication formula, including the p=2 case; obtained exponent addition/divisibility but isolated the missing exponent graph or exponent-p-scaling relation.
4. **Beyond one positive atom:** proved a decision procedure for arbitrary polynomial-matrix systems with one shared power parameter, using Smith normal form and maximal-minor gcds, including negative constraints.
5. **Full normal form and multi-exponent barrier:** positively defined nonzero and the complement of directed R_p, reduced the complete existential target to independent-power linear systems, and proved the unbounded gcd obstruction to a naive univariate extension.

**Final result:** original problem unresolved, five of five substantive attempts used. The positive and negative fragments, interpretation lemmas and exact reductions are partial results, not a complete solution.

# Ordinary Banach–Mazur stability of von Neumann algebras

Active independent research effort. The core targets remain hypotheses pending dependency audit.

For every complex von Neumann algebra M, seek a number epsilon_M>0 such that any complex von Neumann algebra N with ordinary complex Banach–Mazur distance d(M,N)<1+epsilon_M, or d(M_*,N_*)<1+epsilon_M for the canonical preduals, is Jordan *-isomorphic to M. The threshold can depend on M.

The proposed proof is the composition of OpenAI family 295 ordinary bounded Hochschild vanishing with Jean Roydor’s published cohomological stability theorem. These inputs, exact norms and nonseparable scope are being independently checked. No unconditional result or publication readiness is asserted at this checkpoint.

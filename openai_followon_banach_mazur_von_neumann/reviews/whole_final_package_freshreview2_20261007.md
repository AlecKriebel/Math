required conclusion; a Jordan star isomorphism is the appropriate invariant.

The upstream theorem supplies actual primitives for ordinary bounded complex
multilinear self-coefficient cocycles. Its semantics are stronger than reduced
cohomology, density of coboundaries, or a completely bounded subcomplex. The
source's normal-reduction inputs retain this ordinary coefficient setting.

second-ultrapower ordered sums, the Catalan/Haagerup argument and polar unitary,

In particular, moment convergence of rare levels is not confused with norm
convergence. The normalized free sums have uniform operator bounds, and
repeated surviving labels have vanishing coefficient estimates without
commuting ordered coefficient blocks. The limiting positive variable has the
claimed Catalan law and no kernel; finiteness supplies a unitary polar part.
Spectral localization estimates are trace weighted, not multiplied by the

an actual primitive for the original cocycle; the averaging limit need not be
normal. Finite subsets and expectations remove separability using exact

# Exact target and status

T1. For every complex von Neumann algebra M there is epsilon_M>0 such that for every complex von Neumann algebra N, d_BM(M,N)<1+epsilon_M implies that M and N are Jordan *-isomorphic.

T2. The same assertion with d_BM(M_*,N_*) for their canonical preduals, with a common threshold after taking the smaller of any two thresholds.

Definition: d_BM(X,Y)=inf ||T|| ||T^{-1}|| over bounded bijective complex-linear maps with bounded inverse; infinity if none exists. For zero spaces use d(0,0)=1 and infinity for 0 versus a nonzero space, so the zero algebra is harmless. A Jordan *-isomorphism preserves *, the unit, and x∘y=(xy+yx)/2.

Status: both general targets unverified. Candidate conditional implication: H_b^2(M,M)=H_b^3(M,M)=0 implies both targets by Roydor, subject to actual primary-source verification.

Success criteria: actual-image ordinary bounded self-coefficient cohomology input; correct primary theorem field, norm, predual and nonseparable quantifiers; independently checkable argument; cleared attribution and priority; two fresh complete-package reviews; production Zenodo publication and verified tracker row.

Boundaries: N must be a von Neumann algebra. No universal epsilon, arbitrary Banach-space comparison, *-isomorphism, or completely bounded distance substitution. Linear isometry equivalences must be verified. Finite-dimensional, abelian, opposite-algebra, zero, nonseparable and type III cases must be covered or isolated accurately.

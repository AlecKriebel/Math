# Current target (provisional)

There exists an absolute C<infinity such that for every metric space X with finite Markov type-two constant M2(X), every countably additive positive measure space (Omega,Sigma,mu), every S subset X, and every Lipschitz f:S->L1(mu;R), a map F:X->L1(mu;R) exists with F|S=f and Lip(F)<=C M2(X)Lip(f).

L1 consists of a.e.-equivalence classes of real measurable functions with finite integral of absolute value. No sigma-finiteness, completeness, separability, atomicity or localizability restriction is intended; this scope must be proved. M2 is defined using finite reversible stochastic matrices, stationary probability vectors, all positive integer times and all finite tuples in X.

Auxiliary target: N2(L1(mu;R))<=12 sqrt(21) in the Mendel–Naor Cesaro metric Markov cotype convention. Full extension uses their Theorem 1.11, ordinary Banach barycenter (W2 constant 1), weak-star compactness/ultrapower, and a norm-one bidual projection.

Success criteria: independently reconstructed finite-cut proof with all constants; primary-source exact extension and projection hypotheses; full arbitrary-subset proof; literature/companion audit; at least two fresh complete-package review cycles; reviewed PDF and reproducible sources; actual public Zenodo record and exact verified tracker row.

Mandatory exclusions: arbitrary L1 subspaces, noncommutative L1/Schatten-1 targets, p=infinity source. Complex targets require separate distortion proof. Hilbert->ell1 is inherited from OpenAI family332 and is not the proposed new contribution.

## Primary source located

Olivier Lalonde, [*Quantum Colorings of
Spheres*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.10872), arXiv:2606.10872v1, submitted
2026-06-09.

The preprint records the exact graph6 string used here, defines
$G_n=G_{19}\vee K_{n-3}$ (the family denoted $J_n$ in the present note),
proves the rank-one statement $\chi_q^{(1)}(G_n)=n+1$, and explicitly leaves
the unrestricted equality as
a conjecture. It also records $\chi_q(G_{19})=\chi(G_{19})=4$. These are the
prior results to which the present theorem should be compared.

## Priority conclusion and caveat

Within this narrow search, the new contribution is precisely the resolution
of the preprint's unrestricted conjecture, strengthened to the entire stated
family:

\[
\chi_q(G_{19}\vee K_{n-3})=n+1\qquad(n\ge3).
\]

This is evidence for novelty, not an exhaustive priority guarantee. Before
formal submission, an author should repeat the search in MathSciNet,
zbMATH, Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, and current arXiv listings, and
check later versions and citing papers of arXiv:2606.10872. In accordance
with the independent-research policy, no outreach draft was prepared and no
external communication was initiated.

# Scope and literature audit

Search date: 2026-08-24 UTC. This was a targeted search of primary papers and
preprints, not an exhaustive priority search.

## Load-bearing attributions

- Huber, van Iersel, Jones, Moulton, and Veenema-Nipius, *When Are
  Quarnets Sufficient to Reconstruct Semi-Directed Phylogenetic Networks?*,
  proves that binary semi-directed level-2 networks are encoded by quarnets and
  that the blob tree is encoded by quarnets. This supports the submission's
  graph-theoretic level-2 and blob-tree background, but it does not itself prove
  the K2P analytic classification.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01510-5
- Englander et al., *Identifiability of Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks under the
  Jukes-Cantor Model*, states general JC/K2P separation when displayed quartet
  sets differ and the tree-of-blobs consequence. This supports the substance of
  the submission's pointwise topology step. Its K2P character naming uses a
  different equal-sector convention, so its displayed formulas cannot be copied
  literally into the submission's `(0,C,G,T)`, `(1,s,g,s)` convention.
  https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.18.649493
- Brits, Holtgrefe, van Iersel, and Martin, *On Tree-Network Distinguishability
  and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic Networks*, proves level-1 full
  identifiability modulo triangle redirection for JC/K2P/K3P on a probabilistic
  parameter domain and tree-network separation results. It does not subsume the
  claimed strong level-2 K2P-SAME classification.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12919v2
- Holtgrefe et al., *Distinguishing Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks with Quartets
  and Inter-Taxon Quartet Distances*, concerns a canonical form for
  outer-labelled planar, galled level-2 networks using displayed quartets. It is
  combinatorial/model-transfer background, not the same all-strongly-tree-child
  K2P analytic theorem.
  https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4
- Ardiyansyah, *Distinguishing Level-2 Phylogenetic Networks Using Phylogenetic
  Invariants*, studies distinguishability for selected simple and semisimple
  level-2 families; it does not state the submission's global classification.
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.12479
- The Systematic Biology article *Algebraic Invariants for Inferring 4-Leaf
  Semi-Directed Phylogenetic Networks* studies practical four-leaf inference
  under JC and K2P and cites the existing level-2 work. It does not appear to
  establish the present theorem.
  https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/advance-article/doi/10.1093/sysbio/syaf071/8285810

## Novelty and scope conclusion

The targeted search found no prior source that plainly states the same
principal-domain directed-containment/common-germ/triangle-equivalence theorem
for all binary standard semi-directed strongly tree-child level-2 K2P networks,
nor its exact reconstruction and weak-class `4n-3` sharpness package. This is
positive novelty evidence only, not an exhaustive priority guarantee.

The submission correctly distinguishes its scope from mixed-sign, stochastic
boundary, singular-edge, higher-level, weak-class-identifiability,
numerical-stability, bit-complexity, and finite-sample inference claims. The
searched sources do not justify expanding that scope.

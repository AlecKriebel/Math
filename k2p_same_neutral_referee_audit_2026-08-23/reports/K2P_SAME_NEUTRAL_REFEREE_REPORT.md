## 1. Verdict

**HOLD.**

No counterexample to the *corrected* central K2P-SAME theorem was established,
and substantial analytic, finite-census, exact-rank, triangle-germ, weak-class,
integrity, and reproducibility evidence survived independent checks.  However,
the literal displayed-quartet lemma in the article is false under the article's
own Fourier convention.  It is load-bearing for tree-of-blobs recovery and for
the high-volume quartet exclusion class.  The purported exact quartet replay
does not evaluate the printed Fourier coordinates and accepts targeted
convention/formula mutations.  Consequently the printed proof and the current
computational binding do not establish the global theorem.

The defect appears repairable by a uniform coordinate correction: the article
declares character order `(0,C,G,T)` and spectrum `(1,s,g,s)`, so the equal
sector is `{C,T}`.  Replacing the displayed `G/T` quartet separators by their
`C/T` analogues restores the intended zero/strict-sign identities.  That is why
the appropriate recommendation is HOLD rather than REJECT.  Correction,
convention-aware symbolic replay, downstream rebinding, regeneration, and
resealing are required before acceptance.

### Finding 1 — false literal quartet separator

**Classification:** proof-blocking and computational-completeness-blocking;
not on current evidence theorem-fatal.

**Locations and artifacts.**  Article source
`proof_compression_submission/article/main.tex` lines 302–313 and 416–450;
article PDF p. 6; `work/quartet_separation_closure/PROOF.md` lines 8–29;
crosswalk C02.  The authoritative article source has SHA-256
`1107e5395a0e2ad4da0333cda066ae587d9a9854e61aeba3d2aadcf62e23e45b`;
the article PDF has SHA-256
`86b7ace41d025caddcecae2accb04c496a401501b2a6e65233ad60cfc80e3e2a`.

The article fixes `(0,C,G,T)` and edge spectrum `(1,s,g,s)`, so `C` and `T`
are the equal K2P sector.  It nevertheless defines

```text
F_A = q_GGGG - q_GGTT,
G_B = q_GGGG - q_GGTT - q_GTTG + q_GTGT
```

and claims both vanish on `A=12|34`.  Direct tree-monomial substitution gives

```text
F_A = g1*g2*(g3*g4 - s3*s4),
```

which is not an identity.

**Minimal exact reproducer.**  Set every quartet edge to
`(s,g)=(3/4,3/5)`.  This lies strictly in both `D_plus` and the continuous-time
cone: `g>2s-1` and `g>s^2`.  Its transition entries are exactly
`(31/40,1/10,1/40,1/10)`.  On `12|34`,

```text
q_GGGG = 81/625,
q_GGTT = 81/400,
F_A = G_B = -729/10000.
```

On `13|24`, the printed `F_A=-891/40000`, contradicting asserted strict
positivity.  Run:

```sh

  --project isolated_handoff/materials/k2p_principal_d_plus_submission_referee \
  --output outputs/computational/quartet_coordinate_audit.json
```

## 7. Scope and literature audit

The submission consistently limits the main theorem to binary standard
semi-directed strongly tree-child level-2 networks, strict inheritance, and
the positive-eigenvalue `D_plus` component.  The continuous-time result is a
restriction to the strict cone, not a boundary theorem.  The weak construction
is a sharpness family, not weak-class identifiability.  No theorem is asserted
for mixed sign, stochastic boundary, singular edges, higher level,
weak-class identifiability, numerical stability, bit complexity or finite
samples.  No scope inflation was found.

The load-bearing graph background is supported by [Huber et al., *When Are
Quarnets Sufficient to Reconstruct Semi-Directed Phylogenetic
Networks?*](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01510-5),
for binary semi-directed level-2 quarnet/blob-tree reconstruction.  [Englander
et al., *Identifiability of Phylogenetic Level-2 Networks under the
Jukes–Cantor Model*, version 4](https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.18.649493),
supports the substantive K2P displayed-quartet signs in propositions 2.9–2.10
and theorem 2.11 and the tree-of-blobs consequence in corollary 2.12, under its
own character convention.  [Brits et al., *On Tree–Network Distinguishability
and Full Identifiability of Phylogenetic
Networks*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12919v2) establish a related level-1
full-identifiability result modulo triangle phenomena, not this level-2
theorem.  [Holtgrefe et al.](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4)
and [Ardiyansyah](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.12479) concern more restricted
combinatorial or invariant level-2 settings.  The practical four-leaf
JC/K2P study in [*Algebraic Invariants for Inferring 4-Leaf Semi-Directed
Phylogenetic Networks*](https://academic.oup.com/sysbio/advance-article/doi/10.1093/sysbio/syaf071/8285810)
does not establish the present global classification.

A targeted search found no prior primary source plainly stating the same
principal-domain directed-containment/common-germ/triangle-equivalence theorem
for the full claimed strong level-2 K2P class, together with this reconstruction
and weak-sharpness package.  This is positive search evidence, not an exhaustive
priority guarantee.

(SHA-256 recorded in the final artifact index).  Each attribution above was
checked against the primary paper/preprint; none is being used to validate the
submission's new finite classification.

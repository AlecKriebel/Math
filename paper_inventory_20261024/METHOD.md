# Paper inventory assessment method

Author: Alec Kriebel, ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X.

This assessment inventories published Zenodo manuscripts and checks older software releases for separately identifiable archived research papers. A record, a Zenodo concept, a scientific work, and a PDF are different units. Versions, presentation copies, supplements, and papers embedded in software archives are identified explicitly rather than silently double-counted or discarded. The account census is reconciled against previous publication receipts and the existing spreadsheet.

Impact follows the user's existing rubric: assuming the stated theorem is correct, genuinely novel, and published, how important would it be within its actual subfield? A score of 9 resolves a famous central longstanding problem; 8 marks a major subfield advance; 7 a strong specialist result; 6 a solid above-average contribution; 5 narrow useful work; 4 a technical note. Scores are editorial judgments, with roughly 0.5–1 point uncertainty, not citation measures or correctness probabilities.

Added contribution separately credits this paper's work after acknowledging imported theorems and established reductions. A consequence of an important upstream result can have high conditional theorem impact and much lower added contribution. Neither score certifies the imported input.

Publication recommendations assess whether a journal submission is worth pursuing, not whether it will be accepted. They consider scope, added contribution, overlap, presentation, dependency verification, and strength of checkable evidence. An independent researcher's credentials and the use of AI are not automatic deductions. Claims of independent human peer review, formal verification, novelty, or reproducibility are not inferred from internal automated review.

The review is manuscript-based inventory triage supported by existing audit artifacts. Its depth is recorded per paper; it is not a new complete proof audit of every paper or a fresh exhaustive literature search. Exact numerical, field, asymptotic, genericity, input, and attribution boundaries are retained. Apparent mathematical notation errors from PDF extraction are checked against source or page images before being treated as substantive defects.

No person is contacted and no outreach is prepared. The existing spreadsheet's Full List tab is the requested output; other tabs are preserved. Private tracker snapshots and raw authenticated account captures remain local and are excluded from Git publication. Public reports and assessments are checkpointed on remote main without touching unrelated working files or the shared index. No release or DOI is created.

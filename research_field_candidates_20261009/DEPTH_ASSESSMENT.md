# Which candidate could support the deepest new research program?

Date: 2026-10-09. Status: comparative research judgment, not a finding of novelty or a forecast of guaranteed progress.

## Recommendation

For the prospect of a distinctive, connected new theory, prioritize **experiments with an undo guarantee**. For the strongest route into deep existing mathematics and physics, prioritize **finite reservoirs for irreversibility**. Temporary helpers remains the clearest elementary research laboratory.

Two additional independent reviewers agreed on that leading order. They disagreed on the relative position of stable descriptions and finite arithmetic; the evidence does not warrant a precise ordering of those two.

The decisive criterion is theorem reuse: does a structural result at one stage provide a tool for the next? Simply listing probability, quantum mechanics, or biological applications does not demonstrate a new research tree.

## Proposed connected ladder for bounded-undo experimentation

Keep the original model precise: the machine's transition and output rules are known; its original state is unknown. At every transcript prefix, ordinary available actions must permit restoration of that actual original state within the recovery budget. Restoration may use further observations unless a blind-recovery variant is explicitly specified.

1. **Finite classification.** Determine exactly what distinctions among initial states can be learned under a given recovery budget. Classify small machines, minimal obstructions, and optimal decision trees. Required evidence: exact strategies and independently checkable impossibility certificates.
2. **Structural laws.** Prove scalable families separating ordinary identification from recoverable identification. Find quantities controlling recovery cost and bounds on experiment length. Required evidence: an infinite-family theorem, not only a finite atlas.
3. **Resource tradeoffs.** Relate information obtained, experiment length, controller memory, and restoration time. Define each resource explicitly. Required evidence: lower bounds accompanied by matching or near-matching constructions.
4. **Composition.** Determine what happens when machines are connected or auxiliary devices are available. Specify independent versus shared controls and admissible correlations. Do not presume that interaction helps. Required evidence: composition bounds, preserved invariants, or a certified advantage under an explicitly defined coupling.
5. **Local and distributed systems.** Put the devices on a network with local controls and observations. Determine whether learning a global property requires disturbance or recovery over a large region. Required evidence: a structural result inherited from earlier stages that scales beyond enumeration.
6. **Robust experimentation.** Introduce observation errors, stochastic transitions, and approximate recovery, with explicit worst-case or probabilistic guarantees. Required evidence: the earlier concepts must still explain the outcome; otherwise this is a separate research problem rather than a continuation of the same theory.

Possible large-scale targets include sharp tradeoff curves and limits for many copies of a constrained device. These are hypotheses about fruitful directions, not claims that a new universal information law exists.

## Why the other candidates rank differently

- **Finite reservoirs:** coherent storage/time/composition/locality questions and strong connections to physics. However, [set-theoretic dilation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.09255) and [invertible realizations of Markov evolution](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512393) already occupy much of the surrounding framework. The unrestricted seed might also have a short classification. Its depth depends on finding quantitative structure beyond known constructions.
- **Temporary helpers:** likely to yield a ladder of accessible examples, structural bounds, and resource-augmentation questions. Its central optimization is already minimum-label path on an implicit reconfiguration graph; the potential originality is in specific configuration structures and helper-set laws.
- **Stable compact descriptions:** a plausible structural program, but much of its eventual scope falls within sensitivity, recourse, and dynamic optimization. Exact online instability alone is already explained by elementary matching examples.
- **Accuracy versus algebra:** promising sharp extremal questions, especially if one can derive a general theory of approximate numerical laws. At present, the transition from its first frontier to a broader reusable theory is less well supported.

## Novelty boundary and decision gate

Bounded undo is expressible as planning on sets of (original,current) state pairs, constrained to a region from which recovery is possible. That encoding is a useful tool; it is not itself a new discovery. The program should be downgraded if all substantive conclusions follow directly from existing testing/planning results, or if its budget merely adds bookkeeping with no structural consequences.

[State-identification theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1907.11034) is established. The neighboring [restorable-systems testing paper](https://antares.sip.ucm.es/prabanal/research/ictac09extendedversion.pdf) explicitly provides save/load of previously visited configurations and optimizes coverage. Restoring an unknown original state through ordinary controls is a different condition, but that distinction does not certify originality.

Before selecting a long-term program, seek all three: a focused equivalence audit, an independently verified structural theorem for an infinite family, and several follow-on questions that genuinely use that theorem. The appropriate present confidence is moderate. No candidate has yet passed that gate.

This assessment does not start, schedule, or claim completion of the proposed research program.

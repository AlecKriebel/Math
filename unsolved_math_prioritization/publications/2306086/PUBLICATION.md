# Published qualitative research note

A qualitative pre-Schwarzian improvement for real schlicht functions

Preprint and exact verification archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23384924

The normalized real-coefficient schlicht class admits M(z)<4 at each fixed nonreal point, uniformly over the class and on compact nonreal sets. The classical real-diameter radius is sharp; the original displayed expression is zero at zero. An explicit upper gap, the sharp quantitative radius, and general nonreal extremizers remain undetermined in this note.

This is an application of classical coefficient equality, slit-domain rigidity and compactness. No historical firstness, exhaustive novelty clearance or present-openness guarantee is claimed. The original-source limits for Jenkins (1960) and Aleksandrov-Popova (1973) remain explicit in [CURRENT_PRIORITY_AND_STATUS.md](CURRENT_PRIORITY_AND_STATUS.md).

# Published counterexample note

Counterexamples to Satoh's SL(2) representation-filtration conjecture

Preprint and exact verification archive: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23387442

The note gives explicit counterexamples to the equality posed by Satoh in OWR 2018: D_n(5) != A_n(5) for every n >= 4, and D_n(6) != A_n(6) for every n >= 3. The component algebra is the rational SL(2) matrix-entry algebra; A uses the integral free-group lower central series, and D acts on the whole J quotient. The classical Burde and Razmyslov identity credit is explicit. Neither a new PI identity nor a new general PI method is claimed.

The note makes no degree-5 rank-3, every-degree, higher-SL_m, character-algebra, or ordinary IA lower-central conjecture claim. It gives no historical firstness, exhaustive novelty clearance, or current-literature-openness certificate. The 2017/2021 journal and 2025/2026 related-journal source-access limits remain explicit in [CURRENT_PRIORITY_AND_STATUS.md](CURRENT_PRIORITY_AND_STATUS.md).

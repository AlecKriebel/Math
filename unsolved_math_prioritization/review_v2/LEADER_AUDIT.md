- **30001156, Faber-Krahn perimeter correction:** excluded as a direct consequence of a published theorem, not as a new discovery. Berghaus–Georgiev–Monien–Radchenko, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2103.01057 , Theorem1 and Table1, prove lambda1(P_N)−j01²=O(N^-3) for regular N-gons of area pi. Elementary polygon geometry gives L_N²=4 pi N tan(pi/N), hence L_N²−4pi²~4pi^4/(3N²). Therefore the ratio A²(lambda1−pi j01²/A)/(L²−4pi A) tends to zero. The original OWR2009/06 p403 explicitly includes convex domains; the regular polygons satisfy that hypothesis. This source-based deduction makes every universal positive K impossible. Independent confirmation requested; no fresh research attempt counted.

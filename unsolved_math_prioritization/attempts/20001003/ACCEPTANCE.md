AI-assisted, unrefereed research. Acceptance means an internal AI mathematical audit; it is not human peer review, journal acceptance, or formal proof-assistant certification. This is a complete authored proof/audit edition, not a computational reproduction package.

## Accepted claims

- For every nonsingular integer input M, recognition of its fixed embedded row lattice by a connected undirected integer-weighted multigraph reduces to a finite box of edge multiplicities 0,...,D, where D=|det M|. The exact integral multiplier is U=L adj(M)/(det M).
- The full Cauchy–Binet/tree proof establishes determinant as the number of spanning trees, the multiplicity bound D, and diagonal bound nD. Loop deletion preserves the lattice.
- Row membership plus determinant/index equality gives the exact lattice, not only an inclusion or an abstract quotient-group isomorphism.
- The rank-two arithmetic criterion and lower-triangular HNF derivation are complete, with fixed coordinates throughout.
- All eight normal forms below index four are realized. The seven index-four forms contain exactly four positive rows and three obstructions. All 15 rows and the direct obstruction arguments are preserved.
- The lattice generated by (2,0),(1,2) is a smallest-index rank-two obstruction. The lattice generated by (1,0),(0,4) is graphical, although both quotient groups have Smith factors (1,4).

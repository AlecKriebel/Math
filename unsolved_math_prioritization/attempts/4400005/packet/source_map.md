## All-dimensional specification input

Daniel Thompson, **The irregular set for maps with the specification property has full topological pressure**, *Dynamical Systems* 25 (2010), 25-51.

- Published record: https://www.tandfonline.com/doi/full/10.1080/14689360903156237
- Inspected manuscript: https://arxiv.org/pdf/0807.2123
- Locators in arXiv:0807.2123v1: Definition 1.5, page 4; section 4.3, page 20. Both pages were visually inspected. Section 4.3 explicitly covers compact connected negatively curved Riemannian manifolds in general dimension and sampled time maps. Definition 1.5 has prescribed integer intervals and no periodic-shadow requirement.

Xueting Tian, **Different asymptotic behavior versus same dynamical complexity: Recurrence & (ir)regularity**, *Advances in Mathematics* 288 (2016), 464-526.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2015.11.006
- Inspected published PDF: https://archive.ymsc.tsinghua.edu.cn/pacm_download/533/11750-2016advmath2016.pdf
- Locator: section 7.3, printed page 515 (PDF page 52), visually inspected. It documents specification of nonzero time maps of compact mixing Anosov flows, and cites Thompson section 4.3. This is corroboration of the input, not a universality theorem.

## All-dimensional embedding input

David Burguet, **Topological and almost Borel universality for systems with the weak specification property**, *Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems* 40 (2020), 2098-2115.

- Published record: https://doi.org/10.1017/etds.2019.7
- Inspected manuscript: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1901.00666
- Locators in arXiv:1901.00666v1: definitions and finite-entropy convention, pages 1-2; Theorem 1.2, page 3, visually inspected. The theorem supplies almost Borel embeddings under almost weak specification. Numbering in this packet is the inspected manuscript numbering. The publisher record independently confirms the article's publication and bibliographic data; the inspected full text is the arXiv manuscript.
- The author's publication-page PDF button was found to point to an unrelated article. It was not used as theorem evidence.

## Contextual material consulted, not needed as new premises

- Quas-Soo, **Ergodic universality of some topological dynamical systems**, https://web.uvic.ca/~aquas/papers/paper42.pdf, Theorem 7 and section 3.4. This older criterion has additional hypotheses avoided by using Burguet.
- Constantine-Lafont-Thompson, **Strong symbolic dynamics for geodesic flows on CAT(-1) spaces and other metric Anosov flows**, https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/jep.115.pdf, Theorem B and Theorem 3.11. A finite-to-one coding is not itself an embedding certificate for a chosen measure.
- Constantine-Lafont-Thompson, **The weak specification property for geodesic flows on CAT(-1) spaces**, https://people.math.osu.edu/lafont.1/specification.pdf, section 2. Its variable-gap convention must not be substituted for the fixed-gap condition used in this proof.

# Source checks

Checked 2026-10-04. This is a bounded source review, not an exhaustive novelty certificate.

1. [Oberwolfach Report 46/2022](https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2022/46), *Mini-Workshop: Mathematical Foundations of Robust and Generalizable Learning*, printed pp.2659–2686. Rinaldo's contribution is pp.2677–2680; the question and envelope are on p.2679. The official [MFO PDF](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3989/OWR_2022_46.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=4) was read, and printed p.2679 was visually checked. The report's question does not fix an admissible statistical comparator class.

2. Patil, Kuchibhotla, Wei and Rinaldo, [*Mitigating multiple descents: A model-agnostic framework for risk monotonization*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.12937), 2022. The [author-hosted manuscript](https://pratikpatil.io/papers/risk-monotonization.pdf) was inspected, especially pp.3–5, Theorem 3.4, Theorem 3.11, Remark 3.12 and Section 5. The greatest-minorant observation is already present. The formal exact tracking statement uses stronger uniform deterministic approximation; a weaker hypothesis gives an upper bound. One-step modifications can improve the envelope. Its minimax discussion supplies an SNR-dependent factor, not a generic exact benchmark.

3. Hastie, Montanari, Rosset and Tibshirani, [*Surprises in High-Dimensional Ridgeless Least Squares Interpolation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.08560), arXiv v5 (2020), subsequently *Annals of Statistics* 50(2), 949–986 (2022), DOI [10.1214/21-AOS2133](https://doi.org/10.1214/21-AOS2133). The isotropic formulas underpin the Gaussian example. Our displayed risk includes independent test-noise variance 1, whereas an excess-risk convention omits it. The packet separately proves concentration conditional on the full data for its shrinkage example.

4. Patil, Du and Kuchibhotla, [*Bagging in overparameterized learning: Risk characterization and risk monotonization*](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v24/23-0887.html), *JMLR* 24(319):1–113 (2023). The publisher abstract identifies exact bagged linear-model risks and subsample-size selection; it is relevant follow-on work. Its abstract alone is not treated as a proof or disproof of the broader OWR question.

5. The current [author research list](https://pratikpatil.io/research) and targeted searches for the exact risk-monotonization terminology were checked. No verified full resolution of the broad statistical question was found. This is a limited negative finding.

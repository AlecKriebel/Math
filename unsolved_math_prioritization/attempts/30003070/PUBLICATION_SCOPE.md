## 4. Domain of the braid inverse

For (A,B,C)=(bc/(a+c), a+c, ab/(a+c)), the displayed matrix identity requires a+c != 0. Its rational inverse additionally requires A+C=b != 0. The birational inverse is therefore asserted only on the dense open where **both b != 0 and a+c != 0**. It is not an everywhere inverse on the whole identity domain.

# Relative ends of closed-surface mapping class groups: accepted partial theorem

Problem 11000008 / AMR-109-0008, Benson Farb's Question 2.1.

This AI-assisted manuscript and its independent internal AI audit are unrefereed. “Accepted” refers only to the stated internal partial-theorem assessment; no external human peer review, journal acceptance, or formal proof-assistant certification is claimed. The full spectrum for g >= 2 remains unresolved by this work, and no novelty or global-openness claim is made.

## Accepted scope

For the orientation-preserving mapping class group G_g of the closed oriented genus-g surface, with finitely generated subgroups H:

- Zero relative ends is equivalent to finite index.
- For g >= 2, infinite-index H with vcd(H) <= 4g-7 has one relative end. A witness with at least two relative ends must have vcd(H) in {4g-6,4g-5}.
- Genus two realizes 0, 1, 2, and continuum many relative ends. The two-ended and continuum-ended witnesses have generating sets of at most ten and eight elements, respectively.
- The separate genus-zero and genus-one spectra are {0} and {0,continuum}.

This does not classify the full genus-two spectrum or the higher-genus high-dimensional cases. No exclusion of finite values at least three or countably infinite values for g >= 2 is asserted. Relative ends are computed for the exact subgroup pair, using the quotient Schreier graph, rather than ordinary ends of H.

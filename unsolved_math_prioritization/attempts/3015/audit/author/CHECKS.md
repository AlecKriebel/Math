# Author checks for the dimension five partial report

## Mathematical checks

- The category is topological, with arbitrary simplicial triangulations permitted. A PL obstruction is not silently substituted for the simplicial obstruction.
- The closed and connected hypotheses are present wherever top-degree duality and evaluation are used.
- The coefficient map j sends 1 to 2y, not to y. Its image is in ker(mu), and the diagram with the integral Bockstein commutes.
- The argument uses only the absence of an order-two element of Rokhlin invariant one. It makes no assertion that the homology-cobordism group has no torsion.
- Nonorientable top-degree cohomology is computed with constant coefficients by duality into the orientation local system. It is B/2B, not B.
- Reduction H5(M;Z) -> H5(M;F2) is an isomorphism in the nonorientable case. This, not a dimension-independent equivalence between Bocksteins, is used.
- The degree-one Wu formula is applied to degree four on a closed five-manifold.
- In the product proof both characteristic classes pull back, and the relevant class on X has degree five.
- The cover formula uses the degree modulo two and naturality of the stable tangent microbundle. Connected covers are specified.
- The mapping-torus calculation assumes an orientable fiber. It does not require a smooth fiber, a smooth monodromy, a section, or a monodromy-invariant spin structure.
- The homotopy exact sequence gives pi_k(Tf)=pi_k(X) for every k>=2. The nonaspherical published Kronheimer fiber is not presented as an aspherical solution.
- A reversing homeomorphism forces signature zero. The spin congruence is used only for closed oriented spin four-manifolds.
- Neither Delta=0 in dimension four nor residual finiteness is taken to imply PL structure or virtual triangulability.

## Source and scope checks

The complete K3 Problem 5.8 and both remarks were read in the current author PDF and visually inspected on printed page 306. The complete nine-page DFL mathematical article, including its dimension-five discussion, was read. The cited Manolescu theorem, nonsplitting corollary and mapping-torus example, Teichner's spin formula, and Lafont–Ruffoni's Theorem 5.12 and Remark 5.13 were inspected directly in their public author or publisher PDFs.

The retrieved K3 PDF explicitly identifies itself as an author's preliminary version. The Lafont–Ruffoni arXiv version is v2, revised 2 April 2025; its title page dates the file 4 April 2025. Its inspected theorem has range n>=6. These version and dimension distinctions are preserved.

The literature check did not locate a full solution to the exact five-dimensional question. This is a bounded finding and is not a novelty determination or a proof of absence. The report explicitly credits the established mapping-torus mechanism.

## Artifact checks and limitations

The public-safe bundle contains authored mathematical prose and public bibliographic verification metadata only. No third-party PDFs, extracted source text, source screenshots, corpus records, search-result dumps, or private coordination files are included.

There is no executable payload or mathematical checker. The proofs are human-readable mathematical arguments, not machine-verified proofs. Consequently interpreter optimization, import shadowing, executable-entrypoint, and bytecode-cache tests are inapplicable. Archive membership, sizes, cryptographic hashes, absence of links, and relocation integrity are checked as data, without executing archive contents.

These are author checks. A fresh independent mathematical audit is required before publication. The bundle does not claim such an audit has already happened.

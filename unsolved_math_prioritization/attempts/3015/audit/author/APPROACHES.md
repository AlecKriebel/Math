# Five approaches to Kirby Problem 5 point 8

This report contains one bounded mathematical attempt with five substantive approaches. The original existence question is not solved. Each route is accounted for below; literature search is not counted as another mathematical approach.

1. **Compute the complete dimension five obstruction.** Factor the homology-cobordism Bockstein through the integral Bockstein. Use top-degree duality and Manolescu's nonsplitting theorem to prove that the simplicial obstruction vanishes exactly when the characteristic number <w1 Delta,[M]2> vanishes. Outcome: an exact test, with no realization or universal-vanishing result for aspherical manifolds.

2. **Stabilize a four-dimensional example by a circle.** Compute both characteristic classes on X x S1 as pullbacks from X. Their degree-five product vanishes on the four-dimensional base. Outcome: every such product is triangulable, even when X is nonorientable or nontriangulable. The naive product construction cannot answer the question.

3. **Use a mapping torus instead of a product.** For an orientable fiber, calculate the characteristic number as orientation-reversal parity times the fiber's Kirby–Siebenmann invariant. Prove asphericity is exactly asphericity of the fiber. Signature reversal and the spin congruence eliminate every spin fiber. Outcome: a conditional construction from a nonspin, signature-zero aspherical four-manifold with ks=1 and an orientation-reversing homeomorphism. No such fiber-homeomorphism pair is constructed here. The general mechanism is already in Kronheimer's published example and is not claimed as new.

4. **Pass between finite covers and quotients.** Calculate the characteristic number under a finite cover using its degree modulo two. Outcome: every even-degree connected cover of a closed five-manifold is triangulable; odd covers preserve its triangulability status. Asphericity alone does not produce the necessary free action or quotient. This argument is dimension-specific and does not answer K3's separate question in dimension four.

5. **Apply direct and relative hyperbolization.** Check the precise gluing hypotheses in DFL. The missing dimension-five step is a PL structure on the resolved hyperbolized four-dimensional boundary. Zero Kirby–Siebenmann class does not supply it in dimension four. The 2025 cubulation theorem remains in dimensions at least six. Outcome: a verified construction barrier, not a new construction or a theorem that all five-dimensional attempts must fail.

The stopping point is the completion of these five approaches. The deliverable is an honest partial mathematical report. Absence of a solution in this report or in the bounded literature checks is not a proof that no later or unindexed solution exists.

## Later results checked for applicability

- Cheng and van Dobben de Bruyn, *Unbounded negativity on rational surfaces in positive characteristic* (2021), addresses exactly the torus-grid blowup, but over characteristic p>0 with p not dividing m. Its Main Theorem gives square d(3-m)-1 when dm=p^e-1. Section 2.1 describes finite power maps, and Section 4 explicitly separates characteristic-zero questions. This is not a solution to the complex target.
- Hao, *Weak Bounded Negativity Conjecture*, arXiv:1708.09463v1 (2017), published in *Proceedings of the AMS* 147 (2019), proves genus-dependent bounds. The theorem yields formula (3) here; it does not bound curves of unbounded genus. The actual manuscript numbering used in this packet is preserved.
- Misra and Ray, *On Weak bounded negativity conjecture*, arXiv:2408.15187v1 (2024), treats degree-dependent bounds on blowups and weak bounded negativity in families. Theorems 1.10 and 1.13 retain degree or genus parameters, and therefore do not supply a fixed-X bound for all genera.
- Wang, *Self-intersection Number of Negative Curves on Fermat Surfaces*, arXiv:2501.00319v3, revised 9 January 2026, studies hypersurfaces x^d+y^d+z^d+w^d=0 in P^3. Theorem 1.1 and conditional Corollary 1.4 concern those surfaces. They are not the rational torus-grid blowups X_m. The text does not claim an unconditional solution even for all such Fermat hypersurfaces.

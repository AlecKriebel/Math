# Five completed approaches

The approach count concerns substantive mathematical routes, not individual searches or tool calls. The problem remains unresolved after all five.

## 1. Effective anticanonical class and small orders

Prove the blowup intersection formulas and use adjunction, a pencil, and elementary line incidence. This settles m=1,2,3 with optimal signed bounds -1,-1,-2. For m>3, the nef fiber has negative pairing with -K, proving that even a positive multiple of -K cannot be effective. The obstruction is established rather than hidden in an unverified extension of the small-order proof.

## 2. Fermat fibration and symmetric curves

Resolve the complete-intersection pencil, classify all its vertical integral curves, and exploit the transitive root-of-unity action. All vertical curves are controlled; nonexceptional equal-multiplicity curves have nonnegative square. The unresolved class consists of nonsymmetric horizontal curves. Averaging has the wrong effect on self-intersection, as the exact variance identity demonstrates.

## 3. Numerical cone, exact interpolation, and genus-bounded filtering

Combine every line-Bezout test with nef-fiber pairing and adjunction. The explicit D_k classes on X_4 show that these tests still permit unbounded negative square. Then distinguish numerical feasibility from realizability: modular full-column-rank certificates exclude k=1,2, and Hao's proven genus-dependent bound excludes k>=3. This whole attractive numerical counterexample route is closed. The known theorem also shows that a genuine complex counterexample must have unbounded geometric genus.

## 4. Power-image curves and characteristic separation

Test the explicit family responsible for the later positive-characteristic counterexamples. Birational parametrization and the geometry of three unit complex numbers give the exact complex formula (4), so this entire family fails to produce a complex counterexample. The later primary theorem is retained only in its actual characteristic-p scope.

## 5. Divisibility tower and finite power covers

Construct the blowup maps for divisibility and the distinct finite coordinate-power maps. Prove the valid downward propagation of boundedness and upward propagation of unboundedness. Derive the component-intersection identity and identify the uncontrolled cross term that blocks the desired reverse inference. Later work on Fermat hypersurfaces and weighted/genus-dependent bounds does not supply that inference.

## Stopping assessment

No rigorous full resolution or verified prior resolution for the complex fixed-order problem was found. The retained report is a partial-results packet with a precise gap, not a solved-status artifact. A current-source search is necessarily incomplete; absence of a located resolution is not a theorem of global openness. Further work should address actual nonsymmetric horizontal curves of unbounded genus, rather than recycle numerical feasibility, symmetric averaging, or characteristic-p power images.

# Additive clarifications after independent review

These clarifications do not change the frozen proof, its conclusion or its one-turn count.

1. **Suspension action.** With (Sx)_i=x_(i+1), the decoration satisfies Lambda_(S^m x,S^n y)=Lambda_(x,y)-(m,n). Thus Phi([x,u],[y,v])=Lambda_(x,y)-(u,v) respects (x,1)~(Sx,0) and intertwines increasing suspension time with the usual negative-translation action. The independently checked marker test establishes an actual conjugacy, not merely a factor map that could lose the observable.
2. **Dimensional scope.** Van Enter's precursor, arXiv:1310.0267v2, explicitly discusses configurations on Z^d on p.2 and correlation measures on R^d or d-dimensional tori on p.3 before the same question. The Baake–Grimm paper, arXiv:0810.5750v2, p.2, explicitly treats a measure concentrated on a line in the plane as singular continuous. Thus the planar ambient-Haar interpretation is supported by primary context, not just an omitted qualifier.
3. **Substitution identity.** For rho:a->ab,b->ac,c->db,d->dc and a,b->+1,c,d->-1, the legal rho²-fixed seed c|a gives the source's two-sided Rudin–Shapiro sequence. Even positions use letters a,d and odd positions b,c. The binary rho² images of a,b,c,d are +++-, ++-+, --+-, ---+. They yield w(4n+r)=w(n) for r=0,1 and w(4n+r)=(-1)^(n+r)w(n) for r=2,3, with w(0)=1,w(-1)=-1.

The result is confined to the unrestricted higher-dimensional assertion. No one-dimensional resolution or historical-priority certificate is supplied.

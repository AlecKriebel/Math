Let lambda and mu be independently uniform among the p(n) partitions of n. The target is the limiting behavior of P_n=p(n)^(-2) #{(lambda,mu):chi_lambda(mu)=0}. Uniform irreducible characters and uniform conjugacy classes give this measure. A uniform permutation would instead weight mu by 1/z_mu, and Miller's existing theorem that the corresponding zero probability tends to1 does not answer this question. Plancherel sampling of lambda is also a different measure. OWR's surrounding definitions and Question8 were checked in full.

It expressly states that the total-zero asymptotic remains open. Its Conjecture1, attributed to Miller–Scheinerman, predicts P_n~2/log n. The criteria are: typeI, lambda is a mu_1-core; typeII, lambda is a t-core for some part t of mu; typeIII, P_t(mu)>H_t(lambda) for some integer t, where P_t=(1/t) sum_(t|mu_i) mu_i and H_t counts hooks divisible by t. The polynomial cyclotomic-factor criterion counting divisible parts is weaker than this weighted typeIII condition. The two are not conflated.

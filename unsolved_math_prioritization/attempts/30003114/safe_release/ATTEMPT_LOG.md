# Research log: five substantive approaches

Target: 30003114 / OWR-14603-013, rational-parameter small-modulus **counts**. All five approaches ended with an unresolved gap. Source inspection and audit are not counted as extra substantive approaches. No elapsed time or model capability is used as a correctness certificate.

1. Arithmetic separation. Tested whether rational-root and integrality arguments give a denominator-independent exponent. Retained proof: injectivity on the ternary family, lattice counting, and a full result for every fixed denominator cap Q. Gap: log q persists in the exponential scale; a q-dependent prefactor alone does not remove it from this estimate.
2. Lacunary conditioning. Selected every 22nd coefficient, used leading-term domination, and conditioned on all other coefficients. Retained proof: a denominator-free deterministic count of 3^(d+1-floor(d/22)-1) at a uniform exponential threshold. Gap: its count exponent is far above 1/100. Probability decay must be multiplied by 3^(d+1).
3. Collision construction. Pigeonholed binary polynomial values and bounded how many ordered pairs produce each ternary difference. Retained proof: N_d(lambda,t)>=2^(d+2)t/(1/(1-lambda)+t)-1, hence a necessary C>=log 2-1/100. Gap: this obstructs only small proposed C; the target allows larger absolute C.
4. Analytic root localization. Removed origin factors, bounded interior roots by Jensen, factored selected roots with a Blaschke product, and applied Harnack. Retained proof: at most 226 roots in |z|<=0.97 and an explicit exponential-smallness-to-root-distance implication. Gap: neither root count nor rational nonvanishing bounds the number of varying polynomials at the needed uniform scale. The literature's common-root theorem has a stronger n^(-3n) hypothesis.
5. Separated products. Tried high multiplicity at 1 and Thue–Morse-type products as counterexamples. Retained proof: superincreasing exponents preserve {-1,0,1}, give exact multiplicity, and nevertheless yield F(x)>=exp(-380) on the whole target interval. Monomial shifts only add the uniform a^d loss. Gap: arbitrary ternary polynomials lack that factorization.

Exact controls exercise all arithmetic claims on four rational parameters and degrees 1 through 8, plus multi-digit lacunary separation and product identities. Six negative controls detect missing modulus, d=0, wrong alphabet, overlapping product exponents, too-short lacunary spacing and denominator dependence. They are checks of these finite claims and constants, not proof of the target or of the imported analytic facts.

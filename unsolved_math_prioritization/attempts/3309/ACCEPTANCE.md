## Accepted mathematics and hypotheses

1. The exact equivalence U ⇔ B ⇔ W: U quantifies over finite simple graphs with the uniform measure on every acyclic edge subset; B restricts those hosts to simple bipartite graphs; W quantifies over finite loopless multigraphs with arbitrary strictly positive activities. Every statement concerns distinct labeled edges. The even-length parallel-path construction is finite, simple, and bipartite; the acyclicity correspondence and macroforest counts are exact. Conditional independence of different gadgets and the positive marked-individual-edge factors transfer covariance, then continuity gives arbitrary positive weights. The implication is an equivalence of unresolved universal statements, not a proof of any one of them.
2. Both weighted K4 Rayleigh identities cover the two vertex-automorphism orbits of distinct edge pairs and prove strict negative correlation at positive activities. The forest polynomial is explicitly characterized and the identities are exact algebra, not numerical sampling.
3. Parallel and series replacements cover old–old, new–old, and new–new edge pairs; products cover internal and cross-factor pairs; the precisely defined two-terminal edge gluing covers same-side and cross-side pairs, including zero effective activity by continuity. Deletion and contraction preserve the property, with loops created on contraction discarded. These arguments establish the generated family from single edges and K4, not a decomposition of arbitrary graphs.
4. The triangle upper-half-plane zero refutes real-stability of every forest polynomial. The K4 coefficient −2 refutes coefficientwise Rayleigh positivity. Rooted spanning-tree projection has a component-dependent factor, and the triangle admits no fixed positive root-star weights that produce the uniform all-forest law. None is a counterexample to the target inequality.

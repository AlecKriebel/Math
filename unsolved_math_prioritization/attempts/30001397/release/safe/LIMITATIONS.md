# Limitations and remaining gap

The original spherical exact-gauge question is unresolved. Five substantive approaches have been used. No sixth proof search is part of the audit request.

1. No candidate g with positive finite total H_s^g(J) is established. Corollary 2.1 is a conditional reduction, not existence.
2. Failure of uniform ball estimates does not imply failure of exact Hausdorff representation. The pole and parabolic centers are exceptional null points.
3. No theorem reduces every possible gauge to regular variation or to monotone logarithmic corrections. Irregular gauges remain in scope of the original question.
4. Pole recurrence is qualitative in the argument inspected. A quantitative all-scale density law and a shrinking-target/integral test suited to this elliptic system are missing.
5. The invariant measure equivalent to the conformal probability may be infinite. Probability theorems cannot be silently applied to it. The explicit tower model is not the actual elliptic system.
6. The independent Pareto model is proved exactly, but actual excursions are not asserted to be independent or quasi-independent.
7. All h,p,q arithmetic triples in the controls are algebraic tests. No elliptic parameter satisfying the source hypotheses is constructed or certified numerically.
8. Current literature search did not locate a resolution; this is not an exhaustive proof that the problem remains open. The 2023 book's public summaries were inspected, but its full final typeset text was not. The full 2020 arXiv manuscript was retrieved and the relevant sections inspected.
9. The 2004 paper was inspected through an author-hosted PostScript version and a local PDF conversion. The journal's final PDF was not downloaded. The theorem numbering and metric convention were checked on the author version, and relevant conclusions cross-checked with the 2020 manuscript.
10. The problem webpage returned HTTP 403; the selected cached source record was checked against the primary OWR statement. The cached corpora match the live repository manifest's published hashes and byte counts; they were not independently redownloaded from Hugging Face.
11. Ordinary mathematical 'attracted' is interpreted as attraction in the corresponding basin. If one broadens the phrase to include critical points landing exactly on parabolic cycles, the no-critical-points-on-J parabolic subclass cannot be assumed solely from that phrase. The core local-measure obstructions still use explicit conformal-measure hypotheses; no full result for the broader class is claimed.

# GREEN-012: partial result

The unrestricted finite-abelian tuple inequality is not resolved in this attempt.

An authored, computer-assisted partial proof establishes the exact inequality
\(T_G(A)\geq\alpha^{15}|G|^{10}\) for every finite abelian group whenever
\(\alpha\geq20/23\). An explicit scalar criterion improves the threshold to
approximately \(0.86946194679\). The proof combines Fourier moment bounds,
Cauchy–Schwarz, and a fully checked classification of the fixed graph's 32,768
edge subsets. It does not use an asymptotic error term or require distinct coordinates.

The proof also covers every subgroup coset and every complement of a subgroup
coset, and records quotient/product closure. Exact enumeration found no violation
among all 398,350 subsets of the 25 finite abelian group types of order at most 16.
That enumeration is a finite check, not a proof at arbitrary group order.

The modulo-5 indexing remains qualified as a publicly reported author clarification.

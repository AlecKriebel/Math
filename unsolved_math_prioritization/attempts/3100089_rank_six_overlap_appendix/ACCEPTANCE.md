## Arbitrary ambient rank six

For every finite successfully pressable symmetric binary matrix of rank six, the graph on all complete successful pressing words at LCS≥2 is connected and has diameter at most six. Because 2=6−4, this is exactly the original four-deletion and four-insertion threshold. No bound is imposed on the number of original labels.

The accepted proof includes the complete lower-triangular normalization, all 32,768 roots and three exceptional vectors per root, both positive witness branches, literal full-matrix legality and terminal-zero checks, profile recovery, the distinction of actual labels from quotient vectors, duplicate-vector handling, and lifting to every omitted ambient coordinate. The independent audit checked all 98,304 inputs and 98,306 nonidentity edges in normal Python, -O, and -OO modes. Its 66-coordinate stress test supplements the symbolic all-ambient lifting argument; it is not an ambient-size extrapolation.

The proof depends on the accepted full-state/fiber lemmas and the complete five-section terminal/signature theorem. The latter imports only pressability existence from Bixby, Flint, and Miklós. No short-neighbor theorem, negative-search conclusion, basis-exchange claim, universal one-vector component-preservation assertion, or withdrawn connectivity proof is used. Rank at most five was already accepted independently, so the combined original-threshold result holds in ranks at most six.

The positive finite lemma remains computer-assisted. This edition records the exact finite domain, format, checks, counts, and accepted audit but omits the raw positive witness collection and verifier programs. It is not possible to rerun every positive witness from these prose files alone. No replacement symbolic proof of that finite lemma is claimed.

## Obstruction to unique overlap iteration

The displayed rank-eight matrix has exactly the four listed complete words. All eligible unique-overlap contiguous substitutions give exactly two disjoint edges, even when their interval length and edit deficit are unrestricted. The original LCS≥r−4 graph is exactly a connected four-cycle. Both complete 18-row reduced branch tables, the full matrices, all four words, exact LCS values, interval definition, full-state replacement argument, and both graph proofs are preserved.

The obstruction applies also to iterating the prior short-neighbor theorem’s explicit minimal-interval moves, since those moves lie within the larger restricted system considered here. Every word still has a short neighbor, so that theorem remains intact. The example is not a counterexample to original pressing connectivity; it does not classify all four-word languages, prove vertex minimality, or defeat every possible interval-based method. Nonunique one-end truncation languages may support a stronger argument.

# Editorial changes and publication scope

## Preservation of the complete arguments

Every mathematical formula, hypothesis, case division, symbolic reduction, proof argument, aggregate verification count, negative-control conclusion, and small authored example in the two accepted proofs and their complete audits is preserved. The unique-overlap proof and audit are distributed byte-for-byte as accepted, including both complete 18-row trees, all four words, all matrices, the exact original graph and restricted graph, and the exhaustive interval argument.

The rank-six text has only editorial substitutions: public links replace undistributed dependency identities; descriptions of the complete positive witness collection and verification replace undistributed artifact names, sizes, hashes, and executable commands; and its stale pending-audit sentence is updated to the accepted verdict. The full audit is retained rather than replaced by a summary. The separate finite verification contract explains the audited record format, exact domain, and checks. It does not introduce a new mathematical result.

## What this prose edition permits a reader to check

The rank-six symbolic reduction and all-ambient lifting argument are fully present. The mathematical finite lemma, its exact coverage, both witness branches, independent full-state verification, optimization modes, and adversarial controls are all described. The raw positive witnesses and executable verifiers are not distributed. Accordingly, the finite lemma remains a computer-assisted accepted input whose entire replay cannot be reproduced from these prose files alone. There is no claim that symbolic reasoning has replaced that finite step.

The eight-vertex obstruction is different: its small explicit matrices, four words, complete reduced branch tree, LCS values, and graph proofs are authored mathematical evidence included in full. The reduced tree plus its two stated symmetries gives a complete hand-checkable proof without any raw search collection. Those written examples are not a raw state-collection upload.

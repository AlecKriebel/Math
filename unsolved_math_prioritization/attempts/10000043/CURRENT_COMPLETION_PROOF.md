# Explicit completion of the adaptive product-edge law

This fills out PARTIAL.md §2 without changing its partial theorem. Fix p<1, integer M≥1 and a root in the countable locally finite Cartesian product. Use deterministic FIFO incident orders, cache every physical edge on its first query, and accept at most M vertices in each fiber. At every fresh horizontal query over e={u,v}, its height belongs to the eventually accepted height sets at u or v. Their union has at most2M elements; thus at most2M distinct queries occur over e.

Prepare mutually independent Bernoulli(p) reservoirs X(e,j), j=1,…,2M for each unordered base edge, fresh independent vertical query variables, and a separate independent Bernoulli(p) fallback U(a) for every physical product edge a. The next queried physical edge and primitive-variable label depend only on the past transcript, and each primitive is unused when read. Conditional on any finite transcript the next state is independent Bernoulli(p). Distinct queries use distinct primitives. Induction gives exactly the ordinary product exploration's finite-transcript law.

For each n, stop after n fresh queries or termination. Define the whole field W_n by assigned queried states and U(a) on all other edges. In ordinary independent percolation, each finite nonanticipating transcript leaves unqueried edges independent Bernoulli(p); matching transcript probabilities and independent fallback therefore give W_n the full original product law. For each fixed physical edge, W_n eventually stabilizes: a queried edge is assigned at one finite query index, while a never-queried edge always has its fallback. A finite cylinder stabilizes coordinatewise almost surely, so bounded convergence preserves its product probability. Countability gives a measurable limit W with precisely the whole independent product law. No infinite-transcript conditioning, global cap-event conditioning or presumed random-permutation independence is used.

Let Y_e=max_j X(e,j), including UNUSED slots. Disjoint reservoirs give independent homogeneous base bond variables with q_M=1−(1−p)^(2M)<1. Every accepted vertex has an accepted ancestry path, whose horizontal steps project to Y-open edges. Thus its projection lies in the Y-component of the base root. Since pc(G)=1, that component is finite almost surely, and the accepted set has at most M times its size. The whole-field W and Y need not be independent of each other; their separate marginal laws and domination are the required facts.

On the event that the actual W-cluster of the root has at most M vertices in every fiber, no open proposal to a new member can be rejected by a full fiber: it would be an(M+1)st actual cluster member there. Local finiteness makes each queued vertex's incident processing finite and every finite open path eventually processed. Hence that actual cluster is recovered and is finite. Countably many roots and integer M exclude every infinite uniformly capped cluster, including a cap chosen after the configuration. No countable union over arbitrary unbounded capacity functions is asserted.

The safe2M bound is generally sharp for M≥2; M=1 has the stronger one-height bound. Endpoints p=0 are vacuous; p=1 has the entire connected product and infinite fibers directly, while q_M=1 no longer supplies subcritical domination. Combined with PARTIAL.md §1, the exact remaining possibility is an infinite cluster having a finite intersection with every fiber but unbounded sizes over G. This argument neither excludes nor constructs that possibility and asserts no novelty.

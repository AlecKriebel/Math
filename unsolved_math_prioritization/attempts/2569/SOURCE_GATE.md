## Identity

- Catalogue: [2569 / KOU-21.60](https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/2569).
- Source: [Kourovka Notebook, October 2026 revision](https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt.pdf), printed page 186, Problem 21.60, D. Johnston and D. Rumynin.
- Revision provenance: [editors' October update](https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/2026/09/30/october-2026-update-for-the-21st-edition/).

The exact target is the equivalence between semiperfectness of `Z_(p)G` and membership of every projective indecomposable `F_pG`-class in the nonnegative span of reductions of simple `QG`-modules. Both the group and modules use the base fields specified in the problem. The equality is in the ordinary Grothendieck group, not an isomorphism of modules or a split Grothendieck group.

The original page was text-extracted and visually inspected. It leaves this entry unstarred and credits the known necessary implication and the nonmodular case. Adjacent Problem 21.59 concerns character degrees and is a different question.

The exact catalogue page was requested live, but web retrieval failed and a direct HTTP request returned 403. Its live page body is therefore not represented as inspected. The cached catalogue statement agrees with the authoritative October Notebook, which is the target used here.

## Current primary sources

1. D. Johnston and D. Rumynin, [On a question by Roggenkamp about group algebras](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2025.09.014), Journal of Algebra 687 (2026), 776–791; [arXiv:2507.21316v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.21316v2). Full public preprint inspected. Conjecture 9 is the target. Example 3.4 supplies the `F_2A_5` simple/projective data and rational reductions used in the proof. The general extension-lifting assertion of Proposition 3.3 is not an input to the proposed counterexample.
2. F. R. K. Chung, B. Kostant and S. Sternberg, [Groups and the Buckyball](https://fanchung.ucsd.edu/wp/groupb.pdf), Sections 2 and 4. Identifies the binary icosahedral group with `SL(2,5)` and constructs its low-dimensional representations by symmetric powers of the natural `SU(2)` representation. This verifies that the faithful degree-four row used here is an actual character.
3. T. Dokchitser, [GroupNames: SL(2,5)](https://people.maths.bris.ac.uk/~matyd/GroupNames/97/SL(2,5).html). Academic character-table cross-check, including the faithful rational-valued symplectic degree-four character.

The contributing report contains an additional source check of the rational Wedderburn components. That arithmetic classification is not needed for the main proof: the Frobenius–Schur obstruction already rules out a rational lift.

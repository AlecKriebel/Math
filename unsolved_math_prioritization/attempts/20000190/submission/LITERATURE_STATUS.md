# Literature and priority status

Checks dated 2026-10-04. Bounded targeted search, not an exhaustive novelty audit.

1. AIM Algebraic Vision, Reconstruction, problem 1.5. Primary wording checked
   on its canonical HTTP page. The workshop ran May 2-6, 2016; this verifies
   workshop timing, not a separate exact proposal date for the question.
   http://aimpl.org/algvision/1/
   https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/algvision.html
2. Viktor Kocur, Daniel Kyselica, Zuzana Kukelova, *Robust Self-calibration of
   Focal Lengths from the Fundamental Matrix*, CVPR 2024, 5220-5229.
   Main paper section 3.2 and supplement section 3/equation (1) were inspected;
   the supplement formula page was also rendered and read visually. The RFC
   test checks a generated model before scoring it. It is not a pre-root test
   of the seven-point pencil. The paper discusses fixed principal points,
   square pixels, imaginary estimates, and critical camera configurations.
   https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2024/html/Kocur_Robust_Self-calibration_of_Focal_Lengths_from_the_Fundamental_Matrix_CVPR_2024_paper.html
   https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR52733.2024.00499
   The arXiv record currently has v3, revised November 6, 2025; its online text
   was additionally inspected. Do not confuse that revision date with CVPR's
   publication year. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.16304
3. Louis Gaillard and Mohab Safey El Din, *Solving parameter-dependent
   semi-algebraic systems*, ISSAC 2024, 447-456. The privately retrieved arXiv
   version is v2, January 24, 2025. Theorem 2.4 and equation (2) supply the
   classical Hermite-signature/Tarski-query identity and zero-aware sign
   determination. These methods predate this audit. The generic parametric
   hypotheses in that paper are not a blanket solution of every critical
   camera fiber. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.07782v2
   https://doi.org/10.1145/3666000.3669718

The imported report cites Bougnoux and earlier camera-calibration work. Those
bibliographic leads are not represented here as newly read full texts. The
new controls are independent fixtures; the imported report's fixture is not
republished or counted as a new contribution.

Queries included combinations of seven-point, focal positivity, Sturm--Tarski,
Hermite matrix, before computing the fundamental matrix, and real focal length
checking. No independently verified full resolution of the broad AIM question
was found in that bounded search. This negative search result is not evidence
of first priority. The mathematical trace-form extension is explicitly a
classical consequence, and no novel theorem claim is made.

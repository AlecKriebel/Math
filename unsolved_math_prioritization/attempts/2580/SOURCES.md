1. E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov, editors, *The Kourovka Notebook*, 21st edition, October 2026 revision, Problem 21.71, printed/PDF page 187, attributed to D. Kielak.
   - Primary PDF: https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt.pdf
   - Revision notice: https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/2026/09/30/october-2026-update-for-the-21st-edition/
   - The downloaded primary page was visually checked. The problem is unmarked and has no solution comment in this revision. This is dated source status, not a novelty certificate.

2. I. J. Leary, *The Euler class of a Poincare duality group*, Proceedings of the Edinburgh Mathematical Society 45 (2002), 421-448, DOI 10.1017/S0013091500001164.
   - https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/proceedings-of-the-edinburgh-mathematical-society/article/euler-class-of-a-poincare-duality-group/297EAAF2F9D4ED283EDB57ABD657DB9A
   - Its examples of groups FL over C but not Q prevent an invalid descent claim. Same-characteristic equality of ordinary Betti numbers does not imply descent of FL.

3. I. J. Leary and M. Saadetoglu, *The cohomology of Bestvina-Brady groups*, arXiv:0711.5018v1 (2007); Groups, Geometry, and Dynamics 5 (2011), 121-138, DOI 10.4171/GGD/118.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.5018
   - https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/29580?nt=1
   - The arXiv full text was read: Proposition 6, Corollaries 7-9 and Proposition 4 underpin the credited ordinary Bestvina-Brady exclusion in attempt 4. The face-count Euler formula is existing work, not an original result of these notes.

4. R. Kropholler, *Constructing groups of type FP2 over fields but not over the integers*, arXiv:2102.13509v1 (2021).
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13509
   - Its FP2 examples illustrate why coefficientwise finiteness does not automatically imply integral finiteness. They are not asserted here to have the full FL hypotheses or to solve 21.71.

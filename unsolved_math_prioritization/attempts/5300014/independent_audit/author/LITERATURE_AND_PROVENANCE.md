## Recent and foundational primary literature checked

1. Cao, Wang, Yin, *Boundary of the central hyperbolic component II: boundary extension theorem*, arXiv:2509.07350v1. The definitions and statements used in Approach 4 were checked; PDF pp.2 and 30 were visually inspected. The publisher records publication on 22 May 2026, Mathematische Annalen 395, article 69. https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.07350 ; https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-026-03473-x . This compares an algebraic Blaschke boundary with a polynomial geometric boundary. It does not state the requested comparison with each nonmonomial rational slice. The complete external proof is not certified here; the journal PDF was not retrieved.

2. He, Lee, Park, *Analytic Theory on the Space of Blaschke Products: Simultaneous Uniformization and Weil-Petersson Metric*, arXiv:2507.17077v3 (15 September 2026); publisher version published 25 September 2026. Its simultaneous-uniformization theorem concerns fixed-point-marked quasi-Blaschke products and verifies the modern complex-analytic interior setup. Interior biholomorphy does not imply boundary equivalence or inequivalence. Introduction, theorem statements and relevant references were inspected. https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.17077 ; https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnag215 .

3. Luo, Mj, Mukherjee, *Teichmuller spaces, polynomial loci, and degeneration in spaces of algebraic correspondences*, arXiv:2504.13107v2 (4 May 2026). Its introduction explicitly discusses comparison of analytic compactifications, but its results concern group-polynomial correspondences. The special four-punctured-sphere compactification equivalence is in that different setting. It does not yield the universal rational mating assertion. https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13107 .

4. Luo, *On geometrically finite degenerations I: boundaries of main hyperbolic components*, JEMS 26 (2024), 3793-3861. The introduction classifies geometrically finite polynomial boundary maps and proves self-bumping in degree at least four. This supplies no comparison of each K_A with K_0. https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1342 ; https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/31415 .

5. Luo, *On geometrically finite degenerations II: convergence and divergence*, Transactions AMS 375 (2022), 3469-3527; arXiv:2102.00357v2. Theorem 1.3 gives subsequential convergence under quasi-postcritical-finiteness and nonparallel dual laminations. We did not verify those hypotheses for an obstruction sequence, nor obtain incompatible paired limits. Thus a convergence criterion is not a solution of the non-equivalence problem. https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.00357 ; https://doi.org/10.1090/tran/8597 .

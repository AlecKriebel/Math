# Claim boundaries

1. Theorem 4 disproves coherent compact-domain approximation in an unrestricted closed 3-manifold. It is not labeled a disproof of every possible interpretation of Calegari Question 14.2.
2. The original question speaks of converging images of intrinsic balls. Its formal convergence topology was not located in the inspected source. Section 5 proves that image-set convergence does not force coherent approximation of leaf loops.
3. The circle-bundle example is Seifert-fibered. It does not settle a hyperbolic-only restriction.
4. Closed essential surfaces are interpreted as pi_1-injective closed immersed surfaces. Boundary is not allowed. Disk-incompressibility, one-sided incompressibility, or arbitrary nonproper surfaces would require a separate category analysis.
5. The positive linear-torus example is a special case. No virtual-fibering, subgroup-separability, branched-surface, or Kahn–Markovic theorem is claimed to complete an arbitrary prescribed leaf patch.
6. The exact code checks are finite guardrails. The topological and asymptotic conclusions depend on the written proofs; no finite computation establishes the original universal statement.
7. Literature and duplicate checks were bounded. There is no first-resolution, novelty, exhaustive-status, or independent-verification claim.
8. The upstream website was unavailable. The complete fallback statement and research report were inspected after their containing dataset files matched the public manifest's hashes and sizes.
9. No remote write, publication, submission, or external contact was performed. This is an author-frozen packet awaiting a fresh independent audit.

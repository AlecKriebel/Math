## Important distinctions

The OWR kernel is G(x)=Σ_j(log 2)^(x+j)(1−exp(−2^(x+j))). The full permutation paper uses g(x)=G(−x). Its Fourier coefficient therefore has the opposite sign in the gamma argument. The modulus and zero set agree, but a measure identification cannot be inferred from this reflection. A positive scalar c0 is also present in the permutation profile formula and absent as a separate factor in the report's arithmetic formula; such a scalar can be absorbed into measure masses, but it does not identify the measures.

The arithmetic phase is the fractional part of log(log n)/log 2. The permutation phase is the fractional part of log k/log 2. In particular, the two integer parameters must not be silently identified. The target is about the fixed arithmetic profile, rather than arbitrary positive convolutions or the known nonconstant universal kernel.

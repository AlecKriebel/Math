The primary minimal-surface Seshadri lower bound is false. For every integerd>=7,
the construction gives a smooth integral complex projective surfaceS with ample
canonical bundle, K_S^2=6(d-1)(d-3), and an ample integral line bundleL withL^2=6.
An integral curve through a special point has L-degree1 and multiplicityd-1, so
epsilon(S,L;x)<=1/(d-1)<1/(2+(K_S^2)^(1/4)). At d7, K_S^2=144 and the quotient
is1/6. This resolves the exact primary proposed universal inequality, with2
outside the fourth root, documented in2007/2008 and restated as OWR2009Conj2.
Only this upper Seshadri bound is asserted; the surfaces vary with d. No exact
epsilon, fixed-surface vanishing, rank-one or very-general-point claim is made.

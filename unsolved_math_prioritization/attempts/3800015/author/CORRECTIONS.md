# Correction to the two edge hop witness

In the five-line example of Proposition 5, the two-edge hop-optimal route passes through C∩D=(79/7,209/21), not A∩B=(5/7,−20/21).

The A∩B route is a valid one-bend geometric route of length 65/3, but it crosses E twice, at (0,0) and (3,0). Its expanded arrangement path therefore has four edges. The C∩D route has the same length 65/3 and has exactly two arrangement edges: neither of its segment interiors contains another intersection.

The current proof uses C∩D for the hop witness. The exact checker explicitly tests both edges of that witness. This correction leaves the Euclidean optimum 21, the best one-bend length 65/3, and the hop optimum two unchanged.

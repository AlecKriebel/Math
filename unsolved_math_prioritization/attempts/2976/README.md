# KP-4.100: fixed-form ruled case and formal reductions

## Accepted partial result

For every h>=0, the target symplectic-representative assertion holds on
S2 x Sigma_h equipped with any fixed split symplectic form, for every integral
class satisfying the original smooth connected-embedding, positive-area and
adjunction-equality hypotheses. This includes negative-square sections and
nonprimitive square-zero classes. The [full report](MATHEMATICAL_REPORT.md)
retains the sign-controlled degree maps, the Moser adjustment of negative
section graphs, connected cyclic multisections, positive-node smoothing,
and the exhaustive genus/area/Kneser case analysis for all integral classes.
The original symplectic form is retained throughout.

Two accompanying reductions hold under their exact hypotheses:

- Every target input admits a formal isosymplectic differential deformation
  and a same-genus symplectic immersion. Signed transverse double points
  balance. The negative-intersection example shows why balance does not
  imply embeddedness or automatically positive intersections.
- For positive genus and b2+>1, every Seiberg–Witten basic class L satisfies
  |L(c)|<=K(c), including negative-square inputs by the precisely scoped
  Ozsvath–Szabo Corollary 1.7. Thus a class A with K-2PD(A) basic satisfies
  0<=A.c<=K(c). No genus-zero or chamber-independent b2+=1 claim is made.

The general fixed-form four-manifold existence question remains unresolved
by this work. No universal proof or counterexample was found. The ruled
special case is credited to its established literature context, and no
novelty is claimed. The exact target is problem 2976 / KP-4.100; the K3
problem-list PDF pages 272–273 fix the first-Chern sign. Source details and
limits are in [SOURCE_REVIEW.md](SOURCE_REVIEW.md).

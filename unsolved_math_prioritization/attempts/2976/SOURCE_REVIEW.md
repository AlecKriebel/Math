# Source identification and recorded review

Problem 2976 / KP-4.100 concerns a fixed symplectic form on a closed connected
four-manifold and a connected smoothly embedded surface in an integral class
of positive symplectic area satisfying adjunction equality. The target is
some connected embedded symplectic representative of that same class.

The first-Chern sign was checked in the K3 problem list on PDF pages 272–273:
`c1(TX,omega)(c) = 2-2g+c^2`, equivalently `2g-2 = c^2+K(c)` for
`K=-c1(TX,omega)`. Later canonical-class terminology does not reverse the
explicit equation. The source URL is
https://math.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/surv-295-ruberman-watermarked-author-pdf.pdf .

## Theorems used, with their limits

- Tian-Jun Li, *Existence of symplectic surfaces*,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/0812.4929 . The recorded review inspected PDF pages
  3–4, Remark 2.5, Theorems 2.6–2.7 and Proposition 2.8. Equality of quotient
  Euler numbers yields the formal differential deformation. The immersion
  theorem permits a closed domain in real codimension two; the embedding
  theorem requires real codimension at least four. No four-dimensional
  embedding conclusion is inferred from the immersion theorem.
- Ozsvath–Szabo, *The symplectic Thom conjecture*,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9811087 . Corollary 1.7 in the recorded v2,
  PDF page 5 (printed page 97), concerns negative square, positive genus,
  Seiberg–Witten simple type and b2+>1, without a primitivity condition or
  lower bound on the negative square. The recorded audit also inspected
  PDF page 13, Remark 3.3, for the symplectic simple-type theorem. The report
  combines this with the usual nonnegative-square adjunction inequality,
  retaining g>0 and b2+>1 throughout.
- Ryabichev, *Short proof of the Kneser–Edmonds theorem on the degree of a
  map between closed surfaces*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.01041 . The
  recorded v1 Theorem 1 on PDF page 1 gives the degree/genus restriction
  used for projection to the base. The positive cohomological degree in
  this oriented-surface application agrees with the degree convention
  used there. The torus-base case is included.

## Prior work and restricted conclusions

- Bang-He Li and Tian-Jun Li, *Symplectic genus, minimal genus and
  diffeomorphisms*, https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0108227 . The recorded v1
  introduction, Theorems A–C and Section 3 provide rational/ruled context;
  the audit visually inspected PDF page 2. They are not substituted for a
  theorem proving arbitrary fixed-form sufficiency.
- Dorfmeister–Li–Wu, *Stability and existence of surfaces in symplectic
  4-manifolds with b^+=1*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.1089 . Recorded v1
  inspections include PDF pages 3, 6 and 7. Theorem 1.3 begins with a
  symplectic configuration, so it does not construct the target from the
  smooth hypothesis alone. Theorems 1.8 and 1.9 provide respectively the
  rational square-minus-four sphere case and the irrational-ruled sphere
  case. Speculation 1.10 aligns with the target but is not evidence of a
  present-day resolution or a certificate of current openness.
- Li–Usher, *Symplectic forms and surfaces of negative square*,
  https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0601540 . The recorded v1 introduction and
  Section 2 were read, with audit visual inspection of PDF page 3.
  Negative inflation changes the ambient form; it is not used as a
  fixed-form sufficiency proof.

The product construction is an explicit fixed-split-form verification in
the established rational/ruled setting, with prior-work credit retained.
It proves all allowed integral classes on S2 x Sigma_h, for every h>=0,
including negative-square sections and connected nonprimitive square-zero
multisections. It does not settle the original arbitrary-four-manifold
assertion and makes no novelty claim.

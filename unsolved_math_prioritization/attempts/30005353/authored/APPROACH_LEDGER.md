# Five substantive mathematical approaches

The source/semantic gate was completed before substantive work. Source lookup, duplicate checks, primary-paper inspection, testing, review, and packaging are not counted as mathematical approaches.

1. **Algebraic shortest bases and torsion.** Reduced a₂ to a minimum F₂ basis, proved the weakly-fundamental normal-generation criterion, built a simplicial degree-p Moore complex, proved the six-edge systole lemma, and derived exact costs. Result: a strict gap of three with ratio 1+1/(54p). Gap: larger torsion order does not amplify the ratio.

2. **Amplification by gluing and subdivision.** Proved exact additivity under articulation/bridge joins and exact cost scaling under uniform edge subdivision. Applied both to the Moore family. Separately analyzed all triangulations of RP², obtaining coefficient-sensitive exact equalities. Result: valid connected bounded-degree additive gaps, and a no-go theorem for the two elementary ratio-amplification operations. Gap: more coupled graph constructions are needed.

3. **Degree-reduction gadgets.** Constructed tree-replacement and long-path graphs of maximum degree three. Proved lower and upper cost bounds separately for homology and normal generation, including non-simple projected walks. Result: equality of the two extended-real suprema. Gap: the operation transfers large ratios but does not produce them.

4. **Field-dependent systoles and quotient obstructions.** Proved the universal-relator lower bound 3r+d(s−3) for an F₂-homology-vanishing triangular complex, and a normal-rank times systole bound through finite quotients. Tested the explicit torsion and replication families against the criterion and developed the precise sufficient covering-tower conditions. Gap: no family satisfying cheap mod-2 filling together with quantitatively many long independent obstructions was constructed.

5. **Global upper bounds by metric cycle deletion.** Proved a crude rank-times-cost bound and reconstructed the known Rizzi/Kavitha–Rizzi deletion method with explicit cyclic-edge and cycle-rank bookkeeping. Derived a logarithmic bound and planar exclusion (sharpened to credited equality). Gap: logarithmic upper bounds do not decide whether the supremum is finite.

Disposition: **unresolved, 5/5**. All five approaches contain an actual construction, proof, or quantitative obstruction; none is merely a literature search. All mathematical credit is retained, with no novelty certification.

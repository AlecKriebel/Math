# Editorial changes and external input scope

## Complete mathematical coverage

This edition preserves every mathematical formula, domain, case division, proof argument, negative-control conclusion, aggregate check count, and small authored counterexample in the two accepted manuscripts and two substantive audits. The short-neighbor audit includes its full arbitrary-ambient reconstruction and all three additional corollary proofs. The terminal manuscript includes all five sections, particularly the odd-parity replacement construction and auxiliary four-edge bridge.

The editorial changes update stale review status, apply the audit's proved three-word scope clarification, resolve mathematical dependencies to their existing public editions, and replace references to undistributed working artifacts with descriptions of the verification performed. Earlier-version and undistributed verification-record identities are not reproduced. One compiler command is expressed as equivalent prose; no verification conclusion changes. The full texts are not replaced by summaries.

The manifest identifies the distributed prose and public-source metadata. Executable programs, raw result collections, copied scholarly PDFs and extracted source text, and working records are outside this edition. Small explicit matrices and successful words that demonstrate sharpness or the necessity of mathematical hypotheses remain in the written arguments. This is therefore a complete prose edition, not an executable reproduction package.

## Cooper and Whitlatch

The short-neighbor proof uses the unique-pressability characterization of Joshua N. Cooper and Hays W. Whitlatch, *Uniquely Pressable Graphs: Characterization, Enumeration, and Recognition*. Definition 7, printed p. 10, and Theorem 1, printed p. 13, refer to the author-hosted manuscript dated July 18, 2017. The journal article is in Advances in Applied Mathematics 103 (2019), 13–42. The proof verifies the required connectedness reduction and does not assume journal pagination matches the manuscript. The characterization itself is not reproved.

## Bixby Flint and Miklós

The terminal-coordinate proof uses Theorem 4, printed p. 47, of *Proving the pressing game conjecture on linear graphs*, Involve 9(1) (2016), 41–56. This is the general pressability-existence theorem; it is not restricted to linear graphs. Isolated white vertices are ignored. Conjecture 6 on that page supplies the original four-deletion and four-insertion normalization. No external connectivity theorem is imported.

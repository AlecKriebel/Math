# Source and attribution review

Checked 6 October 2026. This is a bounded search report, not an assertion that every publication has been searched.

1. The live unsolvedmath page was unavailable (HTTP 403 in direct retrieval; the search/open tool could not load it). The complete supplied problem record and corresponding empty research-report object were reviewed; their default sorted-JSON hash exactly matched the specified review hash. The statement hash also matched. These bytes are not redistributed.
2. The official EMS report was downloaded, its Hanke contribution was read, and printed page 1912 was visually inspected. It explicitly defines free toral rank and free p-rank through actual free actions on the fixed space. It states the question under the specified simple-space and finite rational-homotopy hypotheses. There is no almost-free or rational-homotopy substitute in that displayed definition.
3. Hanke's 2009 paper on products of spheres gives a large-prime rank bound. Such a bound does not equate free toral rank with free p-rank on all rationally elliptic spaces. It does not obstruct the Wu-manifold construction.
4. The nonzero Stiefel-Whitney number of SU(3)/SO(3) is classical. Debray-Yu (2024), Corollary 4.44 and Proposition 4.45, provide a published explicit account and historical attribution. Their purpose is a bordism/anomaly calculation; they do not claim to resolve the OWR question.
5. Yeroshkin (2015), Proposition 4.2, explicitly studies the circle orbifold quotients of the Wu manifold. The weights used here are the (1,1) specialization of that family. The stabilizer calculation in the authored proof is also proved directly.
6. Galaz-Garcia-Searle (2014), printed p.1421, discusses the failure of free circle subgroups for the Wu manifold in its isometric torus-action setting. Eschenburg-Kollross-Shankar classify free isometric circle actions on symmetric spaces. Neither isometric statement alone would exclude arbitrary topological circle actions. The authored proof instead excludes every free topological involution by covering naturality of Stiefel-Whitney classes.
7. Mundet i Riera's *Discrete Degree of Symmetry of Manifolds* (published online 2024; journal volume 2026) studies effective actions and gives other strict-gap constructions. Its unrestricted invariant is not the same as the free-action invariant here; those examples are not needed for this proof.

Searches using the OWR identifier, problem number, free-rank phrases, Hanke, and Wu manifold did not locate an explicit publication of this precise OWR resolution. That negative result does not establish novelty. Repository searches by the exact problem ID and by its subject, together with the available attempts inventory, found no prior substantive solution to this target. A desk-review entry is not counted as a proof attempt.

## Approach record

The first approach examined the source definitions and general discrete-symmetry literature. It did not itself settle the question. The second approach used an almost-free homogeneous circle action together with a genuinely integral obstruction to every free torus action. The fixed Wu manifold gives a complete witness, so further approaches were stopped rather than inflating the attempt count. Two mathematical approaches were used; fresh independent audit is still pending.

The proof does not need an integral realization theorem, a passage from finite homotopy type to a free action, or a limiting torus action. It also does not infer an infinite-prime conclusion from finite computational examples.

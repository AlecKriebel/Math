# Accepted preprint for problem 30001522

[A classical Wu-manifold answer to a free-rank question](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23422039) by Alec Kriebel, ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X, version 1.0. The single fixed Wu manifold W = SU(3)/SO(3), with the real SO(3) inclusion, is an actual finite simply connected complex of rational homotopy type S^5. For arbitrary continuous free actions on this same space, its free toral rank and free 2-rank are zero and its free p-rank is one for every odd prime; every cyclic group of odd order acts freely and smoothly. This satisfies all the homotopy hypotheses and answers Hanke's printed 2010 existence question. Almost-free and rational toral ranks are different invariants.

# Complete exact examples and how to read them

[EXACT_GEOMETRY_EXAMPLES.json](EXACT_GEOMETRY_EXAMPLES.json) gives nine complete authored examples. Every coordinate is an exact canonical rational string. A triangle is the closed filled convex hull of its three listed vertices. The two roots of the first example are the half-planes x<=0 and y<=0, encoded by rational inequalities a*x+b*y<=c. All other objects are nondegenerate triangles. Each fixture gives every object, every whole maximal edge, every strict common edge witness, and complete face and nonface counts.

The first fixture is the two-half-plane model. Its three bounded triangles are exactly the eta=1/1000 model from [the complete geometric proof](PAIR_GLUING_PROOF.md), with minimum unnormalized witness slack 9/500000. All 31 nonempty subfamilies are checked, giving seventeen faces and fourteen nonfaces.

The three pure block examples are A_1, A_2 and A_3. They use the fixed finite root triangles and the exact scale bands 8^(-i). Their complete subfamily counts are 31, 255 and 2,047, with seventeen, thirty-one and forty-five faces. Three mixed examples have (k,t)=(0,1),(1,1),(2,2), with seven, sixty-three and 1,023 subfamilies and six, twenty and thirty-seven faces. Ears use their own bands after the block bands. Every displayed root degree and whole incidence 2-core follows from the explicit edge inventory.

The final two fixtures are actual insertions across newly introduced pair-edges. The first attaches across {3_0,4_0}, the second across {x3,x4}; their positive rational scales are 1/8 and 1/4096. Each supplies the complete earlier objects, active root-side indices, private crossing, strict separators from every other old triangle, all new coordinates, and all resulting whole edges and witnesses. The checker rebuilds the rational chart, checks agreement with the two local root half-planes, verifies that all new triangles avoid all old nonroots, and compares every earlier triangle unchanged. Their complete nerves have 255 and 2,047 subfamilies. These recursive examples make no incidence-dimension assertion.

The edition's geometric checker is independent of the author programs. It computes all rational intersections of independent boundary lines in the full finite arrangement, and tests which objects contain each point. The origin covers every root-only subfamily. Any feasible intersection containing a triangle is a bounded planar polyhedron with an extreme point at two nonparallel active supporting lines. Consequently the arrangement points determine every intersection, including point and segment contacts. The checker compares all 5,759 nonempty subfamilies across the nine examples against the downward closures of their displayed whole edges. This is a complete finite check, not an extrapolation from sampled pairs.

## Accepted final advance

A complete cyclic nonplanar block can be inserted along a maximal pair-edge whose endpoints both have degree at least two. A nerve-preserving stable rational generic normalization may first be necessary. The insertion preserves every old triangle exactly after normalization. It does not promise preservation of arbitrary original coordinates before that step.

A separate compatible-root order theorem proves incidence dimension at most three under explicit local order hypotheses. It applies to every mixed family A_{k,t} consisting of k copies of the published five-vertex block and t two-parent ears sharing the same root pair and its whole edge, for k,t>=0 with k+t>=1. These families have rational triangle representations and incidence dimension exactly three. Their incidence graphs are 2-connected and equal their entire 2-cores, with root degrees 2k+t+1. They are nonplanar when k>=1. The root pair remains a separator: 3-connectivity is not claimed.

The geometric gluing theorem does not imply general dimension-preserving gluing. The compatible local order patterns have not been proved for every dimension-three hypergraph, and arbitrary larger separators remain outside the result.

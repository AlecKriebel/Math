# Source scope repair for problem 30006513

## Corrected source target

Let M be a countable structure that is homogeneous in a finite relational language, let G = Aut(M), and let k be a positive integer. Does the free permutation module F[M^k] have finite composition length as an FG-module for every coefficient field F?

Source: David M. Evans, Oberwolfach Report 58/2025, p. 3076, https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/52445 . The countable ambient setting is given on p. 3075. Evans, arXiv:2603.29606v2, Question 1.2, repeats this target: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.29606v2 .

## Status distinction

- Literal catalogue formulation, allowing arbitrary omega-categorical M and oligomorphic W: false by a verified, previously published F_2 counterexample.
- Original finite-relational homogeneous finite-length question: not resolved by that counterexample. The current primary literature inspected continues to present it as an open general question with substantial positive cases.
- General omega-categorical ascending-chain condition: a different question, not disproved by a descending chain.

## Credited prior result

Bojańczyk, Fijalkow, Klin, and Moerman, TheoretiCS 3 (2024), Article 13, Theorem 4.16 and Appendix B, prove that F_2[V] for V = direct_sum_N F_2 and G = GL(V) has the strictly descending incidence-submodule chain generated by all n-dimensional linear subspaces, n >= 1. The full proof was retrieved and checked. The projective-sort chain follows by deleting the zero basis coordinate on the augmentation submodule. Faithful scalar extension gives the same failure for every characteristic-2 coefficient field. This is prior-result verification, not novelty.

Published source: https://doi.org/10.46298/theoretics.24.13 . Full proof: https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/bartek.klin/papers/theoretics24.pdf .

No counterexample for all coefficient characteristics is claimed. Yang–Bojańczyk–Klin, LICS 2026, Theorem 5.4 and Corollary 5.5, give finite length for this vector-atom action in characteristic zero. Their all-field result covers the precise unary/binary free-amalgamation hypotheses of Theorem 7.3 and its stated consequences, rather than every finite-relational homogeneous structure. https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.LICS.2026.82 .

## Recommended disposition

Release the prior-proof-availability hold for the literal binary counterexample. Retain the source-formulation correction and the unresolved restricted target.

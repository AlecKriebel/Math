# Hardy spaces on noncompact manifolds: published negative answer

**Problem 30003538 / OWR-15577-010: already_solved, 0/5 proof-attempt turns.** This is a credited source resolution, with no new analytic proof or novelty claim.

The unrestricted assertion is false. In the [original OWR report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46697), Meda's Theorem 1(a), printed p2136, already states that the global heat, Poisson and Riesz Hardy spaces are different on rank-one noncompact symmetric spaces, with the hyperbolic disc named as a prototype.

The later [Martini–Meda–Vallarino–Veronelli paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2023.110240) supplies detailed results. In the accessible [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.02532v1), Theorem 2.10 identifies the full Riesz Hardy space with X^(1/2); Corollary 2.11 shows that compactly supported members are not norm dense on rank-one noncompact symmetric spaces. Corollary 4.9 gives pairwise distinction on Damek–Ricci spaces.

[SOURCE_STATUS.md](SOURCE_STATUS.md) preserves the exact global absolute-value maximal definitions, the full versus modified Riesz distinction and the scope of the atomic obstruction. This rules out Hardy-norm-convergent decompositions into compactly supported atoms in that space. It does not rule out arbitrary noncompact molecules, frames or abstract expansions. The separate restricted-doubling question and local maximal-space equivalences are not resolved by this packet.

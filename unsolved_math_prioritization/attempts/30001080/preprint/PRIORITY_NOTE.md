# Bounded priority review and edition limits

The exact theorem compared is the all-group, arbitrary jointly measurable ambient, sigma-finite-Q converse from full ambient invariance under every invariant xi-only xi-preserving Markov transport to joint mass-stationarity, as posed in Last–Thorisson2009 Problem7.3. No Borel ambient space, finite intensity or sigma-finite xi marginal is added.

Two independent approach families examined citation/author literature and repository/DOI records, followed by a fresh independent source adjudication. No exact competing theorem was identified within the checked primary bodies and bounded records. This supports stating an affirmative answer to the formal2009 problem. It does not certify worldwide firstness, present openness or exhaustive literature coverage.

Checked antecedents include the Annals2009 weighted characterization and Mecke/Palm equivalences; the full published COSA2011 discrete/Borel-state and Cox-extension results; the retained arXiv1405.7566v2 positive-density and stationary-background results; the Modern Palm Theory repository chapter; line-transport, diffuse-allocation, tail-measure and2025 invariant-transport works in their explicitly checked frameworks. Restricted results assuming an already stationary flow or a joint density field do not supply the assigned full converse. Setting X=xi in a Cox extension does not supply its missing joint-Cox invariance premise from the original measure-only hypothesis.

Unreviewed bodies remain: the complete final SPA2015 journal edition (DOI10.1016/j.spa.2015.07.006), the final Modern Palm Theory chapter, the final2018 line-transport journal edition, the2016 BIRS/UBC lecture audiovisual body or transcript, and the2019 Canal-U talk. Source access and repository descriptions were not treated as theorem-body verification. Separately checked2016 Bristol slides are not certified as the BIRS lecture. No equality of unread final and retained author versions is assumed. Forward-citation and repository searches were bounded and nonexhaustive.

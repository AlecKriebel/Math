## C: strict point-only Cox Markov characterization for probability laws

Let G be lcsc Hausdorff Abelian and P a probability law of a nonzero locally finite Radon measure α on its canonical state space. For every invariant counting-preserving Markov rule T observing only the rooted counting configuration, set K_T(α,s,A)=∫T(η+δ_s,s,A)Π_α(dη). Invariance of P under every such K_T is equivalent to canonical mass-stationarity. The sufficient kernels are symmetric observable lazy transports; finite observable symmetrization, positive deweighting, Radon–Nikodym comparison against four σ-finite Campbell measures, two-Poisson-law identifiability and period inversion prove the result.

## D: strict count-only Cox allocation characterization on discrete groups

Let G be countable discrete Abelian and P a σ-finite canonical law of nonzero locally finite α, permitting infinite total P. Invariance under every projected invariant count-only counting-preserving allocation implies full canonical Mecke reversal and mass-stationarity; the direct nonnegative converse gives equivalence. It suffices to use displacement-restricted singleton matchings and identity (and their half mixtures when convenient). Invariance under both deterministic allocations implies invariance under the mixture. Bounded endpoint conductance, localized invariant Jordan currents and finite nonnegative-density iteration rule out each signed current.

Full proof: turn3/DISCRETE_COUNT_ONLY_PROOF.md. The independent adversarial appendix in turn3/DISCRETE_VERIFICATION_APPENDIX.md makes the localized Jordan-pair convention explicit and supplies the infinite-σ-finite-law converse. This is a discrete theorem; for diffuse α on a non-discrete group, each fixed-displacement test has zero off-diagonal intensity and gives no useful jump.

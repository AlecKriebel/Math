# 30001080: measure-only Markov transport characterization of mass-stationarity

**Claimed solved for formal Annals Problem 7.3 and the literal imported all-Markov target, under their stated setting. ROOT accepted bounded priority for the precise formal A claim with disclosed gaps. Fresh whole-preprint review and publication readiness remain pending.** Read SCOPE_AND_CREDIT.md with this result.

For every locally compact second countable Hausdorff Abelian group G, arbitrary measurable ambient space Ω with jointly measurable action, σ-finite Q, and nonzero covariant locally finite Radon ξ, Q is mass-stationary for ξ if and only if it is invariant under every invariant ξ-preserving ξ-measurable Markov transport. The conclusion retains the full joint ambient state; neither a standard-Borel Ω, a σ-finite ξ-marginal, finite intensity, nor finite total ξ mass is assumed. TURN_2.md, Sections 2–5, supplies the proof; the canonical precursor is TURN_1.md. The classical Mecke/Palm equivalence and converse transport invariance are credited to Last–Thorisson (2009).

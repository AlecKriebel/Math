- Exact original: Last, joint with Thorisson, *Bernoulli and Cox transports of stationary random measures*, Oberwolfach Report 47/2008, printed 2673–2674. The full contribution and rendered question page were read. The last paragraph asks about the distribution of the intensity under Cox-derived invariant transports; the preceding paragraph expressly uses the joint intensity/Cox object. This supports the review's explicit intensity-background interpretation. [Report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46189)
- Full formal framework: Last–Thorisson, *Invariant transports of stationary random measures and mass-stationarity*, Annals of Probability 37(2) (2009), 790–813, DOI 10.1214/08-AOP420. Sections 2–4, Definition 6.1, Theorem 6.3 and the complete discussion/questions in Section 7 were read. Problem 7.3 restricts transport kernels to dependence on xi and the starting point but tests the entire abstract law. A transport-kernel is Markovian by definition. [Accessed electronic reprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0906.2062v1)

# Adopted fixed-pair complex signature problem

Row 1793 / ID 20000200 / AIM-ALGEBRAIC_GEOMETRY-0200. Accepted disposition: **scoped_resolved1/5**.

The group is fixed as complex SE(2,C), and the ordered rational invariant coordinates are fixed as (u,v)=(κ²,κ_s), with signed Euclidean curvature and Euclidean arc length. Coordinates and group are not changed during the classification.

The input is every reduced integral one-dimensional closed algebraic S in A²_(u,v), with no genus, degree or rationality restriction. Affine singularities and infinity are included through the smooth projective normalization. Allowed sources are reduced integral affine plane curves of degree greater than one with generically nonzero Euclidean metric. Realization means equality of S with the Zariski closure of the rational standard-signature image.

The accepted result is an input-derived necessary-and-sufficient finite algebraic criterion and reconstruction certificate, including one-dimensional constant-u and v=0 strata. Point signatures are a boundary statement only. Arbitrary additional covers and unknown source functions are eliminated. A preferred cover is proved sufficient, its plane field generation is proved, and the exact prescribed signature image is recovered.

The theorem is over C. Uniform effectivity is only for an explicitly presented finite extension of Q(T), with declared algebraically independent transcendence basis T, exact input representations and a chosen embedding in C. This includes number fields. No claim is made for unrepresented numerical complex coefficients.

Excluded: real descent, smooth closed traversals, other groups or invariant pairs, full Euclidean reflection quotients, reducible sources, higher ambient dimensions and positive characteristic. This is not a broad AIM resolution or a novelty claim. The theoretical algorithm is not a general-purpose implemented decision program or a practical complexity guarantee.

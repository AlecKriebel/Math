# Public citations and inspection limits

The exact mathematical claim is the adopted fixed complex SE(2,C) pair, not every interpretation of the broad AIM question. All source payloads are excluded; source hashes and inspection histories are public metadata only.

- American Institute of Mathematics, [Algebraic vision: Invariants, Problem 3.5](http://aimpl.org/algvision/3/). The broad question does not specify the adopted group, pair, coefficient model or image convention. The retained original item and surrounding boundaries were inspected. An independent live retry returned a gateway error; it is not recorded as a successful fresh retrieval.
- I. A. Kogan, M. Ruddy and C. Vinzant, [Differential signatures of algebraic curves](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.11388v2), SIAM J. Appl. Algebra Geom. 4 (2020), 185–226. Equations (20)–(21), Table 1, Example 2.39 and symmetry-degree statements establish the fixed-pair setting, not this inverse-realizability theorem.
- Robert H. Risch, [The solution of the problem of integration in finite terms](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9904-1970-12454-5), Bull. Amer. Math. Soc. 76 (1970), 605–608. The entire original paper was inspected using the [academic mirror](https://www.cs.ru.nl/~freek/courses/mfocs-2012/risch/S0002-9904-1970-12454-5.pdf), including rendered pp.607–608. The exact coefficient caveat and two-good-reductions torsion mechanism are classical prior art and are expressly credited here.
- J. S. Milne, [Abelian Varieties, v1.1](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/AV110.pdf), Theorem 14.1 and the discussion around Theorem 23.5. The exact finite-type-base specialization argument is also supplied in the authored proof.
- Chad Schoen, [Specialization of the torsion subgroup of the Chow group](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0403550), opening and proposition on PDF pp.1–3, supplies additional divisor-specialization context.
- J.-I. Inoguchi, Y. Jikumaru, K. Kajiwara, K. T. Miura and W. K. Schief, [Log-Aesthetic Curves: Similarity Geometry, Integrable Discretization and Variational Principles](https://arxiv.org/abs/1808.03104v2). The inspected real curvature-law discussion is context, not a proof of the complex algebraic classification.
- T. Duff and M. Ruddy, [Signatures of algebraic curves via numerical algebraic geometry](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jsc.2022.08.003), J. Symbolic Computation 115 (2023), 452–477. Its closure convention is relevant; its Euclidean reflection setting does not replace the fixed ordered pair.

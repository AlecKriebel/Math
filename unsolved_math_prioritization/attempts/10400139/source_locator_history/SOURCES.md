All source PDFs are held locally outside the public attempt packet. URLs and SHA256 hashes are in source_manifest.json. Source lookup is not an author proof turn.

1. **Ohtsuki, Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds**, GT Monographs 4 (2002). Publisher file: https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf. Printed485–487, PDF113–115, Section 7.4. Full definitions on485, phase Problem7.21 on486, exact Problem7.24 and its conjectural equality remark on487. PDF115 was rendered and visually checked. Scope: nonempty links, odd N>1, trivial Borel bundle on S³, Nth-powered invariant. This is not a volume-conjecture target.

2. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, Quantum hyperbolic state sum invariants of 3-manifolds**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0101234v2, version28February2001. Ohtsuki reference[43]. Section 3.1: positive ordered-simplex orientation is called index−1. Section 3.3: integral charge reduction divided by2. Section 4.1 equation(2): N^(−V), negative off-link edge exponent, then Nth power. Section 8, Propositions 8.2,8.3,8.5 and equation(7): full CG and charged tensor formulas. These define the literal old invariant. Section 6 discusses the desired Jones relationship but does not prove it.

3. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, QHI Theory I**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0201240v1, version24January2002. Section 5 equation(7), printed29, preserves N^(−V) and negative off-link exponent. Theorem 5.2 defines the Nth power. Appendix 9, especially(18)–(20), gives the same CG normalizations and tensor assignment; its positive orientation is called index+1. This change of the index name does not change which tensor is assigned to a positive ordered simplex.

4. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, QHI,3-manifolds scissors congruence classes and the volume conjecture**, GT Monographs 4 (2002),13–28: https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-002p.pdf. Printed20–22, especially equation(4) on21. The N^(−V) normalization and Nth power agree with the old definition. The off-link edge exponent is printed positive, inconsistent with the original defining paper and long version; the next proposition asserts projective invariance, which requires the negative exponent for those tensors. This discrepancy was visually confirmed and is not silently propagated. The article is a short survey and explicitly refers to the detailed construction for its tensors.

5. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, Quantum hyperbolic invariants of 3-manifolds with PSL(2,C)-characters**, Topology 43 (2004),1373–1423, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0306280. Downloaded arXiv version, source hash pinned. Theorem 4.14 uses N^(−V) and allows sign plus Nth roots; its invariant called K_N is now a2Nth power. Remark 4.31 distinguishes Borel symmetrization and its root-only ambiguity. Theorem 5.1 states a Jones comparison for the Borel invariant, but is not used without the normalization correction. The 2011 authors explicitly revisit the earlier comparison.

6. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, The Kashaev and quantum hyperbolic link invariants**, Journal of Gökova Geometry Topology 5 (2011),31–85. Primary author copy https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~baseilhac/jggt11-basebene.pdf; journal https://gokovagt.org/journal/2011/basebene.html; arXiv1101.1851. Theorems 1 and2, pp31–32, give modern QHI–mirror Kashaev and Kashaev–Jones relationships. Remark 2 p33 explains the need for a complete proof beyond earlier statements. Remark 3 p36 changes the orientation sign. Equation(2), Remark1(2), p38, changes N^(−V) to N^(2−V), giving unknot value1. Sections4.1–4.2, pp60–63, distinguish Kashaev's state sum and general QHI. Equation(30) has N^(2−V); Corollary 4.2 has root-only phase; Corollary 4.6 p65 explicitly gives the all-link comparison with powers of ζ_N only. It was rendered and read visually. Section 6.2 p70 and Lemma 6.1 p69 give the local matrix checks. The symbol =_N elsewhere permits a sign, so it must not be read as root-only automatically.

7. **Kashaev, Quantum Dilogarithm as a 6j-Symbol**, MPLA 9 (1994),3757–3768, https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9411147v2. Equations(1.13),(1.19),(1.24): CG normalization and 6j basis change. Equations(3.1)–(3.4): charged tensor normalization. Equation(3.13): tetrahedron/face convention. Equation(4.4), printed10: N^(2−V) times state sum and negative edge factor; Q=(sum)^N. Equations(4.5)–(4.7): unknot1, split unknot0, HopfN, example knots. Its invariance proof is initially under a decorated move equivalence; the later literature supplies the full topological treatment.

8. **Kashaev, A Link Invariant from Quantum Dilogarithm**, MPLA 10 (1995),1409–1418, https://arxiv.org/pdf/q-alg/9504020. Theorem 1(2), printed4, identifies the Nth power of the planar invariant with Q(S³,L) for odd N. Equations(4.6),(4.7), printed5, make the charged scalar ac/2 conventions explicit. Equation(4.19), printed6, and the following paragraph distinguish N^(−V) boundary partition functions from the closed invariant with an additional N² factor. This independently confirms that the N² difference is real, not a text-extraction error.

9. **Hitoshi Murakami–Jun Murakami, The colored Jones polynomials and the simplicial volume of a knot**, Acta Math. 186 (2001),85–104, https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9905075v2. Theorem 4.9: the enhanced Kashaev invariant is the normalized N-color Jones evaluation for all links and all N≥2. The introduction specifies unknot value1 and equal color on every component. The PDF's arXiv header identifies v2 dated10June1999; its internal compilation date is later and is not treated as a new mathematical edition. The old QHI application here uses only odd N.

# Result: a rational fixed separator with irrational-only SOS decomposition

One substantive author turn. Complete candidate for the literal fixed-p
existence question in OWR 14/2023 p.804, pending separate review.

On the rational unit ball in R^3, take Scheiderer's explicit real-SOS but
non-rational-SOS ternary quartic f and p=1+f. Let the degree-four rational
moment vector have mass 1, x^4 moment -1, and all other entries zero. Then
L_y(p)=0 and p>=1, so y is nonrepresentable. A fourth-order Taylor-jet argument
at the origin proves that p has no rational SOS certificate in 1+Q(g), at
any certificate degree. The existing Scheiderer quartic and its obstruction
retain credit; no novelty assertion is made.

The quantifiers and normalization are essential. This is one fixed bad p.
The same y has the rationally SOS-certified separator 1+x^4. The exhibited
p has minimum exactly 1; a rational rescaling c*p with c>1 has a rational
module certificate by Powers's theorem for the ball. The stronger strict-margin
reading of the companion preprint is explicitly outside this counterexample.
The OWR's own example has minimum 1, supporting the literal interpretation;
an independent source-coverage verdict is required.

source boundary. The standard-library checker passes 2,059 exact assertions;

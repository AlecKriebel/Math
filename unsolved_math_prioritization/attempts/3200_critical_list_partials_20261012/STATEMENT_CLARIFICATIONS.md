# Incorporated statement clarifications

Independent mathematical review required the nonempty-exception hypothesis to be explicit in two standalone corollaries. Both corrections below are incorporated in NEAR_CONSTANT.md. They do not change the proofs or cover constant assignments.

1. In Section 2, read the sentence beginning “Consequently every assignment differing…” as:
   “Consequently every nonconstant assignment differing from a common Δ-set on at most two edges is colorable.”
2. In Section 3, read the one-color-substitution corollary as:
   “In particular, any assignment obtained from a constant Δ-list assignment by replacing one color in each of between one and Δ edge lists is colorable, with no restriction on where those edges lie.”

Zero substitutions give a constant assignment, which is bad by the graph's assumed criticality. No theorem in this packet is intended to assert otherwise.

The following source-convention qualification is also incorporated in this edition: Kaul–Mudrock's sentence introducing “(Δ+1)-edge-critical” describes (Δ+1)-edge-colorability and Δ-edge-colorability after edge deletion but, literally, does not separately repeat “not Δ-edge-colorable.” Their intended chromatic-index-critical convention and conjecture are consistent with our operational definition, which explicitly includes non-Δ-edge-colorability. Our hypotheses are taken from the original problem and are not inferred solely from that compressed definitional sentence.

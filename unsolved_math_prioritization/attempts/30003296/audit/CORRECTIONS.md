## Optional clarification 1: principal Prym normalization

Location: `PARTIAL.md`, Route 4, first paragraph.

The existing wording about the usual principal Prym polarization is correct. If expanded, specify that for a double cover branched in two points the restriction of the covering Jacobian's principal polarization has type (2,2,2,2); the standard half-polarization on the Prym is principal. This prevents a reader from confusing the principal Prym polarization with the unnormalized restriction.

Suggested additional sentence:

“The restriction of the covering Jacobian polarization has type (2,2,2,2); the standard principal Prym polarization is its half.”

## Optional clarification 2: meaning of the first-order deformation

Location: `PARTIAL.md`, Route 5, final proof paragraph.

The phrase “this fixed parameterized family” and the ordered clutching setup already give the needed scope. If expanded, identify the deformation as a deformation of its morphism to the moduli stack of stable curves. The normal-line computation is a statement about that family, not a universal theorem about arbitrary deformations of coarse-space subvarieties.

Suggested replacement for the sentence beginning “Every first-order deformation”:

“Every first-order deformation of this fixed family, viewed as a morphism to the moduli stack of stable curves, has zero normal smoothing component.”

# Action dependence of measured free indecomposability

**Disposition: partial results; exact source problem unresolved by this work.**

Five distinct substantive approaches were completed. None yields the required pair of free p.m.p. actions or a theorem ruling out every pair. The original free-action, standard atomless probability-space and measured inessential-decomposition conventions are retained. Ergodicity is imposed only where a particular theorem or construction needs it.

- `EXACT_STATEMENT_AND_SOURCES.md`: exact target, definitions, source interfaces and bounded prior-result check.
- `SCOPED_RESULTS.md`: proofs of the quotient-action obstruction, canonical trivialization test and non-FI ergodic extraction, extension-instability reformulation, finite-index/finite-factor transport, and conditional integer-cocycle FI criterion.
- `APPROACH_LEDGER.md`: five approaches, outcomes and stopping points; final substantive count 5/5.
- `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`: public bibliographic and PDF verification metadata only.

Important boundary: the integer-cocycle criterion is conditional. A positive-Betti group/action meeting that criterion and having a second non-FI free action has not been exhibited. The stronger U_fin-superrigid Bernoulli route is obstructed by the cited Peterson–Sinclair theorem. These two hypotheses are not identified.

The surface-group exclusion uses aperiodicity from essential freeness of an infinite group, strong treeability from the cited theorem, and the essential-decomposition conclusion of Alvarez–Gaboriau Proposition 4.6. Mere treeability of one action, or ordinary group indecomposability, is not treated as an answer.

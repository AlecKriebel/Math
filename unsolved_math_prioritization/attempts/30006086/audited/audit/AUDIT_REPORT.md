## 2. Algebraic setup and degree-four proof

Write S for the shuffle ideal generated by letters, V=U(L') for the enveloping algebra of the non-letter free Lie part, and B_n=[V_{n-1},E]. The packet correctly obtains V=S-perpendicular and LoopInv_n=B_n-perpendicular from DPR. Deletion derivations prove B_n is contained in V_n. Therefore LoopInv_n/S_n is the full dual of V_n/B_n.

The quotient by word-minus-one-step-rotation relations is C_n. Cyclically constant coefficients give its full dual. The restriction of the map V_n to C_n kills B_n, so computing its rank before passage to V_n/B_n is legitimate.

PBW symmetrization in degrees 2, 3 and 4 gives exactly the displayed decompositions. The identity that L_n is spanned by brackets of letters with L_{n-1} follows from Jacobi and induction; it is a span statement, not a claim that each Lie element is one bracket. Thus B_3=L_3 and B_4=L_4. The unshuffle coproduct calculation on symmetric products has both cross terms with the correct normalization. The area pairing on the standard L_2 basis is nondegenerate, and its symmetric square gives the required dual of Q_4. The degree-five quotient is also correct: symmetrization is equivariant under the adjoint letter action, so the indicated derivation relations are precisely the additional bracket relations in its L_2 times L_3 summand.

**Accepted:** the direct proof of generation through degree 4, in every finite dimension. The degree-five description supplies no unbounded induction.

## 3. Alternating obstruction in every even degree

For n=2m distinct letters, deletion of any fixed letter in the alternating tensor sums an even alternating sequence of insertion positions and hence gives zero. A one-step cyclic rotation changes the sign because n is even, so every cyclic orbit has total coefficient zero. These establish membership in V_n and zero image in C_n.

The pairing normalization was checked independently. In the shuffle of the m oriented pair words, a distinct-letter word occurs with coefficient equal to the product of its pair-orientation signs. Choosing the first pair's two positions gives the factor 2, the insertion sign (-1)^(p+q-3), and a position sum of m. Consequently the induction gives exactly 2^m m!, not an extra factorial or a divided-power normalization.

Every area is a loop invariant on zero-increment signatures and so is its shuffle product. Pairing with the alternating tensor consequently proves that this tensor is nonzero in Q_n. Its nonzero pairing also rules out membership of the area product in S_n+ConjInv_n. Different sets of 2m basis letters have disjoint multidegrees; their alternating tensors are independent modulo B_n. Alternation is natural, so the stated exterior-power injection follows.

For an idempotent right-closure map with kernel S, c-rcl(c) lies in S. Therefore a conjugation invariant cannot map to the distinct-letter area product under rcl. This matches precisely the distinct-letter non-membership clause of DPR Conjecture 4.16, including the one-area case.

**Accepted:** the stated all-degree non-membership theorem and exterior-power injection. Neither repeated-letter membership nor the mixed-product clause is established. This obstruction refutes only the stronger unrestricted linear formula, not the proposed shuffle algebra generation.

## 5. Equivalence and localization

Letters are loop invariants, so equality on the entire family forces equal increments. For a nonzero common coordinate a, the shuffle identity applied to i shuffle w gives a times every word coefficient. Cancelling a recovers equality of the whole signatures. If just one path is closed, a letter separates them. No division is attempted on the closed-loop locus.

For two closed-loop signatures, their homogeneous components belong to V. Finite-dimensional annihilator duality applied in each degree gives exactly the condition g_n-h_n in B_n. PBW and bracket matrices provide the announced rational coefficient test, with scalar extension for real coordinates. This is properly credited as a reformulation of DPR's characterization. It is still an infinite-degree test and is not a geometric orbit classification. Conjugate closed-loop signatures pass it, but no converse is proved.

**Accepted:** the stated nonclosed extension and coefficient membership criterion, with the stated limitations.

## 6. Sharp detection of the identity class

For a zero-increment group-like series whose first nonzero logarithm component has degree m at least 3, exponential multiplication contributes no product term below 2m. Thus g_n=L_n there. For n at least 3, L_n is contained in [L_{n-1},E] and L_{n-1} is contained in V_{n-1}. Every loop invariant therefore vanishes at each such degree. The degree-1 and degree-2 terms vanish separately.

The finite matrix proof of detection is correct over the stated real ground field. On the rooted word basis of depths at most m, each A_i adds or removes the matching first letter. A word of m operators acting on the root can reach depth m only by creation at every step; operator order yields the original word in the asserted order. Hence the top component of P(A) applied to the root is exactly the nonzero coefficient vector of P.

Reversal of a homogeneous degree-m Lie polynomial has sign (-1)^(m-1), by induction on brackets. Because the A_i are symmetric, the same sign controls matrix transpose. Thus tr(P(A)^2) is that sign times the sum of squares of all entries, and is nonzero. Trace-word coefficients are cyclically constant. The degree-2m signature component is L_{2m}+L_m^2/2, and the trace of the first summand is zero since it is a sum of commutators. This proves actual detection at degree 2m. No convergence or infinite-dimensional trace assertion is used.

For m=2, nonzero signed area already detects at degree 2, as separately stated. The nested path commutators are genuine closed piecewise-linear paths. The filtered group-commutator calculation adds one bracket with a at each step; the coefficient of a^(m-1)b remains 1. Consequently arbitrary detection delays are realized by actual loops in dimension two, rather than just by formal series.

The identity class is therefore exactly the trivial-signature class. Hambly–Lyons gives the tree-like interpretation in the bounded-variation class. Equality of invariant values for general g and h does not transfer to identity values for gh^{-1}; the packet explicitly avoids that invalid step.

**Accepted:** sharp degree 2m for m at least 3, identity-class separation, and absence of any uniform finite degree bound. Arbitrary nontrivial equivalence classes remain unclassified.

# Five substantive mathematical approaches

Problem 30006086 / OWR-14298797-002. The full target remains unsolved, 5/5. Source retrieval, duplicate screening, code packaging and audit preparation are not counted as mathematical turns.

1. **PBW filtration and low-degree induction.** Compute V_2,V_3,V_4 and the bracket quotient, prove that areas generate the degree-4 dual, and derive the degree-5 quotient. Result: a direct every-dimension proof through degree 4. Barrier: new PBW products at every subsequent weight; no all-degree induction.
2. **Alternating tensors against cyclic words.** Construct Ω_{2m}, establish deletion and cyclic cancellation, and compute its exact pairing 2^m m! with disjoint area products. Result: all-degree distinct-letter non-membership, the corresponding clause of DPR Conjecture 4.16, and an exterior-power injection into the obstruction space. Barrier: no exhaustion theorem for that obstruction space, nor repeated-letter/mixed-product result.
3. **Multilinear rank attack and polarization.** Construct exact PBW/cyclic matrices and calculate rational ranks for all multilinear weights 2–8 and every binary multidegree through 13. Use polarization to prove a uniform every-dimension degree-8 generation statement. Result: finite computer-assisted theorems, including binary cyclic ranks 84 and 116 at weights 12 and 13. Barrier: no mathematical bound on the first failure in higher weights.
4. **Localization and coefficient-level equivalence.** Localize at a nonzero increment using i shuffle w to reconstruct every signature coefficient. Derive an explicit linear membership test for pairs on the closed-loop locus from the credited annihilator theorem. Result: complete nonclosed-signature equivalence plus an operational but infinite closed-loop test. Barrier: the source asks for a geometric classification of closed loops, which is not supplied by this reformulation.
5. **Leading log-signature and matrix-trace separation.** Prove vanishing below 2m, construct finite symmetric creation/annihilation matrices detecting the square of the leading Lie polynomial, and realize arbitrarily delayed detection by actual nested-commutator piecewise-linear loops. Result: sharp first-detection degree for the identity class, tree-like interpretation using Hambly–Lyons, and no uniform finite truncation. Barrier: equality of invariants for g,h does not imply that gh^{-1} has identity invariant values, so arbitrary pairs are not classified.

# Partial results on algebraic generators and equivalence of loop invariants

Problem 30006086 / OWR-14298797-002, queue rank 960. Author investigation dated 7 October 2026. Status: **unsolved after five substantive approaches**. This is an AI-assisted, unrefereed research packet; independent review is pending at this author freeze. No novelty claim is made.

## 1. Exact target and scope

The target in Rosa Preiß's contribution to *Directions in Rough Analysis*, Oberwolfach Report 49/2024, printed pp. 2936–2937, is the shuffle-generation conjecture for signature loop invariants, together with a characterization of the equivalence relation they induce on loops. Diehl–Preiß–Reizenstein [DPR], Conjecture 3.12, gives the precise algebraic formulation. The geometric classification of arbitrary pairs of loops is the fourth question in [DPR, Section 5]. A linear-algebra restatement of their Theorem 3.10 is useful but does not by itself answer that geometric question.

We prove the following scoped results.

1. In all ambient dimensions, every loop invariant of tensor degree at most 4 is generated as proposed; a direct PBW proof is given.
2. For every positive integer m, a shuffle product of m signed areas on 2m distinct letters is not a conjugation invariant modulo the letter ideal. This proves the distinct-letter non-membership clause of [DPR, Conjecture 4.16], not its other clauses or Conjecture 3.12.
3. Exact rational computations plus polarization establish the proposed generators in every ambient dimension through degree 8. In dimension 2, the stronger linear statement in [DPR, Conjecture 3.15] holds through degree 13. These are finite computer-assisted statements.
4. Outside the closed-loop locus, equality on all loop invariants is exactly equality of the full signatures. On closed loops there is an explicit degree-by-degree linear membership test, credited to the characterization in [DPR].
5. If the first nonzero log-signature of a loop has degree m at least 3, the least degree of a loop invariant detecting it against the constant loop is exactly 2m. Consequently all loop invariants distinguish the trivial-signature class from every other signature, but no finite degree bound does so uniformly. For bounded-variation paths, Hambly–Lyons identifies this class with tree-like loops.

Neither the unrestricted shuffle-generation conjecture nor the geometric equivalence classification of arbitrary nontrivial loops is resolved.

## 2. Algebraic setup and credited input

Work over R; the exact finite computations use Q. Put E=R^d, T=T(E), with concatenation juxtaposition and shuffle product denoted by `shuffle`. The word basis is orthonormal for the coefficient pairing. Let L be the free Lie algebra in T, with bracket [x,y]=xy-yx, and L'=⊕_{n≥2}L_n. Put

- S=(E)_shuffle, the shuffle ideal generated by letters;
- V=U(L') inside T;
- B_n=[V_{n-1},E], the linear span of all such commutators;
- Q_n=V_n/B_n;
- C_n=T_n/[T_{n-1},E], the cyclic-word quotient.

The letter-deletion derivation ∂_i deletes each occurrence of letter i and sums the results. The following are the input results from [DPR, Lemma 3.5, Proposition 3.6, Corollary 3.7, Remark 3.8 and Theorem 3.10]:

V=S^perp=intersection_i ker ∂_i; LoopInv_n=B_n^perp; hence LoopInv_n/S_n is the full dual of Q_n.

Theorem 2.11 of [DPR] identifies ConjInv with cyclically constant word coefficients. Its restriction to V_n is dual to the natural map j_n:Q_n→C_n. The inclusion B_n⊂V_n follows either from the derivation rule or from the stability of L' under bracket with E. These facts are prior results, not claimed discoveries of this packet.

Signed-area words a_ij=ij-ji are loop invariants, and products of their evaluations correspond to shuffle products. One direct reason is that conjugating a zero-increment group-like series preserves its degree-2 component.

## 3. First approach: the PBW filtration in small degree

PBW symmetrization gives natural vector-space decompositions

V_2=L_2, V_3=L_3, V_4=L_4 ⊕ Sym^2(L_2).

Here a symmetric product x·y is embedded as (xy+yx)/2. Every homogeneous Lie component L_n for n≥2 is spanned by [E,L_{n-1}], by induction and the Jacobi identity. Thus B_2=0, B_3=L_3 and B_4=L_4. Consequently

Q_2=Λ^2 E, Q_3=0, Q_4=Sym^2(Λ^2 E).

To see that area products supply the whole dual in degree 4, use the unshuffle coproduct Δ, for which a Lie element x is primitive. Then

Δ((xy+yx)/2)=(xy+yx)/2⊗1+1⊗(xy+yx)/2+x⊗y+y⊗x.

The pairings of a_ij with the standard basis [e_k,e_l] of L_2 form a nondegenerate diagonal matrix with diagonal 2. Hence shuffles a_ij shuffle a_kl pair nondegenerately with Sym^2(L_2). They therefore span Q_4^*. Constants and the letter ideal account for degrees 0 and 1. This proves the target through degree 4 in every dimension.

At degree 5, PBW gives

Q_5 ≅ (L_2⊗L_3) / span{ [e,x]·y+x·[e,y] : e∈E, x,y∈L_2 },

with symmetric PBW notation used to identify the factors. The L_5 summand is killed. This is an explicit remaining representation quotient, not an induction proving the full conjecture. New PBW products enter in unbounded degrees, which is the gap in this first approach.

## 4. Second approach: an all-degree alternating obstruction

For n=2m and distinct letters e_1,…,e_n, define

Ω_n = sum_{σ∈S_n} sign(σ) e_{σ(1)}…e_{σ(n)},

and let P_m=a_12 shuffle a_34 shuffle … shuffle a_{n-1,n}.

### Proposition 4.1

Ω_n belongs to V_n, has zero image in C_n, and satisfies

〈Ω_n,P_m〉=2^m m!.

In particular P_m does not belong to S_n+ConjInv_n.

### Proof

Fix a letter e_i and fix an ordering of all remaining letters. Its coefficient in ∂_iΩ_n is, up to an overall sign, sum_{r=0}^{n-1}(-1)^r=0 because n is even. Thus every deletion derivative annihilates Ω_n, so Ω_n∈V_n=S_n^perp.

One-step cyclic rotation of a permutation of n distinct letters has sign (-1)^{n-1}=-1. Every cyclic orbit therefore has total coefficient zero in Ω_n. Hence its image in C_n vanishes, and it pairs to zero with every conjugation invariant.

For a permutation word w, the coefficient of w in P_m is the product of the orientation signs of its m prescribed pairs: +1 if the first element precedes the second, -1 otherwise. Each interleaving occurs once because all letters are distinct. Let c_m be the sum of the permutation sign times this coefficient. Choose the two positions p<q of the first pair. Reversing the pair flips both signs, so the two orientations give the same contribution. Removing that pair contributes (-1)^{p+q-3}. Therefore

c_m=2 c_{m-1} sum_{1≤p<q≤2m}(-1)^{p+q-3}=2m c_{m-1}.

The last sum is m: if a_p=(-1)^p then sum_{p<q}a_pa_q=((sum a_p)^2-sum a_p^2)/2=-m. Since c_0=1, c_m=2^m m!.

If P_m=s+c with s∈S_n and c∈ConjInv_n, pairing with Ω_n would give zero, a contradiction. QED.

Because P_m is itself a loop invariant, its nonzero pairing also proves Ω_n∉B_n. Varying the 2m-element set of basis letters gives an injective natural map

Λ^{2m}E → ker(j_{2m}:Q_{2m}→C_{2m}).

Independence follows by pairing with the corresponding disjoint-pair area products, which have disjoint multidegree supports. In particular, the stronger linear formula LoopInv=S+ConjInv+degree-2 areas cannot hold in arbitrary dimension. This is fully consistent with [DPR], which only conjectures that stronger formula for d=2,3 and already records the degree-4 obstruction for d≥4.

The identity ker(rcl)=S and idempotence of rcl from [DPR, Corollary 4.8 and Lemma 4.9] imply c-rcl(c)∈S for every c. Thus P_m=rcl(c) for a conjugation invariant c would contradict Proposition 4.1. This proves the distinct-letter non-membership clause of Conjecture 4.16. We do not prove its repeated-letter clause or its mixed-product clause. We also do not prove that these exterior-power subspaces exhaust ker(j_n) for all n.

## 5. Third approach: exact rational ranks and polarization

### 5.1 Reproducible construction

The accompanying standard-library Python program uses the Chen–Fox–Lyndon factorization w=l_1…l_k, with l_1≥…≥l_k. For each Lyndon word l, P_l is its standard bracket, using its longest proper Lyndon suffix. The ordered concatenation P_w=P_{l_1}…P_{l_k} is a PBW basis element with lexicographically smallest word w and coefficient 1. The subset having no one-letter factor is a basis of V. This follows from PBW applied to a Lyndon basis of L': the non-letter Lyndon Lie elements form a basis of L'.

The program expands [P_w,e_i] in that triangular basis to construct B_n. It also maps each P_w to C_n by summing its coefficients over cyclic orbits. Sparse Gaussian elimination uses fractions.Fraction, so the ranks are over Q with no floating-point or modular inference. The result holds over R by scalar extension. Every commutator is checked to have zero cyclic image, and basis/deletion/dimension controls are included.

### 5.2 Multilinear ranks

In multidegree (1,…,1), the exact ranks are:

| n | dim V | rank B | dim Q | rank j | defect dim Q-rank j |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 2 | 1 | 0 | 1 | 0 | 1 |
| 3 | 2 | 2 | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| 4 | 9 | 6 | 3 | 2 | 1 |
| 5 | 44 | 38 | 6 | 6 | 0 |
| 6 | 265 | 226 | 39 | 38 | 1 |
| 7 | 1854 | 1628 | 226 | 226 | 0 |
| 8 | 14833 | 13204 | 1629 | 1628 | 1 |

For odd n in this range, cyclic invariants restrict onto the full dual of Q_n. For even n the cokernel is one-dimensional. Proposition 4.1 shows that P_{n/2} supplies the missing direction: Ω_n annihilates cyclic invariants but not P_{n/2}. Thus conjugation invariants together with shuffle products of areas generate the multilinear loop invariants modulo S for all n≤8.

### 5.3 Why this proves an every-dimension statement

All the relevant subspaces are natural under linear substitutions of letters: for loop invariants this follows by pulling a path back under the transposed linear map; the statement also follows from the algebraic commutator characterization. Scaling each letter independently preserves these spaces, so each multihomogeneous component of an invariant is invariant.

Let u have multidegree (α_1,…,α_d) and total degree n. Replace letter i by the sum of α_i new letters and select the component in which all n new letters occur once. This full polarization remains a loop invariant. After identifying the α_i new letters with i, the polarized tensor becomes (product_i α_i!)u. The same substitutions preserve S, cyclic invariants and shuffle products of areas. Since the field has characteristic zero, division by that nonzero scalar is allowed. The multilinear result therefore implies

LoopInv_n=S_n+ConjInv_n+span{shuffle products of n/2 areas}

for every d and every even n≤8; for odd n≤7 the area-product summand is absent. Degree 0 is covered by the unit and degree 1 by S. In particular the original shuffle-generation conjecture holds through degree 8 in every dimension.

That table already gives the displayed bracket ranks through degree 13 and cyclic ranks through degree 11; we do not present reproduced entries as new results. The verifier directly fills the two missing binary cyclic ranks, with no historical-priority claim.

The all-degree gap is exact: neither a bound on the emergence of further components of ker(j_n), nor an induction excluding them, is established. Checking finitely many graded pieces cannot settle Conjecture 3.12.

## 6. Fourth approach: localization and linear membership for equivalence

Write g=S(X), h=S(Y). Every letter belongs to S and hence is a loop invariant. Equality on loop invariants therefore forces equality of increments.

### Proposition 6.1

If this common increment is nonzero, equality on all loop invariants is equivalent to g=h.

### Proof

Choose a letter i with common increment a≠0. For every word w, i shuffle w belongs to S and hence is a loop invariant. The group-like shuffle identity gives

a〈g,w〉=〈g,i shuffle w〉=〈h,i shuffle w〉=a〈h,w〉.

Cancel a. Conversely equal signatures agree on all tensor coordinates. A closed loop and a nonclosed path are separated by a letter. QED.

This handles an extension of the equivalence relation away from the intended closed-loop locus; it does not solve the source's loop question.

For two closed-loop signatures, g_n,h_n∈V_n. Finite-dimensional annihilator duality and [DPR, Theorem 3.10] give the exact criterion

X~Y if and only if g_n-h_n ∈ B_n for every n≥1.

There is a constructive version: use the PBW triangular expansion in Section 5 and solve the rational matrix with columns [P_w,e_i] for the difference vector in each degree. When the input signature coordinates are real, the same matrix is used over R. This is an invariant-free coefficient test with explicitly described matrices, but still requires infinitely many degrees. It is a reformulation of credited prior structure, not a geometric characterization.

Conjugate signatures necessarily pass this test. No converse asserting conjugacy is proved. In particular invariant-theoretic separation of arbitrary orbits cannot simply be assumed for a unipotent action.

## 7. Fifth approach: leading Lie terms and the identity class

Let g be a nonidentity group-like series with zero increment, and let log g=L_m+L_{m+1}+… with L_m≠0 and m≥2. Homogeneous components of log g belong to the free Lie algebra. If m=2, an area word detects g in degree 2.

### Theorem 7.1

If m≥3, every positive-degree loop invariant of degree below 2m evaluates to zero on g. Some conjugation invariant of degree 2m has nonzero evaluation. Thus 2m is the exact first detecting degree.

### Proof of vanishing

For n<2m, the exponential expansion gives g_n=L_n, taking L_n=0 for n<m. For n≥3,

L_n=[E,L_{n-1}] as a linear span, and L_{n-1}⊂V_{n-1}.

Thus L_n⊂B_n, which all degree-n loop invariants annihilate. Degrees 1 and 2 vanish because m≥3. This covers every n<2m.

### Proof of detection

We first show that a nonzero homogeneous free Lie polynomial P of degree m has nonzero cyclic image of P^2. Let H have an orthonormal basis indexed by all words of lengths 0 through m over the d letters. Define C_i e_w=e_{iw} when |w|<m and C_i e_w=0 when |w|=m. Let A_i=C_i+C_i^T; these are finite real symmetric matrices.

The length-m component of P(A_1,…,A_d)e_empty is precisely the coefficient vector of P: in a product of m operators, reaching length m requires every factor to be a creation, and prefix creation preserves the word order of the operator product. Therefore P(A)≠0.

Reversal of every degree-m Lie polynomial satisfies rev(P)=(-1)^{m-1}P, by induction on brackets. Since the A_i are symmetric,

P(A)^T=(-1)^{m-1}P(A).

Consequently tr(P(A)^2)=(-1)^{m-1}sum_{r,s}P(A)_{rs}^2≠0.

Define the finite tensor

φ_A=sum_{|w|=2m} tr(A_{w_1}…A_{w_{2m}}) w.

Its coefficients are cyclically constant, so φ_A is a conjugation invariant. Every Lie polynomial of degree at least 2 evaluates to a sum of matrix commutators and has trace zero. Because

g_{2m}=L_{2m}+(1/2)L_m^2,

we obtain

〈g,φ_A〉=(1/2)tr(L_m(A)^2)≠0.

This proves the theorem. All sums and matrices here are finite; no convergence of matrix developments or formal-series evaluation is needed. QED.

### Corollaries and the unresolved pairwise issue

A zero-increment group-like series agrees with the identity on all loop invariants if and only if it is the identity. For continuous bounded-variation paths, the Hambly–Lyons uniqueness theorem [HL] identifies identity signature exactly with tree-like paths. Thus the constant loop's equivalence class is precisely the tree-like loops in this path class.

The first detecting degree is unbounded even among piecewise-linear loops in R^2. Let A,B be unit coordinate segments and use path concatenation with translated endpoints. Put C_2=A B A^{-1} B^{-1}, and recursively C_{m+1}=A C_m A^{-1} C_m^{-1}. Each C_m is a closed piecewise-linear path. The filtered group-commutator expansion gives first nonzero log-signature term

ad_a^{m-1}(b),

of degree m. This Lie polynomial is nonzero: its coefficient of a^{m-1}b is 1. The degree assertion follows by expanding exp(a)g exp(-a)g^{-1} when g=1+P_m+terms of higher degree: its first new term is [a,P_m] in degree m+1. Thus for every finite N there is a nontrivial actual loop whose loop invariants through degree N agree with the constant loop. This does not contradict detection by the full family.

Finally, one cannot apply identity detection to g h^{-1} from the premise that g and h have the same invariant values: that premise does not imply g h^{-1} has the identity's invariant values. No such multiplicativity of the quotient is proved or assumed. Separating the identity class therefore leaves arbitrary nontrivial equivalence classes unresolved.

## 8. Remaining questions and disposition

The five approaches leave three precise barriers:

- An all-degree description of ker(j_n), or another argument proving that the proposed shuffle subalgebra fills LoopInv/S.
- The repeated-letter and mixed-product parts of [DPR, Conjecture 4.16]; the alternating obstruction settles only the all-distinct non-membership clause.
- A geometric description of arbitrary pairs of loop signatures passing all membership tests g_n-h_n∈B_n, including the exact relationship to conjugacy of paths/signatures.

Recommended queue status: `unsolved`, turns `5/5`. The computations certify bounded algebraic statements, not the infinite conjecture. The source search is bounded and does not certify worldwide continued openness or novelty. No publication should occur before the separate mathematical audit.

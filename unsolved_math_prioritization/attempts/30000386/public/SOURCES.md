Bollobás, “The angel and the devil in three dimensions,” in *Combinatorics*,
Oberwolfach Reports 3 (2006), no. 1, printed pages 21–22, Conjecture 1.
The report was organized by László Lovász and Hans Jürgen Prömel.

Both retrieved reports have 68 PDF pages. The target was visually inspected
at official PDF pages 17–18, corresponding to printed pages 21–22. The
[source acceptance contract](TIME_BOMB_ACCEPTANCE_CONTRACT.md) records the
normalized Angel-first, one-announcement-per-round game. The spatial-time
embedding uses steps (a,b,1), a,b in {-1,0,1}; unrestricted three-dimensional
motion is a different game.

## Companion article and authentication limitation

Béla Bollobás and Imre Leader, *The Angel and the Devil in three dimensions*,
Journal of Combinatorial Theory, Series A 113 (2006), issue 1, pages 176–184:
[DOI 10.1016/j.jcta.2005.03.009](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2005.03.009),
[author publication record](https://digitalcommons.memphis.edu/facpubs/5828/).

Citation and abstract metadata were checked, but an authenticated full
publisher PDF was not obtained in this source review. An accessible
[third-party transcription](https://www.scribd.com/document/859743270/1-s2-0-S0097316505000622-main)
supplied provisional leads about the time-bomb conjecture and one/two-time
subcases. Those leads are not treated as authenticated primary-text facts or
independently audited historical proofs. The partial proofs in this package
are authored arguments; no novelty claim is made for them.

# Time-Bomb Conjecture: source-scope and acceptance contract

Audit date: 2026-10-14. This is a statement audit, not a proof or disproof.

## Primary target

Imre Leader, joint work with Béla Bollobás, “The angel and the devil in three dimensions,” Oberwolfach Report 1/2006, printed pp. 21–22, Conjecture 1. Official source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46029?nt=1 .

The target is the speed-one Time-Bomb game on the two-dimensional integer lattice. The source uses the maximum-coordinate (king-move) distance, permits a position to be targeted more than once, and makes a bomb lethal only at its named integer time. The conjecture asks for survival forever. The source's consecutive move description is Angel then Devil, and its loss condition refers to a pair already announced when the Angel makes the corresponding move.

## Audit normalization

The following notation formalizes those rules; it is not an additional theorem.

- Set the initial position to x_0=(0,0) at time 0. This is a translation normalization of the source's arbitrary initial square. There are no initial announcements.
- At round n≥1, the Angel chooses x_n∈Z² with ||x_n−x_(n−1)||_∞≤1. Thus each coordinate changes by −1, 0, or 1; diagonal moves and staying put are included.
- Let B_(n−1) be the set of pairs announced in rounds 1 through n−1. The Angel's move loses exactly when (x_n,n)∈B_(n−1).
- If play has not ended, the Devil observes this move and announces one pair d_n=(p_n,t_n), with p_n∈Z² and t_n a positive integer. Put B_n=B_(n−1)∪{d_n}.
- The source states no uniqueness condition on announcements and no further restriction on t_n. Repeating an identical pair adds nothing. An announcement with t_n≤n has no retroactive effect and cannot hit a later move.
- The budget is one announcement per round, not one forbidden position per explosion time. Several earlier rounds may target different positions at the same future time. At time n at most n−1 announcements have preceded the Angel's move. There is no separate stored-credit or batch-announcement permission.

## Quantified acceptance gate

An affirmative solution must establish ∃σ ∀τ ∀n≥1: the play generated by Angel strategy σ and Devil strategy τ satisfies the movement bound and (x_n,n)∉B_(n−1). Here σ chooses x_n from the finite observed play history through d_(n−1); τ may choose d_n using the observed history through x_n. Neither player is supplied the other's future actions. A single σ must work for arbitrarily long play and every legal adaptive Devil.

The strategy need not be memoryless, computationally efficient, explicitly algorithmic, or based on a local danger function unless an additional claim promises this. Random sampling or numerical evidence alone does not establish the quantified statement.

A negative solution must establish a legal adaptive Devil strategy forcing failure against every Angel strategy. Defeating only one proposed Angel policy is not a disproof.

## Required checks on any claimed solution

1. Preserve speed 1 and the two spatial coordinates throughout any reduction.
2. Preserve announcement timing, the one-per-round budget, and one-time rather than permanent spatial prohibitions.
3. Handle repeated sites, arbitrarily distant future timestamps, and multiple bombs at one timestamp.
4. Prove legal initialization, every transition, and survival at every time, including waiting and any change of scale.
5. Do not replace an online strategy by a path selected with advance knowledge of the complete bomb schedule. The latter changes the order of quantifiers.
6. A theorem giving an online winning strategy for every finite horizon is acceptable if accompanied by a correct compactness or compatible-strategy extraction argument producing the infinite-horizon strategy. A finite set of tested horizons, or mere finite-horizon path existence against known schedules, is insufficient.
7. A result for one preannounced common explosion time, a bounded number of explosion times, ordinary permanent-deletion Angel games at power 2, unrestricted motion in three dimensions, or higher-dimensional directed wedges is not itself the target result. Any transfer needs a fully proved rule-preserving reduction.

The spatial-time correspondence sends x_n to (x_n,n) in Z³. The allowed step becomes (a,b,1), a,b∈{−1,0,1}. A result for a general three-dimensional Angel without this exact height increment does not automatically transfer.

## Status discipline

The 2006 source states a conjecture. This audit has not verified a later exact resolution. That bounded finding is not a certification that the conjecture remains open in 2026. No proof pass or claim of resolution is contained here.

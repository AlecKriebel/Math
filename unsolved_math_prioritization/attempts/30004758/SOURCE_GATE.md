## Prior results and current advance

Mooney–Rakshit, *Singular structures in solutions to the Monge-Ampère equation with point masses*, Mathematics in Engineering 5(5) (2023), 1–11, proves polyhedral constructions in arbitrary dimensions and stability results (Theorems 1.1–1.3). Proposition 2.1 permits broad lower-semicontinuous dual obstacles, but that existence tool is not itself a complete singular-geometry theorem. Section 5 explicitly points toward other obstacle geometries. Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2204.11365v1 ; https://doi.org/10.3934/mine.2023083 .

Rakshit–Sen, *Solutions to Monge-Ampère Equations with Low Codimensional singularities*, arXiv:2605.19142v3 (19 October 2026), is essential new prior art. Theorem 1.5 states that a convex set with C1 boundary supports an entire convex solution smooth off that boundary, whose Alexandrov measure is Lebesgue measure plus f times boundary Hausdorff measure, with bounded nonnegative f bounded below positively. Section 4.4 uses a smooth quadratic restricted to the boundary as obstacle. Thus curved-boundary examples already address the catalogue's existence reading. The manuscript also studies interaction between singular measures; Section 6 retains questions about precise asymptotics and explicit singular-locus descriptions. This is a preprint, not a verified refereed resolution. Its broad theorem statements and brief proof reductions need independent audit before importing their full scope. A bounded smooth strictly convex body is the conservative test class; do not silently upgrade this to arbitrary unbounded obstacles or claim all stability/moduli questions settled. Sources: https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.19142 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2605.19142v3 .

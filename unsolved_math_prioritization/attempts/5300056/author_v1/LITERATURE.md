## Original target

Primary: Feliks Przytycki, “On Invariant Measures for Iterations of Holomorphic Maps,” in B. Bielefeld and M. Lyubich, *Problems in Holomorphic Dynamics*, IMS 1992/7, pp.29–34; target Problem 2.5 on p.32.

- [Stony Brook index](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/open-problems-dynamical-systems)
- [Primary PDF](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/preprints/ims92-7.pdf)

The setup is a holomorphic map defined near the closure of a geometric coding-tree limit set, called a quasi-repeller. M⁺ consists of ergodic invariant probability measures on its closure with positive entropy. The question presupposes equivalence of the pulled-back measure and m on injective branches, defining a positive Jacobian. It asks which such measures satisfy the implication from L²-bounded sums of log J_m f−κ log|f′| to m≪H^κ, with κ=dim_H(m). S_n was introduced with mean subtraction in Problem 2.2. There is no universal expansion assumption. The comments already give expanding maps with Hölder Jacobian, the specified harmonic-measure/RB-domain case, and rational maximal-entropy measures as affirmative cases. They explicitly warn that an L² transfer need not bound sums pointwise.

The source pages were text-inspected and the setup/target equations visually checked. The catalog's “Q10” label is secondary; the primary number is 2.5. The exact UnsolvedMath URL returned HTTP 403, so its present contents/status were not verified.

## Conformal reference measures: important later theorem, different conclusion

Neil Dobbs, *Measures with positive Lyapunov exponent and conformal measures in rational dynamics*, Trans. AMS 364 (2012), 2803–2824; arXiv:0804.3753v2.

- [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0804.3753)
- [Inspected PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0804.3753)

Definition 2 requires the conformal reference measure to be a probability. Theorem 8, under its nonexceptionality and positive-Lyapunov hypotheses, characterizes absolute continuity with respect to that measure by entropy/dimension and inducing conditions. Its target is a specified conformal measure, not automatically Hausdorff measure. Thus it cannot be applied to the merely sigma-finite ν above without an additional argument, and conformal/Hausdorff comparison remains separate. The current PDF's printed date is September 19, 2021, while the arXiv record identifies v2 as April 13, 2010. Both metadata facts are retained without assuming a new version. Relevant definitions and theorem were inspected; no full line-by-line journal-proof audit is claimed.

## Fine inducing and structured equilibrium measures

M. Szostakiewicz, M. Urbański, A. Zdunik, *Fine inducing and equilibrium measures for rational functions of the Riemann sphere*, Israel J. Math. 210 (2015), 399–465, DOI 10.1007/s11856-015-1257-6.

- [Publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11856-015-1257-6)
- [Inspected author-hosted 2011 preprint](https://sites.itservices.cas.unt.edu/~urbanski/papers/FI-1-Dim20110924.pdf)

This treats rational maps and Hölder potentials with pressure gap P(Φ)>sup Φ. The preprint's Theorem 43 concerns dimension rigidity; Theorems 63–64 concern the logarithmic derivative observable and Hausdorff singularity in the non-rigid case. Its normalized Jacobian formula uses the positive continuous equilibrium density. These are substantial structured results, not a theorem for arbitrary measures in the original M⁺. Relevant sections were inspected, including the stated hypotheses and proofs surrounding Lemma 57; the final journal text was not compared and theorem numbers refer to this preprint. This note does not extrapolate its conclusions past the pressure-gap/Hölder assumptions.

## Hausdorff versus conformal measures needs its own theorem

M. Denker, M. Urbański, *Hausdorff measures on Julia sets of subexpanding rational maps*, Israel J. Math. 76 (1991), 193–214, DOI 10.1007/BF02782852.

- [Publisher abstract](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02782852)

The abstract states finiteness, positivity and conformality of the critical-dimensional Hausdorff measure, and existence of an equivalent invariant measure, for its Misiurewicz/subexpanding class. Only the abstract and metadata were recovered. It is not used as an unchecked general-purpose bridge for arbitrary quasi-repellers.

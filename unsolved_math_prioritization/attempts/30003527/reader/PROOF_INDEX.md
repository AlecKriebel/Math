Read in dependency order:

1. [Stretched-book/short-gap proof](proofs/VERTICAL_GAP_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md) and [audit](audits/VERTICAL_GAP_ACCEPTANCE.md). The audit gives an elementary slab deformation-retraction proof, avoiding an unnecessary general cell-like transfer theorem.
2. [Metric-threshold proof](proofs/METRIC_THRESHOLD_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md) and [audit](audits/METRIC_THRESHOLD_ACCEPTANCE.md): midpoint threshold, ambient homeomorphism, shrinking-copy separation, clopen obstruction, and fixed-neighborhood tail contraction.
3. [Regular-radius proof](proofs/REGULAR_RADIUS_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md) and [audit](audits/REGULAR_RADIUS_ACCEPTANCE.md): exact wall threshold, nearest-point convex-hull exclusion, Clarke regularity, and boundary contacts.
4. [Quadratic supplement](proofs/QUADRATIC_TANGENCY_SUPPLEMENT.md) and [audit](audits/QUADRATIC_TANGENCY_ACCEPTANCE.md): curved-wall caps, strict internal tangencies, and global absence of nontrivial vertical boundary segments.
5. [Left-continuity construction](proofs/LEFT_CONTINUITY_COUNTEREXAMPLE.md) and [audit](audits/LEFT_CONTINUITY_ACCEPTANCE.md): auxiliary nets, isolated columns, continuous retraction onto the bad book, finite decomposition at smaller radii, and both Hausdorff limits.

Each proof keeps the full flow and all-enlargements requirements separate. No item establishes a negative answer to the original problem.

### Why abstract collar/quotient information is insufficient

[The collared-quotient proof](proofs/COLLARED_QUOTIENT_OBSTRUCTION.md) proves the genuine stable-entry quotient has finite-polyhedral shape, then constructs an abstract nonmovable compactum with the proposed collar, quotient, and tail properties. [The direct folded-core supplement](proofs/DIRECT_CORE_SUPPLEMENT.md) supplies an elementary unbased nonmovability proof avoiding Takahasi–Higman for this example. [The audit](audits/COLLARED_QUOTIENT_ACCEPTANCE.md) checks both routes, quotient conventions, and actual stopped-flow shape maps. No three-dimensional flow realization is supplied.

[Structural routes](proofs/STRUCTURAL_ROUTES.md) give additional elementary positive criteria and explain why maximal cores and finite-time cores alone do not supply movability homotopies. Their trapping and compatible-core statements are covered by the [structural audit](audits/STRUCTURAL_ACCEPTANCE.md).

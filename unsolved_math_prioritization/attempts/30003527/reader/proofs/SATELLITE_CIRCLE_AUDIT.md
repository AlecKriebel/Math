# Satellite circles do not automatically make a solenoid movable

13 October 2026. Complementary calculation within the same substantive pass 1 for row 1767 / 30003527. This is a topological counterexample to an unqualified auxiliary sufficiency assertion. It is not a flow counterexample to the target problem.

## Claim

There is a dyadic solenoid Σ embedded in R³ and a sequence of pairwise disjoint tame simple closed curves C_n, disjoint from Σ, such that C_n converges to Σ in Hausdorff distance, but

X = Σ ∪ ⋃_{n≥1} C_n

is not movable in the unrestricted ambient-homotopy sense.

## Construction

Use a standard nested-torus realization of the dyadic solenoid:

T₀ ⊃ T₁ ⊃ T₂ ⊃ ···,

where the intended nesting is T_{n+1} ⊂ int T_n, the longitudinal winding number of T_{n+1} in T_n is 2, and Σ = ⋂_{n≥0} T_n. Choose each T_n to be a sufficiently thin tubular neighborhood of its core that a tubular parametrization

h_n : S¹ × D² → T_n

has meridional fibers h_n({z}×D²) of diameter at most ε_n, with ε_n→0. Here is an explicit compatible construction. Start with a smooth solid-torus embedding h₀. Inductively set h_{n+1}=h_n∘E_n, where E_n(z,w)=(z², z/2+b_n w) and 0<b_n<1/4 is chosen so small that every meridional fiber of h_{n+1} has diameter at most ε_{n+1}. Uniform continuity of h_n permits this choice. The two disks over opposite square roots have disjoint centers-distance 1 and radii b_n<1/4, so E_n is an embedding. Its image lies in |w|<3/4. The longitudinal projections satisfy p_n=p_{n+1}² on T_{n+1}. For each compatible inverse-limit sequence of circle coordinates, the associated meridional disks are nested and their diameters tend to zero, so their intersection is a unique point. Conversely each point of Σ supplies such a sequence. Compactness makes this bijection a homeomorphism with the dyadic solenoid.

Since T_{n+1} is compact in int T_n, choose r_n<1 so close to 1 that the parametric torus h_n(S¹×{w:|w|=r_n}) is disjoint from T_{n+1}. On it take the (3,1) curve

C_n = { h_n(e^{3it}, r_n e^{it}) : t∈R/(2πZ) },  n≥1.

This curve is embedded: equality in both coordinates forces the parameters equal modulo 2π. It lies in int T_n\T_{n+1} and represents three times a core generator of H₁(T_n;Z). Thus the C_n are pairwise disjoint and disjoint from Σ.

The projection of C_n to the longitudinal S¹ coordinate is surjective. Every point of T_n therefore lies within ε_n of C_n, while C_n⊂T_n. Since nested nonempty compact sets T_n converge in Hausdorff distance to their intersection Σ, we obtain d_H(C_n,Σ)→0. In particular X is compact.

## Nonmovability proof

Set O = int T₀. Fix the generator identifying H₁(O;Z) with Z. A core circle of T_j represents 2^j in this group, and C_n represents 3·2^n.

Let V be any open neighborhood of X contained in O. Because Σ⊂V and the nested T_n converge to Σ, there exists j≥1 with T_j⊂V. Let α:S¹→T_j⊂V be a core loop. Its homology class in O is 2^j.

Choose k>j. The finitely many curves C_1,…,C_{k-1} are disjoint from T_k and from each other. Choose pairwise disjoint open tubular neighborhoods Q_1,…,Q_{k-1} of these curves, each contained in O and disjoint from T_k. Define

W = int T_k ∪ Q_1 ∪ ··· ∪ Q_{k-1}.

This is an open neighborhood of X: all C_n with n≥k lie in int T_k, as does Σ. Its connected components are int T_k and the Q_n. Their homology-image subgroups in H₁(O;Z) are respectively

2^k Z,  3·2^1 Z, …, 3·2^{k-1} Z.

None contains 2^j: k>j rules out the first, and divisibility by 3 rules out every other subgroup.

Suppose a homotopy H:V×[0,1]→O began with the inclusion and satisfied H(V,1)⊂W. Then H(α(·),1) would be a loop lying in one connected component of W. The homotopy in O preserves its homology class, so that class would still be 2^j, contradicting the subgroup calculation. Thus no such homotopy exists.

For this fixed O, every candidate V fails against some W. This is exactly failure of the required movability quantifiers. If one insists that W⊂V, replace W by W∩V; the same obstruction survives.

## Why this matters for the source and target

K. Kuperberg, *Periodicity generated by adding machines* (2013), p.145, states a movability assertion for a solenoid plus disjoint Hausdorff-approaching simple closed curves without an explicit longitudinal/core condition. The printed passage was visually inspected. The topological hypotheses alone cannot justify that assertion, as the construction above shows. A reading imposing additional dynamical constraints would require those constraints to be stated and proved useful; none are silently supplied here.

- Publisher: https://trim.imath.kiev.ua/index.php/trim/en/article/view/310
- Public proceedings PDF: https://www.imath.kiev.ua/~topology/publications/proceedings.2013.6.6.pdf

By contrast, appropriately chosen core circles in defining tori have compatible winding divisibility; the extra factor 3 is precisely what defeats the proposed homotopy here.

The calculation does not construct a continuous full flow with X as its only admissible invariant enlargement. Even realizing Σ and these C_n as invariant subsets would not exclude other compact invariant movable supersets inside the prescribed neighborhood. Therefore this is an auxiliary-lemma correction and a warning about the periodic-circle route, not a negative answer to row 1767.

## Additional algebraic check: the isolated-set obstruction does not settle this candidate

The same construction also explains why free integral Čech cohomology is insufficient for movability. Take cofinal neighborhoods U_k as above, shrinking each fixed outer tube as k increases. Then U_k has one central solid-torus component and k−1 outer solid-torus components. Write

H¹(U_k;Z) = Z a_k ⊕ ⊕_{1≤n<k} Z b_n.

Under restriction to U_{k+1}, the old b_n persist and

a_k ↦ 2 a_{k+1} + 3 b_k.

The vector (2,3) is primitive in Z²: together with (1,1) it is a basis, since the determinant is −1. Thus every bonding map in this cohomology direct system is a split injection with one free generator added. Its direct limit Ȟ¹(X;Z) is consequently free abelian of countably infinite rank. The analogous H⁰ maps split with one new generator as well, so Ȟ⁰(X;Z) also has countably infinite rank.

Therefore X passes the free-Ȟ¹ necessary condition for isolated invariant compacta proved in OBSTRUCTION_AUDIT.md while remaining nonmovable. It does not satisfy finite-generation hypotheses in degrees 0 and 1. A possible future dynamical construction would have to realize an appropriate X (or equivalent obstruction) as an isolated invariant set without introducing further compact invariant structure that removes the obstruction. No such realization is provided in this pass.

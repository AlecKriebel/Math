## Verdict and frozen inputs

**ACCEPTED as an abstract obstruction to the proposed movability shortcut.** The graph inverse limit is nonmovable in the unbased sense. The attached-circle construction has a genuine collar, an NDR inclusion, a quotient of circle shape, and the claimed internal semiflow tails. The actual isolating-block collar and quotient shape-equivalence arguments are also valid under the stated tame three-dimensional block hypotheses and the explicit empty-entry conventions.

The direct-core supplement gives a valid second proof of the unbased obstruction without Takahasi-Higman. No essential mathematical gap was found. Two wording clarifications are recorded below; neither changes the proof or result.

This is **not** acceptance of a counterexample realized by a flow in R^3, nor of a positive invariant-enlargement theorem. The original unrestricted problem remains unresolved.

## 1. Proper injective graph endomorphism

Let G be the rose with loops a and b, and let f(a)=a and f(b)=bab^{-1}a^{-1}b. The five-edge b-circle, wedged with the a-loop, is folded: the four outgoing labels at the wedge vertex are a, a^{-1}, b, b^{-1}, and the two directions at every intermediate vertex have distinct labels. Thus its labeled map into G is an immersion. The induced homomorphism alpha is injective.

The path labeled b from the basepoint ends at the first intermediate vertex rather than at the basepoint. Since the graph is folded and represents the subgroup generated by a and bab^{-1}a^{-1}b, this proves b is absent from alpha(F), not merely absent from one chosen generating set. The image is proper. Applying the injective map alpha^n to this strict inclusion gives

H_{n+1} < H_n, where H_n=alpha^n(F).

Every H_n has rank two. The graph map is surjective, since its image contains both graph edges. The compact inverse limit X is therefore nonempty and connected, and its finite-graph presentation is a valid shape expansion.

## 2. Unbased nonmovability: Takahasi route

The inverse-system movability condition used in the main report has the right quantifiers. At index zero, movability requires one index j from which maps to all sufficiently late stages give factorizations of the stage-zero bonding map up to homotopy. Taking any k>j gives a map r with f^j homotopic, without basepoint constraints, to f^k r.

An unbased homotopy does not give equality of the based homomorphisms. After choosing a path from r(v) to the target basepoint, its consequence is precisely a containment

H_j <= g H_k g^{-1}

for some g in F. Thus the conjugation allowed in the report is necessary and correctly retained.

The stated Takahasi-Higman theorem applies to ascending chains in the finite-rank ambient free group F. If a finitely generated subgroup H satisfied H < gHg^{-1}, repeated conjugation would give a strictly ascending chain H < gHg^{-1} < g^2Hg^{-2} < ... with the same finite rank at every stage. This contradicts that theorem. Combining the displayed containment with H_k < H_j gives exactly the forbidden strict containment H_k < gH_kg^{-1}.

There is no reliance on stabilization of the abelianized images. Indeed alpha induces the identity on H_1(G;Z), while its actual free-group images strictly decrease. The unbased argument therefore proves the claimed stronger obstruction that homology alone would miss.

Primary-source verification: Kapovich and Myasnikov state and prove the bounded-rank ascending-chain theorem as Theorem 14.1 in their author-hosted manuscript, printed pages 38–39 (PDF pages 38–39). The inspected statement has the finite-rank ambient hypothesis needed here. [Author-hosted PDF](https://web.stevens.edu/algebraic/alexeim/Teaching/GroupTheory626/graph5.pdf); [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202285).

The unbased ANR-sequence criterion and invariance under choice of expansion are stated in S. Mardešić, *A survey of the shape theory of compacta*, Section 5, printed page 296. [Primary-author survey](https://dml.cz/bitstream/handle/10338.dmlcz/700805/Toposym_03-1971-1_64.pdf?sequence=1).

## 3. Unbased nonmovability: direct-core route

The supplement is also accepted. The local-direction test establishes that f is a topological graph immersion, and compositions of locally injective maps remain locally injective. Consequently the word w_n=f^n(b) is reduced, begins and ends with b, and produces no fold when attached to the a-loop.

The length and letter counts can alternatively be checked inductively from

w_{n+1}=w_n a w_n^{-1} a^{-1} w_n.

There is no cancellation at a displayed junction. Starting with w_0=b gives exactly 3^n occurrences of b or b^{-1} and total reduced length 2*3^n-1.

The folded based core Gamma_n consists of the a-loop and the subdivided w_n-circle. Every edge lies on a reduced closed based path. In particular no trimming or further folding is missing. The only edge labeled a that starts and ends at the same vertex is the a-loop at the basepoint. The n=0 case has the same property.

Completing this core to its covering graph attaches trees at the missing directions; it does not introduce further cycles. Thus the entire covering graph has exactly one a-loop. If a lies in gH_ng^{-1}, the vertex reached from the basepoint by g^{-1} must be fixed by the a-edge. To see the implication without assuming cancellation properties of a written word, the subsequent action of g is a bijection on the covering vertices: the path g^{-1}ag closes if and only if the intermediate vertex is unchanged by a. The unique a-loop forces that vertex to be the basepoint. Therefore g belongs to H_n and gH_ng^{-1}=H_n.

Applying this to H_j <= gH_kg^{-1}, using a in H_j, gives H_j <= H_k, contradicting H_k < H_j. This independent argument establishes unbased nonmovability without needing the general ascending-chain theorem.

## 4. The attached collar and the actual quotient

The constant sequences in the fixed a-circle form a closed copy A of S^1 inside X. The adjunction

D_* = X union_A (A times [0,1]), with height 1 glued to A,

is compact and metrizable: it is the closed adjunction of compact metrizable spaces. Its outer circle L_*=A times {0} has a product neighborhood. Collapsing the cylinder vertically to height 1 is a strong deformation retraction onto X. Hence D_* is nonmovable, while L_* is movable and its inclusion is an NDR cofibration.

Importantly, collapsing L_* gives X with the cone on A attached along A. The report does not incorrectly substitute X/A for this mapping cone.

For the finite-stage adjunction P_n, a compatible inverse-limit point with any coordinate in the cylinder below height 1 must have that identical cylinder coordinate at every stage. This is because the bonding map is the identity there and the graph maps never enter that part of the cylinder. Every remaining compatible sequence lies in the graph inverse limit X. These observations prove the asserted homeomorphism lim P_n = D_*.

The quotient claim is equally secure. The preimage of the collapsed outer circle under a bonding map is exactly that outer circle. Hence any non-basepoint compatible quotient sequence has unique compatible lifts; the all-basepoint sequence corresponds to precisely L_*. The resulting bijection D_*/L_* -> lim(P_n/L_*) is continuous, and compactness of the source and the Hausdorff property of the target make it a homeomorphism.

Each P_n/L_* is the a-disk wedged to the b-circle. Contracting the disk to the attachment point is a homotopy equivalence with S^1. On this circle the bonding map has degree one, because the b-exponent sum of bab^{-1}a^{-1}b is one. This proves the bonding maps are homotopy equivalences, a stronger assertion than an isomorphism on first homology alone in an arbitrary space. The quotient system is consequently isomorphic in the pro-homotopy category to a constant circle. Thus D_*/L_* has shape S^1.

All required pieces of the proposed abstract implication are therefore present in the example, yet both D_* and K_*=X are nonmovable.

## 5. Internal semiflow and tails

Using cylinder height t and age s=-log(1-t), translation by r>=0 has the explicit height expression

t -> 1-exp(-r)(1-t).

It fixes X and fixes the gluing circle at height 1. This formula makes joint continuity in the point and finite time r transparent even at the gluing circle. At points of X outside A there is no additional continuity issue, since A is closed and the cylinder attaches only there.

For each fixed finite r the map is injective and has image

D_r = X union (A times [1-exp(-r),1]).

Since D_* is compact and the target is Hausdorff, it is a homeomorphism onto D_r. The tails are nested and their intersection is X. Their eventual containment in every neighborhood of X follows either from the displayed coordinate formula and compactness of A, or from the nested-compact-set argument.

The claimed full-flow extension on a locally compact nonmanifold space is also legitimate. For example attach A times (-infinity,1] to X at height 1 and use the same formula for all real r. This space is locally compact and metrizable; translation preserves the interval (-infinity,1), fixes X, and has the group law. Its compact subspace with height at least 0 is D_*. No assertion that this ambient space is a 3-manifold follows or is needed.

## 6. Actual isolating-block collar

For an actual tame block let D=N^+, L=n^+, E=N^i, K=Inv(N). A point of D\K has finite backward entry time, and its entry point is in L. Conversely a point in L remains in N for all positive time. The uniqueness of entry and the continuous signed entry-time function give the stated inverse pair

(l,s) -> phi_s(l),

p -> (phi_{t^i(p)}(p), -t^i(p)).

An orbit that starts on entry and stays in N cannot hit entry again: such a hit would require an immediately preceding interval outside N. Thus there is no hidden periodic-orbit identification. The two formulas establish a homeomorphism L times [0,infinity) = D\K.

For any finite a>0, the image of L times [0,a) is open in D, by continuity of the extended entry-time function. This is a genuine collar, regardless of how L embeds in the entry surface. Bounded-age pieces are compact and disjoint from K. Consequently any sequence in D\K approaching K has age tending to infinity. The proposed NDR function is therefore continuous when extended by 1 on K, and the proposed homotopy is continuous there because it is already the identity for ages at least 1. The inequalities and the condition u<1 collapse exactly as required. The empty-L case is harmless.

## 7. Actual block quotient shape equivalence

This is accepted as a shape equivalence, not as a conclusion about actual homotopy type of D/L.

For the tame three-dimensional block convention, (N,E) is a finite polyhedral pair after compatible triangulation, and Q=N/E is a compact ANR. The stopped backward flow is jointly continuous, fixes E, and hence descends to Q. Its exact image before quotienting is E union N_t^+: for an unstopped image point the subsequent t units remain in N, while every y in N_t^+ has the preimage phi_t(y). Therefore its compact images M_t decrease to C=(E union D)/E.

Every neighborhood of C contains some M_t. For otherwise points outside that neighborhood could be chosen from arbitrarily late M_t, and compactness would produce a point in their intersection outside C. This step needs containment only; it does not require M_t to be a neighborhood or an ANR.

The set C is invariant under the stopped semiflow. Forward motion of a point of D by time t supplies a preimage for the stopped backward map. Thus no surjectivity onto C is being inferred just from forward invariance.

Choose nested open ANR neighborhoods V_n of C in Q. Open subsets of the metrizable ANR Q are ANRs, so such a neighborhood expansion is available. For increasing T_n with M_{T_n} contained in V_n, define r_n=psi_{T_n}:Q -> V_n. For m>=n the time interval from T_n to T_m gives a homotopy in V_n between r_n and the inclusion of r_m. Composing r_n with V_n -> Q is homotopic to id_Q through the original semiflow.

For the other identity, fix n. Since psi_s(C) is contained in C for all s in [0,T_n], joint continuity and compactness of that interval and of C give a neighborhood W of C with psi_[0,T_n](W) contained in V_n. Select m>=n with V_m contained in W. Restricted to V_m, the time homotopy joins its inclusion in V_n to r_n restricted to V_m, within V_n. These are precisely the two inverse identities in the neighborhood pro-homotopy category. No retraction onto C, ANR property of C, or homological Whitehead theorem has been assumed.

When E is nonempty and L is empty, the quotient C is D with a disjoint basepoint, as the report explicitly stipulates. When E is empty, the argument uses Q=N and C=D directly. The broad summary should always be read with these conventions.

The block inputs were checked against Sánchez-Gabites, *Using an invariant knot of a flow to find additional invariant structure*, arXiv:2403.18805v1: Sections 2.3 and 3 establish the entry-time/block framework; Proposition 5(ii) uses the stopped backward deformation; Section 5.4 supplies tame blocks. The neighborhood pro-homotopy upgrade above is an independently checked argument rather than an attribution of a shape theorem to Proposition 5(ii). [Inspected primary source](https://arxiv.org/html/2403.18805v1).

## 8. Scope and two wording clarifications

1. Replace “the image sets M_t are cofinal among neighborhoods” by “every neighborhood of C eventually contains M_t.” The proof needs only this latter statement and establishes it.
2. Replace “X has finite H^0 and H^1” by “X has finitely generated integral Cech H^0 and H^1.” The actual groups are Z and Z^2; they are not finite groups.

For completeness, the nonrealizability scope is consistent with the cited primary source: its Theorems 10 and 16 give regular blocks and the spine property in the finite-cohomology case. A spine K of a finite three-manifold block B has a cofinal sequence of compact manifold neighborhoods obtained by deleting longer initial segments of its boundary collar; the successive inclusions are homotopy equivalences by sliding a finite collar. Such a K has the shape of B and is movable. Thus the nonmovable X here cannot be an isolated invariant compactum of an R^3 flow. This conclusion does not require interpreting a cohomology isomorphism alone as a shape equivalence.

The accepted result is consequently a precise exclusion of an abstract proof method. Any solution of row 1767 still needs additional ambient three-dimensional information or a different argument. Neither the main report nor its supplement constructs an R^3-flow counterexample, a prescribed open neighborhood preventing every movable invariant enlargement, or a movable enlargement for an arbitrary flow.

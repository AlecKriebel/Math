## Earlier theorem and actual correction

The full two-page [Belegradek–Hu erratum](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-015-1354-1), Math. Ann. 364 (2016), 711–712, was downloaded from the publisher and read. It corrects the earlier integer-topology homeomorphism assertion by inserting a Hölder exponent 0<alpha<1 at every regularity index. It explains that the applied continuity theorem was available in Hölder rather than integer topology. At that time the authors suspected failure at the integer endpoint but had no counterexamples. The erratum explicitly says the paper's other results were unaffected.

The current [Banakh–Belegradek preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1510.07269) is v2, March 1, 2017; v1 dates to October 25, 2015. Sections 2, 8 and 9 provide the topologies, normalizations, and corrected homeomorphism statements. The work was published in J. Math. Soc. Japan 70 (2018), 733–756, [DOI 10.2969/jmsj/07027344](https://doi.org/10.2969/jmsj/07027344). The primary proof audit here uses the accessible arXiv version; the publisher metadata independently confirms publication. There is no reliance on the superseded integer-topology claim.

## Scope of the candidate

The candidate gives separate sequences for r=0, r=1, and every r>=2. It preserves the exact geometric constraints, and in fact uses positive-curvature metrics. The r=1 argument includes a nonlinear curvature-sign estimate: C^1 convergence alone is never used to infer curvature control. The conclusion is failure of the specified parametrization to be a homeomorphism. It does not classify the abstract homeomorphism type of the metric space and does not contradict the noninteger Hölder or C^infinity results.

The construction's normalization is explicit rather than achieved by uncontrolled postcomposition. Its conformal factors are defined by an exact pullback identity; completeness and curvature of those factors follow by isometry. On the noncompact plane all perturbations lie in a fixed disk, so compact-open versus global uniform control creates no escape-to-infinity issue.

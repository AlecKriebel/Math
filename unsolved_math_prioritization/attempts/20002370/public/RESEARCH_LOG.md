# Five substantive approaches

Investigation date: 2026-10-03 UTC. Five mathematical approaches were considered.

## 1. Transfer and lower bounds

Reconstructed coefficient specialization and the one-universal embedding argument. Verified them against Anscombe--Fehm Lemma 5.2. Distinguished unnamed t from a named generator. Proved agreement for one-symbol logic by induction through bound scopes and for sentences with at most two quantifier occurrences.

Outcome: the known one-leading-universal barrier survives; width at least two, but no width-two equivalence theorem follows.

## 2. Cut construction and source arithmetic

Inspected Vollprecht's definitions and cut polynomial. Detected a rational-offset normalization discrepancy on printed p.117. Rebuilt the cut with lambda=sum_{m>=1}2^(-m!), independently established its tail estimates and transcendence, and encoded nonrealization using Denef plus DPRM. Reconstructed the pure three-universal sentence with a genus-one constant-field predicate.

Outcome: a consistent proof of the known one-alternation upper bound; no claimed improvement or author-issued erratum.

## 3. Universal compression

Converted the branch-height-two formula to its prenex version. Tried identifying the two inner universal variables. Proved this replacement is vacuous because a genus-one constant witness is itself constant. Exhaustively checked the corresponding Boolean transformations on two-element predicate structures as a logic control.

Outcome: exact rejection of a tempting compression, not a general impossibility theorem for two leading universals.

## 4. Constant guards and finite-variable strategies

Considered replacing the negated existential constant definition by an existential nonconstant predicate. Proved no nonempty existentially definable set over constants can avoid the constant field, by rational specialization. Re-examined the difference between variable reuse, total quantified variables, and quantifier occurrences. The schema's arithmetic witnesses prevent extracting an exact total-variable count without further analysis.

Outcome: guard substitution blocked. A two-argument guard with an arbitrary nonconstant parameter and broader finite-variable equivalence remain unproved; no shortcut claim retained.

## 5. Bounded-degree and arithmetic certificates

Proved that every fixed rational-function-degree truncation translates to a first-order sentence over the real closed constant field, hence cannot separate the pair. For q_m=s_{m!}, proved the first cut violation occurs exactly at (m+1)!, and reproduced small exact instances. Searched primary-source updates and explicitly separated the related R-versus-R* open question in Anscombe--Fehm from the literal AIM pair.

Outcome: rigorous reason finite degree and finite prefix experiments cannot complete the task. No stronger variable minimum established.

## Final status

Partial, original question unresolved as a whole. Established alternation count is credited to prior results. Leading-universal minimum is only bounded by 2 and 3, and total-variable minimum is undetermined. Finite checks certify only the stated arithmetic and logical identities. The accompanying audit records the scope of independent verification.

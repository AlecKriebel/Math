## S2. Established weak-coupling result

David Damanik and Anton Gorodetski, *Spectral and Quantum Dynamical Properties of the Weakly Coupled Fibonacci Hamiltonian*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 305 (2011), 221-277.

- Publication: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-011-1220-2
- Author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2552
- Checked Theorem 1.2 and thickness definition, preprint p.4; Theorem 1.3, p.5; the gap-opening argument in Section 3.1; and Proposition 3.11, p.20.
- The rendered theorem page confirms two-sided positive-constant bounds. No precise asymptotic constant or parameter monotonicity is asserted by that theorem.

## S3. Numerical method and later problem list

- Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5753
- Chapter: https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-0903-0_9

## Additional current-status exclusions

- Damanik, Gorodetski and Yessen, *The Fibonacci Hamiltonian*: https://arxiv.org/abs/1403.7823. The abstract was checked; its characterization of several fractal/dynamical quantities does not by itself settle thickness monotonicity.
- Colbrook, Embree and Fillman, arXiv:2407.20353, current title *Computing Spectral Size: Rigorous Algorithms and the Limits of Computation*: https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.20353. Downloaded text was checked for the target; no thickness-monotonicity theorem was located.
- Li, Xu and Zhou, *Monotonicity, global symplectification and the stability of Dry Ten Martini Problem*, version 4 dated 30 September 2026: https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.02222v4. Its abstract and introductory scope concern analytic potentials and gap opening via energy-parameter monotonicity. This is not a theorem about coupling monotonicity of the discontinuous Fibonacci potential's spectral thickness.
- Mark Embree's current publication list was checked for relevant newer work: https://personal.math.vt.edu/embree/ .

Searches included exact combinations of Fibonacci Hamiltonian, spectral thickness, monotone/decreasing/monotonicity, and recent years. No applicable full resolution was found. This limited search justifies retaining **unsolved** for this investigation, rather than asserting a definitive universal literature status.

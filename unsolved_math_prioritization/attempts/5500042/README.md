# TOPP 42 general disk-face vertex unfolding counterexample

Problem 5500042 / TOPP 42, checked 9 October 2026 UTC. The general original-edge disk-face assertion has a prior published negative answer, credited to Zachary Abel, Erik D. Demaine, and Martin L. Demaine, *A Topologically Convex Vertex-Ununfoldable Polyhedron*, CCCG 2011, Theorem 4. This theoretical edition preserves the complete authored reconstruction and audit of their perturbed witness.

## Accepted scope

The witness is an embedded closed polyhedral sphere with twelve original polygonal disk faces. Its faces cannot be arranged isometrically in the plane with pairwise disjoint interiors while retaining original edge/vertex incidences that connect all faces. Arbitrary hinge rotations and reflections do not avoid the strict local-angle obstruction. The proof uses neither new cuts through faces nor a triangulation.

The witness is geometrically nonconvex and has nonconvex faces. The separate geometrically convex polyhedron question and the intermediate convex-face question are not resolved by this audit. No global-openness or complete-problem-resolution claim is made for those residual questions.

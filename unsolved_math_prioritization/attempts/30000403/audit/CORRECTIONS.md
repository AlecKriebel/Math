The older unrestricted real cubic-descent claim is a misleading apparent counterexample. The revised statement imposes an additional restriction on the descent isomorphism, absent from the present problem.
4. Keep the universal-constant qualification prominent. Do not replace it by “optimal for every fixed k,G,C,r” or extend the conclusion to actual marked G-cover objects with nontrivial center.

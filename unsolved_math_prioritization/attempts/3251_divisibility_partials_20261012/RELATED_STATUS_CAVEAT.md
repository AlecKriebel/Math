# Related later source: bounded status caveat

Checked 12 October 2026.

Lizhong Chen, *An infinite family of minimally nonperfectly divisible graphs with a bisimplicial vertex*, arXiv:2607.25412v2; revised 4 August 2026, 03:23:47 UTC; first submitted 28 July 2026, 08:08:42 UTC.

Primary record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25412v2

Primary full text: https://arxiv.org/html/2607.25412v2

Theorem 1.2 claims an infinite MNPD family; Corollary 1.3 refutes Hu–Xu–Zhuang Problem 4.1. Inspection covered the construction and the dependency chain: finite rooted-graph Lemma 2.2, symbolic product Lemma 3.1, auxiliary Lemma 4.1, and Section 5. The finite computation has not been rerun in this check. No journal-publication status was verified.

**Additional inference, conditional on that theorem:** Section 5 attaches each nontrivial rooted factor to the remaining connected graph at its root only. Deleting that root separates the factor's other vertices from the rest. Thus these claimed MNPD examples have clique cutsets of size one, although the manuscript does not advertise this implication. The March paper's unrestricted MNPD no-cutvertex/no-clique-cutset discussion should therefore not be repeated as an unqualified current-status claim.

This does not affect the MN2D target. The factors explicitly contain induced C5s, so the larger examples are neither odd-hole-free nor minimally non-2-divisible. Their non-2-divisibility is already witnessed by proper induced odd holes.

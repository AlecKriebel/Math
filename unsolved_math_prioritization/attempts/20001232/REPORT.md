## Scope and audited elementary results

An ordinary fiber consists of ALL nonnegative integer I×J×K arrays with fixed sums x_ij+, x_i+k, x_+jk. Positivity refers to every entry in these three margin arrays, not every cell. A consecutive spectrum difference d means d−1 missing integers. Structural zeros cannot be added as extra constraints.

For logistic covariates (0,1,L), L≥2, q≥6, group sizes (q(L−1),qL,q), and conditioned statistics S=T=qL, subtracting y1+Ly2=qL from y0+y1+y2=qL gives y0=(L−1)y2. Set t=y2. The complete fiber is y=((L−1)t,L(q−t),t), t=0,…,q; all group bounds are equivalent to this interval. Failures are ((L−1)(q−t),Lt,q−t). Both row totals are qL, all group totals ≥6, and the middle spectrum is {0,L,…,qL}. Thus gaps have L−1 missing integers. This is a varying-design construction. The weighted score is NOT an ordinary margin, so it does not settle the ordinary clause.

For every nonempty ordinary 2×J×K fiber, including zero margins, put u_jk=x_+jk and y_jk=x_1jk. Fixed first-layer row/column sums and 0≤y≤u specify a bounded transportation polytope. Between attainable target values a<c, interpolate feasible real arrays to obtain any integer target z. Fix y_j0k0=z, delete that variable, and subtract z from its incident row/column demands. The remaining matrix is a bipartite incidence matrix (sign one vertex class), hence totally unimodular, with integer capacities and demands. A nonempty bounded feasible flow polytope has an integral vertex. Reinstate z and take the complementary layer u−y. Every z between a,c is therefore attainable. This proof does not need Rapallo–Yoshida, positive upper bounds, or connectivity by only 4-cycles.

For a connecting move set M, the first edge crossing above a consecutive attainable value a has next value at least c and at most a+max|m_h|. Therefore c−a≤max|m_h|. This remains true for paths that initially decrease the coordinate.

## Residual approach 1: translate and dilate the universal example

The De Loera–Onn 6×4×3 example was transcribed in probe.py. Its zero-margin fiber has published target spectrum {0,2}. A positive translation only injects its old fiber into a larger fiber: F(b)+t1 is contained in F(b+tA1), but equality would require every new feasible cell to be at least t, which ordinary positive margins do not impose.

The experiment uses scales 1,2,3,5,10 and offsets 0,1,2. For every positive offset, exact integer witnesses were found for EVERY target integer from 0 through the upper bound 2s+3t. Consequently the translated spectrum is rigorously that full interval, independently of the solver's infeasibility claims. For s=1,t=1 it is 0,…,5; for s=1,t=2 all ordinary margins are at least 6 and it is 0,…,8. Witness validation checks nonnegativity, every margin equality, and the target coordinate using integer arithmetic. The zero-offset absent values are only solver diagnostics unless the cited published result or a separate exact argument is used. A subsequent exact rational rank calculation in exact_zero_fiber.py proves the scaled zero-margin fiber has spectrum {0,2,…,2s} for every integer s≥1: its active system has 26 variables, rank 25, parameterization s*c+tau*d, bounds s≤tau≤2s, and target 4s−2tau. Dilation alone therefore does not create arbitrary gap size: the step remains 2, not 2s. Moreover padding_family.py supplies integer witnesses for the all-ones margins with target 0,1,2,3. Minkowski addition with the verified base translated spectrum [0,5] and the exact scaled zero-margin spectrum proves Spec(F(s*b+t*A1))=[0,2s+3t] for EVERY s,t≥1. The upper bound is its incident ij margin. No decomposition claim for arbitrary feasible tables is used. A positive-margin family cannot be obtained by these padding experiments.

## Residual approach 2: facial support instead of imposed structural zeros

A valid route would select arrays of pair-potentials alpha_ij,beta_ik,gamma_jk with w_ijk=alpha_ij+beta_ik+gamma_jk≥0. If margins satisfy alpha·b12+beta·b13+gamma·b23=0, every feasible nonnegative table is supported on E={w=0}. This is a derived support restriction, not an externally imposed structural-zero condition. To admit strictly positive margins, E must cover every pair of indices. To yield arbitrary gaps, one additionally needs a complete-fiber parameterization or exclusion certificate, not merely two feasible endpoints or a circuit supported in E. This identifies a precise missing embedding lemma: encode a large primitive coordinate on a full-pair-covering exposed support while ruling out other integer tables. The existing universality construction explicitly has zero margins and supplies no such lemma.

## Residual approach 3: global congruence obstruction fails

For the complete ordinary three-way margin matrix with I,J,K≥2, the integer kernel contains a 2×2×2 checkerboard move through ANY specified cell, with that cell coefficient 1. Therefore the gcd of target coordinate values over the entire integer kernel is 1. No congruence x_h≡r mod L, L>1, can be deduced from the ordinary margin equations alone. In contrast, the logistic family has a one-dimensional kernel whose middle coefficient is L. Any attempt to transfer that congruence to an ordinary complete format must obtain it through nonnegativity restricting feasible integer points, not through the ordinary margin equations alone. This is an exact obstruction to the direct lattice encoding strategy, not a proof all spectra are intervals.

## Residual approach 4: elementary-cube connectivity and safe padding

The checkerboard cubes anchored at (I,J,K), one for each i<I,j<J,k<K, form a Z-basis of the ordinary integer kernel. To verify this, subtract z_ijk times the corresponding cube to zero every interior cell. The zero two-way margins then successively force the remaining boundary cells to zero. For two tables x,y with the same margins, their difference is consequently a finite sequence of signed unit cubes. Let p_r be the partial sums of that sequence. Taking t≥max(0,max_{r,h}(−x_h−p_r,h)) makes x+t1+p_r nonnegative at every step. Thus sufficiently padded endpoints are connected by unit-coordinate moves, and all target integers between their shifted values occur in the padded fiber. One explicit sufficient bound is t=Σ_{i<I,j<J,k<K}|y_ijk−x_ijk|, since each partial sum has magnitude at most that many unit steps and x≥0. This destroys persistence of a particular gap under sufficiently large uniform padding. It does NOT show small positive margins suffice: the required t depends on endpoints and dimensions. Nor does connectivity in a fiber allowing −1 entries prove nonnegative-fiber connectivity without a justified translation and scope change.

## Residual approach 5: two fixed dimensions cannot support unbounded gaps

Fix J,K and allow I and the margins to vary. There is a uniform finite coordinate-step bound over all these fibers, even without positivity. Here is a standard Graver/Dickson derivation, consistent with the bounded-complexity result cited by De Loera–Onn.

Let B be the row/column sum matrix on J×K slices. Let G be the finite signed Graver set of B. Every slice difference z_i in ker_Z(B) is a conformal sum of elements of G: repeatedly split a nonminimal element in its orthant; termination follows from decreasing l1 norm, and finiteness of minimal orthant elements follows from Dickson's lemma. Because Σ_i z_i=0 entrywise, the counts λ_g of all these labeled pieces satisfy Σ_g λ_g g=0. The nonnegative integer solutions to this last fixed system are conformal sums of a finite set H of nonzero coordinatewise-minimal solutions, again by Dickson's lemma. For one η∈H, select its indicated occurrences from the labeled slice decompositions and aggregate them within each slice, producing a three-way move m. Each slice of m has zero row/column sums and Σ_i m_i=0, so it preserves all ordinary two-way margins. Each m_i is conformal to z_i. Repeating the partition into H pieces yields a conformal decomposition of z into such moves, hence a nonnegative path between x and y.

Write D=max_{η∈H} Σ_g η_g ||g||_infinity (D=0 if H is empty). Every resulting move has every coordinate bounded by D. The path-crossing lemma bounds all consecutive cell differences by max(1,D), uniformly in I and all margins. Thus an arbitrary-gap ordinary three-way family must have at least two dimensions unbounded (after ordering dimensions, the middle size must be unbounded), not merely have every dimension ≥3. The fixed J,K theorem blocks searches restricted to 3×3×K as an arbitrary-gap strategy, though those formats may still exhibit bounded gaps. It leaves the variable-I,variable-J,depth-3 universal regime untouched.

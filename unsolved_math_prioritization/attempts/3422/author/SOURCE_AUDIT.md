## Hypothesis-by-hypothesis alignment

- Target domain: ordinary topological ambient homeomorphisms of the whole Euclidean space; no smoothness or volume condition.
- Target object: an arbitrary embedded compact Cantor set, not necessarily linearly embedded.
- Smallness: global Euclidean displacement; strict sup bounds follow from Frolkina's non-strict convention by requesting epsilon/2.
- Isotopy endpoint: only the final image must be disjoint. The argument does not produce or require disjointness at every positive time.
- Dimensions one and two: independently proved in the report; no unsupported descent from an R^3 ambient map.
- Dimension three: arbitrary two-set separation is the exact imported statement; taking the two sets equal settles self-displacement.
- Dimensions at least four: Krushkal's small-isotopy obstruction, plus the explicit global bounded-displacement dilation construction, rules out sufficiently small homeomorphisms.
- Orientation: a bounded-displacement self-homeomorphism of Euclidean space is isotopic to the identity by the displayed construction, so a separate orientation assumption is unnecessary.
- Compact support: not in the original target and not needed for the reduction.
- Inherited supporting work: Edwards-Kirby local deformation material was consulted, but the earlier unrelated homeomorphism-group work is not counted as a new Cantor-set proof or a same-target inherited attempt.

# Primary inputs and claim map

All PDF versions and byte hashes are pinned in source_manifest.json. Full reading files are excluded from the public packet. URLs below identify primary sources; this map states exactly which facts are imported. All new deductions are in the five turn files, with no novelty claim.

## Original and surgery/quadratic structure

- **Ohtsuki (editor), Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds**, Geometry & Topology Monographs4(2002),377–572. [Full published volume article](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf). Printed525–526, subsection10.3.2 and Question10.21: original Spin^c phase lift and degree motivation. Printed523–524: integral-homology-sphere Y filtration and the all-abelian-target relation between Yd and degree<d. Question10.21's page was inspected visually. No unstated connected-sum axiom is extracted from it.
- **Deloup–Massuyeau, Quadratic functions and Spin^c-structures on three-manifolds**, Topology44(2005),509–555, DOI10.1016/j.top.2004.11.003. [Full published reading copy](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/deloup5.pdf). Definition2.1, Theorem2.3: discriminant functions and the affine Spin^c map; introductory Theorems1/2 and Corollary2: canonical quadratic structures and Y1 classification. Theorem2.2, printed529–530: Spin^c Kirby calculus. Definition3.3: finite-type degree; Remark3.5: compatibility with spin transport. Example3.1: RP3 quadratic labels. Example3.3, printed552: non-torsion S2×S1#RP3 absorption; its explicit coincidence of the listed equivalence and diffeomorphism classes was visually checked. Example3.5, printed554: known T3 spin-Rochlin non-descent. The non-torsion example is not generalized to every possible phase invariant.
- **Massuyeau, Spin Borromean surgeries**, author arXiv math/0104065v2,6January2003, [pinned author PDF](https://massuyea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/papers/Matveev_spin.pdf). Proposition1/Corollary1, pp.9–10: the spin Rochlin degree-one surgery law. Formula(18), p.15: homogeneous van der Blij signature/Wu formula. Lemma12, p.21: the published negative Brown/Rochlin sign, visually checked. Proof of Theorem1, p.23: Poincare/S3 Y equivalence calibration. These are classical inputs to the odd-Chern construction, not new claims.
- **Massuyeau, Surgery equivalence relations for 3-manifolds**, author exposition, publisher DOI10.5802/wbln.38, [author PDF](https://massuyea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/papers/WB.pdf). Theorem3.14 and Corollary3.15, pp.32–33: Y2 classification and its integral-homology-sphere specialization to Rochlin. Following paragraph, p.34: Casson lifts R/8. The Turn5 Y2 argument uses only the integral-homology-sphere case; no general closed-manifold equivalence between all degree-one invariants and Y2 is presumed.
- **Kirby–Melvin, Local surgery formulas for quantum invariants and the Arf invariant**, Geometry & Topology Monographs7(2004),213–233. [Full published PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2004/07/gtm-2004-07-009p.pdf). Section1, printed214–215: the characteristic-sublink formula R=signature(B)-C·C+8Arf(C). Turn4 explicitly supplies the additional integer correction for a nonbinary integral Wu representative.
- **Deloup–Massuyeau, Quadratic functions on torsion groups**, Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra198(2005),105–121. [Full published reading copy](https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/deloup4.pdf). Background verification of discriminant and quadratic conventions. The generalized Brown formula used here is derived in Turn4 from the homogeneous formula and square completion; no additional unlocated classification theorem from this article is needed.

## Floer and torsion inputs in Turn5

- **Ozsváth–Szabó, Absolutely graded Floer homologies and intersection forms for four-manifolds with boundary**, arXiv math/0110170v2,13March2002; later Advances in Mathematics173(2003),179–261, DOI10.1016/S0001-8708(02)00030-0. [Pinned v2 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0110170v2). Theorem1.2, Theorem4.3, Proposition4.2 and the absolute-grading congruence: d is a rational invariant, additive and conjugation-invariant, with d=(c1²-signature)/4 modulo2. Theorem1.3/Theorem5.1: chi_red-d/2 is Casson on integral homology spheres. Section8.1, printed46–47: the oriented P=-Sigma235 and Q=Sigma237 calculations used in Proposition5.1. The exact displayed modulo2 congruence is used, regardless of an abbreviated codomain label in the introduction. The pinned input is the specified full author version, not falsely labelled the final PDF.
- **Ue, The Fukumoto–Furuta and the Ozsváth–Szabó invariants for spherical 3-manifolds**, Banach Center Publications85(2009),121–139, DOI10.4064/bc85-0-9. [Full published PDF](https://www.impan.pl/shop/en/publication/transaction/download/product/85982). Theorem1.1 and the following paragraph, printed122: spherical comparison and the explicit nonspherical Sigma237 example d=0, mu_bar=8. Page inspected visually. The spherical theorem is not extended to arbitrary Seifert or rational homology spheres.
- **Rustamov, Surgery formula for the renormalized Euler characteristic of Heegaard Floer homology**, arXiv math/0409294v3,16June2005. [Pinned full author PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409294v3). Definition in the introduction: hat_chi=chi_red-d/2 on rational homology Spin^c spheres. Theorem3.4, printed5: hat_chi=-tau+lambda with normalized scalar Turaev torsion and Casson–Walker invariant. Section4, printed6: explicit rho congruence modulo2. No finite-type degree-one conclusion is imported from this identity.
- **Massuyeau, Some finiteness properties for the Reidemeister–Turaev torsion of three-manifolds**, arXiv math/0507214v3,4March2009; later Journal of Knot Theory and Its Ramifications19(2010),405–447, DOI10.1142/S0218216510007899. [Pinned author PDF](https://massuyea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/papers/torsion_FTI.pdf). Theorem1, pp.2–3: degree<=d+1 after reduction moduloI^d,d>=1, with Euler structure and homological parametrization, only positive-rank or finite-cyclic G. Theorems4.1/4.2, pp.28–29: exact alternating-sum ideal bounds. They are recorded as insufficient for the required scalar evenness; no unproved transfer from group-ring torsion to scalar parity is made.

## Scope of the finite checks

The scripts check explicit finite quadratic values/isometries, rational odd-primary lift identities, the finite-torsor calibration, exact lattice cocycles, lens-space gauge values, and Turn5 arithmetic. They do not prove that every modeled tuple is realized by a three-manifold, verify an arbitrary Kirby theorem, compute Heegaard Floer groups, or establish the unproved higher-two-primary parity. Those claims require the written proofs and the exact primary inputs above.

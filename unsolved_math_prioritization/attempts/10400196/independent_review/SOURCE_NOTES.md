# Independent source and normalization audit

This is a read-only mathematical review. No external source code was executed. Full PDFs and rendered pages remain reading copies, outside this portable review.

1. **Original scope.** Ohtsuki, *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, printed pp.525–526, especially Question10.21 and its entire preceding paragraph/footnote, were checked in text and the actual rendered p.526. The short wording asks for a mod16 lift of the quadratic Gauss phase and motivates degree1. It does not explicitly give connected-sum additivity, a non-torsion finite-sum prescription, or a full spin-recovery axiom. The author correctly keeps these extra requirements conditional. Original PDF: https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf
2. **Quadratic construction and surgery theory.** Deloup–Massuyeau, *Quadratic functions and complex spin structures on three-manifolds*, Topology44(2005),509–555: introductory Theorem2 and Corollary2; Spin/Spin-c exact sequence; Theorem2.2 Kirby calculus; Theorem2.3 affine quadratic embedding; Definition3.3 and Remark3.5 on pp.545–546; Examples3.1,3.3,3.5 on pp.552–554. The absorbing S2×S1#RP3 example explicitly identifies the relevant **diffeomorphism** classes, not merely Y1 classes. Example3.5's T3 non-descent of full Rochlin is credited existing work and is not used to reject every possible lift. Full publisher-format author-hosted copy: https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/deloup5.pdf
3. **Sign and genuine degree.** Massuyeau, *Spin Borromean surgeries*, Proposition1 and Corollary1 pp.9–10 prove the local Rochlin variation and zero two-Y difference; Lemma12 p.21, checked visually, says Brown(phi)=-R modulo8. The author carries epsilon and does not combine this sign with the original positive convention. The proof near p.23 supplies the Poincaré/S3 Y-equivalence calibration. https://massuyea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/papers/Matveev_spin.pdf
4. **Characteristic formula.** Kirby–Melvin, *Local surgery formulas for quantum invariants and the Arf invariant*, pp.214–215: R=signature minus characteristic self-intersection plus8 Arf. A characteristic sublink is proper: for any of its components, the total linking with the other selected components is even by the characteristic equation. Thus its Arf term is defined. https://msp.org/gtm/2004/07/gtm-2004-07-009p.pdf
5. **Floer normalization and explicit examples.** Ozsváth–Szabó, *Absolutely graded Floer homologies and intersection forms for four-manifolds with boundary*, pinned v2: Theorem1.2 and the absolute grading; Theorem1.3/5.1 for chi_red-d/2=Lambda on integral homology spheres; Section8.1 pp.46–47 for d(-Sigma(2,3,5))=-2, reduced group0, and d(Sigma(2,3,7))=0 with one odd reduced generator. These precise orientations yield Casson values1 and-1, both Rochlin8. No Floer state enumeration was run by the reviewer.
6. **Y2 necessity only.** Massuyeau's surgery survey Corollary3.15 gives the integral-homology-sphere Y2 classification by Rochlin. Ohtsuki printed p.524 supplies the implication from Y2 equivalence to equality of degree<2 invariants. Unique Spin-c structures make the transfer valid for the two homology spheres. No converse for arbitrary closed three-manifolds is claimed. https://massuyea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/papers/WB.pdf
7. **Euler/torsion normalization.** Rustamov Section4 states d=(c1 squared-signature)/4 modulo2; Theorem3.4 identifies chi_red-d/2 with -tau+Lambda in his scalar normalization. The author distinguishes the canonical mod2 grading from parity of a rational absolute number. Ue's 2009 p.122 independently records d(Sigma(2,3,7))=0 and the Neumann–Siebenmann value8.
8. **Insufficient torsion result.** Massuyeau, *Some finiteness properties for the Reidemeister–Turaev torsion*, pinned v3, Theorem1 has the positive-rank-or-finite-cyclic hypothesis and degree bound d+1 for augmentation power d>=1. Theorem4.2, pp.28–29, only yields the finite cyclic two-surgery defect in kappa(Z[G]). It does not supply evenness of the scalar Euler-corrected defect. The author preserves both the scalar/group-ring distinction and the noncyclic limitation.

The complete source hashes and versioned URLs are bound by the author's final source manifest and were checked separately. This audit validates these uses, not historical novelty or exhaustive current literature coverage.

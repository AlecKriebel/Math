# Five bounded approaches

## 1. Recover the exact source and test a prior-resolution route

The source contribution asks for an admissible flag on the original smooth Mori dream space and also announces an affirmative answer. The corresponding manuscript was recovered as arXiv:1612.03861v4. The unrelated astronomy identifier was rejected. The source assertion was not silently converted into either an independently accepted proof or a declaration that the problem is open. The later arXiv:1901.00384v2 also attributes the affirmative flag-on-X result to Postinghel–Urbinati. Reading the detailed construction leaves descent and valuation compatibility uncertified in this audit, even after recognizing the first-boundary indexing intended by the manuscript’s own example.

**Stopping point:** prior affirmative claim documented; an exact prior resolution is not certified by this investigation.

## 2. Audit the birational flag construction

An admissible flag on a modification does not automatically have flag images of the required dimensions. Proposition 4.1 proves a sufficient descent condition and identifies equality of the relevant global-cone slice. The exceptional flag on the blowup of a point shows why the endpoint/isomorphism condition cannot simply be omitted from that argument. A cusp example separates correct intersection dimensions from local smoothness.

**Stopping point:** no argument puts the claimed polyhedral flag in the required isomorphism locus for every X, and no alternative universal descent is proved.

## 3. Audit the projection of ambient toric bodies

The complete restriction series on a smooth plane conic has interval [0,2], whereas a coordinate projection of the standard ambient hyperplane triangle has interval [0,1]. The tangent section makes the additional order from cancellation explicit. This refutes the general projection-from-restriction-surjectivity inference.

**Scope qualification:** this is not presented as a verified counterexample to every hypothesis of the manuscript's special Cox/tropical construction. In particular, the conic has not been identified with the required Cox/tropical construction. The indexing repair is supported by the manuscript’s own Section 5.1.1; the distinct intrinsic and ambient endpoints are expected for such truncation and are not by themselves a contradiction. The diagnostic shows only that the general restricted-volume argument needs further compatibility input.

**Stopping point:** restricted-volume equality alone cannot repair the projection step.

## 4. Strengthen Cox generators to a valuation-compatible hypothesis

Proposition 6.1 proves the associated-graded generation criterion. Proposition 5.1 independently computes a complete value semigroup for a cubic flag on P². The global cone is rational polyhedral while the semigroup is not finitely generated.

**Stopping point:** a finite Cox presentation does not supply the required initial-form generators for an arbitrary flag; a finite Khovanskii basis is a stronger sufficient goal, not the target itself.

## 5. Test known positive regimes against the universal quantifier

The good-flag theorem supplies a conditional positive route, and the surface theorem covers smooth Mori dream surfaces. Their exact additional assumptions were checked. Neither produces the necessary nested Mori dream subvarieties and exceptional-locus compatibility for arbitrary dimension and arbitrary X.

**Stopping point:** retain these as known cases, not a substitute answer. No sixth approach or unsupported universal construction is claimed.

## Overall boundary

The five approaches yield a source/status correction, two explicit obstruction examples, and rigorous conditional criteria. They do not solve or refute the universal existence question. No novelty claim is made.

Its analytic proof has not been independently accepted here and is not a dependency of the elementary proofs. The 27-page PDF displays July 7, 2026; the HTML displays August 24, 2026. Both identify arXiv v1 / July 5, but the formats are not silently identified as the same bytes or text.

The key missing estimate bounds the reciprocal mass of stable multisets avoiding (1-1/N,1] by an absolute multiple of log N. It retains arbitrary multiplicities. Distinct-denominator and fixed-multiplicity cases do not settle it. Finite computations through denominator 12 do not cover arbitrary multisets or establish an optimal exponent.

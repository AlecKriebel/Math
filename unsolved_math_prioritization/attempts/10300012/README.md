# Prescribed polygon-bundle cores: accepted partial results

Problem 10300012 / AMR-102-0012, rank 1247, Calegari Question 6.2. Substantive attempt 1/5. The original base-manifold isotopy and insulator questions remain unresolved by this work.

## Accepted scope and mathematics

The complete [mathematical report](MATHEMATICAL_REPORT.md) works in a closed orientable hyperbolic 3-manifold with a specified ideal-polygon-bundle complementary region of a full genuine essential lamination. The prescribed curve is the ordinary disk-bundle core isotopy class, not an arbitrary knot generating the bundle's fundamental group. Fullness is not replaced by very fullness; a finite side-monodromy permutation is allowed.

1. The prescribed core has unknotted universal lifts. The completed-boundary embedding, proper strip, compactly supported auxiliary isotopy, locally finite lift and inverse applied to the entire strip are included in full.
2. For every requested radius, a finite cover has a homeomorphic lift of that same core isotopic to a simple closed geodesic with a larger embedded tube. For radius greater than log(3)/2, that geodesic has a noncoalescable insulator family. Actual cyclic subgroup separability is used even for proper powers. The cover need not be normal, and no descent or automatic base-group primitivity is asserted.
3. The exact length/tube inequality of Proposition 5.1 gives a conditional base-manifold isotopy and insulator criterion, retaining its primitive-class and embedded-tube hypotheses. Its hyperboloid derivation, strict inequality and stronger rotation-free variant are complete.

The authored helical comparison and the insulator-convexity separation argument are retained. The helix is not a lamination counterexample. Individually unknotted lifts do not produce simultaneous full-group-equivariant straightening.

The [mathematical audit](MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT.md) accepts precisely these partial conclusions and retains the completion-map, strip-isotopy, winding, peripheral-group and proper-power checks. The unresolved obligations are base primitivity, embedded geodesic image, compatible straightening of all lifts, isotopy descent, a full-group insulator family and the geometric hypotheses of the quantitative criterion.

# Five-attempt endpoint

**Full target: unresolved. Budget: exhausted, 5/5 completed substantive attempts.**

The target is the all-dimensional assertion that every compact smooth non-Einstein Ricci soliton has a positive eigenvalue of Perelman's ν-Hessian on the quotient by diffeomorphism and scaling. No proof or counterexample has been obtained. Known spectral characterizations, Kähler cases, and noncompact results retain their prior attribution.

## What the attempts established or reduced

1. Potential times Ricci: an exact quotient representative and Rayleigh numerator; the moment/spectral sign is unresolved.
2. Conformal tests: exact formulas for a general scalar, the centered potential, scalar curvature, and an exponential-potential quotient tensor. The apparently favorable quadratic potential term cancels. Strict positivity remains an unproved moment/resolvent inequality.
3. Coupled potential tensor pencil: an exact 2×2 quadratic matrix, including all gauge and Ricci-line corrections. No positive trace, negative determinant, or universally positive coefficient direction was proved.
4. Finite quotient counterexample mechanism: only invariant unstable modes descend. Standard geometric/topological arguments exclude free isometric quotients of the Koiso–Cao and Wang–Zhu compact surfaces as a shortcut. This does not exclude the mechanism for every possible higher-dimensional soliton.
5. Ground-state/min–max route: candidate conditional statement, pending independent review. In Ric+Hess f=g normalization, ν-stability implies that the first scalar spectral gap for |Ric|²e^{-f}dV is at least that for e^{-f}dV. A strict decrease would certify true quotient instability. No argument forces that strict decrease for every non-Einstein compact shrinker.

## Claims deliberately not made

- No universal instability theorem, counterexample, or resolution in dimension four
- No claim that the conditional spectral comparison is novel
- No inference that failure of any chosen tensor family proves stability
- No change from linear ν-instability to the weaker assertion of dynamical instability
- No omission of gauge/scaling, non-Kähler, higher-dimensional, or quotient cases

The exact algebra replay checks nine identities. They are consistency checks, not verification of a geometric existence theorem or the missing sign inequality. The mathematical packet is frozen for an uninvolved audit; further proof search would require a new explicit decision outside this exhausted attempt budget.

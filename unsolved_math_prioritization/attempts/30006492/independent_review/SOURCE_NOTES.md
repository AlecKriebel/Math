No coefficient field is stated. The violin replacement announced there is credited, not reconstructed from an unavailable full paper.
- [Farinati–Garcia Galofre, Osaka Journal of Mathematics 56 (2019)](https://ir.library.osaka-u.ac.jp/repo/ouka/all/73356/ojm56_03_525.pdf): Definitions 3, 6, 9, Section 1.2, Definition 20 and Theorem 21 supply the virtual cocycle-pair and ordered strand-weight conventions. The diagram on printed p. 532 was visually checked. Examples 25 (pp. 536–537) and 36 (p. 540) supply the cyclic control; Examples 11 (pp. 528–529) and 35 (p. 540) supply the nonabelian twist-second control. These are credited source examples; extra welded descent is checked independently.
- [Lebed–Vendramin](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1509.07067), Definitions 5.1 and 6.1 and Proposition 6.2: guitar and derived conventions require the stated mirror adjustment. The concrete maps were verified directly instead of relying on a convention-independent slogan.
- [Damiani](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1605.02323), Proposition 3.14 and Corollary 3.15: the mixed and welded relations match the chosen rightmost-first convention. The opposite forbidden relation is distinct.
- [Bardakov–Nasybullov](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.06971), Section 4, Problem 1: credited virtual predecessor, not a theorem closing the selected problem.
- [Bartholomew–Fenn](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1004.1320), Section 4: relation background only. The old finite census is not used, and its erratum is not ignored.

All six local PDF hashes match the author's pinned source manifest. Complete source PDFs, extracted text and renderings are reading material and excluded from the review package.

# Sources and bounded literature status

Checked 7 October 2026. The authored proof is a candidate awaiting independent audit. The sources inspected do not establish a prior general resolution; the search is bounded and supports no novelty claim.

## Original problem

- Dietrich Burde (joint work with Karel Dekimpe), “Pre-Lie and Post-Lie Algebra Structures,” in *Mini-Workshop: Poisson and Poisson-type algebras*, Oberwolfach Report 46/2023, contribution pp.2688–2690, exact conjecture p.2689. Workshop: 15–20 October 2023. DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2023/46. Official report PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/48175.
- The target is the generalization of the displayed sl3(C) nonexistence proposition to all semisimple target algebras. The nearby nilpotent-target conjectures and the earlier semisimple-source rigidity theorem are distinct questions.

## Primary post-Lie literature

- D. Burde, K. Dekimpe and M. Monadjem, *Post-Lie algebra structures for perfect Lie algebras*, Communications in Algebra 52(10) (2024), 4255–4267. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1080/00927872.2024.2344638. Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.08985, v1 submitted 15 November 2023. Author's version dated 8 March 2024: https://homepage.univie.ac.at/dietrich.burde/papers/burde_78_perfect.pdf.
  - Section 2 fixes finite-dimensional Lie algebras; Section 3 works over C. Proposition 2.6 gives the transverse-pair formulation; Proposition 2.7 gives the Rota–Baxter formulation.
  - Theorem 3.10 proves the sl3(C) target case. Proposition 3.11 treats a source consisting of a simple Levi factor and one irreducible abelian radical. Proposition 3.12 treats target sl2(C)⊕sl2(C). The introduction leaves the general perfect-source semisimple-target case conjectural.
  - Example 3.13 supplies a reductive target with two-dimensional center, illustrating the importance of the semisimple-target hypothesis.
- D. Burde, K. Dekimpe and M. Monadjem, *Rigidity results for Lie algebras admitting a post-Lie algebra structure*, International Journal of Algebra and Computation 32(8) (2022), 1495–1511. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218196722500679. Preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2205.04218. Theorem 3.3 concerns semisimple source and arbitrary target; it is not the present conjecture. Conjecture 3.5 concerns reductive source and semisimple target and is also separate.

## Classical global inputs, with exact locations

Pavel Etingof, *Lie Groups and Lie Algebras*, MIT 18.755 lecture notes (2024): https://ocw.mit.edu/courses/18-755-lie-groups-and-lie-algebras-ii-spring-2024/mit18_755_s24_lec_full.pdf.

- Proposition 4.12 and Corollary 4.13: orbit-stabilizer and transitive homogeneous spaces.
- Theorems 9.12–9.13 and Corollary 9.14, printed p.55: homomorphism integration and simply connected integration over R and C.
- Theorem 16.7 / Theorem 48.9: Levi decomposition.
- Corollary 43.5: compactness of simply connected compact real forms; Theorem 43.7 and Corollary 43.8, printed p.233: polar decomposition, with its lifting to covering groups explained immediately afterward.
- Theorem 49.1, printed p.265: simply connected solvable groups are diffeomorphic to the corresponding vector space.
- Corollary 49.6, printed p.266: a simply connected complex group has the homotopy type of the compact real form of its semisimple Levi factor.

The proof spells out the determinant identity, its consequence for every orbit, the closed discrete stabilizer, and the radical/top-degree contradiction; no completeness theorem for arbitrary post-Lie structures is imported.

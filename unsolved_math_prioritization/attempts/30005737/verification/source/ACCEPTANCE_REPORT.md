## Exact accepted conclusion

For finite-dimensional complex Lie algebras g and n of equal dimension, if g is unimodular, n is semisimple, and homomorphisms j1,j2: g -> n have invertible difference, then g is semisimple. Consequently no post-Lie structure has perfect nonsemisimple source and semisimple target. This covers arbitrary semisimple products and arbitrary source radical; it requires no closed-image or algebraicity assumption.

The argument is complete: determinant equivariance makes the integrated left-right action globally transitive; the closed discrete stabilizer gives a genuine covering; simply connectedness identifies the underlying manifolds; compact-real-form top homology and Levi decomposition force the radical to vanish. The post-Lie identities supply R and R+I, and perfectness supplies unimodularity.

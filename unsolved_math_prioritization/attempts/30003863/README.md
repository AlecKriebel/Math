# Partial extension theorems for del Pezzo complements

Problem 30003863 / OWR-16169-010. The full cylinder-free versus all-automorphism-extension equivalence remains unsolved here. The accompanying independent mathematical audit accepts the bounded partial results, with no mathematical correction required.

## Results and exact scope

- Cubic complement automorphisms have reduced degree 1 modulo 3 and the displayed exact homogeneous Jacobian power. A nonambient map has degree at least four and contracts only the cubic boundary.
- A degree-four map cannot contract a smooth cubic to a point; a curve center has transverse order one. Its reduced inverse degree belongs to {4,7,10,13,16}.
- Every smooth-cubic automorphism in a specified pointwise line-projection kernel is ambient, with the kernel explicitly classified.
- The natural pointwise double-cover projection kernels for the two Du Val weighted families consist of identity and the ambient deck involution. The square-coefficient argument is retained in full.
- Finite necessary-and-sufficient polynomial identities certify a proposed nonambient cubic-complement automorphism. The complete criterion, primitive normalization, plane/quadric/E6 constructions, and Fermat control are explicit mathematical formulas.

The fixed ambient list includes planes, normal quadrics and cubics in P^3, quartics in P(1,1,1,2), and sextics in P(1,1,2,3). Polynomial and anticanonical degrees are distinguished. Prior geometric results are credited, and no novelty or priority is claimed. The E6 construction has an explicit polar cylinder and does not refute the equivalence.

The exact missing step is arbitrary automorphisms: no proof establishes that they preserve any of these projections or any projection pencil. The finite inverse list is conditional on forward degree four; it is not a global degree bound or an infeasibility result.

# Credits and metadata recommendations

The draft may retain its narrow title and dimension-three theorem. Its possible contribution is the exact Morpurgo heat-maximum comparison, an explicitly specified finite smooth normalized weight, and an auditable quantitative separation. It should not claim that long cylindrical conformal geometry, inverse-radius blowup, Dirichlet trial modes, conformal covariance, min-max or Poisson summation are new.

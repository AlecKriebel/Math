\begin{abstract}
Morpurgo's volume-normalized weighted Yamabe heat trace comparison,
stated for every dimension at least three, is false in dimension three.
We specify one smooth positive conformal weight on the round three-sphere
whose scaled heat trace exceeds \(13/20\) at a specified positive time,
whereas the round maximum is less than \(321/500\).
The argument applies classical cylindrical conformal geometry and
Dirichlet min--max estimates. A finite cylinder length, explicit smooth
caps, and exact rational certificates give quantitative separation.
\end{abstract}

\section{The comparison and the finite result}
Let \(g_0\) be the unit round metric on \(\Sph^n\), with positive Laplacian,
and let
\[
 Y_0=\Delta_{g_0}+\frac{n(n-2)}4,\qquad
 Y_W=W^{-1/2}Y_0W^{-1/2}\quad\text{on }L^2(dV_{g_0}).
\]
For smooth \(W>0\) normalized by
\(\int_{\Sph^n}W^{n/2}\,dV_{g_0}=\operatorname{vol}(\Sph^n,g_0)\),
Conjecture 1 of Morpurgo's contribution to \cite[printed pp.~421--422,
item (16)]{OWR} asks whether the maximum of
\(t^{n/2}\Tr(e^{-tY_W})\) over \(t>0\) is at most the round maximum.

\begin{theorem}\label{thm:main}
There is a smooth strictly positive \(W\) on \(\Sph^3\), with
\(\int W^{3/2}\,dV_{g_0}=2\pi^2\), and an explicitly defined \(t_*>0\), such that
\[
 t_*^{3/2}\Tr(e^{-t_*Y_W})
 >\frac{1106714561}{1700054750}>\frac{13}{20},
 \qquad
 \max_{t>0}t^{3/2}\Tr(e^{-tY_0})<\frac{321}{500}.
\]
Both comparison maxima are attained at positive finite times.
\end{theorem}
This refutes the universal assertion over \(n\geq3\).
The comparison restricted to \(n\geq4\), and the separate monotonicity
conjecture in the following source paragraph, remain unaddressed.
The weaker original thresholds \(13/20\) and \(129/200\) also follow.

Long cylindrical conformal blowups are classical; Ammann and Jammes
used Pinocchio metrics for conformally covariant eigenvalue problems
\cite{AJ}, with earlier constructions cited there.
Their construction, a chosen fixed rescaled cap, ordinary Dirichlet
trial modes, and a strict round maximum bound already give a qualitative
counterexample. We record a specified finite quantitative application
to Morpurgo's comparison. The geometry and spectral mechanism are credited
as classical. Related transformed-trace inequalities occur in \cite{Mor};
their allowed functional classes do not directly state this time-maximum
comparison. The accompanying dated priority audit records inspected
versions and retrieval limits. Historical priority of the exact application,
worldwide firstness, and current openness are unestablished.

\section{A smooth capped cylinder in the round conformal class}
Identify the twice-punctured three-sphere with
\(\mathbb R\times\Sph^2\), using stereographic radius \(r=e^s\). Then
\[
 g_0=\operatorname{sech}^2(s)(ds^2+g_{\Sph^2}).
\]
Fix \(L=10000\). Define
\[
 \eta(x)=
 \begin{cases}0&x\leq0,\\ e^{-1/x}&x>0,\end{cases}
 \quad
 \chi(x)=\frac{\eta(x)}{\eta(x)+\eta(1-x)},\quad
 q(s)=|s|-L/2,\quad u(s)=\exp[-\chi(q(s))\log\cosh(q(s))].
\]
The denominator is everywhere positive. The smooth step \(\chi\)
is zero for \(x\leq0\), one for \(x\geq1\), and has flat endpoint jets.
Thus
\[
 g_L=u(s)^2(ds^2+g_{\Sph^2}),\qquad W_L=u(s)^2\cosh^2(s)
\]
is the exact unit-radius product cylinder on \(|s|\leq L/2\).
The apparent corner of \(|s|\) is harmless because \(u=1\) near zero.
Flatness gives smooth matching at both ends of each transition.
For \(|s|\geq L/2+1\), \(u(s)=\operatorname{sech}(|s|-L/2)\).

At the positive pole use \(\rho=e^{-s}\), and at the negative pole
use \(\rho=e^s\). In either smooth stereographic chart,
\[
 W_L=e^L\frac{(1+\rho^2)^2}{(1+e^L\rho^2)^2},
 \qquad
 g_L=\frac{4e^L}{(1+e^L\rho^2)^2}
       (d\rho^2+\rho^2g_{\Sph^2}).
\]
These coefficients are smooth positive functions of the squared
Cartesian radius, including \(\rho=0\). Hence \(g_L=W_Lg_0\) and \(W_L\)
extend smoothly to the standard compact sphere. The finite constant
\(e^{10000}\) requires no floating-point evaluation.

Writing \(V_L=\operatorname{vol}(g_L)\), the spherical area is \(4\pi\), so
\[
 4\pi L\leq V_L=4\pi\int_{\mathbb R}u(s)^3\,ds<4\pi(L+4).
\tag{1}\label{eq:volume}
\]
Indeed each cap has radial integral at most
\(1+\int_1^\infty8e^{-3q}\,dq=1+8/(3e^3)<2\).
Set
\[
 a=(2\pi^2/V_L)^{2/3},\qquad h=ag_L,\qquad W=aW_L,\qquad t_*=4a.
\tag{2}\label{eq:normalization}
\]
These are explicit finite expressions, with \(V_L\) the specified integral.
They give \(\int W^{3/2}\,dV_{g_0}=a^{3/2}V_L=2\pi^2\).

\section{The operator and the variational heat lower bound}
In dimension three the geometric Yamabe operator is
\(Y_g=\Delta_g+R_g/8\).
Conformal covariance for \(h=Wg_0\) and its volume measure give
\[
 Y_h=W^{-5/4}Y_0W^{1/4},\qquad dV_h=W^{3/2}dV_{g_0}.
\]
The unitary map \(Uf=W^{3/4}f\) from \(L^2(dV_h)\) to \(L^2(dV_{g_0})\)
therefore satisfies \(UY_hU^{-1}=Y_W\).
These identities extend from smooth functions to closed elliptic forms:
all multipliers and their inverses are smooth on the compact sphere.
Moreover
\[
 \langle Y_Wf,f\rangle
 =\int\left(|\nabla(W^{-1/2}f)|^2+\tfrac34|W^{-1/2}f|^2\right)dV_{g_0}
 \geq\frac{3}{4\max W}\|f\|^2.
\]
Thus the spectrum is strictly positive and every positive-time heat trace
is finite. No sign condition on the transition scalar curvature is needed.

On the exact cylinder \(R=2\), hence
\(Y_{g_L}=-\partial_s^2+\Delta_{\Sph^2}+1/4\).
Take the \(\Sph^2\)-constant Dirichlet functions
\(\phi_k(s)=\sin(\pi k(s+L/2)/L)\), extended by zero off the cylinder.
They lie in the global \(H^1\) form domain because their boundary traces
vanish. A jump in normal derivative contributes no delta to the weak
first derivative. Their form and norm matrices are diagonal; on the
first \(k\) modes the largest Rayleigh quotient is
\(\mu_k=1/4+(\pi k/L)^2\).
Min--max for the full closed-manifold spectrum, including multiplicities,
gives \(\lambda_k(g_L)\leq\mu_k\). Consequently
\[
 \Tr(e^{-4Y_{g_L}})\geq e^{-1}\sum_{k\geq1}e^{-4\pi^2k^2/L^2}
 \geq e^{-1}\left(\frac{L}{4\sqrt\pi}-1\right).
\tag{3}\label{eq:trace}
\]
For the last inequality, a decreasing Gaussian has its integer sum at
least its integral from one to infinity; the omitted integral on \([0,1]\)
is at most one. Finite sums and monotone limits justify the trace comparison.

Constant rescaling gives \(Y_h=a^{-1}Y_{g_L}\).
Using \eqref{eq:normalization}, including its time and prefactor, yields
\[
 t_*^{3/2}\Tr(e^{-t_*Y_W})
 =\frac{16\pi^2}{V_L}\Tr(e^{-4Y_{g_L}})
 >\frac{\sqrt\pi}{e}\frac{L-4\sqrt\pi}{L+4}.
\]
The numerator is positive. The elementary enclosures
\(1.772<\sqrt\pi<1.773\) and \(e<2.719\) give
\[
 t_*^{3/2}\Tr(e^{-t_*Y_W})
 >\frac{1772}{2719}\frac{10000000-7092}{10004000}
 =\frac{1106714561}{1700054750}>\frac{13}{20}.
\tag{4}\label{eq:lower}
\]
The exact margin in the last comparison is
\(3357947/3400109500>0\). This proves a finite-length estimate.

\section{A rational all-time round bound}
Round degree \(j-1\) harmonics have Laplace eigenvalue \(j^2-1\)
and multiplicity \(j^2\). Thus the round scaled trace is
\[
 F_0(t)=t^{3/2}\sum_{j\geq1}j^2e^{-t(j^2-1/4)}.
\]
Differentiating Gaussian Poisson summation, justified by locally uniform
Gaussian convergence for positive times, gives
\[
 F_0(t)=\frac{\sqrt\pi}{4}e^{t/4}
 \left[1+2\sum_{m\geq1}
       (1-2\pi^2m^2/t)e^{-\pi^2m^2/t}\right].
\]
For \(0<t\leq7/5\) all correction terms are negative, so
\(F_0(t)<(1.773/4)e^{7/20}\).
Write \(F_0=A+B\), where \(A(t)=t^{3/2}e^{-3t/4}\).
The function \(A\) increases up to two; every summand of \(B\)
decreases for \(t\geq2/5\).
For \(a\geq7/5\), the tail beginning at \(j=3\) has consecutive ratio
at most \((16/9)e^{-7a}<1/2\). Hence
\[
 B(a)\leq D(a):=a^{3/2}
 \left(4e^{-15a/4}+18e^{-35a/4}\right).
\]
For \(7/5\leq a\leq t\leq b\leq2\),
\(F_0(t)\leq A(b)+D(a)\).
The following exact-rational upper enclosures cover that range and small time.
\[
\begin{array}{c|c}
\text{time region}&\text{upper enclosure}\\ \hline
(0,7/5]&629002/10^6\\
{[7/5,3/2]}&631338/10^6\\
{[3/2,8/5]}&636143/10^6\\
{[8/5,17/10]}&639464/10^6\\
{[17/10,9/5]}&641172/10^6\\
{[9/5,19/10]}&641202/10^6\\
{[19/10,2]}&639541/10^6
\end{array}
\tag{5}\label{eq:table}
\]
Here is a complete arithmetic specification for these enclosures.
Let \(P_N(x)=\sum_{k=0}^Nx^k/k!\). For \(0\leq x<N+2\),
\[
 P_N(x)\leq e^x\leq
 P_N(x)+\frac{x^{N+1}}{(N+1)!}\frac{1}{1-x/(N+2)}.
\]
Use its degree-14 upper bound for \(e^{7/20}\) and \(e\).
Replace each negative exponential in \(A,D\) by \(1/P_{50}(x)\),
and each square root of an endpoint by
\((\lfloor10^8\sqrt{x}\rfloor+1)/10^8\), computed by integer bisection.
The six bounds are then rational expressions; exact comparison gives
\eqref{eq:table}. The supplied \texttt{check\_trace\_bounds.py}
records the endpoint formulas and their reduced fractional values.
For the constant enclosures it uses
\(\pi=8\arctan(1/3)+4\arctan(1/7)\), with 24 alternating terms and
the next-term remainder for each arctangent. Tangent addition and
first-quadrant signs prove the identity. Rational squaring verifies
the stated square-root brackets. No floating-point acceptance is used.

For \(t\geq2\) every summand \(t^{3/2}e^{-\lambda t}\) is nonincreasing,
since \(\lambda\geq3/4\). Summing pointwise inequalities gives
\(F_0(t)\leq F_0(2)\), already controlled by the last row.
Thus
\[
 \sup_{t>0}F_0(t)\leq641202/10^6<321/500<129/200.
\]
Together with \eqref{eq:lower}, this proves the comparison in
Theorem~\ref{thm:main}.

\section{Maxima, verification, and attribution}
For either fixed normalized smooth metric, the leading heat coefficient
gives the zero-time limit
\[
 (4\pi)^{-3/2}2\pi^2=\sqrt\pi/4.
\]
At infinity the strictly positive first eigenvalue gives
\[
 \Tr(e^{-tY})\leq e^{-(t-1)\lambda_1}\Tr(e^{-Y}),\qquad t\geq1.
\]
The scaled trace tends to zero.
Continuity for positive time and \eqref{eq:lower} imply attainment
of the weighted supremum inside a compact positive-time interval.
The first round mode at two is
\(2\sqrt2e^{-3/2}>\sqrt\pi/4\), so the same argument applies to the
round supremum. Its inequality follows, for example, by squaring
and using \(128>\pi e^3\) with the certified constant bounds.

The supporting package preserves the original finite proof and three
frozen evidence archives, and includes independent geometric,
spectral, and bounded-priority audits. The new coarse rational certificate
strengthens the original round bound; original certificates remain unchanged.
The portable checks establish arithmetic and artifact integrity.
Smooth extension, conformal covariance, form domains, min--max,
Poisson summation, and heat asymptotics are mathematical proof obligations
checked separately by manual adversarial AI review.
Standard imported results are used with the hypotheses above;
no formal proof-assistant certification is supplied.

The qualitative negative answer is a straightforward application of
classical cylindrical geometry and standard spectral estimates.
Our finite formulas and certificates make that application checkable for
the exact historical question. The source audit found no earlier explicitly
assembled exact refutation in its stated corpus and records its limits,
including unavailable final or older source versions. This bounded finding

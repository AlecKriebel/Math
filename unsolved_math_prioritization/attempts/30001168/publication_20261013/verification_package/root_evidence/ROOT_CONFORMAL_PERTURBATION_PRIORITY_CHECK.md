Dated 2026-10-14 UTC. This is one inspected competing-route check, not the completed priority decision. No new central proof search or source edit.

Shiliang Zhao, arXiv:2012.08757v2 (September27,2022; firstversion December16,2020), has abstract-page title *Conformal Perturbation of Heat Kernels with applications* and PDF title *Notes on conformal perturbation of heat kernels*. The complete eight-page primary manuscript was read; exact PDF hash is in ROOT_ZHAO_RETRIEVAL.json. Published DOI/details were not verified here.

Theorems1.1/1.4 pp2–3 give Gaussian and gradient bounds for the conformal Laplacian in the Laplace–Beltrami sense, under closeness of a metricfactor toone and geometric hypotheses; Corollary1.3 is a fractional variant. Their conclusions have unspecified constants. Introductionp2 explicitly distinguishes h^-1Delta from the conformal metric Laplacian in n>=3. None states a sharp round-normalized maximum comparison or its counterexample for Delta+R/8. The cap weight is far from a smallperturbation. Thus these inspected results do not immediately settle this target. This does not rule out a new argument using that machinery.

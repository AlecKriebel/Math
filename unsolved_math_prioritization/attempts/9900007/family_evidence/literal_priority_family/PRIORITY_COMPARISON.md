Lemma 2.1 (pp740–741) is a sufficient condition for weak convergence of
continuous-time one-time marginals: for each epsilon there may be a different
joint construction, finite offset |D|<=epsilon, finite T, and eventual equality
Z_t=Z′_(t+D) to a strictly stationary right-continuous process. Remark2.1
allows eventual state-metric discrepancy <=epsilon. Later sections develop
Harris recurrence, renewal, ordered Markov and queue mechanisms under their
specific assumptions. These statements do not claim that arbitrary weak
shift convergence implies one fixed synchronous coupling, nor identify the
submitted time-inhomogeneous binary construction. Their shared standard
coupling/conditional-expectation tools are not evidence of novelty of this
application.

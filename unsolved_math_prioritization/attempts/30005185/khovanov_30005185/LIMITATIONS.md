# Limitations and publication boundary

- Universal target remains unresolved; no mathematical novelty asserted.
- The even-exponent reduction uses characteristic different from two. It is not extended to F2 by analogy.
- Integer rank means free rank, not group order or a count of torsion generators.
- The stable-ribbon lemma, knot-theoretic normal-form inputs, Fox–Milnor factorization and known special cases are imported with attribution. Elementary consequences are proved in the authored text.
- Formal complexes are logical controls, not knot realizations.
- Cube computations certify ranks for explicit diagrams only. Knot naming, ribbonness, and local-move pictures are separately sourced geometric facts, not certified by the rank program.
- No 2.4-million-knot census replay, no new search over ribbon knots, and no large modulo-eight counterexample computation was performed.
- Literature review is bounded and dated. Failure to locate a later resolution is not proof that none exists.
- Exact-ID/title-keyword GitHub searches returned no exact target work. These searches are not exhaustive repository-history proofs.
- Source PDFs, extracted source text, raw web/tool responses, source census files, and private coordination material are excluded from the authored package. Only authored exposition, code, generated small-example certificates, public bibliographic/retrieval metadata and verification manifests belong in the freeze.
- Independent audit is pending and required before any publication. No remote write was made by the author.

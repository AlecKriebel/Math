# Exact source: rational versus integral cohomological dimension

ID6200004 / AMR-061-0004 is Mike Davis's Problem4 in Misha Kapovich's *Problems on Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups*. The author-hosted PDF is dated October24,2007 and records the AIM2005 workshop. Printed/PDF page3 was visually checked. It asks for a **torsion-free hyperbolic group G with cd_Q(G)/cd_Z(G)<2/3**. The source's existing examples have dimensions(2,3), attaining equality only. Hyperbolic means Gromov-hyperbolic, not necessarily a real-hyperbolic manifold group. The trivial group has no defined0/0 ratio and cannot be a witness.

Group cd_R is the projective dimension of the trivial RG-module R, equivalently the largest degree of group cohomology with some RG-module, not merely the largest ordinary Betti number H^k(G;R). Torsion-free hyperbolic groups admit finite-dimensional finite classifying spaces. The credited Bestvina–Mess formula relates cd_R(G) to the **relative Čech cohomological dimension** of the compact boundary by a shift of one. For finite-dimensional compacta, integral cohomological dimension equals covering dimension. This is not Hausdorff or conformal dimension, and it is not automatically the largest global cohomology degree of an arbitrary compactum. The boundary theorem supplies special additional structure.

Primary source checks and limitations:
- Bestvina–Mess, JAMS4(1991),469–481, DOI10.1090/S0894-0347-1991-1096169-1, is the original credited theorem. Its publisher download was unavailable in this environment. Exact formulations were checked in Kapovich and in Dranishnikov's primary proof extension, *On Bestvina-Mess formula*, arXivmath/0503018, including its PID coefficients and Z-structure definitions. We do not claim a full new audit of the original1991 paper.
- Arora–Martínez-Pedroza, *Subgroups of word hyperbolic groups in rational dimension2*, GGD15(2021),83–100, arXiv1811.09220v6, defines group cd precisely and reiterates the torsion-free(2,3) examples. Its subgroup theorem is not a ratio improvement.
- Dicks–Leary, *On subgroups of Coxeter groups*, arXiv0711.5011, treats finite and infinitely generated Coxeter systems, coefficient-dependent dimensions and rational Poincaré duality. Hyperbolicity and finite-type hypotheses cannot be inferred for every example in that paper.
- Dranishnikov, *Cohomological dimension of Markov compacta*, Topology Appl154(2007),1341–1358, arXivmath/0611028, constructs high-dimensional compacta with rational dimension1. Markov structure alone does not realize a compactum as a hyperbolic-group boundary; such a realization is a separate missing requirement.
- Lafont–Minemyer–Sorcar–Stover–Wells, MathAnn393(2025),3083–3107, arXiv2502.12906v2, constructs high-vcd hyperbolic Coxeter groups that virtually fiber. Its space notation cd(Z) in Section4 is explicitly **global ordinary cohomology degree**, not the general relative dimension. No rational/integral ratio improvement was stated in the checked results.
- Italiano–Migliorini–Ng, arXiv2606.05091, strengthens algebraic fibering to finitely presented kernels in high dimensions. The checked theorem does not supply the target coefficient ratio.
- Cashen–Dani–Schreve–Stark, published IMRN2026 rnag144, DOI10.1093/imrn/rnag144, develops large **conformal** dimension within fixed topological/vcd families. The bound author manuscript is dated October3,2025; the published July2026 introduction and main statements were also checked. Its orientable Pontryagin sphere should not be conflated with the coefficient-sensitive Pontryagin surfaces used for the(2,3) examples.

Current targeted literature searches found no verified full resolution. This is a bounded eligibility assessment, not proof that no later or unindexed result exists. Source gate uses zero proof turns. Raw sources, screenshots and imported records are not public artifacts.

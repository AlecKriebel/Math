1. Test the literal canonical divisor on a log pair. Take the projective plane with a smooth quartic boundary. The log canonical divisor is the hyperplane class, but every positive ordinary pluricanonical system is empty. Outcome: the literal first question has a counterexample; this does not answer its likely intended logarithmic version or the separate 5I question.
2. Test the sharp boundary-free lower bound by a weighted hypersurface. Use w²=z⁵+x¹⁰+y¹⁰ in P(1,1,2,5). Check smoothness, Cartier canonical class, factorization through the deck involution for degrees at most four, and separation in degree five. Outcome: an explicit smooth index-one example requires exponent five, and degree five embeds this example. No counterexample to 5I.
3. Search finite cyclic plane covers for a counterexample. Compute the eigenspace decomposition and recover affine generators from pluricanonical sections. Outcome: the exact embedding threshold is ceil(d/((n−1)d−3)) for the smooth degree-n cyclic cover with branch degree nd, and its maximum is four. This entire family is excluded as a source of a 5I counterexample.
4. Attack nonnormality through the conductor and infinitesimal separation. Combine restriction surjectivity with the degree-five conductor embedding, then test transverse jets at an ordinary double crossing. Outcome: every length-two scheme contained in the conductor plus boundary is already separated, but an explicit local linear system shows this alone does not control transverse jets. No globalization of the local obstruction to a stable counterexample is established.
5. Optimize the curve-restriction argument at degree five using integer arithmetic. For I≥2, every length-two scheme contracted by |3I(K+Δ)| is separated by |5I(K+Δ)|; any remaining failure meets the base locus of |2I(K+Δ)| and was already separated in degree three. In particular, basepoint-freeness in degree 2I implies very ampleness in degree 5I. For I=1 the same genus estimate leaves precisely numerical pairs (L·B,p_a(B))=(1,3),(2,5). Outcome: a rigorous conditional result and obstruction reduction; the universal question remains open in this attempt.

# Author verification checklist

This is author verification, not an independent referee report.

- Boundary convention: the P² example attacks ordinary K only; its logarithmic divisor is very ample.
- Dimension: all constructed objects and index estimates are surfaces. The local double crossing has Krull dimension two.
- Weighted model: cone gradient, ambient singular points, canonical weight, non-square double cover and degree-five chart/tangent separation are separately checked in the proof.
- Cyclic covers: smooth reduced branch and the precise monogenic algebra are hypotheses. The argument recovers an affine coordinate algebra, not merely a dimension count.
- Conductor: containment of ξ as a scheme is distinguished from having support on the conductor. The transverse dual-number quotient supplies a negative control.
- Index theorem: d=L·B is an integer because L is Cartier; the use of g as an integer in d=1 is explicit. No positivity of χ(O_X) is assumed.
- Restriction: H¹(2L)=0 uses exponent 2I≥2, and C∈|3L| is Cartier. No unjustified vanishing at exponent one is used.
- Product-of-sections step: the degree-two section must be a unit on ξ; the theorem accordingly excludes supports meeting its base locus.
- Gap: global generation of 2L is a condition, never a conclusion. The two index-one numerical exceptions are not asserted to exist geometrically.
- Claims: no new universal optimal exponent, no full solution of the log question, no positive-characteristic extension and no proof of absence of later literature are claimed.
- Automated checks: CHECK_RESULTS.json is the actual output of check_math.py. Four negative controls detect false altered claims. Finite scans supplement the symbolic proof and do not replace it.

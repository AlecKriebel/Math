# Source and literature gate

Checked 30 September 2026. Exact source locations and PDF hashes are in `sources.json`.

The complete [OWR contribution](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46019?nt=1), pp.2279–2282, was read. It first defines ordinary Λ-coalescents and independent per-branch infinite-alleles mutation rate r>0. It then distinguishes the already-known non-simultaneous result from the conjecture for simultaneous mergers. The latter, on p.2281, is the queued target. The original printed conjecture was visually inspected.

The original notation uses a compatible sequence of simplex measures. [Dong's primary follow-up](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.1606) gives the equivalent modern Ξ description, the precise rate per specified merger pattern, multiplicity counts, and freezing interpretation. Its full PDF was read at those inputs. The Bernoulli2006 source itself was blocked by a site interstitial; no full-text access is claimed. The inaccessible publisher version of Möhle2007 was used only for its published abstract. Its Λ-only theorem is already stated in the complete OWR and in Lagerås's retrieved Proposition4.

[Gnedin–Pitman](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307307), Definition1.1, Theorem5.2, equations25–26 and35, and Corollary7.3, fix what regeneration and a possible regenerative rearrangement mean. Their representation is a credited input. “Some regenerative ordering” must not be replaced by failure of one arbitrarily chosen chronological order.

[Lagerås2007](https://staff.math.su.se/andreas/ECP-2007-1844.pdf), Theorem1 and its following paragraph, explicitly distinguishes regenerative litters from allelic families formed by combining litters. This is not a counterexample to the target. Dong–Gnedin–Pitman's earlier paper concerns non-simultaneous coalescents; Dong's subsequent extension supplies the general recursion, not the claimed classification.

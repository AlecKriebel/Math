# Independent acceptance audit: Iwahori restriction

Audit date: 8 October 2026.

## Disposition

ACCEPTED AS CORRECTED PARTIAL ANALYSIS ONLY. The original bundled problem is
unresolved in this work. The negative answer to parameter independence is a
credited application of Prasad's published 1993 theorem. There is no accepted
general presentation of the restricted Iwahori Hecke module, no treatment of all
requested Steinberg products, and no novelty or worldwide literature-completeness
certificate.

This public edition retains the independent mathematical assessment below.
The current derivative adds scope and normalization clarifications, corrects
bibliographic metadata, and narrows a checker comment. It does not supply a
missing proof. CORRECTIONS.patch records contextual corrections; its deleted
lines are superseded own text. The full superseded report and original packet
are excluded, so historical patch application and original replay are NOT_RUN.
The computational section below describes only this publication edition.

## Claim-by-claim mathematical assessment

1. Object and hypotheses: accepted with the derivative's qualification. For the
embedding h -> diag(h,1), the relevant Hecke module is the I_H-fixed space of the
restricted smooth representation. The [T,1] Bernstein component corresponds to
that module under the Iwahori type equivalence; the two are not literally the
same vector space. An affine-Hecke restriction of the finite-dimensional
G-Iwahori-fixed space is a different construction. The convention that
unramified generic means irreducible, generic, and hyperspecial-spherical is
stated. The interpretation of Steinberg products is a convention, not a resolved
ambiguity in the question. The epilogue in OWR39/2021, printed p.2119, must not be
conflated with its preceding tempered/projectivity conjecture.

2. Failure of independence: accepted as an application of existing mathematics.
Prasad's Theorem 1 concerns Hom_GL2(pi,1), hence a trivial quotient, rather than
an invariant vector. Both normalized GL3 principal series 1 x 1 x 1 and
xi x xi x xi are irreducible, generic and spherical when xi is nontrivial
unramified unitary: all pairwise ratios are 1, not |.| or |.|^(-1). Their
Weil-Deligne monodromy is zero. The first parameter has a trivial summand with
generic complementary GL2 parameter 1+1; the second has none. Thus only the
first has a trivial GL2 quotient. The trivial target has unramified normalized
cuspidal support {|.|^(-1/2),|.|^(1/2)} and belongs to the [T,1] block.
Bernstein orthogonality and exact compact averaging transport the different
quotient behavior to nonisomorphic Iwahori Hecke modules. Neither equality of
GL3 central characters nor a nonquadratic xi is required. In particular,
xi(uniformizer)=-1 is valid. The center of the embedded GL2 is not the center
of GL3. The complementary generic GL2 representation is not the trivial target.
The proof relies on the published theorem and the standard GL principal-series
irreducibility criterion, not on a numerical checker.

3. Rank one and extensions: accepted. Chan's Theorems 3.7-3.8 cover the generic
GL2 cases: essentially square-integrable representations and irreducible products
of two GL1 characters. Their GL1 restriction is the Gelfand-Graev model, which
is compact induction from the trivial subgroup in rank one. Its unit-invariant
module is the regular Laurent algebra C[t,t^(-1)]. Thus rank one is an exception
to an unqualified nonprojectivity sentence. The polynomial exact sequence is
an elementary algebra example. At p=(t-2)^2 its cyclic quotient has the displayed
nonzero square-zero nilpotent action. This demonstrates that graded data do not
determine an extension; it does not identify an unproved general restriction
extension class.

4. Six flag orbits: accepted. The line classification into L, a line in U, and a
mixed line gives 1+3+2 cases over any field. In a mixed case, a plane not containing
L has intersection with U independent of the mixed line's U-component. The
remaining stabilizer normalizes that intersection to span(e2). Direct matrix
conjugation gives the three Borel stabilizers and, respectively, matrices
[[1,b],[0,d]], diag(1,d), and [[a,b],[0,1]] for the remaining cases. These give
orbit dimensions [1,1,1,2,3,2] and unique open case 5. The independent checker
uses the full affine equations, including the constant in diag(h,1). Complete
enumeration over F2, F3 and F5 separately verifies disjointness and coverage,
with 21, 52 and 186 total flags. Finite enumeration is supporting evidence;
the field-independent case distinction is the completeness argument. Mackey
layers alone do not give boundary gluing or a full Hecke presentation.

5. Ideal obstruction: accepted over C[u,v]. The kernels of the generating maps
R^2 -> (u,v) and R^2 -> (u^2,v) are generated by (-v,u) and (-v,u^2), since the
two displayed generators are relatively prime. At a maximal ideal away from
the origin one generator is a unit; at the origin both relation entries vanish
in the residue field. Hom to each residue field therefore has the stated equal
dimension. Their first Fitting ideals nevertheless differ. Localization at
1+u and 1+v preserves the difference: modulo (u^2,v), the class of u survives
while 1+u and 1+v are units. This is a general commutative-algebra counterexample
to reconstruction from simple-target Hom dimensions, not an example realized by
p-adic branching. It does not rule out a future reconstruction theorem using
additional structure special to the restricted modules. Chan's Theorem 4.1 is
a quotient-branching criterion; the audit does not claim that criterion alone
specifies the full module or that its entire long proof has been independently
reverified.

6. Conditional lattice lemma: accepted exactly under its written assumptions.
The Z-torsion in M is an H-submodule because Z is central and a domain. It meets
the torsion-free P trivially, so essentiality forces it to vanish. The assumed
annihilation f^N(M/P)=0 then gives f^N M subset P and the stated embedding after
localization. No actual central annihilator, uniform exponent, or required
H_n-stable lattice is established for the restriction problem. Polynomial
zero-factor tests do not establish those representation-theoretic hypotheses.

7. Steinberg and compact data: accepted as limitations. With unnormalized
induction, constants give 0 -> 1 -> Ind_B^GL2(1) -> St_2 -> 0. Rewriting the
middle term in normalized form puts it at a reducibility parameter. A model
confined to irreducible spherical series cannot be substituted into all such
resolutions without singular terms and differentials. Iwasawa decomposition
identifies the full K_G restrictions of the two unramified principal series in
item 2, since both their inducing characters and the modulus are trivial on
B intersect K_G. Their different Iwahori blocks therefore cannot be reconstructed
from compact types alone. The inspected Wang manuscripts address finite
hyperspecial data, have characteristic-zero hypotheses, and do not provide the
missing noncompact translation action in this work.

8. Final scope: accepted. The full module and its parameter-dependent extension
data, together with compatible Steinberg products, remain missing. This is a
statement about this deliverable, not a claim that nobody has solved the problem.

  spaces and explicitly gives the quotient-to-Hecke argument.

  pinned, but their bodies are excluded from this packet.

## Computational acceptance in this publication edition

The current derivative and independent checker are included byte for byte from
the audited derivative and independent check code. Their six baseline runs
cover normal, -O and -OO Python. The semantic runner exercises all four
derivative and all seven independent mutations in each mode, requiring the
complete expected rejection output and nonzero exit for each intended reason.
This gives 33 rejected semantic mutations. The derivative's error-only wrapper
turns uncaught RuntimeError into a stable full diagnostic without changing
mathematical code. The independent checker has its own error handler.

The independent checks include full affine stabilizers, finite-field coverage,
presentation syzygies, and the Laurent-localization witness. They are bounded
exact arithmetic, not proof-assistant verification of p-adic theorems. The
portable validator compares every fresh stdout and stderr byte against externally
bound mode-specific references, together with recursive exact JSON types and
values. There is no output normalization or selected-count-only acceptance.

The fixed bootstrap binds the verifier, control harness and full manifest,
including this audit, acceptance, status, all reference bytes and preparation
receipts. Final validation evidence is pinned outside the packet to avoid a
circular receipt. Every baseline is performed as actual UID=EUID=1000 on 0444
files in a 0555 directory, with failed append/create probes and before/after
whole-delivery hashes. Integrity, strict schema, direct comparator, semantic and
hostile-environment tests are exercised under all three modes. Test fixtures
are temporary copies; the delivered snapshot remains unchanged.

Original-report replay, historical patch application, original-archive replay,
source-body replay, dataset replay and formal proof-assistant verification are
NOT_RUN by this public validator. Public source inspection metadata records
prior scholarly inspection, not a claim that excluded bodies were reread by a
portable check. Hashes establish byte identity only.

# Source audit

## Exact target

- Danny Calegari, *Problems in foliations and laminations of 3-manifolds*, arXiv:math/0209081v1, 8 September 2002, version 0.78. [Full source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0209081). The complete PDF was read; Question 10.6 on printed p. 25 was visually checked. Definition 1.1 supplies the single-closed-transversal definition of tautness; §13.1 supplies the ordinary Godbillon–Vey convention. The question does not say minimal or transversely projective. Its following normal-triangulation remark is retained as source context, not an independently verified differential bound.
- Numeric ID 10300044 is the identity. The pinned source and imported report are preserved separately. The imported report is third-party context, not proof or evidence of a previous Alec attempt. Its asserted positive volume lower bound fails for the fiber foliation. Its asserted fixed-manifold unboundedness had no usable proof; the candidate instead supplies a source-qualified construction.

## Classical realization input

- Takashi Tsuboi, *On 2-cycles of B Diff(S¹) which are represented by foliated S¹-bundles over T²*, Ann. Inst. Fourier 31(2) (1981), 1–59. [Published full text](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.828.pdf). Full 60-page publisher PDF, including cover, obtained and converted to text; printed pp. 1, 3 and 6 visually inspected. Page 1 records Thurston's surjective GV map; p. 3 defines the compact-support group and gives Mather's isomorphism together with the actual compactification/suspension diagram; p. 6 identifies surface representatives of integral H₂. The bar on the Haefliger-space notation was checked in the rendered source. The candidate does not depend on the more ambitious genus-reduction statement later on p. 3.
- Takashi Tsuboi, *Several problems on groups of diffeomorphisms*, author-hosted Geometry and Foliations 2013 conference text, pp. 195–200. [Full six-page source](https://tsuboiweb.matrix.jp/faculty-html-gf2013/abstract_files/gf2013_195-200.pdf). Entire text obtained. Theorem 2.1 and the compact-support specialization are on p. 196; the GV discussion is on p. 197. This is identified as the conference text, not silently relabeled as the later proceedings version.
- The 1970s original Mather and Thurston papers underlying the 1981 diagram were not separately recovered in full. The required precise theorem and its geometric compatibility are stated in the full primary Tsuboi paper; the proof explicitly depends on those classical inputs.

## Ambient hyperbolic manifold

- William P. Thurston, *Hyperbolic structures on 3-manifolds, II*, arXiv:math/9801045v1. [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9801045). Complete 32-page author manuscript obtained. Theorem 0.1, Proposition 2.6 and §5 were checked. The manuscript describes its 1986/1998 provenance. Closed surface fiber and pseudo-Anosov mapping class give a closed hyperbolic mapping torus.
- Robert C. Penner, *A construction of pseudo-Anosov homeomorphisms*, Trans. Amer. Math. Soc. 310(1) (1988), 179–197, Theorem 3.1. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1988-0930079-9). The theorem's filling-multicurve/positive-negative twist statement was recovered in indexed primary-paper text. The publisher's full PDF request returned 403, so full local access to this historical paper is not claimed. Only the standard existence of a pseudo-Anosov mapping class, with a smooth representative fixing a disk, is needed. The disk-fixing property follows by choosing twist supports away from a complementary disk, or by isotopy.

## Other literature checked and exclusions

- Tsuboi's *Area functionals and Godbillon–Vey cocycles*, Ann. Inst. Fourier 42 (1992), 421–447: full primary PDF recovered. Its expanded cocycle-regularity theory is not needed for the smooth block argument. [Source](https://www.numdam.org/article/AIF_1992__42_1-2_421_0.pdf).
- Kitano–Mitsumatsu–Morita, *Real analytic lift of foliations of Thurston and Tsuboi*, arXiv:2606.01017v1 (31 May 2026), complete HTML primary text checked, especially the introduction and Theorem 2.1. [Source](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.01017v1). It corroborates the credited classical GV surjectivity but studies an analytic lifting problem; it is not used as a theorem about taut hyperbolic mapping tori.
- Hurder–Langevin's full earlier author manuscript on dynamics and GV was read, and the 2018 publication metadata/abstract checked. Its hyperbolic *resilient leaf* is a dynamical notion, not a statement that the ambient manifold is hyperbolic. No volume bound is extracted from it.
- The full 2007 Calegari author book was obtained and its GV-cocycle section checked. This supplies context rather than the needed relative-block realization.
- The Gabai paper cited in Calegari's following remark was not recovered in full: its PDF route returned an HTML challenge. A common normalizing triangulation has not been treated as a quantitative GV bound.

The current search did not establish priority of the fixed-hyperbolic-manifold assembly. This package credits its classical construction inputs and makes no novelty assertion. The original statement is answered in its printed, unrestricted taut-foliation scope; a modified minimal-foliation problem would need a separate analysis.

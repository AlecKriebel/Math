# Godbillon–Vey numbers and hyperbolic volume

The [candidate proof](CANDIDATE.md) constructs smooth cooriented taut foliations with Godbillon–Vey numbers `N a`, `a > 0`, on a single fixed closed hyperbolic mapping torus. A common embedded closed transversal meets every leaf. This gives a negative answer to the printed volume-bound question, including a nonlinear bound.

The compactly supported suspension input is classical Thurston–Mather theory, explicitly documented by Tsuboi in 1981. Hyperbolization is credited to Thurston. Priority of the assembled consequence is unestablished. **Candidate; two approaches; independent review pending.**

`python3 verify.py` prints the receipt for 2,660 exact algebraic controls. They check form signs, rescaling and finite stacking, not the classical realization and hyperbolization theorems. See [source notes](SOURCES.md) and [the research log](RESEARCH_LOG.md). No source PDFs are republished.

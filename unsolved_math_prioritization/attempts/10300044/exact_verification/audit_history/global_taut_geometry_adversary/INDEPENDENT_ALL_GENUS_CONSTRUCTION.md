# Independent pseudo-Anosov existence check for every required genus

This optional authored construction checks that the unspecified genus produced by the compact-support suspension cannot obstruct selection of a hyperbolic mapping torus. It proves exactly the same required genus statement and does not change the target or introduce a genus bound on the classical block.

1. Take the hyperbolic torus automorphism with matrix A=[[2,1],[1,1]]. Its eigenvalues are (3±√5)/2, so stable and unstable measured foliations are multiplied by reciprocal factors λ and λ⁻¹ with λ>1. It fixes the origin.

2. Construct a connected three-sheeted cover of the once-punctured torus by assigning its two free generators the permutations h=(12), v=(13). They generate a transitive group. The peripheral commutator [h,v] is a 3-cycle; hence the cover has one peripheral end, of degree three. Cap that end by one point. The resulting closed surface has Euler characteristic 3(−1)+1=−2, hence genus two. The map to the torus is branched of index three over the origin.

3. There are finitely many conjugacy classes of subgroups of index three in a finitely generated group: each gives a transitive permutation representation into S₃. A acts by a permutation of this finite set of covers. Some positive power A^k therefore lifts to this cover. The lift extends over the unique capped point. Pulling back the stable/unstable measured foliations gives two transverse measured foliations, each with six prongs at the branch point, whose measures the lift scales by λ^k and λ^−k. This is the defining pseudo-Anosov structure on a genus-two surface.

4. For any g≥2, take a connected cyclic unbranched cover of the genus-two surface of degree d=g−1, obtained from a primitive epimorphism of its first homology to Z/dZ (d=1 means the identity cover). The Euler characteristic is −2d, so the genus is g. Again, the finite set of degree-d covers is permuted by the genus-two pseudo-Anosov mapping class. Some power lifts. Its lifted measured foliations are still transverse, have only the allowed prong singularities, and scale with λ raised to a positive power. Thus Σ_g admits a pseudo-Anosov mapping class for every g≥2.

5. That orientation-preserving mapping class admits a smooth representative f. This follows, for example, by smoothing a homeomorphism of a surface, or by a smooth Dehn-twist representative. To check the disk-fixing modification independently, choose a small smoothly embedded closed disk D. The oriented parametrized disk embeddings f|D and the inclusion D↪Σ_g are smoothly isotopic: shrink the disks in coordinate charts, move the center along a path, interpolate oriented frames within GL⁺(2,R), and expand. Extend the velocity field of this compact isotopy of disks to a time-dependent vector field on the surface using a cutoff, and integrate it. The resulting ambient isotopy H_t has H₁∘f|D=id. Consequently φ=H₁∘f is smooth, fixes D pointwise, and represents the same pseudo-Anosov mapping class.

The only standard local tool in step 5 is smooth isotopy extension for a disk, whose mechanism is the indicated extension-and-flow construction. No foliation flexibility or volume assertion enters this construction. The hyperbolization theorem still supplies the hyperbolic metric for the resulting mapping torus, exactly as in the original candidate.

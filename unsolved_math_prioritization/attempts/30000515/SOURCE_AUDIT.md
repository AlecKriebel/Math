# Primary-source audit

Historically checked 10 October 2026 UTC during the independent audit. The inspection and current-record statements below describe that audit, not new source inspection during edition preparation. This is an authored statement-level review; copied source text and images are excluded. PDF byte counts and hashes appear in SOURCE_METADATA.json.

- Miles Reid, “Attempt to construct the general simply connected Godeaux surface,” Oberwolfach Report 27/2006, printed pp.1645-1647: the whole contribution was read from the PDF, and the statement on PDF p.31 was inspected visually. The every-member quantifier, four base points, torsion implication and separate simple-connectivity conjecture match the submitted target. The construction discussion does not prove the conjecture. [Public report](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2006/27)

- Catanese-Pignatelli, “On simply connected Godeaux surfaces,” author PDF pp.1-3 and 9-10: the introduction and Lemma 1.10 were inspected, with the latter's formulas checked visually. Its canonical-model notation and standing torsion restriction matter. Its intersection/Hodge-index mechanism supports the source credit, while the audit reconstructs the smooth-surface argument without that restriction. The correct cross coefficient is 2. The title is not a theorem asserting that every torsion-free Godeaux surface is simply connected. [Author PDF](https://pignatelli.maths.unitn.it/papers/godeauxdg.pdf)

- Schreyer-Stenger, arXiv:2009.05357v2: introduction, preliminary torsion statements, Remark 1.8, Lemma 1.9, Theorem 5.1, Corollary 5.2, Proposition 5.6 and summary were inspected directly in the PDF. The construction gives a locally complete eight-dimensional simply connected family via Barlow deformation; its summary retains the special-line issue. Lemma 1.9 also excludes even torsion under the marked simple-base-point condition. The current arXiv record still lists v2. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.05357v2)

- Schreyer-Stenger, arXiv:2201.12065v1: introduction, Lemma 2.1 with proof, Theorem 2.2 with proof, the opening of Section 4, and the summary were inspected. Lemma 2.1 was also rendered and visually checked. It concerns a bicanonical divisor on the canonical model and its pullback, so it is not silently substituted for the smooth-surface claim. Its displayed cross-term typo is explained in AUDIT.md. Section 4 identifies the experiments as finite-field computations, and the summary leaves further special lines unresolved. The current record lists only v1. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2201.12065v1)

- Mendes Lopes-Pardini, arXiv:math/0512483v3: the introduction's opening Noether inequality was read from fresh PDF extraction and visually checked. It applies to every smooth minimal surface of general type, so no q(Y)=0 assumption is needed. The article's stronger main theorems are not necessary for the submitted proof. The current record lists v3, dated 16 March 2007. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0512483v3)

- Franciosi-Rollenske, arXiv:1611.06810v1: the introduction and Theorem A were inspected. The introduction explicitly distinguishes finite algebraic fundamental group from topological fundamental group and recalls the cyclic order-at-most-five result. The article's main theorem concerns stable surfaces with large torsion. Its historical status statement is not a current global-openness certificate. [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.06810v1)

- Dias-Rito, arXiv:2009.12645v3: abstract, introduction and beginning of Section 2 were inspected. Its topological conclusion is for the nonzero Z/2 torsion class and therefore cannot be a counterexample under every-member 2-connectedness. The current record verifies the final version dated 28 April 2026. The audit does not revalidate its computational classification. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.12645v3)

- Rito, with appendix by Gleissner and Ruhland, arXiv:2509.08198v2: abstract and introduction were inspected. The degree-eight abelian cover lies over a singular canonical model. It is not an unramified cover of the smooth numerical Godeaux surface, so it cannot contradict the degree-six bound. The stated family has Z/2 torsion. No ancillary computation was reproduced in this audit. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.08198v2)

- Chen-Shin, arXiv:2608.27317v1: introduction, main statements and Section 2 were inspected. Section 2 explicitly gives Pic~H^2(Z), Num=Pic/Tors and the unimodular pairing in the p_g=q=0 setting. The main bound concerns disjoint (-2)-curves, not the target universal simple-connectivity implication. [Versioned source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27317v1)

The source record therefore supports the submitted proof's dependencies, credit and careful scope. This is a bounded audit of identified sources, not a new literature search for a solution and not an exhaustive status certificate.

## Exact conventions

The input is a finite simplicial complex with a simplex-wise filtration and mod-two persistence pairing. Face notation means codimension one. The original function must strictly decrease on the modified Hasse graph, including upward matched arrows. The filtration uses the subcomplex generated by each sublevel set, rather than treating the function as face-monotone. A tie refinement is allowed. The target is equality of labeled simplex pairs, not equality of barcode endpoint values. GRAPH_CASE.md addresses all these conventions in dimension at most one.

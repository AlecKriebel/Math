# Explicit order scope for rooted equality

The universal-core theorem already assumes n>=5 and is unchanged mathematically.

The rooted lower-bound inequalities remain stated without an order restriction. Their original proof is retained. In particular, applying the theorem after deleting a root leaf remains legitimate when that deletion leaves four vertices.

The public rooted equality statement explicitly uses n>=5. Without an order restriction, K1 is an additional vacuous equality case of 8e>=11(n-1). The root-leaf corollary explicitly uses n>=5 for its equality classification and identifies K2 as the additional vacuous unrestricted equality case: deleting its leaf leaves K1.

For n>=5, equality in the rooted base bound consists exactly of the P4-hub construction with one leaf at each path vertex and at least one block. Equality in the unstrengthened root-leaf corollary consists exactly of that construction with one additional leaf at the hub. These assertions do not classify equality in the strengthened bounds retaining the positive w term.

This is an explicit clarification requested by the mathematical audit, not a new proof, a hidden change to a hypothesis, or a new solution claim. The complete original equality arguments, auxiliary lemma and construction proof are retained. The general ssat(n,C5)=11n/8+O(1) target remains unresolved by this work.

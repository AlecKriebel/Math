# Source and dependency audit

Checked 2026-10-08. This audit distinguishes primary-source identification, inspected theorem scope, and unproved external dependencies. No copied source document is bundled.

## Exact question and intended core

The freshly retrieved April 2026 K3 preliminary PDF is 6,578,041 bytes with SHA-256 ae56518166fe38aaaf555c58614329228afe734e743b111badec4060877fa12f. These bytes match the separately available local copy. Problem 5.4 appears on printed p. 304. Its short question leaves group topology and compactness unstated; its remarks identify the two-dimensional answer and say higher dimensions are unknown. It distinguishes the known local-contractibility result from the ANR question.

The primary Edwards–Kirby paper resolves the compactness ambiguity: Corollary 1.1, printed p. 63, states local contractibility for compact M. Section 6, printed p. 77, explicitly explains why this fails for general noncompact manifolds in compact-open topology and gives the escaping torus/twist example. Corollary 6.1 separately covers interiors of compact manifolds. Section 2 supplies compact-open topology; Corollary 7.3 states relative locally-flat-pair variants. These are local-contractibility statements, not ANR proofs.

The independent exposition by Ferry, Question 13.10 on printed p. 90, explicitly poses the compact topological manifold question and records reduction to boundary-fixed disk groups. It is an older source and is used for formulation and the historical reduction, never as evidence of current status. The exact continuous-fragmentation theorem is not independently reproved in this packet: the authored fragmentation lemma states its required local-section hypothesis conditionally.

## Low-dimensional and infinite-dimensional results

The Luke–Mason original AMS PDF request returned HTTP 403. Its full proof was not inspected. The correct bibliographic identifiers are doi:10.2307/1995974 and doi:10.1090/S0002-9947-1972-0301693-7. K3 and Yagasaki's primary paper, Fact 2.1, independently state the compact-surface theorem. Mason's relative two-cell theorem is likewise recorded by K3, Dijkstra and Yagasaki; no claim of reading its original proof is made.

Yagasaki, arXiv:math/0010223v1, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.1, has an identity-component hypothesis. Its extension/ANR proof in §4 does not make the full homeomorphism group of every noncompact surface an ANR. This prevents a false contradiction with the escaping-twist example.

Ferry's 1977 publisher abstract states the ANR theorem for a compact Hilbert-cube manifold. The packet uses it as an external theorem, not as a theorem about finite-dimensional M. In Route D, I^n×Q is literally a Hilbert cube by reindexing coordinates. The separate neighborhood-retraction/descent step is the unproved requirement; raw slice projection is disproved by the authored mixed-coordinate rotation.

## Retract-theory dependencies

Hanner's 1951 primary paper, Theorems 3.2–3.3 on printed pp. 392–394, was retrieved and inspected through the local-to-global statement and proof. Its stated category is separable metrizable spaces. Both the corrected report and independent fragmentation note use separable metrizable target groups. The conditional local-section proof itself extends maps by finitely many component extensions and requires continuous subgroup inclusions.

ANR/ANE equivalence, contractible ANR implying AR, and the convex extension theorem are standard foundational dependencies. The one-dimensional disk proof also gives a direct partition-of-unity extension, so it does not infer AR merely from convex-looking notation. The finite-dimensional-parameter corollary uses the standard bounded-order refinement characterization of covering dimension; the proved core lemma states the required refinement hypothesis explicitly.

Cauty's 1994 non-AR metric-linear-space example is used as an external counterexample to a generic local-contractibility-to-ANR implication. Its bibliographic abstract was checked, and the distinction is explicitly discussed in the inspected introductory material of Cauty's 2005 primary paper. Algebraic ANR is not equated with topological ANR. The original counterexample is not reconstructed here.

Haver's sufficient finite-dimensional-compact-exhaustion criterion motivates Route E, but its original full proof was not obtained. The packet directly disproves the exhaustion hypothesis by an embedded Hilbert cube and the Baire theorem. No positive ANR conclusion depends on Haver's theorem.

## Status and originality limits

Targeted searches for higher-dimensional homeomorphism-group ANR results, including 2025 and 2026 queries, found no verified later general resolution. That is a bounded search outcome, not an exhaustive proof of absence. Search crawl dates were not used as publication dates. The current dated primary status source is the April 2026 K3 version.

The local derivations are fully authored, but no novelty claim is made. In particular, the escaping-twist phenomenon is explicitly attributed to Edwards–Kirby. The interpolation counterexample only defeats one canonical method; its validity in spatial dimension two, where the desired ANR conclusion is already known, makes that limitation concrete.

# Type A W-algebra isomorphism: exact prior-result audit

Problem 30004021 / OWR-16635-009 has an affirmative prior resolution:

\[
\mathcal W_{-14/3}(\mathfrak{sl}_7,f_{(3,2,2)})
\cong L_{-4/3}(\mathfrak{sl}_2)
\cong\mathcal W_{-8/3}(\mathfrak{sl}_4,f_{(2,2)}).
\]

These are simple graded W-algebra quotients over the complex numbers, with the standard Dynkin conformal structure and central charge −6. The universal W-algebras are different.

The written proof route is Arakawa, van Ekeren and Moreau, *Singularities of nilpotent Slodowy slices and collapsing levels of W-algebras*, Theorem 8.8(2), [Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 12 (2024), e95](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2024.81); the proof reconstruction uses the [authenticated arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13462v3). AVM explicitly credit Allegra for the seven-dimensional example. His thesis proof was unavailable for inspection and is not a dependency of this reconstruction.

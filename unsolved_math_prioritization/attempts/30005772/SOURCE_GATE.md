The questions concern fixed-point-colored permutations, extended polynomially or by the shifted-exponential moment. They ask for an interpretation at arbitrary negative integers, and separately for every integer at even sizes. The report already gives the signed fixed-point difference and explicitly displays negative odd-size values. Those facts are prior context, not a negative resolution. Its proposed route is an almost-bijection leaving unmatched objects.

## Claim boundary

The current packet is **partial**, after five mathematical approaches. Its global ordered-prefix model gives a literal finite-set interpretation for every even-size integer evaluation; its genericity and dependence on a global order are prominent. The packet does not claim a natural local characterization for arbitrary negative colors or a structural solution at every odd size. The local `k=-1` and eventual all-negative-`k` models have exactly the stated domains.

Only original exposition, reproducibility code, source links, and audit records belong in the public packet. Source PDFs, full extracted text, source-page images, corpus records, and private raw history remain excluded.

## Accepted theorem

For dimension d >= 3, the first half-space minimizing homogeneity above 2 is 5/2 when N >= 3 (or the number of components may vary). No degree lies in (2,5/2). Every equality case is a common positive multiple of t times the planar three-sector Y profile in tangential variables, up to tangential rotation and component relabeling. Conversely tY is a full-trace half-ball energy minimizer. The exclusion holds for every finite N; N=2 cannot attain the equality case.

Consequently the boundary frequencies of a global spectral sum optimizer are 2 or at least 5/2 wherever the cited boundary blow-up theorem applies. Its precise quantitative boundary assumptions, including C^(1,alpha) with alpha>0, are retained. An arbitrary C^1 boundary is not substituted for them. The first higher degree is 5/2 and its additive separation above 2 is 1/2; the original report's conflicting delta notation is explained in the manuscripts and audits.

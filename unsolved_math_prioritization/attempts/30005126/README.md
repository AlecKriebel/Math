## Reading the result correctly

The signed path anti-involution sends each arrow α to −σ(α), reversing products. Its preservation of the relation ideal is an explicit hypothesis of the duality-compatible formulation; unsigned σ-stability is not silently substituted for it. The source-literal arrowwise orbit question is recorded separately. The form is fixed, and the acting isometry group is the full group, including full orthogonal rather than special orthogonal factors.

Boos and Cerulli Irelli's published 2025 Theorem 7.1 applies to symmetric type-A quivers with complex path algebra. The two orbit-closure orders also agree on closed invariant relation loci inside that ambient setting, by the restriction argument in the audit. This does not enlarge the ambient-quiver hypothesis to arbitrary representation-directed algebras.

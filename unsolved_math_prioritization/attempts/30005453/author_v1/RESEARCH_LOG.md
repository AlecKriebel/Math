## Approach outcomes (four families; stopped at a complete candidate)

1. Finite-volume concavity alone: insufficient. It does not establish infinite
   uniqueness or stochastic convergence without a limiting argument.
2. Direct edge-ratio squeezing: the natural ratio estimate carries the
   familiar factor 2 alpha and does not close the full range. No full claim
   is based on this route.
3. Vertex-variable maximum principles: closes positive-equilibrium uniqueness
   for all subcritical exponents, and rules out nonconstant bounded positive

   scaled martingale error, strictly positive edge growth, and the complete
   limiting trajectories needed to apply family 3. Together these yield the
   full candidate theorem under its stated hypotheses.

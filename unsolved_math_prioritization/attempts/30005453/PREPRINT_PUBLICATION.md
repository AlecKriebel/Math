# Accepted preprint for problem 30005453

[A vertex maximum principle for subcritical reinforcement](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23408971) by Alec Kriebel, ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X, version 1.0. The literal nonnegative-equilibrium uniqueness assertion is false. The accepted positive-equilibrium theorem is an explicitly corrected formulation: unique strictly positive equilibrium and simultaneous almost-sure coordinatewise homogenization for countable undirected simple bounded-degree graphs, positive bounded-above rates with infimum possibly zero,0<=alpha<1,and unit or finite deterministic positive integer initial counts. It is presented as a credited alternative proof of the positive homogenization result, not as a proof of unrestricted nonnegative uniqueness or a new theorem.

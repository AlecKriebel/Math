## Source formulation

Bruce Reznick, “The Odd Powers of the Motzkin Polynomial, etc.”, Oberwolfach Report14/2023, printed pp.778–781 inclusive, defines real homogeneous form spaces F_(n,m), their nonnegative cone P_(n,m), and their real SOS cone Sigma_(n,m). The contribution's operative question is printed p.779/PDF39; its bibliography continues through p.781/PDF41 before the next contribution.

For positive n, positive even m and a fixed odd exponent q=2k+1, the set is

`C_(n,m,q) = {f in P_(n,m): f^q in Sigma_(n,qm)}`.

The same exponent is fixed before testing addition. This is real homogeneous polynomial SOS, and zero is included. The question allows arbitrary finite n; it does not impose a ternary restriction. Closedness follows from continuity of the power map into the closed SOS cone; nonnegative scaling is immediate. Convexity is equivalent to closure under addition. The q=1 and classical Hilbert equality cases remain convex.

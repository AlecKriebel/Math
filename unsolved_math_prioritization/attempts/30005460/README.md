# 30005460: convexity at a fixed odd SOS power

Original target active. Source gate completed on 1 October 2026; one substantive author turn is complete (1/5). No result or publication promotion has been made.

For positive n, positive even m and a fixed odd q=2k+1, the target is whether

`C_(n,m,q) = { f in R[x_1,...,x_n]_m : f>=0 and f^q is a sum of squares }`

is a closed convex cone in the finite-dimensional real coefficient space. Closedness and nonnegative scaling are established. Fixed-exponent addition is the remaining question.

The April 2026 published Blekherman–Kozhasov–Reznick paper proves convexity of the union over odd exponents. Its Section 6 explicitly still asks about each fixed exponent. Those two conclusions will remain separate throughout the attempt. Classical and published partial results will be credited, with no historical novelty assumption.

Turn 1 proves convexity on every fixed single-circuit support and a sharp exponent barrier for a limited formal SOS-certificate template. See [TURN_1.md](TURN_1.md) and [turn1_checks.json](turn1_checks.json). Neither result resolves the original cone.

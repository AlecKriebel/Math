# Sources and credit

Primary files and hashes are in `source_manifest.json`; complete PDFs and text are local reading inputs, excluded from the portable packet.

- Bruce Reznick, “The Odd Powers of the Motzkin Polynomial, etc.,” Oberwolfach Report14/2023, printed pp778–780. [Official full report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47007), DOI10.4171/owr/2023/14. The exact fixed-exponent question is printed p779/PDF39 and the modified Motzkin cube range is immediately before it. The variables n,m are parameters of the form spaces; no ternary restriction is imposed on the convexity question.
- G. Blekherman, K. Kozhasov and B. Reznick, “On odd powers of nonnegative polynomials that are not sums of squares,” Forum of Mathematics, Sigma14(2026), e65, published online27April2026. [Publisher article](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2026.10221). Theorems5.1–5.3 prove SOS absorption and convexity of the union over odd powers. Section6 still asks fixed-exponent convexity. The exact cube identity used here is printed p24, after Theorem6.3. The author's first version is [2407.21779v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.21779v1).

The finite-dimensional separation theorem, Gaussian Gram compactness argument, positivity of Kronecker products and Schur complements are classical. The full arguments needed here are supplied in `PROOF.md`; their use is not a claim to have invented these tools. The seed and its cube identity are explicitly prior work.

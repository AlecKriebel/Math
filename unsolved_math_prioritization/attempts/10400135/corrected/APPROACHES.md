# Three substantive approaches

## 1. Exact source and current-literature comparison

Recovered the mathematical input from the publisher PDF, including Problem 7.19 and the paragraph defining the three ordinary-cohomology specializations. Inspected original state-sum definitions rather than completing the truncated database title by inference.

The source asks about a compact closed oriented W, a fixed nonempty L, flat B-bundles, and odd N>1. Its K_N is the Nth power of the original Borel state sum. Later PSL(2,C) invariants use different symmetrization/phase conventions; reduced/cusped objects have extra inputs. These distinctions prevent importing a fibered-face theorem for taut structures as an answer.

The 2017 non-ambiguous-structures paper has a face-constancy theorem after a canonical drilling construction, and separately asks a more general taut-structure question. Neither concerns the original additive B-bundle function. The 2026 figure-eight preprint concerns a large-N absolute-value limit for cusped QHI with logarithmic boundary parameters. It does not evaluate the original fixed-N additive function.

Outcome: no verified prior solution of the selected question found, and no claim that a literature search proves it open.

## 2. Gauge-orbit contraction with a full limiting cocycle

Projective scaling alone only proves constancy on rays; a degree-zero function on a punctured vector space could vary across directions. A limit at the zero edge cocycle would be invalid because that cocycle is not full.

The decisive modification is to add one fixed full coboundary. The family b+s a tends to a full trivial-bundle cocycle while representing s a. Nonzero scalar multiples of a are conjugate by diagonal matrices in B. Inspection of the local tensor expressions shows that fullness excludes every potentially vanishing local denominator and permits local holomorphic root choices. Gauge invariance and continuity then identify the common punctured-family value with its value at s=0.

Outcome: a general proof for all complex additive classes; no examples or numerical extrapolations needed. The entire real-additive question, including all Thurston faces, follows immediately.

## 3. Diagonal reduction and the remaining ordinary-cohomology scope

Apply the same contraction to a general upper-triangular cocycle. A fixed vertex gauge makes its diagonal limit full: the required vertex values avoid finitely many proper linear hyperplanes. The result is equality between the invariant of the original bundle and its diagonal reduction.

On the multiplicative character space, local cocycle charts and this full-gauge construction give local holomorphic dependence. Exponentiated classes and finite-coefficient classes are evaluations of this multiplicative function. This does not identify values at different characters or the global fibers for arbitrary W,L.

Outcome: further structural progress, but no full resolution of the broad first sentence of Problem 7.20. Overall disposition remains partial. No unsupported counterexample, novelty claim, or reinterpretation of a boundary invariant is used to close that gap.

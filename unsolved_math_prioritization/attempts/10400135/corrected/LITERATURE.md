# Sources and current-literature limits

Inspection date: 6 October 2026 (UTC).

## Foundational sources

1. T. Ohtsuki, editor, *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002), 377–572; published 1 June 2004. https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf . Section 7.4, printed pp.485–486; Problem 7.20 is on PDF page 114, one-based. The original statement, cohomology maps, and adjacent question were visually inspected. The requested https://unsolvedmath.com/problems/10400135 could not be retrieved by the web tool, so it did not supply the mathematical definitions.

2. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *Quantum Hyperbolic State Sum Invariants of 3-Manifolds*, arXiv:math/0101234v2 (28 February 2001). https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0101234v2 . Sections 3.2, 4.1–4.3 and 8 give the full-cocycle model, original normalization, invariant theorem, projective property, and local tensor factors. The matrix formulas on PDF page 30 were visually inspected. This is the exact original normalization used in PROOF.md.

3. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *QHI Theory, I: 3-Manifolds Scissors Congruence Classes and Quantum Hyperbolic Invariants*, arXiv:math/0201240v1 (2002). https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0201240 . Section 6 includes another statement of projective invariance. No extra theorem from this version is needed in the proof.

4. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *QHI, 3-manifolds scissors congruence classes and the volume conjecture*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002), 13–28. https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-002s.pdf . This is reference [46] of Ohtsuki's discussion. Its idealization equation (5) also records the edge-product identity used in the local denominator check.

5. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *Quantum Hyperbolic Invariants Of 3-Manifolds With PSL(2,C)-Characters*, Topology 43 (2004), 1373–1423, preprint https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0306280 . Remark 4.31 distinguishes the older Borel symmetrization from the general PSL(2,C) construction. This prevents replacing the invariant in the question by another one without proof.

## Later related work inspected

6. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *Analytic families of quantum hyperbolic invariants*, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 15 (2015), 1983–2063. https://imag.umontpellier.fr/~baseilhac/agt-v15-n4-p05-s.pdf . This treats QHI on parameter spaces refining character varieties, including weighted cusped settings. It supplies context, not the missing original-normalization comparison.

7. S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti, *Non ambiguous structures on 3-manifolds and quantum symmetry defects*, Quantum Topology 8 (2017), 749–846. https://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.01174 . Theorems 1.1–1.2 and Questions 1.3 concern taut structures and fibered faces, including constancy after drilling a suitable link. Those are not the additive flat-bundle inputs in Problem 7.20. The paper's reduced invariants and symmetry defects therefore do not directly prove or refute the selected question.

8. S. Baseilhac and F. Ben Aribi, *Volume Conjecture and quantum hyperbolic invariants: the figure eight knot complement*, arXiv:2604.16077v1, submitted 17 April 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16077 . Its Theorem 1.1 concerns a semiclassical limit and a parity condition on logarithmic boundary parameters. The current arXiv listing identifies it as a preprint; no peer-review claim is made. It is not a fixed-N additive-face result. The abstract, introduction and relevant parameter discussion were inspected; no claim of a complete audit of its proof is made.

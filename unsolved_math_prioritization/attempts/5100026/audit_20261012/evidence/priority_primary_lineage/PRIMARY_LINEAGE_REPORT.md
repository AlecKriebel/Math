## Exact comparison target and authenticated inputs

The target concerns an original ellipse with semiaxes a>b>0, a strictly nested nondegenerate confocal **elliptical** caustic, and a billiard orbit of **least** even period N≥4. It includes every real phase and each coprime star turning. Let R_i be intersections of consecutive tangents to the original ellipse. For M equal to either original focus, form the full lines through R_i perpendicular to R_i−M and their consecutive intersections U_i. The target is phase independence of N⁻¹∑U_i, location on the focal axis, C(−M)=−C(M), and C(M)=0 for N=4. Finite real construction is part of the audited theorem. Merely listing an odd primitive orbit an even number of times is excluded; the broader padded interpretation is false.

The primary table literally says **even**. It does not explicitly say least or primitive; neither does that row explicitly state the axial, sign, and N=4-zero refinements. Those qualifications must be attached to the submitted theorem, not silently attributed to the source.

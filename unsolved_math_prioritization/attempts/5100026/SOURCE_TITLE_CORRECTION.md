# Bibliographic title correction, with freeze provenance

The arXiv2004.12497v11 source is **Eighty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard**: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.12497v11 . The final companion is **Fifty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard**: https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0174.pdf .

SOURCES.md item1 in the frozen author packet inadvertently used the final companion's title for the arXiv edition. The independent reviewer identified this and the author acknowledged it before publication. Both URLs, the Table5/k407 mapping and the mathematical definitions were already correct. The mathematical proof is unchanged. The original source file is retained to preserve the exact reviewed freeze; this correction and the independent review give the correct edition titles.

# Classical sieve credit and updated source inspection

12 October 2026. This supplement leaves the frozen mathematical proof and rational constant certificate unchanged.

The finite criterion in Theorem 1 is a specialization of the classical Brun pure sieve: odd Bonferroni truncation, an elementary-symmetric next-term bound, and CRT product-cardinality endpoint errors. It is not a new general sieve principle. Kevin Ford's *Sieve Methods Lecture Notes, Spring 2023*, Theorem 1.5 and Lemma 1.6, give the odd/even truncation and its next-term control: https://ford126.web.illinois.edu/sieve2023.pdf . Terence Tao's *254A, Notes 4: Some sieve theory* (2015), equation (5), Exercise 9 and equation (13), record the multiple-residue CRT remainder and odd lower-sieve mechanism: https://terrytao.wordpress.com/2015/01/21/254a-notes-4-some-sieve-theory/ . Both primary expositions were retrieved for this comparison.

The proof's problem-specific work is its selective forced-prime choice, exact treatment of every leading digit, and control of the growing endpoint-error sum on the square-root scale. Neither that description nor these checks establishes priority for the resulting bound.

The earlier direct publisher-PDF retrieval did not yield readable text. A subsequent retrieval of the official ANTS-hosted Sorenson–Sorenson–Webster manuscript succeeded: https://www.math.auckland.ac.nz/~sgal018/ANTS/papers/Sorenson-Sorenson-Webster.pdf . Its introduction, Sections 5–6 and the separately available 2021 addendum at https://arxiv.org/html/1907.08559v3 distinguish unconditional density estimates from heuristic assertions about the least admissible integer. This resolves the earlier source-access gap for that comparison; it does not certify a comprehensive literature search or novelty.

## Prior result that settles the target

Tom Meyerovitch, *Pseudo-orbit tracing and algebraic actions of countable amenable groups*, Ergodic Theory and Dynamical Systems 39(9), 2570–2591 (2019), [DOI 10.1017/etds.2017.126](https://doi.org/10.1017/etds.2017.126).

The publisher page confirms online publication on 24 January 2018 and the September 2019 journal issue. The inspected mathematical text is the author's [arXiv version 2, 6 February 2017](https://arxiv.org/abs/1701.01318v2), not an asserted inspection of paywalled journal full text.

- Theorem 1.2, preprint page 2: the exact algebraic counterexample.
- Definition 2.6, page 4: whole-group cofinite asymptotic convention.
- Section 4, pages 12–14: direct-sum construction and its full verification.
- Lemma 4.1: arbitrary assignments on a punctured-coordinate set extend.
- Lemma 4.2: exact entropy product.
- Lemma 4.3: absence of nonzero finite-support points.
- Theorem 1.3 is a different, non-algebraic one-homeomorphism construction. It is not used to settle this record.

The key theorem page and official report page 141 were visually checked as well as text-extracted. No correction or retraction affecting this result was identified by the targeted title/arXiv/erratum search. The result is independently corroborated in the official 2022 report. The proof in this packet supplies an additional direct mathematical check.

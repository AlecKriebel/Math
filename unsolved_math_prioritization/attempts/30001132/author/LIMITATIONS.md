# Scope and validation limits

- This is a verification and reconstruction of a published negative result. The final IMRN article already reports a smooth SL4 failure; no novelty is claimed.
- The precise notion of admissibility is Kiritchenko's orbit-to-face construction with Borel subgroups containing the fixed diagonal torus. The proof exhausts these 24 Borel choices. Changing that definition, the torus, the polytope construction, or replacing a face by a union creates a different question.
- The construction uses strictly increasing integral top-row coordinates, equivalently a regular dominant weight and a very ample embedding. It does not assert anything about degenerate weights.
- A Schubert variety being smooth is not the same assertion as its associated polytope face being smooth, simple, or integral-simple. None of these is substituted for admissibility.
- Schubert cycle equality is in the Chow/cohomology group. It does not assert equality of different orbit closures as subsets. Translating a Schubert cell by a Weyl representative changes the subset while preserving its class.
- `w=2413` uses the dimension convention X_w=closure(B+ w B+/B+), with composition specified in the proof. Codimension-indexed opposite Schubert polynomials require a conversion. The direct incidence-variety proof avoids reliance on that conversion.
- The main obstruction uses only necessary predecessor-vertex containment. It does not infer admissibility from vertex containment. The positive results in the all-class enumeration are supplementary controls.
- The target webpage returned HTTP 403 to direct retrieval and was inaccessible via the web tool. Its statement was therefore taken from the identified private fallback corpus and checked against the original published OWR report. This limitation does not affect the final-paper negative statement or the finite certificate.
- Searches finding no previous repository/user attempt are bounded retrieval evidence, not proof that no undocumented attempt exists.
- A fresh independent review has not yet been performed in this author packet. It must check source revision, class conventions, the smoothness argument, all 24 certificate rows, file hashes, and safe-publication inventory before public promotion.
- The safe packet excludes all retrieved source PDFs/text, page screenshots, large corpora, and private coordination. Local source hashes and public bibliographic metadata enable verification without redistributing copyrighted sources.

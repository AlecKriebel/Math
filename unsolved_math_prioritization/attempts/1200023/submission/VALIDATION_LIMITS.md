# Validation limits

1. The general connected nonunimodular target is unresolved. The geometric/flow approaches do not remove that gap.
2. The known unimodular reconstruction depends explicitly on the unweighted mass-transport principle. The corresponding unweighted statement fails for nonunimodular graphs.
3. Grandfather examples are covered by an analytic spanning-tree proof, but an arbitrary nonunimodular transitive graph need not have a spanning tree of the stipulated degree; no such existence theorem is asserted.
4. Exact min-cuts certify all sets within the declared finite supports and only those supports. BFS distances use the infinite graph's complete neighbor oracle. Larger radii and other graphs are untested.
5. Flow feasibility/conservation and cut equality are checked, with small independently enumerated comparisons. This is reproducible code, not a formal proof-assistant development or expert peer review.
6. The finite-cycle, finite-path, infinite-ray, hairy-tree and disconnected-component negative controls intentionally omit one or more target hypotheses. None is presented as a connected vertex-transitive counterexample.
7. The primary catalogue page was unavailable; the title/source association was taken from the hash-pinned public corpus and checked against the primary document. Scholarly PDFs, screenshots, extraction text and the corpora are not distributed in this packet.
8. The literature search was bounded. The Benjamini--Schramm article's full text was not inspected. No novelty, exhaustive priority audit or definitive current-open classification is claimed.
9. Five substantive families are exhausted in this attempt; future substantive proof search would require a separately authorized continuation. A fresh independent audit of this frozen packet remains necessary before any publication.

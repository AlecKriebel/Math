# Independent source notes

- [OWR 56/2016](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46660), Korkmaz Question 13, printed p. 3189: original once-pointed relation question; page visually checked
- [Korkmaz's earlier chapter](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf), Problem 2.8, printed pp. 88–89: explicit right-handed twists and genus at least two; independently movable individual curve representatives
- [Hillman–Pedrotti v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10943v1), 13 April 2026, Theorems 3.12, 3.14 and 3.19, with Lemmas 3.17–3.18: exact ordered disk-extension criterion and the smooth/continuous distinction. The reversal bar is verified in the PDF and HTML. This is a preprint, not a universal positive sphere-base section theorem
- [Smith, 1999](https://doi.org/10.2140/gt.1999.3.211), Theorem 6.2, pp. 231–232: no nontrivial essential sphere-base Lefschetz fibration with Torelli monodromy. The trivial bundle is not the case invoked by Turn 3
- [Baykur–Hamada](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1326), publisher online-first pagination: Proposition 8 pp. 10–12; equations (14)–(15), p. 33; Theorem 14 and its proof, pp. 33–38. The source explicitly distinguishes primitive fiber/pseudosection from a section

The full primary reading copies were inspected at the locations supplied by the author and their hashes checked. The independent numerical/algebraic work does not reconstruct the 48 based curve words. Source PDFs and rendered pages are not included in this review packet. No external outreach or source-code execution was used.

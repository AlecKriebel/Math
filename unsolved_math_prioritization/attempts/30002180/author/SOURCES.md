2. Jean-Pierre Demailly, “Algebraic criteria for Kobayashi hyperbolic projective varieties and jet differentials,” Proc. Sympos. Pure Math. 62, Part 2 (1997), pp. 285–360. Author-hosted PDF: https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~demailly/source_files/hyperbolic.pdf . Inspected formula (6.5) for the polynomial-jet filtration, Definition 7.4 for directed versus total curvature, Theorem 7.8 and its entire-curve argument, and the surrounding algebraic sufficient conditions. These are external foundational inputs; the source also distinguishes different notions of curvature. The hosted PDF has 82 pages and its title dates the course to July 1995.

3. Aleksei Golota, “On negativity of total k-jet curvature and ampleness of the canonical bundle,” https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.02866v3 , PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1708.02866 . The fetched manuscript identifies itself as v3, 3 September 2017, eight pages. Inspected Question 1.0.1, Theorem 1.0.2, Definitions 2.3.1, and the proof architecture in Section 3. The theorem adds total curvature; it is not evidence that the directed version is settled. The arXiv record credits Proposition 3.2.1 to Campana–Păun. No journal-publication claim is made here.

5. Shing-Tung Yau, “Calabi's conjecture and some new results in algebraic geometry,” PNAS 74 (1977), pp. 1798–1799. DOI https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.74.5.1798 . Inspected Theorems 1 and 2 in the two-page scholarly-hosted scan: https://www-fourier.univ-grenoble-alpes.fr/~demailly/source_files/bourbaki_190316/PNAS-1977-Yau-1798-9.pdf .

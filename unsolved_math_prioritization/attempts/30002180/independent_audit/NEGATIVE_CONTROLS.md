# Adversarial negative controls

These controls reject specific invalid inferences; none constructs a counterexample to the original problem.

1. **Dropping the second sphere pairing.** The complex sphere fourth moment has two Kronecker pairings. At repeated indices the coefficient is twice the one-pairing value. Independent factorial-moment checks reject the lost factor.
2. **Directed positivity implies total positivity.** A diagonal form positive on V and negative on a transverse complement rejects this pointwise assertion. Its full trace can be negative; in tower dimension three its determinant can be negative.
3. **Tower tautological c1 equals base c1.** Already on a projective-space fiber at level one, O(1) has degree one on a fiber line, whereas every class pulled back from X has degree zero. These cannot be identified when n≥2.
4. **A positive curve genus forces ambient real c1 nonzero.** Under ambient real-c1 vanishing, the positive genus contribution is balanced by the normal degree. Complete intersections yield a growing genus-to-degree ratio.
5. **Real-c1 vanishing means integral class is zero.** A nonzero torsion class maps to zero in real cohomology. The v2 target explicitly excludes this confusion. No assertion about the existence of a forbidden metric on a torsion-canonical variety is implied.
6. **Ricci-flat means flat or one-signed sectional curvature.** A nonzero algebraic Kähler tensor with zero Ricci contraction takes positive and negative values on different complex lines.
7. **Jet weighted degree equals cotangent tensor degree.** A second derivative has weight two but supplies one cotangent factor in its graded block. Generating-series checks independently verify the graded ranks.
8. **Local boundedness makes every current pullback valid.** An ambient distribution does not determine arbitrary values on a submanifold. The quasi-psh canonical representative, local boundedness near the image, and holomorphic-flow/convolution argument are all used.
9. **A torus directional second derivative has positive mean.** Periodic integration by parts gives zero. The Fourier controls check that differentiation creates no nonzero constant coefficient. This control does not manufacture a translation field on a general Ricci-flat manifold.
10. **Finite assertion totals certify analytic theorems.** Both code outputs explicitly disclaim this. The geometric arguments have been checked separately and remain conditional on their identified standard inputs.
11. **Golota's total-positivity theorem resolves directed positivity.** The inspected theorem retains the stronger hypothesis. Its title or abstract is not used as a replacement for the hypothesis.
12. **Positive geometric hyperbolic index is nonzero first Chern class.** The inspected 2026 theorem does not state that identification. Its manuscript status and limited audit scope remain explicit.
13. **No search hit establishes global absence or openness.** The source and repository search claims remain bounded, and the exact problem webpage/raw corpora remain uninspected.

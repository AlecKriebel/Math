# Sources, prior credit and unresolved status

This third-pass edition incorporates complete authored proofs and independent mathematical audits. It does not begin a new literature search or claim a later publication-status update than the inspected sources recorded here. No novelty or exhaustive-priority claim is made.

- Chudnovsky and Sivaraman, “Perfect divisibility and 2-divisibility,” author PDF dated 20 April 2017: https://web.math.princeton.edu/~mchudnov/Perfect2div.pdf. The target concerns two-part clique-number reduction, not the stronger perfect-divisibility property.
- Chudnovsky, Robertson, Seymour and Thomas, “K4-free graphs with no odd holes,” author PDF dated 5 October 2001, revised 5 August 2009: https://thomas.math.gatech.edu/PAP/k4free.pdf. Theorems 1.3 and 3.2 give the established four-color range. At omega=3, grouping four proper color classes into two pairs gives a division. At omega=2, an odd-hole-free triangle-free graph is bipartite, since a shortest odd cycle would be an odd hole. Thus omega<=3 is already known; the unresolved target frontier is omega>=4. The full decomposition proof is an external theorem dependency and is not re-audited here.
- Hu, Xu and Zhuang, “Some properties of minimally nonperfectly divisible graphs,” arXiv:2603.01967v2, 5 March 2026: https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01967v2#S4 and https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.01967v2. Section 4 already records minimum-degree/no-basin reductions, retention of clique number in cutset pieces, incompatible division traces and minimum-disagreement blocking cliques. Its restricted theorem for minimally nonperfectly divisible graphs is a different statement and is not imported as unrestricted clique-cutset exclusion for non-2-divisible graphs. The earlier [complete source-credit audit](../3251_divisibility_partials_20261012/PRIOR_CREDIT_AUDIT.md) documents the inspected portion and elementary adaptations.
- Hoàng, “On the structure of perfectly divisible graphs,” arXiv:2506.12660v2, 17 June 2025: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12660v2. Conjecture 4.7 marks unrestricted minimal non-2-divisible clique-cutset exclusion as an open frontier in that source. It is not used as a proved closure theorem.
- He, Shi, Wu and Yao, “The perfect divisibility and chromatic number of some odd hole-free graphs,” arXiv:2603.09549v1, 10 March 2026: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09549v1. This is a specialized-class source from the earlier ledger, not a proof of the unrestricted target.

The sharp order proof and all boundary/core extension arguments are elementary and fully reproduced. The boundary and core proofs need neither odd-hole-freeness nor the external omega-three theorem. The published coloring theorem is used only to explain the original target's already settled low-clique-number range.

## Approach 2: Waldschmidt constants and integer rounding

Mechanism: obtain all finite multiplicities from a quantitative asymptotic lower bound.

Result: proved the limit/infimum relation from subadditivity. Derived B_r=m_r*L+C with L=(a+n-1)/n and C=(n-1)(a-1)/n. ## Approach 3: sharp hyperplane star configurations

Mechanism: restrict to each hyperplane, use dimension induction plus differentiation, and peel the product of all defining hyperplanes.

Result: full authored proof that a_(n(r-1)+1)=r*s-n+1 for point stars from s hyperplanes in linear general position in P^n, including explicit extremizing products. The proof tracks both dimension and multiplicity induction and includes the a=s-n+1 base calculation.

Status: complete known-family control, not a novel resolution. Restriction cannot be applied to arbitrary X because the requisite hyperplane incidence structure is absent. Completion estimate: 4%.

# Independent source locators and conventions

1. Schweighofer, *Spectrahedral relaxations of hyperbolicity cones*, OWR12/2020, printed 652–654. Full contribution read; the arbitrary tuple x is defined on 652 and the first conjecture is on 653. The separate second conjecture has the cubic-part condition. [Primary report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46848)
2. Kummer–Sawall, *Three results related to the half-plane property of matroids*, Algebraic Combinatorics8(1)(2025),1–15. Complete final Sections2–3 and rendered pp.4/8 were inspected. Theorem3.3 supplies the extension-to-polymatroid reduction; Theorem3.5 supplies the obstruction. The final publisher metadata records publication3March2025. [Publisher](https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/alco.399/), [full final PDF](https://alco.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/alco.399.pdf)
3. Sawall–Schweighofer, *Amalgamation of real zero polynomials*, Indagationes Mathematicae35(2024),37–59, DOI10.1016/j.indag.2023.08.002. Complete author v2 was read for the earlier example and the later “weak” formulation. Example6.1 has six shared variables; Conjectures7.6/7.7 fix two shared variables. The final publisher layout was not compared. [Complete author v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2305.07403v2)
4. Wagner–Wei, *A criterion for the half-plane property*, full author version0709.1269v1, Theorem3 and the F7^(-4) example/SOS on pp.4–6. The exact identity and its relabelling were independently checked. The 2013 corrigendum is acknowledged but its full text was not accessed. [Primary author version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1269v1)
5. Choe–Oxley–Sokal–Wagner, *Homogeneous multivariate polynomials with the half-plane property*, full author v2. Read the permanent theorem10.2, Corollary10.3, Proposition10.4, Example10.12 and the relevant appendix. This distinguishes equal-coefficient HPP from weak support-only HPP. [Primary author version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0202034v2)

Convention reconciliation: the final paper's Definition2.12 says “nonnegative” for roots of P(te+v), whereas the subsequent cone statements require nonpositive. The frozen assessment explicitly uses the latter consistent convention. A u-power factor may change the entire cone; only the membership of the needed nonnegative-u vectors is used. Neither point changes the published counterexample conclusion.

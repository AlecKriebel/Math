# At most five exceptional lists, in every degree

Row 1751, problem 3200, OPG 388. Authored mathematics and independent audits dated 12 October 2026 UTC. This is the third accepted substantive research round in the present continuation. Its subcubic and higher-degree directions count together as one round. It is a restricted theorem, not a resolution or counterexample to the general conjecture. No novelty claim is made.

## Main result

Let G be finite and simple, with actual maximum degree Δ. Suppose G has no proper Δ-edge-coloring but G−e has one for every edge e. Give every edge a list of exactly Δ colors, and assume the assignment is nonconstant. If the lists differ from some Δ-element palette A on at most five edges, then G has a proper list-edge-coloring.

This result covers every degree, arbitrary exceptional lists and arbitrary exceptional support. The Δ=3 and Δ≥4 exactly-five arguments are complete symbolic proofs. The accepted first-round results supply one through four exceptions and arbitrary-star support; the complete degree-two case is an odd-cycle argument, and degree at most one is vacuous. The exact theorem, proof of the combination and dependency map are in [THEOREM.md](THEOREM.md).

Consequently any hypothetical bad nonconstant assignment must differ from every Δ-element palette on at least six edges. This is a necessary condition, not evidence that a counterexample exists.

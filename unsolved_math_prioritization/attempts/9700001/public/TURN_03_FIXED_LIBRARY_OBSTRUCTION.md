# Attempt 3: an exact obstruction for fixed stopping libraries

This theorem concerns stopping-time maps fixed on a filtered sample space before the process is chosen. It does not concern test algorithms allowed to choose new maps after inspecting the process law, or fixed programs whose price-dependent decisions change when the process changes. That distinction is essential.

Let Omega={0,1}^n with its uniform law, and let F_t reveal the first t bits, with F_0 trivial. A node v is a binary prefix of length t<n; its cylinder has probability p_v=2^{-t}. There are D=2^n-1 such nonterminal nodes. Fix any m<D deterministic F-stopping maps T_1,...,T_m with values in {0,...,n}.

## Theorem 3: fewer than D fixed equalities miss a nonmartingale

There is a rational adapted process Z, with Z_0=0 and |Z_t|<1, such that:

- E Z_{T_j}=0 for every j;
- Z is not an F-martingale;
- the natural filtration of Z is exactly F;
- a deterministic-time or one-step-cylinder stopping witness has nonzero expectation;
- Z and that witness have descriptions and exact evaluation cost polynomial in n and m. The certificate's nonzero advantage need not be inverse-polynomial.

The construction is effective in polynomial time given the stop/continue decisions of the T_j at m+1 selected nodes. In particular this input is obtained efficiently when each T_j is efficiently executable on the revealed bits. No efficient access to an arbitrary unevaluated stopping map is assumed.

### Proof

For each nonterminal node v define the predictable-increment process

W^v_s = 1_{s>|v|} 1_{prefix v},  0<=s<=n.

It jumps by 1 on v's cylinder in the step |v| to |v|+1, and is thereafter constant. Hence

E W^v_{T_j}=P(prefix v and T_j>|v|)=p_v c_{jv},

where c_{jv} is either 0 or 1: the survival event at time |v| is F_{|v|}-measurable and is constant on that atom.

Choose any m+1 distinct nonterminal nodes. The m by (m+1) matrix C=(c_{jv}) has a nonzero rational kernel vector u. Rational elimination produces one with polynomial bit length. Explicitly, take r independent rows and r pivot columns, choose one further column, and use signed r by r minors; their absolute values are at most r!, with the rank-zero case handled by a coordinate vector. Fill unused coordinates with zero. Set d_v=u_v/p_v and normalize so sum_v |d_v|=1. These rational coefficients still have polynomial bit length, since p_v^{-1}=2^{|v|} has at most n+1 bits.

Put W=sum_v d_v W^v. Then |W_s|<=1 and E W_{T_j}=sum_v p_v c_{jv} d_v=0. At every node v its conditional one-step drift is exactly d_v (zero at nodes outside the selected set). At least one such drift is nonzero.

To make the bits observable from the price itself, let

M_t=sum_{i=1}^t (B_i-1/2) 2^{-i},
epsilon=2^{-(n+3)},
Z_t=M_t+epsilon W_t.

M is a martingale, so its expected value at each bounded T_j is zero, by the elementary telescoping proof in Attempt 1. Consequently every required Z test also vanishes. Z has conditional drift epsilon d_v at v, so it is not a martingale. Also |M_t|<1/2 and |epsilon W_t|<=1/16 for n>=1, hence |Z_t|<1.

Write c_t=(1-2^{-t})/2. For a length-t prefix with binary integer k,

M_t=k/2^t-c_t.

Thus 2^t(Z_t+c_t) is within 2^t epsilon<=1/8 of k. Rounding to the nearest integer recovers k uniquely and hence all the first t bits. This is polynomial-time exact rational arithmetic. Z_t is F_t-measurable and F_t is already generated by Z_t, proving natural-filtration equality. This argument makes no claim of stability under measurement noise.

Choose any node v with d_v!=0 and set t=|v|. The unconditional drift on its cylinder is q=epsilon p_v d_v!=0. Both b_t=E Z_t=epsilon sum_{|w|<t}p_w d_w and q are computable from the selected-node list. If b_t!=0, deterministic t is a witness. Otherwise T=t+1 on v's cylinder and t elsewhere has expectation q!=0. Its online execution uses the same rounding decoder. W_s itself is evaluated by checking at most m+1 prefixes, proving the claimed polynomial representation and evaluation bounds. QED.

## Dimension interpretation

On the vector space of all adapted real processes with X_0=0, the D node-drift functionals are independent: any prescribed drift vector is realized by sum_v d_v W^v. The martingale subspace therefore has codimension D. Every fixed stopping equality is one linear condition, which cannot define that subspace using fewer than D conditions. The explicit construction above strengthens this elementary rank argument by preserving bounded values, compact descriptions, observable information, and a simple missed witness.

## What this does not prove

Aldous explicitly allows a class larger than the exact martingales. The theorem does not rule out such useful relaxations, adaptive test generation, law-dependent stopping times, approximation at a specified tolerance, or Markov and other restricted model classes. It does not prove computational hardness for efficiently described stochastic processes. In fact, the supplied sparse drift representation makes its own missed witness easy to find. It shows why a universal fixed short library cannot be promoted to a complete exact certificate merely because its members are easy to run.

## Outcome

The unrestricted fixed-library exact-martingale route has a precise obstruction, but the original question permits much more. Attempt 4 therefore imposes a checkable conditional-law model rather than asserting a universal negative answer.

# Five substantive attempts, 2026-10-03 UTC

These are five consecutive analytic approaches within this investigation, not five independent researchers or five independent reviews. Source identification and repository searches are preliminary checks and do not count as attempts.

1. Finite nested observation partitions. Established the full equivalence among a finite stopping library, zero atom drift, the optional-projection martingale, and fairness against every stopping time in that filtration. Derived explicit witnesses and a quantitative bound. Limitation: a small coarsening can conceal easily visible price information.
2. Feature/survival coverage. Established a weighted approximation bound that identifies exactly what additional approximation guarantee is needed to extend finite feature moments to stopping-rule guarantees. Constructed a four-path natural-filtration example passing every current-state one-step test while allowing a 1/4 stopping gain. Limitation: no universal polynomial coverage theorem.
3. Fixed-family rank obstruction. Proved the exponential codimension obstruction and strengthened it with a sparse rational nullspace process, bounded martingale price encoding, exact polynomial-time decoding, and a simple missed witness. Limitation: fixed maps on the sample space, no adaptive/law-dependent test selection, and possibly tiny advantage.
4. Explicit finite-state Markov representation. Used the genuine full-history conditional-law hypothesis to restore polynomial local certificates, and proved exact upper/lower stopping recursions and witness construction. Limitation: fully observed small known Markov model, not an arbitrary unknown/succinct process.
5. Sampling access. Proved finite-library simultaneous concentration with complete constants, certified witness/accept/inconclusive branches, and the rare-event exact-zero sample lower bound. Limitation: a fixed library and specified gap are indispensable to the stated guarantee; writing a rule and certifying its advantage remain distinct.

Each complete argument is preserved in its numbered TURN file. The source question is still unsolved by this work. All finite controls are in verify.py, with a deterministic exact-rational receipt in VERIFICATION.json. No finite experiment is substituted for the universal mathematical arguments, and no model-specific answer is promoted to an unrestricted resolution.

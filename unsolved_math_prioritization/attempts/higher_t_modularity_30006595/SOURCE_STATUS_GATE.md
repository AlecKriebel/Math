## Primary source and formulation audit

DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2026/7 . Official publisher PDF retrieved; printed p. 469 visually inspected.

The original question concerns higher t-coefficients of H(t,z(tau)) = 3F2(1/2+t,1/2-t,1/2;1,1;z(tau)), after the specified modular substitution. The constant and quadratic terms have modular/antiderivative interpretations. The full generating function also includes a digamma prefactor and a separate odd contribution. The generated formulation conflates these objects and appends the distinct higher-depth discussion. Treat them as separate targets.

The report specifies z(tau)=64 eta(tau)^24 eta(4tau)^24/eta(2tau)^48 and delta=q d/dq. Higher depth means 2 <= ell <= floor(k/2). Its integral structure remains a separate unresolved program. The report describes the underlying joint project as work in progress.

## Research acceptance boundary

Separate (A) modular/iterated-antiderivative structure of the specified H coefficients, (B) reconstruction of the full R(k,1) generator, and (C) higher-depth integral structure. Explain exactly which function class, modular group, weight, normalization and integration constants are established. A formal differential recurrence or a closure statement in a class defined to contain arbitrary antiderivatives is not by itself the requested modular identification. Odd H coefficients vanish by parameter symmetry; this observation does not solve the higher even coefficients or the odd part of the full generator. Finite coefficient fits and known low-order cases are not general resolution. Any narrower result must be labeled partial.

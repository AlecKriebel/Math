# Explicit Chern generators in a non-minimal-rank family

Problem 30000120 / OWR-744-003. Author research date: 2026-10-05.

## Outcome and scope

**The general problem is not solved.** Five approaches are recorded below. The positive result is an explicit, classical-geometric reconstruction for complete conics and products of complete conics with projective planes. These are wonderful compactifications of adjoint symmetric spaces, include arbitrary symmetric rank, and are outside the minimal-rank hypothesis. A second result proves that the Chern classes of the tangent and logarithmic tangent bundles, even taken together, do not suffice on complete conics. Neither result is asserted to be novel. The exact-arithmetic program checks the finite algebra; it does not prove the geometric input or certify novelty, global openness, or peer review.

## 2. Five approaches and their stopping points

### Approach 1: transfer from the equivariant-cohomology literature

Brion–Joshua's [paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/0705.1035), Theorem 2.2.1 and Propositions 3.3.1–3.3.2, gives a toric restriction description and tangent/logarithmic Chern formulas **under minimal rank**. The comparison hypothesis is

    rank(G/K) = rank(G) - rank(K).

Their introductory discussion separates an algebraic description from explicit ring generators. Strickland's [2006 paper](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2006.01.021) computes equivariant cohomology for the wonderful **group** compactification, a particular symmetric space. Its abstract and accessible primary PDF text were checked; the attempted local PDF retrieval was blocked. These sources do not establish the arbitrary-adjoint-symmetric target. No newer general resolution was established by the targeted searches.

**Gap:** an arbitrary-rank replacement, plus actual globally defined equivariant bundles whose Chern classes generate after passage to ordinary cohomology. Localization descriptions alone do not supply it.

### Approach 2: extend homogeneous bundles from the open orbit

Brion's [*Construction of equivariant vector bundles*](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410039), Theorem 1, constructs extensions under minimal rank. Section 3.5(ii) gives a warning: the additive span of the classes of his extension bundles is a proper subspace of rational K-theory. Boundary information therefore cannot simply be discarded. That additive statement by itself does **not** prove that the algebra generated by their Chern classes is proper.

**Gap:** specify sufficient boundary contributions and prove generation; outside minimal rank, also prove local freeness of the proposed extensions. Replacing a coherent extension by an alleged bundle without checking local freeness is invalid.

### Approach 3: try tangent and logarithmic tangent bundles

This natural shortcut fails, including outside minimal rank. On complete conics, proved below, write E for the exceptional divisor and F for the other boundary divisor. Then

    c1(T_X) = 2(E+F),       c1(T_X(-log(E+F))) = E+F,
    dim_Q H²(X,Q) = 2.

Every higher Chern class has cohomological degree at least four. Consequently the degree-two part of the algebra generated by all Chern classes of those two bundles has dimension one. It cannot equal H*(X,Q).

**Result:** a rigorous obstruction to this proposed generator set, not a counterexample to the original problem, which permits other bundles.

### Approach 4: reduce to a finite equivariant K-theory certificate

Here is a precise sufficient condition rather than an existence-based solution. Let X be smooth projective, with odd rational cohomology zero and its rational cycle map an isomorphism. Suppose finitely many explicitly given G-linearized coherent sheaves F_j have Chern characters spanning H^(even)(X,Q), and each has an explicitly given finite resolution by G-equivariant vector bundles E_jk. Then the ordinary Chern classes of the E_jk generate H*(X,Q).

Proof: the character of F_j is the alternating sum of the characters of its resolution terms. Each character component is a rational polynomial in the Chern classes by Newton identities. The algebra generated by those Chern classes contains all the spanning characters, hence the whole cohomology vector space. This proves the condition. It neither supplies the missing sheaves nor constructs their resolutions in the general case.

**Gap:** an explicit finite list with a verified spanning calculation, uniformly for the adjoint symmetric root data. Resolving unspecified generators merely repackages the original existence statement.

### Approach 5: solve explicit non-minimal-rank models and take products

The next sections carry this approach through completely for the stated family, using blow-up geometry before finite computation. The geometric identification, presentation, generation, and product closure are proved. The stopping point is extending the construction to arbitrary irreducible symmetric spaces, not a finite-test cutoff.

## 3. Complete conics: identification and boundary

Let V=C³ and let Z=v₂(P(V*)) be the rank-one locus in P(Sym² V*)=P⁵. Put

    X = Bl_Z P⁵,
    H = pullback of the hyperplane class,
    E = exceptional divisor,
    F = strict transform of the determinant hypersurface.

The PGL₃ action on quadratic forms lifts to X, since Z is invariant. The open orbit of nondegenerate forms has stabilizer PO₃, the fixed subgroup of the inverse-transpose involution of PGL₃. The diagonal torus is inverted by this involution. Therefore

    rank(PGL₃/PO₃)=2,   rank(PGL₃)=2,   rank(PO₃)=1.

This is not minimal rank.

For completeness, near a rank-one form one can choose a nonzero upper-left entry and, after scaling, write its symmetric matrix as

    [1, u, v; u, u²+x, uv+y; v, uv+y, v²+z].

The rank-one center is x=y=z=0; the determinant is xz-y². Thus its multiplicity along Z is two and

    F = 3H-2E.

Blowing up the ideal (x,y,z), its strict transform meets the exceptional P² in the smooth conic xz-y²=0. This local description shows that E and F are smooth and meet transversely. The stabilizer of a rank-one form acts on the projectivized normal directions as on binary quadratic forms; its two orbits distinguish rank one and rank two. Together with the rank-two and rank-three orbits away from E, this gives the four orbits with closures X, E, F, and E∩F. The compactification is therefore wonderful of rank two. This also identifies the classical complete-conics model without relying on a finite experiment.

Both O_X(E) and O_X(F) are PGL₃-equivariant: an invariant Cartier divisor defines its canonical linearization through the action on rational functions. In particular, no unverified descent of O_P⁵(1) to an adjoint group is required.

Classical geometry and blow-up formulas are reviewed in these [hosted blow-up notes](https://math.berkeley.edu/~ragnar/Blow_ups_and_3264_conics.pdf). No authorship is inferred merely from the hosting directory.

## 4. The rational ring and its proof

All degrees in polynomial presentations below are complex codimensions; H and E have degree one. Let z be the positive hyperplane class of Z=P². The restriction sends H to 2z and is surjective over Q, with kernel generated by H³. The normal sequence and Euler sequence give

    c(N_{Z/P⁵}) = (1+2z)^6/(1+z)^3 = 1+9z+30z².

The degree of Z is four. In the standard smooth-center blow-up presentation one may therefore choose the lifted Chern polynomial

    P(t) = t³ + (9/2)Ht² + (15/2)H²t + 4H³.

Its coefficients restrict to the corresponding Chern classes of N, including c3(N)=0; its constant term is the center class [Z]. The exceptional relation is P(-E)=0 and the restriction-kernel relation is H³E=0. Hence

    H*(X,Q) ≅ Q[H,E] / (H³E,
             E³-(9/2)HE²+(15/2)H²E-4H³).

The additive blow-up decomposition supplies a basis

    1,H,H²,H³,H⁴,H⁵;  E,HE,H²E;  E²,HE²,H²E².

It yields the even Betti sequence (1,2,3,3,2,1), with odd groups zero. The polynomial relations reduce to those twelve elements; the geometric additive formula proves their independence, so the displayed quotient is the full cohomology ring rather than merely a surjective presentation.

Since H=(F+2E)/3, the two explicit first Chern classes c1(O(E))=E and c1(O(F))=F generate the ring. Equivalently, in boundary variables a=E and b=F the presentation is

    Q[a,b] / (a(2a+b)³,
               8a³+3a²b-3ab²-8b³).

This follows by an invertible rational linear substitution, not by an experimental fit. The ring is graded in cohomological degrees 2deg(a), 2deg(b).

Checks on sign conventions: integration gives

    ∫H⁵=1,  ∫H²E³=4,  ∫HE⁴=18,  ∫E⁵=51.

The class H'=2H-E of the dual-conic map has ∫(H')⁵=1. These intersection values are consequences of the presentation and match the standard exceptional-divisor convention E|_E=O_{P(N)}(-1).

## 5. Tangent/logarithmic obstruction

The center has codimension three. The canonical-divisor blow-up formula gives

    K_X = -6H+2E,
    c1(T_X) = 6H-2E = 2(E+F).

For the simple-normal-crossing boundary D=E+F,

    c1(T_X(-log D)) = c1(T_X)-[D] = E+F.

By the additive basis, E and F are independent in H²(X,Q). The graded-degree argument in Approach 3 now proves that these two tangent-type bundles do not provide a complete Chern generating set. Notice that this obstruction remains valid even though the individual higher Chern classes might be computable exactly.

## 6. An explicit family of arbitrary symmetric rank

Let Q=P(Sym² C²*)=P² under PGL₂. Its open nondegenerate-form orbit is PGL₂/PO₂; the boundary D is its smooth discriminant conic. It is a wonderful rank-one compactification, and its rational ring is Q[d]/(d³), with d=c1(O(D))=2h. The line bundle O(D) is canonically equivariant. The symmetric rank is one whereas rank(PGL₂)-rank(PO₂)=0.

For any nonnegative integers b,c with b+c>0, take

    X_{b,c} = Q^b × X^c,
    G_{b,c} = (PGL₂)^b × (PGL₃)^c,
    K_{b,c} = (PO₂)^b × (PO₃)^c.

Products of the defining wonderful properties show that this is the wonderful compactification of G_{b,c}/K_{b,c}; its rank is b+2c. The rank difference is c, so it is never minimal rank. By the rational Künneth theorem, the pullbacks of the b+2c boundary line bundles above have first Chern classes generating its entire cohomology ring. Its presentation is the tensor product of the displayed factor presentations, with no mixed relations beyond commutativity. Its half-degree Poincaré polynomial is

    (1+t+t²)^b (1+2t+3t²+3t³+2t⁴+t⁵)^c.

This is an unbounded-rank partial family, not a reduction of every symmetric space to these factors.

## 7. Precisely what remains

For a general adjoint symmetric pair (G,K), identify a finite list of actual G-equivariant vector bundles on its wonderful compactification, describe them explicitly from that pair, and prove their Chern classes generate the full rational ring. The present boundary-line method has been proved only for the factor family in Section 6. It provides no claim about all complete quadrics, higher-rank simple groups, or exceptional symmetric pairs. The dated corpus label does not independently establish present-day global status. The honest result is scoped positive constructions plus a failed-generator obstruction and a documented general gap.

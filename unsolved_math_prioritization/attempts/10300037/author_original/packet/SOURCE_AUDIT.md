# Source and status audit, 2026-10-08 UTC

## Exact original and corrected interpretation

The original is Calegari's 2002 list, Question 9.4 attributed to Delman, printed p.22. Its definitions on p.2 distinguish ideal polygon bundles over S^1 from interstitial I-bundles. The corpus gloss calling the desired region an 'I-bundle of an even-sided polygon over S^1' conflates these constructions. The source also requires the complementary region around the knot and monkey-saddle filling at all nontrivial surgeries; merely obtaining an unrelated taut foliation after each surgery is not an automatic verification of those geometric requirements. The raw source statement and imported records are retained separately; only our own restatement appears in the public proof.

Calegari's p.22, Delman–Roberts' double-diamond theorem page, the later notes p.46, and Delman's 2019 persistence slide were visually inspected. Complete PDF text extractions were available, and the locations described below were read. This is targeted inspection, not a claim that every proof in every source was independently reconstructed.

## What is proved in inspected sources

1. Delman–Roberts, double-diamond replacements, arXiv 1907.01899v2: the explicit even-annulus collar criterion is Proposition 4.10; Definitions 5.3 and 5.8 give the tightness and double-diamond conditions; Theorem 5.10 builds the two-annulus collar; Corollary 5.11 retains the no-sink-disk condition needed for carrying a lamination; Corollary 6.3 supplies a plumbed-band subclass. The PDF is marked v2, 11 December 2019, while its front-page internal date reads 24 August 2021. We pin the actually retrieved bytes and report both labels rather than inventing a revision history. Publication in Contemporary Mathematics 760 (2020), 123–142 is corroborated by the later primary bibliography. Its journal pagination/numbering is not substituted for preprint numbering: Santoro cites the collar criterion as published Proposition 3.10.
2. Delman–Roberts, composite knots, arXiv 1905.04838v3: Section 4 gives annular gluing of strongly realized slopes; Sections 6.3–6.6 give a two-meridional-annulus construction for sums of nontrivial fibered knots. ArXiv and the publisher metadata identify Algebraic & Geometric Topology 21 (2021), 2761–2798. The publisher's linked print-PDF request returned its HTML landing page, so the journal PDF was not inspected. No HTML response is labeled or hashed as a PDF in SOURCE_PINS.json. The preprint's placeholder pagination is retained in private bytes; references in the proof use section/proposition numbers.
3. Santoro, arXiv 2402.01225v2, revised 19 June 2026: Theorem 2.17 restates the even-annulus/noncompact-extension criterion. Theorem 4.1 covers diagrams with more than one twist region, all twist weights greater than one, at least one greater than two, and the two specified associated graphs connected. This is a substantial current subclass result, not an inspected proof covering every alternating diagram. The publisher/author metadata identifies Adv. Math. 492 (2026), 110906; final journal bytes were not compared with the preprint.

## Universal announced result and verification limit

- Delman's official Georgia Topology Conference slides, dated May 25, 2019, slide 12, state the prime non-torus alternating result. Slide 18 explicitly describes a torus collar with a positive even number of meridional cusps and the subsequent stacked-chair filling. Thus the announcement concerns relevant complementary geometry, not solely a generic existence statement. The slide's abbreviated 'any slope' language is interpreted with the nonmeridional exception in the actual definitions; we do not infer a taut foliation of S^3.
- The double-diamond paper's introduction cites a separate work entitled Non-torus alternating knots are persistently foliar, labeled preprint in reference 8. That reference is not itself the full proof.
- The official Calegari course/book-note PDF retrieved on this date has creation metadata January 20, 2024. On p.46, Theorem 7.14 states the prime alternating result, but Definition 7.15 has only its label, and the proof paragraph of Proposition 7.16 describes the intended two-meridional-suture modification without carrying it out. The next sections and references immediately follow. These particular notes are therefore not a complete inspected proof of the universal assertion.
- Baldwin–Ni–Sivek's 2025 journal article, Floer homology and right-veering monodromy, p.266, describes the Delman–Roberts diagrammatic proof as forthcoming; reference 9 labels the named alternating-knot work 'to appear'. This is a statement in that 2025 source, not a claim of its status on every later date.

A bounded fresh search checked the exact title with and without the hyphen, the authors' official pages and their links, arXiv-indexed author/title results, current primary papers and public scholarly search results. Delman's official paper page links the double-diamond and composite papers and the conference slides, but not a downloadable full alternating-knot manuscript. Roberts' official department page links her personal site; that site returned HTTP 403 and was not bypassed. No full copy of the named universal manuscript was retrieved in this audit. This does not establish that no public or private manuscript exists. We did not contact authors or transmit any private material.

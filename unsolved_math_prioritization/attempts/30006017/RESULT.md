# Result: limiting area of random self-overlapping polygons

**30006017 / OWR-14298589-002. Original unresolved after five substantive author turns.**

The exact primary target is the joint law obtained by taking a standard planar Brownian bridge on [0,1], using its n equally spaced increment vectors as the side set, and choosing a flat translation disk uniformly conditional on that set. The area includes developing-map multiplicity. The requested convergence of A_n−(log n)/(2pi), and identification of its law, have not been proved or disproved here.

## Strongest retained results

- **Actual-ensemble reduction.** The annealed boundary-order law is a filling-multiplicity tilt of the ordered Gaussian bridge. Gaussian quadratic energy is independent of the selected unit-energy disk shape. Using the credited source mean asymptotic, removing that radial factor changes the centered area by O(log n/sqrt n) in L1. The hard problem is therefore the selected shape law, not Gaussian radius fluctuations.
- **A rigorous continuum candidate.** For an independent standard excursion e and real Brownian bridge X, integrate absolute horizontal bridge increments over the components of excursion superlevel sets, retaining components of time length above epsilon. This width functional minus log(1/epsilon)/(2pi) converges in L2 with O(epsilon^(1/4)) error. Its limit is the sum of a conditionally centered Gaussian-width fluctuation and a centered critical tree functional plus an explicit finite residual.
- **Credit is essential.** The continuum argument imports Fill–Janson's established centered analytic continuation at exponent 1/2, Janson's uniform conditioned-tree variance bound, and their contour/functional convergence machinery. Those theorems are not claimed as discoveries of this attempt. The supplementary layer covariance and cutoff arguments are stated and proved explicitly.
- **Sharp route obstructions.** Positive random measures can have an exact logarithmic mean and an L2 Abel-renormalized limit while their sharp-cutoff laws fail to converge. The Brownian continuum model overcomes that obstacle through the credited variance estimate. Separately, generic flat disks can have developed boundaries converging uniformly to zero, side energy exactly 2 and quadratic matrices tending to I, but intrinsic areas tending to different positive constants. Thus a boundary scaling limit alone does not provide the missing area transfer.

## The remaining gap

No verified finite bijection or approximation identifies the actual uniform disk area with the continuum cutoff functional plus a controlled error. The finite enumeration/statistics manuscript cited by the report was listed as in preparation and was not located; the available source sketches and conjectural boundary formula are not an area-preserving limit theorem. The current work therefore does not assert that the original limit is the continuum variable plus the source mean constant. Expectation matching is insufficient without random-error and integrability control.

The deterministic disks and abstract random measures are counterexamples to proposed proof routes, not to the source conditional-uniform Gaussian ensemble. The short imported statement's missing Brownian normalization is corrected rather than exploited as a trivial rescaling counterexample.

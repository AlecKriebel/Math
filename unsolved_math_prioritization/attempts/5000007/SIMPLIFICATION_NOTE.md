# Proposed simplification of Lemma3

2026-10-01 03:50UTC. This is separate from the frozen candidate and awaits the same independent review.

The dodecahedral graph has girth5. In particular, a graph-distance-two vertex pairv,w has exactly one common neighboru: it has at least one by definition; two would give a4cycle. If a rotation of order2 exchangesv andw, it fixes their unique common neighboru. But no nonidentity rotation of order2 fixes a dodecahedral vertex, since its rotational stabilizer is cyclic of order3. Contradiction.

Thus the endpoint exclusion follows directly from the virtual-Weierstrass half-turn argument; the30-case exact coordinate certificate can remain a corroborating test rather than a logical dependency. The fact that no rotation involution fixes a vertex is also used in Athreya–Aulicino–Hooper, Theorem5.2.

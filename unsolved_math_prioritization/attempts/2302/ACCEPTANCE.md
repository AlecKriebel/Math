## Unaccepted or unresolved scope

General harmonic rigidity is not established by the inspected February 25 proof. Proposition 41 provides fixed O(ε)+o(1) errors where the subsequent theorem requires o(1), and the claimed fixed-cutoff quantifiers are changed. A diagonal vanishing-error choice can force u→∞, while the compactness/equality stability theorem is only fixed-u. The needed compatibility or uniform modulus is absent. The audit gives three sufficient repair directions but proves none. This is a proof gap, not a counterexample to the theorem and not a certificate that the question is globally open.

The single N=100 witness does not refute a rule that holds for all sufficiently large N at fixed C. Eventual exact greedy optimization and complete optimizer classification remain unresolved by this audit. Harmonic distance o(1) also does not imply cardinality distance o(N/log N); removing the primes in (N/2,N] illustrates that distinction.

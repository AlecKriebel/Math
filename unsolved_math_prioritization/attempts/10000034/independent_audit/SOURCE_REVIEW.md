## Klausen and Kravitz

Frederik Ravn Klausen and Noah Kravitz, *Half-plane non-coexistence without FKG*, [arXiv:2602.12261v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.12261v2), revised 7 August 2026; [full primary text](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.12261v2).

Rehashed both supplied PDF versions; used v2 for the mathematical comparison. Read the definitions and Theorems 1.1–1.2, Section 2.1 including Lemma 2.3 and the shift argument, the stated spanning-tree application, the concluding questions/examples, and the quantitative energy use in Appendix A. The paper's finite-energy definition is uniform. Theorem 1.2 instead assumes finitely many infinite whole-plane clusters and translation invariance plus ergodicity. Its trichotomy explicitly permits neither half-plane process to percolate. Its stated geometric extension concerns rational slopes. The packet does not incorrectly use it to prove existence, irrational-slope results, or conditional FKG. No independent certification of every argument in the paper is claimed.

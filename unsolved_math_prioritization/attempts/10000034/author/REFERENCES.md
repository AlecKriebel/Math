Inspected Theorems 1 and 3, the opening definitions and the square-lattice proof discussion. Its extension is a non-coexistence statement; it does not assert the target transfer implication. The general random-map proof is not independently re-certified here.

[GHZ] Alexander Glazman, Matan Harel and Nathan Zelesko, *Planar percolation and the loop O(n) model*, [arXiv:2508.20917v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.20917v3), revised 19 April 2026.

The target does not grant those additional hypotheses. The whole paper is not independently audited.

## Related past and future coefficient argument

Weihua Liu, *Extended de Finetti theorems for boolean independence and monotone independence*, [arXiv:1505.02215v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.02215v1), [inspected PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1505.02215v1), submitted 9 May 2015. Later publication: *Trans. Amer. Math. Soc.* 370(3) (2018), 1959–2003, [DOI 10.1090/tran/7034](https://doi.org/10.1090/tran/7034); publisher-deposited metadata records online publication on 24 October 2017.

The inspected §6.2, printed pp.32–34, and visually inspected p.33 establish the pertinent attribution. Lemma 6.8 gives a past/future shifted matrix-coefficient identity using bounded Kaplansky approximation. Its assumptions involve a bilateral spreadable sequence of bounded variables, a non-degenerated normal state (faithful GNS representation, not necessarily a faithful state), the specified nonunital-generation condition, and a normal bilateral shift automorphism. Lemma 6.9 and Proposition 6.10 treat shift expectations; Remark 6.11 gives an extension limitation. These passages supply closely related method prior art; they do not state the exact DKW D-generation theorem. The whole paper was not audited. The publisher PDF returned HTTP 403, so lemma numbering and hypotheses here refer to arXiv v1.

## Bounded search and novelty limits

No earlier theorem in the complete arbitrary-unital-A, arbitrary-symmetric-state generality was located. This is a bounded negative search result, not a priority claim or a certificate of worldwide openness. Exact-title, question-number, author, tail-generation, correction/erratum, and nonfaithful/nonnormal searches were used, with primary-source checks at the following limited scopes:

- [KMS quantum symmetric states](https://arxiv.org/abs/1609.01225): abstract, classification setup, tail section, and expectation hypotheses; quantum-symmetric scope.
- [Semi-Cosimplicial Objects and Spreadability](https://arxiv.org/abs/1508.03168), v3: introduction, Theorem 4.5, DKW citation, and tail-term search; algebraic spreadability scope.
- [Markovianity and the Thompson Monoid F+](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.14811), v4: introduction, §2.3, DKW citation and references; faithful-normal setting.
- [General de Finetti type theorems in noncommutative probability](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.05651), v2: Theorem 1.2, §2 conventions and §5 expectations; free faithful-state and stronger Boolean settings.
- [Full classification of de Finetti type theorems for *-random variables in classical and free probability](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16170), v1: introduction, Theorems 2, 3 and 6.3, §2, citation and full-text keyword checks; Theorems 2 and 3 explicitly require a faithful normal state.
- [Tail algebras for monotone and q-deformed exchangeable stochastic processes](https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.04323), v1: introduction, Propositions 3.4 and 3.6, Theorem 3.9 and §4 statements; concrete-system scope.
- [Semi-Cosimplicial Hilbert Spaces with Isometric Coface Operators](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.27373), v1: abstract, introduction, Theorem 2.14 and full-HTML keyword checks; Hilbert-space framework.

These are not full-paper audits. Historical author lists and citation discovery were incomplete; no exhaustive forward-citation graph, inaccessible publisher-version comparison, or author confirmation was obtained. Unindexed, unpublished, differently formulated, revised, or inaccessible work may have been missed. Novelty remains unverified.

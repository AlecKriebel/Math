## Scope and status

No separability, trace, faithfulness, factoriality, quantum symmetry, or normal extension of F is assumed. D retains its recursive unital C*-algebra definition. The proof shows that its first-stage ordinary finite-word moments already generate T as a von Neumann algebra; it does not identify D with a first-stage C*-algebra.

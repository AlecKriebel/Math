# Scoped prior elasticity gap and endpoint audit

For Ball 2002 Problem 4 (2000004 / AMR-019-0004), the accepted prior result is the Almi–Krömer–Molchanova Lavrentiev gap on two disconnected reference components with partial Dirichlet data and Ciarlet–Nečas admissibility. Its energy is homogeneous in the material sense, polyconvex, and has continuity-forcing growth. The accepted ranges are d=2, p>2, 1<q<p/(p−2), and d=3, 3<p<4, 2<q<p/(p−2).

The printed two-dimensional endpoint competitor has infinite inverse-Jacobian energy. This audit identifies a failed construction/proof step, not a false endpoint theorem. It makes no unqualified claim to solve the connected-domain, boundary-only interpretation of Ball's problem, and does not replace CN admissibility by everywhere-injective homeomorphisms.

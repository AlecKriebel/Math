# Partial result for the singleton truncated congruence problem

The general question remains unresolved by this work.

A positive theorem is proved when the canonical forbidden residues obey one of finitely many identities q a = p n + c, with fixed integer triples and q positive, except possibly at a reciprocal-summable set of moduli. The logarithmic density then exists and equals the decreasing limit of densities of the finite survivor sieves.

This allows overlapping moduli, divergent reciprocal sums, unbounded residues, bounded offsets from rational proportions of the modulus, and noninvertible affine congruence parameters. For the entirely patterned class, any finite activation threshold can be assigned separately to each modulus without changing the logarithmic density.

The proof reduces restricted multiples to the classical Davenport–Erdős theorem on finitely many prime-valuation and residue strata at a time. An explicit tail bound justifies the limiting argument. A separate cutoff argument and exhaustive finite identity checks support the reductions. No claim of novelty or full solution is made.

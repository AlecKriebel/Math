# Sources and inspection scope

All listed sources were checked on 6 October 2026. Retrieved source documents are excluded from the authored review package.

## S1. Ben Green, 100 Open Problems

Author-hosted PDF: https://people.maths.ox.ac.uk/greenbj/papers/open-problems.pdf

Inspected Problem 75 and its comments on printed page 36, and the Pinchasi and Conlon-Lim bibliography entries. Page 36 was also rendered and visually inspected. The exact target matches the supplied finite-set question. The retrieved text's sentence about threshold 2 is not established by Pinchasi's displayed D(G)=2 example. Together with the dated status statements in S3-S4, it presents an apparent source discrepancy. No definitive correction, proof of bound 2, or exclusion of a stronger example is claimed. The PDF's own introductory footnote identifies its most recent update as December 2025; no revision date is inferred from search-engine dates.

## S2. Rom Pinchasi, Lines with Many Points on Both Sides

Discrete & Computational Geometry 30 (2003), 415-435.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-003-2826-8
Institutional bibliographic record: https://cris.technion.ac.il/en/publications/lines-with-many-points-on-both-sides/
Author's publication page: https://sites.google.com/view/homepage-of-rom-pinchasi/home
Author-linked manuscript: https://drive.google.com/file/d/1lrOLgVoLd7NFTNDs-Az2BaBfQ8lLOgfZ/view

Retrieved the 19-page author-linked manuscript. Inspected definitions of determined line, open-half-plane discrepancy and D(G), Theorem 1.2, the discrepancy implication on manuscript page 2, and Conjecture 7.1 on manuscript page 19. Manuscript page 2 was rendered and visually inspected, confirming the displayed equality D(G)=2 for Alon's example. The manuscript numbering differs from the journal pagination. No full independent audit of Pinchasi's lengthy theorem proof was performed.

## S3. David Conlon and Jeck Lim, Everywhere unbalanced configurations

Advances in Mathematics 480 (2025), Part A, article 110445.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2025.110445
arXiv record: https://arxiv.org/abs/2308.02466
Author-hosted PDF: https://www.its.caltech.edu/~dconlon/pseudolines.pdf

Inspected the introduction, definitions and Theorems 1.1-1.3, and the publication metadata. The retrieved author manuscript has 28 pages. These sections explicitly distinguish pseudolines from straight lines, restate Pinchasi's upper bound, and describe the known straight-line lower bound as 2. The full construction proof was not independently replayed.

## S4. Bernardo Subercaseaux, Ethan Mackey, Long Qian, and Marijn J. H. Heule, Automated Symmetric Constructions in Discrete Geometry

arXiv version inspected: https://arxiv.org/html/2506.00224v1
PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.00224v1
Authors' accompanying repository: https://github.com/bsubercaseaux/automatic-symmetries

Inspected the abstract, Introduction's everywhere-unbalanced-points subsection, the scope of the realizability distinction, and the authors' repository description. The paper reports a 21-point odd 2-everywhere-unbalanced configuration and says examples for k>=3 remain unknown. The associated SAT computation, minimality claim and coordinates were not independently validated here. They are not a computational certificate for this investigation.

Publication-status supplement checked during independent review: Springer lists a 2026 proceedings chapter in *Intelligent Computer Mathematics* (CICM 2025), LNCS 16136, pp. 29-47, https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-032-07021-0_3 . The public publisher metadata and abstract were inspected; the subscription chapter was not inspected in full. The mathematical status statement above is attributed specifically to the inspected arXiv v1.

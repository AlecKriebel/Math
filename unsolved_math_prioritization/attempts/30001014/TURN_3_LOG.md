# Third substantive author turn

This turn constructs a connected compact metrizable pathological spectrum for the explicitly infinite variant. The mapping torus of an inner free-generator automorphism has a diagonal whose spectrum consists of real-line orbit suspensions accumulating on a common infinity circle; connectedness is proved using their closures. It is not asserted to be an interval.

An interior ideal carries a compactly supported source kernel witness. Ideal-specific maximal-tensor injectivity is proved by extending commuting nondegenerate representations, and the threshold norm is detected by interior evaluation. Quotients then preserve a nonzero extra commutant element for every larger norm. This avoids invalid arbitrary-subalgebra tensor injectivity and unsupported intermediate-norm fiber identifications. Exact finite controls pass. General infinite compact realization remains unresolved3/5.

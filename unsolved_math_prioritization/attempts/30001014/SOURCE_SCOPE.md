## Original question and definitions

The primary source is Simon Wassermann, **MASAs in C*-algebras**, within *C*-Algebras*, Oberwolfach Report37/2008, printed2149–2151. [Official report PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46180); [publisher record](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/2048), DOI10.4171/OWR/2008/37. The report's volume year is2008; EMS records online publication30June2009. Both the official PDF and the initially retrieved TIB repository edition were read, and printed2150/PDF56 was visually checked in both. Their file bytes differ; their relevant mathematical text agrees.

On printed2149, a MASA means a maximal abelian C*-subalgebra. Tensor product without a norm subscript means the minimal C*-tensor product. Proposition4 on printed2150 concerns a unital C*-algebra A and a MASA C such that C⊗C is not maximal abelian in A⊗_β A for every C*-norm β at least a particular norm α, including β=max.

Question4 asks whether **any compact Hausdorff space** can be the spectrum of such a C. No infinitude, absence-of-isolated-points, metrizability, separability or connectedness restriction is written in that question. It also reports that the interval [0,1] occurs. The interval construction is **announced, not supplied** in the report, and is not independently verified here. The adjacent PartII concerns spectra of MASAs in the CAR algebra; that is a separate project and does not assert the nonminimal-tensor pathology.

Formally, the literal universal assertion requires that for every nonempty compact Hausdorff X there exist A and a MASA C isomorphic to C(X) for which C⊗C fails to be a MASA in a C*-completion of A⊙A. The stronger Proposition4 form also requires failure for every β>=α. A counterexample to even the weaker existential-completion assertion refutes the literal universal assertion. The natural **infinite-spectrum realization** question must remain separately identified if finite spaces are excluded as an intended implicit convention.

## The cited construction and positive theorem

Wassermann, *Tensor products of maximal abelian subalgebras of C*-algebras*, Glasgow Math.J.50(2008),209–216, DOI10.1017/S0017089508004151. [Published primary PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/26C8EB9971A8C7152239D44DA0E4CC9A/S0017089508004151a.pdf/tensor-products-of-maximal-abelian-subalgberas-of-c-algebras.pdf); [arXiv0711.0131v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/0711.0131v2), revised27November2007. The complete ten-page arXiv proof was read; the published version's Proposition4 and final questions were checked separately.

Its positive theorem says that the tensor product remains a MASA for every C*-norm if one factor has the Kadison–Singer extension property and the other contains an approximate identity for its ambient algebra. The extension property includes unique pure-state extension and, for a nonunital ambient algebra, exclusion of states annihilating the MASA. It is a property of the inclusion, not merely of an abstract spectrum.

The negative construction is A=C*_r(F2)+K(l²(F2)) in B(l²(F2)), with C generated by the rank-one coordinate projections and1. Thus C is the unitization of c0 on a countable set, and its spectrum is the one-point compactification of a countably infinite discrete space. Proposition4 supplies the pathology via a nonzero kernel of the map from a suitable tensor completion to the minimal completion. The proof uses the non-minimal continuity of the left/right regular representation as a credited classical input; no new proof of that input is claimed. The later nonunital variant is different from Question4's compact-spectrum realization and does not permit a hidden change of quantifiers.

## Current literature and related questions

The historical neighboring Kadison–Singer question is positively resolved by [Marcus–Spielman–Srivastava, Annals182(2015)](https://annals.math.princeton.edu/2015/182-1/p08). That theorem concerns the atomic MASA in B(l²); it cannot be promoted to a realization or impossibility theorem for all embeddings of every compact spectrum.

[Paper-folding models for the CAR algebra](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/ergodic-theory-and-dynamical-systems/article/paperfolding-models-for-the-car-algebra/F814D90D0D497D7568B475F9D5588C1F), Kopsacheilis–Winter, published online16June2026, constructs nonstandard Cantor-spectrum C*-diagonals in the CAR algebra (TheoremsA–B, introduction2542–2545). Its introduction and those statements were read. These are nuclear ambient algebras and unique-extension diagonals, so the result addresses a different spectrum/conjugacy problem; it does not produce Wassermann's nonminimal tensor pathology.

# Independent details for the accepted scoped theorem

This note supplies the operator-algebra details and global arguments used in the audit. It is an authored derivation; the accompanying finite code is not its proof.

## A. The central extension

Let V be the finite-support F2-valued functions on N0. For a,b in V put

    F(a,b)_i = sum over j != i of a_i b_j.

The map is additive in each input and has support contained in supp(a). For x=(a,b,c), y=(d,e,f), define

    xy = (a+d,b+e,c+f+F(a,e)).

The central coordinate of (xy)z and x(yz) agrees because

    F(a,e)+F(a+d,h) = F(d,h)+F(a,e+h).

The identity is zero, and x^{-1}=(a,b,c+F(a,b)); both products with x are zero. All commutators lie in {0} x {0} x V, which is central. For coordinate vectors, direct substitution yields [p_i,q_j]=z_i when i != j, and identity otherwise. Thus the nilpotence class is exactly two, since these commutators are nonidentity. Squaring x leaves only F(a,b) in the central coordinate, so fourth powers are identity.

The subgroup generated by p_i,q_i has four elements: its multiplication never creates a central coordinate. If I has at least two members, each z_i with i in I is a commutator using one other member of I. The p_i, q_i, z_i generate every triple supported in I: form its a and b coordinates and then correct the finite central error. Conversely support never escapes I. This proves all local subgroup descriptions, even for infinite I.

## B. Subgroup expectations without an intersection shortcut

For a countable group G let M=lambda(G)'' on l2(G). Right translations commute with M. The vector delta_e is cyclic for right translations and therefore separating for M. Thus tau(x)=<x delta_e,delta_e> is faithful; it is a normal tracial state, as checked first on the group algebra and extended by normality.

Fix a subgroup H. Let P_H project onto l2(H). Compression x -> P_H x restricted to l2(H) is a normal unital completely positive map. It commutes with the right regular representation of H, because x does and l2(H) reduces these right translations. The commutant of the right regular representation on l2(H) is L(H); hence the compressed operator belongs to L(H).

The inclusion j_H:L(H)->M is normal: decompose l2(G) over right cosets Ht. On each summand, left multiplication by H is unitarily the regular representation on l2(H). Thus j_H is the diagonal amplification of that representation. Composing compression with j_H gives a normal unital completely positive map E_H:M->L(H). It fixes L(H), is L(H)-bimodular, and preserves tau. On a group unitary, its value is lambda(g) if g belongs to H, and zero otherwise. In particular,

    E_H(x) delta_e = P_H(x delta_e).

The identity holds for every x in M by normality and matrix coefficients, not just finite sums. The separating property of delta_e now proves the Fourier-support criterion:

    x belongs to L(H) if and only if x delta_e belongs to l2(H).

Indeed, in the reverse direction E_H(x) and x have the same vector at delta_e, so they are equal. For any family (H_t), the intersection of l2(H_t) is l2(intersection H_t), coefficientwise. Applying the criterion in both directions gives

    intersection_t L(H_t) = L(intersection_t H_t).

Similarly E_H E_K = E_{H intersect K}: they have the same action on every Fourier coefficient and delta_e is separating. These conclusions require no unjustified exchange of algebraic and von Neumann closure.

## C. Faithful random variables and symmetry

Take the group in A. It is countable, so the representation space is separable. A von Neumann algebra represented on a separable Hilbert space has separable predual: its predual is the quotient of the separable trace-class space by its preannihilator. Each four-element site subgroup is C2 x C2. Its group algebra is a four-dimensional commutative von Neumann algebra; the trace assigns weight 1/4 to each minimal spectral projection. Its regular inclusion is injective, normal, unital and trace preserving. The expectations in B condition every local algebra. Both source and ambient modular groups are the identity because the states are traces.

Every coordinate permutation preserves F. The unitary delta_g -> delta_{beta(g)} implements its normal state-preserving automorphism and permutes the random-variable maps themselves. This proves all joint moment identities directly. A coordinate injection likewise defines a group isomorphism onto the subgroup of triples supported on its image. The corresponding regular inclusions are normal by the coset argument in B. In particular the shift is an injective state-preserving normal endomorphism. Arbitrary permutations establish exchangeability, and increasing injections establish spreadability and one-sided stationarity.

## D. Infinite tail, witness and disjoint independence

Let H_I be generated by the prescribed sites, and K_I be all triples supported in I. Every tail [n,infinity) has at least two indices, so H_[n,infinity)=K_[n,infinity). A finitely supported triple in all such subgroups is identity. Part B gives a scalar von Neumann tail.

For I={0,1} and J={0,2}, the intersection H_I intersect H_J is K_{0}, an elementary abelian group of order eight. The singleton H_{0} has order four and omits z_0. Consequently

    L(H_I) intersect L(H_J) = L(K_{0}) strictly contains L(H_{0}).

The unitary lambda(z_0) is an explicit witness, obtained as a commutator from either pair. Its conditional expectation onto the singleton is zero, but it is fixed by the expectations onto both pairs.

If I and J are disjoint, their support subgroups intersect only at identity. Thus E_{H_I}(y)=tau(y)1 for y in L(H_J), by B and normality. For x in L(H_I), bimodularity and trace preservation give tau(xy)=tau(x)tau(y). This includes arbitrary infinite index sets and arbitrary bounded elements. It makes no assertion of commutation or free independence.

For the canonical family, binary joins and increasing exhaustion of all sites are immediate from generation. Any purported factorization preserving the prescribed singleton algebras would, by binary joins, have exactly these two pair algebras. Its meet requirement is therefore impossible.

## E. Verified enlargement

Define C_I=L(K_I) for every I, including empty sets and singletons. Since support intersections are exact, B gives C_I intersect C_J=C_{I intersect J}. The union of the explicit p,q,z generators gives C_I vee C_J=C_{I union J}. Disjoint support gives the same scalar independence as D. Increasing sets exhausting N0 eventually contain each generator, so their algebras generate M. All algebras are trace-conditioned. Coordinate permutations send C_I to C_sigma(I).

Each C_{i} is an eight-dimensional commutative algebra. For at least two indices C_I equals the canonical A_I; only the singleton algebras change. Thus the exact obstruction cannot be strengthened to prohibit arbitrary enlargements, even permutation-covariant ones.

Finally, the construction can use one bounded selfadjoint variable at each site. If X_i=p_i+2q_i, its spectrum is {-3,-1,1,3}; sign(X_i)=q_i and X_i-2 sign(X_i)=p_i. Hence X_i generates the same four-dimensional prescribed site algebra and changes none of the conclusions.

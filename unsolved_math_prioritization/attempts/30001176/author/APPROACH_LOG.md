# Author approach and stopping decision

## Intake and source check

The target was recovered from the complete supplied record and cross-checked against the official OWR PDF, Köstler's Definition 1 and Conjecture 5, printed pp. 521–522. Its cited definition of a minimal random sequence and its tail is in the de Finetti preprint, Definition 1.6. The live problem URL returned HTTP 403 locally and was inaccessible through web retrieval; no claim of live-page inspection is made. Both the full review hash and statement hash matched the intake values. Intake literature triage is not a proof or an earlier attempt.

The report's independence requirement is weaker than tensor independence or free independence, and its additional Boolean meet identity is essential. The cited de Finetti theorem supplies disjoint conditional independence. It does not alone establish the needed intersection for overlapping sets. The 2026 preprint's stronger block-singleton condition likewise is not a stated Boolean-intersection theorem. In the tracial counterexample, block-singleton factorization is already consistent with scalar independence and cyclicity of the trace.

## Substantive approach 1: local central information exposed by a second site

Use a countable, explicitly defined bilinear central extension over F_2. Each site has commuting involutions p_i,q_i, while a cross-site commutator [p_i,q_j] exposes a central coordinate z_i that the i-th site alone cannot generate. The multiplication law preserves support, so removing all initial indices removes every nontrivial group element from the tail. Regular group traces and Fourier support make the required W*-hypotheses and the infinite tail conclusion transparent.

Outcome: a complete candidate counterexample to the canonical filtration, with a concrete central unitary in A_{0,1} intersect A_{0,2} but not in A_0. The singleton-preserving impossibility follows immediately from the join axiom. The sequence can also be represented by one bounded selfadjoint four-valued operator at every site.

All other factorization axioms are verified. A larger singleton algebra restores a factorization, making the interpretation caveat mathematically real rather than cosmetic. The proof does not impose an ambient factor assumption, which Conjecture 5 does not contain; that assumption belongs to the separate Question 3 in the same report.

## Early stop and acceptance boundary

Stop after one substantive construction because it gives a full counterexample within the stated canonical/singleton-preserving scope. No additional speculative approaches are needed. A fresh reviewer must independently check the source interpretation, the group law and local subgroup structure, all von Neumann hypotheses, the infinite tail proof, and the package. Until that review, status remains an author claim, not an accepted resolution.

If “gives rise” is instead interpreted to permit arbitrary enlargement of the site algebras, this packet establishes only the canonical obstruction and does not settle that different formulation. The explicit repaired family in PROOF.md prevents hiding this limitation.

Finite exact checks are diagnostics, not a substitute for the proof. Existing de Finetti and group-algebra machinery is credited. No historical novelty is asserted, and bounded negative literature/repository searches are not exhaustive absence proofs.

## Primary mathematical literature

1. C. L. Stewart, *Exceptional units and cyclic resultants*, Acta Arithmetica 155 (2012), 407–418, DOI https://doi.org/10.4064/aa155-4-5 . Author-hosted copy: https://uwaterloo.ca/pure-mathematics/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/aa155-4-05.pdf . Section 9 and printed pp. 416–417 give small-degree computations, e(d)=d for 1≤d≤6, e(7)<7, e(8)≥7, and the conjecture. The paper's degree-seven computation concerns all monic integer polynomials, including reducible ones; that must not be confused with exact algebraic degree. Its reported computation for degree-eight polynomials with constant term ±1 reaches prefix seven, rather than an asserted all-degree resolution.
2. C. L. Stewart, *Exceptional units and cyclic resultants, II*, Contemporary Mathematics 587 (2013), 191–200. Author-hosted copy: https://uwaterloo.ca/pure-mathematics/sites/default/files/uploads/documents/exceptional_units-5_0.pdf . Sections 5–6 discuss roots of unity and a bounded-coefficient computational table. Table 1 supplies the degree-7, degree-8, and degree-9 lower-bound witness polynomials used here. That table is restricted to coefficients in {−1,0,1}; its maxima are not by themselves upper bounds for e(d).

The catalogue's linked-paper author labels “Hare–Mossinghoff” are incorrect: both linked papers are authored by C. L. Stewart. Hare is acknowledged for computational assistance, and Mossinghoff appears in cited related work. The report's DOI and report year are 2012 even though the catalogue citation labels the report 2013.

## Current-status and novelty limitations

Targeted searches for the exact conjecture, “e(7)”, “e(8)”, “e(9)”, exceptional units, cyclic resultants, and Chinese-remainder approaches found the primary Stewart sources and newer work on other exceptional-unit questions. No verified later resolution of the all-degree conjecture was located. This is a bounded literature check, not proof that the problem remains globally open or that the scoped equalities are historically new.

The original work claimed here consists of the presented exact finite certificate and elementary reductions. Known witness examples and source conjecture are credited. No downloaded source document, copied full text, or source image is part of this deliverable.

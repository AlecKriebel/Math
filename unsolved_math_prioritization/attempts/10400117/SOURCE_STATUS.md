## 2. Verification of the known witness

### Rational homology spheres and distinct homeomorphism types

For a lens space L(p,q) with coprime p,q, H₁ is cyclic of order p. Here both groups are Z/25. In particular the first rational Betti number is zero; closed oriented three-manifold duality gives the remaining rational homology groups of S³.

The usual unoriented lens-space homeomorphism condition is q′≡±q^(±1) mod p. The possibilities generated by q=4 modulo 25 are

    4, 21, 19, 6,

because 4⁻¹≡19 mod 25. They do not include 9. Thus the two spaces are not even unoriented-homeomorphic. Their non-homeomorphism is also stated explicitly in the primary corollary cited below.

### Equal complete LMO invariants

Bar-Natan and Lawrence, [*A Rational Surgery Formula for the LMO Invariant*](https://www.math.toronto.edu/drorbn/papers/RationalSurgery/RationalSurgery.pdf), Proposition 5.1 and Corollary 5.2, printed manuscript pp. 19–20, give a complete graph-valued formula of the form

    Ẑ^LMO(L(p,q)) = W(p) exp[−S(q/p) θ/48].

Here W(p)=〈Ω_x, Ω_x⁻¹Ω_(x/p)〉_x depends only on p, θ is the theta diagram and S is their Dedekind symbol. They explicitly use the same pair and compute

    S(4/25)=S(9/25)=48/25.

Thus the **entire** LMO invariants agree, not just their Casson–Walker terms or a finite-degree truncation. Both first-homology orders are also the same, so a common normalization factor depending on that order does not affect the comparison.

For an elementary exact check, their symbol for positive coprime p,q is 12 times the classical Dedekind sum

    s(q,p)=Σ_(j=1)^(p−1) ((j/p)) ((qj/p)),

where ((x))=x−floor(x)−1/2 for nonintegral x and is zero for integral x. Direct rational summation gives s(4,25)=s(9,25)=4/25. The accompanying verifier checks this and the modular homeomorphism obstruction.

### Unequal quantum invariants

The exact inequality at r=5 is stated in the Hansen–Takata remark itself, in the same quantum-invariant convention as Problem 7.2. SU(2) satisfies the required group hypotheses. The level is allowed: it corresponds to WZW level k=r−2=3.

As an additional primary-source check, Freed–Gompf, [*Computer Calculation of Witten's 3-Manifold Invariant*](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/freedgompf.pdf), Commun. Math. Phys. 141 (1991), 79–117, discuss this pair on p. 100 and give different values in Table 3 on p. 102 at k=3. Their table is a rounded numerical corroboration, **not the exact certificate used here**, and their normalization is not silently identified with a numerical formula for τ₅. The exact level-specific separation used for this source-status finding is the original proposers' statement. No new modular-matrix computation is claimed in this package.

Together, these primary statements and elementary checks verify the already-known witness required by the literal problem.

## 3. Distinction from adjacent Problem 7.5

The earlier campaign target 10400120 is Ohtsuki **Conjecture 7.5**, concerning whether the absolute value of a nonzero quantum invariant depends only on the fundamental group. Its package concerns SU(5), L(5,1) and L(5,2), and unequal nonzero absolute values. It makes no LMO-equality assertion for the pair used here.

Problem 7.2 instead compares quantum invariants to LMO, and only requires different complex values. Equal absolute values would not invalidate its witness. Therefore neither the adjacency of the source numbering nor the earlier campaign calculation makes these targets identical. This audit credits the original Hansen–Takata/Bar-Natan–Lawrence example rather than treating the SU(5) calculation as a new solution to Problem 7.2.

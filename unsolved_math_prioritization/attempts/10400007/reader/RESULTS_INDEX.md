This is not an obstruction to arbitrary higher-rank representations or all local systems.
- [Minimal neutral-charge calculation](pass1_configuration/NEUTRAL_CHARGE_EXTENSION.md): universal shifted groups of rank (n−1)! in the specified sector. Knot-independent graded groups do not rule out knot-sensitive enriched operations.
- [Four-particle nonminimal-neutral calculation](pass2_configuration/NONMINIMAL_NEUTRAL_FOUR_PARTICLES.md): for (1,1,−1,−1), ordinary dimensions 2,4,8,6 in degrees 4,5,6,7 and Borel–Moore dimensions 6,8,4,2 in degrees 5,6,7,8. - [Blanchfield enrichment](pass2_configuration/ENRICHED_CONFIGURATION_PAIRING.md): a genuine intrinsic secondary pairing distinguishes trefoil from mirror. - [Higher-rank finite-field family](pass2_configuration/HIGHER_RANK_FINITE_FIELD_TEST.md): the natural meridian-trace-two SL₂(F₇) representation family has one verified Kinoshita–Terasaka torsion member that cannot occur for any unknot member. This crosses the classical Alexander-one limitation; no Jones extraction from the family is proved. Its Wada-derived fixture is external and not included.

## Accepted scoped descent results

- [Full-complex Euler obstruction](pass1_descent/DESCENT_OBSTRUCTION_AND_BRIDGE.md): the complete plat configuration-chain complexes cannot be related by the claimed stabilization equivalences, and a finite tensor factor does not repair this particular tower. Relative groups, selected classes, subquotients and correspondences are outside that obstruction.

The crossed plat stabilization leaves the separated chamber, so this alone is not a complete descent construction.
- [Independent geometric vacuum check](pass2_descent/AU_DIRECT_VACUUM_CHECK.md): independently explains the direct geometric operations and the necessary stabilization scalar. The complete theorem and final audit discharge its historical list of global proof obligations.

## Accepted scoped Floer results

[PASS1 Floer analysis](pass1_floer/REPORT.md) distinguishes the source's braid-level quantum theory from a knot-invariant complex. It proves the specified filled-divisor extension obstruction and shows that inverting both deformation variables makes the two-variable weights pure gauge. Its mirror and specialization conventions are explicit.

[PASS2 Floer analysis](pass2_floer/REPORT.md), checked by [independent validation](pass2_validation/VALIDATION_REPORT.md), uses actual exact geometric generators for two Markov-equivalent unknot braids. The quantum complexes have ranks 3 and 5 because each preserved grading occurs once, not merely because the Euler polynomials have that many terms. The full Rees complexes have arrows V versus V and UV³. Over any nonzero commutative unital F₂[U,V]-algebra, quasi-isomorphic derived base changes for this pair force both U and V to be units. The accepted pure-gauge argument then removes the divisor-weight refinement.

This is a theorem about that coefficient-only repair of the specified complexes. It does not rule out all Floer theories, geometric relative repairs or intrinsic local-system constructions. The analytic comparison and positivity results used to determine arrows are established source inputs; no independent holomorphic-disc enumeration is claimed.

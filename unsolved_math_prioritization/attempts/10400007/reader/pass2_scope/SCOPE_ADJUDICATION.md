## 2. The exact strengthened definition

For level k, write W_k(Σ) for AU horizontal sections and H_k(Σ)=H₀(Teich(Σ);L_k) for ordinary complex local-system homology. Let θ_Σ:W_k(Σ)→H_k(Σ) be the canonical parallel-transport identification.

For a relative Heegaard presentation E_K=H_g∪_f C of the peripheral exterior, the new definition is

    h_k^geom(K) = θ_T[s_k^(1−g) p_C W_k(f_0) v_D].

Here every ingredient is specified before invoking the RT exterior invariant:

- v_D is the vacuum sphere unit sewn through the all-zero channels of a handlebody disk system.
- p_C is inverse geometric sewing, projection to the zero channels of the compression disks, and vacuum propagation to the boundary torus.
- W_k(f_0) is the geometric extended-surface action of the actual gluing diffeomorphism.
- s_k is the vacuum coordinate of the geometric torus interchange with the weight-zero S³-calibrated lift. Its inverse compensates each nonseparating compression.
- The fundamental filling is the label-1 coordinate after meridian factorization, followed by θ_T inverse. Its one-dimensional gauge cancels against the unknot denominator.

The important difference is the order of definition and proof. PASS1 introduced h by applying an isomorphism to Z_RT(E_K). PASS2 specifies the expression above geometrically and then proves equality with the known state. The comparison does not retroactively turn this into the earlier definition.

AU's sewing is an actual geometric construction on flat sections, not an operation supplied solely by the RT comparison. Theorem 12.1 constructs it; Theorem 12.2 removes coordinate and complex-structure choices; Definitions 12.1–12.2 include the unstable cases; Theorems 12.3–12.4 give the needed naturality and commutation. ## 3. Why the construction now meets the intrinsic request

The relevant three-dimensional output is the homology class of the peripheral exterior, with its meridian filling functional. It is not merely a number attached to a selected Heegaard diagram. An ambient oriented equivalence transports the exterior, peripheral marking and any chosen decomposition; geometric surface naturality transports the defining expression. The proved independence of the decomposition then makes the output intrinsic to the advertised knot-pair input.

Using relative Heegaard data is not the same as leaving the answer dependent on that presentation. The accepted contract permits auxiliary decompositions to compute intrinsic objects. A proof by comparison with a known functor is a legitimate proof of independence; the source does not require every relation to be reproved without existing quantum topology.

The knot dependence is also located explicitly. It is the position of the vacuum under the geometric gluing action, followed by the compression projections. Knots may share an isomorphic boundary homology space while supplying different distinguished classes in it. An invariant extracted from classes and operations is not required to be encoded in the unadorned isomorphism type of its group.

The local system is genuine and geometrically prescribed. On the contractible marked Teichmüller base, H₀ identifies parallel fibers; mapping-class equivariance and the geometric sewing structure retain the nontrivial information. Thus H₀ provides a degree-zero homological realization of the geometric construction. It does not create additional higher-homology information, and no such claim is needed.

The full polynomial, rather than just selected evaluations, is recovered by the proved finite interpolation. A diagram supplies only an independent support bound. Requiring an intrinsic explanation of polynomiality without that comparison or bound would import the separate concerns of the neighboring Problem 1.4 into this target.

## 4. Dependency and novelty limits

The definition relies on prior geometric conformal-block theory. Its proof of independence and Jones equality relies on the AU modular-functor comparison and RT cobordism relations. AU's Theorem 1.1 supplies a structure-preserving comparison, and its introduction explicitly describes the resulting geometric WRT construction. [AU comparison, arXiv:1110.5027v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.5027v2)

These are disclosed mathematical dependencies, not failures of intrinsicness. The result is a reconstruction of established CFT/WRT theory with an explicit degree-zero local-system formulation and polynomial extraction. This assessment does not claim those ingredients, the invariant, or their substantive geometric content are newly discovered.

Two limits remain:

1. This does not supply a local system directly on the knot complement or a configuration space of points in it. It is not an Alexander-style positive-degree complement-homology theorem.
2. This is an affirmative construction within a source-supported meaning of an informal question. It does not prove that this is the unique intended interpretation or that Roberts or the literature has historically credited this precise presentation as a solution.

Neither limit is an identified unmet condition of the source's stated broad request. A stronger complement-space or nontrivial-higher-homology problem could be worthwhile, but it would need to be stated separately. Absence of historical recognition is not a mathematical objection, and lack of novelty is not a reason to leave a completed construction unresolved.

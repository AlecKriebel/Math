# Primary sources and their roles

The following citations support the authored mathematical reports. Source documents and extracted source text are not redistributed. Available byte counts, hashes, inspection locations and retrieval dates are verification metadata; they identify the inspected versions rather than certifying exhaustive literature coverage.

## Main construction

- T. Ohtsuki, editor, Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4, pp. 377–572. Problem 1.7 is on printed p. 385, the ambient-topological context on p. 383, and the normalization on p. 380. [Publisher PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.2140/gtm.2002.4.377).
- J. E. Andersen and K. Ueno, Geometric construction of modular functors from Conformal Field Theory, arXiv:math/0306235v2. Flat vacua systems, horizontal sections, geometric sewing, propagation, extended-surface naturality and analytic sewing convergence. [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0306235v2).
- J. E. Andersen and K. Ueno, Construction of the Witten–Reshetikhin–Turaev TQFT from conformal field theory, arXiv:1110.5027v2. Theorem 1.1 supplies the structure-preserving comparison; §3.2 fixes the skein and twist conventions. [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1110.5027v2).
- C. Blanchet, N. Habegger, G. Masbaum and P. Vogel, Topological quantum field theories derived from the Kauffman bracket, 1995. Colored splitting, reverse-handle normalization and torus core basis. [Primary paper](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/bhmv.pdf).
- P. Gilmer and G. Masbaum, Maslov index, Lagrangians, Mapping Class Groups and TQFT, arXiv:0912.4706v3. Extended-manifold weights, gluing and repeated-entry Maslov vanishing. [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0912.4706v3).
- L. H. Kauffman, State models and the Jones polynomial, 1987. The state expansion and writhe correction used for the finite support bound. [Primary paper](https://webhomes.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/kauffmanjones.pdf).
- P. Ginsparg, Applied Conformal Field Theory, equation (9.50a). Corroboration of the standard SU(2) modular S matrix. The inspection record supplies no retained PDF hash or size for this item; it is not used to define the geometric scalar. [Public lecture PDF](https://homes.psd.uchicago.edu/~sethi/Teaching/P483-F2003/ginsparg.pdf).

## Alternative approaches and comparison examples

- S. Bigelow, A homological definition of the Jones polynomial. Existing punctured-disc/plat intersection model; an important baseline, rather than a newly supplied intrinsic complement construction. [Author PDF](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~bigelow/publications/06.pdf).
- C. Anghel and A. Juhász, Quantum braid Floer homology, arXiv:2601.08805v4, dated 7 September 2026 in the inspected record. The manuscript's braid-level theory, specialization and Markov examples are treated with their version and mirror conventions explicit. [Versioned primary record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.08805v4).
- E. Babson, A. Björner, S. Linusson, J. Shareshian and V. Welker, Complexes of not i-connected graphs. The graph-complex input for the neutral-charge calculation is Proposition 2.1(i). [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9705219).
- S. Friedl and S. Vidussi, A vanishing theorem for twisted Alexander polynomials with applications to symplectic 4-manifolds. Higher-rank representations are outside the rank-one generic-acyclicity obstruction. [Author paper](https://friedl.app.uni-regensburg.de/papers/vanish-sw.pdf).
- S. Friedl and M. Powell, A calculation of Blanchfield pairings of 3-manifolds and knots, arXiv:1512.04603v2. The intrinsic secondary pairing and the exact matrix conventions are checked separately in the supplementary report. [Primary version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1512.04603v2).
- The Knot Atlas, K11n42. Public source for the externally supplied planar-diagram validation input. Copied fixture data are excluded; the package retains only authorized verification metadata and authored derived results. [Public source record](https://katlas.org/wiki/K11n42).

The reader does not claim that the absence of an entry on a historical solution list proves current openness, or that a title containing “intrinsic” establishes the exact construction claimed here.

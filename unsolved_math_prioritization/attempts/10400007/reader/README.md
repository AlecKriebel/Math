# The Jones polynomial through geometric local system homology

Roberts Problem 1.7 · problem 10400007 · research row 1786 · 14 October 2026

This reader gives an accepted affirmative construction within the documented broad source-supported reading of Roberts's question. It uses ordinary degree-zero local-system homology on surface Teichmüller spaces. The knot-exterior state and fundamental filling are defined geometrically before comparison with WRT theory; exact finite interpolation recovers the full source-normalized Jones polynomial for every smooth/tame oriented knot in oriented S³.

This is a synthesis of known CFT/WRT theory, with no historical-recognition or novelty claim. It is a knot theorem; it does not claim the full oriented-link extension or a positive-degree local system directly on the knot complement.

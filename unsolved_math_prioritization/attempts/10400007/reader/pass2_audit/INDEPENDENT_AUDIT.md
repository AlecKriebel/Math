## 2. Direct geometric exterior class: exact acceptance

    h_k^geom(K)=θ_T[D_k^(g−1) p_C W_k(f,0) v_D].

This definition contains no preassigned RT exterior state and no computed Jones scalar. The fundamental filling is independently defined by meridian factorization, label-1 projection and the normalized two-point block coordinate. A fixed nonzero rescaling of that one-dimensional coordinate cancels from the knot/unknot ratio.

## 3. Relative chamber construction

Over Q(Q,D), or Q(Q) after the Jones specialization, the two-puncture local chain complex has boundary (Q−1,Q−1). This concentration is not asserted integrally, where Q−1 is not invertible.

Choose the compact dual-coefficient cycle γ and the unique Borel–Moore η with intersection one. Künneth supplies the relative degree shift. The Borel–Moore map is open restriction followed by contraction of η; the opposite ordinary map is

    b ↦ (−1)^n j_*(b×γ).

Ordinary chains have compact images, so this pushforward needs no improper Borel–Moore pushforward. The product-intersection sign (−1)^n is cancelled by the displayed sign, proving the stated adjunction. The relative insertion/contraction are inverses in the specified relative group, not between the full configuration groups.

This is an accepted partial construction. It is not yet plat stabilization: the crossing mixing an old and a new puncture leaves the isolated product chamber. ## 4. Knot-sensitive enrichment and its exact limit

The Laurent-coefficient Alexander module with its Blanchfield pairing is a genuine intrinsic secondary local-system operation. The absolute-to-relative map, Poincaré–Lefschetz duality, inverse Bockstein and evaluation give a natural nonsingular Hermitian form valued in Q(u)/Q[u±1]. The field-localized acyclicity does not erase this torsion linking datum.

The adopted conjugate-first convention has matrix

    B=(1−u)(uA−Aᵀ)⁻¹

on coker(uA−Aᵀ). Direct algebra verifies BM=(1−u)I, bar(M)ᵀB=(u−1)u⁻¹I, and bar(B)ᵀ=B. Thus the presentation and sesquilinearity conventions are consistent.

For the displayed trefoil matrix, the cyclic generator has self-pairing u/(u²−u+1) modulo Laurent polynomials; the mirror has its negative. The standard −Aᵀ mirror matrix is congruent to −A by the explicit basis swap. An isometry would require f bar(f)=−1 in Q(ζ₆), whereas a nonzero f=a+bζ₆ has norm a²+ab+b²>0. The pairing therefore genuinely distinguishes the trefoil and its mirror.

The secondary intersection picture is also sound: compact bounding chains intersect the deck-translated diagonal graphs with finitely many nonzero counts. Quotienting the product cover by simultaneous translation gives the difference-character coefficient system. Division by an annihilating Laurent polynomial and reduction modulo the Laurent ring are essential; this is not a pairing on already-zero rational-function homology groups.

For Alexander-one knots the module and pairing vanish. The externally supplied K11n42 planar diagram is the concrete test against a full Jones extraction through this datum alone. The validator independently evaluated all 2,048 bracket states, obtaining writhe −1 and a nonconstant source-normalized Jones polynomial, and a 10×10 Fox–Alexander cofactor determinant −u⁵. The Laurent-unit determinant proves Δ=1. A trefoil fixture calibrated the conventions. The copied diagrams remain private external inputs, while the public authored report retains computed results, citations and hashes. This limitation applies only to that module-with-pairing, not all neutral configurations or higher-rank systems.

## 6. Accepted coefficient-only Floer obstruction

The final exact planar validation authenticates three generators for the first positive one-twist unknot braid and five for the positive two-twist unknot braid. Depending on the action-composition convention, the second is σ₁σ₂ or σ₂σ₁; these are conjugate and Markov-equivalent to the first. No conclusion depends on choosing one multiplication notation.

The exact PL maps have positively oriented rational triangles and fixed degree-one boundary, so they are homeomorphisms; smoothing preserves the certified transverse intersections. Source puncture/arc placement, q-anchor detours, local-system winding/discriminant gradings and signs were checked independently. Each (A,Q) grading occurs once, so the quantum differential vanishes and ranks are genuinely 3 and 5. Jones regrading leaves the same zero differential; the equal scalar Euler specializations do not identify the complexes.

For the full unknot knot-Floer differential, each nonzero-A block contains two generators and must be acyclic. The source's monotonicity of W fixes the direction. Because divisor exponents depend only on the endpoint B,C values, all contributing discs carry the same monomial. Consequently the first Rees complex has one V-arrow and a surviving free generator; the second has V and UV³ arrows and one surviving free generator. This deduction uses the published analytic comparison/positivity and standard unknot HFK, not an unperformed holomorphic-disc enumeration.

Let A be any nonzero commutative unital F₂[U,V]-algebra. If these two derived base changes are quasi-isomorphic, their derived residue-field specializations are isomorphic. The original finite free complexes are perfect, so this is legitimate even for nonflat quotients. At a residue field where v=0 the dimensions are 3 and 5; with v≠0 but u=0 they are 1 and 3. Therefore neither u nor v lies in any maximal ideal: both are units.

The accepted PASS1 pure-gauge change of basis then removes all weights for every braid. Hence a coefficient-only base change that repairs this Markov pair cannot preserve a nonunit divisor-weight refinement. This is an accepted theorem about the specified Rees complex over commutative coefficient algebras, not a statement about all Floer theories, geometric relative repairs or local-system constructions.

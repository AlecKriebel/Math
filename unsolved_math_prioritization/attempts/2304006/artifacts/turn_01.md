# Attempt1 — Extend the single-term Rouché proof

**Outcome: partial; no full resolution.**

Read the entire Makai–Turán 1963 proof and tried to extend its small-coefficient /
large-coefficient dichotomy to aHn+bHm. The Taylor majorant around -1/2 gives
criterion (2.1) in `PROOF.md`; the exact product bound around a real Hermite zero
gives criterion (2.2). Both prove actual zeros for their indicated coefficient
regions, including complex coefficients.

The missing step is a degree-independent covering of all (a,b) by suitable
regions/contours. A two-term sum can cancel at an arbitrary chosen point, so
neither coefficient's magnitude alone supplies the lower bound used in the
single-term proof. No claim is made that these criteria cover all parameters.

Completion estimate toward the unrestricted target: 10%, heuristic only.

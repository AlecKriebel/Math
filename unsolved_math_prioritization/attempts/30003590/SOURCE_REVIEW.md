# Source dependencies and scope of the ample-subvariety example

Problem 30003590 / OWR-15586-001 is Problem 1(1), printed p. 2633, in
Oberwolfach Report 43/2017. The report introduces ampleness for smooth
subvarieties of complex projective manifolds on p. 2632 and motivates the
question by higher codimension. Its wording does not supply a fresh
singular-ambient convention. The intended smooth-ambient problem remains
unresolved by this work.

## What this edition establishes

The full proof and audit show that the normal singular quadric threefold
X = V(x0*x3 − x1*x2) in complex projective four-space has numerical surface
space R^2, but its genuine positive cone of integral Ottem-ample surfaces
is exactly the positive diagonal ray R_{>0}(1,1). Effective non-diagonal
classes approach the ample hyperplane class, so the cone is not open in
the full numerical space. The smooth hyperplane also supplies the ray if
only smooth or only lci generators are permitted. This does not make X
smooth. The example has codimension one.

For a normal integral projective T over C of dimension n at least two,
the positive cone generated by all integral ample codimension-one centers
is the image of the ordinary ample-divisor cone under the numerical
Cartier-to-Weil map. It is open in the full cycle space exactly when that
map is surjective. Its injectivity gives the equivalent rank criterion.
The theorem does not impose a smooth-center restriction on arbitrary T.

The proof includes the complete optional elementary-transform Chern-test
argument already supplied and checked by the independent audit. No
mathematical correction was required. Neither a smooth-ambient
counterexample nor a general smooth higher-codimension openness theorem
is established. No claim that the original workshop problem is solved,
or that novelty or worldwide current problem status is certified, is made.

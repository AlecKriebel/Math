# Credited affirmative answer to Question 5.4

Let `p: Out(F_6) -> GL(6,Z)` be induced by abelianization. For the second Morita class `mu_2 in H_8(Out(F_6);Q)`, the answer is

`p_*(mu_2) != 0`.

This is the `k=2` case of Alexander Kupers, Jeremy Miller and Peter Patzt, **The Morita classes are nonzero**, [arXiv:2609.12951v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.12951v1), Theorem A and Section 5.1. Its introduction also explicitly identifies the Bridson–Vogtmann question. The arXiv submission is dated 11 September 2026; the downloaded v1 PDF has a 14 September 2026 date line. As checked on 1 October 2026, the author research page lists it as a preprint. We do not describe it as a published or community-certified theorem.

The queue's original source is the author-hosted edited-volume PDF, Chapter 20, Question 5.4, printed page 330 / PDF page 337. It asks about **homology**, with trivial rational coefficients. The class is in `Out`, while KMP first uses its established lift to `Aut`; the induced map to `GL` factors through `Out`, yielding precisely the requested conclusion.

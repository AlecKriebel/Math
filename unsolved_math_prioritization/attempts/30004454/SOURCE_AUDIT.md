# Primary-source readiness and version audit

Inspection date: 8 October 2026. This is a bounded source check, not an exhaustive literature census.

## Exact target source

Olga Varghese's contribution to Oberwolfach Report 16/2020, pp. 890–891, poses the finite-rank infinite-Coxeter automorphism quotient question. The surrounding definition uses a finite simplicial graph with all finite product orders represented as edges, including label 2.

- Report: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46853
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2020/16

The complete PDF was downloaded and its relevant contribution inspected by text extraction. Its older centerless-clique assertion is recorded as historical, not silently substituted for the corrected paper.

## Controlling current version

Olga Varghese, *Coxeter quotients of the automorphism group of a Coxeter group*, Algebraic & Geometric Topology 26(7) (2026), 2353–2362.

- Publisher article: https://msp.org/agt/2026/26-7/p01.xhtml
- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2140/agt.2026.26.2353
- Publisher PDF: https://msp.org/agt/2026/26-7/agt-v26-n7-p01-s.pdf
- Publication date confirmed on publisher page and PDF: 1 September 2026
- Publisher page also states: revised 16 May 2025; accepted 1 July 2025

The complete publisher PDF was downloaded; the exact conjecture, statements, and relevant proofs were inspected. Conjecture 1.2 remains the full target. The principal inputs used here are the finite-Out projection lemma, the finite-index clique-conjugating subgroup, and finite-index special automorphisms. The paper's odd-factor and even-group results are credited prior work.

## Bounded later-literature check

Exact-title searches, searches combining Coxeter automorphisms with the quotient/virtual-indicability conjecture, and the author's institutional publication list were checked. The 2024/2025 paper *On normal subgroups in automorphism groups* was also inspected at abstract/introduction level; its graph-product normal-subgroup results do not themselves provide the all-Coxeter quotient conclusion. A separate 2024 *On quotients of Coxeter groups* paper concerns quotients of Coxeter groups, not automatically those of their automorphism groups. No full-target resolution was established by these checks.

The source search was completed before the five substantive approaches. Source checks and supporting calculations were not counted as additional independent proof approaches.

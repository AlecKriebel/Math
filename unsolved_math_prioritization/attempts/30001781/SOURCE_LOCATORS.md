# Primary source and theorem locations

Full source files are pinned by `source_manifest_v2.json` and are not redistributed. Page references below refer to the pinned version unless journal pagination is explicitly stated.

- **Original target:** OWR 24/2011, *Asymptotic Geometric Analysis*, Litvak contribution, printed pp. 1343–1344. Original row assumptions and maximal submatrix definition, displayed logs-outside-radicals scale, and removal-of-unconditionality conjecture. Adjacent Latała contribution pp. 1323–1325 supplies context. The exact relevant pages were visually inspected. `SOURCE_GATE.md` records the imported formula error and prior-campaign check.
- **Sharp unconditional tool:** Adamczak, Latała, Litvak, Pajor, Tomczak-Jaegermann, *Chevet type inequality and norms of submatrices*, Studia Mathematica 210 (2012), 35–56. Theorem 3.1 and Corollary 3.2, journal pp. 42–45, give the general convex-body expectation/tail estimate for a fully isotropic unconditional log-concave matrix. Theorem 4.2 gives the sharp sparse-submatrix benchmark. The complete final PDF is pinned, in addition to arXiv 1107.4066v1. Our turn 1 applies these tools to a latent matrix; it does not drop their unconditionality hypothesis.
- **Arbitrary log-concave rows:** Adamczak et al., *Tail estimates for norms of sums of log-concave random vectors*, PLMS 108 (2014), 600–637, DOI 10.1112/plms/pdt031. The pinned full author PDF is named `ensemble2011.pdf` for historical download reasons; that filename is not the final publication date. Section 2, equation (2.1), author PDF p. 11: one-dimensional subexponential control and the preserved log-concavity assumptions. Theorem 3.1, p. 12: Euclidean strong/weak moments. Theorem 3.4, pp. 14–15: uniform coordinate-projection estimates. Theorem 4.3, pp. 17–18: weighted sums and the coefficient-infinity regimes. Theorem 5.1, p. 20: the known full general-row bound reproduced in `RESULT.md`. The proof of Section 5 is relevant to the unresolved multiscale loss. The final publisher metadata was checked; proof reading uses this complete author manuscript, with its own pagination.
- **Later sharp comparison scope:** Latała–Strzelecka, *Chevet-type inequalities for subexponential Weibull matrices*, Electronic Journal of Probability 29 (2024), article 97, DOI 10.1214/24-EJP1151. The pinned final paper concerns Weibull and unconditional log-concave extensions. It does not provide the missing arbitrary-row comparison used as a hypothesis in turn 5. It is background/current-source checking, not a theorem input to a claimed new general-row bound.
- **Conditional weak–strong route:** Rafał Latała, *On some problems concerning log-concave random vectors*, complete author-hosted survey at the URL in the manifest. Section 3, PDF pp. 6–10; Conjecture 12 on p. 7 is the exact proposed arbitrary-norm moment comparison. Theorem 14 and Remark 16 on p. 8 retain the ell_r and embedding-distortion constants; Theorem 18 on p. 9 concerns coordinate maxima of an isotropic vector and the following paragraph explains why arbitrary linear images do not inherit that isotropy. This is used only to formulate a sufficient condition and rule out a misapplication. The file nickname `latala-problems2017.pdf` is not a claim that this reading establishes the conjecture's present status. No recent abstract-only claim is used as a proof input.

Classical elementary inputs proved or explained in the packet include Gamma/Dirichlet factorization and moments, conditional Jensen, scalar Bernstein from independent subexponential variables, finite Euclidean nets, order-statistic union bounds, tail integration and Markov's inequality. Their usage and independence assumptions are explicit in the corresponding turn files.

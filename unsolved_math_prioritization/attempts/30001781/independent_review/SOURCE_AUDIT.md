# Source audit and bibliographic corrections: 30001781

## Exact original

The actual OWR24/2011 report is **Random Matrices, Geometric Functional Analysis and Algorithms**, not *Asymptotic Geometric Analysis*. The full pinned report is available at [EMS](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46338). Litvak's contribution, “A Chevet type inequality and norms of submatrices of a random matrix in the unconditional case,” occupies printed1343–1344, PDF57–58. Both pages were visually checked and the complete contribution was read. RESULT.md's report-title slip does not change the correctly located source question. This correction was communicated to the author, who requested preservation of the frozen files and a versioned review supplement.

The source scale is sqrt(m) log(3N/m)+sqrt(k) log(3n/k). It is an up-to-universal-constant sharp order, with qualitative high probability in the short report. It assumes independent isotropic log-concave rows and asks whether the unconditionality hypothesis may be removed. Nothing in the five-turn packet settles this general question. The source typo in the imported record does not provide a counterexample to the genuine target.

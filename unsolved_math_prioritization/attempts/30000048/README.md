## Reviewed conclusions

- Every nonnegative integral mean-one virtual character on SU(2)^r is one irreducible character square
- On SU(3), the full support range a+b≤4 is classified without assuming center invariance
- Unrestricted SU(3) affine single-irrep functions and integral convex combinations of irreducible squares are classified
- On SU(3), an integral mean-one virtual character admitting a finite Hermitian sum of character-polynomial squares is one irreducible square, even with non-diagonal Gram matrices

The arbitrary-SU(3) positivity-to-SOS implication remains unproved. The non-SOS discriminant example has Haar mean 15 and is explicitly not a counterexample to the mean-one question. Other higher-rank simple groups and mixed products remain outside these results.

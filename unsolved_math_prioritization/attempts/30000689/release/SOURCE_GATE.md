## Exact target

The target quantifies over arbitrary finite triangulations of the topological sphere S^{2d}, and over missing d-faces with d+1 vertices. The desired obstruction is to arbitrary topological embedding of the augmented d-skeleton. It is not merely a restriction on a straight-line realization, a PL embedding, or completion as the entire d-skeleton of another sphere.

The catalogue page [30000689](https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/30000689) was inaccessible to the web tool. The pinned catalogue record was used as a fallback. Its statement agrees with Conjecture 4 in Eran Nevo's joint-work report with Uli Wagner, printed p. 238 of [Oberwolfach Report 4/2007](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46090). The source page was read as extracted text and visually checked. The later full Nevo–Wagner paper states the same question as Conjecture 1.3.

## Relevant prior results

- Nevo–Wagner Theorem 1.2 already proves the conclusion when the source is a PL sphere. This is credited, not claimed as a new result.
- Their Theorem 1.4 says the augmented complex cannot be the entire d-skeleton of another triangulated sphere. That weaker statement is insufficient to rule out an embedding as a proper subcomplex and is not substituted for the target.
- Modern Lefschetz results apply to the prospective ambient PL sphere, yielding a middle-degree surjection. The manuscript uses the published Karu–Xiao Theorems 1.2–1.3, rather than relying on an unqualified assertion that a face-number theorem settles arbitrary topological embeddings.
- The Adiprasito–Patáková extension theorem retains the original complex without subdivision. This distinction is essential to the face-ring dimension comparison.
- Haefliger–Weber's existence range is verified separately and applies here only for d≥3.
- The d=2 argument extends the local Nevo–Wagner proof using a flag subdivision as its induction base and a contractible-complement lemma. This extension is a deduction in the present manuscript; it was not found explicitly stated in the checked literature.

All theorem locations and links are in PROOF.md. A targeted search found no explicit prior full resolution of this exact formulation. This is bounded literature checking and does not establish novelty or first priority. No external researcher was contacted.

# Limits and unresolved gap

1. **Convention is decisive.** Crown graphs solve the reading that allows disconnected strongly regular distance graphs. They do not solve the connected/nondegenerate reading. The latter is the research target treated as unresolved here. The proposer already knew the crown exception in 2019; no claim of correcting the editor's open status is made.
2. **No primitive realization.** An eigenmatrix, integer intersection array, nonnegative Krein parameters, or feasible triple-intersection system does not construct a graph. No primitive example was found.
3. **No global nonexistence proof.** The sieve is exhaustive only for its explicit range 2≤t≤30 and its stated tests. It retains 154 arrays after five exact certificates. Some have prior published nonexistence proofs, and others might also have been ruled out by uninspected work.
4. **Literature scope.** The 2019 classification's relevant statement/proof passages and the 2020 small-type-III article were read through the web PDF tool. Direct downloads returned HTTP 403, so no local byte hashes are claimed for them. Other downloaded PDFs have verified hashes. Full proofs of all cited external exclusions were not independently audited. The 2023 exclusion was verified at its primary abstract/bibliographic page; its full PDF retrieval failed.
5. **Published arithmetic is not blindly imported.** The web-rendered 2020 proof has apparent arithmetic inconsistencies in its t=3 discussion: c=9 gives c²+4t²(c+1)=441, a square. Our t=3 exclusion instead uses exact shell parity for (t,c,a)=(3,9,5), and an independently replayed certificate for (3,4,4). The source's conclusions are not used as a substitute for these proofs.
6. **Prior attempts.** Exact GitHub code/PR/commit/branch searches yielded no target match, and a narrow immutable-base attempt-directory lookup returned 404. This does not establish absence of deleted branches, unindexed artifacts, private working trees, or conversation-only work. No personal-context summary was accepted as mathematical evidence.
7. **Computational trust boundary.** The rational verifiers regenerate all displayed certificate equations. They depend on the mathematical derivation in PROOFS.md and ordinary Python arithmetic. They are not a Lean/Coq formalization. The symbolic verifier is supplementary. The discovery LP's numerical status is never accepted as a proof.
8. **Publication boundary.** Only authored exposition/code/certificates and public verification metadata belong to this payload. No source PDFs, extracted source text, corpus contents, or coordination material are included. No remote write was performed. Independent audit remains pending.

# Function Theory Problem 3.30: credited classical resolution

For the double-layer operator on continuous densities on a smooth planar Jordan curve, finite rank occurs exactly for circles. A circle has rank one over the scalar field of its density space. This is a literature-based status correction, with an independent AI-assisted audit of the exact hypotheses and operator reduction. It is not a new rigidity theorem or a new proof of the classical theorem.

Read the [full independent audit](audit/AUDIT.md), [acceptance decision](audit/ACCEPTANCE.json), and [author's reduction](author/RESOLUTION.md).

## Prior result and exact scope

[Miyanishi, arXiv:1806.03657v1, Corollary 5.1, p.11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1806.03657v1#page=11) gives the relevant finite-rank characterization for bounded C^{2,alpha} regions. Smooth Jordan curves meet that hypothesis. Earlier explicit planar statements occur in [Khavinson-Putinar-Shapiro, author manuscript, section 8.2, p.75](https://web.math.ucsb.edu/~mputinar/poincare.pdf#page=75), and [Miyanishi-Suzuki, arXiv:1501.03627v1, p.6](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1501.03627v1#page=6). These sources credit the classical result associated with Shapiro's 1992 monograph. The original book proof was not inspected and remains an external dependency.

The original item is [Hayman-Lingham, Problem 3.30, printed p.69 / PDF p.70](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.07200v2#page=70). The audit checks the signed source-normal kernel, K_MS = -T/pi, continuous diagonal extension, continuous-to-L2 rank transfer, real/complex scalar fields, and the circle's nonzero rank-one kernel. It assumes neither analytic boundary nor uniform spectral information. It makes no claim about rough or self-intersecting curves.

The arXiv record identifies Miyanishi-Suzuki v1 as submitted on 15 January 2015; the inspected served PDF title page displays 22 September 2021. Both dates and the pinned PDF identity are preserved in [source verification](audit/SOURCE_VERIFICATION.json), without inferring why the dates differ. Several papers recording one classical dependency are not treated as independent proofs or as evidence establishing historical priority.

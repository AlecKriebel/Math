# Five substantive approaches

All five were completed. The original question remains unresolved.

1. **Triangulation and Ehrhart coefficients.** Proved the dimension-one,
   planar, and one-interior-point base cases, credited where established.
   Derived the all-dimensional boundary-sensitive bound (H), criterion
   (HB), and the full 3D case I<=5. The formal vector (1,11,11,7) shows the listed coefficient constraints
   alone leave a gap of two normalized-volume units. It is not a realizable
   polytope claim.
2. **Quotient-lattice packing.** Proved the known crosspolytope case by showing
   at most two interior points per nonzero quotient coset and one in the zero
   coset. Derived an interior-preserving containment certificate. The scaled
   octahedron inside a cube explicitly blocks unconditional transfer of the
   interior count across inclusion.
3. **Successive minima.** Derived a sufficient condition from Minkowski's
   lower bound and evaluated its failure on [-2,2]^3. The failed condition
   does not contradict the original volume inequality.
4. **Product and free-sum geometry.** Proved strict product closure and free-sum
   closure with any origin-symmetric one-interior-point factor, including
   nonreflexive ones. Proved the needed volume and interior-count identities,
   producing certified classes in unbounded dimensions.
5. **Published asymptotic bound and exact coefficient.** Checked Henze's
   theorem and exact Laguerre coefficient. Derived a second explicit
   boundary-sensitive certificate, keeping closed/interior counts and
   epsilon/dimension quantifiers distinct. Replayed 37 exact 3D examples,
   seven residue examples, twenty coefficient identities, and 4,900 product
   parameter checks as supporting implementation evidence.

No actual prior user/repository attempt was located by the bounded checks;
catalog desk triage was not counted as a substantive attempt. No remote
mutation, publication, or external outreach was performed in this task.

# Limitations and nonclaims

1. The all-genus target is unresolved. In particular no actual value for degree L^2 D^13 on the genus-five compactification is obtained.
2. The EGH vanishing theorem is imported from primary literature. Its full singular-fiber GRR proof is not independently re-proved in this packet.
3. Isogeny arguments apply to abelian schemes and the specified normalized divisor classes. They cannot automatically be extended across semiabelic degeneration.
4. Birational comparison requires rational Cartier classes, a common refinement, and the stated bound on the image of the discrepancy support. It is not invariance under arbitrary replacement of the boundary divisor.
5. The determinant computation concerns pure theta/Poincare monomials. Positive-codimension base factors and compactified boundary corrections require separate treatment.
6. The arithmetic checks verify formulas, finite identities, and internal source discrepancies. They are not computations in the full Chow ring of A_5^Perf.
7. The journal version of EGH could not be retrieved. Discrepancy claims are confined to the exact arXiv v1 bytes inspected. Higher-genus finite-sum outputs are not promoted to established intersection numbers.
8. The public problem page returned HTTP 403 after a failed web-tool fetch. The target was obtained from the authorized cached catalogue, then matched to the official Oberwolfach report and primary paper.
9. Literature and repository searches found no full resolution or exact earlier repository attempt. Negative search results are not proof of absence. No external correspondence was initiated.
10. This is an unaudited author packet. No acceptance, peer review, novelty, or completed independent validation is claimed.

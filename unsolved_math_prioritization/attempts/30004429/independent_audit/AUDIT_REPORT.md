## 2. Authored-proof audit

### Section 2: finite energy and support — PASS

The four-neighbor field gives c_beta = 1/(1+exp(8 beta)). Conditional expectation transfers both bounds to every sigma-algebra generated by other spins. The chain rule gives a lower bound c_beta^k for every prescribed k-site string and upper bound (1-c_beta)^k. The exterior marginal therefore has full support and no atoms. Neither the finite-temperature assumption nor the phase quantifier is lost.

### Theorem 1: essential oscillation — PASS

For each positive-measure exterior cylinder, every finer cylinder conditional is an average of the same conditional random variable q. Thus it lies between that cylinder's essential lower and upper bounds. Martingale convergence, applied on positive-measure sets where q approaches an essential endpoint, proves the reverse infimum/supremum inequalities.

There are only countably many cylinders, finite conditional values, and rational endpoint tolerances. A common full-measure martingale set is consequently available; the proof does not need an uncountable intersection of probability-one events.

Nested cylinders make the lower bounds increasing and the upper bounds decreasing. Their intervals contain q_star at every configuration, including exceptional configurations, because q_star is defined by the limsup of finite conditional values. This gives full-product-space continuity wherever their lengths vanish. Conversely, continuity of any version on an entire cylinder forces its essential oscillation to be small. Null-set changes therefore cannot remove points with positive limiting essential oscillation.

This is a genuine all-configuration continuity extension for one singleton conditional. The argument does not silently replace the product topology by a relative topology on a typical set. For the target, the finite-energy bounds also make q_star everywhere positive and bounded away from one.

### Theorem 2: extremal monochromatic annuli — PASS

Finite-volume ferromagnetic Ising comparison remains valid with mixed-sign pins: pinning a spin produces an external field, and the remaining pair interactions remain ferromagnetic. Increasing a pin increases the origin's plus probability. Positive limiting cylinder probabilities justify passing the finite conditional ratios to the plus-phase limit.

For each fixed inner pattern, all-plus and all-minus outer assignments therefore maximize and minimize all finer finite conditional values. Enlarging an all-plus annulus raises its value because the old value is an average over the newly exposed spins; all-minus lowers it. The supremum and infimum identities from Theorem 1 give exactly the asserted endpoint limits.

This proof uses the strong monotonicity of finite Ising laws and their limit. It does not infer conditional monotonicity for every merely associated probability measure. No value at an infinite null boundary is assumed, and no almost-sure monotonicity is upgraded without justification to every boundary.

The unresolved quantity is the limit, as the inner segment grows, of the difference between those two infinite-annulus endpoint limits. The volume limit comes first, at fixed inner and outer lengths; the outer length then tends to infinity; the inner length comes last. A prescribed relation between the two lengths does not suffice. The decreasing B_n makes the proposed convergence-in-probability criterion sufficient, but the candidate does not establish it.

### Proposition 3: summable one-sided transfer — PASS

Here a process having g must mean that the process is compatible with that transition function. This standard meaning is also necessary for the chain-rule identity.

Stationarity gives the same a.s. transition identity at all integer times. Positivity permits conditioning on each finite word without zero denominators. Taking the countable intersection over finite words and times yields the common full-measure set needed for the Bayes product formula. Forced finite prefixes do not require an unsupported assertion at every original past.

The jth likelihood-ratio summand compares histories with a shared suffix of length j-1 and is bounded by v_(j-1). Summability yields a uniformly convergent continuous log-odds series; logistic transformation gives the desired positive continuous version. Martingale convergence identifies that version a.s. This is a sufficient criterion with its hypothesis intact, not a deduction from mere g-continuity.

Bethuelsen–Conache Proposition 3.2 excludes the required summable variations in the phase-transition regime. Uniform non-nullness makes variations of g and log g comparable. The candidate correctly leaves its weaker local-uniform tail route unproved.

For clarity, a global extension for that weaker route can use limsup Q_m. Local uniform control of the log-odds tails then supplies continuity at the specified good point; pointwise convergence only at that point would not supply it. This is an explanatory completion of the proposed sufficient route, not a proof of its hypothesis for the target.

### Section 6: countercontrols — PASS

The finite-minus set is countable, dense, null, shift invariant, and invariant under finite modifications. Its complement is dense. A tail-dependent Bernoulli parameter therefore gives proper, mutually consistent finite-volume kernels. The iid law sees the fair parameter almost surely, although the selected singleton version is discontinuous everywhere. This correctly refutes an arbitrary-version formulation, not the existential formulation.

In the two-component product mixture, the strong law reveals the latent Bernoulli parameter from the exterior. Both components give positive mass to every finite cylinder, so their essential infimum and supremum remain 1/4 and 3/4 in every cylinder. Martingale convergence coexists with persistent essential oscillation. The example is explicitly nonergodic and is not substituted for the extremal Ising projection.

The rare-evidence example correctly has a log-supermodular finite-dimensional law. With indicator-valued Bernoulli variables, its covariance formulas are exact. It shows that small unconditional dependence can be amplified by rare evidence even within this attractive family. It neither produces a single phi-mixing counterexample nor refutes a specific Ising estimate.

### Section 7: original summable Dyson specification — PASS

For finite nonnegative inverse temperature, the logistic derivative in the physical field is at most beta/2. Two exterior fields agreeing through distance n differ by at most twice the absolute interaction tail. The stated beta-times-tail bound follows. For alpha > 1, the integral comparison gives the stated 2 beta n^(1-alpha)/(alpha-1) bound for n >= 1.

Each finite-volume boundary Hamiltonian is an absolutely and uniformly convergent sum. The resulting finite positive partition functions and kernels are continuous. This does not imply continuity of every version, of a one-sided g-function, or of a transformed long-range model. Phase coexistence does not alter the canonical specification.

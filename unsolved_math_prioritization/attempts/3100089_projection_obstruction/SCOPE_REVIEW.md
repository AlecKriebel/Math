# Scope and publication review

## Accepted mathematical content

The proof treats precisely the rank-r path configuration and its single norm-one augmentation for each integer r≥2. It constructs every successful augmented word and every full-matrix trajectory fiber, proves the exact minimum reverse-fiber recovery distance in the word graph, separately proves the full quotient distance while allowing free representative changes, and proves augmented connectivity under the stronger one-deletion/one-insertion rule.

The reverse fiber is the starting set for the word-distance claim. The quotient target is the old trajectory on the full augmented label set, including its new-label representative. These meanings are preserved throughout the proof, audit, README, and acceptance.

## Logical boundary

The distance ceil((r−1)/4) is unbounded as r grows. This excludes a universal bounded radius for recovering arbitrary augmented states into the old-coordinate realized part. It does not exclude nonlocal component preservation or unrestricted path rerouting. Neither the original path nor the augmented path is disconnected; the former has one successful word, and the latter is connected already at LCS≥r−1. The general original BFM conjecture is unresolved by this appendix.

No standalone theorem about the proposed residual component-equivalence formulation is included or accepted here. The original proof's precise sentence leaving nonlocal approaches open is retained. No novelty, priority, or comprehensive current literature-status claim is made.

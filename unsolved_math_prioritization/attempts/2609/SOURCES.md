   - Primary text: https://arxiv.org/html/2609.16227v1
   - PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.16227v1

The exact count 1728 is a stronger result in the same paper; this packet reproduces that count by a newly written exact checker.
   - The arXiv record and PDF both display 30 July 2026. We preserve the displayed date and literal identifier rather than inferring a date from the identifier prefix. No journal reference or later version appeared in the inspected record. We call this an existing preprint, without asserting editorial acceptance, peer review, or chronological first priority.
   - The paper discloses use of ChatGPT during its preparation and gives the author responsibility for the manuscript. This packet independently reconstructs the stated counterexample rather than treating the source's claim as sufficient evidence.

# Validation limits

The note is a theorem application relying on published mathematics, with an explicit bridge to the original hypothesis set. It is not a formal proof-assistant development or an independent reproving of GHP's lengthy compactification theorem. The bibliography is a focused search, not a claim of exhaustive historical priority analysis.

The executable test is an exact finite permutation model for the semidirect-product relation and suspension quotient. It is deliberately not the infinite surface group and does not construct a hyperbolic 3-manifold. Hyperbolicity, compactification/nullity, AR topology, and covering dimension are justified in the written proof through cited theorems and their checked hypotheses. Finite tests cannot certify those properties.

The unresolved Bestvina-example attribution in the 2026 preprint is excluded from the proof. Both spaces in the surface-bundle example are ordinary topological S^2; the obstruction concerns the group action. There is no claim of metric or conformal equivalence. The work does not address a modified question imposing minimality or uniform convergence on the EZ-action.

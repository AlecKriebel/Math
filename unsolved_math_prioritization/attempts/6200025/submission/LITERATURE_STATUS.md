# Literature status and attribution

**already_solved (negative), as a consequence of a published theorem.**

The decisive source is Guilbault--Healy--Pietsch, *Group boundaries for semidirect products with Z*, DOI 10.4171/GGD/750. The publisher records submission 2022-02-14, acceptance 2022-07-17, online publication 2023-11-10, and volume 18 (2024), pages 869--919. Theorem 1.6 and Section 7.1 are the essential pair: the first constructs the EZ-structure; the second specifies an action that fixes the two suspension poles. Existing hyperbolic closed surface bundles yield the negative answer.

Searches for the exact article title with correction/erratum terms found no relevant correction as of 2026-10-04; this bounded search is not proof that no correction exists.

Additional sources checked:
- Farrell--Lafont, *EZ-structures and topological applications*, arXiv:math/0405260, Definition 1.1. This verifies that the extension is over the compactification, not an unrelated action assigned to a boundary set.
- Guilbault--Moran, *Proper homotopy types and Z-boundaries of spaces admitting geometric group actions*, Theorems 1.3 and 1.5. Boundary swapping and shape equivalence do not themselves assert equivariant homeomorphism.
- Kandybo--Świątkowski, *EZ-boundaries, splittings over finite subgroups, and dense amalgams*, arXiv:2603.05742v2, Definition 2.9, Section 4.3, and Section 6. The work gives end-structure results in a broader setting. Its Section 6 attributes hyperbolic-boundary nonuniqueness to Bestvina 1996 Example 3.1. We did not obtain the latter original paper and do not certify that attribution, any equivariance upgrade, or earliest priority.
- Bowditch, *A topological characterisation of hyperbolic groups*, Theorem 0.1. Perfect compactness plus a proper cocompact triple action would imply canonical boundary action; the arbitrary EZ axioms alone do not grant those additional dynamics.

No assertion is made that the complete problem's negative resolution is original here, that all hyperbolic groups have noncanonical EZ-boundaries, or that every nonunique ordinary Z-boundary extends to an EZ-boundary. No outside researchers were contacted.

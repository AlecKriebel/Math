# Source interpretation and literature check

Checked on 5 October 2026.

## Governing source

The requested landing page, https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/30005356, was tried first. The web reader could not open it and a direct request returned HTTP 403. No successful live inspection of that page is claimed.

The linked publisher PDF, https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46996, was retrieved in full. The two relevant pages, printed 97–98, were inspected both as extracted text and as rendered images. They set up ACFH in the field language with a multiplicative unary operator, without restricting characteristic. The question on the second page uses the integral polynomial ring in that operator. This is the scope adopted in the proof.

## Later version of the research paper

The arXiv abstract identifies v4, revised 17 January 2025, as the latest version available in the check: https://arxiv.org/abs/2212.02115 . The complete versioned PDF was retrieved: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2212.02115v4 . Sections 3.3 and 5.1 retain positive-characteristic models and the integral polynomial question; the relevant definition and question were visually checked on PDF pages 22 and 25. Corollary 5.7 supplies faithfulness, which is also proved independently in this packet.

The published article is *Generic multiplicative endomorphism of a field*, *Annals of Pure and Applied Logic* 176(4), 103554 (2025), https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apal.2025.103554 . Publisher search metadata confirms the publication, but opening the full ScienceDirect page returned 403. No claim of final-journal full-text inspection is made.

## Other checks and limits

The author's public publication and preprint list was inspected at https://choum.net/~chris/page_perso/ . Targeted searches covered the article title, definable endomorphisms, ACFH with Frobenius or inverse Frobenius, the localized coefficient ring, Question 5.8, and an erratum. No later resolution or correction of the specific classification question was located in this bounded search. That is not a proof that none exists and does not support a novelty claim.

The imported record mentions a neighboring question about the forking existence axiom. Scott Mutchnik's *An NSOP1 theory without the existence axiom*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13082, answers that broader question. Its abstract was checked. It is not used as a resolution of the multiplicative-endomorphism question.

Only the stated locations in the scholarly sources were inspected. Complete downloads do not imply a full audit of those papers. The proof in this packet relies on the elementary field facts, the definition of existential closedness, and the established existence of ACFH models; it does not import the papers' deeper independence-theoretic arguments.

## 1 The original model-side open condition is incomplete

Use the packet's notation

- U = w_2^3 - 1, V = w_3^3 - 1, W = w_4^3 - 1, Q = w_5^3 - 1;
- D = s^2 U - V;
- q = 2(V-sU)/D;
- v_2 = 1+q, v_3 = 1+sq, v_4 = 1+tq, v_5 = 1+rq;
- T = (v_2^2-1)/(v_2^2-w_2^3) = q(q+2)/(q(q+2)-U).

The original text gives U D q (v_2^2-w_2^3) != 0 on the model side, in addition to the original x_1 y_1 != 0 chart. The model-side product alone does not ensure the original chart. The missing condition is T != 0. Since U != 0 already implies T-1 != 0 when the displayed denominator is nonzero, T != 0 is equivalently q+2 != 0 after q != 0 is imposed.

Here is an exact counterexample. Choose a cube root alpha of 2. For n=4 take

s=t=0 and w_2=w_3=w_4=alpha.

For n=5 additionally take r=0 and w_5=alpha. Then U=V=W=Q=1, where Q is omitted for n=4. Both displayed model equations vanish. Direct calculation gives

D=-1, q=-2, v_2=-1, v_i=1 for i>=3,
v_2^2-w_2^3=-1,
U D q (v_2^2-w_2^3)=-2,
T=0.

Thus the old listed product is nonzero, yet every proposed elliptic lift has x_1^3=0. Ratios defined using x_1 cannot be recovered on that chart. This does not contradict a birational map, which is allowed to have a smaller domain. It does invalidate the literal sufficiency of that listed model-side condition.

The issue is not explained away by selecting only the dominant component. The counterexample lies in its closure. Set e to be a parameter and put

q_e=-2+2e, s_e=e, t_e=e+e^2, r_e=e+2e^2, U_e=1,
U_i(e)=s_i(e)(s_i(e)q_e+2)/(q_e+2).

Take V_e=U_3(e), W_e=U_4(e), Q_e=U_5(e). Both model equations hold identically. For generic e the base coordinates are nondegenerate and the corrected domain below is satisfied. At e=0 all U_i tend to 1 and s,t,r tend to 0. The cube equations w_i^3=1+U_i(e) have formal roots with chosen constant term alpha, since 3 alpha^2 != 0. This provides an arc from the dominant open to the bad point. The exact identities and limits are reproduced by the independent script.

## 4 The full unsaturated fivefold intersection is reducible

The generic complete intersection over C(s,t,r) is geometrically integral. This must not be expanded into a claim that the full unsaturated equations in A^7 define a geometrically integral variety.

Indeed, for any cube root epsilon of 1, the locus

has dimension five and makes both equations vanish identically. It is vertical over the base because s=0. There are further dimension-five loci

These also make both equations vanish. The first defining equation is irreducible: its coefficients have content one in C[s,t], and its projective generic fiber is a smooth, geometrically integral diagonal cubic surface. The second equation is not a multiple of the first because its w_5^3 coefficient is -s(s-1). Hence the two equations form a codimension-two complete intersection in A^7, of dimension five. The displayed irreducible dimension-five vertical loci are therefore genuine components, not just isolated special points. Each of them has D=0 and is excluded by Omega_5.

The controlling replacement is: take the unique component dominating the base, then the stated dense open; alternatively work directly with the generic fiber and its function field. No assertion of integrality of the unlocalized, unsaturated global ideal is retained. An explicit primary decomposition or saturation equality is not required for, and is not asserted by, this field-level correction.

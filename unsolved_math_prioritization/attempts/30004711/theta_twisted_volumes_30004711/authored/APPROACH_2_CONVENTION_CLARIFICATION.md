1. In the local chart `θ'=aθ`, the coefficient `h` in `K=K0+h bar θ θ` is a metric coefficient on the odd **tangent** line `V`, with frame `∂_θ` and transformation `h'=|a|^(-2)h`. With standard Chern sign,
   `c1(V,h)=-(i/(2π))∂bar∂log|h|`.
   The Berezin orientation induced by this complex line gives this Euler form. The opposite real odd orientation gives its negative.

2. In the usual holomorphic supermoduli deformation convention, `V=F=R¹π_*θ^∨`. The canonical 3/2-differential Petersson metric is naturally on `F^∨`. To compare it to the coefficient of the tangent supermetric, use the **inverse metric** on `F`. A logarithmic metric-ratio comparison requires two metrics on the same line.

3. On the unrigidified compactified odd-spin component, the global Ramond-boundary calculation gives `F=H^(-3)`, `F^∨=H³`, and `H²=λ`. Hence the complex-oriented compactified values are precisely
   `∫c1(F)=-1/32` and `∫c1(F^∨)=+1/32`.
   The sign is determined by the identified line/orientation; it is not subsequently adjustable to fit the original torsion convention.

4. The number `1/32` is first an algebraic compactified stack characteristic number. Interpreting it as the canonical open-locus metric integral uses the canonical extension theorem, a separate analytic input. Interpreting it as the actual torsion integral further requires the stated metric/form/retraction/convention comparison, or sufficient direct boundary control. The line isomorphism alone does not prove either analytic passage.

5. The local algebra is independently consistent with Roček–Wadhwa, *On Calabi-Yau supermanifolds*, arXiv:hep-th/0408188v1, equations (9)–(11), printed p.2 / PDF p.3. Penner–Zeitlin 1509.06302v4 proves the relevant real form and negative odd block, but its Omnibus B and C displays have a factor-of-two normalization difference; they are not used here as a proof of the actual torsion/complex metric comparison.

These clarifications leave the conditional rank-one mechanism and the magnitude 1/32 intact. They do not settle the literal OWR identity or add another directed author approach.

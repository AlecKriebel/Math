# Acceptance report: scoped porous-medium results

Four frozen manuscripts have been independently audited in full and accepted for their individual stated claims. No mathematical correction patch is required.

1. **Approach 2:** convergence of the original compact-domain frozen-proximal GMN scheme for positive-time Barenblatt data with physical-wall clearance, in every dimension and for every m>1.
2. **Approach 3:** the same convergence estimate for a smooth, uniformly nondegenerate moving free boundary with uniform tubular geometry and wall clearance. This is conditional on the reference pressure geometry.
3. **Approach 4:** the same estimate for the specified symmetric orthant-box reflecting-contact Barenblatt solutions. This permits actual wall contact, but not generic first wall impact.
4. **Approach 5:** exact stationary particle configurations with ε=ch² and bounded regularized energy whose limiting curve is not a porous-medium solution. Every proximal minimizer has the same stationary barycenters. The ratio δ_N²/ε is a fixed positive number; this is not a counterexample under δ_N²/ε→0.

Detailed independent arguments, frozen input hashes, source checks, and diagnostic limitations are in the separate numbered audit reports. `AUDIT_MANIFEST.json` pins the exact inputs and finished reports. The mathematical arguments, rather than finite checks, establish each verdict.

The broader question about arbitrary irregular solutions remains unresolved by these manuscripts. Singular or degenerate interfaces, topology changes, generic wall impact, and general nonlinear-flux identification are not established. The historical first-approach bytes were not recovered or audited, and no acceptance relies on them. This report makes no literature-priority claim.

No source PDF, source HTML, or extracted third-party source text is part of the authored mathematical report. No publication was performed by this audit.

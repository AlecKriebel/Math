# Limits and audit gate

1. The live UnsolvedMath page was attempted through web and direct retrieval;
   the latter returned HTTP 403. Its current contents were not verified.
   The fallback corpus was byte-matched to the repository's pinned public
   dataset manifest before extracting this ID.
2. The 2009 publisher abstract and publication history were inspected.
   Full text was not recovered from publisher, author, or official
   repository searches. No hash is invented for an unavailable PDF.
3. The negative result is strongly corroborated in the 2011 paper and 2017
   survey. Direct inspection of the construction, exact space/scalar
   hypotheses, and proof remains unavailable. In particular, complex
   scalars must not be inferred solely from the abstract.
4. Proposed disposition: attribution-only `already_solved`, pending a fresh
   independent audit of the evidence. This is not `verified_solved`, a new
   result, or a proof-complete packet. If direct primary-hypothesis checking
   is mandatory, retain a source-inspection hold.
5. Tests check integrity and scope contracts. They do not validate a
   counterexample, prove an infinite-dimensional result, or audit the
   cited proof.
6. No remote writes, public uploads, outreach, releases, or DOI deposits
   were performed. Only files listed by FREEZE_MANIFEST.json are proposed
   for later publication. PDFs, extracted source text, raw catalogue records,
   corpora, and private coordination data are excluded.

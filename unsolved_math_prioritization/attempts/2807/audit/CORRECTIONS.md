# Precision corrections to preserve beside the frozen attempt

These are nonfatal clarifications. The frozen public files have not been edited.

1. **Lemma 2, proof.md line 21: completion preimages.** For arbitrary finitely generated groups, replace literal “intersection” with “inverse image under the canonical map to the profinite completion.” A group need not inject into its completion. The finite-index correspondence and Theorem 4 remain valid without residual finiteness.

2. **Lemma 6, proof.md lines 73–81: name the exact variety.** Explicitly use Garden–Tillmann's affine variety of trace characters. In positive characteristic the invariant-theoretic quotient and the trace-character construction should not be identified as varieties without justification. The present proof needs only the latter's finite-type structure and its character-valued points, and is valid with this convention.

3. **Pass 4, proof.md line 95: keep the external hypothesis exact.** Prefer “Cheetham-West–Lê treat the case of finitely many conjugacy classes of SL2(C) representations, with an algebraic representation having nonintegral trace” to the looser “finite-character case.” Theorem 1.3(5) has the conjugacy-class hypothesis. The frozen attempt does not itself prove the full external transfer theorem or that finite character set implies finite conjugacy-class set.

4. **Source example: retain quarantine.** Do not repair the printed C50/C40 mismatch by guessing which side is wrong. The characteristic-43 root count is six distinct roots (two double), while all other odd primes have eight distinct roots. This arithmetic correction does not itself invalidate the claimed zero-dimensionality or certify the census manifold.

None of these notes licenses a change from `unsolved` or creates a general Haken-invariance proof.

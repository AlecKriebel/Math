# Original scope, known results and access qualifications

The exact full primary source is [Thomas Ward, Six problems in algebraic dynamics, updated December 2006](https://www.imath.kiev.ua/~skolyada/kevin.pdf), Problem F on pp.2–4. All five pages were read and p.3 was rendered and visually checked. The local map is scalar multiplication on Q_p and the logarithm is normalized by log_p(p)=0. Topological-conjugacy invariance is expressly an example of a proposed entropy axiom. Thus the complete elementary contradiction to that axiom plus the normalization does not formally rule out every broader interpretation of meaningful entropy-like.

The surrounding mixed-motive and Deligne-period discussion motivates the question but imposes no extra motive or global number-field condition on the local example. No motive theorem is used in the obstruction. Standard topological conjugacy uses an arbitrary homeomorphism; requiring an additive group isomorphism is a materially stronger restriction, explicitly distinguished in the proof.

The source's own December 2006 update already credits Deninger's p-adic determinant construction. The complete retrieved [Deninger paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0608539), arXiv v2 stamp 19 October 2007 with an internal 29 May 2022 date, was read for the introduction, periodic-count definition, p-adic Jensen formula and Theorem 1.1. Its [publisher metadata](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-0-8176-4745-2_10) identifies the chapter in Algebra, Arithmetic, and Geometry, Progress in Mathematics269 (2009), pp.423–442; the page also lists later online publication. The final typeset chapter was not obtained. This note applies the precisely stated known theorem and does not independently reprove its determinant or regulator machinery.

The theorem concerns compact Pontryagin-dual systems associated to integer Laurent polynomials without zeros on the p-adic unit torus. In the explicit application f(t)=pt−(1+p²), the root lies outside that torus and the Jensen value is nonzero. Compactness distinguishes X_f from Q_p. On Q_p itself, all periodic-point counts for a nonunit scalar are one and the periodic entropy is zero. This directly shows why there is no contradiction with the known theorem.

The full final published [Katagiri paper](https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kodaimath/44/2/44_323/_pdf), Kodai Mathematical Journal44(2)(2021),323–333, was obtained and read for Definition1.1, Theorem1.3, Theorem2.2 and Corollary2.3. The final numbering differs from its 2019 preprint. It gives a number-field norm formula and a selected finite-prime-set solenoid formula, with explicit module and eigenvalue/expansiveness conditions. These are credited compact-system results and do not impose the impossible local normalization.

The full [Hebert–Klimek–McBride–Peoples preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.09381) was checked in Section7. It constructs related base-r coordinates for contractions of Z_p and identifies associated crossed-product algebras by their valuation parameter. It is relevant background against any novelty presumption, but its C*-algebra isomorphism statement is not used in place of the explicit topological conjugacy proved here.

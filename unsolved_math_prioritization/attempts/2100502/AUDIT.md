For a finite-dimensional complex alternating pencil, let r be its maximum rank and give the simultaneous-isotropic locus in Gr(dim(V)-r/2,V) its reduced structure. It is smooth exactly when every Jordan pencil block has dimension 2. Kronecker blocks are unrestricted. In that case the variety is the product of LG(m_lambda,2m_lambda), where m_lambda counts dimension-2 pencil blocks at the eigenvalue lambda; its dimension is the sum of m_lambda(m_lambda+1)/2. The empty product is a point. A dimension-2n pencil block corresponds to two operator chains of length n, rather than one chain of length 2n.

## Baseline correction retained in full

Kozlov v1 Lemma 3.3(1), PDF page 21, is false for an arbitrary subspace U. The correct relation is A|U=0 iff P(U) is contained in U-perp_B. In the dimension-4 block W=(C[t]/t^2)e direct-sum (C[t]/t^2)f with P=t and B the coefficient-of-t determinant pairing, U=C e is isotropic for both alternating forms but Pe=te is not in U.

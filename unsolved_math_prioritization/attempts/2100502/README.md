# Reduced complex bi-Lagrangian smoothness

Problem 2100502 / AMR-020-0502. Public proof/audit edition, 2026-10-11.

This AI-assisted, unrefereed prose edition presents one internal AI mathematical reconstruction of a prior candidate. It is not human peer review or formal proof-assistant certification, and is not a computational reproduction package. AUDIT.md records editorial checks of that same reconstruction; it is not a second independent mathematical audit. The prior candidate receives explicit credit; no novelty or priority is claimed.

The reduced bi-Lagrangian variety of a general finite-dimensional complex alternating pencil is smooth exactly when every Jordan pencil block has dimension 2; odd Kronecker blocks are unrestricted. The smooth case is a product of ordinary Lagrangian Grassmannians. Read PROOF.md for the complete argument, including the algebraic reductions, exact reduced hypersurface chart and scheme-level involution obstruction.

Credit belongs to the prior anonymous, AI-assisted, unrefereed candidate *A sharp smoothness criterion for bi-Lagrangian Grassmannians*, version 0.2.0-candidate, at [pinned commit 855d8cc](https://github.com/ipitchford/sharp-bilagrangian-smoothness/tree/855d8cc512b7cd51af437b6e033ec0e0ae8b32ed). We supply the accepted full reconstruction and source correction without a novelty claim. Kozlov's proposed criterion is cited and his overstrong arbitrary-subspace lemma is explicitly corrected; the Lagrangian specialization is valid.

## Adversarial mathematical checks

### 1. Membership of every proposed plane

Write g = sl_3(C), A = diag(2,-1,-1), and let W be the lower-right sl_2 block. A is nonzero and is linearly independent of every nonzero B in W, since its upper-left entry is 2 while every B has upper-left entry zero. Thus L_B = CA + CB is always two-dimensional.

For B with coordinates (x,y,z), the lower block squares to (x²+yz)I_2. When x²+yz is nonzero, it diagonalizes over C with distinct eigenvalues. A commutes with the block change of basis, and A,B then span the entire diagonal traceless two-plane. This is a point of the defining Cartan orbit. The complement of x²+yz=0 is nonempty and dense in P(W); its closure is all P(W). Since X is closed, every L_B belongs to X, including every nonzero nilpotent block. No assertion that an arbitrary commuting plane lies in the orbit is needed.

As an extra boundary check, B(t)=E_23+t diag(0,1,-1) is regular semisimple for t≠0 and converges to E_23. The polynomial parametrization (x,y,z)=(uv,-u²,v²) checks the nilpotent conic identically. The original density argument already covers all its points.

### 2. Closed embedding, not only an immersion

The Plücker map is induced by the injective linear map W → ∧²g, B ↦ A∧B. Therefore P(W) is linearly closed embedded in the Grassmannian, and its factorization through the closed subvariety X remains a closed embedding. In particular, the target's weaker immersion requirement is satisfied. The restricted Plücker polarization is O_{P²}(1).

### 3. Global sections and the pointwise normal test

Conjugation acts algebraically on the orbit closure X. Every D in U=span(E_12,E_13,E_21,E_31) therefore gives a globally defined tangent vector field on X. Restriction to F and passage to the normal quotient are morphisms of algebraic vector bundles. This constructs an actual bundle map U⊗O_F → N_{F/X}; it is not merely a collection of independently chosen normal vectors.

At L_B, Grassmannian tangent vectors are maps L_B→g/L_B. Every tangent vector of F evaluates to zero on A, because the moving planes all contain the same fixed vector A. If the conjugation vector field of D has zero normal class, it is tangent to F, hence [D,A] lies in L_B. But [D,A] is off-block while L_B is block-diagonal. Their intersection is zero, so [D,A]=0. On U, D↦[D,A] is the constant invertible diagonal operator with entries −3,−3,3,3. Thus every fiber map is injective, regardless of B. Smoothness of X and F makes the normal quotient locally free of rank 6−2=4. An everywhere invertible rank-four bundle morphism is a bundle isomorphism. This proves algebraic, hence holomorphic, triviality over all F.

A useful stronger description makes gluing explicit. Off-block projection induces a fixed map g/L_B→U because each L_B is block-diagonal. Evaluate a Grassmannian tangent vector at the globally fixed A and project to U. This annihilates T_F and induces a morphism N_{F/X}→U⊗O_F. Composed with the proposed normal frame, it is the same constant diagonal operator. Its inverse gives a global inverse to the frame. No local-coordinate or nilpotent-boundary extension is being assumed.

### 4. Independent all-charts tangent computation

The new check_independent.py was written separately from the submitted script. It works with the tangent equation

`[a,B] + [A,b] = 0`

for the abelian-plane locus, modulo changes of basis in L_B. On each of the charts x=1, y=1 and z=1, it checks polynomial identities showing that two tangent directions of F and the four conjugation directions satisfy this equation. Their six-column matrix has the constant nonzero minor 81. Thus the six directions are independent at every point of each chart. A rank-six Jacobian minor is respectively −324, 162, 162. The polynomial identity with the six independent kernel columns also proves the Jacobian rank is at most six after every specialization. Hence its rank is exactly six everywhere on F, including the nilpotent conic.

This is an all-points symbolic check over C, rather than a generic-rank computation extrapolated to a boundary. It confirms both normal independence and the expected six-dimensional tangent space locally along F. It does not replace the cited global smoothness, projectivity, Fano or Picard theorems. Supplementary tests include complex nilpotent representatives, invalid B=0, block fields that fail the fixed-A test, and characteristic-three degeneration.

### 5. All global target hypotheses

The restricted Plücker line bundle H is ample, Pic(X)=ZH and −K_X=3H by the cited results. Kodaira vanishing applied to K_X⊗(−K_X) gives H¹(O_X)=H²(O_X)=0. The exponential exact sequence therefore identifies Pic(X) with H²(X,Z), giving b_2=1. The Fano index three distinguishes X from P^6, whose index is seven. With k=2 and dim X=6, the strict target range holds. These are the full stated hypotheses; the separate rigid-target assertion is not a conclusion of this disproof.

### 6. The family cross-check also works

For the exceptional-divisor family q:E→Z of projective planes, the exact sequence on a fiber F_z is

`0 → T_{F_z} → T_E|_{F_z} → T_zZ⊗O_{F_z} → 0`.

The evaluation differential descends to a map T_zZ⊗O_{F_z}→N_{F_z/X}. Evaluation is dominant and generically finite of degree three, and hence its differential is invertible at a general point in characteristic zero. For a general z, the normal map is therefore invertible somewhere on F_z. Adjunction and the Plücker degree give det N = O_{P²}. Its determinant section is a global regular function on P², up to constant one-dimensional factors. Nonzero somewhere implies nonzero everywhere. This justifies triviality for general family members without incorrectly inferring triviality from c_1=0 alone. The direct proof above is stronger for the specifically displayed plane and is logically independent of this cross-check.

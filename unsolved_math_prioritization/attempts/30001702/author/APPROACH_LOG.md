# Bounded approach log

Status: PARTIAL_UNRESOLVED. Five substantive approaches; no universal solution or counterexample. Source retrieval and scope checks are prerequisites, not claimed mathematical attempts.

1. Homological face-vector route. Applied simplicial-poset h-double-prime nonnegativity and symmetry, derived the exact central-binomial identity and vertex refinement. This proves the target in dimensions one and two but leaves a factorial gap from dimension three onward.

2. Crystallization and low-dimensional reduction. Inspected the Basak–Datta theorem with its contracted hypothesis intact. Combined it with face-vector identities and a justified colored-dipole reduction, obtaining two necessary five-vertex obstruction vectors and the complete-graph/parity constraints. No gluing search was performed; realizability remains open in this work.

3. Periodic lattice/volume route. Proved the factorial lower bound for unimodular lattice triangulations admitting regular sublattice quotients, and constructed equality examples in every dimension. Exact finite quotient checks verify regularity, incidence and homology. The geometric hypotheses are not known for arbitrary simplicial posets, so the restriction cannot be dropped.

4. Product and mapping-torus construction route. The standard staircase product has an exact excess (p+q)!pq over the factorial target. Inspected Basak's mapping-torus construction and its 120-facet output in the relevant four-dimensional setting; it provides no smaller target example, and unspecified monodromy must not be mistaken for the identity. No general counterexample results from these constructions.

5. Cup-product/Borsuk–Ulam route. Checked the 2026 Avvakumov–Karasev theorem directly against the regular-cell hypotheses. Its 2^d bound applies but is weaker here. The exact random-sign probability is exponential, not factorial. No additional argument was found to close that gap.

Literature-category checks also excluded a false-positive minimality claim: Ayzenberg's 2020 permutohedral subdivision minimizes maximal polyhedral cells, equivalently vertices of the dual simplicial subdivision. It does not assert minimality of the dual simplicial facets.

Stop condition: the five-approach budget is exhausted, with a rigorous partial result and explicit remaining obstruction. A fresh independent audit must assess the frozen claims before any publication.

## What the provenance can establish

Source hashes identify inspected versions. Inspection summaries are records of the stated earlier reviews; assembling this reader did not repeat the literature search or add a mathematical pass. A publication date, title, hash, or unsuccessful bounded search cannot establish a new theorem or a priority claim. Source PDFs and extracted source text are not included.

## Source and interpretation

The source is Dominik Kwietniak's contribution, “Sumset Phenomenon and Ergodic Theory,” in Oberwolfach Report 50/2012. The target appears on printed page 3031 (PDF page 47), explicitly attributed to Question 5.4 of reference [7]. The workshop was held 7–13 October 2012; the report was published 7 August 2013. Citation: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2012/50 ; report PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46420 .

The antecedent is John T. Griesmer, “Sumsets of dense sets and sparse sets,” Israel J. Math. 190 (2012), 229–252, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s11856-012-0008-1 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.2278 . Question 5.4 appears on page 17 of the arXiv PDF. It asks for information about integer sets A whose sum with every positive-upper-Banach-density integer set is piecewise syndetic; a separate alternative asks for piecewise Bohr sums. These are two classes, not an assertion that the two properties are equivalent. The source already gives sufficient conditions using equidistributed averaging sequences (Theorem 1.4), including zero-Banach-density examples, and a necessary obstruction from null Haar-measure orbit closures (Proposition 4.2). Reproving these is not new progress.

The supplied record's claim that A is undefined is incorrect: both the original question and the cleaned statement specify A and the universal condition. The wording is broad but meaningful. Do not replace it silently with a particular conjectured equivalence.

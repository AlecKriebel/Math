## Kerr–Naryshkin

[Elementary amenability and almost finiteness](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.05273), v3 dated 30 October 2025, was freshly downloaded in full. Introduction pp.1–5, the definition and zero-dimensional characterization p.6, Theorem A p.3, extension statements and final bibliography were inspected; p.6 was rendered and viewed. Source size and hash are in ROOT_ALMOST_FINITE_SOURCE_RETRIEVAL.json.

Theorem A does cover every free continuous W action on Cantor space, because W is elementary amenable and Cantor space is zero-dimensional. Its conclusion is almost finiteness. In zero dimension the characterization provides finite open castles covering the space for each chosen Følner tolerance. This does not itself provide increasing finite Borel equivalence relations capturing each orbit pair, which is the present target. The paper's two uses of hyperfiniteness are about the measured von Neumann crossed product in its introduction, not an asserted all-space Borel orbit-relation theorem. Thus Theorem A is important broader topological context, but no exact resolution of the requested Borel target is established by the inspected theorem. We do not claim to prove that almost finiteness can never imply Borel hyperfiniteness under further hypotheses.

## Elek–Timár

[The Almost Finite–Purely Infinite Dichotomy for Minimal Amenable Ample Groupoids](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.06713), v2 dated 7 October 2026, was freshly downloaded in full. Introduction pp.2–4, groupoid/principality dictionary pp.5–10, Borel definitions and Theorem3 pp.11–12, clopen Theorem5 p.25 and strong-almost-finiteness conclusion pp.37–38 were inspected. Printed p.3 was rendered and viewed.

Its groupoid dichotomy concerns minimal principal topologically amenable Cantor groupoids and concludes strong almost finiteness when an invariant measure exists. Minimal-action relative genericity would not by itself answer genericity in all actions: actions preserving a specified proper clopen subset have nonempty open neighborhoods, so minimal actions are not dense in this full space.

More decisively, its Borel Theorems2/3 provide, for each tolerance, a finite Borel partition of uniformly bounded diameter and small relative boundary. The introduction expressly distinguishes these nonnested partitions from a Borel hyperfinite exhaustion. No Borel hyperfiniteness conclusion is claimed there. This source therefore supplies no exact theorem predecessor to PR815. Its newer result that amenable free Cantor actions are almost finite must not be mistaken for the present entire-space Borel conclusion.

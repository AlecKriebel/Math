## Decisive mathematical checks

1. **Group and coordinates.** The multiplication law follows from the shift representation and has inverse (−σ_{−k}f,−k). The conjugation is t^{-r} a_0 t^r=a_{−r}, with the author's sign. The prescribed residue r_m(x) makes x↔(t^{-r_m(x)}x,r_m(x)) a Borel inverse bijection with B_m×{0,…,m−1}. This works with nonfree lamps, repeated action images, or trivial lamps.
2. **Metric hypotheses.** The loop-free slab graph has at most four neighbors. Its finite-radius balls are uniformly finite because each is contained in the images of finitely many move words. Its graph distance is Borel: each distance≤k relation is a finite union of graphs of partial Borel compositions with Borel domains. Hence it is a proper Borel extended metric with countable finite-distance classes. Width one has only lamp edges and satisfies the same argument.
3. **Finite-dimensional transfer.** K_m is abstractly Z^m, although its action may have a kernel. Corollary 5.5 supplies a finite dimension for this arbitrary Borel action. The Borel version of Lemma 2.2 and Lemma 3.1 supply a fixed finite number of base colors for all radii; uniformly finite components follow from uniformly bounded diameters and uniformly finite group balls. Projecting a same-color radius chain gives a base finite-move chain. Short connecting paths may have other colors, which does not affect their endpoints. A component over at most C base points has at most mC points, and therefore uniformly bounded slab diameter. No dimension bound uniform in m is needed.
4. **Metric compatibility.** If m divides n, a forbidden n-residue reduces to the forbidden m-residue, so the graph inclusion is literal. Consequently ρ_n≤ρ_m on all finite previous-distance pairs. The exact supremum in Theorem 7.3 is bounded by the radius. That theorem requires neither free actions nor a common dimension bound over its metric sequence.
5. **Regular exhaustion.** The only forward shift edge missing at every factorial stage begins at profinite height −1. Its invariant saturation is the entire integer-height preimage. On the complement every generator edge appears at some stage, and every finite word's finitely many edges appear jointly at a late stage. The compatible-metric theorem then gives hyperfiniteness of the full restricted orbit relation. This never uses general closure under increasing unions of hyperfinite relations.
6. **Exceptional exhaustion.** On p^{-1}(Z), the maps h and q(x)=t^{-h(x)}x identify the set with B×Z. Orbit equivalence is exactly N-equivalence of the q-images, in both directions. Corollary 7.5 gives finite increasing base relations F_j. The proposed relation H_j has classes inside the band |h|≤j of cardinality (2j+1)|C|, where C is an F_j-class; outside the band classes are singletons. It is Borel, finite, increasing, and exhausts every exceptional orbit pair, including negative heights. The disjoint union of the two invariant-piece exhaustions is a finite increasing exhaustion of E_W on all X.

A pullback without a height cutoff would fail: even one base point would have infinitely many integer-height lifts. Deleting only p^{-1}({−1}) would fail invariance. Reversing the lamp sign or allowing the wrap edge would fail the slab identity. The author's proof contains the correct choices in all four places.

# Source audit, 8 October 2026 UTC

## Exact historical target

OWR 19/2017, *Discrete Geometry*, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2017/19), report of the 9–15 April 2017 workshop. Printed p.1173, Conjecture 2 states the threshold-four primal question. The adjacent remark attributes failure of threshold three to Géza Tóth (personal communication); that construction is not reconstructed here. The relevant text and a local rendered page were inspected. The catalog's 2018 bibliographical label does not change the report's workshop year.

## Historical versions must not be treated as current status

Keszegh–Pálvölgyi, *Proper Coloring of Geometric Hypergraphs*: the [SoCG 2017 version](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.SoCG.2017.47) conjectured threshold three. [arXiv:1612.02158v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.02158v2), dated 15 April 2019, instead asks for some threshold and records Tóth's obstruction to three while retaining four as possible. Its polygon theorem depends on the fixed polygon and does not settle arbitrary disks. These relevant definitions and statements were inspected; their old open-status wording is historical.

## Decisive primary source

Damásdi–Pálvölgyi, [*Realizing an m-Uniform Four-Chromatic Hypergraph with Disks*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-021-4846-5), Combinatorica 42 (Suppl 1), 1027–1048 (2022). The publisher records publication on 21 September 2022 and the negative theorem in its abstract. Full journal-PDF retrieval returned HTML, not a valid PDF; no journal full-proof inspection is claimed.

The complete relevant proof in [arXiv:2011.12187v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.12187v1) was read: Observation 8, Lemma 9, Theorem 12, Claim 14, and Lemma 16, through the end of Section 3. Its statement and construction pages were rendered locally. The geometric realization is accepted as a published external input, rather than independently regenerated. The finite-tree obstruction alone would not suffice without the geometric realization.

## Later corroboration and nearby positive results

[Damásdi–Keszegh–Pach–Pálvölgyi–Tóth, *Coloring Geometric Hypergraphs: A Survey*, arXiv:2512.09509v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.09509v1), Sections 6–8, confirms the four-color large-edge parameter for disks, distinguishes fixed-shape translates, and leaves the dual disk question separate. This is a preprint survey; no journal acceptance is claimed. Relevant sections were inspected, not every proof in the survey.

[Ackerman–Keszegh–Pálvölgyi, *Coloring Hypergraphs Defined by Stabbed Pseudo-Disks and ABAB-Free Hypergraphs*](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1290231), SIAM J. Discrete Math. 34 (2020), 2250–2269, gives three colors already at threshold two for families with nonempty total intersection. Theorem 1 and definitions were inspected in [the preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1902.08468). No arbitrary-family conclusion follows.

[Damásdi–Pálvölgyi, *Three-chromatic geometric hypergraphs*](https://ems.press/journals/jems/articles/14298085), JEMS 28 (2026), 2637–2657, published online 28 August 2024, concerns translates of one fixed convex body. Publisher metadata, abstract and theorem statement were inspected; its full proof was not audited for this task.

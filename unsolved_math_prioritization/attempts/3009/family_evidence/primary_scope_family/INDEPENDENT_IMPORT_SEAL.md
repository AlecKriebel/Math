# Independent theorem and scope evaluation

Sealed before reading original independent_review/REVIEW.md or any parent/sibling conclusions.

The hypotheses supplied to the sphere theorem are exactly global uniform recurrence, orientation preservation, and at least three distinct fixed points. The proper straight-line homotopy is sufficient for degree +1; intermediate injectivity is unnecessary. The chordal tail bound follows from all-powers displacement and makes the extension recurrent. The filled orbit-ball closure is compact, connected, nondegenerate, invariant, and nonseparating; no local connectedness is imported. Two separated radius-3D disks force distinct finite fixed points. Both primary theorem texts match those uses.

Kolev–Pérouème v3 (2009-03-02) defines recurrence by a common positive sequence n_k tending to infinity and uniform metric return on a compact space; regularity requires equicontinuity of every iterate and is explicitly stronger. Its disk consequence is already explicit. Boroński v1 (2015-10-22), Theorem A, applies to an invariant acyclic continuum; the constructed set contains an open ball and meets its nondegeneracy convention.

The candidate proves the line and plane homeomorphism cases, and boundary-fixed closed interval/disk cases, hence those dimensions of the diffeomorphism variant as well. It supplies no derivative convergence estimate, arbitrary-dimensional theorem, manifold-wide local theorem, compact cyclic closure, or novelty certificate. The primary K3 statement does not literally impose n_j→+infinity, but its conventional subsequence-of-powers recurrence excludes a constant zeroth power; a sequence of distinct negative powers can be inverted in the compact-open homeomorphism topology on R^n. This conventional clarification is acceptable and should be visible. The dated literature review is historical evidence, not an exhaustive current-open assertion.

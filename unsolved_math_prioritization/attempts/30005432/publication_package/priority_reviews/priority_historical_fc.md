## Comparators checked beyond literal formula searches

Cedó–Smoktunowicz–Vendramin, arXiv:1806.01127 / Proc.Lond.Math.Soc.118(2019), Theorem5.2 (Rump2007 credit), gives the classical additive Z / circle D_infinity example n circle m=n+(-1)^n m. Its (s)-set is 2Z, an additive ideal, and its additive group has no C4 torsion. Theorem5.8 reverses the group types: circle Z and additive D_infinity. Neither is brace-isomorphic to the candidate.

Nishant, arXiv:2112.10343v1 (December20, 2021), Section4 Example1, gives an infinite Z x C extension using inversion in the additive extension as well as the circle extension. The displayed exponent contains an undefined n, visually confirmed; it was not silently repaired. For nontrivial inversion on C4 the additive group is nonabelian, unlike Z x C4. When inversion is trivial on C2 it loses the required action. Other finite order-four examples on that page are finite. Published DOI10.1142/S0219498824500099 body inaccessible, so this comparison is limited to the primary preprint.

Facchini–Pompili, arXiv:2301.09133v2 (August25, 2023), Remark3.4 and Remark4.5, provides Z x Z digroup constructions and eight trivial-Z extension choices. The valid nontrivial examples have nonabelian additive groups; the only additive direct Z^2 case is trivial. They do not exhibit the selected nontrivial C4/V4 source. Quotienting a Z^2 case to manufacture C4/V4 would require an additional mechanism not displayed there; no selected candidate quotient is credited without proof.

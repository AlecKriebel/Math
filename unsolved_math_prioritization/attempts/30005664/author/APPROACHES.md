# Five substantive approaches

Preparation was not counted as a proof attempt: exact problem/source identification, complete pinned-corpus hash checks, targeted current-literature search, and actual repository history/path/PR checks.

1. **Direct Clifford/Euler–Lagrange construction.** Inserted the candidate into the massive threshold equation in general spinor dimension. Retained Proposition 1, including H¹ admissibility, mass scaling, and the Birman–Schwinger upper-bound argument. The d=2,3 construction and bound are credited to DGPV. Gap: a solution of the equation does not maximize the variational quotient globally.
2. **Optimization across threshold widths.** Freed the width while keeping an explicit threshold eigenstate, then minimized the fixed L^p norm. Retained Proposition 2. Gap: this is one branch, not all rational potentials or all radial potentials.
3. **Pohozaev/virial rigidity.** Compared amplitude and L²-preserving dilation variations. Retained Proposition 3 in its expressly stated differentiability class. Gap: the identity constrains mass splitting and excludes a finite critical stationary solution at p=d in that class, but is not a sharp norm lower bound.
4. **Conformal/boundary scaling.** Tested whether the massless scale-invariant endpoint could certify the fixed-mass problem. Retained Proposition 4 and the dilation calculation. Gap: concentration and the changing mass prevent the unmodified conformal argument from identifying a fixed-width optimizer.
5. **Schur complement and angular decomposition.** Derived and factored the exact two-dimensional threshold form for the candidate in every Fourier mode. Retained Proposition 5 and its nonradial lower bound. Also gave an exact weighted-modulus counterexample to an intended scalar reduction. Gap: positivity at the frozen candidate is not an inequality uniform over competing potentials.

Stop after 5/5. Original problem unresolved. No claim that every conceivable method has been exhausted; no novelty, acceptance, or human-referee claim.

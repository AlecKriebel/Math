## Disposition and remaining gap

The exact algebraic results are stated for integral reduced images, rank-three cameras, and an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field. The two-camera distinct-center regime is treated with arbitrary singularities and epipole incidences; elementary constant-pencil and coincident-center weak-existence criteria are separated. Degree/smoothness tests via exact saturation do not constitute a uniform closed-form or efficient classification in every degenerate regime. General nonreduced/reducible conventions, real visible reconstruction and uncertainty remain outside the result. The broad source record stays **unsolved**.

## Why the affirmative construction works

The quotient action on S3 in C2 is generated by d(z1,z2)=(zeta z1,zeta^3 z2), where zeta has order eight. Complex-coordinate swap has positive real determinant and satisfies s d=d^3 s. It descends to an orientation-preserving self-diffeomorphism of L(8,3), inducing multiplication by three on its cyclic first homology. An explicit radial local isotopy makes a representative equal to the identity near a ball without changing this homology action.

That representative extends over the untwisted spin by its product with the identity on the punctured 3-manifold times S1 and by the identity over the S2 times D2 cap. Van Kampen gives C8, with the same multiplication-by-three action. Meier's spun-trisection theorem applied to a genus-one Heegaard splitting supplies a balanced (3,1) trisection T. Its image F(T) is diffeomorphic to T by construction; no naturality assertion about the existence theorem is needed.

Each sector surjects onto the ambient fundamental group. A generator of each rank-one sector therefore maps to a unit of Z/8, determined up to sign. The two sign classes are {1,7} and {3,5}, exchanged by multiplication by three. An ambient isotopy acts trivially on ambient homology. Even after a permutation of sectors, equality of the three-sector sign-class multisets would require an equal number of the two classes in an odd multiset. This is impossible. The argument excludes a trisection stabilizer, rather than merely noticing that F is homologically nontrivial.

The spin has Euler characteristic two. For an arbitrary possibly unbalanced competitor, g=k1+k2+k3, and every ki is at least one because its sector surjects onto the nontrivial ambient group. Thus g is at least three, proving minimality of the exhibited pair.

The exact dependencies, direct sector-surjectivity proof, ball-fixing construction, quotient convention and stabilization limits are in the proof and both independent reviews.

## What remains open in part (b)

Four additional approaches have valid bounded outcomes:

1. Framed circle surgery kills the cyclic fundamental group and yields Euler characteristic four and b2=2. Transporting the surgery through F also transports the ambient diffeomorphism; the rank-one cyclic invariant disappears after simple connectivity. This does not rule out every non-equivariant surgery approach.
2. Doubling the known inequivalent relative (2,1;0,2) trisections gives closed balanced (5,1) trisections on S2 times S2. The distinction proved here requires a designated positive half. Intrinsic recovery of that half or seam, and unmarked non-diffeomorphism, remain unproved.
3. The integral classification of efficient ordered homological triples reduces to congruence of symmetric unimodular forms. It is not a classification of geometric trisection diagrams, does not certify Torelli regluings, and does not address the non-efficient (5,1) triples by that classification.
4. The central-surface exterior in a closed simply connected oriented manifold has H1=Z, H2=H2(X) direct-sum Z^(2g), and H3=H4=0. These groups do not distinguish the proposed fixed-manifold, fixed-genus examples. Their fundamental groups and peripheral data were not computed.

These are five substantive approaches in total, including the completed part-(a) construction. Audits, source retrieval and verification reruns are not extra proof-search turns.

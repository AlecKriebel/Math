## Original scope and retrieval limits

The AIM item concerns whether mixed mock modular forms obey differential equations with respect to a modular function. Its legacy location is http://aimpl.org/gromwitnumthry/5/ (item 5.2). The legacy page fetch timed out during recovery, so the exact question is not independently live-page verified. The accessible workshop report https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/gromwitnumthryrep.pdf establishes workshop context, not the exact sentence.

The broad question must not be silently reduced to the specific weight-zero h4 example. Raw versus weight-normalized unknowns, scalar linear ODEs over a modular-function field, nonlinear differential-algebraic relations, MLDEs in tau, and completion PDEs have different scopes.

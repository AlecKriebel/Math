# Five substantive approach families

These are five mathematically distinct approaches, not five tool calls or a claim that five separate chat messages were emitted. Source identification, the literature check, and finite diagnostic reruns are not extra research turns. All are unsuccessful at resolving the full conjecture.

1. **Conjugacy averaging.** Constructed an explicit averaging conjugacy for one lift and controlled displacement of powers of the other lift. Retained the complete known global m-bound proof and its q=1 consequence. Applying it to w^q cannot improve the bound: both the word's block count and translation number scale by q.
2. **Exact product formula and powers.** Used the credited Jankins–Neumann–Naimi formula, proved attainment of a candidate when the defect is positive, tracked reduction of denominators under taking word powers, and proved the target for every (ab)^m at real inputs. No independent-generator substitution or arbitrary root existence assumption is used.
3. **Full-period orbit averaging.** Checked the exact scope of Chowdhury's prior theorem and proved its rational case directly by phase injectivity, all-gap coverage, and cyclic counting. This covers an output denominator equal to an input denominator; the hypothesized full coverage is unavailable in the general case.
4. **Non-full periodic orbits.** Derived the exact missing inequality (5.2); constructed a valid finite-order configuration refuting the natural phasewise replacement; implemented and cross-checked the exact Calegari–Walker oracle over a bounded family. The negative controls concern wrong proof steps, not the conjecture.
5. **Limit and quotient rigidity.** Proved the denominator-divergence argument at slippery points, recovered the conditional implication from the refined bound, and gave an explicit dihedral calculation of the half-rotation lower-left value. These limit facts do not imply the pointwise finite-denominator bound.

The full retained arguments and their exact obstacles are in RESULT.md. None is omitted on the ground that it failed. The complete problem remains unresolved by this investigation after 5/5 approaches.

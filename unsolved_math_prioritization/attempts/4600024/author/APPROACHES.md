# Three substantive approaches; early stop for a complete candidate

1. **Canonical finite-type neighborhoods.** Replaced arbitrary SFT supershifts and arbitrary-radius extensions by the original completed local rule on canonical finite-forbidden-word approximations. Proved invariance under choosing a different completion and an exact all-length regular-intersection test at each fixed approximation order. This initially gives only a positive semidecision: testing successively larger orders has no failure stopping certificate.
2. **Invertibility and explicit obstruction families.** Proved the automorphism case holds exactly for SFT domains. The identity of the mixing even shift gives an infinite family of locally admissible forbidden odd-gap words, ruling out every approximation order. This excludes a universal-existence answer but does not classify non-injective surjective self-codes. Constant maps were kept separate because they violate surjectivity.
3. **Locally testable separation.** Expressed bad input blocks as a regular two-sided ideal. The saturation argument converts every LT[k] separator into a canonical SFT witness of order at least k. The published Place–van Rooijen–Zeitoun finite-bound theorem removes the unbounded search in approach 1 and supplies the complete candidate criterion in PROOF.md. Stopped here rather than manufacture fourth and fifth attempts.

The third approach is a proved reduction to an established external theorem, awaiting independent audit. It is not a declaration of novelty, a claimed existing publication resolving the exact question, or a solution of Boyle Problems 16.1 or 16.2.

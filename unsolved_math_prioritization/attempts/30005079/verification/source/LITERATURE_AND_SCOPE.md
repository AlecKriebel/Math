Checked 2026-10-07. Problem 30005079 / OWR-10252925-004.

## Original question

Harold Blum's contribution, “Degenerations of unstable Fano varieties and Kahler-Ricci soliton,” occupies pp. 888–889 of *Toric Geometry*, Oberwolfach Report 17/2022. The workshop dates were 27 March–2 April 2022. The relevant sentence on p. 889 is:

“An important problem is to explicitly describe non-trivial examples and, hopefully, find one where the valuation v is not divisorial.”

The preceding discussion explicitly concerns the canonical first degeneration X→Z minimizing H, with nontriviality meaning X is not isomorphic to Z. It does not impose a dimension limit, exclude product varieties, or require both steps to change the underlying variety. The target reconstructed in the queue is faithful to this context. Source: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2022/17 and https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46955.

## Literature distinctions

The supplied Miao–Wang DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-025-03202-w belongs to *Mathematische Annalen* 392 (2025), 4483–4523, not *Mathematische Zeitschrift*. Its paper, “Kähler-Ricci solitons on Fano threefolds with non-trivial moduli,” is https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.14212v4. Nonzero solitons and nontrivial moduli alone do not meet the question's requirement that the canonical first central variety differ from the original.

A more directly relevant primary paper is Miao–Wang, “Optimal Degenerations of K-unstable Fano threefolds,” https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.13999v2, revised 13 April 2026. Its Corollaries 1.4 and 1.8 give explicit nontrivial optimal degenerations in family 2.23, but both displayed minimizing valuations are positive real multiples of prime-divisor valuations. The publisher also lists the paper under DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2026.111194, *Advances in Mathematics* 503 (2026), article 111194. Our mathematical inspection used the openly accessible dated arXiv v2.

No located primary source was found already stating the exact V×F_1 rank-two example proved in the candidate. That is a bounded search result, not a novelty claim. The construction is presented as an authored deduction from the precise published inputs, not as an attribution of a nondivisorial example to Miao–Wang alone.

## What the candidate adds

The candidate explicitly combines a nontrivially degenerating threefold with a toric surface. Its two exact entropy weights have an irrational ratio, proved using Hermite–Lindemann and a valuation at infinity. The proof also checks the associated graded section ring and derives weighted semistability from a product weak soliton before invoking Han–Li's criterion. Thus it does not infer global optimality merely from separate numerical minimizations.

The F_1 factor has an isomorphic central fiber. The total fivefold degeneration is nevertheless nontrivial: its original variety is smooth and its central fiber is singular. The real filtration has rational rank two, so no rescaling makes it an integral one-parameter test configuration.

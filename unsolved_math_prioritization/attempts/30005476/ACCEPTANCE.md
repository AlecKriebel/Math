## Credit and scope

Kummer–Riener’s final 2025 article, printed page 42, already announces Nicolà
Schnieper’s proof using the Shift Theorem and identifies his 2024 Bern master’s
thesis, *Orbit closures and G-irreducibility*, in reference [21]. The thesis
itself was not retrieved or independently verified. This is an alternative
proof of that previously announced result, with no first-resolution or novelty claim.

The accepted theorem concerns all finite positive widths, all algebraically
closed fields, ordinary prime spectra, finite-support permutations, and the
topological orbit quotient followed by its Kolmogorov quotient. In particular
it contains the original uncountable-field setting. It is not a theorem about
only rational points or an invariant-ring/GIT quotient.

## Accepted reasoning

The complete countable-ring lemma, scalar-extension intersection lemma,
geometric-integrality specialization argument, descent/ascent construction,
prime-core realization, sobriety argument, and every remaining spectral axiom
are retained. The finite-union-of-orbit-closures corollary is also accepted.
The mathematical audit found no blocking defect in that argument.

Reducedness, repeated powers in the multiplicative set, the per-ideal field of
definition, nonclosed generic points, all characteristics, and finite width
are explicitly checked. No ordinary Noetherianity of the countable polynomial
ring is assumed, and no arbitrary-flat-intersection assertion is used.

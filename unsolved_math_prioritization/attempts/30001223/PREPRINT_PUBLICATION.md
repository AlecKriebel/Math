# Accepted preprint for problem 30001223

[Simple Young modules with nonprojective polynomial injective hulls](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23418581) by Alec Kriebel, ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X, version 1.0. For every odd prime p over an algebraically closed field of characteristic p at q=1, r=2p-1 and lambda=(2p-2,1), the source-convention Young module is simple of dimension2p-2 but I_n(lambda) is nonprojective for EVERY n>=2. This completely refutes both broad proposed bridges in Miyachi's printed2009 Conjecture7; the stronger index-set proposal is not an assumed established equivalence. The separate DeVisscher-Donkin classification, characteristic-zero nontrivial-root variants and p2 are outside the result.

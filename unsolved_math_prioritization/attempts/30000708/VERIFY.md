# Verification scope

Run `python check_source_example.py` with pre-existing SymPy 1.14.0.
The deterministic output is `source_example_receipt.json`. This is
algebraic validation of a published example, not a proof substitute or
an original research turn.

The script checks the real density, homogeneity, the complex-line
primitive and its endpoints, the absolute bound, the polar density,
and finite exterior/infinite origin total variation. Fubini is justified
analytically by the absolute exterior integrability. The uniqueness
theorems themselves are read from the complete primary manuscript and
must be checked by the independent source reviewer, not inferred from
finite tests.

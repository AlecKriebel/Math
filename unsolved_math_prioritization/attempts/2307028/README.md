## Scope

Continuity holds on an arbitrary plane domain. At every center, the circle integral divided by r squared tends to zero through all sufficiently small positive admissible radii. No uniformity in the center is assumed. Exact local vanishing is a stronger special case. Selected radius sequences and a big-O bound are not covered.

The historical source is [Hayman and Lingham, Research Problems in Function Theory](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200v2), Problem 7.28. The harmonic mean-value input is [Kuznetsov's survey](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.08312v2), Theorem 1.6; the audit also supplies a direct proof.

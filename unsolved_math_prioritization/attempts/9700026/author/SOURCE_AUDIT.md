# Source and claim boundary

Reviewed 6 October 2026.

- The 2007 notes [1] explicitly permit alpha>0 and beta>0 on printed p.2. Their notation convention on p.7 introduces no exponent normalization. Section 6.4 on p.40 specifies the fixed-city ODE and the alpha>1 collapse clause. Page 40 was checked in extracted text and a rendered image. Parameter references across the notes were inspected; no restriction tying alpha to beta was found.
- The 2012 paper [2], section 2, instead specifies 0<alpha<=1. It does not state Conjecture 32. Its stochastic process permits new cities. These differences matter: the attached correction addresses the literal 2007 ODE statement, not a conjecture about that stochastic process.
- The maintained author page still links both documents. Targeted current searches found no identified resolution of this exact ODE claim; absence of a search result establishes neither novelty nor open status.
- The problem webpage could not be read live: the web tool failed and direct retrieval returned HTTP 403. Its exact-ID imported record was verified privately against the complete corpus and catalog review hash. The mathematical claim was independently checked in the actual primary PDF.

# Spherical Fibonacci separation: full first-turn candidate

For the exact unshifted rational-angle Fibonacci lattice of OWR 40/2012, the candidate proves that the minimum Euclidean chordal distance is 2/sqrt(F_n) for every nontrivial size F_n>=2. The pole pair attains it.

The proof combines an exact two-latitude chord inequality with an elementary Cassini/quadratic-form residue bound. It is uniform over all pairs and handles Fibonacci sizes 2, 3 and 5 separately. Both printed endpoint-index conventions are congruent. Shifted heights, irrational-angle spirals, discrepancy asymptotics and optimal packing among arbitrary point sets are outside the theorem.

Neither finite computation replaces the written argument.

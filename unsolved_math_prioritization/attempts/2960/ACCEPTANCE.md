## Mathematical acceptance

The original report and independent audit are accepted unchanged. The target is
an existential choice of a closed oriented smooth four-manifold followed by a
universal quantifier over prescribed finite subgroups of `pi0 Diff+(X)`. A realization
is an exact homomorphism whose composition with the smooth-component quotient is
the specified inclusion. Topological isotopy, homological actions, and homotopy-coherent
actions are distinct problems.

The five approaches give the following accepted results and boundaries:

1. A conditional CP2 complement criterion, rational-vector-space averaging, and an
   exact algebraic countermodel show why a torsion-free smooth kernel plus a realized
   quotient does not suffice. The needed kernel and complement hypotheses are unproved.
2. Linear actions on T4 realize every finite subgroup of its standard SL(4,Z) subgroup
   and its conjugates. Affine translations add no smooth mapping classes. The full
   smooth kernel and nonstandard complements remain uncontrolled.
3. Every finite subgroup of the standard product-and-factor-swap subgroup of a product
   of genus-at-least-two surfaces is realized. Conjugating a finite swapped subgroup
   into a finite surface-group wreath product supplies one compatible surface action.
   This is not a theorem about every finite subgroup of the full four-dimensional
   smooth mapping class group.
4. The metric fixed-point criterion retains the stabilizer-extension splitting
   condition. The Pin(2) nonsplitting example shows why fixedness alone is insufficient.
5. Euler-characteristic congruence and faithful tangent representations give necessary
   elementary-abelian rank bounds when the prime does not divide the Euler characteristic.
   They do not obstruct every four-manifold, in particular Euler-zero candidates.

The independent wreath calculation uses permutations of six points, not the original
triple-multiplication implementation. It enumerates 112 subgroups, 52 with swaps,
and all 228 swapped representative choices. The report's general algebraic proof is
the theorem; this enumeration is a finite corroboration. No finite test computes a
smooth mapping class group, proves a missing smooth-kernel hypothesis, or resolves
the original question.

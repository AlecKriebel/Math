# Substantive turn 3: exact cancellation inside the two localized flows

**Unreviewed partial, 3/5 substantive author turns. The original generalized-tame membership question is unresolved.** Date: 2026-10-01.

This turn classifies exactly which words in the source's two localized flows are polynomial automorphisms. It gives a relative obstruction and a concrete abstract character. Neither extends here to all allowed polynomial locally nilpotent derivation (LND) factors; that distinction is essential.

## 1. The localized group and composition convention

Use R=C[x,y,z], K=C(x), u=x^2*y+z^2 and v=z. Thus K[y,z]=K[u,v]. Products denote composition of ring maps, as in Turns 1 and 2. For a in C put

    A_a(u)=u,                  A_a(v)=v+a*x*u,
    B_a(u)=u+2*a*x*v^3,        B_a(v)=v.

These are the two source-credited localized exponentials. They obey A_a A_b=A_(a+b) and B_a B_b=B_(a+b). On R[x^(-1)],

    A_a(y)=y-2*a*z*(x^2*y+z^2)/x-a^2*(x^2*y+z^2)^2,
    B_a(y)=y+2*a*z^3/x.

For nonzero a both maps have actual poles along x=0. In contrast,

    g_a := A_a B_a = gamma_a

is the polynomial family in Turn 2, including g_0=1 and g_1=gamma. Let F be the subgroup of Aut_K K[u,v] generated by all A_a and B_a.

## 2. No extra relations: F is a free product

**Lemma 1.** The natural homomorphism (C,+)*(C,+) -> F is an isomorphism.

Proof. A reduced word alternates nonzero letters from the two additive groups. Every nonidentity abstract reduced word is conjugate either to a nonzero single letter or to an alternating cyclically reduced word. By cyclic rotation the latter has the form

    W=A_(a_1) B_(b_1) ... A_(a_r) B_(b_r),

where r>=1 and all a_i,b_i are nonzero. For one block, the images of (u,v) have degrees (3,1) in (u,v). If a preceding product has images (U,V) of degrees (3^j,3^(j-1)), appending A_a B_b gives images

    U+2*b*x*(V+a*x*U)^3,    V+a*x*U.

Because a,b,x are nonzero in K and deg U>deg V, their degrees are (3^(j+1),3^j), without leading-term cancellation. Thus W has degrees (3^r,3^(r-1)); its n-th power has degrees (3^(rn),3^(rn-1)). It cannot be the identity. Single nonzero letters are also nonidentity, and conjugation preserves nonidentity. This proves injectivity. The degree calculation is over K, where x is a nonzero scalar. QED.

## 3. Exact polynomial intersection

Define the additive character

    chi:F -> (C,+),       chi(A_a)=a, chi(B_a)=-a.

Let H=<g_a : a in C>. Direct reduction of words gives

    A_b g_a A_(-b) = g_(a+b) g_b^(-1).             (1)

Hence H is normalized by every A_b. Also B_a=A_(-a) g_a, so F=H A, where A={A_a}. Since chi(H)=0 and chi restricts to the identity on A, each element has a unique expression h A_c, with h in H and c=chi(f). In particular H=ker chi.

**Theorem 2.** Under the fixed embedding R subset K[u,v],

    F intersect Aut_C(R) = H.                      (2)

Proof. Each g_a and its inverse is polynomial by the explicit Turn 2 formulas, so H is contained in the left side. Conversely, let f=h A_c belong to Aut_C(R). Then A_c=h^(-1) f would also be a polynomial automorphism. The coefficient of x^(-1) in A_c(y) is -2*c*z^3, which is nonzero for c!=0. Thus c=0 and f=h is in H. QED.

This is a complete cancellation classification inside F, not a classification of all x-fixing polynomial automorphisms or of all generalized-tame maps.

## 4. The polynomial intersection is freely generated by the family

**Theorem 3.** H is the free group on {g_a : a in C, a!=0}.

Here is an explicit argument, rather than an appeal to a tree action. Let L be the abstract free group on symbols G_a (a!=0), with G_0=1. For b in C define

    alpha_b(G_a)=G_(a+b) G_b^(-1).

Substitution shows alpha_b alpha_c=alpha_(b+c); alpha_0 is the identity. Thus these maps are automorphisms of L. Form the semidirect product L semidirect (C,+), with A_b implementing alpha_b. In that product define B_a=A_(-a) G_a. Then

    B_a B_b
      = A_(-(a+b)) alpha_b(G_a) G_b
      = A_(-(a+b)) G_(a+b)
      = B_(a+b).

Therefore the free product of the A and B additive groups maps to this semidirect product. Conversely, send G_a to A_a B_a and send A_b to A_b in F. Identity (1) verifies the required semidirect relation. These homomorphisms are inverse on all generators. Together with Lemma 1, this identifies H with L and proves the claim.

## 5. A rigorous relative obstruction to single allowed factors

**Corollary 4.** Every nonidentity element of H has unbounded degrees of iterates, and hence is not the exponential of a polynomial LND on R.

Indeed, a nonidentity element of ker chi cannot be conjugate in F to a nonzero single A or B letter, because chi is conjugacy invariant and such a letter has nonzero chi. It is therefore conjugate to one of the mixed cyclically reduced words in Lemma 1. Their iterate degrees grow exponentially. Conjugation by a fixed polynomial K-automorphism cannot make these degrees bounded: deg(P Q) <= deg P deg Q applied in both directions bounds the change by the constant deg C deg C^(-1).

If h=exp(D) for a polynomial LND on R, then h^n=exp(nD). For each coordinate q, only finitely many D^j(q) are nonzero. Consequently the total coordinate degrees of exp(nD), for integer n, are bounded independently of n. Replacing y by (u-v^2)/x^2 gives a uniform bound on its K[u,v] degrees, contradicting the preceding growth. This reasoning does not presume D(x)=0.

Thus gamma cannot be factored using polynomial LND exponentials each of which itself belongs to F: the only such individual element of F is the identity. This statement does **not** exclude a factorization with intermediate factors outside F, including ones that move x.

Indeed, Turn 2 already supplies nonidentity elements g_t g_s^(-1) in H which are finite products of polynomial LND exponentials in the larger group. For t,s nonzero and t!=s these words are nonidentity by Theorem 3. Their individual allowed factors cannot all lie in F. This is an explicit warning against confusing the relative obstruction with an obstruction to the original generated subgroup.

## 6. The surviving abstract character and its precise limit

Let U be the normal subgroup generated by all polynomial LND exponentials on R. Since H is free, there is a homomorphism

    epsilon:H -> Z,       epsilon(g_a)=1 (a!=0).    (3)

Let J be the normal closure in H of all g_t g_s^(-1), for nonzero t,s. Identifying every free generator gives

    H/J = Z,   J=ker epsilon.                      (4)

For lambda!=0 the determinant-one polynomial diagonal map d_lambda from Turn 2 lies in U and conjugates g_s to g_(lambda^4*s). Over C every nonzero t/s has a fourth root, so g_t g_s^(-1) is in U. Normality gives J subset H intersect U. Therefore the image of H in Aut(R)/U is a quotient of the cyclic group Z, generated by the coset of gamma.

No assertion that epsilon vanishes on H intersect U has been proved. That missing assertion would itself imply nonmembership of gamma and cannot be supplied by the free-group calculation alone. Conversely, exhibiting an allowed polynomial-LND word whose value lies in H with epsilon equal to 1 or -1 would prove gamma in U; two such values with coprime augmentations would also suffice. The known relative words have augmentation zero.

The character epsilon is discontinuous along the polynomial family g_a at a=0: it takes value 1 for every nonzero a and 0 at the identity. This agrees with the closure result in Turn 2 and explains why extending it to a regular quotient cannot provide the desired obstruction. No extension to an abstract quotient of the full automorphism group has been established either.

## 7. Audit of the general generation literature

Arzhantsev, Flenner, Kaliman, Kutzschebauch and Zaidenberg, *Flexible varieties and automorphism groups*, Duke Math. J. 162 (2013), 767-823, DOI [10.1215/00127094-2080132](https://doi.org/10.1215/00127094-2080132), defines SAut as the subgroup generated by additive-group actions. The complete final preprint [arXiv:1011.5375v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.5375v2) was checked. Its Theorem 0.1 (p.2) concerns flexibility and transitivity. Its Theorem 0.2 (p.3, detailed as Theorem 4.14) realizes compatible finite volume-preserving jets. Neither states that every Jacobian-one polynomial automorphism of C^3 belongs to this generated subgroup.

In particular, the broad finite-jet interpolation result is existing literature; the Turn 2 refinement uses the eight specified flow directions for this target. Neither version assembles the independently obtained finite jets into one finite exact polynomial word. The source's rational factorization and the 2012 stable-tameness theorem remain credited with their different, explicitly limited scopes.

## 8. Checkpoint and remaining gap

The exact result of this turn is (2), the free basis in Theorem 3, and the relative growth obstruction. The checker verifies finite symbolic identities, semidirect normal forms and sample degrees; the all-word and all-iterate assertions rest on the proofs above.

The original target still asks for a finite word in polynomial LND exponentials allowing factors outside F, or a valid invariant of that entire generated subgroup. Exact cancellation among the two original localized flow directions has now been classified, but it does not close that global gap. No ind-group closedness, compatible infinite-jet lifting, or novelty claim is made.

# Substantive turn 5: nonlinear multi-flow words and exact global certificates

**Unreviewed partial. Five substantive author turns completed; original target unsolved.** Date: 2026-10-01.

The final turn attacks finite products rather than only a single exponential or commutator. It excludes all words in six concrete polynomial LND flows, including directions moving x, and gives a finite algebraic-certificate formulation for membership using arbitrary polynomial LNDs. The six-flow obstruction is not extended to the full allowed subgroup.

## 1. Six genuine polynomial flows

For every ordered permutation (i,j,k) of (x,y,z), put

    Delta_i=x_i^2*x_j+x_k^2,
    D_i=Delta_i*(x_i^2*partial_(x_k)-2*x_k*partial_(x_j)).

The notation D_i here includes the whole ordered triple; there are six choices. The derivation kills x_i and Delta_i, and

    D_i(x_k)=x_i^2*Delta_i,       D_i^2(x_k)=0,
    D_i(x_j)=-2*x_k*Delta_i,
    D_i^2(x_j)=-2*x_i^2*Delta_i^2, D_i^3(x_j)=0.

It is therefore a polynomial LND. Its flow N_(i,j,k)(t)=exp(t D_i) is

    x_i -> x_i,
    x_k -> x_k+t*x_i^2*Delta_i,
    x_j -> x_j-2*t*x_k*Delta_i-t^2*x_i^2*Delta_i^2.              (1)

All six entire additive groups lie in the full allowed subgroup U. Four of the six directions move x. They are coordinate-permutation conjugates of familiar invariant-multiple, Nagata-type polynomial LNDs. They are not the two nonpolynomial localized flows of Turn 3.

## 2. An all-word degree-cone theorem

**Theorem 1.** The subgroup generated by the six flows (1) is their abstract free product. Every nonidentity element represented by a reduced word of length l has degree8 if l=1, and degree strictly greater than 8*2^(l-1) if l>=2. In particular gamma, whose total degree is11, is not in this subgroup, even after adjoining coordinate permutations.

Proof. Adjacent letters from the same flow group combine by addition of their parameters; delete a zero parameter. It suffices to analyze a word in which all remaining parameters are nonzero and consecutive directions are distinct.

For a single letter, the coordinate degrees, listed as fixed / largest / middle, are

    (p,H,M)=(1,8,5).

The highest terms are -t^2*x_i^6*x_j^2 and t*x_i^4*x_j, so these degrees are exact. We prove inductively that after any reduced word the last direction identifies a cone

    M>4p,        H=2M-2p.                                    (2)

In particular H>M>p>0. For the next direction, let P,Q,R be the existing degrees of its fixed, x_j and x_k coordinate, respectively. If

    d=max(2P+Q,2R)

has a unique maximizing term, substitution in (1) gives new degrees

    P,       H_new=2P+2d,       M_new=2P+d.                    (3)

The squared-Delta term in the x_j image strictly dominates its other terms: its degree minus the degree R+d of the linear-in-t term is 2P+d-R>0. The new x_k term similarly strictly dominates its old value. Thus no parameter cancellation is hidden in (3).

There are only five cases, because repeating the same direction was removed:

- Fixed coordinate is the previous smallest p, with the other two roles reversed. Then Q=M, R=H and d=2H>2p+M.
- Fixed coordinate is the previous middle M and Q=H, R=p. Then d=2M+H>2p.
- Fixed coordinate is M and Q=p, R=H. Here 2H-(2M+p)=2M-5p>0 by (2), so d=2H.
- Fixed coordinate is the previous largest H and Q=M, R=p. Then d=2H+M.
- Fixed coordinate is H and Q=p, R=M. Here (2H+p)-2M=2M-3p>0, so d=2H+p.

The omitted repeated direction is exactly the case in which 2p+H=2M ties and the preserved Delta may cancel; it has already been combined using the additive-group law. Every genuinely new direction above has a unique leading term and d>2P. Formula (3) therefore gives M_new>4P and H_new=2M_new-2P, preserving the cone. In every case H_new>2H. This proves the degree bound by induction, uniformly for all nonzero complex times and every word length.

Every nonempty abstract reduced word consequently has degree greater than1 and is nonidentity. This proves the free-product assertion. Coordinate permutations normalize the set of six groups. Every word with these permutations can be rewritten as a flow word followed by one permutation; permutations do not change degree. The possible degrees are therefore1,8 or strictly greater than16, excluding gamma's degree11. QED.

This is a rigorous failure of a concrete multi-nonlinear-factor construction. The subgroup does not contain all polynomial LND exponentials or all tame automorphisms. For example translations were not adjoined to this degree-cone theorem. Its conclusion cannot be promoted to nonmembership in U.

## 3. The affine mixing gap from Turn 4 can also be closed

The preceding multi-flow result is the principal new construction test. Independently, the previously missing affine directions admit a short degree argument.

**Theorem 2.** For every invertible affine map A of C^3, the iterates of gamma A have unbounded degree. Consequently gamma is not a single arbitrary polynomial LND exponential padded on either side by arbitrary affine maps. Nor is gamma=[H,A] for any polynomial automorphism H and affine A.

For a tuple of polynomial coordinates of positive degrees p,q,r, suppose 2p+q differs from 2r, and set d=max(2p+q,2r). In the source gamma formula, Delta has degree d without cancellation, and the gamma outputs have degrees

    P=p,        R=2p+3d,        S=p+d,

in the (x,y,z) order. The cubic-Delta term in gamma(y) uniquely dominates all others. These degrees satisfy P<S<R and R>2S.

After an invertible affine mixing, each coordinate degree is one of P,S,R: the three degrees before mixing are distinct, so its highest nonzero coefficient cannot cancel. At least one coordinate has degree R. For any resulting triple (p',q',r'), the equality 2p'+q'=2r' is impossible:

- If p'=R, the left side is larger than2R.
- If p'<R but r'=R, the left side is at most2S+R<2R.
- Otherwise q'=R, and the left side is larger than R>2S>=2r'.

Starting from (x,y,z), the first Delta comparison is3>2 and the gamma output degrees are (1,11,4). The argument therefore inducts through every iterate of gamma A. At each stage d is at least the current maximum coordinate degree, so the next maximum 2p+3d is more than three times that maximum. This proves unbounded degree for arbitrary affine mixing, including maps moving x and mixing y into z.

Left/right affine padding reduces to gamma A by affine conjugacy. A single LND exponential has bounded iterate degrees, so it is excluded. Finally, if gamma=[H,A], the identity

    (gamma A)^n=H A^n H^(-1)

would bound its degree uniformly in n, also impossible.

This remains an obstruction to a limited word form. The explicit h=exp(z^3*partial_y) exp(y*partial_z) from Turn 4 belongs to U while having a translation-commutator obstruction. Unbounded degrees of products in U remain entirely possible. Theorem 2 does not change that fact.

## 4. Exact finite algebraic systems for arbitrary LND words

There is a precise global membership formulation which avoids restricting the unknown derivations to the preceding families.

Fix integers l>=1, b>=1 and m>=1. Introduce arbitrary polynomial derivations

    E_i=sum_(j=1)^3 P_(i,j)(x,y,z)*partial_(x_j),    i=1,...,l,

where every P_(i,j) has degree at most b and every coefficient is an unknown. Impose all coefficient equations in

    E_i^m(x_j)=0      for all i,j.                            (4)

These finite equations certify local nilpotence on the whole polynomial ring: a monomial of total degree N is killed by E_i^(N(m-1)+1), by the repeated Leibniz rule. Form the polynomial coordinate maps

    F_i(x_j)=sum_(k=0)^(m-1) E_i^k(x_j)/k!,                  (5)

and impose all coefficient equations in

    F_1 F_2 ... F_l = gamma.                                 (6)

Equations (4)-(6) are finite polynomial equations with rational coefficients in finitely many unknowns. They are necessary and sufficient for a factorization with these bounds. A solution gives genuine global polynomial LNDs and their exponential automorphisms. Conversely, any finite factorization of gamma in U has finite coefficient degrees and finite coordinate nilpotence bounds, so it gives a solution for some (l,b,m). Parameters of flows are absorbed into the derivations.

One explicit degree bound is

    deg F_i <= Q := 1+(m-1)(b-1),
    deg(F_1...F_l) <= Q^l,                                  (7)

because a derivation with coefficient degree at most b raises degree by at most b-1. Thus (6) compares only finitely many coefficients, up to max(11,Q^l). This also identifies what a finite-jet argument would need: a factorization with fixed bounds and agreement through that full degree bound. The independent jet words of Turn 2 have no such uniform bound and do not establish (6).

**Arithmetic consequence.** Gamma belongs to the subgroup generated by polynomial LND exponentials over C if and only if it has such a finite factorization with all derivation coefficients algebraic over Q. Indeed, a complex solution of one of these rational finite systems makes its ideal proper after extension to the algebraic closure of Q. The [Hilbert Nullstellensatz, Stacks Project tag 00FV](https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FV), gives a point with algebraic-number coordinates. Conversely these coefficients embed into C. All finitely many coefficients lie in a common number field.

This is an exact reduction, not a search success. No system yielding (6) for gamma has been solved here. No a priori bound on l,b,m has been found, and failure at finitely many bounds would not prove nonmembership. The reduction does show that an eventual positive answer has a finite algebraic certificate; it does not furnish a terminating negative test.

## 5. Final five-turn disposition

The concrete all-word construction using six polynomial nonlinear flow directions and coordinate permutations fails by Theorem 1. Arbitrary affine padding does not reduce gamma to one exponential by Theorem 2. The unrestricted question is exactly the existence of a solution to at least one of the systems (4)-(6), with no bounds currently known.

The original problem therefore remains **unsolved after5/5 substantive author turns**. Closure, exact finite jets, rational two-factor expressions, the six-variable stabilization theorem and restricted free-product obstructions have not been substituted for a finite three-variable polynomial-LND factorization or a universal obstruction. The packet is now ready for independent adversarial review as a collection of scoped partial results, with no novelty claim.

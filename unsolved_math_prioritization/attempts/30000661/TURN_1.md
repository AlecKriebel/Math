# Substantive turn 1: normalization, the polynomial inverse, and the one-exponential obstruction

**Unreviewed partial; original generalized-tame membership unresolved.** Date: 2026-10-01. This turn reconstructs the source's localized exponential factorization, identifies precisely where it fails to be polynomial, and obtains a cubic iteration normal form. It excludes a single polynomial LND exponential, not membership in the subgroup generated by many such maps.

All compositions below are compositions of ring homomorphisms: (sigma tau)(f)=sigma(tau(f)). This convention is important when comparing them with compositions of point maps.

## 1. Polynomial inverse and Jacobian

Let R=C[x,y,z], Delta=x^2*y+z^2, and set

    Theta=Delta-2*x*z^3.

The displayed gamma satisfies

    gamma(Delta)=Delta+2*x*gamma(z)^3,
    gamma(Theta)=Delta.                              (1)

Its inverse is the polynomial map

    gamma^(-1)(x)=x,
    gamma^(-1)(z)=z-x*Theta,
    gamma^(-1)(y)=y+2*x*y*z-4*z^4-Theta^2.            (2)

For example, recovering Delta_old=Theta_new and z_old=z-x*Theta gives

    y_old=[Theta-(z-x*Theta)^2]/x^2
          =y+2*x*y*z-4*z^4-Theta^2,

so the apparent denominator cancels. Direct substitution in both orders verifies (2) as a polynomial identity, including x=0. The Jacobian determinant is exactly one. Thus none of the subgroup questions below is being replaced by the unproved general Jacobian conjecture.

## 2. The source's two exponential factors are localized factors

Work in R[x^(-1)]. Put

    D0=x^2*partial_z-2*z*partial_y,
    N=exp((Delta/x)*D0),
    E=exp((2*z^3/x)*partial_y).

D0 is locally nilpotent, fixes x and annihilates Delta. Hence its multiple Delta/x is still locally nilpotent over this localized ring. Explicitly,

    N(z)=z+x*Delta,
    N(y)=y-2*z*Delta/x-Delta^2,
    E(z)=z,
    E(y)=y+2*z^3/x.

Then

    gamma=N E.                                      (3)

The y image is N(y)+2*N(z)^3/x, and expansion gives exactly the OWR polynomial. This realizes the rational factorization already credited in the source; it is not claimed as a new solution.

Both factors have actual poles at x=0. Multiplying their y images by x and setting x=0 gives −2*z^3 for N and +2*z^3 for E. Neither individually maps R into R. Consequently (3) is not an allowed generalized-tame factorization over C[x,y,z]. Its pole cancellation in the product is the issue to be resolved, not a reason to accept its factors as generators.

For every nonzero specialization x=c, those factors are genuine polynomial LND exponentials on C[y,z]. The zero specialization of gamma is the elementary shear

    (y,z) -> (y+5*z^4,z).

Thus every constant-x fiber has a generalized-tame description. This fiberwise observation is not a global three-variable factorization. The known Berson–van den Essen–Wright stable result, credited in SOURCE_SCOPE.md, likewise allows extra variables and does not remove this gap.

## 3. Cubic normal form over C(x)

Over K=C(x), the pair (u,v)=(Delta,z) is a polynomial coordinate pair of K[y,z], since y=(u-v^2)/x^2. Formula (1) becomes

    gamma(v)=v+x*u,
    gamma(u)=u+2*x*(v+x*u)^3.

Setting w=v+x*u gives the cubic normal form

    gamma(v)=w,
    gamma(w)=2*w-v+2*x^2*w^3.                       (4)

This is an invertible degree-three Hénon-type map over K. The coordinate pair (v,w) is not a polynomial coordinate pair over C[x]: its Jacobian with respect to (y,z) is −x^3. Therefore the K-coordinate simplification is not a polynomial conjugacy in the ambient three-variable automorphism group.

Writing z_n=gamma^n(z), equation (4) gives the exact recurrence

    z_0=z,
    z_1=z+x*Delta,
    z_(n+2)=2*z_(n+1)-z_n+2*x^2*z_(n+1)^3.          (5)

Degree here means total degree in y,z over the coefficient field K. Induction in (5) shows

    deg_(y,z)(z_n)=2*3^(n-1),  n>=1,

with leading term

    2^((3^(n-1)-1)/2) * x^(2*3^(n-1)-1)
      * z^(2*3^(n-1)).                              (6)

The cubic term strictly dominates both lower-degree recurrence terms, and its leading coefficient is nonzero in characteristic zero. This proves the formula for every n; the finite computed iterates are only controls.

## 4. A genuine but insufficient obstruction

Gamma is not exp(D) for any polynomial locally nilpotent derivation D of R, even if D is allowed to move x.

Indeed, if gamma=exp(D), then gamma^n(z)=exp(nD)(z). Local nilpotence gives a fixed integer M with D^M(z)=0, so all these iterates lie in the finite-dimensional C-span of

    z, D(z), ..., D^(M-1)(z).

Their polynomial degrees would be uniformly bounded, contradicting (6). The same argument excludes a polynomial conjugate of a single LND exponential: conjugation carries a polynomial LND to another polynomial LND.

This does **not** exclude a product of exponentials. Even ordinary tame Hénon maps have exponential degree growth while being finite products of elementary shears and linear maps. Treating this dynamical obstruction as nonmembership in the generated subgroup would therefore be invalid.

## 5. Removing the tame factors from the exact remaining question

Define U explicitly to be the subgroup of Aut(R) generated by all exp(D) with D a polynomial LND. Notation such as SAut is not uniform across the literature, so the definition matters more than the symbol.

U is normal in Aut(R): conjugating exp(D) by a polynomial automorphism gives exp of the conjugated polynomial LND. Also every element of U has Jacobian one. For an individual additive-group action its Jacobian is a unit in C[t] and is one at t=0, hence identically one; determinants then multiply.

Every determinant-one tame automorphism belongs to U. Polynomial elementary shears and translations are LND exponentials, while SL3(C) is generated by elementary transvections. Gaussian elimination factors any affine linear part into such determinant-one maps and a single diagonal scaling. Since U is normal, all scalings in a tame/exponential word can be moved to one end, and their combined determinant is the only surviving scalar factor.

Consequently, for the source group G generated by tame maps and polynomial LND exponentials,

    G intersect {Jacobian=1} = U.                    (7)

Since gamma has Jacobian one, its original generalized-tame membership is equivalent to gamma belonging to U. This is an exact reformulation, not a membership proof. No argument has shown that every Jacobian-one automorphism belongs to U.

In particular, excluding products whose factors all fix x would still be insufficient: the source permits polynomial LNDs and tame maps moving x, and (7) does not remove that freedom.

## Outcome and next gap

The source polynomial is a genuine automorphism with an explicit inverse. Its rational exponential factorization has unavoidable poles in the displayed factors; each constant-x fiber is harmless; its cubic iterate growth excludes a single polynomial exponential. The still-open task in this campaign is to produce a finite polynomial-LND factorization in three variables or a valid obstruction to every such factorization, including factors moving x. Stabilization, localization, fiberwise factorizations and degree growth alone do not provide either conclusion.

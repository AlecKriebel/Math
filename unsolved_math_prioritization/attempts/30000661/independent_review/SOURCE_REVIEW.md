# Primary-source and scope review

The official OWR01/2007 PDF, printed p73/PDF p69, was visually inspected. Makar-Limanov's exact polynomial and the definition of generalized tame agree with the packet. The question stays in C[x,y,z]; no requirement that intermediate factors fix x is present. The source describes its rational exponential factorization and treats the displayed map as a possible counterexample. Neither description answers the polynomial-factor question. Source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46087?nt=1

The full final Berson–van den Essen–Wright preprint v10 was checked at Theorem4.10, Remark4.8 and Corollary4.9. It gives stable tameness; the coordinate-fixing characteristic-zero case embeds in TA6 after three extra variables. It supplies no factorization in the original three variables. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.3151v10

The full Arzhantsev et al. final preprint v2 was checked at the SAut definition, Theorems0.1 and0.2, and section1.20. Transitivity and compatible finite jet interpolation have the stated scope. They do not assert generation of all three-variable Jacobian-one automorphisms. The paper itself distinguishes that broader question. This historical wording is not a certification of present-day openness. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.5375v2

The primary 2026 Adamus–Hajto abstract states a plane Pascal-finite factorization result; it does not state a three-dimensional resolution. Only the abstract was needed for the packet's negative scope comparison. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.09340

Chevalley constructibility and the finite-type Nullstellensatz were checked against the Stacks Project statements. The candidate uses them only for finite-dimensional algebraic groups and finite coefficient systems, respectively. Sources: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/054K and https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00FV

All three pinned PDF hashes were independently verified. Full PDFs, extracted texts and rendered pages are reading copies and are not part of the portable review. No claim of novelty or exhaustive current-literature coverage is made.

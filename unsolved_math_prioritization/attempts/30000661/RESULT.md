# 30000661: five-turn partial result

**Original target: unsolved, 5/5 substantive author turns. Independent review pending. No novelty claim.**

The exact OWR-1452-025 question concerns the explicit polynomial automorphism gamma of C[x,y,z] in `SOURCE_SCOPE.md`. It asks whether gamma belongs to the subgroup generated by tame automorphisms and exponentials of polynomial locally nilpotent derivations (LNDs), in these same three variables. Factors may move x. For this Jacobian-one target the question is equivalent to membership in U, the subgroup generated by all polynomial LND exponentials.

No such finite factorization or obstruction to all such factorizations has been found. The following scoped results were obtained.

## Proved partial claims submitted for review

1. **Exact inverse and localized form.** Gamma and its polynomial inverse have Jacobian one. The source's two-exponential rational factorization is reconstructed; its individual factors have actual x=0 poles. A cubic Hénon form over C(x) gives exact exponential iterate growth, excluding only one polynomial LND exponential. (`TURN_1.md`)
2. **Polynomial relative words, closure and finite jets.** Every nonzero-parameter relative map gamma_t gamma_s^(-1), t,s nonzero, is a finite product of genuine polynomial LND exponentials, including factors moving x. Gamma is a bounded-degree coefficient limit of such words from eight fixed flow directions. Every finite jet of gamma is exactly in their generated jet subgroup. No full-group closedness or compatible bounded-length word is proved. (`TURN_2.md`)
3. **Exact localized cancellation group.** The two source-localized flows generate a free product of additive groups. Their intersection with polynomial automorphisms is the free group on gamma_t, t!=0. Every nonidentity element of that intersection has unbounded iterate degree. Its abstract augmentation does not have a proved extension killing the full U. In fact known relative words in this intersection already belong to U through factors outside the localized group. (`TURN_3.md`)
4. **Moving-coordinate commutator obstruction.** Gamma cannot be [H,d] for any polynomial H and flag-preserving affine d, including every translation. This follows from exact twisted-product growth versus telescoping. It also excludes one arbitrary LND exponential padded by such affine factors. An explicit allowed two-flow example has the same translation-commutator obstruction, showing why it is not an obstruction to U. (`TURN_4.md`)
5. **Multi-factor polynomial-flow exclusion and global certificate reduction.** All finite reduced words in the six coordinate-permuted invariant-multiple LND flows defined in Turn5 satisfy a degree cone and form a free product. Their degrees, even after adjoining coordinate permutations, exclude gamma's degree11. This is a subgroup of U, not all U. A separate degree-separation argument extends the affine-padding and affine-commutator obstruction to every affine map. Finally, arbitrary finite polynomial-LND membership is equivalent to solvability of one of a countable family of explicit finite polynomial systems with nilpotence certificates. A complex factorization, if one exists, can be chosen over a number field. No such system for gamma is solved, and no uniform search bound is known. (`TURN_5.md`)

## Exact unresolved issue

There may exist a finite word in other polynomial LND exponentials, with unrestricted degrees, coordinate nilpotence bounds, length and intermediate motion of x. None of the restricted subgroup or bounded-word-form arguments excludes it. Conversely, the coefficient limit and finite jets do not produce that word. This is the original three-variable membership gap, not a remaining bibliography task.

The finite algebraic systems in Turn5 formulate this gap without restricting the unknown derivations. They do not turn finitely many failed tests into a negative answer. The six-flow degree cone is an all-word proof only for its explicitly named generators.

## Existing literature and source limits

The exact question and rational-factorization observation are credited to L. Makar-Limanov's contribution in [OWR01/2007, printed p.73](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46087?nt=1). The broader generation conjecture there is attributed to A. Joseph. The report calls gamma a potential counterexample; it does not prove one.

Berson, van den Essen and Wright's [final preprint arXiv:0707.3151v10](https://arxiv.org/abs/0707.3151v10), Theorem4.10, Remark4.8 and Corollary4.9, supplies an existing stable-tameness theorem, giving this coordinate-fixing map a tame description after three extra variables. It does not descend the factors to the three-variable polynomial-LND subgroup.

Arzhantsev et al.'s [arXiv:1011.5375v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.5375v2) supplies established flexibility, transitivity and finite volume-preserving jet interpolation. Those statements do not assert exact generation of every Jacobian-one automorphism of C^3. The candidate's finite-jet result for eight particular flows is separated from that broader prior interpolation theorem.

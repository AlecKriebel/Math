## Complete proofs and audits

1. [Genuine indicator phases and positivity-to-indicator rounding](INDICATOR_PHASE_PROOF.md), with [the full independent audit](AUDIT_INDICATOR_PHASE.md). Strong Sidon, exactly triple-avoiding, uniform-profile integer indicators achieve limiting phase −1/32; the corresponding single-half-integer-coset indicators achieve −1/20 with the required shifted phase. Their leading negative phase comes from rational congruence peaks. Their Fourier residual supremum is of order √L, so they do not meet global residual smallness. The quantitative rounding theorem preserves leading phase under its precise near-idempotence and moment hypotheses, but only exact zero weighted defect/count guarantees exact Sidonicity/avoidance. The if-and-only-if statements concern positive support, not the rounded set. Both explicit approximate-versus-exact countercontrols and the separation from the earlier signed model are proved in full. Neither phase reaches −1/14.

2. [Canonical Bose–Chowla lift carries](BOSE_CHOWLA_CARRY_PROOF.md), with [the full independent audit](AUDIT_BOSE_CHOWLA_CARRIES.md) and [its original acceptance record](BOSE_CHOWLA_ACCEPTANCE.md). For canonical length-q lifts of affine Bose–Chowla p-sets, q=p²−1, the ordered carry counts are uniformly (1/6, 2/3, 1/6)p²+o(p²) as the odd field order tends to infinity. The integer and single-half-integer-coset lifts are treated separately. At least (1/30−o(1))p deletions are needed for triple-sum-freeness. The complete algebraic parametrization, exceptional cases, nonprincipal CRT-character argument, relative-topology convergence, endpoint treatment, and repeated-entry incidence argument are retained. The external input is established function-field Dirichlet RH. This is a construction-specific theorem; it gives no effective finite threshold and covers neither arbitrary Sidon seeds nor noncanonical or longer lifts.

3. [Exact overlap refinement and asymmetric relaxation](ASYMMETRIC_CAPACITY_PROOF.md), with [the full independent audit](AUDIT_ASYMMETRIC_CAPACITY.md). An unconditional finite window inequality retains variance, core–unpaired overlap, and complementary-pair corrections. The exact optimum of the stated continuous relaxation is 1+(1−1/√2)κ after all its centered core-mass bounds with parameter κ are imposed. Substituting the hypothetical, still unproved symmetric target κ=2/√3 gives 1+(2−√2)/√3≈1.338204, rather than the conjectural 2/√3≈1.154701. Extra uniform/proportional-profile assumptions give the sharp relaxation value √(5/3)≈1.290994. Neither continuous model is claimed realizable by integer sets. The symmetric target itself is not proved here.

The original problem concerns A⊆{1,…,N} with at most one integer having more than one unordered representation a+b, including equal summands. The proofs have their own local notation.

# Scope clarifications for the reviewed release

The frozen author packet and full independent mathematical audit are preserved separately and byte-for-byte. The audit accepts the work as an **unsolved five-approach investigation**. This release makes no full-target resolution, transcendental-entire counterexample, or novelty claim.

1. **Boole-map convention.** The nonescape argument concerns real orbits of T(w)=w−1/w that remain finite and avoid the pole at every iterate. Under the rational map's spherical extension, prepoles such as 1→0→∞→∞ do escape. They are excluded from the all-defined finite-plane control. The rational map is not a counterexample to the transcendental-entire question.
2. **Exponential controls.** The original finite samples check exp(t)≤1+2t. The analytical proof uses the sharper convexity bound exp(t)≤1+(e−1)t on [0,1]. The separate audit includes exact finite samples of that sharper bound and a negative control outside the stated interval. Finite checks supplement the proof; they do not replace it.
3. **Chosen orbit.** The logarithmic growth calculation is for the specified real orbit starting at zero, and for each fixed iterate along that orbit. It is not an assertion that every interior orbit fails the existential summability criterion.
4. **Boundary notation.** Expressions involving a Riemann map at boundary points require radial limits φ* or another explicitly defined extension. No continuous boundary extension is presumed.

The frozen readiness file records the state before independent audit. The separate `audit/` directory supplies that later review. The result remains **unsolved, 5/5**. These clarifications do not revise any frozen proof or its source claims.

## Exact claim and success criteria

The target map is the incidence projection P:E→R6, where E consists of pairs of coefficients p=(a1,a2,a0,b1,b2,b0) and real roots (x,y) of x²−y²+a1x+a2y+a0=0 and xy+b1x+b2y+b0=0. An admissible set U is open in all R6 and has a continuous strict section, equivalently an exact real-root selection. The mathematical conclusion stipulated for audit is: P has no local strict section at the zero coefficient vector, so there is no admissible open cover of any cardinality. No discriminant complement, filtered cover, arbitrary-set cover, homotopy section, or approximate selection is substituted.

A priority certificate would require either (i) a previously published theorem whose hypotheses directly imply this literal conclusion once the target is identified, with the necessary target-specific verification shown, or (ii) a primary-body explicit resolution/formulation diagnosis of this six-coefficient Problem 2A. Familiarity of square-root monodromy is evidence about mechanism and likely contribution scale, not a documentary certificate of (ii). The audit should distinguish deductions from source statements.

## Independent mathematical interpretation and boundaries

The pullback of P along i(u+iv)=(0,0,−u,0,0,−v/2) is exactly the square map z↦z²; no extra root branch appears. A local strict section of P at zero would restrict to s on a disk with s(w)²=w. Restricting to a circle then gives a degree contradiction: 2deg(s/|s|)=1. This is an additional target-specific deduction using classical tools; I do not attribute the six-coefficient conclusion to a paper that merely has those tools.

No-cardinality cover is sharper information than the conventional statement that the finite-cover invariant is +∞. Infinity conventions usually amalgamate two cases: some infinite cover exists but no finite one, and no admissible cover exists. Here the latter is verified, because zero is not in any admissible open set. Puncturing the complex square base admits two slit-domain sections; restoring zero destroys local sectionability. General ramification alone is not enough to infer this: e.g. a real cubic has a continuous inverse at its critical value. The actual target-specific square pullback is essential.

Ordinary homotopy sectional category of P over contractible R6 is one in an unnormalized convention (zero normalized): choose a constant map to any point of E, and P composed with it is homotopic to the identity of R6. This is our deduction, not a quoted computation; it shows why homotopy invariance erases the obstruction. Likewise any fixed approximation tolerance permits the zero root-value choice near the origin on the square slice. Neither statement solves a repaired problem.

## Decisive published theorem

[Eisermann, The number of knot group representations is not a Vassiliev invariant](https://pnp.mathematik.uni-stuttgart.de/igt/eiserm/publications/twistseq.pdf), Proc.Amer.Math.Soc.128(2000),1555–1561, DOI10.1090/S0002-9939-99-05287-9. The full7-page author-hosted version was obtained. Theorem3 and its complete proof in §1, including Lemma4, Corollary5 and Lemma6, were read. Theorem3 and the corollary were visually checked. The theorem explicitly permits F:K→C and an arbitrary integer-valued upper bound depending on braid index. Thus there is no scalar-extension or irrational-log gap.

For a fixed finite quandle of size q, braid color propagation gives q≤h_X(K)≤q^{b(K)}. Consequently the real logarithm has absolute value at most q b(K), which is an admissible bound in the published theorem. This direct application covers every knot and every finite connected quandle in the original question. The singleton quandle gives the constant-zero logarithm. No full quandle enumeration or growth conjecture is needed.

The author's [publication page](https://pnp.mathematik.uni-stuttgart.de/igt/eiserm/publications/) corroborates the publication and links the full text. The2003 paper [A geometric characterization of Vassiliev invariants](https://pnp.mathematik.uni-stuttgart.de/igt/eiserm/publications/geomchar.pdf) was checked as related context; its rational-valued formulation is not the load-bearing theorem used here. The2000 theorem already has the required complex target.

# Parameter-scope addendum

The counterexample for the directly defined six-relation algebra is characteristic-free. Its only restriction is beta² != 1; alpha is arbitrary. Algebraic independence over the prime subfield implies this restriction in every characteristic.

For comparison with the 2012 paper, its displayed parameters are

c=(theta-1)/(theta+1), d=(rho-1)/(rho+1).

When the characteristic is not 2 these are invertible fractional-linear changes, with theta=(1+c)/(1-c) and rho=(1+d)/(1-d). They preserve algebraic independence. Moreover

1-d²=4*rho/(rho+1)² != 0

for the algebraically independent pairs in the paper. Thus the presented-algebra counterexample also applies to the paper's generic family in these characteristics.

In characteristic 2, those fractional expressions equal 1 wherever their denominators are nonzero. Consequently the journal's parametrization cannot be used to identify arbitrary independent c,d with its rho,theta family there. The characteristic-2 conclusion here concerns the algebra defined directly by the six relations in the question and OWR presentation. It does not claim that the collapsing journal parametrization is valid or supplies an independent generic pair in characteristic 2.

This clarification does not alter the proof, map, ideal, or complete-counterexample verdict for problem 30001478. The original frozen proof already works directly from the presentation and avoids importing a characteristic hypothesis from the parameter change.

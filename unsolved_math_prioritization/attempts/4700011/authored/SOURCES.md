# Source scope and method credit

1. Armengol Gasull, *Some open problems in low dimensional dynamical systems*, arXiv:2012.02524 (2020), Section 2.8, equations (12)–(13), Problem 11.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.02524
   - https://arxiv.org/html/2012.02524v1
   - Inspected for the exact recurrence class, common-global-period definition, permitted rescaling/index dilation, and historical low-order context.

2. Anna Cima, Armengol Gasull, Francesc Mañosas, *On periodic rational difference equations of order k*, Journal of Difference Equations and Applications 10 (2004), 549–559.
   - https://doi.org/10.1080/10236190410001667977
   - https://portalrecerca.uab.cat/en/publications/on-periodic-rational-difference-equations-of-order-k/
   - The bibliographic record and abstract were checked. The full paper's proof was not inspected in this work. Accordingly, independently reconstructed subfamily arguments are not presented as literature-novel results.

3. Armengol Gasull, *Different approaches to the global periodicity problem*, ICDEA 2012, Barcelona, slides.
   - https://www.gsd.uab.cat/icdea2012/Slides/Gasull.pdf
   - Relevant method credit: local averaging linearization, fixed-point roots-of-unity conditions, normal-form obstructions, and the historical classification question. The manuscript supplies proofs of the tools it uses rather than asking the reader to assume an uninspected result.

The retained results are mathematical partial progress. The source checks above do not establish that no later or unindexed work contains any of these conclusions.

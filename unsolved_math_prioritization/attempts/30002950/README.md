## Result and remaining gap

For the canonical minimal slope-adapted reference lattice in the stated GL_h minuscule setting, every Dieudonne lattice has a unique smallest minimal hull. When its bi-infinitesimal part is nonzero, a cyclic sublattice with the same hull has colength

    C = sum_i (m_i−1)(n_i−1)/2 + sum_{i<j} m_i n_j,

with the simple factors ordered by increasing slope, including repeated copies. For every lattice in this domain, d(M)=length(P(M)/M) is at most C, and d(M)=C if and only if a(M)=1. This makes the entire a-number-one locus saturated under the invariant-function strata and their J-orbits.

The result holds for W(k) and k[[t]] with the stated q-Frobenius. The full characteristic transfer, including bounded exponent division, Witt-vector carries, cross-slope leading terms and repeated-slope finite-field independence, is retained. With one distinct interior slope and any ordinary factors, the published basic theorem implies the single-orbit assertion. The entirely ordinary locus has a=0 and is treated separately.

The full single J-orbit assertion for two or more distinct interior slopes remains unresolved. Minimal hull and colength alone do not determine the entire invariant function. The rank-six gluing example shows why an integral direct sum of interior slope projections cannot be assumed. Sampled J-profiles and finite arithmetic controls are compatibility/adverse evidence only, not a universal orbit theorem. No novelty is claimed.

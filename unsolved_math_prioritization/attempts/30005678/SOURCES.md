## Original target

The pinned record is reproduced in `source_record.json`. Its edited statement asks to characterize fully two-Segal Waldhausen constructions and find natural left-only examples. The actual [OWR contribution](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47483?nt=1), Julie Bergner, printed pp. 1945–1946, discusses this extension informally. It explains the polygon-triangulation definition, the known exact-category and augmented-stable-double-Segal framework, the left condition for cofibration categories, and the artificial nature of then-known separating examples. It does not specify a weak-equivalence realization. The full contribution, not only the extracted sentence, was read.

## Follow-up and variant gate

The complete primary [Carawan paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.11561) was retrieved. Its current arXiv page lists only v1 (19 May 2024). Definitions 3.1 and 3.8 allow the ordinary cofibration-category/Waldhausen structure used here without a cylinder axiom. Sections 7 and 8 distinguish the groupoid realization from the categorical variants. Page 23 expressly leaves general weak-equivalence realizations apart; Proposition 7.7 assumes saturation and a mapping cylinder. Proposition 9.1 is a sufficient categorical condition, not the characterization asserted in our Theorem 1. The left theorem and the existence of artificial separating examples are credited prior results.

The groupoid construction is also different from realizing all morphisms in S_n: a zero object makes the latter levelwise contractible. The present free-group non-lifting argument is about maximal groupoids and survives realization for precisely that reason.

The BIRS workshop report was available only in indexed primary excerpts; its full PDF request failed. Nothing in the mathematical note depends on it. The complete Carawan appendix independently establishes the terminology correspondence.

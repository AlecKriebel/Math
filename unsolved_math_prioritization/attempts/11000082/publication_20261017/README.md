# Accepted full theorem and published alternative-proof preprint

For every fixed closed oriented genus g>=2 and every fixed finite Johnson depth k>=3, pseudo-Anosov dilatation multiplicity in I_g(k) is unbounded when classes are counted up to ambient orientation-preserving Mod_g conjugacy. For every N a finite family of N distinct ambient classes has one common exact dilatation. The common value may vary with N.

This answers the precise multiplicity question in Farb Question7.6. The original arithmetic/tree proof required no mathematical repair. A separately reconstructed and freshly verified alternative proof takes a Schottky subgroup in the fixed filtration term, then uses compactness of its convex core to control conjugacy in the full discrete disk stabilizer. Horowitz trace words supply the equal-dilatation families. The note does not assert ambient primitivity, simple loops, a single value of infinite multiplicity or a result for the intersection of all Johnson terms.

The full theorem was already explicitly printed in Alper Ferudun, Unbounded Multiplicity in the Dilatation Spectrum of Every Term of the Johnson Filtration: An Observation on a Question of Farb (11October2026), DOI10.5281/zenodo.23301731.

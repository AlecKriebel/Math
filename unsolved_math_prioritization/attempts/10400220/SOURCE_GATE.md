# Source gate: mutation and ordinary unknotting number

Target10400220 / AMR-103-0220 is Ohtsuki Problem12.15, printed538, attributed to M. Boileau and Kirby1997 Problem1.69(C). It asks whether Conway-mutant knots can have different ordinary unknotting numbers. Mutation means cutting along a sphere meeting a knot in four points and regluing by one of the three standard half-turns; the source concerns knots in S3, not links, genus-two mutations or arbitrary tangle replacements. The unknotting number is minimized over all diagrams and ordinary crossing changes.

The same question is part(b) of dataset2662 / modern Kirby Problem1.3. That is a protected subpart alias for this campaign target. Part(a) of2662, the lower bound for connected sums of nontrivial knots, is different and is not being attempted or closed here. Exact-ID PR/branch/main-path searches for both10400220 and2662, plus a mutant/unknotting PR search, returned no prior campaign attempt. Catalog2662 is deferred/unranked; the inspected QUEUE has only10400220, and a live row check is required before publication.

Primary current constraints:
- The2026 Kirby problem list, printed13, still lists this as Problem1.3(b)
- Gordon–Luecke, AGT6(2006), Theorem7.1, pp2082–2083, proves that having unknotting number1 is invariant under Conway mutation, including its exceptional EM-knot/tangle cases
- Conway mutation preserves the double branched cover, so a lower bound determined solely by that unmarked3-manifold cannot distinguish a mutant pair. In particular, the standard unknot-preservation fact and the u=1 theorem rule out0-versus-positive and1-versus-larger pairs
- Brittenham–Hermiller arXiv2506.24088v2(2025) disproves connected-sum additivity, a different Kirby subquestion. Section3 explicitly describes their mutation search as ongoing and explains why an assumed additive lower bound in that search failed

Thus a potential counterexample must have smaller unknotting number at least2 and needs certified upper/lower bounds that do not merely compare signature or an unchanged branched cover. Differing genus or slice genus, by themselves, do not certify different unknotting numbers. No current full resolution was found in the primary sources checked. This is a dated status gate, not an exhaustive priority search.

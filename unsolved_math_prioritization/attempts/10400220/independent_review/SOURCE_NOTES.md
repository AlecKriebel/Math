# Primary-source scope checked independently

1. **Ohtsuki, Problem 12.15**, printed 538 / PDF 166, [full source](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf). The preceding slicing question is a different target. The question and following genus remark were visually checked.
2. **Gordon–Luecke**, *Knots with unknotting number 1 and essential Conway spheres*, AGT 6 (2006), 2051–2116, [arXiv v2 reading copy](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0601265v2). Theorem 7.1 and its three-case proof are on pp.2082–2083. Theorem 8.2 appears in the introductory summary and in full on p.2085; case (2) retains the EM nonlocal arc. The v2 arXiv record was revised in 2009. No date change to the journal publication is implied.
3. **Kim–Livingston**, *Knot mutation: 4-genus and algebraic concordance*, PJM 220 (2005), 87–105, [full publisher text](https://msp.org/pjm/2005/220-1/pjm-v220-n1-p05-s.pdf). Sections 3–5 supply the Seifert form, inverse/reverse conventions, third-cover eigenspaces and simultaneous finite-range genus theorem. The ordinary crossing-number deductions are separately scoped.
4. **Livingston**, AGT 2 (2002), 1051–1060, [Section 2](https://msp.org/agt/2002/2-2/agt-v2-n2-p14-p.pdf), supplies the classical crossing-change/double-cover surgery setting. The full paper had also been read during the separate source correction for 10400219. That task and its slicing conclusion are not substituted for ordinary unknotting here.
5. **Stoimenow**, *Vassiliev invariants and rational knots of unknotting number one*, [author PDF](https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/rtan.pdf), version 18 February 2002. Theorem 1.2 on p.2 and its proof on pp.9–10 give arbitrary prescribed positive unknotting number at fixed bounded finite-type data. The packet does not require its stronger eventual alternating clause. The realization does not retain a chosen mutation class.
6. **Stoimenow tables**, [notation and mutation description](https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/ptab/), checked on 1 October 2026. The page explicitly uses combined alternating/nonalternating indices and ignores mirroring. It gives 91 groups through twelve crossings and 774 at thirteen. Its page also cautions that table classifications warrant independent verification. This review checks the pinned join, not the entire table classification.
7. **KnotInfo**, [official download page](https://knotinfo.org/homelinks/database_download.php), identifies the workbook and its paired value/reference columns. The pinned workbook was read without modification. The braid word and cells for 11n76/11n78 match the packet. Blank references are insufficient evidence for either a theorem or a table-error allegation.
8. **Kanenobu–Matsumura**, [2014 primary preprint](https://www.i-repository.net/contents/osakacu/kiyo/111F0000020-14-21.pdf), Remark 3.1, printed p.4. The unreferenced-value observation includes 11n76. It is a dated provenance observation, not an assertion that no later proof can exist.
9. **Brittenham–Hermiller 2017/2025, Taniyama, Lee 2026 and the 2026 Kirby list:** the full pinned primary texts were inspected at the claimed statements. The nonadditivity example is a different connected-sum issue. Failure of a minimal-diagram lowering rule is distinct from arbitrarily bad diagrams. Lee's September 2026 Theorem 5.1 is described as a reported computational preprint result; neither author nor reviewer reran its external state-enumeration software. None of the packet's approved partial theorems depends on independently certifying that computational result.

All reading-file hashes are checked in HASH_CHECK.json. Reading copies, workbook, table files and rendered images are excluded from the portable review. No external source software was executed, and no outreach was made.

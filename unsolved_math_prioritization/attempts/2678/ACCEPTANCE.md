# Acceptance: asymmetric-knot detection in the closed oriented category

**ACCEPT_SCOPED_PRIOR_RESULT_CORE.** For every closed, connected, oriented smooth 3-manifold Y, there exists one asymmetric hyperbolic knot K such that f(K) is not ambiently isotopic to K for every nonidentity orientation-preserving smooth mapping class [f] of Y. Thus a specified nontrivial separating-sphere twist is detected by a knot in this category. Knots are allowed to be unoriented embedded circles; no framing is required.

3. The interior point-moving and point-pushing conversion from trivial outer automorphism to an unpointed exterior isotopy, allowing boundary motion.
4. The complete continuous collar family, its inverse and gluing, leaving precisely a boundary-fixed solid-torus homeomorphism.
5. The relative solid-torus kernel and TOP/smooth comparison, used only at the path-component level. Smooth isotopy extension, tubular-neighborhood uniqueness, and standard hyperbolic exterior topology are explicit background dependencies.

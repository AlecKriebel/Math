# Five bounded approaches

All five approaches are complete for this packet. The unrestricted target is unsolved. Routine source retrieval and integrity checks are not counted as mathematical approaches.

1. **Test the exact category and low-genus quantifiers.** The Hopf bundle times a circle gives a rigorous genus-one nonflat example over S^2, and restriction gives a three-dimensional-base example. This exposes the missing low-genus hypothesis in a literal reading of monodromy-only classification. It does not resolve the intended g >= 2 question.
2. **Eliminate the abelian flux obstruction.** Apply the existing Kotschick–Morita extended crossed flux map. Prove the semidirect-product quotient and simultaneous Hamiltonian relator normalization. This is a definite partial answer to the elementary obstruction suggested by the source, with no novelty claim.
3. **Try to cancel the Hamiltonian defect.** Prove the exact commutator-length formula for identity-monodromy stabilization. Perfectness proves finiteness, but does not force zero stabilization. Hofer infima isolate an unresolved gap; no unjustified attainment or lower-bound argument is accepted.
4. **Construct actual lifts and compare dimensions.** Free-group factorization gives genuine area-preserving flatness, including free-fundamental-group three-manifolds. Pullback to a surface times a circle is flat exactly when the original bundle is flat. These are elementary scoped positive cases and a reduction, not an arbitrary-base theorem.
5. **Stress-test natural obstructions.** The product/diagonal example demonstrates that section-preserving nonflatness can coexist with ordinary flatness. Higher MMM classes vanish for dimensional reasons here, nonzero signature is compatible with flatness, and deck-invariant or bordism statements do not imply unrestricted nonflatness or flatness of a prescribed bundle. No remaining general obstruction is proved.

The stopping point is a rigorous, source-grounded partial analysis after the five-approach bound. The first-order flux obstruction is removed, but the nonabelian relation problem is not solved.

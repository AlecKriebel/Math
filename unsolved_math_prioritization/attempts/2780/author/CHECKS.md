## Mathematical checks

- The low-genus example is a torus bundle, and its total-space fundamental group is Z rather than Z^2. A smooth flat compact-fiber bundle over S^2 must be trivial.
- The source's unrestricted displayed quantifier and its hyperbolic-fiber interpretation are distinguished. No g >= 2 counterexample is claimed.
- The structural inputs hold for smooth closed oriented fibers of genus at least two. No sphere/torus flux formula is substituted.
- The action on H^1 is the inverse-pullback left action, matching the crossed identity and semidirect multiplication.
- Surjectivity of Psi uses left multiplication by an identity-component element, so the adjustment is v-c(a), without an omitted action factor.
- The kernel of Psi is Hamiltonian. Kernel of the crossed map alone is a subgroup but is not asserted normal in G.
- Every relator lies simultaneously in that crossed-map kernel and in the identity component. This gives Hamiltonian relators, not trivial relators.
- The original flatness question is not restricted without justification to zero-extended-flux representations.
- The stabilization relation uses the inverse of the original defect. Added generators have identity mapping class and may have nontrivial actual holonomy.
- The exact minimum ranges over all original lifts and uses commutator length in the identity component. Hamiltonian commutator length only supplies a finite upper bound.
- Perfectness is used only to conclude finite commutator length. It is not confused with uniform perfectness or with the zero-cost statement.
- The Hofer infimum is over all Hamiltonian defects of lifts, including any genuinely flat lift; no compactness or attainment is assumed.
- Free-group factorization yields a homomorphism by the free universal property and then a flat bundle by the hyperbolic-fiber classification input.
- Restriction of pullback flat structures proves the surface-to-three-manifold implication in the correct direction.
- The diagonal normal Euler number obstructs making the diagonal horizontal, while the same total bundle explicitly remains flat.
- Neither deck invariance, bordism equivalence, homology surjectivity, nor an area-preserving obstruction is promoted to an unrestricted smooth result.

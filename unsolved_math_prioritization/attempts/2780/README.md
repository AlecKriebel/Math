## Accepted scope

For smooth closed oriented fibers of genus at least two, the existing Kotschick–Morita extended-flux theorem gives the abstract group quotient Symp/Ham isomorphic to H^1 semidirect Mod. Generator representatives can simultaneously put every presentation relator in Ham. Hamiltonian relators need not equal the identity.

The exact identity-monodromy stabilization cost is the minimum, over **all** original lift tuples, of commutator length in the identity component of the inverse relator defect. It is finite; no zero-cost theorem or positive minimized obstruction is obtained. Restricting to zero-extended-flux tuples would change the question. A zero Hofer infimum does not imply identity attainment.

Actual flat subcases include monodromy factoring through a free group. Pullback to a surface times a circle preserves and reflects flatness. The Hopf-derived torus example is a lower-genus formulation boundary, not a solution to the intended hyperbolic-fiber problem. The diagonal-section obstruction is about a specified horizontal section, not unrestricted flatness.

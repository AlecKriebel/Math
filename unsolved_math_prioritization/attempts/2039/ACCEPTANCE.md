## Result

Accept the exact full theorem at the level of the independently checked written proof in Shisheng Li, arXiv:2609.32140v1, Theorem 1.1:

Every positive rational number with squarefree reduced denominator is a finite sum of distinct unit fractions whose denominators are products of exactly two distinct primes.

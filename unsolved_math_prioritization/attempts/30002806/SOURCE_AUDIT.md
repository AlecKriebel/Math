# Source and readiness audit

The original is Sina Ober-Blöbaum, “Discrete variational formulations for the optimal control and multirate integration of dynamical systems,” OWR12/2015, printed pp.631–633, in https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2015/12. The full contribution was read. It defines discrete Lagrange–d’Alembert dynamics, a running-plus-terminal objective, and a direct/indirect optimality diagram. The general-variational-integrator question occurs on p.632, with the diagram and references on p.633. “Adjoint” here means a costate, not the time-reversed numerical method sometimes also called an adjoint integrator.

The supplied Campos–Ober-Blöbaum–Trélat paper is a published 2015 article. Theorem5.2 concerns a convergent symplectic Galerkin method with positive quadrature weights and its specified control/cost discretization, regularity/coercivity/uniqueness hypotheses and a positive control Hamiltonian Hessian. Its conclusions still distinguish general variational methods, constrained or nonsmooth controls, and abnormal minimizers. These are not omitted hypotheses of a theorem valid for arbitrary discretizations.

Tran–Southworth–Leok, Journal of Nonlinear Science34:95(2024), Theorem3.3 gives the general one-step cotangent-lift characterization; Proposition3.3 requires variational equivariance and sensitivity regularity for equal approximation order. This distinguishes the mechanical state symplectic form from the extended state-costate symplectic form. It is relevant newer literature, but does not identify an arbitrary preselected optimal-control discretization with the required lift.

Konopik et al., Multibody System Dynamics(2026), DOI10.1007/s11044-025-10138-1, develops a control Lagrangian and proves the matching forced state scheme for a specified low-order family(Theorem3.7), with a corresponding order result(Proposition3.8). The Journal of Nonlinear Science precursor (online2025; volume36:11,2026), DOI10.1007/s00332-025-10229-5, concerns affine-controlled second-order systems with quadratic control cost. Neither inspected statement establishes every variational method’s commutation property. The September2026 multirate article’s public abstract discusses forward dynamics and variational error analysis; no inaccessible full-proof claim is made for it.

The precise remaining issue is transfer from a stated class of state discretizations to the same-order sensitivity, cost and control optimality discretizations. A method being variational is not the same assertion as being variationally equivariant under tangent lifting. No source-status resolution is asserted here.

The 2015 paper’s conclusions explicitly leave a proof of convergence of the adjoint variables to future work, despite the proven identification of schemes. Matching discrete equations and proving convergence of their boundary-value solutions are distinct. The original p.632 was also checked as a rendered page.

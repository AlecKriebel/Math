## Theorems used

1. Anton Bernshteyn, *Equivariant maps to subshifts whose points have small stabilizers*, Journal of Modern Dynamics 19 (2023), 1-30. [Publisher DOI](https://doi.org/10.3934/jmd.2023001); [arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.09673v3), 10 October 2022. Theorem 1.5 and Corollary 1.6 on preprint p. 2 require continuity. Theorem 1.1 on p. 1 records the universal free binary-subshift theorem. These statements and adjacent hypotheses were read; no full independent reproof of the published theorem is claimed.
2. Brandon Seward and Robin D. Tucker-Drob, *Borel structurability on the 2-shift of a countable group*, Annals of Pure and Applied Logic 167 (2016), 1-21. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.apal.2015.07.005); [arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.4184v1). Theorem 1.1 on preprint p. 3 provides the Borel equivariant free-shift map. That paper uses a left-coordinate shift convention; inversion of coordinates conjugates it to the right-coordinate convention used here. The stronger compact-image version is also explicitly stated in reference 1, Theorem 1.1, and reference 3, Theorem 8.1.
3. Anton Bernshteyn and Joshua Frisch, *Flows with minimal subdynamics*, [arXiv:2509.03139v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.03139v2), 13 September 2025. Theorem 1.3 on p. 2 and Corollary 1.11 on p. 3 give free compact metrizable flows with simultaneous minimality for countably many infinite subsets. Section 8, especially Theorem 8.1 and Proposition 8.2, pp. 21-22, develops a Baire-image method. These are preprint results, not asserted here to have a verified journal publication. Lemma 2 in `analysis.md` is a direct, separately written consequence for centralizers; novelty is not claimed.

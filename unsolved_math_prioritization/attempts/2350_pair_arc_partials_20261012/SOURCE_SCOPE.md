# Bounded prior-work comparison and its limits

12 October 2026 UTC. This is an authored source-scope note, not a novelty or priority certificate.

A targeted public-source check of the preceding canonical Bose–Chowla 4/5 retention theorem found no exact matching theorem or inspected result that immediately subsumes its complete statement. That comparison concerned the 4/5 antecedent, not an exhaustive search for the new spectral norm, nonlinear gap or rational-arc results. No claim of novelty follows from a bounded search miss.

The key applicability distinctions are:

- Ortega and Prendiville, *Extremal Sidon Sets are Fourier Uniform, with Applications to Partition Regularity*, J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux 35 (2023), 115–134: Theorem 1.15 assumes at least five nonzero coefficients. The four-variable equation (1,1,1,−1) fails that hypothesis. Their Fourier-uniformity and residue-equidistribution results remain established background. The theorem statement was inspected in the [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2110.13447); the [published record](https://jtnb.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/jtnb.1239/) identifies the article.
- Nathan Tung, *Linear equations and chromatic thresholds in B_h sets*, [arXiv:2606.30767v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.30767v1): Theorems 1.2, 1.3 and 1.8 require variable-count, coefficient or higher-order hypotheses absent from this four-variable equation. Theorem 1.3 assumes h≥3 and k>2h; Theorem 1.8 requires a zero-sum subcollection of at least five coefficients. Proposition 1.4 is Fourier pseudorandomness in a different finite-field setting, not the asserted canonical zero-carry retention theorem.
- Béla Bajnok, *On the maximum size of a (k,l)-sum-free subset of an abelian group*, [arXiv:0803.4486](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0803.4486), Theorem 6: its cyclic-group (3,1)-sum-free condition forbids all modular coincidences. Genuine integer avoidance forbids only zero carry. Thus the modular result's stronger hypothesis is unavailable here; it also concerns the whole ambient group rather than a fixed Bose–Chowla seed.
- Nicholas Katz, *An Estimate for Character Sums*, JAMS 2 (1989), 197–200, [author copy](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~nmk/old/estcharsums.pdf), Theorem 1, and Lei Fu and Daqing Wan, *A Class of Incomplete Character Sums*, [arXiv:1303.3650v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1303.3650v3), Proposition 1.2 and Theorem 4.2: these provide established analytic ingredients. They do not by themselves verify the particular fiber, geometric nontriviality, carry and arbitrary-deletion arguments. Polynomial Dirichlet RH is likewise an established input; Kopparty's [author-written exposition](https://www.math.toronto.edu/swastik/courses/rutgers/finitefields-F13/zeta.pdf) is cited as an exposition, not as an original proof of RH.

Here is the elementary reason that merely citing square-root Fourier decay is insufficient for the standard four-variable estimate. For a p-point seed in Z/(p²−1), its trivial-frequency main term has order p². A bound max|fhat(ξ)|=O(√p), combined only with Parseval, gives an absolute nontrivial fourth-moment error at most O(p)·p=O(p²), still the main-term scale. This does not rule out a different successful Fourier argument; it identifies the missing saving in that immediate estimate. Moreover, equidistribution of the complete seed does not imply equidistribution of an adversarial subset.

These comparisons were based on inspected source statements, not search snippets. The check also examined finite-field incidence, construction and related terminology routes, but it was not an exhaustive citation-graph search. Older non-English literature, inaccessible historical full texts, unindexed manuscripts and very recent work remain possible gaps. The official Erdős 864 page failed to load during this check. Only the publisher record/abstract of Erdős–Freud's *On sums of a Sidon-sequence* was available, and the exact historical passage in Erdős's *Some of my forgotten problems in number theory* was not freshly read. Therefore no current problem-status or historical theorem-level conclusion is inferred from those unavailable texts. The source documents are not distributed with this note.

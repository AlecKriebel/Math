## Full proofs and full mathematical audits

1. [A genuine arbitrary-subset spectral pair law](SPECTRAL_PAIR_PROOF.md), with [the full independent arithmetic and retention audit](AUDIT_PAIR_MIXING.md). For every nonprincipal multiplicative character of the quadratic extension, the stated matrix has operator norm at most sqrt(5p^(3/2)+2p)+sqrt(2p), hence at most 4p^(3/4). There is no character-order restriction. The proof explicitly treats exceptional columns, quadratic conjugate collisions and denominator zeros. The resulting weighted source-pair law holds for arbitrary subsets depending on all parameters, rather than merely for unrestricted row sums.

2. [The complete continuum gap certificate](CONTINUUM_GAP_PROOF.md), with [its full independent measurable-analysis audit](AUDIT_CONTINUUM_GAP.md). Every measurable d:[0,1]→[0,1] satisfying the stated nonlinear cover inequality almost everywhere has integral at least 1216/6075. The exact margin above 1/5 is 1/6075; the optional improved margin is (18−8√5)/675. The proof smooths the singular coupling before using the almost-everywhere inequality and uses fixed-function L¹ continuity, a positive level set and an exact six-inequality certificate. No numerical optimum or uniqueness of a minimizer is assumed.

3. [The combined retention corollary](RETENTION_COROLLARY.md). For the full affine p-point Bose–Chowla family, q=p²−1, every triple-sum-free subset of either specified positive canonical lift has size at most (4859/6075+o(1))p. The optional leading constant is 4/5−(18−8√5)/675. This is uniform over all odd finite-field orders and allowed translations and unit dilations, with integer and single-coset half-integer lifts treated separately. It bounds retention and does not construct a subset attaining the bound. It says nothing about arbitrary Sidon sets, arbitrary or longer lifts, or mixed lattice cosets; no effective threshold or sharpness is claimed.

4. [Rational-arc escape and residue capacity](RATIONAL_ARC_PROOF.md), with [the full independent audit](AUDIT_RATIONAL_ARC.md) and [complete acceptance statement](RATIONAL_ARC_ACCEPTANCE.md). The finite residue-capacity inequality applies to arbitrary strong Sidon sets. A congruence-only triple-avoidance certificate modulo m=o(√L) forces |B|²≤(1/2+o(1))L. For a macroscopically uniform sequence of density c, the combined nonzero fixed-rational profiles have squared L² mass at most 1−c². Their signed quartic contribution is bounded by (3√3/16)(1−c²)²L. A hypothetical avoiding sequence with c²≥2/3 must consequently carry at least Δ(c²)L of negative signed contribution outside every fixed-denominator family of arcs, where Δ(x)=x²/6−(3√3/16)(1−x)². The cutoff is an iterated subsequential limit, not an arbitrary growing denominator or shrinking-scale theorem. This is a necessary condition, not a contradiction. The additional signed-decorrelation hypothesis that would imply supersaturation remains unproved. The complete compatible-residue weak relaxation still allows mass one.

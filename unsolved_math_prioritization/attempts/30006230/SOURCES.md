## GJR primary framework: keep versions distinct

Debdip Ganguly, K. Jotsaroop, Prasun Roychowdhury, [arXiv:2404.05510](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.05510).

- **v1, 8 April 2024:** *Hardy and Rellich identities and inequalities for Grushin operators via spherical vector fields and Bessel pairs*. The stated range is n ≥ 1 / Q ≥ 3. Theorem 1.3: p.6; definitions: p.13; Corollary 3.1: p.14. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.05510v1).
- **v2, 20 March 2025:** *Hardy and Rellich identities and inequalities for Baouendi-Grushin operators via spherical vector fields*. The stated range begins at n ≥ 2 / Q ≥ 4. Theorem 1.3: pp.6–7; definitions: pp.13–14; Corollary 3.1: p.15. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.05510v2).
- **v3, arXiv revision 3 October 2026:** same shorter title and n ≥ 2 / Q ≥ 4 range. Theorem 1.3: p.7; definitions: p.14; Corollary 3.1: p.16. The PDF itself prints 6 October 2026 as its document date. The inspected arXiv metadata labels the manuscript final and to appear in *Annali della Scuola Normale Superiore di Pisa, Classe di Scienze*. This is author-supplied manuscript status, not an independently recovered journal version of record or DOI. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.05510v3).

The OWR reference uses the 2024 title matching v1 but does not explicitly select an arXiv version. The workshop preceded v2, while report publication followed it. No reason for the dimension change or author-authenticated choice of converse formulation is inferred.

## Mathematical precedents and attribution

- Nassif Ghoussoub and Amir Moradifam, *Bessel pairs and optimal Hardy and Hardy–Rellich inequalities*, Math. Ann. 349 (2011), 1–57, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-010-0510-x). The inspected historical author manuscript is titled *Bessel potentials and optimal Hardy and Hardy–Rellich inequalities*, [arXiv:0709.1954v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0709.1954v1), 12 September 2007. Its Theorems 2.1 and 3.3 are Euclidean precedents with additional hypotheses. The 35-page manuscript is not asserted identical to the journal version; its endpoint assumptions and scale conventions are not silently imported here.
- Daniel Lenz, Peter Stollmann and Ivan Veselić, *The Allegretto–Piepenbrink Theorem for Strongly Local Dirichlet Forms*, [arXiv:0811.2135v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0811.2135v2). The regular Hardy proof credits the classical Sturm/Allegretto–Piepenbrink principle but supplies the needed one-dimensional argument itself. It does not assert that every hypothesis of the general Dirichlet-form theorem has been checked for these weights.
- GJR v3 Theorems 6.1–6.3 contain all-function/radial symmetrization statements for V = 1, with an extra one-dimensional assumption in Theorem 6.3. They are relevant prior results and are not stated as the arbitrary-(V,W) converse proved here. See the source-scope materials for the precise separation.

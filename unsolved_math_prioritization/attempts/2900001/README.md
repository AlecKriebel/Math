# Finite Betti poset and ideal Stanley depth verification

This report concerns squarefree monomial ideals I with exactly five minimal generators in S=K[x1,...,x6], over every field K. The accepted finite computational result is: if the positive Betti posets of I and (I:x6) are isomorphic, then their ideal Stanley depths in the same ring S are equal. Equality of unstructured numerical Betti data is not substituted for Betti-poset isomorphism. The unrestricted conjecture is not resolved by this work.

REPORT.md retains the authored reduction, enumeration, homology, field-independence, Stanley-depth certification, worked-example discussion, and remaining-obstruction sections. It adds explicit coverage and certificate-soundness proofs and explains what the public text alone can establish. ACCEPTANCE.md retains the full mathematical audit and its exact acceptance scope, with distinct later recheck information.

Only authored exposition and aggregate verification metadata are included. Exhaustive case records, raw certificates, executable scripts, copied research sources, and private coordination material are excluded. The worked example's raw certificate lists are also excluded. This means the package does not enable full independent replay of the finite computation. A reader can check the mathematical reduction and certification logic, or write a new implementation, but cannot verify all computational findings from the supplied files alone. No external peer review, formal proof-assistant derivation, or novelty claim is asserted.

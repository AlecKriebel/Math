# Source dependencies and claim boundaries

Problem 30003033 / OWR-14211-008 concerns a principal-series connection
for the stated orthogonal negative/isotropic-pair indefinite theta family.
The accompanying proof establishes an accepted partial result. It does
not solve the original unrestricted connection problem.

## Source roles

1. Westerholt-Raum's complete two-page contribution, printed pp. 138–139
   in Oberwolfach Reports 3/2016, supplies the question, pair construction,
   first-/second-order annihilators and Casimir equation. It already
   describes Schrödinger–Weil extensions and reducible indecomposable
   modules. That viewpoint is credited, not claimed as new.
   [Original report](https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2016/3)
2. WR15 supplies the precise orientation, product-type orthogonal-pair
   construction, completion and qualified modularity statements. The full
   30-page preprint was read; the published version was compared only at
   the identified operator/subspace/kernel/corollary locations. Product
   type does not assert an arithmetic product decomposition of the lattice.
   [Preprint v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.5603v3),
   [published version](https://doi.org/10.1186/s40687-015-0032-y)
3. WR17 supplies qualified rational-isotropic-edge convergence/modularity
   context. Its full 18-page preprint was read. Neither it nor WR15 is
   treated as a principal-series realization theorem. This edition also
   includes the audit's direct chamberwise rational-ray convergence and
   finite-order derivative bound. Irrational edges remain outside that
   unconditional proof. [Preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1608.08874v2)
4. Conley–Raum supplies covariant-operator conventions, the virtual Levi
   splitting and Casimir normalization. The weight-dependent scalar
   shift is retained for descendants. The recorded relevant sections
   and visual equation checks are identified in the metadata, rather
   than described as a full reading of unrelated sections.
   [Preprint v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.2897v2)
5. Sun supplies the smooth Fréchet moderate-growth category equivalence
   and distribution-vector conventions. The nonembedding theorem uses
   that category and the strong dual of the smooth contragredient. It
   does not cover all distributions in a noncompact chart or unrestricted
   smooth, meromorphic, hyperfunction, or exponential-growth vectors.
   [Source](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.5508v2)
6. Zwegers was independently checked at the normalized error-function
   definition and shifted elliptic-variable formula. This was a targeted
   normalization cross-check, not a full reading of the thesis. The thesis
   date is 2002; the inspected arXiv version is v1 from 2008.
   [Thesis version](https://arxiv.org/abs/0807.4834)

## Exact source discrepancy

The completion here uses E with limiting magnitudes one, matching the
sign term; relative normalization matters. It is not a verbatim copy of
every symbol in the 2015 incomplete-gamma definition. Arithmetic modularity
and discriminant Weil type retain the integral/even lattice convention.

The printed WR15 ordinary lowering operator and the additional kernel
subspace assertion conflict with an explicit nonzero elliptic Fourier
coefficient of the normalized theta completion. The proof reproduces
the audit's coefficient and Fourier-uniqueness argument. The correct
residual lowering equation F_0 Theta = 0 and the original OWR annihilators
remain verified. This establishes a literal incompatibility for that
extra assertion; it neither refutes every theorem in the source nor
supplies a complete reconciliation or corrected restatement of all
source claims.

## What is proved and what remains

The general-rank Weyl-algebra extension, essential oscillator socle,
Gaussian-shadow faithfulness, shifted Casimir, specified induction
parameter, and ordinary smooth/distribution-vector obstruction have
complete written arguments. The algebraic Hermite injection is
infinitesimal; its Schwartz completion has the genuine group injection.
The full extension's larger meromorphic/localized induced realization,
global maps and boundary behavior, and general irrational-isotropic
analytic coverage remain unresolved.

# Public sources

1. Jorge Vitorio Pereira, with Federico Lo Bianco, Erwan Rousseau and Frederic Touzet, *Rational endomorphisms of codimension one foliations*, in *Komplexe Analysis - Algebraicity and Transcendence*, Oberwolfach Report 24/2020, pp.1293–1295. [Official report page](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/1703871); [primary PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46857). The exact question is Conjecture 4, printed p.1294.

2. Federico Lo Bianco, Jorge Vitorio Pereira, Erwan Rousseau, Frederic Touzet, *Rational endomorphisms of codimension one holomorphic foliations*, J. Reine Angew. Math. 789 (2022), 43–101. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2022-0023); [author-hosted published PDF](https://frederictouzet.perso.math.cnrs.fr/ARTICLES/end.pdf); [arXiv revision record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.12541). Governing full-paper question: Conjecture D, printed p.46. The rational infinitesimal-symmetry argument in this package is the classical mechanism of Proposition 2.2, not a new result.

3. Gabriel Fazoli, *Jets of flat partial connections II*, arXiv:2509.13510v1 (2025). [Primary revision record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.13510); [inspected PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.13510v1). Theorem C(A), Proposition 5.3, Remark 5.4.

4. Benoit Claudon, Frederic Touzet, *Structure of Kahler foliations with negative transverse Ricci curvature*, arXiv:2301.06473v2 (2025 revision). [Primary revision record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2301.06473); [inspected author PDF](https://frederictouzet.perso.math.cnrs.fr/ARTICLES/KF.pdf). Regularity conventions, Theorem E and Section 8.

Current author research lists: [Pereira](https://w3.impa.br/~jvp/artigos.html), [Rousseau](https://eroussea.perso.math.cnrs.fr/recherche.html), [Touzet](https://frederictouzet.perso.math.cnrs.fr/).

# Research log

Original target: nonexistence of nonzero weight -n scalar conformal invariants admitting complete metric contractions of Schouten jets, in every even n >= 4. The five numbered attempts are the complete research budget. Completion estimates below are rough assessments of progress toward the original all-dimensional target, not probabilities of correctness or percentages of cases proved.

- 3 October 2026, 11:19 UTC, attempt 1: established the undifferentiated-P ideal lemma and critical length restriction. Estimated completion: 10%. The Cotton and higher ordered-jet sectors remain.
- 3 October 2026, 11:22 UTC, attempt 2: established first-jet vanishing via actual finite-jet realization and an explicit Cotton translation span. Estimated completion: 20%. Second and higher Schouten derivatives remain.
- 3 October 2026, 11:25 UTC, attempt 3: established the full n=6 subcase from classical classification and three exact Ricci-flat witnesses. Estimated completion: 30%. This is a subcase, not a replacement for the original target.
- 3 October 2026, 11:27 UTC, attempt 4: excluded the complete span of the two specified dimension-eight divergence examples by a nonsingular two-metric restriction matrix. Estimated completion: 30%. The rest of the dimension-eight invariant space is unclassified here.
- 3 October 2026, 11:30 UTC, attempt 5: found an explicit nonzero quartic Weyl invariant invisible to every diagonal-curvature test. Estimated completion: 25%. This blocks extending the method by adding only Kasner examples. General Ricci-flat restriction injectivity remains unsupported.

The subsequent fresh independent audit found no fatal gap in the scoped results and reproduced the dimension-six and dimension-eight certificates by independent coordinate and ambient calculations. Its verdict and exact remaining gap are in audit/FRESH_AUDIT.md. The audit is validation of the five attempts, not a sixth research attempt. Overall original-problem status remains unsolved at 5/5; no novelty claim is made.

# Primary sources and interpretation

## Original question

Neil Olver, “Sums of powers,” [Oberwolfach Report 50/2018](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46772), printed p. 3015, PDF page 47. The page was rendered and visually checked. It uses the rational base **2/3**, not the extracted cube-root expression. The generalized formulation fixes a rational base and supplies rational coefficients. The binary-encoding/bit-complexity interpretation is explicit in the attempt. The pinned catalogue’s cube-root desk route is not used as mathematical evidence.

## Classical equality and the easy bases

H. W. Lenstra Jr., [Finding small degree factors of lacunary polynomials](https://pub.math.leidenuniv.nl/~lenstrahw/PUBLICATIONS/1999a/art.pdf), 1999, pp. 267–276. The official-host theorem/abstract states a polynomial bit-time algorithm for bounded-degree factors, including all rational roots when the degree bound is one. The local PDF is image-only; the theorem was read through the official-host web extraction. The elementary equality/carry arguments in this package are written out and are not claimed as new results. The original OWR source also explicitly distinguishes easy integer and inverse-integer bases.

## Precision dependence is not removed

Michael Sagraloff, [A Near-Optimal Algorithm for Computing Real Roots of Sparse Polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.6011), 2014. The primary abstract distinguishes polynomially many rational arithmetic operations from bit complexity depending on the numerical degree. Only the abstract was used here; it is not cited as a polynomial sparse-input bit algorithm.

Gorav Jindal and Michael Sagraloff, [Efficiently Computing Real Roots of Sparse Polynomials](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.06979v1), 2017, Theorems 3–4. The full relevant theorem statements and discussion were read: covering has an explicit requested-precision parameter, and isolation has explicit separation dependence. Neither parameter is discarded in the present analysis.

Emma Boniface, Weixun Deng and J. Maurice Rojas, [Trinomials and Deterministic Complexity Limits for Real Solving](https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.06115v2), revised May 2025. The introduction, Corollary 1.4, Lemma 1.5 and limitations were read. The positive sign result is restricted by an ill-conditioning hypothesis; the more general variable-rational-point question is not silently identified with Olver’s fixed-base formulation. No hardness conclusion for the latter is inferred.

## Rational-base context

Shigeki Akiyama, Christiane Frougny and Jacques Sakarovitch, [Powers of rationals modulo 1 and rational base number systems](https://www.math.tsukuba.ac.jp/~akiyama/papers/3half_H65fullTH-060406.pdf), accessed author manuscript dated 6 April 2006; later Israel Journal of Mathematics 168 (2008), 53–91. The abstract, introduction and Euclidean-division construction/Theorem 1 were inspected. That source uses its own power/digit scaling convention. Turn 5 proves the exact convention and sparse-gap bound it needs rather than importing a dense-word result as a binary-exponent comparison algorithm.

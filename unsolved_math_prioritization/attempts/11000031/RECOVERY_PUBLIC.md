## Original scope
Farb's Problem 3.10 asks for a finitely presented subgroup of the mapping class group of a closed orientable surface with infinitely many conjugacy classes of finite subgroups, with conjugacy taken inside the subgroup. The source does not ask for a smallest possible genus or classification. Its neighboring discussion of the isomorphism problem is not part of Problem 3.10. The original source is PDF page 34, printed page 27, of the author-hosted volume.

## Decisive prior theorem
Brady–Clay–Dani Theorem 3.1 supplies, for genus g at least 3, a subgroup of type F_(g−3) with infinitely many conjugacy classes of order-two elements. The authors explicitly identify this as solving Farb's Problem 3.10. Taking g=5 gives type F_2, equivalently finite presentability. Every order-two subgroup has a unique nonidentity element, so conjugacy of these subgroups is equivalent to conjugacy of their involutions. Hence the published theorem gives exactly the requested finite-subgroup property.

The theorem concerns closed orientable surfaces, not merely punctured surfaces or extended mapping class groups. The paper's construction uses a product of g−2 rank-two free groups generated by paired Dehn twists, extended by an involution exchanging each pair. The kernel of the invariant map to the integers is the desired subgroup. The product-kernel finiteness and conjugacy results are credited prior inputs, not new results here.

# Limits of the investigation

The main target is unresolved. These are partial reductions and counterexamples to intermediate shortcuts.

1. Initiality is closure under simplicity predecessors, not order-convexity.
2. Every model must preserve the exact group or field language. Expanded exponential or differential theories do not answer this question.
3. The source theorem using proper-class models does not immediately settle the set-model hypothesis.
4. Containing every short-birthday surreal does not imply that the entire image consists of short-birthday surreals.
5. Full coefficient groups and truncation closure do not force division of infinite series.
6. The explicit Hahn groups are not claimed saturated. No universal theorem for all models of their complete theories is proved.
7. A real coefficient field, divisible exponent group, and an integer part do not force a particular field to be real closed.
8. Taking an initial hull can change the first-order theory.
9. The literature search was bounded. Absence of a located later result is not a certified global openness statement.
10. The finite exact checks support the concrete controls only. They do not formalize NBG, surreal arithmetic, the cited embedding criteria, or Omitting Types.
11. No proof of independence from NBG, large-cardinal requirement, or impossibility of a set-model proof is asserted.
12. Novelty of the partial observations was not established.
13. A fresh independent audit is required before publication. No remote write was performed.

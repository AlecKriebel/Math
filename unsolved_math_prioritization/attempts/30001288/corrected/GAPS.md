# Remaining gaps and claim boundaries

## Author disposition

The connected, consistently rationalized motivic-Albanese/full-morphic-Jacobian comparison is **unresolved after five substantive approaches**. The literal full-sheaf statement has a projective-line obstruction. These are different conclusions and must stay separate.

## Proved or source-supported parts

1. In the primary conventions, the full Chow sheaf of P¹ in codimension one is the constant rational sheaf. Its Albanese reflector remains that sheaf, while the relevant morphic Jacobian is zero.
2. The full Albanese reflector retains the component sheaf. For this rational Chow sheaf over C, taking its connected part before or after Alb gives canonically isomorphic objects; the proof uses an auxiliary split component quotient.
3. The explicitly repaired comparison holds for divisors, zero-cycles on smooth projective varieties, and all nontrivial codimensions of C × Pᵐ. Smooth open curves satisfy a separate, explicit localization formula.
4. For smooth projective X, both repaired targets have surjections from the same direct sum of curve Jacobians. Their map-compatible equality is equivalent to equality of two specified relation subgroups.
5. The full morphic target has an exact rational kernel J(K)⊗Q over the finite coniveau Jacobian. This kernel is nonzero in Walker's examples. Weight-zero extensions can impose nontrivial period relations.

## Not proved

- Equality, or either general inclusion, of the two relation subgroups R_A and R_W.
- A construction of the desired canonical comparison for every smooth projective variety in arbitrary codimension.
- The corresponding comparison for singular varieties, or an unrestricted nonproper comparison in higher dimensions.
- A general exchange of Alb with kernels, sheaf evaluation over nonclosed fields, or integral connected-component calculations.
- A five-lemma argument that ignores derived higher Picard terms in localization.
- That all 1-motivic sheaves are finite-dimensional abelian varieties, or that a weight-zero quotient can be discarded before taking a Jacobian.
- That descent of the finite Walker intermediate Jacobian settles the full morphic-target question.
- A complete survey certifying that no later work resolves the intended comparison.

## Five approach families

1. Picard representability, extended by boundary-divisor localization on curves.
2. Smooth-proper duality and the universal Albanese on zero-cycles.
3. Correspondence projectors and the projective-bundle decomposition over a curve.
4. Curve-family generation and explicit comparison of relation subgroups.
5. Ind-mixed-Hodge exact sequences, the full Lawson kernel, and period-quotient obstructions.

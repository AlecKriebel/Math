# Approach 2: Transfer approximately central embeddings

For common-unit unital algebras, first choose an approximately central unital copy of Z in A and then transport that copy across the near inclusion into B. The transport constant from Christensen–Sinclair–Smith–White–Winter is 152√γ on a prescribed finite set. Including the unit makes the transported embedding unital by the distance-one projection fact.

Expanding the commutator gives exactly the upper bound

    ε+2γ+304√γ.

The expansion and all contraction assumptions appear in §3 of `MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT.md`. For fixed positive γ, this estimate does not establish arbitrarily small commutators. This is a limitation of the proof, not a lower bound and not a counterexample to the problem.

The constant sequence example in M₂⊗Z proves that arbitrary fixed-error choices can destroy centrality even when A=B. Merely diagonalizing such choices does not fix this.

Outcome: rigorous finite-set estimate and a precise error-improvement gap. No argument was found to eliminate the fixed γ contribution.

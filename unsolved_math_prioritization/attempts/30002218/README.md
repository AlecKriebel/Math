## Accepted scope

For common-unit unital C*-subalgebras A and B of B(H), with A Jiang–Su-stable, the retained-parameter argument proves that d(A,B) < 1/16000000 implies d_cb(A,B) < 1/42 and an isomorphism of scaled Cuntz semigroups. The common unit may be a proper projection. No arbitrary-support or arbitrary-nonunital quantitative reconstruction is claimed.

The source-stated radius **1/6422957 is not independently certified**. The cited PTWW proof chain drops a leading factor k between Lemma 4.12 and Proposition 4.13. The corrected proof retains it and uses the deliberately conservative radius above. Failure of that estimate at a larger radius is not a counterexample to the source theorem. The separate conditional central-sequence criterion uses γ < γ₁ < 1/12600000 to obtain the strict uniform near inclusion required by the embedding theorem. Its missing premise remains unproved from ordinary closeness.

# Approach 4: Transfer directly in the central-sequence algebra

I formulated and proved a conditional criterion in §4 of `MATHEMATICAL_AUDIT.md`. For separable common-unit unital A and B, with A Z-stable, a one-sided near inclusion F_A⊂_γF_B at γ<1/12600000 suffices, where F_A=Q(A)∩A′ and F_B=Q(B)∩B′ are represented in Q(C).

The proof applies the existing strongly self-absorbing embedding theorem to the separable copy of Z in F_A, verifies the resulting map is unital, and invokes the established central-sequence absorption criterion for B. The potentially nonseparable F_B is permitted as the embedding target. No coordinate lift of a homomorphism out of Q(B) is assumed.

The original distance estimate does not yet supply this near inclusion. Proximity to Q(B) and proximity to a commutant are not proximity to their intersection. The rotating diagonal-algebra example in M₂ proves discontinuity of a general relative-commutant intersection map, so a generic continuity argument is false. It is carefully scoped and is not a counterexample to the original question.

Outcome: a proved conditional bridge, with its stronger premise explicitly unresolved. No novelty claim.

## Accepted mathematical scope

The question concerns the double cyclic cover of S^3 branched over a hyperbolic
L-space knot. The desired conclusion is that the cover is not an L-space.
The five genuinely different routes are integral monodromy and cyclotomic
algebra; boundary rotation and taut foliations; standard positive Hopf-tree
plumbing and ADE forms; Floer surgery exact triangles; and the Khovanov-to-Floer
spectral sequence. Source identification and finite computation add no turns.

The report proves a finite-order homological-monodromy obstruction from a
unimodular definite Seifert form, the exact two-slope residual window when
0 < c < 1/2, the definite-tree ADE classification, and explicit algebraic
rank/cancellation models showing why the proposed Floer-rank inferences fail.
The c >= 1/2 and actual standard positive Hopf-tree subclasses are excluded
under the stated imported topological hypotheses. Finite examples supplement
the written arguments; they do not prove universal topology.

A hypothetical counterexample must still have genus at least three, a definite
fiber Seifert form, finite-order integral homological monodromy, cyclotomic
Alexander polynomial, pseudo-Anosov geometric monodromy, 0 < c < 1/2 after the
positive mirror choice, and no applicable standard Hopf-tree or BKL-exponent
at-least-two exclusion. These are necessary reductions, not an existence claim.

## Source corrections and remaining boundaries

The applicable orderability result is Boyer–Gordon–Hu, *Recalibrating R-order
trees and Homeo+(S1)-representations of link groups*, Theorem 7.2 and Corollary
7.3 ([arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.10357v3)). It distinguishes
left-orderability at cover order two from the non-L-space result at orders at
least three. The differently cited Seifert-links paper requires a
Seifert-exterior hypothesis unavailable for a hyperbolic knot. Orderability
alone cannot supply the desired Floer conclusion.

The Baker–Kegel family ([arXiv v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.12013v3)) defeats
the older Li–Ni route: it has hyperbolic L-space knots with cyclotomic Alexander
polynomials and finite-order homological monodromy. The published signature
formula excludes these knots from the target problem by their nondefiniteness.
The August 2026 revision addresses K3 Problem 1.21(c)(iii), not Problem 1.22.
Those source-status observations belong to the accepted research and audit;
this packaging pass makes no new literature-completeness or manuscript-status
claim. The source-free replay does not independently recompute knot signatures.

# What the five shared passes established

All five passes concern the same fixed-mesh cubic and quartic questions. Each pass can contain several proof branches and independent audits. The lifetime count before this sequence is unknown. This final checkpoint reports five newly documented shared substantive passes and closes this sequence without a sixth.

## First pass: separate the cubic answer from the quartic reduction

Qiu's 41-vertex, 56-triangle construction refutes universal cubic vertex-value interpolation. The full cubic space has dimension 107 and vertex-evaluation rank 40. The [credited proof and exact obstruction](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/first_edition/CUBIC_PROOF.md) and its [independent audit](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/first_edition/CUBIC_AUDIT.md) verify the entire space, geometry and attainable hyperplane. Source coordinates remain in the cited preprint rather than being redistributed.

For quartics, the [exact reduction](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/first_edition/QUARTIC_REDUCTION.md) is Rm=Hj. Boundary fans impose no retained compatibility row; only even nonsingular interior vertices do. A possible left dependency is a cycle-balanced closed active row component. The [convex-support theorem](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/first_edition/CONVEX_SUPPORT.md) proves that its induced support is convex and contains at least three original nonsingular degree-four interior vertices with full original stars. Thus meshes with at most two such vertices interpolate arbitrary quartic jets. Qiu's cubic-counterexample mesh and an explicit collinear eighteen-vertex family also interpolate arbitrary quartic jets. These quartic claims have a separate [audit](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/first_edition/QUARTIC_AUDIT.md).

## Second pass: make balance an exact obstruction and distinguish signs from magnitudes

The [second-pass theory](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/SECOND_PASS_THEORY.md) proves that every genuinely balanced closed component gives incompatible quartic jets. A suitable single nonzero coordinate derivative at a degree-four support vertex suffices with all values zero. This removes the first pass's separate nonzero-local-jet-form condition. It also gives normalized homogeneous balance and a one-way construction of a compact C1 quadratic. The [theory audit](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/THEORY_AUDIT.md) checks that scope.

The [construction branch](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/SECOND_PASS_CONSTRUCTIONS.md) proves at least five strict support corners, verifies a rational sign-balanced but magnitude-unbalanced disk, and excludes a three-parameter symmetric family with its possible proper components. The rational disk has closed support, but its actual reduced matrix has full row rank twelve and its cycle product is 683875575/3191327216, not one. The [construction audit](fourth_edition/third_edition/second_edition/CONSTRUCTION_AUDIT.md) independently confirms these distinctions. A closed support or sign pattern alone is not an obstruction.

## Third pass: exclude a full topology and complete nested rings, with corrected local cofactor scope

The [pencil-collapse proof](fourth_edition/third_edition/THIRD_PASS_CEVA_OBSTRUCTION.md) proves quartic interpolation for every admissible realization of the specified full eighteen-vertex, twenty-eight-triangle topology. Its degree-four stars force every prospective closed component to contain the whole twelve-vertex core. The [ring acceptance](fourth_edition/third_edition/INDEPENDENT_RING_ACCEPTANCE.md) verifies the full-topology quantifier.

The [nested-ring proof](fourth_edition/third_edition/THIRD_PASS_NESTED_RING_EXTENSION.md) excludes complete closed cores consisting of the prescribed central triangulation and any positive finite number of complete six-vertex annuli, independently forward or reverse at each layer. Its [acceptance](fourth_edition/third_edition/INDEPENDENT_NESTED_RING_ACCEPTANCE.md) does not claim universal rank in larger meshes: irregular proper components remain outside that theorem.

The [cofactor normal form](fourth_edition/third_edition/COFACTOR_NORMAL_FORM.md) and [acceptance](fourth_edition/third_edition/INDEPENDENT_COFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) characterize the special local proportionality and supply exact controls against overstrong maximum-principle or Hessian-sign arguments. The mandatory [incident-segment correction](fourth_edition/third_edition/CORRECTION_NOTE.md) distinguishes a piecewise function from polynomial continuation of an adjacent piece. The restricted patch-gluing obstruction uses actual full even fans and incident segments.

## Fourth pass: global incidence restrictions and a genuine nonpencil control

The [global incidence proof](fourth_edition/FOURTH_PASS_GLOBAL_INCIDENCE.md) proves global proper three-coloring, boundary-color parity and the refined degree-four count. It excludes closed supports whose three color classes lie on three distinct concurrent projective lines. This geometric containment remains an additional hypothesis, not a consequence proved for all closed supports. Its line/base propagation rules are sufficient rules, not a complete classification. The [global acceptance](fourth_edition/INDEPENDENT_GLOBAL_INCIDENCE_ACCEPTANCE.md) includes a nonconvex eight-vertex control with actual reduced rank two.

The [incidence-changing extension](fourth_edition/FOURTH_PASS_NONPENCIL_EXTENSION.md) passes from a twelve-vertex seed through a reflex-ear intermediate to a fifteen-vertex disk outside the color-pencil class. Their actual reduced ranks are six, seven and eight, equal to their row counts; every stage remains nonconvex. The [extension acceptance](fourth_edition/INDEPENDENT_NONPENCIL_EXTENSION_ACCEPTANCE.md) first accepts the seed's finite mathematics here. The seed was not part of the third pass's accepted ring theorem. No closed balanced support or quartic counterexample was obtained.

## Fifth and final pass: settle the exterior interface and stop this additive candidate exactly

The [final theory](FIFTH_PASS_BOUNDARY_COMPLETION.md) proves that the exact adjacent-turn inequalities suffice for a conforming canonical forced-Q collar, including arbitrary straight subdivisions. Activity and every forced-Q boundary balance, together with the stated parity and internal balances, give a necessary and sufficient finite completion test. It also proves that boundary chords can only be a noncrossing matching of straight vertices, each on its forced exterior line. No strict corner can meet a boundary chord, and every triangle incident with a boundary edge has an interior vertex opposite that edge. The [independent acceptance](INDEPENDENT_BOUNDARY_COMPLETION_ACCEPTANCE.md) covers the geometric and algebraic arguments.

The [final construction theorem](FIFTH_PASS_ADDITIVE_OBSTRUCTION.md) rules out every convex internally balanced conforming completion of the exact fifteen-vertex candidate that retains its old faces and coordinates. Its twenty-eight interior equations have rank fourteen on fifteen weights, leaving only common scaling. At each reflex vertex a forced line strictly misses the whole available cone, so a necessary new triangle cannot exist. Its [independent acceptance](INDEPENDENT_ADDITIVE_OBSTRUCTION_ACCEPTANCE.md) retains the candidate-specific hypotheses. Changing the old triangulation or geometry is outside this theorem.

The target is now the exact finite balanced-support interface in the [final statement](README.md#fixed-model-and-final-unresolved-statement). The canonical collar is proved whenever its inequalities hold. What remains unresolved is whether a legal convex support outside the excluded classes can satisfy all the internal and boundary balances, or whether all such supports can be excluded. No sixth pass, quartic resolution or new priority claim is part of this checkpoint.

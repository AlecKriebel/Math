# Accepted corrections and exact scope

## Mandatory third-pass mathematical correction

The original local-cofactor claim about agreement of the piecewise function on a whole supporting line was false. The corrected [normal form](fourth_edition/third_edition/COFACTOR_NORMAL_FORM.md) says that the piecewise function agrees with its common polynomial on each incident edge segment. Adjacent polynomial pieces separately agree on the full line by polynomial continuation; a different piece can be used on a nonincident opposite ray.

The [original correction note](fourth_edition/third_edition/CORRECTION_NOTE.md) gives two exact controls and the corrected three-ray lemma. It applies only to three actual incident nondegenerate segments on distinct supporting lines. The patch-gluing argument retains the full-even-fan and quartic-proportionality hypotheses. The [independent cofactor acceptance](fourth_edition/third_edition/INDEPENDENT_COFACTOR_ACCEPTANCE.md) accepts this corrected scope. No numerical coefficient was changed, and the invalid whole-line statement is not revived by the fifth-pass boundary-chord proof.

The separate clarification that all twelve pencil-core vertices are distinct repeats the already required conforming-realization model; it does not add a generic-position assumption.

## Fourth-pass editorial correction

The construction's underlying space is a disk before and after the extension. In the corrected public fourth edition, the phrase “change of topology” is replaced by “change of triangulation incidence.” This fixes the remaining sentence in the [nonpencil extension](fourth_edition/FOURTH_PASS_NONPENCIL_EXTENSION.md). No mathematical datum, theorem, rank, coordinate, incidence or limitation changes. The corrected edition's manifest records the new text identity. Its frozen predecessor remains distinct.

## Historical acceptance is not enlarged

The twelve-vertex auxiliary seed was explicitly outside the independently accepted third-pass ring theorem. Its geometry, balances and actual reduced rank were first independently accepted in the fourth pass's [extension audit](fourth_edition/INDEPENDENT_NONPENCIL_EXTENSION_ACCEPTANCE.md). The final checkpoint does not rewrite third-pass acceptance to include it.

The fifth-pass theorems are accepted only with their own independent reports. The final construction excludes additive completions preserving old faces and coordinates. It is not a theorem forbidding a different starting disk, altered coordinates, subdivision or retriangulation. The final theory settles a canonical exterior interface subject to precise inequalities, activity and all balance equations. It neither produces a balanced convex disk nor excludes all such disks.

The statement that x_v+x_w is nonzero is proved in the fifth-pass report for an actual balanced active edge of a conforming mesh. Earlier homogeneous arguments were deliberately formulated without dividing by that sum. Their unchanged wording is historical and does not invalidate their equations.

The cubic answer remains Qiu's credited negative result. The unrestricted quartic question and bundled row remain unresolved. The final pass count is five newly documented shared passes, with unknown lifetime total. Audits, corrections and preparation add no pass; no sixth substantive pass is introduced.

On printed page 12 of Brian H. Bowditch's *A Cartan-Hadamard theorem for median metric spaces*, revised 12 May 2023, the displayed approximate-median intersection repeats the pair `(a,b)` as its third factor. The intended third factor is `(c,a)`. This was independently checked in the rendered PDF, not inferred only from text extraction.

The literal repeated-pair intersection always contains `b`, even in a three-point equilateral metric space. It therefore cannot express approximate median existence. The frozen report already uses all three distinct pairs correctly. The interpretation must remain explicit when comparing its term “almost modular” with the manuscript.

## Five substantive approaches

1. Recast each restriction multiplicity as a composition-factor multiplicity in one root-conjugacy-orbit permutation module. Reconstructed the aggregate square-root identity and its exact limitation; established known empty-root, nonreal-local-block, and identity-root cases. General positive root case remains.
2. Proved the precise central-quotient reduction. Both sides scale by the same orbit-fiber multiplier t∈{1,2}; a modular Grothendieck-group argument supplies the multiplicity equality. Reduced the general local formula to the open involution-orbit conjecture on sections. No full resolution.
3. Proved the formula for all subsections in the standard C₃⋊E model, for arbitrary index-two pairs of 2-groups D<E. Both real and nonreal local blocks were treated. Abstract defect-pair realization is not a transfer theorem for arbitrary blocks.
4. Proved equivalence under product with any finite 2-group, retaining centralizers and conjugacy-orbit sizes exactly. Explained why this valid special transfer does not justify a general Morita-equivalence reduction.
5. Constructed and verified a nonnilpotent real one-simple-module block of an explicit group of order 864. Proved its defect pair, projective character, and all six involution-orbit equalities. Exact checks recover the nonzero multiplicities 2 and 6. No counterexample found.

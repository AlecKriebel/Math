## Checked mathematical sources

- [K] Michael Kapovich, *Dirichlet fundamental domains and topology of projective varieties*, Invent. Math. 194 (2013), 631–672. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/s00222-013-0453-4 . Author-hosted published PDF: https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/tiling.pdf . Checked Question 1.1; Theorems 1.2, 1.4 and 1.6; Lemma 4.12; Proposition 4.13; and Section 10. General G is realized with NC and Whitney umbrellas. The NC-only clause assumes that G is a compact hyperbolic 3-manifold group with possibly empty convex boundary. The quotient comparison requires the centerless group hypothesis in Proposition 4.13. The published numbering is used throughout this packet; the arXiv text https://arxiv.org/abs/1201.3129 has different local numbering.
- [KK] Michael Kapovich and János Kollár, *Fundamental groups of links of isolated singularities*, J. Amer. Math. Soc. 27 (2014), 929–952. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1090/S0894-0347-2014-00807-9 . Author-hosted published PDF: https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/jams807.pdf . Theorem 2 gives a **reducible** complex projective SNC surface for arbitrary finitely presented G. Its statement explicitly distinguishes this result from the irreducible NC-only question.
- [BM] Edward Bierstone and Pierre D. Milman, *Resolution except for minimal singularities I*, Adv. Math. 231 (2012), 3022–3053. DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2012.08.002 . Author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/1107.5595 ; PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/1107.5595 . Example 1.7 (preprint p. 5) explains the branch-monodromy obstruction to eliminating a pinch point by a birational morphism preserving the NC locus. Theorem 1.11 retains NC and pinch points in the surface case. This is a theorem about restricted birational modification; it is not a negative answer to the realization question.

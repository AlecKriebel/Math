## General unit-square manuscript

Dunster, *The general Brannan coefficient conjecture II: Meijer-function approximations*, arXiv:2606.11621v2, revised 2026-07-17. Its normalized generating function, parameter domain and Theorem 1 match the conventional unit-square assertion, including β=1. Theorem 2 imports the companion paper away from x=−1; Theorems 3 and 4 divide the remaining region using a scaled-angle cutoff. The available PDF and HTML were inspected for these statements and for the computational method.

Section 6 describes high-precision grid sampling biased toward parameter boundaries, scalar stationary-point searches with sign-change bracketing, and cross-checks between Maple and MATLAB quadrature. It also describes a finite search interval in an auxiliary integration variable justified numerically. These descriptions are not a machine-checkable global interval certificate over all continuously varying parameters. This investigation did not reproduce the Maple source, certify the global extrema, bound every omitted tail, or audit the companion paper's dependency chain. This is an audit limitation; it is not a claim that the conjecture or the manuscript is false.

The PDF and experimental HTML number formulas differently. References here use section and theorem numbers, avoiding an unsupported equation-number identification. Part II's arXiv record supplies a preprint claim; the companion is marked in press in its bibliography. No peer-reviewed publication status is invented for part II.

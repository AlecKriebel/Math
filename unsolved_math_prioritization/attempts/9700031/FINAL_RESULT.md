## Exact source target

Aldous's 2012 Open Problem 31, renumbered Open Problem 5 in the published 2014 paper, asks about traffic generated by dx dy |y−x|^(−beta), 2<beta<4. The target is almost-sure local finiteness on the major-road sets E(infinity,r), hence a sigma-finite traffic measure on their union and the push-forward scaling c^(beta−5). The 2012 wording allows additional regularity to be stated explicitly.

The major roads are defined by Euclidean distance from both endpoints of witnessing routes, not by a speed threshold. The continuum realization and source sampling definitions must be respected. `SOURCE_AUDIT.md` and `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` record the complete pinned fallback, both primary editions, later literature and prior-attempt checks. These source/literature checks are bounded research, not novelty certification.

## Results obtained

1. **Moment criterion (Turn 1).** Under the explicit jointly measurable continuum route realization (JM), a finite s-th route-length moment, s≥1, gives local traffic finiteness for 2<beta<4−2/(s+1). All finite moments suffice for the full interval (2,4). The first-moment case covers (2,3). The older D log(1+D) endpoint result is preserved historically and is strengthened by Turn 3.

2. **Pathwise geometric criterion (Turn 2).** A local uniform route confinement radius omega(t) satisfying integral t^(1−beta) omega(t)^2 dt<infinity gives locally bounded traffic density with respect to major-road length. Near-linear confinement covers every beta in (2,4), without high route-length moments. Such confinement is an extra hypothesis, not claimed for every SIRSN. A translated-ball identity also simplifies the earlier moment proof.

3. **Critical exponent (Turn 3).** Under (JM), the ordinary first route-length moment and major-road intensity suffice at beta=3. Each dyadic road-size band has exact finite expected critical traffic 2π|A| E[D] log 2 in a bounded area A. Every bounded spatial window meets only finitely many sufficiently large road-size bands almost surely. Consequently the whole cutoff traffic is locally finite almost surely, even though its expectation on every positive-area window is infinite. All moments of order strictly below one are finite on bounded discs. Together with Turn 1 this covers 2<beta≤3.

4. **Sampled traffic completion (Turn 4).** Under the explicitly measurable countably sampled source setup (CS), exchangeable importance-weighted route averages define the critical traffic measure, independently of the positive endpoint sampling density. It agrees with the continuum integral whenever (JM) exists and has the prescribed covariance. The construction does not assert a deterministic jointly measurable route map from arbitrary bare FDDs. At beta>3 the same method works under the stated positive intrinsic road-size moment, which is not established generally.

5. **Counterexample-route restrictions (Turn 5).** The critical theorem rules out positive-length measurable mandatory-cut roads with two positive-area endpoint populations. A separate scalar heavy-mark model obeys the current first-moment, band and elementary packing bounds but has no positive mark moment. It demonstrates a limit of those scalar estimates; it is expressly not a planar routing construction or a SIRSN counterexample.

The sigma-finiteness and source scaling statements accompany the affirmative ranges. The bounded-stretch binary hierarchy and standard probability/geometry tools are credited source material, not asserted as new discoveries.

## Sharp remaining gap

For beta=3+alpha>3, the exact finite-expectation criterion is the intrinsic road-size moment

    M_alpha = E integral_{R(0,(1,0))} S(w)^alpha dH^1(w) < infinity,

where S(w)=sup{r:w in E_r}. The expectation identity is

    E T_{beta,r}(A) = 2π |A| r^(3−beta) M_{beta−3}/(beta−3).

This is sufficient for almost-sure local finiteness but has not been proved from ordinary SIRSN axioms. It is not claimed necessary for the original almost-sure statement. A more refined quenched argument could succeed even if this expectation is infinite. No such argument and no full SIRSN counterexample were obtained.

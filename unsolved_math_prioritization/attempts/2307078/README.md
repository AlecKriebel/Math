# Rubel planar equilibrium problem 2307078

## Established resolution

Problem 7.78 of Hayman and Lingham's *Research Problems in Function Theory* has a negative answer in the complex plane: an infinite sequence of pairwise distinct nonzero complex charge locations with summable reciprocal moduli cannot produce a zero-free reciprocal-difference sum. In fact, the sum has infinitely many distinct zeros away from its poles.

This report explains an established result of J. Clunie, A. Eremenko and J. Rossi (1993), already credited in Hayman and Lingham's 2018 Update 7.78. It makes no novelty claim. The complete authored proof uses a genus-zero canonical product, an explicit sublinear logarithmic growth bound, and an elementary finite-zero lemma. It does not invoke an entire-function factorization theorem.

## Sources and scope

The primary source is [Clunie, Eremenko and Rossi, *On equilibrium points of logarithmic and Newtonian potentials*](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/cer.pdf), *Journal of the London Mathematical Society* (2) 47 (1993), 309–320, especially the paragraph preceding Theorem 2.1 on printed p. 310. The original question and historical resolution are in [Hayman and Lingham, arXiv:1809.07200v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200v2), printed pp. 185–186 and Update 7.78.

The accepted statement is exactly the infinite, pairwise-distinct, unit-charge planar case. This report makes no claim about arbitrary positive real charges, higher-dimensional Newtonian analogues, or their present research status.

# Primary-source audit

Checked 30 September 2026.

1. R. İnanç Baykur, Robion C. Kirby, and Daniel Ruberman, *K3 – A New Problem
   List in Low-Dimensional Topology*, April 2026 preliminary AMS version.
   [Full author-hosted PDF](https://bpb-us-e2.wpmucdn.com/websites.umass.edu/dist/b/22144/files/2026/04/K3-problem-list-watermarked.pdf).
   Problem 5.5 and remarks, printed p.304, were read and visually checked.
   The source distinguishes known codimension-one/two results from the general
   higher-codimension question. Its suggested pairwise surgery/torus-trick route
   is not invoked here as an available theorem.
2. Charles Livingston, *Connected sums of codimension two locally flat
   submanifolds*, Proceedings of the Royal Society of Edinburgh Section A
   154 (2024), 1937–1944; published online 16 January 2023.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/prm.2022.87),
   [full published PDF](https://www.cambridge.org/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/7EED1223FB3A57F19BE6C328127FD665/S0308210522000877a.pdf/connected-sums-of-codimension-two-locally-flat-submanifolds.pdf).
   The full paper was read. Relevant parts are the orientation and interior-sum
   conventions; Theorem 3.2; Lemmas 3.3–3.5; Problems 4.1–4.2; and the smooth
   comparison in Appendix A. The higher-codimension normal-bundle issue is an
   obstruction to extending this proof, not itself a counterexample to the
   connected-sum statement.
3. Livingston, the earlier version of the same paper,
   [arXiv:2104.03930v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.03930), 8 April 2021.
   The full ten-page PDF was read. Its Theorem 1 states the ordinary sphere
   homeomorphism isotopy theorem; Appendix A gives its background; Appendix C
   asks the higher-codimension question. This is the version containing the
   Appendix C cited by K3.

## Identifier correction

Published reference [17] gives the correct connected-sum title but lists
arXiv:2110.03502v1. The [actual arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.03502)
is Livingston's *Intrinsic symmetry groups of links*, a different paper.
That PDF was retrieved only to check the mismatch and is not a mathematical
input. The title-matched preprint above is the correct source for the earlier
appendices. This is a bibliographic correction, not a refutation of any theorem.

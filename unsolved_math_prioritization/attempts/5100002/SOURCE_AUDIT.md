## Exact source

The full arXiv source is Reznik–Garcia–Koiller, arXiv:2004.12497v11,29October2020. Sections1–2 define the nondegenerate confocal ellipse pair, ordinary billiard reflection and signed polygon areas. Section3.1 and Table2 p5 define k103=A′/A, k105=the product of internal half-angle sines, and k108=k103/k105 for N≡2(mod4). The full published companion is Arnold Mathematical Journal7(2021),341–355, DOI10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y, published online18February2021; its Table2 p345 retains exactly that quotient and parity. Both complete PDFs and both rendered tables were inspected. The arXiv record has v11 as its current version. Source PDFs/images remain outside the repository package.

The constructed examples are convex, simple, positively oriented hexagons with six distinct successive vertices and least period6. They share outer axes2,1 and caustic parameter4/9, with positive caustic semiaxes. Their sides support and touch the caustic inside the segments. Full ordinary reflection identities are verified. The outer polygons are the adjacent tangent intersections at the orbit vertices, not caustic-contact polygons, pedal polygons or antipedals. All signed areas are positive, all half-angle sines are positive, and no denominator vanishes.

The source’s k101 normalization Σcos(theta)=JL−N is also checked and agrees with the ordinary internal-angle convention in both examples. Stachel’s later motion paper uses an exterior turning angle instead; its notation was checked but no theorem from it is needed here.

# Status and scope

**UNSOLVED after five shared substantive passes.** The original question is unchanged. This is a report of what the study proves and fails to prove, not a claim that the problem has no solution or that no later literature could settle it.

For each prime p, put k=⌊√p⌋ and c=⌊100√p⌋. Choose A uniformly from the k-subsets of G=Z/pZ. Define

    τ(A)=min{|T|: A+T=G},
    R_m(A)=min_{|T|≤m}|G\(A+T)|.

Every minimization permits arbitrary dependence of T on A. The affirmative target is P[τ(A)≤c]→1 along all primes. A negative answer needs ε>0 and arbitrarily large primes for which P[τ(A)≤c]≤1−ε. No result here meets either target.

## Exact boundaries

- 100k≤c≤100k+99. The two caps are not declared equivalent at their endpoints. The sufficient positive constructions retain integer slack explicitly.
- The sampling law is uniform of exactly k elements. A Bernoulli calculation must be conditioned or coupled with the appropriate direction and error. A result on a product group is not transferred to prime cyclic groups without proof.
- Bounds for fixed centre sets, low-entropy menus, bounded doubling, bounded unions of maps, or specified seed decoders retain those restrictions. They are not bounds on the unrestricted existence event.
- A statement about a random centre set conditional on A is not a statement about a centre set optimized after A is observed. In particular, E min is not min E.
- Fixed-R pencil theorems keep R independent of p. The logarithmically growing-R patch example has an excessive full-pencil cost and does not yield an original-budget cover.
- At every small prime used in the original finite censuses the coefficient-100 cap exceeds p. Smaller auxiliary budgets and some alternative finite set sizes test identities, examples, and invalid implications only.
- The mathematically proved asymptotic statements follow from written proofs, not from curve fitting, numerical trends, or absence of finite counterexamples.

## Provenance and constants

Five stages are a provenance convention for the completed study. Public supplements retain their stage-specific mathematical claims. Earlier stages used |ΔR_m|≤2m and exp(−u²/(8km²)); these are valid but conservative. The final stage proves |ΔR_m|≤m and exp(−u²/(2km²)) by comparing cardinalities relative to the same (k−1)-element core. No historical receipt is silently rewritten to claim the newer constant.

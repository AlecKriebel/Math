# Source and version audit

Checked 7 October 2026. Source PDFs themselves are not included in this packet. The mathematical note treats only the hypotheses actually written, while preserving the separate intended stronger question.

## OWR 2010 contribution

The official EMS report PDF was downloaded, its relevant contribution read, and printed pages 27-28 visually inspected. The mathematical body is on pp. 27-28; its final reference continues onto p. 29, which the independent audit also inspected. The report's ambient setting is a saturated o-minimal expansion of a field. Its displayed (A2) uses simply connected definable sets without the word open; the next page replaces simple connectedness by contractibility in (A1*) and (A2*). No separate continuity hypothesis occurs in that contribution. The report also credits the definably compact group case to earlier work. These observations concern the wording of the contribution, not a claim that the later literature retains those omissions.

## Berarducci-Mamino: 2009 preprint versus 2011 publication

The downloaded arXiv version is v2, revised 30 November 2009, 21 pages. Its Assumption 4.1 on PDF page 5 likewise lacks an open-approximant requirement. The arXiv record lists no later version. It should not be treated as identical to the published article.

The search-indexed text of the **published** article's printed page 567 instead gives open approximants in Assumption 4.1(3), and Theorem 4.4 explicitly concludes continuity. Publisher metadata independently confirms DOI 10.1112/jlms/jdq080, pages 563-586, and revision 28 July 2010.

**Verification limit:** the complete 2011 publisher PDF could not be downloaded: the OUP endpoint returned HTTP 403, and the publicly indexed CiteSeer PDF link redirected to HTTP 404. The exact published hypothesis was inspected in indexed primary-document text, not in locally saved PDF pixels. Consequently no 2011 PDF hash is claimed. The main counterexample and the 2018 theorem-scope comparison do not depend on successful 2011 full-PDF retrieval.

## Achille-Berarducci: 2017 preprint and 2018 accepted postprint

Both were downloaded and inspected. The institutional postprint identifies its final revision as 10 April 2018 and is 25 pages; the arXiv v1 is 24 pages. The institutional record identifies the 2018 Selecta Mathematica publication and the public accepted manuscript separately from its restricted publisher file, which was not requested.

In the accepted postprint, Assumption 12.1 and Theorem 12.2 on page 19 require a triangulable logic quotient and countable decreasing contractible open fiber approximants. Theorem 12.2 gives pointed homotopy comparison, including positive-degree groups and localization to open quotient subsets. Section 8 uses triangulable for a compact finite-polyhedron space. These exact extra hypotheses are retained in our attribution.

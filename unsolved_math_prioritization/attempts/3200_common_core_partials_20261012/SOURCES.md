Search absence does not establish novelty or comprehensive literature status.

https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0012365X9090113V
It concerns amenable colorings and restraints. No equivalence with the present common-intersection edge-list theorem was inferred, and no theorem from its uninspected full text was used. A direct attempt to open the previously noted S0166218X96001278 publisher page returned an internal error; that failed retrieval is not recorded as an inspection.

Results about strong, star, normal or injective edge colorings impose different constraints and were not substituted for ordinary list edge coloring.

## Logical dependencies

The theorem's sole graph-theoretic input is the stated ordinary edge-criticality assumption. The local matching recoloring argument is proved in full, as are the connectedness reduction, palette collapse and restoration. The support frontier and the explicit critical example also have complete symbolic or explicit matching-certificate proofs. Finite tests are corroboration only. All general statements retain the finite simple maximum-degree-three hypotheses and the nonconstant-list qualification.

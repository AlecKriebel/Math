# Substantive investigation log

## Investigation 1: identify the exact target and examine the published resolution

The exact statement was recovered from the primary problem list after the catalogue locator returned HTTP 403. The 2018 no-progress update was checked against later primary literature. The 1999 Aharonov–Shapiro–Solynin paper was identified as the resolution, and their 2006 Theorem 4 states precisely the target minimum. The 1999 full text was unavailable; the 1974 announcement was found to be conditional.

The public 2006 OCR supplied the exact extremal and the complete typically-real calculation. Those algebraic and analytic steps were recomputed with an explicit probability-measure normalization. The minimized quantity for potentially nonunivalent typically-real functions is Dirichlet energy, or area counted with multiplicity.

An initial attempt to justify the full-class reduction used the biangle comparison in the 2001 OCR. Checking its cited 1993 lemma against page images exposed an unresolved inequality-direction discrepancy. That route does not constitute a verified comparison and has been removed from the proof.

The replacement uses the ordinary conformal-radius symmetrization inequality. Its exact definition and sign were checked in the openly available English Dubinin 1994 paper, including page images. The coefficient comparison is written out from the negative slit: its complement meets every circle beyond the slit endpoint, ordinary circular symmetrization preserves the resulting missing negative points, and strict conformal-radius monotonicity supplies a contradiction if the comparison fails. Expansion at zero then yields the coefficient inequality. Radial exhaustion covers general univalent maps.

**Outcome:** attributed prior resolution, with a reconstructed proof of the minimum using the stated classical inputs. Full-class uniqueness in the nontrivial range is not independently proved. This remains one substantive investigation and a correction within it; no five-attempt budget or new solution is claimed.

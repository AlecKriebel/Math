2. Tom Meyerovitch and Yinon Spinka, Entropy-efficient finitary codings, arXiv:2201.06542v1, 17 January 2022. Theorem 1.1 and Questions 8.2--8.3, printed pp.1 and 34--35. Question 8.3 explicitly requires finite-valued X. Its neighboring question asks about a stretched-exponential variant for high-temperature Ising. Text and rendered p.35 inspected. The general entropy-efficient result does not provide the spatial estimate asked for here.

3. Nate Harvey, Alexander E. Holroyd, Yuval Peres and Dan Romik, Universal finitary codes with exponential tails. Author-hosted PDF, Theorems 1--2, printed pp.2--3, and Section 5, printed pp.30--32. Theorem 2 applies to irreducible aperiodic finite-state Markov chains under a strict entropy gap. These restrictions are retained. Text and rendered p.3 inspected.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0502484
   - https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~romik/data/uploads/papers/expoplms.pdf

4. Uri Gabor, Moments of finitary factor maps between Bernoulli processes, arXiv:2509.06018v1, 7 September 2025. Theorem 1.2, printed p.4, gives a same-entropy exponential-tail obstruction unless IID marginals agree up to symbol permutation. This is a publicly posted preprint. It is used only for the prescribed-source, uniform-tail corollary; the core alphabet counterexample and composition/compactness arguments do not depend on it. Text inspected pp.1--4.
   - https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.06018
   - https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.06018

5. J.-R. Chazottes, S. Gallo and D. Y. Takahashi, Finitary coding and Gaussian concentration for random fields, arXiv:2602.14618v2, 26 March 2026 (PDF dated 27 March). Abstract, coding applications, and concluding discussion checked for relevance. This concerns concentration consequences of coding hypotheses; it does not supply a low-entropy exponential recoding theorem. It is not an input to the proofs here.

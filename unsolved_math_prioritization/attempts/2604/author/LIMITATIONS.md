# Limitations

1. The original existence question remains unresolved by this investigation. “Still open” describes the inspected editorial state and failure to locate a verified later solution, not a proof that no solution exists anywhere.
2. All five approaches are exclusions or reductions. None gives a universal classification of almost simple nonsimple groups.
3. The PGL_2 collision census is bounded: 183 odd prime powers q ≤ 1,000, with witnesses searched among odd prime powers r ≤ 10,000. No all-q theorem is inferred.
4. The symplectic theorem covers the explicitly specified natural-module field-semilinear groups. It does not silently include exceptional graph automorphisms.
5. Finite enumeration checks are exact, but they are not formal proof-assistant verification. The infinite-family claims rest on the written proofs.
6. Recognition by spectrum, recognition by graph plus order, and recognition by the labelled graph are stronger-input questions. Their positive results do not establish unlabelled graph recognisability.
7. Literature was inspected only at the locations described in SOURCE_VERIFICATION.json. Full imported CFSG, modular-character, and representation-theoretic arguments were not independently audited.
8. A classification allowing a quotient with a nontrivial solvable radical does not by itself construct such a radical extension. No existence claim is deduced solely from a list of possible quotients.
9. Current repository search found no exact earlier artifact for 2604/21.95, but this is not an exhaustive absence proof across deleted branches, unpublished working copies or unindexed personal files.
10. Author replay is not independent audit. Publication requires a fresh uninvolved audit of the frozen packet.

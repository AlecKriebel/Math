1. **Graph obstructions and reduction.** Reproved the universal-vertex direct-product obstruction and the unchanged-socle collision. Outcome: rigorous exclusion tests, including literature coverage of A_6 exceptional extensions. Gap: these are necessary conditions only; satisfying them does not produce recognisability.
2. **Deleted permutation modules.** Constructed the characteristic-two deleted/fully-deleted modules for S_n, proved that every nonadjacent odd prime acts without fixed vectors, and used the affine-extension lemma. Outcome: all S_n, n ≥ 5, excluded by an infinite family of labelled-graph competitors. Exact enumerations and two module-choice negative controls passed. Gap: a generic outer extension has no supplied analogue of this module.
3. **Semilinear symplectic transvections.** Used a nonzero fixed vector to construct an involution commuting with the odd-prime element. Outcome: all concrete characteristic-two symplectic field-semilinear groups are excluded, including PSL_2(2^f) field extensions. Exact finite-field matrix, field-automorphism and affine enumerations passed for q=4,8. Gap: exceptional graph actions and odd-characteristic analogues are not covered by this transvection argument.
4. **Rank-one odd-characteristic topology.** Derived the PGL_2(q) graph from Jordan forms. Found explicit different-order graph partners for the labelled-recognisable q=169,289 candidates, and for every odd prime power 5 ≤ q ≤ 1,000. Outcome: 183 exclusions with exact vertex maps; four full projective-matrix enumerations checked the formula. Gap: no theorem controls the required prime-divisor-count pattern for all q, and a finite unmatched search would not certify recognisability.
5. **Sporadic outer-extension route.** Inspected the 2024/2026 simple-group unlabelled classification and the 2009 outer-extension graph study. Outcome: four unchanged-socle exclusions and a direct-product exclusion for Aut(McL), relying on the cited graph equalities. Necessary quotient classifications were kept separate from actual competing constructions. Gap: no full radical-elimination and arbitrary-label analysis for a remaining nonsimple sporadic candidate was achieved.

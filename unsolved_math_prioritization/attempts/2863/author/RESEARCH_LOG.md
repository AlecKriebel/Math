# Five substantive approaches to KP-3.65

Date: 2026-10-03. Goal: prove or refute the rank-one recognition implication for every closed connected oriented prime 3-manifold. These are five mathematical approaches in one investigation, not a claim of five independent researchers or five formal validations. No full solution was obtained.

## 1. Character specialization at A=-1

Mechanism: turn generic rank one into a singleton SL(2,C) character space, then use abelian characters and Zentner's theorem to recognize S^3.

Work and outcome: proved the singleton-character recognition lemma with its homology reduction. Tested the crucial specialization inference on R direct-sum R/(A+1). Its generic dimension is one but its A=-1 fiber has dimension two. Thus the proposed implication fails even for finitely generated abstract modules; generic rank is not the specialized dimension.

Exact gap: an additional condition excluding the relevant torsion jump is required. This route alone does not address S^1 x S^2 or any other non-tame rank-one candidate.

Checkpoint estimate toward a full general resolution: 5%, a subjective assessment of progress rather than a probability or measured success rate.

## 2. Odd-root bounds and the torsion quotient

Mechanism: use every odd-order root simultaneously rather than the classical fiber alone, and separate torsion from the torsion-free quotient before specializing.

Work and outcome: proved 0 -> T/pT -> M/pM -> (M/T)/p(M/T) -> 0 without finite generation or splitting. A cleared-denominator dependence proves that the last term has dimension at most the generic rank. Combined with the DKS root-of-unity theorem, this gives the quantitative torsion-fiber inequality in PROOF.md, Section 4. At rank one, every nonspherical Y must have a nonzero fiber at every odd cyclotomic prime.

Stress tests: distinguished the fiber of R/(p^e), of dimension one over R/(p), from its total primary length e. The divisible torsion module R[1/p]/R shows why existence of a p-torsion element does not imply a nonzero torsion fiber. The torsion-free module R[1/p] shows why the torsion-free fiber dimension need not equal generic rank.

Exact gap: no topological argument excludes the all-orders torsion pattern for a remaining prime Y. The abstract direct-sum example in Section 7 realizes that pattern and generic rank one algebraically.

Checkpoint estimate: 10%; a useful exact obstruction has been isolated, but it does not settle the topology.

## 3. Abelian representations and homology grading

Mechanism: force enough independent generic skeins from nontrivial first homology, or at least quantify the character obstruction explicitly.

Work and outcome: proved the inversion-orbit formula (|H|+|H[2]|)/2 for diagonal characters when H=H_1(Y;Z) is finite, including why no further trace identifications occur. For positive first Betti number, exhibited infinitely many characters through a quotient onto Z. The torsion-fiber bound therefore becomes explicit, and all its fibers must be infinite-dimensional in the positive-Betti case.

Stress test: the skein relations preserve mod-2 link homology, but existence of a grading does not establish nonvanishing in each localized sector. S^1 x S^2 has two mod-2 classes and generic rank one, already defeating a universal bound by the number of mod-2 classes.

Exact gap: proving generic nonvanishing in a second homology sector for every remaining manifold would be a new topological input, not a consequence of the grading. For integral homology spheres the abelian count is only one, so irreducible characters and the torsion barrier remain essential.

Checkpoint estimate: 10%; the candidate restrictions are sharper, but the general gap remains.

## 4. Dehn-filling finite generation and denominator control

Mechanism: place rank-one manifolds within the finite-generation classes where recognition is known, using peripheral annihilating polynomials and Dehn filling.

Work and outcome: traced DKS Theorems 4.2–4.3 through the Newton-polygon corner-unit condition. Their figure-eight and torus-knot filling families lie in the finite-generation case away from the explicitly excluded slopes; rank-one recognition follows for these members. The conditional argument actually needs only one missing cyclotomic torsion prime, and finite generation of the torsion submodule suffices.

Stress test: generic generation by one element does not bound integral denominators or the annihilators of infinitely many torsion classes. The module E in Section 7 is already generated by one element after localization while retaining one torsion summand at every odd cyclotomic prime. Peripheral coefficients invertible in Q(A) need not be units in Q[A,A^{-1}].

Exact gap: a uniform integral or torsion-support statement is missing for a general prime Y. The exceptional filling slopes are not silently included. No full skein-rank formula or census search is claimed.

Checkpoint estimate: 10%; established classes are recovered, without a mechanism covering arbitrary prime manifolds.

## 5. Quantum-evaluation independence

Mechanism: for a rational homology sphere, use the nonzero empty skein as a prospective rank-one basis. Every other fixed skein would then have quantum evaluations given by a single rational-function multiple at almost all roots.

Work and outcome: derived this necessary rational-function relation directly from F-linearity of the evaluation map. Proved the Chinese-remainder interpolation obstruction: any finite collection of compatible root values is matched by a rational-coefficient polynomial. Finite samples alone therefore cannot disprove the required global rational relation. Read the 2026 Kitaeff theorem giving a nonzero element in the evaluation kernel, so universal injectivity cannot be assumed as a shortcut.

Exact gap: no fixed link with the required provable all-orders nonrational normalized evaluations is constructed uniformly for the remaining prime manifolds. For positive-Betti manifolds, even the rational-homology-sphere empty-skein theorem cannot simply be reused.

Final checkpoint estimate: 10% toward the unrestricted goal. The estimate is not a claim that a fixed fraction of an eventual proof is known.

## Final disposition and verification

Unsolved after five substantive approaches. The proof, source limits, and exact gaps are preserved. The portable checker passes 860 finite exact algebraic controls covering cyclotomic fibers, primary multiplicities, finite abelian character counts, compatible interpolation and the cited scalar cancellation. Those controls do not compute any manifold's generic skein rank and do not replace the written universal arguments or credited external topology.

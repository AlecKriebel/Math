## Accepted conclusions

1. Literal plane: for every integer ell>=1, the minimal Laplace–Beltrami operator for the frame (partial_x, x(x^(2ell)+z^2) partial_z) on R^2 minus {x=0}, with initial domain C_c^infinity and Riemannian measure dx dz/[|x|(x^(2ell)+z^2)], is not essentially self-adjoint. The proof constructs two maximal-domain functions with a nonzero boundary form at infinity and a genuine nonzero vector in ker(H*+1). No deficiency-index dimension is claimed.
2. Product criterion: for g=dx^2+h_x on (R minus {0}) times N, a=-x partial_x log m>=1 together with the stated compactly supported fibre cutoffs uniform on every bounded x-strip implies essential self-adjointness. The exact weighted identity and full compact three-cutoff limit argument are retained.
3. Complete family: for N=R or a circle, smooth c>0 and rho>=0, with sup c and sup(c rho) finite, the coefficient c(z)x(x^(2ell)+rho(z)) has the complete ambient structure and an essentially self-adjoint minimal operator for every integer ell>=0. Positivity is pointwise; no lower uniform bound on c is required. An unbounded rho is allowed only when c rho remains bounded.
4. PRS torus Examples 7.7 and 7.8: for n>=2, integer ell>=1 and the stated smooth nonnegative f_0 on the compact torus, the same product proof gives essential self-adjointness for all ell. The displayed frame determinant in both examples is F^(n-1).
5. The first-order criterion and compact-resolvent inheritance are accepted only with the exact respective PRS/FPR geometric, collar, regularity, compactness and hypersurface hypotheses preserved in Section 6. The analytic moving-ridge example demonstrates failure of a weak-Hardy sufficient method; it proves neither non-essential-self-adjointness nor a counterexample to the analytic conjecture.
6. The corrected older-criterion range for positive integer ell is n=2: ell=1; n>=3: 1<=ell<=n, when f_0 has both zero and positive values on the connected torus. The necessity assertion does not cover constant or strictly positive f_0 without zeros. Section 7 gives the full polynomial minimization, ratio-realization and fixed-remainder argument. The complete replacement paragraph is reproduced in AUDIT.md's appendix.

## Domain and remaining scope

The literal entire-plane formula is incomplete at infinity. The original OWR discussion has a standing ambient-completeness assumption, and its prior ell=1 example is a torus family. These are different global problems. The accepted negative literal-plane conclusion is not a counterexample to the original complete-setting analytic conjecture. The accepted positive complete-family conclusion is not a localization theorem for arbitrary complete structures agreeing with the polynomial formula only near the exceptional point.

Arbitrary complete local extensions and the general real-analytic no-tangency conjecture remain unresolved by this work.

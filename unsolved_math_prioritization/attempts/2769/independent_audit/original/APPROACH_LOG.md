# Five bounded mathematical approaches

1. Abelianization on a one-boundary torus. Proved that every admissible factorization has length 12 and recalled its chain-relation realization. Outcome: a known exact subcase; no higher-genus extension.
2. Genus-two congruence and geography inequalities. Re-derived the seven-node obstruction and equality type from published necessary constraints. Outcome: an established low-genus result; the genus-specific hypotheses do not cover the remaining pairs.
3. Euler characteristic and Betti-number minimization. Derived the exact formulas and retained the first-Betti-number term. Outcome: a correct objective function, but no sharp bound on its value across all admissible four-manifolds.
4. Symplectic representation and candidate-word verification. Gave a concrete positive word passing the closed-homology identity test while representing an interior separating twist rather than the target. Outcome: a decisive obstruction to using that relaxation as an acceptance test; no exhaustive shorter-word exclusion.
5. Hyperelliptic and other restricted monodromy classes. Checked the direction of subset bounds and the recent handlebody-monodromy manuscript's hypotheses. Outcome: restricted lower bounds cannot certify the unrestricted minimum without an additional reduction theorem.

Stop condition: all five approaches leave the general sharp lower-bound and realizing-word obligations unresolved. Result is partial and stalled. Literature and source triage preceding these approaches was not counted as a mathematical approach.

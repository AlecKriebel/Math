## Exact source and target

Primary report: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46948, DOI 10.4171/owr/2022/7, printed p423, “Interpolation spaces” attributed to Shekhtman. The entire problem was visually checked on PDF page 41. It fixes a field K of characteristic zero and asks for the smallest number of fixed four-dimensional K-linear subspaces of K[x,y] such that every four-element subset of K² has surjective evaluation on at least one member. Equivalently one of the corresponding 4×4 evaluation determinants must be nonzero. There is no degree bound, restriction to monomial spaces, generic-position hypothesis or freedom to choose the family after seeing the points.

Write this minimum m_4(K). A different possible reading asks for the least uniform bound valid for every such K, with families permitted to depend on K. Field-specific examples do not settle that worst-case question. No author clarification of the intended quantifier has been obtained. The printed three-point preamble is not valid for all characteristic-zero fields; TURN_1.md explains the precise issue without claiming it resolves the four-point problem.

The imported original_statement incorrectly includes the neighboring spline question. That material is excluded. The dataset’s 2023 bibliographic label is not dismissed merely because the workshop/report is dated 2022.

# Turn 2: an unrestricted algebraically closed lower bound of four

AI-assisted mathematical proof candidate; independent review pending.

## Theorem and exact scope

For every algebraically closed field K of characteristic zero,

4 ≤ m_4(K) ≤ 5.

Here m_4(K) is the fixed-family four-dimensional polynomial interpolation minimum defined in TURN_1.md. The lower bound allows arbitrary polynomial degrees and arbitrary four-dimensional subspaces. Consequently the least bound valid for all characteristic-zero fields also lies between four and five. This does not assert m_4(R)≥4, nor m_4(Q)≥4. TURN_1.md's Q(t) example remains valid.

The upper bound is the credited five-space construction already verified in turn 1. This turn supplies a local-cohomology lower bound, using classical arrangement theory. No novelty claim is made.

## 1. Determinants and the collision locus

Let R=K[x_1,y_1,…,x_4,y_4]. For each pair i<j, put

I_ij=(x_i−x_j,y_i−y_j), and I=∩_{i<j} I_ij.

The closed set V(I) consists exactly of the ordered four-tuples with a repeated point. Suppose s four-dimensional polynomial spaces cover every distinct four-point set. Choose a basis in each space and let D_1,…,D_s∈R be the corresponding evaluation determinants. Each D_a vanishes on every collision, since two rows then agree. Conversely, at any ordered tuple of four distinct points, one D_a is nonzero by the covering hypothesis. Algebraic closedness and the Nullstellensatz therefore give

sqrt((D_1,…,D_s))=I.

Local cohomology depends only on the radical of the support ideal. A Čech complex on s generators has no terms above degree s, so H_I^j(R)=0 whenever j>s. Thus it is enough to show H_I^4(R)≠0.

This step is exactly where a cover of K-rational tuples becomes an algebraic zero-locus statement. Over a nonclosed field such a promotion is unjustified, and we do not make it.

## 2. The cited arrangement input

Use Álvarez Montaner, García López and Zarzuela Armengou, Local cohomology, arrangements of subspaces and monomial ideals, Advances in Mathematics 174 (2003), 35–56, DOI 10.1016/S0001-8708(02)00050-6. Primary author-hosted manuscript: https://web.mat.upc.edu/josep.alvarez/pdf/aim03.pdf . Theorem 1.2 and Corollary 1.3, manuscript pp7–10, give a filtration of H_I^r(R) with successive quotients indexed by intersections p of arrangement components. The contribution at p of codimension h is

H_{I_p}^h(R) ⊗_K reduced-H_{h−r−1}(P_{>p};K),

where intersections are ordered by inclusion and P_{>p} is their strict upper interval, represented by its order complex. We use a nonzero associated-graded contribution only; we do not claim that the filtration splits.

## 3. The full-diagonal interval computed explicitly

Intersections of the six components are indexed by partitions of {1,2,3,4} with at least one nonsingleton block. Points carrying indices in one block are required to coincide. Let p be the full diagonal, corresponding to the one-block partition. It has dimension two and codimension h=6 in K⁸.

Strictly above p are exactly:

- six three-block partitions of type 2+1+1;
- four two-block partitions of type 3+1;
- three two-block partitions of type 2+2.

The discrete four-block partition represents the ambient space, not an intersection of a nonempty collection of components, and is excluded. The order complex is a graph: there is no chain of three distinct elements among the two displayed ranks. It has 13 vertices. Each 3+1 partition is comparable to three of the pair partitions, yielding 12 edges. Each 2+2 partition is comparable to two, yielding another six. Hence it has 18 edges.

The graph is connected. For any two pair partitions, their pairs either share an index or are disjoint. In the first case both are adjacent to the 3+1 partition on their three indices; in the second both are adjacent to the corresponding 2+2 partition. Every two-block vertex has at least one such neighbor. Thus every vertex belongs to the same component.

A connected graph with V vertices and E edges has first homology dimension E−V+1 over every field. This graph consequently has

dim_K reduced-H_1(P_{>p};K)=18−13+1=6.

There are no two-simplices, so these six cycles cannot be killed by higher boundaries. The accompanying exact checker independently enumerates all partitions, incidences and the rational boundary rank.

## 4. Nonvanishing and conclusion

Choose linear coordinates consisting of x_1,y_1 and the six differences x_i−x_1,y_i−y_1 for i=2,3,4. In those coordinates I_p is generated by six independent variables. Its top local cohomology H_{I_p}^6(R) is nonzero: the inverse product of those six variables represents a nonzero class in the top Čech quotient, because Laurent monomials with all six exponents negative cannot occur in the sum of the localizations omitting a variable.

Set r=4 in Section 2. The full-diagonal contribution is

H_{I_p}^6(R) ⊗_K K^6,

since h−r−1=1. It is nonzero, and therefore H_I^4(R)≠0. Section 1 implies s≥4. Combining with the classical five-member cover proves the theorem. ∎

## 5. What remains after turn 2

This narrows the algebraically closed minimum to {4,5}; it does not decide between them. A construction of four spaces would need genuine evaluation determinants of fixed four-dimensional spaces, not merely four arbitrary equations cutting out the collision set. Conversely, a lower bound of five must exploit an obstruction stronger than the nonzero fourth local cohomology established here. For the real field, the only unrestricted lower bound established in this packet is still two. The exact function-field value and conditional number-field consequence from turn 1 are unchanged.

## Route 1: latent geometry of raw response slices

Mechanism: exact conditional distribution on a circular tube.
Derived support length `2b+h`, variance `b²/3+h²/12`, and nonzero limiting
oriented and sign-invariant tangent losses for one vector per response slice.
This makes the obstruction precise even with infinite data. It does not
exclude covariate-refined tangents or general regression. Estimate: 10%.

## Route 2: weighted Fourier deconvolution

Mechanism: undo response convolution for moments weighted by covariate
coordinates and their products. Proved identification when the independent
noise characteristic function is known and nowhere zero, and `L²` consistency
for bounded weights under known Gaussian noise with logarithmic cutoff.
Gap: unknown-noise handling, uniform shrinking windows, denominators, spectral
gaps and prediction guarantees at useful dimension-efficient rates.
Estimate: 15%.

## Route 3: pilot-denoised response slicing

Mechanism: an independent uniformly accurate pilot replaces noisy labels in
the slicing step. Proved latent-width bound `(h+2δ)/m` for a lower-Lipschitz
link. Gap: obtaining the required pilot efficiently is central to the original
problem, and geometry/assignment still need control. Cross-fitting alone is
not a bias-removal theorem. Estimate: 15%.

## Route 4: vanishing Euclidean guard

Mechanism: add `λ_n ||x-z||` to the tangent-based neighbor dissimilarity and
reserve independent responses for regression. Proved an order-statistic radius
comparison and a complete fixed-dimensional consistency theorem. The bound
`O(n^(-1/2)+n^(-s/(2D)))` exposes the loss of dimension-efficient learning.
This answers a weaker literal interpretation under extra design hypotheses,
but does not close the research goal used here. Estimate: 20%.

## Route 5: higher-order local geometry

Mechanism: increase local geometric approximation order. The flat graph
`exp(-1/t²)` gives an exact all-orders jet obstruction on a fixed-width slice
for merely smooth curves. This is not an impossibility for data-driven
polynomial fitting across the slice. Gap: noise-robust estimation of growing
geometric complexity with a vanishing error and acceptable sample cost.
Estimate: 20%.

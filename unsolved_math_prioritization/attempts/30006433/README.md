## Accepted scope

Every finite-free integral algebra of additive rank d<=2 with arbitrary bilinear multiplication is covered. Associativity, commutativity, nilpotence, and a multiplicative identity are not assumed. A subalgebra is a finite-index additive subgroup closed under multiplication and need not contain an identity.

For rank two the intrinsic binary cubic f_A(v)=det(v,v*v) controls all four exact formula cases. The topological degree is -2, and the common strictly positive leading value is (1+chi(S_1(A_C)))/3, where S_1 is the reduced projective variety of one-dimensional complex subalgebras. The four values are 1, 2/3, 1, and 4/3 for the zero cubic, triple root, double-plus-simple roots, and three distinct roots. Ranks zero and one are included.

The proof keeps actual root counts at bad primes and reduced multiplicity root schemes at good primes. Its good-prime Denef formula applies to every finite local-field extension, including ramified ones, without a prime-independent root-count assumption. Topological normalization is Rossmann's (1-q^(-1))^d, with leading behavior at s=infinity, and the reduced function is justified by coefficientwise Euler specialization.

The conditional general specialization lemma has an explicit nonnegative-integer normalization exponent d and regular, nonvertical-denominator hypotheses. It supplies neither positivity nor a general algebra theorem. Artificial rational countermodels are not algebraic counterexamples, and the rank-three Heisenberg example marks the scope boundary. Neither arbitrary-rank target is solved by this packet.

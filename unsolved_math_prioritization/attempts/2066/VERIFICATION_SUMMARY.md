## Written mathematics

The complete authored audit reconstructs the prior theorem for every finite
0 <= m < n. Existence holds exactly when m <= 1. Completeness is eventual, deletion
acts on indexed occurrences, and each deletion can have its own threshold.
Binary and Fibonacci witnesses retain the two initial Fibonacci ones. The central-
interval argument includes repeated-value normalization, both transitions,
diverging output cuts and the bounded-potential contradiction through PDF page 11.

tolerance. The bounded and signed integer reductions are retained in full.

exponential examples for complete sequences*, arXiv:2609.25107v1. The inspected

`9060ebcc3812b9a33bf08cc8dbdf6b5c8714acda2f54ed4ecfca7c152cb8dcc2`.
Its separate exponential-sequence results and original base-2 Problem 354 are
outside acceptance. No mathematical correction was required.

# Five approaches and outcomes

1. **Affine invariant quotient.** For the rank-one symmetric pair the invariant ab has two distinct regular nilpotent K-orbits over zero. Result: it is not a geometric quotient on the regular sheet. A categorical/affine quotient cannot be silently substituted for an orbit quotient.

2. **One p-Slodowy slice.** The slices (1,c) and (c,1) separately cover only a != 0 and b != 0. Result: one slice does not cover the sheet. A closure statement is weaker than surjectivity. This failure motivated use of Bulois–Hivert's actual open-cover theorem.

3. **Glue the nilpotent slices in type A.** Combine that open cover with the G-slice section property furnished by connected nilpotent centralizers. Result: Theorem A and Corollary B in RESULT.md construct a geometric quotient scheme for every type-A K-sheet, with explicit regular overlap maps and categorical descent. The target may be nonseparated. This is a consequence assembled from established results; novelty is not asserted.

4. **Require a separated quotient.** The two curves (1,z) and (z,1) are generically K-conjugate but specialize to distinct K-orbits. Result: separatedness forces their images to coincide at zero, ruling out an orbit-space geometric quotient. The doubled-origin phenomenon is already published and is credited accordingly.

5. **Extend beyond the connected-centralizer criterion.** The finite G-component-group quotient and quotient-stack rigidification were examined as possible extensions. Result: the regular K-sheet has a published solution by Hameister–Morrissey, but arbitrary nonregular sheets require additional local K-orbit separation and scheme-representability arguments. Those gaps are not filled by finite checks or by sheet smoothness. Stop with the proved type-A result and this explicit remaining gap.

Overall classification: partial result for the all-type question; negative if separated targets are required; affirmative for type A with possibly nonseparated schemes. No blanket solved-in-literature or new-solution claim is made.

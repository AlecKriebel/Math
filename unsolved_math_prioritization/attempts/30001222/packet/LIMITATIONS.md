# Claim boundaries

1. No full affirmative or negative resolution is claimed. “Unresolved” describes this investigation, not a proof that no later result exists anywhere.
2. The two 36-dimensional parameter examples satisfy all displayed algebraic conditions except that a stable equivalence of Morita type has not been established. They must never be advertised as counterexamples to the target.
3. The nine-dimensional deformation is positively excluded from stable equivalence of Morita type, by unequal HH1 dimensions. Its matching centers and Loewy layers do not bypass this obstruction.
4. The numerical bimodule result is stated for split local algebras. Arbitrary local algebras over general fields may have nontrivial residue division algebras; this hypothesis is not removed.
5. The tilting-complex proposition assumes a derived equivalence. Stable equivalence of Morita type is not silently strengthened to derived equivalence.
6. Kessar's known theorem assumes an algebraically closed field of characteristic three and the specific algebra $k\langle x,y\rangle/(x^3,y^3,xy+yx)$. It is not quoted as arbitrary-field nine-dimensional rigidity.
7. The original report has a prime-characteristic field fixed in its context. The catalogue's arbitrary-field phrasing is recorded as a scope expansion, not erased.
8. Parameter conventions differ between sources. This packet uses $xy=qyx$; sources using $yx=qxy$ have the inverse parameter. No conclusion relies on conflating them.
9. No full Hochschild ring or restricted Lie-algebra isomorphism is claimed for the 36-dimensional pair. Only HH1 vector-space dimensions were computed and proved equal.
10. The current preprint about the Auslander–Reiten conjecture is used only for scope comparison. Its proof has not been independently audited here, and none of the partial theorems depends on it.
11. Literature and duplicate searches were bounded, read-only checks. Search absence is not a mathematical proof of novelty or global open status.
12. The exact script verifies finite examples. Universal partial statements have authored proofs; finite computation is not used as a universal quantifier.
13. An independent audit is still required. This author packet has not undergone human peer review and contains no claim of such review.
14. No source PDFs, extracted source text, original dataset contents, private coordination files, or credential material is part of this packet. All bibliography metadata and scholarly URLs concern public sources.

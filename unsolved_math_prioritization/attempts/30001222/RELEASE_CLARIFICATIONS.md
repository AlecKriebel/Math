# Release scope clarifications

These conventions accompany the frozen author packet and full independent audit; no historical bytes are rewritten.

1. All derived equivalences in Proposition 3 and its summaries are **k-linear**, and all algebra and center isomorphisms are k-algebra isomorphisms. The proposition asserts that k-linearly derived-equivalent finite-dimensional local k-algebras are isomorphic as k-algebras. It supplies no lift of a stable equivalence of Morita type.
2. The [Benson–Kessar–Linckelmann theorem and Corollary 1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1604.04437) require parameter order **e ≥ 2** dividing p−1, odd characteristic p, the specified truncations x^p=y^p=0, and a split local symmetric stable-Morita partner. Its generator bound is 2e, not an assertion of two generators. The parameter convention in that source is inverse to the packet's. The target's e=1 commutative case, including its additional equal-dimension and center-isomorphism hypotheses, follows separately from Proposition 1; it is not a proof of the cited bound for arbitrary partners.
3. For the **36-dimensional F11 pair**, stable equivalence of Morita type is **UNDETERMINED**. Neither an equivalence nor an obstruction to one has been established. For the **9-dimensional characteristic-three deformation pair**, such an equivalence is **EXCLUDED**, by HH1 dimensions 8 and 7. The former matching invariants do not turn it into a counterexample; the latter is a rejected candidate.
4. Ordinary-center isomorphism is an independent target hypothesis. Stable-center invariance concerns a quotient. The independently checked ordinary/stable-center dimensions are 12/11 for the F11 examples and 6/6 for the characteristic-three examples. No ordinary-center invariance is inferred from stable-center invariance.
5. The result remains **unsolved after five substantive approaches**. Finite assertion counts are arithmetic controls, not proof counts; finite matrix enumeration is not arbitrary-field classification; and bounded literature searches do not certify global novelty or open status.

## Prior attempt audit gates

The prior nested Schur-complement identity is a sufficient-certificate framework, not an executed PDE certificate. It assumes the main analytic/numerical obstacles: a genuine C1 Fredholm-Lagrangian path, compatible operator charts, uniformly invertible tails, controlled coupling and derivatives, exhaustive crossing boxes, and verified chart overlap. No interval PDE solver was run.

A substantive continuation should verify:
1. Norm-C1 dependence and a fixed Hilbert decomposition; bounded self-adjoint blocks and a uniform inverse bound on the infinite tail.
2. Exact Schur kernel equivalence and crossing-form congruence, including derivatives of inverse blocks.
3. Global crossing-box ownership, so chart overlaps cannot double count; endpoints and switch points transverse.
4. The scalar crossing condition excludes multiple or tangential crossings; any block extension needs a separately proved inertia certificate.
5. The rectangle example is an interior spectral-flow benchmark. Its sign must be reconciled with the chosen boundary Maslov convention by an actual theorem, not a free sign declaration.
6. One concrete nontrivial physical-planar example needs full analytic/tail validation before claiming a computational method has actually been demonstrated.

These are recommended audit tasks, not assertions that the recovered conditional Schur theorem is false. Its kernel/crossing-form argument appears structurally sound under its explicit assumptions. Novelty is unestablished and likely low for the constituent reductions.

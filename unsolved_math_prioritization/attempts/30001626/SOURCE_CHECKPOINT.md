## Exact primary question and setting

Tumpach's contribution *Root theory of L*-algebras and applications* in OWR51/2010, printed pp.3011–3013, contains the exact question on p.3012. That page explicitly identifies the background as the theory of **simple separable L*-algebras**. It also discusses both complex and real classifications, citing Balachandran and Alagia. The question about inner-automorphism conjugacy is adjacent but is a different target; it is not bundled into this numeric record.

The whole OWR PDF was downloaded from EMS and the exact question page rendered and inspected. The imported short statement omits the contextual separability condition. We therefore distinguish the intended separable question from an unqualified nonseparable reading rather than using a nonseparable example as if it answered the intended problem.

The relevant Cartan notion is a maximal abelian *-stable subalgebra in the L*-algebra sense, not a Cartan masa in a von Neumann algebra with a separate regularity/expectation condition, and not an arbitrary locally nilpotent algebraic Cartan in a finitary Lie algebra.

## Known structural inputs read

- Tumpach's full arXiv:math-ph/0703042 manuscript, Section2 pp.2–3, defines this Cartan notion, credits Schue for maximal abelianness, and explicitly states the Hilbert root decomposition relative to a given Cartan in equation(3). It explicitly notes that the root set is countable or finite in a separable L*-algebra. This is the core established input. The article was subsequently published in Forum Mathematicum21(2009),375–393; exact page references here are to the full arXiv manuscript.
- Alagia, *Cartan subalgebras of Banach-Lie algebras of operators*, Pacific J.Math.98(1982),1–15, complete publisher PDF. The introduction explicitly covers real simple separable L*-algebras and realizes them as Hilbert–Schmidt operator algebras. Proposition2.1 on pp.3–4 gives the countable simultaneous eigenspace decomposition for a real abelian L*-subalgebra of Hilbert–Schmidt operators. This corroborates the real scope and supplies an alternative operator interpretation.
- Beltita–Patnaik–Weiss, *Cartan subalgebras of operator ideals*, full arXiv1408.4897, definitions and Section4 checked. Its inner-unitary conjugacy problem is distinct. It does not turn the present centralizer problem into a claim about all conjugacy classes.

Searches also located the classical Balachandran–Parthasarathy1966 two-page Cartan paper, but its full text was not recovered and no exact theorem is inferred from its title. No exhaustive novelty search or priority claim is made. The proof route is an elementary Baire-category consequence of the already established root decomposition; those inputs must remain credited.

## Intended proof gate

A valid argument must show that the Cartan is complete, that the countably many root functionals are continuous and nonzero, and that avoiding their kernels eliminates all nonzero root components in the completed Hilbert sum. For real forms, complexification must preserve the Cartan and semisimplicity; it is not enough to prove only a complex or finite-dimensional analogy. A separate nonseparable example may explain why the source's separability matters but cannot replace this affirmative argument.

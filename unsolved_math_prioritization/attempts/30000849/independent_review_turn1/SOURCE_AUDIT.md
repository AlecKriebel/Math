# Source audit and scope decision

## Original target

[OWR46/2007](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3032/OWR_2007_46.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1), da Costa's full contribution, printed2754–2756/PDF28–30, was read. The equation and Conjecture1 were also visually inspected. Its displayed birth coefficient, pure time composition, size exponent and amplitude exponent agree with the frozen candidate's literal reading. Zero data and p=1/4, constant input1 satisfy the stated hypotheses. The scale constant is unspecified, but the proof covers every positive finite value.

The coefficient conflicts with the source's own p=0 reduction. The candidate separately proves its obstruction for the standard coefficient too, so the conclusion is not just a coefficient-typo complaint. Its independent existence and fixed-ray argument refute the displayed normalization. Corrected physical self-similarity remains unproved and undisproved by this result.

All four full PDFs match the author manifest. The literal displayed conjecture is the source-qualified target receiving PASS. No current-literature search can by itself certify novelty or historical priority, and none is used that way. Reading inputs and images are excluded from the portable review files.

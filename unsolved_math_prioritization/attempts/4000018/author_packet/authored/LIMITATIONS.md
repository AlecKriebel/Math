# Limitations and exact remaining questions

1. The original problem is open-ended. Neither “somehow related” nor an optimal coefficient is mathematically specified. This packet resolves several precise implications, not that entire request.
2. The primary counterexample concerns arbitrary reversible Markov kernels. It is not a counterexample within canonical strongly local heat flow, smooth Brownian motion, or RCD heat flow.
3. Failure of BE(1,N) in the reset example is at the exact chosen curvature 1. The same generator satisfies BE(K,N) for K<1 and N sufficiently large. No claim excludes all lowered-curvature relationships.
4. The infinite-dimensional reset example's metric and generator are not intrinsically matched. The distinction is exactly why its numerical transport coefficient cannot bound geometric dimension.
5. The finite-dimensional positive implication uses stronger W2 literature and sacrifices constants. No equivalence of the exact W1 inequality with BE(K,N) is established, and no optimal formula C=f(N) is proved.
6. The small-time coefficient infimum at most 2N (or N for the half-Laplacian normalization) is not a claim that this limiting value is itself admissible on a nonzero horizon.
7. Euclidean bounds n-1≤C*≤n are calibration at zero curvature. No new positive-curvature diameter theorem or sharpness claim follows from them.
8. In the original theorem, epsilon-geodesicity, the scale restriction and the finite-epsilon correction are indispensable to the cited argument. Two-point or other nongeodesic examples are not used to refute a geodesic theorem.
9. Later CD/RCD notation and finite/infinite dimension conventions have not been conflated. A statement about linear canonical heat flow does not cover every synthetic CD space or every semigroup on a manifold.
10. The live catalog webpage was blocked (HTTP 403). The original scholarly PDF was successfully retrieved, and the live repository queue was read. Catalog triage is background, not authority for mathematical status.
11. Full primary preprint PDFs were obtained; the exact relevant statements and their assumptions were inspected, with key equations visually checked. Journal metadata was verified separately. The complete final journal typesettings were not obtained, and no assertion of identical pagination/theorem numbering is made.
12. Literature searches found related dimensional heat-flow results but no verified exact-target resolution. Search absence is not evidence of nonexistence. No claim of novelty is made for the elementary reset or Gaussian arguments.
13. The Python controls check exact algebra, test-family witnesses, rational sufficient inequalities, source-file metadata and packet consistency. They are not a formal proof assistant certificate, an exhaustive search over all spaces, or a proof of the original open problem.
14. No external messages, remote repository writes, source redistribution, publication, merge, or release were performed in this investigation. Only the authored/ directory and its reproducibility outputs are intended for a safe publication packet. Private reading material and coordination records are excluded.

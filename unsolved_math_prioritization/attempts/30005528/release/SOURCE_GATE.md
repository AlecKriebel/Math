# Source and claim boundary

## Primary source

Felipe Gonçalves, *Linear domino size problem*, Question 1, printed p.1254, in *Group Actions and Harmonic Analysis in Number Theory*, Oberwolfach Reports 20 (2023), pp.1195–1262, report 22/2023. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2023/22); [official PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47016). Problem-session chair: Jens Marklof; notes: Paul Nelson.

The printed definition uses ordered pairs of arbitrary symbols. Question 1 requires an infinite compact alphabet and continuous positive size. It does not require a packing realization. Our relation is closed even though that extra condition is not stated. The original page was checked both as extracted text and as a rendered image.

## Related primary paper

F. Gonçalves and G. Vedana, *Sphere packings in Euclidean space with forbidden distances*, Forum of Mathematics, Sigma 13 (2025), e49, 1–35. [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1017/fms.2025.9).

Theorem 13 treats finite domino sets. Conjecture 4 concerns optimal periodic packings for compact sets of allowed short distances. Its Section 4 uses special block relations determined by all consecutive partial sums. An arbitrary graph of a circle rotation has not been shown to arise from those relations. Our counterexample therefore does not settle that conjecture or the motivating packing question in the report.

## Attribution and search limit

Targeted searches checked the exact problem title, “linear domino game,” and Gonçalves with domino periodicity; the two primary items above were inspected. The elementary irrational-rotation mechanism is standard. No prior published presentation of this precise counterexample was located in these checks, but this is not an exhaustive literature review and establishes no historical priority.

Source PDFs and full extracted source texts are not redistributed in this packet.

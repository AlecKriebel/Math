A genuine author attempt to overcome the same-space derivative-loss obstruction by a holomorphic interior neighborhood. The exact centered Gaussian model gives O(h) and O(h²) operator estimates via Cauchy bounds, an excited-data iterate quotient theorem, and a first multiplier correction. This is an additional analytic model, not an identification with the original PDE. The original remains unresolved at 3/5.

## Mathematical tests and conclusions

1. **Compression:** The commutator word is correct without commuting factors inside any one copy. The identity s_(i−1)=c_i s_i makes s_i s_(i−1)^−1=c_i^−1 in the submitted multiplication convention. Commutation is used only between displaced copies. No injective direct-product embedding is needed. The identity element requires zero commutators.
2. **Portable product:** The group must consist of endpoints of smooth isotopies with one compact total support. Smooth perfectness is an imported theorem for that group. For finitely many factors, put their map supports in a disk B_R, choose L>2R, and use a bump-supported vector field equal to L∂x on the entire swept corridor B_R+[0,m](L,0). ODE uniqueness then gives m+1 disjoint translated copies. The displacement and all compression factors remain in the same compact-isotopy group. Compactness of M is essential to this proof.
3. **Two sphere disks:** With cutoff χ having genuine plateaus, a_s=(f_(sχ),id) and b_s=(f_s f_(sχ)^−1,id) have smooth inverse families, start at the identity, and have fixed compact total supports in the two respective charts. Endpoint order is b_1 a_1=(f,id). In particular b_s is not replaced by f_(s(1−χ)) for a general non-one-parameter isotopy. Compactly supported local factors and their isotopies extend smoothly by the identity. Two local commutators per chart give the ambient bound four, including all powers of f.
4. **Consequences:** Any ambient homogeneous quasimorphism vanishes on these stabilized elements by the uniform bound on all powers. For a positive-genus surface, an abstract retraction onto the stabilized surface group would contradict Bowden–Hensel–Webb's unbounded ordinary commutator length; genus one is included. This contradiction does not address genus zero or arbitrary alternative extension constructions.
5. **General cutoff:** On S² the rotation with point-dependent time τ=1/2+xy has tangent rank one at (0,1,0). The angular derivative is zero and the transverse derivative is one. Thus the naive universal cutoff fails even for smooth τ in [0,1]. This is an obstruction to that construction, not a proof that every ambient factorization fails.
6. **Probability and averaging:** Arbitrarily small positive contractions and corridor translations in one larger chart map a coordinate ball to N disjoint images by individually constructed identity-component diffeomorphisms. Invariance implies Nμ(B)≤1 for every N; a finite cover contradicts total mass one. This handles arbitrary Borel probabilities, including atomic and singular measures, and dimensions one and higher. A connected zero-dimensional manifold has a Dirac invariant probability and is expressly excluded. The cocycle calculation has exactly the signed pushforward error displayed in the submission, subject to its integrability assumptions. No invariant probability blocks the direct invariant-measure implementation only.
7. **Handles and products:** Explicit product Morse indices are 0,1,3,4 for S¹×S³. Standard connected sums have counts (1,k,0,k,1); no H₂-vanishing inference is used to remove handles. For finitely many perfect component groups, product commutator length is the maximum of the component lengths, by projection and padding with identity commutators. Subgroup inclusion decreases ambient length. The fragmentation estimate is an upper bound and cannot produce the requested lower bound. A homogeneous quasimorphism on a central extension descends precisely when it vanishes on the central kernel.

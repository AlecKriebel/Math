# Historical status and source reconciliation

The proposed classification is `already_solved`, using one substantive historical-result route. Its meaning is bounded: the request for a nontrivial condition has classical answers, and the specific shrinking-inradius suggestion is refuted by an established capacity-zero obstruction. This is neither a complete classification of all permissible omitted-value sets nor a claim to a new theorem.

Hayman–Lingham, arXiv:1809.07200v2, gives the relevant reports in Updates 5.5, 5.7 and 5.40 (printed pages 86, 87 and 100):

- Update 5.5 attributes the capacity-zero obstruction to Fernández and a positive-capacity Bloch-domain sufficient condition to Pommerenke.
- Update 5.7 credits vanishing coefficients for certain universal covering maps, then retains an open-status sentence for subordinate maps.
- Update 5.40 explicitly gives Fernández counterexamples for arbitrary maps into shrinking-inradius domains with polar complements.

Reading the latter two reports together exposes an editorial inconsistency in the retained open-status sentence. The negative example is also consistent with Update 5.5. It would be incorrect to use that isolated sentence to assert that the suggested implication was still open in 2018.

The public publisher record verifies Fernández's article, journal, year, page span and DOI. It does not verify the theorem's proof. The original article was not available in full text; no theorem number, page-specific theorem location, or independent verification of its construction is claimed. Pommerenke's original article likewise was not obtained, and its more general sufficient criterion is background only: the positive strip criterion in this packet has its own complete proof.

Searches were bounded. They are not an exhaustive modern-literature survey, a novelty certificate, or evidence of absence of related results. The classification rests on a positive historical source assertion rather than on failure to find a newer paper.

The explicit puncture geometry and coefficient arguments in `PROOF.md` have been checked by the author. A fresh independent audit must separately decide whether the historical-source evidence meets its acceptance threshold and verify all mathematical hypotheses before any publication.

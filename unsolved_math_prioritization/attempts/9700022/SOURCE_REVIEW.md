## Unit-current-pair model and the source heuristic

David Aldous, [Empires and Percolation](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Research/OP/empires.html),
states the unit-current-pair rule, named starts, conjecture and individual-boundary
identity, with 2009 and 2017 updates. The recorded HTML identity is 3,612 bytes,
SHA-256 efb17f4574fd713eeffdab4afe7792e121f8056d57b8f634f48553379eda7919.
It was read in full and publicly reopened during the recorded reviews. Continued
presentation of the question is not an exhaustive current-literature certificate.

D. J. Aldous, J. R. Ong and W. Zhou, *Empires and percolation: stochastic merging
of adjacent regions*, Journal of Physics A: Mathematical and Theoretical 43 (2010),
025001, published online 10 December 2009:
[author PDF](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Papers/me123.pdf),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1088/1751-8113/43/2/025001),
[arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/0911.0601).
The recorded PDF identity is 506,674 bytes, SHA-256
078b8879e8b5ed20bb44e94d0b0574c2ddf3e9f1a5832232ef5500394cef6dbc.
The full ten-page text was read. The author review visually checked page 7;
the independent audit visually checked pages 6 and 7 using local renderings
after a browser screenshot attempt failed, and reopened the author PDF.

Section 2 discusses refinement couplings and cautions that pointwise rate
inequalities alone need not yield such a coupling. The authored construction
here supplies its coupling directly. Sections 3 and 3.1 label the hegemony
argument a heuristic and identify repeated labels and nonconsecutive adjacency
as obstacles. The repeated-label witness illustrates that acknowledged obstacle.
The factor-two correction follows from the explicitly printed idealized-chain
rates and equation (9); it changes transient occupation integrals by one half
and does not alter convergence or validate the heuristic geometric model.

## Critical square-lattice endpoint

Béla Bollobás and Oliver Riordan, *A short proof of the Harris–Kesten Theorem*,
Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 38 (2006), 470–484:
[arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0410359),
[version 3 HTML](https://arxiv.org/html/math/0410359v3),
[DOI](https://doi.org/10.1112/S002460930601842X).
The recorded version date is 16 June 2005. Section 4, Theorem 8 and its proof,
were checked for critical square-lattice bond nonpercolation at p=1/2. This
endpoint fact, rather than the numerical critical probability alone, supports
the simultaneous square-cell interval [0, log 2]. No local PDF identity is
claimed for this source.

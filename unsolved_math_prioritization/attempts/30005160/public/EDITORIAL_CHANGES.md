Stage metadata and exploratory material were removed. The mathematical calculations retain their accepted scope; original source files remain unchanged and are hash-bound in the inventory.

Surrounding scope statements were reconciled with later accepted results:

- All three corrected cubic dominant cones are included. Symmetric slices and their neighborhoods are distinguished from the remaining unrestricted strict-triangle question.
- The factorized-quartet comparison is labelled double-flip only.
- Auxiliary-order failures are distinguished from actual power or Green counterexamples.

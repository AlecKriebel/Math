# Turn 2: a class-two obstruction to retracting onto the lower mapping torus

Date: 2026-10-03. This route tries to turn the explicit subgroup Gamma<G into a geometric splitting or retraction. It proves the following exact, limited result:

**Proposition. The natural subgroup Gamma is not a retract of G. Equivalently for this purpose, no retraction F5→F3 fixing a,b,c intertwines phi and alpha.**

No novelty or quasi-isometry conclusion is claimed.

## Reduction to two word equations

If R:G→Gamma were a retraction, it would fix a,b,c,t. By Turn 1 the two abelianizations are Z, generated by t; since d,e are homologically zero in G, their images lie in the kernel F3 of Gamma's height map. Put x=R(d), y=R(e). The conjugation relations require

    alpha(x)=y a,          alpha(y)=y x.                 (1)

Conversely an equivariant retraction F5→F3 would extend to such a mapping-torus retraction, fixing t.

In abelianization, if X,Y are the exponent vectors of x,y and A is the lower matrix, then AX=Y+e_a and AY=Y+X. Eliminating Y gives

    (A^2-A-I)X=-e_a+e_b.

The coefficient matrix is [-1 1 -1;-1 0 0;1 -1 0], with determinant -1. Its unique integral solution is X=(-1,-1,1), Y=(0,0,-1).

## Class-two calculation

Work in N=F3/gamma_3(F3), using [p,q]=p q p^-1 q^-1. Every pair with those exponent vectors has unique expressions

    x=a^-1 b^-1 c [a,b]^u0 [a,c]^u1 [b,c]^u2,
    y=c^-1 [a,b]^v0 [a,c]^v1 [b,c]^v2,

where all six exponents are integers. The commutators are central. Expand noncommutatively to degree two, with a↦1+A, a^-1↦1-A+A^2, and likewise for b,c. Equating coefficients in (1) gives six independent equations:

    -u1-v0+1=0,        -u2-v1=0,
    u0-u2-v2+1=0,      -u0-v0-v1-1=0,
    -u1-v1-v2+1=0,     -u2+v0-2v2+1=0.

Here these A,B,C denote formal noncommuting variables, not the homology matrix. The formulas can alternatively be checked by central-commutator collection.

The first three equations give v0=1-u1, v1=-u2, v2=u0-u2+1. Substitution into the last three yields

    u0-u1-u2=-2,
    -u0-u1+2u2=0,
    -2u0-u1+u2=0.

Consequently u0=-u2, u1=3u2 and 5u2=2. No integer solution exists. In fact the unique rational solution is

    u=(-2/5,6/5,2/5),      v=(-1/5,-2/5,1/5).

Therefore even the necessary class-two quotient equations have no solution, and neither does (1) in the free group. This proves the proposition.

## Interpretation and limitation

The quotient F5/normal-closure(F3) carries the Fibonacci automorphism, but the extension's lower-word twist cannot be removed by an equivariant retraction. This rules out a particularly simple route to decomposing the mapping torus. It does not rule out invariant complementary free factors in every marking, virtual retractions, general cyclic splittings, or arbitrary quasi-isometries. The obstruction is tied to the specified subgroup and stable letter, not a proposed unmarked QI invariant.

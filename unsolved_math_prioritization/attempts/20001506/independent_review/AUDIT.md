## 1. Cyclic-cover homology: PASS

Abelianizing a mapping-torus presentation gives `Z ⊕ coker(M^n−I)` for the displayed cyclic covers. Both diagonal blocks have no unit-modulus eigenvalue, so all the stated cokernels are finite. The cubic has one real root `ρ>1` and a complex conjugate pair of modulus `ρ^(−1/2)`; the quadratic roots are `τ` and `−τ^(−1)`. This proves the all-n Betti-number assertion rather than merely checking sample covers.

An independent exact control uses trace recurrences. Set

- `p_n = tr(A^n)`, with `(p_0,p_1,p_2)=(3,0,2)` and `p_n=p_(n−2)+p_(n−3)`;
- `q_n = tr(A^(−n))`, with `(q_0,q_1,q_2)=(3,−1,1)` and `q_n=−q_(n−1)+q_(n−3)`;
- `L_n` the Lucas sequence, with `L_0=2,L_1=1`.

Since `det A=1`, the degree-three determinant expansion gives

`det(A^n−I)=p_n−q_n`.

For the quadratic block,

`|det(B^n−I)| = L_n−1−(−1)^n`.

Fraction-free Bareiss determinants agree with both formulas through n=30, independently of the packet's permutation determinant implementation. The block-upper-triangular form proves the torsion ratio for every n. Factoring by the eigenvalues proves the two exponential growth rates.

At n=1, both abelianizations are exactly Z; hence each epimorphism to Z is the displayed height or its negative. This makes the towers canonical under isomorphism. It supplies no quasi-isometric recognition theorem.

## 3. Attracting cylinders and proper lamination inclusion: PASS

The crucial definition was checked directly in Appendix A of [Dowdall–Guerch–Gupta–Mutanguha–Uyanik](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.10433v1). It requires birecurrence and a forward-invariant attracting neighborhood whose iterates form a neighborhood basis. The construction meets these conditions.

Here is an explicit all-n core argument filling in every quantifier behind the finite margins.

Let `θ=φ^3`, so every positive generator image has length at least 2 and at most 7. Use `θ(e)=(ed)e(aedb)`, with the middle occurrence distinguished. Iteration gives an increasing family of centered words `γ_n=θ^n(e)`, exhausting a positive bi-infinite line ℓ. Its unbased θ-image is itself.

Every sufficiently remote annulus on both sides contains any fixed γ_m: both ed and aedb contain e, every θ-iterate of e contains e, and applying θ^m to those occurrences gives γ_m. Since every finite subpath of ℓ lies in some γ_m, this proves birecurrence on both ends.

[Francaviglia–Martino–Syrigos, Lemma 2.34](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.3747.pdf) gives the bounded-cancellation estimate `BCC≤Lip·vol`. Applied to the equivariant map between the appropriately marked universal-cover trees, it gives `C≤7·5=35`. No relative-train-track hypothesis is needed for this estimate.

Take `γ_3`, of length 162, with centered margins 73 and 88. Let U be the cylinder of unoriented lines containing γ_3. A line in U may have completely arbitrary reduced exterior rays. When θ is applied, at most 35 letters of the positive core image disappear at each boundary. The center survives. Inductively retain the positive central subpath after each step and let its left and right margins be `r_k,s_k`. Bounded cancellation and minimum expansion imply

`r_(k+1) ≥ 2r_k−35`, `s_(k+1) ≥ 2s_k−35`.

Consequently, uniformly over all initial lines in U,

`r_k ≥ 35+38·2^k`, `s_k ≥ 35+53·2^k`.

Every retained core is a centered subpath of ℓ. These bounds preserve γ_3 already at the first step, proving `θ(U)⊂U`. They also imply that, for every finite subpath β of ℓ, some k satisfies `θ^k(U)⊂Cyl(β)`. Because θ is a homeomorphism of line space and fixes ℓ, every θ^k(U) is an open neighborhood of ℓ. This proves the full neighborhood-basis assertion, not just pointwise growth of selected positive iterates.

For the lower line, use `η=φ^5` and `η(a)=c a b`. Its full-rose Lipschitz constant is 21, giving `C≤105`; every positive image has length at least 3. An entirely explicit seed is `η^5(a)`, of length 816 with centered margins 464 and 351. Both exceed 105, so even the conservative recurrence `r↦2r−105` proves attraction for arbitrary exterior rays in the full rank-five line space. The lower restriction is primitive, giving birecurrence. The cited fully irreducible lower example in Behrstock–Bestvina–Clay, Example 5.24, supplies its unique attracting lamination and depth one.

Containment can be shown without a finite-language extrapolation. Each lower finite segment lies in some `α^(5n)(a)`. All three words `α^5(a),α^5(b),α^5(c)` contain a. Hence `α^r(a)` contains a for every r≥5. Choose m with `3m−5n≥5`; then `α^(5n)(a)` is a subword of `α^(3m)(a)=θ^m(a)`. Since θ(e) contains a, the latter occurs in `θ^(m+1)(e)`, and hence in ℓ. This proves every lower generic-line segment belongs to the top language and therefore proves the closed-lamination inclusion.

It is proper because ℓ contains upper letters, whereas the lower closure does not. The top line is nonperiodic: it contains upper letters and arbitrarily long lower-only subwords. A periodic line with an upper letter has uniformly bounded lower-only runs.

Thus `δ(α)=1` and `δ(φ)≥2`. Corollary 5.7 of the cited 2026 paper gives noncommensurability. Its introduction continues to describe quasi-isometry invariance of depth as conjectural. No exhaustion of the full lamination set, and thus no claim `δ(φ)=2`, follows from this argument.

## 4. Periodic conjugacy: PASS, with exact finite limits

The exterior-square spectral argument is sound. Among all products of two eigenvalues of M, the only root of unity is the product of the two quadratic roots, namely −1. The other product moduli are respectively `sqrt(ρ)`, `1/ρ`, `ρτ`, `ρ/τ`, `τ/sqrt(ρ)`, and `1/(τ sqrt(ρ))`, none equal to one. Distinct eigenvalues make M and its exterior square diagonalizable.

Independent action on collected commutator normal forms verifies `Wv=−v`, `rank(W+I)=9`, and `rank(W−I)=10`, with exactly the packet's integral vector v. The all-period conclusions follow: odd-period classes have zero degree-two class, and even-period classes have degree-two class in Z v. The final coordinate of v justifies integrality.

There is also an enumeration-free proof of the claimed length-six exclusion:

1. A nonzero multiple of v involves all five generators. A balanced word with all five generators has length at least 10.
2. Every nonempty cyclically reduced balanced word of length at most six has nonzero class in `γ_2/γ_3`. If it uses two generators, choose one occurring just once with each sign. Cutting cyclically at those letters leaves two nonempty powers of the other generator; the word is conjugate, up to signs and renaming, to `[a^k,b]`, with `|k|=1` or 2. Its commutator coefficient is nonzero. If it uses three generators, each occurs exactly once with each sign. Draw a chord between inverse occurrences on the cyclic word. A crossing pair gives a nonzero corresponding degree-two coefficient. If no pair crosses, a noncrossing perfect matching has an adjacent matched pair, contradicting cyclic reduction. One-generator nontrivial balanced reduced words are impossible.
3. These facts contradict the necessary class-two filter for every possible period.

The independent multiset-permutation enumerator reproduces all four raw counts, orbit counts and filtered counts. All 40 length-eight survivors have zero class-two class; this is consistent with step 1. Independently reduced substitutions have no conjugate or inverse-conjugate return during iterates 1 through 12. The complete survivor list and their twelfth cyclic lengths are recorded in the audit JSON.

Nothing here excludes a length-eight class with a larger period, a longer periodic class, or all possible Nielsen paths. It is not an atoroidality certificate.

## 5. Gap exponent and metric-density control: PASS

The weighted lower-run recurrence is valid because every top image begins with a top letter, every top image retains one, and only d contributes an additional lower suffix a. Therefore no two old lower runs merge across a surviving upper letter, and at most one letter a is prepended to an image of an old run. With weights `(1,ρ,ρ^2)`, α scales lower weighted length by ρ, giving the stated upper bound.

The identity

`φ^n(e)=φ^(n−1)(e) φ^(n−2)(e) α^(n−2)(a)`

supplies the lower bound for every n≥2. Weight comparison gives the packet's estimates for ordinary run length. The eigenvector `(1,1,τ−1,1,τ)` is correct and has a nonzero Perron contribution from the e seed. It yields `T_n=Θ(τ^n)` and asymptotic top-letter frequency 1/2.

Thus `L_n=Θ(T_n^κ)`, with `κ=log(ρ)/log(τ)=0.5843571576574038…`. Direct positive words through n=18 satisfy the all-n bounds; this is an independent finite control, while the recurrence supplies the proof.

Changing lower-edge lengths to k and upper-edge lengths to 1 changes the metric share of upper edges to `1/(k+1)`. Fixed positive edge weights are bilipschitz equivalent, so raw density is not a group quasi-isometry invariant. The gap exponent survives that particular rescaling and powers, but the chosen fiber, lower free factor and forward dynamics have not been recognized under an arbitrary quasi-isometry. Uniqueness of an algebraic height homomorphism does not provide that missing recognition.

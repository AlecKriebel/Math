The exact target is a universal implication about measurable deterministic allocations, not about one chosen family. Write the origin shift as theta_t and require a jointly measurable flow and a flow-covariant, locally finite, nonzero diffuse measure xi. An allocation is generated by a measurable pi of the observed pair: tau(omega,s)=s+pi(theta_s omega). Consequently tau(theta_t omega,s-t)=tau(omega,s)-t. Its pushforward of xi must equal xi. The conclusion to test is equality in distribution of the *joint pair* after shifting by pi. The 2009 setting allows a general sample space, while Thorisson's question explicitly makes pi a function of (X,xi). Auxiliary ambient randomness must therefore be distinguished from a deterministic function of that pair.

The mass-stationarity test is stronger than measure preservation. For every relatively compact positive-Haar C with Haar-null boundary, U is uniform on C, V is sampled from xi restricted and normalized on C-U, and (theta_V omega,U+V) must have the distribution of (omega,U). A failure in the first marginal alone suffices. A Palm/Mecke identity is an alternative necessary test, but a direct test will be preferable if available.

My independent candidate obstruction is a continuous stabilizer. In a product lift xi=nu tensor Lebesgue, with theta_(a,b) omega=theta_a omega, covariance at (0,b), for every b, forces

    tau(omega,(x,y+b))=tau(omega,(x,y))+(0,b).

Thus tau has the form (f_omega(x),y+g_omega(x)), without requiring injectivity or surjectivity. For discrete-support nu=sum_i w_i delta_x_i, each positive fiber is indivisible in its *quotient label*, despite xi being diffuse. Integrating against a target test h(z)k(v) with integral k=1 forces

    sum_i w_i 1{f_omega(x_i) in B}=nu(B).

Incommensurable positive weights, together with an appropriate no-collision property of the labels, can force preservation of separate component point measures. The continuous fiber remains fully movable by arbitrary measurable vertical translations g; these translations do not move the underlying state. This mechanism could genuinely evade the usual diffuse splitting intuition. It does not require that all diffuse measure-preserving maps are translations: only maps equivariant with respect to the stabilizer must be translations on each fiber.

Potential fatal gaps to seek after this checkpoint:

1. A claimed allocation class ignores maps depending on X or extra ambient state that the source permits.
2. Covariance holds only for each group element outside a group-element-dependent null set; the simultaneous stabilizer conclusion is then unjustified without repair.
3. A measure identity is checked only on support points or finite windows without proving a global pushforward equality on the sigma-finite product.
4. The quotient atom argument assumes a bijection: arbitrary deterministic allocations can be many-to-one and can send support to unsupported points only on zero source mass.
5. A fixed continuous coordinate secretly marks an origin and destroys the proposed stabilizer.
6. The proposed law is invariant only in its measure marginal, while the theorem requires the entire pair.
7. The non-Palm witness merely invokes the atomic base without checking that the diffuse lift transfers the relevant mass-stationarity property.

Positive controls are mandatory: identity must be admitted; arbitrary fiber translations are admissible; and a familiar component-preserving point-shift should be admitted if the construction supplies one. A deliberately nonequivariant continuous splitting map and an allocation using an independent stationary background will have separate verdicts and cannot refute the background-free claim.

This is a mechanism reconstruction and falsification plan, not a verdict on the unseen candidate. It does not assert that the required construction, its universal allocation classification, or the non-mass-stationarity witness has been supplied correctly.

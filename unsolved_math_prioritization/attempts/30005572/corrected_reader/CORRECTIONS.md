# Mathematical corrections and scope history

The final statement is partial and the original problem remains UNSOLVED. The complete accepted arguments are included in [PROOF_INDEX.md](PROOF_INDEX.md). This history identifies mathematical repairs rather than presenting rejected versions as accepted proofs. All listed reviews are internal AI reviews, not journal peer review.

## 1 Constants and trace normalization

A genuine constant five-torsion kernel occurs in the unmodified full-CM trace-fixed Gersten complex when five ramifies. Quotienting constants before taking fixed parts repairs a different complex; it does not erase that kernel. The subsequent ordinary cocycle lift does not on its own prove trace-compatible normalization. The scalar-five splitting later supplies a canonical eventual-and-T5-fixed family only for auxiliaries prime to five. The earlier two-stage trace-representative test remains valid and evaluates to zero on this restricted family. See P01, P02, P09–P11, and P14.

## 2 Coefficients and stabilizers

Finite Bianchi stabilizers require two and three to be inverted, not five. This group/coarse-space fact must be separated from the motivic trace obstruction and from the coefficient scope of any cited source. The corrected stabilizer proof is P06, SHA-256 55300f71adeef0e0d8e8dea4a578122c9f49116cf1a6b9119fb028e06da04055.

## 4 Source tame sign

The standard differential printed in the early proofs is not reciprocal to its own displayed standard tame formula. The source Sharifi–Venkatesh differential is the reciprocal convention. Qualification L2 gives the exact chain isomorphism J2 = -Id, J1 = J0 = Id. With the primitive fixed, it negates the K2 cocycle and all correction cochains. Homogeneous existence and vanishing statements survive; exact nonzero edge values and source comparisons must carry this sign. The final Cremona-edge orientation remains unproved.

## 5 Product models and mixed coordinate changes

An early broad transport phrase is restricted by L1. Product-coordinate model isomorphisms with transported shear carry the chosen primitive literally. Arbitrary mixed maps need a normalized comparison coboundary between independently chosen primitives and a regularity check before specialization. Deleting a word from the older proof would conceal this substantive qualification. Both L1 and L2 are included unchanged.

The first symbol-review draft paired the second-coordinate projection with the wrong collapsed fibers. ## 9 Final exceptional-five arithmetic scope

The accepted P32 permits arbitrary wild depth above five by a top-degree corestriction and Nakayama argument; it does not average the wild group. Its assumptions also exclude five-divisible class number and tame level primes whose norm is one modulo five. The unrestricted class-number-only claim is not made. An early illustrative assertion that seven is inert in Q(sqrt(-5)) was corrected: seven splits. The frozen examples use levels (3), (13), and (11), with the last outside the theorem.

The exact general exceptional smoothing quotient in P31 is Z/5^a with its residual cyclotomic action. At depth at least three it must not be replaced by the ordinary augmentation maximal ideal. Its exact obstruction test has not been evaluated on every actual surviving family.

## 10 Final cusp scope

P33 distinguishes a strict all-boundary criterion from a broader Gamma0 reflection criterion on the W_N-trivial target projection. W_N is not an assumed source-character action. Existing-map and unit-injectivity hypotheses remain necessary in arithmetic applications even though the topology itself covers small levels.

P34 identifies two still unevaluated five-primary Galois-invariant cusp values in the Gaussian level (2+i)^2, where an actual unaugmented integer-ring-valued open map is already available. Ambient boundary rank two does not prove either value nonzero; degree-five invariants are not identified with descent.

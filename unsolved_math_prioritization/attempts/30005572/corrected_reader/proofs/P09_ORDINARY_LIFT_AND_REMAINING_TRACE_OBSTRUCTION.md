# Ramified-five lifting: the ordinary obstruction vanishes

Authored mathematical argument, 14 October 2026. Shared PASS 2 only. This note assumes the trace-fixed primitive; it does not prove its existence. It does not modify an accepted PASS 1 result.

## Result and exact scope

Put `R = Z[1/6]`. Let `A = E_1 ×_L E_2` be a product of elliptic curves over a number field `L`, with the action of the maximal imaginary-quadratic order `O` defined over `L`. Let `c` be a nonzero integral ideal. Write

`Z_c = N(c)^2 [0] − [A[c]]`.

Let `Γ` be any subgroup of the group of `O`-linear `L`-automorphisms of `A`. Suppose a trace-fixed Gersten primitive `η` with `∂η = Z_c` has been specified. The accepted constant-quotient repair gives the cocycle

`θ̄ : Γ → Q = (K_2(L(A))⊗R / C)^tf`, where `C = K_2(L)⊗R`,

characterized by `∂θ̄(γ) = γη − η`.

**Theorem.** The ordinary connecting obstruction

`δ[θ̄] ∈ H²(Γ,C)`

is zero. In particular, there exists a genuine `K_2(L(A))⊗R`-valued cocycle `Θ` lifting this particular quotient cocycle, with exactly the prescribed residue `γη−η`. A finite cochain construction is given below. It uses integral Bézout combinations, not division by five.

The theorem holds on every CM component, without a class-number-one hypothesis, a congruence-subgroup surjectivity hypothesis, or a character projection. Its conclusion concerns **ordinary** cocycles. It does **not** assert that the values of `Θ` are trace-fixed, that the lift is canonical, or that independently chosen component lifts satisfy the needed isogeny/Hecke/CM-normalization identities. Those are genuine remaining obligations.

Two ingredients prove the theorem:

1. A scalar trace operator gives a cocycle lifting `5θ̄`, and hence `5δ[θ̄]=0`.
2. A nonzero auxiliary torsion point outside `Z_c`, with field and group orbit degrees prime to five, gives a second annihilator prime to five for the same obstruction.

The second argument is proved here without assuming that Galois acts by scalars or that `Γ` has full reduction.

## 1. Notation and the first obstruction

Use the accepted Gersten notation

`C_2 = K_2(L(A))⊗R`,

`C_1 = (⊕_D L(D)^×)⊗R`,

`C_0 = (⊕_x Z)⊗R`.

Constants inject into `C_2`, by the rational-origin retraction in PASS 1. Let `T_α=[α]_*` denote the simultaneous scalar CM traces on both factors. On constants, `T_α` is multiplication by `N(α)^2`; for a rational integer `m`, it is multiplication by `m^4`.

For an exceptional element `β`, write `M_β` for its prime-to-`β` CM multiplication monoid, and set

`Q_β = (C_2/C)^{M_β}`, `G_β = inverse image of Q_β in C_2`.

Enlarge `β` to be divisible by `5`. The accepted proof constructs `θ̄` with values in one `Q_β`: the original primitive `η` is fixed by `M_β`, and the residue-lifting operator in that proof preserves this same fixed module. The scalar traces commute with `Γ`.

The still-unsettled earlier question is whether `C_2^tf → Q` is surjective. For a fixed `β`, an element `q∈Q_β` and an ordinary representative `x∈C_2` give the explicit norm defect

`a_q(α) = (T_α−1)x ∈ C`.

It satisfies

`a_q(αα′)=N(α)^2 a_q(α′)+a_q(α)`.

Changing `x` by a constant `b` changes this defect by `(N(α)^2−1)b`. Thus its monoid-cohomology class vanishes exactly when `q` has an `M_β`-fixed representative. Eventual trace invariance permits enlarging `β`. The ordinary cocycle theorem below does not prove that these individual monoid classes vanish.

## 2. A genuine fivefold trace-fixed lift

Choose positive rational integers `m` prime to a positive integer containing every rational prime below `β`. As established in the accepted constant-kernel proof, the ideal generated by all `m^4−1` in `R` is exactly `(5)`: all admissible `m` are prime to five, primes at least seven can be excluded from the gcd by CRT, and CRT modulo twenty-five makes its five-adic valuation exactly one.

Consequently there are finitely many admissible `m_j` and `b_j∈R` with

`Σ_j b_j (1−m_j^4)=5`.

Define the commuting trace operator

`B_β = Σ_j b_j (T_{m_j}−m_j^4)`.

It kills `C` and has augmentation `5`. Therefore

`S_β : Q_β → C_2`, `S_β(q)=B_β x`

is independent of the ordinary representative `x` of `q`. For `α∈M_β`, the element `(T_α−1)x` is constant, so

`(T_α−1)S_β(q)=B_β(T_α−1)x=0`.

Thus `S_β(q)` is trace-fixed and projects to `5q`. The map `S_β` is `R`-linear and `Γ`-equivariant. In particular,

`S_β∘θ̄`

is an actual trace-fixed cocycle lifting `5θ̄`, with residue `5(γη−η)`.

For example, if `2` is admissible, one can take

`S_β(q)=(16x−T_2x)/3`.

If `3` is admissible, one can take `(81x−T_3x)/16`. These are identities over `R`; neither divides by five.

It follows that the cokernel of invariant representatives is killed by five and, by the connecting homomorphism,

`5δ[θ̄]=0`.

Different choices of `B_β` may change `S_β(q)` by a trace-fixed constant, hence by an element of `C[5]` in the ramified-five case. One must not call this fivefold lift canonical. There is a canonical twenty-fivefold lift: choose any trace-fixed lift `z` of `5q` and set `σ(q)=5z`. Its independence follows because any two such `z` differ by `C[5]`. It is additive, natural under maps preserving constants and the trace-fixed filtration, and projects to `25q`.

## 3. Local vanishing at a fixed point outside the cycle

The following local lemma is the key geometric input.

**Local lemma.** Let `L′/L` be finite, let `T∈A(L′)` have coefficient zero in the base-changed cycle `Z_c`, and let `H⊂Γ` fix `T`. The restriction and base change of `δ[θ̄]` is zero in

`H²(H,K_2(L′)⊗R)`.

**Proof.** Choose ordinary lifts `x_γ∈C_2` of each `θ̄(γ)`, taking `x_1=0`. Their group coboundary

`k(γ,δ)=γx_δ−x_{γδ}+x_γ`

is constant, since `θ̄` is a cocycle. It is a constant-valued two-cocycle representing `δ[θ̄]`.

Let `B=O_{A_{L′},T}`. It is a regular local ring of dimension two, essentially smooth over the characteristic-zero field `L′`, with residue field `L′`. Use the exact Gersten sequence for `K_2`:

`0 → K_2(B) → K_2(Frac B) → ⊕_{height(p)=1} K_1(k(p)) → K_0(L′) → 0`.

Tensoring by `R` preserves exactness. Restrict the base-changed `η` to this local complex. Its final residue is zero, because the coefficient of `T` in `Z_c` is zero. Hence there is an `a∈K_2(L′(A))⊗R` with

`∂a = η_B`.

For `h∈H`, put

`z_h = x_h|_{L′(A)} − (ha−a)`.

Its local Gersten residues vanish, so `z_h∈K_2(B)⊗R`. Residue-field pullback `e_T^*:K_2(B)→K_2(L′)` is a canonical homomorphism and is `H`-equivariant. Define

`r(h)=e_T^*(z_h)`.

Since `d²a=0`, taking a group coboundary gives

`dr = res_{L′/L}(k|_H)`.

This explicitly trivializes the restricted obstruction. No tangential specialization, division, or neglect of other points is involved. The entire global complex was restricted to a local ring; cycles outside that local ring map to zero, and evaluation is the identity on constants. ∎

## 4. A suitable torsion point always exists

Choose a prime ideal `q` of `O`, prime to `c`, such that

`n=Nq≡2 or 3 (mod 5)`.

There are infinitely many such choices. Indeed, `F` is imaginary quadratic, so

`F∩Q(ζ_5)=Q`,

because the unique quadratic subfield of `Q(ζ_5)` is the real field `Q(√5)`. Chebotarev in `FQ(ζ_5)/Q` supplies rational primes `ℓ` which split in `F` and satisfy `ℓ≡2 mod5` (or `3 mod5`). Either prime `q|ℓ` has norm `ℓ`. Finitely many prescribed primes can be excluded.

Over an algebraic closure,

`A[q] ≅ (O/q)^2`

as an `O/q`-module. This follows factor by factor from the full-CM lattice description: for any invertible `O`-lattice defining one of the elliptic curves, its `q`-torsion is one-dimensional over `O/q`.

Let `L′=L(A[q])`. Because the full CM actions are defined over `L`, Galois acts `O/q`-linearly, so

`Gal(L′/L) ↪ GL_2(O/q)`.

The order of the latter group is

`n(n−1)^2(n+1)`,

which is prime to five for the specified congruences. Thus `e=[L′:L]` is prime to five. No scalar Galois-action assertion is needed.

Choose any nonzero `T∈A[q](L′)` and set `H=Γ_T`. The action of `Γ` on `A[q]` also factors through a subgroup of `GL_2(O/q)`. Therefore

`d=[Γ:H]`

divides the order of that subgroup, and is prime to five. No full-reduction assertion for `Γ` is needed. Since `q` is prime to `c`, the intersection of `A[q]` and `A[c]` is just zero. Hence `T` is outside the support of `Z_c`.

The local lemma applies. Field corestriction followed by group corestriction gives

`ed·δ[θ̄]=0`.

Here field restriction followed by transfer on constants is multiplication by `e`; group restriction followed by corestriction on cohomology is multiplication by `d`. These are two distinct transfers, both for the same original coefficient group after the field transfer.

Since `gcd(ed,5)=1` and `5δ[θ̄]=0`, integer Bézout proves `δ[θ̄]=0`. Neither transfer degree is inverted in `R`.

## 5. Explicit ordinary cocycle correction

The proof can be implemented entirely at cochain level. This also makes clear that existence has not been inferred by arbitrarily dividing a finite `K_2` class.

Keep the ordinary lifts `x`, their constant defect `k=dx`, and the trace-fixed fivefold lift `S_βθ̄`. Define the constant-valued one-cochain

`u=5x−S_βθ̄`.

Then `du=5k`.

Use the torsion point of §4 and the cochain `r:H→K_2(L′)⊗R` of §3. Let

`r_0=Norm_{L′/L} r`.

It satisfies `dr_0=e·k|_H`.

Choose representatives `T_0` for the right cosets `H\Γ`. For `t∈T_0` and `γ∈Γ`, write

`tγ=h(t,γ)t′`, where `h(t,γ)∈H`, `t′∈T_0`.

For a one-cochain `f` on `H` with trivial coefficients define

`Tr(f)(γ)=Σ_{t∈T_0} f(h(t,γ))`.

The usual transfer of two-cochains uses the corresponding successive `h` values. For the normalized two-cocycle `k`, set

`A_k(γ)=Σ_{t∈T_0} [k(t,γ)−k(h(t,γ),t′)]`.

The two-cocycle identity, applied to `tγ=h(t,γ)t′`, gives the explicit chain-homotopy formula

`Tr(k|_H)=d·k−d_group A_k`.

The symbol `d` before `k` here is the integer index; `d_group` denotes the cochain differential. Consequently the one-cochain

`v=Tr(r_0)+e A_k`

satisfies

`d_group v=ed·k`.

Choose integers `a,b` with `5a+ed·b=1`. Then

`Θ=x−a u−b v`

is a genuine cocycle, lifts `θ̄` pointwise, and has the exact prescribed Gersten residue. Equivalently it is the Bézout combination of the fivefold cocycle `S_βθ̄` and the `ed`-fold cocycle `ed·x−v`.

The arbitrary choices here are ordinary representatives, one local Gersten primitive, a torsion point, and coset representatives. They are legitimate choices in a construction, but independence of them in the desired constant-sensitive target has not been proved. Any two resulting lifts of the fixed quotient cocycle differ by a constant-valued one-cocycle. For lifts of the cohomology class, the ambiguity is the usual image of `H¹(Γ,C)`.

## 6. Actual test field and all components

For `F=Q(√−5)` and `c` prime to the prime

`q=(2,1+√−5)`,

one can take this `q`, whose norm is `2`. Then `GL_2(F_2)` has order six. The auxiliary torsion field degree divides six, and the point-stabilizer index is also prime to five. This gives an actual ramified-five CM instance of the vanishing theorem, not merely an abstract module example.

If `c` meets this prime, choose a prime above `3` of norm three when available outside `c`, or use the Chebotarev choice in §4. There is no exclusion on the original auxiliary ideal.

For the full source tuple `E_{i_1}×E_{i_2}`, the argument applies to every pair. A common prime `q` may be used for the finite tuple. One may also adjoin all of their `q`-torsion simultaneously: the resulting Galois group embeds in a finite product of groups of order prime to five, so its degree remains prime to five. Thus the construction does not discard a class-group component. It does not by itself make the resulting choices compatible with isogenies between these components.

## 7. Matching the known cocycle after inverting five

There is a useful further normalization which still does not settle trace invariance over `R`.

The number-field group `C=K_2(L)⊗R` is torsion, by the localization sequence with finite tame kernel and finite residue multiplicative groups. It has the canonical direct sum decomposition into its five-primary subgroup and its prime-to-five subgroup. Multiplication by five is an automorphism of the latter.

For an ordinary lift `Θ` from §5, the difference

`a_Θ=5Θ−S_βθ̄`

is a constant-valued cocycle. Project it onto the prime-to-five summand of `C`, and let `b_Θ` be its unique preimage under multiplication by five **on that summand**. Replace `Θ` by `Θ−b_Θ`.

Now `5Θ−S_βθ̄` is five-primary. After extending coefficients to `Z[1/30]`, the new `Θ` therefore agrees with the unique trace-fixed cocycle having the specified residue. For the primitive used in LSSW §3.4, this is the geometric cocycle constructed there.

This is not arbitrary division in the five-primary group. It uses a canonical automorphism on the prime-to-five summand and leaves the five-primary ambiguity intact. The normalized lifts can still differ by five-primary constant cocycles. No integral ray-field descent or arithmetic integrality follows from this comparison alone.

## 8. The remaining obstruction and what was not proved

The ordinary `H²(Γ,C)` obstruction is now eliminated for the actual auxiliary CM cycles under the primitive hypothesis. The earlier invariant-representative problem is not eliminated.

In particular, for the ordinary lift `Θ`,

`(T_α−1)Θ(γ) ∈ C`

may be nonzero. Removing these defects requires one constant-valued one-cocycle that solves the full simultaneous norm equations, possibly after enlarging the exceptional set. The torsion-point proof uses automorphisms fixing a point; a trace norm is a finite pushforward with all its preimages. The local-point argument cannot simply be reused for that monoid of norms.

Still unproved:

- Surjectivity of `C_2^tf → Q` for the ramified-five fields, or even for every value of this auxiliary quotient cocycle.
- A canonical lift compatible with every component-changing isogeny, theta normalization, CM/Galois descent, and Hecke operator.
- A residue primitive `η` over exactly `R` in all required cases; this belongs to the separate PASS 2 lane.
- The original relative theta-symbol formula, auxiliary removal, integral absolute target, compactification, and the bad-prime identities.

The nonzero trace-fixed constants accepted in PASS 1 remain nonzero. This theorem neither disproves nor bypasses that result: it splits a particular ordinary group-cohomology obstruction by additional CM torsion geometry. It supplies no nonzero counterexample to the original map. LSSW work over `Z[1/30]`; no error in their statements is asserted.

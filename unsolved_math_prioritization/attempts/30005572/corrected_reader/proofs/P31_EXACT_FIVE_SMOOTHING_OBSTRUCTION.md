# Exact ramified-five smoothing depth and the actual remaining obstruction

Authored proof, 14 October 2026. Shared final PASS 5/5, row 1777 / 30005572. All classes below are those of the accepted actual arithmetic family. The obstruction is bounded and characterized, not asserted nonzero.

## 1. Exact smoothing ideal at every five-adic depth

Let five ramify in the imaginary quadratic field F, with (5)=p^2. Let N be a proper unit-injective level, n=v_p(N), L=F(N), and Q=Q5 x Q' its ray group. Let chi:Q' -> {+1,-1} be the character of F(sqrt(5))/F, extended trivially on Q5. Write

A=Z5[Q5],  a=max(1,ceil(n/2)),

and let J be the ideal of A generated by

s_c=(Nc)^2-chi(R_N(c)') R_N(c)_5

for all integral auxiliary ideals c prime to 5N. This is precisely the smoothing action on the exceptional block, using the accepted Artin convention.

**Theorem.** The squared five-adic cyclotomic character modulo 5^a factors through Q. After removing its Teichmuller character chi, it gives a character

rho_a:Q5 -> (Z/5^a)^x.

There is a canonical isomorphism

A/J is isomorphic to Z/5^a,

given by g -> rho_a(g). In particular J is generated by 5^a and the elements g_j-rhohat_a(g_j), where the g_j generate Q5 and rhohat_a(g_j) is any Z5 lift of rho_a(g_j).

At n=0,1,2 this recovers J=(5,I_Q5). At n=3,4 the quotient is Z/25, and the Q5 action on it need not be trivial. Thus at deeper five-level one must retain the cyclotomic action rather than replace J by the ordinary augmentation maximal ideal.

## 2. Local norm calculation and a principal smoothing factor

At the ramified quadratic completion K=F_p, the extension K/Q5 is tamely ramified of degree two. For n>=1,

Norm_{K/Q5}(1+p^n) = 1+5^{ceil(n/2)} Z5.                    (2.1)

One elementary verification uses the logarithm. Since v5(x)>=1/2>1/(5-1) for x in p, log and exp identify the principal unit filtration with the additive ideal filtration and commute with norm/trace. To check the trace explicitly, write K=Q5(pi) with pi^2=5u for u in Z5^x. Then O_K=Z5[pi], trace(a+b pi)=2a, p^(2k)=(5^k), and p^(2k+1)=(5^(k+1),5^k pi). Consequently

Tr_{K/Q5}(p^n)=5^{floor((n+1)/2)} Z5.

This exponent equals ceil(n/2), proving (2.1). Squaring is an automorphism of each group 1+5^r Z5, so it does not change that exponent.

For n=0, the residue norm of a local unit is its square in F5^x, and its squared norm is consequently one modulo five. Its principal-unit part still realizes elements of valuation exactly one. Thus the smallest possible valuation of Norm(alpha)^2-1 for a local unit alpha congruent to one modulo p^n (with no congruence at n=0) is precisely a=max(1,ceil(n/2)).

The Chinese remainder theorem combines such a local residue to sufficiently high five-adic precision with alpha congruent to one modulo the prime-to-five part of N. Choose an integral alpha satisfying these conditions and prime to five; it is then prime to N. It may also avoid any prescribed finite set of extra primes. Its principal ray ideal c0=(alpha) has

R_N(c0)=1,  s_c0=Norm_{F/Q}(alpha)^2-1=5^a u0,

with u0 in Z5^x. In particular 5^a belongs to J, without inverting five or invoking a fractional auxiliary ideal.

Every principal ray element has squared norm one modulo 5^a by the same local norm calculation, and some has squared norm not one modulo 5^{a+1}. Thus norm-squared modulo 5^a factors through Q, whereas modulo 5^{a+1} it does not. The Artin/norm correspondence identifies it with the squared cyclotomic character. Its prime-to-five part is chi, giving rho_a.

## 3. The entire quotient, not just its support

The map A -> Z/5^a, g -> rho_a(g), kills every s_c, hence induces a surjection A/J -> Z/5^a.

Conversely choose, for each generator g_j of Q5, an integral auxiliary ideal c_j prime to 5N with ray class (g_j,1). Such representatives exist by Chebotarev and avoidance of a finite set of primes. The relation s_cj=0 makes

g_j=(Nc_j)^2

in A/J. Every generator of A/J is therefore a scalar; the scalar map Z5 -> A/J is surjective. The principal ideal c0 of Section 2 forces 5^a=0. Hence A/J is a quotient of Z/5^a. Together with the first surjection this proves the stated isomorphism and the generator description of J.

This proof shows exactly where the exponent comes from. It is not inferred solely from the mod-five exceptional character or from the class number.

## 4. A sharp exponent bound on the actual existence obstruction

Fix a source component, and put

M=H^1(Gamma0(N)_i, (K2(O'(N)) tensor Z5)^(chi)).

Let a_c=[z_{c,i,N}]^(chi) denote the **actual accepted auxiliary classes**. Their integral exchange relation says

s_d a_c=s_c a_d.

For any A-module M write E(M) for the families satisfying these exchange relations, and delta(u)=(s_c u)_c. Auxiliary removal exists precisely when the class of (a_c) in E(M)/delta(M) is zero.

Choose c0 and u0 as in Section 2, and set v=u0^(-1) a_c0. Exchange gives the actual identities

s_c v=5^a a_c for every c.                                  (4.1)

Therefore

**5^a annihilates the obstruction of the actual family in E(M)/delta(M).**

For every level prime to five, a=1: the entire remaining existence obstruction is killed by five, not merely supported at the exceptional maximal ideal. This is not a proof that it vanishes.

The following two-stage criterion is exact and involves no arbitrary division in K2:

1. Compute v modulo 5^a M. If it is nonzero, removal is impossible for this actual family.
2. If it is zero, choose u with 5^a u=v, and put r_c=a_c-s_c u. Equation (4.1) proves r_c belongs to T=M[5^a]. The family r lies in E(T). Its class in E(T)/delta(T) is independent of the choice of u. Removal exists if and only if this class is zero.

Indeed another solution of the first equation differs from u by an element of T, which changes r by delta(T). Vanishing of the second class supplies the correction in T that solves every smoothing equation. Conversely an unaugmented class solves both tests.

When a solution exists, its exact ambiguity is

M[J] = {m: 5^a m=0 and g_j m=rhohat_a(g_j)m for all j}.         (4.2)

For n=0,1,2 this is M[5]^{Q5}. For n>=3 it can have higher five-power exponent with a nontrivial cyclotomic Q5 action. Neither the first-stage division nor the second-stage compatibility is discharged by the exchange relation alone. The class v is an actual multiple constructed from the auxiliary system; no independence of v in M from c0 is claimed. The combined vanishing criterion and the existence obstruction are independent of this presentation.

## 5. Why adding a five-divisible auxiliary is not a free solution

When (N,5)=1, a hypothetical auxiliary at a prime over five would have a five-adic unit smoothing factor. Constructing it, with the needed exchange relation, would indeed remove the exceptional block. This possibility was examined rather than silently excluded.

It cannot be obtained merely by reusing the accepted scalar-five normalization. For an ideal d with O !=d dividing (5), the geometric degree-zero cycle is

e_d=(Nd)^2[0]-[A[d]].

Since multiplication by five sends every d-torsion point to zero,

[5]_* e_d=0,

whereas e_d is nonzero. A primitive eta_d with boundary e_d therefore cannot satisfy [5]_*eta_d=eta_d. The hypothesis that enabled the canonical PASS 3 splitting is actually absent.

Replacing the desired eigenvalue one by zero does not repair uniqueness: on eventual trace-fixed constant five-torsion, [5]_* acts as 625=0. Thus the same nonzero constant ambiguity is compatible with that proposed zero-eigenvalue condition. This is an exact failure of that normalization route, not a proof that no other five-divisible auxiliary construction exists.

The all-prime kernel-incidence Hecke theorem uses exact norms of the existing family and does not manufacture the missing auxiliary. Its pull-push identities alone therefore cannot be substituted for the unproved exchange relation with d.

## 6. What the final attempt establishes and leaves open

The companion RAMIFIED_FIVE_TAME_RAY_REMOVAL.md proves actual exceptional-family vanishing and unique removal under an explicit restricted arithmetic hypothesis. That theorem is separate from this ideal calculation.

Outside that range, this note computes the exact smoothing quotient, its ramification depth, the exponent bound on the actual obstruction, the exact ambiguity, and a two-stage test. No nonzero first or second obstruction has been computed for a surviving actual auxiliary family, including F=Q(sqrt(-5)), N=(11). Its nonzero ambient residue module is still not a counterexample.

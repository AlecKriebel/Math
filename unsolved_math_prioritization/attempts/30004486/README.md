## Accepted result

Fix κ>0 on the unit round sphere, degree Q=0, and the credited Melcher–Sakellaris interval Iκ=(4π,4π+εκ). Over every compact subinterval, all constrained minimizers form a strongly H¹ compact family. The value e(j) is locally semiconcave and locally Lipschitz. Every minimizer has a unique continuous axial multiplier ω, and uniformly quadratic upper supports give

- e′+(j)=min over M(j) of ω
- e′−(j)=max over M(j) of ω

The extrema are attained. At differentiability points every minimizing multiplier equals e′(j). For a smooth minimizing profile and the declared Landau–Lifshitz convention, the corresponding jointly rotating trajectory is static exactly when ω=0.

The full weak-H¹ rank argument includes transport equivariance and polar endpoint limits. The exact momentum corrections preserve degree. BCL applies to arbitrary bounded H¹ sequences converging almost everywhere; only the signed Jacobian defect is quantized. Strong compactness follows later from minimality and energy convergence, not from absence of Jacobian atoms alone.

## What remains open

No argument excludes zero frequency, establishes a slope sign, monotonicity, global concavity, a nonzero frequency lower bound, a full energy law or endpoint asymptotics. The range is only Iκ, with fixed κ. Constants are local to compact subintervals and are not uniform at either endpoint or as κ varies. No uniqueness or smoothness of every weak minimizer is claimed. At a corner, zero between the extreme slopes need not be an attained multiplier.

# Primary-source scope, 2026-10-05 UTC

## Problem statement

Walter K. Hayman and Eleanor F. Lingham, *Research Problems in Function
Theory (New Edition)*, arXiv:1809.07200v2, 21 September 2018.

- https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200v2
- https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.07200v2
- Problem and Update 2.19: printed p. 30, PDF page index 30.

The complete entry was inspected in extracted text and in a rendered image.
It asks for a sharp complex-point modulus under unequal half-axis caps.
The displayed equal-cap estimate uses upper-half-plane y; the reconstruction
uses |y| for the symmetric whole-plane version. The update credits a
subharmonic analogue to Gol'dberg--Levin and the real-axis sharp bound
to Eremenko. The 2019 Springer edition's publisher record was checked,
but its full text was not inspected:
https://link.springer.com/book/10.1007/978-3-030-25165-9.

## Real-axis result and historical nonreal status

A. Eremenko, *Progress in Entire and Meromorphic Functions*, 15 May 2003.

- https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/dvi/progr.pdf
- Entry 2.19, printed p. 4; reference [15], printed p. 8.

The PDF was downloaded and the entry inspected in extracted text and a
rendered image. It explicitly distinguishes the solved real-axis question
from the then-unresolved complex-point question with unequal caps. This
is historical status evidence, not an exhaustive 2026 literature search.

A. E. Eremenko, *Entire functions bounded on the real axis*, Dokl. Akad.
Nauk SSSR 300(3) (1988), 544--546; English translation, Soviet Math.
Dokl. 37(3) (1988), 693--695.

- https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan/v300/i3/p544
- https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=dan&option_lang=eng&paperid=7615&what=fullt

The publisher record and the extracted text of all three Russian pages
were inspected. The opening and Theorem 1 concern the real-axis extremal
envelope; p. 545 also states classical Bernstein. Raw PDF download returned
HTTP 403, and page-image retrieval failed. The extracted formulas have OCR
defects, so this reconstruction neither reproduces the conformal-map
formula nor claims a complete independent verification of that theorem.
The English translation was not retrieved. The result is credited and
kept separate from the nonreal target.

## Subharmonic predecessor

A. A. Gol'dberg and B. Ya. Levin, *Entire functions which are bounded on
the real axis*, Dokl. Akad. Nauk SSSR 157 (1964), 19--21.

- https://www.mathnet.ru/eng/dan29782

The citation is corroborated by Hayman--Lingham reference [321] and the
1988 paper's reference [2]. The supplied publisher page and a volume/page
route returned retrieval errors in this reconstruction. No primary text
or proof from this paper was inspected. Accordingly, no identification
of our elementary step bound with their exact subharmonic result is made.

## Credit and search limits

Phragmen--Lindelof, Bernstein, Montel, the identity theorem, elementary
Poisson theory, the maximum modulus principle, the sinc integral, and
simultaneous recurrence are classical. The packet gives its own proofs
of the deductions and of the specific sinc/recurrence constructions.
The sources support the stated provenance and historical scope; they do
not establish novelty of the reconstructed deductions or an exhaustive
current open-status claim. The package's unsolved label reports its own
failure to determine the general sharp nonreal bound.

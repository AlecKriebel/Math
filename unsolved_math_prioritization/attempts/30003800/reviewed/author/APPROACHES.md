# Five substantive approaches

Each approach concerns problem 30003800. The inherited corpus/report and GitHub
eligibility checks preceded these approaches and are not counted as proof work.

1. **Apply the published vanishing theorems at their actual scope.** Read the
   primary OWR question and coefficients, then compare the displayed hypotheses
   of Merkurjev's general/type-A results and Baek's B/C/D and exceptional results.
   Odd-primary vanishing and homogeneous/all-exceptional vanishing are available;
   arbitrary B+C+D or classical-exceptional couplings are not covered by those
   displayed hypotheses. No complete prior resolution was verified.
2. **Localize the central isogeny at two.** Replace the central kernel by its
   2-primary subgroup using Merkurjev's prime-to-prime isogeny theorem. Prove
   that all odd-center simple factors then split off and contribute zero. The
   remaining obstruction is confined to A_(2r-1), B, C, D and E7 with a genuine
   central 2-primary quotient. This proves a reduction, not vanishing.
3. **Factor the actual central subgroup.** Prove the direct-product and central
   partition criteria. They settle blocks covered by known families and detect
   why merely disconnected Dynkin diagrams or full pairwise projections are
   inadequate. A parity subgroup on three coordinates supplies an exact
   structural diagnostic. Cross-block central subgroups remain.
4. **Detect E7 via a classical subgroup.** Prove a common-central-kernel torsor
   lifting lemma and use Petrov's odd-degree D6+A1 construction without removing
   mixed central identifications. This reduces universal vanishing to arbitrary
   mixed classical groups. A padding argument plus Baek's mixed theorem proves
   all central quotients of E7^a x Spin_12^b x SL_2^c, including unequal
   multiplicities, and the odd-center extensions described in the report.
5. **Attempt independent-factor residue detection on B3+C3.** For
   (Spin_7 x Sp_6)/diag(mu_2), compute the exact central lifting condition and
   produce an explicit pair with nonzero quaternion obstruction. Independent
   factor tests cannot be passed through the coupled quotient. An even-degree
   splitting field loses the odd-degree injectivity argument. This diagnosis
   neither constructs an unramified class nor proves vanishing; the missing
   compatible degree-three invariant/residue analysis is left explicit.

Terminal disposition: bounded partial result; full problem unsolved in this
investigation; 5/5 approaches. No further proof approach is represented as

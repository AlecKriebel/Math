## Accepted scope and remaining gap

Over an algebraically closed field of characteristic zero, for degree-three unramified cohomology with Q/Z(2) coefficients:

- The central-isogeny reduction retains arbitrary coupled central 2-primary kernels and removes the odd-center factors.
- The common-kernel torsor-lifting and odd-degree detection arguments reduce universal vanishing to arbitrary mixed classical groups while preserving the central identifications.
- Every central quotient of E7^a x Spin_12^b x SL_2^c has vanishing unramified cohomology for arbitrary nonnegative a, b, c, including zero and unequal multiplicities. The stated odd-center additions are allowed. The deductions credit Petrov, Baek, and Merkurjev.
- The B3+C3 quaternion example is a degree-two lifting obstruction. It does not exhibit a nonzero degree-three unramified class.

General coupled classical central 2-primary quotients and positive characteristic remain unresolved in this investigation. There is no full-solution, counterexample, novelty, worldwide-openness, human-peer-review, or formal-verification claim.

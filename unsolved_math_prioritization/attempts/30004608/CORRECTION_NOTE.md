# Correction to the optional plus one sharpening

The corrected proof retains the inclusive candidate condition 1 <= Delta(r) <= r-1. It may be sharpened to 2 <= Delta(r) <= r-1 only when the arrangement is independently known not to be nearly free, equivalently d3>d2.

The original candidate used the phrase “strict plus-one generatedness” for this extra hypothesis. That phrase is insufficient under Abe’s terminology: Definition 1.1(2) defines strictness by a nonzero linear level coefficient, and Proposition 5.1 proves strictness for every plus-one generated central three-arrangement. Nearly free arrangements therefore remain strict while satisfying d3=d2 and nu=1. See [Abe, Plus-one generated and next to free arrangements of hyperplanes](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1808.04697v3), PDF pp. 2 and 20.

This correction does not change the main sufficient criterion or its proof. The corrected proof also states the field, essentiality, grading, degree-range, arithmetic-cost, and novelty limits explicitly. The 18-line example is already Malara–Pokora–Tutaj-Gasinska Example 5.10; Example 5.12 at n=7 shows that the sufficient test is not necessary. The packet claims a credited sufficient consequence of published theorems under independently established plus-one generatedness. It makes no full-classification or novelty claim.

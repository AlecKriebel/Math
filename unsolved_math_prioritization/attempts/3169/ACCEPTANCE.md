## Exact theorem

Let B={0,1,2,3} induce K4, and let y_i=7−i be four independent additional vertices, with y_i adjacent precisely to B\{i}. The resulting finite simple 3-connected graph H has eight vertices and eighteen edges. For every vector of strictly positive real edge lengths, at least one simple cycle is geodesic and nonperipheral. Geodesic means that every pair of cycle vertices has a shortest ambient path along a cycle arc; equal-length alternatives are allowed. Peripheral means induced and nonseparating. In this finite positive-length realization, vertex-pair and all-points geodesicity are equivalent.

# Primary-source review, 2026-10-05 UTC

## Original question

The [2014 publisher report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46499), printed p.390, explicitly uses ordinary series and an open half-plane of convergence. Its reference to Montgomery–Vorhauer Problem 24 records an earlier question; that older collection was not independently inspected. A [2017 restatement](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46710), printed p.3067, Problem 6, mentions the reciprocal-zeta conditional route. The [2023 report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/47016?nt=1), printed p.1257, Question 6, repeats the convergence issue and references the then-existing Hilberdink–Saias preprint. It predates the withdrawal.

## Claimed resolution that cannot be used

Hilberdink and Saias, [arXiv:1812.11880](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.11880), originally submitted in 2018, claimed a family of ordinary examples using subsets of rational primes. The first-version PDF was inspected at its definitions, Theorem 1, construction outline, convergence section, and final proof. It is not a valid dependency: version 2, posted 26 April 2024, withdraws the paper, stating that the main proof contains an error that cannot be repaired. The landing page still displays the old success abstract, so the abstract alone is misleading as a status check. The notice does not specify an erroneous lemma, and this investigation does not assign one or claim to have repaired the argument.

## A valid nearby result, with a different class of series

Broucke and Vindas, [arXiv:2102.08478v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.08478v2), [Math. Z. 307, article 62 (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00209-024-03526-4), distinguish the ordinary question from their Proposition 1.4. They construct a general Dirichlet series using Beurling generalized integers, with coefficients in {-1,0,1}, convergence abscissa 1/2, and one zero at 1 in that convergence half-plane. Theorem 3.1 supplies generalized-integer counting and generalized Möbius estimates; Section 3 identifies the reciprocal generalized zeta function and its simple zero. The theorem, proof section, and the explicit ordinary/general distinction were read; p.3 was also visually inspected. Their substantial random approximation construction is a credited published input, not re-proved here. Their paper does not settle the original ordinary-series target. It supports Approach 5's conditional-on-that-theorem rounding deduction.

## Prescribed analytic divisors do not settle convergence

Seip, [arXiv:1812.11729v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1812.11729v2), [J. Anal. Math. 141 (2020), 331–381](https://doi.org/10.1007/s11854-020-0126-3), constructs Helson zeta functions with prescribed zeros and poles. Its definition of sigma(chi) on p.2 measures meromorphic continuation. Theorem 1.1 and the following main statements were read. They do not provide the needed bound on the ordinary series' convergence abscissa for the singleton choice discussed in Approach 4.

Bochkov and Romanov, [arXiv:2106.15949v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.15949v1), [J. Funct. Anal. 282 (2022), 109398](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jfa.2022.109398), also prescribe divisors of continuations. The introduction and theorem statement were inspected, not its complete proof. Bochkov's [2023 finite-value refinement](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.12847) was inspected at the publisher abstract only. Its restricted coefficient values do not change the distinction between convergence and continuation.

## Search scope and conclusions

Queries included the exact title, the original identifier, Balazard with single/unique/one zero and ordinary Dirichlet series, the withdrawn arXiv identifier, current-year variants, and the cited Helson/Beurling constructions. A current author's publication list and publisher/arXiv version pages were checked. Search discovery also surfaced informal discussions and stale aggregators; these were not used as mathematical authorities. A textbook search snippet was not treated as inspected full text because the linked PDF returned 404.

No valid complete ordinary-series resolution was established in these inspected sources. This supports reporting this attempt as unresolved; it does not certify global openness, completeness of the bibliography, or historical novelty of the elementary deductions. PDFs, extracts, screenshots, and raw website content are excluded from the publication payload.

# Five attempted approaches

The target is KP-2.33, a finitely generated torsion-free obstruction to *every faithful homeomorphism action* on a prescribed closed surface. Exactly five substantive approach families were pursued. Retrieving sources and checking prior work are not counted as proof approaches.

1. **Construct surface actions from orderability.** Built a faithful action of every countable left-orderable group on a dense rational order, completed it to the real line, and extended it by an explicit diamond-disk formula. This rigorously removes all left-orderable groups from the candidate pool. It gives no construction for all torsion-free groups.
2. **Try non-orderability as the obstruction.** Checked Hyde's finitely generated non-left-orderable disk subgroup and the periodic disk theorem. Proved the bounded-displacement radial-extension mechanism and its failure for an unbounded polar twist. A credited finitely generated torsion-free non-left-orderable example acts on every surface; the proposed converse is false.
3. **Test a torsion-free higher-rank lattice.** Verified Γ(3) in SL(4,Z), including an elementary prime-order contradiction. Imported the exact BFH C² finite-index theorem. Tried passage to topological rigidity through full-group induction; computed index 12,130,560 and exhibited the connectedness/Euler-residue failures in the Ye route. The unrestricted C⁰ candidate remains unresolved.
4. **Test Le Roux's free-planar obstruction.** Reconstructed the group G₂ as an iterated semidirect product of a countably generated free group by two infinite cyclic groups. Established left-orderability and hence faithful disk-supported actions on every surface, although Le Roux excludes free orientation-preserving planar actions. This specific candidate fails to solve the target.
5. **Try modern local-action rigidity.** Checked the 2026 Koberda–Lodha generic-group theorem and the locally approximating rigidity hypotheses. Proved that a faithful locally moving torsion-free surface action forces Zⁿ for every n. The cyclic group already separates this strengthened category from arbitrary faithful surface actions. No argument removes local moving or supplies the required finitely generated witness.

**Final disposition: unsolved, 5/5.** No sixth approach, general solution, counterexample to the original question, or novelty claim. Complete proofs and the unresolved steps are in PROOF.md. Independent review remains pending at this author freeze.

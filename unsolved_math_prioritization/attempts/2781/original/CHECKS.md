# Author checks and limitations

## Mathematical checks

This is a data-only proof packet, without executable code or a formal proof certificate. These are author checks, not an independent audit.

- **Exact quantifiers:** the target concerns arbitrary faithful C⁰ actions; free, proper, smooth, measure-preserving, locally moving and locally approximating are additional hypotheses. No orientability restriction is inserted into the target.
- **Proposition 1:** the lexicographic order is countable, dense and endpoint-free; left multiplication is faithful and increasing. Completion respects composition by equality on Q. In the disk formula, the bound |u'|≤1−|t| handles the two otherwise singular tips. The equator detects faithfulness, and boundary fixation permits extension by identity.
- **Proposition 2:** non-orderability is imported from Hyde, not inferred from torsion. Torsion-freeness is separately supplied by Constantin–Kolev Proposition 3.2. The radial-extension proof uses bounded displacement of each map; no uniform bound over the whole group is required. The polar-twist control shows why arbitrary plane actions do not automatically extend to the radial boundary.
- **Proposition 3:** reduction modulo 3 first forces the hypothetical prime order to equal 3. The second expansion has exponents k and 2k−1, both at least one. Infinitude is witnessed by I+3kE₁₂, and finite generation uses finite index. The BFH theorem directly includes finite-index subgroups and dimension 2≤4−2. Its C² hypothesis is not removed. The index calculation is 80·78·72·54/2=12,130,560; induction introduces disconnectedness and Euler characteristic divisible by 6.
- **Proposition 4:** the automorphism J inverts *free generators*, not arbitrary words in place. It has order two and commutes with the index shift. The automorphism θ respects b cᵢ b⁻¹=cᵢ⁻¹ and has the explicitly described inverse. Both homomorphisms between the presented group and the iterated semidirect product are inverse on a,b,c₀. Left-order extension requires no invariant order under conjugation.
- **Proposition 5:** disjoint supported homeomorphisms commute; restricting a relation to each disk gives independence of all powers. Both faithfulness and torsion-freeness are used. This establishes only a locally-moving obstruction. The faithful cyclic disk action is a control against silently dropping that hypothesis.

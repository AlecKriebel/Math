## Accepted mathematical content

1. Under the expressly stated macroscopic uniform-profile hypothesis (U), every mixed quartic integral ∫|G|^(4−j)|R|^j, j=1,2,3, is o(L). The proof separates a fixed scaled major arc from a controlled tail; it does not infer microscopic fourth-order convergence from weak convergence.
2. The residual energy is (2c²−(2/3)c⁴+o(1))L, and the triple-to-single count is (c⁴/6+o(1))L+Re ∫R³ conjugate(R). Ordinary near-maximal strong Sidon sequences satisfy (U). Their residual fourth moment is (4/3+o(1))L. An L⁴-small-error transfer is therefore unavailable.
3. The residual supremum is at least (√(2c−(2/3)c³)+o(1))L^(1/4). The conventional absolute-value telescoping bound would need an o(L^(1/4)) residual to certify o(L) transfer. This excludes that estimate as a sufficient route; it does not exclude all Fourier arguments or all signed cancellation.
4. Under (U), triple avoidance forces the signed phase average to tend to −c²/[4(3−c²)]. The additional unproved phase lower bound with parameter κ≥0 implies c²≤12κ/(1+4κ). The threshold κ=1/14 yields the conjectural symmetric-core constant only within the uniform-profile class; κ<1/8 would exclude a near-maximal avoiding sequence.
5. The character-weighted expansion is uniform in the integer shift. The three interval carries have leading coefficients 1/6, 2/3, 1/6. In the separately assumed strong modular, near-perfect setting, their residual coefficients sum to o(L); absent zero carry requires a specific compensating bias in the other carries, which this result does not prohibit.
6. For radii contained in the single coset Z+1/2, the integral shift is one, the signed residual phase factor is e(−α), and the leading constants remain unchanged. This is not a statement for arbitrary mixed subsets of (1/2)Z.
7. The measurable-function model and real-coefficient polynomial model meet the stated leading scalar moments and zero-frequency condition while the signed quartic functional can vanish exactly. Their coefficients are not constrained to be nonnegative or indicators. The polynomial model has a genuinely negative frequency-3 coefficient. These are analytic countermodels to a norm-only implication, not counterexamples to the arithmetic conjecture.

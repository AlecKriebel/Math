# Primary-source audit notes

- Modern K3 preliminary author PDF, Problem4.8 printed p.197: full statement
  and remarks checked visually. The arbitrary ambient allows a connected sum;
  the K3 specialization is separately stated. URL:
  https://math.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/surv-295-ruberman-watermarked-author-pdf.pdf
- Baykur, arXiv:1704.04491v3: all three pages read, Theorem1 and Remark2
  visually/textually checked. Ordinary longitude-glued surgery is obtained
  through the zero-surgery/round-handle treatment of Remark2. The S^2 x S^2
  conclusion is available in the spin case. URL:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/1704.04491v3
- Choi–Park–Yun, full published Asian J. Math.23 (2019),735–748: Theorem1.1,
  p.738 longitude condition, Section3 and regular-fiber monodromy pp.741–742
  checked. Additional double-node assumptions are retained. URL:
  https://archive.intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/ajm/2019/0023/0005/AJM-2019-0023-0005-a002.pdf
- Fintushel–Stern, full arXiv:dg-ga/9612014v2 introduction: zero-surgery
  meridian model, simply connected exterior hypothesis and the possible
  nonuniqueness of gluing were checked. URL:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/dg-ga/9612014v2
- Ozawa, arXiv:math/9811045: introductory recall of ordinary genus additivity
  was verified, with its distinction from free genus retained. URL:
  https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9811045
- Akbulut, arXiv:1011.6274: auxiliary introduction and stabilization discussion
  checked, without replacing the main theorem by diagram examples whose
  complement groups might differ. URL: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.6274

# Sources and status audit

Checked 30 September 2026. Primary PDFs were retrieved in full; the stated
sections and hypotheses were checked. No claim is made to have independently
reproved every gauge- or Floer-theoretic theorem in the cited papers.

1. R. İnanç Baykur, Robion C. Kirby, and Daniel Ruberman, *K3 – A New Problem
   List in Low-Dimensional Topology*, April 2026 preliminary version.
   [Author-hosted PDF](https://bpb-us-e2.wpmucdn.com/websites.umass.edu/dist/b/22144/files/2026/04/K3-problem-list-watermarked.pdf).
   Problem 3.71 and remarks, printed pp.182–183, visually checked. The target
   uses smooth mod-2 balls. The neighboring rational and topological questions
   are different targets; their conclusions were not substituted.
2. Christian Bohr and Ronnie Lee, *Homology cobordism and classical knot
   invariants*, Commentarii Mathematici Helvetici 77 (2002), 363–382.
   [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00014-002-8344-0),
   [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0104042).
   Section 2 defines the smooth mod-2 cobordism group, its Rokhlin invariant,
   and invariants that vanish on mod-2 balls. It supplies constraints rather
   than the requested nonzero kernel example.
3. Selman Akbulut and Kyle Larson, *Brieskorn spheres bounding rational balls*,
   Proceedings of the AMS 146 (2018), 1817–1824.
   [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1704.07739).
   Introduction, Theorem 1, Lemma 2, and the subsequent Rokhlin computation.
   The three-handle necessity used here is explicitly prior work in the
   introduction. The odd-parameter nonbounding examples are excluded from
   the present target by their Rokhlin invariant. The other parameter values
   are not thereby classified.
4. Oğuz Şavk, *More Brieskorn spheres bounding rational balls*,
   arXiv:1912.04654v3, 7 December 2020.
   [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1912.04654).
   Theorems 1.1–1.2 and the surgery mechanism distinguish rational fillings,
   the stated nontrivial subfamilies, and known integral-ball examples.
5. Jaewon Lee and Oğuz Şavk, *On homology spheres of the trivial local
   equivalence class*, arXiv:2508.15384v2, 26 February 2026.
   [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.15384).
   Theorem 1.2 and the introduction were checked. Nontriviality even under
   rational homology cobordism is crucial; trivial local-equivalence data do
   not give mod-2 fillings. No full independent audit of its instanton proof
   is claimed.
6. Jeffrey Meier, *Trisections and spun 4-manifolds*, Mathematical Research
   Letters 25 (2018), 1497–1524.
   [Full preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.01214).
   Section 2 recalls the ordinary spin construction used for the negative
   control. Its trisection classification results are not needed. The homology
   of the specific filling is calculated directly in `OBSTRUCTION.md`.

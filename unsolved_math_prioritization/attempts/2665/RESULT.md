# Kirby Problem 1.6 / 2665: five-turn scoped partial outcome

**Original unresolved after 5/5 substantive author turns. Independent review pending.**

The K3 main question prescribes two S-equivalent integral Seifert forms and
asks for one oriented knot in S^3 bounding surfaces with those forms. The
boundary knot is to be chosen. Intersections of the two surfaces are allowed.
The stronger remark asks for one knot realizing every form in an entire
S-class; this quantifier is kept separate. Neither full question is settled.

## Retained mathematical results

1. Integral singular-core reduction, followed by Trotter's determinant-one/prime
   congruence theorem and geometric enlargement, gives a fixed-knot realization
   of every form in an S-class whose normalized Alexander polynomial has unit
   or prime absolute leading coefficient. The empty-core class is included.
2. For primitive genus-one cores of odd square discriminant N², the credited
   AFMW theorems give the exact one-step set T_N and generated subgroup H_N.
   Equality T_N=H_N settles all prescribed pairs in those classes, including
   further composite-leading examples. Classes with at most two core types
   also have a whole-class common knot. Larger pairwise existence statements
   are not promoted to whole-class realization.
3. Any actual same-knot Kakimizu path maps to the arithmetic form graph, giving
   a distance lower bound. An explicit determinant -342 S-equivalent pair has
   arithmetic distance three, so published atoroidal genus-one diameter bounds
   exclude every atoroidal common boundary. A satellite common boundary remains
   possible. A separate published hyperbolic structure theorem bounds the
   N=13 class to four core form types at one hyperbolic knot.
4. Genus-one surfaces are confined by an annulus-free companion exterior.
   Zero-framed untying preserves the matrices of every contained surface,
   giving a further obstruction for the specified simple satellite subclass.
   Nonzero framing changes have an exact rank-one formula. Matching primitive
   annular self-pairings 30 and 120 does not supply a shared band or boundary.
5. Ohtsuki's published two-loop formula, with Ito's cosmetic-crossing method
   credited, excludes every nonzero fixed-unframed-spine single-band re-framing
   with nonzero determinant. This rules out the natural f=11 and f=2 attempts
   for the two gap pairs irrespective of band knotting. If the unframed spine
   data are allowed to change, the elementary coefficient integrality conditions
   can be matched formally throughout the primitive S-class. Formal matching
   is not a tangle construction or a knot-isotopy theorem.

## Precise unresolved gap

Already the forms [[1,7],[6,0]] and [[12,7],[6,0]], with determinant -42,
are S-equivalent and noncongruent but cannot be realized disjointly. No
intersecting common-boundary realization or unrestricted obstruction has
been found. The determinant -342 pair requires a satellite boundary if it
can be realized. Essential-annulus companions, more complex satellite
patterns and changes in the actual spine tangle remain unexcluded. No
argument proves that every allowed realization must follow one of the
unsuccessful constructions considered here.

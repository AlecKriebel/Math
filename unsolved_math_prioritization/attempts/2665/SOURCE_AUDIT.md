2665 / KP-1.6, K3 2026 Problem 1.6, printed pp.14–15. The complete pinned
record and current author PDF were read; both relevant pages were rendered
and inspected. The main statement is a prescribed-pair existence question.
Remark (3) describes one knot for an entire S-equivalence class as a
rephrasing, but this is a stronger quantifier pattern and is tracked separately.
No equivalence of those two formulations is assumed here. The label is the
K3 edition, not an unverified identification with a differently numbered
older Kirby list.

Forms are integral, with unimodular alternating skew part, represented in
integral homology bases of connected oriented Seifert surfaces of knots in
S^3. Genus need not agree for the prescribed two matrices. The surfaces need
not be disjoint. A fixed knot is to be found, not supplied as part of the input.
Changing the knot by doubled-delta moves is not a realization on the same knot.

Full Aka–Feller–Miller–Wieser v5 (January 16, 2026) was read at Theorem 1.1,

and leaves the intersecting genus-one realization question open. Its
factorization of S-equivalence does not glue its different one-step knots
into a single knot. A fixed prescribed knot is an even stronger demand,
and its Section 5.5 counterexample does not refute the main K3 question.

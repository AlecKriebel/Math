## 4. Annulus-free confinement and zero-framed untying

The confinement proof is valid with its explicit annulus-free companion hypothesis. A minimal genus-one Seifert surface and an essential companion torus may be put in minimal position with every intersection circle essential in both. A circle parallel to the surface boundary would isotope the boundary knot onto the companion torus. Meridional, winding-one and absolute winding-at-least-two cases respectively contradict genusone, essentiality of that torus, or the Schubert cable genus bound for a nontrivial companion. This part of Ozawa's argument uses none of his later free-surface hypothesis.

On a punctured torus all remaining disjoint essential circles are parallel nonseparating circles. Cutting leaves the boundary-containing pair of pants and annuli. The pants is inside the solid torus, so all outside components are annuli. Their cores cannot compress in the companion exterior, because that would compress the original minimal Seifert surface in the knot exterior. In an irreducible manifold with incompressible boundary, an incompressible boundary-compressible annulus is boundary-parallel; with no essential annuli, every outside annulus is therefore boundary-parallel. Among the finite disjoint collection, an innermost boundary-parallel product region can be chosen with no other annulus inside, allowing removal of two intersection curves. Repetition contradicts minimality unless the surface lies wholly inside. No general classification of free genus-one satellites is assumed.

Different members of a finite family can be isotoped into the torus separately, relative to their common boundary, because intersections between them are permitted. This gives exactly the simultaneous existence needed; it does not preserve any pairwise-disjoint arrangement.

For two orientation-preserving solid-torus embeddings, the difference of ambient linking numbers factors through H1 in both variables. Changing a cycle by a bounding2-chain changes each embedding's linking by the same internal intersection number. Consequently the difference is the integer framing multiple f of the product of longitudinal windings. Applying this also to positive push-offs proves A_new=A_old+f ww^T entrywise, including self-pairings. Preferred-longitude matching makes f=0. All contained surfaces then preserve their matrices under the same zero-framed untying, while the common boundary becomes the same untied pattern knot.

The form-type inclusion is one-way and requires only contained surfaces from the original knot. Nonzero Alexander breadth ensures the untied pattern is still genusone in the applications. Its being atoroidal or hyperbolic is an additional hypothesis, not a consequence of untying. The stated extension of the N37/N13 restrictions to that simple satellite subclass is therefore correct, and essential-annulus or iterated-satellite possibilities remain open.

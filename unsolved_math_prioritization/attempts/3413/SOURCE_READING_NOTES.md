# Additional source reading note

The fixed-boundary equivariant Dehn lemma is credited to Meeks–Yau and to the topological proof by Allan Edmonds, *A topological proof of the equivariant Dehn lemma*, Transactions AMS297(1986),605–615, DOI10.1090/S0002-9947-1986-0854087-X.

The theorem on printed606 and its surrounding category/hypothesis discussion were inspected in the author's public full-text rendering:
https://www.researchgate.net/publication/265109397_A_topological_proof_of_the_equivariant_Dehn_lemma
The page identifies the content as uploaded by the author. The result fixes the specified null-homotopic boundary curve, requires its translates to be equal or disjoint and requires transversality to the exceptional set; the resulting disk has equal-or-disjoint translates. The text is in the PL category and does not assume irreducibility. The smooth application and equivariant-triangulation alternative are explained in Turn5.

Attempts to obtain the Meeks–Yau original PDF encountered a verification page, and the Edmonds publisher/author-PDF downloads were unavailable. No PDF hash or full-PDF inspection is claimed for these. The fixed-boundary result itself, rather than a weaker unspecified equivariant loop theorem, is the input used. Independent review should verify precisely this version and its applicability to the longitude constructed in Turn5.

The other seven PDFs in source_manifest.json were available as full reading copies. Their complete bibliographic locators and the exact used theorem scopes are in the turn files. The reading directory is excluded from publication.

# Consolidated proof guide and dependency map

The original3413 source target remains **unsolved5/5**. This guide indexes the exact scoped claims and does not enlarge them.

- **Turn1:** signed-cycle conjugacy/order data including the domain kernel; local finite-order orientation-reversal lemma; negative cycles must have length m/2. Source finite-action extension before Budney Definition3.3 is essential.
- **Turn2:** transfer of torsion linking under a free cyclic cover; invariant-circle linking is coprime to the cover order; complete free ambient-unlink pattern classification with constructions. Classical input: nonsingular linking pairing.
- **Turn3:** branched-cover linking normalization; two positively invariant unlink components force a common pointwise fixed axis; at most two axes have coprime stabilizer orders; a negative cycle forces its axis kernel to be exactly C2. These yield additional source obstructions and the signed-plus-positive-singleton ambient family. Classical inputs: Smith rotation theorem and the equivariant-unknot/common-axis theorem.
- **Turn4:** connected free-quotient hyperbolic completion using Myers with every boundary/monodromy hypothesis checked; finite cyclic-extension index/multiplicity test once a signed completion exists. The singular quotient and the full-group issues are separate.
- **Turn5:** fixed-boundary equivariant Dehn disk gives equality of a proper positive orbit's stabilizer with the full axis kernel. This closes the **ambient** classification in all cases, with explicit smooth disk-bounding constructions. Classical input: the fixed-boundary equivariant Dehn lemma, including null-homotopy, equivariant boundary curve and exceptional-set transversality. A conditional Myers gluing certificate is proved but its general existence is not.

Theorem dependencies are one-way: Turns1–4 do not assume Turn5, and the ambient constructions do not assume hyperbolicity. The full original representation classification is not used as an input to any partial proof. A finite source symmetry group is always the entire oriented-L0 group when the original target is discussed; constructed ambient groups or subgroups are expressly labeled as such.

The central review risks are the H-invariant preferred longitude in the **full unlink exterior**, the exact prescribed-boundary Dehn-lemma conclusion, all signed/unsigned multiplicity cases, the connected lift under generator monodromy, and accidental extra cyclic source symmetries. No quotient is treated as an ordinary manifold after discarding branch data.

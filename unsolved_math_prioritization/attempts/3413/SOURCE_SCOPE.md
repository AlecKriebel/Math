# Source and prior-work gate: 3413 / OPG-37131

The original Open Problem Garden page (posted7November2009) was read in full, including its discussion and bibliography. It asks which representations occur from the symmetry group of a hyperbolic link L=(L0,L1,...,Ln) in S3 when L1 union ... union Ln is an unlink. Only deleting the distinguished L0 is required to leave an unlink; the stronger condition that every proper sublink is trivial is not assumed. The ambient diffeomorphisms preserve the orientation of S3 and the orientation of L0, while other components may be permuted and reversed. The target is pi0 Diff(the disjoint union of those circles), the signed symmetric group (Z/2)^n semidirect S_n. This is not the mapping class group of the unlink complement.

Budney's full primary preprint math/0506524v4 was recovered. Its introduction and section3 call the full relevant finite cyclic isometry group B_L, matching the OPG A_L through the extendable symmetry identification. The separate subgroup A_f depends on knots spliced into the remaining components and must not replace B_L. Proposition3.5 proves faithfulness on the distinguished circle, not faithfulness of the signed-permutation representation. The last section already credits cyclic n-cycle examples, Borromean/Whitehead cases and a family of signed n-cycles. These are prior examples, not new results. The primary DOI is10.1112/plms/pdp058; the imported pdq020 citation is incorrect.

## Conventions that the research must retain

- The full symmetry group must be realized, not merely a chosen cyclic subgroup of a larger symmetry group
- Domain order and any kernel of the signed action matter; they are not replaced by the image order
- L0 need not be an unknot; the source paper includes n=0, so that endpoint cannot be silently discarded
- Relabeling/reorienting the other components conjugates the signed action; changing an abstract cyclic generator changes its image by a unit power. Any classification must state which of these identifications it uses
- A topological cyclic action preserving an unlink does not by itself construct a hyperbolic link with exactly the desired full symmetry group
- Knot-space component calculations and an in-principle symmetry computation are not automatically an all-representations realization theorem

# A reader's guide to the constructions

## 1. Exact mathematical setting

Fix an oriented handlebody V_g of genus g ≥ 2 and its closed, unmarked oriented boundary S_g. There is no retained boundary component, marked point, or fixed boundary disk in the target groups. Write

- H_g ≤ Mod(S_g) for mapping classes extending over V_g;
- I_g for the kernel of the action on H₁(S_g; Z);
- K_g for the subgroup generated by separating surface twists;
- HI_g = H_g ∩ I_g and HK_g = H_g ∩ K_g.

The source request allows either branch. The separate arguments here cover both, at the level of explicit, generally infinite algebraic generating families. They use integral homology throughout.

## 2. Genus two: one balanced-meridian family for both groups

Choose a theta-graph disk system D₀,D₁,D₂ with disjoint nonseparating boundary curves of homology classes a,b,a+b. Here a,b form an integral basis of the meridian Lagrangian. Set t_i = T_{∂D_i}. For every essential properly embedded disk D, write [∂D] = pa+qb and define

    B_D = T_{∂D} t₀^(pq−p²) t₁^(pq−q²) t₂^(−pq).

Then HI₂ = HK₂ = ⟨ B_D : D an essential disk ⟩, with ordinary subgroup generation.

Why this works:

1. Luft's closed theorem identifies the kernel of H₂ → Out(F₂) with the group T₂ generated by meridian twists. Nielsen's natural Out(F₂) → GL₂(Z) is an isomorphism. Thus every element of HI₂ lies in T₂.
2. A disk twist of class (p,q) has homology shear block [[p²,pq],[pq,q²]]. The reference twists give a unimodular basis of Sym₂(Z), determinant −1.
3. The three commuting twists provide an actual group-homomorphic section of this shear map. The displayed exponents cancel the entire homology block.
4. The correction subgroup ⟨t₀,t₁,t₂⟩ normalizes the family: s B_D s⁻¹ = B_{s(D)}. Together with Luft generation and injectivity of the section, this proves ordinary generation. Correcting individual generators alone would not suffice without this normalization step.
5. The closed equality I₂=K₂ identifies the two target labels.

Changing the disk-boundary orientation changes (p,q) to (−p,−q) and leaves B_D unchanged. Separating disks give B_D=T_{∂D}; the proof does not claim those entries alone suffice. This genus-two proof does not extrapolate a genus-at-least-three theorem.

Full proof: [Genus-two generators](pass1_genus_two/GENUS_TWO_GENERATORS.md).

## 3. Higher-genus Torelli intersection: the closed Omori family

For g ≥ 3, fix Omori's genus-one homotopical bounding-pair map h, with orientation chosen so its closed Johnson class is [a₁∧b₁∧b₂]. Omori's disk-fixed normal-generation theorem descends under the disk-forgetting map because the full preimage of closed HI_g is the disk-fixed intersection and the restriction is surjective. Thus

    HI_g = ⟨ w h^ε w⁻¹ : w ∈ H_g, ε ∈ {−1,+1} ⟩.

This is one normal generator in the ambient H_g, or equivalently the full H_g-conjugacy family as ordinary generators. It is not ordinary cyclic generation, nor a normal-generation statement with ambient HI_g. This result is attributed to Omori, with the closed-model transfer proved explicitly.

For discrete indices, w ranges over all finite words in Wajnryb's closed five-generator alphabet and inverses. The complete constructive note defines the geometric curves and the words A₁,S₁,R₁₂,T₁,U.

## 4. Higher-genus Johnson intersection: explicit lattice correction

Let H=H₁(S_g; Z), with meridian classes a_i and dual classes b_i, and set

    ω = Σ_i a_i∧b_i,
    κ(x) = x∧ω,
    W = ker(Λ³H → Λ³(H/⟨a₁,…,a_g⟩)).

The closed Johnson homomorphism has target Λ³H/κ(H) and kernel K_g. Its image on HI_g is exactly W/κ(H), an integral lattice of rank

    r = binom(2g,3) − binom(g,3) − 2g.

The quotient/image theorem is established prior work, attributed to Morita in the inspected literature. The reader supplies explicit monomial-lift formulas and an integral coordinate algorithm:

- Seed conjugates and elementary block-diagonal handle slides give every a_i∧b_j∧b_k.
- Disk-shear commutators give every a_i∧a_j∧b_k, including the essential exceptional case k=j with its inverse sign.
- A further shear gives every a_i∧a_j∧a_k.
- Each κ(x) has g−1 coefficients ±1; the supports of these 2g relations are pairwise disjoint. Delete a selected unit pivot from each support. The remaining monomials form a genuine integral basis, with no division except by ±1 and no rational saturation assumption.

Let p₁,…,p_r be the explicit mapping-class lifts of this basis, and set t_n=p₁^n₁⋯p_r^n_r for every n∈Z^r. These p_i need not commute. Let c(w,ε) be the exact integral coordinate vector of τ(w h^ε w⁻¹), calculated by an exterior product and the unit-pivot reduction. Define

    G(n,w,ε) = t_n (w h^ε w⁻¹) t_(n+c(w,ε))⁻¹.

All n∈Z^r, all finite ambient words w, and both signs are allowed. Every output is automatically in HK_g, since its Johnson value is n+c−(n+c)=0. Conversely, a zero-image product of the known HI generators factors into these G's by adjacent telescoping cancellation of actual t-words. This proves ordinary generation of the full HK_g, rather than only of a quotient.

No parameter is selected by testing target-group membership. Auxiliary matrix lifts are fixed by terminating shortlex searches for specified integral homology matrices in the known ambient generating alphabet. This does not assume an algorithm for decomposing an arbitrary target mapping class.

An optional equivalent normal family is given in the full proof; its normal closure is in HI_g and includes the correction-lift commutators. It is not finite normal generation.

Full construction: [Lattice–Schreier generators](pass1_constructive/CONSTRUCTIVE_JOHNSON_GENERATORS.md). Closed transfer and integral quotient: [Source-and-acceptance audit](pass1_closed_transfer/SOURCE_ACCEPTANCE_REPORT.md).

## 5. A separate obstruction to separating-meridian generation

In rank g ≥ 3, take partial conjugations A:x₁↦x₂x₁x₂⁻¹ and B:x₂↦x₃x₂x₃⁻¹, fixing all other basis generators. With right-to-left composition, [A,B] sends x₁ to w x₁ w⁻¹ with w=x₃x₂⁻¹x₃⁻¹x₂, and fixes the other generators.

Every nonzero power is non-inner: an inner representative fixing x₂ and x₃ would have conjugator in ⟨x₂⟩∩⟨x₃⟩={1}, but the image of x₁ has reduced length 8|n|+1. Torelli lifts of A,B have a commutator f in HK_g, since the Johnson target is abelian. Its outer action has infinite order. All meridian twists have trivial outer action. Consequently the subgroup generated by separating meridian twists has infinite index in full HK_g.

The same argument applies inside every finite-index Γ≤H_g, using a positive power of f. This makes the corresponding finite-index premise in the cited version of Holden's Theorem D unattainable in this model; the conditional implication itself is not refuted. The separating-meridian proposal is Conjecture 7.1 in arXiv:2607.07587v1. No claim about later revisions or the author's intended alternative formulation is made.

This obstruction is neither a replacement generating family nor the reason the constructive family is accepted. It does not settle the stronger genus-two separating-meridian question.

Full proof: [Outer-action obstruction](pass1_johnson_obstruction/PROOF.md).

## 1. Free-group obstruction

Use right-to-left composition. Write A for the partial conjugation of x1 by x2, and B for the partial conjugation of x2 by x3. Then

    Phi = A B A^-1 B^-1
    w = x3 x2^-1 x3^-1 x2
    Phi(x1) = w x1 w^-1
    Phi(xi) = xi for i >= 2.

This can be checked without assuming any commutator convention implicitly: B^-1 fixes x1; A^-1 sends it to x2^-1 x1 x2; B substitutes x3 x2 x3^-1 for x2; A then conjugates the sole x1 by x2. The resulting initial four letters are exactly w, followed by x1 and w^-1. On x2 the initial B^-1 conjugation is undone by B, since A and A^-1 fix x2 and x3. The other basis generators are fixed throughout.

The word w uses no x1, is nonempty and cyclically reduced, and is fixed by Phi. Therefore induction and the inverse map give

    Phi^n(x1) = w^n x1 w^-n

for every integer n. If n is nonzero, this displayed word is freely reduced and has length 8|n|+1. The proof does not rely on testing a bounded set of powers.

If Phi^n were inner, its conjugating word would centralize both x2 and x3. The reduced-word normal form in the free product generated by a basis element and the remaining basis elements gives C(x2)=<x2> and C(x3)=<x3>. Their intersection is trivial. Thus an inner Phi^n would be the identity, contradicting the preceding reduced length. Every nonzero power is non-inner, so the outer class has infinite order.

### Lifts and membership

Both A and B lie in IA. The original proof supplies disk-fixed Torelli lifts using Omori's specified homotopical bounding pair and its handle permutation. Its alternative closed lifting proof is also correct: an extending lift of an outer IA class acts on boundary homology by U(Q)=[[I,Q],[0,I]], with Q integral symmetric. Disk-twist transvections on a_i and a_i+a_j span the whole integral symmetric-matrix lattice, since

    (ei+ej)(ei+ej)^T - ei ei^T - ej ej^T = Eij+Eji.

Thus a product with block -Q corrects homology without changing the outer action. This argument only needs that disk twists act trivially on the handlebody's outer fundamental-group action; it does not identify Aut and Out kernels.

For lifts a,b in HI, the surface Johnson homomorphism is a homomorphism to an abelian group. Its kernel is generated by separating surface twists. Consequently [a,b] belongs to HK. This is a closed integral theorem; a rational statement about the abelianization would not suffice and is not being used.

Every separating meridian twist has trivial outer action. If f is the lifted commutator, no nonzero power of f lies in that outer-action kernel. The left cosets f^n M of the subgroup M generated by separating meridian twists are pairwise distinct. Normality of M is not required for this coset argument. The larger subgroup HK intersected with the outer-action kernel likewise has infinite index in HK.

For a finite-index subgroup Gamma of H, the action of <f> on the finite coset set gives some positive f^m in Gamma. The same argument with f^m proves the stated infinite-index strengthening. This invalidates the proposed generating family for full HK in genus at least three. It is not by itself a replacement generating theorem and does not refute the logical truth of a theorem stated with an impossible hypothesis.

## 2. Genus-two balanced-meridian generators

### Topological reference system

The regular neighborhood of a theta graph is a genus-two handlebody. A transverse disk in each of its three edge tubes gives three disjoint, nonisotopic, nonseparating meridians. Cutting any two edges of the spine leaves a tree, so any two corresponding disks form a complete disk system. The boundary complement of all three curves is two pairs of pants. The pants boundary relation permits orientations with classes a,b,a+b. In particular, a,b are a basis of the integral meridian Lagrangian, not merely a rational basis.

The three right twists t0,t1,t2 commute because the curves are disjoint. Their homology blocks have coordinate columns

    (1,0,0), (0,0,1), (1,1,1)

in the order (upper-left, off-diagonal, lower-right). The determinant is -1. Their images form an integral basis of Sym2(Z), and the commuting twists provide a group-homomorphic section

    sigma([r s; s t]) = t0^(r-s) t1^(t-s) t2^s.

There is no appeal here to a general splitting of a group extension: the displayed commuting lifts establish this particular splitting.

### Membership and orientation

For a disk boundary class pa+qb, its twist block has coordinates (p^2,pq,q^2). Thus the correction is exactly

    B_D = T_D t0^(pq-p^2) t1^(pq-q^2) t2^(-pq).

All three resulting homology coordinates vanish identically as polynomials in p and q. Reversing the disk boundary orientation negates both p and q, leaving each exponent unchanged. Reversing the common twist-sign convention negates every homology block, which also leaves the displayed correction exponents valid. The checker verifies these identities universally over Z[p,q]; these are not sampled numerical cases.

Every factor extends over the handlebody, and the whole product acts trivially on boundary homology. Hence every B_D belongs to HI2. The equality I2=K2 then puts it in HK2.

### The ordinary-generation step

Let T be the closed Luft/twist group and let chi:T→Sym2(Z) be the homology block. Nielsen's rank-two theorem says the natural map Out(F2)→GL2(Z) is injective. A surface Torelli class therefore has trivial outer handlebody action, giving HI2=T intersected with I2=ker chi. This is the essential genus-two specialization.

Let A=<t0,t1,t2> and W=<B_D for all essential disks D>. Every element s of A fixes the meridian homology lattice pointwise and sends disks to disks. Since A is abelian,

    s B_D s^-1 = B_(s(D)).

Thus A normalizes W. Each meridian twist is B_D sigma(chi(T_D)), so Luft's theorem gives T=WA. The restriction chi|A is injective, by the unimodular calculation. An element ws in ker chi must consequently have s=1. This proves W=ker chi as an ordinary subgroup, with no missing conjugates.

In particular, the proof does not make the generally false inference that generators corrected individually to have zero image automatically generate a kernel. The extra normalization argument supplies exactly what that inference would otherwise lack.

The split expression T=HI2 semidirect A is valid. It does not assert that HI2 is finitely generated, that the displayed family is minimal, or that separating meridian twists alone suffice.

## 3. Integral Johnson-image construction

Write H=A plus B, with ordered basis a1,...,ag,b1,...,bg, where A is the meridian Lagrangian and <ai,bj>=delta_ij. Put omega=sum ai wedge bi and kappa(x)=x wedge omega. Let W be the free lattice spanned by exterior triples containing at least one a. All of kappa(H) lies in W.

The geometric seed is a genus-one homotopical bounding-pair map h with Johnson class

    q = a1 wedge b1 wedge b2,

up to choosing h or h^-1 and the consistent BP orientation. The side of the bounding pair contains the first handle; the common nonmeridional class is b2. The bounding-pair formula gives b2 wedge (a1 wedge b1)=q. I independently inspected the retained Omori Figure 1 and the published bounding-pair formula in Putman Section 2.4. This geometric input must remain stated separately from algebraic tests: computing with the symbol q does not prove that a mapping class has that Johnson value.

Omori's known normal-generation theorem, in the correct closed interpretation, says that HI is generated by H-conjugates of h and h^-1 for g>=3. A closed/decorated transfer can also be justified directly: any extending boundary representative can be isotoped to fix a chosen boundary disk pointwise; the boundary isotopy extends over a handlebody collar. Hence H^D→H is onto. The integral H1 map from the once-bordered surface to its capped surface is an isomorphism, so the preimage of HI is HI^D. Disk-fixed normal generation therefore descends. This does not assert an injective forgetful map or identify its kernel with an Out kernel.

Every handlebody symplectic matrix preserves A and hence W. Johnson equivariance and normal generation give the upper inclusion tau(HI)⊂W/kappa(H). The following words supply every basis monomial integrally, proving the reverse inclusion.

### Realizing ABB

Let D(i,j) be a handle-permuted seed with tau(D(i,j))=ai wedge bi wedge bj, for i≠j. For distinct i,j,k choose Uij acting on homology by

    aj -> aj+ai;  bi -> bi-bj;

and fixing the other relevant basis elements. This is diag(I+Eij,(I+Eij)^(-T)). A lift in H exists by the integral parabolic image theorem; it need not be in HI.

For U in H and d in HI, normality implies [U,d] is in HI, and equivariance gives

    tau([U,d]) = (Lambda^3 U - I) tau(d).

One must not justify this by saying every such commutator is a commutator of two Torelli elements.

Define B(i;j,k), j≠k, by D(j,k) if i=j, by D(k,j)^-1 if i=k, and by [Uij,D(j,k)] otherwise. Its image is exactly ai wedge bj wedge bk. In the third case Uij fixes bj and bk because i is distinct from both. In the second case the inverse compensates for interchanging the two b factors. Thus all indices, including repeated handle indices across the a and b factors, are covered.

### Realizing AAB and AAA

Let Tj denote a meridian twist with bj→bj+aj. For i<j, set

    A(i,j;k) = [Tj,B(i;j,k)]          if k≠j,
    A(i,j;j) = [Ti,B(j;i,j)]^-1.

The first image is ai wedge aj wedge bk. In the second case the unswitched commutator gives aj wedge ai wedge bj, so the inverse is necessary. This handles the potentially missed case k=j without using an invalid B(i;j,j).

For i<j<k, [Tk,A(i,j;k)] has image ai wedge aj wedge ak. Therefore every canonical exterior basis vector of W is represented by an explicitly constructed element of HI, integrally and with coefficient +1.

### Closed quotient, torsion, and normal form

For every basis element x, the relation R_x=kappa(x) has g-1 nonzero terms, all with coefficient +1 or -1. A nonzero triple occurring in a relation contains one complete pair {aj,bj}, and its remaining letter is x. An exterior triple cannot contain two complete pairs, since it has only three distinct letters. Consequently the supports of the 2g relations R_x are pairwise disjoint.

Choose nu(i)=i+1 cyclically. For x=ai or bi, choose pivot p_x to be the sorted triple {x,a_nu(i),b_nu(i)}. Its coefficient epsilon_x in R_x is a unit. These 2g pivots are distinct. Every vector v in W has the integral representative

    N(v) = v - sum_x (coefficient_(p_x)(v) / epsilon_x) R_x.

Division is only by +1 or -1. Disjoint supports make all pivot coefficients vanish without reintroducing any pivot. The remaining canonical monomials span W/kappa(H). They are independent: if a linear combination of them equals sum c_x R_x, comparing pivot p_x gives c_x=0 individually. This proves an actual Z-basis and torsion-freeness, rather than merely a rational rank calculation.

The rank is therefore

    binomial(2g,3) - binomial(g,3) - 2g.

The algebraic construction also gives zero quotient rank when g=2; that consistency check does not extend the g>=3 Omori input to genus two.

## 4. Kernel rewriting with noncommuting basis lifts

Let z1,...,zr be the constructed HI elements whose Johnson classes form the quotient basis. They need not commute. Define the set-theoretic section

    t_n = z1^n1 ... zr^nr,  n in Z^r,

with this fixed order. Then tau(t_n)=n and t_0=1. If s=w h^epsilon w^-1 is a normal generator and c(s) is its integral Johnson coordinate vector, put

    R(n,s) = t_n s t_(n+c(s))^-1.

Each R(n,s) extends and has Johnson image zero, so it belongs to HK. Conversely, express f in HK as s1...sk and let n_j=c(s1)+...+c(sj), with n_0=n_k=0. Then

    product from j=1 to k of R(n_(j-1),s_j) = s1...sk = f,

by adjacent cancellation of the actual words t_(n_j)^-1 t_(n_j). This telescoping does not assume t_(m+n)=t_m t_n and does not assume the basis lifts commute. It proves ordinary generation of the exact kernel.

The indices are lattice points and explicitly indexed H-words. They do not require testing whether a candidate word already belongs to HK. The resulting family is infinite; no finite-generation, minimality, effective short-word bound, or novelty claim follows from this rewriting.

The completed manuscript uses Wajnryb's five-element ambient alphabet and makes every needed elementary matrix lift the first matching word in shortlex order. The search is not a disguised membership enumeration of HK: all words are in the known ambient group, equality of their integral homology matrices is decidable, and the parabolic image theorem guarantees termination. I checked the stated S1, Ti, R12, and U expressions against Definition 3 and the exact five-generator assertion against Theorem 19, printed page 213, in the retained full Wajnryb text.

Its optional normal-family formulation also passes. Quotient HI by the normal closure, in HI, of the corrected generators s t_c(s)^-1 and the basis-lift commutators. This quotient is generated by commuting basis lifts. The Johnson map from that quotient to Z^r and the map back induced by the basis lifts compose to the identity on Z^r. Since the latter map is surjective, it is an isomorphism. The normal subgroup is therefore exactly HK. No normal-generation claim in a different ambient group is inferred.

## 6. Reproducibility and evidence limits

Run from this directory:

    python3 verify_exact_formulas.py
    python3 -O verify_exact_formulas.py

The checker has no external dependencies and uses explicit runtime conditions rather than Python assertions, so optimized execution retains all checks. The output records the checked proof-file hashes and checker hash. The provided outputs agree byte-for-byte.

- Free-word tests: ranks 3 through 13, powers -20 through 20; direct substitution, inverses, reduced words, all basis images, and length formulas.
- Genus-two algebra: universal sparse-polynomial identities over Z[p,q], sign invariance, exact symplectic matrix multiplication, and a unimodular inverse.
- Johnson algebra: genera 2 through 10; all index choices in the three monomial types, disjoint relation supports, unit pivots, quotient reduction, and invariance under addition of every defining relation.

Finite tests are supplementary evidence. The centralizer argument proves all-power non-innerness; the topology and normalization argument prove genus-two generation; the index-general wedge identities and disjoint-support argument prove the integral image and quotient formulas in all specified genera. The checker alone proves none of the cited topological or generation theorems.

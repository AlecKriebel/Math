# Explicit generators for closed handlebody intersections

**Birman Problem 2.8 · record 1788 · ID 11000165 · accepted algebraic-construction edition, 14 October 2026**

For a fixed oriented genus-g handlebody V with closed, unmarked boundary S, let H_g be its handlebody subgroup, I_g the surface Torelli group, and K_g the subgroup generated by separating surface twists. This reader gives explicit ordinary generating families for both H_g ∩ I_g and H_g ∩ K_g for every source genus g ≥ 2.

These are generally infinite algebraic families obtained from established theory. No novelty, finite-generation, minimality, presentation, computational-efficiency, or stronger short geometric-generator claim is made. Acceptance is the recorded mathematical audit of these particular constructions, not external peer review or an assertion that every customary interpretation of an open geometric problem has been resolved.

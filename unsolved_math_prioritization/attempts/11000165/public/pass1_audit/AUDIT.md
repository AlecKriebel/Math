# Independent adversarial audit: closed handlebody intersections

Date: 14 October 2026. Shared mathematical pass 1.

## Scope and decision standard

This audit fixes the closed, unmarked oriented boundary surface of a fixed oriented genus-g handlebody, integral surface homology, and g≥2. The two groups are HI_g=H_g∩I_g and HK_g=H_g∩K_g, with K_g generated by separating surface twists. The original question permits either branch; the findings below distinguish them. No finite-generation requirement, novelty assertion, or theorem claim about an unverified conjecture is introduced.

The acceptance test is an independently specified family, membership, and ordinary generation (or a normal family with its conjugating group named). An arbitrary list of words accepted because they belong to HK is excluded. A geometric disk family with a fixed homology correction, or a Schreier family indexed by explicit lattice vectors and a known ambient-conjugacy family, can pass: the indices are determined without testing membership in the sought subgroup.

## 1. Source and convention gates

1. Omori's arXiv:1607.06553v1, Theorem 1.2, is disk-fixed and assumes g≥3. It supplies a fixed genus-one homotopical bounding-pair map h normally generating the disk-fixed HI in the disk-fixed H. Figures 1 and 3 identify its geometry and induced partial conjugation. It is prior work, not a new result in this pass.
2. The closed transfer is valid. Hensel's primer §3, printed pp.10–11, explicitly makes the point- and disk-fixed handlebody groups full preimages under forgetful maps. Capping one boundary disk preserves integral first homology. Thus the full preimage of closed HI is disk-fixed HI, the restriction onto closed HI is surjective, and the image of Omori's ambient normal closure is the ambient H_g-normal closure of the closed h. This is stronger than the mere inclusion sufficient for the outer-action obstruction.
3. Putman's arXiv:0904.0467v4, §1 pp.2–3 and §2.4 p.12, identifies the closed Johnson target as Λ³H/(H∧ω) and proves its kernel is the separating-twist group. The whole-surface specialization of Theorem A applies. Hence [I_g,I_g]⊂K_g integrally. This does not merely identify rational kernels or finite-index subgroups.
4. The boundary action on a meridian Lagrangian and the handlebody Out action must remain distinct. Hensel's Theorem 6.4 is the closed statement ker(H_g→Out(F_g))=the group generated by all meridian twists. Omori's corresponding decorated map uses Aut(F_g), not Out(F_g).
5. Genus two is special: the natural Out(F₂)→GL₂(Z) is an isomorphism. An exact research-paper statement is Bogopolski–Martino–Ventura, *The automorphism group of a free-by-cyclic group in rank 2*, §3 Theorem 3.1, printed/PDF p.8. The theorem was inspected in the author-hosted complete PDF. Birman's source gives I₂=K₂; Putman's §1 also independently records this genus-two equality.

Sources: [Omori](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06553v1); [Hensel primer](https://www.math.lmu.de/~hensel/papers/hno4.pdf); [Putman](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.0467v4); [Bogopolski–Martino–Ventura](https://www.math.uni-duesseldorf.de/~bogopolski/Artikel/2007_Mar_Ven.pdf); [Birman source](https://math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf).

These checks authenticate the input theorems and their precise models. They are not a fresh proof of every classical theorem cited.

## 2. Genus-two balanced-disk family: PASS

The audited report is `../pass1_genus_two/GENUS_TWO_GENERATORS.md` (exact reviewed bytes are pinned in the final manifest).

A regular neighborhood of a theta graph gives three disjoint essential nonseparating co-core disks D₀,D₁,D₂. Any two form a complete disk system. The two complementary pairs of pants give orientations with classes a,b,a+b. This proves both existence and integrality of the asserted basis; an unverified drawing is not required.

Set t_i=T_{∂D_i}. For any essential meridian disk D with [∂D]=pa+qb, the family is

    B_D=T_{∂D} t₀^(pq−p²) t₁^(pq−q²) t₂^(−pq).

The orientation ambiguity is harmless because all coefficients are quadratic. If D separates, p=q=0, and this entry is exactly the separating meridian twist.

The decisive algebra is exact over Z. The meridian twist group T₂ has homology map χ:T₂→Sym₂(Z) sending T_D to vvᵀ with v=(p,q), under a consistent common transvection convention. The three reference images form an integral basis with determinant −1. Since the twists commute, they define a homomorphic section

    σ([[r,s],[s,t]])=t₀^(r−s)t₁^(t−s)t₂^s.

Thus B_D=T_Dσ(vvᵀ)⁻¹ lies in kerχ. A sign reversal of the common transvection convention changes both sides together and leaves the exponents unchanged.

The potential omission of Schreier conjugates has been closed correctly. Write A=⟨t₀,t₁,t₂⟩ and W=⟨B_D⟩. Every a∈A fixes the meridian Lagrangian pointwise, preserves the disk set, and commutes with σ(vvᵀ). Therefore aB_Da⁻¹=B_{a(D)}. Hence A normalizes W. Every disk twist is in WA, so Luft's theorem gives T₂=WA. The section is injective, so kerχ=W. This proof covers inverses automatically; it does not silently assume all group words are positive.

Finally HI₂⊂T₂ follows from the natural Out(F₂)→GL₂(Z) isomorphism, since every surface-Torelli element acts trivially on handlebody homology. Consequently W=HI₂=HK₂. Nothing here extrapolates Omori's or Pitsch's g≥3 statements to genus two. It is a complete ordinary generating family in the original closed model, with one arbitrary meridian twist and three fixed correcting twists. Whether separating meridians alone suffice in genus two is not decided or required.

## 3. Outer-action obstruction for g≥3: PASS

The audited report is `../pass1_johnson_obstruction/PROOF.md`.

The report uses two partial conjugations A=C₁₂ and B=C₂₃. With right-to-left composition, their commutator conjugates x₁ by

    w=x₃ x₂⁻¹ x₃⁻¹ x₂

and fixes all x_i for i≥2. The stated word and signs are correct. Every nonzero power fixes x₂ and x₃ yet sends x₁ to wⁿx₁w⁻ⁿ, a reduced word of length 8|n|+1. An inner automorphism fixing x₂ and x₃ would have conjugator in ⟨x₂⟩∩⟨x₃⟩={1}; hence those powers are non-inner.

I independently checked a second witness, C₁₂ and C₁₃, using a separate exact word-reduction program. Its commutator conjugates x₁ by x₃⁻¹x₂⁻¹x₃x₂ and fixes every other basis generator. This gives the same all-power non-inner certificate, independent of the chain-pair calculation.

Omori's §2.1 equation (2.1) supplies exact IA lifts in disk-fixed HI. Forgetting the disk preserves Torelli membership and commutes with Aut→Out. Closed lifts a,b therefore have [a,b]∈[HI_g,HI_g]⊂HK_g and infinite-order outer image. The alternative lift proof in the report is valid too: correct an arbitrary outer-IA lift by disk twists whose integral homology shears realize the negative of its symmetric block. The elementary matrices e_ie_iᵀ and (e_i+e_j)(e_i+e_j)ᵀ span Sym_g(Z), so this correction has no missing denominator.

Every meridian twist has trivial outer image. It follows that the separating-meridian subgroup, and even HK_g∩ker(H_g→Out(F_g)), has infinite index in HK_g for all g≥3. For any finite-index Γ≤H_g, a positive power of the constructed commutator lies in Γ and retains infinite-order outer image. Therefore the subgroup K(Γ) generated by powers of separating meridian twists in Γ has infinite index in Γ∩HK_g.

This is a counterexample to the stated full-HK generator proposal in Holden arXiv:2607.07587v1, Conjecture 7.1, in every g≥3. In particular it applies to g≥4, the range of that version's surrounding applications. It also makes the finite-index hypothesis in that version's Theorem D unattainable in this locked model. It does not refute the conditional implication in Theorem D, turn a conjecture into a theorem, establish an author's intended alternative meaning, or certify the status of later versions. Refuting that candidate family alone would not solve the original open-ended generator request.

## 4. Integral Johnson image and generic kernel-family audit

Use a_i as a basis of the meridian Lagrangian L and b_i as dual classes. The image of the standard closed Omori map, with an appropriate BP orientation, is

    q=[a₁∧b₁∧b₂] in J=Λ³H/(H∧ω).

The geometry was visually checked in Omori's Figure 1: the bounding pair encloses the first handle and has common class the second longitude. Both the sign and basis convention must be fixed consistently, not inferred from another author's use of a/b labels.

Let W=ker(Λ³H→Λ³(H/L)); its monomial basis comprises ABB, AAB, and AAA terms. The parabolic H_g-homology action preserves W. The following identities show that the integral orbit span of q before quotienting is all of W:

- Permutations yield a_i∧b_i∧b_j (up to sign).
- For distinct i,j,k, P=diag(I+E_ij,(I+E_ij)⁻ᵀ) gives (Λ³P−I)(a_j∧b_j∧b_k)=a_i∧b_j∧b_k.
- The diagonal disk shear b_j↦b_j+a_j converts ABB to AAB by taking a difference. Choose j different from the retained b-index; when necessary swap the two a-indices first.
- The same shear on the b-index in a_i∧a_j∧b_k gives a_i∧a_j∧a_k for distinct indices.

Every operation is an integral difference of orbit elements, not a rational spanning argument. It follows from the normal-generation input that τ(HI_g)=W/(H∧ω).

The relations x∧ω, for basis vectors x, have pairwise disjoint monomial supports: a degree-three monomial has at most one complete symplectic handle pair, and its remaining basis vector uniquely determines x. Each relation has g−1 nonzero unit coefficients. Deleting one chosen unit pivot from each support gives an explicit integral basis of the quotient, of rank

    r=binom(2g,3)−binom(g,3)−2g.

Thus there is no torsion or hidden finite-index issue. An independent exact exterior-algebra checker verifies every identity and disjoint-unit-pivot claim for g=3,…,8; the index-general argument above proves all g≥3.

For any explicit word lifts p₁,…,p_r of this basis, put t_n=p₁^n₁⋯p_r^n_r. If S is the known explicit HBP conjugacy generating family for HI_g and c(s) is the integer-coordinate vector of τ(s), then

    R(n,s)=t_n s t_(n+c(s))⁻¹,   n∈Z^r, s∈S,

is a genuine ordinary generating family for ker(τ|HI_g). Membership is automatic. If f=s₁⋯s_k has zero image and n_j=Σ_(i≤j)c(s_i), then f is the telescoping product of R(n_(j−1),s_j), since t_0=1. The t_n need not be a homomorphic section and the p_i need not commute. Any proof assuming they commute would be invalid; the telescoping proof does not.

This family passes the explicitness test when S, c(s), the lattice basis, and its lifts are provided independently. It is a generic algebraic Schreier construction applied to established geometric generators. It does not assert a new finite or simple geometric generating theorem. Merely replacing S by all zero-Johnson words would fail this test; the audited construction does not do so.

## 7. Final coverage and acceptance

**All source genera and both named branches are covered, at the explicit algebraic-generating-family level specified above.** The original “and/or” has not been rewritten as a conjunction; the stronger combined coverage is a conclusion of the separate proofs.

- **g=2, HI₂ and HK₂:** the same ordinary balanced-meridian family B_D generates both, since I₂=K₂. This is one coincident case, not two independent theorems. The proof uses the classical closed Luft kernel, Nielsen rank-two theorem, and a concrete integral section.
- **g≥3, HI_g:** Omori's established theorem descends to the closed model. One fixed HBP map normally generates in the ambient **H_g**; its full H_g-conjugacy family is an ordinary generating family. This is attributed prior work.
- **g≥3, HK_g:** the displayed G(n,w,ε) family is an ordinary generating family, with all indices unrestricted and all correction maps and coefficients explicit. Its optional normal-family formulation takes normal closure in **HI_g**, and includes the finite collection of pairwise correction-lift commutators. It is an application of the Johnson kernel theorem, known HI generation/image results, and the classical Schreier mechanism; novelty is not certified.

The failed separating-meridian candidate is a separate finding: in g≥3 it has infinite index in full HK_g. It is neither needed for the successful construction nor a substitute for it. No result here says that HK_g has trivial outer action. The genus-two question whether separating meridians alone suffice remains a stronger optional question outside the accepted construction.

No unresolved mathematical gap was found in the three final proofs under their stated classical inputs. Acceptance does not assert finite generation, minimality, a short geometric list, a presentation, computational efficiency, or a novel resolution of a currently open problem. This is mathematical acceptance, with no claim of external peer review.

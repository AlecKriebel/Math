# Public sources and attribution

The following public sources were used in the accepted proof and audits. Locators and inspection history are retained from those reports. The package contains no copied source PDFs, extracted source text, or page images. The public build performs no new literature pass and makes no exhaustive novelty claim.

1. Joan S. Birman, *The topology of 3-manifolds, Heegaard distance and the mapping class group of a 2-manifold*, [arXiv:math/0502545v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0502545v3), Problem 9; also book Problem 2.8 in [Problems on Mapping Class Groups and Related Topics](https://math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf). Source scope and closed genus-two equality.
2. Genki Omori, *A small normal generating set for the handlebody subgroup of the Torelli group*, [arXiv:1607.06553v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.06553v1), Theorem 1.2, Figures 1 and 3, equation (2.1), Lemma 3.1. The normal-generation theorem is disk-fixed and g≥3; the proofs here supply its closed transfer.
3. Sebastian Hensel, *A primer on handlebody groups*, [author PDF](https://www.math.lmu.de/~hensel/papers/hno4.pdf), §3 pp.10–11, Theorems 6.3–6.4 p.21, §7 pp.23–24. Full-preimage forgetful maps, closed outer action and Luft kernel, integral homology image.
4. E. Luft, *Actions of the homeotopy group of an orientable 3-dimensional handlebody*, Math. Ann. 234 (1978), 279–292, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01420650). The closed statement was authenticated in Hensel's restatement; this work does not claim independent inspection of Luft's original proof.
5. O. Bogopolski, A. Martino and E. Ventura, *The automorphism group of a free-by-cyclic group in rank 2*, [author PDF](https://www.math.uni-duesseldorf.de/~bogopolski/Artikel/2007_Mar_Ven.pdf), §3 Theorem 3.1 p.8. Exact natural rank-two Aut/Inn/Out statement attributed to Nielsen; Nielsen's original proof was not rechecked.
6. Andrew Putman, *The Johnson homomorphism and its kernel*, [arXiv:0904.0467v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.0467v4), Theorem A, §2.4 and Lemma 2.8; genus-two discussion p.4. Closed integral target, separating-twist kernel, bounding-pair formula, and capping diagram.
7. Shigeyuki Morita, *Casson's invariant for homology 3-spheres and characteristic classes of surface bundles I*, [public scan](https://margalit.droppages.net/classes/math8803/references/MoritaI.pdf), Lemma 2.5, printed pp.310–312. Integral disk-fixed handlebody Johnson image; the closed quotient follows by capping.
8. Bronisław Wajnryb, *Mapping class group of a handlebody*, Fundamenta Mathematicae 158 (1998), 195–228, [publisher PDF](https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/fm/fm158/fm15831.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4064/fm-158-3-195-228), Definitions 2–3 and Theorem 19, printed p.213. Closed five-generator alphabet for g>1. Only the generation assertion is needed; no blanket acceptance of every presentation relation is implied.
9. Annie Holden, *Johnson homomorphisms and the second rational cohomology of handlebody Torelli groups*, [arXiv:2509.03742v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.03742v1), Proposition 9.1, pp.26–27. Image comparison; its contraction projection involving 1/(g−1) is not used as an integral section.
10. Annie Holden, *Abelianizations of finite-index subgroups of the handlebody group*, [arXiv:2607.07587v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.07587v1), §6.2, Proposition 7.3, Conjecture 7.1, Theorem D. Established closed image attribution to Morita; the separating-meridian proposal is explicitly conjectural. The inspection on 14 October 2026 recorded only v1, submitted 8 July 2026. This dated observation makes no claim about subsequent versions or later publication status.
11. Annie Holden, *The Birman–Craggs–Johnson homomorphism and the handlebody Torelli group*, [arXiv:2607.25191v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2607.25191v1), Theorem A. Nearby image/cohomology results are distinguished from full subgroup generation.
12. W. Pitsch, *Trivial cocycles and invariants of homology 3-spheres*, [arXiv:math/0605725v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0605725v1), General conventions. Its g≥3 restriction is not extrapolated to genus two.

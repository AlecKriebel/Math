## Accepted results

For every integer n>=5, Ferudun's Theorem D proves Fuchs Conjecture 2.3, including the stronger four-vertex criterion and the clockwise type pattern A_0,A_1,...,A_(n-3),A_0. Theorem F refutes both the bare-existence and infinitude formulations of Conjecture 2.4: exactly A_0 points belong to infinitely many reachable polygons; each other type belongs to at most one. The explicit reachable point w_n=2+3zeta+zeta^2, zeta=exp(2pi i/n), belongs to none.

The setting is linear SL(2,R) images fixing the distinguished origin, in the original normalized sector. Types use Fuchs's coherent segment-parity and diagonal convention. The isolated unconditional A_0 difference-angle shortcut is false. No translated-polygon interpretation, n=3,4 conclusion, all-seven-conjecture audit or acceptance of Theorem E is claimed.

A separate elementary argument at n=6 proves that the original-lattice point (5,4) belongs to no reachable hexagon, independently of type ambiguity, D/F or the all-n theorem. Both complete audit arguments and all analytical constructions are retained; no mathematical correction was required.

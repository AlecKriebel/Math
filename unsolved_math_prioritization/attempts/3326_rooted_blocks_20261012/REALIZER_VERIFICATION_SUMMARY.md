# Root-5 wedge incidence-order certificates

The authored theorem and its scope are in [ROOT5_DIMENSION_PROOF.md](ROOT5_DIMENSION_PROOF.md). [EXACT_CERTIFICATES.json](EXACT_CERTIFICATES.json) contains complete three-order certificates for two and three blocks, together with full pair witnesses. The historical exact verification checks every ordered pair and finite instances of the proved uniform four-order formula. Its controls replay positives and five genuine negative controls under normal Python, -O, and -OO.

The two historical verification procedures use only the Python standard library; their executable programs are excluded from this prose edition. The SciPy search used to discover the finite orders is private and unnecessary for verification. Its k=4 run timed out without a feasible solution; that timeout has no evidentiary role. The dimension lower bound for k>=4 is proved analytically, and is not a triangle nonrepresentability claim.

The theorem does not settle the general triangle-representation problem. The source five-vertex example and published three-order realizer are credited in the mathematical report and SOURCE_IDENTITIES.json. No claim of literature novelty is made for the wedge theorem.

Two accepted advances are recorded here.

1. An explicit exponential specialization of the published reversed-order coordinate construction, followed by radial normalization, realizes the full nerve of every finite covered hypergraph from a length-d incidence realizer in R^(d-1). The full nerve is exactly the downward closure of its edges; for a Sperner hypergraph its maximal faces are exactly the listed edges. The direct minimum-radial-lift argument supplies the previously omitted deduction. Source credit remains with Ossona de Mendez's coordinate construction and the published convex consequence of de Fraysseix, Ossona de Mendez and Rosenstiehl.
2. For a finite covered loopless linear hypergraph of incidence dimension at most three, nondegenerate rational filled triangles suffice whenever every surviving vertex-node in its incidence graph 2-core has degree at most three. Degrees from attached trees are unbounded. A complete whole-edge backbone argument, rational pendant graft, and uniform connected nonplanar dimension-three family are supplied.

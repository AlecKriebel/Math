# Result: probability-only tail equality remains unresolved after five turns

**Proposed disposition: unsolved,5/5 substantive author turns, pending independent review.** This is a scoped partial packet. It does not give the probability-only proof requested for every stationary finite-alphabet process.

## Exact original scope

Jeffrey Steif's Question C in Oberwolfach Report11/2020, printed632 (report published2021), asks for a proof that the remote-past and remote-future sigma fields of a stationary finite-spin process coincide modulo null sets, using only probability theory and no entropy theory. A standard entropy-based equality theorem is already known. Ergodicity, finite Markov memory, mixing and reversibility are not source assumptions. Bilateral tails are a separate issue and can be larger.

Primary source: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46847

## Complete scoped results

1. TURN_1.md: an exact L² projection-defect reformulation; a probability-only finite stationary Markov classification of all three tails by recurrent class and periodic phase, including mixtures and zero-mass transient states; weak-limit and infinite-alphabet coding-limit obstructions.
2. TURN_2.md: for finite hidden-Markov observations, all three tails are generated by the quotient of hidden phases having the same conditional observation law. An invariant-column proof shows that words of length at most s−1 suffice for s supported deterministic hidden states. Finite stochastic emissions are handled by the joint-state construction, with its corresponding state-count bound.
3. TURN_3.md: a complete-connections kernel with uniform overlap v_0<1 and summable total-variation continuity has a unique stationary compatible law if one exists, and both one-sided tails are trivial. An explicit uniform remote-future coupling bound and a genuine infinite-memory example are supplied. No bilateral conclusion is drawn from this mixing criterion.
4. TURN_4.md: finite-mean finitary codes give corresponding past, future and bilateral tail inclusions via decisive-block decoders. Two-sided finite-mean finitary isomorphisms transfer equality. Mere almost-sure finitariness need not provide integrability, as an explicit variable-radius code shows.
5. TURN_5.md: arbitrary standard-Borel mixtures of conditionally mixing, common-D-stationary laws have both tails generated by their random conditional path law, with no uniform mixing rate or parameter identifiability assumption. Bilateral equality needs a separate component hypothesis. A beta-Bernoulli example and an explicit binary irrational-rotation reconstruction demonstrate non-atomic common tails and the failure of an automatic conditional-mixing reduction.

The original unrestricted gap is the same in every result: no valid entropy-free reduction places all stationary finite-alphabet processes in the proved subclasses or forces the general projection defect to vanish. No claimed original counterexample, general representation theorem or novelty certification is made.

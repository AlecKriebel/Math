# Competing FPP with vanishing-density high-degree seeds

The [candidate proof](CANDIDATE.md) gives an affirmative existence result in the original source’s explicitly allowed growing-seed regime. For every unbounded finite-variance degree law supported on integers at least two and every fixed positive rate disadvantage, degree-only seeds of diverging minimum degree can have vanishing vertex and half-edge density while capturing almost every vertex in probability.

The deterministic threshold grows sufficiently slowly. The stronger type starts uniformly within a fixed low-degree class. Single-hub/fixed-budget thresholds and adversarial graph-aware placement are outside this conclusion. **Candidate, two approaches; separate adversarial AI review passed.** Existing branching-exploration and random-regular growing-seed results are credited; priority is unestablished.

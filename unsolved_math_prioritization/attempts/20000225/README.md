# A tangency obstruction to a proposed irreducible Harbourne construction

## Accepted bounded result

Over the complex numbers, graph curves of bidegrees (1,a) and (b,1), for positive a,b with ab>1, have total tangency excess at most 2a+2b−4. Consequently a pair of bidegrees (1,n),(n,1) cannot have n distinct contacts of intersection multiplicity n when n≥5, even without symmetry. This blocks the graph-pair construction proposed in Orevkov §1.3 in exactly the range where its hypothetical plane-curve family would have proper-point Harbourne constant below −2.

The first blocked construction route would have degree 26 and 30 ordinary quintuple points, with hypothetical h=−37/15; its five order-five contacts require 20 units against a budget of 16.

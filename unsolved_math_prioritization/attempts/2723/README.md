# KP-1.64: published twofold branched-cover counterexamples

Problem 2723 / KP-1.64, checked 9 October 2026 UTC. This theoretical source-audit edition verifies precise consequences of existing published mathematics. It does not resolve the full three-part problem or claim a new original result.

## Accepted scope

Let T be a positively transverse oriented link in the standard contact sphere, and equip each cyclic branched cover with its canonical induced contact structure.

1. **Clause (a) is refuted.** A Stein-fillable induced double contact branched cover does not force T to be transversely isotopic to a quasipositive braid closure.
2. **The twofold instance of clause (c) is refuted.** Stein fillability of that double cover does not force slice-Bennequin sharpness for the given transverse representative. Consequently a blanket formulation allowing order two is also false.
3. **Clause (b) is not settled here.** Its hypothesis requires a Stein-fillable induced cyclic cover for some order n > 2. The inspected sources and this audit do not establish a counterexample or the implication. A separately imposed higher-order-only version of clause (c) is likewise not settled here. These are bounded audit conclusions, not claims of global openness.

A single explicit witness for the accepted negative conclusions is the positive transverse closure of

\[
 b_6=(\sigma_1\sigma_2)^6\sigma_1^5\sigma_2^{-21}\in B_3.
\]

It is a knot with exponent sum -4 and self-linking number -7. Its induced contact double branched cover is Stein fillable. Its entire oriented smooth knot type is nonquasipositive, by a separate Hayden/generalized-Jones argument; nonquasipositivity of one braid alone would not establish that stronger statement. Since \(2g_4(K)-1\ge-1>-7\), the slice-Bennequin inequality is strictly unsharp for this transverse representative.

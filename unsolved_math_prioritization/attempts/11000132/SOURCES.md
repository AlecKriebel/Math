## Current-literature access limitation

The complete 23-page text was not retrieved. Its scope cannot be exhaustively assessed from its preview, and this package does not certify the absence of a later solution.

Penner also credits Haas–Susskind's genus-two four-parameter train-track formulas. That original paper was not retrieved, so we cite only the source's historical attribution and do not use an unverified theorem from it.

## Precise distinctions

The requested operation is component counting for an admissible weighted curve/arc family. Serial splitting is already acknowledged by Penner. AHT and Lackenby supply strong existing algorithms in normal/standard descriptions; an unstated conversion bound from arbitrary Dehn–Thurston input is not assumed. The expanded two-involution closed-case formula is prior meander machinery; the boundary correction is proved directly. The negative meander theorem concerns a gcd of two homogeneous integer polynomials, not arbitrary closed-form expressions.

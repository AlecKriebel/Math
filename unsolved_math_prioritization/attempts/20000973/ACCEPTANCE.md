## Accepted result

Anonymous, *An exact criterion for symmetry-respecting critical-group torsors*, version 0.2.0-candidate, supplies a correct necessary-and-sufficient finite criterion for an Aut(G)-compatible simply transitive Jac(G)-action on the spanning trees of a finite connected graph, using the graph-induced action on Jac(G). The cohomology-class count and actual-action-map count are correct. The simple generalized-theta classification and the simple cactus construction are also correct.

## Required limits

- This is acceptance of the exact minimum automorphism-equivariant existence formulation. The recorder's 2013 Problem 8 says that naturality should at least respect automorphisms; it leaves possible stronger requirements unspecified. [Primary question, PDF page 4](https://www.samuelfhopkins.com/docs/aim_chip-firing_problems.pdf).
- General isomorphism-compatible selection follows after choosing an action on each isomorphism-class representative. A uniquely preferred action, minor compatibility, or a polynomial-time algorithm does not follow.
- The criterion improves the organization, counting and obstruction analysis of a finite problem. Mere finite decidability was already immediate by enumerating bijections.
- The affine reduction, fixed-point obstruction and unicyclic positive construction are acknowledged prior ingredients. This audit establishes no historical novelty or priority.
- The manuscript remains publicly labeled anonymous, AI-assisted and unrefereed. This audit is also AI-assisted, not human peer review or formal verification.

## Disposition

The prior-candidate correctness uncertainty is resolved in favor of bounded acceptance. The complete proof audit supplies the justification for accepting that minimum result.

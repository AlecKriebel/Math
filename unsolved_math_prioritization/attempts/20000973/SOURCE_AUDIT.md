## Ancillary bibliography and scope checks

These sources provide context rather than dependencies of the audited criterion or family proofs. Their titles, abstracts and indicated subject scopes were checked; their full proofs were not audited here. Initial DOI-tool requests for four items failed, after which accessible primary arXiv records were used. These retrieval failures did not block any accepted theorem.

- Baker and Wang, *The Bernardi process and torsor structures on spanning trees*: the abstract explicitly works with ribbon graphs and removes choices in the planar ribbon case. https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.5084
- Shokrieh and Wright, *Torsor Structures on Spanning Trees* in the arXiv record, published as *On Torsor Structures on Spanning Trees*: concerns comparison of two torsor actions on ribbon graphs. https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.10370 and https://epubs.siam.org/doi/10.1137/22M149154X
- Ganguly and McDonough, *Rotor-Routing Induces the Only Consistent Sandpile Torsor Structure on Plane Graphs*: the abstract specifies plane graphs and deletion-contraction consistency, with four related consistent algorithms. https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.15079
- Ding, McDonough, Tóthmérész and Yuen, *A Consistent Sandpile Torsor Algorithm for Regular Matroids*: the paper's introduction describes circuit-cocircuit signature data and a precise consistency question. https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.03999 and https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0195669825001076
- Baker, Ding and Kim, *The Jacobian of a regular orthogonal matroid and torsor structures on spanning quasi-trees of ribbon graphs*: the current arXiv record is version 3, revised 18 June 2026. Its abstract changes the spanning objects to quasi-trees and the group to the orthogonal-matroid Jacobian. https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.08796

These inspections support the candidate's scope distinctions. They do not prove that no other historical source already contains the exact criterion or a family classification. The correct novelty verdict remains unestablished.

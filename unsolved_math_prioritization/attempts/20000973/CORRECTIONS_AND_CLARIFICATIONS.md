## Recommended clarifications

1. Keep the phrase “minimum Aut(G)-equivariance requirement” wherever a summary says the 2013 question is answered. Do not replace it with an unqualified claim that all naturality requirements are solved.
2. Explain that isomorphism-functorial selection is independent of the transporting isomorphism after an action is chosen on a representative. It does not make that representative's choice unique or satisfy additional graph-morphism axioms.
3. In a reduced-Laplacian implementation, explicitly use e_{g(v)}-e_{g(q)} when an automorphism moves the coordinate sink q. Permuting only nonsink coordinates is not the induced action in general.
4. For the Smith-form calculation, record the well-definedness condition M D_in Z^p contained in D_out Z^q. The matrices in the proof satisfy it; including it prevents misuse with mixed cyclic moduli.
5. Specify again that A is fixed when counting action maps. The formula does not divide by automorphisms of A. Equivariant torsor isomorphisms preserve each acting group element.
6. In multigraph language, graph automorphisms include incidence-preserving edge permutations. The two-vertex n-parallel-edge obstruction uses n>=3. Removing loops from the Laplacian and tree computations does not authorize enlarging the prescribed automorphism group.
7. Cite the original question as Problem 8 on physical PDF page 4 of the recorder copy. The source preface says its speaker remarks are summaries rather than direct quotations.
8. Distinguish Wagner's ordinary permutation-representation obstruction from an obstruction to every affine twist. The candidate does this correctly; shortened summaries must retain the distinction.
9. Avoid describing finite enumeration as a new decidability theorem. The substantive accepted outputs are simultaneous cohomological feasibility, counts, constructions and the stated family classifications.
10. Repair the missing space before the “Unrefereed proof candidate” marker and give stable labels to displayed equations. The raw Markdown refers to equation numbers without visible numbered display labels. These are editorial issues only.

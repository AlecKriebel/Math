## Accepted mathematical scope

Acceptance concerns only the prior manuscript's exact minimum
full-Aut(G)-equivariant torsor criterion, cohomology/action counts and stated
simple generalized-theta and cactus theorems. No stronger unspecified
naturality, efficient algorithm or historical priority is established. No
mathematical correction is required. This is an AI-assisted, unrefereed
internal audit, not human peer review or formal proof-assistant certification.

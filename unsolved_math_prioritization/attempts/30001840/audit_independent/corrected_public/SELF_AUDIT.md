# Author self-audit and finite-check limits

This is an author self-check, not an independent acceptance report.

## Failure modes checked

- The original report's curve has a minus sign on 5x^3. Alternate slides/snippets can show a different sign convention; the report PDF is the governing source.
- The base field matters. The geometric generic, arithmetic generic over K(t), arithmetic generic over Q(t), and arithmetic rational-fiber images are not interchangeable.
- The residual result at three is exceptional; the prime five is excluded from the residual theorem used here. The integral lifting argument needs unramified coefficient rings and p>=5 and is applied only to ell>=7.
- Surjective prime-ideal projections at split primes are not enough. The squared infinity traces rule out the Goursat graph, including projective central quotients.
- The first congruence layer is explicitly obtained from arbitrary unipotent lifts; subsequent layers follow by powering and closedness. An open subgroup alone would not prove equality.
- The Q-arithmetic target is an RM normalizer. Over K it is the centralizer with scalar determinant. Cyclotomic surjectivity uses K ramification at 5 and excludes ell=5.
- Infinity is a rational Weierstrass point, not a rational nonzero two-torsion class. The generic invariant subspace calculation and the t=0 contrast are both supplied.
- The coarse moduli argument uses only the nonzero invariant A and variation of C, with an explicit isomorphism for t and -t. It does not assert a Shimura-curve characterization.
- The root-polynomial discriminant and the singular fibers t=+/-2 agree. The counterexample t=0 is smooth.
- Finite cardinalities for an abstract matrix group, especially modulo 5, do not bind an integral lattice in this Jacobian. They are not asserted to settle an unproved Tate image.
- Cadoret--Moonen openness and Lang--Lang group-index formulas retain their hypotheses and do not silently fill the packet's gaps.

## Reproduction

Run: python3 checks.py

The program uses only the Python standard library. It enumerates the groups generated by the stated U,V over O/pO for p=2,3,5,7, checks determinants of every enumerated element, checks (UV)^5=-I, checks each cardinality, and records the complete element-order distribution for p=3. It also performs 40 exact rational substitution controls for the Chebyshev identity and counts the affine/dihedral permutations. The output is deterministic and was rerun byte-for-byte against FINITE_CHECKS.json.

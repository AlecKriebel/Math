# Source and scope audit

All retrieval and inspection below occurred on 2026-10-07. SOURCE_MANIFEST.json binds the actual downloaded PDF bytes, not a claim that every future download will be byte-identical.

- The full official Oberwolfach report was retrieved through the public TIB repository. The complete Kohel contribution and references, printed 1998--2000, were text-inspected; printed 1998 was also raster-inspected. The speaker is David Kohel. The report is 35/2011. Its questions distinguish moduli image, fixed levels, and Tate-module image, and explicitly use the absolute Galois group of Q in the arithmetic discussion. They give no rational value of t or quantifier class of fibers. The following source paragraph says the geometric and first arithmetic aspects are easily characterized; it does not announce that every level or integral image is unresolved.
- Tautz--Top--Verberkmoes (1991) supplies the family and RM construction. Theorem 1 and Sections 2--3 were inspected. The prime parameter of that construction is 5 here; it is not the varying Tate prime ell. The correspondence is defined over the real cyclotomic subfield. No assertion that all endomorphisms are Q-rational is imported.
- Darmon--Mestre (2000), printed 304--311, was retrieved in full. Definitions, Propositions 1.1, 2.1, 2.2 and 3.1, their relevant proofs, and the separate Section 4 construction were inspected. Printed 305 and 308 were raster-inspected. Their C_1(s) agrees with our curve under s=-t/2; their C_2 is a different sextic. Their standing exclusions p !=2,r become ell !=2,5. The (p,r)=(3,5) projective exception belongs to C_1 and must be retained. Section 4's twists of C_2 are not statements about our generic arithmetic image.
- Takei's March 2014 thesis was retrieved in full. Chapter 4, especially Proposition 4.1.1 and its normalization, was inspected; printed 124 (PDF page 127) was raster-inspected. Only its invariant formulas are used in the proof. Later period-coordinate calculations are not imported without checking. His 2016 published paper was also retrieved and its introduction and Theorem 2.11 scope inspected; it concerns prime-ideal congruence quotients of specified triangle groups.
- Kucharczyk's 2015 thesis was retrieved in full. Section 4.3, Propositions 4.2--4.3, were inspected in text and on printed/PDF page 42. This verifies precisely the finite-group automorphism input used in split-prime independence.
- Cadoret--Moonen arXiv:1508.06426 was retrieved in full; the abstract, introduction and Theorem A were inspected. The theorem is a conditional integral/adelic open-image statement, with an additional Hodge-maximality hypothesis for its adelic conclusion. It is not an exact image computation or an automatic uniform theorem for all fibers.
- Lang--Lang arXiv:1401.0775 was retrieved in full; the introduction and Section 6 formulas were inspected. This is highly relevant later work on congruence indices in the specified Hecke group H_5, including powers of 2 and 3 and levels prime to 6. The current packet does not establish the necessary exact identification with the Jacobian's integral polarized monodromy lattice. Therefore the report makes no claim that its own remaining bad-prime gaps are globally open.

No checked source was identified as a full parameter-dependent arithmetic-fiber solution of Kohel's broad question. This is a bounded literature finding, not an exhaustive proof of current open status.

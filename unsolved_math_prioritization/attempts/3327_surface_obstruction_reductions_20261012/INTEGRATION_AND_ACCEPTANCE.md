The [structural report](STRUCTURAL_REPORT.md) and [independent audit](INDEPENDENT_AUDIT.md) retain the complete authored proofs. The proof target requires all proper minors, including deletion and contraction. Normalized positive nonorientable genus is distinguished throughout from Euler genus and from conventions assigning zero to trees.

The accepted equivalence is existence of a target if and only if existence of a positive orientably simple Euler-critical ordinary graph or a positive ordinary Euler hopper. Doubling is conditional on such a hopper. No terminal-labelled restricted hopper class is silently substituted for the ordinary all-proper-minor definition.

The global necessary conditions are triangle-freeness, minimum degree four, h≥2, and at least nine vertices. The h=2 frontier is 3-connected, 4-regular, triangle-free and K_(2,3)-free, of order at least 12, with each edge in two or three squares and some edge in three. The all-two-square case is excluded; a compatible global face choice for the mixed case remains unproved. These are necessary conditions, not sufficient conditions.

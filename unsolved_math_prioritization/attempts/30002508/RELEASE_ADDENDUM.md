## Independently verified endpoint strengthening

The audit proves N₁ = E₁ with the notation of the frozen proof. Here is its argument for convenient reference.

For f ∈ N₁, let C = C(f), and let F(t) = Ct − f(t) above 1 and F(t) = 0 below 1. Then Tf = K almost everywhere above 1 for a constant K. Finite Möbius inversion gives

F(t) = Σ_(n≤t) Tf(t/n) = K floor(t)

almost everywhere. The cancellation identity is Σ_(d|k) μ(d) = 0 for k > 1 and 1 for k = 1. Exceptional null sets remain null under the countably many dilations involved. Consequently

f(t) = (C − K)t + K{t}.

Square integrability against dt/t² forces C = K: a nonzero linear term cannot be canceled at infinity by a bounded fractional-part term. The below-1 restriction then gives f = Ke₁ on the whole half-line. Hence N₁ = E₁. From E₁ ⊆ E_(1+) ⊆ N₁, one gets E_(1+) = E₁ and D(1+) = D(1).

This supplements the author's already established strict inequality D(λ) < D(1) for λ > 1. It does not prove continuity at arbitrary λ > 1 or compare D(λ) and D(ν) for arbitrary 1 < λ < ν.

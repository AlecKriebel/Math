## 1. Exact problem

M. Balazard, section 8 of the problem session, in A. Defant, M. Maestre, E. Saksman, and K. Seip (organizers), *Dirichlet Series and Function Theory in Polydiscs*, Oberwolfach Reports 11 (2014), 335–393; Report 06/2014, printed p. 390.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2014/06
- Publisher landing page: https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/12866
- Publisher PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46499?nt=1
- Catalogue URL attempted first: https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/30002508

The catalogue URL could not be fetched. The supplied record was checked against the official report's online PDF text. It asks both continuity and strict decrease of the distance with a continuous interval of allowed dilations. The report uses the weighted half-line Hilbert space. Replacing span by closed span leaves the distance unchanged. The analysis takes the requested domain to be lambda > 1 and labels lambda = 1 as an endpoint extension.

The official report was available as online text. A local source PDF was not retrieved, and the rendered-page check was unavailable. No source PDF or extracted full text is redistributed in this packet.

## 2. Closely related, but different, theorem

Nicolas Jousse, *Étude d'un problème de continuité lié à l'hypothèse de Riemann*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 55(4) (2005), 1373–1410.

- DOI and journal record: https://numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.2127/
- Official full paper: https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.2127.pdf
- Relevant locations: introduction pp. 1373–1375; definitions and Mellin identity p. 1402; Theorem 7 and Corollary 1 pp. 1404–1405.

The fixed-tuple theorem and the strictly decreasing term-count error concern a finite number of freely chosen parameters. Theorem 7 applies after t = 1/x and theta = 1/a. It supplies no uniform control as the number of parameters tends to infinity. Our use is limited to continuity of each finite-n optimization; the passage to the full distance remains a gap. The global term-count inequality is not used as an interval-cutoff inequality.

## 3. Literature and identity controls

Targeted searches used combinations of Nyman–Beurling, distance, continuity, strict monotonicity, strictly decreasing, and Balazard, and searches for the Jousse paper. No primary publication resolving both exact cutoff questions was identified. This is a bounded search result, not a comprehensive proof that no resolution exists.

The official 2014 report still posed the cutoff questions after the 2005 finite-tuple result. This chronological check is consistent with the mathematical distinction; it does not establish the current global literature status.

Related catalogue subjects involving a real-variable proof of Nyman's criterion, autocorrelation, or an invariant Dirichlet-series subspace are different targets. No transfer of a claimed resolution from those subjects is made.

# Published theorem and priority qualification

As of 18 October 2026, the current reviewed result for problem 30001321 /
OWR-4081-005 is the research note **Coarse endpoint concentration at every
diverging scale for one-dimensional space-time random walks**, Alec Kriebel,
[DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23445342](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23445342).
The record includes the paper and a portable verification archive.

For each fixed iid space-time nearest-neighbor environment law on the line
with 0<p<1 almost surely, the paper proves full coarse L1 convergence of
quenched versus annealed endpoint laws with superpolynomial environmental
fixed-epsilon tails, for every deterministic diverging real interval length
and deterministic alignment. Constants depend on the fixed law and thresholds
may depend on the scale sequence. This is not a uniform assertion as the law
approaches deterministic arrows, a static or correlated-environment theorem,
or an assertion for environment-selected partitions.

The proof gives an affirmative d=1 assertion contrary to the printed
negative conjecture in Berger's contribution to the 2009 Oberwolfach report
(DOI 10.4171/OWR/2009/38, pp.2151-2152). This identifies the historical question;
it does not certify that the problem remained open when this note was written.

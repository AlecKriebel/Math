# Exact-source and literature audit

The maintained [Aldous page](https://www.stat.berkeley.edu/~aldous/Research/OP/compact.html) still identifies the topological realization as the remaining task. The entire original seven-page note and all five 2018 slides were read. The last slide was visually checked: its proposed integrated L² distance uses exp(−t). It requests a complete separable state metric and the Feller property; it does not require that particular distance, and does not say the state space must be compact. The original note treats uniform stationary distributions for simplicity and anticipates general reversible stationary probabilities.

The formal finite-chain assumptions used here are irreducibility, reversibility, time normalization G(1)=2 and uniform trace bounds at every positive time. Density-array convergence is convergence of finite collections of stationary-sampled density entries, including diagonal entries, not assumed weak convergence of sample paths. The notes’ informal cutoff dichotomy is not used to replace those explicit assumptions.

Towsner, Limits of sequences of Markov chains, Electron. J. Probab.20(2015), no.77, DOI10.1214/EJP.v20-4188, is the credited measure-theoretic result. The complete available arXiv source was obtained; Definitions2.5–2.6, the common spectral representation, Theorems3.4–3.5 and the Section7 twin-free/saturated discussion were inspected. The positive-time continuity in Definition2.6 is different from the unspecified pinning at time zero in the slides. Our positive theorem explicitly assumes a strongly continuous L² semigroup, and is not advertised as deriving all such regularity from that definition.

Landim, A topology for limits of Markov chains, arXiv1310.3646, develops a path-space topology to handle instantaneous states in metastable limits. Its full PDF was obtained and the abstract/introduction inspected for scope. It is not treated as a proof of the Aldous state-topology conjecture. Searches for subsequent primary treatments did not establish a full solution; this is a bounded source audit, not an exhaustive openness or priority certificate.

The three partial approaches are kept separate: (1) the two-dimensional torus is proved to be a bounded normalized uniform-grid density-array limit and makes the displayed distance infinite; (2) a conditional trace-weighted Hilbert completion yields positive-time continuous densities; (3) an explicit reversible trace-class process makes that completion fail stochastic continuity at an added midpoint. The third example is asserted for the stated semigroup class, without claiming a separately verified finite-uniform-grid approximation of its mixed atomic/torus space. None disproves every possible Feller topology.

No source PDFs or slides are included in the public package. No novelty or human peer-review claim is made.

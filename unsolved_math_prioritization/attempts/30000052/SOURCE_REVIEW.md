## Exact primary question

Erich Novak, *New bounds for the star discrepancy*, reporting joint work with Aicke Hinrichs, in *Discrepancy Theory and Its Applications*, Oberwolfach Report 13/2004, printed pp. 696–699. [Report DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2004/13), [official PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/45858).

The discrepancy definition on printed p. 697 uses normalized indexed counting with weight 1/n and integration over anchored boxes. Printed p. 698 asks the displayed expected-p-th-moment comparison after defining the shifted generalized lattice with continuously integrated generator and shift. The p-discrepancy discussion specifies even p. The source is a question, not a theorem asserting the comparison.

## What is and is not settled

A single counterexample settles the universally quantified comparison negatively. The self-contained proof supplies n=3,d=1,p=4, and an independent geometric lower-bound proof supplies n=4,d=1,p=4. The p=2 equality and n<=2 equality are consistency checks, not substitutes for the full target. Results about the existence of carefully selected good lattices or other weighted discrepancies are not contradicted.

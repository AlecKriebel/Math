# Literature correction and exact stopping point

The original [Erickson problem page](https://jeffe.cs.illinois.edu/open/comb.html) already contains a November 1999 update reporting the superlinear neighborly-cubical construction. Accordingly, treating the 2N subquestion as unresolved is incorrect. The historical page also warns through its index that it is not evidence of present-day openness.

A stronger checked convex-polytope lower bound is due to Nevo, Santos and Wilson: triangular-bipyramid facets can occur in order N^(3/2). In their [2014 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1408.3501), Theorem 4.6 provides a regular subdivision on 2kl+2+l^2 points, for odd k, with (2k-2)l^2 bipyramidal cells. Corollary 4.8 lifts this to convex 4-polytopes. Taking k=l gives the exponent. The paper's quadratic construction concerns polyhedral spheres; its introduction explicitly leaves the convex-polytope quadratic question open. The published article is [Many triangulated odd-dimensional spheres](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00208-015-1232-x), online 2015, volume 364 (2016), pages 737–762. We do not infer that all other facets in the lifted polytope are simplicial, or claim a quasisimplicial construction on this evidence alone.

The recent [Avvakumov–Hubard preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.18047), v1 (March 2025), constructs cubical spheres with order N^(5/4) facets. Its theorem is about sphere cubulations, and supplies no asserted convex realization of those examples. Its introduction still identifies a subquadratic upper bound as a challenge even with convexity. This does not prove a complete 2026 literature survey, nor settle the general extremal question.

Stopping point: the explicit subquestion is settled by prior work; the full sharp maximum is not determined here. The verified bounds include a convex lower exponent 3/2 and a quadratic upper bound. No sharp formula, improved exponent, novelty, or global current-status theorem is claimed. Search absence is not mathematical evidence of openness. The obsolete inherited triage was literature-only and did not disqualify this investigation under the prior-attempt gate.

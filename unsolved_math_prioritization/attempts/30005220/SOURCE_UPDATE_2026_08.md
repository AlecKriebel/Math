# Additive primary-source update

During independent review, a newer primary source was identified:

Nguyen N. Hung, *On the p-rationality of Deligne–Lusztig characters*,
[arXiv:2608.17871v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.17871v1), 18 August 2026.

Section 4.2 explicitly continues to discuss the Navarro–Tiep Sylow-restriction
assertion as a conjecture. Theorem 4.5 establishes an additional case for finite
reductive groups at their defining characteristic. This is credited prior work,
not a result of this packet. The theorem does not cover arbitrary finite groups.

The source's displayed root subscripts are not used to redefine the target.
The precise formulation remains the extension-index statement in Navarro's
original Conjecture A, OWR 39/2022, p.2276, and Navarro–Tiep's 2021 Conjecture C.
In particular, binary conductor equality alone is not substituted for the
required equality of fields.

This update supersedes only the frozen README's date of the latest inspected
source. The five mathematical attempts, their scope, and the unsolved status
are unchanged. No exhaustive present-day literature or novelty claim is made.

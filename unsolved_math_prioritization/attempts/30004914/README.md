# Two local Exact Matching proof objections

This proof-only edition preserves two independently reviewed mathematical objections to Yuefeng Du's *Bipartite Exact Matching in P*, arXiv:2604.01571v3 (9 April 2026). It is associated with record 30004914 / OWR-8415356-025, duplicate 30004915 / OWR-8415356-026, and related record 3000034 / AMR-029-0034.

## Accepted scope

1. An actual four-row graph-cofactor support family satisfies the stated hypotheses of Theorem 4.4 but violates its final coprimality clause and the valuation conclusion of Corollary 4.6(ii). With u=lambda+2, the surviving functional is -6 lambda^2 u, the generalized Vandermonde determinant is -6u, and their quotient is lambda^2. The factorization itself holds in this example.
2. A colored six-cycle has feasible red-count set {1}, while each of its two tight-cut contraction factors has target set {0,1}. Independent addition produces the false targets 0 and 2. This refutes the independent target-sum inference in Proposition 2.3 under the standard brace convention; the same original cut-edge identity must be retained when gluing.

[AUDIT.md](AUDIT.md) contains the complete written constructions, calculations, hypothesis checks, source pinpoints, correct one-cut compatibility relation, and every limitation. Its two mathematical sections are unchanged from the accepted independent review. Only its introductory and concluding administrative framing and derived-text metadata were edited for this standalone edition.

## Limits

The graph-cofactor exhibit is not matching covered or a brace, and no realization as an actual index-1 brace patch is certified. The universal source statements under objection impose no such hypotheses. The six-cycle result uses the standard convention in which C4 is a brace; the audit explicitly discusses the malformed subset range in the manuscript's literal Definition 3.1.

Neither exhibit is an ASNC counterexample. This work does not disprove the existence of a deterministic polynomial-time Exact Matching algorithm, certify a repaired polynomial-time algorithm, or establish a Lean-kernel inconsistency. No formalization repository was accessed or built. No broader mathematical audit, all-claims review, historical novelty, human peer review, or formal proof-assistant certification is asserted. The original research target remains unresolved by this work.

# Five-approach record

1. **Direct angular coverage.** Proved escape for at most two mirrors and the shadow-length criterion. Constructed a disjoint four-mirror arrangement that blocks every first leg, so a purely direct-gap argument fails. Proved an open interval of reflected escapes in that arrangement.
2. **Unfolding and coding.** Reconstructed credited countability of endpoint and periodic directions. Derived the explicit L/((k-1)delta) common-prefix bound. Infinite itineraries can still be uncountable; this does not settle trapping.
3. **Conserved observables.** Proved escape for concurrent supports by an exact radial-square identity and parallel supports by conserved tangential velocity. Neither hypothesis holds for general configurations.
4. **Rational approximation.** Verified the exact published rational-angle theorem and its endpoint-convention bridge. Reconstructed the credited open-dense regular-escape consequence. The limit lacks uniform escape time/clearance, so density does not prove universal escape.
5. **Quadratic escape functions.** Classified globally reflection-monotone positive-definite quadratics: their existence forces concurrent supports. This rigorously diagnoses the limit of that strategy rather than claiming nonconcurrent trapping.

Status: unsolved. No further proof-search approach was used. Literature verification and finite checking are not counted as additional proof approaches.

# Limitations

- The full all-configurations, all-directions conjecture is unresolved here. The five-approach budget is exhausted.
- The general regular-source arguments assume the source avoids mirror closures. The original target also permits the source-list ambiguity of endpoint sources; this is explicitly preserved, not silently removed.
- Rational-angle means rational multiples of pi between supporting lines, not rational Cartesian coordinates.
- The concurrent-lines identity proves all regular rays escape. An all-ray version additionally specifies transparent endpoints and straight continuation of grazing rays. No absorbing-endpoint all-ray conclusion is claimed.
- The quadratic obstruction requires positive definiteness and derivative monotonicity for every incident velocity on both sides of every mirror. It is not a no-go theorem for all possible escape proofs.
- Finite exact checks and prefix contraction do not bound the number of infinite itineraries, establish escape for arbitrary irrational-angle arrangements, or rule out exceptional limiting configurations.
- The UnsolvedMath web page returned 403. The primary TOPP page was retrieved, and complete imported files were independently hashed against the repository manifest.
- Only specified original-source pages were visually inspected. Milovich's entire long theorem proof was not independently reconstructed.
- No historical priority or novelty claim is made. No independent review has yet accepted these frozen author artifacts.

# Plane-curve rough-M semicontinuity: scoped counterexample candidate

Problem ID: 30001345. Queue rank: 974. Source question: OWR-4086-003.

The explicit delta-constant family in `PROOF.md` specializes twelve ordinary triple points to one ordinary ninefold point. Its total delta is 36 on every fiber, while the sum of rough invariants \(K(K+D)\) changes from 7 to 12.

**Claim boundary:** a complete negative example for summed rough-M semicontinuity in the unrestricted reduced multibranch formulation. It does not settle the unibranched parametric Conjecture 3.8 of the published Borodzik paper. It makes no fine-M conjecture-resolution or novelty claim. The line arrangement is the classical dual Hesse arrangement.

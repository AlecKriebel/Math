## Mathematical coverage

All checks passed:

- 179 strictly convex polygons tested by the maximum-triangle cap recursion and compatible rainbow-packing construction. The suite includes unique nondegenerate convex hulls of all subsets of a 3-by-3 integer grid, plus specified parabolic polygons.
- 176 polygons had every triangulation enumerated, totaling 2,399 triangulations. Counts matched the Catalan formula and all triangle areas summed exactly to the polygon area. Constructed guarantees never exceeded the exact optimum.
- Triangles, the unit square, and the explicit pentagon and hexagon witnesses had their entire triangulation sets independently regenerated by ear deletion. The enumerations agreed. Witness optima were respectively 1,1,2,2; the pentagon and hexagon area multisets matched REPORT.md.
- The constructive suite included polygons with 30 vertices; these larger cases were not exhaustively enumerated.
- 998 integer n values checked both bound definitions and recurrence/threshold boundaries.
- 30 triangles in two obliquely embedded integer polygons checked the primitive-normal formula, all three coordinate projections, and cube bounds. Every triangulation of those two polygons checked the total-mass inequality.
- An eight-vertex exact rational circular-arc polygon checked the equal-area prescribed-root fan example.
- Seven geometry/input negative controls rejected degeneracy, reverse orientation, repeated vertices, floating-point coordinates, invalid n, Boolean n, and an oversized exhaustive-enumeration request.

These are bounded tests, not universal numerical certification. Universal claims rest on REPORT.md's proofs. The code does not decide t(n), t_L(n,m), or unavoidable triple repetition, and it does not prove novelty.

- Reznik, Garcia and Koiller, arXiv2004.12497v11, pp.1–3 and Table4 p.6: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.12497v11
- Final *Fifty New Invariants*, printed pp.341–343 and Table4 p.346: https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0174.pdf
- Bialy–Tabachnikov, Theorem4.1 pp.1348–1349 and Theorem4.3 pp.1350–1351: https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10408490

Both Table4 images were visually checked. The source quantity is signed-area centroid C2 prime, not neighboring vertex centroid C0 prime. Outer sides are the original tangent lines. The model has a>b>0, confocal elliptic caustic and permits stars. The source formula divides by signed area, making the candidate's exact zero-circle qualification necessary and natural. Bialy–Tabachnikov's existing vertex-centroid theorem is credited but does not alone settle this target. Full PDFs were read from their pinned local copies; hashes are in HASH_CHECK.json. No historical-priority certification is made.

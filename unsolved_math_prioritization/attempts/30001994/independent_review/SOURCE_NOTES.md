# Independent source reading: 30001994

1. Bastian Harrach, joint with Lilian Arnold, “Inverse Eddy Current Problems,” OWR11/2012, printed630–633. The entire contribution was read, and printed631 was independently rendered and inspected. The question concerns extending the ordinary correction map A -> phi_A in the displayed unified formulation. The introductory assumptions allow a nonnegative bounded scalar conductivity with bounded support. The existing theorem adds an essential positive lower bound on the conductor. The OWR line showing the ball inclusion is printed with a reversed inclusion symbol; the complete published framework below makes bounded support explicit. [Official full report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46385)

2. Arnold–Harrach, *A unified variational formulation for the parabolic-elliptic eddy current equations*, SIAM Journal on Applied Mathematics72(2) (2012),558–576, DOI10.1137/110831477. The official author-hosted PDF is the full published version. Sections1–2.1 and the full Lemma3.1/proof, equations3.1–3.5, and Theorem3.2 were read. Printed566–567 were independently rendered and inspected. The weighted space L2_rho uses the spatial-decay factor (1+|x|²)^(-1/2); it is ordinary L2 on compact sets. W(curl) requires this local L2 property in addition to an L2 distributional curl. Lemma3.1 actually constructs an ordinary globally L2 curl-free correction via H1 conductor potentials and a harmonic exterior extension. The proposed extension cannot be justified merely by constructing a vector in a conductivity-weighted completion. [Full published PDF](https://www.math.uni-frankfurt.de/~harrach/publications/eddycurrents.pdf)

3. Pauly–Picard–Trostorff–Waurick, *On a Class of Degenerate Abstract Parabolic Problems and Applications to Some Eddy Current Models*, 2018 report. The introduction and Section4 around Hypothesis4.2 were read. That hypothesis represents sigma by an operator on the conductor that is strictly positive definite; Proposition4.3 then uses the closed range. This is a different degeneracy from conductivity approaching zero inside the conductor and supplies no universal ordinary-gradient extension theorem for the present target. [Full author PDF](https://wwwpub.zih.tu-dresden.de/~dipa615c/pdf/rep_due_maxwell_deg-eddy.pdf)

No reliance is placed on unpublished correspondence, an abstract-only theorem, or a claimed exhaustive novelty search. Source copies and rendered pages are local reading material and are excluded from the portable review.

4. The later Pauly et al. publisher proof was also checked at Assumption4.3 (PDF23), where the same strictly positive conductor operator is explicit. [Publisher proof hosted by an author](https://wwwpub.zih.tu-dresden.de/~dipa615c/pdf/jfa01-proof.pdf)

5. Francini–Franzina–Vessella, *Existence and regularity for eddy current system with non-smooth conductivity*: the full author manuscript was available and its first-page condition(1.1ii) was read. It assumes uniform positive definiteness of the matrix conductivity. Non-smoothness of a uniformly positive coefficient is different from the present smooth coefficient with an essential lower bound of zero. [Primary manuscript](https://cvgmt.sns.it/media/doc/paper/4568/fra-fra-ves.pdf)

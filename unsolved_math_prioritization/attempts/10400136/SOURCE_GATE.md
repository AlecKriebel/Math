## Recovered identity and exact scope

The required starting page, [UnsolvedMath 10400136](https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/10400136), was inaccessible to the web reader and returned HTTP 403 to a direct request. The pinned catalogue recovered the identity; its generic open-status note is not a proof. The source was then retrieved independently from the publisher.

The original heading is **Problem 7.21**, attributed to **S. Baseilhac and R. Benedetti**. “Phase ambiguity of quantum hyperbolic state sums” is our descriptive title, not a missing subtitle recovered from the source. The printed source appears on p. 486, PDF page 114, in §7.4 of [Ohtsuki, *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf). The volume is dated 2002; the article itself records publication on 1 June 2004.

The preceding page fixes a compact closed oriented 3-manifold W, a nonempty link L⊂W, a flat principal B-bundle ρ, with B the upper-triangular subgroup of SL₂(C), and an odd integer N>1. Decorated triangulations carry a full B-valued cocycle, branching, and integral charge. Their state sums H_N(T) agree up to μ_N, while K_N(W,L,ρ)=H_N(T)^N is an invariant. The problem asks to explain that μ_N ambiguity and, if possible, use suitable extra structure on the triple to refine K_N. The following remark singles out branching and charge as the source of phase data and suggests combings, framings, and spin-type structures. This is a research request, not a single binary conjecture.

A complete positive result here would specify independently meaningful extra data, a computable phase correction or refined state sum, and proofs of presentation independence and compatibility with the original K_N for all triples in this scope. A root selected from K_N by itself, a cusped-only invariant, or a sign correction does not establish that result.

## Primary literature and exact limitations

1. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, *Analytic families of quantum hyperbolic invariants* (2015)**, [published PDF](https://msp.org/agt/2015/15-4/agt-v15-n4-p05-s.pdf). Theorem 1.1(3) removes the sign under its stated hypotheses, retaining μ_N. Proposition 8.2 handles patterns admitting branched triangulations without those weight restrictions. Theorem 8.4 and equations (66)–(68) expose the determinant-normalized scalar in the pentagon calculation. These are genuine advances, not a choice of the remaining Nth-root phase.

2. **Baseilhac–Benedetti, *Non ambiguous structures on 3-manifolds and quantum symmetry defects* (2017)**, [publisher](https://ems.press/journals/qt/articles/15134), [primary preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.01174). Theorem 1.6 and Corollary 1.8 give non-ambiguous cusped refinements modulo μ₄N; Corollary 1.11 normalizes the defect modulo μ₄. Proposition 1.13 and §8.7 are essential: for closed pairs the normalized defect is trivial modulo μ₄, and relative-taut reduced invariants do not distinguish the added structure. The successful cusped construction cannot simply be transferred to the original closed triples.

3. **Garoufalidis–Yu, *A relation between the Baseilhac-Benedetti and the Bonahon-Liu-Wong-Yang invariants* (January 2026)**, [arXiv:2601.03554](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.03554). Remark 3.12, pp. 14–15, reduces the BB mapping-class ambiguity to μ_n and explicitly retains it. The comparison concerns punctured-surface mapping classes with suitable generic decorated characters.

4. **Baseilhac–Ben Aribi, *Volume Conjecture and quantum hyperbolic invariants: the figure eight knot complement* (April 2026)**, [arXiv:2604.16077](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.16077). Page 2 still treats the QHI modulo μ₂N; §3.3, equation (76), retains μ₄N in its factorization. This is contemporary evidence of the retained phase convention, not a theorem that no other refinement can exist.

5. **McPhail-Snyder, *A quantization of the SL₂(C) Chern-Simons invariant of tangle exteriors*, v3 (May 2026)**, [arXiv:2509.02365v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.02365v3). This constructs phase-unambiguous geometric tangle/link invariants. Section 1.6.1, p. 8, states the comparison to BB, with a Chern-Simons-root normalization, as an expectation. Section 1.2 leaves the extension to general 3-manifolds for future work. Equations (1.1) and (1.3) supply the peripheral transformation laws tested in Attempt 5. These results are an important route, not a verified solution in the original generality.

Searches also checked Ishibashi’s *Cyclic Quantum Teichmüller Theory* ([published July 2026](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-026-05604-6)); its projective representation and mapping-torus comparison do not assert the required closed-triple theorem. Searches combined the exact problem number, proposer names, phase anomaly, root of unity, non-ambiguous structures, quantum-hyperbolic refinements, and Chern-Simons normalization. No inspected primary source establishes the full requested refinement. This bounded negative search does not prove that the problem is open in every possible interpretation.

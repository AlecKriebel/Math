# Sources and access qualification

## Primary question and affirmative announcement

1. **Ed Swartz, f-vectors and three-manifold complexity**, OWR 8/2011, printed pp. 409–412. The full contribution was read. Its Theorem 8 already treats three-dimensional boundary finiteness; the final paragraph on p. 412 asks about higher dimensions. The rendered p. 412 was inspected directly. [EMS full report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46323); [MFO full report](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3223/OWR_2011_08.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).
2. **Ed Swartz, Face Enumeration on Manifolds**, OWR 24/2012, printed pp. 1427–1429. The full contribution was read. Rendered pp. 1427 and 1429 verify connectedness, the simplicial versus semi-simplicial distinction, the precise complexity minimum and Theorem 7. This report supplies an exact affirmative statement but not its proof. [EMS full report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46393); [MFO full report](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3295/OWR_2012_24.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

The report years refer to the workshops. The local PDF metadata indicate later production dates; those metadata are not used to infer a separate theorem publication date. The theorem's attribution “S. '11” is the report's own label, not an independently recovered proof.

## Supporting and related literature

3. **Ed Swartz, Topological finiteness for edge-vertex enumeration**, Advances in Mathematics 219(5) (2008), 1722–1728, DOI 10.1016/j.aim.2008.07.010. The full author manuscript dated 4 December 2007 was read, including the proof of Theorem 2.1. It treats connected combinatorial manifolds without boundary. The author's official publication list and Crossref corroborate the corrected citation. The final journal full text was not obtained; the DOI landing page returned an unavailable-site response. [Author manuscript](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~ebs/Edges.pdf); [author publication list](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~ebs/papers.html); [Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.aim.2008.07.010).
4. **Ed Swartz, Thirty-five years and counting**, arXiv:1411.0987v1 (2014), especially §6, pp. 23–25. The full file was retrieved and the relevant boundary discussion read. It records the same boundary invariant and related lower bounds, but no full proof of the desired finiteness result was identified. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1411.0987).
5. **Isabella Novik and Ed Swartz, g-vectors of manifolds with boundary**, Algebraic Combinatorics 3(4) (2020), 887–911, DOI 10.5802/alco.121. The full published PDF was retrieved; the introduction, completion construction, main statements and relevant §7 handle/decomposition discussion were inspected. They do not, by themselves, constitute an independently checked derivation of the exact finiteness theorem in this package. [Published paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/10.5802/alco.121.pdf).
6. **Isabella Novik and Ed Swartz, Face numbers of pseudomanifolds with isolated singularities**, arXiv:1004.5100v1 (2010), later Mathematical Scandinavica 110 (2012), 198–222. The full arXiv file was retrieved and its introduction, organization and relevant theorem statements checked for a possible finiteness argument. No such theorem was identified. Attempts to retrieve the journal PDF returned HTTP 404; no final-typeset proof comparison is claimed. This is a search record, not a dependency of the elementary deductions. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/1004.5100).

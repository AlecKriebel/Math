# Bounded positive boundary-twist factorizations under pairwise intersection at most one

Problem 11000150 / AMR-109-0150, rank 1256. Accepted partial result, substantive attempt 1 of 5.

## Established result

For each fixed genus g >= 1, positive factorizations of the single boundary twist on a compact connected oriented genus-g surface with one boundary component, whose factor curves have pairwise geometric intersection at most one, form finitely many classes modulo simultaneous orientation-preserving boundary-fixing conjugation. In particular their lengths have an attained finite maximum M(g).

Repeated factors are allowed. Disk-bounding twist curves are excluded. Boundary-parallel curves may be included, but occur only as the singleton boundary-twist factorization. Genus is fixed and mapping classes fix the boundary pointwise.

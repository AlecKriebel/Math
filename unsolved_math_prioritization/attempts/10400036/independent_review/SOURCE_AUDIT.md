## The source-transfer warning

The actual printed Proposition 6.11 was inspected, including its “any word” wording and the proof's use of a particular product of conjugated meridians. The split-third-component/Hopf-pair example genuinely distinguishes two free words for one closed-group longitude: the empty word and a commutator have different integral mixed Magnus coefficients. Thus literal lift-independent integer transfer is invalid in that example. This says nothing adverse about the paper's quotient-valued main theorem, where precisely such lower-order ambiguity can disappear. The author instead uses a canonical cut-open longitude and proves the required formula directly. This narrowly stated warning is accepted; a broader allegation would not be supported.

## Conventions and limits

The author's incoming-bottom based string-link convention and positive meridians are consistent throughout. Polyak's drawn downward orientation can be converted by an orientation-preserving rotation of the ambient model; it is not a license to change the basing of the same input and preserve every integer coefficient. In the classical triple comparison, the author explicitly chooses the negative of the ordered surface-intersection orientation, matching the cited minus-triple-point formula. This is a numerical comparison in the pairwise-zero domain, not an all-order isotopy comparison.

The publication disposition supported by this audit is **unsolved, 5/5**, with credited scoped partials and an explicit remaining comparison problem. A complete known algebraic formula is not itself certification of the original recursively derived-link construction.

## Primary inputs and current-status distinctions

- **Ohtsuki (ed.), 2002 problem list, pp. 408–409.** Full publisher PDF recovered, including the surrounding definitions and both numbered demands. A fresh download hash-matches the campaign's earlier source copy. The record's neighboring virtual-knot text is excluded.
- **Polyak, 2005, Skein relations for Milnor's mu-invariants, AGT 5, 1471–1479.** Full primary PDF recovered. The theory treats nonrepeating string-link invariants without lower-vanishing assumptions and provides skein formulas. It credits the canonical meridians/parallels that remove closed-link indeterminacy.
- **Kravchenko–Polyak, 2011, Diassociative algebras and Milnor's invariants for tangles, LMP 95, 297–316.** Full author PDF recovered. Section 2.2 defines the boundary-based invariants; Theorem 4.6 identifies ordered tree counts in Gauss diagrams with appropriate nonrepeating Milnor invariants. This is established combinatorial progress, not automatically the requested iterated-Seifert-linking construction.
- **Mellor–Melvin, 2003, A geometric interpretation of Milnor's triple linking numbers, AGT 3, 557–568.** Full publisher PDF recovered. Its surface formula includes lower-order terms and is stated for closed links modulo the gcd of pairwise linking numbers. That quotient must not be silently replaced with an integer string-link equality.
- **Davis–Nagel–Orson–Powell, 2020, Surface systems and triple linking numbers, Indiana Univ. Math. J. 69, 2505–2547.** Full publisher-format author copy recovered. The paper refines simultaneous triple-linking indeterminacy and gives based C-complex/longitude formulas; Lemma 6.12 constructs a C-complex subordinate to a chosen basing. Its source category and quotient-valued conclusions require care when used for string links.
- **Koytcheff, 2014, The Milnor triple linking number of string links by cut-and-paste topology, AGT 14, 1205–1247.** Primary abstract and bibliographic scope verified. It constructs a configuration-space/Pontryagin–Thom description of the triple invariant. This already provides a different topological interpretation in the triple case. The full PDF retrieval timed out on both publisher and arXiv attempts; only the verified primary abstract is used here, not an unseen proof.
- **Koytcheff–Volić, 2019, Milnor invariants of string links, trivalent trees, and configuration space integrals, Topology Appl. 251, 47–69.** Primary abstract and author summary verified. The paper gives a configuration-integral correspondence and a partial explicit recipe; its existence does not alone identify Polyak's displayed derived-link expression. Full-PDF retrieval timed out; no proof dependency is placed on an unseen full text.
- **Stees, 2026, Milnor's invariants for knots and links in closed orientable 3-manifolds, GT 30, 983–1050.** Full publisher PDF recovered. Its introduction, main-result scope, and comparison sections concern general ambient three-manifolds and lower-central/homotopy data. No theorem answering this exact unrestricted iterated-Seifert presentation was identified in those checked sections.

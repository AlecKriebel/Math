## Approaches and the stopping point

1. **Surgery exactness and map quantifiers.** The exact criterion is proved in Proposition 1. Replacing “some map” by “the initial map” is invalid. Producing a single nonzero invariant is consequently insufficient for a counterexample.
2. **Spherical normal-invariant cancellation.** Proposition 2 proves a restricted consequence with Wh = 0 and a w₂-trivial spherical invariant. The assembly-injective almost-spin case and the cited torsion-free three-manifold-group result supply prior positive cases. The required extra hypotheses do not cover the target's generality.
3. **Descent from stable classification.** Even in the orientation-compatible setting of normal-type bordism, forgetting to the stable classification does not supply a bordism over the unstabilized target M. A stable homeomorphism need not represent the stabilization of the selected f, or of a selected h ∘ f. Neither the needed realization by a simple self-equivalence nor a suitable cancellation theorem was obtained. This is the exact gap, not a proof of impossibility.

Its research entry only provides literature triage, no proof. Its round-carpet/building rigidity remark is not accepted as a proof of co-Hopficity: a rigidity theorem for surjective quasi-isometries does not settle arbitrary quasi-isometric embeddings. No imported claim is promoted on that basis.

We use the usual analytic convention: the Loewner representative is Ahlfors Q-regular and Q-Loewner, Q>1, and belongs to the boundary's quasisymmetric gauge. Where a deduction only assumes attainment of Ahlfors regular conformal dimension, that weaker hypothesis is stated separately. We do not silently equate combinatorial Loewner with analytic Loewner or the visual metric with a specified Loewner representative. Any exceptional Q=1 interpretation requires a separate check; the sphere/circle positive case is elementary.

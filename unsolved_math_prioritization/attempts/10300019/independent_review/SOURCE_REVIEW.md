# Independent source review

Calegari, *Problems in foliations and laminations of3-manifolds*, math/0209081v1, Definitions1.3 and1.9 and Section7 pp13–14 were read. Both target pages were visually checked. Question7.4 and its complete continuation concern unmeasured normal addition and possible spaces of carried laminations; they do not define the proposed monotone-equivalence relation or topology. The neighboring taut-ideal-triangulation question is separate. Source: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0209081

Brittenham's PJM paper was checked at pp217–219. Its normal-disk definition requires compact disk plaques, each meeting any tetrahedron edge at most once. Its general normalization theorem may yield a different lamination. The companion CAG paper, Theorem1 p465, instead isotopes the original essential lamination under the no-holonomy hypothesis. Neither gives simultaneous compatible normalization for an arbitrary pair. Sources: https://msp.org/pjm/1995/168-2/pjm-v168-n2-p02-p.pdf and https://intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cag/1995/0003/0003/CAG-1995-0003-0003-a003.pdf

Schleimer's thesis Section2.6 gives finite normal-coordinate addition and regular exchange, with normal-isotopy uniqueness in that finite setting. Those are credited classical inputs. Source: https://sschleimer.warwick.ac.uk/Maths/thesis.pdf

Hatcher's manuscript explicitly reports its unfinished status. Its sector-weight and complementary-I-bundle construction on pp7–9 was read with that limitation. The packet uses neither an unproved global injectivity statement nor a classification of arbitrary unmeasured holonomy. Source: https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/Papers/ML.pdf

All five source hashes match. Full PDFs, extracted text and rendered pages remain reading inputs. The limited source audit does not certify novelty or exhaustive current historical openness. The source's full unmeasured and topology breadth is retained in the unresolved disposition.

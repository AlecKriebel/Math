# Publication addendum to the preserved freezes

The independent audit passes the scoped authored mathematics without required mathematical repairs. Read this addendum together with [audit/AUDIT.md](audit/AUDIT.md), especially its section 6; no frozen file has been silently corrected.

## Bibliography

Felix Christian Clemen, Adrian Dumitrescu and Dingyuan Liu's journal version is titled *On multiplicities of interpoint distance* (singular), Acta Mathematica Hungarica 177 (2025), 231–245, DOI [10.1007/s10474-025-01562-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10474-025-01562-y). The [arXiv v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.04283v5) title is *On multiplicities of interpoint distances* (plural). The plural journal-title wording retained in the historical author freeze is corrected here. The source hashes, author identities, attribution and substantive theorem usage are unchanged.

## Exposition

In the center m=4 case of Theorem 4, none of the four cyclic gaps exceeds pi: each other gap is at least pi/3, and the four sum to 2pi. In the fully collinear case, any alleged cycle in a fixed-distance graph has a leftmost vertex with at most one neighbor, which establishes acyclicity. These are explanatory expansions, not mathematical changes.

## External draft caution and nondependency

Rafik Zeraoulia's [working draft](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.26754.95684) states an incorrect graph assertion in Lemma 1(2). A four-cycle and a five-cycle sharing exactly one vertex have eight vertices, nine edges, and degree sequence (4,2,2,2,2,2,2,2), contradicting the assertion that there must be two vertices of degree at least three. The independent checker reconstructs this certificate. Its faulty proof bounds the unique high degree by three.

This abstract graph countercontrol does not itself refute a planar realization claim. The draft's displayed n>=2 formulation also needs n>=5, and its n=7 classification input was not independently established here. Neither its claimed n=7 result nor its n=8 reduction is promoted as verified. The authored all-but-one-cocircular theorem does not use the draft, its lemma, or its classification claims.

## Scope remains unresolved

Theorem 4 covers every n>=5 under the n-1-cocircular hypothesis. No novelty is claimed. The exact 61-point example is credited to the CDL construction idea: minimum and second-largest distance multiplicities are 63, while diameter multiplicity is 21. It only obstructs choosing those two extreme candidates; it is not a target counterexample. The general Erdős–Pach problem remains unsolved by this investigation after five substantive approaches.

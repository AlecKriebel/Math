# Acceptance and exact-scope disposition

Problem 30003711 / OWR-15987-022; audit date 2026-10-06.

## Accepted theorem

For X = CP^9 minus the discriminant of singular cubic equations, let Y to X be the cover whose fiber is the set of nine flex points. With ordinary Schwarz genus defined as the least number of open section domains, the candidate proves

- unnormalized genus = 8;
- normalized sectional category = 7.

The upper bound is accepted after independent adversarial review of the full projective Hessian group, all affine linear-part cases, scalar ambiguity, common fixed sheets, nonfree circle isotropy, tubular extension, the dimension-seven coarse quotient, the colored refinement, and the actual covering pullback. The lower bound is the explicitly cited Chen–Wan theorem for the same invariant and coefficient space.

No mathematical patch is required. The frozen author proof has not been altered. The proof uses 1 of the permitted 5 substantive approaches. This audit did not add a new proof-search approach or use finite numerical evidence to replace a universal argument.

## Qualification that must remain attached

The complete supplied record identifies the Chen–Wan 8-versus-9 section-genus question, so that curated target is settled by the accepted theorem. However, the definition immediately preceding Question 5 in the original OWR report literally asks for connected open domains on which the whole cover is trivial. The submitted proof does not establish that stronger requirement or determine its minimum.

This is not merely a terminological worry. The circle orbit with stabilizer containing C=diag(1,1,omega) has monodromy fixing three flexes and cycling the other six in two three-cycles. It admits a one-sheet section but not simultaneous trivialization of all nine sheets. The color domains also need not be connected.

Permitted mathematical description: “Ordinary section-based Schwarz genus is 8, resolving the Chen–Wan 8-versus-9 target; the literal connected/full-trivialization reading of OWR remains outside this result.”

Do not describe this as an unconditional resolution of the stronger literal OWR problem, a formal verification, a proof of a new exact algorithmic branching complexity, or a novelty/priority finding.

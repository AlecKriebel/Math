# Source and scope audit

## Exact target

The complete 74-page primary OWR PDF was retrieved. Valdez's full relevant
contribution, printed pages 885--888, was read, including its definitions,
Question 4, surrounding realization theorems and final remarks. Question 4
on printed page 887 was additionally inspected as a rendered image. It
asks for the prescribed subgroup of GL^+(2,R), with arbitrary closed
Cantor end space, infinite genus, tameness and no planar ends. The witness
uses a nonempty three-point space. The source's separate finite-area lattice
question is not being answered.

The OWR summary classification contains an apparent contraction-sign
misprint in its displayed countable case; Question 4 itself explicitly
requires no contracting elements. The 2017 journal paper's Theorem 0.1 and
Definition 1.14 remove any ambiguity. The proof witness preserves lengths
exactly and is unaffected by that misprint.

## Credited later results and limitations

The full 32-page 2017 RMV journal PDF was retrieved. Its introductory
realization theorems, classification of underlying surfaces, and translation/
tameness definitions were inspected. Its positive results include the
one-ended and Cantor-ended regimes and other specified end spaces; they
are not an arbitrary-end realization theorem.

The full 29-page ARSVW v2 PDF was retrieved, and the explicitly versioned
PDF was separately obtained and matched byte-for-byte. Inspected portions
include the main isometry and Veech statements, topological definitions,
Theorem 2.18 and Corollary 2.19, Definition 3.1 and Section 3.1, and the
proof of Theorem 1.8. Pages 13 and 14 were also rendered and visually
inspected. Its isometry group is exactly the rotational derivative
preimage; its definition has no flat-completeness requirement. Its
self-similar and virtually-cyclic realization results must retain their
hypotheses and cannot be promoted to universal tame realization.

The inspected v2 is dated 23 February 2026. The author's public publication
page states that the paper is to appear in Algebraic & Geometric Topology.
This packet does not claim inspection of a final typeset journal version
or independently certify the journal's publication process.

## Dependency and proof audit

The negative answer is an explicit application of the credited
finite-isometry obstruction. The packet also supplies its underlying
uniformization/normalizer reduction and the concrete finite-end core.
Uniformization and orientable-surface classification remain imported
classical theorems. They are clearly identified, rather than falsely
represented as newly reproved foundations.

Key non-shortcuts checked:

- A Veech group is a derivative image; it need not act on the surface.
  The proof acts with its full rotational preimage instead.
- An infinite derivative image forces an infinite preimage even with a
  nontrivial translation kernel. No splitting is needed.
- Rotational derivatives give holomorphic maps and flat isometries.
  Determinant one by itself does not.
- Flat incompleteness and omitted infinite-angle completion points do not
  invalidate the underlying conformal argument.
- A disjoint image of the connected core would trap it in one end component,
  leaving at least two other ends in one complementary component.
- The rational matrix has infinite order by an all-n congruence proof,
  not a finite numerical search or a plotted irrational angle.
- Three points of the Cantor set satisfy all end-space requirements.
- The claim concerns the prescribed matrix subgroup, not merely its abstract
  isomorphism type.

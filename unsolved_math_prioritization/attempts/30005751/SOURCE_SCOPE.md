# Source scope: finite axiomatizability of TEIP

The exact question is Jeřábek's Question6 on printed p3040 of [OWR53/2023](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/48178), in the complete contribution pp3038–3041. The report was published in2024. The language is L_OR={0,1,+,multiplication,<}, on nonnegative parts of discretely ordered rings. IOpen includes induction for all quantifier-free formulas with parameters. “Finitely axiomatizable over IOpen” means finitely many *additional sentences in this same language*.

TEIP is the first-order theory of exponential integer parts of real-closed exponential fields, with exp normalized by exp(1)=2. In the L_OR reduct the theory is unchanged by imposing the growth axiom. Models of TEIP need not themselves admit the required exponential expansion without an elementary extension.

The current [author PDF](https://users.math.cas.cz/~jerabek/papers/teip.pdf), dated2025-10-06 and corresponding to the2026 publication record, retains the question as Question5.1. It axiomatizes TEIP by IOpen plus the finite-round empty-position power-of-two game sentences. The known strict hierarchy in Theorem5.16 concerns formulas with a fixed initial response parameter. It holds even over true arithmetic, which already contains TEIP, so it is not a proof that TEIP needs infinitely many extra axioms over IOpen.

Finite axiomatizations in languages expanded by exponentiation or a power predicate are credited prior results and do not answer the reduct-language question. No later full resolution was found in targeted current checks; no novelty or comprehensive-openness certification is claimed.

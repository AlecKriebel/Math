## Published construction verified

order*, Journal of Pure and Applied Algebra 227 (2023), 107350, is available

in the University of Ljubljana repository. Both were downloaded privately.

without relying on the article's unrelated Bass-conjecture assumptions.

for a required group extension, so arbitrary replacement spaces are covered.

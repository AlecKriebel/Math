The full functor, exact bulk normalization, historical model and specified α-induction correspondences are included in that acceptance. This is an existing-result application. Novelty and present-day openness of other formulations are not certified.

The six mathematical studies form one shared research pass. Source recovery and source-scope inspection preceded that pass. They did not themselves prove the application. The preliminary source contract and source-scope report remain in the package to make that distinction inspectable; their future-tense research recommendations are not the package's current mathematical verdict.

The accepted categorical theorem requires:

- Full fusion categories over the complex numbers and a full strong monoidal inclusion
- The inherited spherical pivotal structure, associator, duality and morphism spaces
- The fixed inherited trace on the restricted regular bimodule
- Each bulk's own nonzero spherical global dimension and compatible normalization choices

No positivity, unitarity, pseudo-unitarity, braiding, modularity or invertible S-matrix is imposed on the general construction. A signed pointed example tests a non-positive chosen spherical structure; its underlying fusion category is still pseudo-unitary.

The historical account does not print a complete rigid/pivotal/unit axiom list. Coverage is for complete admissible spherical state-sum data as specified in the model proof, and their genuine based restrictions. It is not a reconstruction theorem for bare numerical arrays satisfying three unspecified equations or for arbitrary linear subalgebras. This interpretive boundary is not evidence of a new unresolved mathematical counterexample.

# The precise relation adopted for Problem 9 4

Let \(\iota:\mathcal D\hookrightarrow\mathcal C\) be a full fusion-subcategory inclusion over \(\mathbb C\), with the spherical pivotal structure on \(\mathcal D\) inherited from \(\mathcal C\). Equivalently for the source-model interpretation, start from complete finite spherical Barrett–Westbury state-sum data, retain a subset of simple labels closed under the unit, duals and all tensor-product summands, and retain the full multiplicity spaces and the original structural maps.

The output is an oriented \((3,2)\)-dimensional defect TQFT \(Z\), the two bulk labels \(\mathcal D,\mathcal C\), monoidal natural isomorphisms
\[
J_{\mathcal A}:Z\vert_{\mathcal A}\xrightarrow{\sim}\operatorname{TVBW}_{\mathcal A},
\qquad \mathcal A\in\{\mathcal D,\mathcal C\},
\]
and the distinguished traced surface label
\[
\mathcal M_\iota={}_{\mathcal D}\mathcal C_{\mathcal C},\qquad
 d\triangleright m\triangleleft c=\iota(d)\otimes m\otimes c,
\qquad t_m(f)=\operatorname{tr}_{\mathcal C}(f).
\]
The domain consists of the compatibly labelled oriented conically stratified closed surfaces and compact three-dimensional bordisms in the cited construction. It includes all required compatible junction labels. Its functorial sewing is a sewing law for decorated bordisms.

A wall cylinder yields a map from the \(\mathcal D\)-bulk surface space to the \(\mathcal C\)-bulk surface space. Such maps respect diffeomorphisms and disjoint unions, but are not asserted natural with respect to every undecorated topology-changing bordism. The interface need not be invertible. No ordinary-TQFT equivalence, universal injection, manifold-independent quotient, or comparison of arbitrary algebra subspaces is required or asserted.

This adopts a specific meaningful answer to the source's qualitative relation question. It does not replace that question by a stronger conjecture.

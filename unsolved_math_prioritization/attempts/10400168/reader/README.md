# An inclusion induced interface between spherical state sum TQFTs

This authored research reader addresses Kawahigashi Problem 9.4 in Ohtsuki's *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, printed page 510. Problem identity: 10400168 / AMR-103-0168; record 1787. Date: 14 October 2026.

For a full fusion-subcategory inclusion with inherited spherical structure over the complex numbers, the ambient category, with one regular action restricted to the subcategory, carries its inherited spherical trace. It therefore supplies an inclusion-dependent oriented surface interface in the published Carqueville–Müller defect TQFT. The two ordinary bulk restrictions are monoidally naturally isomorphic to the separately normalized TVBW theories. This is an application of existing theory with explicit hypothesis verification, not a novelty claim.

The source asks for a relation and does not prescribe a unique type of comparison. The accepted interpretation uses complete spherical Barrett–Westbury data and compatible restrictions of their simple-label bases. The historical introductory axiom sketch is not itself a complete categorical definition. The source-model translation and the precise α-induction inclusions are part of the stated result, not tacit assumptions.

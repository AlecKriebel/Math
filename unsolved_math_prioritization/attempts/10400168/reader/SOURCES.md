# Primary sources and inspection limits

This reader synthesizes the authored reports and their documented source inspections. The metadata files contain public titles, URLs, versions, source-file sizes and hashes, and inspection locations. They do not contain the source documents. A retained preprint is not described as a freshly verified publisher copy.

## The target and state sum model

- Ohtsuki, *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002). Sections 9.1–9.2, printed pp. 506–510; equation (48), p. 509; Kawahigashi Problem 9.4 and its α-induction remark, p. 510. [Primary PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf#page=134)
- Barrett–Westbury, *Invariants of Piecewise-Linear 3-Manifolds*. Complete spherical input, Definitions 2.1–2.13 and Theorem 5.1. Inspected arXiv v3; the independent auditor records web-text inspection without a new local byte verification. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/hep-th/9311155v3)
- Turaev–Virelizier, *On two approaches to 3-dimensional TQFTs*. Inspected arXiv v5. Sections 1–6, §9.5 and Appendix for traces and state sums; Theorems 11.1–11.2 for the separate TV/center comparisons. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1006.3501v5)

## The full defect construction and bulk comparison

- Carqueville–Müller, *Orbifold Completion of 3-Categories*, Communications in Mathematical Physics 407, article 8 (2026), published online 6 December 2025. Complete version-of-record PDF inspected. Definition 5.16, Proposition 5.17, Theorem 5.18, Theorem 6.4 and Example 6.5; published pp. 52–54 and 62–63, with trace/Euler setup on pp. 47–51. [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-025-05434-y)
- Carqueville–Runkel–Schaumann, *Orbifolds of Reshetikhin–Turaev TQFTs*, Theory and Applications of Categories 35 (2020), 513–561. Complete arXiv v2 inspected, Theorem 4.5 and equation (4.70), p. 41. A fresh publisher-PDF retrieval was unsuccessful; the bulk comparison is verified from the named preprint. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.01483v2#page=41)
- Meusburger, *State sum models with defects based on spherical fusion categories*. Inspected arXiv v2, Definitions 1.13 and Examples 1.14–1.15, §4.1 and Theorem 5.16. Its fixed-boundary invariance is not substituted for CM's full defect functor. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2205.06874v2)
- Schaumann, *Traces on module categories over fusion categories*. Inspected arXiv v3; regular trace conventions and the restricted hypothesis of Proposition 5.8. The inherited regular trace here is checked directly. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1206.5716v3)

## Alpha induction and the singular S boundary

- Böckenhauer–Evans–Kawahigashi, *Longo–Rehren subfactors arising from α-induction*. Inspected arXiv v1, Assumption 2.1, system definitions, Theorems 3.9/4.6 and Corollaries 3.10/4.8. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0002154v1)
- Kirillov–Ostrik, *On a q-analog of the McKay correspondence and the ADE classification of sl(2) conformal field theories*. Inspected arXiv v3, §§1 and 3, with the distinct modularity assumptions of §4. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0101219v3)
- Sato, *Orbifold aspects of the Longo–Rehren subfactors*. The inspected arXiv v1 retains even-center, minimal-extension and abelian-group conditions for the stated formulas. The journal record does not substitute for a fresh version-of-record proof inspection. [Inspected preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0404509v1) · [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-005-1292-y)
- The Q-system terminology is crosschecked in Carpi–Gaudio–Giorgetti–Hillier, Definitions 2.5, 2.7, 2.12, 2.16–2.18. [Published source](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00220-023-04722-9). The model report separately cites Kawahigashi's §2 for local/nonlocal Q-systems and degenerate braiding. [Inspected preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2302.05577v3)

## Relative centers and the two central actions

- Gelaki–Naidu–Nikshych, *Centers of graded fusion categories*, Algebra & Number Theory 3 (2009), 959–990. Complete arXiv v1 inspected, Definition 2.1 and Remark 2.4, pp. 3–5. The preliminary relative-center result applies without a grading hypothesis; later graded results have additional assumptions. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0905.3117v1)
- Kong–Zheng, *The center functor is fully faithful*, Advances in Mathematics 339 (2018), 749–779. Complete arXiv v3 inspected, especially pp. 10, 13–14 and 22–24. Source-page images were checked for the reversed-braiding convention. This is not a new version-of-record full-text verification. [Inspected version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1507.00503v3) · [Journal record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2018.09.031)

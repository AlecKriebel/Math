# Five-approach research record

Problem 30002086, OWR-11789-007. Date: 3 October 2026.

## Readiness

The problem page was attempted first and was inaccessible. A pinned catalogue record identified the source, then the official report verified the actual equation and quantifiers. The original source is in a four-dimensional application; the catalogue's version has no dimension restriction. The live rank 502 row was queued at 0/5, and exact-ID repository/branch checks did not locate an existing attempt.

## Approach 1: Hamilton identity and scalar deficit

Normalized the potential independently of the mass-one entropy convention. Proved the equivalence of the target to an absolute scalar-potential gap outside a compact set, and derived the scalar-saturation properties of any bad sequence. Re-proved the known strict-quadratic scalar sufficient condition with explicit absorption thresholds. Checked compact, Gaussian, and Einstein–Euclidean product cases.

Outcome: strong conditional estimates, no unrestricted gap. Quadratic growth of the potential cannot itself be differentiated into a gradient lower bound.

## Approach 2: second variation along minimizing geodesics

Integrated the shrinking equation along a geodesic from a minimizing basepoint. Applied the summed index form with unit endpoint cutoffs, obtaining exact coefficient 2/3 for each endpoint curvature contribution. Recovered linear gradient growth under uniform Ricci upper bounds and isolated the uncontrolled far endpoint term.

Outcome: a transparent proof under stronger curvature assumptions; the endpoint term remains unavailable in the general case. Checked the actual hypotheses in Fang–Man–Zhang rather than relying on the broad paper title.

## Approach 3: weighted Bochner / maximum principle

Derived the exact equation for gradient-square and its trace-Hessian lower bound. Checked the sign at small-gradient minima and at critical points of the potential. Examined the scalar equation as well.

Outcome: the large positive Laplacian of gradient-square is compatible with a small local minimum. Far-out critical points cannot be local minima of the potential, but that leaves saddles/maxima. No contradiction is obtained.

## Approach 4: topology and properness

Proved the product-end consequence of properness and absence of far-out critical points. Then constructed a smooth proper profile on the Euclidean line with the sharp quadratic leading coefficient, gradient-square at most the profile, unique critical point, and positive gradient tending to zero at specified escaping points. Proved explicitly that this is not a shrinking soliton.

Outcome: the profile defeats the shortcut from growth plus noncritical ends to a uniform gradient bound. Recent topology and ends papers preserve the needed distinction and do not close the unrestricted problem.

## Approach 5: normalized bad-sequence blow-up

Rescaled a hypothetical bad sequence to normalize its scalar curvature. Derived the conditional steady-limit equations and checked an explicit complete scaled-cigar product satisfying those equations and the prescribed basepoint values. Did not assert it can occur as such a blow-up. Inspected the 2015 compactness note: its alternative curvature argument removes the gradient hypothesis from a theorem without proving that hypothesis.

Outcome: even the formal limiting equations do not give a contradiction, and smooth compactness at escaping points is an additional unproved step.

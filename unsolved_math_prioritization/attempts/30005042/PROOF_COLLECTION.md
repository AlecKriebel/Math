# Complete five-turn proof collection

The full proofs are linked below in dependency order. No proof is replaced by a checklist or finite experiment.

1. [Turn1](turns/TURN_1.md): exact coupled tree iteration, integrable smoothing law classification, expected local L1-function convergence, and the topology gap.
2. [Turn2](turns/TURN_2.md): deterministic monotone-curve random-threshold lemma, symmetrization, conditional generation maximal inequality, locally uniform convergence under local p>1 moments, and a bounded source process outside the original increment assumption.
3. [Turn3](turns/TURN_3.md): moving parameter mesh, adaptive truncation preserving monotonicity, exact X log X large-family and square-summable-increment results, local logarithmic moment above two sufficient for functional convergence, and a generic martingale countercontrol.
4. [Turn4](turns/TURN_4.md): uniform scalar temporal-maximal bounds, uniform integrability and first-moment equicontinuity at the endpoint; exact geometric mixed moments and rational continued-fraction enclosures for its bivariate law.
5. [Turn5](turns/TURN_5.md): equivalent Ulam process law, intrinsic versus inherited birth times, marked-jump mean measure, common all-generation jump envelope, and l2 jump-size convergence at the endpoint.

Dependencies: Turn1 uses classical scalar Kesten–Stigum. Turn2 supplies its maximal lemma explicitly. Turn3 uses that lemma only at p=2 after truncation. Turn4 uses the same centered truncation but applies Doob only at a fixed parameter. Turn5 uses Turn4's uniform bound on the expected scalar generation maximum, evaluated at a birth parameter independent of the new descendant copies. It never assumes an expected supremum over parameters.

The [consolidated result](RESULT.md) states the exact partial scope and unresolved source target. Historical intermediate status/manifests remain unchanged; the full manifest binds the complete final author packet.

## Conventions actually used

The natural endpoint is finite X(lambda)log^+X(lambda) at every parameter, uniformly dominated on each compact by the right endpoint. The stronger functional theorem states its extra logarithmic power explicitly. Only local compact intervals with left endpoint strictly greater than1 are used; no critical-parameter conclusion is made.

The inverse-Bernoulli geometric construction requires its right-continuous version at random threshold ties to meet HReg. Using the strict comparison after the decreasing parameter change gives that version without changing any deterministic finite-dimensional distribution. Turn4 spells this out. No other marginal coupling is substituted.

The weak-moment question is distinct from giving elementary one-dimensional distributions. The geometric marginal was already solved in the manuscript. This packet's exact third-moment and continued-fraction results concern the true joint coupling and do not resolve the full simple-process request.

All full PDFs, extracted text, renders and the imported dataset record remain outside the portable author packet. Source lookup does not consume a substantive author turn.

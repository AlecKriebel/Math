## Exact unresolved implication

The variable-lattice construction controls the inradius of the ambient domain D, including d_D(r) → ∞ and an arbitrary prescribed slow divergent upper envelope. The Fernández theorem attributed in the inspected problem collection concerns the existence of an unbounded-coefficient holomorphic map into that domain. It does not, in the statement available here, supply a surjective map or establish convergence to infinity of the actual-image inradius. Inclusion gives an upper bound, not the missing lower bound.

No unbounded-coefficient map has been proved here to have actual-image inradius both tending to infinity and bounded by an arbitrary prescribed slow divergent envelope. Limsup divergence must not be substituted for this limit. The upper-envelope-only negative conclusion remains conditional on the qualified historical theorem; its original proof was unavailable for independent verification.

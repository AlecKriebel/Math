## 2. Defect formula and compactification theorem

Thus H²(P¹,j_*V)=H²_c(U,V), dual to the invariant space of V^∨, hence zero. The remaining Euler characteristic is −h¹. This does not need an unproved polarization assumption. The familiar formula is properly credited to [Coates–Corti–Galkin–Golyshev–Kasprzyk, §2](https://kasprzyk.work/research/pdf/proc_ems.pdf).

On a nonsingular curve, IC(V)=j_*V[1]; this is ordinary j_*, not Rj_*. Taking degree-zero hypercohomology gives H¹(P¹,j_*V) as a noncanonical direct summand of H³(Z,Q). Consequently its dimension is at most b₃(Z), and Proposition 1 supplies nonnegativity.

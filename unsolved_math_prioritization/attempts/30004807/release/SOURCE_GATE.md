# Source and claim boundary

## The exact primary conjecture

The relevant contribution is Annegret Burtscher, *Matter particles, naked singularities, and a weak second Bianchi identity*, printed pp. 2231–2233 in Oberwolfach Report 40/2021, *Mathematical Aspects of General Relativity*. [Report DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2021/40); [official PDF](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3893/OWR_2021_40.pdf?isAllowed=y&sequence=1).

Printed p. 2232 identifies the admissible class by regular zero-area singularities of negative ZAS mass on spacelike slices. The conjecture itself is on the next page, p. 2233: it asserts the identity for “all spacetimes with admissible timelike singularities” of that class. Both pages were checked in extracted text and rendered page images. Stopping at p. 2232 would miss the universal conjecture.

The report also states the broader goal of finding useful metric conditions and developing a matter/evolution theory. Refuting its stated universal geometric claim does not complete that broader program.

## The weak-test convention

The primary technical reference is [Burtscher–Kiessling–Tahvildar-Zadeh, arXiv:1901.00813v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.00813v3), published in *Classical and Quantum Gravity* 38 (2021), 185001.

- Section 1.3 uses the constant-time spacelike slices and blows the singular point up to a boundary sphere.
- The footnote to Theorem 1.1 explicitly permits support on the inner boundary.
- Definition 2.1 and equation (2.5) test on this manifold with boundary. The latter displays a potentially nonzero normal component of the test field.
- Our radial test is smooth in exterior Cartesian coordinates and has angular-independent spherical components, so it meets even the additional angular-independence convention in equation (2.5).
- Compact support only in the punctured open manifold gives a different, trivially interior identity. Smoothness after collapsing the sphere is also a different test convention, not silently identified with the source convention here.

## Geometric definition

[Bray–Jauregui, arXiv:0909.0522](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.0522), Definitions 4, 6, 7 and 10, supplies the ZAS definition, regular and harmonic resolutions, and regular mass. The counterexample has the standard negative-mass Schwarzschild spatial metric, so its slices meet those definitions directly.

## Existing results and qualification

BKTZ's theorem includes a strict curvature improvement (G^\mu{}_{\nu}=O(x^{-5+\kappa})), \(\kappa>0\). The example has the borderline order (x^{-5}) and does not contradict that theorem. It violates the null energy condition. The literal report conjecture imposes no independent energy condition, specified matter theory or further curvature/lapse bound; any such strengthened question is outside this result.

# Independent acceptance audit: totally nonnegative critical varieties

Problem 30004938, campaign rank 945. Audit date: 7 October 2026.

## Disposition

**ACCEPT the stated partial mathematical results, with one minor terminology correction. The original stratified-polytopality problem remains UNRESOLVED.**

This review found no substantive defect in the extreme-rank reduction, bowtie product homeomorphism, two-path obstruction, or strictly concave angle-simplex lemma. The graph-energy implication is accepted only as a conditional theorem. The source-stratification identification remains unproved even for the bowtie.

The original package records five author approaches. This acceptance audit, replication work, and correction do not constitute additional author approaches. Originals were preserved. No repository, queue, publication, or problem-status change was made by this reviewer.

The one correction is the phrase “Grassmann-necklace complements” in approach 2. The displayed sets are **J_r = I_r minus {r}**, not the complements of I_r in [5]. Their values, signs, matrix, and ensuing proof are correct. A minimal separate patch is provided in `patches/01_necklace_terminology.patch`; the corrected standalone approach is in `patches/02_bowtie_product_topology.corrected.md`.

## 1. Exact target and primary-source gate

The workshop question is in Pavel Galashin's contribution on printed pages 3106–3107 of [OWR 57/2021](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46935). The introduction of [Totally nonnegative critical varieties, arXiv:2110.08548v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.08548v2) fixes the mathematical meaning: comparison with a convex polytope is as a stratified space. Ordinary homeomorphism to a polytope, or merely to a ball, does not settle that question.

The following source points were checked directly in the supplied primary PDFs/text and against the public arXiv pages:

- Definitions 2.1–2.5: loopless bounded affine permutations satisfy j < f(j) <= j+n. A strand starts at b_j^+ and terminates at b_fbar(j)^-. Crossing labels are the **terminal** labels. Connectedness is connectedness of the union of straight strand arrows, equivalently their crossing graph.
- An f-admissible tuple satisfies theta_p < theta_q < theta_p+pi whenever p<q is a crossing. A common angle shift is immaterial; changing the normalization from theta_1=0 to theta_2=0 in the bowtie is legitimate.
- Section 3.4: the affine poset is generated by p < q < p+n for each crossing p<q.
- Definition 3.2 and Section 3.2: proper tubes have size at least two, are finite convex connected subsets with no repeated residue class, and periodic tubings must satisfy the infinite directed acyclicity condition.
- Theorem 4.1 gives a continuous **surjection** from the affine poset cyclohedron to the critical closure.
- Definition 4.9 takes the common refinement of the images of its **open faces**. It is not generally the restriction of the positroid stratification. Conjecture 4.10, that such images coincide or are disjoint, cannot be assumed.
- Theorem 1.5 gives the stratified hypersimplex conclusion for top cells with 2<=k<=n-1. The source does not assert the same global side-length quotient for arbitrary lower cells.
- [Critical varieties in the Grassmannian, arXiv:2102.13339v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13339v2), equations (1.5)–(1.6), Theorem 1.17, and equation (2.3), supply the bowtie curve formula and necklace convention. Theorem 1.17 requires pairwise noncongruence of angles modulo pi; Section 6 treats the more general situation. The submitted argument correctly uses continuity rather than applying the generic theorem on a rank-deficient locus.

The public arXiv pages still identify 2110.08548v2 as the final version and 2102.13339v2 as the latest version checked. The original source PDFs' six recorded byte counts and SHA-256 hashes match the supplied files. This review made a bounded additional search for “critical varieties” with polytopality and injectivity-conjecture terms; it found no resolution that changes the disposition. This is not a claim of exhaustive current-literature coverage. Search-engine crawl/publication labels were not treated as mathematical publication dates.

## 2. Approach 1: displacement bounds and extreme ranks

**Accepted.** If f(j)-j is 1, the two endpoints of that arrow are adjacent in the alternating boundary order. If it is n, they are again adjacent, on the other side of b_j^+. In either case the chord crosses no other strand. Distinct arrows have distinct endpoints in the 2n-point boundary model, so this is an isolated component for n>1.

Therefore connected diagrams satisfy 2<=f(j)-j<=n-1. Their displacement sum is kn. At k=2 every displacement must be 2, and at k=n-1 every displacement must be n-1. Ranks 1 and n have no connected diagrams with n>1. The low-n classification follows without numerical enumeration.

This is specifically **strand connectedness**. It must not be rephrased as matroid connectedness. For example, the rank-one uniform matroid on n>=2 elements is connected as a matroid, whereas its top-shift strand diagram consists of isolated adjacent-endpoint strands.

The bowtie lift (3,6,5,7,9) has displacement sum 15 and rank 3, and its terminal-label crossing set is exactly {12,13,23,24,25,45}. It is connected and non-top, so it really blocks the proposed connected-component induction at n=5. The product statement for disconnected components is being used only at the source-authorized topological level; it is not independently being upgraded to a new stratification assertion.

A new checker uses sorted, alternating boundary endpoints with strands initially labeled by their **start** points, independently of the submitted terminal-label implementation. It agrees for every permutation through n=8 and verifies the displacement bounds for every connected case encountered. Counts by rank include:

- n=3: {2:1}; n=4: {2:1,3:1}
- n=5: {2:1,3:11,4:1}; n=6: {2:1,3:36,4:36,5:1}
- n=7: {2:1,3:92,4:358,5:92,6:1}
- n=8: {2:1,3:211,4:2017,5:2017,6:211,7:1}

The exhaustive checks corroborate, rather than replace, the all-n proof.

## 3. Approach 2: complete bowtie topology

**Accepted, including the entire boundary.**

### 3.1 Domain and side-triangle parametrization

The six source crossing inequalities, after theta_2=0, are exactly

    theta=(-a,0,b,c,d),  a>0, b>0, a+b<pi, 0<c<d<pi.

There is no additional relation between b and c or d, or between -a and c or d modulo pi, beyond the displayed conditions. Thus the two pairs (a,b) and (c,d) vary independently.

The first sine triple has the angles (a,b,pi-a-b) of an ordinary nondegenerate Euclidean triangle; the second has angles (c,d-c,pi-d). The sine law and SSS uniqueness therefore give a bijection of each two-angle domain with positive triangle side ratios. Normalization by sum 2 converts strict triangle inequalities into positivity and the upper bounds x_i<1. Its closure is exactly T=Delta_(2,3), with 0<=x_i<=1 and sum x_i=2.

### 3.2 Necklace, signs, and measurement

Using the affine necklace formula I_r={f(j) mod 5: j<r<=f(j)} gives

    I_1=124, I_2=234, I_3=134, I_4=145, I_5=125.

Subtracting r from I_r gives the submitted J_1=24, J_2=34, J_3=14, J_4=15, J_5=12. The source sign exponent is the count of j<r with fbar(j)<=j, giving (1,1,-1,-1,1).

With l_1=sin(t+a), l_2=sin t, the identities

    A*l_3=-B*l_1+C*l_2,
    A*l_4=-D*l_1+X*l_2,
    A*l_5=-F*l_1+Y*l_2

derive the submitted matrix directly from gamma_r=epsilon_r times the product of l_p for p in J_r. Here A=sin a>0, so this is a valid quadratic-function basis throughout the admissible domain. The ten determinants divided by their common factor A^3 X are exactly

    (0,BE,BD,AE,AD,AF,CE,CD,CF,0).

The sine relation DY-FX=-AE has the stated sign. In addition to reproducing the symbolic computation, the independent checker reconstructs all gamma columns in the fixed basis sin(t)^2, sin(t)cos(t), cos(t)^2 from the source necklace formula at **540 exact rational unit-circle configurations**. Their determinant vectors agree projectively with the product formula.

### 3.3 The excluded generic locus is handled correctly

At X=sin(a+c)=0, admissibility forces a+c=pi. All raw minors vanish there, and the naive coefficient matrix actually has rank two. For example,

    a=pi/3, b=pi/6, c=2pi/3, d=5pi/6

is admissible and has that rank drop, verified exactly. Dividing by A^3 X at this point would be invalid. The submitted proof does not do that.

Instead, the generic admissible subset is dense: the excluded angle coincidences form a finite union of proper affine hyperplanes in this bounded open angular domain. The original positive-edge boundary measurement is continuous everywhere in the domain. The displayed product vector is continuous and never zero because A,B,C,D,E,F are all positive there. The equality of its projective class with measurement on the generic dense subset extends to all admissible tuples. This also handles nongeneric loci other than X=0; the generic theorem is not being silently asserted on any of them.

### 3.4 Boundary injectivity and closure

On all of T x T, L=A+C=2-B>=1 and R=D+E=2-F>=1. Hence

    Q=AD+AE+CD+CE=LR>=1.

Every projective ratio used by the inverse has the same positive denominator Q. It remains valid when some coordinates vanish, including all nine vertex pairs and every edge/vertex/interior combination. The inverse reconstructs B/L, F/R, A/L, C/L, D/R, E/R and then L,R. Symbolic substitution with C=2-A-B and F=2-D-E verifies all six recovered coordinates identically, not just on samples.

Thus H is a continuous injection from the compact polytope T x T into projective space. Its image is homeomorphic to T x T. Since its relative-interior image is exactly the critical cell, compactness and density prove that its full image is exactly the critical closure. Decomposability and total nonnegativity at degenerate triples follow also by closure of the Grassmannian and of its nonnegative part; one need not apply the rank-deficient raw matrix at a degenerate parameter.

This proves an unstratified product-of-triangles homeomorphism. It does **not** identify the Definition 4.9 strata, and it does not settle the original question even for this example.

## 4. Approach 3: failure of the unchanged global quotient

**Accepted as a counterexample to the proposed factorization, not to polytopality.** Both theta(t)=(-t,0,t,pi/3,2pi/3) and eta(t)=(-2t,0,t,pi/3,2pi/3) lie in the admissible domain and are globally cyclically ordered for sufficiently small t>0.

Their first sine ratios tend to (1:1:2) and (2:1:3), respectively, whereas D=E=F=sqrt(3)/2. The resulting Plucker vectors are the two displayed vectors, distinguished by the projectively intrinsic nonzero-coordinate ratio Delta_134/Delta_124, with limits 1 and 2. These limits follow exactly from sin(mt)/sin(t) tending to m; the submitted numerical samples are only corroboration.

The two small adjacent side lengths tend to zero and the other three tend to sqrt(3), so the sum-2 global cyclic side-length vector tends to (0,0,2/3,2/3,2/3) along both paths. Any continuous map from that unchanged side-length quotient to the lower-cell critical closure agreeing with measurement would have to assign one value to this common limit. The two distinct critical limits contradict this. No conclusion about other quotient constructions follows.

## 5. Approach 4: angle energy and conditional gauge criterion

**Analytic lemma accepted; all-graph injectivity remains conditional.**

The Hessian identity Lambda''(x)=-cot(x) is correct. On positive r-tuples summing to pi, r>=3, at most one angle can exceed pi/2. The all-acute and right-angle cases give positive definiteness of sum cot(alpha_i)z_i^2 on sum z_i=0 immediately. In the obtuse case, put beta=pi-alpha_r<pi/2. The remaining r-1>=2 positive angles have

    sum tan(alpha_i) < tan(beta).

Weighted Cauchy–Schwarz and z_r=-sum_(i<r) z_i yield strict positivity if z_r is nonzero; otherwise positivity follows from the positive remaining cotangents. No unhandled equality case remains.

The degree assumption matters: r=2 would give Lambda(alpha)+Lambda(pi-alpha)=0 and no strict concavity. In the source's contracted reduced bipartite model with black boundary, black interior vertices have degree at least three: interior leaves must be boundary-adjacent, which would make such leaves white, and contracted black interior degree-two vertices cannot be boundary-adjacent. Thus the black-vertex blocks under discussion are within the proved r>=3 range. Circular gap sums and cyclic edge labels follow from Lemma 4.4 of 2110.08548v2.

For the summed energy, each block Hessian is negative definite on its angle-sum-zero subspace. The global Hessian's kernel is exactly the joint kernel of all gap derivatives. Removing component rotations yields strict concavity precisely under the stated kernel hypothesis.

If equality of measurements implies a gauge difference Uu and L^T Uu=0, equality of gradients follows with the correct sign. On the convex normalized admissible domain, strict concavity then gives equality of parameters. This is a correct sufficient criterion. The white-vertex orientation/gauge-annihilation argument and the required all-graph kernel assertion are explicitly unfinished. The weak-separation paragraph is heuristic supporting work and has not been accepted as a proof of either hypothesis. No global injectivity or boundary theorem is licensed by this audit.

## 6. Approach 5: exact tubes, periodic faces, and the remaining gap

**The 37 tube classes are accepted exactly.** The crossing relations reduce to the periodic chains 1<2<3<6 and 2<4<5<7. A convex tube's induced Hasse edges are ambient covers; any ambient cover is one of the generating edges, with integer jump at most four. A connected set of at most five residues has a spanning tree with at most four such edges, so its span is at most 16. The submitted window therefore includes every canonical tube and every potentially intermediate element needed for convexity.

A second independently implemented enumeration grows connected sets directly along covers and tests convexity using an exact periodic shortest-shift order relation. It returns precisely the submitted 37 classes, by size (6,10,12,9); their largest actual span is 7. A single proper tube and its translates do form a valid tubing here, as also verified by the periodic-cycle check, so these are the cyclohedron facets under the source face theorem.

**Separate audit strengthening: the displayed face counts can be certified exactly.** The new check in `tubes/independent_audit.py` removes the finite-window limitation for the combinatorial tubing counts. For tube types A,B, let m(A,B) be the least translate shift for which A and B+5m are disjoint and some element of A precedes some element of B+5m. Each tube has a directed edge to its own +5 translate. Thus a nonpositive-weight cycle in the finite graph of tube types yields a zero-shift closed walk in the infinite graph, appending +1 self-translation steps if necessary. Conversely, an infinite directed cycle projects to a zero-shift closed walk whose edge weights are bounded below by these minima. If all simple cycles of minima have positive total weight, such a walk is impossible. Enumerating simple cycles among at most four tube types is finite and exact.

This certificate returns codimension counts (1,37,189,304,152), agreeing with the original five-translate test on every compatible candidate tubing. It also reproduces all 49 **combinatorial predicted product-face labels** without a translate-window bound. This strengthens the finite combinatorics only. It does not independently establish equality of geometric face images with those product strata.

The circle-scale/linear-scale/pair-collision mechanism in approach 5 is consistent with the source's normalized chord-limit construction. The assertion about a mixed connected tube containing residue 2 is sound: deleting all residue-2 vertices disconnects the relevant two chain pieces of the ambient Hasse graph. The proposed recursive surjectivity construction, however, is not supplied. Neither this certificate nor an Euler identity nor one point in each product-face type proves that every point of the predicted product-face interior occurs in every asserted cyclohedron open-face image.

That missing equality of image sets is substantive and remains the exact barrier to a stratified conclusion. The universal problem would remain open even if that bowtie-specific step were completed.

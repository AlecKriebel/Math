# Source and scope audit: totally nonnegative critical varieties

Checked 7 October 2026. Problem identifier 30004938; OWR identifier OWR-8415362-003.

## Mathematical target

The short workshop question occurs in Pavel Galashin's contribution on pages 3106–3107 of [Oberwolfach Report 57/2021](https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2021/57). The report points to [Totally nonnegative critical varieties, arXiv:2110.08548](https://arxiv.org/abs/2110.08548). The latter's introduction specifies an isomorphism with a convex polytope as a stratified space. Thus the target is a stratification-preserving homeomorphism, not literal affine convexity of the image inside the Grassmannian.

The precise conventions used in the attempt are those of arXiv:2110.08548v2, revised 14 March 2023:

- A critical cell is parameterized by admissible angles and lies in a real totally nonnegative Grassmannian. Its nonnegative critical variety is its closure there.
- The indexing objects are loopless bounded affine permutations. Equivalently ordinary permutations can be used, with fixed points lifted as coloops.
- For a connected strand diagram the angle parameter space is the interior of an affine order polytope. Disconnected diagrams have a product factorization.
- Definition 4.9 uses the common refinement of the images of all open affine-poset-cyclohedron faces. It is not simply the ambient positroid-cell partition.
- Theorem 4.1 gives a continuous surjection from that cyclohedron. It does not give a homeomorphism.
- Theorem 1.5 identifies the top-cell critical closure with the second hypersimplex for 2<=k<=n−1. The degenerate k=1,n cases are points.

The final-version discussion on page 6 expressly says that the lower-cell infinitesimal-ratio and injectivity issues remain obstacles. Conjecture 4.10 on page 18 concerns whether any two open-face images coincide or are disjoint; it is not assumed in these attempts.

## Boundary formula and genericity

The explicit trigonometric curve formula used in approach 2 is from [Critical varieties in the Grassmannian, arXiv:2102.13339v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.13339v2), Theorem 1.17 and Section 6. Its stated genericity hypotheses matter: the bowtie calculation encounters an admissible locus on which the naive coefficient matrix drops rank. The authored proof removes a common Plücker factor on the generic set and then uses continuity of the boundary measurement map. It does not apply the generic span theorem directly on the exceptional locus.

## Current-literature check and its limits

The arXiv abstract currently lists v2 of 2110.08548 as the final version to appear in IMRN. The publisher records [IMRN 2024(5), 3649–3689](https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnad084), first published 29 April 2023. Its abstract states the cyclohedron-image result and the top-cell hypersimplex result.

Searches for the precise title, critical-variety polytopality and the injectivity conjecture, together with the author's [current publication list](https://galashin.github.io/papers.html), found no later resolution of the universal stratified-polytopality question. This is a bounded negative finding, not a claim of exhaustive literature coverage.

The two most recent potentially relevant papers on that list, [Amplituhedra and origami I, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09574v2) and [Amplituhedra and origami II](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03439), were retrieved and searched for critical varieties, the injectivity conjecture, cyclohedra and the hypersimplex/polytope target. Their main stated results concern amplituhedron triangulations, origami and T-duality. The targeted text search did not reveal a solution to this question. These searches are not represented as full mathematical reviews of those papers.

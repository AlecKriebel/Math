# Five bounded substantive approaches

1. Source and degree-theorem route. Matched the two-component question against the original OWR and Batyrev–Nill; found the undefined t notation, zero exception, total-versus-separate-degree issue, and the September 2026 Knupfer–Nill claimed degree resolution. It does not settle finiteness. No attempt to reprove an already claimed degree theorem.
2. Direct local-polynomial calculation. Computed the primitive reflexive triangle and its dual, giving 1−u−v+uv; used pyramids to retain the zero exception for every nonnegative CY-dimension. This repairs scope, not the finiteness question.
3. Boundedness-theorem route. Derived the bounded-index and n≤0 cases, checked Lorenz–Nill's smooth theorem and Borisov–Li's geometric boundedness theorem. The latter explicitly leaves arbitrary reflexive Gorenstein cones outside its conclusion.
4. Free-join route. Proved the exact truncated-finiteness reduction to free-join-indecomposable positive-CY factors and calculated an unbounded-dimensional scalar-multiplication family. No bound on the indecomposable factors was obtained.
5. Identity-only obstruction route. Constructed the formal Q_m family satisfying positivity, expected degree, dualities, boundary symmetry, and both derivative identities, with infinitely many scalar classes. Realization is unproved, so it is not a counterexample.

Stopping condition: the five-approach budget is exhausted. Overall result remains a scoped partial and source correction. The catalog desk review was not counted as an earlier mathematical attempt. No original-conjecture proof, geometric counterexample, novelty claim, or general current-openness certification is made.

# Five substantive approaches

Problem 30005171, rank 948. Mathematical status: unresolved. Five approaches were actually pursued; source retrieval, literature review, duplicate checks, coding, rendering, and packaging are not counted as additional approaches. No novelty claim.

1. Extend the plane linear series over the smooth abstract family. A base-point-free limiting net forces a prime-degree plane embedding by the degree factorization and the equality case of the genus formula. A nonplanar limit must have a fixed part of degree b with 1 <= b <= p-4 and p-b = m e, m,e >= 2. This recovers abstract hyperellipticity in degree five. Gap: the fixed part cannot in general be eliminated, and its relation to an ambient surface still needs proof.
2. Strengthen the first route using the canonical (p-3)-rd root and every Hilbert-function semicontinuity inequality. Hyperelliptic curves with L=O(pP), at a Weierstrass point, satisfy all of these tests for every odd p >= 5. Degree seven is a literature-certified negative control for sufficiency. Gap: multiplication, polarized deformation, and actual smoothing are not provided. These are numerical test objects, not counterexamples.
3. Use the Manetti class group and adjunction to constrain every possible Cartier realization. A smooth Cartier curve avoids the singularities; its genus forces the product alpha of the remaining Markov entries to divide p. Only the plane or a one-singularity index-p surface can occur. Gap: this starts after the sought embedding exists, so it cannot establish that embedding. It proves no new prime case.
4. Use semistable dual graphs and the positive rank-one Picard intersection form. A reduced nodal divisor avoiding the surface singularities, with smooth stable replacement of genus at least two, is already smooth irreducible. Gap: general Calabi-Yau limits may be nonreduced, nonnodal, or meet the singular surface locus.
5. Attack the first unproved prime with threshold and value-semigroup obstructions. For rational unicuspidal degree-11 plane curves, delta=45 and lct>=3/11 force exactly twelve Puiseux signatures. The manuscript proves exhaustion by multiplicity and strict gcd descent before the case checks. All twelve violate the Borodzik-Livingston distribution identity. Gap: reducible/nonreduced divisors, multiple singularities, and singular ambient surfaces remain. The result agrees with DeVleming-Singh's existing degree-at-most-30 classification and is not presented as novel.

Full proofs, conditions, citations and the separate exhaustion argument are in manuscript.tex / manuscript.pdf. verify_math.py independently replays the exact finite arithmetic with standard Python only. The full conjecture remains open in this packet after 5/5 approaches.

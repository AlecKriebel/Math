# Minimal carry-statement clarification

No source file was modified. These edits are recommended for any standalone or publication edition.

1. Replace the opening sentence of Section 4 with:

“Let q be a positive integer, and let b₁,…,bₖ be integers with pairwise distinct residues modulo q whose residue set B is a strong Sidon set of cardinality k.”

Reason: distinct integers may have repeated residues. The carry partition requires a bijection between lifted points and B. This matches the intended use of a lift and the inherited |B| = k notation.

2. Replace the final alternative formulation of the half-integer paragraph with:

“Alternatively, if B lies in the half-integer coset Z + 1/2 and q is odd, map b to (2b)·2⁻¹ in Z/q. When these images are pairwise distinct and strong Sidon, the quantity a+b+c−d is integer-valued and its multiples of q give the direct carry decomposition.”

Reason: direct integer-valued carries apply to the single half-integer coset. Scaling all elements by 2 remains the general safe method for subsets of (1/2)Z.

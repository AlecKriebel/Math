## 1. Sharp designs and moment energies

Reconstructed both exact Gram matrices from the primary construction source;
verified PSD, rank, valencies, centroid, tight-frame and third-tensor
identities. Proved global optimality for absolutely monotonic polynomials
through degree three. Computed positive fourth moments, so the standard
sharp-design theorem cannot continue the argument: strengths 7 and 5 would
be required, while both codes have strength 3. Neither unrestricted quartic
optimality nor a counterexample resulted. Retained proof: §§1–2.

## 2. All-degree two-point LP route

Examined the exact equality conditions, not just a bounded numerical LP.
Evaluated the first seven Gegenbauer moments exactly and used a Laplace
integral estimate to prove every moment of degree at least four is positive,
uniformly over all degrees. Consequently every sharp positive-coefficient
polynomial certificate would have degree at most three. A quartic potential
cannot touch such a minorant at three or four interior values. This rules
out the entire polynomial sharp-certificate route for that potential. It
does not disprove the conjecture. Prior non-LP-universality is credited.
Retained proof: §3.

## 3. Local deformation and force balance

Verified exact shell-balance identities, then computed the nearest-shell
tangent frame bounds at every vertex. All 104 PSD certificates are rational
and cover the full tangent spaces. Combined the bounds with the differentiated
energy formula to prove positive definite one-point Hessians for every
nonconstant power potential, then for every nonconstant completely monotonic
potential. This rules out all infinitesimal single-point descent directions
and proves single-point strict local minima. It leaves simultaneous motions
and distant configurations uncontrolled. Retained proof: §4.

## 4. Exact comparison with the explicit rival family

Derived the full pair distribution of the BBCGKS §4.1 family from two
tetrahedra and S4 permutation parity. The low-degree gaps are explicit.
For powers 3–99, integer Bernstein subdivision proves strict positivity on
the entire larger interval [0,1/5], using 351 leaves of maximum depth 9.
An elementary two-case bound covers every power from 100 onward. Reflection
of the family parameter is controlled by an exact nonnegative odd-binomial
sum. Nonnegative power expansion then proves dominance for the full potential
class, including equality cases. This is a complete *restricted* comparison,
not a classification of all competitors. Retained proof: §5.

## 5. Finite Hermite / higher-point reduction

Applied the full Hermite remainder and nonnegative divided-difference
arguments to the two exact node sets. Reduced a sufficient strengthening
to four explicit global polynomial inequalities in (n,N)=(10,40) and two in
(14,64), with exact rational target energies. The first four low-degree
basis cases were proved from the design identities; the six remaining
unrestricted inequalities were not certified. The 2024 three-point result
has different dimension, cardinality and interpolation nodes and does not
provide the needed matrices. No SDP was solved and no solver output is
presented as a certificate. Some basis potentials are outside the original
potential cone, so the reduction is sufficient, not necessary. Retained
argument and exact gap: §6.

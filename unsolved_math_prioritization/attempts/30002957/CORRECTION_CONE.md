# Additive correction: the cone aperture in TURN_1 §1

The frozen TURN_1.md says that the cones have half-angle π/3 and then bounds the angle between two directions in the same cone by π/3. That implication is incorrect. Replace **half-angle π/3** there by **half-angle π/6**. Cover the sphere by finitely many such cones (using compactness and an open cover, or slightly smaller covering cones contained in these). Two directions in one cone are then at angle at most π/3, so y·z ≥ ||y||||z||/2 exactly as used.

Each corrected cone still has the same strictly positive volume κ r^d inside a radius-r ball. No numerical value of κ or of the finite cover size M was asserted in turn 1. Its tail, all-moment, Palm, intensity, boundary, L1 and planar conclusions therefore remain valid without further alteration. Turn 2 uses those corrected conclusions and has no dependence on a numerical cone constant.

This corrects a proof step, not a theorem or an additional author turn. The original file and its historical manifests are retained unchanged. All later proofs and review should apply this correction.

## Exact original question

The catalogue union is not used as the target. The intended right-hand divisor is the one created at a selected free point.

The actual preceding definition uses equal combinatorial type and contact at least i, counting common blown-up centers. The displayed conjecture's bare equivalence symbol is not defined there. Remark4.14's first-exceptional specialization is incompatible with one mechanical global zero-based point indexing. The manuscript states rather than conceals this issue. Its table separately computes the last-shared normalization, the literal zero-based created-divisor expression, fixing the selected free c3, one-based local labeling, and the conditional exact-contact reading. It does not claim those are every conceivable alternative statement, nor identify one as an author-approved correction.

The source's pp5–7 explicitly include nonsatellite prime divisors and extra free blowups after the associated curve has been resolved. Combinatorial type includes the entire minimal exceptional graph/proximity data. Thus no satellite-only, singular-curvetta, or minimal-embedded-resolution hypothesis excludes the example. The selected c3 is genuinely free independently of any convention concerning the origin. Both c4 and c5 still follow it. The source's pp24–25 concern closed Nash sets and density of the transverse locus; replacing closures with raw transverse strata would change the question.

# Provenance of the proof-only edition

## Mathematical content and attribution

The underlying Bell-amplification obstruction is established prior work. Brannan–Gao–Junge, arXiv:2007.06138v2, §4.4, explicitly gives GE((I−τ₂)⊗id_M₂)<1 in the paragraph immediately before Proposition 4.21. The statement persists in their published Advances in Mathematics 394 (2022), 108129, p. 51. Combined with the qubit GE(1,∞) theorem of Münch–Wirth–Zhang (2024), it already implies the ordinary same-bound tensorization failure considered here.

The supplied proof is an explicit direct certificate and reconstruction of that consequence. It is not a claim of a new counterexample, a new Bell-state mechanism, historical priority or that the source question remained open before this work. [The full attribution addendum](ATTRIBUTION_ADDENDUM.md) gives the source comparison and conventions. The original proof and mathematical audit are retained unchanged; this later source review qualifies the presentation of their accepted result.

The historical source question occurs in M. Wirth's contribution, joint work with H. Zhang, to Oberwolfach Report 2/2021, printed pp. 78–79. Its symmetric quantum Markov semigroup class does not require ergodicity. The later ergodicity qualification concerns entropy decay to scalar equilibrium. The source display lacks the curvature exponential, so this edition uses Wirth–Zhang's precise GE convention in arXiv:2007.13506v2, Definition 2.5.

The qubit depolarizing factor estimate is established prior work: Münch–Wirth–Zhang, arXiv:2401.05179v1, Theorem 3.23, with Remark 3.24 covering arbitrary complex observables for symmetric means. COUNTEREXAMPLE.md supplies a full independent analytic proof of this special case and directly calculates the tensor failure using the full ambient logarithmic weighted norm. The complete-GE theorem concerns a stronger condition and is not contradicted.

SOURCE_PROVENANCE.json gives the public titles, URLs, raw PDF byte counts and hashes, relevant inspected sections, bibliographic status and bounded search limits. The public PDF identities were independently rehashed while preparing this edition. Rehashing verifies source-copy identity and does not extend the original inspection or certify a source theorem.

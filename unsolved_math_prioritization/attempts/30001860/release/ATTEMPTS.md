# Five substantively distinct approaches retained

The broad target was not declared solved. Each approach retains its proofs in PROOF.md; none is merely a request to attempt a missing theorem.

1. **Semisimple spectral reduction.** Remove the odd-order unipotent factor and identify the negative eigenspace with the top nonzero 2-adic eigenvalue-order layer (Lemma 2.1). This exactly identifies the statistic and verifies applicability of torus counting. Missing for this approach alone: distribution of the mass of the maximal layer as dimension grows.

2. **Centralizer and involution-fiber decomposition.** Prove the finite-group fiber identity and exact GL convolution in scalar-powered and bounded-order factors (Lemma 2.2). This avoids assuming an element is uniformly distributed among involutions. Missing for this approach alone: uniform estimates or asymptotic equivalents for the two factor distributions over the balanced range and all layers.

3. **Arithmetic fixed-rank and quotient tests.** Compute p(GL_2(q))=(1-4^(-v2(q-1)))/3 and p(SL_2(q))=0, including full proofs and two incompatible q-growth subsequences. Prove that GL_3(3)'s exact half-open predicate is not invariant under central scalar multiplication (§7). This blocks incorrect parameter and quotient reductions; it is not a counterexample to rank-growth asymptotics. Missing: an actual rank-growing obstruction or leading asymptotic formula.

4. **Unsigned torus/cycle-index upper bound.** Use the credited LNP transfer theorem, prove a cycle-pointing identity, an exact fractional-avoidance product, and a dyadic estimate, then split low and high order layers (§§3-5). Obtain a uniform reciprocal-log upper bound for GL and U, and with LNP a matching order of magnitude. Missing: a leading equivalent or a proof that the log-scaled probabilities converge. The deliberately loose constants do not supply this.

5. **Signed Weyl-group extension and constrained-group transfer.** Prove the signed-cycle bound for symplectic and odd orthogonal groups, handle both even orthogonal signs by conditioning with the necessary factor two, and prove the exact finite-index upper bound for SL/SU intermediates (§6 and §1). Missing: q-uniform determinant-constrained estimates, conformal and other group-form coverage, projective image-predicate estimates, and leading asymptotics. No arbitrary-lift shortcut is used.

# Controlling scope clarifications

This file controls interpretation of the unchanged frozen bundle.

1. PROOF.md section 7's heading “Exact rank-two and quotient controls” should read “Exact dimension-two and quotient controls.” GL_2 and SL_2 have natural dimension 2 and semisimple Lie rank 1. GL_3 and Sp_4 are the Lie-rank-two controls.
2. GL_N/U_N upper bounds apply for N>=2. The LNP lower bound and its order-of-magnitude combination require N>=3, since rank=N-1>=2. The lower bound must never be applied to SL_2, whose target set is empty.
3. For Sp_(2r), SO_(2r+1), and SO^±_(2r), the stated bounds and lower-bound combination use r>=2.
4. Reciprocal-log asymptotics concern rank tending to infinity. They do not concern q tending to infinity at fixed rank, nor assert an asymptotic equivalent or convergence of a log-scaled proportion.
5. Uniformity in odd q applies to the six listed matrix families. The SL/GL and SU/U intermediate-group extension is fixed-q only, through indices at most q-1 and q+1. No q-uniform upper bound for those intermediates follows from that argument.
6. U means the unitary isometry fixed-point group defined by LNP section 3.1, not a unitary similitude group inferred from introductory notation.
7. LNP is used with a connected reductive algebraic group and its finite Frobenius fixed-point group. Disconnected orthogonal groups, similitudes, Spin/half-spin forms, and projective arbitrary-lift predicates are not included by analogy.

These clarifications leave the audited numerical constants and the valid scoped theorem unchanged. The frozen source was not edited.

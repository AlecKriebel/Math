# Correction history and retained qualifications

12 October 2026. Acceptance of the zero/common-factor/ternary packet applies to the corrected mathematical version only. Two edits preceded that acceptance:

1. The original description called the ternary-digit family broader than the binary-digit family. This was rejected. They are incomparable: ternary signs can satisfy \(e_1=e_2=1,e_3=-1\), contrary to the binary identity \(e_3=e_1e_2\); binary signs can satisfy \(e_1=e_2=e_3=1,e_4=-1\), contrary to the ternary identity \(e_4=e_1e_3\). The accepted text says they are different families and keeps both restrictions explicit.
2. The common-factor logarithmic recurrence had a Latin-u/Greek-nu notation mismatch. The accepted version consistently uses \(\nu_n\) for its reconstructed product coefficients. The recurrence, numerical thresholds and mathematical conclusion did not change.

The hybrid/prefix packet was accepted with its complete final two-step criterion. It does not accept unfinished cases with exceptional index 1 or 2. All integer domains, factor-deleted cofactors, finite horizons and label multiplicities remain explicit.

The preceding [general radial caveat](../GENERAL_RADIAL_CAVEAT.md) remains in force. Arbitrary finitely many factors can provide partition-scale suppression on the positive real radius. The Jensen argument is an angular zero-multiplicity necessary condition and supplies no radial-impossibility replacement.

The subsequently accepted full-ternary theorem adds a new conclusion rather than altering the earlier finite-cap results. The earlier statements that zero sparsity and the finite-cap arguments alone do not exclude the whole ternary family remain accurate. Separate later-status notices link to the new theorem and review. The new proof uses the global coefficient bound one and exhausts the combined coefficient/Jensen search at degree 59; no sharp coefficient-only threshold of 59 is claimed.

This public edition preserves every displayed mathematical formula, table, frontier-count sequence, state-domain/transition specification and explicit sharpness witness in the accepted six proofs and three substantive reviews.

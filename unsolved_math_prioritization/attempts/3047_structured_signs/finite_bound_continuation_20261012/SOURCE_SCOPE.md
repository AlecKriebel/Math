## Original unrestricted question

The public problem statement was inspected as HTML on 12 October 2026. It asks about independently changing signs in the unrestricted-partition factors to obtain total coefficients in the target alphabet and records the coefficient-parity observation.

David S. Newman, [A Product Related to Unrestricted Partitions](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/146359/a-product-related-to-unrestricted-partitions), MathOverflow, posted 30 October 2013. Its HTML was also inspected on 12 October 2026. It gives the same unrestricted formulation and discusses finite examples. Those historical computations were not rerun or certified for this appendix.

These sources establish the context of the question, not the restricted-family theorem proved here. No quoted third-party text, source PDF or copied web page is distributed.

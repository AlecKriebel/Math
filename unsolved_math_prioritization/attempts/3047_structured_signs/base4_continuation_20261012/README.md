## Exact family and conclusion

The original factors are required to have the form

\[
F_m(q)=\prod_{j\ge0}
\left(1+a_{m,j}q^{m4^j}+b_{m,j}q^{2m4^j}+c_{m,j}q^{3m4^j}\right),
\qquad a_{m,j},b_{m,j},c_{m,j}\in\{-1,1\}.
\]

All three signs in every block are independent, all positive integer factors remain, and no finite bound on the number of noncyclotomic blocks is imposed. Unique base-four expansion gives every original factor coefficient absolute value one. The class contains the binary-digit family by pairing adjacent binary digit factors; it also allows the cubic sign to disagree with the product of the first two signs arbitrarily often.

For this entire base-four family, no assignment has all product coefficients in the target alphabet. The argument supposes the global coefficient bound one, derives exact necessary parity-refined Jensen inequalities, and combines those inequalities with a complete coefficient recurrence. Its first empty combined frontier is degree 77. This is not a claimed sharp coefficient-only obstruction degree or the largest possible finite good prefix.

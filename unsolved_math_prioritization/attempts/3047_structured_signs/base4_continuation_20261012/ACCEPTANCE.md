## Accepted theorem

No base-four digit-multiplicative sign assignment, retaining every original partition factor and independently choosing all three signs in every cubic digit block, makes every coefficient of the full product belong to \(\{-1,0,1\}\). The class contains the binary-digit family, and permits arbitrarily many noncyclotomic cubic blocks. It still restricts the original independently signed coefficient arrays.

The first empty level of the specified coefficient-plus-global-Jensen search is 77. Its hypotheses include the desired coefficient bound one at all degrees. Degree 77 is not accepted as a sharp coefficient-only obstruction degree, as the longest possible finite good prefix, or as a universal first bad degree for arbitrary finite base-four prefixes.

## Completed independent historical verification

The reviewer reconstructed every ordered tree state by exact integer logarithmic derivatives, rather than reusing the original polynomial multiplication or ratio-update routine. Every residual division was checked for integrality, each accepted coefficient was checked against partition parity, and each possible child and complete child-list closing marker was compared. This was full-state verification, not a comparison of counts or hashes alone.

- 11,151,089 states, including the root, were checked
- The largest frontier has 710,118 states at degree 54
- Degree 76 has 2,708 states; degrees 77 and the margin level 78 are empty
- 9,107,474 attempted choices were rejected by Jensen and 26,095,524 by coefficient tests
- The exact weighted-profile cache had 70,639 rejected and 15,992 accepted profiles
- Direct partition dynamic programming and a separate pentagonal XOR recurrence agreed on all 513 parity bits
- Direct exact rational summation and the integer envelope formula agreed at every one of 21 radii
- Original cubic multisets and compressed products agreed for group sizes one through five, supplementing the general realization proof
- Normal and both optimized execution modes produced byte-identical complete reports, including every rational envelope
- Eight independent malformed-tree controls were rejected, including deletion, duplication, domain corruption, premature or missing closing markers, trailing data and a well-formed infeasible leaf
- A separately sanitized original computation reproduced its complete tree and output log; only the eleven expected progress lines appeared on stderr, with no sanitizer errors

The review also proves the safety of checked signed 128-bit multiply-and-add intermediates and bounds the remaining degree-78 residuals by \(p(78)=12,132,164\). Independent reconstruction uses arbitrary-precision arithmetic and does not rely on fixed-width safety for its mathematical conclusion.

## Public evidence and exclusions

The complete authored proof and full substantive review are distributed. Programs, raw tree states, detailed machine reports, private input inventories and copied third-party sources are not distributed. The manifest binds only actual public members. This is not an executable public replay package. The public recurrences, domains, encoding and review permit independent reimplementation.

No accepted claim resolves the unrestricted sign problem, determines the sharp longest finite base-four coefficient prefix, proves that every base-four product has unbounded coefficients, establishes a partition-parity density, or classifies arbitrary zero-free signed series. The unrestricted compactness discussion states an existence equivalence and does not identify an explicit or sharp coefficient-only cutoff for this theorem. Separate later work is outside this accepted packet.

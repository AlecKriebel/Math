# Sources and scope

Checked 30 September 2026.

1. Arthur J. Krener, “Open Problem: Global Observability of Spaces of Negative Curvature,” in *Control Theory: On the Way to New Application Fields*, OWR 11/2009, printed pp.674–675. [Full report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46211), [publisher record](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/3394), DOI10.4171/OWR/2009/11. The complete contribution and its displayed Gramian were read and both pages visually checked. The state is an n-dimensional manifold described by local coordinates. Output dimension p is unrestricted. The conjecture has no simple-connectivity or nonzero-vector-field hypothesis.
2. John Nash, “The Imbedding Problem for Riemannian Manifolds,” *Annals of Mathematics*63(1956),20–63, DOI10.2307/1969989. [Full original paper, university-hosted scan](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~feehan/teaching/math866/nash.pdf). The exact compact smooth theorem, Theorem2 on printed p.59, was visually checked after OCR of the scanned PDF. It gives a C^k isometric embedding for 3≤k≤∞, into dimension n(3n+11)/2. In particular, a smooth compact Riemannian surface embeds isometrically in R17. This classical theorem is imported, not independently reproved in full.
3. Allen Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, §1.3, Proposition1.36 and the covering-classification discussion. [Author-hosted full book](https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf). The subgroup-realization statement and its construction were read. The usual genus-two presentation and a surjection to Z/2 give the required connected two-sheeted covering. Smooth structure is transported by the covering charts.
4. Michael P. Hitchman, *Geometry with an Introduction to Cosmic Topology*, [§7.7, Example7.7.11](https://mphitchman.com/geometry/section7-7.html). This author-hosted account gives the hyperbolic octagon construction and genus-two quotient. It is supporting exposition for the standard elementary construction, not a new theorem in this package.
5. Arthur J. Krener and Kayo Ide, “Measures of Unobservability,” *Proceedings of the IEEE Conference on Decision and Control*,2009,6401–6406. [Full institutional-repository copy](https://calhoun.nps.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/fe670a91-21d7-4ff0-a25e-01be2e5d4164/content), [stable record](https://hdl.handle.net/10945/52019). The definition of injective output-history observability, local linearization, Gramian, and coordinate/output scaling remarks were inspected. The paper's local sensitivity analysis does not state the missing global injectivity conclusion. The empirical finite-difference Gramian discussed later in that paper is not substituted for the exact differential Gramian in the counterexample.

## Literature search and attribution

Bounded searches used the exact OWR title, Krener with negative curvature and observability, Gramian with covering maps, and Nash with global observability. They found the original report and its cited paper, but no verified earlier explicit resolution of this precise conjecture. Unrelated results about Schrödinger or wave-equation observability on negatively curved spaces concern different observation maps and are not used.

The counterexample relies on classical geometry and covering theory. A failure to find an earlier printed counterexample does not establish priority; no historical novelty claim is made.

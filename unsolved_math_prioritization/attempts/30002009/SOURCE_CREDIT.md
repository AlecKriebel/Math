# Sources and attribution

The argument is an authored synthesis of standard topology, Lie theory and effective real algebraic geometry. Its algorithmic encoding, the classical rank-one model, and the elementary modular formality obstruction are not presented as novel research. Source documents and extracts are not part of this authored prose.

1. Daniel Juteau, problem-session question in *Enveloping Algebras and Geometric Representation Theory*, Oberwolfach Report 13/2012, printed p. 804. This is the original derived-complex question; the surrounding characteristic-zero representation is relevant to how strongly one interprets the requested description. DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2012/13 . Official report: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46383 .

2. Arthur Garnier, *Dirichlet–Voronoi domain and injectivity radius of flag manifolds – equivariant cell structure on O(3)/O(1)^3*, arXiv:2011.06338v5, arXiv version date 30 June 2026; manuscript front-page date 1 July 2026. Introduction, Proposition 3.2 and the explicit real-flag computation delimit the prior geometric method. Its conditional general approach and real SL₃ calculation are not an unrestricted complex-G answer. https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06338v5 .

3. Rocco Chirivì, Arthur Garnier and Mauro Spreafico, *Cellularization for exceptional spherical space forms and the flag manifold of SL₃(R)*, arXiv:2006.14417v2, 30 April 2021. Prior explicit equivariant cell calculations for spherical space forms and the real SL₃ flag. https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.14417v2 .

4. Saugata Basu, *Algorithms in Real Algebraic Geometry: A Survey*, arXiv:1409.1534. Sections 2.1 and 3.5 supply the established effective real-quantifier-elimination and semialgebraic-triangulation background. The authored enumeration procedure uses these standard decidability and existence inputs. https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1534 . Author's PDF: https://www.math.purdue.edu/~sbasu/raag_survey2011_final-sep4-2014.pdf .

5. Hans Delfs and Manfred Knebusch, *Semialgebraic Topology over a Real Closed Field II: Basic Theory of Semialgebraic Spaces*, Math. Z. 178 (1981), 175–213. Classical real-closed-field setting for semialgebraic topology and triangulation, including work over the real algebraic numbers. Bibliographic record: https://eudml.org/doc/173096 . This reference was not inspected theorem by theorem during the audit. The construction can alternatively use the effective triangulation directly described in Source 4; AUDIT.md also supplies the algebraic-parameter reduction.

6. Meinolf Geck, *On the construction of semisimple Lie algebras and Chevalley groups*, arXiv:1602.04583v5, 26 September 2016, §§3–5. Explicit rational root-string matrix construction and identification with the semisimple Lie algebra, drawing on Lusztig's earlier work. This is the constructive Lie-algebra input in Appendix A, not an original part of this note. https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.04583v5 .

7. Brian Conrad and Aaron Landesman, *Compact Lie Groups* course notes, especially the maximal-torus and Weyl-group discussions and Appendix K. Standard compact-group centralizers, normalizers and reflection representations. https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/210CPage/handouts/lie_groups_notes.pdf .

8. Zhiwei Yun, *Introduction to Real Group Representations*, §1.7. Polar decomposition used in the compact-to-complex comparison. https://math.stanford.edu/~conrad/JLseminar/Notes/L6.pdf .

9. Allen Hatcher, *Algebraic Topology*, covering-space lifting in §1.3; simplicial-to-singular comparison, Theorem 2.27; cellular homology in §2.2; and products/Künneth in Chapter 3. The rank-one CW construction and the comparison maps use classical material of this kind. https://pi.math.cornell.edu/~hatcher/AT/AT.pdf .

10. A. W. Knapp, *Structure Theory of Semisimple Lie Groups*, Proceedings of Symposia in Pure Mathematics 61 (1997), 1–27. Sections 3–4 support the automorphism identity component, compact real forms, Killing form, and eigenspace construction checked in the independent audit. https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~aknapp/pdf-files/1-27.pdf .

Public-source status was checked during authorship and audit on 10 October 2026. This edition preserves those recorded checks; edition preparation does not claim fresh source retrieval or inspection. The notes do not assert an exhaustive literature search or infer priority from the absence of a found reference. The later Achar–Riche modular mixed-perverse results are not substituted for the arbitrary-field derived k[W]-module problem.

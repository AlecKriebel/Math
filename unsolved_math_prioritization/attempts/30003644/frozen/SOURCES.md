## Primary sources

**S1.** F. Bayart, K. Matomäki, E. Saksman, K. Seip, organizers,
*Mini-Workshop: Interplay between Number Theory and Analysis for Dirichlet
Series*, Oberwolfach Reports 14 (2017), 3035–3069.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2017/51
Publisher PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46710
The exact target is the open-problems session, Problem 2 (Erdős and Ingham,
communicated by Michel Balazard), Question 1, printed p.3065 (PDF page 31).
The neighboring Question 2 states the monotone-positive-function version
with coefficient 61/30 and credits its equivalence to the 1964 paper. Both
were checked visually in the publisher PDF.

**S2.** *Analytic Number Theory*, Oberwolfach Report 51/2025, pp.2701–2764.
DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2025/51
Publisher PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/52435
The same finite polynomial is asked on printed p.2757, item (2), immediately
after an unrelated question about sums of three 3-full numbers. This
explains the overlapping subquestion in catalogue record 30006466 /
OWR-14299577-019. No claim about that record's unrelated 3-full-number
questions is made here.

**S3.** Fredy Yip, *On a problem of Erdős and Ingham*,
arXiv:2512.16528v1, 18 December 2025.
https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.16528
https://arxiv.org/html/2512.16528v1
Read the complete four-page paper. Theorem 1.3 permits an infinite subset
of the integers and realizes any prescribed complex value at any fixed
nonzero real t. Section 3 explicitly distinguishes finite sets; Question
3.2 on p.3 leaves the exact {2,3,5} problem open. Theorem 1.2 recalls the
Erdős–Ingham Tauberian equivalence with a zero-below-one normalization.
The conclusion and exact polynomial were also checked visually.

**S4.** P. Erdős and A. E. Ingham, *Arithmetical Tauberian theorems*,
Acta Arithmetica 9 (1964), 341–356.
https://eudml.org/doc/207489
https://matwbn.icm.edu.pl/ksiazki/aa/aa9/aa9134.pdf
The scan was downloaded for private reading, OCRed for navigation, and
visually checked at pp.347–349 and 354–355. Theorem 4 is on p.347; the
oscillatory necessity construction is on pp.349–350; related Kronecker/Bohr
arguments appear on pp.353–354; the {2,3,5} question is on p.355. The
Tauberian construction in the note is a credited specialization of this
classical argument. The web-reader route to the PDF returned 403, but a
normal direct download succeeded. OCR was not treated as authoritative
mathematical notation.

**S5.** Michel Waldschmidt, *The Role of Complex Conjugation in
Transcendental Number Theory*, in *Diophantine Equations*, N. Saradha, ed.,
Tata Institute of Fundamental Research / Narosa, 2007.
Author-hosted PDF:
https://webusers.imj-prg.fr/~michel.waldschmidt/articles/pdf/DION2005proceedings.pdf
Theorem 2.1 on p.4 states the six exponentials theorem, credited to
Siegel, Lang and Ramachandra. Conjecture 2.3 on p.5 states the full Schanuel
conjecture. These are the precise external arithmetic inputs to Approach 5.
The latter is used only in an explicitly conditional proposition.

## Discovery aids and limitations

- The requested starting page,
  https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/30003644,
  was attempted first and returned an accessibility error / HTTP 403.
  Its frozen catalogue record was used only to locate the exact primary
  report; the primary report was read and visually checked independently.
- https://www.erdosproblems.com/967 is a useful index, whose search-visible
  text identifies the infinite-sequence disproof and keeps finite sets
  separate. The page itself returned 403 in the direct reader. No conclusion
  here relies on its status badge; Yip's primary paper was read instead.
- The 2025 preprint *Erdős Problem #967 on Dirichlet Series: A Dynamical
  Systems Reformulation*, https://www.preprints.org/manuscript/202512.2763,
  was located. It explicitly disclaims resolving the problem, and its
  displayed shift conventions and uniform-gap claims were not used. For
  the present exact polynomial, Proposition 2 independently shows why a
  positive uniform gap cannot hold.
- Searches combined the exact polynomial, Erdős–Ingham, finite sequences,
  Yip and the source DOI, including a 2026 search. No verified later
  unconditional resolution of the finite {2,3,5} question was found.

Classical Kronecker approximation, the implicit function theorem, and
Rouché's theorem are explicitly identified where used. No full-text source
is redistributed in this authored packet.

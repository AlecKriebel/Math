# Ohtsuki Problem 12.6: public recovery projection

Row 1885; problem ID 10400212; AMR-103-0212. Status: NOT SOLVED by this work.

The target asks for a new existence proof of a universal finite-type knot invariant using knotted trivalent graph presentations and operations. It is not a question about whether universal knot invariants exist. Ohtsuki's source already describes extensions and generator constructions.

The source is Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds, edited by T. Ohtsuki, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002), section 12.4, printed page 534, PDF page 162. The section footnote identifies T. Ohtsuki following seminar talks of D. Thurston; no proposer is explicitly attached to Problem 12.6. Do not transfer authorship from a neighboring conjecture.

Its operations are connected sum, unzip, bubbling, and unknot creation; half-integer framings and nonorientable ribbon neighborhoods are allowed. These are not silently interchangeable with the ordinary or dotted Bar-Natan–Dancso packages.

Thurston's 2002 algebra of KTGs is foundational and predates the target.

# Public sources and inspection scope

The following are bibliographic references and the audit's recorded inspection boundaries. Source documents, copied source text, and images are excluded. Exact PDF byte counts, SHA-256 hashes, version identities, and retrieval/inspection metadata appear in PUBLIC_SOURCE_METADATA.json. Preparation rechecked those retained bytes; the inspection statements below refer to the accepted audit.

## Original hard-capacity problem

Britta Peis, *Open Problem: Fare Zone Assignment*, in *Combinatorial Optimization*, Oberwolfach Report 50/2024, printed page 2992, PDF page 44.

Public PDF: https://oa.tib.eu/renate/bitstreams/706cca58-da6b-4d8c-a979-ebf6e92658f0/download

The audit inspected the relevant paragraph and displayed program as text and as a rendered page. It records hard capacity constraints and does not infer that unspecified signs or number encodings were expressly supplied by the original paragraph.

## Prior greedy framework

Julián Mestre, *Greedy in Approximation Algorithms*, Algorithms - ESA 2006, LNCS 4168, pages 528-539.

DOI: https://doi.org/10.1007/11841036_48

Publisher: https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/11841036_48

Official preview: https://page-one.springer.com/pdf/preview/10.1007/11841036_48

Only the official two-page preview, printed pages 528-529, was retrieved and inspected. It supports attribution of the general k-extendible greedy framework. The complete chapter was not retrieved; the application and full greedy argument are supplied in this packet.

## Ordinary weighted matching

Harold N. Gabow, *Data Structures for Weighted Matching and Extensions to b-matching and f-factors*, arXiv:1611.07541v1, 22 November 2016.

Version: https://arxiv.org/abs/1611.07541v1

PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1611.07541v1

The audit inspected Section 1, PDF pages 1-2, for the established ordinary weighted-matching result and the distinct conventions for generalized matchings. The packet gives an explicit reduction to ordinary matching and does not claim that an uncapacitated perfect b-matching definition is interchangeable with its degree-upper-bound problem. The complete blossom-algorithm proof was not independently audited.

## Later dropout model

Martin Hoefer, Lennart Kauther, Philipp Pabst, Britta Peis, and Khai Van Tran, *Fare Zone Assignment on Trees*, arXiv:2512.19493v3, 24 April 2026.

Version: https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.19493v3

PDF: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2512.19493v3

The audit inspected Definition 1 and surrounding model text on PDF pages 2-3, page 4 for congestion terminology, page 7 for Theorem 3's scope, and page 1 for version information; page 2 was visually inspected. The model permits cut-budget violations with zero revenue from the affected commodity. This establishes the relevant model distinction but does not constitute a complete audit of the paper's approximation proofs. The source is the pinned arXiv manuscript; no journal acceptance or peer-review status is asserted.

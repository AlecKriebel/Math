# A counterexample to the SL2 representation-ring filtration equality

Date: 2026-10-05. Problem identifier: 30003703 / OWR-15987-013.

## Statement and conventions

Let F_n be freely generated by x_1,...,x_n, and let Gamma_r F_n be its integral lower central series, with Gamma_1 F_n = F_n. Put

    A_n(k) = ker(Aut(F_n) -> Aut(F_n / Gamma_{k+1} F_n)).

The representation ring in this note is Satoh's rational algebra R: the Q-subalgebra of complex-valued functions on Hom(F_n, SL(2,C)) generated by every matrix-entry function a_ij(g). It is not the Grothendieck ring of representations or the ring generated only by characters. Let J be its ideal generated by a_ij(g)-delta_ij, and set

    D_n(k) = ker(Aut(F_n) -> Aut(J/J^{k+1})).

These are the definitions in Satoh's contribution to Oberwolfach Report 2/2018, printed pp. 88-89. Its conjecture asks for equality for all k>=1, in the discussion for n>=3. The same source records equality for k<=4. The argument below disproves the universal conjecture, without using the different conjecture comparing A_n with the lower central series of IA_n.

Group commutators are [u,v]=uvu^{-1}v^{-1}. Lie or associative commutators are [U,V]=UV-VU. All nested brackets have the displayed parenthesization.

### Theorem

1. For every n>=4 there is an explicit element of D_n(5) that is not in A_n(5).
2. For every n>=3 there is an explicit element of D_n(6) that is not in A_n(6).

Consequently the two filtrations are unequal for each rank in the stated range n>=3. We do not claim failure in every degree, or decide equality at degree 5 when n=3. No novelty claim is made.

## 1. A matrix identity

For traceless 2-by-2 matrices X,Y,Z over a commutative Q-algebra, put

    P(X,Y,Z) = [[[X,Y],[X,Z]],X].

Then P(X,Y,Z)=0.

Proof. Cayley-Hamilton gives X^2=-det(X)I. If U=[X,Y], then

    XU+UX = X^2Y-YX^2 = 0.

Likewise, V=[X,Z] satisfies XV+VX=0. Therefore

    XUV = -UXV = UVX,
    XVU = -VXU = VUX.

It follows that [U,V] commutes with X, proving the identity. This argument uses no division and remains valid over any commutative ring for traceless matrices. In particular, substitution Z=[X,Y] gives

    Q(X,Y) = [[[X,Y],[X,[X,Y]]],X] = 0

for traceless 2-by-2 matrices. The degrees of P and Q are 5 and 6, respectively.

## 2. Filtered commutator calculation

Let B be any associative unital ring with a multiplicative filtration by two-sided ideals B_{>=r}, and let U=1+u and V=1+v be invertible, where u has filtration at least r>=1 and v at least s>=1. Then

    UVU^{-1}V^{-1}-1 = (uv-vu)U^{-1}V^{-1}.

Also U^{-1}-1=-uU^{-1} and V^{-1}-1=-vV^{-1}. Thus the left side has filtration at least r+s, and its degree-(r+s) class is the commutator of the degree-r class of u with the degree-s class of v. Higher-order terms in either input change the result only in degree at least r+s+1.

The same statement applies to matrices over a commutative filtered ring, with entrywise ideals as the filtration. Repeated application computes the leading term of any nested group commutator as its correspondingly bracketed Lie polynomial. No convergence or analytic approximation is involved: every assertion is in a finite quotient by a power of an ideal.

## 3. Two group words and their representation orders

Define

    w = [[[x_1,x_2],[x_1,x_3]],x_1],
    v = [[[x_1,x_2],[x_1,[x_1,x_2]]],x_1].

The word w uses three generators; v uses two. Consider the tautological matrices M_i=(a_ab(x_i)) in SL_2(R), and write B_i=M_i-I. Every entry of B_i belongs to J. The determinant equation gives

    tr(B_i) = -det(B_i) in J^2.

Hence the degree-one matrix X_i=B_i modulo J^2 is traceless in the commutative associated graded ring gr_J R. The filtered commutator calculation implies

    M(w)-I belongs to Mat_2(J^5),
    its degree-five class is P(X_1,X_2,X_3),
    M(v)-I belongs to Mat_2(J^6),
    its degree-six class is Q(X_1,X_2).

Section 1 makes both displayed leading classes zero. Therefore

    M(w)-I belongs to Mat_2(J^6),
    M(v)-I belongs to Mat_2(J^7).                 (1)

This proves actual ideal-power membership in the rational component-function ring R, not merely vanishing on selected points, numerical approximations, or trace invariance. It does not require identifying gr_J R with a polynomial ring.

## 4. The same words survive in the required nilpotent quotients

Let T=Z<<X_1,...,X_n>> be the ring of noncommutative formal power series with its degree filtration T_{>=r}. Its elements with constant term 1 form a group. By freeness there is a homomorphism

    E:F_n -> 1+T_{>=1},       E(x_i)=1+X_i,

where E(x_i^{-1})=1-X_i+X_i^2-X_i^3+... . Induction from the filtered commutator calculation shows

    E(Gamma_r F_n) is contained in 1+T_{>=r}.   (2)

Only this easy direction is needed; no injectivity theorem about E and no converse dimension-subgroup theorem is being assumed.

Direct use of the same leading-term formula gives

    E(w)=1+P(X_1,X_2,X_3) modulo T_{>=6},
    E(v)=1+Q(X_1,X_2) modulo T_{>=7}.

These Lie polynomials are nonzero in the free associative algebra. Here is a small coefficient certificate for each, with X=X_1, Y=X_2, Z=X_3:

* In P, the coefficient of XXYXZ is -1. Indeed P=CX-XC with C=[XY-YX,XZ-ZX]. The monomial XXYXZ cannot occur in CX because it ends in Z. It occurs once in -XC, from -X(XY)(XZ).
* In Q, the coefficient of XXXYXY is +1. Write U=XY-YX and V=[X,U]=XXY-2XYX+YXX. Then Q=(UV-VU)X-X(UV-VU). The target monomial does not end in X, so it can only arise from -XUV+XVU. The polynomial UV has no term XXYXY, whereas VU has precisely the contribution (XXY)(XY), with coefficient 1.

It follows from (2) that

    w is not in Gamma_6 F_n,
    v is not in Gamma_7 F_n.                   (3)

These are integral nonmembership results. Rational coefficients in R have not replaced Gamma_r F_n by a rational or isolator filtration.

## 5. Automorphisms and the two failures

If a word u does not involve a particular free generator x_t, the assignment

    phi_u(x_t)=x_t u,       phi_u(x_i)=x_i for i!=t

defines an automorphism: its inverse fixes the other generators and sends x_t to x_t u^{-1}. This explicit inverse is valid because both assignments fix u.

The ring R is generated as a Q-algebra by the entries of the n matrices M_i. To see this, matrix entries of positive words are polynomials in these entries, and M_i^{-1} is the adjugate matrix because det(M_i)=1, so inverse-generator entries also are polynomials. Thus if phi_u fixes these generator entries modulo J^{k+1}, it fixes all of R modulo J^{k+1}, and therefore acts trivially on J/J^{k+1}.

For n>=4, take u=w and t=4, fixing all further generators as well. Equation (1) gives

    M(phi_w(x_4))-M(x_4)=M_4(M(w)-I) in Mat_2(J^6).

All other generator matrices are fixed exactly. Therefore phi_w belongs to D_n(5). But x_4^{-1}phi_w(x_4)=w does not belong to Gamma_6 F_n by (3), so phi_w does not belong to A_n(5).

For n>=3, take u=v and t=3, again fixing all further generators. Equation (1) gives triviality modulo J^7 and hence phi_v in D_n(6). Equation (3) gives phi_v not in A_n(6).

This proves both parts of the theorem. If a convention makes the ring action use phi^{-1} rather than phi, its kernel is unchanged: an invertible linear action is trivial exactly when its inverse is trivial. No action-side convention affects the conclusion.

## Scope and verification

The proof concerns actual free-group automorphisms and the rational matrix-entry algebra attached to SL(2,C) representations, precisely as in the cited OWR statement. It is not an inner-automorphism argument in a character ring. The matrix-size restriction is essential; the polynomial P is not a universal Lie identity of 3-by-3 matrices. The mathematical proof above is independent of the accompanying exact-arithmetic script. That script recomputes the relevant universal matrix identities, full truncated noncommutative word expansions, coefficient certificates, explicit automorphism inverses, and negative controls.

The source check did not establish whether this elementary counterexample mechanism has appeared previously. The claimed result is the proved counterexample, not a claim of historical priority or a claim that the question remained globally open until this note.

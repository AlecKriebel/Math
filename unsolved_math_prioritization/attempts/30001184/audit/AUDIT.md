## 2. All-time construction: independent verification

Let X be the extended real interval, with endpoints distinguished. It is compact metrizable, for example using the extension of the arctangent metric. Translation by any finite real number fixes the endpoints and is a homeomorphism. Moreover, its displacement in that metric is bounded uniformly in x by the absolute translation amount. Consequently translation acts point-norm continuously on C(X,M2).

The function g in the proof is continuous: its positive-side limit at zero is zero, its limit at positive infinity is pi/4, and it is zero on the negative half-line and at negative infinity. No derivative at zero is required. Thus V(x)=R(g(x)) is a continuous unitary-valued function.

There is an independent way to verify all cocycle and continuity assertions at once. Set eta(f)(x)=V(x)f(x)V(x)* and let tau_t be translation. Then

    alpha_t = eta^(-1) tau_t eta.

This is exactly the packet's formula. Therefore alpha is an automorphism group, including negative times, and is point-norm continuous because eta is an isometric *-automorphism. Equivalently, direct cancellation proves u_s(x)u_t(x+s)=u_(s+t)(x). No commutation assumption on the matrices is required for that cancellation.

The C*-algebra A generated by alpha_r(D), r>=0, contains the ambient identity, is separable as a C*-subalgebra of the separable ambient algebra, and is invariant under every alpha_t for t>=0. Continuity, multiplicativity, preservation of adjoints, and preservation of the unit all survive restriction. Injectivity survives because alpha_t is injective on the larger algebra. Surjectivity of its restriction is unnecessary.

This choice of A proves precisely the requested forward-generation condition. It does not rely on extra, unobserved summands of the ambient algebra. It also makes A the smallest forward-invariant closed *-subalgebra of the ambient system containing D. This statement concerns generation, not another theory's stronger Markov-dilation minimality.

## 3. Inclusion, expectation, and Markov maps

The inclusion i identifies C^2 with constant diagonal matrices. It is an isometric unital *-embedding. Evaluation at zero is a unital *-homomorphism. Diagonal extraction has the Kraus formula

    Delta(z) = q z q + (1-q) z (1-q),

so it is completely positive and unital. Their composite p is therefore UCP. For diagonal b and c, p(i(b)fi(c))=b p(f)c, while p i=id. Hence i p is a norm-one, positive, idempotent bimodule projection onto i(C^2), as required. Its range lies in A because D is already contained in A.

At x=0, u_t(0)=R(g(t)) for every t>=0. The two diagonal entries after conjugation have coefficients cos^2(g(t)) and sin^2(g(t)), equal respectively to (1+exp(-t))/2 and (1-exp(-t))/2. Thus the displayed P_t is valid at every nonnegative time. Writing P_t=J+exp(-t)H, the identities J^2=J, H^2=H, JH=HJ=0 prove the semigroup law for all real s,t>=0. Its entries are nonnegative and each row sums to one. Complete positivity follows either from the UCP compression formula or positivity on C^2. Analytic dependence of P_t proves uniform continuity of the Markov semigroup.

Point-norm continuity of theta is sufficient here; the proof never needs uniform continuity of theta as a map into the operator norm on all bounded maps. The square-root behavior of g near zero introduces no continuity defect.

## 4. GNS correspondence and representation kernel

For z,w in M2, the module inner product Delta(z*w) records the ordinary inner products of corresponding columns. Therefore its norm is the maximum of the two column norms. It is definite and complete, and the right action is multiplication by diagonal matrices. Left multiplication by an evaluated matrix is bounded and has left multiplication by its adjoint as adjoint. The vector xi=I has inner product 1.

For s=log 2, evaluation of theta_s(q) at zero is the rank-one projection Q with entries 3/4, sqrt(3)/4, sqrt(3)/4, and 1/4. Together q, 1-q, and Q generate all four matrix units: qQ(1-q)=(sqrt(3)/4)e12, and taking its adjoint produces e21. Thus evaluation of A at zero is all of M2. It follows that A xi D spans the claimed module. The canonical GNS construction is therefore unitarily identifiable with this concrete cyclic correspondence.

The left representation is exactly left multiplication by f(0). It vanishes precisely when f(0)=0. In particular, this is the kernel of the entire representation, a closed two-sided ideal, not just the null space of the expectation or a single scalar state. Conversely, faithfulness of Delta on positive matrices already implies that p(f*f)=0 forces f(0)=0 in this example. No faithfulness of p on A is inferred.

The two column spaces are the components selected by the central minimal projections of D. Every adjointable D-linear operator preserves them, so B^a(E)=M2 direct-sum M2. The image pi(A) is the diagonal copy of M2. Also pi i is faithful on D. The module is full because its inner products include 1_D. None of these additional favorable features restores kernel invariance.

## 5. Exact obstruction

For a=q theta_s(q) q-(3/4)q, evaluation at zero is zero. Thus a lies in ker pi. At x=s, the cocycle angle is g(2s)-g(s), and the half-angle calculation gives

    a(s) = d q,   d = (3 sqrt(5)-3)/16 > 0.

After applying theta_s and evaluating at zero, the result is d Q. Hence

    p theta_s(a) = d diag(3/4,1/4) != 0.

The original program's radical calculation was replayed. An independently written program, using squarefree-radicand dictionaries rather than the candidate's fixed field-basis implementation, obtains the same result, including the translated square and exact matrix-unit recovery.

If a map beta_s intertwined the represented dynamics, comparing f=a with f=0 would require both beta_s(0)=pi(theta_s(a)) and beta_s(0)=0. These values are different. Thus there is no induced map on pi(A) at this time, and in particular no endomorphism extension on B^a(E) implementing this fixed dynamics and this fixed representation. This is a first-stage obstruction; no product-system extension theorem is needed.

## 6. Additional independent stress test: failure at every positive time

The obstruction is not an isolated radical coincidence. For any s>0, put r=exp(-s) and define

    a_s = q theta_s(q) q - ((1+r)/2) q.

The same evaluation argument gives pi(a_s)=0. Using cos(2g(s))=r and cos(2g(2s))=r^2 yields

    a_s(s) = d_s q,
    d_s = ((1-r^2)/2) (sqrt(1+r^2)-r) > 0,
    p theta_s(a_s) = d_s diag((1+r)/2,(1-r)/2).

Indeed cos(2(g(2s)-g(s)))=r^3+(1-r^2)sqrt(1+r^2), which immediately gives this expression. Positivity is strict for every 0<r<1.

Even the witnesses themselves can be chosen positive. On the positive half-line,

    g'(x) = 1/(2 sqrt(exp(2x)-1)),

which is positive and strictly decreasing. Continuity at zero and concavity imply 0<=g(x+s)-g(x)<=g(s) for x>=0. For x<=0 the same bounds follow directly from the definition, and at the endpoints the difference is zero. Since cos^2 decreases on [0,pi/4], it follows pointwise that a_s>=0. It is nonzero, since its value at either endpoint is ((1-r)/2)q. Thus the obstruction does not arise from a mistaken cancellation of positive and negative terms in a scalar state.

This strengthening is an audit observation, not a correction necessary for the accepted frozen proof.

- Failure at compactification endpoints: ruled out by endpoint continuity and translation fixing both endpoints.

## 8. Classification and publication boundary

The universal generation-only claim asserts success for every dilation in the stated category. A single such dilation failing kernel invariance refutes that assertion, regardless of what may hold conditional on invariance. A hypothesis logically weaker than forward generation cannot fix this universal statement while leaving the same background category: the present example would still satisfy that weaker hypothesis.

This does not produce a useful sufficient replacement condition or classify which invariant-kernel dilations extend. The exact invariance criterion displayed in the proof is necessary compatibility, not a disguised solution of the independent extension problem.

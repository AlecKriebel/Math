## The narrow printed-definition correction

For the final 2017 Datta–Murai PDF, p. 7, take sigma={z1,z2,z3,z4} and the stacked 4-ball with facets sigma union {x} and sigma union {y}. The edge {z1,x} belongs to R, but the printed precursor {x} does not; it is omitted by the displayed R_x(k)/R_y(k) construction. This is a valid example satisfying the interior-face hypotheses.

The repair is to require the output a union {z_k} to belong to R, rather than its precursor a. Equivalently, use R_x(k)={a in R: z_k in a and a minus {z_k} lies in the x-side of the link}. PROOF.md gives the complete witness and explains why the double-link compatibility restores the intended cut. No novelty claim is made for noticing this correction, and no source author was contacted.

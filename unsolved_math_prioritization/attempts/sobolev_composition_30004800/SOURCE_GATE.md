Publisher report: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2021/40 ; https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46918 . Author preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2112.02126 . The report was published November 26, 2022; 2021 is its volume/workshop year.

The intended setting is real-valued functions on the square two-dimensional torus. Let N_s denote the weighted sum of squared Fourier-coefficient suprema over the time parameter. The source explicitly distinguishes the ordinary Sobolev estimate with the time supremum outside the sum. Its exponential basis requires integer lattice indices Z², although the source prints N²; complex coefficients require absolute squares. These are stated mathematical normalization issues, not an authenticated author erratum.

# KP-3.77: audited boundary partial

**Unsolved, stopped after four of five approaches. No existence, general nonexistence, or novelty claim.**

Rank 916, catalog ID 2875, modern Kirby Problem 3.77 asks for a compact orientable hyperbolic 4-manifold whose entire totally geodesic boundary is one hyperbolic rational homology 3-sphere.

## Accepted scope

- The free orientation-reversing involution obstruction is credited to Ferrari–Kolpakov–Reid.
- Every connected orientable finite cover of the relevant nonorientable filling has at least two boundary components. The even-boundary parity obstruction is restricted to the stated gluing operations.
- The double-and-cut reformulation requires a connected closed ambient manifold; separation supplies two-sidedness and orientability. This is the only clarification in the accepted derivative.
- The connected-sum tubing route loses hyperbolicity. The separating L-space result is not an orientable single totally geodesic boundary construction.
- Eta, homology, intersection-form and volume formulas are necessary conditions only. Formal compatible numerical data do not realize a manifold or metric.

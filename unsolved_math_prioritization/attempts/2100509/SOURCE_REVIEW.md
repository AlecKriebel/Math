## Prior obstruction and hypotheses

Bolsinov–Konyaev–Matveev, *Nijenhuis Geometry*, arXiv:1903.04603v2, https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.04603v2 , has Theorem 6.1 on PDF page 41. During proof review the page was visually inspected, the statement and surrounding proof were read, and the arXiv bibliographic record was reopened.

The theorem is about Nijenhuis tensors on a closed connected smooth manifold: a nonreal eigenvalue at one point persists everywhere with the same algebraic multiplicity. The preceding real-analytic paragraph belongs to the preceding proposition's proof, not to Theorem 6.1. The paper's general setup is smooth; no real-analytic hypothesis, constant Segre type, or global gl-regularity is imposed by this theorem.

For the compact-support problem, extending the tensor by zero from R^n to S^n yields exactly the closed connected setting. It is zero on a neighborhood of infinity, so persistence of any nonreal eigenvalue is impossible. This checks the prior obstruction under the required smooth, potentially singular hypotheses.

The publication metadata is Advances in Mathematics 394 (2022), 108001, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2021.108001 .

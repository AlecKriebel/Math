# Prismatic generic-point vanishing: source-corrected research checkpoint

**Catalogue ID:** 30006336 / OWR-14299291-005. **Local outcome:** `unsolved`.
**Checked:** 4 October 2026. **Proof-attempt budget:** five distinct approaches, 5/5.

The exact source question concerns an integral **separated quotient** of relative
prismatic cohomology. The catalogue's short wording loses this essential qualifier.
Esnault–Kisin–Petrov subsequently announced the desired extension in 2025 talks.
Those announcements must be credited, but a proof-bearing version of their joint
manuscript was not located or verified in this investigation. This packet does not
claim that the problem is currently open in the literature, that the announcement
is wrong, or that the observations below are novel.

The checkable mathematical contribution here is limited to a first-order
Hodge–Tate divisibility deduction and explicit countermodels to several proposed
lifting shortcuts. No proof or counterexample settling the exact separated
prismatic target has been obtained.

# Five substantive approaches

Each row records an actual proof approach, not a count of searches or tool calls.
All five end with an explicit gap; no sixth search turn is claimed.

1. **Hodge–Tate edge map and integral first-order vanishing.**
   Mechanism: proper formal functions and Nakayama remove global i-forms;
   affine Hodge–Tate comparison and the coefficient triangle imply
   image(M_X^i→M_U^i)⊂dM_U^i. Full deduction is in §1 of the note.
   Result: proved as a known-comparison consequence. Gap: lifting the divided
   class back to a source class, or establishing all m-adic orders.

2. **Sheaf/Bockstein descent through successive I-thickenings.**
   Mechanism: use C/d^r→C/d^(r+1)→C/d and kill global obstruction classes.
   A precise conditional sheaf lemma is proved in §2. Result: it would suffice
   to control d-torsion and affine edge maps in the integral sheaf complex.
   Gap: neither torsion-freeness nor integral affine edge-isomorphism follows
   from the Hodge–Tate comparison; global division encounters H¹(F[d]).
   Status: blocked without a new all-order Bockstein input.

3. **Frobenius/Nygaard amplification.**
   Mechanism: φ* C≃Lη_d C and inverse Frobenius up to d^i might amplify
   divisibility. Result: exact d_r=u^(p^r)−p factors and necessary cancellation
   steps identified. The A/(u) control disproves replacing individual d_r
   divisibilities by m-adic convergence. Gap: full integral semilinear argument
   with torsion control and a growing order estimate. Status: unfinished.

4. **Comparison and finite-thickening detection.**
   Mechanism: promote the reported fixed I² result, or combine specializations,
   to detect a zero integral map. Result: multiplication by d^N, by u(u−p),
   and the identity of A/(p) rigorously refute the three generic inference rules.
   Gap: an additional geometric conservativity theorem is required. These
   controls are not prismatic counterexamples. Status: shortcut ruled out.

5. **Topological obstruction and explicit cochain models.**
   Mechanism: test whether derived completeness removes the separated quotient
   and enables a limit argument. Result: the explicit restricted-power-series
   form in §5 gives a non-torsion nonzero class in ⋂p^nH¹ of a derived-complete
   two-term flat complex. Gap: it is not in the image of a demonstrated smooth
   proper source, so it cannot refute or prove the source target. Status:
   separatedness shortcut ruled out; geometric image estimate remains.

The current literature check is separate from these five mathematical routes.
The 2025 affirmative announcements preclude claiming independent novelty.

# Release scope and audit clarifications

The author packet and full independent audit are preserved without editing.
The audit accepts an unresolved partial checkpoint and requires retention of the
Esnault–Kisin–Petrov prior-announcement hold. This is not expert peer review.

The checked deduction is first order: image(Hⁱ_Δ(X̂/A)→Hⁱ_Δ(Û/A)) is contained
in d Hⁱ_Δ(Û/A). It does not establish membership in every mⁿ Hⁱ_Δ(Û/A).
The Hodge–Tate edge map is natural; coherent affine acyclicity on Û kills the
positive hypercohomology filtration, without requiring global degeneration.
Reduction modulo d commutes with derived global sections because A/d is the
perfect cone of multiplication by d. The kernel identity requires no assumption
that multiplication by d is injective on Hⁱ_Δ(Û/A).

Read the conditional sheaf lemma in the bounded-below setting, where its natural
hypercohomology edge maps are defined. The explicit Breuil–Kisin convention is
an interpretation supplied by the notes, not an extra formula quoted from the
short source. The joint manuscript's in-preparation citation describes that
source version; the bounded unsuccessful manuscript search proves no general
nonpublication claim.

The local free-module and cochain controls rule out proposed formal shortcuts.
They are not geometric counterexamples. No stronger conclusion about complete
non-separated cohomology, absolute prismatic cohomology or the generalized Hodge
conjecture is asserted. Only authored analysis, source URLs/fingerprints, code,
and results are included; no third-party source PDF or full text is redistributed.

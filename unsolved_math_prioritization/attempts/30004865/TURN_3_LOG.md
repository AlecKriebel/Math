# Third substantive author turn

This turn sought a mixed multipartite family admitting exact fixed-tester analysis for every parity. On classical Schmidt-correlated states, orthogonal output blocks isolate the diagonal SIC tensor. A new explicit trilinear certificate, bounded on arbitrary complex vectors through a two-by-two positive-semidefinite inequality, saturates every local diagonal image simultaneously. Combining it with paired bilinear certificates covers odd and even numbers of parties, giving exact SIC and realignment values.

The known state family, grouped realignment formula, and separability/genuine-entanglement characterization are credited to Zhao–Fei–Wang. The derivation supplies the full individual-factor complex projective norm and the SIC relation without treating a real-norm computation as complex. The original universal completeness counterexample and the fixed-test incomparability remain preserved. General mixed-state detection regions are not classified.

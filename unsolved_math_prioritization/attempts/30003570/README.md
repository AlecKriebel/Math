## Accepted scope

The setting is the full primitively quasi-polarized K3 arithmetic quotient, with the closed effective cone in its finite-dimensional real Picard space modulo linear equivalence. It is not the ample-polarization locus or an arbitrary compactification's numerical divisor cone.

- Under the explicitly stated literature inputs, degree-two equality of the primitive-Heegner and pseudo-effective cones is equivalent to the slope endpoint s(F2) = 150/57. Strict inclusion would have a prime effective divisor witness of finite slope below 150/57.
- The accepted arguments use the positive Hodge convention of the later slope and NL-cone papers, and the reduced unigonal class.
- Closed-cone unigonal extremality and the published two-ray cone computation remain external literature inputs. Their full proofs and computations were not independently certified here.
- The February 2026 preprint's bound 1/1984 <= s(F2) <= 150/57 leaves the endpoint undecided. The lower-bound coefficient was not recomputed.
- Other accepted elementary reductions address Hodge twisting, supporting functionals and prime witnesses, the rank-two extremality exception, compactification restriction with closure, and boundary-safe covering curves. They do not complete the original problem.

# Publication scope for the combined dipole result

The original [OWR report](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/3995/OWR_2022_43.pdf?sequence=4), pp. 2580–2582, describes a compact particle and point-membership cubic DDA sampling but supplies no technical boundary assumption. Its open question concerns isolated nonreal spectral approximation outside the static lattice interval.

The approved combined claim is:

1. Under voxel-mask convergence in measure (G), the finite diagonal-omitting matrices have the exact reviewed off-interval spectral approximation, including algebraic multiplicity and norm convergence of embedded Riesz projections. This includes all bounded Lipschitz and boundary-measure-zero particles.
2. For arbitrary compact measurable supports without geometric consistency, that universal approximation assertion is false. At explicit positive real frequency 1/10000, a compact positive-volume **empty-interior** support has an isolated nonreal continuum eigenvalue but every matrix on h=1/n is the 3×3 zero matrix. The sole sample point is an isolated measure-zero anchor.

Do not collapse these into either an unrestricted affirmative theorem or a claim that DDA fails for ordinary physical particles. Do not describe the counterexample as having positive interior, smooth boundary, a known numerical eigenvalue, or an effective specified hole index. The no-novelty disclaimer and classical-source credits remain required.

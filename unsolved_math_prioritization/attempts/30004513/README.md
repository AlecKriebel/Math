# Constrained stars: credited sharp bound, original bundle partial

Problem 30004513 / OWR-1703876-006.

For integers b>=1 and k>=0, the sharp arbitrary-b-iatlon threshold is
p(K_{1,k},b)=bk+1. The same upper bound holds for closure drawings under the
(b+1)-point path-component pair condition, with sharpness in every Euclidean
dimension d>=1, including the plane.

This is a source-credit reconstruction and audit of Alper Ferudun’s
September 30, 2026 unrefereed preprint, A Proof of Paták’s kb+1 Conjecture for
Constrained Stars, with Improved Bounds for Complete Graphs.
[Source](https://eulersolve.org/papers/owr-1703876-006/).
The general sharp-star and monotone-system coloring theorems are credited
to Ferudun. Paták retains credit for the original questions, known b=1,2
cases, b-iatlon lower construction and join mechanism; k=0,1 are elementary.
No new theorem, proof novelty or priority is claimed.

The checked join consequence gives p(K_n,b)<=1+b+...+b^(n−1). The Ramsey
lower bound R(n,b+1)<=p(K_n,b) concerns arbitrary b-iatlon graphs and is not
asserted to be planar-closure realizable. The directly checked closure lower
bound is (n−1)b+1. Exact general clique thresholds and the question about
nontrivial higher homology/homotopy remain unanswered here. The original
three-question bundle is PARTIAL.

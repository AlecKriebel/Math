# Version-status check for a newer 1324 growth preprint

Inspected 14 October 2026 during final shared PASS5. This is source-status verification, not an independent audit of the preprint's mathematical proof.

The primary arXiv page for Charles C. Norton, *A new lower bound for the growth rate of Av(1324)*, identifies v3 as the revision dated 4 September 2026. Its abstract reports 10.412263. Its version comments explicitly withdraw v1's 10.617 and v2's 10.466425; the latter withdrawal concerns the tilted construction and its certification. [Primary versioned arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20292v3).

A search result still displayed the obsolete 10.617 abstract. Opening the primary version history resolved that discrepancy. That withdrawn value must not be used to claim a strict height-three gap. The surviving stated value 10.412263 is below 6+2sqrt(5), approximately 10.472136, so it would not supply that gap either.

Only the abstract, version history and withdrawal comments were checked for this conclusion. No peer-review status or independent correctness of the surviving preprint theorem is asserted. The established PASS5 height-two theorem continues to use the previously inspected BBEPP lower bound 81/8.

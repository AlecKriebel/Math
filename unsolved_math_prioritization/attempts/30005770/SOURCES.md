# Public sources and inspection limits

This file summarizes the source roles recorded in the accepted proofs and audit metadata. It is not a new literature search, a literature-exhaustive openness claim, a novelty assessment, or certification of every cited paper. The metadata records inspections on 14 October 2026. Public titles, URLs, versions, hashes and byte counts may be checked without distributing the source documents; no third-party PDF or copied source text is included.

## Target and credited coverage

- **Mini-Workshop: Permutation Patterns**, Oberwolfach Reports 21 (2024), no.1, pp.273–308, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2024/6. Troyka's contribution, joint with Bevan, states the target on printed p.298. Bevan's contribution gives the uniform model. [Public report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/48646?nt=1), [source metadata](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/SOURCE_PINS.json).
- **Kaitlyn Hohmeier and Erik Slivken, Permuton limits for some permutations avoiding a single pattern**, arXiv:2502.19541v3, 24 February 2026. Theorem 1.1 supplies the credited family after symmetry; Section 6 describes 1342 and 1324 as conjectural in that manuscript. Its p.2 total-variation definition conflicts with the p.3 weak-topology theorem wording. The target here is weak convergence, and that source's full proof was not independently audited. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.19541v3).
- The **Permutation Patterns 2023 booklet**, p.123, and **Bevan's problem slide** record a 1342/2431 announcement. Their earlier date and the later v3 conjectural status are both retained. No reason, formal withdrawal, or priority conclusion is inferred. [Booklet](https://2023.permutationpatterns.com/booklet/bookletco.pdf#page=123), [slide](https://2023.permutationpatterns.com/p/problem_session/bevan_scalinglimitproblem.pdf#page=1).
- **Neal Madras and Gökhan Yıldırım, Longest Monotone Subsequences and Rare Regions of Pattern-Avoiding Permutations.** The inspected Theorems 1(a) and 17(a) give rare/nonrare-region statements under their hypotheses; they do not identify the target limit's support. The older Atapour–Madras 2014 full text was not inspected. [Published paper](https://www.combinatorics.org/ojs/index.php/eljc/article/download/v24i4p13/pdf/).

## Inputs to the proved infinite families

- **Miklós Bóna, Exact enumeration of 1342-avoiding permutations: a close link with labeled trees and planar maps**, arXiv:math/9702223v1, 11 February 1997. The credited input is the exact labeled-tree/forest bijection and preservation of minima. The preprint's child-before-parent traversal is postorder despite its terminology. Its displayed indexed count and expanded full-series denominator contain inconsistencies with its own definitions. The authored [kernel appendix](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass1_entropy/KERNEL_ENUMERATION_APPENDIX.md) derives the needed series from the tree specification. **No claim is made that these displayed inconsistencies occur in the final publication.** [Inspected preprint](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9702223), [independent source pins](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/independent_family_reaudit/SOURCE_PINS.json), [convention audit](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass1_bijection_audit/SOURCE_CONVENTION_PIN.md).
- **Miklós Bóna, The limit of a Stanley–Wilf sequence is not always rational, and layered patterns beat monotone patterns**, arXiv:math/0403502v2, 13 May 2004; published in J. Combin. Theory Ser. A 110 (2005), 223–235. Proposition 5.1, Lemma 5.3 and Theorem 5.4 are used with the starts-with-1 restriction for the exact growth equality and minima deletion. This counting equality is not itself a geometric limit theorem. [Inspected v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0403502v2), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2004.07.014), [pins](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass2_protected_tail_extension/SOURCE_PINS.json).

## Staircase and analytic inputs

- **David Bevan, Robert Brignall, Andrew Elvey Price and Jay Pantone, A structural characterisation of Av(1324) and new bounds on its growth rate.** The pinned primary PDF supplies the greedy staircase, domino enumeration and rigorous growth bounds. Different accepted notes inspect Proposition 1, Theorems 2, 8, 9 and 16, including the counting constructions where needed. The positive low-chain theorem needs only λ≥81/8>8. The inspected 10.271012 lower bound is used for a source-qualified comparison, without claiming it is the latest optimal bound. [Primary PDF](https://oro.open.ac.uk/68534/1/1324-staircase-els.pdf), [staircase pins](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass2_1324_shape/SOURCE_PINS.json), [later theorem pins](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass5_pair_counts/SOURCE_PINS.json).
- The earlier conditional compact-operator argument cites **Demkowicz (2024)** for Riesz projections/compact eigenvalues and **Nonnenmacher (2022)** for compact approximation and spectrum. Relevant page ranges and PDF hashes are in [spectral source pins](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass2_staircase_bias/SPECTRAL_SOURCE_PINS.json). These inputs do not establish the strict squared-kernel gap; the package later proves that gap impossible.
- **Michael H. Albert, Robert Brignall, Nik Ruškuc and Vincent Vatter, Rationality For Subclasses of 321-Avoiding Permutations**, Section 2, supports the monotone staircase correspondence after reflection. **David Bevan, Growth rates of permutation grid classes, tours on graphs, and the spectral radius**, Theorem 3.6, is credited for the general grid growth formula. The comparison model's specific fixed-width formula is proved directly by entropy maximization in the authored note. [Monotone staircase source](https://www.rbrignall.org.uk/papers/av321.pdf), [grid growth source](https://oro.open.ac.uk/38963/1/GrowthRatesRevision3.pdf), [inspection record](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass5_collective_windows/SOURCE_INSPECTION.json).

## Recent manuscript status checks only

- **Charles C. Norton, A new lower bound for the growth rate of Av(1324)**, arXiv:2608.20292v3, 4 September 2026. The version-specific abstract/history/comments explicitly withdraw the earlier 10.617 and 10.466425 claims; v3 states **10.412263**. It is below 6+2√5≈10.472136, so no height-three strict gap follows. The surviving preprint proof is not adopted or independently certified here. [Versioned record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.20292v3), [status explanation](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass5_concentration_audit/NEW_LOWER_BOUND_STATUS.md), [status metadata](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass5_concentration_audit/VERSION_STATUS_METADATA.json).
- **Robert Brignall, Shuffle automata and the growth of 1324-avoiding permutations**, arXiv:2610.02535v1, 1 October 2026, was inspected for structural/status context and a cross-check of Norton's surviving claim. No theorem here depends on its reported new upper bound. [Versioned manuscript](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02535v1).
- **Protected tails and polynomial-time enumeration of permutations avoiding a direct sum of an increasing pattern and 231**, arXiv:2609.15642v2, 1 October 2026, supplies an inspected exact enumeration/sampling route, not an inspected limit theorem. It is not needed by the accepted family proofs. [Versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.15642v2), [inspection metadata](diagonal_permuton_1778_20261014/pass1_uncovered_case/SOURCE_PINS.json).

# Sources and credit

This edition preserves the earlier inspected sources and public PDF metadata. It does not claim a fresh retrieval, later publication-status update, novelty or exhaustive priority search.

- Chudnovsky and Sivaraman, “Perfect divisibility and 2-divisibility,” author PDF dated 20 April 2017: https://web.math.princeton.edu/~mchudnov/Perfect2div.pdf. The target is two-part clique-number reduction, a different property from perfect divisibility.
- Chudnovsky, Robertson, Seymour and Thomas, “K4-free graphs with no odd holes,” JCTB 100 (2010), 313–331, Theorem 1.3: https://thomas.math.gatech.edu/PAP/k4free.pdf. The author PDF is dated 5 October 2001 and revised 5 August 2009. Its K4-free odd-hole-free four-color theorem implies the necessary K4 inside X at k=4. Its full proof is not re-audited here. The known omega<=3 target range follows by grouping four proper color classes at omega=3 and by bipartiteness at omega=2.
- Hu, Xu and Zhuang, “Some properties of minimally nonperfectly divisible graphs,” arXiv:2603.01967v2, 5 March 2026: https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01967v2 and https://arxiv.org/html/2603.01967v2#S4. Section 4 provides relevant no-basin, minimum-degree, clique-cutset trace-incompatibility and blocking-clique reductions. The inspected source is a preprint. Restricted minimal-nonperfect-divisibility statements are not unrestricted 2-divisibility or clique-cutset closure. See the [earlier complete source-credit audit](../3251_divisibility_partials_20261012/PRIOR_CREDIT_AUDIT.md).
- Hoàng, “On the structure of perfectly divisible graphs,” arXiv:2506.12660v2, 17 June 2025: https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12660v2. Conjecture 4.7 records unrestricted minimal non-2-divisible clique-cutset exclusion as open in that source; it is not imported as a theorem.
- He, Shi, Wu and Yao, “The perfect divisibility and chromatic number of some odd hole-free graphs,” arXiv:2603.09549v1, 10 March 2026: https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09549v1. This is a specialized-class source, not a solution of the unrestricted target.

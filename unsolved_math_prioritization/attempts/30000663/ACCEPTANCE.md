## Accepted mathematical content

1. X=Spec C[x,y,z]/(x²y−z²+1) is an integral, smooth, normal affine complex surface. The action of Gm of weights (1,−2,0) is effective.
2. φ(x,y,z)=(2xz,y,2z²−1) is an everywhere regular endomorphism preserving X and commuting with the identical source and target action. Two explicit quadratic étale algebra charts cover the source, including z=0, so φ is globally étale, not merely generically unramified.
3. The invariant ring is C[z]. The induced quotient map is z↦2z²−1; the nonzero relative differential fiber at z=0 proves ramification, of index two.
4. The exact image is X minus the entire line {y=0,z=−1}. The map is finite étale of degree two onto this open, with two points in every nonempty fiber. The map to X is dominant and nonsurjective.
5. The original report imposes no surjectivity hypothesis on this question. Since Gm is connected reductive, the example also refutes the unrestricted connected-reductive assertion printed there.

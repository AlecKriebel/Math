# 5100064 / arXiv k905: inner focal-inversion areas

The inner polygon consists of caustic contact points. For every primitive even orbit with a strict nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic, its two ordinary unit-circle focal vertex inversions have equal signed areas. Thus their ratio is 1 wherever the common area is nonzero. A constant extension at a common zero is separate from the raw quotient.

An exact primitive four-orbit on x²/2+y²=1 shows the qualification is necessary: all original and caustic data are nondegenerate, every inverted vertex is finite and distinct, but each inverted vertex polygon is collinear and has area zero.

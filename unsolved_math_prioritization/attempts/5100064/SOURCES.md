# Exact source, known input, and readiness gate

## Source target

Reznik–Garcia–Koiller, *Eighty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard*, arXiv:2004.12497v11, **29 October 2020**, https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.12497v11. Table 10, printed p.12, row **k905**: double-primed focal inverse areas have ratio 1 for even N. The double prime denotes the **inner polygon of caustic tangency points**, as established by Figure 1, the preliminaries, and the symbols appendix. Section 3.9 defines ordinary unit-radius focal inversion and a straight polygon through the inverted vertices. Section 2, equation (1), defines signed shoelace area.

The source introduction restricts the elliptic billiard ensemble to a confocal ellipse pair. The proof restores a strict nondegenerate caustic and least period, including primitive stars. It does not silently import the different symmetry rules for hyperbolic caustics or count a repeated odd list as even primitive.

The published companion, *Fifty New Invariants*, Arnold Mathematical Journal 7 (2021), 341–355, https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0174.pdf, **omits k905 and the 900-series table altogether**. Its Table 9 p.350 contains only the five retained inversive rows k801, k802,a/b, k803, k804; its Table 10 is a video list. The printed arXiv Table 10 and final Table 9 pages were visually inspected. The prior report's implication of the same k905 row in both editions is not supported.

## Classical input and credit

- Hellmuth Stachel, *The geometry of billiards in ellipses and their Poncelet grids*, Journal of Geometry 112, article 40 (2021), https://doi.org/10.1007/s00022-021-00606-2. Corollary 4.2(i), pp.22–23 and its proof, states the central symmetry for even period and odd turning number with an elliptical caustic. The preceding gcd discussion distinguishes primitive periods.
- Hellmuth Stachel, *On the Motion of Billiards in Ellipses*, European Journal of Mathematics 8 (2022), 1602–1622, https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2. Theorem 4.3 and (4.9), p.1614, provide canonical orbit coordinates and primitive rotation; the contact parameters u±Δu on p.1616, leading to Theorem 4.6, verify the same shift for the inner contacts. This supplies a direct check of the contact-point order and distinctness used in the proof and numerical diagnostics.

The neighboring arXiv rows k903,b and k904,b already credit symmetry for equality of original and outer inverse areas. The current inner-polygon assertion is the same classical geometric mechanism after checking its actual derived points. We do not present it as a new integrability theorem or historical discovery.

## Closest classical candidates

[Báez-Duarte 2000, arXiv:math/0011254v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011254v1) was inspected in the introduction, Section 2's endpoint statements, Section 3, and Section 4's qualifications. Its real integral implication is reproduced with full credit in Proposition 3. Its converse invokes the usual zero-free machinery; the subcritical Lp conclusion is distinguished from L2. Proposition 2.2 and Remark 2.4 explicitly show that the actual Möbius endpoint moment is infinite, using known critical-line zeros. That fact is background, not an unproved assumption in our real-variable derivations.

[Báez-Duarte's strengthening](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0202141v2), published in 2003, replaces real dilation parameters with integers. The complete seven-page author preprint was retrieved; the theorem, finite damped approximants, proof, and comments were read. The proof uses Littlewood-type Dirichlet-series convergence, Mellin–Plancherel and the zeta functional equation. Our finite damped sums are the sign-changed reciprocal-coordinate versions of its Section 2.2 approximants. No novelty is claimed for this family. The 2003 bibliographic attribution is also given in the later OWR report. A current direct fetch of the Bdim catalogue page returned HTTP 502; its full catalogue page was not relied on.

# Source review and exact accepted scope

## Originating question

J. P. C. Greenlees, *The singularity category and duality for complete
intersection groups*, Oberwolfach Report 9/2026, pp. 592–595, Question 2.2
on printed p. 594 (PDF page 54), is the originating source for problem
30006658 / OWR-14299915-021.

- Report DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2026/9
- Official PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/53607?nt=1

The accepted theorem concerns every prime p, every field k of characteristic
p, and every compact Lie group G with non-p-nilpotent component group.
For R = C_*(Omega((BG)^p-completed); k), the canonical k-cellularization map
to R induces zero in every homotopy degree. Completion precedes looping.
The theorem includes the positive-dimensional question and needs no
orientability of the adjoint representation. Zero on all homotopy groups
is the conclusion; nullity of the morphism in the derived category is not.
No coefficient-ring generalization is asserted.

## Credited analytic inputs

Dwyer, Greenlees and Iyengar, *Duality in algebra and topology*, Advances
in Mathematics 200 (2006), 357–402, supplies compact proxy-smallness and
Morita theory: Definition 4.6, Remark 4.8, Theorem 4.9, Proposition 4.17,
§4.22 and §5.7. The last section supplies both cochain and completed-loop-chain
proxy-smallness for arbitrary compact Lie groups over Fp, without an
orientability hypothesis. The report extends the chain-algebra compact proxy
to every characteristic-p field by scalar extension. It does not commute
an infinite cochain dual with extension of scalars.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2005.11.004
- Publisher-version mirror: https://www.maths.ed.ac.uk/~v1ranick/papers/dwgriy.pdf
- Primary arXiv version: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0510247

C. Lomp, *Integrals in Hopf algebras over rings*, §2.4, records the finite
one-sided ideal criterion and credits the field theorem to Sweedler. The
report includes its complete finite coefficient-space and Hopf-module proof,
uses opposite-and-coopposite for left ideals without an inverse-antipode
assumption, and treats graded signs by parity bosonization in odd
characteristic. In characteristic two it legitimately forgets the grading.

- Primary manuscript: https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0307046

K. Ishiguro, *Classifying spaces of compact Lie groups that are p-compact
for all prime numbers*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 10 (2007), 195–211,
Proposition 1.3(a), gives the necessary implication from p-compactness of BG
to p-nilpotence of its component group. The proof uses its contrapositive
only. It therefore obtains infinite-dimensional total homology of the
completed loop space under the theorem's hypothesis.

- DOI: https://doi.org/10.2140/gtm.2007.10.195
- Official PDF: https://msp.org/gtm/2007/10/gtm-2007-10-011p.pdf

Ishiguro's 2007 article explicitly restates Proposition 3.1 of his *Toral
groups and classifying spaces of p-compact groups*, Contemporary Mathematics
271 (2001), 155–167. The original 2001 article was not separately inspected;
the author's explicit primary restatement was inspected.

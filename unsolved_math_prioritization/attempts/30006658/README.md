# Cellularization vanishing for non-p-nilpotent component groups

Problem 30006658 / OWR-14299915-021 has a complete authored proof accepted
for its full stated scope by the accompanying independent mathematical
audit on 10 October 2026, with no required mathematical correction.

For every prime p, every field k of characteristic p, and every compact Lie
group G whose finite component group is not p-nilpotent, put
R = C_*(Omega((BG)^p-completed); k). The canonical k-cellularization map to
R is zero on all homotopy groups. Completion precedes looping. The theorem
requires no adjoint-orientability assumption, nilpotent action or
spectral-sequence collapse. It does not assert that the map is null in the
derived category, and does not extend the statement to coefficient rings.

The proof includes the finite-ideal Hopf-module argument and the graded-sign
reduction, compact-proxy factorization for each homotopy class, scalar
extension to arbitrary characteristic-p fields, and the application of
Ishiguro's necessary implication. The question is credited to J. P. C.
Greenlees. Dwyer–Greenlees–Iyengar, Lomp/Sweedler and Ishiguro receive credit
for the constituent prior theorems. The report depends on no omitted program.

This is an AI-assisted, unrefereed manuscript and audit. Acceptance denotes
the accompanying mathematical review; it does not mean external human peer
review, journal acceptance or formal proof-assistant certification. There
is no novelty, first-in-literature or exhaustive current-openness claim.

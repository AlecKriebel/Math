# Operative source clarification S1

This addendum and the independent audit are operative for this publication. The author freeze and audit freeze are immutable historical artifacts. Their statements that audit or publication had not yet occurred describe their respective preparation times; they do not supersede the acceptance recorded here.

The 2008 report defines the strict cone C_d by scal(R) > d ||R_W||, but its displayed preservation theorem is for the closure of C_d. That closure is the target closed cone after the substitution c = 2n(n-1)/d^2. The report states PDE sufficiency; necessity for PDE preservation is separately conjectural. Neither the strict definition nor the closure theorem may be silently substituted for the closed-cone ODE equivalence in the 2007 target.

Source: Christoph Böhm, joint with Burkhard Wilking, “Ricci flow in higher dimensions,” OWR 34/2008, p. 1942. [Primary report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46179). The audit records visual inspection of the overbars on the hash-matched PDF. The source PDF is not distributed.

The 2007 report's repeated even-parity wording is a printed defect. Reading the second case as odd with interval membership is an inferred repair, not an official erratum.

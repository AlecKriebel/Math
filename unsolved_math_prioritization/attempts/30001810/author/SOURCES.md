# Source and status audit

Checked 2026-10-07. This file records public bibliographic facts and inspection results, not copied source documents.

## Primary problem

Michelle Bucher, “Euler characteristic, simplicial volume and the Schläfli volume formula,” contribution to *Arithmetic Groups vs. Mapping Class Groups: Similarities, Analogies and Differences*, Oberwolfach Report 30/2011, pp. 1673–1675. [Report PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46346). [Report DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2011/30).

The definition and Question 4 were read in the complete PDF; printed pp. 1674–1675 (PDF pages 38–39) were also rendered and visually inspected. The surrounding definition fixes closed oriented manifolds and real fundamental cycles; the theorem explicitly supplies the smooth setting. The question has no dimension exclusion. Its simplex condition requires an immersion on an open neighborhood of the entire simplex. No pseudomanifold target is specified.

The report lists equality for locally symmetric spaces modeled on real hyperbolic space, SL(n,R)/SO(n), and products of these. These are attributed source statements, not independently reproved in this packet.

The manuscript cited for the Euler-characteristic comparison is M. Bucher, *Euler characteristic, immersive simplicial volume and generalized Schläfli Formulas*, described there as a preprint in preparation in 2011. No separate copy or later publication of that exact title was located. The [author's public publication list](https://www.unige.ch/~bucherka/Publications.html) was checked; absence there does not establish nonexistence. The two source URLs in the problem corpus lead to the same OWR report, so they are not independent evidence.

## A relevant homology theorem that does not settle the norm

Jean Cerf, *Combinatoire des simplexes sans singularités I. Le cas différentiable*, Annales de l'Institut Fourier 48 (1998), 1129–1166. [DOI and official article](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/articles/10.5802/aif.1652/). [Publisher PDF](https://aif.centre-mersenne.org/item/10.5802/aif.1652.pdf).

Section 5.1, printed p. 1156, gives the immersed-simplex extension of Lalonde's theorem: the canonical homology map is an isomorphism in degrees below the specified codimension and a surjection in that codimension. In the unfoliated interpretation this distinguishes degrees below the manifold dimension from the top degree. No isometric ℓ¹ assertion is supplied by that theorem. Surjectivity gives representatives, not equality of their infimal norms. The introduction and precise Section 5.1 statement were inspected, not every proof in the paper.

One initial download from a NUMDAM mirror timed out after partial transfer and failed PDF parsing. It was not accepted as a complete source. A second download from the article's publisher endpoint completed and parsed successfully. Hashes below refer only to complete accepted PDFs.

## Later neighboring literature and the scope of its progress

Clara Löh, Marco Moraschini, George Raptis, *On the simplicial volume and the Euler characteristic of (aspherical) manifolds*, Research in the Mathematical Sciences 9 (2022), article 44. [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.08115), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40687-022-00341-3), [author PDF](https://loeh.app.uni-regensburg.de/preprints/euler.pdf).

The introduction and the bounded-cohomology discussion were inspected in the complete author PDF. They describe the broader Euler-characteristic vanishing problem and several strategies. This is contextual evidence, not a claimed proof of Bucher's immersive equality.

Inkang Kim, Xueyuan Wan, *Positivity of simplicial volume for closed nonpositively curved four-manifolds with nonzero Euler characteristic*, [arXiv:2506.09524v4](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.09524v4), [versioned PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.09524v4). The submission record dates v4 to 17 July 2026 and calls it a final version. The introduction and Theorem 1.4 were checked in the 19-page PDF: the stated estimate is ||M||≥|χ(M)|/11 for closed nonpositively curved 4-manifolds. It concerns ordinary simplicial volume, adds curvature and dimension hypotheses, and does not state ordinary/immersive equality. The full proof was not independently audited. The HTML rendering and PDF display different internal manuscript dates; the versioned PDF and its byte hash are the archival basis here, with no attempt to reconcile those dates.

## Status conclusion and limits

Exact-phrase searches for immersive simplicial volume, the supporting manuscript title, and broader author/topic searches found no later credited general solution. This finite search cannot establish that no solution exists. The correct disposition is: general comparison unresolved in this investigation; five mathematical approaches yield the proved partials and obstruction in the report. No work in this packet depends on the correctness of the unpublished Euler bound or the recent four-dimensional estimate.

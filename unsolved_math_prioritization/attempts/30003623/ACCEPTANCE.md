## Accepted conclusion

Let E be a regular compact subset of the real line with c=Cap(E)>0. Let T_n be the monic degree-n polynomial minimizing the supremum norm on E. If ||T_n||_E/c^n is bounded for every n>=1 by one finite constant, then the sum of the Green-function maxima over all bounded gaps of E is finite and E has positive Lebesgue measure.

Thus the requested non-Parreau–Widom example does not exist, and the requested zero-Lebesgue-measure example does not exist. The source also states the exact equivalence with bounded equilibrium L2 Widom factors and the PW condition; the audit's decisive reconstructed contribution is verification of the unrestricted real converse and its direct entropy-based positive-measure consequence.

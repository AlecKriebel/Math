# Real Chebyshev converse: an audit of an existing proof

Target: 30003623 / OWR-15954-001.

Both original existence questions have negative answers. For a regular compact subset E of the real line with positive logarithmic capacity, bounded monic Chebyshev Widom factors in every degree force E to be Parreau–Widom and to have positive Lebesgue measure. Consequently neither a non-Parreau–Widom example nor a zero-measure example exists within the stated class.

The result is due to Gökalp Alpan and Maxim Zinchenko, *A four-way Szegő theorem for L^p extremal polynomials on subsets of R*, arXiv:2608.17321v1, submitted August 18, 2026. Corollary 3.7 is the exact target, supported by Lemmas 3.3–3.4 and Theorem 3.5. The audit found no gap requiring a theorem repair.

## Exact scope

Regularity for the Dirichlet problem, compactness, containment in the real line, and positive logarithmic capacity are retained. The bound is uniform over all polynomial degrees; boundedness of a subsequence is insufficient. The unweighted target uses the equilibrium probability measure, weight 1, and no isolated added support points. Positive Lebesgue measure and a canonical generator are conclusions or unnecessary hypotheses, respectively, rather than assumptions in the converse.

No extension to irregular sets, arbitrary complex planar sets, or bounded subsequences is asserted. The exact unweighted proof is fully reconstructed. Weighted reductions and isolated-point arguments have the narrower inspection coverage stated in PROOF.md, Section 3; standard external regular-measure and zero-distribution results were not independently re-proved.

## Exact source target

For a finite simple undirected graph G with n>=2 vertices, let w be the number of distinct vertex degrees. The [complete original OPG page](https://www.openproblemgarden.org/op/melnikovs_valency_variety_problem) asks whether

    chi(G) > ceil( floor(w/2)/(n-w) ).

Its formula image was independently downloaded and visually read: the outer bracket is ceiling, the inner bracket is floor, and the prose uses strict greater-than. The numerator is not w itself. The full source discussion and bibliography were read.

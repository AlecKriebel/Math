## Independent all-real reconstruction

I reconstructed this chain before relying on prior review conclusions:

1. Translate a focus to zero. The defining equations are Z·A=|A|² and Z·B=|B|². Noncollinear focal endpoint vectors give a nonzero determinant. Solving gives |Z|²=|A-B|²/sin²(alpha). The ordinary sideline height is h=|A||B||sin(alpha)|/|A-B|, so the positive root is q=|A||B|/h. This is an actual ordinary norm, without signed-root substitution.
2. A tangent to the strictly inner ellipse has its contact strictly between the outer intersections. At each vertex the rays toward its two contacts bound the cone containing the caustic. The caustic lies on opposite sides of those rays. Reversing the incoming ray and selecting the other tangent preserves its side. Nonretracing continuation therefore propagates one side throughout the cyclic list; reversing the list makes it the left side. This local argument assumes neither convexity of the polygon, winding one, sorted vertex parameters, nor even period.
3. Set n=(u,v), t=(-v,u), K=a²u²+b²v², rho=sqrt(K-lambda)>0. Completing the square in the outer ellipse along the sideline gives M=(a²rho u/K,b²rho v/K), ell=ab sqrt(lambda)/K, A=M-ell t and B=M+ell t. The linear term vanishes, the quadratic coefficient is K/(a²b²), and the constant is rho²/K, verifying both endpoints and their direction.
4. Heights h_sigma=rho-sigma c u have product b²-lambda>0 and sum 2rho>0. Both are strictly positive ordinary heights; equivalently both foci are inside the caustic. Neither focal endpoint determinant vanishes. Every antipedal intersection is finite and its norm strictly positive.
5. On the outer ellipse, the positive focal radius is a-sigma c x/a, bounded below by a-c>0. The chord gives x_A+x_B=2a²rho u/K and x_A x_B=a²(rho²-b²v²)/K. Expansion yields R_sigma=(a²h_sigma²+b²lambda)/K, hence q_sigma=(a²h_sigma+b²lambda/h_sigma)/K.
6. Subtraction gives (2cu/K)[-a²+b²lambda/(b²-lambda)]. The chord also gives y_B-y_A=2ab sqrt(lambda)u/K. These identify exactly Gamma=c[-a²+b²lambda/(b²-lambda)]/(ab sqrt(lambda)), without division by u, displacement or Gamma.
7. The fixed caustic gives the same coefficient on every left-oriented edge. Closure telescopes the vertical displacements to zero. Every summand is positive and finite, so the ratio denominator is nonzero. Reversal preserves unoriented antipedal intersections and reverses the displacement coefficient's sign. Repeated traversal repeats the summands.

Thus odd primitive periods, stars of every admitted winding, and repeats of odd primitive orbits are covered, including even-length repeated lists that need not be centrally paired. Central inversion alone would not settle that last case. The global orientation step is necessary: arbitrary independent edge reversal would invalidate a common displacement coefficient. The manuscript handles it correctly.

Horizontal edges have u=0, equal heights and equal norms, so both identity sides vanish. Gamma vanishes at lambda=a²b²/(a²+b²), strictly inside the domain, where each edge already has equal norms. Its sign changes without changing individual positivity. At lambda→0 the chord collapses; at lambda→b² strict focal-height positivity can fail and norms can diverge. Unsupported continuity cannot import those limits into the regular theorem. The circular limit has coincident foci and immediate equality wherever nonsingular; it is not needed here. Units agree: q/displacement are lengths, lambda/K are squared lengths, Gamma is dimensionless.

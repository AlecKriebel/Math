# Source and prior-attempt gate: ID 2650 / KOU-21.141

Checked 2026-10-03, before budgeted proof attempts.

## Exact target

P. A. Zalesskii asks whether the proper amalgamated free pro-p product
G = G1 amalg_H G2 is coherent when G1 and G2 are coherent and H is
polycyclic in the pro-p sense. Coherence here quantifies over all finitely
generated closed subgroups and means finite pro-p presentability. All groups,
subgroups, maps, generation and normal closures in this dossier are topological.
The conventional proper-amalgam interpretation identifies the factors with
closed subgroups of G; no assertion about an arbitrary noninjective pushout is
made. The full question is not reduced to finite, procyclic or malnormal H.

## Primary mathematical sources checked

1. Z. Chatzidakis and P. Zalesskii, *Pro-p groups acting on trees with finitely
   many maximal vertex stabilizers up to conjugation*, Israel J. Math. 247
   (2022), 593–634; corrected arXiv v2, 12 February 2023.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.06867v2 . Theorems 5.1 and 6.8 give finite
   graph-of-groups descriptions under finite-maximal-stabilizer or procyclic-edge
   hypotheses, respectively. These are prior results, not new achievements here.
2. I. Castellano and P. A. Zalesskii, *A pro-p version of Sela’s accessibility
   and Poincaré duality pro-p groups*, DOI 10.4171/GGD/769, published online
   2023, journal volume 2024. https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/33381 .
   Corollary 3.14 proves coherence with analytic H malnormal in one factor.
   Theorem 3.15 provides the acylindrical subgroup decomposition used below.
   Removing the malnormal/acylindrical hypothesis is not justified by this paper.
3. I. Snopce and P. Zalesskii, *Right-angled Artin pro-p groups*, Bull. London
   Math. Soc. (2022), DOI 10.1112/blms.12664.
   https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01685 . Theorem 1.6 characterizes coherence by
   chordality of the defining finite graph; Section 5 gives explicit incoherent
   subgroups for induced cycles. This is a counterexample-search baseline.

Current primary literature searches on the exact target and pro-p coherence
with polycyclic amalgamation found no full resolution. The October Notebook
still listing it is additional dated evidence, not a completeness guarantee.
The abstract-group analogue alone is insufficient: general subgroup
Bass–Serre decompositions do not transfer automatically to pro-p trees.

## Budget and success criterion

Five substantive written attempts, counted honestly in sequence. Retrieval,
source review, independent checking and packaging are not attempts. A full
solution needs either a proof for every finitely generated closed K <= G or
an explicit proper amalgam satisfying the stated hypotheses with a finitely
generated closed subgroup proved not finitely presented. Every special-case
result below will be labeled as such, with no first-priority claim.

# Attempt 4: test a rank-only accessibility bound

Time: 2026-10-03T13:27:00Z. Budget: 4/5.
Outcome: an explicit family rules out a uniform edge bound depending only
on generator number and subgroup rank of the edge groups. It is not an
incoherent group. Full-target completion estimate: 15%, lowered because
this simple accessibility mechanism fails.

## Proposed route

Polycyclic H has finite rank: its closed subgroups need at most some fixed
number of generators. A tempting extension of the procyclic theorem would
bound the number of edges of every reduced finite graph by a function of
just d(K) and that rank. The following family shows this cannot work in
that form, even for rank at most two and faithful actions after quotienting.

## A chain of abelian rank-two vertices

Fix p and n >= 1. For i=0,...,n put V_i = <x_i,y_i> = Z_p^2. Join consecutive
vertices by an edge E_i = Z_p^2 whose two generators a_i,b_i map as

a_i -> x_i^p,   b_i -> y_i          at V_i,
a_i -> x_(i+1), b_i -> y_(i+1)^p    at V_(i+1).

Each edge inclusion has index p, so no edge can be collapsed by the usual
reduction rule. The fundamental pro-p group P_n has the presentation

<x_0,y_0,...,x_n,y_n |
 [x_i,y_i]=1 (0<=i<=n),
 x_(i+1)=x_i^p, y_i=y_(i+1)^p (0<=i<n)>.

This graph is proper. To prove it, map P_n continuously onto Z_p^2 with
x_i -> (p^i,0), y_i -> (0,p^(n-i)). All displayed relations hold, and the
restriction from each V_i is the injective diagonal map with these entries.
If a vertex element had been killed in P_n, its image under this composite
would be zero, contradicting injectivity. This verifies properness rather
than assuming it for a proposed pro-p amalgam.

Eliminating the intermediate generators, and writing x=x_0, y=y_n, gives

P_n = <x,y | [x^(p^i),y^(p^(n-i))]=1, 0<=i<=n>_pro-p.

Thus d(P_n)<=2; the surjection to Z_p^2 makes d(P_n)=2. This is a proper
reduced graph with n edges, all edge and vertex groups Z_p^2. There is no
uniform bound on n in terms of d=2 and rank=2 alone.

## Remove the nonfaithful kernel

To ensure the preceding observation is not solely an artifact of a common
kernel, set C_n = <x^(p^n),y^(p^n)>. The endpoint relations say that each
of its two generators commutes with both x and y, so C_n is central. Its
image in Z_p^2 is the lattice p^n Z_p^2 and proves C_n is isomorphic to
Z_p^2. It lies in every vertex group and every edge group.

Put Q_n=P_n/C_n. Quotienting the graph by this common normal subgroup gives
a proper reduced graph of finite p-groups. The vertex at i is

V_i/C_n = C_(p^(n-i)) x C_(p^i),

where C_(p^0)=1. The edge at i is

E_i/C_n = C_(p^(n-i-1)) x C_(p^i).

Vertex and edge orders are p^n and p^(n-1), respectively, so both edge
inclusions still have index p. These groups inject into (Z/p^n)^2 by the
same diagonal maps, establishing properness of the quotient graph.
Q_n is generated by x,y and has abelianization (C_(p^n))^2, so d(Q_n)=2.
All closed subgroups of its finite abelian edge groups need at most two
generators.

The endpoint vertex groups map injectively to the two coordinate axes of
(C_(p^n))^2. Their intersection inside Q_n is therefore trivial: an element
in both has zero abelian image, and injectivity on either endpoint group
then makes it the identity. The kernel of the action on the standard
pro-p tree fixes these two vertices, so lies in that trivial intersection.
Hence this action is faithful. The n-edge graphs are still reduced.

Conclusion: even a restriction to faithful actions does not permit a
universal edge bound that uses only d(K) and the maximum subgroup-generator
rank of the edge groups. Dependence on additional structure, edge orders,
a fixed action, or an acylindricity parameter cannot be dropped this way.

## Why this is not a counterexample to the actual problem

Every Q_n is coherent. It is the fundamental group of a finite proper graph
of finite p-groups. Choose an open normal subgroup meeting the finitely many
finite vertex groups trivially; it acts freely on the standard pro-p tree,
and hence is free pro-p. Since Q_n is finitely generated, that subgroup has
finite rank and is coherent. Finite-index permanence proves Q_n coherent.
Alternatively one can build the finite graph successively with the
finite-edge case established in Attempt 2.

Since C_n is polycyclic and Q_n coherent, the kernel lifting lemma proves
P_n coherent. So the family is a precise obstruction to one proposed
proof route, not evidence of incoherence. Nor does it exhibit a single
inaccessible group: n varies along with the group. It cannot refute an
accessibility bound allowed to depend on the full group.

## Exact auxiliary checks

chain_obstruction.py verifies 128 parameter pairs (p in {2,3,5,7},
1<=n<=32) for the rank 2n of the edge-relation matrix modulo p, leaving
exactly two generator dimensions, and for the lattice/order/index formulas.
It separately enumerates 15 small finite lattice systems (p in {2,3,5},
1<=n<=5), checking all adjacent intersections and the trivial endpoint
intersection. All passed. These finite checks do not establish the
universal pro-p claims; those are supplied by the preceding proof.

No minimal-relation-number claim is made about the n+1 relators, and no
historical novelty is claimed for this elementary construction.

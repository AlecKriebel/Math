# Verification scope

The proof is an all-order structural partial. These finite checks exercise implementations and explicit examples; they do not establish a new finite bound for the original conjecture.

`the original checker` computes a separate Floyd-Warshall relation from actual arcs and compares it with the author's queue-based reachability. Normal Python, `-O`, and `-OO` receipts agree substantively.

- All 46,879 labeled colored tournaments of orders one through four are decoded from all six states of every unordered pair. Exact counts by order are 1, 6, 216, and 46,656. The numbers balanced in every color are 1, 0, 6, and 0. Source sets, spanning colors, balance, and actual-arc cycle coverage agree with the independent matrix oracle.
- All 9,331 oriented three-colored circulants at orders 1, 3, 5, 7, 9, 11 are checked: counts 1, 6, 36, 216, 1,296, 7,776. Every independently selected offset pair has all six orientation/color choices. Supplied shift generators are verified on every matrix entry. Every case has a spanning strongly connected monochromatic color.
- All 1,296 choices of merging and reversing the four affine-line colors over F_3^2 into a three-color palette are tested. Translation generators, exact spanning colors, source sets and coverage are checked. This provides a noncyclic-group family in addition to circulants.
- Prime full-core examples have orders 7, 9, 11, 13, 17, 27, 51, and 101. All have no cyclic rainbow triangle and whole incidence cores. Primeness uses the exact-cycle certificate. Separately, every proper nonsingleton subset is checked at the first four orders: 119, 501, 2,035, and 8,177 subsets, totaling 10,832. Larger primeness conclusions use the all-order proof, not subset enumeration.
- Sharp palette examples have p=3,5,7, orders 9,25,49, and palettes 4,6,8. Every monochromatic SCC has size p; none spans. All vertices remain sources.
- A transitive action on nine vertices includes a nonidentity color-preserving shear fixing vertex zero. The generated group is therefore transitive and nonregular. Its exact spanning color set is {0}.
- Twisted vertex/color rotations are checked at orders 3,9,15,21,27,33,39,45. The special order-three orbit triangle is cyclic in each case and rainbow exactly when nine does not divide the order. These are action fixtures, not rainbow-free counterexamples.
- Eighteen explicit negative/boundary controls distinguish color-preserving from color-permuting transitivity, balance from a spanning color, uncolored regularity from colorwise balance, three from four colors, exact Hamilton-cycle color classes from classes with extra arcs or disconnected cycles, source from sink, and cyclic from transitive rainbow triples. The singleton convention is checked.
- Twenty-four malformed-input gates reject invalid matrices, loops, missing/doubled pair orientations, Boolean colors/vertices, bad palettes/permutations, bad family orders, composite field parameters and invalid cycle/module certificates.
- Eight actual source mutations are detected by explicit failed controls: uncolored balance, merged strong colors, ignored arc colors, ignored transitivity, extra Hamilton-color edges, disconnected cycles, nonreflexive singleton reachability, and reversed sources. No timeout is counted as a successful mutation detection.

A preliminary verification identified an inadequate mutation test: accepting a direction-only automorphism was later rejected by the balance invariant, so it did not test the direct arc-color validator independently. A direct color-preserving-automorphism rejection control was added, and the complete suite was rerun. Preliminary runs provide no accepted receipt.

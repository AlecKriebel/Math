# Public sources and inspection boundaries

The principal dependency is Agelos Georgakopoulos and Philipp Sprüssel, [On 3-coloured tournaments](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0904.1967v2), arXiv:0904.1967v2, 17 April 2009; [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/0904.1967). Its Lemma 2.1 credits Shen Minggang for the failed-predecessor obstruction-cycle structure. Lemma 2.5 supplies proper-forward-interval paths; Proposition 2.4 and Theorem 3.1 supply the incidence requirements used in the search and core arguments. Those are credited premises, not original claims of this edition.

The recovered public PDF has SHA-256 37c70064ec08aba920ed480061d68328341e61a0ddb2738a7100fb0d75660279 and 142,691 bytes. The PDF version date is distinguished from later typesetting dates. The author and auditors inspected recovered textual material and relevant source statements and arguments at the scope recorded in their complete reports. The structural auditor independently read Lemma 2.1 and its general-minimal-counterexample hypotheses. The finite lane inspected the relevant interval and incidence material, with the incidence results retained as external dependencies. These inspections do not certify every proof in the paper. Source PDFs and extracted source text are not redistributed.

Mira Bhargava, [Finiteness criteria for coverings of groups by finitely many subgroups or cosets](https://dergipark.org.tr/en/download/article-file/232921), International Electronic Journal of Algebra 2 (2007), 83–89, Theorem 1(ii), states the classical Scorza characterization of three-proper-subgroup covers through a Klein-four quotient. The structural author and auditor inspected the journal PDF's extracted first page. In the tournament application, monochromatic SCC block stabilizers cover the acting group; an odd-order tournament automorphism group cannot have that quotient. The elementary component-size proof is self-contained and independent of Scorza's theorem. The relationship is contextual attribution, not a novelty claim.

The structural audit attempted web screenshots of both PDFs; retrieval failed, and textual inspection succeeded. No visual-page inspection is claimed from those attempts. Limited primary-record and terminology searches did not establish a dedicated standard name for this exact tournament formulation and do not establish novelty, priority, literature completeness, or current general open status.

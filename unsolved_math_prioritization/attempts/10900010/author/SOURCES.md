## CT Normal surfaces and the singular norm inequality

Daryl Cooper, Stephan Tillmann, *The Thurston norm via normal surfaces*, Pacific Journal of Mathematics 239 (2009), 1–15.

https://msp.org/pjm/2009/239-1/pjm-v239-n1-p01-p.pdf

Inspected the setup and hypotheses on printed pages 1–2, normal-surface formulation, and printed pages 12–13, including Proposition 12 and the proof of Theorem 5. Printed page 13 is PDF page 15 in the retrieved publisher file and was visually inspected. It explicitly applies the equality of embedded and singular norms to bound the norm of an immersed surface's class by χ_-. Its finite triangulation algorithms do not themselves encode descent from an arbitrary infinite cover.

## FHS Least area hypotheses

Michael Freedman, Joel Hass, Peter Scott, *Least area incompressible surfaces in 3-manifolds*, Inventiones Mathematicae 71 (1983), 609–642.

https://doi.org/10.1007/BF02095997

https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~hass/Research/Papers/Minimal/FreedmanHassScott82.pdf

Inspected Theorem 5.1, stated on printed page 609/PDF page 1 and again in §5, and the §6 disjointness discussion including Theorem 6.2. The first page was visually inspected. The embedding theorem assumes homotopy to a two-sided embedding and includes a double-cover-of-a-one-sided-surface alternative. The disjointness result assumes homotopically disjoint maps. Neither hypothesis is obtained merely by fixing the edge-cover homology class.

## SS Intersection numbers and compatible splittings

Peter Scott, Gadde A. Swarup, *Splittings of groups and intersection numbers*, Geometry & Topology 4 (2000), 179–218.

https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9906004

https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9906004

Inspected introductory distinctions between subgroups and splittings, Theorem 2.5, and Theorem 2.8. The latter permits a commensurable subgroup. These are not assertions of minimal Thurston norm or injective descent for a representative of a given edge class.

## JKS Current splitting complexity result

Andrei Jaikin-Zapirain, Monika Kudlinska, Pablo Sánchez-Peralta, *Thurston norm, polytopes and splitting complexity*, arXiv:2606.31774v2, 31 August 2026.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31774

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.31774

Inspected the definition of splitting complexity on page 2, Theorem 1.3 and Corollary 1.5 on page 3, and the proof of that corollary. Page 3 was visually inspected. The conclusion selects an admissible splitting dual to a nonzero character; it does not hold fixed an arbitrary given edge stabilizer. The current v2 was used rather than relying on the older abstract returned by some search results. It is a preprint; this packet does not infer peer-review status.

## Ci Current sutured hierarchy method

Alessandro V. Cigna, *Sutured manifold hierarchies and the Thurston norm*, arXiv:2604.19611v2, 13 July 2026.

https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.19611

https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.19611

Inspected Theorems 1.1–1.2, the description of the hierarchy/Euler-class method, and its scope. Page 1 was visually inspected. These results give compact-manifold norm certificates; they do not establish the translate-disjointness condition for a prescribed infinite edge cover. It is a preprint; no peer-review status is inferred.

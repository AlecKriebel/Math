# Source, question, and literature scope

Inspection date: 2026-10-06 UTC.

## Primary question and 2018 update

The complete 256-page PDF of Hayman and Lingham's *Research Problems in Function Theory (New Edition)*, arXiv:1809.07200v2, was available for inspection. Problem 7.42, printed page 173 (PDF page 174), and Update 7.42, printed page 174 (PDF page 175), were checked in extracted text and rendered original pages. The update says: “No progress on this problem has been reported to us.” This is a statement of the editors' reports at that time, not proof of current openness or historical novelty.

The question concerns the envelope of positive harmonic functions normalized at a base point. Its disc example is equality of a fixed boundary Poisson kernel with the envelope along the radius pointing to its pole; it asks whether the corresponding equality along a Green line from the base continues in multiply connected domains. PROOF.md disproves that property by excluding all non-base contact points in an entire neighborhood of the base.

The 2018 printing has a repeated phrase and inconsistent symbols in the displayed supremum/normalization. The proof uses the definition directly, with \(h(a)\leq1\) and \(K_\xi(a)=1\), so it does not depend on interpreting the defective supremum display. This is not a counterexample based on a typographical error.

## Related primary literature and exact scope

1. D. A. Herron, *The Harnack and other conformally invariant metrics*, Kodai Mathematical Journal 10 (1987), 9--19. Complete 11-page publisher PDF inspected. Theorem 1 relates the metric density to gradients of positive harmonic functions; Theorems 4--5 use Poisson kernels and calculate an antipodal distance in an annulus. These are relevant background, not an identified proof of the fixed-kernel Green-line property or its negation. The symmetric Harnack distance considered there must not be silently substituted for the one-sided normalized envelope used in Problem 7.42.
   https://www.jstage.jst.go.jp/article/kodaimath1978/10/1/10_1_9/_pdf/-char/en

2. H. S. Bear and Wayne Smith, *An Extension of the Schwarz--Pick Lemma*. Complete 10-page author-hosted manuscript inspected. Its opening pages carry provisional journal fields; that file is therefore identified by its actual manuscript title, not silently treated as a final published version. Its main theorem and corollary discuss equality of Harnack and hyperbolic distances and the punctured-disc exception. They do not directly settle the prescribed Martin-kernel contact property considered here.
   https://math.hawaii.edu/~wayne/mathpapers/Harnack2.pdf

## Mathematical status

The outcome rests on the explicit proof in PROOF.md, not on a literature non-result. It is a full counterexample candidate to the stated equality-along-a-line extension and remains subject to independent mathematical audit. A weaker reading asking only for some isolated contact point away from the base is not claimed to be settled by the local theorem.

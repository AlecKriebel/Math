# Search scope and limitations

Searches were run on 2026-10-12 PDT / 2026-10-13 UTC. They covered exact Problem 7.42 wording and attribution; Arsove–Johnson Green-line work; prescribed Martin-kernel contact/equality; annular Poisson extremality; Harnack metrics and their directional/gradient forms; current Harnack improvements; and citations in primary papers. Search results were leads, not evidence of absence.

Representative exact searches included `Harnack function Green line`, `Arsove Johnson Harnack Green lines Martin kernel`, `Harnack 7.42 Hayman`, `M. Arsove and G. Johnson`, `Martin kernel touches`, `annulus Harnack Poisson kernel extremal`, `Harnack envelope annulus`, `Green's lines methods Arsove Johnson`, `Comparing invariant distances and conformal metrics on Riemann surfaces`, and `Conformal invariants defined by harmonic functions on Riemann surfaces`. Broad searches produced substantial irrelevant PDE/probability results; these were not treated as support.

Bibliography chasing used Herron (1987), both Bear–Smith author manuscripts, Köhn (1966), Leutwiler (1987), Shiga (2016), Armitage's 1994 survey, Kargar (2024), and Svetlik (2025/2026). Official publisher/registrar metadata was used to correct titles, dates, and DOIs. Not all final typeset papers were accessible.

Access gaps: full 2019 Hayman–Lingham Chapter 7; Arsove–Johnson Memoirs 91 (1970); Tanaka (1977) Project Euclid; Goldstein–Kuran (1988); Bear (1991); Herron–Minda (2001). Older Arsove–Johnson conference report summaries were visible in official AMS search indexing, while direct downloads returned 403. Project Euclid returned an anti-bot response; no restriction was bypassed. The original question inspected in full is therefore arXiv:1809.07200v2, not the 2019 chapter. The unsolvedmath page was inaccessible; no live openness/status inferred.

A failed query, inaccessible paper, or absence from the inspected sources cannot certify worldwide openness or novelty. No external individual was contacted, and no outreach was prepared. Further licensed-access inspection of the named source gaps could improve the priority audit; this is a research note, not a request to contact anyone.

The official Springer extra-material ZIP was obtained and its 5-page PDF (four printed pages plus publisher cover), headed *List of known updates: January 2021*, read in full. There is no Problem 7.42 entry. This later editorial supplement still does not certify 2026 openness.

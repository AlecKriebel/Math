# Bibliographic clarification, preserving the author freeze

The cited Kapovich problem-list PDF is dated **October 24, 2007** on its first page. Its introduction identifies the **2005 AIM workshop** as the occasion when most of the problems were collected. The catalog's proposed year 2005 is therefore consistent with the source; it should not be described as the PDF's manuscript date.

Suggested expanded reference:

Misha Kapovich, *Problems on boundaries of groups and Kleinian groups*, manuscript dated October 24, 2007, collecting problems from the 2005 AIM workshop; Problem 14 and preceding hypotheses, p. 5. https://www.math.ucdavis.edu/~kapovich/EPR/problems.pdf

This is a bibliographic clarification, not a mathematical correction. The frozen author archive is unchanged.

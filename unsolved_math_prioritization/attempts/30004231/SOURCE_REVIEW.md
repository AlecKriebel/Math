# Source review: totally mixed face posets

Historical source inspections: 10 October 2026. This file reports scholarly provenance and bounded checks, not mathematical novelty. Edition preparation rehashed the frozen source PDF bytes but did not perform new retrieval, source-text inspection or literature search.

## Original problem

Sam Payne, “The complex of totally mixed faces,” in the open-problem collection compiled by Joseph Doolittle, Oberwolfach Report 39/2019, printed page 2464, PDF page 70. Report DOI: https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2019/39. Official PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46818.

The complete official PDF had previously been retrieved. Its bytes were rehashed, and PDF page 70 was visually inspected during the proof review. The two dimension conditions, both requested conclusions, the ambient n, and the absence of any stated full-dimension/general-position/simplicial/facets-only hypothesis were verified visually. The web tool's fresh direct open of the official PDF returned an internal error, but search retrieved the same official problem page and the frozen full local PDF remained readable. The independent audit subsequently opened the official PDF successfully, independently rehashed the complete PDF, and rendered and visually inspected page 70. No mathematical conclusion depends on that web-open failure.

## Cited primary paper

Karim Adiprasito, Philip Brinkmann, Arnau Padrol, Pavel Patak, Zuzana Patakova, and Raman Sanyal, *Colorful simplicial depth, Minkowski sums, and generalized Gale transforms*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.00347. The current arXiv record lists v1, submitted 1 July 2016. The inspected full PDF is that v1; page 3 was inspected visually during the proof review, and the definition and related later discussion were checked in its extracted text.

The official published article, https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2019/6/1894/4085561, identifies IMRN 2019(6), pages 1894-1919, DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/imrn/rnx184, published online 19 August 2017. It was read through the current official publisher page. Its section 1.2 uses summand facets in its totally mixed definition; the relevant Gale statement is Lemma 3.8 in the published version, rather than Lemma 3.7 in arXiv v1. The positivity-plus-additivity poset in Payne's printed problem is not identified with that other object in this report.

An earlier primary conference abstract by Payne was located at https://sci-tech.ksc.kwansei.ac.jp/~hohsugi/CREST/abstract%28ver0%29.pdf. The retrieved abstract discusses positive-dimensional summand faces and related topology; it states no additive-dimension condition in the abstract. Only the indexed abstract text was inspected; no claim of a complete-PDF inspection is made for this source. This is contextual evidence, not a theorem or a replacement problem statement.

## Accepted scope and distribution

The accompanying complete proof and audit resolve both printed unrestricted assertions negatively with full-dimensional lattice polytopes in R5, under the standard order-complex convention. They preserve both positive summand dimensions and dimension additivity. No genericity variant, facets-only reinterpretation or altered realization is claimed. The R3 six-point example refutes only the homology assertion and remains shellable in dimension zero.

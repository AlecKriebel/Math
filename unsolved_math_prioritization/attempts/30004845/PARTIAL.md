# 30004845: bubbles force higher nerve homology

**Status: scoped partial theorem; the full bubble-free conjecture remains unresolved.** Independent adversarial review is pending. The argument uses established results of Guetta and Ara; no priority or human-peer-review claim is made.

## 1. Exact target and conventions

The [official Oberwolfach report, printed p. 2505](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46922), reports Léonard Guetta's conjecture: a two-dimensional polygraph has the same polygraphic and nerve homology if and only if it is bubble-free. This concerns the **free strict 2-category** generated by the polygraph, regarded as a strict omega-category with identity cells above dimension 2. It is not a claim about every strict 2-category or a category presented by adding relations between its generating 2-cells.

Use the Street nerve $N_\omega C$ and integral homology. A bubble is a nonidentity 2-cell

$$\beta:1_A\Longrightarrow1_A.$$

The condition excludes all such composites, not just generating 2-cells. In [Guetta's thesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.12662), Definition 4.5.7 and Conjecture 6.6.5, equality is expressed by invertibility of the canonical comparison

$$\pi_C:H^{\rm Sing}(C)\longrightarrow H^{\rm pol}(C)$$

in the derived category of nonnegative chain complexes. Its direction is from nerve homology to polygraphic homology. For a free strict 2-category with generators $\Sigma_0,\Sigma_1,\Sigma_2$, the latter is computed by the complex

$$\mathbb Z\Sigma_0\xleftarrow{\partial_1}\mathbb Z\Sigma_1
\xleftarrow{\partial_2}\mathbb Z\Sigma_2\longleftarrow0\longleftarrow\cdots,$$

where each boundary counts the target generators minus the source generators. Consequently $H_q^{\rm pol}(C)=0$ for every $q>2$. These identifications are Guetta's Lemma 4.2.6, Proposition 4.2.8 and the cofibrancy of free polygraphs.

## 2. A detection lemma for a bubble

Write $B^2\mathbb N$ for the strict 2-category with one object, only its identity 1-cell, and 2-cells the additive commutative monoid $\mathbb N$. Both compositions are addition; 0 is the identity 2-cell. Denote its generating 2-cell 1 by $u$.

**Lemma.** Let $C$ be any small strict 2-category. Suppose there are a strict 2-functor

$$w:C\longrightarrow B^2\mathbb N$$

and a bubble $\beta$ of $C$ with $w(\beta)=m>0$. Then for every $r\ge1$, the group $H_{2r}(N_\omega C;\mathbb Z)$ contains an infinite cyclic subgroup. In particular it is nonzero.

**Proof.** The free universal property of $B^2\mathbb N$ supplies a strict 2-functor

$$j:B^2\mathbb N\longrightarrow C,\qquad j(u)=\beta,$$

with its object sent to $A$. Concretely, the interchange and unit laws make the horizontal and vertical compositions on $\operatorname{End}_2(1_A)$ agree and commute, so the map $n\mapsto\beta^n$ preserves both compositions. The composite $w\circ j$ is the strict 2-functor $\mu_m$ induced by multiplication by $m$ on $\mathbb N$.

The inclusion $\mathbb N\hookrightarrow\mathbb Z$ induces a weak equivalence of Street nerves

$$N_\omega(B^2\mathbb N)\longrightarrow N_\omega(B^2\mathbb Z),$$

and the target is a $K(\mathbb Z,2)$. These are [Ara's Theorems 4.7 and 4.9, Example 4.10](https://www.normalesup.org/~ara/files/thmB.pdf). The equivalence is induced by the inclusion itself, hence commutes with multiplication by $m$. On $K(\mathbb Z,2)$ this multiplication acts by $m$ on $\pi_2$ and therefore on $H^2$. For clarity, this identification is natural in the abelian group: the loop-space identification in Ara's proof reduces it to multiplication on the fundamental group of $B\mathbb Z$.

Recall $K(\mathbb Z,2)\simeq\mathbb{CP}^{\infty}$, with

$$H^*(K(\mathbb Z,2);\mathbb Z)=\mathbb Z[c],\qquad |c|=2.$$

Thus $\mu_m^*(c^r)=m^r c^r$. Pairing with the integral homology generator in degree $2r$ shows

$$H_{2r}(N_\omega\mu_m)=m^r:\mathbb Z\longrightarrow\mathbb Z.$$

Functoriality gives

$$H_{2r}(N_\omega w)\,H_{2r}(N_\omega j)=m^r\operatorname{id}_{\mathbb Z}.$$

Since $m^r\ne0$, $H_{2r}(N_\omega j)$ is injective. Its image is the asserted infinite cyclic subgroup. There is no claim of an integral splitting when $m>1$. ∎

## 3. Application to every free strict 2-category

**Theorem.** If a small free strict 2-category $C$ contains a bubble, then

$$H_{2r}(N_\omega C;\mathbb Z)\ne0\quad\text{for every }r\ge1.$$

In particular $C$ is not homologically coherent. Equivalently, homological coherence implies bubble-freeness for all free strict 2-categories.

**Proof.** Define $w:C\to B^2\mathbb N$ by sending every object to its unique object, every generating 1-cell to its identity 1-cell, and every generating 2-cell to $1\in\mathbb N$. All source and target conditions hold, so the universal property gives a strict 2-functor. This is the total generator-counting functor; individual generator weights and their additivity are established in Guetta's Lemma 1.3.4 and Proposition 1.3.5.

For every 2-cell $x$, $w(x)$ is the number of generating 2-cell occurrences in any composite expression for $x$. Associativity, units, and interchange preserve that number. Each expression is finite, even if the polygraph has infinitely many generators. An expression involving no generating 2-cells is a composite of identity 2-cells and hence is itself an identity 2-cell. Therefore every nonidentity 2-cell, in particular any bubble $\beta$, has $w(\beta)>0$.

Apply the detection lemma. Already $H_4(N_\omega C;\mathbb Z)\ne0$, whereas $H_4^{\rm pol}(C)=0$. Hence the comparison cannot be an isomorphism, even at the level of abstract homology groups. ∎

The proof uses no finite-generation, connectedness, source-nonempty, or target-nonempty hypothesis. It detects a composite bubble whether or not any generator is itself a bubble.

## 4. Checks against the basic examples

1. For $C=B^2\mathbb N$, the theorem recovers the established Ara–Maltsiniotis example discussed in Guetta §4.5.2: nerve homotopy type $K(\mathbb Z,2)$, but polygraphic homology only in degrees 0 and 2. This example is credited, not rediscovered.

2. Consider the free 2-category with one object $A$, one generating 1-cell $f$, and two generating 2-cells $\alpha:1_A\Rightarrow f$ and $\gamma:f\Rightarrow1_A$. Neither generator is a bubble, but $\beta=\gamma\circ\alpha$ is a nonidentity bubble of total weight 2. The polygraphic differential is $\partial_2\alpha=f$, $\partial_2\gamma=-f$. The detection factorization composes to multiplication by 2, and acts by $2^r$ in degree $2r$. Guetta §6.5.3 already proves that this example has nerve homotopy type $K(\mathbb Z,2)$ by a more specific retraction; it is only a diagnostic here.

3. The freeness assumption is essential to this argument. In a presented 2-category, a nonidentity idempotent 2-cell $e$ may satisfy $e+e=e$. A functor to $B^2\mathbb N$ must then send $e$ to 0. For example, for the commutative monoid $M=\{0,e\}$ with $e$ absorbing, the category $B^2M$ has a bubble but no positive detecting weight. No conclusion about its polygraphic homology is drawn here. This example only prevents extending the positive-counting argument without its hypothesis.

## 5. The remaining direction is not proved

The converse would require proving that every bubble-free free strict 2-category has an invertible canonical comparison. The result above only rules out bubbles; it provides no contraction or spectral sequence proving the comparison for the remaining categories.

Two distinct requirements must not be conflated: the nerve's homology must vanish in every degree above 2, **and** its degree 2 comparison must be an isomorphism. Guetta §6.6.6 explicitly conditions a reformulation using only higher-degree vanishing on a separate degree 2 comparison conjecture. This package does not assume that conjecture. Standard comparison in degrees 0 and 1, and known special examples, do not fill that gap.

The thesis gives an attachment criterion in Corollary 4.5.11 and§6.4.1: a suitable pushout of free coherent categories, along a folk cofibration, preserves coherence **if the square is also Thomason homotopy cocartesian**. Bubble-freeness alone has not been shown here to supply that extra property for arbitrary attachments. Applying ordinary cellular-homology intuition without that proof would simply assume the missing point.

The [2024 local-system paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10466) retains the nerve-to-polygraphic comparison and proves it for 1-categories. The [24 September 2026 groupoidal preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29967), Theorem 8.2.1, compares resolution models for 1-categories and groupoids; it does not establish the conjecture for arbitrary free 2-categories. No complete current resolution was verified in this bounded search.

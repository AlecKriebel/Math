## 1. Original scope and comparison direction

The complete contribution on printed p.2505 of [OWR 46/2021](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46922) asks for an equivalence between bubble-freeness and agreement of the polygraphic and nerve homologies of a two-dimensional polygraph. It explicitly quantifies over all 2-cells, including composites. The relevant category is freely generated by the polygraph, with only identities above dimension two. It is not an arbitrary 2-category with additional relations between 2-generators.

In [Guetta's thesis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.12662), Definition 4.5.7 uses the canonical morphism from singular/nerve homology **to** polygraphic homology, in the derived category. Proposition 4.2.8 describes the abelianized chain complex of a free category in terms of its generators, with target-minus-source differential. Its free/cofibrant hypothesis makes this compute polygraphic homology. In the two-dimensional case, the complex vanishes above degree two. Thus the nonzero degree-four nerve group in the partial theorem already rules out coherence, irrespective of any low-degree comparison issue.

The displayed comparison direction in the frozen artifact is correct. Isolated reversed displays elsewhere do not override the explicit definition. No inversion of the general comparison is used.

## 2. Generator counting and the bubble map

For a free strict 2-category C, map all objects to the unique object of B²N, all generating 1-cells to its identity, and every generating 2-cell to 1. Every generating 2-cell then has the required parallel source and target images. Freeness gives a strict 2-functor w. This is exactly the type of suspended-monoid universal property in Guetta's Lemma 1.3.4; Proposition 1.3.5 supplies the individual additive weights.

A free 2-cell has a finite expression in generators, identities and compositions. Associativity, unit laws and interchange preserve the total number of 2-generator occurrences. Expressions of weight zero involve only identity 2-cells, whose composites are identities. Consequently every nonidentity 2-cell has strictly positive weight. This remains valid for an infinite generating set: each individual cell uses only finitely many generators, and there is no infinite sum or completion in this free-category construction.

Let β:1_A⇒1_A be a bubble with w(β)=m>0. The endomorphisms of the identity 1-cell carry two unital operations. Interchange gives the Eckmann–Hilton identity, so the operations agree and commute. Thus n↦βⁿ preserves both compositions and defines a strict functor j:B²N→C. The composite wj is exactly multiplication by m on N. This is an equality of strict functors, not merely a map on a guessed collection of homology generators.

The use of all generators, rather than only generating bubbles, is essential and handled correctly. For the example with 1⇒f⇒1, the composite is a bubble of total weight two even though neither individual generator is a bubble.

## 3. Classifying space and multiplication normalization

[Ara, Theorems 4.7 and 4.9 and Example 4.10](https://www.normalesup.org/~ara/files/thmB.pdf), apply to the ordinary ordered abelian group Z and its positive monoid N. The order is directed, hence its poset is aspherical. Crucially, the proof of Theorem 4.9 establishes that the **inclusion-induced** functor B²N→B²Z is a Thomason equivalence. It does not merely assert that their realizations happen to have the same abstract homotopy type. Theorem 4.7 identifies the latter nerve as K(Z,2). The loop-space proof is natural in the group maps, and multiplication commutes strictly with the inclusion. I inspected the actual theorem and proof on the rendered manuscript p.25.

There is a useful independent low-degree check of the claimed degree. In the normalized Street nerve of B²N, the positive integers label the nondegenerate 2-simplices, and all 1-simplices are degenerate. A tetrahedron has face labels a=012, b=013, c=023, d=123 satisfying a+c=b+d. Its boundary is [d]−[c]+[b]−[a]. The choice (a,b,c,d)=(n−1,n,1,0) gives the relation

[n] = [n−1]+[1].

Every such boundary is killed by the degree function [n]↦n, and these relations identify every [n] with n[1]. Thus H₂ is canonically Z, with the class of 1 a generator. Multiplication by m sends [n] to [mn], hence acts by m on H₂. This checks the normalization directly rather than relying on an arbitrary choice of an equivalence to K(Z,2).

The integral cohomology ring of K(Z,2)≃CP∞ is Z[c], with |c|=2. A map acting by m in degree two consequently sends cʳ to mʳcʳ. The class cʳ is an integral cohomology generator dual to the degree-2r homology generator. Therefore the induced homology map is precisely multiplication by mʳ. No factorial is present. Factorials arise in a different normalization involving Pontryagin products, which is not being used here.

## 4. Injection, torsion and the homological conclusion

Applying integral homology to the strict factorization gives

w_* j_* = mʳ id_Z

in every degree 2r>0. If j_*(n)=0, applying w_* gives mʳn=0 in Z, and hence n=0. Thus j_* is injective. This argument makes no torsion-freeness assumption on H₂ᵣ(NωC;Z), and remains valid if that middle group has arbitrary torsion. It supplies an infinite cyclic subgroup.

It does not in general supply an integral direct summand when m>1. The inclusion 2Z⊂Z already illustrates why a nonzero composite is insufficient for a retraction. The frozen statement expressly avoids that stronger claim. Its conclusion at r=2 gives nonzero H₄ of the nerve, while the free two-dimensional polygraphic chain complex has H₄=0. Hence C is not coherent. The argument proves precisely the stated necessary direction.

The nonfree negative control is legitimate. In a presented 2-category with a nonidentity idempotent bubble e, an additive N-valued functor must satisfy w(e)+w(e)=w(e), forcing w(e)=0. This shows the failure of the positive-counting premise outside the free setting. The artifact does not use it to assert an unverified polygraphic-homology computation or to decide the original conjecture.

## 5. What remains open in this package

The necessity argument gives no proof that a bubble-free free 2-category has vanishing nerve homology above dimension two, and no proof that its degree-two canonical comparison is an isomorphism. Guetta §6.6.6 explicitly makes the higher-degree-only reformulation conditional on a separate degree-two comparison statement. That additional conclusion is not assumed here.

The attachment strategy also requires a Thomason homotopy-cocartesian square in addition to the appropriate folk cofibration. Bubble-freeness has not been shown to guarantee that condition for arbitrary attachments. Invoking ordinary cellular-homology intuition would omit exactly the missing comparison of homotopy theories.

The full retrieved [2024 local-systems paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2309.10466v2), Section 6, proves its comparison for input 1-categories and retains the general nerve-to-polygraphic direction. Its Scholie 6.7 still uses B²N to distinguish the homotopy theories. The [September 2026 groupoidal paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.29967v1), Theorem 8.2.1, explicitly starts with a 1-category; changing the resolution model does not make it a theorem for arbitrary free 2-categories. The March 2026 exposition likewise discusses the standard bubble mismatch. The package's limited literature conclusion is appropriately qualified; this audit does not claim exhaustive priority research or a full reconstruction of these comparison theorems.

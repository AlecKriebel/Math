## Scope of this package

The necessity argument in PARTIAL.md is a direct synthesis of positive generator counting and Ara's natural classifying-space theorem. Its full all-bubble statement was not found explicitly in these sources, but no novelty claim is made. The proof does not assume the separate degree2 comparison conjecture. The converse and any general bubble-free homotopy-cocartesian attachment theorem remain unproved here.

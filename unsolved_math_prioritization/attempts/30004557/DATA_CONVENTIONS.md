# Constructive data conventions for the corrected five-attempt release

This file is part of every conditional statement in ATTEMPT_1–ATTEMPT_5. These are explicit hypotheses for informal Bishop-style mathematical constructions, not an assertion that every formal foundation or every ordinary semantic equivalence supplies them. The original problem remains unresolved after five attempts.

1. **Presented syntax.** Signature symbols, specified finite arities, axiom presentations, contexts, formulas and finite derivations form supplied sets. A derivation carries evidence for axiom membership. If symbol equality is setoid equality, syntax carries that equality and its evidence. No enumeration or decision test for derivability is assumed.
2. **Proof-carrying records.** An admissible object, arrow, sequent or diagram contains its finite admissibility or equality derivations. Indexing families use these records, not a selected proof for each true assertion. Hom equality is a witnessed setoid relation. Smallness refers to these presented categories; no representative is chosen from each quotient hom-class or object-isomorphism class.
3. **Effective operations.** Functors act on raw object/arrow records and their equality evidence. Specified finite limits, images, finite disjoint sums and effective quotients include operations producing universal-property factors and their equality witnesses. Preservation data include the comparison maps, inverse maps where appropriate, and proofs. Here “effective” means supplied operations on these mathematical presentations; it does not add a recursive decision procedure or a finite encoding assumption.
4. **Equivalence data.** Inverse functors and natural isomorphisms, including inverse components and naturality witnesses, are supplied. Bare full faithfulness or essential surjectivity does not select a hom-lift or an inverse. Where needed, hom-lifts are explicit operations. Attempt 3 derives such operations from its given inverse data without imposing triangle identities; Attempt 5 requires an effective Yoneda hom-lift, supplied by identity evaluation for literal subcanonical Yoneda.
5. **Sorts and stages.** Nullary products, empty predicates and empty finite sums are allowed; the initial object may equivalently be the false subsort of a terminal sort. No old sort is forced inhabited. Each stage can introduce a set-indexed family of independent definitions of unbounded finite arities and formula lengths. The number of stages must be finite globally. No finite-total-signature theorem follows.
6. **Functor convention.** Ambient change uses pretopos functors, preserving finite limits, finite disjoint sums and effective quotients (hence the needed images). A merely regular/Barr-exact functor is insufficient.
7. **Semantic reconstruction.** Attempt 4 assumes supplied pseudonatural model-equivalence data on the indicated presented pretoposes. Attempt 5 assumes the uniform finite-cover, local-surjectivity, compact-kernel, reflection and equality operations stated there. It does not infer them from a bare ordinary classifying-topos equivalence. Their derivation from the original source-intended constructive semantic premise remains unproved.

The full proof audit and independent supplement in `audits/` are part of the release. They validate conditional claims with these conventions and the explicit presentation repairs. They do not establish a formalization in every interpretation of Bishop mathematics, an original-scope solution, or a global literature-status theorem.

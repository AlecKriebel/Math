# Sources, access and priority

Checked 4 October 2026. Bibliographic facts and the mathematical facts used below were read from primary sources. All locally downloaded source files are private research inputs and are absent from the portable candidate package.

## Used sources

[S1] Kimihiko Motegi, with Tetsuya Ito and Masakazu Teragaito, “Dehn fillings on knot groups,” Low-dimensional Topology, Oberwolfach Reports 8/2020, pp.491–493; entire report pp.465–516. DOI https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2020/8 . Primary PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46844 . Defines the standard slope convention, the filling-kernel exact sequence, and the final rank-two question. Printed p.493 (PDF page 29) visually checked.

[S2] William P. Thurston, The Geometry and Topology of Three-Manifolds, 1980 lecture notes, electronic edition 1.1 (2002), Chapter 4. Primary institutional archive: https://library.slmath.org/nonmsri/gt3m/ ; chapter PDF https://library.slmath.org/nonmsri/gt3m/PDF/4.pdf . §§4.1–4.3 give the figure-eight complete hyperbolic structure; Theorem 4.7, printed p.61, classifies hyperbolic fillings. Printed p.70 describes the torus mapping torus with A=((2,1),(1,1)). Its surgery subscript is printed with a D-shaped glyph, followed immediately by discussion of (0,1) and (0,−1). We do not treat that glyph alone as verification of the zero-slope identification: [S4] independently states that fact. The matrix is supplementary; the obstruction applies to every torus bundle. Printed pp.61 and 70 were visually checked.

[S3] Mark Brittenham and Ying-Qing Wu, “The classification of exceptional Dehn surgeries on 2-bridge knots,” Communications in Analysis and Geometry 9 (2001), 97–113, DOI https://doi.org/10.4310/cag.2001.v9.n1.a4 . Author preprint https://homepage.math.uiowa.edu/~wu/papers/p32.pdf ; arXiv https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0011005 . Theorem 1.1(4) on preprint pp.1–2 gives precisely nine rational exceptional slopes for 4₁, and identifies 0,±4 as toroidal. The paper expressly attributes this case to Thurston. Its preliminary discussion defines rational slope conventions and excludes ∞.

[S4] Vu The Khoi, “On the integral of log x dy/y − log y dx/x over the A-polynomial curves,” Acta Mathematica Vietnamica 33(3) (2008), 519–528. Institutional PDF https://math.ac.vn/public/uploads/files/0803519.pdf . §3.1, printed p.524, explicitly identifies zero surgery on the figure-eight knot as a torus bundle. Only that topological identification is used; no numerical character-variety calculation is an input to the proof.

[S5] Sergei Gukov and Ciprian Manolescu, “A two-variable series for knot complements,” author PDF https://web.stanford.edu/~cm5/surgeries.pdf . §9.4, printed p.71, lists the nine exceptional slopes, the toroidal cases, and Seifert bases (2,3,7), (2,4,5), (3,3,4) for positive slopes 1,2,3. The displayed equality for positive slope 2 has negative subscript −3 in the downloaded PDF; we do not use that equality. The negative slopes follow instead from the figure-eight amphichirality recorded by [S2]. The displayed positive-slope base types are the only additional imported data. The page was visually checked.

[S6] Tetsuya Ito, Kimihiko Motegi and Masakazu Teragaito, “Dehn filling and the knot group II: Ubiquity of persistent elements,” arXiv:2604.01697v1, 2 April 2026. https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.01697 ; https://arxiv.org/html/2604.01697v1 . §7.1, pp.16–17, defines persistent subgroups and records the rank-two question; the final question was visually checked in the PDF. The authors report cyclic results and no known hyperbolic rank-two examples. This does not certify absence of a negative example for the universal reading and does not turn the present counterexample into a solution of the existential version.

## Adjacent literature, not proof dependencies

Ito–Motegi–Teragaito, “Dehn filling and the knot group III: cyclic persistent subgroups,” Bol. Soc. Mat. Mex. 30, 99 (2024), https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40590-024-00674-9 . Publisher abstract and bibliography were accessible; full text was subscription-restricted and was not used. The abstract describes the cyclic equivalence with no finite surgery. Cyclic and rank-two persistent subgroups must not be conflated.

The current arXiv record https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.15738 is “Dehn filling and the knot group I: Realization Property,” v4 dated 30 June 2025. Its element-wise realization results are adjacent, not needed to prove the obstruction.

## Bounded priority search and limits

Queries included the exact title and identifier, “persistent free” with “rank two,” “persistent subgroup” with “figure-eight,” “Dehn filling” with “solvable” and the authors' names, cyclic-persistent article searches, and figure-eight exceptional/finite surgery searches. The directly relevant primary sources above were read, including the April 2026 question. No directly stated earlier answer to the exact OWR formulation was located. Search omission and inaccessible full texts remain limitations; absence of a search result does not establish novelty.

The negative argument is an elementary consequence of classical filling topology and the law in every metabelian group. It is presented with full credit to those facts. No assertion of first solution, original topological classification, formal verification, or human peer review is made.

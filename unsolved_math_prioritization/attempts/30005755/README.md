# Canonical decomposition cones and TF equivalence: scoped partial results

Problem **30005755**, rank **801**, OWR-14298157-005. The general problem remains **unsolved after 5/5 approaches**. Independent audit: **PASS_SCOPED_PARTIAL**, with no required mathematical correction. This package is a draft research record, with no novelty or full-resolution claim.

## Accepted scope

- A finite torsion-witness sufficient criterion, elementary semisimple cases, and product reduction.
- For the displayed **12-dimensional wild nonhereditary algebra**, at eta=(1,1,-1), the exact real TF class is {(a,b,-b): a>0, b>0}.
- The cone generated by canonical summands of **all positive multiples** is the closed cone generated by (1,0,0) and (0,1,-1). The single-decomposition cone is only the ray through eta. This distinction is not a counterexample to the original conjecture.
- The independent audit additionally computes E(eta,eta)=1 for this example.

Every module means a finite-dimensional **right** module. TF equivalence is tested against the whole category and includes **all real weights**. The algebraically closed-field hypothesis is retained for generic-decomposition and positive-combination theorems. Exact computations support the scoped textual proofs; they do not enumerate all modules or prove the general conjecture.

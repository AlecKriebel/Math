## Established paper

Asai–Iyama, Semistable torsion classes and canonical decompositions in Grothendieck groups, Proceedings of the London Mathematical Society 129 (2024), e12639, https://doi.org/10.1112/plms.12639 . The published full-text page was inspected, particularly Theorem 1.1, Conjecture 1.2, Theorem 1.3, Theorem 3.14, Corollary 3.15, and Examples 5.7–5.9. The available author PDF https://arxiv.org/pdf/2112.14908v3 was independently downloaded and inspected. The publisher PDF request returned 403; no publisher-PDF hash is claimed.

The published paper proves the inclusion from the multiple-cone relative interior into the TF class, and equality for hereditary and E-tame algebras. These hypotheses cannot be silently removed. In particular some hereditary algebras are representation-wild, so 'wild' alone is not a separator between known and unknown cases. The paper also gives the one-point-extension construction used for the scoped calculation in PROOF.md. We independently check the needed determinant and derive a finite-module reverse-inclusion certificate; we claim no novelty for the construction or its previously known ray-condition failure.

## Newer preprint

Haerizadeh–Yassemi, The cones of g-vectors, https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.15822v3 . The abstract page lists v3 on 19 June 2026. Its versioned PDF was downloaded, read in the relevant sections, and byte-compared with the unversioned latest PDF; the two files are identical. The PDF itself is dated June 23, 2026. The HTML renderer displays a different date, so it is not used to infer a submission date.

This preprint still states a modified conjecture with ri(F(theta)) on the left. It reports finite rational simplicial generation by a sufficiently divisible multiple, equality for tame weights, and a dimension criterion for the modified equality; it also treats a codimension-one case. These reported advances are relevant, but even accepting them does not settle the original equality without addressing class boundary points and the remaining hypotheses. They have not been independently audited in full here and are not inputs to PROOF.md. No general solution or counterexample was located in the searched sources.

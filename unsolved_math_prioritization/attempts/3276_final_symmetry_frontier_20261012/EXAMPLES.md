# Full authored boundary fixtures

Every entry -1 means no arc in that ordered direction. Nonnegative entries are actual arc colors. Every pair has exactly one direction. These twenty full matrices are authored examples, not raw enumeration datasets. Vertex labels are row and column positions starting at zero. The finite examples also include their exact distance-state vectors and independently recomputed properties. Full mathematical uses and limitations appear in the proofs.

Structural fixtures cover the four-color affine failure of the small-palette extension, nonunique strong-color choices, a rainbow-free twisted source without a spanning color, a rigid exact-predecessor example with actual triangle 0->2->4->0, and a strict-period-three cycle word without the global shift. The finite predecessor-only model has exactly 18 cyclic rainbow triangles; it is not a conjecture counterexample. The three-cycle tests initial-state rejection.

## Scope and remaining gap

The target asks whether the standard restricted **regular** wreath product of any two finitely generated Hopfian groups is Hopfian. The accepted partial theorems address basic epimorphisms for centerless or perfect bases, faithful transitive actions with finite products of nonabelian simple lamp groups, certain semisimple central quotients, and a finite-central-residue criterion for derived Hopficity. All hypotheses remain exactly as stated in `TURN_1.md` through `TURN_5.md`; `FINAL_RESULT.md` summarizes them.

General nonbasic epimorphisms outside those quotient classes remain uncontrolled. The nonperfect central behavior retains the credited abelian stable-finiteness obstruction, even after adjoining an appropriate fixed nonabelian direct factor. The failed central-extension mechanism is not a realized counterexample. Neither a proof nor a counterexample to the original assertion has been obtained.

# Public source review and attribution

## Scope and source history

The recorded proof review and independent AI-assisted audit took place on
10 October 2026. This edition performed no new scholarly-source retrieval,
visual inspection, or literature search. Source identities and inspection
limits below are historical metadata, not redistributed source content.
They do not certify novelty, bibliographic priority, exhaustive later
literature, or worldwide current openness.

## The exact open question

[TOPP Problem 29](https://topp.openproblem.net/p29) asks about a convex
3-polytope and a tetrahedralization with a Hamiltonian path in its dual.
It displayed Open when read during the proof review and independent audit.
The recorded HTML identity is 5,503 bytes, SHA-256
`62724854ea5851a1c182e0a70d772803fe84b6e7fd1c02130543eb12ccbcd7b7`.
The target here uses original extreme vertices, no Steiner vertices,
positive-volume face-to-face tetrahedra and triangular-face adjacency.
Coplanar original vertices and nonsimplicial facets remain allowed.
Extra prescribed nonextreme points and Hamiltonian cycles are different targets.

## Escalona–Fabila-Monroy–Urrutia

[Hamiltonian Tetrahedralizations with Steiner Points, arXiv:1210.5484](https://arxiv.org/abs/1210.5484),
[PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1210.5484), supplies the general-position
predecessor of the degree-three reinsertion argument (Lemma 3) and the
boundary-dual positive-class route (Theorem 4 and Corollary 5).
The recorded PDF is 163,749 bytes, SHA-256
`42067f52d14f0bcb17039224accb9b10755df655a5abb26f843a36d71a89898a`.
The full eleven-page extracted text was read; physical pages 3 and 7
were independently rendered and visually inspected during the audit.
The 2012 arXiv v1 history and internal 2021 PDF date do not establish
an additional arXiv version.

The [journal version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40590-015-0080-8),
Bol. Soc. Mat. Mex. 23 (2017), 537–547, was published online on
25 November 2015. The accessible publisher abstract and metadata were
checked. Its stated improved Steiner bound is floor((m−2)/2)−1,
whereas the supplied arXiv PDF states floor((m−2)/2).
The journal full proof was not accessed or inspected. The improved bound
is credited only as a published abstract statement and is not used to
prove this edition's no-Steiner partial. General-position and small-hull
qualifications are retained rather than broadened silently.
The recorded publisher HTML is 277,065 bytes, SHA-256
`5ec75e6dc7912630d165f36df4035ab5c809c1dfbcde97079988a6a6d12b3c06`.

The paper's 84-point obstruction applies to common-vertex/pulling
tetrahedralizations, not every tetrahedralization of the same polytope.
No fresh full audit of that construction is claimed.

## Holton–McKay and the appended erratum

[D. A. Holton and B. D. McKay, The smallest non-Hamiltonian
3-connected cubic planar graphs have 38 vertices](https://users.cecs.anu.edu.au/~bdm/papers/HoltonCubic38.pdf),
J. Combin. Theory Ser. B 45 (1988), 305–319, was retrieved from
McKay's author-hosted site with the appended 1989 erratum.
The recorded PDF is 722,440 bytes, SHA-256
`7c9ebe1f7fd1457a89d7b70fd0c6d0f8074d230cc21297d6f96da76c6b749fd8`.
Physical pages 1–3 and 16 were visually inspected during the audit.
Theorem 1.1 on printed page 306 gives the at-most-36-vertex
Hamiltonicity theorem used in the credited simplicial at-most-20-vertex
polytope corollary. The [1989 erratum](https://doi.org/10.1016/0095-8956(89)90025-7)
adds a case to Theorem 1.2 at orders 38, 40, and 42. It does not change
Theorem 1.1. Adding this citation is a bibliographic improvement only.
The complete classification was not re-enumerated or fully re-audited.
The corollary is not extended to all nonsimplicial twenty-vertex polytopes.

## Earlier pulling obstruction

[F. Chin, Q.-H. Ding, and C. A. Wang, On Hamiltonian
Tetrahedralizations of Convex Polyhedra](https://www.aporc.org/LNOR/5/ISORA2005F16.pdf),
ISORA 2005, 206–216, gives the 92-vertex predecessor for pulling
triangulations. The recorded PDF identity is 377,348 bytes, SHA-256
`8b92caca6dba33b818a08682339078096d8d2a60972846cb232112608d119357`.
No fresh full proof audit of that construction is claimed. It does not
resolve the unrestricted existence question.

## Distribution and remaining gap

Only authored proof, audit, acceptance, status, source discussion and public
source metadata are distributed. Source PDFs, images, extracted third-party
text, programs, generated certificate arrays, raw outputs and datasets are
excluded. Finite checks are historical supporting metadata. The general
no-Steiner Hamiltonian-path problem remains unresolved by this work;
remaining minimum-degree-four cores require a construction or a genuine
counterexample excluding every tetrahedralization.

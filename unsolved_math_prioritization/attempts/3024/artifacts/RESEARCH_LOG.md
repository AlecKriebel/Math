# Five substantive attempts

Problem 3024 / KP-5.17. Research date: 2026-10-03. The investigation did not find a full solution or a prior resolution. These five routes were developed and checked together; the numbering identifies distinct mathematical attempts, not separate claims of five user-chat sessions.

1. **Convex primitive interpolation.** Proved that the boundary-outward primitive space is convex and that a common quantitative Lyapunov function makes its endpoint segment Weinstein. Tested the unconditional inference with explicit compact annulus endpoints. Their exact-difference segment has a periodic midpoint, while a separate rotation homotopy connects the endpoints. The attempted universal affine proof therefore fails for a certified reason. See PROOF.md §2.

2. **Exact perturbation continuation.** Reduced H¹=0 to an exact primitive difference and proved a quantitative local continuation statement for perturbations supported away from critical points and boundary. Attempted to concatenate such neighborhoods globally; the necessary persistence of Lyapunov bounds was not established, and the exact example from route 1 demonstrates the danger. No simply connected counterexample was obtained. See §3.

3. **Cohomological obstruction.** Tested whether the class of the difference of primitives must remain zero under Weinstein homotopy. Constructed complete fixed-form cylinder families with two Morse points and a nonzero varying H¹ class, including full completeness and admissibility checks. Thus the simple proposed obstruction is false. See §4.

4. **Symplectic mapping classes and Moser transport.** Proved the pullback statement for a symplectic isotopy and calculated the exact Moser derivative formula. The method does not reach a nontrivial mapping class, control all boundary flows, or provide Lyapunov functions for intermediate primitives. No invariant separating Weinstein-homotopy components was obtained. See §5.

5. **Formal-class / flexible lifting.** Constructed an explicit compatible-almost-complex path, checked the hypotheses and relevant proof mechanism of the flexible uniqueness theorem, and attempted to extend its critical-handle lifting step without looseness. That step is unproved for general endpoints. Recent distinct-form Stein examples do not satisfy the fixed-ω hypothesis. See §6.

The resulting status is **unsolved, 5/5**. The note makes no historical-priority claim. Forty-four exact algebraic assertions reproduce the finite calculations; the written arguments, not computation, establish the stated special cases and limitations.

# Scope

Target: OWR-822-005 / problem 30000214, Nevo's Problem 8 in OWR 17/2005 and Problem 3.7 in arXiv:math/0505334v2.

The questioned assertion is a finite cover of every finite d-dimensional doubly Cohen–Macaulay complex over a fixed field by d-dimensional homology-sphere subcomplexes, d>1. Each new piece must overlap the preceding union in a set containing a d-face, and the same family must admit an admissible order beginning with any selected piece.

Accepted result after independent internal AI mathematical/source review: a counterexample of dimension two over the fixed coefficient field Q, also valid over every characteristic-zero field. Its absence of any local homology-sphere subcomplex is proved over every field. An unqualified assertion across fields is therefore false. No construction over every individually prescribed positive characteristic is claimed.

The finite field F_11 used to construct the building is separate from the simplicial homology coefficient field Q. The proof never replaces K by a subdivision, never enlarges a subcomplex to an immersed image, and never weakens the local homology-sphere condition to global homology or a minimal cycle. No strengthening of the overlap or ordering requirements is involved: the failure occurs before either requirement can be imposed.

The example is specified by an exact finite algebraic construction, namely the orbit complex of the LSV PGL_3(F_11((y))) building under the kernel of its nine-dimensional polynomial conjugation representation reduced modulo t^9. The quotient is not enumerated facet by facet. Finiteness, unchanged links, purity, all Cohen–Macaulay conditions for it and every vertex deletion, and sphere exclusion are proved symbolically.

The underlying code is supplementary diagnostic code and is not distributed in this edition. Floating-point eigenvalues are explicitly diagnostic only; all proof inequalities are exact. No completeness claim rests on a computer search or an unexamined candidate list.

This work makes no novelty, priority, or comprehensive later-literature claim. The independent internal AI review accepted the stated mathematical scope. These authored documents remain unrefereed; this acceptance is not external human peer review, journal acceptance or formal proof-assistant certification.

# A negative answer to the homology-sphere cover question

Problem 30000214, Nevo's Problem 8 in OWR 17/2005 and Problem 3.7 in arXiv:math/0505334v2. Edition dated 10 October 2026.

Project author: Alec Kriebel, [ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X](https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X).

## Accepted result

There exists a finite two-dimensional simplicial complex K that is doubly Cohen–Macaulay over Q, and hence over every characteristic-zero field, but contains no two-dimensional homology-sphere subcomplex over any field. Homology sphere includes the homology conditions on every face link, including the empty face. Thus the unqualified sphere-cover assertion is false already at existence of an unordered cover; its overlap and reordering requirements never arise.

The building is defined over F_11, which is distinct from the homology coefficient field Q. No counterexample for every individually prescribed positive characteristic is claimed. The quotient is given by an exact finite algebraic construction rather than an enumeration of all vertices or facets. No subdivision, immersed image, global-homology-only sphere, or minimal-cycle substitute is used.

The complete proof constructs the quotient of the affine PGL_3(F_11((y))) building using the kernel of the faithful nine-dimensional polynomial conjugation representation modulo t^9. It proves the displacement bound, unchanged projective-plane links, strict normalized gaps for the irregular deleted links, weighted cohomology vanishing, all Cohen–Macaulay and same-dimension deletion conditions, and the any-field local-link Euler obstruction to sphere pieces.

The LSV building, lattice and representation inputs are unconditional. No global Ramanujan-spectrum theorem or Jacquet–Langlands correspondence is used. The local spectrum and weighted cocycle argument are proved directly. No novelty, priority, or exhaustive later-literature claim is made.

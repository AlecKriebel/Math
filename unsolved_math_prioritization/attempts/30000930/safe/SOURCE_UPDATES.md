# Source conventions and literature update

## Orientation qualification

The original report prescribes unweighted pullback, cup product, and pushforward but does not explicitly fix orientation signs at that definition. Therefore the original packet's nonassociativity result must always be qualified as referring to ordinary complex-oriented Gysin maps, equivalently f=1 in the final paper's convention. It does not justify classifying the general target as solved or refuted. The final paper's Section 4.3 has inconsistent alpha labels; neither the geometric proof nor this release uses those labels to determine orientations. The unambiguous f=1 discussion on p.500 is sufficient.

The final paper gives an appropriate weighted convolution isomorphic to the arc algebra, while leaving the full Yoneda comparison separate. All-k compatibility of the half-canonical coherent sheaves with that associative product is the intended target retained here.

## Bibliographic distinction

The original report labels its Cautis–Kamnitzer reference as the sl2 paper but lists arXiv:0710.3216, which is the sl(m) sequel. The sl2 primary preprint is arXiv:math/0701194. This investigation inspected that actual sl2 source, especially Section 7.2 and Theorem 8.2.

The inspected Anno–Nandakumar arXiv file is v1 of 1602.00768 (2016), not the final journal PDF. The AMS final-PDF endpoints returned 403. Publisher-deposited Crossref metadata for DOI 10.1090/tran/8765 was retrieved successfully; its abstract explicitly distinguishes Ext-space computations from the conjectural annular multiplication, and its references list a further multiplication manuscript as in preparation. We do not claim to have read the inaccessible final journal PDF.

## A newer collection-level formality result

Mladenov, Differential graded categories in holomorphic symplectic geometry, arXiv:2604.06630v1 (8 April 2026), states whole-collection formality in Theorem 1.1.1 / Theorem 5.4.9 for countable collections of orientable compact Kähler Lagrangians with pairwise clean intersections. Thus the 2024 article's collection-level conjectural discussion should not be treated as the latest literature.

The newer preprint also states a stronger deformation result in Lemma 5.4.7 and Corollary 5.4.8: finite relevant dg subcategories have a trivial formal deformation of composition. We inspected those arguments and the definitions of the compared categories, but have not independently audited the full preprint. The virtual de Rham multiplication in Section 5.3 is defined using the Yoneda product; identifying it with a specific weighted arc convolution remains an additional comparison. The local Fukaya comparison in Conjecture 1.5.3 is still stated conjecturally there. No all-k identification of the requested arc multiplication was verified.

The k=2 theorem in this release depends on the published 2024 pairwise-module theorem, not on the 2026 preprint. Its abstract algebra classification and all parameterized table checks are supplied separately.

## Status language

Use: general weighted Yoneda comparison unresolved in this investigation after five substantive approaches; k=1 and k=2 partial cases; known f=1 obstruction with ordinary complex orientations; new literature lead from 2026.

Do not use: the Ext/arc conjecture is refuted, already solved, globally known to remain open, or a new first solution. A bounded search establishes none of those unqualified claims.

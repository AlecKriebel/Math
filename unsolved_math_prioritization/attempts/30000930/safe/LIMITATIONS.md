# Limits of the continued release

- The general all-k weighted coherent-sheaf Yoneda/arc-algebra comparison is not solved here. The appropriate campaign disposition is unsolved after 5/5 approaches, with partial results.
- The k=2 proof uses Mladenov's published pairwise-module theorem and proper-support Serre duality. The attached algebra computation does not independently prove those theorems or automatically certify the geometric application. Fresh independent review must check every hypothesis and the two-sided-action argument.
- The original unweighted obstruction is ordinary-complex-orientation specific. The original report does not explicitly fix those signs, so it cannot alone settle the intended target.
- The k=2 algebra isomorphism is abstract and graded. It does not select a canonical basis, an equivariant lift, a unique normalization of trace, or a dg/A-infinity equivalence respecting all cup and cap functors.
- The local Koszul result is an affine-model calculation. It does not assert that the entire Springer neighborhood splits globally, or that local products determine global Yoneda composition.
- The higher-rank calculation concerns linear balance and grading constraints in a simultaneous cohomological model. It does not establish a family of actual coherent-sheaf Ext algebras or a family of globally associative products.
- The newer 2026 preprint states collection formality and triviality of a relevant formal deformation. We inspected its relevant claims but did not independently audit the whole proof or identify its multiplication with the specific weighted arc product. It is not used in the k=2 theorem.
- The AMS final Anno–Nandakumar PDF could not be downloaded. The 2016 primary preprint and final publisher-deposited metadata were distinguished explicitly. Neither the 2008 early multiplication claim nor stronger abstract language was accepted as a verified full resolution.
- No exhaustive current-literature status or novelty assertion is made.
- The original frozen archive and complete prior portable audit are unchanged and hash-bound in history/. Their previous verdict does not cover this new release.
- Source PDFs, source text, Crossref response contents, private catalogue entries, and private coordination are excluded. Public source titles, URLs, hashes, sizes, and inspection metadata are included.
- No remote repository writes were performed. A new uninvolved audit is required before publication.

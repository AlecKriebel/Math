## Original target

*MATRIX–MFO Tandem Workshop: Stochastic Reinforcement Processes and Graphs*, Oberwolfach Reports 12/2023, DOI https://doi.org/10.4171/owr/2023/12. Original context is PDF pp. 15–18, Open Problems 1 and 2 at PDF p. 17 / printed 655. The text distinguishes arbitrary positive rates for forests from equal rates for whiskers, states the then-known >2 and >25 bounds, and suggests >4/3 for both. Its triangle obstruction concerns strict alpha<4/3. The report was inspected during the historical source review; no copied report or extracted text is distributed.

## Stochastic theorem actually used

Christian Hirsch, Mark Holmes, Victor Kleptsyn, *Infinite WARM graphs III: strong reinforcement regime*, Nonlinearity 36 (2023), 3013–3042, published 28 April 2023. DOI https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-6544/acc9a0. Published PDF https://pure.au.dk/ws/portalfiles/portal/418609000/Hirsch_2023_Nonlinearity_36_3013.pdf, freshly opened through the web tool on 10 October 2026. The inspected full PDF has the identity recorded in SOURCE_METADATA.json.

- PDF p. 3 / printed 3014 gives unit initial tallies and the continuous-time firing model.
- Theorem 1 on PDF p. 5 / printed 3016 gives finite-graph almost-sure convergence to stable/critical equilibria and positive-probability convergence to each strictly stable equilibrium.
- Proposition 1 gives E_infinity=E_plus.
- Theorem 2 addresses finite components in infinite graphs under additional hypotheses. We do not import an assertion about all infinite components.
- The published introduction still cites >2 for forests and >25 for equal-rate whiskers and explicitly calls for improving the latter.

## Previous deterministic structural theorem

Mark Holmes, Victor Kleptsyn, *Proof of the WARM whisker conjecture for neuronal connections*, Chaos 27 (2017), 043104. DOI https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4978683. Author manuscript https://researchers.ms.unimelb.edu.au/~mholmes1@unimelb/Reinforced-graphs_final.pdf, March 2017, 28 PDF pages, retrieved directly on 10 October 2026 and retained privately.

Theorem 2(a) gives the equal-rate >25 whisker conclusion; Theorem 3 / Corollary 1 give the >2 forest conclusion. Section 3 contains the least-weight/champion argument. Our proof rederives the only least-weight consequence it needs under the exact equal-rate and alpha>2 hypotheses. Example 1 already warns that unequal vertex rates admit stable long paths; our rational path is a scope check, with no novelty claim for that phenomenon.

## Authored contribution and dependency limits

The accepted manuscript proves finite-graph cycle instability and the forest threshold alpha>4/3 for arbitrary positive rates. For equal rates, its self-contained five-interval symbolic inequalities and analytic tail give whisker support for alpha>=17/4. The equal-rate whisker interval 4/3<alpha<17/4 remains OPEN. The 4/3 equality endpoint is excluded. The rational unequal-rate diameter-four path is a scope control, consistent with the already known failure of unrestricted-rate whisker conclusions.

The 2023 stochastic theorem is an imported published input. The 2017 least-weight idea and prior bounds, and the known triangle obstruction, are credited rather than claimed as new. The proof does not depend on a generated certificate, coefficient table, omitted program, exploratory search or dataset. It makes no infinite-graph, loop/multigraph, novelty or priority claim.

# Source-summary clarification C1

The independently reviewed six-file author snapshot remains byte-for-byte preserved in public/.

In public/RESEARCH.md, section 0, item 1, the abbreviated description of Murai–Novik–Zheng's edge-participation result should read **“non-simplex prime simplicial polytopes.”** Their definition of prime includes the simplex, whereas a simplex has no nonzero affine 2-stress. The exact alternative hypothesis is “no missing faces of dimension at least d−1.”

This explicitly resolves the independent audit's C1 recommendation. It does not change the target, any proof, any finite test, or the unsolved disposition: the target's missing-face hypothesis already excludes a simplex.

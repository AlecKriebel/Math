## Six pinned scholarly PDFs

All six complete PDF byte counts and SHA-256 digests match the author metadata. Fresh independent pdftotext extractions reproduce all six stored extracted-text files byte for byte. This establishes consistency of the local source bytes and their extractions. It does not by itself prove a new download of those exact bytes from the current remote server; this review makes no such claim.

- [Wolf-Wu](https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.02983): Theorems 1.1-1.2, Definition 1.4, and Proposition 3.1 were read. The obstruction includes the geometric-speed restriction on a family of closed surfaces; it cannot be promoted to all minimal foliations. The Jacobi equation used by the author matches the inspected formula. The unversioned arXiv landing page was available; a direct versioned landing-page request initially missed the web cache.
- [Hass, v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04145v2): The 15 November 2025 version's introduction, Theorem 1.1, and Corollary 1.1 were inspected. Its examples forbid quasi-area-minimizing fibrations on some surface bundles, not all foliations on all closed hyperbolic manifolds. The PDF title includes quasi-minimal fibrations while the landing-page title remains the shorter older wording. The author's citation follows the actual PDF title, which is appropriate.
- [Coskunuzer-Huang-Lowe-Vargas Pallete, v3](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13795v3): Theorem 1.2, the Section 2 conventions, and Section 7.2 were read; page 22 was independently regenerated and visually inspected. The setting is complete orientable hyperbolic manifolds with finitely generated fundamental group; the relevant alternative concerns doubly degenerate bounded-geometry products S x R. Either every member contains an appropriate closed minimal surface, or some member admits a minimal foliation. The alternatives need not be exclusive. This neither establishes a closed ambient example nor supplies a descent argument. Question 7.1 is still presented as open in the 12 June 2026 version. The landing page and HTML were independently available on the audit date. The publisher-text identity was not established here.
- [Guaraco-Lima-Vargas Pallete, v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07504v1): Theorem 1.5 permits leaves whose mean curvature is nowhere zero in addition to minimal leaves. Its foliation existence theorem therefore does not solve the all-minimal question.
- [Rovenski-Walczak](https://publi.math.unideb.hu/paper/2168/download/10_5486_PMD_2017_7668.pdf): The introduction's equation (2) gives the classical integrated second-mean-curvature identity. Substituting Ric=-2 and sigma_2=-s/2 yields the author's integral balance with the correct sign. The full historical proofs behind its citations were not independently re-audited and are not claimed as reviewed.

# Source audit and statement scope

Checked 2026-10-06. This is a bounded literature and repository check, not proof that all relevant literature has been found.

## Exact target

K3, Problem 3.18, printed pages 143-144, asks: “Is there a closed hyperbolic 3-manifold that is foliated with minimal leaves?” The statement was checked in both extracted text and rendered pages of the author-hosted PDF. Its remarks distinguish a chosen hyperbolic metric from a metric supplied by a taut-foliation theorem.

Consequently the ambient manifold is closed, and minimality means zero mean curvature in its hyperbolic metric. Compactness of leaves, a fibration over the circle, a principal-curvature bound, and a geometric-flow speed rule are not assumptions of the target. The entry does not give a transverse differentiability exponent. The propositions in this package explicitly concern smooth foliations, without claiming that this resolves any weaker regularity interpretation. Singular laminations, intrinsic hyperbolicity of leaves, and dynamical minimality (dense leaves) are different notions.

The exact public record URL https://unsolvedmath.com/problems/2816 was requested. The web reader returned an internal error, and an ordinary direct read returned HTTP 403. Its live contents were not verified. The authenticated local record matches the K3 question, with separately recorded dataset and statement hashes. No live-page match is asserted.

## Primary and author-hosted sources

- **[K3]** R. Inanc Baykur, Robion C. Kirby, Daniel Ruberman, *K3: A New Problem List in Low-Dimensional Topology*, author preliminary version, Problem 3.18. https://math.berkeley.edu/sites/default/files/surv-295-ruberman-watermarked-author-pdf.pdf . Inspected printed pages 143-144 and title-page status. The PDF says it is a preliminary version made available with AMS permission; it was not edited or included in this package.
- **[WW20]** Michael Wolf and Yunhui Wu, *Non-existence of geometric minimal foliations in hyperbolic three-manifolds*, Comment. Math. Helv. 95 (2020), 167-182, https://doi.org/10.4171/CMH/484 ; inspected author preprint https://arxiv.org/abs/1901.02983v1 . Theorems 1.1-1.2 exclude locally geometric families of closed minimal surfaces. Definition 1.4 requires a C2 product embedding and normal speed depending only on the squared second fundamental form at the point. Proposition 3.1 gives the Jacobi equation. Dropping the geometric condition is not justified.
- **[Hass]** Joel Hass, *Minimal and quasi-minimal fibrations of hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.04145v2 (15 November 2025). Inspected introduction, Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.1. The revised PDF proves that for any genus `g>=2`, `0<=mu<1`, `lambda>=1`, some hyperbolic surface bundles admit no fibration by `(mu,lambda)`-quasi-area-minimizing fibers. These examples exclude minimal fibrations on those manifolds; they do not rule out all foliations on every closed hyperbolic manifold. The older catalog title omits the revised quasi-minimal wording.
- **[CHLV26]** Baris Coskunuzer, Zheng Huang, Ben Lowe, Franco Vargas Pallete, *Existence of Minimal Surfaces in Infinite Volume Hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.13795v3 (12 June 2026). Inspected Theorem 1.2, Section 2 conventions, and Section 7.2, including rendered p.22. Theorem 1.2 is an alternative for doubly degenerate `S times R` manifolds with bounded geometry; its two outcomes need not be exclusive. It neither asserts a closed example nor makes a noncompact foliation descend to a closed manifold. Question 7.1 still asks about a closed hyperbolic manifold containing a local minimal foliation. This is dated positive evidence of an unresolved related question, not a substitute for K3's exact global statement. A publisher DOI, https://doi.org/10.1515/crelle-2026-0064 , was discovered but did not open successfully; journal-text identity was not checked.
- **[GLVP21]** Marco A. M. Guaraco, Vanderson Lima, Franco Vargas Pallete, *Mean curvature flow in homology and foliations of hyperbolic 3-manifolds*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.07504v1 (16 May 2021). Inspected introduction and Theorem 1.5. It supplies global foliations in the stated quasi-Fuchsian or fibered cases with each leaf minimal or with nowhere-vanishing mean curvature. Allowing the latter does not produce an all-minimal foliation.
- **[RW17]** Vladimir Rovenski and Pawel Walczak, *Integral formulae for codimension-one foliated Randers spaces*, Publ. Math. Debrecen 91 (2017), 95-110, https://doi.org/10.5486/PMD.2017.7668 . Inspected publisher PDF https://publi.math.unideb.hu/paper/2168/download/10_5486_PMD_2017_7668.pdf , introduction equation (2), and references. It explicitly states the classical integrated second-mean-curvature identity used here. Its historical references were not independently read in full; the present proof does not rely on any uninspected historical proof.

No claim of historical novelty, priority, exhaustive search, or human peer review follows from the search results.

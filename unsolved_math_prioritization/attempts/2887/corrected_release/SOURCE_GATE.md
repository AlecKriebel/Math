## Current literature and exact applicability

- KPR author manuscript: full relevant statements, Table 1, Section 5, and proof of Proposition 1.6 read. Propositions 1.4 and 1.6 fail, respectively, the homeomorphism and orientability gates. Theorem 1.2 is simple homotopy equivalence, not unconditional homeomorphism. Theorem 1.1 and Corollary 1.3 have distinct additional hypotheses.
- Akbulut–Yasui: full short paper retrieved; Theorem 1.1 and the sphere-surgery definition read. The required odd class is spherical in M°_f; an arbitrary odd class in M is not the same condition. Its handle-calculus theorem is an external input.
- Gabai: the published author offprint was checked, rather than relying only on the earlier 2017 draft. Theorem 1.2 uses a common embedded transverse sphere with trivial normal bundle and excludes two-torsion in π₁.
- AKMR: author-hosted manuscript *Infinite families of homologous 2-spheres in 4-manifolds*, Lemma 2.3 and its proof, pp.4–5, text and diagram visually inspected. A 2023 author CV lists the 2018 manuscript as unpublished. No peer-reviewed publication was verified. This is not the differently titled 2025 Auckly–Ruberman paper.
- Torres: arXiv:2508.19142v1, Theorem A and introduction checked in full local text. Cambridge publisher metadata verifies publication online 26 March 2026. The examples are nonorientable and their orientation covers are diffeomorphic.
- Searches included exact K3/Gluck problem strings, orientable exotic Gluck twists with 2025/2026 terms, concordant/homotopic spheres, odd spherical classes, and gauge invariants. A 2026 author webpage claiming standardness for certain twist-roll-spin/cork-double families was seen; it was not independently verified or used. Such a restricted standardness claim would not answer the present general existence questions.

No verified full answer to KP-4.11 was found. This package does not claim an exhaustive literature census or establish global open status as a theorem.

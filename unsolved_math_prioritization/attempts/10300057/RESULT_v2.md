# Five-turn partial outcome, source-corrected version 2: virtual isotopy limits of taut foliations

**10300057 / Calegari Question13.4: original unsolved, 5/5 substantive author turns. Independent review pending.**

The target asks for taut foliations F,G on one hyperbolic 3-manifold such that a lifted isotopy orbit of G converges geometrically to the lift of F in a finite cover, while no corresponding downstairs sequence exists. The cited source interprets geometric convergence as tangent-plane convergence. Homotopy of plane fields, contact isotopy, and isotopy-orbit closure of foliations are different requirements.

## Retained results and exact limits

- Turn1: the foliated seminorm of a transferred real homology class scales exactly by covering degree. The upstairs premise therefore already forces the source's downstairs norm inequality. In the closed setting, lifted plane fields must be homotopic; odd-degree covers cannot hide normal-orientability differences, and cooriented Euler classes agree rationally up to sign. These are necessary conditions only.
- Turn2: any witnessing finite cover can be replaced by a regular normal-core cover by lifting its identity isotopies. Deck averaging gives genuine downstairs convergence under explicit integrated C2 bounds on generators and a vanishing integrated C1 deck defect. Endpoint plane-field convergence alone does not provide these hypotheses. A local exact Frobenius calculation disproves naive averaging of defining forms.
- Turn3: on hyperbolic positive15-surgery on the figure-eight knot, smooth cooriented plane fields with Euler classes2c and8c have nonhomotopic underlying unoriented fields downstairs but homotopic lifts to the15-fold cyclic cover. This is a plane-field analogue, not a foliation example.
- Turn4: the figure-eight large-surgery Floer complex has reduced homology only in the spin structure. The Spin-c-specific taut-foliation nonvanishing argument therefore forces Euler class zero on that15-surgery, excluding taut realization of the proposed2c and8c fields in the stated regularity. The plane-field construction remains valid, but its proposed realization route fails.
- Turn5: for closed oriented coorientable C2 taut foliations on hyperbolic3-manifolds, Vogel's canonical positive/negative contact isotopy classes are constant on a foliation's identity-isotopy orbit closure and commute with finite-cover pullback. A two-choice invariant accounts for reversing coorientation. Equal contact data do not imply the missing upstairs foliation convergence.

No actual pair satisfying the original upstairs premise and downstairs obstruction has been constructed. Nor has arbitrary upstairs convergence been shown to descend. None of the additional closedness, smoothness, coorientation, control or classification hypotheses is silently substituted for the full source question.

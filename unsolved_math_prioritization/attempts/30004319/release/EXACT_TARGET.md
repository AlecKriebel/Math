# Exact target and conventions

The source question is:

> Is every ring which parametrizes an A2-graded group alternative?

It occurs in Torben Wiedemann's contribution to *Mini-Workshop: Rank One Groups
and Exceptional Algebraic Groups*, Oberwolfach Report 52/2019, printed p. 3261,
DOI [10.4171/OWR/2019/52](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2019/52).
The workshop took place in November 2019; the report was published in 2020.

We use a nonzero unital distributive ring R, without assuming associativity.
Its associator is (a,b,c)=(ab)c-a(bc). The desired conclusion is
(a,a,b)=(b,a,a)=0 for every a,b in R, including in characteristic two.
Neither a division hypothesis nor an extra plumpness, stability, finiteness,
linear representation, or characteristic hypothesis may be inserted.

Write the roots of A2 as e_i-e_j, i≠j, for 1≤i,j≤3. In standard coordinates
the root maps x_ij:(R,+)→U_ij are additive isomorphisms and satisfy

- [x_ij(a),x_jk(b)]=x_ik(ab) for distinct i,j,k;
- [x_ij(a),x_kl(b)]=1 if j≠k and i≠l.

Here [g,h]=g^(-1)h^(-1)gh, and conjugation is g^w=w^(-1)gw.
The group is generated by the six nontrivial root groups. For every root there
is a Weyl element in U_-α U_α U_-α carrying the root groups by the corresponding
reflection. For a positive system Π and α outside Π, U_α∩U_Π={1}.
These are the conventions of Wiedemann's Definitions 2.5.2 and 5.6.2, specialised
to standard A2 signs. The question is about this full structure, not merely the
three positive root groups or a presentation with potentially collapsed root maps.

“Strong unit” means an element u having v with
L_u L_v=L_v L_u=R_u R_v=R_v R_u=id. Here L_u(z)=uz and R_u(z)=zu.
This is stronger than uv=vu=1 in a general nonassociative ring, and stronger
than separately requiring L_u and R_u to be bijections.

Success would be a proof for every such R, or a particular nonalternative R with
a fully verified A2-graded group and faithful root coordinates. This checkpoint
does neither. It supplies necessary conditions, restricted conclusions, and
failed-route controls without changing the target.

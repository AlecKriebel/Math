# Alternativity of parameters for A2-graded groups

Problem 30004319 / OWR-17295-003. Research checkpoint, 4 October 2026.

**Status: unsolved. Five substantive approaches used; no complete solution or counterexample.**

The question in the original source is genuine and remains explicitly open in
Wiedemann's 2024 monograph. The rank-at-least-three classification does not answer
it. The additional 2025–2026 sources checked here do not supply the missing rank-two
theorem. This is a bounded literature check, not a certification of global absence.

The strongest results in this checkpoint are:

1. A conditional alternativity theorem: a coordinatising ring is alternative if
   every element is a strong unit plus an element of its nucleus.
2. A proved necessary fourfold annihilation condition, obtained from opposite-root
   commutators. It rules out a concrete eight-element ring even though that ring
   passes all strong-unit Moufang tests.
3. Exact finite controls over all 4,096 labelled unital three-dimensional
   F2-algebra tables. Of these, 3,900 nonalternative tables pass the unit test;
   the opposite-root condition rejects 3,324 of them. The remaining 576 are
   **not** counterexamples: no corresponding full graded group has been constructed.
4. A universal-presentation formulation isolating the remaining injectivity and
   nondegeneracy obligations, plus explicit obstructions to positive-root-only
   and naive matrix/Lie-algebra arguments.

No novelty claim is made for the partial lemmas. Their proofs are included so that
their precise content does not depend on novelty or on computational interpretation.

# Problem 3.17: topological construction of the two-loop polynomial

**ID:** 10400067 / AMR-103-0067, rank 447.

**Disposition:** unsolved after five substantive attempts. No new general

## What was obtained

1. **An exact reduced-specialization obstruction.** In
   R=Q[x^±1,y^±1,z^±1]/(xyz-1), theta-symmetric polynomials form Q[u,v],
   where a=x+y+z, b=xy+yz+zx, u=a+b and v=ab. The kernel of
   F↦F(t,1,t^-1) is generated by
   D=u^2-4v=(x-1)^2(y-1)^2(z-1)^2. Knowing the whole reduced invariant
   therefore does not give a formal reconstruction of the full numerator.
2. **A precise description of unweighted cyclic residue information.**
   For f=sum c_ab x^a y^b, averaging over an n-th root grid gives the sum
   of coefficients with n dividing both exponents. The whole sequence
   determines the totals on gcd shells, rather than individual coefficients.
3. **An explicit joint-kernel certificate.** The nonzero integral polynomial
   H=-D(u+2)(v+2u-3) has 54 monomials. It vanishes under the reduced
   specialization and every unweighted cyclic root-grid average. Its
   common-power dilations yield nonzero joint-kernel elements with any
   prescribed finite logarithmic Taylor jet zero. This is an algebraic
   obstruction, not a realized pair of knots.
4. **A credited relative comparison.** Moussard's 2019 odd-graded-piece
   vanishing and the 2025 Audoux–Moussard graded comparison imply that
   Z_2^Kri-Z_2^Les is constant on each integral Blanchfield-module class,
   in their common diagram normalization. The proof is in Turn 4. The
   missing class-dependent calibration is not determined by normalizing
   only at the unknot.
5. **Finite bounded reconstruction.** A Laurent numerator supported in
   [-d,d]^2 is recovered explicitly from logarithmic moments with
   0<=i,j<=2d, or from a complete weighted Fourier transform on one
   sufficiently large root grid. With Ohtsuki's d=2g bound, a total jet
   through degree 8g suffices. The required topological measurements
   have not been constructed or identified with the exact target here.

## Why this does not settle the original question

Lescop gives a genuine topological configuration-space construction, but
its arbitrary-knot comparison is not established by the sources inspected.
The 2025 Kricker–Lescop theorem is an associated-graded comparison; Turn 4
isolates the remaining constants. Bar-Natan–van der Veen's May 2026
version explicitly retains equality of its theta with Ohtsuki's as
Conjecture 25. Matching reduced invariants or cover residues cannot simply
replace those missing comparison proofs.

The proposed inverse transforms are complete only when their inputs are
known. Evaluating those inputs from the Kontsevich invariant itself would
not supply the independent geometric explanation sought here. We have
not proved that a suitable topological input is impossible.

The obstruction polynomials live in the ambient target ring. We do not
claim they are differences of values realized by knots. Conversely, an
additional theorem characterizing the actual image could conceivably
make some otherwise noninjective measurements sufficient on that image.

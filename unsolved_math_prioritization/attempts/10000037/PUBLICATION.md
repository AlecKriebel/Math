The five-page PDF, editable-source and exact-verification ZIP and upload kit are in publication/.

The exact target is the context-supported all-supercritical reading of Benjamini's2013 Problem9.6. The original printed sentence omits its probability quantifier. No uniquely intended missing quantifier or negative answer to a weaker near-one statement is claimed. The proof covers every actual graph vertex, exact series reduction and infinite-component correspondence, all-root survival, finite-cut dual parity, every weak-path start and length, and independence of the infinite events. It uses no transitivity, uniqueness monotonicity or deterministic cluster-count theorem.

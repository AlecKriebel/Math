## Comparisons

- Hilário, Sá, Sanchis and Teixeira, [*Percolation on stretched lattices: a static renormalization approach*](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00440-026-01470-4), PTRF 195 (2026), 451–496. Series-path effective probabilities are established methodology. Its annealed uniqueness discussion uses invariance, ergodicity and finite energy; it does not cover every fixed nonstationary stretching pattern.
- Häggström, Peres and Schonmann, [*Percolation on transitive graphs as a coalescent process: relentless merging followed by simultaneous uniqueness*](https://www.math.ucla.edu/~rhs/papers/hps.pdf), author PDF dated 8 July 1999. Its uniform connection hypotheses must not be replaced by survival at a single root.
- De Lima, Martineau, Sanna and Valesin, [*Approximation on slabs and uniqueness for Bernoulli percolation with a sublattice of defects*](https://alea.impa.br/articles/v19/19-67.pdf), ALEA 19 (2022), 1767–1797; [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2010.06736v2). Its coordinate-defect and symmetry hypotheses differ from opposite angular regions.
- Teixeira, [*Percolation and local isoperimetric inequalities*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.5923v1), Theorem 1.4; Candellero–Teixeira, [*Percolation and isoperimetry on roughly transitive graphs*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1507.07765v3), Theorem 1.8; Hutchcroft–Tointon, [*Non-triviality of the phase transition for percolation on finite transitive graphs*](https://doi.org/10.4171/JEMS/1453), Theorem 1.8. The inspected conclusions give high-density uniqueness under their respective hypotheses, without identifying the lower endpoint with p_c.
- Benjamini–Kozma, [*Uniqueness of percolation on products with Z*](https://alea.math.cnrs.fr/articles/v10/10-02.pdf), ALEA 10 (2013), 15–25. Its critical nonuniqueness example does not assert Euclidean-lattice quasi-isometry and is not relabeled as this supercritical example.

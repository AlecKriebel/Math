# Supercritical bond nonuniqueness on a graph quasi-isometric to the square lattice

Authored research reader, 14 October 2026. Problem 10000037 / AMR-099-0037, row 1785.

**A deterministic degree-four counterexample gives a negative answer to the context-supported full-supercritical uniqueness formulation.** Every edge uses the same independent bond parameter. The graph is uniformly quasi-isometric to Z², with critical probability at most 0.9; at p = 0.95, the probability of at least two infinite clusters is greater than 1/3.

This also refutes the stronger assertion of almost-sure at-most-one infinite cluster at every fixed parameter. The original source is an open question whose parameter range is implicit in its surrounding discussion. This is not a correction of an incorrect published theorem. Historical novelty remains **uncertified**.

The substantive negative result is in dimension two, which suffices to refute either universal formulation. In dimension one, every connected bounded-degree graph quasi-isometric to Z has p_c = 1, no infinite clusters at a fixed p < 1, and exactly one at p = 1.

“Accepted” refers to the included independent mathematical audit, not external peer review. No claim is made about the exact number of infinite clusters, their almost-sure multiplicity, uniqueness monotonicity, or the behavior at the canonical graph's critical parameter.

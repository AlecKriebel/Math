## Scope and source interpretation

The package's conclusion remains a degree-four, deterministic graph quasi-isometric to Z² with p_c at most 9/10 and, at p = 19/20, probability of at least two infinite iid bond clusters strictly above 2200741829/6461571200, hence above 1/3. The common parameter applies to actual graph edges. Effective inhomogeneity arises through fixed finite subdivision gadgets.

The primary question is Open Problem 9.6 in Itai Benjamini's *Coarse Geometry and Randomness*, 30 October 2013 author manuscript, printed/PDF page 64. I independently retrieved the [preserved manuscript](https://arquivo.pt/noFrame/replay/20201231041548id_/http://www.wisdom.weizmann.ac.il/~itai/stflouraug24.pdf), obtaining the retained identity of 1,101,523 bytes and SHA-256 aaab65b3acf65f4d21657da94464e9d5edb93969a4c353805099a220a0717cfe. Text checks covered pages 5, 9, 32–33 and 63–65; visual checks covered pages 9, 63 and 64. These support the general-graph bond model, multiplicative-and-additive metric convention, and contextual supercritical interpretation used in the reader. The problem sentence leaves its parameter quantifier implicit; the reader correctly identifies that interpretive limitation rather than attributing an explicit all-parameter quantifier to the source.

The dimension-two witness negates the context-supported universal full-supercritical statement and the stronger universal all-parameter at-most-one normalization. The separate dimension-one endpoint is retained. Neither critical-parameter behavior nor an exact value of p_c is claimed for the canonical graph. The reader does not claim exactly two clusters, almost-sure multiplicity, or uniqueness monotonicity. Optional higher-dimensional, additive-only, and reflection variants remain supplementary under the scope of the original mathematical acceptance.

The source is an open question, not an erroneous theorem. The reader distinguishes high-density uniqueness from uniqueness throughout the supercritical range and leaves historical priority and novelty uncertified. This packaging review adds no completeness claim to the bounded literature search.

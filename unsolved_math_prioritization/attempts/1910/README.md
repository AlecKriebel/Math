# Reduced-denominator growth: accepted partial theorem (EP68 / 1910)

This unrefereed, AI-assisted proof and independent internal AI audit establish unconditional growth results for the reduced denominators of partial sums of the factorial-minus-one series. “Accepted” means only the audit's stated partial mathematical scope. No external human peer review, journal acceptance, formal proof-assistant certification, novelty, or fresh worldwide open-status determination is claimed.

For H_N = sum_{n=2}^N 1/(n!-1), let Q_N be its positive reduced denominator and S the infinite sum. The normalized logarithm log(Q_N)/(N^(3/2) log N) has limsup at least 1/3. For every fixed 0 < c < 1/3, the indices satisfying log Q_N >= c N^(3/2) log N have lower asymptotic density at least 1/2. Along each such set, Q_N(S-H_N) tends to positive infinity.

These conclusions do not resolve rationality or irrationality of S. They do not establish pointwise growth for all N, a claim at c = 1/3, density exactly 1/2 or density one, or exclusion of a favorable sparse subsequence. The original irrationality problem remains unresolved by this work.

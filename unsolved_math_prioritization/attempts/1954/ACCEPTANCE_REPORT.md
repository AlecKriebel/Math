## Accepted result

**ACCEPT PRIOR HARMONIC COROLLARY BY EXPLICIT THEOREM IMPORT.**

For a countably infinite set \(A\subset(1,\infty)\) with \(|kx-y|\geq1\) for every ordered pair of distinct elements and every positive integer \(k\), the sum \(\sum_{x\in A,\ x<n}1/x\) is \(o(\log n)\).

The underlying research result belongs to Koukoulopoulos, Lamzouri and Lichtman, [arXiv:2502.09539v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.09539v1), Theorem 1. The contribution of this packet is an authored, complete elementary application and audit. All non-elementary mathematical authority is explicitly assigned to that external theorem.

# Prior harmonic corollary for integer dilation separation

Let A be a subset of (1,infinity) such that |kx-y| >= 1 for all distinct x,y in A and all positive integers k. Then

H_A(X) = sum_{x in A, x <= X} 1/x = o(log X), as real X tends to infinity.

Consequently sum_{x in A, x < n} 1/x = o(log n) along positive integers n tending to infinity. This is the harmonic alternative in Erdős Problem 143 (record 1954), already implied by Koukoulopoulos, Lamzouri and Lichtman, arXiv:2502.09539v1, Theorem 1.

Unit separation at k=1 proves the source theorem's exact discreteness assumption, including exclusion of real accumulation points and local finiteness. If the nonnegative harmonic ratio failed to tend to zero, it would have positive upper limit.

The original conjecture asks for positive association of all increasing observables of a fixed-color indicator set on one bipartition class, for every finite bipartite graph and every integer q>=3. Neither a full proof nor a proper-coloring counterexample is established here. Even the source's four-vertex two-conjunction special case remains open in this packet.

Read FINAL_RESULT.md for the preserved author result and independent_review/INDEPENDENT_REVIEW.md for the complete review. Historical files that say review pending are unchanged freeze-time records; this additive wrapper supplies the current reviewed status.

## Strongest general conclusions

- Every single fixed-color indicator has nonnegative covariance with every increasing observable, by a monotone Kempe bijection
- Every marginal on at most three observed coordinates is associated, including triples inside a larger graph
- Prescribed terminal-color extension counts increase under merging color classes; equality with the monochromatic count is characterized by graph components

Other scoped results cover forests, integrated-side degree at most two via the credited fuzzy-Potts theorem, full-side-size-three FKG, an explicitly associated side-four family despite FKG failure, and a conditioned subdivision representation with its exact unconditioned edge-cover-event consequence.

## Critical distinction

The final negative law is an **abstract color-symmetric probability law**, with no certified graph realization. It is a counterexample to sufficiency of certain abstract necessary conditions, not a graph counterexample to the original conjecture. The unperturbed law is proved unrealizable; the strict perturbation remains unrealized. Pinned-law and stronger FKG failures also remain separate from the unconditioned source question.

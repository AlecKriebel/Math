# Polar cylinders and affine complements: credited prior resolution

Problem 30003862 / OWR-16169-009. Disposition: **ACCEPT_CREDITED_PRIOR_RESOLUTION**, with **zero new proof-search turns**.

In the source's exact normal Du Val del Pezzo hypersurface setting over an algebraically closed characteristic-zero field, an anticanonical polar cylinder on the surface exists if and only if its affine complement contains a principal open cylinder. Hence their absence conditions are equivalent. The polar boundary is effective rational and Q-linearly equivalent to the anticanonical divisor. The embedding is part of the hypotheses.

The equivalence is credited to Jihun Park, with the earlier implication credited to Ivan Cheltsov, Adrien Dubouloz and Park. It is reported established in the original 2018 Oberwolfach contribution, stated in Park's institutional Corollary 3.2, and corroborated in the published survey's Theorem 4.20. Park's paper appeared in Transformation Groups 27 (2022), 651–657, online 4 July 2020. Exact numbered statements were inspected in the institutional preprint, not the subscription journal PDF.

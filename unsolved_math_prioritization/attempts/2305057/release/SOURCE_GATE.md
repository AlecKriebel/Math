# Source and attribution gate

## Exact target

- Numeric ID: **2305057**.
- Code: **AMR-022-5057**.
- Title: **Research Problems in Function Theory — Problem 5.57**.
- Proposer: **A. Baernstein II**.
- [Catalogue page](https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/2305057) was inaccessible to the web reader. A pinned catalogue record supplied the statement, which was then checked against the original mathematical sources.

The target is the capacity-zero omitted-set dichotomy for holomorphic functions on the disc, with boundary exceptions measured by arc length and with **every** Stolz angle at the chosen boundary point. It concerns omitted values, not just the closure of the image or multiplicity-weighted area.

## Primary sources checked

1. J. M. Anderson, K. F. Barth, and D. A. Brannan, *Research problems in complex analysis*, Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 9 (1977), 129–162, Problem 5.57 on p.138. The [original report scan](https://citeseerx.ist.psu.edu/document?doi=ea1cdeb32c106ad049da5f856f5bb77162129243&repid=rep1&type=pdf) contains the Baernstein attribution and the logarithmic-capacity formulation.
2. W. K. Hayman and E. F. Lingham, *Research Problems in Function Theory (New Edition)*, [arXiv:1809.07200](https://arxiv.org/abs/1809.07200), 2018, Problem 5.57 on printed pp.106–107. Its update reports no progress. That dated statement is superseded by the source below; it is not current evidence that the problem remains open.
3. S. J. Gardiner and M. Manolaki, *A strong form of Plessner's theorem*, Advances in Mathematics 376 (2021), 107489, [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aim.2020.107489), [author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.05874). Theorem 1 concerns infinite image-circle length counting multiplicity. It does not assert that the omitted set is polar. Its introduction expressly distinguishes the older area/capacity questions.
4. O. Ivrii, *Non-tangential ranges of holomorphic functions at Plessner points*, [arXiv:2609.18785v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.18785v1), 16 September 2026. Theorem 1.1(i),(iii), pp.2–3, and the proof in §§2–5 and 7, pp.4–13, were inspected. The introduction explicitly identifies Problems 5.20 and 5.57 and gives negative answers. The HTML and PDF versions agree on the formula, hypotheses, and density conclusion.

Ivrii also proves results about the exact non-tangential range and its Hausdorff dimension. Those additional theorems are not needed for the negative answer verified here and are not independently certified by this packet.

The pinned catalogue also contains **2305020 / AMR-022-5020**, Collingwood's Problem 5.20, asking for an area-zero exceptional-value strengthening. It is related but weaker, not an identical formulation. Ivrii's same counterexample answers both. It must not be counted as two new discoveries. The repository's related-target groups contained no match for these IDs. These searches are bounded checks, not a proof that no historical file anywhere has discussed the subject.

## Scope and provenance

This is a credited verification note about an existing preprint. All resolution credit belongs to Ivrii. No new theorem or first-priority claim is made. Full scholarly PDFs, source corpora, and private operational records are excluded from this public packet. The source's informal sharpness sentence about universal covers is not needed: for empty or singleton omitted sets the universal covering surface is parabolic rather than the disc, so that sentence should not be repeated without a qualification.

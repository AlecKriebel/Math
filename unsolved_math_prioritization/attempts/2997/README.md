## Accepted scope

For a smooth closed connected oriented four-dimensional open book with c binding components and total binding genus G, c - G = 1 - b1(X) + b2(X)/2. In the simply connected case with b2(X)=2k, the number s of spherical binding components satisfies

s = k + 1 + sum over g_i >= 2 of (g_i - 1) >= k + 1.

This counts components rather than independent ambient homology classes. The bound is sharp on standard #_k(S2 x S2), including standard S4 when k=0.

For a simply connected X, smooth monodromy isotopic to the identity **relative to a boundary collar** gives exactly these standard connected sums. The proof uses explicit product pages and their smooth boundaries. The known sphere twist on S2 x I acts trivially on integral homology but gives CP2 # overline(CP2), so homological triviality does not replace the relative-isotopy hypothesis.

The unrestricted simply connected signature-zero existence question remains unresolved. No arbitrary exotic smoothing is classified, no essential-sphere or gauge-theoretic obstruction follows from the count alone, and no novelty is claimed. The cited general simplicial-volume and parallelizability counterexamples are nonsimply connected.

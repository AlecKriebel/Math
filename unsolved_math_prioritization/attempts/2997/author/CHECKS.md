# Author checks

## Mathematical checks performed

- Euler-characteristic gluing accounts for the mapping torus, binding cap, and common boundary.
- Oriented Poincare duality is used only for the closed oriented ambient manifold.
- The genus identity includes torus components with contribution zero and higher-genus components with negative contribution to c-G.
- Empty binding is explicitly excluded from the simply connected case by Euler characteristic.
- Identity-monodromy van Kampen handles disconnected boundary by separate cap attachments.
- Poincare-Lefschetz duality uses H_2(P,boundary P)=H^1(P), with integral coefficients.
- Capping the page invokes only the three-dimensional Poincare and Schoenflies theorems.
- The k=0 case is S^4; k=1 gives S^2 x S^2 and two spherical bindings.
- The sharpness construction uses an explicit product of a punctured S^3 with D^2, not an inference from equality of intersection forms.
- A known sphere-twist example checks the essential distinction between homologically trivial and isotopy-trivial monodromy.
- No homological essentialness of the counted binding spheres is assumed.
- The general nonsimply connected obstructions are separated from the simply connected question.

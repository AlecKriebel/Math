# Five substantive approaches

Date: 2026-10-05 UTC. Status: unresolved after 5/5 approaches. A search, normalization check, or repeated numerical run is not counted as a separate approach.

1. **Fourier spectral relaxation.** Diagonalized L+2I and derived the exact positive-resolvent criterion, its extremizers, and the Parseval bound H(A)≤a(1+a)/4. Proves the target for every real f whenever k(1+a)≤4, including every density at least 1/8. Obstruction: the mean-and-Parseval relaxation at a=1/32 permits q=165/128, so it loses essential indicator structure. Complete proof: PROOF.md §§1–2.
2. **Affine-subspace / coding approach.** Used Fourier support on the annihilator and systematic pivot coordinates. Proves the full target for every affine subspace in every dimension, with the exact subcube resolvent and the only extremal affine sets for that auxiliary bound. Obtains the necessary universal coefficient ceiling 2. Obstruction: general indicators do not have flat Fourier magnitudes on a linear code. Complete proof: §3.
3. **Entropy tensorization and binary coarse-graining.** Proved the two-point logarithmic-Sobolev inequality, entropy convexity and tensorization, and a binary relative-entropy estimate. Recovers the universal coefficient 2 ln2, with an explicit additive term, but does not reach 2. Obstruction: a fixed additive reserve cannot repair the logarithmic coefficient lost in this relaxation as density decreases. Complete proof: §4.
4. **Supported-function transfer and exterior minimization.** Checked the precise scope of the credited support-restricted theorem. Derived the Schur complement and exact minimizing exterior extension. The singleton example disproves discarding the exterior correction; the Q_4 parity example disproves absorbing that correction by 2I in operator order. These are rejected proof bridges, not target counterexamples. Complete derivations: §5.
5. **Kernel rearrangement and downset recursion.** Derived the rational distance kernel, proved coordinate compression increases its pair sum, and reduced the problem to downsets. Complete exact-arithmetic finite certification covers all sets for n≤4 and all downsets for n=5. Obstruction: no all-dimension inductive bound for the cross-kernel term is established. Complete proof and certification method: §6; retained code and results accompany this file.

No route produces a source-consistent counterexample or unrestricted proof. No result is claimed novel. The partial theorem for supported functions is credited to Samorodnitsky, not relabeled as a solution of the target.

# Limitations and potential misuse

1. **Unresolved original.** Do not label this solved, refuted, already solved, or a proof of the universal conjecture. Recommended outcome is unsolved 5/5.
2. **Integral coefficients.** Generic finite-dimensionality, finite generation, and characteristic-zero specialization do not imply R-torsion-freeness. Integer torsion requires a separate exclusion.
3. **Surfaces.** Essential spheres must be included in the intended interpretation. Atoroidal manifolds may contain higher-genus incompressible surfaces. Boundary-parallel surfaces are allowed; properly embedded surfaces with boundary are not the quantified closed surfaces.
4. **Manifold class.** The old source does not explicitly require compactness. Each result here states where compactness is needed. One-sided surfaces are not silently identified with two-sided surfaces.
5. **Source status.** Some inspected sources are preprints; full final journal bodies were not always available. Abstract claims exceeding the inspected theorem are not promoted to verified proofs. The Sept. 2026 revision of the nonreduced-character paper is identified explicitly.
6. **Fiber count.** One primary cyclic summand contributes one dimension to the corresponding fiber regardless of its exponent. The control suite exposes the difference from torsion length.
7. **No topology counterexample.** The abstract modules R/(A-2), R/(2,A-1), and the ideal (2,A-1) are diagnostic examples. No claim is made that they occur as the complete skein module of a manifold in the hypothesis.
8. **Finite computations.** The control suite tests finite algebraic cases and exact formulas. It cannot establish a universal topological implication or completeness of arbitrary handle-slide relations.
9. **Certificate preconditions.** The minor and normal-form criteria apply only to a full presentation. A bounded diagram search cannot establish its completeness by itself. The unit-minor condition is sufficient, not necessary.
10. **No freeness substitution.** Torsion-free and free are distinct over R. The original asks for torsion-freeness. Conditional freeness results in this packet are explicitly stronger sufficient criteria.
11. **Audit state.** The author packet is frozen for a fresh independent audit. No audit result, remote publication, or acceptance is implied by its existence.

# Primary-source verification

Status checked through 2026-10-04 UTC. A source reporting a theorem is distinguished from an independently reconstructed proof. Imported geometric results are not formally certified by the finite checker.

## Original target

[O] T. Ohtsuki (editor), *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002). Publisher PDF: https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf . Section 4.1, printed pages 446-447, PDF pages 74-75. The definition, diagram, coefficient ring, and Conjecture 4.3 were textually inspected; page 74 was also rendered and visually inspected. The notation is S_(2,infinity), R=Z[A±1], oriented M, unoriented framed links. Compactness and a separate one-sided-surface convention are not stated in that sentence. The adjacent sphere discussion prevents discarding essential spheres. The following page names the pair-of-pants times circle as a test case. The target web page https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/10400081 was inaccessible through the web reader; the primary source was retrieved directly.

[P] J. H. Przytycki, *Fundamentals of Kauffman bracket skein modules*, arXiv:math/9809113v1 (1998), later Kobe J. Math. 16 (1999), 45-66. https://arxiv.org/abs/math/9809113 . Inspected Definition 2.1, Proposition 2.2, Theorem 2.3, and the essential-sphere discussion. Proposition 2.2 supplies coefficient change, 3-handle invariance and handle-slide presentations; Theorem 2.3 lists product, lens-space, S^1 x S^2 and Whitehead calculations. The source's Whitehead example is torsion-free but not free, reinforcing why those properties must remain distinct in the noncompact problem.

## Modern status and coefficient checks

[DKS-small] R. Detcherry, E. Kalfagianni and A. S. Sikora, *Kauffman bracket skein modules of small 3-manifolds*, arXiv:2305.16188v3 (13 February 2025); Advances in Mathematics 467 (2025), 110169. https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.16188 . Inspected introduction, Sections 2-3 and 10, and the stated surgery formulas. The relevant conclusions distinguish P=Q[A±1] from K=Q(A). Tame P-modules plus reduced character scheme exclude (A+1)-primary torsion and compute generic rank. They do not automatically exclude other polynomial or integer torsion. Proposition 3.3 gives K-bases, not integral spanning sets. In the authored argument, fiber excess is correctly counted by cyclic blocks; no equality with the Q-dimension of the full primary torsion is imported. The author-hosted journal-PDF route returned HTML; the validated arXiv PDF was used. The final journal body was not compared.

[DKS-Seifert] R. Detcherry, E. Kalfagianni and A. S. Sikora, *Skein modules and character varieties of Seifert manifolds*, arXiv:2405.18557v2 (25 August 2025); Transactions of the AMS, Series B 12 (2025), 1264-1288. https://arxiv.org/abs/2405.18557 . Inspected introduction, Proposition 2.1, Section 4's finite-generation claims and Section 5.4's basis statement. Proposition 2.1 was also visually checked on PDF page 4. Theorem 1.2 proves equivalence involving finite generation/tameness for closed Seifert manifolds. Integral torsion-freeness is a separately listed property. Theorem 5.15 ends with a basis over K. No full integral torsion-freeness result is inferred from these statements.

[BD-torsion] G. Belletti and R. Detcherry, *On torsion in the Kauffman bracket skein module of 3-manifolds*, arXiv:2406.17454v1 (25 June 2024); IMRN 2025(21), rnaf333, published 5 November 2025. https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.17454 ; journal metadata/abstract: https://academic.oup.com/imrn/article/2025/21/rnaf333/8315094 . Inspected introduction, the dimension-jump argument, higher-genus examples, and Section 6. Theorem 1.5 in the inspected preprint proves that an essential closed surface in an orientable Seifert manifold produces torsion, the reverse direction from this task. The abstract advertises an equivalence; that broader claim is not adopted without a checked proof of the needed direction. Its attribution sentence also reverses the direction written in the original Ohtsuki/Kirby conjecture. Journal full text was purchase-restricted and not accessed. No conclusion about a correction in the final body is claimed.

[K-shared] E. Kalfagianni, *Torsion in skein modules and shared knot surgeries*, arXiv:2608.12668v1 (12 August 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.12668 . The introduction and theorem statements were inspected, together with the description of the Haken examples. The opening still presents the irreducible/no-two-sided-incompressible-surface freeness assertion as conjectural. Its new torsion examples explicitly contain such surfaces. Its hyperbolic surgery computations are over K. This provides recent status evidence, not proof that no later resolution could exist. Its displayed definition of tameness uses Z; this packet uses the rational-Laurent definition actually required by DKS and does not rely on that displayed definition.

[D-nonreduced] R. Detcherry, *Small Seifert 3-manifolds with non-reduced SL_2(C)-character scheme*, arXiv:2602.08760v2 (24 September 2026). https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.08760v2 . Inspected introduction and main statements. The revision reports correction of an order-two-deformation mistake. It determines multiplicities of exceptional abelian characters; this is a character-scheme result, not an integral skein torsion-freeness theorem. The packet neither equates nonreducedness with torsion nor imports its numerical multiplicity formulas.

[BD-effective] G. Belletti and R. Detcherry, *An effective proof of finiteness for Kauffman bracket skein modules*, arXiv:2507.02589v1 (3 July 2025). https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.02589 . Inspected the coefficient conventions, Definition 1.1, Theorem 1.2, Remarks 1.3-1.4, and corollaries. The boundary result localizes both A and the selected boundary curves. The source itself notes that this localization may make a nonzero skein module zero. It cannot supply the injectivity required in the present problem.

[FTFKB] M. Farajzadeh-Tehrani, C. Frohman and J. Kania-Bartoszynska, *The Kauffman bracket skein module at an irreducible representation*, arXiv:2402.17037v1 (26 February 2024); Quantum Topology, online first 28 June 2025, DOI 10.4171/QT/241. https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.17037 ; https://ems.press/journals/qt/articles/14298917 . The arXiv introduction and stated specialization/localization theorems were inspected, and the publisher abstract was screened. The final abstract has broader rational-homology-sphere hypotheses than v1. No uninspected final theorem is used as a premise. These are root-of-unity localization and generic-dimension results, not the requested universal integral assertion.

[Chen] H. Chen, *Torsion in Kauffman bracket skein module of a 4-strand Montesinos knot exterior*, arXiv:2311.01177; Journal of the London Mathematical Society 112(6) (2025), e70398. https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01177 ; https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms.70398 . Abstract screened only. Its stated target is Kirby's atoroidal question, which allows higher-genus closed surfaces. No proof detail or complete topological classification of those exteriors is imported here.

# Disconnected subsurface preimages under finite surface covers

**Unsolved in general after two substantive attempts.** The exact four-holed- and three-holed-sphere questions are not settled by this package.

[PARTIAL.md](PARTIAL.md) proves sufficient conditions involving cyclic intermediate covers, generator counts, and a base-genus bound in terms of the finite monodromy image. It also gives an explicit S3 example showing why passing to a regular refinement cannot automatically solve the original irregular-cover problem. These are partial results with no novelty claim.

[SOURCE_AUDIT.md](SOURCE_AUDIT.md) records the original scope, prior-attempt checks, related literature, and the limitation on a universal trivial-monodromy strategy. The actual model was gpt-6-astra with xhigh reasoning. A [separate adversarial AI review](review/REVIEW.md) passed the exact frozen partial note without required corrections. The general problem remains unresolved and novelty is unconfirmed.

Run `python check_monodromy.py` from this directory for 31 exact checks of the S3 negative control and four cyclic examples. The checks do not enumerate all subsurfaces or prove either universal statement. Source records, checksums, readiness, and the two-turn ledger preserve provenance.

# Exponential-sum magnitudes: complete classification through degree three

Problem 30005275 / OWR-11695860-014. The unrestricted-degree target remains **OPEN in both prime regimes**.

For reduced degrees at most three over F_p and p>=11, [Theorem A](PROOF.md) gives a complete classification, including constants, linears, quadratics, every cubic degeneracy and the linear/pure-cubic permutation overlap. For cubics the relation is exactly g(X)=f(uX+v)+w with u nonzero. Equality at one nonzero frequency already implies equality at every nonzero frequency.

For rational polynomials in these degrees, [Theorem B](PROOF.md) classifies equality at every sufficiently large prime. In the cubic case u,v,w are rational. Pure-cubic descent uses the classical Chebotarev density theorem. One prime or merely infinitely many primes does not justify that rational conclusion.

The complete proof and all substantive audit findings are preserved. The independent audit requires no mathematical correction. Its source-description correction is applied in [SOURCE_REVIEW.md](SOURCE_REVIEW.md): arXiv-v1 Definition 1.6 also imposes simple roots of f and degree d-1 for f', besides squarefree f' and distinct critical values. That source's tension with arbitrary output translation is documented and is not a dependency of Theorems A or B.

The proof retains all scoped obstructions. X^2 and X^6 agree for arbitrarily large primes 2 modulo 3 without complex affine equivalence, but not at all sufficiently large primes. X^4 and -4X^4 agree at every odd prime without rational input affine equivalence when output scaling is restricted to +/-1; complex affine equivalence does hold. The p=5 complementary-fiber cubic example is a small-prime boundary control. The bound p>=11 is sufficient, with no sharpness claim.

Credit: Arnab Mitra, Omer Offen and Eitan Sayag, *Klyachko Models for Ladder Representations*, Documenta Mathematica 22 (2017), 611–657, Theorem 10.3 / Theorem 1.2, [DOI 10.4171/DM/574](https://doi.org/10.4171/DM/574).

The original increasing-order two-segment essentially Speh pairs become adjacent nu-translate pairs in the decreasing-order Langlands multisegment. Mitra's Q is the later paper's L, not Z. The published proof establishes sufficiency by descending a standard-module invariant functional past the Lapid–Minguez kernel, and necessity by the proved set case of the geometric-lemma combinatorics. The empty-intersection and zero-kernel-term cases have been checked explicitly.

Accepted scope: all such smooth complex ladders over a nonarchimedean local field of characteristic zero, all ranks, arbitrary cuspidal degree, with no unitarity or proper-ladder requirement. Residue characteristic 2 is included.

# Sources inspected for the independent audit

1. Ahmed Laghribi, joint with Roberto Aravire and Manuel O'Ryan, *Differential and Quadratic Forms in Characteristic Two*, in *Algebraic Cobordism and Projective Homogeneous Varieties*, Oberwolfach Report 5/2016, pp. 248–251, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2016/5. The contribution was inspected in extracted text; the function-field convention and Question 2 were independently rendered and visually checked on PDF pages 34 and 37. [Official PDF](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46609)

2. Roberto Aravire, Ahmed Laghribi, Manuel O'Ryan, *Graded Witt kernels of the compositum of multiquadratic extensions with the function fields of Pfister forms*, Journal of Algebra 449 (2016), 635–659. Theorem statements and the affine-quadric convention were checked in text and independently rendered manuscript pages 3–4. [Author manuscript](https://laghribi.perso.math.cnrs.fr/Witt-kernels%28new-version%29-2.pdf)

3. Roberto Aravire, Ahmed Laghribi, Manuel O'Ryan, *Cohomological kernels of mixed extensions in characteristic 2*, Journal of Algebra 542 (2020), 249–276, DOI 10.1016/j.jalgebra.2019.09.012. The inspected author manuscript is dated 15 August 2019. Its introduction and Definition 1.3/Theorem 1.4 were checked, including independent visual inspection of pages 3–4. It supplies literature context; the audit does not use Theorem 1.4 as a substitute for three-summand nonmembership. [Author manuscript](https://laghribi.perso.math.cnrs.fr/cohom-kernels.pdf)

4. Adam Chapman and Anne Quéguiner-Mathieu, *Minimal quadratic forms for the function field of a conic in characteristic 2*, arXiv:2111.15251. Page 3 was checked for the Kato-symbol/Brauer correspondence, with the preceding symbol conventions on page 2. This is a targeted dependency check, not a review of the paper's main theorem. [PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2111.15251)

5. J. S. Milne, *Class Field Theory*, Chapter IV, Theorem 3.14, printed p. 137, PDF page 146 in the version rendered by the web reader. The relative Brauer-group theorem and adjacent crossed-product construction were directly checked through the author's web-accessible PDF text. A byte download returned HTTP 406 and a separate screenshot request was unavailable; no local PDF hash or visual inspection is claimed for this reference. [Author PDF](https://www.jmilne.org/math/CourseNotes/CFT.pdf)

6. John Voight, *Quaternion Algebras*, Graduate Texts in Mathematics 288 (2021), Chapter 6, Theorem 6.4.11. The publisher's full HTML theorem explicitly gives equivalence of splitting and the characteristic-two norm condition. It was directly inspected. [Publisher chapter](https://link.springer.com/chapter/10.1007/978-3-030-56694-4_6)

7. Laghribi's current publication list independently confirms the 2016 and 2020 bibliographic records, including volume 542 for the mixed-kernel paper. A freshly downloaded publisher-deposited Crossref record agrees. The separate web-reader Crossref request failed, but the direct HTTP download succeeded and its bytes matched the supplied record. [Author list](https://laghribi.perso.math.cnrs.fr/research.html), [Crossref record](https://api.crossref.org/works/10.1016/j.jalgebra.2019.09.012)

# Substantive author turn 4

This turn tested the axioms-only route to symmetry. It constructs a finite-rank family satisfying every axiom in Benson's Definition1.1.1, even its strengthened closed condition, whose complex algebra is semisimple but whose Banach involution is nonsymmetric. All positive-element gamma duality identities still hold. The family is explicitly proved not to be an actual finite-group Green ring: its indecomposable dimension list omits the necessary first syzygy of the nonprojective trivial module.

This is a counterexample to a stronger abstract assertion and an obstruction to a proof route, not to the original finite-group problem. The checker passes 47,981 exact finite assertions over 98 parameter choices. No priority claim is made. Author count: 4/5.

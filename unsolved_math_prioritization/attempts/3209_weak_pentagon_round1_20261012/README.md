## What is established

- Full symbolic proofs give one-direction normalization fixing every pentagon edge, arbitrary-boundary extensions for subdivided claws and adjacent double claws, and an elementary maximum-average-degree bound. The bound mad < 19/8 is strictly subsumed by the published triangle-free mad < 12/5 theorem with target C5 and is not a frontier improvement.
- Precise square-boundary and locally injective-coloring obstructions explain why stronger naive reductions fail. They do not refute the original conjecture.
- An explicit authored 14-vertex triangle-free cubic graph has a single-vertex-local-maximum cut whose complement cannot be a prescribed canonical Clebsch color class. Its hand proof is included. Its global maximum cut size is 18, and both normalized global maximum cuts have explicit positive witnesses.
- All 106,284 supplied catalog records and all 330,886 normalized global-maximum-cut pairs through order 20 passed independent exact verification. Every local-maximum cut in the supplied catalogs through order 12 also extends: 213 pairs on 31 records. Completeness of those catalogs up to isomorphism remains an external provider premise. The global-maximum strengthening at all orders is unresolved.

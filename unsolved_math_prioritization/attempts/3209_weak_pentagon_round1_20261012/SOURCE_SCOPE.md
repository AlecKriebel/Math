# Weak pentagon: checked literature scope

Inspection date: 2026-10-12 (UTC). Target: every finite simple triangle-free cubic graph maps homomorphically to the Clebsch graph PQ4; equivalently, its edges admit five classes with bipartite complements.

## Conclusion and status discipline

No full resolution of that target was established by this bounded literature check. The original problem page and DeVos–Šámal explicitly formulate it as a conjecture. The positive results inspected below retain additional hypotheses. Their existence, and search silence, do not establish a globally complete October 2026 literature frontier. Recommended status: **unresolved in this work; full published resolution not verified**. Do not turn an old source's conjecture label or a crawler timestamp into a current-status theorem.

## Verified positive results

1. **High-girth subcubic graphs.** DeVos–Šámal, arXiv:math/0602580v2, revised 23 October 2009, Theorem 1.3, p. 3: maximum degree at most three and girth at least 17 imply a PQ4 homomorphism, with a linear-time construction. Proposition 1.1, p. 2 gives the cut-complement/projective-cube equivalences. Conjecture 1.4, p. 3 is exactly the unrestricted triangle-free cubic target. Section 2 reduces the result to the computer-assisted Lemma 2.4 on the tree 2T9; the algorithm's constants are described as impractically large. Statement, reduction, and dependency were inspected; the author program/certificate was not rerun or independently audited. The journal publication is J. Graph Theory 66(3), 241–259 (2011), first published 28 January 2011, DOI [10.1002/jgt.20580](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/abs/10.1002/jgt.20580). Numbering/page claims here belong to [arXiv v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0602580v2).

2. **Triangle-free K5-minor-free graphs, without a degree bound.** Naserasr–Nigussie–Škrekovski prove this class maps to Clebsch: Theorem 16, p. 10 in the [2005 institutional preprint](https://iti.mff.cuni.cz/series/2005/268.pdf). Its stronger mixed-graph Theorem 20, p. 15 is proved by gluing Wagner pieces using Lemma 17, Theorem 18 and Lemma 19. H5 means PQ4 here. The [publisher's 2009 abstract](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0012365X09002581) independently confirms the claimed class; publication is Discrete Mathematics 309(18), 5789–5798, 28 September 2009, DOI 10.1016/j.disc.2009.04.032. Journal theorem numbering was not checked against a full journal PDF. Preprint statement and gluing/dependency structure were inspected, not the complete proof. This contains the planar case but is not the class of all cubic graphs.

3. **Sparse triangle-free graphs have the stronger C5 target.** Borodin–Hartke–Ivanova–Kostochka–West, [Circular (5,2)-coloring of sparse graphs](https://www.mathnet.ru/semr116), Siberian Electronic Mathematical Reports 5 (2008), 417–426: Theorem 5, printed p. 418, proves triangle-free mad(G)<12/5 implies G→C5. There is no maximum-degree assumption. Published 29 October 2008; received 14 August 2008. The [publisher PDF](https://www.mathnet.ru/php/getFT.phtml?jrnid=semr&paperid=116&what=fullt) was read through the web PDF text service. The separately downloaded [author preprint](https://www.dwest.web.illinois.edu/pubs/olegnew.pdf), dated 22 May 2007, gives the same result as Theorem 1.1, p. 2. Statements and elementary preliminaries were inspected, not the full discharging argument. Because C5 embeds in Clebsch and 19/8<12/5, a mad<19/8 Clebsch corollary is strictly subsumed; it is not a new frontier result. The sharpness example for the C5 target does not imply sharpness for Clebsch.

4. **Finer C5-critical density.** Dvořák–Postle, [arXiv:1411.6668v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.6668v1), 24 November 2014, Theorem 1, p. 2: every C5-critical graph other than C3 satisfies 4e≥5v−2. Theorem 6, p. 5 strengthens this to 4e≥5v−1 outside two additional explicitly described ten-vertex exceptions E1,E2. Corollary 3 covers planar or projective-planar girth≥10; Corollary 4 covers planar odd-girth≥11. Journal: Combinatorica 37, 863–886 (2017), [DOI 10.1007/s00493-016-3356-3](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00493-016-3356-3), online 17 October 2016. Statements inspected; complete proof not audited. The additive density bound must not be silently replaced by a uniform mad<5/2 theorem.

## Recent papers: provenance and scope

5. **A modern direct proof of the planar case.** Naserasr–Yu, [On the packing number of antibalanced signed simple planar graphs of negative girth at least 5](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10878-023-01103-9), J. Combinatorial Optimization 47, article 9 (2024), published 12 February 2024, accepted 26 December 2023. In the [author manuscript](https://www.irif.fr/~reza/pdfs/papers/NY24.pdf), Theorem 7, p. 5 gives packing number at least five under exactly the title hypotheses. Taking the all-negative signature recovers triangle-free planar→Clebsch. The introduction explains that Guenin's earlier proof was unpublished; the new proof uses the girth-four result of Naserasr–Yu (2023) and the four-color theorem. Concluding remarks, p. 22 explicitly reaffirm the K5-minor-free extension from the 2009 paper, while saying the planar discharging method does not directly extend. Theorem and dependencies checked; full proof not audited.

6. **Chen–Naserasr–Sarti is not a cubic resolution.** [Signed Projective Cubes, a Homomorphism Point of View](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/jgt.70046), J. Graph Theory 113(1), 38–56 (September 2026), first published 21 April 2026; accepted 3 April 2026; revised 7 August 2025. Publisher full HTML and the [author PDF](https://sarti.pages.math.cnrs.fr/home/SPC2025.pdf) were inspected. Theorem 28 is conditional on signed planar homomorphism bounds. Corollary 30 bounds planar graphs of odd girth at least seven by a particular twelve-vertex triangle-free graph K3*C4; the article identifies K4*C4 with PQ4. Thus this is a planar refinement, not arbitrary triangle-free cubic coverage. Section 7.2 treats extensions to signed minor-closed classes conditionally. The paper's arXiv entry [2406.10814v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.10814v1) remains the older 16 June 2024 version; do not conflate it with the revised publisher text. Relevant statements/hypotheses checked, not a complete proof audit.

## Implications for the present continuation

- Credit the sparse 19/8 consequence as an elementary proof of a known subclass. Its value may be self-contained extension machinery, not an improved threshold.
- Boundary-extension lemmas can be assessed independently, but literature novelty has not been established for them.
- A single 4/5-size cut is a necessary consequence, not a sufficient certificate for the five-class target.
- The additional hypotheses in the positive classes above must remain explicit in queue, theorem, audit and acceptance text.
- All downloaded source documents and extracted text are retained only as inspection materials; no copied source material was published.

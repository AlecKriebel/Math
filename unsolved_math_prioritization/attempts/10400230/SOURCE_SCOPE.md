# 10400230 / AMR-103-0230: determinants of prime alternating achiral knots

## Exact target

Given an odd positive integer n, other than 1, 9 and 49, admitting a representation n=a²+b² with integers a,b, construct a **prime alternating achiral knot** of determinant n, or give a rigorous counterexample. All three knot properties are required simultaneously. The unknot is not prime. Here achiral means equivalent to its mirror image; no choice of positive versus negative amphicheirality is imposed. The determinant is |Δ_K(−1)|.

Primary source: T. Ohtsuki (editor), *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002), Problem 12.25 and its complete remark on printed p. 541, PDF p. 169. The problem is due to A. Stoimenow. [Publisher PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf). The exact contribution and its boundaries were read and visually checked. The imported title “Problem 12.25 — (A.” is truncated; its actual mathematical statement agrees with the primary source. The source's reference [374] is the preprint of the subsequent published paper below.

## Existing results must be credited

A. Stoimenow, *Square numbers, spanning trees and invariants of achiral knots*, Communications in Analysis and Geometry 13(3) (2005), 591–631, DOI 10.4310/CAG.2005.v13.n3.a5. [Publisher PDF](https://intlpress.com/site/pub/files/_fulltext/journals/cag/2005/0013/0003/CAG-2005-0013-0003-a005.pdf), [arXiv record](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0003172).

Sections 4.1–4.2 and the relevant graph correspondence in Section 6.1 were read. Conjecture 4.20 and Theorem 4.21 on printed p. 606 (PDF p. 16) were visually checked; text extraction drops some negation signs and must not override the page image.

- Theorem 4.1 characterizes determinants of achiral knots by odd sums of two squares. Either primeness or alternation separately can be arranged. This is not the simultaneous target.
- Theorem 4.2 gives an achiral rational-knot realization when the two summands have coprime square roots. Those nontrivial rational knots are prime and alternating. This entire case is prior work.
- Proposition 4.8 excludes 9 and 49; determinant 1 forces the alternating unknot.
- Conjecture 4.20 is the exact remaining target, not a theorem. Its reported finite verification through n=2000 is not a universal proof.
- Theorem 4.21 proves the entire odd-perfect-square case other than 1,9,49. The construction and exceptional low parameters are prior work.
- Claim 4.25 obstructs one particular three-tangle arborescent template for infinitely many n=5p², p≡3 mod4 prime. It is explicitly not an original-question counterexample: other templates realize the small cases.
- Planar self-dual graphs with no cut vertex and odd spanning-tree count provide prime alternating achiral knots by the checkerboard construction. The paper gives its graph formulation in Proposition 6.6 and Conjecture 6.12. Any use of this correspondence must preserve the cut-vertex condition and the embedding/symmetry details; counting spanning trees alone is insufficient.

The 2001 author problem list repeats the target as Problem 8, p. 2: [author copy](https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/12pb.pdf). The 2007 continuation *Square numbers and polynomial invariants of achiral knots* concerns leading polynomial coefficients, not a full realization theorem for these determinants: [author manuscript](https://stoimenov.net/stoimeno/homepage/papers/achirpol.pdf). Its introduction and principal theorem scopes were inspected.

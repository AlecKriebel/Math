# Result: scoped realizations; the original conjecture is unresolved

**Original status: unresolved,5/5 substantive author turns.** Ohtsuki Problem12.25 asks for a prime alternating achiral knot of every odd determinant that is a sum of two squares, except1,9,49. This packet does not settle that full assertion.

## Strongest constructive theorem

For every odd prime p and every integer e≥0, there exists a prime alternating achiral knot of determinant

    5p^{2e}.

The proof constructs explicitly paired plane-dual networks, checks the no-cut-vertex condition, evaluates exact tree/forest counts and uses norm-Euclidean arithmetic. Turn3 proves the non-rational prime ray5p² for p≡3mod4. Turn4 supplies fourth-power closure and the credited primitive case, completing this prime-power statement. No infinitude of prime values of an unproved quadratic pattern is assumed.

Other scoped results:

- Explicit family9(52k²+28k+5), k≥0, outside the perfect-square and primitive rational cases
- Exact classification of5p² in the four-integer-parameter wheel family, showing that family alone misses infinitely many valid inputs; rational substitutions subsequently overcome all of those prime-ray misses
- Fourth-power extension of the primitive realization class; in particular all odd sums of two squares whose3mod4 prime exponents are divisible by4, with the prior perfect-square theorem used when the primitive core is1
- No terminal-proper balanced unit network of any graph type can have squarefree common tree/forest count>1. This limits a multiplication strategy; it does not exclude a knot of the corresponding determinant

## Sharp gap

Arbitrary primitive norm cores with mixed squarefree bad-prime factors remain unconstructed. No multiplication theorem for arbitrary prime alternating achiral knots has been proved. Connected sum or attaching a cyclic graph block would preserve determinant multiplication but lose primeness, and is explicitly excluded.

# Dependencies and the meaning of the core

For the original distinct maximum-cardinality clique family M, the high-incidence core X consists exactly of vertices occurring in at least three members of M. Incidences are never recomputed from G[X], a residual formula, or inclusionwise maximal cliques. Every prescribed set P must contain X for the projection theorem. Parallel dual edges must be retained; incidence-one marks poison their entire component; repeated boundary vertices on opposite sides discard the signed component.

The complete fifth-pass projection proof uses the accepted fourth-pass [exact propagation and signed-tree theorem](../3251_exact_core_20261012/EXACT_CORE_PROOFS.md), its [complete audit](../3251_exact_core_20261012/EXACT_CORE_AUDIT.md), and the explicit [complexity correction](COMPLEXITY_CORRECTION.md). The fourth-pass [chordal family](../3251_exact_core_20261012/FAMILY_PROOFS.md) and [audit](../3251_exact_core_20261012/FAMILY_AUDIT.md) separately show that a valid division of the induced core need not extend. The final chordal realizations and repeated-leaf bad prescriptions are different: their bad prescriptions are not core divisions.

The structural arguments use the third-pass [high-incidence-core theorem](../3251_boundary_core_20261012/HIGH_INCIDENCE_CORE_PROOFS.md), [boundary and mixed-constraint proof](../3251_boundary_core_20261012/BOUNDARY_EXTENSION_PROOFS.md), [complete boundary/core audit](../3251_boundary_core_20261012/BOUNDARY_CORE_AUDIT.md), and the earlier [sharp-order proof](../3251_boundary_core_20261012/SHARP_ORDER_PROOFS.md) and [audit](../3251_boundary_core_20261012/SHARP_ORDER_AUDIT.md). The initial [maximum-clique audit](../3251_divisibility_partials_20261012/HYPERGRAPH_AUDIT.md) records the CRST source check. Final improvements rely explicitly on Gallai and CRST; no fresh complete proof audit of either external theorem is asserted.

The repeated-leaf supplement uses the separately accepted final projection theorem. The structural proof is independent of the companion realization argument. Complete originals and their fixed dependency inventories were checked; the public manifest identifies only the versions actually distributed here.

The input-list bounds assume the original maximum-clique list is already supplied. Constructing it from arbitrary graph input is not claimed polynomial. Core-division extension, compatible clique-cutset traces, and inheritance of odd-hole-freeness by the maximum-clique shadow are not assumptions or conclusions of this edition.

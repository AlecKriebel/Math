## Folkman lower bound

Aleksandar Bikov and Nedyalko Nenov, “Lower bounding the Folkman numbers F_v(a_1,...,a_s;m-1),” Annual of Sofia University, Faculty of Mathematics and Informatics 104 (2017), 39–53. The directly inspected primary journal [PDF](https://www.fmi.uni-sofia.bg/sites/default/files/biblio/fulltext/104-039-053.pdf), Theorem 5.2, proves F_v(4,4;5)>=19. The [arXiv v1 PDF](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1711.01535v1) was identified separately. Definitions, the theorem and proof outline, Algorithm 3.5 and Table 5 were inspected. The large external graph enumeration was not reproduced or independently certified. The exact historical bound is imported; neither this numerical lower bound nor the computational proof is claimed as ours.

Indivisibility at clique number k is exactly the vertex-Ramsey relation G -> (K_k,K_k)^v with no K_(k+1). Thus the source theorem gives n>=19 for every k=4 indivisible graph. The independently derived n>=4k-3 structural bound must not be described as a new or best-known Folkman numerical bound.

The same primary source credits Xu and Shao, “On the lower bound for F_v(k,k;k+1) and F_e(3,4;5),” Utilitas Mathematica 81 (2010), 187–192, with F_v(p,p;p+1)>=4p-1. The original proof and complete parameter scope were not inspected, so that assertion is recorded as prior credit and is not an independently checked theorem imported by this edition.

## Critical graph theorem

Gallai's vertex-critical theorem states that a vertex-critical graph with connected complement has n>=2 chi-1. The convention and non-strict inequality were checked in Matej Stehlík's author-hosted lecture [“Gallai's theorem on colour-critical graphs and related results”](https://oc.g-scop.grenoble-inp.fr/fichiers/stehlik.pdf), 18 September 2009, PDF pages 2 and 10. Local text extraction resolved a glyph misreading in one web extraction. Stehlík's primary paper [“Critical graphs with connected complements”](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0095-8956(03)00069-8), JCTB 89 (2003), 189–194, has a publisher abstract stating a strengthening. Gallai is an external dependency, not a freshly re-audited proof or new claim.

## Odd hole free coloring and earlier context

Chudnovsky, Robertson, Seymour and Thomas (CRST), [“K4-free graphs with no odd holes”](https://thomas.math.gatech.edu/PAP/k4free.pdf), JCTB 100 (2010), 313–331, Theorem 1.3, supplies four-colorability. The author PDF is dated 5 October 2001 and revised 5 August 2009. The source statement was checked earlier; its full proof is not re-audited here. It is needed for the odd-hole-free critical-factor classification and core consequences. The known target range omega<=3 follows by grouping four proper colors at omega=3 and bipartiteness at omega=2.

Earlier context retained in the metadata comprises Chudnovsky and Sivaraman's [“Perfect divisibility and 2-divisibility”](https://web.math.princeton.edu/~mchudnov/Perfect2div.pdf); Hu, Xu and Zhuang's [“Some properties of minimally nonperfectly divisible graphs”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.01967v2), inspected as a preprint; Hoàng's [“On the structure of perfectly divisible graphs”](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12660v2), whose Conjecture 4.7 records unrestricted clique-cutset exclusion as open in that source; and He, Shi, Wu and Yao's [specialized-class paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.09549v1). These do not solve the unrestricted target or authorize unrestricted cutset gluing. The [earlier complete prior-credit audit](../3251_divisibility_partials_20261012/PRIOR_CREDIT_AUDIT.md) supplies the detailed distinction.

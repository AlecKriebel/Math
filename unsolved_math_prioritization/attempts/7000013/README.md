# 7000013: a triangulated nonconvex counterexample

**Candidate after one substantive turn; independent source-category and mathematical review pending.**

The finite example consists of a box with a raised box moved across a planar face. A compactly supported area-preserving PL map supplies530 corresponding nondegenerate triangular faces with exactly equal oriented area vectors. Both surfaces are embedded closed spheres. Exact centroid geometry proves they are not congruent.

Coplanar adjacent triangles are essential. The candidate answers the broad triangulated-surface convention, supported by standard primary definitions, while Ghomi's printed problem does not define faces or impose a nonflatness restriction. A maximal-facet/nonflat reinterpretation is explicitly left separate. No historical novelty is claimed.

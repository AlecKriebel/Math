2. **Global radial harmonic solution:** R. Mazzeo, J. Swoboda, H. Weiss and F. Witt, *Ends of the moduli space of Higgs bundles*, MPIM preprint 2014(40), June 3, 2014. Full primary PDF https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/2649/1/preprint_2014_40.pdf . Section 3.2, printed pages 14–16 (the PDF includes four preliminary cover pages), equations (20)–(28), Lemma 3.3 and Corollary 3.4: radial ODE, exact scaling, complete differentiated asymptotics, smooth extension and decay. Section 3.3, printed pages 16–17, Proposition 3.5: the holomorphic/unitary frame conversion. The input is explicitly classical, with its Painleve solution and earlier references credited in that paper. We use the full primary proof and formulas, not a numerical fit.

3. **All-order recursion/oper comparison:** O. Dumitrescu and M. Mulase, *Interplay between opers, quantum curves, WKB analysis, and Higgs bundles*, SIGMA 17 (2021), 036, 53 pages. Full journal-layout author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.00511v3 (July 2, 2021); journal landing page https://sigma-journal.com/2021/036/ . Direct final-PDF requests returned 406; the proof was read in the pinned complete arXiv version. Remark 5.7, printed page 37: residue/PDE recursion distinction in general. Theorem 6.1 and proof, pages 37–43: normalized rank-two WKB comparison. Sections 7.1–7.2, pages 45–49: actual Airy example, equation (7.9), normalized WKB (7.11)–(7.18), normalization parametrization (7.19), and explicit residue/PDE equivalence near (7.21). The paper's coordinate s has x=4/s^2,y=-2/s; our t=-2/s. Its primitive base s=0 is our t=infinity. The negative WKB sheet is the decaying Airy solution. The all-order theorem is credited, not inferred from finitely many coefficient checks.

4. **Wild polynomial category and warning about the stronger limit:** E. Dumas and A. Neitzke, *Opers and nonabelian Hodge: numerical studies*, complete author version https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00503v3 (February 3, 2025), corresponding to the Experimental Mathematics article. Section 2.4, pages 6–7, equations (2.8)–(2.12): polynomial Higgs field, self-duality PDE, compatible logarithmic growth at infinity and flat family. Section 2.5: Stokes data are end data of connections, not ordinary monodromy on the simply connected plane. Section 2.9, particularly equation (2.24): the expected polynomial conformal limit and consequences for Stokes invariants; their numerical investigations are not a general proof. The scalar PDE and growth definitions are used here; their conjectural limit is not used as an input.

5. **Compact comparison excluded as a wild proof:** Dumitrescu et al., *Opers versus nonabelian Hodge*, full original version https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.02172v1 . This source explains the broader context but is not used to assert a wild Airy theorem.

## What the sources and new deduction each do

MSWW supplies the actual harmonic metric and all needed smooth end estimates. The exact rescaling in the fixed holomorphic frame yields the compact connection limit proved in this attempt. DM supplies the complete all-order EO/PDE/WKB Airy theorem; elementary elimination identifies its operator with that limit. The statement is a concrete worked example in the requested relation, with every analytic and formal object specified. Historical novelty and a stronger moduli-space limit are outside the claim.

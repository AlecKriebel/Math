# Source scope and reading receipt

- Original: [AIM workshop, 2015](https://aimath.org/pastworkshops/spectralhiggs.html), with the exact problem recovered from the pinned [AimPL archive](https://web.archive.org/web/20260116071517id_/http://aimpl.org/spectralhiggs/1/). The record identifier ending `3072310` contains Boalch's Problem 1.28; it asks for a relationship in at least one example. The primary page includes no attached strengthening to Stokes-coordinate convergence. The live endpoint's availability failure is not hidden.
- [MSWW complete preprint](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/2649/1/preprint_2014_40.pdf), Section 3.2, equations (20)–(28), Lemma 3.3 and Corollary 3.4, and Section 3.3/Proposition 3.5. These supply the actual global radial solution, differentiated expansions and frame conversion. The small-R limit is checked using those formulas in the fixed holomorphic frame.
- [Dumitrescu–Mulase journal-layout author text](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.00511v3), *Interplay between opers, quantum curves, WKB analysis, and Higgs bundles*, SIGMA 17 (2021), 036. Remark 5.7 limits the general EO/PDE equivalence. Theorem 6.1 and Section 7 give the specifically normalized Airy all-order result, including the coordinate s, primitive base and scalar equation. The supplied arXiv v3 is the complete reading copy; no claim of retrieving the blocked publisher PDF is made.
- [Dumas–Neitzke](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2007.00503v3), Sections 2.2–2.5 and 2.9. Their scalar PDE, growth and flat-family definitions identify the polynomial wild category. Their general polynomial conformal-limit expectation is not an input to this proof. Their distinction between trivial ordinary monodromy on C and Stokes data at infinity remains essential.
- [Opers versus nonabelian Hodge, original version](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1607.02172v1), Section 1.5. The compact theorem does not prove the meromorphic extension and is not used as if it did.
- The older DM lectures, Hohl–Jakob and Mochizuki reading copies were hash-checked and treated as context, as the author says. The review does not rely on an unproved step from compact convergence to end-data convergence, or need to reprove a general wild correspondence from those sources.

All mathematical identifications are made in the displayed affine holomorphic frame or as meromorphic data with the given growth filtration. No equality of incompatible compactification lattices is assumed. The proof answers the literal worked-example request at this scope; it does not answer a stronger question about a full family of wild character-variety coordinates.

Full PDFs, extracted texts, archived HTML and reading images are excluded from the portable review. All hashes and exact locations are bound in HASH_CHECK.json and the author's source manifests. No outside source executable or external communication was used.

## Source distinctions controlling this attempt

- Dumitrescu–Mulase2021 gives the rank-two oper/PDE-recursion/WKB identification. For higher-genus spectral curves, its Remark5.7 explicitly says the residue and PDE recursions are not generally equivalent. A generic compact example cannot simply identify them. The Airy genus-zero example in Section7 does give their equivalence with fixed primitives and normalizations.
- The compact2016 oper/nonabelian-Hodge theorem fixes a Fuchsian projective structure and a point of the Hitchin section. Section1.5 excludes the meromorphic extension. It cannot be cited as a wild Airy theorem.
- The classical Mazzeo–Swoboda–Weiss–Witt fiducial solution is a global radial harmonic-bundle model on the plane, with a simple spectral zero and controlled asymptotics. Its exact scaling offers a direct route to an Airy conformal limit, which is being examined as author turn1.
- Formal WKB equality alone does not choose analytic sectorial solutions. Compact convergence of connections must not be promoted to convergence of Stokes data at the irregular end.

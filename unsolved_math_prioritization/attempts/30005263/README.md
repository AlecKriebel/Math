# Gaussian Lipschitz Brenier map: credited preprint proof audit

Problem 30005263 / OWR-11695859-006.

The exact original Gaussian target has a checked proof in Maja Gwóźdź,
Caffarelli Estimates under Lipschitz Perturbations, arXiv:2609.04052v1,
September 2026. With B=-f, its dimension-free Lipschitz bound applies to
every globally Lipschitz f for which e^f times standard Gaussian is a
probability measure, in every finite dimension. The map is the quadratic-cost
Brenier map and has a globally Lipschitz representative.
[Credited preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.04052).

This is a source-credit audit, not a claim of a new proof, novelty or priority.
The original conjecture is credited to Max Fathi, in joint work with
Dan Mikulincer and Yair Shenfeld, Oberwolfach Report 49/2022, Conjecture 1.
The source remains a September 2026 preprint: journal acceptance and peer
review are not established by the inspected public metadata.

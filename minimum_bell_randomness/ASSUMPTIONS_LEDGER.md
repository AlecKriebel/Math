# Assumptions ledger

- Local Hilbert spaces are arbitrary finite-dimensional complex Hilbert
  spaces unless a theorem explicitly states a commuting-operator extension.
- Eve holds an arbitrary purifying quantum system.
- Every physical measurement is projective with \(d\) labeled outcomes; zero
  projectors are allowed.
- Certification is from one real linear Bell score unless a result explicitly
  states that several statistics are fixed.
- Observed uniformity is not identified with privacy. The target conclusion
  is the operator equality
  \(\sigma_E^{ab}=\rho_E/d^2\).
- Equality statements are initially on the support of the tested state, not
  automatically on unused local sectors.
- Direct sums, classical flags, complex conjugation, transposition, and
  outcome relabelings remain in scope for every falsification audit.
- Literature statements supplied in the research prompt are context, not
  mathematical evidence. Core derivations are reconstructed independently
  before any targeted priority audit.

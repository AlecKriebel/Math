## Independently reconstructed argument

1. Let `X = Spec(C)` be smooth and integral. To prove `Pic(C) → Pic(C[t])` is onto, identify Picard groups with class groups because both schemes are regular. After localizing by all nonzero elements of C, the generic-fiber ring is the PID `K[t]`; subtracting a rational principal divisor removes all horizontal prime-divisor components. Every remaining height-one prime P has a nonzero contraction to C. Choose nonzero `c ∈ P ∩ C` and a prime q minimal over `(c)` with `q ⊆ P ∩ C`. Such q exists by localizing at `P ∩ C` and contracting a minimal prime over c. Principal ideal theorem and integrality give `ht(q) = 1`. Since `q[t]` is a nonzero prime contained in height-one P, equality follows. Localizing `C_q[t]` at `q[t]` leaves its maximal ideal generated by the original DVR uniformizer, so the pullback divisor has multiplicity one. Thus the residual divisor is pulled back from C. Evaluation at zero is a left inverse and proves injectivity.

2. Both `α*L` and `π*L` restrict to L at zero. The Picard isomorphism therefore supplies an isomorphism between them. Its restriction at zero is an automorphism of L, hence multiplication by a unit of C. Precomposing by that automorphism's inverse, pulled back along π, gives the required normalized isomorphism.

3. The ratio of two such normalized isomorphisms is an automorphism of a line bundle on `X × A¹`, hence an element of `C[t]^*`. Since C is a domain, degree additivity proves `C[t]^* = C^*`. Evaluation at zero of the ratio is the unit 1 on all of X, so the ratio is exactly 1. This remains true if C itself has nonconstant units; there is no hidden hypothesis `C^* = C_field^*`.

4. The two cocycle expressions are isomorphisms from the same line bundle to the same line bundle over `X × A²`. Their quotient is in `C[s,t]^* = C^*` and restricts to 1 at s = 0. Therefore the quotient is 1. The normalized isomorphism is a genuine algebraic Ga linearization. Fiberwise linearity preserves the nonzero-vector locus and makes the action commute with fiber scaling.

5. When the nonzero-vector locus is affine, the action coaction is a polynomial `ρ(r) = Σ δ_n(r)t^n` for each regular function r. Comparing coefficients of the Ga action law gives `δ_i δ_j = binomial(i+j,i) δ_(i+j)`. In characteristic zero this gives `δ_n = δ_1^n/n!`; polynomial finiteness implies local nilpotence. Commutation with fiber scaling preserves each fiber-weight summand.

6. For a homogeneous D_0 of shift j, the valuation torus action σ_λ fixes R because all coefficient elements really have degree zero in the specified Laurent-ring embedding. The map `σ_λ ⊗ id_Rt` is therefore well defined on the fiber product and extends to a linear bundle automorphism of the total space L. The explicit rules `τ ↦ λ^(-1)τ`, `V ↦ λ²V`, and `Rt ↦ Rt` agree with its action on the nonzero-vector locus.

7. Conjugating the lifted additive action by this bundle automorphism yields a normalized linear lift of `exp(λ^j tD_0)`. Reparametrizing the original lifted action by `t ↦ λ^j t` yields a second normalized linear lift of precisely the same base action. Uniqueness forces equality. Comparing coefficients of t gives `σ̃_λ Dt σ̃_λ^(-1) = λ^j Dt`. If r has valuation degree h, then `σ̃_λ(Dt r) = λ^(h+j) Dt r`. The finite homogeneous decomposition of `Dt r`, together with linear independence of distinct Laurent monomials in λ over C, forces `Dt r` into degree h+j.

## Adversarial checks and limits

- The degree-action lift needs the coefficient map `R → G` to be the actual coefficient inclusion, and the source makes this explicit. It would fail if an alternative abstract presentation assigned nonzero degrees to coefficient elements; that is not the presented construction.
- Conjugation must use a bundle automorphism extending to the zero section, not merely an automorphism of `L^×`. The source proves this by the Cartesian pullback description of L. This is exactly the extra information needed for uniqueness of linear lifts to apply.
- The actions on coordinate rings have the opposite composition order from the corresponding point maps, but both the conjugated base action and its lift use the same coordinate-ring convention. There is no sign or inverse inconsistency in the factor `λ^j`.
- Zero D_0 gives the unique trivial additive lift; nonzero D_0 remains nonzero upon restriction because `G → Gt` is injective. Negative shifts j cause no difficulty since λ is invertible.
- Integrality (or another hypothesis guaranteeing polynomial units are parameter independent) matters for uniqueness. Outside the stated hypotheses, let `C = C_field[ε]/(ε²)`, give the base the trivial Ga action, and take the trivial line bundle. Multiplication of fibers by `1 + εt` is an additional normalized linear Ga action: `(1+εs)(1+εt)=1+ε(s+t)`. This is a counterexample only after dropping the smooth integral base assumption.
- Characteristic zero matters for the coaction-to-derivation/exponential step, and it is present here. In characteristic p, the Ga translation `x ↦ x+t^p` can have zero first derivative; therefore a zero infinitesimal derivation would no longer determine the action.

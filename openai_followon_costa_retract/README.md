# An explicit complex polynomial retract in five variables

Author: Alec Kriebel; ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X.

## Exact theorem and contribution
Explicit split C-algebra maps realize OpenAI family047's smooth integral fourfold A=C[p,s,u,F,J]/(H), x=s²+u³+p²F, H=x²F−(1+2sx)J−p²J²−pu, as a nonpolynomial retract of C^[5] of transcendence degree four. The five-component idempotent is polynomial everywhere, including p=0. This gives a negative answer to Costa's assertion over C in ambient dimension five. It does not settle the ambient-four-variable question.

The cancellation construction, stabilization and nonpolynomiality theorem belong to OpenAI. The cylinder-to-retract bridge is classical. Our contribution is an explicit transport and verification package; no independent cancellation breakthrough or first-priority claim.

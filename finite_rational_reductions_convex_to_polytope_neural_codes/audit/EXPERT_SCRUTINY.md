# Expert scrutiny checklist

The following proof locations are the highest-value targets for external review.

1. Polytope monotonicity: projection estimate for arbitrary mixed apex combinations.
2. Polytope monotonicity: strict `>1/2` threshold and equality boundary.
3. Polytope monotonicity: simultaneous caps associated with the same maximal word.
4. Binary-meet reduction: inclusion-minimal maximal-word family and strictness of both superwords.
5. Binary-meet reduction: nondecreasing-cardinality deletion schedule and survival of endpoints.
6. Rational bridge: extension from `[0,1]` to a bounded open interval.
7. Rational bridge: simultaneous interval endpoints and equality-class rationalization.
8. Fixed-arrangement theorem: finite cell family and relatively open lower-dimensional cells.
9. Fixed-arrangement theorem: tangent closures and weak/strict separation.
10. Protected-supercode theorem: exact preservation of inactive memberships at witnesses.
11. Protected-supercode theorem: trivial-neuron convention and ambient compact containment.
12. Repair-atlas theorem: compactness of the complete closed unwanted carrier.
13. Repair-atlas theorem: source containment after grouping or convexifying patches.
14. Sequential-repair calibration: exact code before, during, and after synchronized repair.
15. Retraction firewall: verify no statement relies on the retracted good-core subdivision or universal simultaneous globalization.

# Journal target memo

Audit date: 2026-08-15.

## 1. Discrete & Computational Geometry

**Recommendation: first journal target.**

- **Scope fit:** Strong. The paper centers on convex sets, polytopes, hyperplane arrangements, cell decompositions, separation, and finite geometric constructions. DCG explicitly covers polytopes, spatial subdivisions, arrangements, convex hulls, and related discrete geometry.
- **Expected format:** Full research article. The 28-page manuscript is within the normal range for a proof-heavy geometry paper.
- **Novelty threshold:** High but compatible with a coherent theorem suite around a recognized conjecture. The paper should foreground the exact finite reductions and fixed-arrangement theorem rather than the biological framing.
- **Page-length risk:** Low-to-moderate; no restrictive public page limit was located.
- **Open access / preprint:** Hybrid journal; arXiv posting is standard in this community. Optional open access has an APC, but subscription publication is available.
- **Likely reviewers:** Convex/discrete geometers, neural-code researchers familiar with convexity, oriented-matroid researchers.
- **Primary risk:** Editors may judge a reduction paper insufficient without resolving the conjecture. The strong finite construction results and exact remaining obstruction must be made prominent.

## 2. SIAM Journal on Applied Algebra and Geometry

- **Scope fit:** Strong historical and community fit. Several foundational neural-code papers appeared in SIAGA, and the present work connects applied algebraic geometry, combinatorics, polytopes, and oriented matroids.
- **Expected format:** Research article with reproducibility material.
- **Novelty threshold:** Strong theorem suite and clear distinction from existing convexity criteria required.
- **Page-length risk:** Moderate. SIAM guidance encourages concise papers; the current 28-page article may benefit from moving part of the convention and cap audits to an online appendix.
- **Open access / preprint:** SIAM journals permit preprints; open-access options depend on the publication route.
- **Likely reviewers:** Neural-code, applied algebraic geometry, combinatorics, and oriented-matroid researchers.
- **Primary risk:** The paper is more convex-geometric than algebraic; editorial fit depends on the oriented-matroid and code-morphism framing.

## 3. Advances in Applied Mathematics

- **Scope fit:** Good. The journal publishes rigorous original work in discrete and applied mathematics and has published neural-code convexity papers.
- **Expected format:** Full research article; current length is plausible.
- **Novelty threshold:** High. The narrative must make the binary-meet reduction, rational bridges, and finite-atlas package look like a unified advance rather than a collection of lemmas.
- **Page-length risk:** Low-to-moderate.
- **Open access / preprint:** Elsevier hybrid publication; preprint posting is generally compatible, subject to the current author agreement.
- **Likely reviewers:** Discrete mathematicians and researchers in neural-code convexity.
- **Primary risk:** The paper may be viewed as too specialized unless the convex-geometric relevance is emphasized.

## Ranking rationale

DCG is ranked first because the main techniques and statements are geometric and polytopal. SIAGA is the strongest community-continuity option and may be preferred if expert feedback emphasizes the neural-code/oriented-matroid aspects. Advances in Applied Mathematics is a credible third target with prior neural-code precedent.

Dear Editors,

Please consider the manuscript “Finite Rational Reductions for the Convex-to-Polytope Conjecture in Neural Codes” for publication in Discrete & Computational Geometry.

A neural code records all relative membership atoms of a finite family of receptive fields. A conjecture of Kunin, Lienkaemper, and Rosen asks whether every code realized by open convex sets can also be realized by interiors of polytopes, possibly in higher dimension. The manuscript does not claim to resolve that conjecture. Instead, it develops a unified finite reduction theory.

The main results are an explicit polytope-monotonicity construction; universal reductions to immediate covers, single nonmaximal deletions, and then binary-meet deletions; a finite rational interval bridge attached to every open-convex binary-meet deletion; exact polyhedralization of one arbitrary convex neuron over a fixed finite polytopal arrangement; and a finite protected-supercode and repair-atlas construction for arbitrary open convex realizations. Together these results show that the unresolved step is a finite simultaneous convex-attachment problem for repeated neurons, rather than uncontrolled local curvature or infinitely many local repairs.

The paper includes exact rational calibrations and a deterministic no-network verification package. The computational material checks finite examples only; all general results are proved mathematically in the manuscript. The manuscript is 28 pages with eight figures.

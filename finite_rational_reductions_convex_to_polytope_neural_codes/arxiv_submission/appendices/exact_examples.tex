\section{Exact finite examples and verifier scope}
\label{app:exact-examples}

The accompanying scripts use exact rational arithmetic.  They check finite calibrations; they do not replace the universal proofs.

\subsection{Rational interval events}
The verifier represents interval endpoints by rational numbers and evaluates membership on every endpoint class and complementary open cell.  It includes simultaneous starts and ends, empty intervals, full intervals, and marked endpoint words.

\subsection{Fixed-arrangement cells}
Small rational arrangements include a tangent cell, a lower-dimensional cell, a wholly outside atom, and a split atom with an outside witness on a separator boundary.  Exact halfspace signs confirm the theorem's classifications.

\subsection{Sequential and synchronized repair}
For \Cref{ex:sequential-failure}, exact polygon membership confirms:
\begin{enumerate}
\item the protected words $13$ and $123$ occur;
\item the first synchronized equality $P_1=P_3$ removes word $1$;
\item $(-1,-3/10)$ has word $1$ after the later one-neuron hull;
\item $P_1=P_3=P^{\rm hull}$ realizes exactly $\{13,123\}$.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{One-round failure}
The optional Phase-XIX calibration verifies a three-neuron source code $\{\varnothing,1,2,123\}$, a protected supercode with an unwanted $12$ atom, a complete $123$ repair patch covering the old carrier, and a remote rational $12$ witness after the one-round synchronized hull.  A second common-core polytope eliminates the defect.  This example is included to falsify an overly strong globalization conjecture, not as a component of any universal proof.

\subsection{Scope statement}
The general theorems are human-readable mathematical proofs.  The scripts certify only the stated finite coordinate examples, combinatorial deletion orders, and rational endpoint data.  Failure of a finite search or a particular ansatz is nowhere used as evidence of nonpolytopality.

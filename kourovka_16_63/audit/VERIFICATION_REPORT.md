Success requires a valid bracket, an unambiguous finite group, the exact underlying order, a proof covering every finite automorphism, exact computational certificates, and a justified passage from finite precision to exact Smith data.

## Strongest verified result and proof chain

1. The displayed rank-31 bracket is an integer Lie bracket. At p=1009 its ambient lattice is perfect, has one-dimensional centre and is generated by the stated x,y.
2. The full ambient finite-field automorphism group is classified, including the projective linear action and swap component. The chosen flag has trivial full and infinitesimal stabilizers.
3. Every finite automorphism at depth i >= 7 preserves the ambient lattice and reduces to that trivial flag stabilizer. Every derivation solution enters the corresponding logarithm domain. This step rules out uncounted finite automorphisms.
4. Integral exponential/logarithm preserve the exact p^i congruence, and the tensor argument gives a bijection between the entire automorphism set and the derivation kernel.
5. The scaled matrix K has exact rank 931, nullity 30, and nonzero Smith valuation multiplicities 0:87, 1:55, 2:758, 3:6, 4:25. Their sum is 1689. Rank 30 for the rational inner derivations bounds rank(K) above by 931, closing the possible hidden high-valuation gap in finite-precision elimination.
6. Hence the complete finite Lie-ring automorphism order is p^(30i+1689). At i=1689 this equals p^(31i)=p^52359.
7. The quotient Lie ring has class at most 846 < 1009. The finite Lazard correspondence applies and preserves its entire automorphism group, giving the exact group statement.

The class bound is used only where checked; the formula for all i >= 7 is a Lie-ring formula, not an unchecked group assertion beyond the Lazard range.

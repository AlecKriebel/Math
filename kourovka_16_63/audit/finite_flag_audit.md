### 2. Radical and complements

After fixing sl2 pointwise, characteristicity of N and N', and the explicit

The U-to-W mixing coefficient b gives a T component 2ab P5 in [φU,φU].

### 4. Full flag rigidity

F3 ∩ N is exactly the highest-weight line kU0. Modulo N', inner
exponentials act trivially and τ fixes U. Preservation of that line forces the
PGL2 component into the upper Borel: a binary linear form whose sixth power
is a multiple of X6 must itself be a multiple of X. There is no exceptional
characteristic phenomenon at 1009.

F2 projects isomorphically to P=span(h,e+f). Preserving its Killing-orthogonal
line span(e−f), for an upper-triangular representative [[a,b],[0,1]], gives
b=0 and a²=1. This leaves exactly s=1 and s=diag(-1,1).

For the second case, preservation of F2 forces x ↦ x and y ↦ −y. Fixing x
forces the U component of n modulo N' to have weight zero, hence to be a
multiple of U3. The y equation requires (e+f)n_U = −2U1, whereas its left
side is a multiple of 3U2+3U4. This is impossible. The same obstruction
applies whether or not τ is present because τ fixes U.

For s=1 with τ present, the V⊕R equation has right side
R0−V0+V2−R2. Its nonzero weights 4 and 0 cannot occur when e+f acts on a
weight-zero vector, which produces only weights 2 and −2. This case is also
excluded. In the remaining case the automorphism is inner and fixes the
generators x,y, so it is the identity on B.

For derivations, the quotient component must preserve both P and the highest
line. Writing it αh+βe, the condition on [s,h] forces β=0 and the condition
on [s,e+f] forces α=0. Since F2 ∩ N=0, the derivation then vanishes on x,y
and therefore on B. The separate rank-30 flag computation agrees.

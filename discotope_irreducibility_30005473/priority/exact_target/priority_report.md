## The exact claim that was checked

The candidate proves two distinct claims. First, for every finite sum of ellipsoidal discs of dimension at least two, the complex Zariski closure (E) of all exposed points is irreducible, including nongeneric and lower-dimensional sums. Second, if the sum is full-dimensional and each subset of summand spans has the maximal possible dimension, the original purely nonlinear variety (S) equals (E). The second claim supplies the bridge needed to solve the original formulation, rather than only its later restatement.

In the [2022 journal article](https://lematematiche.dmi.unict.it/index.php/lematematiche/article/download/2338/1156/6734), Definition 3.3 defines (S) using sums of relative boundary points that lie on the total boundary. Conjecture 8.2, p. 167, asks for irreducibility of this (S) for a generic discotope with no segment summands. Its discussion separately anticipates critical-locus irreducibility, birationality, and degree computations. Those stronger questions are not part of the conjecture's stated conclusion. The original conjecture page was also checked visually.

In [Meroni's 2023 report](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/4031/OWR_2023_15.pdf?sequence=4), p. 830, (E) is explicitly the Zariski closure of the exposed points. Conjecture 1 asks for its irreducibility under genericity and summand dimensions at least two, and refers back to the 2022 conjecture. These definitions are not identical by inspection. The candidate's nongeneric counterexample and generic equality theorem make the distinction material. The report describes the unresolved regime as total summand boundary dimension greater than (d-1) with some summand dimension greater than two. The conjecture page was checked visually.

# Independent audit of citing and subsequent literature

Checked 30 September 2026 (America/Los_Angeles); final checkpoint 1 October 2026, 03:44 UTC. This audit was conducted independently of the other audit reports. Only the candidate and primary sources were used for the mathematical comparison.

**Finding:** No exact prior theorem proving irreducibility of the complex Zariski closure of exposed points of every finite Minkowski sum of generalized discs of ranks at least two was identified in the primary sources checked. No subsequent source checked resolves the original generic mixed-rank conjecture for the purely nonlinear part either. This is a bounded literature-search finding, not confirmation of historical priority or novelty. The known full-rank support parametrization and the original special-case irreducibility theorems must receive credit.

## Claims compared

The candidate is `/Users/alec/Documents/Math/pr9_adversarial_review_20260929/source_snapshot/candidate.md`.

- Candidate Theorem 1: for arbitrary finitely many generalized discs of dimensions at least two, including nongeneric and non-full-dimensional arrangements, the complex Zariski closure (E(D)) of exposed points is irreducible.
- Candidate Theorem 4: if the spans satisfy (dim(sum_{i\in J}L_i)=min(d,sum_{i\in J}dim L_i)) for every (J), and the sum is full dimensional, then (S(D)=E(D)). Here (S(D)) is the complex Zariski closure of the intersection of the sum of the summand relative boundaries with the boundary of (D).
- These are distinct claims. The 2022 paper conjectures irreducibility of (S(D)); the 2023 workshop report defines and conjectures irreducibility of (E(D)). Irreducibility of (E(D)) alone does not establish the 2022 statement without a proof identifying the two sets under the relevant hypotheses.

This audit checks prior statements and their hypotheses. It does not validate the candidate proof.

## Original and later conjecture statements

**Gesmundo–Meroni, The Geometry of Discotopes.** The journal article's publication page gives 27 June 2022; arXiv v1 is 1 November 2021 and v2 is 28 July 2022. The journal paper assumes full-dimensional discotopes and defines (S) in Definition 3.3, p.149. Conjecture 8.2, p.167, predicts irreducibility of (S) for a generic discotope of type ((0,N_2,\ldots,N_d)). It does not assert the candidate's unrestricted theorem about (E).

The exact prior special cases are Remark 3.4 (all summands full dimensional), Theorem 4.3 (generic arrangement with (sum_m(m-1)N_m\le d-1), degree (2^N)), and Theorem 6.1 (generic sums of two-dimensional discs with (N\ge d-1), irreducibility of (S) and equality with the Zariski closure of extreme points; degree at most (2^N\binom{N}{d-1})). These overlap proper subfamilies of the candidate and do not give the unrestricted mixed-rank result. The critical-locus and degree questions remain separate from the candidate's stated claims. [Journal article and metadata](https://lematematiche.dmi.unict.it/index.php/lematematiche/article/view/2338), [arXiv history](https://arxiv.org/abs/2111.01241).

**Chiara Meroni, Two convex conjectures for different flavours, Oberwolfach Report 15/2023.** The workshop occurred 19–24 March 2023. The contribution is pp.829–832; p.830 contains Definition 1, Theorem 2, and Conjecture 1. Definition 1 uses the Zariski closure of exposed points, (E). Conjecture 1 still assumes generic discs of dimension at least two; the preceding discussion ties genericity to transversality of their linear spans. Theorem 2 recites the known special cases. The report cites the 2022 paper as reference [5] and identifies Conjecture 8.2 as the antecedent, although the 2022 and 2023 definitions require the distinction above. This is an explicit later open-conjecture statement, not a solution. [Primary workshop report](https://publications.mfo.de/bitstream/handle/mfo/4031/OWR_2023_15.pdf?sequence=4), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2023/15).

## Related ellipsoidal-sum results

Two older primary papers were read to check whether the same support parametrization had already supplied the whole result. They precede the original discotope preprint and are not presented here as citing papers.

**Chirikjian–Shiffman, Closed-Form Parametric Equation for the Minkowski Sum of m Ellipsoids in R^N and Associated Volume Bounds.** arXiv v1 30 December 2020; v2 28 March 2021. Theorem 1.1 assumes symmetric positive-definite square matrices (A_i) and gives (x(n)=sum_i A_i^2n/\|A_i n\|). This is the candidate's support formula in the full-rank case. The paper studies curvature and volume; no algebraic irreducibility assertion was found. It does not treat singular matrices representing lower-dimensional discs. The parametrization is an established ingredient and should not be claimed as novel. [Primary source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15163).

**Ruan–Chirikjian, Closed-form Minkowski sums of convex bodies with smooth positively curved boundaries.** arXiv v1 31 December 2020; v2 12 October 2021; Computer-Aided Design 143 (2022), article 103133. Theorem 4.1 works with inverse Gauss maps of smooth positively curved boundaries. Section 5.1 uses a positive-definite ellipsoid matrix and recovers the corresponding two-ellipsoid formula. Ambient lower-dimensional discs do not meet these hypersurface hypotheses. No complex Zariski-closure irreducibility claim was found. [Primary arXiv source](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.15461), [publisher](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0010448521001445).

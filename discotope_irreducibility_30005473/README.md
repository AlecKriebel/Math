# Discotope exposed-point irreducibility

Alec Kriebel, independent researcher; ORCID 0009-0001-9320-500X. Version 1.0, September 30, 2026. Unrefereed, with extensive generative-AI assistance disclosed.

The research note proves irreducibility of the complex exposed-point closure for all finite sums of ellipsoidal discs of dimension at least two, without genericity or full dimensionality. A separate theorem proves equality with the 2022 purely nonlinear part under general position, resolving the two stated conjectures. Historical first priority is not asserted; see the dated priority audit.

                   Hypersingular Riesz-energy constants on surfaces
                     from triangular-lattice universal optimality
                                                     Alec Kriebel∗

                                                October 6, 2026


                                                      Abstract
              We deduce the exact two-dimensional hypersingular minimal Riesz-energy constant for every
          real s > 2 from OpenAI’s recently released triangular-lattice universal-optimality theorem. The
          constant is the Epstein sum of the covolume-one triangular lattice. An explicit gap-periodization
          argument relates the ﬁnite square problem to the source theorem’s centered-disk lower energy,
          with an inter-box error of order N 1−s/2 for each ﬁxed gap parameter. A lattice crop gives
          the matching upper bound. The corrected Hardin–Saﬀ universality theorem then gives the
          ordered-pair leading asymptotic on every smooth compact embedded surface of positive area,
          including surfaces with smooth boundary, for ambient Euclidean distance. This resolves the
          two-dimensional s > 2 case of the explicit lattice-constant conjecture recorded by Brauchart,
          Hardin and Saﬀ. The universal-optimality breakthrough, ﬁnite-to-inﬁnite reduction and mani-
          fold theorem are inherited results; this note gives a self-contained account of the known passage
          and records its planar surface consequence.

4    Transfer to surfaces and veriﬁcation scope

Hardin and Saﬀ [2, Theorem 2.4] prove that, for s > d, a compact d-rectiﬁable manifold K ⊂ Rd
of positive Hd measure satisﬁes
                                         Es (K, N )    Cs,d
                                       lim          = d       .
                                     N →∞ N  1+s/d   H (K)s/d
                                                 ∪
Their deﬁnition (19) is a ﬁnite union K = k φk (Kk ), where each Kk ⊂ Rd is compact and φk is
bi-Lipschitz on an open set Gk ⊃ Kk . The theorem uses ambient Euclidean distance, ordered pairs,
and the unit-cube-normalized constant. We use the corrected proof, with the addendum included
in the cited arXiv version, rather than the uncorrected neighborhood assertion in Proposition 8.3.
    A smooth compact embedded surface with smooth boundary has ﬁnitely many such compact
chart pieces. At a boundary point, its smooth chart on a half-disk extends to an open planar neigh-
borhood; after shrinking, the extension is bi-Lipschitz by the full-rank derivative and local graph
representation. The pieces on the half-disk remain compact subsets of that open neighborhood.
Thus the s > d theorem allows boundary here. The extra containment in a C 1 manifold without
boundary in the source’s s = d clause is not needed for s > 2.
    For completeness, the s > d addendum’s neighborhood lemma is used only inside a compact
regular thickening: cover each chart piece by ﬁnitely many closed cubes contained in Gk and take
the union of their bi-Lipschitz images. This is a compact d-regular set containing K. Consequently
the closure of each relative neighborhood is compact and its positive-distance step is valid. No
unrestricted unbounded-neighborhood version of that lemma is invoked. Applying the theorem
with d = 2 and (1) proves (2); the sphere has area 4π.

                                                Abstract
         We give an almost surely consistent spectral-equation estimator for a general smooth sym-
     metric diﬀusion tensor and an unknown smooth invariant density on a known bounded con-
     nected smooth domain. Observations are stationary exact positions at one known ﬁxed positive
     lag; reﬂection is conormal, and ﬁxed ellipticity and density bounds are known. A symmetric
     ﬁnite-rank estimate of the stationary pair kernel supplies all its positive empirical eigenpairs.
     Transition-eigenvalue fourth powers and vanishing ridge regularization give a basis-invariant
     ﬁt. We prove full spectral-jet excitation and construct the required derivative-consistent ker-
     nels from the dependent data. The tensor and its separately ﬁtted divergence converge locally
     uniformly; an ellipticity-clipped tensor converges globally in L2 . This provides a convergence
     analysis for the spectral reconstruction question posed by Reiß in OWR24/2017, in the explicit
     smooth model stated here. No rate or numerical implementation is claimed. Spectral ﬁtting
     and eigenvalue-power weighting have established predecessors.

Established foundations and priority scope. Fixed-lag identiﬁcation of a reversible generator
is established by Hansen–Scheinkman [2, Proposition 5.5]; multivariate spectral and Dirichlet-
form foundations appear in Chen–Hansen–Scheinkman [3]. Crommelin–Vanden-Eijnden already
proposed nonparametric drift/tensor spectral residual ﬁtting [4]; their 2011 author manuscript uses
powers of transition eigenvalues as weights [5, Eq. (4.7)]. Neither idea is introduced here. Gobet–
Hoﬀmann–Reiß and Chorowski develop scalar one-dimensional spectral inference [6, 7]. Nickl,
Giordano–Wang and Alberti–Barnes–Jambhale–Nickl supply multidimensional scalar-conductivity
ﬁxed-lag theory [8, 9, 10]. In particular, [10, Lemma 3.8] is a close gradient-excitation predecessor
for our cutoﬀ-jet argument and credits earlier work of Alberti. Deterministic matrix inversion from
internal solutions has gradient/Hessian and variational predecessors [11, 12].
    A dated bounded primary-source audit located no theorem encompassing the particular tensor
spectral-consistency result proved here. This is not a worldwide priority guarantee. In partic-
ular the full 2011 text inspected was the CWI author manuscript revised August 31, 2011; the
publisher-ﬁnal text was inaccessible, and identity with it is not assumed. Statements about its de-
ferred sample/discretization and inﬁnite-expansion analyses refer to that manuscript [5, Sections 3.2
and 4.2]. Further edition and access limits are recorded in the supplement. Classical nonlinear in-
verse regularization [15] also yields an alternative estimator-existence argument when its diﬀusion
forward-map hypotheses are supplied; a separately veriﬁed derivation of that application accompa-
nies this note. That current derivation is not a located earlier publication and does not establish
consistency of the spectral ﬁt studied here. No claim of being the ﬁrst possible consistent tensor
estimator is made.

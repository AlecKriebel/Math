                              Lane–Emden hyperbola

                                                 Abstract
          We record the consequences of the subcritical entire Liouville theorem in OpenAI’s 2026
      Hénon–Lane–Emden preprint for the unweighted system −∆u = v p , −∆v = uq . For n ≥ 3,
      p, q > 1, and 1/(p + 1) + 1/(q + 1) > (n − 2)/n, the established reductions of Poláčik–Quittner–
      Souplet give universal pointwise estimates on arbitrary proper domains and zero-Dirichlet half-
      space nonexistence. We detail the resulting gradient and exterior estimates and the boundary
      blow-up argument giving uniform bounds and compactness on ﬁxed bounded C 2 domains, with
      exact function spaces. The full subcritical scope is an immediate consequence of the cited Liou-
      ville input; no independent solution of that conjecture or new Liouville-to-estimate machinery
      is claimed.

2    Entire input and interior normalization
Lemma 2. Under (1), the only bounded nonnegative classical solution of (2) on Rn is (0, 0).
Proof. The unweighted corollary of [1] excludes strictly positive C 2 (Rn ) solution pairs, even without
boundedness. If either nonnegative component vanishes at a point, the strong maximum princi-
ple for its superharmonic equation makes it identically zero. Its equation then forces the other
component to be zero. Otherwise both components are strictly positive, and the cited theorem
applies.

4    Fixed-domain boundary blow-up and compactness
We give the remaining argument explicitly. On a bounded C 2 domain, a classical zero-Dirichlet
pair continuous on the closure already belongs to W 2,r (Ω) ∩ W01,r (Ω) for every 1 < r < ∞: its
right sides are bounded; solve the corresponding linear Dirichlet problems, and compare by the
maximum principle. Indeed the diﬀerence is harmonic, continuous on the closure, and zero on the
boundary. The global Poisson estimates on a C 1,1 domain (which a bounded C 2 domain is) give
the stated regularity. We use also their local boundary versions and Sobolev embeddings; standard
forms are in [6].

       A diagonal subsequence converges in C 1 on compact subsets of H and in C 2 on compact subsets
of H to a bounded nonnegative solution (U, V ), continuous and zero on ∂H. The transformed
equations converge in distributions to the Laplacian equations; interior regularity upgrades the
limit to C 2 (H). Normalization passes at (0, t). If t = 0, continuity and the zero trace contradict
normalization. If t > 0, the limiting pair is nontrivial, contradicting the half-space theorem. Both
cases exclude the assumed blow-up, proving (4).
       The uniform L∞ bound now gives uniform global W 2,r bounds for every ﬁnite r > 1. Compact
Sobolev embeddings, using a larger r if necessary, give a subsequence converging in C 1,γ (Ω) for
any ﬁxed γ < 1. The limiting pair retains nonnegativity and zero trace, and its equations hold
in distributions and then classically in the interior. The right sides converge uniformly. For each
ﬁnite r > 1, the global linear Dirichlet estimate applied to diﬀerences gives

Thus the compactness is strong, not just weak W 2,r compactness. On a C 2,θ domain, the already
uniform Lipschitz bounds make the right sides uniformly C 0,θ ; global Schauder estimates give
uniform C 2,θ bounds and compactness in C 2,η , η < θ. This completes Theorem 1.


5    Limits and veriﬁcation disclosure
The strict hyperbola and p, q > 1 are used as stated. No critical or supercritical case, weighted
boundary estimate, parabolic estimate, or weak-solution regularity assertion is included. The con-
ditional asymptotically-power extension in PQS Theorem 7.3 is established work; we do not present
it as a new extension here. Spatially varying coeﬃcients are outside this note.

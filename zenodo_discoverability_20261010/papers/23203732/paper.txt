1    Question, conventions, and attribution
For a countable probability-measure-preserving (p.m.p.) equivalence relation R on a standard
atomless probability space, the cost is the inﬁmum, over generating graphings, of the sum of the
domain measures of their partial isomorphisms. Gaboriau proved
                                                     (2)        (2)
                                  Cost(R) − 1 ≥ β1 (R) − β0 (R)                                          (1)
and asked whether equality always holds [2, Corollary 3.23 and p. 129]; see also his FAQ [3, p. 1].
                                                    (2)
The zeroth term is essential. Inﬁnite classes have β0 = 0; a free action of a ﬁnite group of order
                 (2)       (2)
q instead has β0 = 1/q, β1 = 0, and cost 1 − 1/q.
    For clarity, we write
              Cost∗ (Γ) = inf{Cost(Rα ) : α is an essentially free p.m.p. action of Γ}
for group inﬁmum cost [1, Deﬁnition I.5]. The action-speciﬁc equality, the relation-level equality,
and equality for this inﬁmum are distinct assertions; Gaboriau’s notes state them separately [4,
Question 2.65].
    The group and both cost estimates used below are OpenAI’s [5, Theorem 1.1]. Its manuscript
is dated October 5, 2026; the public repository version checked here is the October 6 release at
commit adc7f1241b42e322a6451854ab7e4b4c146bf78a. The general implication from a failure
of ﬁxed price to a cost–Betti counterexample was already explicit in Popa–Shlyakhtenko–Vaes [6,
Remark 6.6], whose ﬁrst arXiv version appeared in 2018. The cost-limit deduction for this particular
group was already recorded conditionally in our public preliminary triage [9, §7 and accompanying
audit]. This note expands that observation with an independent cellular calculation of all L2 -Betti
numbers and a focused validation record for the upstream cost input. No independent ﬁxed-price
discovery or priority for the classical implication is asserted. The bounded priority audit found no
earlier instance of the particular direct calculation below; that search does not certify novelty.

subset of X is therefore null or conull. For a Γ-invariant measurable subset of YM , each height
section is t-invariant and hence null or conull. Translation by a cycles the heights and equates
their measures. Thus the subset is null or conull. The countable and ﬁnite products used here are
standard atomless probability spaces.

6    Dependency and veriﬁcation scope
The positive cost bound remains the substantial input from OpenAI. Its proof proceeds through
relative graphing compression and deployment, transfer to ranks of ﬁnite permutation presenta-
tions, and expansion with a coeﬃcient-sensitive planar shortening argument. The supplementary
veriﬁcation record identiﬁes the exact statements, source hashes, independent proof audits, and
boundary checks for those inputs. The proof of Theorem 5 uses that input explicitly; it does not
independently establish failure of ﬁxed price. The direct all-degree vanishing proof in Proposition 3
uses neither cost bound. The group inﬁmum-cost calculation additionally uses Proposition 4. Both
remain valid independently of the Bernoulli lower-bound argument.
    The source’s INPUTS.md says that its intermediate source record does not establish that it was
the ﬁnal revision. The version actually used is pinned and the current-public-version check is
recorded in the supplement. No relevant Lean formalization was identiﬁed in the source catalogue,
and no result here is claimed machine-formalized. The package provides proof-audit artifacts and
reproducible exact arithmetic rather than a formal proof certiﬁcate of the upstream construction.

                                                     Abstract
          The experimental entry k108 in Table 2 of Reznik–Garcia–Koiller asserts constancy of
      the outer-tangent area ratio divided by the product of original half-angle sines for N ≡ 2
      (mod 4). Two strictly convex primitive six-period trajectories on one confocal billiard family
      give 11664/3125 and 3645/1024, disproving the printed assertion. The horizontal six-period co-
      ordinates and caustic are prior work of Garcia–Reznik, printed in their November 2020 preprint.
      This note is an attributed corrective application of established geometry, with exact reproducibil-
      ity certificates; it claims neither new six-period geometry nor historical firstness.

Disclosure. AI tools were used extensively in mathematical development, source comparison,
drafting, exact computation, and adversarial checking. This is an unrefereed preprint draft without
conventional human peer review. AI checks are fallible and are not proof-assistant formalization.
Historical firstness remains unconfirmed. The written proof and supporting computations are
provided for independent scrutiny.

4    Reproducibility, historical limits, and disclosure
The support package provides four exact checkers copied from guarded preparation sources: sym-
bolic coordinates, independently scaled rational coordinates, quadratic-field geometry with a poly-
nomial check of the tangent map, and projective/support-area calculations. Their inputs are explicit
hardcoded certificates, not a parser of this manuscript. A runner pins the source bytes, copies them
into fresh output directories, executes normally and with Python optimization, compares scientific
results, and checks false-input rejection. Guards use explicit exceptions and survive optimization;
no floating value decides a result. The symbolic checker requires SymPy 1.14.0; the other three use
only the standard library. These are reproducible diagnostics of a written proof, not a formal proof
system. Two copied historical proof texts are retained solely for frozen receipt binding and preserve
their earlier attribution omissions; the present note supplies the corrected credit and source scope.
    A bounded primary-source comparison found the quotient/parity unchanged in all eleven arXiv
versions and the journal article, and did not identify an earlier explicit correction of this exact
entry. This is not an absence or priority certificate. The search covered the version chain, pub-
lisher/Crossref correction records, selected author follow-ups and book, relevant general theorems,
targeted public metadata/index queries, and an incomplete citing-paper list. It did not cover every
repository, edition, unindexed or deleted item, or private knowledge. The 2021 SCG proceedings
could not be retrieved, the Dragović–Radnović monograph was not obtained, and some citing works
were only screened. Historical firstness and earliest independent discovery remain unconfirmed. No
outside person was contacted and no outreach was prepared.

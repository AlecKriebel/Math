                                                   Abstract
          This supplement supplies the complete foundational proofs used in the main paper. We formal-
      ize the chronologically constrained typed response category of a ﬁnite temporal NMSC presentation,
      prove ﬁnite timed-Hankel rank, construct the canonical future-response spaces, establish reachable–
      observable minimality and unique typed realization, prove coordinate and event-reﬁnement invari-
      ance, give a ﬁnite breakpoint-jet and proposed-model equality criterion, derive the conﬂuent ordered-
      simplex formulas without rate-diﬀerence localization, apply the Frisch–Siu compact-germ theorem
      to ﬁnite locus determination, and prove the strict arbitrary-order two-taxon JC collision by an
      exact Vandermonde transversality calculation. The supplement also expands the Borel sequence–
      genealogy inverse, one-reticulation port quotient, and increasing-order consistency arguments.


1     Genealogy and tensor spaces
Fix a ﬁnite labelled sampled set S, |S| = M ≥ 2, and a ﬁnite alphabet of size κ. Let TS be the ﬁnite
set of rooted labelled binary tree topologies. For T ∈ TS , its open metric cone is (0, ∞)E(T ) subject
to the contemporaneous ultrametric equations. Allowing zero edge lengths and identifying trees after
every zero-edge contraction produces the canonical genealogy space GS of additive pseudometrics. It
is a ﬁnite quotient of closed polyhedral cones and hence a locally compact standard Borel space.
    For a ﬁxed ﬁnite generator Q and stationary distribution π, the pruning map on a topology cone
is obtained by multiplying entries of eQte and summing ﬁnitely many hidden states. It is real analytic
in the positive edge lengths and continuous on contracted-edge faces. Therefore

                                             VQ : GS −→ ∆κM −1

is Borel. Its image lies in the compact simplex, and its closure there is compact, although genealogy
times are unbounded.

Lemma 1.1 (Canonical additive-tree inverse). Let DS be the set of ﬁnite additive pseudometrics on
S. There is a Borel map from DS to canonical contracted unrooted metric trees that is inverse to the
leaf-distance map.

Proof. For each candidate split A | Ac , the Buneman isolation index is a minimum of ﬁnitely many
linear combinations of distance entries. It is therefore continuous. The positive isolation indices select
a compatible split system; zero indices are omitted. A deterministic lexicographic rule handles equality
faces. Edge lengths are the isolation indices and pendant lengths are ﬁnite linear expressions. This
deﬁnes a map by ﬁnitely many continuous formulas on a ﬁnite Borel stratiﬁcation, hence a Borel map.
Uniqueness of the additive-tree representation proves inversion.

Lemma 1.2 (Ultrametric rooting). A ﬁnite unrooted additive tree metric that is induced by a con-
temporaneous rooted genealogy has a unique point equidistant from all labelled leaves, after suppressing
zero edges. This point and all rooted vertex times are Borel functions of the distance matrix.
Proof. Let i, j be a lexicographically selected diameter pair. In an ultrametric tree their path length
is twice the common root height, and the root is its midpoint. For every other leaf k, the ultrametric
inequalities force the path from k to meet the i–j path at the unique location giving the same radius.
Thus the midpoint is independent of the selected diameter pair. Midpoints and path intersections are
piecewise linear in recovered edge lengths, proving Borel measurability. For M = 2 the statement is
exactly the midpoint rule.

3.1     Admissible timed words
An object is a triple (e, P, t) with t in the interior of epoch e. An elementary arrow is one of:

 (a) a no-mark duration u > 0 staying inside one epoch and partition;

14     Exact-input and positivity caveats
The residual cone at (q, t) is the intersection of the half-spaces f (z) ≥ 0 over all compatible suﬃxes.
It is closed and convex, pointed because suﬃx evaluations separate residual functions, and generating
because actual nonnegative preﬁx residuals span the ﬁber. Every legal preﬁx-extension map preserves
this compatible family of cones. The construction does not supply a simplicial cone or a stochastic
occupancy basis in the minimal dimension. Consequently it does not provide an NMSC network
realization of the abstract minimal response. Positivity as an ordered function space and a ﬁnite
biological occupancy realization are diﬀerent assertions.
     The ﬁnite equivalence algorithm assumes exact ﬁnite ﬂow matrices over a domain with a terminat-
ing zero test. General symbolic equality of arbitrary elementary real expressions is not claimed. The
mathematical equality theorem itself is independent of that computational representation.

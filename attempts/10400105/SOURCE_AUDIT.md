## Original problem and omitted notation

T. Ohtsuki (editor), *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002), pp.377–572. [Publisher screen PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf), Problem 5.9 on printed p.464, PDF p.92; [publisher print PDF](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024p.pdf) agrees. Read Chapter 5 and especially pp.459, 461, 463–464; p.464 was visually inspected.

The target is a lift-sensitive generalization of the quandle cocycle invariant. The preceding data are an arbitrary abelian group A with a quandle action X→Aut(A), normalized generalized cochains, a degree-two class α, and its affine extension A×X. PROOF.md gives the exact formulas. No field, faithful-action, or connectedness condition occurs here. Ordinary finite group-ring sums require finite X (or a finite coloring set); the unrestricted construction is an indexed family. A is not assumed finite. The previous trivial-action discussion identifies liftability with zero scalar weight, but that equivalence cannot simply be carried into the generalized case. The extracted trailing section heading belongs to Problem 5.10 and is excluded.

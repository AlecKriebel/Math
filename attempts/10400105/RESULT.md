**Candidate full construction; independent review pending. One of five substantive author turns used.**

For each knot-quandle representation C to X, the pulled-back generalized degree-two class vanishes exactly when C lifts to the source's extension Y. A finite diagram gives an explicit affine system that tests this condition. Pairing its exact liftability indicator with the published generalized cocycle weight gives a state sum that specializes to the ordinary cocycle invariant and separates liftable from nonliftable colorings.

Finite X makes this a finite element of Z[A][v]; A can be any abelian group. For arbitrary X the corresponding indexed family is retained without claiming that an infinite group-ring sum exists. Finite A gives exact lift counts, and finitely generated A gives an integer-linear algorithm when its action is specified.

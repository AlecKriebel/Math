## Exact source

The target is Reznik–Garcia–Koiller, *Eighty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard*, arXiv:2004.12497v11, 29 October 2020, §3.9 and Table 9 printed p. 11:

    A_j^dagger · A_(j,ant),       N = 0 modulo 4.

The first factor is the signed area of the polygon obtained by inverting each ORIGINAL orbit vertex in the unit circle about an ORIGINAL focus f_j, then joining successive inverted vertices by straight segments. It is not the area bounded by the curved inversions of complete sides. The second factor is the signed area of the ORIGINAL orbit's antipedal polygon about that same focus, whose side through P_i is perpendicular to P_i−f_j.

No centroid, outer polygon, inner polygon, elliptic inversion, polar area, dual area, or outer-locus focus occurs in this exact row. The surrounding source definitions introduce some of those objects for other rows only. The source explicitly specifies signed shoelace areas and a confocal pair of ellipses, with a>b>0. Primitive stars with strict elliptical caustics are included; hyperbolic/degenerate caustics and parity obtained by artificial repetitions are not silently added.

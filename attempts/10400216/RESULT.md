## Exact question and interpretation

Dylan Thurston's Problem 12.11, Ohtsuki's collection, printed p.536, asks for a condition on shadow diagrams which holds for shadows arising from alternating knots and supplies a hyperbolic-volume lower bound. The surrounding text already cites Lackenby's planar alternating-diagram bounds and emphasizes the potential efficiency of more general shadows. It does not prescribe a local condition or an optimal coefficient. This packet does not count a reformulation of that already-known planar case, or a criterion whose alternating-coverage hypothesis remains unproved, as a full solution of the intended extension.

The shadows are Turaev's gleamed simple polyhedra with the appropriate boundary/framing reconstruction. They are not undecorated planar knot projections or bridge-trisection shadows. All volume conclusions concern the reconstructed actual hyperbolic exterior, or a clearly specified different manifold. Nonhyperbolic alternating knots require a separate Gromov-norm convention and are not silently assigned a hyperbolic volume.

## Accepted-for-review partial claims

1. **Turn 1: canonical long-slope obstruction.** Every connected nonempty planar canonical shadow has a bounded face with at most three corner incidences. Its canonical gleam gives a formal squared slope at most 18, below 4pi^2. This blocks unchanged transfer to a theorem requiring every filling slope longer than 2pi. It says nothing universal about arbitrary shadow moves.
2. **Turn 2: a genuine relative filling criterion.** For an explicitly certified partial filling of a special-shadow drilled manifold, the long-slope test is exactly the integer condition min(4g^2+k^2)>=40. Credited geometric theorems then give a quantitative lower bound for that very manifold. A collapsible disk/annulus presentation retaining a corner bound must have V>=5+u to qualify. Neither a universal alternating-preserving conversion nor a lower-bound transfer across short omitted fillings has been proved.
3. **Turn 3: canonical saturation and a sign-only countercontrol.** Within the marked canonical planar class, sum of absolute black gleams equals the crossing count exactly when the diagram is alternating. This recovers the known twist-number volume bound. An exact one-component wheel diagram has positive black total gleams and negative white total gleams but is not alternating as a diagram. It refutes that weaker sign test, not the source question or the possibility of another alternating diagram for its knot.
4. **Turn 4: explicit exterior-preserving compression.** An all-k two-twist alternating knot family has bounded volume but unbounded canonical crossing/vertex counts and absolute gleam mass. The standard false-boundary collapse removes every crossing incident to the selected outside face and leaves one true vertex. These actual exteriors have ordinary shadow complexity one. Thus no coercive scalar lower bound using the raw canonical counts or gleam norms can cover the entire family. This does not rule out a normalized condition.
5. **Turn 5: the exact guts correction.** For a correctly identified essential-surface cut and its full characteristic decomposition, the guts deficit equals minus the surface Euler characteristic minus the total negative Euler characteristic of the characteristic I-bundle bases. A complete upper bound on that product contribution gives a valid volume lower bound. Parallel fibers show why raw surface Euler characteristic cannot replace guts. The credited alternating checkerboard calculation is recovered with the correct half-factor and one-sided-surface normalization.

## The missing full-source step

A useful condition intrinsic to a general efficient or moved shadow still needs either:

- an exterior-preserving conversion covering the alternating class and meeting the verified relative filling criterion, including control of short fillings; or
- a construction of essential surfaces from the decorated shadow, with a complete, correctly bounded characteristic-product contribution, or an equally rigorous alternative geometric lower-bound mechanism.

The packet proves neither construction. Canonical planar markings and conditional topological certificates are retained with their explicit restrictions. The obstructions concern particular attempted shortcuts and are not impossibility results for Problem 12.11.

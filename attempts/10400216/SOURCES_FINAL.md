# Final primary-source map and reading limits

The exact target is Ohtsuki's Problem 12.11 by Dylan Thurston, printed p.536. The complete question and surrounding Section 12.4 were recovered, including the link-exterior/boundary diagram. The imported title truncation and appended unrelated exposition are not used as the mathematical statement.

Complete PDFs are available as local reading copies for all ten files in SOURCE_INDEX.json. The locators below identify the passages used; possession of a complete PDF is not a claim that every theorem in it was independently reproved. No reading PDF, full text or page image is part of the portable packet.

1. Ohtsuki (editor), *Problems on invariants of knots and 3-manifolds*, Geometry & Topology Monographs 4 (2002): [screen](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024s.pdf), [print](https://msp.org/gtm/2002/04/gtm-2002-04-024p.pdf). These are separately hashed editions of the same work. Printed pp.533–536, especially Problem12.11 and Figure22, govern the scope. The question page was visually checked.
2. Dylan P. Thurston, *The algebra of knotted trivalent graphs and Turaev's shadow world*, GTM4(2002),337–362; [arXiv:math/0311458v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0311458v2). Printed p.351 gives the canonical half-corner gleam rule; pp.354–356 define the relative simple/gleamed polyhedra and reconstruction. The corner diagram was visually checked. The reversed-direction prose sentence on p.361 is not used as a complexity theorem.
3. Costantino–Thurston, *3-manifolds efficiently bound 4-manifolds*, Journal of Topology1(2008),703–745; [arXiv:math/0506577v3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0506577v3). Section3.2 defines internal/external/false boundary, the canonical construction and outside-region collapse. Example3.15/Figure7 on preprint p.14 was visually checked. Propositions3.33–3.34 give octahedral blocks and cusp lattices; Theorem3.37 is the shadow-complexity/Gromov-norm inequality. Theorem5.5 concerns the minimum, not arbitrary presentation size.
4. Ishikawa–Koda, *Stable maps and branched shadows of 3-manifolds*, [arXiv:1403.0596v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1403.0596v1). Exact reliance is on Section5, Proposition5.1 and Lemma5.3, pp.39–43: closed special-shadow reconstruction, simultaneous cusp scales and half-integral gleams. The journal article is Math.Ann.367(2017),1819–1863, DOI10.1007/s00208-016-1403-4; later sources use Section6. The publisher PDF endpoint returned HTML, so no final-typeset line-by-line comparison is claimed. Knot-family parameter conventions elsewhere in the preprint are not used.
5. Lackenby, *The volume of hyperbolic alternating link complements*, [arXiv:math/0012185v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0012185v2), with Agol–D.Thurston appendix. Theorem1 and the introductory Menasco statement give the diagrammatic hypotheses. Section3/Theorem5 gives the actual checkerboard-guts Euler characteristics and explains the one-sided frontier replacement. Twist-reduction and the original planar conditions are retained. The stronger appendix upper constant is mentioned but not needed for the turn4 obstruction.
6. Futer–Kalfagianni–Purcell, *Dehn filling, volume, and the Jones polynomial*, JDG78(2008),429–464; [arXiv:math/0612138v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0612138v4). Theorem1.1 p.2 explicitly permits a subset of cusps and disjoint horoball neighborhoods. Theorem1.2 p.3 requires seven crossings in every twist region; it is not universal alternating coverage. No code or fitted volume data from that paper is executed or used.
7. Iain Moffatt, *Partial duals of plane graphs, separability and the graphs of knots*, AGT12(2012),1099–1136; [publisher PDF](https://msp.org/agt/2012/12-2/agt-v12-n2-p16-s.pdf). Section3.1 printed1107, propertiesT1–T3, supplies the signed Tait/medial construction. The elementary uniform-sign/alternation argument and exact rotation certificates are written in the turn files.
8. Agol–Storm–Thurston, with an appendix by Dunfield, *Lower bounds on volumes of hyperbolic Haken 3-manifolds*, JAMS20(2007),1053–1077; [arXiv:math/0506338v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0506338v2). Definition2.1 and Section2 describe guts and explicitly record the Miyamoto geodesic-boundary inequality; Theorem9.1 p.14 supplies the finite-volume double/Gromov-norm bound. The standalone Miyamoto paper was not independently retrieved. The packet uses the inequality in the form explicitly stated in this complete primary source and keeps its essential-surface/guts hypotheses.
9. Futer–Kalfagianni–Purcell, *Cusp areas of Farey manifolds and applications to knot theory*, IMRN2010(23),4434–4497; [arXiv:0808.2716v4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0808.2716v4). Sections2 and4 discuss hyperbolic once-punctured-torus bundles, including the figure-eight example. This supports existence for the parallel-fiber control; no cusp-area estimate or fitted volume is needed.

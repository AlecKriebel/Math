# Independent primary-source audit

Checked 2026-10-01. Local reading copies are excluded from this portable review.

1. **Original question.** Catino, joint with Colazzo and Stefanelli, OWR 51/2019, pp.3233–3235, Question 2 on p.3234. The full report was read and the rendered definition/question pages visually inspected. The source specifies associative addition, completely regular multiplication, commuting local group inverses, the displayed compatibility law, and the associated two-coordinate map. It asks for conditions yielding a solution. It does not impose finiteness, additive cancellation, Clifford multiplication or bijectivity. [Official report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46831), DOI 10.4171/OWR/2019/51.

2. **2021 generalized semi-braces paper.** The complete preprint and full publisher text were inspected, particularly Definition 3.1 and §4. The group-based criterion (4.2) uses the multiplicative identity; Theorem 4.2's wording must be read alongside its proof's component-solution assumptions. The usable strong-semilattice construction does not classify every completely regular/arbitrary-addition structure. The nearby right-zero-addition example prints the other projection's pair; direct substitution distinguishes `(ab,b^0)` from `(a^0,ab)`. The reviewed proofs use the defining map and prove both criteria themselves. No broad classification is inferred from the imprecise wording. [Final publisher PDF](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/forum-2020-0082/pdf?licenseType=open-access), Forum Math. 33(3) (2021), 757–772, DOI 10.1515/forum-2020-0082; [complete preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01606v1). Final text access succeeded through the web tool; no downloaded final-PDF hash is claimed.

3. **Inverse semi-braces.** The full manuscript's definition and Theorem 7 retain inverse-semigroup multiplication and supply a sufficient result. They do not remove the original general completely regular hypothesis or provide a necessary-and-sufficient unrestricted answer. [Primary manuscript](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05730).

4. **Weak braces.** The complete published paper's definition and Theorem 11 impose inverse structures and extra compatibility. These assumptions remain material. [Published copy](https://d-nb.info/1262161584/34), Semigroup Forum 104 (2022), 228–255, DOI 10.1007/s00233-022-10264-8.

5. **Dual weak braces.** The full 2024 publisher text, especially Theorem 1, describes the strong-semilattice structure under the dual weak-brace hypotheses; it is not a structure theorem for arbitrary source semi-braces. [Primary article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00009-024-02611-6). The later deformation work likewise retains dual weak braces. [Primary deformation article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10231-024-01502-7).

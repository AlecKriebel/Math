## Original scope

Francesco Catino, joint with Ilaria Colazzo and Paola Stefanelli, *Generalized semi-braces and set-theoretic solutions of the Yang–Baxter equation*, OWR51/2019, printed pp.3233–3235. The target is Question2 on p.3234, not the neighboring generalized-radical-ring Question1. The definitions and question were read in full and pp.3233–3234 visually inspected. [Official report](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46831), DOI10.4171/owr/2019/51.

Addition is an arbitrary associative semigroup. Multiplication is completely regular; each element has its commuting group inverse, not a globally available group identity. The compatibility equation and map are reproduced in TURN_1.md. The source allows degenerate, non-bijective solutions. Completely regular does not mean inverse or Clifford. No additive cancellation, commutativity or finite cardinality is imposed.

The source already gives strong-semilattice constructions and a right-zero-addition/right-cryptogroup criterion. These are credited input and calibration cases rather than a new claimed full classification.

## Later primary literature and limits

1. Catino–Colazzo–Stefanelli, *Set-theoretic solutions to the Yang–Baxter equation and generalized semi-braces*, Forum Math.33(3)(2021),757–772, DOI10.1515/forum-2020-0082. Full preprint [arXiv2004.01606v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.01606v1), submitted3April2020; the final publisher PDF was readable through the web tool. Direct downloads at both publisher PDF URLs returned empty bodies, so no final-file hash is invented. The final definition is3.1; the construction theorems are4.1–4.2. Equation4.2 is a necessary-and-sufficient criterion for an ordinary left semi-brace whose multiplicative structure is a group. Theorem4.2's displayed “generalized” wording cannot remove that hypothesis: its proof invokes the group-based component criterion and its identity0. The independently usable general statement is that compatible strong semilattices of component solutions give solutions. This does not classify all generalized left semi-braces.

   The paragraph after equation4.2 discusses right-zero addition but also displays the pair (a^0,ab), which direct substitution assigns to left-zero addition. TURN_1.md separates the two actual formulas and verifies the announced right-zero criterion directly. The defining formula for r is never replaced by an inconsistent example expression. [Publisher PDF](https://www.degruyterbrill.com/document/doi/10.1515/forum-2020-0082/pdf?licenseType=open-access); [author publication record](https://www.ilariacolazzo.info/new/publications/).

2. Catino–Mazzotta–Stefanelli, *Inverse semi-braces and the Yang-Baxter equation*, [arXiv2007.05730](https://arxiv.org/abs/2007.05730). Its Theorem7 gives sufficient conditions under inverse-semigroup multiplication. Inverse semi-braces intersect the source class in the Clifford multiplicative case; they are not a replacement definition for completely regular multiplication. The group-based characterization is explicitly distinguished in the paper. Its sufficiency theorem is not treated as a necessary condition here.

3. Catino–Mazzotta–Miccoli–Stefanelli, *Set-theoretic solutions of the Yang–Baxter equation associated to weak braces*, Semigroup Forum104(2022),228–255, DOI10.1007/s00233-022-10264-8. Full [primary published copy](https://d-nb.info/1262161584/34), [publisher](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00233-022-10264-8). Its Theorem11 gives solutions for weak braces, with both operations inverse and additional compatibility. Those hypotheses do not hold for an arbitrary source semi-brace.

4. Catino–Mazzotta–Stefanelli, *Solutions of the Yang–Baxter Equation and Strong Semilattices of Skew Braces* (2024), DOI10.1007/s00009-024-02611-6, [full publisher text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00009-024-02611-6). Theorem1 describes dual weak braces as strong semilattices of skew braces. The multiplicative Clifford and additive inverse assumptions remain material. The later [deformation paper](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10231-024-01502-7) also explicitly concerns dual weak braces.

A targeted current search did not locate a full characterization in the original completely regular/arbitrary-addition setting. That negative search is not proof of current historical openness. No novelty is claimed for the partial results being developed.

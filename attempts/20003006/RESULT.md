## Precise theorem

For based finite reflexive symmetric digital graphs, use full categorical adjacency on finite integer boxes and their time cylinders. Basepoint extension to a larger box and boundary-fixed homotopy define D_n. Then F(X)=|Cl(X)| is a functor, D_n(X,x₀) is naturally isomorphic to π_n(F(X),x₀) for every n≥1, and D₀ agrees with components. The groups are abelian for n≥2.

Degree 1 agrees with the Lupton–Oprea–Scoville subdivision-based group through Lupton–Scoville's published edge-group theorem. Degree 2 has exactly the Lupton–Musin–Scoville–Staecker–Treviño-Marroquín equivalence relation; a boundary-preserving regional caterpillar homotopy identifies its published diagonal-block multiplication with first-coordinate concatenation.

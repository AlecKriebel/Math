## Exact answer and definitions

The stable complex monic quadratic set in C², viewed as R⁴, fails set prox-regularity at z² for every fixed SPD real coefficient inner product. The closed-set projection characterization and an attainment argument yield at least two distinct attained global nearest stable coefficient polynomials at unstable inputs in every neighborhood of z². A full ambient normal and compact localization establish the corresponding full exact-degree C³ result. The epigraph theorem is about the locally closed whole epigraph as a set; its covector obstruction is horizontal. A separate direct proof shows that the monic quadratic abscissa is function prox-regular at the origin relative to every finite limiting subgradient. This last statement uses constants depending on the finite reference subgradient and the real Euclidean coefficient metric.

No real-only or all-degree classification, explicit nonunique input and minimizing pair, general theorem about nonlinear parameter or matrix distances, firstness, or present-day openness is asserted. Global closedness is asserted for the monic stable set; for the full exact-degree set only relative closedness on nonzero leading coefficient is used, followed by compact localization. A horizontal representing epigraph vector is claimed only for product metrics; with a mixed coefficient/height metric the support covector is horizontal while its representing vector may tilt.

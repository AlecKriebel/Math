# Primary sources and attribution

All summaries here are authored paraphrases. Inspections were performed 21 October 2026. Full literature proofs have not been re-audited.

1. Natasha Morrison's problem-session contribution, *Combinatorics, Probability and Computing*, Oberwolfach Report 22/2022, Conjecture 16, printed p.1229 / PDF p.65. https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46961 ; DOI https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2022/22 . Inspected extracted text and rendered full page. Its functional definition and rank-only conjecture are the audited target; no maximum-girth condition appears there.
2. Nina Kamčev, Anita Liebenau, Natasha Morrison, *On uncommon systems of equations*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.08986 . Inspected local PDF extraction: introduction's irredundancy definition, Section 2 functional normalization and collision discussion, Conjecture 6.1, and concluding Sidorenko discussion. The conjecture requires s(L)=k−1.
3. Daniel Altman, Anita Liebenau, *On the uncommonness of minimal rank-2 systems of linear equations*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.18908 . Inspected local v1 PDF extraction: Conjecture 1.1, Theorem 1.2, Section 2, and start of Theorem 3.1 proof. These give the maximum-girth hypothesis, sufficiently large prime setting, and equivalence to all two-column minors being nonsingular. Publication metadata recovered previously: Bulletin of the London Mathematical Society 57 (2025), 2991–3004; https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.70135 . No new publisher proof audit performed here.
4. Dingding Dong, Anqi Li, Yufei Zhao, *Uncommon linear systems of two equations*, v2, 21 May 2024, https://arxiv.org/html/2404.17005v2 . Inspected Definitions 1.1–1.3, Remark 1.4, Definition 1.7, Theorems 1.8–1.9. These specify integer coefficients, irredundancy (including nonzero columns), functional commonness, maximum girth, and a matrix-dependent large-prime threshold. No journal-status conclusion inferred.
5. Nina Kamčev, Anita Liebenau, Natasha Morrison, *Towards a characterisation of Sidorenko systems*, https://arxiv.org/html/2107.14413v3 ; bibliographic page https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.14413 ; DOI https://doi.org/10.1093/qmath/haad013 . Inspected graph-template definition immediately before Theorem 1.3 and Theorem 1.3, plus the introductory definitions. Its tree-template result already encompasses the counterexample: blocks (x1,x2), (x3,x4), (x5,x6), common block form u−v, and path edges 1–2 and 2–3. The arXiv bibliographic record lists Quarterly Journal of Mathematics 74(3), September 2023, 957–974. This is prior mathematical credit, not a claim that the present audit discovered a new Sidorenko system.

The proof in REPORT.md is a self-contained application of normalized correlation and Jensen's inequality. Broad historical credit for arithmetic commonness and Sidorenko systems belongs to the cited literature, including Saad–Wolf and Fox–Pham–Zhao as discussed there. No priority claim is made for this elementary example or argument.

# Post-audit scope clarifications

Date: 2026-10-04. Status remains **unsolved, 5/5**. The twelve original author files and thirteen independent-review files are preserved byte-for-byte. The independent audit accepts the stated partial results and identifies no blocking mathematical correction. This addendum adopts all four clarifications without rewriting the frozen record.

1. **The excluded period-two boundary.** In the scalar proof, t=2k-1 lies in (0,1), and q=-log(t) is finite. The point t=0, k=1/2 corresponds to q=infinity and is a separate fixed-critical boundary. It is not in the complete metric space [0,infinity) of finite q used in the contraction argument. Its lift stays P_(1/2); it does not approach the distinct positive period-two fixed point. The finite boundary q=0, t=1 is a harmless extension of that recurrence and is a different boundary.

2. **The 2026 manuscript's global assertions.** Benedicks–Rodrigues Propositions 3.3–3.4 assert global pairwise nonexpansion and strict distance decrease on their proposed Teichmüller space. Those claims should not be described as merely local. The manuscript also uses locally defined inverse-root coordinates and derivative/transversality conclusions. The audit did not independently validate the proposed global construction, establish its identification with this normalized real update for every allowed initial map, or derive the universal convergence theorem needed here. Its stated monotonicity results therefore have not been adopted as a resolution of this target.

3. **The nearby polynomial theorem's hypotheses.** The original 1990 discussion assumes actual postcritical finiteness and absence of a Thurston obstruction for its polynomial-lift convergence assertion. Periodic/preperiodic kneading is a broader symbolic condition. The later Question 2 uses that symbolic wording and does not explicitly resolve its output-sequence/topology ambiguity. Our fixed-critical conjugated-map cycles refute the stronger full-map interpretation, while their lifts are constant; they are not counterexamples to lift convergence.

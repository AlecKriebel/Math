# Five substantive approaches

These are five mathematical routes, not five tool calls. They are complete for the conclusions stated below and exhausted for the larger lift-convergence target. Source lookup alone is not counted as a route.

1. **Holomorphic-polynomial transfer.** Attempted to carry polynomial pullback contraction to arbitrary real exponents. Derived the exact affine normalization (9) and proved that a single-valued holomorphic germ agreeing with the real power law on both sides exists only for even integer exponent. Outcome: the direct substitution route is blocked; another geometric construction would be needed. Completion estimate toward full target: 5%.
2. **Finite marked-orbit dynamics and a global metric.** Derived the normalized finite pullback (3), its order constraints, the finite-orbit lower bound on the critical value, and the unbounded derivative (4). Proved a conditional compact-orbit/strict-distance-decrease convergence criterion. Outcome: an actual global metric, interior fixed point, and nondegeneration estimate are missing. Also gave an exact periodic-kneading/non-finite-orbit control. Completion estimate: 10%.
3. **Low-period scalar reduction.** Used the exact period-two relation to derive (5), changed to logarithmic coordinates, and proved a global contraction with constant 1/alpha. Fixed-critical and endpoint-preperiodic cases provide exact boundary controls. Outcome: complete all-real-exponent special cases, but no extension to arbitrary itineraries. Completion estimate: 15%.
4. **Local transversality and exponent continuation.** Investigated whether local inverse-root contraction or approximation by special exponents closes the global iteration argument. Gave an explicit continuous family R_epsilon whose iterates converge for every positive epsilon but fail at the limiting parameter, despite uniform map convergence. Outcome: local attraction/monotonicity and finite-time continuity do not supply the missing global, uniform-in-time estimates. The 2026 manuscript was not independently certified. Completion estimate: 15%.
5. **Dynamical counterexample on the unmarked fibers.** Derived the exact fixed-critical functional transformation g(t) -> g(t^alpha)^(1/alpha), then constructed log-periodic nontrivial two-cycles for every alpha>1. Outcome: complete counterexample to universal convergence of the conjugated maps under continuous strict-unimodality hypotheses; all lifting maps nevertheless stay constant. This resolves the stronger interpretation only, so the imported lift question remains unsolved. Completion estimate toward the full lift target: 15%.

The estimates are subjective planning checkpoints, not probabilities of correctness or fractions of a proof. All exact residual gaps and exclusions appear in `RESULT.md`.

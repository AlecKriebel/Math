# Counterexample route: final five-pass status

16 October 2026. E-6322 remains unresolved by this route. No actual full-class global minimizing deformation with essential determinant infimum zero has been constructed. No result here refutes the frozen benchmark or establishes its universal positive lower bound.

## Final-pass results

1. `DEGENERATE_TANGENT_OBSTRUCTIONS.md` excludes nontrivial nonnegative-Jacobian strong annular weighted tangents in the energy-supercritical range s+3m>6 with C^1 angular profiles, and in the balanced range s+3m=6 with C^2 profiles. It does not assume strictly positive angular determinant. The proof uses whole-density rescaling, the full limiting Euler system and a local pressure identity.
2. `SUBCRITICAL_DENSE_POSITIVE_SET.md` excludes C^2 subcritical profiles with a simple smallest weight and a dense positive-determinant set. At the scalar critical exponent, a Stokes identity replaces the unavailable positive-degree argument.
3. `CRITICAL_AXIS_REPAIR_REJECTION.md` provides a compliant finite-energy family with genuinely nonaffine diffeomorphic boundary trace and critical leading target-axis preimages, then rejects its local stationarity using non-axis determinant fibers. This includes subcritical repeated-lowest-weight examples, beyond the first two general statements. These maps are explicitly not minimizing counterexamples.

## Sharp conditional remainder

For strong W^{1,infinity} weighted annular tangents with C^2 angular data, positive weights, total weight s>3 and a nontrivial limiting determinant, the remaining profile class is subcritical (s+3m<6) and has at least one of:

- two equal smallest weights, for which the limiting leading equation is a coupled two-component system;
- an open angular region where the leading determinant vanishes.

A leading determinant that vanishes identically is not excluded. Nor do these results establish that a singular minimizer has a weighted homogeneous tangent, strong annular convergence, or the stated profile regularity. General nonhomogeneous singular behavior and irregular boundary degeneration remain outside the exclusion theorems. A future counterexample would still require all frozen data assumptions and a global comparison certificate against the entire finite-energy class; stationarity alone would not suffice.

## Relation to the earlier passes

The first four passes excluded affine-on-sphere cores, exact smooth weighted power collapse, uniformly positive weighted annular tangents, and several admissible repaired examples. The final pass permits vanishing limiting angular determinants in substantial additional cases. It does not convert a sequence of ansatz exclusions into a theorem about every minimizer.

All coefficients remain arbitrary fixed positive numbers. The density, exponents, full comparison class and complete smooth diffeomorphic boundary condition have stayed fixed throughout. There is no sixth pass in this assignment and no publication or external write has been made by this route.

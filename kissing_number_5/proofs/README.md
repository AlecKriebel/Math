# Proofs

Only arguments whose hypotheses and boundary cases are explicit belong here.
Candidate arguments with unresolved theorem-strength lemmas must say so at the
top and link those gaps to `CLAIMS_LEDGER.md`.

`fixed41_three_point_formulation.md` derives the exact \(N=41\) marginal
identity, states a finite Bachoc--Vallentin moment/SOS formulation, and
documents an exact degree-5 pseudo-distribution barrier. It is explicitly not
a resolution.

`fixed41_bv_all_harmonics.md` proves, by exact finite LDL checks plus an
analytic parity-tail argument, that the strongest stored fixed-41
pseudo-distribution passes every harmonic degree with unrestricted radial
degree. It also proves all of its ordinary two-point moments positive. This is
an all-degree three-point-relaxation barrier, not a spherical code.

`degree2_bv_barrier.md` gives an exact labeled 41-vertex pseudo-Gram object
that survives all \(3\times3\) principal minors and all BV blocks through
total degree two.  It then derives a simple degree-three residual-vector
square that every genuine common Gram source satisfies and that the
pseudo-object violates exactly.  The note localizes the failure to colored
deep--middle wedges but does not yet prove a universal upper bound.

`max_volume_semialgebraic_reduction.md` gives a bidirectionally exact compact
190-variable formulation of a hypothetical 41-code. It proves a strict frame
lower bound, a rational lower bound on a maximum-volume basis determinant, and
the resulting hierarchy of coefficient-minor pruning inequalities. It is a
certified reduction, not a nonexistence proof.

`local_link_geometry.md` proves exact projection and link bounds and records an
exact contact-free inclusion-maximal code. `antipodal_bound.md` proves the
restricted sharp antipodal bound 40, and `negative_tail_graph.md` turns it
into a universal forced-negative graph constraint for a hypothetical
41-point code.

`local_hybrid_barrier.md` proves exact Pfender, deep-cap, common-center, and
rank-deficit inequalities. It also supplies an exact mass-41 two-point
pseudo-measure surviving their aggregate scalar consequences, so it is a
barrier report rather than an upper-bound proof.

`weighted_residual_barrier.md`, `local_hybrid_degree3_barrier.md`, and
`local_hybrid_degree3_rank_barrier.md`, and
`local_hybrid_degree3_rank_color_barrier.md` record successive exact triple
pseudo-measures.  Together they distinguish degree-three BV positivity,
rank-five spectral moments, wedge capacities, color-degree covariance, and
individual-color graphicality instead of conflating those independent
necessary conditions.

`fixed41_rank_mixture_separator.md` exactly rejects the direct convex mixture
of the all-degree and rank-feasible witnesses with one finite \(H_{3,9}\)
radial direction.  It is a scoped barrier, not a universal separation.

`rank_five_spectral_moment.md` and `rank_five_four_cycle_moments.md` prove
sharp spectral inequalities and exact cycle expansions forced by rank five.
The missing all-distinct four-cycle statistic is stated explicitly.

`sparse_deep_graph_stability.md` classifies the 23- and 24-edge deep graphs,
proves angular and root-system obstructions, and supplies a rank-20
countermodel to the aggregate kernel route.
`quantitative_root_system_stability.md` makes its zero-slack argument
effective by exact determinant rounding.

`split_kernel_abstract_barrier.md` and
`split_kernel_full_interval_barrier.md` give exact 41-row counterexamples to
progressively stronger abstractions of the quadratic kernel.  They identify
the original kissing entries and nonlinear common-source identity as the
remaining indispensable information.

`tverberg_moment_barrier.md` proves the exact degree-two Tverberg consequence
at 41 points and gives an exact rank-five code showing why that consequence
alone is insufficient.

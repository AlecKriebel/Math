### Adversarially rejected rank shortcut

- For any five-dimensional code, the entrywise kernel
  \(K_{ij}=(G_{ij}+1)(G_{ij}-1/2)\) has diagonal one, nonpositive
  off-diagonal entries, rank at most \(1+5+14=20\), and at most one negative
  eigenvalue.
- The tempting generic lemma `rank(K) >= N/2` is false.  Applying the same
  construction to the exact 60-point \(D_6\) root system gives a matrix of
  rank at most \(1+6+20=27<30\), still PSD minus one rank-one term.
- Any viable rank route must therefore use information special to dimension
  five beyond this sign and inertia pattern.

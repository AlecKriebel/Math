# Research log

## 2026-07-23

- Audited the proposed universal half-rank lemma for
  \(M=W-4I\), with negative diagonal, nonnegative off diagonal, and positive
  inertia at most one.
- Found an exact order-41 counterexample from the point graph of the
  symplectic generalized quadrangle \(W(3,3)\), plus one isolated vertex.
  Its shifted matrix has off-diagonal entries \(0,2\), inertia
  \((1,16,24)\), and rank seventeen.
- Checked that the counterexample even satisfies
  \(M\preceq(6/5)J\), with PSD remainder of rank seventeen.
- Identified and certified the missing common-source information.  If this
  \(W\) came from a rank-five Gram matrix, its degree-two harmonic Gram
  matrix would simultaneously satisfy
  \(\operatorname{tr}H_2^2\leq923/8\) and
  \(\operatorname{tr}H_2^2\geq1681/14\), an exact gap of \(263/56\).
- Proved the sharp surviving semidefinite statement: the half-rank bound
  holds when the positive inertia is zero, but fails as soon as one positive
  eigenvalue is allowed.

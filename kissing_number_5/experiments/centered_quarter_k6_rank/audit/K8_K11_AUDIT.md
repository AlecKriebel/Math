# Independent audit of the K8--K11 local hierarchy

Audit timestamp: 2026-07-24T05:36Z.

## Result

The exact direct K8, K9, K10, and K11 local certificates pass audit, as do
the two K8/K9 frozen-support nonextension certificates, the K10
frozen-support size certificate, the exhaustive per-source K11 extension
catalog, and the new maximal-extension certificate for the 51 selected K11
atoms.  Every result has the narrow scope stated below.  The direct hierarchy
does not imply a global 41-point code.  The K11 maximal-extension theorem
excludes a quarter-grid 41-point code containing one of its 51 selected
atoms, but does not classify all K11 atoms and therefore does not prove a
global upper bound.

## Direct-certificate checks

The independent script in this directory imports none of the shipped
verifiers.
It uses exact rational Gaussian elimination, rather than the shipped
fraction-free Bareiss determinant, on every principal submatrix of every
stored atom.  It checked 13,005 K8, 26,061 K9, 52,173 K10, and 104,397
K11 principal minors.  All are nonnegative, every minor of order at least six
is zero, and every atom has a positive fifth-order minor.  The minimum positive
normalized fifth minor in all four certificates is \(3/512\).  Thus all 204
atoms are PSD of rank exactly five.

The audit independently rebuilt all edge and triangle counts.  The weights are
strictly positive and sum to one; the edge marginal is exactly
\(\alpha/40\), and the triangle marginal is exactly \(\nu/1560\).  The source
normalizations are \(\sum\alpha=40\) and \(\sum\nu=1560\).  The value \(1/2\)
is an exact grid endpoint, so no strict-inequality or rounding issue occurs.

The numerical LP reports and exact reconstruction stages also regenerated
byte-for-byte under the recorded environment.  The exact certificates do not
trust the LP residuals.

The exact range-equation generation is complete for its stated source set.
Each source atom is PSD of rank five, so it has a positive-definite
five-vertex principal block \(B\).  Any PSD rank-at-most-five one-vertex
extension must put the new vector in the span of that block.  Its five grid
correlations \(w\) are therefore among the enumerated \(7^5\) choices, its
norm is exactly the equation
\(w^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{adj}(B)w=4\det B\), and every remaining
correlation is forced by \(h^{\mathsf T}\operatorname{adj}(B)w/\det B\).
Conversely those equations construct a rank-five PSD extension.  Permuting a
source representative does not lose triangle-count vectors because it can be
relabelled back before adding the last vertex.  Deduplicating equal
triangle-count vectors is safe for the marginal LP.

## Frozen-support enumeration checks for K8 and K9

For K7 to K8, expanding the 51 source orbits gives 221,340 distinct labeled
K7 matrices.  Grouping them by the common labeled K6 face gives the audited
distribution

```text
group size:       1       2      3    4    6    8
number:      168660   19080   3600  360  240  105
```

Its zeroth, first, and second size moments are respectively 192,045 overlap
keys, 221,340 supported faces, and 298,500 ordered joins.  Each join fixes all
K8 edges except edge 67.  Trying its seven grid colors gives exactly 2,089,500
cases.  Any K8 whose eight K7 faces are supported appears in this enumeration
by taking its faces deleting vertices 7 and 6.  The exact enumeration finds
zero survivors.

For K8 to K9, expanding the 51 source orbits gives 1,824,480 distinct labeled
K8 matrices.  Grouping by the common labeled K7 face gives

```text
group size:         1       2      3      4     6    8   24
number:       1506960   97020  15960  11340  3360  630  210
```

The corresponding moments are 1,635,480 overlap keys, 1,824,480 supported
faces, and 2,502,360 ordered joins.  The seven colors of edge 78 give
17,516,520 cases.

The packed core was inspected edge-by-edge.  Its first record is the face
deleting vertex 8 and its second is the face deleting vertex 7.  For every
other deleted vertex \(d=0,\ldots,6\), the first 27 packed local K8 edges are
exactly the edges inherited from those two records, while the omitted 28th
local edge is the same global edge 78.  The intersection of the seven
allowed-color masks is therefore exactly equivalent to testing all seven
remaining faces for each color.  The two source faces are supported by
construction.  Hence every supported K9 is covered.  The full run finds zero
survivors.  Existing tests also compare this packed method with a separately
written tuple-based brute-force oracle on small supports.

As an additional independent differential test, the core was compared with a
direct 36-edge tuple oracle on the union of five structured K8 orbits (constant
color, one marked edge, one marked star, one marked triangle, and a marked
4+4 cut).  The 128 labeled faces formed 99 overlap groups and 242 ordered
joins, hence 1,694 explicit color trials.  Both implementations found exactly
130 supported labeled K9 trials.

Since both face-count matrices have no columns while the frozen target has a
positive coordinate, the recorded one-coordinate Farkas vectors and negative
target pairings are exact.

## K10 frozen-support size audit

The K10 frozen-support artifact is a size certificate, not a nonextension
certificate.  The exact orbit computation verifies that the 51 selected K9
orbits contain 16,057,440 distinct labeled K9 matrices:

\[
1(90{,}720)+12(181{,}440)+38(362{,}880)=16{,}057{,}440.
\]

Any complete gluing by common K8 faces has at least one ordered self-pair per
record, and every pair requires seven colors for the missing edge.  Hence the
search has at least 112,402,080 exact color trials.  This proves the stated
resource lower bound only; it supplies neither an extension nor an
obstruction.

## K11 exhaustive per-source catalog

For each of the 51 stored labeled K10 atoms, the catalog verifier independently
enumerates every one-vertex quarter-grid rank-five extension using the exact
basis equations.  It reproduces 1,642 labeled K11 patterns and 1,508 distinct
triangle-count vectors.  The all-extension and deduplicated catalogs are both
authenticated by the hashes above.  This exhausts extensions of those 51
labeled K10 atoms, not all possible K11 atoms.

## Exact maximal extensions of the selected K11 atoms

The support-specific theorem in
`../k11/maximal_extension/EXACT_THEOREM.md` was independently reproduced.
For each selected K11 atom, the verifier fixes a checked positive-definite
five-vertex basis and exhausts all \(7^5=16{,}807\) possible scaled
correlation rows.  The unit-norm equation and all six forced source
correlations are checked with integers.  It then regenerates the candidate
compatibility graph.

For each graph, the certificate supplies both a clique of size \(m\) and a
proper coloring with \(m\) colors.  These elementary witnesses prove the
clique number exactly without trusting the branch-and-bound search.  Candidate
sets have size 12--71, maximum additional-point counts are 7--29, and exact
maximum total sizes are 18--40.  The atoms reaching 40 are

```text
6, 7, 9, 10, 18, 23, 27, 41, 43, 44, 47, 49, 50.
```

Thus none of the 51 selected atoms can occur in a quarter-grid K41.  This
statement is exact but support-specific: no theorem here says that every K11
subcode of a hypothetical quarter-grid K41 belongs to this list.  The
certificate also makes no statement about non-grid inner products.

The maximal-extension verifier uses explicit exceptions rather than
`assert`, pins both source and witness hashes, reconstructs the source
rank-five Gram representation, and passes unchanged under `python -O`.
Five tests include altered coloring, clique, candidate-count, and hash
witnesses, all of which are rejected.

## Classification and construction follow-up

The thirteen stored clique witnesses of total size 40 were reconstructed as
complete exact Gram matrices and classified by explicit Gram-preserving
permutations.  Atoms 6 and 23 yield \(D_5\); the other eleven yield \(L_5\).
None of these particular completions is \(Q_5\) or \(R_5\).  The exact
classification certificate exports all 520 rational-over-\(\sqrt2\)
coordinate rows and all thirteen complete Gram matrices; its SHA-256 is

```text
ccabd04602c5481d40fa16d5979a7cbcb04fa3ece357f3c97d39e881f1bef0a0
```

This classifies the stored maximum cliques, not every maximum clique for each
K11 atom.

A two-mode, 26-run numerical N41 challenge was then seeded from all thirteen
completions.  Direct insertion plus unrestricted release and one-to-two
replacement plus unrestricted release both failed to reach \(1/2\).  The best
recomputed maximum was 0.5213989457472671, worse than the repository's prior
0.514994652512166 near miss.  These are explicitly labeled numerical
observations, not obstructions.  The deterministic result hash is

```text
2049ff1827e1f30298bf9a289be9773e498dd1f0dc4c5adb24f7a104c1c99465
```

## Reproduction and toolchain notes

Run the independent direct audit with:

```sh
PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python \
  experiments/centered_quarter_k6_rank/audit/independent_direct_certificate_audit.py
```

All shipped K8--K11 verifiers and their tests pass under CPython 3.14.6.
The K8/K9 verifiers also pass under CPython 3.11.8.  Python 3.10 or newer is
required by syntax and `zip(..., strict=True)`.  The K10/K11 direct verifiers
use the same standard-library mechanisms.

Two cautions should be retained in clean-environment instructions:

1. The shipped K8--K11 direct and catalog verifiers express checks with Python
   `assert`; they must not be invoked with `python -O`.  The independent
   principal-minor audit and the new maximal-extension verifier use explicit
   exceptions and remain active under `-O`.
2. The K9 frozen-support core and K10 size-audit core use
   `unsigned __int128`; the K9 core also uses `__builtin_popcount`.  They build
   with the recorded Apple Clang 21 toolchain and should build with GCC/Clang,
   but these are compiler extensions rather than portable ISO C++20 and are
   not expected to build with MSVC unchanged.  The README descriptions of
   these cores as “C++20 standard-library” are therefore too broad.

One harmless wording correction is advisable in all four direct proof notes:
“56/84/120/165 triangle types” means triangle **faces**; there are 51
triangle orbit types.

# Research log

- Tested whether greatest-family reciprocity might follow from a static
  complementary-exchange symmetry.  A random equality-static graph
  `HwFY?]B` immediately refuted that idea: one exchange is non-dominating
  and its complement is deleted in the first kernel round.
- Wrote a deterministic deletion-rank census and ran it over all 261,080
  connected unlabeled order-nine graphs emitted by the local nauty `geng`.

  eternal-equality graphs, but no one-sided greatest-family survivor.
- Finite deletion ranks can differ and reach round three.  The surviving
  invariant is therefore coinductive winning status, not static domination
  or equality of deletion ranks.

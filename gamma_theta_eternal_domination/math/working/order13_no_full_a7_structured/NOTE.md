\[
  \gamma(G)=\gamma^\infty(G)=3<\theta(G).
\tag{1.1}
\]

\[
  \gamma(G)=i(G)=\alpha(G)=\gamma^\infty(G)=3.
\tag{1.2}
\]

\[
  S=\{a,b,c\}.
\]

Every maximum independent set belongs to every optimal eternal family, so
\(S\in\mathcal F\).  For \(x\notin S\), put

\[
  L(x)=\{u\in S:S-u+x\in\mathcal F\},
  \qquad
  \sigma(x)=N_{\overline G}(x)\cap S.
\tag{1.3}
\]

response target at order \(13\), so

\[
  1\le |L(x)|\le2
  \qquad(x\notin S).
\tag{1.4}
\]

and permute the three anchors so that their omitted colors are \(a\) and
\(c\).  For each type, the accepted physical-representative and

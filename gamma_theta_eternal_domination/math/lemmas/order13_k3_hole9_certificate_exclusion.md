## The branch theorem

### Theorem

Relative to C-050 and the accepted inputs of C-055, there is no graph \(G\)
of order 13 satisfying

\[
 \gamma(G)=\gamma^\infty(G)=3<\theta(G)
\tag{1}
\]

for which \(H=\overline G\) contains a hub-free induced \(C_9\).

Consequently, after also applying the accepted near-spanning-hole theorem
C-053, every order-13 counterexample with common parameter three would have a
hub-free induced \(C_5\) or \(C_7\) in its complement.  Equivalently, the
live parameter-three template cover reduces from

\[
 \{F^{13}_5,F^{13}_7,F^{13}_9\}
\quad\text{to}\quad
 \{F^{13}_5,F^{13}_7\}.
\tag{2}
\]

### Proof

Suppose for contradiction that \(G\) satisfies (1) and
\(H=\overline G\) contains a hub-free induced \(C_9\).

The parameter chain collapses between its equal endpoints:

\[
 \gamma(G)=i(G)=\alpha(G)=\gamma^\infty(G)=3.
\tag{3}
\]

In particular, no two-set dominates \(G\).  By the pair/common-neighbor
dictionary in C-055, every vertex pair in \(H\) has an external common
\(H\)-neighbor.

Orient and label the selected induced cycle

\[
 0,1,\ldots,8
\]

cyclically.  The endpoints \(0,1\) of its named rim edge have an external
common \(H\)-neighbor; label one such vertex \(9\), and label the remaining
three vertices arbitrarily as \(10,11,12\).  This is only a relabeling of the
whole graph.  It introduces no signature order, reflection constraint,
DoubleLex condition, or unrelated anchor.

Because \(\gamma^\infty(G)=3\), there is a nonempty one-guard eternal family
of dominating triples.  Because \(\alpha(G)=3\), \(H\) has no \(K_4\), while
\(\{0,1,9\}\) is an \(H\)-triangle.  Because \(\gamma(G)=3\), every \(H\)-pair
has an external common neighbor.  The chosen rim is induced and hub-free.
Relative to C-050, \(G\) is connected by the component-additivity argument in
C-055.  Finally,

\[

so every proper three-coloring of \(H\) is forbidden by the complete

**Audit date:** 16 August 2026 (America/Los_Angeles)
**Target:** the final ConStRAINeD status correction and bioRxiv upload materials
**Outcome:** correction accepted; no mathematical or presentation regression

## ConStRAINeD status adjudication

The live official ConStRAINeD results page was rechecked. Its overview
describes the two-dimensional result as a complete proof and sketches the
approach. On the same page, item 16 lists the corresponding five-author
manuscript under “In preparation.” An exact-title search located conference
announcements but no public manuscript.

The current manuscript and bibliography therefore state both facets and retain
the qualified “we did not locate a public manuscript” language. The comparison
of public scopes remains unchanged: the announced result fixes two species and
permits broader reaction-graph structure, while the present theorem permits
arbitrarily many species under one linkage class and molecularity at most two.
Neither is represented as superseding the other.

## bioRxiv workflow audit

The official submission guide, portal help, scope/categories page, FAQ,
screening description, funder-field announcement, DOI policy, and license
update guide were rechecked. The new human-only upload checklist records:

- one main PDF and one separately posted supplementary PDF;
- Systems Biology and New Results;
- exact title, author, affiliation, email, ORCID, abstract source, keywords,
  competing-interest statement, and data/code disclosures;
- no funder and no grant number in the dedicated funding fields;
- the manuscript AI disclosure and a concise fallback only if a live portal
  field is presented;
- final proof approval, acknowledgment, manuscript-identifier, and post checks;
  and
- automatic `10.64898` DOI generation at final author approval, rather than
  the obsolete claim that no DOI exists until posting.

The checklist does not perform an upload or external communication. It directs
the author to recheck the dated literature status immediately before final
approval and to prepare a successor release if the date or public record has
changed.

## Fresh adversarial review

After the edit, the shared manuscript source, all three main PDFs, and the
supplementary-note PDF were reread and rendered. The theorem and load-bearing
proof are unchanged. The new status sentence is accurately attributed,
cautious in tone, and introduces no scope, citation, line-breaking, or page-
layout problem. The title, abstract, declarations, repository pointers, upload
instructions, and metadata were cross-checked for consistency.

The complete Version 1.2.0 verifier and all three canonical report copies are
carried byte-for-byte from Version 1.2.1. Computational checks remain
falsification and reproducibility aids, not part of the universal proof.

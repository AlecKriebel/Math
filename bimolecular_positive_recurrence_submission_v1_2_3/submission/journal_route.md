# Later journal route: recommendation and submission-day checklist

## Recommendation

Submit first to the Applied Probability journals, expecting assignment to the
*Journal of Applied Probability* (JAP). The theorem is a concise qualitative
positive-recurrence result for a concrete CTMC class, directly extends a 2020
JAP result, and uses Foster, trace-chain, and regenerative arguments familiar
to that readership. The joint JAP/AAP submission route lets the Applied
Probability editors determine which of the two journals is the better fit.

*Stochastic Processes and their Applications* (SPA) is a credible fallback,
especially if the presentation is broadened beyond reaction-network readers,
but it requires more submission-specific conversion and is less directly tied
to the paper being extended.

This recommendation concerns fit, not acceptance probability. Recheck the
live journal instructions when the journal submission is actually made.

## Intended sequence

1. Submit the Version 1.2.3 preprint to bioRxiv Systems Biology as a New Result.
2. After posting, add the bioRxiv DOI and posting date to the manuscript
   preprint-status note, cover letter, repository page, references, and the
   portal fields specifically designated for preprints or related identifiers.
3. Submit the journal PDF through the joint Applied Probability route and
   disclose the public bioRxiv preprint plainly. Keep the supplementary note
   in the repository/archival package rather than uploading it to JAP unless
   an editor requests it.
4. Do not simultaneously submit to SPA or any other journal.

## Applied Probability/JAP submission-day checks

- Upload `manuscript/main_jap.pdf` as the identified manuscript for the current
  single-anonymous review model. The main paper is self-contained; do not
  upload `manuscript/supplementary_note.pdf` unless an editor requests it.
- Use the 4--10-line nontechnical abstract already selected by
  `main_jap.tex`.
- Confirm title, author name, affiliation, ORCID, correspondence, funding,
  competing interests, data/code availability, and AI-use declaration in
  their corresponding portal fields; enter the bioRxiv DOI only where the
  portal requests preprints or related identifiers.
- Recheck the review model at submission time; the present instructions use
  single-anonymous review and therefore identified files.
- Confirm that the manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere and state
  that the bioRxiv record is an unrefereed preprint.
- Recheck the journal's length, source-file, reference, AI, and supplementary
  material instructions on the submission date.

## SPA fallback conversion still required

Do not submit the present JAP wrapper directly to SPA. Before an SPA
submission, complete and verify all of the following:

- supply the author's full postal affiliation, country, and telephone number
  in the submission system or manuscript as required;
- reduce the keyword list to no more than seven;
- convert references and citations to SPA/Elsevier's required numbered style;
- provide the requested glossary or define specialist terms for the broader
  stochastic-processes audience;
- upload editable LaTeX source and all associated files, not only the PDF;
- create the declaration document with Elsevier's current declarations tool;
- use Elsevier's exact required generative-AI heading and add method-level
  disclosure of substantive research use where the live policy requires it;
  and
- regenerate the cover letter and all portal metadata for SPA rather than
  reusing the Applied Probability versions.

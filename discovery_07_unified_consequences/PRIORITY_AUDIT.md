# Priority and scope audit

Audit cutoff: **22 July 2026**. This is a source-bounded research audit, not a
proof of worldwide priority or independent mathematical validation.

## Bottom line

The paper's defensible center is not the number of named conjectures affected.
It is the explicit, coefficient-complete mechanism joining one formal inverse
series to two operator obstructions:

- an every-exponent counterexample to the Special Image Conjecture in
  dimension 14;
- every-order nonvanishing for explicit 30-variable degree-eight and
  44-variable quartic homogeneous Hessian-nilpotent polynomials.

The 14-variable map's Campbell, Chamberland--Meisters, Kulikov, stability, and
fixed-point consequences are direct transformations of one noninjective
constant-spectrum map. They are presented as one cascade, not as independent
discoveries.

## Classical ingredients and prior work

- Bass--Connell--Wright established the formal-inverse and degree-reduction
  framework for the Jacobian conjecture.
- Kulikov recorded the nilpotent-map formulation `JN(n)` and the classical
  dimension bookkeeping behind degree reduction.
- de Bondt and van den Essen gave the reduction to symmetric Jacobian maps.
- Zhao developed the Hessian-nilpotent inverse formula, the Vanishing
  Conjecture, and the Image Conjecture framework.
- Following Dayan Liu and Xiaosong Sun's terminology, the relevant
  polynomial-ring specialization is the Special Image Conjecture `SIC(n)`.
- Campbell and Chamberland--Meisters stated the injectivity and fixed-point
  conjectures quoted in the manuscript.

None of those general reductions or conjecture formulations is claimed as
new. The numerical identity `3 + (4-2) + (6-2) + (7-2) = 14` is classical
degree bookkeeping; the abstract possibility of dimension 14 is not a
discovery.

## Narrow candidate contributions

Subject to expert verification and a necessarily incomplete literature
search, this artifact supplies:

1. the exact sparse 14-variable regular-nilpotent realization with its reduced
   rational three-point fiber and weighted conjugacy of the punctured pencil;
2. a fixed multiplier proving `SIC(14)` failure at every positive exponent by
   closed coefficient formulas;
3. a 30-variable homogeneous degree-eight Hessian-nilpotent polynomial whose
   Vanishing obstruction is nonzero at every order;
4. an every-order upgrade of the explicit 44-variable homogeneous quartic
   certificate, again by closed formulas rather than finite extrapolation;
5. one self-contained transfer theorem explaining why these are manifestations
   of the same inverse-series mechanism.

At the cutoff, no earlier public artifact was located with this exact
14-variable map, an explicit `SIC(n)` witness for `n <= 14`, or the stated
every-order formula for the 44-variable quartic. Fast-moving public, private,
or unindexed work may supersede this observation.

Zihan Zhang's 20 July note already derives existential failure of the Image
and quartic Vanishing Conjectures from the announced counterexample and asks
for a small explicit quartic witness. The present priority claim is therefore
about the explicit fixed-dimensional, sparse, every-order realizations—not the
bare logical consequences. Christopher Long's 20 July paper concerns explicit
Gaussian Moments counterexamples and is not claimed here.

## What is not claimed

- No global minimum dimension or monomial count is claimed. The 14-variable
  construction is optimal only inside the precisely stated constant-state
  realization ansatz proved in Discovery 06.
- SIC failure is not used to infer conjectures connected to it only by a
  one-way implication in the opposite direction.
- The classical Markus--Yamabe and discrete Markus--Yamabe problems already
  had older counterexamples. The present construction only gives another
  rigid realization.
- The integral-coefficient conjugate and the pencil member with integral
  collision points are different normal forms.
- The 30D and 44D statements concern different tradeoffs. Neither is called
  unconditionally smallest.
- Exact computation verifies encoded identities; it does not establish
  literature priority or replace peer review.

## Permitted language

Use “explicit,” “every exponent,” “every order,” “the smallest explicit
dimension located in our audit at the cutoff,” and “optimal within the stated
ansatz.” Avoid “first ever,” “smallest possible,” “solves all these problems
independently,” or a conjecture tally that obscures the common transformation
family.

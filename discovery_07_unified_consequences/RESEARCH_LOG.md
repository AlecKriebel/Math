## Archival decision

Discoveries 03, 05, and 06 remain public as timestamped technical precursors
and certificate sources. Their claims are consolidated here rather than
counted as separate current papers. Discovery 04 remains independent because
its iterated monodromy results do not depend on the inverse-series transfer.

The default destination for future corollaries of this mechanism is this log
or an appendix to Discovery 07. A new discovery number is reserved for a
meaningful extremal improvement, a new explicit reduction class, or another
conceptual theorem.

# Outcome Q adversarial audit plan

Status: **CLOSED — OUTCOME Q REJECTED; NOT AUTHORIZED FOR INTEGRATION**

Outcome Q is treated as an untrusted candidate extension of the independently
verified Outcome P theorem.  No file in `unpacked/` is an active theorem input
until the gates below are closed.

## Locked gates

1. **Package integrity.** Verify the outer archive, both internal manifests,
   the embedded Git bundle, and clean regeneration from primitive inputs.
2. **Convention fidelity.** Compare `sd0`, cleanup, and Holtgrefe conventions
   literally against the cited definitions; reject any silent treatment of
   mixed parallel artifacts, 2-sub-blobs, or rooting fibres.
3. **Structural zipper theorem.** Independently prove or refute that the stated
   root zipper is the only additional tree-child cleanup presentation and
   that its contraction produces an admissible already-simple rooting.
4. **JC model theorem.** Independently derive the complete two-terminal
   Fourier tensor, strict analytic section, rank statement, and contextual
   contraction claim on the open parameter domain.
5. **Class and frontier claims.** Rebuild the rooting-fibre and strictness
   witnesses without importing the primary implementation.
6. **Quotient theorem.** Check well-definedness of the cleanup model image and
   every logical implication used to transfer one-sided containment and
   symmetric overlap from Outcome P.
7. **Mutation sensitivity.** Add adversarial changes targeting omitted cleanup
   rootings, altered arrowheads, non-simple outputs, boundary parameters,
   false literal convention equality, and unjustified 2-sub-blob erasure.
8. **Manuscript and release.** Review every PDF page, source statement,
   dependency edge, transcript, and clean-clone command.

## Release rule

Outcome Q may be incorporated only after the main audit and independent
reviewers agree that every load-bearing gate is verified.  A verifier that
prints `PASS` is evidence only for the assertions it actually recomputes.

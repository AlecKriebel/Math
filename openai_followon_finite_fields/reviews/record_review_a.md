### Algebraic and complexity checks

- For squarefree degree `u`, CRT gives component fields of degrees `m*u_a`; the `q`-fixed algebra has dimension `s` over `K`, whereas the `p`-fixed algebra has dimension `s` over `F_p` in ambient dimension `um`. Treating `p`-Frobenius as `K`-linear would be wrong; the manuscript and implementation use the correct base fields.
- The trace proof needs all `m` power-basis coordinates. Its nondegeneracy proof survives `p|m` even when `Tr(1)=0`. The displayed quotient-algebra trace, unlike coefficientwise trace, does give the component traces needed for gcd separation.
- A `p`-fixed coordinate’s first power relation is the product over its distinct occurring `F_p` values. It is squarefree, split, and has degree at most `s`. The reference code checks prime-oracle root products exactly before using them. No integer factorization or primitive-root task is hidden in this interface.
- Trace calls are bounded by `ms`, direct calls by `s`; degree sums by `ms^2` or `s^2`. Across disjoint squarefree supports whose degrees sum to at most `n`, these give `mn`, `n`, `mn^2`, `n^2` as claimed. The displayed loose nonoracle arithmetic counts cover powering, linear algebra, repeated dependence solves and gcd refinement. Field coordinates have bounded residue bit lengths; no rational coefficient heights enter.
- Multiplicity preprocessing was checked factor by factor. The derivative-zero residual goes through the inverse coefficient Frobenius `a -> a^(p^(m-1))`, with exponents having `O(mL)` bits. This is necessary for extensions and is explicitly implemented. No loop of numerical length `p^(m-1)` is being charged as polynomial.
- The representation test uses modular Frobenius gcds for every degree through `floor(m/2)` and the `p^m` identity. A reducible modulus has a factor in that range, including a repeated-factor modulus; degree one works. Primality is an explicit promise for the programs and may be checked independently in the mathematical model.
- Construction degree is numeric `d`, with lifted degree `N=md`; the lifted dense input has `O(dm^2L)` bits, still polynomial in supplied-field input plus dense output. Trial division of `N` does not purport to factor arbitrary binary integers efficiently. The Shoup variants’ systems have dimensions at most `N^2`, inputs of degree at most `N^2`, and polynomially many prime-factor calls. I checked the odd-prime root-of-unity/order argument, the trace generator proof, the exceptional `2`-power branch, the Artin–Schreier trace certificate and coprime-compositum generation argument; no gap was found.
- The repaired `F_3` counterexample to a blanket nonsquare-to-arbitrary-`2`-power binomial rule is real. The package retains it and does not use the false rule. Fixed-`r` extraction is only the dense `X^r-a` factoring consequence; its cost is polynomial in numeric `r`, not binary `log r`.

### Pivotal upstream interfaces

Family 142’s table/factor recursion reduces `Phi_q` to degree `q-1`, using only previously built table entries. This is a strict induction, rather than a factoring oracle assumed while proving factoring. The norm proof’s unknown solution is used to justify a promise, not supplied to the algorithm; the local orders and computed global-section fiber are the effective inputs. The fiber-idempotent argument uses the actual parabolic shape and trace-pairing radical, so a generic unsupported search for a rank-one matrix is not substituted. The geometry retains an infinity *divisor* lattice coordinate, not just its Jacobian class, and the filtration/Frobenius argument gives field extension degree `q^v_q(N) <= N`.

For auxiliary primes, the application field is `Q(mu_(12q))`, and `p` is unramified there in the large-characteristic branch. Eisenstein gives the cyclic Kummer extension of degree `q`. Consequently its zeta factorization needs finite-order Hecke characters over varying cyclotomic bases. The narrower family-003 Dirichlet/Eisenstein-field statements cannot replace this input. The ledger and manuscript correctly name family 029 Theorem 1.2, not a nonexistent all-field Lean proof.

I checked the additive sieve’s norm-separation/box scaling, the zero detector’s residue cancellation and density exponent, the good-row reciprocal estimate, contour directions and numerical powers, and final Mellin holomorphy. Field-dependent constants and onsets in family 029 do not change its final absolute exclusion width. Auxiliary size is charged numerically (`E`), never as `log E`. I found no interface mismatch or circular use of the target zero-free strip in these inspected steps.

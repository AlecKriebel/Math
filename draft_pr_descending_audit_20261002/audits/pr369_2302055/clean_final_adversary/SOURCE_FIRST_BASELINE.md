# Source-first baseline (immutable)

The original problem is Hayman–Lingham (2018), Problem 2.55, printed page 48/PDF page 49 (locally extracted lines 2085–2093): for every three nonconstant entire functions f_i:C→C and every ambient entire F:C³→C bounded on V={f_1(z_1)+f_2(z_2)+f_3(z_3)=0}, must F|_V be one constant? The conclusion concerns the restriction, not global constancy of F. Singular points, possible disconnected fibers, omitted values, and arbitrary inverse singular sets are included. A finite family of examples cannot answer this universal problem.

Independently retrieved primary source baseline:

- Demailly's Bulletin paper (1979), original printed pp. 179–180/PDF pp. 1–2: the n≥3 separated-variable hypersurface is stated irreducible by Rubel–Squires–Taylor; Demailly expressly says the Liouville answer is positive whenever one summand is rational. For entire summands, rational means polynomial. This already covers every polynomial-coordinate subclass, including affine coordinates. The stated irreducibility attribution is not independently proved here.
- Demailly's exact two-exponential curve S={e^x+e^y=1} has no nonconstant bounded holomorphic functions; Theorem 2 gives stronger polynomial-growth extension. His CRAS note, p.40/PDF p.2, Corollary 2, expressly proves bounded holomorphic functions on {e^x+e^y=f(w)} are constant for nonzero meromorphic f. Therefore a two-affine-exponential summand special case with arbitrary third nonconstant entire summand is prior art. The f(w)=0 exceptional fiber is a union of lines and must be handled separately in any fiber argument. Demailly p.190/PDF p.12 observes finite connected coverings preserve Liouville; finite-cover proofs are established mechanisms.
- Lin–Zaidenberg (1997), all spaces assumed connected, Definition 1.3 and Theorem 1.6: an ultra-Liouville complex base has Liouville connected hypernilpotent Galois coverings. A Zariski open subset of a compact complex space is ultra-Liouville. Merely Liouville is insufficient as a base; arbitrary solvable or arbitrary abelian covers over merely Liouville spaces cannot be substituted. Their Remark 1.9.2 explicitly treats {e^x+e^y=1} as a Z² cover of C\{0,1}, Liouville but not ultra-Liouville.
- Rempe–Gillen–Sixsmith (2016), Definition 2.1 and Remark 2.2: singular values are the closed critical/asymptotic obstruction set; removing the entire closed set yields a covering. Critical-value discreteness alone does not yield a global covering. Lemma 2.8 requires an isolated singular value and excludes critical values in the chosen neighborhood.

Independent deductions: an affine summand makes V an entire graph over C² and so bounded ambient restrictions are constant by two-variable Liouville. For a polynomial coordinate of degree d, elementary symmetric functions of the d bounded branch values can be globally holomorphic through branching, yielding constants and hence a finite image for F|_V. A further connectedness/irreducibility or intersection argument is required to conclude one constant. Infinite inverse fibers preclude this finite symmetric construction. For a regular pullback over a connected ultra-Liouville base, connected abelian cover components inherit Liouville; distinct components can still carry different constants until a bridging mechanism is proved.

2. A below-2 Waldschmidt restriction is not the gap-one condition. Let k be any field and R=k[x0,x1,x2,x3]. Let Z be the reduced union of the six coordinate lines, with radical ideal I=intersection over 0<=i<j<=3 of (xi,xj). Each minimal prime is generated by two variables. Localizing there makes the other variables units, and contraction of its mth localized power is (xi,xj)^m. Thus the symbolic power is the intersection of these six ordinary prime powers. These are monomial ideals, so a polynomial belongs precisely when each of its nonzero monomial terms belongs. A monomial x0^a0...x3^a3 belongs to I^(m) exactly when ai+aj>=m for all six pairs.

For m=1 this forces at least three positive exponents: two zero exponents violate their pair constraint. Degree three is attained, e.g. x0*x1*x2. Hence alpha(I)=3. For m=2 summing the six inequalities gives 3*(a0+a1+a2+a3)>=12, so degree>=4. The monomial x0*x1*x2*x3 attains it. Thus alpha(I^(2))=4=alpha(I)+1.

For general m, the same sum gives total degree>=2m. For m=2q, (x0*x1*x2*x3)^q attains degree4q=2m. For m=2q+1, total degree2m=4q+2 cannot occur: equality in the summed inequalities would force all six pair sums to equal the odd integer m, hence all ai=m/2, impossible for integral exponents. The exponent vector (q,q+1,q+1,q+1) attains degree4q+3 and satisfies every pair. Therefore alpha(I^(2q))=4q and alpha(I^(2q+1))=4q+3. Dividing by m and taking the limit yields Waldschmidt constant exactly2.

This is an exact algebraic example in every characteristic. It proves that a strict below-2 hypothesis does not include all gap-one line arrangements. It does NOT authenticate the hypothesis, statements, corollaries or full scope of HM2021, whose final body remains missing. Nor does it exclude possible separate conclusions in that paper or subsequent papers.

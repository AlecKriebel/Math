Bergman Section 3 defines (3): every increasing countable subgroup exhaustion reaches G. Finitely generated groups satisfy it. Condition (4), denoted CB here: for every group generating subset U there is a finite bound on lengths of words using U and U^{-1}. MB is the analogous positive-word condition for every subset U whose positive words exhaust G. The bound may depend on U. No topology/cardinality restrictions.

MB implies CB by applying MB to U union U^{-1}. Bergman proves (3) plus CB implies MB: W_j=(U union {1})^j intersection (U^{-1} union {1})^j is symmetric, exhausts G, and some generated subgroup <W_j> is G; CB gives bounded W_j word length. Unbounded symmetric subadditive length refutes (3) plus CB, not necessarily CB alone (Lemma 10).

Question 9 has both directions on the residual CB without (3) class. Kourovka separation seeks only CB without MB. A positive cone that group generates but does not monoid generate is excluded. Bergman Lemma 11's proper monoid is therefore not a separation.

Independent initial constraints: any separator is infinite and not finitely generated, has countable cofinality, and requires an actual positively generating set with unbounded positive lengths. Finite examples are algebraic controls only; no finite simulation or arbitrary unbounded length can certify or falsify CB in an infinite candidate without its quantifiers.

The primary question is Kourovka Notebook 21.16 (printed p.179): whether a group can have finite width for every group-generating set but fail finite width for some genuinely monoid-generating set. A width bound may depend on the set. There are no topology or cardinality restrictions. Define B_X(n)=union_{j=0}^n X^j, l_X^+(g)=min{n:g in B_X(n)}, and l_X^sym(g)=l_{X union X^-1}^+(g). CB means every X with <X>_grp=G has bounded l_X^sym. MB means every X with <X>_mon=G has bounded l_X^+. A group-generating proper submonoid is not a counterexample, since its positive length is infinite outside its generated monoid.

I independently found the primary notebook text in root_raw_sources/kourovka_oct2026.txt and retrieved Bergman from his Berkeley endpoint https://math.berkeley.edu/~gbergman/papers/Sym_Omega:1.lms.pdf. The notebook attributes its question to Bergman, Question 9, Generating infinite symmetric groups (Bull. LMS 38 (2006), 429–440). The original Question 9 is more specific: it asks both possible behaviors for groups satisfying CB but not uncountable cofinality. Bergman conditions (3) and (4), Lemma 7, Lemma 10 and the discussion preceding Question 9 are essential background. CB is closed under quotients, extensions and finite-index overgroups. The conjunction CB + uncountable cofinality is equivalent to boundedness of every symmetric integer-valued subadditive length. This stronger property implies MB: for a monoid generator X, L(g)=l_X^+(g)+l_X^+(g^-1) is a finite symmetric subadditive integer-valued function. Thus the stronger class cannot separate CB from MB. This argument is independent of Maltcev attribution.

Rosendal, A topological version of the Bergman property, independently supplies equivalent strong-cofinality/action/metric characterizations and the Polish topological analogue property (OB). Property (OB) restricts to continuous actions; it is weaker than the abstract algebraic property, and compactness alone says nothing about arbitrary algebraic generators. His Polish arguments use sets with the Baire property, and analytic sets have that property in a Polish space. This supports a topology-qualified scope, never unrestricted CB/MB.

Generic length functions on countable groups (Jarnevic, Osin, Oyakawa, arXiv:2206.10712v2) concerns symmetric length functions and countable groups. It cannot supply a positive-width counterexample on a CB group. Any invocation needs its actual hypotheses and is only background for a failed family route.

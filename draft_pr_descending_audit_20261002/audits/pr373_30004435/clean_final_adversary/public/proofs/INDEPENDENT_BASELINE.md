# Independent source-first baseline

This baseline was formed before reading any candidate mathematical artifact, code, receipts, old review, root proof, or sibling proof. The only candidate material accessed was SOURCE_MANIFEST.json and TURN_3_SOURCE.json as permitted source locators. Four primary PDFs were fetched anew, hashed separately, extracted and their critical formulas rendered privately. The four fresh byte hashes equal the supplied source-locator hashes. No third-party assets are publication candidates.

Exact target: for every stationary process on a finite alphabet, identify the completed left and right remote tails modulo its own probability law using probability alone, without using entropy or importing a theorem whose proof carries the same difficulty. Triviality of both tails on a subclass, equality on a subclass, or finite computations are not the full target. The bilateral tail is a distinct object.

Source reconstruction: OWR Question C, PDF page 32 / printed 632, expressly asks for a proof method rather than a new identity. Al-Najjar–Shmaya pp.13–14 explicitly import the completed identity using entropy and exhibit the infinite alphabet process storing every preceding coin at each present coordinate. Łemańczyk Appendix B.1.8 uses entropy cancellation and finite entropy of a generating partition; B.3.2 distinguishes the stationary class/period phase and eliminates transient stationary mass; B.3.3 warns that strong mixing does not force bilateral triviality. BFG Sections4.1–4.4 use uniform normalized-chain continuity, a maximal coupling, and a decreasing reset envelope. Their return-mass estimate controls a coordinate or finite block. A full future coupling bound needs the stronger eventual permanent agreement argument, not a casual replacement of fixed block estimates by an infinite union.

Original mechanism expectations and falsification obligations:

* Finite Markov: the completed tails should equal the recurrent class and cyclic phase at time zero. An exterior bridge argument can also identify the bilateral tail; positivity is required only on stationary support and long compatible paths. Deterministic periods, mixtures of classes, and zero stationary transient mass are boundary controls.
* Finite hidden Markov: observable tails can retain only distinctions among phase-conditioned observable laws. A hidden phase that emits a constant is not observable. For finite linear representations, equality of all word probabilities is checkable by a stabilized finite reachable-vector span. An arbitrary short word cutoff requires a dimension argument. Same one-sided law must be connected to the two-sided stationary phase law with phase/block stationarity, not assumed.
* Infinite memory: uniform summable variation and a strictly positive common-coupling chance permit eventual agreement uniformly over histories. Pointwise convergence or finite-word convergence supplies neither uniform tail-event control nor the probability of any mismatch after n. Unknown normalization is an additional assumption.
* Coding: finite mean locally decisive radius gives a summable crossing bound. Merely finite a.s. radius gives no such union estimate. One-sided and bilateral input exteriors must be controlled separately. For a nontrivial common input tail, a factor intersection is an extra theorem; equality of input tails alone does not justify commuting intersection with sigma-field generation.
* Conditional mixing: stationarity of almost every conditional law, a countably generated conditioning factor, conditional alpha-mixing, and measurability of any quotient of conditional laws are substantive assumptions. Pointwise conditional rates can be integrated by dominated convergence, without inventing a common rate. Shift-invariant conditioning is weaker than every desired conditional stationary representation unless verified.
* Boundaries: irrational rotation binary coding can have full nontrivial left/right tails, ruling out blanket tail triviality. Positive-noise chains weakly converge to a nontrivial-tail constant process, ruling out closure arguments based only on convergence of fixed blocks. Infinite alphabet stored histories genuinely break the identity.

Original exact controls are in code/original_controls.py with its whole generated stream in replays/original_controls.json. They test hidden-phase collapse, phase discrimination needing two letters, deterministic periodic tails, reset-chain return masses, geometric crossing bounds and the weak-limit boundary. These are controls, not evidence for all stationary processes.

Strongest source-first conclusion: the proposed families can plausibly yield correct probability-only subclass results. No mechanism here yet removes every mixing/coding/finite-state premise to cover arbitrary finite-alphabet stationary processes. That transfer is the exact central gap.

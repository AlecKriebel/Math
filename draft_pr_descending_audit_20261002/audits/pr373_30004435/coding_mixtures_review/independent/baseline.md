## Exact source target and success condition

For every stationary law mu on a finite alphabet A to the power Z, prove that the completed remote-past tail and completed remote-future tail are equal, using probability theory without entropy theory. Here Tminus is the intersection over n>=0 of sigma(X_i:i<=-n), and Tplus the analogous intersection sigma(X_i:i>=n); equality is in the mu measure algebra. The theorem's truth is already stated in Steif Question C; the requested contribution is the method at the full quantifier. A proof for finite Markov chains, exchangeable processes, mixing processes, a coding subclass, or a latent-mixture subclass is partial unless an independent probability-only reduction puts every stationary finite alphabet law into that subclass.

## Independent theorem C: integrable locally witnessed finitary transfer

Let mu be a stationary law on finite A^Z. Let phi:A^Z -> B^Z be a shift commuting code defined on a shift invariant Borel set G of full mu measure, where B is finite. Assume there is a Borel radius R(x)>=0 with E_mu R<infinity and the local witness property: for x in G, all z in G agreeing with x on [-R(x),R(x)] satisfy phi(z)_0=phi(x)_0. Then the completed tails of Y=phi(X) pull back into the corresponding completed tails of X. Consequently, tail equality for X implies it for Y. This is an entropy-free subclass transfer, not a universal solution.

Proof with the off-support issue resolved. Call a centered finite word certified if its cylinder meets G and phi_0 is constant on that intersection. There are countably many words. For any configuration z, define the total coordinate extension tilde(phi)_0(z) by the value of its first certified centered word, if one exists, and by a fixed default otherwise. Centered words matching z are nested; two certified matching words have the same value because the smaller cylinder contains the larger, and the larger meets G. Thus the definition is unambiguous and Borel. On G it agrees with phi_0 by local witnessing. Shift it to every coordinate.

For fixed N, replace coordinates x_i with i>-N by a deterministic default word, obtaining s_N(x). For i<=-N, if R(T^i x)<(-N-i+1), its certificate lies wholly in the retained half-line, so tilde(phi)(s_N(x))_i=phi(x)_i. Stationarity and integrability give

sum_{i<=-N} mu[R(T^i X)>=(-N-i+1)] = sum_{d>=1} mu[R>=d] = E_mu R < infinity.

Borel-Cantelli therefore gives only finitely many mismatches in that negative half-line, mu almost surely. A raw past-tail event E on B^Z is unchanged when two outputs eventually agree toward minus infinity: membership is determined by each negative half-line, so any finitely located differences, and arbitrary future differences, are irrelevant. Hence 1_E(phi(x))=1_E(tilde(phi)(s_N(x))) almost surely. The right-hand side is sigma(X_i:i<=-N) measurable. This works for each N. The future proof replaces i<N and uses the same estimate.

Completion is essential. For decreasing raw fields F_n, any event belonging to every completed F_n has raw representatives E_n in F_n with the same event modulo null sets. limsup_n E_n is in every F_m and represents it almost surely. Thus intersection of completed fields and completion of their intersection agree modulo null sets. Pulling back an output-null difference is input-null. This also provides a raw representative with exact finite-change invariance; it is unnecessary and generally impossible to make an arbitrary full-measure coding domain stable under all finite edits. Integrability is a sufficient hypothesis for this mechanism; this proof does not assert necessity or prove transfer for merely a.s.-finite radius.

## Independent theorem M: arbitrary-parameter stationary alpha-mixing mixtures

Let Theta be a standard Borel space with arbitrary probability lambda, including nonatomic lambda. Let theta -> mu_theta be a Borel probability kernel on finite A^Z. Assume that for lambda-almost every theta, mu_theta is stationary and alpha mixing between past and future half-lines, with no common rate required. Put mu=integral mu_theta dlambda. Let Q(theta) be the sequence of probabilities of a fixed countable enumeration of all finite cylinders. This observable law quotient, rather than an arbitrary latent parameter, is the correct conclusion. On the observed process, both completed tails equal the completed sigma field generated by the empirically recovered Q.

Component tail triviality. For a raw future-tail E and a finite cylinder C, place C before r and regard E as measurable after n. Alpha mixing bounds their covariance by a quantity tending to zero. Thus E is independent of every cylinder, then of the full process field by a monotone-class argument; using C=E after extension gives mu_theta(E)=mu_theta(E)^2. The same holds for the past. This is an explicit uniform-over-tail-events mixing condition; ordinary system mixing alone does not yield this step.

Recovery. Alpha mixing implies ergodicity. Apply the ergodic theorem under mu_theta to the indicator of each cylinder, for both shift directions. The countable collection yields simultaneous forward and backward frequency limits Q(theta) on one component-full set. For any fixed cutoff, deleting finitely many averaging terms leaves the same limit and all remaining terms use only the corresponding remote half-line. Hence the frequencies are measurable in each tail. Their failures of existence can be assigned fixed values, preserving exact tail measurability. Integrate the component null sets using the kernel; no uniform mixing rate and no union over uncountably many theta is needed.

Upper inclusion. For a raw tail event E, p(theta)=mu_theta(E) lies in {0,1} for lambda-almost every theta, and 1_E(X)=p(theta) jointly almost surely. Probability-law evaluation rho -> rho(E) is Borel for any Borel E on A^Z, and the countable cylinder vector determines rho; therefore p(theta)=h_E(Q(theta)) for a measurable h_E. Integrating mu_theta[E triangle {h_E(Q(X))=1}]=0 gives the observed inclusion into completed sigma(Q(X)). For a completed tail representative the global null set has component measure zero for lambda-almost every theta, so the same argument applies. The reverse inclusion was furnished by both empirical recoveries.

Boundary case: Theta=(P,V) with P nonatomic and V an independent unused uniform variable, and mu_(p,v)=Bernoulli(p)^Z. Both tails recover P, but cannot recover V. Claiming equality with sigma(Theta) would be false. Identifiability of the law quotient, or an explicit observable injectivity condition modulo lambda, is required. Conditioning a periodic chain on a phase generally destroys one-step stationarity; it cannot be inserted into this theorem without a block or cyclic reformulation.

## Independent obstruction O: ergodicity is not tail triviality

Let a be irrational, U uniform on the circle, and X_n=1_[0,b)(U+n a), with 0<b<1. Every remote future and past determines U: for u!=v, the symmetric difference between [0,b) and its translate by v-u contains a nonempty open arc; every forward and backward irrational orbit enters that arc arbitrarily far away. Thus the half-infinite binary coding maps are injective. They are Borel maps between standard Borel spaces, so their inverses on their images are measurable. All one-sided tails equal the full observed sigma(U), which is nontrivial. Rotation is ergodic by its Fourier character criterion, so the invariant-event field is trivial. Tails are stable as fields under shifting but their individual events need not be shift invariant. Along n_j with n_j a mod1 ->0, E[X_0 X_nj] ->b rather than b^2, so this process is not system mixing, and in particular not alpha mixing.

## Independent obstruction W: weak approximation does not transfer tail conclusions

For reset probability q>0, take a uniform phase U_n on the circle, advancing by a between resets and replacing the phase by a fresh independent uniform value at each reset. The phase process is stationary. A common reset couples two histories, so its past/future dependence, and the binary observed dependence, is bounded by the no-reset probability (1-q)^n. The binary observed process is alpha mixing and both one-sided tails are trivial. As q->0, a block of length m differs from the rotation block only if a reset occurs, with probability at most 1-(1-q)^(m-1). Thus finite-dimensional laws converge to the rotation law, whose tails are full and nontrivial. The same example has finite-state versions: uniform grids, grid rotations approximating a, and positive reset probabilities tending to zero. Every grid phase chain is a finite irreducible aperiodic Doeblin chain, but the binary weak limit recovers a continuous phase. Tail information and decoder validity are not continuous in this topology.

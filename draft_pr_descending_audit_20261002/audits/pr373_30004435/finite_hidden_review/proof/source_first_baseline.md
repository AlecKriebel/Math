## Exact target and scope

The target is the following method request, not a conjecture of equality. For **every** stationary process indexed by Z with a finite single-coordinate alphabet, prove equality modulo null sets of the past tail and future tail using probability theory without entropy theory. The completed fields are T_minus = completion(intersection_n sigma(X_k:k<=-n)) and T_plus = completion(intersection_n sigma(X_k:k>=n)). No Markov, finite hidden state, ergodicity, mixing, positivity, or reversibility assumption is allowed in the full target.

Primary locators inspected: OWR 11/2020, PDF page 32 / printed page 632, all of Steif's section 3 and Question C; arXiv 1406.6670 PDF pages 13-14, Theorem 5.2 and Example 6.1; Lemanczyk thesis Appendix B, PDF/printed pages 111-116 including the entropy proof and finite Markov subsection. The arXiv paper identifies completed fields and explicitly invokes a finite-alphabet entropy theorem. The thesis treats the finite Markov class/phase result as known and presents a general entropy route. It is therefore invalid to present a finite Markov/HMM result as a solution of the exact target or assume the general result while claiming a probability-only derivation.

## Independent universal finite Markov reconstruction

Assume a finite transition matrix P, stationary probability pi, and the two-sided stationary Markov law. Ignore states with pi(s)=0; this is a null-set restriction, not irreducibility. All transient states have zero pi: from any transient state there is almost surely eventual entry to a closed recurrent class, so P(Y_n transient) tends to 0, whereas stationarity makes it constant. Positive-mass support is a disjoint union of closed irreducible classes C. In each class let d_C be the gcd of closed-walk lengths. Directed-graph paths give a cyclic partition C_0,...,C_(d_C-1), with every positive transition advancing phase by one. Let L=(C,j) be the class and time-zero phase. All retained L values have positive mass. For any integer t, the phase of Y_t minus t modulo d_C recovers L, so sigma(L) lies in each one-sided tail.

For completeness the only finite-chain mixing ingredient is elementary: on each C_j, P^d_C is irreducible aperiodic. Its directed graph has a finite collection of return lengths with gcd 1. Finite concatenations of these loops include every sufficiently large integer (the numerical-semigroup fact follows by selecting, for each residue modulo one return length, a nonnegative loop combination with that residue). Append fixed paths between each pair of vertices. This gives an exponent r for which (P^d_C)^r is strictly positive on C_j. If epsilon is the least entry of that matrix, its rows have common mass |C_j|epsilon; coupling/Doeblin contraction gives geometric total-variation convergence to the unique invariant distribution on C_j. These distributions are the normalized restrictions of pi. This argument uses finite matrices and coupling, not entropy.

The reversed kernel on the positive support is P_star(t,s)=pi(s)P(s,t)/pi(t), has the same closed classes and periods, with cyclic orientation reversed. The same convergence applies to it. Fix a bounded finite cylinder f=f(Y_a,...,Y_b). For n>b, conditioning f on the whole future from n is equivalent to conditioning on Y_n (conditional independence of the Markov past and future at the separating state). Under L=l the reversed transition across the gap n-b converges, along the appropriate cyclic phases, uniformly over finitely many possible separating states. Consequently

  || E[f | sigma(Y_k:k>=n)] - E[f|L] ||_infinity -> 0

where the supremum is over positive-mass states only. Equivalently use the Bayes factor P^(n-b)(Y_b,s)/P(Y_n=s|L=l), whose denominator is the positive normalized invariant mass at the appropriate phase. The gap remainder modulo d_C is handled by a bounded number of transitions; there are finitely many remainders. Reverse martingale convergence identifies E[f|T_plus]=E[f|L]. Finite cylinders are dense in L1 of the full two-sided process (increasing finite-coordinate fields and martingale convergence), and conditional expectation is an L1 contraction, so the identity holds for every integrable f. Taking f=1_A for A in T_plus gives 1_A=E[1_A|L], hence A is sigma(L)-measurable modulo null sets. Reverse time gives T_minus=sigma(L)=T_plus. Ergodicity and aperiodicity were never imposed globally. For a periodic irreducible chain the phase is retained; an ergodic chain need not have trivial tails.

Completions cause no extra assumption here: for decreasing fields F_n and an event A belonging modulo null sets to every F_n, choose representatives A_n in F_n and define liminf A_n. This lies in every F_n because a finite prefix of A_n does not affect liminf, and equals A almost surely since the countable union of exceptional null sets is null. Thus intersection of completed F_n agrees with completion of intersection F_n. Cylinder density and finite L apply on the completed probability space as well.

## Independent universal finite HMM reconstruction

Assume Y as above and a finite output alphabet A with emission probabilities e_s(a). Conditional on the entire hidden path, outputs X_t are independent and have probabilities e_(Y_t)(a). Deterministic emissions, zero probabilities, and identical emission rows are permitted. The pair Z_t=(Y_t,X_t) is a stationary finite Markov chain with

  Q((s,a),(t,b))=P(s,t)e_t(b),  rho(s,a)=pi(s)e_s(a).

Remove zero-rho pairs. Each positive pair lies over the same hidden closed class/cyclic phase as its state. Every hidden closed walk can be lifted to a pair closed walk by selecting supported emissions and fixing the final emission, and every pair closed walk projects to a hidden closed walk. Hence periods and class/phase labels of Q are exactly those of Y. The finite Markov result gives both joint one-sided tails sigma(L). Every observable tail event is a joint tail event and therefore a function of L; it need not reveal all of L.

Let mu_l be the conditional **full observed process law** given L=l. Define l~l' iff mu_l=mu_l', and q(L) the equivalence class. Then

  T_minus(X) = sigma(q(L)) = T_plus(X), modulo null sets.

Upper bound: if an observable tail event A equals h(L) almost surely, h takes values 0 or 1 on every positive-mass l. Its conditional probability is mu_l(A)=h(l). Equal conditional observed laws force h(l)=h(l'), so A is a function of q(L).

Lower bound: take d a common multiple of all d_C. Conditional on any l, the hidden d-skeleton on its cyclic set is stationary irreducible aperiodic, with geometric mixing. A fixed finite observed-word indicator sampled at times kd has covariance bounded by K theta^(separation) after ignoring its finite overlap length; conditional variance of its N-sample average is O(1/N). Chebyshev plus Borel-Cantelli along N=m^2 proves almost-sure convergence, and boundedness interpolates between consecutive squares. This proves in both time directions that d-sampled frequencies equal mu_l of that word. The limiting frequencies do not change after deleting any finite number of samples, and hence belong to every observable one-sided field. Right-infinite word probabilities separate distinct mu_l: each conditional law is invariant under shift d, so any full finite cylinder can be translated into nonnegative coordinates and is determined by prefix-word marginals. There are finitely many labels, so finitely many distinguishing words suffice to identify q(L). Thus q(L) belongs to both observable tails. This is a probability-only universal theorem for this model class, not a universal theorem for every finite-alphabet process.

## Exact finite-dimensional comparison of conditional output laws

For N positive-mass hidden states, let D_a=diag(e_s(a)), M_a=D_a P, and alpha_l be pi normalized on the time-zero cyclic set. An output word w=a_0...a_(r-1) has probability alpha_l M_(a_0)...M_(a_(r-1)) 1. Let V_k=span{M_w 1:|w|<=k}. Then V_0=span{1} and V_(k+1)=span(V_k,{M_a v:a in A,v in V_k}). Once V_(k+1)=V_k, closure makes every later space equal. If no equality has occurred the dimension strictly rises from 1, so V_(N-1) is saturated. Consequently equality of word probabilities for all words of lengths <=N-1 proves equality for all right-infinite laws. Their common shift-d invariance then proves full-law equality. This statement uses a common model; comparing different models requires the direct sum and a bound using the sum of their dimensions. Longer safe bounds are acceptable; a heuristic smaller bound is not.

## Pre-candidate adversarial controls and gaps

The controls to be authored before candidate code use exact rational arithmetic and independent graph/linear-algebra implementations. They must include: transient and zero-stationary-mass states, unused closed classes, nonergodic mixtures, nonreversible kernels, deterministic cycles, cyclic phase recovery, constant/identical/zero emission rows, erased hidden phases/classes, identical first marginals with distinguishable length-two words, exact N-1 word-bound sharpness for general initial distributions, and a complete 3-state denominator-2 stochastic-matrix scan. Finite tests supplement, and cannot replace, the universal argument above.

A 4-state period-2 witness has transitions 0->2, 1->3, and 2/3->0 or 1 with equal probability, emission 0 at states 0/2 and 1 at states 1/3. Both phases have fair one-letter distributions, but P(00|phase {0,1})=1/2 and P(00|phase {2,3})=1/4. Thus immediate emission marginals are not the observable-law quotient. Conversely constant emissions merge all labels even for disjoint hidden recurrent classes and unequal hidden periods.

Strongest verified mathematical result at this checkpoint: the independent universal finite Markov and finite state-emitting HMM statements above follow by probability-only arguments, with tails identified as hidden class/phase and observable-law quotient respectively. Exact remaining gap to Steif Question C: finite hidden-state structure supplies a finite sufficient label and geometric forgetting. An arbitrary stationary finite-alphabet process need have neither. Replacing it by an unsupported finite-label assertion transfers the central difficulty and is blocked without a new mechanism.

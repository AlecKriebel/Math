## Exact scope

The source considers a delayed renewal process with i.i.d. strictly positive, non-lattice interarrival times of infinite mean. The delay is nonnegative and independent; zero delay is allowed. At a deterministic time t, D_t is the length of the renewal interval containing t, with the next renewal taken strictly after t. Problem 1.2 asks whether some nondecreasing deterministic normalizer phi gives a proper nondegenerate distributional limit for D_t/phi(t), and specifically asks about phi(t)=E[min(X_1,t)]. Both questions are part of the target. A counterexample must rule out every admissible normalizer for one allowed renewal law, not merely reject the suggested normalizer. A nondegenerate limit means a finite real-valued random variable whose distribution is not a point mass.

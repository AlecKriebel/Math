## Exact target authenticated from primary indexed text

The institutional preprint [Some Open Probability Problems](https://cms.dm.uba.ar/depto/public/Some%20Open%20Problems-preprint.pdf), section 1, gives S_n=S_0+sum_{i=1}^n X_i with nonnegative independent delay, strictly positive i.i.d. recurrence times, and non-lattice law in the specific sense P(X_1 in dZ)<1 for every d>0. Null recurrence means E[X_1]=infinity. It defines N_t=inf{n>=0:S_n>t}, age A_t=t-S_{N_t-1}, residual life B_t=S_{N_t}-t, total life D_t=X_{N_t}=A_t+B_t, and relative age U_t=A_t/D_t. Problem 1.2 asks whether there is a nondecreasing phi for which D_t/phi(t) tends in distribution to a nondegenerate random variable, and specifically proposes phi(t)=E[min{X_1,t}]. Indexed text also includes the preceding theorem that unscaled D_t escapes to infinity in the null-recurrent case. This paragraph is a paraphrase, not a transcription.

Finite almost surely real-valued recurrence times are the ordinary meaning of the source's random variables; the retrieved text does not explicitly print an additional P(X_1<infinity)=1 clause. The all-scale counterexample target should impose this explicitly. Choosing S_0=0 removes the source's pre-first-renewal indexing convention issue.

The [publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11134-011-9241-2) independently authenticates Hermann Thorisson, the published title Open problems in renewal, coupling and Palm theory, Queueing Systems 68, 313–319 (2011), DOI 10.1007/s11134-011-9241-2. The [author institution record](https://iris.landsbokasafn.is/en/publications/open-problems-in-renewal-coupling-and-palm-theory/) corroborates this identity. The UBA institutional preprint index lists the preprint under 2010. The publisher online date is 1 July 2011 and the institution issue date is August 2011; these are compatible publication stages.

**Priority question:** was a finite-a.s., strictly positive, non-lattice, infinite-mean recurrence law already known such that EVERY finite positive deterministic nondecreasing eventual scale phi fails to give D_t/phi(t) a proper finite nondegenerate weak limit along the full t->infinity limit? Failure of phi=t, failure of the suggested truncated mean, a subsequence limit alone, or a statement about A_t/D_t is insufficient. A published theorem ruling out every deterministic scale without monotonicity would suffice. Proper mixed limits with an atom at zero must be covered.

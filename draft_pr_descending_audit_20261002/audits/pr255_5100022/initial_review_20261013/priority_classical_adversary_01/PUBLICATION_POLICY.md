# Local reading copies and publishable audit outputs

Do not publish sources/ or receipts/web*.json. These contain complete third-party papers, extracted paper text, table images or raw web excerpts retained solely for local reading. Full PDFs are excluded even where openly licensed to keep the verification package focused and avoid accidental redistribution.

Publishable authored outputs: REPORT.md, BIBLIOGRAPHY.md, QUERY_LOG.md, RESEARCH_LOG.md, construction_checks.py, construction_check_result.json, PUBLICATION_POLICY.md and FINAL_AUDIT_INTEGRITY.json.

Publishable provenance receipts: receipts/PRIMARY_SOURCE_DOWNLOADS.json, receipts/FOLLOWUP_DOWNLOADS.json and receipts/INPUT_AND_TABLE_INTEGRITY.json. They contain source URLs, hashes, byte counts, status information and authored scope records, rather than full paper content.

fetch_sources.py is an optional reproducibility aid, not a proof checker. Source access might change; its current authenticated receipt is the historical evidence. No worldwide firstness certificate is asserted.

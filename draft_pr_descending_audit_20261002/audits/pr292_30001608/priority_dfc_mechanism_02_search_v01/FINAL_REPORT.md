## Independent algebra and counterexamples

**Lumping.** The projection(A+B,X,Y) gives the same state for(2,0,1,0) and(0,2,1,0), but rates to(Z-1,X+1,Y) are2 and0. Therefore this projection is not a strong Markov lump. Forgetting latent first choices cannot identify Oguz's chain. Similarly N=A+B+X+Y is not a Markov lump: at(2,0,0,0) the total departure rate is0, while at(0,0,1,1), with the sameN=2, it is4/3. The latter explicitly also demonstrates why a scalarM/M/infinity population analogy is insufficient.

**Denominator and global clock.** LetS=A+B+X+Y+1. Whole-population service rates obtained by replacingD withS cannot be changed to these target rates by a common clock while retaining constant arrivals. Nonzero arrival rates force the clock multiplier to be constant (also when lambda is globally rescaled); D/S varies between1 at empty and1/(M+1) at(M,0,0,0). Constant time/rate scaling preserves load ratios and cannot turn a fixed-capacity small-load hypothesis into the target's all-load theorem.

**Nonexplosion versus drift.** Each nonarrival rate is bounded by its decremented coordinate, so the total rate is at mostlambda+N. Only arrivals increaseN at ratelambda, and every peer makes at most two download transitions before departing. This is an elementary nonexplosion bound. It is not a recurrence proof: QN=lambda-(2XY+X+Y)/D is positivelambda on the infiniteX=Y=0 plane. A linear population Foster shortcut fails.

**Polynomial clock and reaction networks.** DQ has a natural14reaction stochastic mass-action representation:0->A/B (lambda/2); X/Y->X/Y+A/B (lambda/2); A+X->2X,A->X,B+Y->2Y,B->Y; X+Y->Y,X+Y->X; X/Y->0 (remaining rates1). Grouping by jump gives exactlyD times the six target rates. The representation is binary but is not weakly reversible(2X is a sink), not double-full(2A/2B missing), not strongly endotactic(w=eX makes A+X source-maximal while A+X->2X points outward), and lacks requiredA/B outflows in fully-open corollaries. Full stoichiometric span follows from inflows andA->X/B->Y, so w is not orthogonal. The precise Anderson–Kim and Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim–Nguyen hypotheses were inspected. This rejects that direct representation, not every conceivable hidden-species embedding or different theorem.

Even a hypothetical positive-recurrence theorem forDQ would not settleQ without its original holding-time clock. Formal invariant weights forQ areD pi_fast and require finite weighted mass. A checkable counterexample shows why: reflecting symmetric birth-death withq(0,1)=1 andq(n,n±1)=1 forn>=1 is null recurrent; the same jump chain after multiplying rates atn by2^n is positive recurrent, with normalized invariantweights2^{-(n+1)}. The embedded reflected random walk hits0 infinitely often; independentExp(1) holdings at0 prevent explosion in either chain. In a cycle from0, probability of hittingn is1/n and expected visits after that hit are2n, so expected visits at eachn>=1 are2. Base holdings mean1/2 give infinite expected cycle time. Accelerated holdings mean2^{-(n+1)} give cycle mean1+sum_{n>=1}2^{-n}=2. Detailed balance verifies the geometric invariant weights. This is an analytic counterexample; finite-grid balance checks are only corroboration.

# Verdure's prior full-torsion criterion and the pentagonal application

Verdure's published *Lagrange resolvents and torsion of elliptic curves*, International Journal of Pure and Applied Mathematics 33(1) (2006), 75–92, already gives the full 5-torsion rationality criterion for the universal Tate curve used by the candidate. The [author-hosted original](https://math.uit.no/ansatte/hugues/papers/IJPAM.pdf) is an 18-page scan. All original pages have been read visually; empty text extraction was not treated as a readable text source.

Theorem 5 on printed 84 concerns a field of characteristic different from 5, a primitive fifth root of unity, and a Tate parameter away from the discriminant roots. The Tate equation on 83 is exactly the candidate's D_beta when Verdure's t equals beta. With zeta + zeta^-1 = (sqrt(5)-1)/2, his two discriminant roots are alpha_5 = phi^5 = c and beta_5 = -phi^-5 = -c^-1. He proves that all 25 points are rational after adjoining zeta if and only if (t-alpha_5)/(t-beta_5) is a fifth power.

In the candidate's parameter, beta = (11-5 sqrt(5))lambda/[2(lambda+5 sqrt(5))] and iota(beta) = -1/(lambda+c). Thus Verdure's Kummer value is

    (beta-c)/(beta+c^-1) = c/iota(beta) = -c(lambda+c).

For theta^5 = lambda+c, a fifth root is -phi theta. The sign and scalar are fifth powers, so this is the same Kummer extension as the candidate's. The portable exact comparison checks this rational identity and the roots independently of source files.

Proposition 3 and Corollary 1 on 80–81 show that, once a rational nonzero 5-torsion point and zeta are available, the full coordinate field is cyclic of degree 1 or 5 and already equals the field generated by zeta and the x-coordinate of a complementary torsion point. The proof of Theorem 5 on 84–88 works over Z[1/5][T] with polynomial coordinate expressions. On 88 it explicitly preserves specialization because the discriminant and the required parameter differences remain nonzero. Consequently this is a prior universal specialization criterion, rather than only a generic function-field parametrization. Adjoining a fifth root makes all torsion rational; the degree bound identifies the exact Kummer field when the value is not already a fifth power. This implication applies to the candidate's nonsingular Tate model over its twist-splitting field with zeta.

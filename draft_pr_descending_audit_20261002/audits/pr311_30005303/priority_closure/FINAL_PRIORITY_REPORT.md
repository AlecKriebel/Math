## Exact target

Fix a finite simple graph G=(V,E) and the binary cube. The source defines MI by the literal product of finite real functions on ORIGINAL edges and M2 by full global Markov intersected with MTP2. Conjecture 1 says MI intersect M2 is closed under pointwise limits of normalized distributions. Zeros are permitted. There is no permission to replace finite factors by infinity, add hidden variables, enlarge the graph, or return only extended-exponential-family parameters.

Real edge factors can be replaced by their absolute values: the absolute value of their product equals the already nonnegative mass. Thus finite nonnegative factors are sufficient. Every MTP2 inequality and finite conditional-independence relation is polynomial, so both conditions persist under normalized finite-state limits. The remaining issue is exact finite edge factors at the boundary.

Unaries are a convenient intermediate notation. On a nonisolated vertex they can be absorbed into an incident original edge. A literal edge product is independent of isolated coordinates and hence imposes their uniform independent distribution. Scalars, including normalization and the uniform isolate factor, can be absorbed in an existing edge. If E is empty and V nonempty, the literal normalized empty-product family is empty; a tacit overall scalar convention instead gives the singleton uniform family. Both are closed. The empty vertex set has the unique mass 1. The source's clique and arbitrary-lattice conjectures are stronger separate claims, not equivalent replacements.

## Exact classical cut/compactness specialization

Let A(G) be the positive normalized laws
p(x) proportional to exp(sum_i h_i x_i + sum_{ij in E} J_ij x_i x_j),
where h_i are finite real and J_ij>=0. This paragraph initially includes unaries; literal isolates are handled below.

Set E(x)=-sum_i h_i x_i-sum J_ij x_i x_j. Orient every original edge arbitrarily. Write
-J_ij x_i x_j = J_ij x_i(1-x_j)-J_ij x_i.
Thus E is a sum of nonnegative directed crossing costs and unary terms b_i x_i. A nonnegative b_i is encoded by i->t of capacity b_i; a negative b_i by s->i of capacity -b_i. Use x_i=1 for the source side. The cut capacity is C(x)=E(x)+kappa for a scalar kappa. Nonterminal arcs use only original edges. This is the pairwise submodular construction in KZ and KR, with no added nonterminal vertices.

Take a maximum flow f of value v, and define combined residual capacities
r_uv=c_uv-f_uv+f_vu>=0.
For every s-t cut U,
sum_{u in U,v outside U} r_uv
= C(U)-sum_cross f_uv+sum_reverse_cross f_vu
= C(U)-v.
The last equality is flow conservation. Since min C=v, the residual cut cost is E(x)-min E. These are finite real capacities; zero capacities can be omitted. Real maximum-flow existence follows on a finite network from the compact feasible-flow polytope and the max-flow/min-cut theorem. Picard–Queyranne's residual relation describes precisely the zero-cost cuts.

Therefore W(x)=exp(-E(x)+min E) is a product of terminal unary and directed ORIGINAL-edge factors with values in (0,1]. For an undirected edge, combine the two directed residual factors:
psi_ij(a,b)=exp(-r_ij a(1-b)-r_ji b(1-a)).
It has diagonal entries 1 and nonnegative determinant. At a minimum configuration W=1, and everywhere W<=1. Hence
1 <= Z=sum_x W(x) <= 2^|V|.
There are finitely many scalar factor entries in [0,1]. For any convergent sequence in A(G), select a subsequence on which all entries converge. Their finite products converge to W_star, and Z_star>=1. Dividing by Z_star supplies finite nonnegative factors for the given normalized distribution limit. Original edges are preserved; no infinite values or auxiliary variable marginalization occurs. MTP2 is also polynomial-closed.

This proves closure(A(G)) is contained in the finite edge-and-unary MTP2 class. Conversely A(G) is contained in that class. Equality with its closure additionally requires density for all zero-containing edge-MTP2 laws. The cut theorems establish this compactness half by an elementary specialization; citing only them as the entire source solution omits the density premise.

For literal MI, require h_i=0 on isolates, retain their independent uniform law throughout, absorb unaries at nonisolated vertices and scalar normalization into original edges. The bounded gauge does not authorize nonuniform isolated coordinates.

## Why density at zeros needs a separate argument

Here is the complete bridge used in the released TURN_2 proof, rechecked mathematically in this audit. It is not asserted to be a theorem explicitly stated in the earlier cut papers.

Suppose p has finite unary and edge factors and is MTP2. Its support S is a lattice. Factorization implies that S equals the intersection of its actual unary and edge projections: any assignment meeting those projection conditions uses only factor cells positive somewhere on S, hence every factor is positive at that assignment. After removing pins, each active edge projection contains 00 and 11, so it is full, equality, or one directional implication. The implications' strongly connected classes are ties; the quotient is a poset, and its upsets are exactly S.

Log p on S is a sum of finite logs of the occurring local cells. Extend each local log arbitrarily to missing cells and write it as a binary quadratic polynomial. Substitute pins and tied coordinates. Terms between comparable classes reduce to affine terms; the remaining expression is
c+sum_K H_K y_K+sum_{K,L incomparable} K_KL y_K y_L.
If no original edge joins K and L, its coefficient is zero. For incomparable K,L, set every strict successor of K or L to 1, set K,L to 0, and all other classes to 0. This and its three K/L flips are permitted upsets. The MTP2 log-square difference equals K_KL, so K_KL>=0. This controls the AGGREGATE coefficient across tied classes; it need not control an arbitrary individual original potential.

Lift H_K to a representative coordinate and every nonnegative K_KL to one original cross-class edge. This gives a finite attractive quadratic polynomial L(x) agreeing with log p up to its scalar on S. Let D(x) be the sum of unary pin violations and all directional ORIGINAL-edge implication violations x_i(1-x_j), using both directions for equality. Then S={D=0}. In L-tD, every quadratic pair coefficient is nonnegative: each violation contributes +t x_i x_j, with only unary changes otherwise. Thus
p_t(x) proportional to exp(L(x)-tD(x))
lies in A(G) for finite t, preserves original edges, and tends to p as t tends to infinity. The normalizer tends to the positive finite sum over S. Isolates allowed by a literal original edge model are uniform, so no penalty or field on an isolate is needed.

This supplies density, rather than assuming that replacing original zero potential entries by epsilon preserves attraction. It proves the stronger equality between the zero-containing factor class and closure(A(G)). The prior natural-support contraction route proves the source's closure statement directly, without proving this stronger density characterization.

## Boundary controls and falsification

The public script boundary_controls.py and its actual stdout/stderr capture use exact rational arithmetic.

* Pinned-potential control: phi_1=(1,0), phi_2=(1,1), and psi_12=[[1,1],[1,0]] yield normalized p=(1/2,1/2,0,0) on 00,01,10,11. All MTP2 inequalities hold, while the given psi determinant is -1. This falsifies the global-to-given-potentials reverse implication at zeros. It does not prevent a different attractive representation.
* Naive smoothing control: replacing zeros in those same factors by epsilon=1/10 produces a strictly positive distribution that fails MTP2 at 01,10. A density proof must reparameterize first.
* Frustrated-cycle control: on edges 12,23,34,41 take E=|x1-x2|+|x2-x3|+|x3-x4|+1-|x4-x1|. Its zero-temperature uniform ground-state law has eight support points and every original-edge projection is full. It therefore cannot have finite original-edge factors, although it is a limit of positive original-edge laws. It fails MTP2, as required. General finite local factor images are not compact merely because distributions are.
* Tie control: uniform {00,11} is MTP2 and finite-edge factorizing, but is outside original-coordinate naturality. The contraction comparison explicitly resolves this case.

These controls are falsification checks of tempting shortcuts. They do not replace the mathematical proofs.

# Independent focal-geometry historical-priority acceptance criteria

Frozen before reading any candidate, mathematical audit, or earlier priority material.

## Claims to distinguish
- E: Equality of the signed pedal-area ratios at the two foci: A+/A− = B+/B−, wherever the denominator is defined, for nondegenerate nested confocal ellipses with an elliptical caustic and periodic primitive convex or star billiard families, N≥3. Extension through zero areas must be formulated as a determinant/cross-product identity. Reversals and repeats must be checked separately rather than inferred from unsigned pictures.
- M: The stronger phase-independent positive multiplier B_j = C0 A_j at both foci, with the same C0>0 for the entire periodic family. Exact meanings of A and B and the formula for C0 must come from authenticated source/candidate mathematics, not labels.
- C: Phase constancy of the common focal ratio. This is a distinct hypothesis and must not be bundled with E or M. A source claiming C may be wrong without anticipating a correct proof of E or M.

## Exact-priority standards
1. Reconstruct the primary original question from both arXiv 2004.12497v11 Table 7 k606 and published DOI 10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y Table 7 k607, checking the geometric objects, area convention, wording and dates.
2. A prior exact result must assert E or M explicitly, or imply it by a fully specified theorem and a checkable short specialization with all hypotheses, signs, and periodicity conditions satisfied. Mere numerical observation, abstract, search snippet, low-N identity, unsigned claim without orientation analysis, or an equivalent reformulation with an unproved central lemma is not a completed full-domain theorem.
3. Assess priority of E and M separately, and separate proof priority from conjecture/observation priority. Record earliest supported dates (preprint version, publication, repository when available) without conflating first posting and later version contents.
4. Classify each source as full equivalent, restricted/partial, mechanism only, negative correction, or unresolved. A generalized quadratic area formula can anticipate M only if its applicability and phase-independent proportionality are explicitly derived, not assumed.
5. Check primitive convex, star, N=3, reversals, repeats, vanishing signed area/denominator cases, and confocal elliptical caustics. Hyperbolic caustics, degenerate trajectories, external Poncelet families, arbitrary polygons, or an unsigned-only result do not automatically cover this domain.
6. Search distinct relevant families: Reznik/Garcia/Koiller/Roitman/Helman follow-ups; earlier focal pedal/conic/Steiner geometry; Poncelet area and general quadratic-area theorems; exact triangle corrections. Preserve evidence for full texts and theorem/proof locators.
7. Read relevant full primary texts. Search absence cannot certify firstness. If a potentially equivalent unavailable source or a hypothesis mismatch remains, state it as an exact gap.
8. Maintain source custody: URL, UTC, actual native argv/cwd/exit/stdout/stderr of retrieval and extraction, original bytes SHA-256, and locators. Keep copyrighted full texts private.
9. No communication with outside individuals, no live publication or git actions. Findings are bounded evidence for root adjudication only.

## Success criterion and bounded conclusion
A completed audit gives authenticated target reconstruction, a source-by-source comparison table for E/M/C, exact evidence/derivation where prior equivalence is supported, and a bounded statement of the strongest verified historical finding and remaining gaps. A negative result is only “no full equivalent theorem found in audited sources,” never unconditional originality.

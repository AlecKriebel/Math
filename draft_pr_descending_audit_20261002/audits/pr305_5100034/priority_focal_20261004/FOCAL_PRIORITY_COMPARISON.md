## Exact claims and the source's history

Let A+ and A− be signed shoelace areas of the ordered feet from the two common foci onto original billiard sidelines. Let B+ and B− be the corresponding areas for the outer tangent polygon, whose sidelines are tangents to the boundary ellipse at billiard vertices. The domain here is a nondegenerate nested confocal ellipse pair with elliptical caustic, every admitted primitive convex or coprime star winding N≥3, orientation reversals, and repeated traversals. Supporting-line projections, rather than segment-clamped projections, are essential.

* E is the pointwise identity A+/A− = B+/B−, with denominators nonzero in this domain.
* M is the stronger statement B_j=C0 A_j for both foci, where C0>0 depends only on the fixed ellipse and caustic, not phase.
* C says the common ratio in E is independent of phase. C is false. M does not imply C.

The earliest source version directly recovered here is **arXiv:2004.12497v1**, *Forty New Invariants*, submitted 26 April 2020. Its §3.6, printed pp.5–6, defines both focal-pedal pairs; Table5 p.6 **k605** displays E, labels it all N, dates the observation 4/20, and gives a question mark for proof. In **v11**, submitted 29 October 2020, Table7 p.9 labels this same equation **k606**. In the published *Fifty New Invariants*, DOI10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y, Table7 printed p.349 (PDF9) labels it **k607**. Online publication was 18 February 2021. Both Table7 pages were rendered and visually checked. The neighboring product rows and even-period ratio rows must not be substituted for this row.

The journal's §3.7 p.349 explicitly proves the two individual even-period ratios are one by central symmetry; Table7 k604,a/b records that proof. Thus E's primitive-even case is already an immediate published consequence. An even number of listed vertices obtained by repeating an odd primitive orbit does not have this central symmetry. Reversal and repetition extend a valid primitive signed identity by common sign and scale factors, not by a new symmetry argument.

The source's invariant-table framing suggests phase constancy, but the displayed equation is E. Discovery of E cannot be claimed by this project: the original authors already stated it in April2020. Their historical proof question mark does not certify that a proof is absent in current literature. The source does not display the positive, phase-independent multiplier M.

## Classical reduction and its exact gap

For a fixed polygon V with internal angles θ_i, Steiner's finite identity gives

    S_pedal(M) = S(V)/2 − (1/8) Σ sin(2θ_i) |M−V_i|².

Thus S_pedal is a radial quadratic plus a linear term. Applying this separately to V=P(t) and V=P_outer(t) gives two quadratics with **phase-dependent coefficients**. At M=(±c,0), E still requires a relationship between their odd linear coefficients and even terms; M additionally requires the relationship's scalar to be independent of phase. Neither is furnished by fixed-polygon quadraticity. Calling this relationship “automatic by Steiner” simply relocates the central difficulty and is a **blocked route**, unless an independently proved billiard coefficient relation is supplied.

Similarly, Sturm's triangle formula

    S_pedal(F) = S(T) [R(T)²−|F−O(T)|²] / [4R(T)²]

reduces N3 to explicit circle-power and center-coordinate identities. Published triangular parametrizations and center formulas make this a short algebraic check, so an unrestricted claim that even the triangular case has no predecessor would be unwarranted. I did not certify an exact prior published N3 E/M proof or a complete published equivalence from these inputs. This is an explicit low-N implication gap, not evidence for originality.

One boundary control refutes an indiscriminate level-circle shortcut. For the fixed cyclic quadrilateral (1,0),(0,1),(−1,0),(3/5,−4/5), direct exact supporting-line projection gives

    S_pedal(x,y) = 9/10 − 3x/10.

The quadratic coefficient is zero but the area is not constant. Level sets are lines. `classical_boundary_control.py` derives all coefficients of the degree≤2 polynomial with rational arithmetic; its final native receipt has exit0 and empty stderr. The initial script bytes are retained in `classical_boundary_control_initial.py` with the original receipt's declared hash. This qualifies degeneracies of the circle-locus wording; it does not challenge Steiner's decomposition identity or the scoped billiard proof.

## C and the bounded verdict

ROOT's independently checked exact triangle has squared boundary axes21,16, caustic squared axes189/25,64/25 and vertices (√21,0),(−3√21/5,±16/5). Its focal areas are

    A± = 84(7√21 ± √5)/625,
    B± = 7(7√21 ± √5)/10,
    B±/A± = 125/24.

Central inversion preserves the family and swaps the two foci, giving reciprocal unequal focal ratios. This disproves C while preserving E/M. This report uses ROOT's passed mathematical gate as a dependency; it does not re-promote a literature observation into a proof. None of the inspected focal follow-up statements supplies a prior explicit correction of C. The earlier exact triangle formulas make such counterexamples accessible, so **first negative correction is not certified**.

The strongest justified priority disposition for this family is:

1. **E's statement is established prior art**, observed by Reznik/Garcia/Koiller in April2020. Primitive-even E also has a published symmetry proof. Do not describe E as a newly discovered invariant.
2. **No full-domain proof of E or full equivalent M was identified in the full primary theorem sections audited here.** This is a bounded non-location result, not a claim that such work does not exist. Odd primitive star scope, positive denominators, common positive multiplier and repetition handling require comparison with any further full theorem.
3. **M may be the candidate's stronger contribution, but priority is unresolved.** Classical quadraticity, triangular circle powers, earlier meromorphic cancellation, and the October2026 restricted bridges must be credited. A global new-proof claim needs ROOT's independent broader-family adjudication and resolution of access/implication gaps.
4. **C is false in the source domain.** No firstness claim for the correction is supported by this audit. Original-source wording must receive the positive/negative split rather than be silently rewritten.

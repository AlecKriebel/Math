## Strongest verified author claims, pending independent review

- **Exact multipartite profile at every density.** An all-support-size moment minimization proves the candidate's optimality over every complete multipartite sequence, with a finite-size identity uniform in the number of parts. This is an elementary reconstruction of the credited candidate family, without a novelty claim.
- **Density-preserving complete-join replacement.** Arbitrary internal blocks of density at most1/2 can be replaced by bipartite blocks, preserving global edge density and increasing induced-C4 density. With the credited Pikhurko–Razborov triangle-stability theorem, every asymptotic triangle-minimizing sequence satisfies the proposed C4 bound.
- **Explicit non-multipartite ties.** A paw blow-up with masses(4,2,2,1)/9 has edge density16/27 and induced-C4 density64/243, exactly the proposed value, but has edit distance at least8/729 from every multipartite graphon. This is consistent with known triangle-minimizer flexibility and is not a source counterexample.
- **Qualified local stability.** The candidate is strictly optimal under density-preserving small-L∞-amplitude perturbations, with an explicit remainder bound. Exact tying paths are arbitrarily close in L1 and show why this calculation cannot prove edit-distance uniqueness or global optimality.
- **Full profile for weighted cographs and their limits.** The proposed bound is closed under complete joins and disjoint unions. Cotree induction proves it for all weighted cographs, without any size or depth bound, and hence for every cograph sequence. The high-density disjoint-union step has an explicit positive gap.
- **Exact rank-one profile.** For W(s,t)=f(s)f(t),0≤f≤1, the full vertical profile is[0,R(p)], where R(p)=3p²/16 for p≤1/2 and R(p)=3p⁴(1−p)² for p≥1/2. Equality cases are classified. This family is strictly below the achievable multipartite construction at every interior density, with explicit gaps.

## Remaining gap

There is no proof that every arbitrary host lies in one of these classes, has asymptotically minimum triangle density, or can be replaced at the same edge density by a controlled host. Non-cograph, non-rank-one hosts with positive triangle excess remain unbounded by the candidate profile. The original conjecture is neither proved nor refuted.

## Exact target

Röver–Sapir Question 111, OWR 26/2018, printed page 1625:

> Does every finitely presented subgroup of F have torsion-free abelianisation?

Here F is the usual orientation-preserving dyadic piecewise-linear group of the unit interval, with finitely many breakpoints and slopes integral powers of 2. There is no closedness, solvability, finite-index, or orbit hypothesis in the question. Finite presentation cannot be replaced by finite generation. Source PDF: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46748 ; publisher: https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/16167 ; DOI 10.4171/OWR/2018/26. Report 15 (2018), no.2, 1579–1633; workshop 3–9 June 2018; actual publication 12 April 2019. Thus the imported 2019 citation is a publication date, not an incorrect workshop year.

The full surrounding discussion and Röver's preceding example on printed 1624–1625 were read; printed 1625 was inspected visually. The known example lies in Z wr Z and is generated by t, a_0^2 and a_0^{-1}a_1. It has 2-torsion in its abelianization. The source does not claim it is finitely presented. The nearby question about embedding Cleary's irrational-slope F_tau in F is distinct. Its known torsion in abelianization is no counterexample without an embedding.

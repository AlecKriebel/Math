# Final author result: Thompson-F subgroup abelianizations

**Original unresolved; five substantive turns completed.** No sixth author search. Full independent review is pending. This packet does not claim a new resolution or historical novelty.

Exact target: Röver–Sapir Question111, OWR26/2018 printed1625, whether every finitely presented subgroup of F has torsion-free abelianization. Report year2018; actual publication12April2019. No solvability, closedness, finite-index or orbit hypothesis is present in the source.

## Scoped results

1. Every FP_2(R) subgroup of (Z^d) wr Z is free abelian, for every nonzero commutative coefficient ring R. This uses the credited Bieri–Strebel splitting result in BGK Theorem2.7. Röver's ideal family has H_m_ab = Z² plus Z/m, and every finite abelian torsion group occurs in a finitely generated wreath variant, but all these variants fail FP_2.
2. A subdirect product whose factors have nonabelian simple derived subgroups and torsion-free abelian quotients has torsion-free abelianization. If its interval projections in F^r contain F′, its abelianization is exactly its combined endpoint-slope image, free of rank at most2r. A congruence fibre product of full F factors does not recreate the wreath torsion.
3. Every solvable PL image of an FP_2(R) group without nonabelian free subgroups is abelian. The proof retains the original domain through Bleak's restricted-sum/wreath hierarchy; it does not assume quotient images inherit FP_2. In particular the solvable class of the original question is positive. Both classification and splitting inputs are credited.
4. Every nontrivial finitely generated normal subgroup of a finite-piece PL interval group is detected by an ambient real character (integer-valued in F). Consequently every nonidentity abelianization-torsion witness has an infinitely generated normal closure. Finite normal generation is not ordinary finite generation; finite presentation does not close this gap.
5. PL interval groups have unique roots and balanced powers, by the credited one-bump germ method. Explicit finitely presented torsion-free bi-orderable class-two integer groups with those same abstract properties have abelianization Z² plus Z/m. They are **not F subgroups**: every homomorphism of these examples into PL_+(I) kills their central torsion witness. Thus these abstract proxy hypotheses cannot replace the actual embedding condition.

Closed subgroups are an already known positive class: they are diagram groups, whose integral homology is free abelian by Guba–Sapir. An imported desk suggestion to search for diagram-group torsion is corrected. The fresh September2026 closed-core finiteness results do not remove their closed/full-core hypotheses.

## Exact remaining gap

An arbitrary finitely presented subgroup of F need not be solvable, closed, a diagram group, a large-projection subdirect product, or have finitely generated derived subgroup. Neither root uniqueness nor bi-orderability implies that its derived subgroup is root-closed. The packet proves no universal bridge to the restricted positive classes and constructs no finitely presented embedded counterexample.

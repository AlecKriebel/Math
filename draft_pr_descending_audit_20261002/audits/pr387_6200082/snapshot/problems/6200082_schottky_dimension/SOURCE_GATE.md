Lewis Bowen, Cheeger constants and L²-Betti numbers, Duke Mathematical Journal164(3)(2015),569–615, DOI10.1215/00127094-2871415, proves the relevant free-group uniform Cheeger gap in Corollary1.3, printed571, and the dimension consequence in Corollary1.4, printed572. Important proof-history qualification: the random-net continuity argument in Bowen's Theorem4.1 was incomplete. Abért–Bergeron–Biringer–Gelander, Convergence of normalized Betti numbers in nonpositive curvature, Duke Mathematical Journal172(4)(2023),633–700, DOI10.1215/00127094-2022-0029, give the replacement argument.

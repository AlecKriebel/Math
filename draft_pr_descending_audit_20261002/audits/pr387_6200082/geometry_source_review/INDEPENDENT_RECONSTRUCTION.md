Original: Kapovich, *Problems on Boundaries of Groups and Kleinian Groups*, 24 October 2007, Problem 82 (Lewis Bowen), printed p21/PDF page21. For every epsilon > 0, does there exist a free convex-cocompact G < Isom(real H4) whose full limit set has Hausdorff dimension > 3-epsilon? The narrower Schottky case is also expressly asked. The primary item imposes neither a rank bound nor orientation preservation. The surrounding item explicitly defines classical Schottky via round spheres; its narrower classical conclusions cannot by themselves settle the broader free-group question.

Independently read/visually verified published Bowen pages571-572 before reading the prior review. His geometric condition means free and properly discontinuous action, not a cocompact action on all H4. His G2 condition requires residual finiteness and countability of the ambient abstract group, and the lower second Betti vanishing net for EVERY finitely generated subgroup. Direct free-group verification: each such subgroup H is finite-rank free (Nielsen-Schreier); every finite-index normal N of H is free and has a graph as K(N,1), hence b2(N)=0. Thus every ratio b2(N)/[H:N] is exactly zero. No approximation theorem or 3-manifold theorem is needed for this check. Residual finiteness holds for a finite-rank free group, and an actually geometric convex-cocompact subgroup is finitely generated and countable.

Bowen Theorem1.2 with X=H4,d=2 and any residually finite torsion-free uniform lattice L applies once its corrected continuity dependency is established. The positivity b2^(2)(L)>0 is precisely his Lemma8.1 (published601-602), referring to Lueck Theorem5.12. The lattice is a separate witness; it is not assumed to be free or drawn from the target family. Consequently a common c>0 exists with h(H4/G)>=c for every target G, independently of G rank or generators.

For M=H4/G complete and boundaryless, Cheeger gives lambda0(M)>=h(M)^2/4. In the non-elementary convex-cocompact case, delta=dim_H(full limit set)=critical exponent in curvature-minus-one normalization and round visual metric. Sullivan gives lambda0=delta(3-delta) for delta>=3/2; below that threshold lambda0=9/4. Pick 0<c0<=min(c,1). For delta>=3/2, (delta-3/2)^2<=9/4-c0^2/4, and so delta<=3/2+sqrt(9/4-c0^2/4)=D<3. For delta<3/2 the same D works. Rank0 or rank1 elementary groups have zero-dimensional (empty/finite) limit sets and are harmless.

This is a deduction from prior results, not a novel proof or an explicit numerical bound: c is existential. The exact remaining central dependency is the validity of the later replacement for Bowen Theorem4.1 in this H4 application. No prior candidate review has yet been read.

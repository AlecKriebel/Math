## Literal source and claim

EMS OWR 49/2012, Franz contribution, printed 2954–2956, was independently fetched, extracted, rendered, and visually inspected. It poses sharpness for rank r>=5 of the nonfree rational equivariant-cohomology syzygy bound. Printed 2955 Corollary 2 and 2956 use the letter n in their floor formulas; this letter is not defined as the torus rank there. The underlying Allday–Franz–Puppe arXiv:1111.0957v2 Corollary 1.4 and Proposition 5.12 explicitly use order >=r/2. Thus the mathematically justified reading of the historical question is: for each integer r>=5, is there a rational Poincare duality T=(S^1)^r space with nonfree H_T^*(X;Q) of exact syzygy order floor((r-1)/2)? The source spelling must remain distinguished from this justified interpretation.

The answer is established prior literature. Franz arXiv:1403.4485 (v1 2014-03-18; v3 2015-04-07; v4 2023-06-12; IMRN 2015, 13379–13405) supplies big polygon spaces and Corollary 5.3. Current v4 explicitly corrects degree shifts in Proposition 5.1. Franz–Huang arXiv:1904.01051v1, AGT 20 (2020), 2657–2675, determines order mu(ell)-1. These dependencies are mathematical assumptions unless their proofs are independently reconstructed; finite computation alone cannot promote the universal theorem.

## Independent geometric derivation

Take a=b=1, n=2m+1 odd, ell=(1,...,1). Define X inside C^(2n) by |u_i|^2+|z_i|^2=1 and sum_i u_i=0. More generally the same argument works for generic nonnegative ell and a,b>=1. Generic means no subset has the same total length as its complement. Odd equilateral ell is generic because a signed sum of an odd number of ones is nonzero. In even rank r=2m+2 use ell=(0,1,...,1), whose active rank is odd; the zero coordinate is an independent S^3 with an additional S^1 acting on z.

The equations define a closed subset of a product of unit spheres, hence compact. It is nonempty at u=0 and all z unit. To prove regularity, a real linear dependence of the gradients has sphere coefficients alpha_i and complex linear-constraint coefficient w. For each i it gives 2 alpha_i z_i=0 and 2 alpha_i u_i+ell_i w=0. A zero length coordinate forces alpha_i=0 because (u_i,z_i) is a nonzero unit vector. If w=0 all alpha vanish. If w!=0, every positive-length coordinate has nonzero alpha, z_i=0 and u_i a signed unit vector in the common w direction. The linear equation then produces a forbidden signed sum of the positive lengths. Therefore the differential has rank r+2a everywhere. This is a universal proof, not an extrapolation from the Jacobian sample. It also explains why even equilateral lengths can be singular.

The trivial oriented target of the defining submersion gives a trivial oriented normal bundle; ambient complex orientation induces orientation on X. Dimension is (2a+2b-1)r-2a, hence 3r-2 here. The scalar z-action preserves the equations. It is effective since the point u=0, every z_i!=0 has trivial stabilizer. Stabilizers are coordinate subtori, so finite infinitesimal orbit types. For connectedness scale u to zero while increasing the norms of z continuously, picking a direction when a z starts at zero; the locus u=0 is (S^(2b-1))^r, connected for b>=1. For b=1 this is T; for b>1 it is a product of larger odd spheres. Smooth compact orientable manifolds satisfy rational PD and finite cohomology. None of these conclusions requires a symplectic form or algebraic complex smoothness.

## Independent algebra and exact universal gap

Over Q[t_1,...,t_n], |t_i|=2, the sequence t_i^b for b>=1 is regular: successive quotients have a monomial basis in previous variables and multiplication by the next variable power is injective. Its Koszul complex has differential sum (-1)^position t_i^b e_(J minus i), with d^2=0 by opposite signs. It is a free resolution of Q[t]/(t_i^b). Reduction mod the homogeneous maximal ideal makes every differential zero, so the last Tor is nonzero. The qth Koszul syzygy has exact order q for 0<=q<n; its tail gives the lower order and minimal resolution has pd=n-q and depth q at the maximal ideal, forbidding the next order. The top syzygy is free and needs a separate convention: it is a syzygy of every order, not an exact finite order n in the unrestricted definition.

Franz's geometric cohomology identification is the central non-elementary input. Proposition 5.1 has free summands and Koszul summands K_(b,m), K_(b,m+2), with grading shifts; their minimum order is m. Corrected degree shifts must be tracked if the graded decomposition is stated. For the odd equilateral chamber, the independent later invariant gives another check: a long subset has size>=m+1; precisely size m+1 subsets have short one-element-deleted facets, all m+1 of them. Hence mu=m+1 and order m. Zero coordinate extensions leave this number unchanged: deleting a zero cannot cross long to short, while deleting an active coordinate is controlled by active cardinality. Product equivariant Kunneth gives a finite free direct sum of copies of the original module over a polynomial extension, preserving exact order by localization at the active-variable prime. Global depth at the full maximal ideal increases with extra variables and is not a substitute for this local test.

## Falsifiable checks and controls chosen before candidate access

1. Signed Koszul matrices: verify d^2=0, exact generic-fibre ranks at every adjacent position, nonzero residue Tor, and reject intentionally unsigned matrices. This tests mechanism and signs, not an asserted order formula.
2. Constraint Jacobian: exact rational rank at regular u=0 points, including zero length coordinates, and exact deficient rank at even equilateral collinear balanced fixed points. This tests the necessity of genericity.
3. Enumerate long sets and deleted short facets with exact integer arithmetic for odd equilateral, zero-extended, dominant-length (mu=1, empty fixed set), and balanced nongeneric controls. Exclude wall data rather than treating equality as a short set.

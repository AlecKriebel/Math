# Independent geometry and Morse reconstruction (sealed before candidate access)

## Scope and exact claim

We audit the geometric route to examples with effective torus rank r >= 5 and nonfree rational equivariant cohomology of syzygy order floor((r-1)/2), in the rational Poincare duality setting. The target is an existence claim for every such rank, not a theorem computing the order for every generic length vector. Source qualification is restricted to the primary sources actually acquired; it is not a worldwide completeness claim.

Source first: EMS OWR49/2012, Franz contribution, printed 2954-2956, was downloaded, extracted and visually inspected before any candidate or history read. It defines T=(S^1)^r, R=Q[t_1,...,t_r]. Its printed bound uses n without defining n here; the adjacent discussion uses r. We record this literal discrepancy. The intended rank-dependent threshold is independently confirmed by Theorem 1.1 of Franz, Big polygon spaces, arXiv:1403.4485v4 (2023 corrected revision of IMRN 2015, 13379-13405, DOI 10.1093/imrn/rnv090): m >= r/2 forces freeness for compact orientable T-manifolds.

## Construction and regularity, without polygon-space citations

Let a,b >= 1, n=2a, k=2b, d=n+k-1 and q=n-1. In (R^n x R^k)^r let

X(l) = { (u,z): ||u_j||^2+||z_j||^2=1 for every j, sum_j l_j u_j=0 }.

A nonnegative vector l is generic when sum_j epsilon_j l_j != 0 for every choice epsilon_j in {+1,-1}. It cannot be identically zero. Signs of length components can be removed by changing signs of the corresponding u_j. T acts by scalar complex multiplication on each z_j in C^b.

X is closed in the product of compact spheres V^r and is nonempty: set every u_j=0 and each z_j a unit vector. To prove smoothness, suppose a linear combination of the defining differentials vanishes. There are lambda_j in R and eta in R^n with

2 lambda_j u_j + l_j eta=0,    2 lambda_j z_j=0.

If eta=0, every lambda_j=0 because (u_j,z_j) has unit norm. If eta!=0, then for each positive l_j we have lambda_j!=0, z_j=0, and u_j=epsilon_j eta/||eta||. Zero-length coordinates force lambda_j=0. The defining linear constraint now gives sum_j epsilon_j l_j=0, contradicting genericity. Hence the differentials have rank r+n everywhere. X is a closed smooth manifold of dimension rd-n. This proof also handles zero coordinates directly.

Its normal bundle is globally framed by the r+n gradients in their fixed order. The ambient real vector space and the equation target have fixed orientations, so the regular-level convention orients X. The T-action preserves the equation map and the ambient orientation and therefore this orientation. Equivalently, connectedness of T rules out orientation reversal.

To prove path-connectedness explicitly, join (u,z) to u=0 by u_j(t)=t u_j, 0<=t<=1, and z_j(t)=sqrt(1-t^2||u_j||^2) xi_j, where xi_j=z_j/||z_j|| when z_j!=0 and otherwise is any unit vector. This is continuous even at t=1,z_j=0. The linear constraint is preserved. The endpoint subset is (S^{2b-1})^r, which is connected for b>=1. It is T only for b=1: the literal source proof on p.4 states this subset is T for arbitrary b, an innocuous but real dimension error.

At u=0 all z_j!=0, so the stabilizer is trivial. Thus the action is effective and admits free orbits. At every point the stabilizer is exactly the coordinate subtorus generated by the factors with z_j=0. There are at most 2^r orbit types, all connected. The orbit skeleton X_i is precisely the union of the algebraic conditions in which at most i of the z_j are nonzero. These are closed semialgebraic subsets of a compact smooth manifold. Compact smooth manifolds have finite ordinary cohomology; each skeleton and pair has finite ordinary cohomology by finite semialgebraic triangulation. If a theorem is stated for finite T-CW complexes, the separate standard equivariant triangulation theorem for compact smooth T-manifolds supplies that hypothesis; the Morse calculation itself does not require inventing a finite CW structure.

## Zero lengths, chambers, odd and even ranks

For l=(0,l'), the equations separate literally into V x X(l'), and the torus action separates into the new S^1 acting on the C^b factor of V and T' acting on X(l'). Removing zero components preserves genericity. For rank r=2m+1, l=(1,...,1) is generic because every signed sum is odd; J is short exactly when |J|<=m. The equilateral vector of even rank is not generic: take exactly half the plus signs. For rank r=2m+2, l=(0,1,...,1) is generic because its 2m+1 nonzero terms have odd signed sums. Its space is V x X(1,...,1) with independent torus factors and remains effective, smooth, compact, connected and oriented. Its dimension rd-n agrees exactly with d+((r-1)d-n).

The open chamber condition admits zero lengths; zero is not automatically a wall. A positive perturbation of a zero length smaller than the minimum signed gap stays in the same chamber. Chamberwise diffeomorphism follows by lifting a parameter vector field along the regular-level family over the compact line segment inside a chamber, using an invariant metric. Compact fibres prevent flow escape. This is sufficient for the even-rank existence argument; no assertion about all generic orders is needed.

## Correct Morse function and exact local Hessian

For positive lengths use Y=V^r\X(l) and

f(u,z)=-||sum_j l_j u_j||^2.

It is negative on Y. Its derivative is -2 <sum l_j u_j, sum l_j v_j>. The literal source (3.9) omits l_j and (3.10) has a plus sign. These cannot be used unchanged for general l. For example, r=2,l=(1,2),u=(1,-1),z=0 lies in Y, but the printed unweighted function is zero and critical there; it is not the claimed negative exhaustion. The corrected weighted function is the natural one.

Put S=sum l_j u_j. At a critical point, the ambient gradient restricted to each factor sphere is radial: (l_j S,0)=lambda_j (u_j,z_j). Since l_j>0 and S!=0, lambda_j!=0, z_j=0, ||u_j||=1, and each u_j is parallel or antiparallel to S. Choosing v=S/||S|| gives u_j=s_j v, where s_j=-1 on J and +1 off J. Then c=sum s_j l_j=l(J^c)-l(J)>0, so J is short. Conversely these points are critical. Thus each critical manifold P_J is S^q, and there is one for every short J. Its value is -c^2. Genericity guarantees c>0.

Here is a direct local calculation, independent of the source's critical-point and Hessian citations. At v choose x_j in v-perp and w_j in R^k, and write

u_j=s_j sqrt(1-||x_j||^2-||w_j||^2)v+x_j,    z_j=w_j.

The quadratic part of f+c^2 is

Q = c sum_j s_j l_j (||x_j||^2+||w_j||^2) - ||sum_j l_j x_j||^2.

The Hessian is 2Q. Each of the q real x-coordinate blocks has matrix A=c diag(s_j l_j)-l l^T, and each w_j block is c s_j l_j I_k. A s=0. Set D=c diag(s_j l_j). Then D is invertible, D^{-1}l=s/c, and l^T D^{-1}l=1. With v0=D^{-1}l, decompose x=y+t v0 with l^T y=0. Direct expansion gives x^T A x=y^T D y, with the t direction zero. The vector v0 has positive D-norm 1. Therefore the restriction of D to l-perp removes one positive direction, and A has exactly |J| negative, r-|J|-1 positive, and one zero eigenvalue. The w blocks have |J|k negative and (r-|J|)k positive directions. Total index is |J|(q+k)=|J|d; nullity is q, exactly TP_J. Thus f is Morse-Bott. This is a symbolic proof for all positive generic vectors, not finite-check extrapolation.

The source p.6 says f|V_J has a minimum at P_J, then infers index dim V_J. In the negative-function convention the relevant restriction has a maximum. At r=3,l=(1,1,1),J={1}, outside coordinates fixed v, f(-v,v,v)=-1 while f(v,v,v)=-9, so the printed minimum claim is false. Nor can a direct-sum-of-coordinate-tangents argument establish nondegeneracy while retaining the q tangent directions of P_J. The Hessian above supplies the missing correct argument.

## Negative bundle and rational/integral perfection

Define W_J by allowing factors in J to range over V and making the other u_i all equal h in S^q, z_i=0. Positive shortness gives ||sum l_j u_j|| >= l(J^c)-l(J)>0, so W_J lies in Y. In its tangent space at P_J let the common x_i outside J be h, and put y_j=x_j+h for j in J. The quadratic form simplifies exactly to

Q|TW_J = -c sum_{j in J} l_j ||y_j||^2 - ||sum_{j in J} l_j y_j||^2 - c sum_{j in J} l_j ||w_j||^2.

Its only kernel is the critical tangent h; the quotient by TP_J is negative definite. Its dimension is |J|d, the full negative index. Hence spectral projection gives an isomorphism between the actual negative eigenspace bundle and the normal bundle of P_J inside W_J (a maximal negative subbundle need not be literally the orthogonal eigenspace for an arbitrary chosen metric). As a T-vector bundle that normal bundle is

E_J = direct_sum_{j in J} (TS^q direct_sum (S^q x C_j^b)),

with T trivial on TS^q and the jth character on C_j^b. This also verifies orientability. The relative Thom homology has two generators in degrees |J|d and |J|d+q, represented by V_J and W_J. V_J meets P_J once in the fibre direction; W_J represents the Thom lift of the sphere fundamental class. Their maxima are -c^2, so they belong to the correct sublevel stage.

Reflect one real coordinate inside each C_j^b. These commuting involutions preserve f and act trivially on P_J. Reflection j reverses the negative-bundle orientation precisely when j in J. Both relative Thom generators transform by the distinct character indexed by J. Earlier critical manifolds carry different characters; simultaneous critical values are treated together. The connecting map of the filtration is equivariant and hence vanishes over Q by character separation. Inductively the old homology is free abelian with the cycle basis V_I,W_I; over Z, an opposite-character component satisfies 2 delta=0 and the target is torsion-free, so it vanishes there too. This proves perfection and the integral cycle basis. Compact negative sublevels exhaust Y; above the largest critical value no new critical points occur, so the same calculation computes H_*(Y).

This mechanism is essential. For nonempty J the equivariant Euler class of E_J is zero: q is odd, so e(TS^q)=0 and each summand contributes e(TS^q)t_j^b=0. Therefore an argument claiming perfection from injectivity of the negative-bundle Euler class would fail. The nonzero Euler class used in the inclusion map below belongs to a different bundle.

Zero-length Morse factors should be split off before this derivation. D is not invertible if a length is zero, and the critical locus then contains the complete V factor; treating its old P_J as the complete critical set is false. Product Kunneth or a positive chamber perturbation handles zero lengths without that misuse.

## Equivariant basis, Euler class, and orientation sign

Both V^r and Y have ordinary cycle bases represented by compact oriented T-invariant submanifolds V_J and W_J. Their fundamental classes have equivariant lifts. The spectral sequence with E2=H_*(.) tensor R therefore collapses; by the lift basis argument their equivariant homologies are free R-modules on these cycles. This is not a consequence of ordinary Morse perfection alone: the invariant cycles are the additional check.

For the factor sphere V=S(C^a direct_sum C^b), its fixed sphere is Vbar=S(C^a). Its normal bundle is Vbar x C^b of scalar weight one. With the boundary orientation of the complex vector space, the Euler class is t^b, degree 2b. Product orientations agree because passing the even-dimensional complex normal factor across the radial direction gives no sign. Hence [Vbar]_T=t^b[V]_T. This normal bundle has no TS^q factor and must not be confused with E_J.

The diagonal of (Vbar)^{J^c} maps in ordinary homology to the sum of the one-factor sphere classes, each with coefficient +1, as follows by pairing with the one-factor top cohomology generators. Applying each factor's Euler class and then the cross product gives

iota_*^T[V_J]_T=[V_J]_T,
iota_*^T[W_J]_T=sum_{j notin J} (-1)^{#{i in J:i>j}} t_j^b [V_{J union {j}}]_T.

Orient W_J as (increasing J factors) x (common sphere). Bringing the final j factor into increasing order crosses #{i in J:i>j} factors of odd dimension d. This gives exactly the displayed sign. The homological degree check is |W_J|=|J|d+q=(|J|+1)d-2b; with negative homological grading, multiplication by t_j^b raises degree by 2b and therefore preserves the total map degree.

PAL duality and the long exact pair sequence give

0 -> coker(iota_*^T)[rd] -> H_T^*(X) -> ker(iota_*^T)[rd-1] -> 0.

This is a module extension, not an automatic direct-sum decomposition. The source v4 explicitly fixes degree shifts in Proposition 5.1 relative to earlier versions. After normalizing Koszul syzygies to be generated in degree zero, the equilateral summand shifts are K_{b,m}[md] and K_{b,m+2}[(m+2)d-2q-1], together with free R[|J|d] for |J|<m and R[|J|d-q-1] for |J|>m+1. A submission using an older unqualified shift needs correction even if its order-only result is unaffected.

## Limits, falsifiers, and evidence status

The direct regular-value, connectedness, stabilizer, genericity, weighted Hessian, negative-bundle, reflection perfection, and Euler/sign computations above are proved for their stated scope. Exact rational checks are controls on the local formulas, not proofs of their universality. They intentionally include wall degeneracy, zero-length splitting, b>1 connectedness dimension, and failure of the printed unweighted function. The all-generic-length equality syzord=mu(l)-1 is only Conjecture 6.6 in the acquired v4 source; its finite checks through rank nine do not authorize promotion to a theorem. The sharp existence construction uses only the equilateral case and the zero-length product. Algebraic syzygy order and the global upper bound should be independently audited by their assigned families.

2. Final paper, printed pp.347–348, Section3.5 and Table5: https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0174.pdf . This edition has the same target and prime/centroid convention. This is the credited canonical parametrization, with the modulus equal to the caustic eccentricity. We state the exact parameters and derive the actual outer-vertex intersection anew.

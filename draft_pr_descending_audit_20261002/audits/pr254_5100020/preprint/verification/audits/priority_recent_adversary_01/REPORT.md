## Exact claim and test for coverage

For a fixed ellipse and strictly nested nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic, the product of the ordered signed area of the **original-side pedal at the origin** and that of the **original-vertex antipedal at the origin** is phase-independent for every primitive least odd period N>=3 and admissible coprime star winding. The antipedal lines are `P_i dot X=|P_i|²`; all consecutive real intersections must be finite. A zero signed antipedal area is permitted. Circles have a separate regular-star argument. Outer-tangent polygons, caustic-contact polygons, focal constructions, unsigned face sums, triangle-only identities, and even lists created by repeating odd orbits are not interchangeable with this claim.

A priority objection requires an earlier checkable proof of that result, or an earlier combination of checkable results implying it under these hypotheses. An exact target-code match is unnecessary. Conversely, a conjecture table, an observed video invariant, a shared analytic tool, or a reformulation carrying the same missing area trace is insufficient.

## Earlier focal product: the title is narrower than its lemma, but the lemma still does not cover this target

Alper Ferudun's [Focal Pedal-Antipedal Area Products in Elliptic Billiards](https://eulersolve.org/papers/amr-050-0021/) is archived as [Zenodo 23079799](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23079799). The record's server creation is **2026-10-01 10:00:33.366348 UTC**, before the candidate's reported author window. I authenticated the original deposited source ZIP against Zenodo's MD5 and read its full manuscript. Its original `main.tex` SHA256 `7bdaae97eacd19214d46c175bc7d9d119d02af7a3593877ec49ef182fbeb8247` equals the current manuscript.

Lemma4 proves `q=R A` for **any centrally paired** elliptic-caustic trajectory at a **fixed focus**, beyond the main title's period0mod4 restriction. With alpha=a², beta=b², D=alpha beta, its coefficient is

`R=[D(3 beta-alpha)+lambda(alpha-beta)²]/[2D(beta-lambda)]`.

The proof combines genuinely opposite edges and uses focal distance identities. An odd primitive vertex set on a nondegenerate ellipse cannot be centrally paired: negation has no fixed vertex and would partition the set into pairs. Repeating an odd orbit creates duplicates, not the missing opposite edges. Setting the focal displacement to zero in the focal identities forces a circle, not an arbitrary noncircular ellipse. Therefore the combination with PR204 has two failed hypotheses, and does not cover k403,a. Extending this lemma to odd-origin antipedals would require new construction-specific mathematics; this route is **blocked as an unsupported transfer**, not a priority objection.

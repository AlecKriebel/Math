# Primary bibliography and actual reading scope

Audit date: 2026-10-13 UTC. Full sources are private reading copies. Download provenance and hashes are published as facts in the final integrity manifest; raw responses and third-party contents are excluded.

1. Dan Reznik, Ronaldo Garcia, Jair Koiller. Forty New Invariants of N-Periodics in the Elliptic Billiard, [arXiv2004.12497v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.12497v1), 26 April 2020. Read definitions, signed-area convention, related original/inner/outer/pedal tables, complete antipedal table and references; visually checked Table4 p5. Exact k403,a appears as an unproved observation. Later [v11](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2004.12497v11), 29 October 2020, is titled Eighty New Invariants; visually checked Table5 p7. Published Fifty New Invariants, Arnold Mathematical Journal7 (2021),341–355, [DOI10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-021-00174-y), online18 February2021: definitions/source scopes and Table5 p348 visually checked.

2. Arseniy Akopyan, Richard Schwartz, Serge Tabachnikov. Billiards in Ellipses Revisited, European Journal of Mathematics8 (2022),1313–1327, online9 September2020, [DOI10.1007/s40879-020-00426-9](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-020-00426-9). [Published reading copy](https://par.nsf.gov/servlets/purl/10408491). Read introduction and theorem statements, polar-duality background, grid connection, and complete §7 proof of Theorem1.3. The area-ratio polygon uses tangents to the ellipse.

3. Ana C. Chávez-Caliz. More About Areas and Centers of Poncelet Polygons, Arnold Mathematical Journal7 (2021),91–106, online14 August2020, [DOI10.1007/s40598-020-00154-8](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-020-00154-8). [Published PDF](https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/020-0154.pdf). Read theorem scopes, algebraic-area/degeneration context and complete §5/Theorem6 proof. Its original/outer product has even parity.

4. Misha Bialy, Serge Tabachnikov. Dan Reznik's Identities and More, [arXiv2001.08469v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2001.08469v2), 5 May2020, [DOI10.1007/s40879-020-00428-7](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-020-00428-7). Read the generating-function construction and proofs, ellipse corollaries, outer-pedal theorem and focal/origin distance-product theorem. No target antipedal-area theorem occurs.

5. Hellmuth Stachel. On the Motion of Billiards in Ellipses, European Journal of Mathematics8 (2022),1602–1622, online24 January2022, [DOI10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2). [Institutional copy](https://repositum.tuwien.at/bitstream/20.500.12708/136087/1/Stachel-2022-European%20Journal%20of%20Applied%20Mathematics-vor.pdf). Read theorem inventory, canonical-coordinate §4.3 and equation4.9, relevant proof and surrounding motion context. The actual journal is European Journal of Mathematics despite the reading-copy filename.

6. Hellmuth Stachel. The Geometry of Billiards in Ellipses and their Poncelet Grids, Journal of Geometry112, article40 (2021), published18 October2021, [DOI10.1007/s00022-021-00606-2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00022-021-00606-2), first [arXiv2105.03362](https://arxiv.org/abs/2105.03362)7 May2021. Read grid/normal/polar constructions and all theorem/corollary scopes, complete center-pedal angle-sum argument/Theorem4.7 and tangent-distance Corollary4.9. They are not an antipedal-area identity.

7. Mark Levi, Serge Tabachnikov. The Poncelet Grid and the Billiard in an Ellipse, [arXiv math/0511009](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0511009),2005; American Mathematical Monthly114 (2007),895–908. Read grid theorem, complete relevant set/conic constructions, integrability/closure statements and geometric meaning of the preserved phase-space area form. Euclidean derived polygon area is not that phase-space form.

8. Hellmuth Stachel. A Triple of Projective Billiards, KoG26 (2022), [published PDF](https://hrcak.srce.hr/en/file/417317). Read definitions of original, inner/contact and outer/polar polygons, Lemma1, complete theorem/corollary inventory and relevant constructions. They are tangent/diagonal grids, not the original origin antipedal. Initial URL without /en/ failedHTTP418; the alternate primary URL succeeded.

9. Pedro Roitman, Ronaldo Garcia, Dan Reznik. New Invariants of Poncelet–Jacobi Bicentric Polygons, Arnold Mathematical Journal7 (2021),619–637, online18 August2021, [DOI10.1007/s40598-021-00188-6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-021-00188-6), [published PDF](https://armj.math.stonybrook.edu/pdf-Springer-final/021-0188.pdf).

# Reading-copy publication policy

Only authored REPORT.md, BIBLIOGRAPHY.md, QUERY_LOG.md, RESEARCH_LOG.md, construction_checks.py, CONSTRUCTION_CHECKS.json, this policy, and FINAL_AUDIT_INTEGRITY.json are suitable for the public owned checkpoint or verification package.

Exclude the entire sources/ directory, the entire receipts/ directory, and referenced parent primary_source_reading_copies/. They contain full third-party papers, extracted text/images, or raw provider/web content kept only for local reading and provenance verification.

The manifest may publish source URLs, bibliographic facts, byte counts and hashes without copying the full third-party contents. No assertion of permission to republish an entire downloaded source follows from its accessibility.

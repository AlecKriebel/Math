## Finite input-to-surface construction

Use Palu–Pilaud–Plamondon (PPP) Definitions 2.2, 4.6, 4.8 and Theorem 4.10. At each vertex fill incoming and outgoing incidence lists to two with fresh blossom arrows. A relation completion is one of the two permutations of two slots: an incoming/outgoing pair is related precisely when their slots are matched. Gentleness makes the prescribed subtable extendible. If both original incidence lists are nonempty, one prescribed entry determines the permutation; otherwise different choices differ by relabeling fresh blossom arrows. Choose the first admissible completion under fixed finite labels.

For each completed arrow use a counterclockwise lozenge with corners G,S,R,T. Glue incoming TG to outgoing GS at a nonrelation, and incoming RT to outgoing SR at a relation. The gluing reverses polygon-boundary orientation. Green is the white dissection of the input algebra; red is its black dual. At an original vertex the two complementary matchings give a four-sector circle link. At a colored corner the permitted or relation successor graph gives an interval link for a chain and a circle for a cycle. Fresh blossom leaves give interval links. Thus the finite quotient is an oriented surface, with all repeated corner occurrences retained.

A green cycle would give unbounded nonzero path length, so finite-dimensionality excludes white punctures. Red cycles supply black punctures. Each original algebra vertex is the crossing of one concatenated white arc with one concatenated black arc. Auxiliary blossom points subdivide boundaries and are not extra colored marks or algebra vertices. PPP's inverse construction identifies the resulting white-dissection bound quiver with the input; the local rule agrees with APS Definition 4.1. A nonempty finite component has a blossom, since a finite permitted-successor graph with no exit would have a cycle; it therefore has an original boundary.

Cutting along the black dissection retains only green gluings. Each component is a finite chain of disks joined along boundary intervals, hence a disk with one white boundary occurrence. Black punctures become corner occurrences on these cut disks. This also follows from APS Proposition 3.11 with the colors exchanged. The absence of white punctures is essential.

Triangulation is finite and rational. Insert a center in each lozenge and matched midpoints on paired edges, and fan into corner–midpoint–center triangles. Each such triangle has three distinct quotient vertex types. Preserve genuine edge and face occurrences; barycentric subdivision by vertex–edge–face flags separates repeated endpoint incidences and gives a genuine finite simplicial triangulation. All chart coordinates and affine seam maps are rational. Subdivide puncture stars until disjoint, then cut off small rational polygonal neighborhoods. Denote the compact puncture-truncated surface by S0; original boundaries and puncture ends retain distinct labels. Computing components, boundary cycles, Euler characteristic and g=(2-h-chi)/2 is finite graph traversal on this known oriented manifold.

## Explicit certificate search

For g=0 take the empty handle system. For g>0 enumerate at height H all ordered 2g-tuples of closed PL curves, with at most H segments in total, each segment in one triangle chart, and rational barycentric endpoint coordinates of bounded numerator and denominator. Use one canonical endpoint ordering on each quotient edge. The common seam parameter is t in that ordering; an oppositely ordered local chart uses 1-t. Every height is finite and computable; every finite rational encoding occurs at some height.

Accept a tuple (a1,b1,...,ag,bg) only when all curves are simple, interior, avoid collars and vertices, and have no overlaps, reversals, or unwanted multiple crossings. Each ai,bi pair must have one transverse intersection, oriented with sign +1; all other curve pairs are disjoint. Require disjoint once-holed-torus regular neighborhoods Ni and connected genus-zero complement S0 minus the interiors of the Ni. Require general position with the finite dual dissection. For the disk-side predicate use **each maximal portion between successive dual-dissection crossings**, not each elementary triangle-chart PL edge. Each portion must be a proper simple crosscut of its cut disk, with occurrence copies retained.

These predicates are decidable. Rational determinants and rational linear equations give intersections and their signs. Split graph edges into side copies and vertices into cyclically ordered sectors to cut the finite arrangement. Finite traversals give component and boundary counts and Euler characteristic. A regular neighborhood consists of vertex disks and edge bands in the recorded rotation order. For a once-crossing pair its spine has one vertex, two loop edges and alternating rotation; its neighborhood has chi=-1 and one boundary, hence genus one. Widths can be chosen by finite rational enumeration satisfying incidence inequalities: nonincident disjoint compact segments have positive separation, and adjacent incidences are handled within vertex disks. Complement triangulations preserve these sector occurrences. No homeomorphism decision oracle or effective surface classifier is assumed.

The acceptance conditions certify the required geometric handle basis after filling all ends. They are stronger than merely asking for a symplectic integral homology matrix. Surface classification guarantees g disjoint handles with connected planar complement and simple core pairs crossing once; it is used only for existence. A simultaneous small perturbation gives general position with finitely many fixed edges and vertices while preserving the prescribed crossings. Joint rational PL approximation in small separated neighborhoods preserves embeddedness, crossing rotations, and complementary topology. Seam points are chosen once and shared between charts, so rational approximation does not break exact joining conditions.

Every handle curve intersects the dual dissection: it intersects its partner once and cannot be contained in one disk. Every maximal cut portion is a proper embedded interval with distinct boundary occurrences, even for a loop with only one physical crossing. A proper interval separates a disk into two sides. Thus a generic rational handle system satisfies the extra crosscut predicate. Its finite encoding appears at finite H, proving termination without a bound for that H.

## Exact winding calculation and exceptional cases

Choose simple polygonal collars around each original boundary and black puncture, oriented with the retained surface on the right. Put them in general position with the dissection. A puncture has incident black arcs because otherwise it would lie inside a dual-complement disk. A noncontractible boundary parallel cannot lie in one such disk. In an unpunctured disk component, nonempty input gives a nonempty dissection and its dual, so a sufficiently small whole-boundary collar crosses dual-arc ends. These observations remove the zero-crossing exception for nonempty components.

Cut every handle and peripheral curve at dual crossings. Determine which side of each maximal crosscut contains the disk's unique white boundary occurrence using finite oriented sector traversal. Sum +1 for left and -1 for right. Local orthogonal smoothing at crossings and corner smoothing occur in disjoint small disks by embedded isotopy; no curl or crossing is introduced, and side data and regular homotopy are preserved. APS Lemma 3.18 and Proposition 3.20 therefore identify the integer sum with the canonical line-field winding.

For the isolated field block the surface is a disk with two marks of each color and one white/black dual-arc pair; its boundary collar crosses twice and has winding +2. For the square-zero loop the surface is an annulus with one original white mark; the outer and puncture windings are +1 and -1. The same physical seam crossing has distinct cut-side occurrences. An empty component is treated by the zero-category output above, without applying these formulas to an empty dissection.

## Comparison key and corrected sufficiency proof

For each connected component record g,b,p and the multiset R of **paired** (n(c),w(c)) over all b+p ends, where n is the white boundary count on an original boundary and zero at a puncture. Original boundaries have positive n. Total boundary marks, if returned, are 2 sum n. Return curve encodings and raw handle windings as separate certificates, excluded from the comparison key.

The remaining handle datum H is:

- g=0: empty.
- g=1: nonnegative gcd of w(a1),w(b1) and every peripheral w(c)+2; gcd of all zero entries is zero.
- g>=2: ODD if some winding on the handles or peripherals is odd; otherwise EVEN_RADICAL_NONZERO if some peripheral w is 0 modulo 4; otherwise the Arf bit sum_i (w(ai)/2+1)(w(bi)/2+1) modulo 2.

The classification justification is **LP Theorem 1.2.4 on the compact core, followed by APS Theorem 6.1/Remark 7.2**. It does not rely on the literal puncture-truncated sufficient direction of printed APS Theorem 7.4.

Equality of paired records supplies an end matching preserving boundary type, white count and winding. Surface classification and oriented boundary collars give a marked diffeomorphism Phi implementing it. Restrict the canonical line fields to S0 and compare eta with Phi^* eta' on that same core. Restriction to the core is a bijection on line-field homotopy classes, since each omitted end is a cylinder and extends a core section uniquely up to homotopy. Every core boundary winding matches. LP Theorem 1.2.4 then gives exactly the displayed H conditions for equivalence under the mapping class group fixing all core boundaries pointwise. Its representative extends along the collars, preserving the marks and punctures after Phi's permitted permutation. APS 6.1/7.2 supplies derived equivalence. Conversely those APS results preserve every peripheral winding, and the LP invariants, proving necessity.

For an identically oriented curve LP winding has the opposite sign to APS winding; LP boundary orientation is also opposite to our peripheral orientation. Thus peripheral integers coincide in the two conventions, while handle integers negate. Gcd, parity and the even Arf expression are invariant under that negation. The peripheral +2 terms are unchanged. This sign check is necessary, not a claim that arbitrary reversal of peripheral conventions is harmless.

LP identifies the genus-one gcd with the ideal generator of all nonseparating simple-curve windings, ensuring basis independence. A peripheral detour changes winding by w(c)+2, as the pair-of-pants identity also shows. In higher genus winding modulo two is a homology functional, so testing the handles and ends detects it. If all windings are even, q(gamma)=w(gamma)/2+1 is the quadratic refinement over F2. Its radical is spanned by peripheral classes. It vanishes on that radical exactly when all peripheral windings are 2 modulo 4; only then does it descend to the capped closed surface and have a basis-independent Arf invariant. Otherwise a handle-only Arf sum can depend on the lift and is omitted. This verifies every branch and excludes raw basis data from K.

A connected quiver block has intrinsically indecomposable bounded derived category: every indecomposable vertex projective belongs to one factor of an orthogonal product decomposition; nonzero maps at adjacent vertices put all such projectives in the same factor. Any other factor is orthogonal to all projectives in all degrees, hence has zero cohomology. This does not assume finite global dimension or generation by finite cones. An equivalence therefore permutes connected factors, giving the multiset of connected K=(g,b,p,R,H). The zero category corresponds to the empty multiset.

## Why the source correction is mandatory

Take two oriented full-relation cycles of lengths m,n>=3 and add one unrelated bridge from the first cycle to the second. Denote the algebra A(m,n). It is gentle; permitted paths have counts m+n,m+n+1,2,1 in lengths 0,1,2,3, and none longer. Its dimension is 2(m+n)+4. PPP's finite construction gives g=0,b=1,p=2, white boundary count m+n-1, outer winding m+n-2, and puncture windings -m,-n. Consequently A(3,5) and A(4,4) have equal original-boundary and topological data but different puncture winding multisets. They are separated by APS 6.1, and by their AG data. The submitted key already separates them; the printed 7.4 range restricted to j<=b would not. The all-end LP deduction above repairs the proof without changing the numerical algorithm.

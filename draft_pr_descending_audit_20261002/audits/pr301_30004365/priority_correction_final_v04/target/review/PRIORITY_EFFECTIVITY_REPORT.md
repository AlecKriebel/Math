## The strongest old constructive mechanism

Vegter and Yap, *Computational Complexity of Combinatorial Surfaces*, SoCG1990 pp.102–111, explicitly compute a refinement of a **closed orientable triangulated surface** and families of simple closed edge paths C_i,D_i. Lemma4.3, printed p.107, gives one intersection per pair and disjoint pair unions. The construction and proof occupy pp.107–111; Lemma4.8, p.110, ensures a connected complement, and Lemma4.9, p.111, identifies it as a sphere with g holes. Thus this is the needed geometry, not an arbitrary homology symplectic matrix. Their O(gn) bound applies to the closed-surface subroutine only. The published proceedings text is an extended abstract with some maintenance details deferred; its constructive output and geometric proof are present in the body. [Institutional primary PDF](https://pure.rug.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/3354078/1990ProcSCGVegter.pdf).

Lazarus–Pocchiola–Vegter–Verroust2001 independently supply canonical PL generators with a disk complement (Theorem1, §§3–4, pp.80–89). Their incremental proof gives noninterleaved pairs at the basepoint and a connected-sum decomposition. Our local basepoint surgery converts these to the separated handle system. The 1990 result is stronger for this audit because it outputs those pairs directly. [Primary proceedings PDF in HAL](https://hal.science/hal-00804691/document), DOI10.1145/378583.378630. HAL's2013 deposit date is not the2001 publication date.

The closed-input hypothesis is material. Neither the1990 final remark about boundary extensions nor an abstract symplectic basis suffices by itself. `CANONICAL_TO_HANDLE_DERIVATION.md` supplies the finite rational PL conversion: cap all h=b+p ends, compute pairs, shrink/move each cap by a finite PL ambient homeomorphism into the planar complement while fixing earlier caps, and pull the graph back. Removing caps leaves a connected planar complement with g+h boundary cycles. **No line field is extended across any cap.** Winding is evaluated on the resulting curves in the original compact core. This is an independent elementary construction, not a theorem being falsely attributed to either paper.

PPP's polygon-gluing construction (Definitions4.6/4.8, Theorem4.10 and Remark4.11, pp.10–12 of the pinned arXiv text) is finite. Add rational triangulation, the old pair algorithm and end conversion, generic position, APS's finite signed-side winding rule, then LP's integer gcd/parity/Arf operations. APS Theorem4.1 and Remark5.2 in v1 provide the complete arbitrary finite-dimensional gentle derived-equivalence bridge; LP alone has a narrower algebra application. This composes a terminating numerical algorithm from source inputs available by **4April2019**, the first APS submission. That date is the availability of ingredients, **not** an earlier publication date for this composition.

## LP effectivity and the puncture range

LP v1, dated19January2018, already gives the full numerical orbit classifier (then Theorem1.2.5; current v5 Theorem1.2.4, pp.7–9; publication Theorem1.8). Its proof reduces the orbit question to boundary windings, the genus-one gcd and finite parity/quadratic-form data. The introduction calls these invariants computable. Reading the full proof does not reveal an uncomputed orbit oracle after geometric windings are supplied. The proof's mapping-class actions justify completeness; the algorithm evaluates finite arithmetic rather than searching those actions. LP's algebra corollary concerns homologically smooth graded algebras and is sufficient there; APS supplies the unrestricted finite-dimensional necessity/sufficiency used here. [LP primary history and versions](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.06370), [publisher text](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00208-019-01894-5).

Every LP version uses all d compact boundary components. Applying that theorem to the truncated gentle surface means d=b+p. Marked-boundary and puncture ends remain distinguished by the stop counts paired with winding. No new punctured-surface classifier is needed. In genus1, LP may omit one redundant boundary from the gcd: the relation sum_j(w(∂j)+2)=0 shows that adding it leaves the gcd unchanged.

The version comparison is recorded in `VERSION_COMPARISON.json`: APS v1–v3 truncate both paired n and w at j≤b in Theorem5.4(2), despite using a permutation on b+p ends. The final2023 statement extends n to b+p but retains w only through b. Its quoted LP range is also truncated, whereas its proof uses all c in B. This is an old statement/proof mismatch, not a new LP classification theorem. ROOT's A(3,5)/A(4,4) witness usefully exposes it; this family relies on ROOT's accepted witness mathematics and does not claim to have independently reproduced that full computation. [APS history](https://arxiv.org/abs/1904.02555), [final publisher record](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s00029-022-00822-x).

Official histories give APS submissions4,9,11April2019 and final publication14March2023; LP submissions19January2018,9February2018,4November2018,27February2019,27August2019 and online publication29August2019 (issue2020). Some downloaded arXiv PDFs have later TeX build-date headers; historical dates above come from the official version history, not those headers. ## Counterarguments to novelty and the strongest defensible contribution

The adversarial objection is concrete: the supposed high-genus basis bottleneck was already algorithmically solved in general surface topology, including the geometric intersections and complement condition. Quiver-to-surface construction and side-count winding were explicit before the submission, while the numerical classifier was advertised as computable. The current enumeration is another effective route to the same old geometric witnesses. The local end conversion and source correction may warrant writing down, but neither hides a new mapping-class or homeomorphism decision problem.

The contrary evidence must also remain visible. Full OWR2020 pp.161–164 explicitly pose the algorithmic question; final APS Remark7.6 still says their geometric basis construction is not presented as an algorithm producing quiver walks. It would therefore be inaccurate to claim APS itself already published the desired walk procedure. Those remarks leave room for an explicit gentle-specific bridge. They do not make the old constructive topology theorem disappear, or prove historical novelty of a broad computability claim.

A defensible narrower contribution is a fully credited constructive supplement: an explicit finite certificate search or composition, exact winding evaluation, an all-end numerical formulation, and a checked concrete witness to the printed-range error. Its exact presentation/counterexample priority remains to be assessed across the other families. This family does **not** clear wording such as first solution, new complete classification, first computability proof, new symplectic-basis algorithm, practical implementation, or new complexity result.

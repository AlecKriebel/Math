## 1. Matching completion and manifold links

For incoming and outgoing lists of length at most two, relation/nonrelation gentleness says each row and column contains at most one of each value. A complete 2-by-2 table therefore has related entries on one permutation and unrelated entries on the other. If both original lists are nonempty, any prescribed entry chooses one of the two permutations. If one list is empty, no entry is prescribed and a fresh-slot transposition identifies the choices. The independent exhaustive local control checks all 13 allowed subtable patterns. Loops cause an arrow to appear in both lists, not to reduce either list's length; the same argument applies.

The lozenge corners G,S,R,T are counterclockwise. A nonrelation glues incoming T-G to outgoing G-S, identifying G with G and T with S. A relation glues incoming R-T to outgoing S-R, identifying R with R and T with S. Thus every pairing reverses the boundary direction. At an original quiver vertex, the link has one sector for each of its two incoming and two outgoing arrow occurrences. The two complementary matchings alternate, producing one four-sector circle, not two disconnected links. At a G corner the allowed successor graph has indegree/outdegree at most one. A chain makes an interval link; a cyclic component makes a circle link. The R corner follows the related successor graph. A blossom leaf has one polygon sector and an interval link. These cover every quotient vertex type, proving orientability and the manifold property.

The new controls reconstruct link germs independently: each polygon corner adds an edge joining its two side-germ occurrences, and seam identification glues the corresponding endpoint germs. Connectedness and degrees are checked at every quotient vertex; boundary walks are checked for branching and merging. This is stronger than testing chi alone. An intentional infinite-dimensional two-loop control has no boundary and has two white punctures; it demonstrates why the finite-dimensional promise is necessary for the disk argument.

A white successor cycle repeats a nonzero monomial path indefinitely, so is excluded. If no blossoms existed, every arrow would have a permitted successor in the finite arrow graph, forcing a cycle. The isolated vertex instead has four blossoms directly. Hence every nonempty finite component has an original boundary and positive white count. Every colored boundary component alternates G and R, with blossoms only auxiliary subdivisions.

Cutting the red dissection deletes all red gluing. The surviving components follow finite permitted chains of lozenges, joined along boundary intervals. Each is a disk with one G occurrence on its boundary. An R cycle supplies boundary corner occurrences of those disks, rather than an interior hole in them. This independently explains APS 3.11 with colors exchanged.

## 2. Genuine rational triangulation

A raw polygon quotient need not be simplicial. In particular the square-zero-loop quotient has two pairs of fan triangles with identical vertex sets. Treating a simplex as only its quotient vertex set would lose faces and break chi. This is an actual adversarial control, not a hypothetical warning.

Insert one center per lozenge and one midpoint per quotient paired edge. Fan triangles have exactly one corner, midpoint and center. These are three disjoint vertex types, so the closure of each fan triangle and edge embeds even when other triangles have duplicate vertex triples or share more than one edge. Preserve edge and face occurrences. In the barycentric subdivision a triangle is a flag (original vertex, original edge, original face). Each edge has distinct endpoints and each face has distinct edge occurrences; consequently every flag has three distinct new vertices, and the six flags per face are distinct. Distinct original faces have distinct face-barycenters; distinct parallel original edges have distinct edge-barycenters. Shared subdivision edges correspond precisely to shared original incidences. This gives an honest simplicial complex after this subdivision; a second subdivision is unnecessary for this particular fan, although safe if desired.

All center/midpoint/flag coordinates and seam maps are rational. Each puncture is a distinct triangulation vertex. Refining its star and choosing sufficiently small rational offsets gives disjoint polygonal puncture neighborhoods. Boundary collars are also finite annuli in this known triangulated manifold and can be cut inward at rational widths. Their existence follows from interval links and the finite boundary cycles, without classifying an arbitrary complex.

Controls check 12 surfaces (six edge fixtures and six A(m,n) instances). Square-zero-loop duplicate fan triples: 2; occurrence-based barycentric triangles: 144, no duplicate flag simplex and no edge with more than two incident triangles. The finite controls also include genuine genus-one and genus-two finite-dimensional gentle inputs, with permitted-path counts [2,3,2,1] and [4,7,6,5,4,3,2,1]. They validate the presence of handle branches in this construction; they do not compute handle windings or implement the search.

## 3. Every acceptance test is finite, and the search has a witness

For a joined rational PL encoding, first identify seam endpoints through one canonical edge parameter. A locally reversed chart uses 1-t. The exact t=2/5 control would fail if two reversed local coordinates were naively equated. Triangles meeting at a seam or corner are considered through their recorded incidences. Adjacent curve segments are allowed their common endpoint, while other segment contacts, retraced pieces, overlaps and selfcontacts are rejected. All such cases use rational equations, determinant signs and finite local sector ordering; there is no floating-point tolerance.

For an embedded graph, rational transverse intersections determine the arrangement subdivision. Cutting duplicates graph-edge sides and splits vertices according to the cyclic sector intervals. The resulting finite complex has explicit components, boundary cycles and Euler characteristic. The original complex is a known manifold, and local cuts preserve that fact. Thus the genus formula is being applied to known manifolds with known orientation. It is not a genus-recognition decision oracle.

Disjoint candidate graphs have positive separation between nonincident compact segment pieces. Choose pairwise small vertex disks and then edge bands avoiding every nonincident piece. Finitely many strict rational incidence inequalities describe this condition. A rational witness exists by openness and density. One may enumerate widths after checking graph embeddedness and incidences; this search is then guaranteed to halt even if the later complement is unsuitable. No bad candidate needs an unbounded search for a nonexistent neighborhood. At the sole transverse ai/bi crossing, the four rays alternate. A regular neighborhood has one graph vertex and two loop edges, chi=-1. Its boundary rotation permutation has one cycle, giving a once-holed torus. For g disjoint such neighborhoods and planar connected complement, the cores become a geometric handle system after capping ends.

Existence uses the standard classification of a compact connected orientable surface with boundary, solely to establish a witness. Choose disjoint handles and core pairs with one crossing each, away from all collars. In small crossing disks preserve the alternating four-ray pattern. Outside those disks the finitely many embedded curve pieces have disjoint narrow tubular neighborhoods. Simultaneous small perturbation makes them transverse to the triangulation and dual dissection, avoids vertices, and avoids coincident crossings. Split them at triangle crossings. Choose each seam point once at a nearby rational parameter, shared between both charts. Choose interior PL nodes nearby rationally. Because triangles are convex and every separation/crossing condition has positive margin, sufficiently close choices preserve embeddedness and crossing rotations. The resulting embedded arrangement is ambient-isotopic to the original one inside the small neighborhoods, so the regular neighborhoods and complementary topology are preserved. It is a finite rational encoding and occurs at some finite height.

A handle curve cannot miss the dual: a closed curve inside a cut disk would be contractible in the surface and have algebraic intersection zero with every closed curve, contradicting its once-crossing partner. Between successive dual crossings, an embedded curve is a proper simple interval in one cut disk. Even for one physical crossing, cutting gives two different boundary-side occurrences. The interval endpoints are distinct occurrences; otherwise transversality or embeddedness fails. A proper simple interval separates a disk. Therefore the extra maximal-crosscut test follows automatically for the generic witness. Applying this test instead to every elementary triangle segment is wrong: the recorded three-segment rational crosscut has interior intermediate nodes, so two elementary pieces are not proper disk crosscuts. This falsifies that possible implementation, while the corrected maximal-portion wording survives.

## 4. Winding and collars

Each maximal portion has two endpoint occurrences and a unique white boundary occurrence on exactly one of its two sides. Finite cutting followed by oriented sector traversal identifies the side. No angle measurement is needed. Original-boundary truncation does not destroy this label: it remains on the recorded end collar of the pretruncation cut disk; the curves remain away from that collar, so the side is unchanged. This is an optional useful clarification of what disk/occurrence the predicate uses.

A puncture has incident black arcs: otherwise it would occur inside a dual-complement component, contrary to the disks obtained above. A peripheral curve about a noncontractible end cannot lie in one dual disk. The only contractible peripheral case is an unpunctured disk component. Its nonempty algebra provides at least one dual arc, and a sufficiently narrow full-boundary parallel meets the ends of that arc. Thus all peripheral signed sums have crossings. A puncture parallel oriented with retained core on the right goes counterclockwise around the removed point; each crossed red sector has its G occurrence outside, to its right. Its winding is minus its red valence.

At each dual crossing choose a small disk disjoint from all other crossings and PL corners. A local embedded isotopy makes the curve orthogonal to a smooth representative of the dual arc. Smooth PL corners in other such disks without curls. The resulting curves are regular-homotopic to the rounded initial embedded curves, preserving sides and winding, so APS 3.18 applies to whole maximal portions. Crossing disks can also be disjoint from the ai/bi crossings by general position. This justifies adding signs without silently changing winding by an even curl.

The isolated field has a disk, two white/two black marks and one dual pair. Clockwise whole-boundary parallel crosses twice, with white occurrences to its left, yielding +2. ## 5. Compact core, sign conventions, and every LP branch

APS §2 explicitly uses open surfaces whose original boundaries are absent. Consequently v01's literal puncture-only closed core did not lie inside the line-field domain. It was compatible with an intended inward core embedding, but explicit repair was required for a formal restriction argument. v02 removes original-boundary collars and puncture neighborhoods; its closed core lies entirely in the domain and closes this issue.

The surface is core plus finitely many cylinders. Core inclusion is a deformation equivalence. Trivialize P(TS) over the cylinders, respecting its restriction to the core. Homotopy classes of sections form an H^1 torsor; H^1 restriction is an isomorphism, proving the stated bijection of line-field classes. A representative in the pure mapping class group can be chosen equal to the identity near each boundary, then extends by the identity on cylinders. An initial marked diffeomorphism implements the required end permutation and colored mark matching. On every original boundary, equal positive white counts give an orientation-preserving bijection of the alternating colored marks; zero-count ends are punctures. Thus the extension meets APS 6.1/7.2 hypotheses.

In a positively oriented tangent trivialization, LP winding is deg(tangent)-deg(line field), whereas APS 3.2 is deg(line field)-deg(tangent); the orthogonal rotation D adds a constant and does not change degree. Hence LP=-APS for the same oriented curve. LP's natural boundary direction has the core on the left; our peripheral direction has core on the right. Reversing the peripheral curve therefore cancels the winding-sign difference. Peripheral integers agree, handles negate. gcd, parity and (w/2+1) mod2 for even w are unchanged by handle negation. In particular w(c)+2 remains w(c)+2, not -w(c)+2.

LP Theorem 1.2.4 is applied to d=b+p actual boundaries of the common compact core. It requires equality at all d. Genus zero has no further datum. Genus one has the nonnegative ideal generator gcd(w(a),w(b),all w(c)+2), including gcd(0,...,0)=0. For a peripheral detour a' formed by a pair of pants, orient the three pants boundaries as a,c,-a'. APS Euler identity gives w(a)+w(c)-w(a')=-2, hence w(a')=w(a)+w(c)+2. This checks the sign independently.

In genus at least two, handles and peripheral classes span H_1, so winding mod2 vanishes exactly when all listed windings are even. If it is nonzero the ODD branch is complete after all boundary values have been fixed. In the even case q([gamma])=w(gamma)/2+1 is quadratic. The radical is the peripheral span (dimension d-1), and its q values are w(c)/2+1. It vanishes precisely when every peripheral w=2 mod4. Only then q descends to closed-surface homology and its Arf bit is basis-independent. If a radical class r has q(r)=1, adding r to both members of a handle pair with q(a)=q(b)=0 changes their contribution from 0 to 1. The finite control exhibits this at g=2: q=x1*y1+x2*y2+z, with radical z and simultaneous lifts of a2,b2 by z. It checks all 32 forms with one radical dimension and all 16 lifts; 120 Arf-changing lift examples occur when q(radical) is nonzero, and none occur when it is zero. This directly verifies why the corrected key must omit handle Arf there.

The radical-lift mechanism also has a geometric realization: let u=a2+b2, represented by smoothing the core pair, and let u+r be a simple peripheral detour. Modulo two the composition of twists T_(u+r) T_u sends x to x+(x.u)r. It adds r to both a2 and b2, preserving the peripheral classes. The resulting geometric handle system therefore changes the raw handle Arf in the q(r)=1 example. The phenomenon is not an artifact of allowing unrealizable integral lifts.

The LP theorem supplies sufficiency and necessity on the core. Its complete section and formulas, not only the abstract, were inspected. APS 6.1/7.2 then supplies the algebra equivalence and converse preservation of every peripheral. There is no reliance on the puncture-deficient literal printed APS 7.3 or 7.4 range.

## 6. Connected factors, zero, and source witness

For a categorical product decomposition D^b(A)=C x D, a vertex projective P_v has local endomorphism ring: its quotient by the positive-length paths is k, and the positive-length paths form a nilpotent ideal by finite dimensionality. (This ring need not itself be k; the square-zero loop is an explicit example.) Thus P_v is indecomposable and lies in one factor. Every quiver arrow gives a nonzero adjacent-projective map (with the direction depending on the left-module convention). Connectedness puts all projectives in one factor. For X in the other factor, Hom(P_v,X[n])=0 for all v,n. Because P_v is projective, this group is Hom_A(P_v,H^n(X)), which detects the v-component of H^n(X). All cohomology vanishes, so X=0. This proof requires no finite global dimension or finite-cone generation. It proves intrinsic indecomposability and justifies permuting connected factors under an equivalence. The empty input gives zero category; k gives the nonzero isolated-disk key.

For A(m,n), label first-cycle arrows a_i and second-cycle arrows b_j, and add bridge d from vertex 0 of the first cycle to vertex 0 of the second. Declare all a_i*a_{i+1} and b_j*b_{j+1} related; both a_{m-1}*d and d*b_0 are unrelated. At bridge endpoints there is one relation and one nonrelation continuation/predecessor, so gentleness holds. Positive-length permitted paths are: all m+n+1 arrows; the two paths a_{m-1}d and db_0; the single path a_{m-1}db_0; no longer paths. Including m+n idempotents gives dimension 2(m+n)+4. This is independently enumerated exactly.

The permitted chains contribute m+n-1 white boundary marks. The two full-relation cycles contribute red interior valences m and n. The fresh quotient boundary traversal gives one boundary, chi of the filled-puncture surface 1, hence g=0. The truncated core has d=3 and chi=-1. Puncture winding is -m,-n by the sector argument; sum of all windings is -2, so outer winding m+n-2. APS 6.2 yields AG records (0,m),(0,n),(m+n-1,1). Thus A(3,5) and A(4,4) both have n=7, outer w=6, dimension 20, g=0,b=1,p=2, but puncture winding multisets differ. The keys and AG records separate them. This contradicts the literal printed sufficiency range j<=b for these genus-zero inputs. The original submitted algorithm already retained puncture records; its incorrect logical sufficiency citation is what must change. The corrected numerical procedure is unchanged, apart from explicit empty-input convention and proof-precision repairs.

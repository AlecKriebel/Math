## Source paraphrase (sole source summary; 158 words)

The contribution treats unit-square quadrangulations, distinguishes Abelian, quadratic, and quartic holonomy, and studies cylinder areas divided by total area. At fixed stratum, uniform square-tiled surfaces with at most N squares have a limiting area-multiset law as N grows; automorphism weighting gives the same limit. The two coordinate-direction laws become independent. Genus then grows. The known large-genus theorem concerns the principal quadratic stratum with 4g−4 simple zeros: cylinder number has logarithmic mean, large areas have a Poisson–Dirichlet limit, and areas multiplied by 4g−4 have a small-area Poisson limit. The source mentions all-stratum conjectures separately. Problem 2 seeks understanding between those scales and illustrates this by counting areas in [g^−1/2,2g^−1/2]. Problem 3 separately asks about N proportional to g. Problem 2 supplies neither a required convergence topology nor an explicit all-strata quantifier.

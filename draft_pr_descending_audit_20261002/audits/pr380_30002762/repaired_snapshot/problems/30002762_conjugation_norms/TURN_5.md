# Turn 5: finite braid-shift truncations and their infinite-relator limit

Fifth and final substantive turn. The original existence question remains unresolved. This turn tests a concrete attempt to make the credited infinite-braid example finitely presented: retain finitely many local braid/commutation relations and adjoin a shift. Every finite truncation admits an ordinary homogeneous quasimorphism detecting the proposed element. With all distance-commutation relations imposed, that same element instead has bounded powers. Neither construction provides the requested counterexample. The braid signature and displacement theorems used below are credited primary inputs, not newly claimed results.

## 1. A concrete family of finite presentations

For each integer K≥2 set

    G_K=<a,t | a a_1 a=a_1 a a_1,
                  [a,a_j]=1 for 2≤j≤K>,
    a_j=t^j a t^{-j}.

This is a finite presentation on two ordinary generators, with K relations. Let

    w=a^{-4} a_1 a² a_1.                                  (1)

The word has total a-exponent zero and total t-exponent zero. It is modeled on the explicit Brandenbursky–Kędra braid α=σ_1^{-4}σ_2σ_1²σ_2. Exponent cancellation alone gives no information about its normal word norm or quasimorphism detection.

## 2. A surjection to a finite braid group

Take N=K+2 and let B_N have standard Artin generators σ_1,...,σ_{N-1}. Put δ=σ_1...σ_{N-1}. The Artin relations imply

    δ σ_i δ^{-1}=σ_{i+1},      1≤i≤N-2.                  (2)

For a direct verification, in δσ_i commute the rightmost σ_i past σ_j for j≥i+2, apply σ_iσ_{i+1}σ_i=σ_{i+1}σ_iσ_{i+1}, and commute the resulting σ_{i+1} past σ_1,...,σ_{i-1}. The result is σ_{i+1}δ. This uses no wraparound conjugation assertion.

Define φ_K(a)=σ_1 and φ_K(t)=δ. Repeatedly applying(2) gives φ_K(a_j)=σ_{j+1} for 0≤j≤K=N-2. The one braid relation maps to the standard relation for σ_1,σ_2. Each listed commutation relation maps to [σ_1,σ_{j+1}]=1, valid because j≥2. Hence φ_K is a homomorphism. It is surjective, since its image contains all the standard generators by(2).

The image of w is exactly

    α=σ_1^{-4}σ_2σ_1²σ_2 in B_N.                         (3)

No injectivity of a local braid subgroup in G_K is needed for this argument.

## 3. A credited ordinary homogeneous detector

Brandenbursky–Kędra, *Concordance group and stable commutator length in braid groups*, AGT15(2015)2859–2884, §4.8 printed2882–2883, computes the homogenized link-signature quasimorphism on B_n at α for every n≥3. The exact printed formula, visually checked on2883, is

    overline(sign)_n(α)=-2.                               (4)

Primary PDF: https://msp.org/agt/2015/15-5/agt-v15-n5-p11-p.pdf . The calculation uses the commuting braids η_{2,n}=σ_1² and η_{3,n}=σ_2σ_1²σ_2, whose homogeneous signature values are both2, so α=η_{2,n}^{-2}η_{3,n} has value-2. The signature-quasimorphism theorem and these values are credited there to Gambaudo–Ghys; this packet does not replace their proof by a simulation.

Pull back this ordinary homogeneous quasimorphism along φ_K. It has value-2 on w. If its defect is D_K and C_K=max(|q(a)|,|q(t)|), homogeneity and conjugacy invariance give, for every product of ℓ conjugates of a^{±1},t^{±1},

    |q(g)|≤ℓ(C_K+D_K).

Thus ||w^n||≥2|n|/(C_K+D_K), with a finite positive denominator. The upper bound ||w^n||≤|n| ||w|| is immediate. Therefore, for every finite K, w generates a detected undistorted cyclic subgroup of the finitely presented G_K. It violates neither dichotomy.

No bound uniform in K on the detecting constants is asserted. This conclusion concerns the specified element w; it does not prove that all other elements of G_K satisfy either dichotomy.

## 4. Adding every distant-commutation relation changes the answer

Let G_infinity be the group with the same two generators and braid relation, but with [a,a_j]=1 for every integer j≥2. We do not assert that this displayed infinite presentation proves the abstract group is not finitely presented; no such assertion is needed.

Conjugating the relations shows that a_i and a_j commute whenever |i-j|≥2. Let H=<a_0,a_1>. It is an image of B_3, because its generators satisfy the braid relation. In the abelianization of B_3 the two standard generators coincide, so the word (1), with exponent sum zero, belongs to B_3'. Consequently its image w belongs to H', and all w^n belong to H'.

The fixed element F=t³ strongly m-displaces H for every m: the subgroup F^i H F^{-i} is generated by a_{3i},a_{3i+1}; for distinct i,j≥0, every index in these two generating sets differs by at least2. Their generators therefore commute pairwise, and hence the entire subgroups commute.

Apply the credited Burago–Ivanov–Polterovich displacement inequality, Theorem2.2(i) in https://arxiv.org/pdf/0710.1412 (published in *Groups of Diffeomorphisms*, ASPM52(2008)221–250). In its exact form, if F m-displaces H and h in H' has commutator length m in H, then ν(h)≤14ν(F). For each fixed n, the commutator length of w^n in H is finite. The same F displaces H for that value of m, so in the two-generator normal word norm,

    ||w^n||≤14||t³||≤42    for every integer n.            (5)

The identity case is immediate. This is the full norm inequality, not merely the weaker conclusion that commutator length in G_infinity is at most two. All hypotheses of the primary algebraic displacement theorem have been checked.

Thus the finite truncations have a detected linear-growth element, whereas the infinite-relator shift construction makes that element bounded. The latter has introduced a fixed-cost displacer that was not available inside the original infinite braid group. This explains why the credited infinite-braid counterexample cannot simply be transported through this shift repair without a metric-preservation proof.

## 5. Final outcome and computational role

The finite Artin-action checker verifies the exact relator images and conjugation identities for a range of K using freely reduced words, not a floating representation. The all-K proof is the uniform Artin calculation(2). It also verifies the index-separation criterion for arbitrarily parametrized finite displacement patterns in a finite range. The signature value and displacement inequality are primary analytic/algebraic inputs explicitly cited above; the checker does not claim to recompute knot signatures or certify all infinite groups.

Together the five turns exclude broad virtually metabelian and split-abelian-extension mechanisms, supply an exact stable-norm algorithm on a promised bounded-derived class, and show why this concrete finite braid-shift repair fails. No finitely presented counterexample has been constructed, and no theorem that all finitely presented groups obey the dichotomies has been proved. Original unsolved5/5. Author search stops here; no sixth turn or novelty certification.

# Independent pre-candidate baseline

Sealed before reading any candidate, history, root, or sibling audit report. Scope: ordinary unweighted conjugation-invariant word norms and the finite-presentation requirement. Source inspected: EMS46555, Report 3/2015, Geometric Topology, workshop 18–24 January 2015, printed 199–200 (PDF indices 12–13), text plus both rendered pages. Target: a finitely presented group with either an unbounded distorted cyclic subgroup or an unbounded cyclic subgroup undetected by every homogeneous quasimorphism. Finite normal generation suffices to define the report's metric; finite presentation implies finite ordinary generation. No resolution is presumed.

Let A be a finite symmetric ordinary generating set and L the word norm generated by all its conjugates. L(1)=0, L(g^-1)=L(g), L(xgx^-1)=L(g), L(gh)<=L(g)+L(h). These properties alone give the following independent controls.

1. Power lengths are subadditive, so stable length lim L(g^n)/n exists and equals inf L(g^n)/n. Bounded implies stable length zero, but the converse requires proof and is not formal. Undistortion is equivalent to strictly positive stable length here because the upper bound is nL(g), while the infimum gives L(g^n)>=n tau(g).
2. For a homogeneous quasimorphism phi of defect D, conjugacy invariance and phi(g^-1)=-phi(g) hold. A product of k conjugate generators has |phi(product)|<=k M+(k-1)D, M=max_{a in A}|phi(a)|. Hence phi(g) nonzero gives L(g^n)>=n|phi(g)|/(M+D), when M+D>0. A vanishing homogeneous quasimorphism lower bound never proves boundedness.
3. Quotient maps are norm non-increasing if images of A form the target generators. Embeddings alone give upper norm comparison from the subgroup to the ambient group, never the lower comparison required to preserve unbounded/distorted cyclic behavior. A retraction supplies such a lower bound relative to suitable finite generating sets. Higman-style embeddings therefore transfer the central difficulty unless metric control is proved.
4. Every commutator satisfies L([x,y])<=2 min(L(x),L(y)), by writing a product of two conjugates of either argument. In the integral Heisenberg group, central z=[x,y] has z^n=[x^n,y], so L(z^n)<=2 L(y), including negative n. Classical sqrt distortion of this central cyclic subgroup is irrelevant to this norm.
5. If t g t^-1=g^m with m>=2, [t,g^n]=g^{(m-1)n}; thus L(g^{(m-1)n})<=2L(t). Division with remainder gives L(g^N)<=2L(t)+(m-2)L(g) for all integers N. This is bounded, despite classical exponential distortion in BS(1,m). If t g t^-1=g^-1, [t,g^n]=g^-2n and all powers have bound 2L(t)+L(g), with the extra odd residue essential. A finite dihedral check initially rejected a too-strong bound 2, correctly exposing this residue issue before sealing.
6. In an abelian finitely generated group, conjugacy adds no generators. Every infinite-order g is detected by a homomorphism to R, therefore linearly undistorted. Infinite Z with infinitely many singleton conjugacy-class generators is outside the finite-conjugacy-class hypothesis.
7. A finite cyclic quotient C_q has L(a^q)=0 versus L(a^q)=q in infinite Z. Therefore finite wraparound can falsely imitate compression and cannot support a universal infinite-group proof. Finite exact BFS can falsify a claimed identity or bound for a quotient but cannot establish the required infinite growth.
8. An infinite displayed presentation does not establish non-finite-presentability: the presentation of Z with generator a and countably many redundant relators [a,a], for example, is infinite but the group is finitely presented. Any such assertion needs an invariant or direct proof. Conversely finite-presented ambient containment does not establish metric preservation.

Executable controls implement exact finite conjugacy-generator BFS for dihedral and Heisenberg quotients, exact affine arithmetic over Q proving the BS commutator identity at sampled integer parameters (with the universal identity proved above), and finite cyclic wraparound adversaries. They are controls, not universal proof certificates.

Success criteria for later candidate review: all five declared partial findings must be correct with explicit norm domain, generation and presentation hypotheses; every cited theorem must apply literally; simulations must state bounds and their limitations; every wrapper must preserve unresolved original status. A PASS can only be scoped to partial findings and package consistency.

## Exact comparison target

The frozen candidate concerns the omitted-diagonal matrix with entries h^3 K_kappa(x_m-x_n), translated cubic grids, fixed complex kappa, and the continuum normalization S_kappa=A_kappa-I/3. The particle is bounded measurable and satisfies the explicit center-voxel consistency condition |Omega_h symmetric_difference Omega| -> 0. The excluded set is the exact static lattice numerical-range interval, not numerical guesses of its endpoints. Claimed outputs are eventual resolvent bounds and absence of pollution on compact sets off that interval, isolated eigenvalue approximation with algebraic multiplicity, and norm convergence of embedded Riesz projections. Boundary-null bounded particles, hence bounded Lipschitz particles, qualify.

The separate frozen counterexample uses a positive-volume compact empty-interior support with an isolated null anchor, kappa=1/10000, and h=1/n; all discrete matrices are 3 by 3 zero while a nonreal continuum eigenvalue persists. This addresses an unrestricted measurable-support reading. It is not a geometry classification, and does not by itself answer the usual regular-particle numerical question.

The candidate-specific comparison that remains to assess for originality is the realization of this actual omitted-diagonal DDA on a common Hilbert space: static strong consistency with translated cubic cells; norm convergence of the weakly singular dynamic correction on changing particle masks; and the consequent spectral statement under explicit center-voxel consistency. This is the audit's decomposition, not a theorem that these steps are themselves historically new. The frozen argument adds an exact real-frequency nonreal-pole obstruction to this DDA normalization. I found no same-team source containing that exact construction, but did not perform a separate comprehensive priority search of all sampling pathologies, ball resonance perturbations or measurable-domain spectral continuity. No first-counterexample claim follows from this audit.

## Exact question
Let G be finite, k any field of characteristic p, and a_k(G) the split Green ring on finite-dimensional indecomposable kG-modules. Its complexification has norm sum |c_M| dim_k M and conjugate-linear dual involution. The completion is a commutative complex Banach *-algebra. The OWR14/2019 contribution pp847–849 asks whether every element x has Spec(x*x) contained in [0,infinity), equivalently every bounded complex-valued character is a *-character. The coefficient field C belongs to functional analysis and does not replace the modular representation field k. Showing only module-by-module gamma identities, finite examples, a maximal quotient, or stable endotrivial sectors does not settle this claim.

## Scalar extension mechanism
For any field extension K/k, finite-dimensional Hom spaces base-change: K tensor Hom_A(U,V) = Hom_{K A}(KU,KV). If U,V are non-isomorphic indecomposables, every composite U->V->U lies in rad End_A(U), because an invertible composite would make U a summand of V and hence isomorphic to V. This radical is nilpotent. If KU and KV shared a nonzero summand, projection/inclusion maps would give a nonzero idempotent composite KU->KV->KU inside K tensor rad End_A(U), a nilpotent ideal: contradiction. Thus different indecomposables have disjoint sets of scalar-extended indecomposable summands, although an individual indecomposable may split. Positive multiplicities and preservation of total dimension then prove exact weighted-l1 isometry on all complex virtual combinations, not merely positive modules. Tensor and dual commute with extension; completion gives a unital isometric *-embedding. No algebraic-closure perfection/separability hypothesis is required for this argument.

## Symmetry inheritance mechanism
For a unital isometric *-subalgebra A of symmetric commutative B, spectral radii agree because the norm of each power agrees. For selfadjoint h in A and real t, r_B(exp(ith))=1; thus r_A(exp(ith))=1. If a character of A took h to alpha+ibeta with beta nonzero, |exp(it(alpha+ibeta))|=exp(-t beta)>1 for one sign of t, contradiction. Hence A is symmetric. This proves descent of symmetry under field extension without an unsupported assertion that all subalgebra characters extend to B.

## Countable reduction
Every weighted-l1 element has countable support. Starting from it, repeatedly adding duals and all indecomposable summands of products of already chosen modules yields a countable tensor- and dual-closed basis set containing the unit. Its closed span embeds isometrically. A nonreal character value on a selfadjoint element restricts to this subalgebra; conversely symmetry of every such subalgebra yields symmetry of the full algebra. This is an equivalent reformulation and is blocked as a resolution unless a new positivity or character mechanism is supplied. Finite polynomial constraints must include multiplication for products outside the initial finite list and the full dimension bound; satisfiable finite pieces do not themselves establish or falsify an infinite character.

## Weighted group sector
For an abelian group T, symmetric submultiplicative weight w with w(e)=1 and w(t)>=1, every character of l1(T,w) is determined by a homomorphism lambda:T->C^times with |lambda(t)|<=w(t). If w(t^n)^(1/n)->1 for every t, applying the inequality to t^n and t^-n forces |lambda(t)|=1; characters are *-characters, so the algebra is symmetric. Conversely an exponential weight on Z admits off-unit-circle evaluations and is a negative boundary. Stable endotrivial modules supply this weight using core dimensions and gamma(T)=1. This establishes only their closed stable sector.

## Actual p-group lifting
For nontrivial p-group P, q=|P|, p= [kP] satisfies p^2=q p and p[M]=dim(M)p. Let e=p/q and f=1-e; e,f are orthogonal central selfadjoint idempotents, with e norm=1 and f norm=2. In the closed subalgebra generated by all chosen indecomposable stable endotrivials and p, the stable quotient retains their weighted basis. Its canonical lift sends stable [T] to f[T]=[T]-(dim T/q)p, of norm exactly 2 dim T. For any stable combination, norm of its lift is <=2 weighted norm; quotient norm <= lift norm. Thus the full sector is a topological direct product of the stable weighted group algebra and C e; it is symmetric. The inverse at a point exists exactly when both stable and scalar components are invertible. This does not cover non-endotrivial modules or other finite groups' multiple projective indecomposables. The trivial p-group/semisimple case must be separated because the stable unit vanishes.

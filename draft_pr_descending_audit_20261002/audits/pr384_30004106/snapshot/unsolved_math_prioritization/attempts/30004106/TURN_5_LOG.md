# Fifth substantive author turn

The fifth turn sought to enlarge the positive endotrivial family using actual induction from subgroups, rather than extending the abstract axioms or positive-module restriction estimates already shown inadequate.

For elementary abelian groups, a complement and a local endomorphism algebra prove that induced endotrivial cores are indecomposable. Cyclic restrictions recover their inducing subgroup, and Krull-Schmidt recovers the stable class. This proves the required injection into actual Green coordinates. A normalized tensor formula gives an intersection algebra. Finite subgroup-lattice Möbius idempotents split it; leading actual-module coordinates give a lower norm bound, while restriction of cores gives an upper bound. These bounds justify the completed product decomposition and symmetry. An explicit F8 module shows that the positive family can be proper.

The original universal symmetry question remains unresolved after the full five-turn budget. No sixth author search is included. The finite checks are algebraic controls; they do not replace the completion proof or establish a species on the remaining module directions.

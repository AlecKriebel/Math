# Substantive author turn 1

This turn tested a reduction of the universal symmetry problem rather than merely restating its primary definition. It proved that algebraic extension of the modular coefficient field gives an isometric *-embedding of the full weighted completion, so symmetry over an algebraic closure descends. It then reduced any obstruction to one indecomposable module and its countable tensor/dual closure, with a two-term self-adjoint witness and an exact compactness criterion for bounded-species feasibility.

A separate nonstandard involution on l¹(Z) demonstrates why positive-element radius identities alone do not settle general Banach symmetry. It is explicitly outside the Green-ring class. No actual module counterexample or universal proof was obtained. The finite checker includes a genuine F3[C4] to F9[C4] splitting pattern and 23,589 exact controls; it does not prove infinite compactness by testing. Author count: 1/5.

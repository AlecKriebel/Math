# Independent source-first reconstruction

Read order: frozen source URL metadata; independently downloaded OWR 2019/14; full printed pages 847–849 extracted locally; page 849 visually inspected; accepted 2022 Benson manuscript Definition 1.1.1, Proposition 3.4.2, Section 4.4. No candidate TURN proof or prior verdict was read before this checkpoint.

For a finite group G and any field k of characteristic p, a(G) is the **split** Green ring: free abelian on all isomorphism classes of finite dimensional indecomposable kG-modules. Relations identify direct sums, not short exact sequences. Tensor product is over k. The completed algebra is weighted l1 on this entire basis: A={sum c_M[M]: sum |c_M|dim_k M<infinity}. Its scalar field is C irrespective of k. Star sends c_M[M] to conjugate(c_M)[M*]. All signed and complex elements, and infinitely supported completion elements, are in scope.

The original question on printed page 849 is whether A is symmetric: Spec_A(x*x) is contained in [0,infinity) for every x in A. The visually confirmed bars mean s(x*)=conjugate(s(x)), and s([M*])=conjugate(s([M])). A real valued module species condition, or a positive cone condition, is insufficient. Equivalently every dimension bounded species on a(G) is a star character. The weighted bound is |s([M])|<=dim_k M. A single nonstar bounded species is a counterexample; universal symmetry is the success criterion. Restriction to stable/projective quotient, maximal quotient, a finite generated subalgebra, syzygies, or endotrivials is a partial result unless every ambient species is covered.

For a representation ideal X, A_X removes its basis support and has quotient norm sum outside X |c_M|dim M. Symmetry of A descends to all these quotients and would imply gamma_X(M tensor M*)=gamma_X(M)^2. This consequence is not a substitute for full symmetry, and a pair of distinct self dual module classes does not itself falsify symmetry.

The OWR report explicitly recalls five abstract axioms but discusses finite groups. Independently read accepted 2022 Definition 1.1.1: positive integral finite structure constants; a basis unit; basis permutation star preserving multiplication; (ii) a positive unit coefficient forces dual indices; (iii) absent unit in xx* forces multiplicity [xx*x:x]>=2; positive integral star invariant ring dimension; and a positive nonzero finite regular element rho with x rho=dim(x)rho. Strong (ii prime) additionally forces unit multiplicity one. An abstract nonsymmetric ring satisfying these does not automatically realize a finite group Green ring.

Deductions before candidate: an isometric scalar extension must preserve disjoint indecomposable supports, not merely dimensions; an injective extension map can only give spectral containment unless a character extension/spectral permanence theorem is supplied; a compactness reduction must keep a fixed positive defect and impose every product relation. An ambient finite torsion unit has roots of unity as species values, and a selfadjoint combination can therefore have zero restriction without nonreal ambient spectrum. A regular element alone does not force symmetry of all the abstract axioms.

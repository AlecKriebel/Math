# Sealed source-first reconstruction and controls

Written before reading any candidate proof, author conclusion, family review, root mathematical reconstruction, or original final adversary.

The original primary problem is Benson's final question on printed p.849 of OWR14/2019 (PDF p.9). For every finite group G and every field k of characteristic p, take the split Green ring with indecomposable kG-modules as its additive basis, complexify, and complete in sum |a_M| dim_k M. Does every continuous unital species satisfy s(M*)=conj(s(M))? Equivalently, every self-adjoint COMPLETED signed/complex element has real ambient spectrum, or every x*x has nonnegative spectrum. Neither ordinary characters, nonnegative module growth, nor a stable/maximal quotient by itself proves this. A counterexample requires an actual group/module realization and a bounded species on the FULL completion.

I freshly fetched the primary OWR and all five listed source files. OWR p.847 specifies k arbitrary, split direct-sum relations, dimension weighted complex l1 completion and conjugate-linear duality; p.849 has the question. Visually inspected all three printed pages. ArXiv v2 2008.13155 dated 2022 explicitly says modular finite-group symmetry is unknown at §3.4, and Proposition2.9.10/3.4.2 identifies the equivalences. Its closed-ideal inheritance is one-way. This limited source set cannot certify present-day absence of all other solutions or novelty.

Fresh falsification mechanisms designed at this stage:

1. ACTUAL induction/locality control: use permutation modules of E=C_p^2 as actual G-sets. Build diagonal tensor permutation action and decompose every orbit; check P_U P_V=[E:U+V]P_(U intersect V), including distinct lines, equal lines, trivial/full subgroups. Establish locality directly from cyclic p permutation modules' endomorphism algebra F_p[x]/(x^p), not by assuming independent formal basis symbols. Check Jordan kernel growth in characteristic p and Frobenius order.
2. COMPLETED block control: normalize q_U=P_U/[E:U]. Obtain meet-semilattice multiplication. Möbius e_U=sum_(V<=U) mu(V,U)q_V gives actual idempotents and onto finite block coordinates. Compute exact forward and inverse norm constants in the actual dimension weighted basis. Equal coefficient cancellation at large degree cannot invalidate finite bounds; direct coordinate projections remain bounded. This is only a finite actual permutation subring control, not full Green-ring symmetry.
3. INSEPARABLE scalar extension control: k=F_2(t), D=k[s]/(s^2-t). Extending to k(s) produces dual numbers rather than a semisimple algebra. This falsifies blanket scalar preservation of Jacobson radicals/semisimplicity. The valid isometry proof must instead use the disjoint orthogonal idempotents of End(M direct-sum N)/rad and nilpotence of scalar-extended original radical: disjoint indecomposable supports survive for every field extension even if each support splits or becomes nonsemisimple.
4. NONREALIZABILITY control: any finite-group realization of an abstract basis generator of dimension d must have integer traces of powers with group-order torsion constraints and tensor-trivial coefficient governed by duality. In particular a unital actual dimension-one module is invertible and has finite order since G is finite, so a formal unbounded Laurent family of dimension-one powers cannot realize finite G. An abstract axiom counterexample alone cannot refute the primary problem.
5. AMBIENT spectral control: an injective closed finite-dimensional unital subalgebra can lose spectrum in its ambient algebra only when its internal inverse is absent; but finite-dimensional algebraic spectral idempotents are polynomials and their nonzero embedding forces each algebraic root to persist. For an actual Q8 finite ring calculate minimal-polynomial annihilators and explicit idempotents before calling a signed spectrum ambient. No use of stable quotient values as automatically full species.
6. ENDOTRIVIAL dependency control: a core-bounded species with gamma(T)=gamma(T*)=1 and TT*=1+P has |s(T)|=|s(T*)|=1 when s(P)=0, hence conjugation; this is a credited known gamma and endotrivial consequence. It does not constrain species with nonzero projective values absent the regular-element relation.

All these are adversarial controls and necessary proof obligations; none asserts the original universal claim. Initial completion estimate 15% of this audit; research problem is still a hypothesis.

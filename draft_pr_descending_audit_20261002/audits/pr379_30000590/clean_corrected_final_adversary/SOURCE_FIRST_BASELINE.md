# Independent PR 379 source-first baseline

This reviewer is reused from unrelated PR 377, not newly spawned. As of this seal I have never read PR 379 candidate, historical candidate, root, sibling, or previous review content. The exact original question was independently read from all three rendered Davis contribution pages, printed 2588–2590, of OWR 43/2006. This baseline predates any candidate access.

## Exact target and conventions

Printed 2590 asks: “Suppose a group Γ is virtually type FP. Is H^*(Γ; ZΓ) always a finitely generated Γ-module?” This concerns integral group-ring coefficients and the direct sum over all degrees. The coefficient module is left ZΓ; the commuting right multiplication supplies the residual right Γ-module on cohomology. Inversion translates handedness, but does not identify residual multiplication with the original coefficient action. A finite-index subgroup of type FP is meant. Davis's primary Poincaré duality survey defines FP_R by a finite-length resolution of R by finitely generated projective RΓ-modules (printed 4, lines 150–155 of private extraction). This is stronger than merely FP∞. Type FP over Z has finite cohomological dimension and excludes nontrivial torsion. A virtually FP group can have torsion.

## Independent deductions

1. Finite-index reduction is exact. For H ≤ Γ of finite index, let π_H: ZΓ → ZH discard terms outside H. The map a ↦ (x ↦ π_H(xa)) identifies ZΓ with Coind_H^Γ ZH as left Γ, right H bimodules; coinduced functions satisfy f(hx)=hf(x), Γ acts by (g f)(x)=f(xg). The right H action is pointwise. A finite left-coset transversal makes this bijective, because π_H(xa) recovers every coefficient of a. Shapiro therefore identifies Res_H H^*(Γ;ZΓ) with H^*(H;ZH) as right H-modules. A right Γ-module M is finitely generated over Γ iff Res_H M is finitely generated over H: expand each Γ generator by a finite right-coset transversal in one direction; the converse is immediate. This proves equivalence of the universal virtually-FP question with the universal FP question, and proves bounded degree support even for torsion in Γ. It does not answer the FP question.

2. If 0→P_n→⋯→P_0→Z→0 is an FP resolution, each Hom_{ZΓ}(P_i,ZΓ) is finitely generated projective as a right module, because dualization preserves finite direct-summand splittings. In top degree H^n is a quotient of P_n^*, hence finitely generated. Degrees > n vanish. In intermediate degrees finite generation requires control of kernels. If ZΓ is right Noetherian, all such kernels are finitely generated and the answer is positive. Coherence alone is insufficient to claim every kernel unless its map has a suitable finitely presented target; the finite-projective cochain terms do give that target, so right coherence is also sufficient. The original question supplies neither hypothesis.

3. Finite groups are positive boundary cases: ZF is coinduced from Z, hence H^q(F;ZF)=0 for q>0, and H^0 is the rank-one norm subgroup with trivial right action. For infinite Γ, H^0 vanishes since a left-invariant finitely supported function cannot have nonzero coefficient.

4. F_2 has a length-one finite free resolution, so H^1(F_2;ZF_2) is the cokernel of the map R→R², r↦((a−1)r,(b−1)r), and is generated by two residual right-module classes. Its abelian group need not be finitely generated. Abelian infinite rank is no negative answer to the original question.

5. Finite free cochain terms by themselves do not prove finite generation. For A=k⊕V with V infinite dimensional and V²=0, multiplication by 0≠v∈V on A has kernel V, not finitely generated: multiplication on V factors through k. Its two-term cochain complex has finitely generated free terms and non-finitely-generated H^0. This is an actual ring-level counterexample only. It is not a group ring and not the dual of a demonstrated FP resolution of a trivial group module; using it to refute Davis would transfer the central difficulty to an unsupported realization claim.

## Source checks and falsifiable controls fixed before candidate access

The supplied MSP p14 PDF is Habiro–Meilhan, On the Kontsevich integral of Brunnian links, not Davis–Dymara–Januszkiewicz–Okun. The correct primary DDJO article was independently located and fetched as AGT 6 (2006), 1289–1318, p09. Its introductory corollary gives finite generation for Coxeter groups using a finite filtration. Davis–Okun's primary graph-products article explicitly warns that the associated graded module cannot in general be replaced by the actual module (free product of two infinite cyclic groups is a counterexample); its general Artin result is for the Salvetti complex unless K(π,1) is known. Its FP Bestvina–Brady scope requires an acyclic finite flag complex, and finite/infinite graph-product hypotheses must be retained.

Precommitted controls: (A) exhaust the S_3/H projection/coinduction bimodule equations for H=⟨(01)⟩; (B) cyclic finite-group differential matrices over several finite fields, including characteristic dividing group order; (C) truncated square-zero rings with unbounded annihilator generator count; (D) require independent proof, not enumeration, for all general positive claims; (E) check top degree, H^0, trivial group, torsion, FP∞ versus finite FP, residual handedness, associated-graded filtration versus splitting, finite-index normality, and extension finiteness in every claimed partial; (F) exact Git/base byte bindings and complete stdout/stderr replays are separate from mathematical proof. General group-realization remains an explicit gap unless an actual FP group and non-finitely-generated cohomology are constructed.

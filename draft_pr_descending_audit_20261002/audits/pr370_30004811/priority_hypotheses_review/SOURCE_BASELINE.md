## Original question and interpretation

The exact report is OWR 40/2021, DOI 10.4171/OWR/2021/40, meeting 29 August–4 September 2021, Jauregui contribution on printed pp. 2255–2258, PDF pp. 99–102. Its conjecture on printed p.2257 is: “We conjecture that mCV (M, g) = mADM (M, g) holds for general asymptotically flat metrics.” (Mathematical notation normalized only.) Its definition is the supremum over **all compact exhaustions** of limsup `r_V(K)-cap(K)`, where `r_V=(3|K|/(4π))^(1/3)`. Thus coordinate-ball asymptotics alone do not establish it.

The report's immediately preceding results impose distinct conditions: lower bound with nonnegative scalar curvature outside a compact set; coordinate-ball upper bound with globally nonnegative scalar curvature and empty/minimal boundary; general-exhaustion upper bound with harmonic flatness and nonnegative ADM mass. Its underlying Jauregui 2020 paper, following Corollary8, explicitly conjectures the general-exhaustion inequality under Theorem6's globally nonnegative scalar curvature and empty/minimal boundary. I will distinguish this physically standard intended interpretation from the literal unqualified OWR sentence. A claim resolving all AF metrics without curvature/boundary conditions needs additional evidence and is vulnerable to negative-mass examples.

The OWR prose capacity definition omits the factor `1/(4π)` although its inequality and Schwarzschild claim force normalized capacity. Jauregui's exact equation(3) contains it. Correct normalization: `cap(K)=inf (1/(4π))∫|∇ψ|²dV`, ψ=0 on K, ψ→1 at infinity; Euclidean radius-r ball has capacity r. Replacing ψ by 1−ψ gives the compact-support/vanishing-at-infinity convention used by BFM.

## Strongest independently supported original-resolution claim

For smooth connected complete **one-ended** O₂(r^−τ) AF3 with τ>1/2, R∈L¹, **globally R≥0**, and compact boundary empty or smooth minimal, the exact OWR capacity-volume mass equals ADM mass for **all compact exhaustions**. Proof chain: Jauregui Th5 lower bound + BFM Th5.5 at p=2 (or Th5.6 after definition matching) upper bound + Jauregui–Lee equality. BFM's C⁰ upper inequality has no H₂ restriction; the C² isoperimetric equality has no H₂ restriction. Therefore this chain covers the standard interpretation even when the standalone BFM Th1.3's H₂ condition has not been checked. Credit BFM2023 for the essential new upper inequality, and Jauregui/JL for the other sides. Neither cataloguing nor algebraic restatement is a new equality theorem.

The literal no-curvature/no-boundary OWR wording is not covered. Multiple ends, genuinely nonsmooth metrics, arbitrary nonminimal boundary, scalar curvature merely eventually nonnegative, and mass coordinate invariance at decay τ≤1/2 also cannot be promoted from these exact results. Benatti's extra generality concerns equivalence of capacity and isoperimetric masses with its own global assumptions.

## Source-independent falsification mechanism for the literal unqualified sentence

Let m<0 and M={|x|≥R₀}, R₀>|m|/2, with g=U⁴δ, U=1+m/(2|x|). This is smooth complete as a manifold with compact boundary, scalar-flat, one-ended, O₂(r⁻¹) AF, with ADM mass m; the boundary is not minimal. Choose nested offset balls K_j=M∩B_{L_j}(D_j e₁), L_j=j³, D_j=L_j−j, j>R₀. Then K_j contains M∩B_j and exhausts M, while D_j/L_j→1.

On the exterior of K_j, ψ=(1−L_j/|x−D_j e₁|)/U is its capacitary potential since Δ(Uψ)=0 and U is harmonic. Its asymptotic coefficient gives `cap_g(K_j)=L_j+m/2` exactly. Newton's shell formula gives `∫_{B_L(De₁)}|x|⁻¹dx=2π(L²−D²/3)` when D<L. Expanding U⁶ and removing the fixed inner ball gives `|K_j|=(4π/3)L_j³+6πm(L_j²−D_j²/3)+O(L_j)`. Hence `r_V(K_j)=L_j+(3m/2)−mD_j²/(2L_j²)+o(1)`, so the deficit tends `m/2>m`. This supplies an explicit counterexample to the literal equality without minimal-boundary/global-PMT hypotheses. A smooth compact positive conformal fill-in gives the same exterior and negative ADM mass but may have negative scalar curvature in the core; compact fill-in changes volume by O(1) and does not alter the limit. It then refutes curvature-free equality even with empty boundary. These are predictions from equations, not claims inferred from candidate code. Check algebra only after the candidate-mathematics seal.

## Success and falsification criteria fixed before candidate access

- Verify the mass definitions, capacity convention, domain/exhaustion class, limsup/sup order, smoothness, ends, decay/integrability, scalar curvature, boundary/topology assumptions, version numbers, and priority against the exact primary sources.
- A valid resolved status must name the intended standard R≥0/empty-minimal-boundary scope, explain why the arbitrary-exhaustion inequality is supplied, credit the established result, and keep broader literal wording separate. A bare resolved status for all AF metrics fails.
- A formula on balls, Taylor identity, or finite numerical sample is only a calibration. It does not prove the upper bound over arbitrary exhaustions.
- Reject circular routes that assume the capacity-volume upper bound, global isoperimetric inequality at ADM mass without an applicable theorem, or unproved minimizer asymptotics.
- Boundary controls: Euclidean m=0; positive Schwarzschild m>0 exterior horizon; arbitrary exhaustions vs balls; negative m with nonminimal boundary; τ near1/2; finite vs infinite mass; C⁰ vs C¹ vs O₂ assumptions; H₂ constraint retained where actually needed; p endpoint restrictions.

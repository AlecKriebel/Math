# Capacity–volume versus ADM mass: two exact source scopes

One substantive turn; full independent review pending. No novelty certification.

1. In the standard class explicitly conjectured in Jauregui's full paper, namely smooth complete one-ended AF3-manifolds with nonnegative scalar curvature and empty/minimal compact boundary, equality is a credited prior result. The exact chain is m_ADM≤m_CV≤m_iso=m_ADM. SIGMA2023 Theorem5.6 supplies the missing upper comparison, without the extra topology assumption of that paper's separate Theorem1.3. The known sign issue in the upper-bound proof is harmless in this nonnegative-mass class and is explicitly documented.

2. Without the global nonnegative scalar-curvature hypothesis, the broad literal all-AF sentence is false. A smooth compact fill of the mass−2 Schwarzschild end, onR³ with no boundary or singularity, admits nested balls centered at(R/2)e₁ with radiusR. Their capacity is exactlyR−1 and volume radius isR−11/4+O(R^−1). Therefore m_CV≥−7/4>−2=m_ADM. This does not claim the exact global value of m_CV.

Suggested disposition: credited standard-class resolution with a separately reviewed literal-scope counterexample; one author turn used. Final label belongs to the independent publication gate. It must not be presented as a newly solved nonnegative-scalar-curvature conjecture.

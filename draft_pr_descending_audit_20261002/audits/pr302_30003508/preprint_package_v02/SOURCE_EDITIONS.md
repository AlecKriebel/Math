# Primary-source scope and edition notes

Audit date:2026-10-05 UTC. Target30003508 / OWR-15432-001, original PR302 head eb6e0e999521d84a65f9857d338cad76b84d30db, original claimed_solved after2/5 author turns. The current acceptance concerns the precise smooth tensor spectral-consistency theorem stated in the note.

The original problem is Markus Reiss's contribution with Jakub Chorowski, Emmanuel Gobet and Marc Hoffmann, “SDE estimation from discrete observations as inverse problems,” printed1507–1509 in [OWR24/2017](https://ems.press/journals/owr/articles/15432), DOI10.4171/OWR/2017/24. The full contribution was read and all three official PDF pages were visually checked. Its report/event year is2017; official publication is28April2018. It asks for convergence of matrix reconstruction from empirical eigenpairs and invariant density, with retained K still to be chosen. It supplies neither a fixed multidimensional estimator nor an additional sensor-noise observation model. Our known smooth bounded connected domain, smooth unknown S/mu, known bounds, stationary exact fixed-lag observations and natural conormal reflection are explicit model assumptions. Reconstructing S is equivalent locally to recovering covariance2S/mu, not to confusing S with the covariance itself.

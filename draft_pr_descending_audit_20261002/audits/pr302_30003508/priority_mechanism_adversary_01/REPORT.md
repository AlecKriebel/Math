## Hardest alternative mechanism and its precise limits

The ordinary joint kernel estimate can be much weaker than the PR's derivative estimator: L2 convergence suffices for a regularized forward-model fit. The alternative uses n_j=2^j and bandwidth(j+1)^(-4), with L2 boundary bias O(sqrt(h_j)), integrated spectral-gap variance, and summable probabilities. It does not assume global smoothness of the zero extension. Penalizing a predetermined Hs norm, s>d/2+2, at alpha_j=1/(j+1) bounds estimated coefficients without knowing the true Hs norm. Unknown mu is recovered through the joint-density marginal. Common-H1 conjugated forms avoid changing conormal operator domains; resolvent continuity, continuous semigroup functional calculus, and uniform elliptic eigenvalue tails give Hilbert–Schmidt joint-kernel continuity. Countable feasible approximate minimization makes the construction measurable. These details are essential: compact-inverse continuity on a single known compact class alone would not cover all unrestricted smooth truths.

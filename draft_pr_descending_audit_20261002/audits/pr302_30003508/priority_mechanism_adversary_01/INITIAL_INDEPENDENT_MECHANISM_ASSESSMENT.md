Chen–Hansen–Scheinkman (2009) is a very serious exact multivariate reversible precedent for spectral identification and NPC construction. Its section 7 requires the diffusion matrix already known or consistently estimated, and expressly leaves formal statistical justification for future work. Section 8 likewise excludes inference. Nickl (2024) genuinely establishes fixed-lag multivariate statistical consistency, but the unknown coefficient is scalar f, not a general tensor; the stated theorem uses convex smooth domains and f prescribed near the boundary. Its density/potential discussion is an extension mechanism, not anisotropy. Koskela–Spanò–Jenkins (2018/2019) requires known unit diffusion covariance; section 2 expressly says even a Lamperti reduction requires the covariance known a priori.

Modern generator approaches need closer attention: Mauroy–Goncalves (2017/2020) has a genuine convergence theorem, but it takes sampling interval to zero and assumes deterministic noiseless snapshot flows. Its process-noise extension has a constant additive scalar variance. Zhou–Meng–Liu (2025) estimates general drift and covariance through resolvent/Yosida approximations, but statistical construction uses repeated independently started trajectories and high-precision quadrature; its single-trajectory appendix sends the noise to zero. These differences are material to the exact fixed-lag, one stationary trajectory target, regardless of numerical low-sampling-rate demonstrations.

General identification from log P is longstanding and must not be promoted as novel. Generic compact inverse/sieve consistency is a genuine adversarial alternative: injectivity on compact smooth coefficient classes can turn a strongly consistent transition-density estimate into coefficient consistency. However, one must verify forward continuity for variable conormal boundary conditions, inverse topology, union of unknown smoothness classes, regularization selection and actual empirical supply. Those ingredients cannot be transferred to an unnamed general theorem without checking the hypotheses. This family will audit that possible reduction explicitly before concluding.

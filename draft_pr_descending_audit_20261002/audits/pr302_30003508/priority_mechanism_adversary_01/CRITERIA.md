# Independent mechanism-family priority criteria

Frozen before reading submitted priority reports or other new reviewers' findings, 2026-10-05 UTC.

Target: actual empirical, almost-sure local uniform recovery of every smooth general anisotropic symmetric uniformly elliptic diffusion tensor S and separately fitted div S, with clipped global L2 recovery, from one stationary trajectory observed at an arbitrary fixed known positive lag. Unknown smooth positive normalized invariant density; known connected bounded C-infinity domain in dimension at least two; reversible generator mu^-1 div(S grad), conormal reflection; known ellipticity and density bounds. No assumed oracle spectral data, fixed finite model, scalar isotropy, one dimension, shrinking sampling lag, rates, or external repeated experiments.

A prior result blocks priority if its proved theorem contains this result, or if its proved assumptions and an explicitly verified elementary reduction give the same data-consistency claim. A general compact or sieve inverse-consistency theorem can qualify even with a different algorithm. Merely knowing log P determines a self-adjoint generator establishes identification, not automatically continuity in estimated transition operators or a valid empirical construction. Conversely, a novel construction cannot rescue a claim to have solved an already resolved statistical question.

Search families: fixed-lag general diffusion estimation; semigroup/generator inversion and identification; spectral Kolmogorov PDE reconstruction; multivariate nonparametric or Bayesian consistency; operator-learning estimators and inverse elliptic coefficient problems. Locate using search, then inspect decisive full primary sources, including theorem hypotheses and proof mechanism. Record dates, sources, strongest exact covered statement, and the missing hypothesis. No absolute worldwide-firstness certificate.

Early independence: do not consult the submitted priority comparison or the direct-target review until an initial mechanism assessment has been written. Independent mathematical acceptance supplied by ROOT is a gate, not evidence of novelty.

Custody: native processes have actual PID, argv, cwd, UTC start/end, exit, full stdout and stderr, executing-source and executable identities. Web search outputs are tool evidence without invented operating-system PID. Preserve failed downloads. Final authored reports, source pins, native receipts and inventories are frozen; third-party PDFs/text remain private. No Git, index, service, publication, merge, shared-control or tracked-file changes; no individual or other-chat communication. New material budget 35 MB.

Success: either identify a mathematically covering prior theorem and show the reduction, or produce a bounded substantive audit with all promising mechanisms precisely excluded and an explicit remaining historical gap. Completion percentages estimate this family's research work, not the probability of novelty.

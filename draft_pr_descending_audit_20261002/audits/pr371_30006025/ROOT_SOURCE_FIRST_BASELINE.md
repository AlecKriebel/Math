## Independently read source premises

Its cited Chapuy[4] is the2009preprint/2010PTRF paper0804.0546, whose construction applies to a dominant subset with marked triples. It is distinct from an unrestricted trisection theorem. The original PDF1,12–13 states the marked-tree reciprocal bijection, the unique cyclic gluing preserving one face, and the reverse gluing creating three faces. Euler changes must therefore be checked with the actual face convention.

CFF1202.3252v5 PDF1–3,6–7 gives rooted orientable unicellular maps, C-permutations with odd signed cycles, and the graph-preserving correspondence2^(n+1)E_g(n)≈T_g(n). A C-tree on n edges has n+1 vertices before quotient; genus=(n+1−number_of_cycles)/2. This is a finite correspondence with multiplicity and underlying graph preservation. Its statement does not supply a hyperbolic metric or the WP distribution.

Janson–Louf2111.11903v2 PDF1–4 uses g→∞ and g=o(n), scale sqrt(n/(12g)); the rescaled cycle process has intensity(cosh(t)−1)/t. Its proposed core/hyperbolic coupling is expressly conjectural. The BGL published2025model PDF2–4 uses positive edge lengths, fixed face count, total face perimeter asymptotic12g, with spectrum excluding face cycles. Its corollary gives the limiting systole law, not an identity of finite-genus geometric surface laws. The companion arXivv1 was read separately and is not interchangeable byte evidence for the published version.

Mirzakhani–Petri author PDF1–2 defines normalized finite WP volume; the displayed exact density is(e^t+e^(−t)−2)/(2t)=(cosh(t)−1)/t. The nearby small-epsilon approximation is not used as an exact coefficient. Consequently the limiting distribution has positive mass in every interval(0,epsilon) and an unbounded support, as follows by integrating the exact positive density. This can falsify a candidate fixed systole bound; finite simulations alone cannot prove a whole-process coupling.

Uniform-dessins2306.09543 PDF3–6 separately constructs an index-E subgroup from the stabilizer of a labelled dessin edge; uniform passports imply torsion freeness because every nontrivial power of an elliptic generator acts without fixed points. The surface quotient and geodesic edges are credited classical facts. Nonuniform passports require a separate smoothness check.

Delaygue2210.12046v2 PDF1 states classical Hermite–Lindemann and Lindemann–Weierstrass premises; no new E-function result is relevant. Izmestiev1409.7681 PDF1–3 gives a disk isoperimetric inequality L²≥4piA+A² for hyperbolic cone metrics with negatively curved interior cone points. Boundary sectors may have any positive angle; they are not subject to the interior cone restriction. A locally planar development may overlap, so replacing this theorem by an embedded-plane argument would need extra justification. The result is credited to earlier Alexandrov/Bol inputs.

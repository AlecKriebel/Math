## Independent acceptance criteria and anticipated falsifications

A geometric lift must identify objects, markings and metric conventions. CFF Theorem 5 gives a graph-preserving bijection 2^(n+1) E_g(n) ≃ T_g(n); it is not a smooth hyperbolic realization or a WP pushforward theorem. The construction is rooted and oriented. Its signs/multiplicities and chosen matching matter for sampling maps, but the preserved underlying graph permits transferring edge lengths as combinatorial labels.

For a closed smooth curvature -1 polygon-gluing surface, angle totals at each identified vertex must equal 2pi. A topological vertex merge alone supplies no angle, length or holonomy data. Trees with leaves cannot sit as a nondegenerate geodesic one-face map with an ordinary smooth polygon face without cusp/spike boundary behavior. Explicit genus0 and genus1 need separate handling: a closed smooth hyperbolic surface exists only for g≥2.

For a uniform clean dessin obtained by subdividing all edges of a d-regular one-face map with e edges, the monodromy acts on 2e darts: types (2,d,2e), index 2e, not e. Equal full cycle lengths make the stabilizer preimage torsion-free, by conjugacy of every finite-order triangle element to an elliptic-generator power. The orientation-preserving subgroup, rather than the reflection group, uniformizes a closed oriented surface. Riemann–Hurwitz must match g. Vertex degrees that merely divide a larger signature produce torsion and cone points; removing torsion by another covering changes degree/genus and needs an explicit argument.

A hyperbolic triangle group admits a conjugate with algebraic matrices: rational angles have algebraic trig values; its defining triangle can be built using algebraic hyperbolic cosine laws. Thus hyperbolic word traces are algebraic, exp(length/2) is algebraic, and any positive closed-geodesic length is transcendental by Hermite–Lindemann. This restricts exact length preservation for algebraic edge labels; it does not by itself exclude approximate coupling or weak convergence of discrete support to continuous laws.

For the triangle family (2,3,q), q≥7, Philippe gives shortest positive hyperbolic translation 2 arcosh(2cos²(pi/q)-1/2). The function increases with q because cos(pi/q) increases, so its common positive lower bound is its q=7 value. A torsion-free subgroup cannot contain a shorter hyperbolic translation than its ambient group. Therefore any unrescaled sampler supported on such subgroups has a fixed systolic gap and cannot reproduce WP's positive limiting count probability below that gap. This conclusion should be tested against the exact candidate family; arbitrary triangle signatures need not share this particular bound.

For WP, Mirzakhani–Petri's exact mean is integral (exp(t)+exp(-t)-2)/(2t)dt, equal to OWR's density. The nearby small-epsilon text appears to have a factor2 error: direct series gives lambda(0,epsilon) ~ epsilon²/4. Only the exact display is admissible. Janson–Louf scale discrete graph lengths by sqrt(12g/n), not by the cubic core's number of edges. A deterministic global scale a converts density to rho(t/a)/a; preserving rho(t)=(cosh t-1)/t forces a=1 by its expansion t/2+t³/24+…. This rules out a proposed nontrivial rescaling only if the candidate claims preservation of that law, not arbitrary metric rescaling.

At fixed g, countably many discrete combinatorial inputs and fixed algebraic constructions have atomic/countably supported outputs of WP measure zero (smooth volume on positive-dimensional moduli). This blocks exact WP sampling but not weak asymptotic convergence. A uniform support gap supplies the stronger asymptotic obstruction. A continuous edge-law lift could avoid atomicity and requires an actual pushforward/Jacobian argument.

Cutting a closed curvature -1 genus-g surface along a finite embedded geodesic one-face map gives a disk with area4pi(g-1) and boundary length2L, where L is total edge length counting edges once. Izmestiev's hyperbolic disk inequality applies even with unrestricted positive boundary sector angles: 4L²≥4piA+A², hence L≥2pi sqrt(g(g-1)). It also applies to interior cone angles≥2pi, but positive cone curvature invalidates this use. Flat sectors may exceed 2pi at the boundary, so a planar nonoverlapping development cannot be presumed.

## Scope boundaries

Finite word searches, cycle enumeration, and numerical positivity cannot certify all group elements, infinite random laws, or universal topology. A successful finite replay only checks reproducibility of the declared finite calculation. Original Question4 may admit other geometric lifts with extra length, angle or holonomy data; scoped obstructions and a classical triangle construction are partial progress, not a full solution or verified novelty claim.

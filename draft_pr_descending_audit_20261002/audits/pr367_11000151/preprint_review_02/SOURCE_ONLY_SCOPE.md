The precise algebraic question concerns the standard Artin group A5=B6 modulo C5^5=P, where C5=a1a2a3a4 and P=a5a4a3a2a1a1a2a3a4a5. For every positive word in these five fixed generators representing C5^10, the source asks whether its length is40,30,20 and whether its ordered factorization is Hurwitz-equivalent to C5^10, (a1a2a3a4a5)^6, or P^2 respectively. Wajnryb gives the quotient relation literally; no injectivity into a mapping class group should be assumed. The question is a restricted fixed-generator algebraic question, not a classification of arbitrary conjugate twists or of all Lefschetz fibrations.

Auroux's elementary Hurwitz move is (u,v)->(uvu^-1,u); its inverse is (u,v)->(v,v^-1uv). It preserves product and tuple length; intermediates may be conjugates.

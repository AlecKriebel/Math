The submitted module is a classical cyclic-word BT1 module. In the notation of
Pries and Ulmer, *On BT1 group schemes and Fermat curves*, New York J. Math.
27 (2021), 705–739, §3.2.1, write `w=u5...u0=vvfvff`. Reading the indices
in increasing order gives `u0,...,u5=f,f,v,f,v,v`. Their defining maps are
`F(ej)=e(j+1)` at an f-letter and `V(e(j+1))=ej` at a v-letter, with
indices modulo six. These give exactly the submitted maps, including
`V(e0)=e5`. Thus no new underlying module is being constructed outside the
classical Kraft classification. The corresponding forward-edge notation is
`FFVFVV`; it reverses the displayed-letter order used by Pries–Ulmer.

The word is primitive, and its letter complement is not a cyclic rotation.
The classical duality rule in §4.1 consequently already establishes this
module's non-self-duality. The submitted square-image invariant is a direct,
independently verified proof of that existing structural fact. A different
proof does not establish new priority for the underlying object or for generic
existence of non-self-dual BT1 groups.

The exact priority comparison still needs the original question's full
combination: the supersingular elliptic p-torsion filtration on the special
fiber and an admissible finite flat p-killed Witt-ring lift. The module and
duality classification alone does not state these two additional properties.
Whether an earlier primary source gives this combination, an equivalent
counterexample, or a stronger theorem unambiguously yielding it remains open
in this audit. No absolute first-discovery claim is warranted.

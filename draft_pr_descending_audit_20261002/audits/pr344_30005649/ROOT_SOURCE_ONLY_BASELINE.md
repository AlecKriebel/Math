## Exact original question
Naotake Takao's contribution in Oberwolfach Report 42/2023 runs from printed 2476 through 2479, with its references at the top of 2480. I read the whole contribution and references and visually inspected all five corresponding PDF pages 100--104. The overall setup has p>3 and k perfect of characteristic p, W=W(k). The source distinguishes the p-torsion finite flat commutative scheme mathcal G over W of rank p^(2n) from its special fiber G over k. Definition 1(3) defines quasi-supersingularity separately for these two bases: a filtration with successive quotients the p-torsion of supersingular elliptic curves over that same base. The filtration condition is stronger than connectedness, nilpotence of F and V, or an equal count of F and V symbols.

Proposition 2 on printed 2479 has hypothesis that the SPECIAL FIBER G is quasi-supersingular, and concludes that both G and mathcal G are self-dual for n<=2. The ensuing unnumbered question asks whether its assertion holds for n>=3. The statement is not a demand that mathcal G itself have a lifted qss filtration. The previous Proposition 1 says qss of the special fiber need not lift to qss over W. A counterexample to the broad assertion can disprove either of its two self-duality conclusions, but the precise component established must be declared. A k-only construction needs a justified lift over W to inhabit the original setting; a lift with non-self-dual special fiber automatically cannot be self-dual over W, since Cartier duality and an isomorphism commute with reduction.

This auxiliary question is separate from Problem 1 about inertia acting on curve torsion and from the second unnumbered higher-genus Raynaud-type question. A negative answer to auxiliary self-duality does not solve the Coleman conjecture.

## Independent approach families and falsifiable gates
(1) Dieudonne algebra: construct or classify modules of dimension 2n with p killed and sigma/sigma-inverse semilinear F,V, and FV=VF=0; prove a real qss filtration, then compare with the correctly defined Cartier dual. Over an algebraic closure, arbitrary k-linear similarity of constant F_p matrices is insufficient because semilinearity changes the equations. A claimed determinant obstruction must remain true for arbitrary coefficients over k, not merely an exhaustive search over F_p.
(2) Finite group schemes and lifting: check that a module is realized by a finite commutative group scheme; every filtration subquotient is E[p], not alpha_p+alpha_p; check existence of a valid unramified W lift using an actual theorem with all hypotheses or an explicit Honda/Fontaine--Laffaille object. This is a separate step from an abstract special-fiber matrix example.
(3) Intrinsic invariants/classification: investigate invariants under Cartier duality (kernel/image/intersection data of F,V and iterates, cyclic word classifications or extension data), and try to produce or rule out non-self-dual examples independently. Equal dimension/ranks F,V alone does not prove self-duality. Any candidate minimal-rank assertion needs a proof and the n<=2 boundary must agree with the source.

No counterexample or theorem is established by this source-only baseline. My initial algebraic ideas remain hypotheses: cyclic-word or filtered extension methods might yield a rank p^6 example, but qss membership and W liftability are central unproved gaps. Do not promote these guesses or import a candidate witness before this baseline is frozen.

\[
\sum_{l=1}^\infty h_1(l-u)^2=\sum_{j=0}^\infty h_1(j+(1-u))^2.

Two elementary class checks guard against confusing different assumptions. First, $\nu_s=\delta_1$ for $s<1$ and $\nu_s=\delta_1+\delta_2$ for $s\ge1$ has bounded quadratic rate and is nonsymmetric. $\nu(dz)=1_{0<|z|<e^{-1}}|z|^{-3}\log^{-2}(1/|z|)dz$
has $\int z^2\nu(dz)=2$ but divergent $r$th small-jump moment for every $r<2$ (substitute $y=\log(1/|z|)$). Hence the bounded-quadratic-rate assumption genuinely permits index2. These examples verify scope; they assert no new priority result.

# Primary source capsules

These are bounded authored summaries of external source facts. The independent mathematical deductions and classification judgments are in REPORT.md. No external full text is a public artifact.

## S1. Original controlling source (under200 words; 18-word quotation)

Mark Podolskij, *Various limit theorems for ambit processes*, OWR09/2013, printed pp491–493, especially p492. Read the complete contribution, including its Brownian and field sections and references. It defines squared first increments on a mesh tending to zero. For the pure-jump moving average it gives a compound-Poisson stable limit at every fixed positive time, with kernel x^alpha f(x), 0<alpha<1/2, smooth exponentially decaying f and f(0) nonzero. Its final pure-jump sentence is: “We conjecture that similar asymptotic result holds for a rather general pure jump semimartingale driver (given sufficient conditions).” No exact maximal driver class, characteristic conditions, or functional topology is specified. The next paragraph changes to continuous ambit fields.

Primary: https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46440

## S2. Established predecessor (under200 words)

Basse-O’Connor–Heinrich–Podolskij, *On limit theory for Lévy semi-stationary processes*, arXiv1604.02307v2 (14 October2016), Bernoulli24(4A) (2018),3117–3146, Theorem1.2(i). Its model integrates (g(t-s)-g0(-s)) sigma_(s-) against a symmetric pure-jump Lévy process L relative to the filtration; sigma is adapted càdlàg. Let beta be L’s jump index. Assumption(A) requires g~c0 t^alpha at zero; a tail exponent theta in(0,2]; bounded L^theta kernel difference; k derivatives, the near-zero derivative bound, and eventually decreasing L^theta absolute first/kth derivatives. Put H_s=g^(k)(-s)sigma_s 1_(s<=-delta). Assumption(B1) requires some 0<rho<=1∧theta and beta'>beta, beta'>=p with E[(integral(|H|^rho∨|H|^beta')ds)^(1∨p/2)] finite; rho<1 if theta=1. With beta<2, p>=1, p>beta and alpha<k-1/p, the theorem gives functional F-stable M1 convergence on the 1/n grid to the independently uniformly marked jump sum. Remark1.4 excludes J1/J2. No independence of sigma and L is imposed.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/pdf/1604.02307v2
Publisher DOI: https://doi.org/10.3150/17-BEJ956

## S3. Earlier Lévy-only theorem (under200 words)

Basse-O’Connor–Lachièze-Rey–Podolskij, *Power variation for a class of stationary increments Lévy driven moving averages*, Ann.Probab.45(6B) (2017),4477–4528, Theorem1.1(i), gives the related marked stable limit for a symmetric Lévy driver under its assumptions. This review uses S2 for the non-Lévy comparison.

Primary: https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.06679

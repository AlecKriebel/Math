## Exact accepted claim

For a simple pure-jump measure with diffuse predictable compensator
`nu_s(dz) ds`, bounded density drift, and a deterministic global bound on
`int z^2 nu_s(dz)`, the two-sided convolution with
`g(x)=1_(x>0)x^alpha f(x)`, `0<alpha<1/2`, exponentially controlled `f,f'`,
has the stated normalized quadratic-variation limit jointly at every fixed
finite list of deterministic horizons, along every deterministic positive
mesh sequence tending to zero. Stability is relative to the entire original
sigma field. The limit uses consistently attached independent uniform jump
phases and the bounded continuous periodic weight `W_alpha`.

The result asserts sufficient conditions, not every semimartingale or a
maximal driver class. It asserts no functional topology. Deterministic time
atoms, continuous martingale noise, uncontrolled singular drift and the two
alpha endpoints are excluded.

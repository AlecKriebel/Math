**Original 9700034 remains unsolved after five substantive author turns.**

The target is Aldous's 2012 Problem34, renumbered Problem8 in the 2014 paper:
finite expected maximum Euclidean route length over iid uniform destinations.
Travel time, Euclidean arclength and uncountable continuum maxima are distinct.

The five turns establish a credited Kahn-model uniform corollary, an FDD
increment criterion, sampled tail-visibility criteria, unconditional finite
exterior-arc/root localization, and a mixture reduction with a fixed-base
affine no-go theorem. Any additional moment, route-length triangle or joint-
law assumptions remain explicit. The finite exterior union is only almost
surely finite; its mean and the terminal endpoint maxima are still open in
general. Scalar examples are diagnostic and have no asserted SIRSN realization.

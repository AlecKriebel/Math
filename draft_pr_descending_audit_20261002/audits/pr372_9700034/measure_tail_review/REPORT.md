## Exact target and universal baseline

For every planar SIRSN in the stated class, let U_i be iid uniform on the unit disk, independent of the route process. The target is E M<infinity, where M=sup_i len R(0,U_i). This is Aldous long2012 Open Problem34, retained as published2014 Open Problem8. The expectation includes network randomness and destinations; routes are compatible prescribed paths but need not minimize Euclidean length or obey a route-length triangle inequality.

If m=E D_1, the ordinary invariances yield E len R(0,U_1)=2m/3. For M_n=max_{i<=n}len R(0,U_i), E M_n<=2nm/3 and E M_n/n->0. Neither proves a uniform expected maximum. Monotone convergence says E M=lim_n E M_n, allowing infinity. A uniform finite bound on E M_n is necessary and sufficient.

With a jointly measurable routing-length field, iid sampling gives M=esssup_z len R(0,z) almost surely, with respect to normalized disk area. Its exact tail is P(q_omega(t)>0), where q_omega(t) is the area fraction of destinations whose lengths exceed t. The marginal route tail is E q_omega(t). Integrability of the latter does not control the former. Without such a field realization, TURN3 proves the same support-tail statement using a permutation-invariant empirical frequency Q(t) in the countably sampled law.

A null-set spike can change a pointwise spatial supremum without changing any independent countable sample. Consequently pointwise, essential and dense-grid suprema must not be interchanged without explicit regularity.

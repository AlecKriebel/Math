## One-dimensional mechanism and direct limits

For a repetitive word the Rauzy graph has p(n) vertices, p(n+1) edges and connected underlying graph; its H1 rank is p(n+1)-p(n)+1. If p(n)<=Cn eventually, the increments cannot eventually exceed every integer: telescoping would violate the linear bound. Therefore a cofinal sequence has bounded rank. Any finite family in the direct limit lifts together to a later member of that sequence, hence is dependent beyond the bound. This is Julien v1 Theorem 5.10 / Proposition 5.16; published numbering differs. No assertion about uniformly bounded increments is needed.

In higher dimension cell counts of order n^d do not yield constant Betti ranks or surviving rank. The relevant finite-vector-space condition for rank<=B is: for every source stage i there exists later j for which the image from stage i has dimension<=B. It implies any B+1 limit classes are dependent. Conversely if limit rank<=B, choose a basis of the finite source space's eventual kernel, kill that finite basis at one later stage, and the finite-stage image has rank<=B. The later stage depends on i. There need not be a uniform delay. Independent controls implement births b with lifetime b+1; every stage dies, lifetimes are unbounded. Wedges of circles with constant versus injective bonding independently falsify inference from growing finite-stage Betti numbers.

## Specified actions and coefficients

In a profinite odometer Z-action, two distinct points agree modulo2^N; adding any integer preserves that agreement. Their equal-phase suspension points remain uniformly close for all times. This disproves expansivity for that specified action; conjugacy to an FLC aperiodic tiling action would contradict the tiling's expansivity. It does not exclude another action on the same underlying compact space or every possible homeomorphic tiling realization. Minimality, freeness, local Euclidean plaques, or finite rational rank alone do not supply expansivity/FLC.

No dimension-only bound on all n>=1 complexity coefficients follows: arbitrary alphabets already give arbitrary p(1). That elementary observation alone says nothing about asymptotic coefficients. For the stronger asymptotic example, choose an irrational circle rotation and q>=3 partition endpoints in distinct rotation orbits, with every gap<1/2. The n-step boundary set has exactly qn points. Each word cylinder is an intersection of arcs of length<1/2 and is connected when nonempty, so different atoms give different words: p(n)=qn. Minimality/repetitivity follows from irrational rotation on nonempty cylinder arcs; aperiodicity follows from the irrational action. The split-circle continuous rational step-function module has constants Q and jump module D=ker(Q[t,t^-1]^q→Q) (total jump zero). Since multiplication t−1 has no invariants on D or the Laurent module, the exact coinvariant sequence gives dim D/(t−1)D=q and dim C/(t−1)C=q+1. Thus each suspension has finite H1 rank q+1, while these ranks and asymptotic complexity coefficients are unbounded across q. This falsifies a uniform bound; it does not falsify finiteness for any one system.

## Exact comparison target

For a fixed noncircular ellipse and nondegenerate confocal elliptic caustic supporting a primitive even-period family, let the pedal vertices be the perpendicular feet of fixed M on the boundary tangents at the billiard vertices, in trajectory order. The target is phase independence of

\[
 C'_2(M)=\frac{\sum_i[Q_i,Q_{i+1}](Q_i+Q_{i+1})}{3\sum_i[Q_i,Q_{i+1}]}.
\]

The quotient is required to be defined. The submitted candidate distinguishes a fixed zero-area circle for negative d from a varying defined centroid, and proves universal definedness for simple winding-one even families. This audit accepts that domain as its target; independent correctness verification of the complete proof belongs to the separate mathematical audits.

## Exact controls against false identifications

1. In the four-period rectangle frame the pedal vertices are (A,My),(Mx,B),(-A,My),(Mx,-B). Direct sums give C0=M/2. Shoelace area and first moment give S=2AB and C2=M/3. The two centroid quantities differ even in this elementary case. The rational instance A=2, B=3, M=(1,2) is retained in `EXACT_COMPARISON_CONTROLS.json`.
2. Euclidean projection does not commute with an anisotropic affine stretch. For M=(1,0), line y=x, and L=diag(2,1), L(proj(M))=(1,1/2), whereas proj(L(M)) on the stretched line y=x/2 is (8/5,4/5). Thus a circular regular-polygon pedal theorem cannot be lifted to an ellipse merely because area centroids themselves are affine covariant.
3. The signed quotient is distinct from an unsigned centroid for self-intersecting stars. The original definitions and the candidate agree on the signed convention, including the denominator qualification. No claim concerning unsigned-region centroids has been treated as evidence for the target.

The direct identification routes through k306, circle stretching, or a generic Poncelet centroid-locus theorem are therefore **blocked as substitutes for a k307 proof**. The last route would transfer the central difficulty to an unsupported Poncelet identification plus collapse-to-a-point assertion. This does not rule out a materially new proof using those ideas.

An additional exact comparison identifies the submitted doubled-normal polygon with a Poncelet configuration on the unit circle whose caustic ellipse has one focus at the circle center. Its caustic center is v/2, and the candidate telescope places its signed area centroid at v/3. See `CIRCLE_FOCUS_INTERMEDIATE_SCOPE.md` for the support-equation derivation and nesting check. A pre-existing proof of this intermediate could deserve method credit, but it would still leave the mixed-support cubic pedal-moment reconstruction. No novelty is asserted for the cyclic lemma, this focus specialization, or the classical circle-normalization mechanism.

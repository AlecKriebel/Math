## Claim under test

The claim of prior resolution requires a checkable chain, with no central missing step: (i) the same positive-particle drift/jump process, (ii) its invariant law as a jointly defined object, and (iii) an explicit joint law on the relevant entire fixed-time configuration. A coincidence of one-point laws or an analogy of generators is insufficient.

## Strongest novelty-saving objections to investigate

1. **Chronology and source intent:** If Aldous explicitly posed the question in February 2018 despite pre-2018 Schur-process work, that is meaningful evidence of an identification gap. It does not logically establish novelty, but should prevent assuming that recognizable old ingredients already constituted an answer.
2. **Different parameters:** A multitime Plancherel chain can describe chronological growth of a partition. The target needs spatial finite-dimensional distributions of the stationary particle configuration. The exact parameter and shape mapping must be proved.
3. **First row versus full process:** Explicit laws for each longest increasing subsequence separately do not imply joint laws across nested rectangles. Coherence and compatible coupling must be available and identified.
4. **Dynamics versus invariant representation:** An explicit shape process may not prove that the intended particle point process has the drift and nearest-right jump generator. A limiting representation for pile-top ranks may require further work before it is an invariant joint law.
5. **Finite and infinite prefixes:** A finite rectangle/square construction may include births, boundary effects, or a finite total point count. These must not silently change the fixed finite-particle projection or require an unproved infinite-volume limit.
6. **“Fairly explicit” criterion:** A fully specified Schur chain could already satisfy the qualitative request; the candidate's rational certificate could nevertheless be new as a formulation. Conversely, an existential random-rank mixture with an unspecified joint rank law is not the same level of explicitness.
7. **Publication versus deduction:** A short deduction from prior constructions can still be a new identification. The audit must distinguish direct old statements, mechanical specialization, and genuinely new reasoning; the existence of classical tools alone does not settle priority.

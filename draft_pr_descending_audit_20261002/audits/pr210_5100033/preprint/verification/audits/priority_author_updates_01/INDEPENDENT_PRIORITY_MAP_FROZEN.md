## Exact statement to compare

An outer ellipse E: x²/a²+y²/b²=1 with a>b>0 has original foci F±=(±sqrt(a²−b²),0). Fix a strictly nested nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic and a closed billiard orbit P=(P_j) of odd least period N≥3 with coprime turning number τ (including stars). The outer polygon P′ has consecutive intersections of tangents to E at consecutive P_j. Its side lines are those tangents. Orthogonal feet of the original foci on these ordered tangent lines form two ordered pedal polygons. Their signed shoelace areas B± have phase-independent product B+B−. Cyclic index shifts and simultaneous reversal preserve the target. Repetition r of an odd primitive orbit scales both signed areas by r and the product by r²; this is a deduction, not explicit source terminology. Even least period, hyperbolic caustics, collapsed caustics, and replacement of original foci are outside this exact target.

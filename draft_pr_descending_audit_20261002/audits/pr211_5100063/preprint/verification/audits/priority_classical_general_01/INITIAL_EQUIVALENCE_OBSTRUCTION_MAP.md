## Exact object and success criterion

Fix a real ellipse E with a>b>0 and a nondegenerate nested confocal elliptic caustic. A family has odd least period N>=3 and arbitrary coprime winding. Q_i is the intersection of the tangents to E at consecutive original billiard vertices. B_+ and B_- are straight-edge trajectory-order signed shoelace areas after unit inversions of Q_i about the ORIGINAL foci (+/-sqrt(a²-b²),0). Target: B_+ B_- is independent of phase. Fixed r-fold traversals scale the product by r².

Anticipation requires a prior published theorem with these objects and scope, or an explicit correct deduction using previously established transformations/identities. Similar proof methods, isolated N=3 statements, original-vertex inversions, or own-focus inversion do not suffice. Every transfer must retain original foci and signed straight edges, including stars.

## Independent route hypotheses (before reading full new sources)

1. Akopyan–Schwartz–Tabachnikov (AST), arXiv:2001.02934: area ratio for odd Poncelet polygons in concentric ellipses; polar/Minkowski duality; complex cancellation. Potential transfer requires a precise formula expressing this focal-inverse product in their invariant quantities, not merely replacing an area ratio by an area product.
2. Poncelet grid/Schwartz–Tabachnikov centroid and rational-function theorems: possible broad rigidity for cyclic traces. Potential gap: rational observables on a periodic elliptic curve need not have constant trace; pole residues must cancel for this exact observable.
3. Bialy/Tabachnikov generating functions and integral invariants: potential focal-distance/area expression in integrals. Potential gap: perimeter/angle invariants alone do not imply weighted inverse-edge area sums.
4. Stachel canonical Jacobi parametrization and cyclic elliptic identities: potential exact identity for a trace times its half-period translate. Potential gap: finding a standard general divisor identity does not itself show the geometric trace has the required divisor; the latter may be the problem's main content.
5. Glutsyuk algebraic/rational integrability and confocality/duality: possible theorem for all rational invariants of Poncelet families. Potential gap: rational integrability concerns dynamics and caustics, not arbitrary derived areas.
6. Own-focus original-inverse transfers: outer locus is generally nonconfocal and can reverse major axis. A transfer through original inverse areas or a fixed area ratio must be proved. A numerical counterexample to an alleged universal transfer would block that transfer, not the target.

## Controls sought

- An explicit nonconfocal/axis-reversed outer locus.
- A family in which individual B_+ varies, despite the product being constant.
- A counterexample to extending a generic concentric-Poncelet focal-product claim without the billiard hypotheses, or to conflating inverse-vertex area with polar-dual area.
- If a cyclic identity applies, identify its exact parameters, allowed poles and exceptional periods; do not claim an identity name alone as anticipation.

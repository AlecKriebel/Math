## Target distinctions kept throughout

The strongest verified conclusion from the above corpus is that the **method** and its abstract half-period product lemma are old. No exact general geometric anticipation was verified. That statement is deliberately narrower than “the result is new.”

Original-vertex inversion (k903,a), outer-locus own-focus inversion (k906), unsigned face areas and inverse arcs are different claims. None was silently substituted. The source's question mark only records the source edition's attribution status and is not evidence that later or older literature lacks a proof. The two exact homothetic Poncelet phases block an overbroad inner-focus theorem; correctly aligned target diagnostics leave the k806,a ratio route open pending an actual prior proof.

## Exact comparison gate

Let `C_e` be the signed shoelace area obtained by unit inversion of the original billiard vertices about original focus e. Let `B_e` be the signed shoelace area obtained by unit inversion of the consecutive boundary-tangent intersections about that same original focus. The target is `B_+ B_- = constant` for a fixed strictly inner nondegenerate confocal elliptical caustic, every odd least period N >= 3, every admitted coprime star winding. Reversal changes both area signs. A fixed r-fold repetition scales the product by r^2; this is not a new least-period case.

Literal coverage requires those vertices, both original centers, straight joining of inverted vertices, signed trajectory order, and the full parity/winding scope. The following implication is exact but conditional:

`[C_+ C_- constant] + [B_+/C_+ constant] + [B_-/C_- constant] => B_+ B_- constant`.

Thus a genuine prior all-odd ratio theorem, combined with a genuine original-product theorem, would anticipate the conclusion even without an explicit k904,a theorem. Neither perimeter conservation nor constancy of the ordinary uninverted ratio `A'/A` supplies those ratio premises. Assuming the still-unsupported k806,a bridge merely transfers the central difficulty and is blocked as a priority argument.

Focal inversion about the *outer locus's own foci* is another construction (v11 k906). The outer locus is generally nonconfocal with the billiard and can have the opposite major-axis direction. Inversion of entire edges into arcs and unsigned face-union area also fail the comparison gate.

There is also an exact obstruction to directly reusing the original focal norm-square lemma. For a locus `(x,y)=(-A_o s,B_o t)`, `s^2+t^2=1`, squared distance to original `(c,0)` is

`D(s)=(A_o^2-B_o^2)s^2+2 c A_o s+(B_o^2+c^2)`.

This polynomial is a square only if `B_o^2[c^2-(A_o^2-B_o^2)]=0`. With `B_o>0`, this requires the original centers to be the locus's foci. In the exact example `A_o^2-B_o^2=-125/3 !=5`; the condition fails. Thus a substitution of outer Q for original P in the recent original-product proof is unsupported. This observation refutes that direct substitution; it does not refute adaptability of its method or the target theorem.

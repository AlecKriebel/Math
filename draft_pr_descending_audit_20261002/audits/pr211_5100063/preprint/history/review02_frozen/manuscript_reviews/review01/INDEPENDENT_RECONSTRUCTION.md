Independent logical reconstruction: canonical vertices have increment delta=4K*tau/N. Tangent intersections have Jacobi coordinates (-Ao sn u, Bo cn u). The inverse area is a cyclic sum of an edge function of sn u and dn u. On the sn torus (4K,2iK'), two first-denominator zeros have doubled multiplicity and another degree-two fiber gives simple poles. Reflection about 3K+iK' makes the double Laurent coefficients opposite; those poles are one step apart. The cyclic trace therefore has only simple poles in one real orbit. On the smaller torus (4K/N,4iK'), anti-periodicity pairs its two poles. Positivity on the real axis prevents a zero trace, so both poles actually occur. Reflection about a pole forces zeros at its real half-period shift. Divisor degree two gives complementary zeros and thus a constant half-period product. Switching original foci is the real shift 2K, congruent to that half-period precisely for odd N.

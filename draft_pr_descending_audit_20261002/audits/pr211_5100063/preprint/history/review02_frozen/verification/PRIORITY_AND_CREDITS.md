# Priority scope and credits

The defensible claim is: **a proof of the odd-period outer focal-inverse
area-product invariant conjectured as k904,a in the pinned 2020 source, using
established Jacobi and meromorphic methods.** Historical novelty is undetermined;
first-proof priority is not established. The note must not describe the
conjecture as currently open worldwide, claim a new general elliptic identity,
or infer priority from unsuccessful searches.

The earliest literal printing verified in this audit is
[arXiv:2004.12497v10](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12497v10), posted 25 October 2020,
Table 9, k808,a. The same formula is renamed k904,a in
[v11](https://arxiv.org/abs/2004.12497v11), posted 29 October 2020, Table 10.
The table's `10/20` means the month and year; it does not establish a discovery
day. The shorter published *Fifty New Invariants* companion's actual inverse
table omits this product. That omission neither proves it nor settles later
status. Original submitted triage dating and proposed-year fields are retained
verbatim under `original_submission/` and superseded for current attribution by
the versioned-source audit.

The proof credits [Stachel's canonical parametrization](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40879-021-00524-2),
Theorem 4.3 and equation (4.9), and standard
[DLMF Jacobi-function identities](https://dlmf.nist.gov/22.4).
The source's auxiliary printed dn real-shift sign slip is not propagated:
dn(u+2K)=dn(u). The required formulas are independently checked.
[Akopyan–Schwartz–Tabachnikov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2001.02934v2) establish
classical complex methods for billiard invariants.
[Khare–Lakshminarayan–Sukhatme's cyclic identities](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0207019v2)
and [local reconstruction methods](https://arxiv.org/abs/math-ph/0306028v1)
already supply the abstract principal-part/half-period mechanism once the
target-specific divisor is proved. [Chavez-Caliz, Theorem 6](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40598-020-00154-8)
is earlier complementary-zero/pole area-product work for a different,
untransformed even-period observable. None of that established machinery is
claimed as new here.

Related earlier triangular original-vertex results, including
[Garcia–Reznik](https://arxiv.org/abs/2011.06640v1) and
[Reznik–Garcia–Helman](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.03020v2), deserve credit.
Alper Ferudun's recent preprint
[*Products of the Two Focal Inversion Areas in Elliptic Billiards*](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23096994),
2 October 2026, treats the general odd **original-vertex** product. It is
independent related work by that author, not this campaign's publication.
The present target inverts tangent-intersection outer vertices about the
original foci; those generally are not the foci of the outer locus. Directly
substituting those vertices into an original-locus focal-norm lemma is invalid.

[Roitman–Garcia–Reznik](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.11260v3) prove related
inverse-perimeter and polar/pedal results. Their final Table 1 marks the linked
area statements experimental. A proved general outer/original inverse-area
ratio could provide another route when combined with an original-product
theorem, but no such prior general ratio proof was verified in the audited
corpus. Assuming that unproved bridge transfers the central difficulty and is
not an anticipation argument. General untransformed area-ratio or invariant-form
theorems do not identify this Euclidean focal inverse-area product without
further hypotheses. The exact constructions in `scope_controls/` make two
overbroad proposed transfers falsifiable.

                                                     Abstract
          For a periodic billiard family between strictly nested confocal ellipses, form the outer tangent
      polygon and project the ellipse center onto its side lines. We prove constancy of the product
      of their signed areas when the period is not divisible by four, including coprime stars and
      repeated traversals. This proves the assertion labelled k303,b in the 2020 invariant list and its
      2021 published edition. The actual center-foot map is a fixed linear image of the edge-vector
      polygon of a phase-shifted billiard. Explicit area formulas reduce the assertion to a classical
      odd cyclic Jacobi identity, also giving the invariant’s value. The same full theorem is stated and
      proved in Ferudun’s contemporary version 1.1; no novelty, historical independence or priority
      claim is made here. A source and verification package accompanies this unrefereed, extensively
      AI-assisted proof and verification note.

4    Attribution, evidence and limitations
The note proves the originally conjectured invariant in the stated domain. It does not establish
that this is a new theorem or the first proof. Ferudun’s version 1.1 [9] contains the exact full result.
Its Zenodo record was created on 1 October 2026 at 18:12:36 UTC; version 1.0 was created at
16:56:14 UTC. GitHub server check-suite timestamps place receipt of this note’s unchanged candi-
date proof tree at 05:35:19 UTC that day; PR 209 was created at 05:48:56 UTC. These observations
do not certify past public repository visibility, exclude an earlier website posting, or establish his-
torical independent non-derivation. The bounded priority audit and its byte-bound chronology are
included in the supplement. They leave historical ordering and novelty undetermined. The old
trace identities, foot/velocity and hodograph mechanisms, canonical coordinates and meromorphic
method are expressly credited above. Chavez-Caliz’s even original/outer area product [8, Theorem

    The verification package preserves the original candidate and inherited review unchanged, three

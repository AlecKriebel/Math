## Printed codomain issue and actual mathematical bridge

At zero the source residual derivative into arbitrary continuous energy pairs is z -> -(z,z_t). A pair (0,tg) with g nonzero cannot lie in that image, since its position requires z=0 and hence z_t=0. The manuscript avoids that step: its compatible residual y+L(y^5)-Lu maps the compatible zero-data Banach space into itself and has identity derivative in y. The bounded five-linear product proves smoothness; contraction gives the stronger full-space O(A^5) remainder. This is a complete direct proof rather than inheritance of the printed surjectivity claim.

The old BW necessary criterion applies to the constructed reduced functional, but is conditional. It does not choose wave data, establish a genuine local optimizer, select a zero-cone threshold or supply the concentrating witness. Those steps are explicitly proved here. A short present derivation using established tools cannot be dated to an earlier theorem merely because its inputs predate this submission. Conversely, a genuine prior constructive theorem covering those simultaneous local premises would count without any auxiliary global or target theorem.

The current local core also follows for any positive cap by the composite terminal-square estimate plus S(u)=Lu+o(A^2); this establishes a real strict local optimizer before invoking any abstract NDC criterion. Such a current proof is an alternative mathematical mechanism check, not evidence of earlier publication. The target-zero instance in the same framework has positive L2-squared growth, so abstract membership and a zero cone alone do not decide the sign of the outcome.

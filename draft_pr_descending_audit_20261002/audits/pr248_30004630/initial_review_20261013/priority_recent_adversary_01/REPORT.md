## Exact core proposition screened

The source problem is the defocusing quintic Dirichlet wave equation on a smooth bounded three-dimensional domain with energy initial data, distributed controls, the pointwise constraint `||u(t)||_L2 <= omega(t)`, terminal displacement tracking, positive temporal `L1(L2)` sparsity and `L4(L12)` state penalties, and optional nonnegative terminal-velocity `H^-1` cost. The implication under audit deletes `beta2>0` but retains strict positivity of the source substitute second variation on every nonzero member of its **ordinary critical cone**, and its positive L2-squared growth conclusion.

The submitted realization uses zero initial data on the unit ball, `T=1`, target `pi e`, `gamma=beta1=1`, a fixed small positive cap, and the normalized first radial Dirichlet mode. Zero is a genuine optimizer; the ordinary cone is `{0}`. Vanishing-amplitude pulses converge in L-infinity and lose L2-squared growth. The fresh mathematical reports verify the actual quintic remainder, feasibility and optimality. These stronger verified facts fix the object to compare, but priority requires only the weaker local-optimizer core above.

The optional results are a local L1-squared lower bound and estimates relative to the fixed zero optimizer under target changes: all optimizers have L1 distance `O(||eta||)` and L2 distance `O(sqrt(||eta||))`; on the positive radial ray their L1 mass is `Theta(epsilon)` and L2 distance is at least `c epsilon^(3/4)` for each fixed velocity weight. They are not pairwise Lipschitz selections, do not imply perturbed L-infinity convergence, and cannot rescue a case already covered at the core level.

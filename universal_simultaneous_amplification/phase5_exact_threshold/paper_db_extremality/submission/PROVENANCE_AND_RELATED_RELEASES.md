# Provenance and related public software archives

This note is the source of truth for disclosures about public artifacts in
this research program. It distinguishes public availability from formal
publication and keeps Papers I and II separate.

## Direct predecessor incorporated into Paper I

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21753405> archives the GitHub release
`universal-db-obstruction-v1.0.0`, titled *No universal death--birth
amplifier, v1.0.0*. It contains an earlier fixed-graph obstruction manuscript,
source, and exact verification materials. The Zenodo record is a **software
archive** created from a public GitHub release. It is not a peer-reviewed
journal article and not a bioRxiv preprint.

The present Paper I is a major superseding version. It incorporates that
fixed-graph theorem and adds the full
fitness-two local-optimality theorem, the ancestry-coverage and stationary-
collision framework, the all-order Hessian-sector certificates, and a unified
scope and quantifier analysis. It should therefore be disclosed as a
substantial consolidation and extension that supersedes the earlier
manuscript version. The old DOI must not be used as the persistent identifier
for the new paper.

## Separate companion workstream (Paper II)

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21850042> is a **software archive** for
the historical lower-bound release *Simultaneous amplification below fitness
three halves*. It was superseded within its own workstream.

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21852072> is a **software archive** for
the replacement release *Simultaneous amplification beyond fitness three
halves*. It contains a separate construction and exact certificates concerning
a fitness-independent graph family that amplifies under both update rules.

Neither DOI is a peer-reviewed publication or a bioRxiv preprint. Neither is
the archival identifier for Paper I. They are related because the two papers
share a broader research program, but their principal theorem statements and
proof mechanisms are distinct:

- Paper I: fixed finite death--Birth rigidity, strong selection, and local
  complete-kernel optimality at fitness two;
- Paper II: a growing graph construction giving a lower bound for robust
  simultaneous Birth--death and death--Birth amplification.

If both papers are under editorial review near the same time, the human author
should disclose the companion manuscript and provide it confidentially if a
portal requests related material. This note does not authorize or prepare any
external outreach.

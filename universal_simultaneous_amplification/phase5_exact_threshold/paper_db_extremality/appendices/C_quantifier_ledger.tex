\section{Quantifier and scope ledger}\label{app:quantifiers}

The statements in this paper have deliberately different orders of
quantification.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Fitness-two local theorem.}
For every $n\ge3$ there is an $\eta_n>0$ such that every positive loopless
row-stochastic $P$ with $0<\|P-J_n\|<\eta_n$ satisfies
$\rho_{\dB}(P,2)<\rho_{\dB}(J_n,2)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Full-tangent curvature.}
For every $n\ge3$ and every $\delta\in\calT_n\setminus\{0\}$,
\[
 D\rho_{\dB}(J_n,2)[\delta]=0,\qquad
 D^2\rho_{\dB}(J_n,2)[\delta,\delta]<0.
\]

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Fixed-graph strong selection.}
For every fixed $P$ that is not dynamically complete, there is an $r_0(P)$
such that $\rho_{\dB}(P,r)<\rho_{\dB}(J_n,r)$ for every $r>r_0(P)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Triangle theorem.}
Every positive weighted triangle and every $r>1$ obey complete-graph
maximality, with strictness away from equal weights.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Open global fitness-two statement.}
It remains open whether every $n$ and every undirected weighted $P$ satisfy
$\rho_{\dB}(P,2)\le\rho_{\dB}(J_n,2)$.

\medskip
\noindent\textbf{Open asymptotic-family statement.}
It also remains open whether there is one sequence $(P_n)$ such that, for
every fixed $r>1$, some $n_0(r)$ makes every $P_n$ with $n\ge n_0(r)$ an
amplifier.

The proved rows do not imply either open row.  In particular, local
optimality has a radius $\eta_n$ that need not be bounded below, and the strong-selection threshold
$r_0(P_n)$ may diverge with $n$.

# Research log: action/support regular-combination obstruction

## 2026-08-13 19:45 PDT -- exact tangent obstruction

- Restricted the audit to the two globally nonnegative endpoint action
  remainders and the already proved scaled-first slack.  No kernel, graph,
  parameter, or scalar-coefficient search was performed.
- Used the analytic reversible two-state eigenmode family.  All endpoint
  labels converge to the same homogeneous datum as the perturbation vanishes,
  independently of the kernel eigenvalue.
- Computed the exact quadratic coefficients of the support, scaled-first
  slack, both action remainders, and all four scalar/Picone components.
- Proved that a regular kernel-independent positive exact combination is
  impossible.  The scaled-first coefficient is killed by its nonzero
  `lambda^2` term, after which the target slope requires aggregate Picone
  coefficient `-r/2`.
- Scope is deliberately precise: coefficient formulas singular or
  discontinuous at the homogeneous endpoint and genuinely linked signed
  identities remain open.  The result does not prove or refute the support
  inequality.

# Research plan

1. Derive the complete-graph Bd and dB baselines directly from the mutant-count
   birth--death chain.
2. Derive general weak- and strong-selection endpoint criteria for arbitrary
   finite connected undirected weighted graphs.
3. Implement two independent exact solvers: a full subset-state solver and a
   symmetry-lumped solver whose transition equality is machine checked.
4. Search small rational weighted graphs and low-parameter equitable families
   for endpoint-compatible candidates.
5. For a candidate, construct exact rational comparison functions and certify
   their signs on `r > 1`; otherwise sharpen endpoint evidence into a universal
   obstruction.
6. Produce a manuscript, diagrams, exact certificates, and a one-command
   verifier.  Only then conduct a narrow literature audit.

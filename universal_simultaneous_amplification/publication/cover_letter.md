Dear Editors,

Please consider the enclosed manuscript as a Research Article for PLOS
Computational Biology.

The manuscript studies a basic question in evolutionary dynamics on population
structures: can one fixed finite weighted network increase the fixation
probability of every beneficial mutation under death–birth updating? The paper
gives a negative answer. For complete directed support, it derives the first
strong-selection correction and expresses the deficit from the well-mixed
baseline as a sum of nonnegative squares over incoming weight columns. The
equality condition identifies exactly the weightings whose full type-state
process is equivalent to the complete-graph process. Together with the prior
transience theorem for strongly connected noncomplete supports and a separate
source-component bound for non-strong supports, this closes the fixed finite
directed case.

The paper also derives the exact incomplete-support limit for undirected
graphs, classifies every positive weighted triangle at all beneficial
fitnesses, and gives the corresponding all-beneficial-fitness classifications
for the natural symmetric 1+3 and 2+2 four-vertex families. It carefully distinguishes
the resolved fixed-graph quantifier from the open large-population order in
which the size threshold may depend on fitness.

I believe the manuscript fits the journal's readership because it connects
population structure, stochastic evolutionary dynamics, fixation theory, and
exact computation. The universal statements are mathematical proofs rather
than conclusions from sampled fitness values. Reproducibility materials include
literal transition builders, symbolic certificates, adversarial proof audits,
and a one-command build target. No empirical dataset, human participants, or
animal subjects are involved.

Generative-AI systems were used substantively in exploration, derivation,
adversarial proof analysis, software development, literature support, and
manuscript preparation. The named human author determined the released scope
and claims and accepts responsibility for the work. The manuscript includes
this disclosure.

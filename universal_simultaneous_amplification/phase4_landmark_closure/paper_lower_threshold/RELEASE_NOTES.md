# Preprint v1.0 release notes

This public research snapshot establishes the explicit lower bound

\[
R_{\mathrm{sim}}\ge 3/2.
\]

It contains the complete paper, deterministic PDF, source, and exact replay
package for the center--triangle construction. It also includes:

- the exact order-`N^-2` endpoint suppression certificate for that family;
- exact finite and growing refutations of the proposed normalized-product
  endpoint separator;
- an exact refutation of the balanced normalized arithmetic separator;
- proof that `1/3` is the maximal possible Bd coefficient in a universal
  convex affine separator, together with an exact necessary coefficient
  window;
- the one-third affine theorem for every positively weighted triangle; and
- scoped portal and arbitrary-weight clique--pendant obstruction theorems.

The universal endpoint disjunction, every universal finite upper bound, and
the exact value of `R_sim` remain open. This is a public preprint and exact
certificate release, not a peer-reviewed journal publication or journal
submission.

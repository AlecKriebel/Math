Date: 2026-08-08 (America/Los_Angeles)

This audit was performed only after the construction, asymptotic proof, and
exact algebraic certificate had been derived independently.  It was targeted
rather than broad and is not an exhaustive priority search.

The directly relevant primary source located was:

- J. Svoboda, S. S. Joshi, J. Tkadlec, and K. Chatterjee,
  [`Amplifiers of selection for the Moran process with both Birth-death and
  death-Birth updating`](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pcbi.1012008),
  `PLoS Computational Biology` 20 (2024), e1012008.

`1<r<1.2` and presents improvement of the range as future work.  Targeted
searches by title, threshold, and citations found no claimed interval beyond
`1.2`, no dilute `K_2`-satellite plus hub-leaf mechanism, and no occurrence
of the sextic threshold in this package.

Accordingly, the audit supports novelty of the proved bound
`R_sim>=1.5028569127905696...`, but it does not constitute a proof of
priority or replace peer review.

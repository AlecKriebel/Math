  entry class and a vanishing protected class.
- **PROVED:** for collision-free dB establishment, column stochasticity and
  strict concavity give uniform survival at most `1-1/r`, with equality only
  for an isothermal mixing law.
- Built the exact rare-colony chain for a fixed protected gadget overlaid on
  complete support, retaining all `2^k-1` nonempty local mutant states.

  quadratic coefficient
  `(r-1)/r^2 [Var(d)+2(r-1) sum_edges(a_e^2)/k]` for every nonzero gadget.
  This closes all bounded protected-state and bounded multiscale variants of
  the weak dense-overlay mechanism at leading order.
- **PROVED:** heterogeneous partner overlays also have a nonpositive Bd cubic
  coefficient; equality is only the weighted-regular equal-pair case.
- **EXACTLY VERIFIED:** independent symbolic reconstruction for gadget orders
  two, three, and four, plus the general partner Bd series.
- **NUMERICALLY OBSERVED:** broad orders-three/four colony searches and finite
  complete-plus-matching tests produced no simultaneous candidate.  These
  computations are not used in the proof.
- **OPEN:** growing/mesoscopic gadget order and finite-population perturbations

      under consideration before starting this fallback submission.
- [ ] Select the correct original-research article type in the live portal.
- [ ] Preserve the biological framing: fixation of an advantageous mutant,
      replacement order, and robustness of population structure.
- [ ] Confirm title, abstract, author, declarations, and manuscript file agree

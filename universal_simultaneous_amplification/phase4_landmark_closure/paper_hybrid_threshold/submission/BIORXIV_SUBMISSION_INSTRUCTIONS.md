## Portal walkthrough

The official help currently describes the workflow below. Follow the live
portal if its labels or order differ.

1. **Start a new bioRxiv submission.** Confirm that the server is bioRxiv.
2. **Manuscript Metadata.** Enter one author, choose **New Results**, choose
   **Evolutionary Biology**, and paste the plain-text title, running title (if
   requested), abstract, and keywords from `BIORXIV_METADATA.md`. If a separate
   Author Type field appears, choose **Regular Article** if that accurately
   describes the offered option.
3. **Author Information.** Enter Alec Kriebel as the sole corresponding author,
   check the email and ORCID, and complete all truthful affiliation and private
   address fields. Never copy the `[[POSTAL_ADDRESS]]` token into the portal.
4. **Funding and declarations.** Report no external funding and do not invent a
   funder or grant. Use the copy-ready competing-interests, ethics,
   contribution, availability, AI-assistance, and prior-online-material text
   wherever the live form requests it. Answer any additional live questions
   truthfully. If asked whether this or substantially similar material has
   appeared online, disclose both Zenodo records; do not answer **No** merely
   because neither record was on bioRxiv.
5. **File Upload Metadata.** State one manuscript file, zero separate image
   files, and one supplemental file if the portal requests counts.
6. **Supplemental File Upload.** Upload the frozen archive and use the suggested
   label above.
7. **Manuscript File Upload.** Upload the frozen 21-page PDF. Because the PDF
   already includes every figure and table, do not add separate image files.
8. **License and terms.** Read the current choices and select the author's
   license. CC BY 4.0 is the recommended open-reuse default, but this is a
   human decision. Before selecting it, confirm that the author owns or
   controls the necessary copyright and can grant the selected license,
   including compatibility with the prior Zenodo deposits. Read and accept the
   current terms only if they are accurate and acceptable.
9. **Submission Approval / Ready to Proof.** Uploading alone is not submission.
   Inspect every portal section and the displayed manuscript before approving.
10. **Confirmation.** Only the human author presses the final approval button.
    Retain the submission confirmation and manuscript identifier.

## Proof inspection before approval

Confirm all of the following in the portal's rendered record:

- the PDF has exactly 21 pages and opens without a password prompt;
- the title, sole author, affiliation, email, and ORCID are correct;
- the portal abstract has no raw LaTeX commands or mangled special characters;
- `R_sim`, `R_hyb`, `sigma`, `lambda`, inequalities, fractions, and the sextic
  display correctly in the manuscript PDF;
- the construction diagram, all theorem statements, page breaks, references,
  and hyperlinks are intact;
- no tracked changes, internal review text, or private placeholder is visible;
- the supplemental archive has the intended filename and label and can be
  downloaded from the proof interface if that interface permits it; and
- the prior-version disclosure is complete and does not misidentify an older
  Zenodo DOI as the identifier of this revision.

## What to expect after approval

bioRxiv screens submissions for metadata completeness, scope, article category,
prior online appearance, and biological relevance before posting. Its public
guidance says screening often takes about 24--48 hours, but delays are possible
and no exact posting time can be scheduled. The scope note in
`BIORXIV_METADATA.md` is available if a screener asks why a mathematical paper
belongs in Evolutionary Biology.

After submission and again after posting:

1. retain the confirmation email and exact manuscript identifier;
2. record the assigned bioRxiv DOI, URL, and posting date exactly as issued;
   do not assume a DOI prefix;
3. download and compare the posted manuscript and supplement with the frozen
   files where the service permits;
4. update `BIORXIV_METADATA.md` and `PROVENANCE_AND_RELATED_RELEASES.md` with the
   assigned record, without changing the already-posted artifacts; and
5. disclose the preprint accurately in the later *Journal of Mathematical
   Biology* submission. Springer Nature's public policy states that preprints
   are not prior publication.

If a revision is posted, upload the complete revised manuscript and its
corresponding supplement together. Do not leave a revised proof paired with a
stale verifier archive.

# PLOS Computational Biology submission checklist

> **DRAFT — NOT SUBMITTED.** Only the human author may enter personal data,
> make declarations, accept terms, select editors or reviewers, or submit.

The official PLOS Computational Biology submission guidelines were checked on
2026-08-08. They currently accept an initial LaTeX manuscript as a single PDF,
permit unrestricted manuscript length, limit the abstract to 300 words, ask
Research Article authors for a distinct first-person nontechnical Author
Summary of 150--200 words, and require a Data Availability Statement covering
both data and code. Recheck the live requirements immediately before use.

## Manuscript and article identity

- [ ] Human confirms PLOS Computational Biology as the journal.
- [ ] Human confirms Research Article as the article type.
- [ ] Full title: *Simultaneous amplification beyond fitness three halves*.
- [ ] Candidate short title: *Simultaneous amplification beyond three halves*.
- [x] Initial-submission PDF is complete, deterministic, and visually audited.
- [x] Abstract draft is below 300 words.
- [x] First-person Author Summary draft is 178 words under the package count.
- [ ] Insert or replace the manuscript's shorter Author Summary with the
      150--200-word submission version.
- [ ] Human verifies the final title, abstract, Author Summary, references, and
      scope statements against the uploaded PDF.

## Author and declaration fields — human only

- [ ] Legal author name, affiliation, country, and corresponding-author data.
- [ ] ORCID, if used.
- [ ] Final authorship and contribution roles.
- [ ] Competing-interests declaration.
- [ ] Funding and funder-role declaration.
- [ ] Exclusive-consideration and related-manuscript declaration.
- [ ] Preprint DOI entered as `10.5281/zenodo.21852072`.
- [ ] AI-assistance wording and placement approved against the live policy.
- [ ] Ethics questions answered by the human; the work uses no participants,
      animals, clinical data, or personal data.
- [ ] Copyright, open-access terms, fees, and any fee-assistance request read
      and accepted by the human.

## Data, code, and reproducibility

- [x] No empirical dataset exists.
- [x] Data and Code Availability draft names the GitHub release and Zenodo DOI.
- [x] Public PDF and scoped certificate archive are uploaded and hash-verified.
- [x] Clean archive was unpacked, dependencies bootstrapped, and exact replay
      completed successfully.
- [x] Exact labelled-chain, coefficient, and Sturm certificates replay.
- [x] No sampled numerical fixation value is used to prove an asymptotic claim.
- [ ] Human confirms the repository license and any journal-specific
      Supporting Information upload choice.

## Portal files and final checks

- [x] Initial manuscript PDF.
- [x] Editable LaTeX source and certificate archive.
- [x] Abstract, Author Summary, availability statement, AI disclosure, and
      cover-letter drafts.
- [ ] Human supplies all personal and funding declarations.
- [ ] Human checks whether the embedded TikZ diagram needs a separate figure
      upload at initial submission or only at revision.
- [ ] Human compares every portal field with the frozen PDF and DOI record.
- [ ] Human authorizes and performs submission.

No journal submission is complete until the human explicitly performs the
portal action and receives confirmation from the journal.

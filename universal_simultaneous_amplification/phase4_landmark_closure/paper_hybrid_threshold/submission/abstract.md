# Abstract

We study fixation from a uniformly random single mutant on finite connected
loopless undirected weighted graphs under both Birth--death and death--Birth
Moran updating. Let (R_{\mathrm{hyb}}) be the unique root in
((3/2,151/100)) of

\[
R^6-8R^5+22R^4-30R^3+21R^2-6R+1.
\]

We construct one fitness-independent graph family which, for every fixed
(1<r<R_{\mathrm{hyb}}), strictly amplifies fixation under both rules for all
sufficiently large populations. Numerically,
(R_{\mathrm{hyb}}=1.5028569127905696\ldots), and hence
(R_{\mathrm{sim}}\ge R_{\mathrm{hyb}}>3/2). The family combines a large
clique, dilute hub pendants, and dilute internally heavy two-vertex satellites
coupled to the clique through positive weak edges. We derive the exact finite
weak-cut trace and control establishment and the entire path to fixation.
Exact optimization of the two leading corrections yields the displayed
sextic. Independent labelled-event and symbolic verifiers audit the lumping,
trace coefficients, root isolation, and tangency. This disproves the proposed
exact threshold (3/2) and exactly refutes every graph-independent convex
affine endpoint separator. The unrestricted exact value of
(R_{\mathrm{sim}}) remains open.

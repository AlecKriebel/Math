The manuscript asks whether one population structure, chosen independently of
mutant fitness, can increase uniformly initialized fixation probability under

interval. It gives an explicit affirmative construction beyond fitness
three halves. The graph family combines a growing clique core, dilute hub
pendants, and dilute internally heavy two-vertex satellites. Exact trace
equations and quantitative post-establishment arguments prove simultaneous
amplification for every fixed fitness below

\[
R_{\mathrm{hyb}}=1.5028569127905696\ldots>3/2,
\]

disproves the proposed (3/2) phase boundary. It also supplies an exact finite
counterexample showing that no graph-independent convex affine endpoint
separator can prove a universal obstruction. The paper clearly states that
the unrestricted exact threshold and every matching universal upper bound
remain open.

aggregation, exact rational and symbolic certificates, Sturm root isolation,
two asymptotic proof paths, a hostile proof audit, and a clean-extraction replay
bootstrap. No empirical dataset, human participants, animal subjects, or
personal data are involved.

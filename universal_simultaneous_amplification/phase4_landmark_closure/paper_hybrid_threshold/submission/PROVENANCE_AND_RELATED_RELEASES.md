# Provenance and related public source/software archives

This note is the source of truth for disclosures about public artifacts in
the simultaneous-amplification workstream.  It distinguishes public
availability from peer-reviewed publication and keeps Papers I and II
separate.

## Direct predecessor of the present manuscript

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21852072> archives the GitHub release
`simultaneous-amplification-beyond-three-halves-v1.0.0`.  It is a public
full-repository **source/software archive** containing the unrefereed v1
manuscript, source, and exact verification materials; the accompanying tag and
release notes call that manuscript “preprint v1.”  It is not a peer-reviewed
journal article and was not posted through bioRxiv.

The present Paper II is a major superseding revision of that manuscript.  It
retains the same central lower-bound theorem while consolidating the finite
weak-cut Schur argument, gain-scale establishment and cleanup estimates,
reciprocal-invasion and sweep bounds, architecture-optimality boundary,
rational specialization, literature context, declarations, and deterministic
standalone package.  The old DOI must be disclosed as a prior public version,
not reused as the persistent identifier for the revised package.

The public project page
<https://aleckriebel.github.io/Math/papers/simultaneous-amplification-beyond-three-halves/>
may also display the earlier version until the human author updates it.  Its
existence should be disclosed wherever a submission system asks about prior
online dissemination.

## Earlier Paper II construction

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21850042> is the superseded
**source/software archive** for *Simultaneous amplification below fitness
three halves*.  Its center--triangle family proved the earlier lower bound
`R_sim>=3/2`.  It is an unrefereed repository snapshot, not a peer-reviewed
publication or bioRxiv posting, and is not the identifier for the present
revision.

## Distinct companion Paper I

DOI <https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.21753405> is a **source/software archive**
for the historical fixed-graph death--Birth obstruction.  The current Paper I
is the separate manuscript *Complete-Graph Extremality under Death--Birth
Updating: Fitness-Two Local Optimality and Strong-Selection Rigidity*.

Paper I concerns fixed finite dB rigidity and complete-kernel extremality.
Paper II concerns one growing graph family that amplifies under both update
rules.  If the papers are simultaneously public or under editorial review,
the human author should disclose the companion manuscript wherever a portal
asks for closely related work.  This note does not authorize external contact.

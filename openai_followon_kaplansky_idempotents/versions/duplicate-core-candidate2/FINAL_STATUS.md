## Strongest checked theorem

The exact OpenAI source constructs a finitely presented torsion-free group G
with a finite two-dimensional classifying complex and a,b,c in F_2[G] satisfying
ab=1, ac=0 and c≠0. For R=F_2[G], e=1-ba is a scalar idempotent different
from 0 and 1. P=eR is a nonzero cyclic projective right module, with
R_R≅R_R⊕P and [P]=0 in K_0(R). The same group and scalar e work over every
characteristic-two extension field. The proof uses explicit left-multiplication
right-module maps and coefficient-by-coefficient injectivity.

The substantial group construction belongs to OpenAI; the algebra and
K_0 splitting are classical. This effort adds a complete consequence proof,
detailed independent source audits, accurate scope/provenance and a tested
research packet. It establishes no new mathematical discovery beyond the
already disclosed consequence.

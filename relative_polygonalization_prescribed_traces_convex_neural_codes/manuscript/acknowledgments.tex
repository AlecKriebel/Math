responsibility for every mathematical claim, citation, and conclusion.  The

\paragraph{Code availability.}
A deterministic no-network verification package accompanies the manuscript.
It checks the finite rational examples, prescribed traces, protected
witnesses, shared-edge data, tetrahedral edge consistency, manifests, and
fresh compilation.  The computation is illustrative and does not replace the
general proof.

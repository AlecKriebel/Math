The v4 full text was read locally. The final-version download returned HTTP 403, so theorem numbering below refers to v4. The published abstract confirms that the conjecture remains conjectural and that all PSL(2,q) and SL(2,q) cases are proved. The older catalogue triage mentioning only symmetric groups materially understates prior progress. Already known results include virtual projectivity; root-count/indicator formulas; normal Sylow and normal p-complement cases; normal-p-subgroup lifting; cyclic Sylow p-subgroups; defining-characteristic BN-pair cases; and the stated rank-one families. Reconstructions of these facts are credited, not presented as new solutions.

C. A. Schroeder, *Refining invariants of finite groups with class functions*, https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.26464v1 (22 September 2026), is a relevant recent primary source. Its function Pi_{p',G} has value |C_G(g)| on p-regular g; that differs from Psi_{p,G}, which counts only p-elements in the centralizer. Positivity of Pi does not itself establish positivity of Psi. The paper explicitly distinguishes the two constructions.

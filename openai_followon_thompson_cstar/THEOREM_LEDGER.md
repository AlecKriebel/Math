# Exact targets and success criteria

## Core conjectural target

For each discrete group G in {F,T,Aut(F),Comm(F)}, the reduced group C*-algebra C*_r(G), the norm closure of the left regular representation on l²(G), is simple and has exactly one tracial state, namely τ(a)=⟨aδ_e,δ_e⟩.

F denotes increasing finite dyadic piecewise-linear homeomorphisms of [0,1] with slopes powers of 2. T is the standard circle counterpart preserving dyadic points. Aut(F) means the full abstract automorphism group; Comm(F) consists of equivalence classes of isomorphisms between finite-index subgroups, equivalent when agreeing on a further finite-index subgroup. Countability and precise source action models must be checked explicitly.

## Strongest initially justified result

Conditional on nonamenability of standard F, the four simplicity statements follow from Le Boudec–Matte Bon, Corollaries 4.2 and 4.4. Primary statements, hypotheses, countability, and the unique-trace implication remain under fresh independent audit. The announced family 248 theorem is a dependency to verify, not an assumption of truth.

## Publication success

Require a valid full proof chain, no known material upstream gap, accurate inherited-vs-new attribution and fresh priority search, a concise compiled and reproducible note with exact reviewed hashes, at least two independent complete-package reviews with a clean fresh final review, a verified production Zenodo record and assigned DOI, and one verified row in the specified tracker. Withhold duplicate novelty publication if the entire package is already public. A draft DOI is not publication.

## Boundary attacks

Full group C*-algebras are excluded. Group simplicity is a different property. V and nV have prior C*-simplicity results. Arbitrary embeddings of an abstract copy of F do not satisfy the prescribed action-level hypotheses. Any optional extension must match the precise circle/line embedding in the primary source.

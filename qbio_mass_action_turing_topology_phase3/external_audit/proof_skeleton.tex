\section*{1. Mass-action and conservation}
At a positive equilibrium, define $v_r=k_r(x^*)^{y_r}$ and
$h_i=(x_i^*)^{-1}$.  Direct differentiation gives
\[
 J=\Gamma\diag(v)Y^T\diag(h),\qquad v>0,\quad\Gamma v=0,
\]
and every such pair is realized by $x_i^*=h_i^{-1}$ and
$k_r=v_r/(x^*)^{y_r}$.  No deficiency or weak-reversibility assumption is
used.

Put $S=\im\Gamma$.  Since $J(\R^n)\subseteq S$, if $J|_S$ is Hurwitz then
$J(S)=S$, $S\cap\ker J=0$, and rank--nullity gives
$\R^n=S\oplus\ker J$.  Thus conservation zeros are semisimple and
\[
 \det(\lambda I-J)=\lambda^{n-\dim S}
 \det(\lambda I_S-J|_S)>0\quad(\lambda>0).
\]
A nonconstant Neumann eigenfunction has zero mean.  Hence every species
amplitude preserves integrated conservation laws; unequal diffusion generally
does not preserve $S$, so the nonzero-mode matrix is tested on $\R^n$.

Choose a rational basis $B$ of $S$ and left inverse $C$.  The reduced matrix
$R=CJB$ represents $J|_S$.  Replacing $B$ by $BT$ conjugates $R$ by $T$, so
Routh--Hurwitz inequalities are coordinate-independent.

\section*{2. Fixed-J theorem and the network iff}
For $D=\diag(d)$, multilinearity gives
\[
 \det(D-J)=\sum_{I\subseteq[n]}(-1)^{|I|}\det J[I,I]
 \prod_{j\notin I}d_j.
\]
If every signed principal minor is nonnegative, replacing $d_j$ by
$d_j+\lambda$ makes this determinant strictly positive for every $\lambda>0$;
no positive real eigenvalue of $J-D$ is possible.

If one coefficient indexed by $I$ is negative, set $d_j=t$ off $I$ and
$d_j=t^{-1}$ on $I$.  Its exponent exceeds the exponent of the $K$-term by
$|I\triangle K|$, so it uniquely dominates for large finite $t$.  Then
$\det(D-J)<0$; the monic characteristic polynomial of $J-D$ is negative at
zero and positive at large positive argument, yielding a positive real root.
For Hurwitz $J$, the empty and full coefficients are positive.

Now write $A(v)=\Gamma\diag(v)Y^T$.  Principal column scaling gives
\[
 (-1)^{|I|}\det(A(v)\diag(h))[I,I]
 =\Bigl(\prod_{i\in I}h_i\Bigr)(-1)^{|I|}\det A(v)[I,I].
\]
Combining the parametrization, relative Hurwitz test, and fixed-J theorem gives
the exact network iff.

For existential-real membership, Boolean selectors satisfy
\[
 \det(I-S_b-S_bA(v)S_b)=(-1)^{|I|}\det A(v)[I,I].
\]
Rational bases have polynomial encoding length, and determinant,
characteristic-coefficient, and Hurwitz circuits have polynomial size.

\section*{3. Open-cube family and row splitting}
For a `PARTITION` input, pad to $a\in\mathbb Z^m$ with $m=k^2$, and set
\[
 Q=I+aa^T,
 \quad\beta=1-\frac1{2m(1+a^Ta)},
 \quad B(x,y)=\begin{pmatrix}-kQ&y\\x^T&k\beta\end{pmatrix}.
\]
A partition sign vector $t$ gives $x=rt$, $y=-rt$ with
$r=(1+\beta)/2$.  Orthogonal decomposition leaves one $2\times2$ block whose
trace is negative and determinant $k^2(r^2-\beta)>0$.

Conversely, interpolate from $(0,0)$, which has exactly one positive
eigenvalue, to a Hurwitz endpoint.  An interior singular point satisfies
\[
 \theta^2x^TQ^{-1}(-y)=m\beta.
\]
Since $\theta<1$, the cube maximum exceeds $m\beta$.  Positive definiteness and
Sherman--Morrison give
\[
 \max_{x,z\in[-1,1]^m}x^TQ^{-1}z
 =m-\frac{\min_{t\in\{\pm1\}^m}(a^Tt)^2}{1+a^Ta}.
\]
The strict half-unit gap forces the integer $a^Tt$ to be zero.

Write $B(q)=B_0+UX(q)$ and define
\[
 L(q)=\begin{pmatrix}B_0&U\\X(q)(B_0+I)&X(q)U-I\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Direct multiplication gives
\[
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\-X&I\end{pmatrix}L(q)
 \begin{pmatrix}I&0\\X&I\end{pmatrix}
 =\begin{pmatrix}B(q)&U\\0&-I\end{pmatrix}.
\]
Every varying row of $L$ contains exactly one scalar parameter.

\section*{4. Scaling elimination and mass-action realization}
Suppose $L(q)D$ is Hurwitz for some arbitrary positive diagonal $D$.  At
$q=0$, block triangularity shows that $L(0)D$ has exactly one positive
eigenvalue: the $-kQD_t$ block is similar to a negative definite matrix, one
scalar block is positive, and the added block is negative.  The signed
determinant therefore changes sign along $L(\theta q)D$.  At an interior
singularity, nonsingularity of $D$ and
$\det L(q)=\det B(q)$ transfer singularity to $B(\theta q)$.  The strict
open-cube argument then forces a partition.  This eliminates arbitrary
relative right scaling only for the constructed family.

For a variable row $a+qb$, clear denominators by $c$ and choose an integer base
complex $y^0$ so that $y^\pm=y^0+c(a\pm b)$ are nonnegative.  Use reactions
\[
 y^+\to y^++e_i,
 \quad y^-\to y^-+e_i,
 \quad y^0\to y^0-e_i.
\]
Every positive steady flux is $(u,w,u+w)$ and contributes
\[
 c((u+w)a+(u-w)b)=\rho(a+qb),
 \quad q=\frac{u-w}{u+w}\in(-1,1).
\]
Every $\rho>0,q\in(-1,1)$ is attained.  Fixed rows use a two-reaction pair.
All flux constraints separate by row, proving equality of the complete image.

Thus every homogeneous Jacobian is $RL(q)H$, similar to $L(q)HR$.  A fixed
positive diagonal entry creates a negative signed singleton minor whenever
the Jacobian is Hurwitz.  The network is admissible exactly for YES partition
instances.  Counts are $3m+1$ species and $8m+2$ reactions.  Molecularity is
unbounded.

\section*{5. Fixed n, certificates, and audit boundary}
Let $K=\{v\geq0:\Gamma v=0\}$ and $P=\Phi(K)$ with
$\Phi(v)=\Gamma\diag(v)Y^T$.  A strict positive flux implies
$\operatorname{ri}K=K\cap\R_{>0}^m$; linear maps preserve relative interiors,
so positive factors are exactly $\operatorname{ri}P$.

Every extreme ray of $K$ is a support-minimal positive dependency and has
support at most $\operatorname{rank}\Gamma+1\leq n+1$.  For fixed $n$, enumerate
all bounded supports, compute exact minor generators, map them to $P$, and in
fixed dimension compute span, lineality, and relative facets.  The remaining
formula has $n^2+n$ real variables (plus one for designated mobility), fixed
degree, and polynomial coefficient data.  Fixed-variable real-algebraic
decision is polynomial in bit complexity.

For certificates, equationize strict signs by $gz^2-1=0$ or
$(-g)z^2-1=0$.  A YES branch has a real-algebraic sample point.  An infeasible
branch has a Real Nullstellensatz identity
\[
 -1=\sum_j s_j^2+\sum_kq_kF_k.
\]
These objects are finite and exactly checkable; no polynomial size or practical
general generator is claimed.

The audit attacked the three critical steps independently: arbitrary right
scaling, equality of the entire reaction row image, and fixed-dimensional bit
complexity.  It found no central defect.  The result remains limited to weak
stationary linear instability, weak NP-hardness, and unbounded-molecularity
hardness; external priority and peer review remain necessary.

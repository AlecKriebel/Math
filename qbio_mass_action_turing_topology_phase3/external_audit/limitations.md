# Limitations

1. The result is weak stationary **linear** instability: a positive real eigenvalue of one nonzero mode.
2. It does not prove the stationary crossing is first, simple, or transverse.
3. It does not exclude an earlier Turing-Hopf/wave instability.
4. It does not prove a nonlinear nonconstant equilibrium or persistent pattern.
5. It does not give a finite forbidden-motif or Turing-core characterization.
6. The NP-hardness is weak, from `PARTITION`.
7. The reduction uses complexes of unbounded molecularity.
8. Existential-real membership is proved; hardness/completeness is not.
9. Exact certificates exist, but no polynomial size or practical general generator is proved.
10. The bundled tools do not constitute a complete general quantifier-elimination solver.
11. Priority has not yet been confirmed by external specialists or peer review.

# Priority comparison in one page

- **Anma-Sakamoto-Yoneda and fixed-J matrix theory:** already provide the closest diffusion-destabilization ideas for a selected Jacobian. The present contribution is not claimed to be the fixed-J observation; it is the elimination over all positive classical-mass-action realizations of an indexed network.
- **Mincheva-Craciun:** graph-theoretic conditions for zero-eigenvalue Turing instability in mass-action networks, described as necessary rather than an arbitrary-network iff decision and complexity theorem.
- **Diego-Marcon-Muller-Sharpe and network atlases:** interaction-topology results and computational surveys for general Turing systems, not the exact indexed `(Gamma,Y)` classical-mass-action decision problem.
- **Vassena-Stadler / child selections:** structural sufficient instability cores for parameter-rich kinetics. Classical mass action has coupled derivatives through positive flux and equilibrium scaling.
- **Piskovsky:** complete stationary/wave inequalities for a fixed three-species Jacobian, not existential realization over arbitrary reaction lists.
- **Conradi-Mincheva-Uecker (2026):** sufficient spatial-instability conditions for networks admitting monomial steady-state parametrizations, not arbitrary-network iff or complexity.
- **Blondel-Tsitsiklis:** source NP-hard interval-stability theorem. The row-splitting scaling elimination and mass-action realization are additional steps.

No exact match to the combined theorem was found in the dated audit. This is a priority assessment pending specialist confirmation, not a guarantee.

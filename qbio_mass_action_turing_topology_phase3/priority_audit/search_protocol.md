# Current-priority search protocol

**Search date:** 2026-08-13 (America/Los_Angeles)

## Target theorem fingerprint

The search was keyed to the conjunction of:

1. finite **classical mass-action** reaction networks given by indexed `(Gamma,Y)`;
2. existential choice of positive rates, positive equilibrium, and positive diagonal diffusion;
3. homogeneous stability relative to the stoichiometric subspace;
4. a nonzero mode with a **positive real** eigenvalue (weak stationary scope);
5. an exact semialgebraic iff formula;
6. NP-hardness or existential-real complexity;
7. polynomial-time decision for fixed species count;
8. finite exact two-sided certificates.

A paper matching only fixed-J criteria, generalized/parameter-rich kinetics, a sufficient motif, one network family, or nonlinear pattern bifurcation was not treated as anticipating the combined result.

## Services and queries

Searched arXiv, publisher pages, Crossref-indexed title/DOI records, Semantic Scholar, general web searches designed to surface Google Scholar records and citation lists, and accessible cited/citing trails. Direct complete-record access to MathSciNet and zbMATH was not available in this environment; their titles and references were sought through AMS/zbMATH-indexed web results and publisher citation trails.

Core query families included:

- `mass-action Turing admissibility complexity`
- `reaction-network diffusion instability NP-hard`
- `reaction-hypergraph Turing criterion`
- `semialgebraic Turing instability reaction network`
- `interval Hurwitz stability chemical reaction network`
- `positive diagonal stabilizability mass action`
- `unstable subsystem Turing mass action`
- `child selection spatial instability`
- `reaction network realization arbitrary Jacobian`
- `fixed-species reaction network decision algorithm`
- exact-phrase combinations of `stationary diffusion-driven instability`, `mass-action`, `NP-hard`, `existential theory of the reals`, and `fixed species`.

## Citation-chaining procedure

For the closest sources, their references and later citing sources were searched for:

- fixed-J unstable-subsystem and additive-D-stability theorems;
- topology/interaction-graph studies;
- zero-eigenvalue graph criteria for mass-action systems;
- unstable cores and child selections;
- monomial steady-state spatial-instability conditions;
- three-species stationary/wave criteria;
- interval-matrix stability hardness;
- positive diagonal stabilizability reviews;
- computational complexity of other chemical-reaction-network problems.

## Stopping rule and limitation

Searches were stopped after repeated exact-fingerprint queries returned only the same neighboring families and no result combining the eight features above. This is a documented good-faith priority audit, not a proof that an inaccessible, unpublished, differently indexed, or differently worded theorem does not exist. The prepared expert inquiries are therefore part of the external-audit plan.

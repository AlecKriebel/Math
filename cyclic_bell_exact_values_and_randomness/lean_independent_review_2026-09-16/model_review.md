## Statement correspondence and concrete evidence

All Lean paths below are relative to `package/lean_formalization`.

1. **Finite tensor domain is physical and unrestricted in finite dimension.** `CyclicBell/GeneralModel.lean:14` requires a positive semidefinite density with trace one. `:19` requires an actual PVM, allowing zero projections, and `:40` places Alice and Bob on separate finite coordinate factors. `CyclicBell/GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:18` existentially ranges over all finite carriers and all such strategies. There is no full-rank, simple-spectrum, dimension-d, Bell saturation, or witness-specific field. This matches `reference/manuscript/main.tex:215` and `:233`. Order-d unitarity and Fourier projector reconstruction are proved in `GeneralModel.lean`, rather than inserted into probability definitions.

2. **Commuting domain is not secretly finite dimensional.** `CyclicBell/GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:32` existentially ranges over a complete complex Hilbert space. `CyclicBell/GeneralCommutingModel.lean:15` defines a normalized vector, Alice/Bob PVMs, and all cross-party projection commutators. Behavior is the actual vector expectation of a projection product (`:62`). Nonnegativity, normalization, and the complex-correlator bridge are proved, so taking real parts cannot conceal a nonphysical complex behavior. This is the manuscript's projective commuting model (`main.tex:235`). The use of Lean's `Type` universe is a size convention, not a finite-dimensional restriction.

3. **Finite-to-commuting embedding is constructed, not assumed.** `CyclicBell/GeneralHilbertBridge.lean:101` defines purification by vectorizing the positive square-root factor. The file proves its norm and moments, realizes matrices on the standard Euclidean Hilbert space, and constructs the PVM placements in `finiteToCommuting` (`:173`). `finiteToCommuting_behavior` (`:192`) proves equality of the entire behavior. This is used to prove `Qq_subset_Qqc` (`GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:58`); the embedding is not a field in either model or an added hypothesis in the final value theorems.

4. **Approximate domain is the actual product-topology closure.** `Qqa` is literally `closure (Qq ...)` (`GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:28`). `CyclicBell/GeneralCoverageClosureContainment.lean:13` constructs a commuting realization for every convergent sequence of commuting behaviors, and `:32` derives closedness for finite input alphabets. `:37` proves Qqa ⊆ Qqc. The finite-input condition exactly covers the paper's scenarios; the package does not assert sequential closedness is sufficient for arbitrary non-first-countable input products.

5. **Closure realization has no hidden realization premise.** I read the complete chain `GeneralCoverageUltralimit.lean`, `GeneralCoverageGNS.lean`, `GeneralCoverageMomentModel.lean`, `GeneralCoverageCompletionOperators.lean`, and `GeneralCoverageReconstruction.lean`. Its mechanism is:
   - actual projection words have vector norm at most one and hence uniformly bounded scalar kernels;
   - the ultrafilter is a genuine extension of `atTop`, with complex-ball compactness proving convergence;
   - Hermitian positive finite Gram forms survive the limit;
   - finite formal combinations receive the resulting seminorm, and the genuine uniform completion both separates null vectors and completes the space;
   - prepending each projection symbol is contractive, so extends to a continuous operator;
   - selfadjointness, orthogonality, completeness, idempotence, and cross-party commutation are proved on the dense word span and transferred to the completed space;
   - the empty-word vector has norm one and the length-two moments equal the limiting behavior.
   In particular, `PositiveKernel.positive` is discharged from source vectors (`GeneralCoverageMomentModel.lean:226`); it is not an assumption of closedness. `reconstructedModel` (`GeneralCoverageReconstruction.lean:111`) fills each physical field with those proofs. Degenerate Gram kernels are permitted, which is necessary here.

6. **Bell values are real suprema, not definitions of the desired answer.** `CyclicBell/GeneralBehavior.lean:112` uses `sSup (f '' S)`. `:114` proves both nonemptiness and boundedness before using conditional completeness of ℝ. `three_model_suprema` (`:126`) extends a genuine universal upper bound to closure by continuity and uses an actual finite attaining point. `GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:169`, `:180`, `:193`, and `:203` instantiate the four paper endpoints. Reduced strategies actually drop Bob's additional setting (`:120`), avoiding a hidden augmented-model restriction. These value proofs do not depend on or assume Qqa ⊆ Qqc: continuity suffices for the qa bound, while the separate closure theorem proves the contextual inclusion independently.

7. **Adversarial domains match the stated order of operations.** `CyclicBell/GeneralTripartite.lean:16` defines arbitrary positive, complete d²-outcome Eve POVMs, with no idempotence restriction. This matches `main.tex:279` and avoids both closing only saturated finite strategies and allowing arbitrary extensions of a qa marginal.

## Follow-up falsification: second-family correlator coverage

The coordinator requested an additional independent check of whether the second-family theorem's entire first-harmonic matrix and Bob-adjoint convention are explicitly formalized. I searched the delivered source independently, including all uses of `secondAlice`, `secondWeight`, and `secondPermutationStrategy`, rather than assuming a missing endpoint from theorem names alone.

**Finding: the package contains the ingredients for a short proof, but I did not find an assembled physical theorem for these clauses of Theorem `thm:second`.** The manuscript explicitly claims permutation independence of the entire d-by-(d+1) complex first-harmonic matrix and the outcome-inversion interpretation of Bob adjoints (`reference/manuscript/main.tex:1012` onward). Its proof additionally gives the entries λ_l ω^(-ly), the additional-column entries δ_(l0), and all vanishing local first moments (`main.tex:1070` onward).

- `CyclicBell/GeneralCoveragePermutation.lean:160`, `linear_permutation_all_harmonics_invariant`, quantifies Alice input `x : Fin 2`. `CyclicBell/GeneralCoverageWitness.lean:86`, `conditional_permutation_complete_harmonics`, likewise covers two Alice settings in the separate conditional affine theorem. Neither is an endpoint for the second family's d Alice settings.
- `CyclicBell/GeneralSecondWitness.lean:96` onward proves the second-family Fourier compression, Bell terms, attainment, target-table identification, and nonuniformity. `secondPermutation_term` at line 136 concerns the **real weighted expectation with the Fourier-compressed Bob observable**, not each individual complex correlator. `GeneralCorrelationValues.lean:137` covers only the real aligned `(l=0, Bob=none)` entry. The declared `second_all_dimension_counterexample` at line 196 does not state full correlator invariance or the adjoint convention.
- However, this is an **assembly/coverage gap, not an unsupported invariance argument**. The fully general `first_harmonic_permutation` at `CyclicBell/GeneralCycles.lean:113` works for arbitrary complex weight functions, with no `PermutationData` assumptions. For each l, instantiate its second conclusion with `z j = star (secondWeight l j)` and `s = polarPhase y`, then rewrite the already-proved physical encodings (`GeneralSecondWitness.lean:71`, `GeneralFirstWitness.lean:44`, and `weighted_entry_conjugate`). Its right side has no permutation. `added_first_harmonics` at `GeneralCycles.lean:129` gives the same fact for Bob's added setting, for every l. `weighted_trace_zero` at line 76 together with `phi_local_left/right` gives all local first moments. Thus all-row invariance follows by a short formal composition; the existing delivered statements stop just before making that composition explicit.
- The manuscript's explicit reduced entry formula can additionally use `polarTransform_shift` (`GeneralSecondWitness.lean:84`) and `polarTransform_compression` (`GeneralSecondCoefficients.lean:197`), plus the unit prefactor identity. Its additional-column δ_(l0) formula uses the standard character sum after expanding `secondWeight`. These ingredients are present, but I did not find their specialization into a statement about the full `secondPermutationStrategy` behavior.
- For the alternate convention, `CyclicBell/GeneralPhaseTables.lean:23` defines `negateMeasurement M` by effects `M.effect (-b)`, so the physical relabeling operation exists. I did not find a theorem proving `encoded (negateMeasurement M) = (encoded M).conjTranspose`, nor a second-family theorem relating Bob-adjoint target probabilities to `p(a,-b)`. `measurement_spectral_star`, `chi_star`, and `sum_negate` should make this a short general lemma, but that formal bridge is not delivered. The D4 regression named `bob_adjoint_is_wrong` tests an uncorrected adjoint convention; it does not supply the missing outcome-relabeling bridge.

Consequently, I would not claim literal exhaustive formalization of every clause of `thm:second` from this package alone. The appropriate repair is to add explicit all-row complex correlator/local-moment and Bob-inversion endpoint theorems, with their manuscript mapping and axiom audit entries. This follow-up does not change the positive verdict on the physical-model/closure track above, nor reveal a false mathematical result.

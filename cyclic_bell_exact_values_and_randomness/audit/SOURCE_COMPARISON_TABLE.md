## Normalization and conditioning boundary

The formula printed beside Conjecture 2 of arXiv:2606.21362v3 contains an
extra factor \(d\) in the denominator. The source's operator definition,
reduced value, aligned term, and reported qutrit augmented value instead give
\(M_d+1\). The canonical manuscript uses that operator normalization and
states the logical consequence exactly:
\[
 \langle\overline{\mathcal I}_d\rangle=M_d+1
 \not\Longrightarrow
 G(AB\mid1,d,E)=\frac1{d^2}
 \qquad(d\ge4).
\]
This is a scalar-value counterexample. It is not a claim that the complete
canonical behavior fails to certify maximal randomness.

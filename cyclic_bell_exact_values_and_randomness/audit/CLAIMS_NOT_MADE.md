# Claims not made

**Audit date:** 9 August 2026

This checklist fixes the negative scope of the canonical manuscript. It
should be read together with the claims ledger and theorem crosswalk.

## Attribution and priority

- We do not claim to have introduced either cyclic Bell family, its
  normalization, or its canonical strategies.
- We do not claim the source's general bound
  \(\beta_q(\mathcal I_d)\le d\sqrt2\), its NPA evidence through \(d=6\),
  or the second-family value and SOS.
- We do not claim stand-alone novelty for polar decomposition, functional
  calculus, the scalar half-angle extremum, or the cosecant-square identity.
- We do not claim absolute or definitive priority. All novelty statements
  remain qualified by “to our knowledge.”
- The support-rigidity theorem and private-MUB lemma are restored from the
  preserved standalone manuscripts; they are not presented as new lead
  results of this revision.
- The binary \(3\sqrt3\) benchmark is not claimed as new. Up to an output
  flip, it is prior work of Wooltorton--Brown--Colbeck.

## Exact values and equality structure

- We do not classify the complete maximizing face of either family.
- We do not claim that every maximizer is a weighted shift or arises from the
  displayed phase-permutation orbit.
- Equal root multiplicities on \(K=\operatorname{supp}\rho_A\) do not imply
  a Weyl representation, uniqueness, or self-testing.
- The support-rigidity theorem is not asserted for approximate maximizers,
  \(qa\), \(qc\), infinite-dimensional support, or \(K^\perp\).
- The hostile finite-dimensional tests are regression evidence, not a proof
  of the all-dimensional statements.

## Randomness and Conjecture 2

- We do not say broadly that the originating authors' randomness analysis is
  false.
- We refute only the normalized scalar implication of Conjecture 2 for the
  displayed first augmented operator and every \(d\ge4\): attaining
  \(M_d+1\) does not force \(G(AB\mid1,d,E)=1/d^2\).
- We do not challenge a calculation conditioned on the complete canonical
  behavior, or claim that the canonical realization lacks maximal global
  randomness.
- We do not determine the exact worst-case guessing probability or entropy at
  the Bell maximum. The explicit strategy gives a lower bound on guessing
  probability, not a proof of global worst-case optimality.
- We do not settle the analogous permutation question for \(d=2\) or
  \(d=3\).
- Uniform observed probabilities are not identified with privacy against a
  purifying adversary; the manuscript keeps those notions separate.

## Behavior, rigidity, and models

- Behavior-level nonuniqueness modulo local output relabelings is not a full
  classification of strategy equivalence under isometries, ancillas, direct
  sums, or transposition.
- The commuting-operator exact-value theorem does not automatically extend
  every finite-dimensional rigidity or privacy statement to \(qa\) or
  \(qc\).
- No robustness or self-testing theorem for a modified cyclic family is
  claimed.

## Setting complexity

- The one-input result is a standard device-independent locality baseline,
  not a claim about trusted one-input devices.
- The private-MUB lemma is sufficient only. It proves neither necessity nor
  the existence of a Bell functional that enforces its hypotheses.
- The computational-MUB proposition excludes only the displayed
  operator-span, coefficientwise-bounded exposure route.
- We do not prove a general \((2,3,d,d)\) impossibility, a universal
  minimum-setting law, or failure of every third-setting repair.

## Review and publication status

- Passing internal adversarial checks and verification scripts is not
  external peer review.
- No source author has endorsed, reviewed, or collaborated on the manuscript.
- No journal submission, arXiv submission, DOI registration, or external
  review is implied by the repository materials.

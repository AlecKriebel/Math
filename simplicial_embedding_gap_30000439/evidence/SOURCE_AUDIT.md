## Exact original question

[The requested problem page](https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/30000439) was attempted first and was inaccessible to the web reader. The full pinned dataset record is preserved in `source_record.json`. The original [Brehm contribution](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46044), pp. 701–702 of *Oberwolfach Report* 12/2006, asks whether the two minimum dimensions can differ by more than one. It explicitly highlights complexes of dimensions two and three. It does not require the complex to be a manifold.

The earlier [Brehm–Sarkaria preprint](https://archive.mpim-bonn.mpg.de/id/eprint/1946/1/preprint_1992_52.pdf) constructs gaps between the two embedding notions in a fixed ambient dimension and uses linking-number constraints. That does not by itself settle a dimension gap of two.

## Primary results actually used

The [journal record](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.disc.2026.115331) was already accessible on the check date, with issue date December 2026. The proof here uses the accessible current preprint, not a prediction about the future issue.
- Seunghun Lee and Eran Nevo, [*On colorings of hypergraphs embeddable in R^d*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2307.14195), current version dated 9 March 2026, Lemma 3.1: linear (d+1)-uniform hypergraphs are PL embeddable in R^d for d at least three. The [published version](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00454-026-00856-4) states the same lemma. Its hypergraph convention is exactly the simplicial complex generated by the hyperedges, as checked in the introduction and Section 3.
- Alan Frieze and Michał Karoński, [author-hosted *Introduction to Random Graphs*](https://www.math.cmu.edu/~af1p/BOOK.pdf), Section 34.6, Theorem 34.13, equation (34.36): Janson's lower-tail inequality, including the diagonal contribution in the overlap denominator.

Newman's current concluding remarks explicitly retract an earlier odd-dimensional threshold claim. The present construction uses only dimension four, so it does not import the withdrawn statement.

## Other relevant literature checked

Frick–Hu–Scheel–Simon, [*Embedding dimensions of simplicial complexes on few vertices*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.04855), proves equivalence of continuous and linear embeddability in a small-vertex regime. Our construction is far outside that regime. The known nonrealizable Möbius strips and surface-realization literature do not supply the two-dimensional gap asserted here.

## Novelty boundary

The candidate's mechanism combines an existing uniform random nonembedding argument with a small-deletion cleaning step and an existing PL inflation result. The cleaning must be uniform over both geometry and deletion sets; this is proved in the candidate rather than inferred from the original random threshold theorem.

Bounded searches for the exact gap question, linear-hypergraph embedding dimension, and the combination of Newman with Lee–Nevo did not locate an earlier statement of this construction. That is not proof of priority. No novel-discovery claim or upstream status change should be made without separate review of both correctness and prior art.

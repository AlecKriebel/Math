# Priority and scope audit

Audit cutoff: 22 July 2026. This is a provisional research audit, not a proof
of worldwide priority.

## What is not claimed

- The formula \(3+(4-2)+(6-2)+(7-2)=14\) is part of the classical degree
  bookkeeping behind Kulikov's reduction. The abstract possibility of a
  14-dimensional construction is not new.
- No global minimal dimension is claimed for a noninjective polynomial map
  with unipotent Jacobian.
- No global minimal monomial count is claimed.
- The classical dynamical Markus--Yamabe conjecture already has older
  counterexamples; the present statement concerns the algebraic conjectures
  identified in the note.

## Narrow claims supported by this artifact

- An explicit rational 14-variable map with 24 terms, determinant pencil one,
  generically regular-nilpotent nonlinear Jacobian, and a displayed rational
  three-point fiber.
- An explicit \(\operatorname{SIC}(14)\) witness whose multiplier obstruction
  is nonzero for every positive exponent.
- Optimality of 11 states and 24 terms inside the precisely defined
  constant-state realization ansatz in NOTE Section 8.

At the cutoff, the audit located no earlier public artifact with this exact
map or an explicit \(\operatorname{SIC}(n)\) counterexample for \(n\le14\).
Fast-moving public, private, or unindexed work may supersede that observation.

## Permitted language

Prefer “the smallest explicit dimension located in our audit at the cutoff”
and “optimal within the constant-state realization ansatz.” Do not use
“smallest possible,” “globally minimal,” or “first ever” without new
independent evidence.

## Primary-source conclusions

Bottino–Laurentini, *The Visual Hull of Smooth Curved Objects* (2004), DOI https://doi.org/10.1109/TPAMI.2004.130 , author PDF https://cgvg.polito.it/Articles/The%20Visual%20Hull%20of%20Curved%20Objects.pdf :

- Sections 1.1 and 2 concern an opaque object with a generic smooth boundary. The external hull uses surrounding viewpoints outside its convex hull, not a finite camera set or the internal hull. Perspective viewpoints occupy three-space; parallel-view events occupy a direction sphere.
- Proposition 1 characterizes hull membership by the absence of an object-disjoint straight line through the point.
- Sections 2–3 distinguish original-object boundary from artificial boundary. Proposition 5 restricts the latter's candidate ruled surfaces to tangent crossing and triple point. Two-contact candidates require parallel normals, hence a common tangent plane. Three-contact candidates use a shared tangent line.
- Genericity excludes exceptional alignments; higher-than-three contact lines may be isolated but do not sweep independent surface families. Sections 3–4 impose curvature and visibility pruning, so candidate membership is not sufficiency.

Kohn–Sturmfels–Trager, *Changing Views on Curves and Surfaces*, Acta Mathematica Vietnamica 43 (2018), 1–29; https://doi.org/10.1007/s40306-017-0240-1 ; https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.01877 :

Section 4 / Theorem 4.1 classifies a general projective surface's algebraic visual-event locus, not the real hull. Its five components are flecnodal (order-four contact), cusp crossing (3+2), tritangent (2+2+2), edge (envelope of bitangent planes), and parabolic (principal tangents at parabolic points). These represent six event names because parabolic events include lip and beak-to-beak. Thus smoothness does not abolish cusp or higher-contact events. Generality of the complex projective surface is stronger than merely having a smooth chosen real boundary. The edge generators join the two tangent-plane contacts, whereas tritangent generators are lines, not tritangent planes. Theorem 4.1's degree formulas require generality and omit components absent in low degree. Section 6 notes special-surface degree/multiplicity changes, including the Fermat quartic edge surface. The inspected arXiv manuscript is v2, revised 2017-11-11; the journal publication is 2018.

Bottino–Laurentini, *The visual hull of piecewise smooth objects* (2008), DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.cviu.2006.12.003 ; publisher https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S1077314206002190 :

## Interpretation and acceptance cautions

The following are audit inferences, not new theorems:

1. A usable algebraic specialization must fix a real compact solid and its boundary, an external continuum-of-viewpoints model, and separate its artificial boundary from original-surface patches. A bare complex algebraic surface has no opaque interior or visibility convention. Compact regular-closed semialgebraic language in the imported report is its formalization, not wording supplied by AIM.
2. For algebraic degree claims, separately impose the general smooth irreducible complex projective hypothesis. A real affine boundary can be smooth although its projective closure is special or singular.
3. Excluding cusp crossing, flecnodal, and parabolic components from generic artificial boundary patches is not excluding those projection singularities from the visual-event locus. Nor does it prove an arbitrary singular-surface result. Lower-dimensional seams, component intersections, and exceptional higher-contact lines need separate treatment if a statement quantifies over every point rather than surface patches.
4. A finite camera collection produces a cone-intersection reconstruction with camera-dependent boundaries. It must not be identified with this external-hull theorem without an argument.
5. The imported degree-spectrum statement is a conditional algebraic consequence of containment plus irreducibility. This audit checks its cited formulas, but does not establish novelty, realizability of every alternative, or a pointwise treatment of all degeneracies. Do not promote it to a solution of the original question.
6. A generated title saying “only generic smooth visual-hull event types” is acceptable only as shorthand for this already-known necessary candidate classification. It is misleading if read as all visual events, all hull boundary, all real active patches, or all algebraic surfaces.

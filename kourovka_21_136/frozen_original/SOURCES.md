# Source identity and literature scope

Checked 2026-10-03.

1. E. I. Khukhro and V. D. Mazurov (eds.), The Kourovka Notebook, 21st edition,
   October 2026 revision, printed p. 197, Problem 21.136.
   https://kourovkanotebookorg.wordpress.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/21tkt.pdf
   The exact entry was visually checked. It has no resolution annotation.

2. John S. Wilson, Profinite groups with few conjugacy classes of elements of
   infinite order, Archiv der Mathematik 120 (2023), 557-563.
   https://doi.org/10.1007/s00013-023-01851-4
   Published primary copy:
   https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/handle/1810/350748
   Earlier preprint: https://arxiv.org/abs/2209.14753
   Finite generation is essential to the finiteness theorem used here. Attempt 1
   documents the displayed Corollary 3.1 scope issue on published p. 561.

3. Yiftach Barnea, Rachel Camina, Mikhail Ershov, and Mark L. Lewis, On groups
   that can be covered by conjugates of finitely many cyclic or procyclic
   subgroups, author-hosted final 2025 manuscript.
   https://m-ershov.github.io/Research/ncc_final2025.pdf
   Section 1.3 concerns cyclic covering numbers. These count subgroups up to
   conjugation, not infinite-order element classes; that distinction prevents
   interpreting the paper as a resolution of this problem.

4. Exact catalogue identity: https://www.unsolvedmath.com/problems/2645
   The live page returned HTTP 403 during this check. Its live content was not
   read. Identity was recovered from the supplied catalogue snapshot and matched
   to the primary Notebook. Problem 21.137 is a different, adjacent problem and
   is not the subject of this work.

The literature search was bounded. No verified unrestricted resolution was
located; that is not a proof of absence of later or unindexed work. The arguments
in this package are offered as elementary, scope-limited proofs without priority
claims.

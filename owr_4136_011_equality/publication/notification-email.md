Subject: A short preprint on your second-moment equality conjecture

Dear Professor Fradelizi,

I thought you might be interested in a short preprint addressing the equality conjecture following Theorem 1.1 of your paper with Grigoris Paouris and Carsten Schütt, *Simplices in the Euclidean Ball*.

The note proves that, under the original extreme-point norm hypothesis, equality holds for an arbitrary convex body exactly when it is a simplex with all vertices on the radius-r sphere. The argument extends the polytope proof through a countable extreme-point simplex partition and an exact deficit identity; equality forces all piece centroids to coincide, leaving one simplex.

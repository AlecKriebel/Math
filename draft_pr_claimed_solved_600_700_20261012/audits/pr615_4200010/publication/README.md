# A positive-volume ugly band in a smooth Hamiltonian system

Alec Kriebel — ORCID https://orcid.org/0009-0001-9320-500X

Version 1.0.0, 12 October 2026. AI tools were used extensively in developing,
verifying, and preparing the work. This is an unrefereed research note; no human
peer review or formal proof certification is claimed.

The note gives an explicit affirmative answer to item 10 of Oliver Knill's
2000 Hamiltonian problem list, under the stated unrestricted smooth symplectic
and spectral almost-periodicity conventions. It constructs a compact autonomous
Hamiltonian with a band of full Liouville volume 1/4, all Lyapunov exponents zero,
and no positive-measure almost-periodic invariant region. Each band energy
component is already ergodic and non-almost-periodic. Historical first priority
is not asserted; the ingredients are classical and receive credit in the note.
The separate weak-mixing guess is not resolved.

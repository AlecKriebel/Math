## Continuous construction

The two-endpoint compactification X of R is compact and metrizable. Translation fixes endpoints and is jointly continuous: bounded time changes preserve sufficiently far tails in prescribed endpoint neighborhoods. The continuous g has limits 0 and pi/4, so V=R(g) is a continuous unitary path on X. The product V(x)*V(x+t) telescopes under composition; conjugation plus translation therefore gives an automorphism group alpha on C(X,M2). For each f, joint continuity on compact time intervals times X gives point-norm continuity. The singular derivative of g at zero is irrelevant to this argument.

A is exactly the closed algebra generated by alpha_r(D), r>=0. Alpha_t shifts each generator to alpha_(r+t)(D), so restrictions theta_t are well-defined injective unital endomorphisms. Rational nonnegative r suffice by point-norm continuity, proving separability. Constant-diagonal i is a unital embedding; p=Delta ev_0 is UCP, fixes D, and is D-bimodular. Thus ip is a conditional expectation.

At zero, conjugating a diagonal b by R(g(t)) and extracting the diagonal gives the stochastic matrix P_t=J+exp(-t)K. J,K are complementary orthogonal projections. Their algebra proves P_s P_t=P_(s+t) for all nonnegative real times, not merely sampled times.

## Actual representation kernel

The module E=M2 has right diagonal action and inner product Delta(z*w); the diagonal entries of z*z are the squared column norms, so this is definite. The algebraic GNS map a tensor b -> a(0)b preserves the inner product. Evaluation of q and theta_(log 2)(q) gives q and Q with nonzero off-diagonal entries; qQ(1-q), its adjoint, q and 1-q give all matrix units. Thus ev_0(A)=M2 and the GNS map is onto, proving the concrete GNS identification and completeness. The left action is f -> left multiplication by f(0), and its full kernel is precisely f(0)=0. It is not inferred merely from a state-vector null space. The two column modules give B^a(E)=M2 direct-sum M2 and pi(A) its diagonal copy. The vector I has inner product 1, proving fullness.

## Global positive witness and all positive times

Fix s>0 and r=exp(-s), so 0<r<1. Compression q theta_s(q)q is the function cos^2(g(x+s)-g(x))q. Its zero-point coefficient is (1+r)/2, making a_s=q theta_s(q)q-(1+r)q/2 an actual element of ker pi.

For x>0, g'(x)=1/[2 sqrt(exp(2x)-1)] is positive and decreasing, and its improper integral from 0 is finite. Consequently the increment over [x,x+s] is at most its increment over [0,s]. For x<=0 it is either zero or g(x+s)<=g(s); endpoints have zero increment. All increments are in [0,g(s)] subset [0,pi/4]. Since cos^2 decreases there, the coefficient of a_s is nonnegative everywhere, so a_s>=0 in A.

At x=s, the angle subtraction formula gives

    d_s = [(1-r^2)(sqrt(1+r^2)-r)]/2.

Both factors are strictly positive: 1-r^2>0 and the square of sqrt(1+r^2) exceeds r^2 by 1. Thus a_s(s)=d_s q is nonzero, and theta_s(a_s)(0)=d_s Q_s is nonzero for every s>0. This proves kernel noninvariance at each positive time, rather than just failure of invariance at all times collectively. The exact s=log 2 coefficient is 3(sqrt(5)-1)/16. The boundary s=0 has coefficient zero, as required; d_s tends to 1/2 as s tends to infinity. No finite computation establishes the continuous quantifier; the analytic argument does.

Any map intertwining pi and theta_s would have to give distinct values to the identical inputs pi(a_s)=pi(0), so the impossibility holds before the extension problem. p(a_s)=0 while p theta_s(a_s)=d_s Delta(Q_s) is nonzero. Thus the expectation is nonfaithful and the dilation is not strong. A weak dilation with this unital i would have corner projection 1 and ip=id_A; that cannot hold since ev_0(A)=M2 while D is proper. The assertions do not concern normal von Neumann continuity, faithful expectations, other dilations or stronger minimality.

## Finite-image proposition

Assume invariant ker pi and finite-dimensional C=pi(A). The descended beta_t is point-norm continuous (lift each fixed c through pi), hence operator-norm continuous on finite-dimensional C. S_h=integral_0^h beta_u du satisfies S_h/h -> I and is invertible for small h. The semigroup law gives beta_t S_h=integral_t^(t+h) beta_u du; the right derivative at zero is (beta_h-I)S_h^(-1). Hence beta has a generator delta. Differentiating multiplicativity and preservation of adjoints gives a *-derivation.

j=pi i is faithful: j(b)=0 implies p(i(b*b))=b*b=0. The GNS vector satisfies j(b)xi=xi b because p is a conditional expectation; the norm squared of this difference is zero by the bimodule identities. Hence bar p(c)=<xi,c xi> is B-bimodular and bar p j=id. The compressed generator is L=bar p delta j. For b,c in B, the Leibniz rule for delta and bimodularity of bar p give L(bc)=L(b)c+bL(c); adjoints also pass through. The proposition is valid under its stated conditional-expectation hypotheses.

## Prior-result implication

The separate source adversary independently inspects BFH's actual finite amplitude model and verifies its path angle sign(x) arccos(exp(-abs(x)/2)). This continuous path has finite endpoint limits, so the same endpoint compactification makes its clock-cocycle action point-norm continuous. Pure finite-bath compression is a conditional expectation. Forward-generated restriction retains the compression and leaves a finite-dimensional GNS image because the evaluation-and-bath representation factors through a finite matrix algebra. For its decaying projection q, T_t(q)=exp(-t)q, so qL(q)q=-q. For any derivation, differentiating q^2=q and multiplying both sides by q gives qL(q)q=0. The proposition therefore rules out invariant kernel at every time for that known construction. It does not by itself prove failure at every individual positive time or supply a globally positive witness. The paper properly identifies the prior unstated corollary and limits its refinement to the explicit small model and witnesses.

adjointable algebra of that fixed GNS module. This does not decide extension

minimality criterion, or decide the source's second future-algebra problem.

## 1. All-real ambient automorphism group

Let `X=[-infinity,+infinity]` with the order topology. The map
`x |-> (2/pi) arctan(x)`, with the endpoint limits, is a homeomorphism from
`X` to `[-1,1]`. Thus the space is compact and metrizable. For each finite
real `t`, define `h_t(x)=x+t`, fixing the endpoints. This is an order
homeomorphism with inverse `h_-t`.

Joint continuity of `(t,x)|->h_t(x)` needs a check at the endpoints. Around
any finite `t0`, restrict `|t-t0|<1`, so `|t|<=|t0|+1`. If the target
neighborhood of `+infinity` contains `(M,+infinity]`, then the source tail
`(M+|t0|+1,+infinity]` maps into it for all those `t`. The negative endpoint
has the corresponding lower-tail argument. On finite points ordinary
addition is continuous. This proves joint continuity on all `R x X`, rather
than only for each individual time.

The packet's `g` is continuous at zero because `arccos(exp(-x))->0` as
`x->0+`; its limits at the endpoints are `0` and `pi/4`. It follows that
`V(x)=R(g(x))` is a continuous unitary in `C(X,M2)`.

There is a direct conjugacy that avoids relying solely on a cocycle
calculation. Set

```
Gamma(f)(x) = V(x) f(x) V(x)^*,
tau_t(f)(x) = f(x+t).
```

Both `Gamma` and `tau_t` are unital *-automorphisms, and

```
alpha_t = Gamma^-1 tau_t Gamma.
```

Expanding this identity gives precisely the packet's
`u_t(x)=V(x)^*V(x+t)` and its prescribed `alpha_t`. Therefore for every
`s,t in R`, `alpha_s alpha_t=alpha_(s+t)` and `alpha_t^-1=alpha_-t`.
Independently, `u_s(x)u_t(x+s)=u_(s+t)(x)` follows by cancellation; the
placement of adjoints and the translation direction are correct.

For each fixed `f`, `(t,x)|->f(x+t)` is continuous. Restricting `t` to a
compact neighborhood of `t0` gives a continuous function on a compact
metrizable product, hence uniformly continuous. Consequently
`||tau_t(f)-tau_t0(f)||_infinity->0`, and conjugacy gives the same conclusion
for `alpha_t`. This proves point-norm continuity at every real time. It does
not require differentiability of `g`, which indeed has a cusp at zero.

At either infinite endpoint, `u_t=1`; all endpoint formulas therefore agree
with the prescribed translation action. No hidden negative-time restriction
or discontinuity of the ambient group remains.

## 2. Exact generation, invariance, and separability

Let `D` be the constant diagonal copy of `C^2`, generated by the unit and
`q=diag(1,0)`. Define `A=C*(alpha_r(D):r>=0)` inside the ambient algebra.
It contains the ambient unit. Since `alpha_t alpha_r(D)=alpha_(t+r)(D)`,
the image `alpha_t(A)` lies in `A` for `t>=0`. Restricting an automorphism
gives an injective unital *-endomorphism `theta_t:A->A`. Composition and
point-norm continuity restrict from the ambient group.

All generators at irrational times are norm limits of generators at
nonnegative rational times. Thus `A` is generated by a countable set and
is separable; rational complex coefficients in *-polynomials furnish a
countable dense subset. This is also the smallest C*-subalgebra of the
ambient algebra containing `D` and invariant under every forward
`alpha_t`: any such subalgebra must contain all the displayed generators.
Hence the requested algebraic generation/minimality is exact. It is not a
claim of minimality with specified multitime Markov moments.

There is no attached independent summand in the definition of `A`. Every
generator evaluates to the same diagonal matrix at both endpoints, so all
elements of `A` have equal diagonal endpoint values. In particular the
larger ambient algebra is not silently used as the dilation algebra.
No claim that negative-time ambient automorphisms preserve `A` is needed.

## 3. Genuine completely positive conditional expectation

Let `ev0:A->M2` be evaluation at zero, a unital *-homomorphism. The map
`Delta(z)=q z q+(1-q)z(1-q)` is a unital completely positive map onto
the diagonal matrices: this Kraus decomposition proves complete positivity
at every matrix amplification. Set `p=Delta ev0`. Then `p` is unital
completely positive and `p i=id_D` for constant diagonal inclusion `i`.

For `b1,b2 in D`, diagonal multiplication commutes with diagonal extraction:
`p(i(b1) f i(b2))=b1 p(f)b2`. Hence `i p` is a unital, positive,
contractive, idempotent `D`-bimodule map onto `i(D)`, and is a conditional
expectation in the C*-algebra category. Complete positivity and
contractivity are inherited from the two maps, not assumed from an
unverified scalar calculation.

Faithfulness of `Delta` on positive matrices is valid: for `z>=0`, zero
diagonal forces `trace(z)=0`, so `z=0`; equivalently `Delta(w^*w)=0`
means both columns of `w` vanish. This does **not** imply faithfulness of
`p` on `A`, because evaluation loses information. Section 6 explicitly
exhibits a nonzero positive element whose expectation is zero.

## 4. Every nonnegative real time gives the Markov semigroup

At `x=0`, `V(0)=1`, so for `t>=0`,
`theta_t(diag(b1,b2))(0)=R(g(t))diag(b1,b2)R(g(t))^*`.
Applying `Delta` gives the two weights `cos^2(g(t))` and `sin^2(g(t))`.
Since `cos(2g(t))=exp(-t)`, including `t=0`, these equal
`(1+exp(-t))/2` and `(1-exp(-t))/2`.

Write

```
J = 1/2 [[1,1],[1,1]],
H = 1/2 [[1,-1],[-1,1]],
P_t = J+exp(-t)H.
```

Direct multiplication gives `J^2=J`, `H^2=H`, `JH=HJ=0` and `J+H=1`.
Therefore `P_s P_t=P_(s+t)` for **all real `s,t>=0`**, without reducing to
integer times or extrapolating from samples. The weights are nonnegative
and sum to one. Matrix amplifications of the maps on `C^2` act on pairs of
positive matrices by these nonnegative linear combinations; this proves
complete positivity explicitly. `P_0=1`, and the bounded generator is
`L=-H=1/2 [[-1,1],[1,-1]]`. Thus `P_t=exp(tL)` is uniformly continuous.

The quantifier here is all real nonnegative times. For `t<0`, the ambient
compression at zero is the identity because `g(t)=0`. It is not the claimed
formula `J+exp(-t)H`, which would have a negative off-diagonal weight, and
does not constitute a Markov group. The packet makes no such negative-time
claim. As `t->infinity`, `P_t->J`; as `t->0+`, `P_t->1`. Both limits agree
with the continuous rotation formula.

## 5. Actual universal GNS correspondence and adjointable algebra

Start with the algebraic Paschke construction `A tensor_alg D`, with

```
<a tensor b,c tensor d> = b^* p(a^*c) d.
```

Define the map on finite sums

```
Phi(sum_j a_j tensor b_j) = sum_j a_j(0)b_j in M2.
```

Using the `D`-bimodule property of `Delta`, the inner product of two such
images is exactly `Delta(Phi(z)^*Phi(w))`, equal to the algebraic GNS
inner product. The null space is therefore exactly the kernel of `Phi`,
because `Delta(z^*z)=0` forces `z=0`. Quotienting by this null space and
completing produces the concrete Hilbert `D`-module

```
E=M2,   right action z.b=z b,   <z,w>=Delta(z^*w).
```

Its norm is the maximum of the Euclidean norms of the two columns; it is
complete since it is finite-dimensional.

Surjectivity and cyclicity must be checked, not presumed. At `s=log(2)`,
`ev0(theta_s(q))=Q`, where

```
Q = [[3/4,sqrt(3)/4],[sqrt(3)/4,1/4]].
```

The evaluation image contains `q`, `1-q`,
`q Q(1-q)=(sqrt(3)/4)e12`, and its adjoint. These generate all matrix
units. Thus `ev0(A)=M2` and `Phi` is onto already before completion.
The vector `xi=1` corresponds to `1 tensor 1`, has `<xi,xi>=1_D`,
and `A xi D` spans all `E` (indeed `A xi=M2`). This establishes the
actual cyclic GNS correspondence, rather than just a representation
producing the same scalar state.

The transported left action is `pi(f)z=f(0)z`. It is adjointable with
adjoint `pi(f^*)`, nondegenerate, and

```
ker pi = {f in A:f(0)=0}.
```

The converse follows by applying `pi(f)` to the unit matrix. Independently,
`p(f^*f)=0` is equivalent to `f(0)=0`; in this particular example the
GNS vector null space happens to be a two-sided ideal, so it agrees with
the full representation kernel. The proof does not incorrectly identify
these spaces in general. The general criterion
`p(h^*f^*f h)=0 for every h in A` gives the same kernel here.

Let `e1,e2` be the two central minimal idempotents of `D`. Right
`D`-linearity forces every module operator to preserve `E e1` and `E e2`,
the two column spaces, each canonically `C^2`. Conversely any pair of
matrices acting separately on the columns is `D`-linear and adjointable;
its adjoint is the pair of conjugate transposes. Therefore

```
B^a(E)=M2 direct-sum M2,
pi(A)={(z,z):z in M2}.
```

Fullness follows from `<xi,xi>=1_D`; it does not force `pi` faithful.
The two direct-summand units are column projections, so this calculation
does not confuse the matrix algebra with the larger algebra of all
complex-linear operators on the four-dimensional vector space `M2`.

## 6. Exact witness, including an all-positive-time strengthening

There is a useful uniform calculation. Fix any `s>0`, set `r=exp(-s)`,
and let `q_s=theta_s(q)`. Put

```
c_s = (1+r)/2,
a_s = q q_s q - c_s q in A.
```

At zero, `q_s(0)=R(g(s))qR(g(s))^*`, whose upper-left entry is `c_s`.
Thus `a_s(0)=0`, and `a_s` belongs to the true representation kernel.
At `x=s`, its upper-left coefficient is

```
d_s = cos^2(g(2s)-g(s)) - cos^2(g(s)).
```

Let `2g(s)=arccos(r)` and `2g(2s)=arccos(r^2)`. Both angles lie in
`(0,pi/2)`, so their sines have positive square roots. The difference
formula gives

```
cos(2g(2s)-2g(s))
  = r^3 + sqrt(1-r^4)sqrt(1-r^2)
  = r^3 + (1-r^2)sqrt(1+r^2).
```

Subtracting `r=cos(2g(s))` and dividing by two yields

```
d_s = (1-r^2)(sqrt(1+r^2)-r)/2 > 0.
```

Both factors are strictly positive for `0<r<1`. There is no cancellation
or sign ambiguity in this proof. At zero after applying the endomorphism,

```
theta_s(a_s)(0)=R(g(s)) a_s(s) R(g(s))^*=d_s q_s(0) != 0.
```

Consequently **for every `s>0`, `theta_s(ker pi)` is not contained in
`ker pi`**. For `s=log(2)`, `r=1/2` and this gives exactly

```
d_s=3(sqrt(5)-1)/16,
theta_s(a_s)(0)=d_s [[3/4,sqrt(3)/4],[sqrt(3)/4,1/4]].
```

The fresh exact checker derives these identities using rational arithmetic
in `Q(sqrt(2),sqrt(3),sqrt(5))`, choosing the rotations from their exact
double-angle cosines. It passes 22 exact assertions, including the cyclic
matrix units and the shifted positive square. It imports neither the
original verifier nor any prior audit's code.

As an additional falsification check, the witness `a_s` is actually
globally positive. For `x>0`,
`g'(x)=1/(2 sqrt(exp(2x)-1))` is positive and decreasing. The increment
`g(x+s)-g(x)` is therefore at most `g(s)-g(0)=g(s)`, as is seen by
integrating this decreasing derivative on `[x,x+s]` and `[0,s]` (the
improper integral at zero converges). For `x<=0`, the same bound follows
from `g(x)=0`, monotonicity, and `x+s<=s`. At the endpoints the increment
vanishes. Thus `0<=g(x+s)-g(x)<=g(s)<=pi/4` on all `X`. Since `cos^2`
decreases on `[0,pi/4]`, the coefficient of `q` in `a_s(x)` is
nonnegative everywhere. It is strictly positive at `x=s`, so `a_s` is a
nonzero positive element in `ker pi`.

Global positivity is not needed for the descent obstruction. Its square
`b_s=a_s^2` is also positive, nonzero, and has `p(b_s)=0`; nevertheless
`theta_s(b_s)(0)=d_s^2 q_s(0)!=0`. This directly establishes
nonfaithfulness of `p` and gives the same obstruction with a positive
kernel element. At `s=0` the witness vanishes, as it must. The formula
has `d_s/s->sqrt(2)-1` as `s->0+` and `d_s->1/2` as `s->infinity`;
the failure is compatible with point-norm continuity at zero.

## 7. Consequence, attempted escapes, and exact remaining gaps

Suppose any map `beta_s` on `pi(A)` satisfied
`beta_s(pi(f))=pi(theta_s(f))` for every `f in A`. With `f=0`, its value
at zero must be zero. With `f=a_s`, its value at the same input would have
to be nonzero. This is a contradiction even before requiring linearity,
positivity, or multiplicativity. An extension on `B^a(E)` intertwining
the same fixed representation would imply such a map by restriction and
is equally impossible. A quotient semigroup cannot be formed here.

The following possible objections were checked:

- **Faithfulness:** faithfulness of the fiber expectation is proved;
  faithfulness of the full expectation is neither assumed nor true. The
  source expressly asks about descent through the GNS representation, so
  its possible kernel is the very issue under review.
- **Strong dilation:** the additional equation `p theta_t=T_t p` on all
  `A` fails on `a_s`. The source's general-dilation diagram only requires
  this equation on `i(B)`; therefore this is a legitimate general example.
- **Corner/weak dilation:** if this unital inclusion were a corner inclusion,
  its corner unit would be the unit of `A`, forcing the corner expectation
  to be the identity. The actual expectation is not the identity. The
  example does not claim a weak/corner counterexample.
- **Normality:** this is a C*-algebra example. No normal von Neumann
  extension or bidual continuity is asserted or needed.
- **Time direction:** ambient automorphisms exist at every real time;
  the dilation and Markov semigroup are required at every nonnegative
  real time. No negative-time preservation of `A` is used.
- **Generation:** `A` is the exact smallest forward-invariant algebra
  containing the observed algebra, not an arbitrary enlarged ambient
  algebra. Generation does not imply the missing kernel compatibility.
- **Different representations or dilations:** neither replacing the
  expectation's fixed cyclic GNS module nor constructing some other
  dilation of the same `T` answers whether this specified semigroup
  descends through this specified representation.

There is no mandatory mathematical repair of the packet's scoped theorem
identified by this review. Before any broader solved-status interpretation,
the following boundaries are mandatory: the conditional second-stage
extension problem is still open here; a useful stronger minimality condition
is not supplied; the second source problem is not addressed; literature
priority remains a separate audit. The replay's success provides artifact
integrity and finite identity reproducibility, while the preceding written
derivations provide the universal analytic and operator proofs.

## Credit requirements

BFH is mandatory for the construction mechanism and the acknowledged short source-negative corollary. DMB is direct prior credit when presenting finite-unitary paths, square-root initial behavior, or the derivative obstruction; its Appendix B already contains the elementary derivative mechanism. vom Ende is useful rigorous corroboration and should be cited if bounded autonomous Hamiltonians are discussed. The reviewer general-expectation lemma is independently proved and should be labeled as an adaptation/observation, not attributed as a literal fixed-GNS theorem to either source.

OWR remains mandatory as the target. Skeide-Sumesh and Shalit-Skeide are appropriate for the existing fixed-intertwining/category discussion. Kaad-Skeide, Paired E0, and the 2026 thesis are optional audit exclusions and need not clutter the paper.

# Independent deductions, before earlier whole-package verdicts

The concrete question tested is whether exact forward generation implies descent through this dilation's fixed GNS representation. This is not a classification of replacement minimality axioms or a theorem about strong dilations.

1. The endpoint compactification is compact metrizable. Translation fixes endpoints and is jointly continuous because bounded changes of time preserve sufficiently distant tails. V is continuous there; its translated cocycle telescopes. Therefore alpha is an automorphism group, and compactness gives point-norm continuity for each fixed f. Restricting to the algebra generated by forward translates gives injectivity and the *exact* claimed generation, not a quotient or a separately adjoined kernel summand. Rational translates suffice, by norm continuity, for separability.
2. Evaluation followed by diagonal extraction is UCP and a diagonal bimodule retraction; composing with i gives a unital conditional expectation. At zero, rotation by g(t) gives P_t=J+exp(-t)K. Orthogonal idempotents J,K imply the semigroup law for all real t>=0.

This has two independent C2-fibres, so its adjointable algebra is M2 plus M2, while left multiplication is the diagonal M2 copy. The representation kernel is exactly evaluation zero. No general inference from a single vector nullspace is used.
4. For s>0 and r=exp(-s), a_s(x)=[cos²(g(x+s)-g(x))-(1+r)/2]q vanishes at zero. The derivative g'(x)=1/[2sqrt(exp(2x)-1)] is positive, decreasing and integrable at zero. Its increment over every positive translated interval is at most g(s); for negative x the same bound follows by monotonicity, and at the endpoints the increment is zero. Thus a_s is globally positive.

## Primary-text-completed BFH deduction

In the bath-first basis 00,01,10,11, H exchanges 01 with 10 and fixes 00 and 11. For c=cos G, z=sin G, the dual reduced channel sends [[a,w],[w*,b]] to [[c² a+z² b,c w],[c w*,b]]. In particular, for q=|0><0|, T_t(q)=exp(-t)q, and L(q)=-q. This is verified independently by explicit symbolic matrices in independent_math.py.

The forward-generated restriction remains a general conditional-expectation dilation and is injective and point-norm continuous by the endpoint argument already proved. The GNS module embeds in 4×2 matrices by a⊗b ↦ a(0)S b, S(v)=|1>⊗v, with inner product z*w (or its diagonal for B=C2); hence its represented image is finite dimensional. Thus the prior clock supplies the claimed short unstated negative corollary.

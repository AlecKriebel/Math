# Current result, priority and scope

This notice supersedes the historical packet's bounded source discussion.
The frozen packet and its original audit are preserved as research evidence;
their claims that further review was pending describe their creation stage.

The accepted result is a counterexample to the concrete forward-generation-only
GNS descent proposal in Michael Skeide's first module-approach problem, OWR
2009 pp. 542–543 (general definition pp. 497–498), DOI
[10.4171/OWR/2009/09](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2009/09).
The fixed expectation-GNS kernel fails to be invariant at every positive time.
The small M2/C2 model supplies an explicit nonzero globally positive witness
at each such time. Failure of descent refutes the offered descent-plus-extension
conjunction.

The construction's clock/evaluation/translated-unitary mechanism is established
in Burgarth–Facchi–Hillier, arXiv:2010.00279v2, Ann. Henri Poincaré 24 (2023),
325–347, [10.1007/s00023-022-01211-y](https://doi.org/10.1007/s00023-022-01211-y).
Indeed their earlier amplitude-damping example, after routine compactification
and forward-generated restriction, already yields this negative phenomenon
through a short finite-GNS derivation obstruction. Two independent reviewers
proved that unstated corollary. Their paper is not claimed to have explicitly
answered the OWR problem.

Dive–Mintert–Burgarth, Phys. Rev. A 92 (2015), 032111,
[10.1103/PhysRevA.92.032111](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.92.032111),
is the finite-unitary/first-derivative antecedent. Vom Ende's bounded-Hamiltonian
formulation is cited as related corroboration:
[10.1142/S1230161223500038](https://doi.org/10.1142/S1230161223500038).

This is an explicit formulation-specific corollary of known clock machinery,
with a smaller matrix realization and exact positive witnesses. No new general
dilation method, exhaustive novelty, historical first priority, independent
historical development or plagiarism finding is asserted. Bounded primary-source
searches on 13 October 2026 did not locate an explicit earlier publication of the
exact fixed-GNS source-resolution statement. The same known mechanism also
gives every-positive-time selfadjoint witnesses in a finite-bath specialization;
its odd-extension witnesses are not globally positive.

Useful stronger/incomparable sufficient minimality conditions, extension after
kernel invariance is separately imposed, a normal von Neumann version,
nonexistence of another dilation and the source's second future-algebra question
remain outside the result. This is a general C*-dilation, not a strong or
weak/corner counterexample.

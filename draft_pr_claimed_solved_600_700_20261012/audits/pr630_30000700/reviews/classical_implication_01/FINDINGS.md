## Mandatory issues before promoting a novel research solution

### M1. Integrate the directly relevant earlier zero-sharing examples

LXY2009 Example 1 is more relevant than a generic observation that
quadratics have been familiar since calculus: it prints exactly the
zero-sharing boundary obstruction when replacing CM by IM in an adjacent
derivative-sharing theorem. Taking its beta=2b gives the frozen k=2
example, and f''=b supplies the target's remaining hypothesis. The literal
negative answer is consequently a checkable short corollary of this
published counterexample.

**Repair:** cite the example and state the parameter map. Do not describe
the zero-sharing obstruction as a newly discovered mechanism. The precise
OWR application may be described as an observation without a priority claim.

### M2. Credit the known structural results and square-exponential template

The polynomial structural step is exactly Proposition 7 of NY2001 after
showing a=0; the rest is a degree count. The square-exponential shape is
Theorem 1.2(d)/Corollary 1.1 of LXY2009 under a specified variable scaling.
The target derivative restriction still requires the explicit lambda
calculation; the prior theorem's own hypotheses differ.

**Repair:** describe the polynomial statement as an elementary corollary
and the transcendental family as a specialization of the known template.
Include the derivative calculation, and explicitly avoid claiming that
LXY2009 itself states the exact higher-derivative condition.

### M3. Separate literal correction from intended-problem resolution

The OWR printing supports the literal negative answer, and the current
title already narrows it appropriately. This does not establish the
historical intended problem, which may or may not have excluded zero. The
nearby nonzero hypotheses and the 2009 boundary examples are contextual
evidence, not proof of Fang's intention.

**Repair:** maintain the exact literal scope. A publication as a modest
clarification is logically coherent, but its modest framing alone cannot
establish novelty or fulfill a goal requiring a substantive novel research
contribution. Do not count it as resolving the strengthened nonzero-a
problem. Avoid an immutable research-result release on this audit alone.

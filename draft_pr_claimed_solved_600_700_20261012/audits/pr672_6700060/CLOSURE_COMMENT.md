The priority issue is decisive for accepting this as a new resolution. Yuchen Bi's [*Dihedral Rigidity for Convex Polytopes by Smooth Approximation*, arXiv:2608.06320v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06320v1), posted 6 August 2026, Theorem 1.1, already includes the required equality of normal inner products on closed facet intersections. For the stated competitor, pull back its Euclidean metric: it is smooth and positive definite through the corners, has scalar curvature zero, preserves face mean curvature, and converts interior-angle nonincrease into exactly Bi's exterior-angle hypothesis. The theorem gives angle equality directly. The reference polytope's extremality is not needed. The planar case follows by classical turning, or by applying the same prior theorem to the polygon's metric product with an interval; dimension one is vacuous.

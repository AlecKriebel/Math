# Classical identity versus the filtration counterexample

Independent audit, PR #681 / problem 30003703. This note distinguishes proved implications from historical assertions. Its source files and exact controls are pinned separately. No other review family's conclusion was used to decide the classical overlap. The parent supplied the additional 2025 tangent-algebra lead after the initial searches; its hypotheses were then checked independently.

## 1. Exact claim tested

The native proof uses the commutative rational matrix-entry algebra on Hom(F_n,SL(2,C)), with augmentation ideal J, and the integral lower central series Gamma of F_n. The comparison is between A_n(k), the kernel on F_n/Gamma_(k+1), and D_n(k), the kernel on J/J^(k+1). The proposed counterexamples are D_n(5) minus A_n(5) for n >= 4 and D_n(6) minus A_n(6) for n >= 3. The rank-three degree-five case is not claimed. Character algebras, the ordinary lower-central-series Andreadakis conjecture, formal Lie automorphisms, and wild automorphisms in PI quotients are different objects until a precise comparison is proved.

The words are w=[[[x1,x2],[x1,x3]],x1] and v=[[[x1,x2],[x1,[x1,x2]]],x1]. Their leading free associative polynomials are P(X,Y,Z)=[[[X,Y],[X,Z]],X] and Q(X,Y)=P(X,Y,[X,Y]), of degrees five and six. The first degree refers to the displacement word; the corresponding first Johnson degree of the automorphism is one smaller. This offset must be retained when comparing other authors' tangent-filtration indexing.

## 2. The identity itself has an explicit prior source

Burde, *Derivation double Lie algebras*, arXiv:1411.0950v2, displays [z,[[z,x],[z,y]]]=0 as identity (4). Under z=X, x=Y, y=Z this is exactly -P(X,Y,Z)=0, with no omitted linearization. Theorem 4.14 states that identity (4), together with the standard degree-five Lie identity, is a basis for the identities of sl2 in characteristic zero, attributing that basis to Razmyslov's 1973 paper. Thus no novelty in P is available.

Burde's identity (6), attributed to Filippov, is

    [z,[[w,x],[w,y]]] = [w,[[z,w],[x,y]]].

The specialization z=w makes the right-hand side zero, so the left-hand side is exactly -P(w,x,y). The script recomputes this specialization in a free associative algebra, rather than inferring it from similar-looking formulas. Q is then the weighted substitution Z=[X,Y]. This supplies an independent source-bound route to the exact identities used by the native proof. I did not inspect the original Razmyslov or Filippov texts; Burde's explicit displayed identity is itself an inspected earlier source, while the original historical attribution is reported as Burde's attribution.

An elementary proof also shows that trace zero is unnecessary for P. For arbitrary 2-by-2 matrices over a commutative ring, Cayley-Hamilton gives X^2=tX-dI. If U=[X,Y], then XU+UX=tU, and likewise XV+VX=tV for V=[X,Z]. Consequently XUV=(tU-UX)V=tUV-U(tV-VX)=UVX, and similarly XVU=VUX. Therefore [UV-VU,X]=0. The native weaker trace-zero statement is valid. This extension is independently confirmed symbolically for twelve generic entries and requires no division, diagonalization, reducedness or field assumption.

The Wagner-Hall identity [[x,y]^2,z]=0, weak identities [x^2,y]=0 for traceless x, the standard associative identity s4, and the classification of degree-five sl2 identities are classical PI ingredients. They must not be presented as new discoveries. Their existence alone does not establish a previously published resolution of this particular automorphism-filtration comparison.

## 3. Exact bridge still needed after the old identity

Here is the complete logical map that a claimed prior covering theorem would have to supply.

1. A nested group commutator whose input matrices are 1+B_i has leading term the correspondingly bracketed polynomial in the degree-one classes. This is the standard filtered identity [1+u,1+v]-1=(uv-vu)(1+u)^(-1)(1+v)^(-1). For w and v, the old identities erase the leading degrees five and six. Hence their matrix entries minus I lie in J^6 and J^7 in the actual component algebra.
2. The same leading polynomials remain nonzero in the free integral associative algebra: coefficients of XXYXZ in P and XXXYXY in Q are -1 and +1. The elementary Magnus inclusion Gamma_r -> 1+degree>=r suffices to conclude w not in Gamma_6 and v not in Gamma_7. No converse dimension-subgroup theorem, rational isolator, or integral BCH construction is necessary.
3. The spare-generator assignments x4 -> x4*w and x3 -> x3*v are actual integral free-group automorphisms, since the displaced generator does not occur in the multiplier and the inverse uses its inverse word. This is a realization step, not merely a free-Lie derivation.
4. The component algebra is generated by entries of the free generator matrices. Its inverse entries are adjugate entries. Thus the matrix congruences imply identity on the full quotient R/J^(k+1), and on J/J^(k+1), while the Magnus certificates obstruct A_n(k).

Each step is short and checkable. Their elementary nature does not establish that the specific application has already been published. Equally, the validity of a newly written bridge is not evidence that no one wrote it earlier. The bounded historical verdict must keep both limitations.

A Lie PI must not be substituted for a group law. With A=((1,1),(0,1)), B=((1,0),(1,1)), C=((2,1),(1,1)), all in SL2(Z), the exact word w evaluates to ((-101,390),(-36,139)), not I. The matrix has determinant one. The original argument concerns augmentation order, so this is fully consistent with the theorem and is an effective negative control against an invalid prior-implication shortcut.

## 4. Generic-matrix and relative-Lie literature

Drensky and Fındık, arXiv:1009.5186v1, explicitly describe the relatively free Lie algebra F_m(sl2) as the Lie algebra of generic traceless matrices; its kernel is the ideal of Lie identities. Theorem 1(i) records Razmyslov's weak-identity description. The paper then studies continuous inner automorphisms of completions and their finite nilpotent Lie quotients, using BCH and generic coordinates.

This confirms that the polynomial-evaluation kernel in the first infinitesimal layer is classical. It does not state that a given integral free-group word gives a spare-generator automorphism in Satoh's D_n(k) minus A_n(k). Rational continuous automorphisms and inner automorphisms of a relative-Lie quotient are not silently identifiable with Aut(F_n). In particular, existence in a completion does not ensure an automorphism of the discrete free group, while the spare-generator construction directly proves one. No exact theorem in this inspected paper supplies the complete four-step target map.

## 5. The additional 2025 tangent-algebra lead

The public preprint of Shestakov and Umirbaev is arXiv:2507.20486v1, dated 28 July 2025. The publisher record is DOI 10.1016/j.laa.2025.12.020; I inspected the publisher preview and the complete 23-page v1 text, not the complete final journal text.

Its A is a relatively free algebra over a characteristic-zero field, graded by polynomial degree. IA(A,k) consists of algebra automorphisms inducing identity on A/(A_(k+1)+A_(k+2)+...). Its tangent is a homogeneous derivation formed from the first nonzero displacement. Theorem 1 establishes the graded tangent Lie algebra; Theorem 2 constrains divergence in specified varieties. Proposition 1 tests automorphisms of A/I which cannot be approximated by tame lifts. Lemma 10 treats Bergman's x1 -> x1+[x1,x2]^2 in a free matrix PI quotient.

The discrete free group appears in the introduction as motivation for the analogy. The target rational component-function ring, its ideal J at the trivial SL2 representation, and Satoh's D_n(k) do not appear in the body's definitions or results. The two cited Satoh works concern lower-central-series/Johnson topics, not this component-algebra comparison. Also, wildness or failure to lift an automorphism of a quotient runs in a different direction from producing the explicitly liftable free-group transvection used here.

I subsequently retrieved the current arXiv:2507.20486v2 (15 October 2025; 25 pages), independently binding its SHA-256 02d0bb10345cde6c03fe142c5c98d8009cd2dff5ca406ee2cc916f4c6007c30c. Its relevant definitions and Theorems 1, 2 and 5, plus Lemma 10, were inspected directly. The v1 lifting Proposition 1 is numbered Theorem 5 in v2. The newly added v2 Proposition 1 gives homogeneity and GL_n-invariance of derivation subalgebras; the other changes inspected retain the relative-algebra setup. These do not supply the missing target bridge. I do not infer that the complete final journal text equals either preprint.

Attempting A=L_n, or A=the generic M2 algebra, changes the object being acted on. Attempting A=Q[F_n] does not satisfy the paper's free-algebra-with-positive-polynomial-degree setup: inverses of the group generators and the expansion at the identity are missing. Completion and logarithms might permit comparison of associated graded objects, but would still require the specific finite J-power and discrete integral realization steps above. Those comparisons are not furnished by the cited theorem. No verified exact prior counterexample follows from this source as inspected.

## 6. Interpretation and falsifiable priority boundary

The strongest supported statement is: the exact PI ingredient is old, the four-step counterexample proof is valid, and no inspected classical or general theorem has been mapped to an older complete statement of these two counterexamples. This is not an exhaustive absence theorem, a certificate of firstness, or evidence the question was still open in 2026. The original PI texts and the final 2026 tangent-algebra text were not inspected in full; the source ledger records those limits. The separate Satoh-specific literature audit remains necessary.

A concise note can defensibly say that it gives explicit counterexamples to the equality formulated in the 2018 report, using a classical degree-five identity. It should credit Burde Eq.(4)/Theorem4.14 and report Razmyslov's attribution through that inspected source. It should state the exact rank/degree ranges and retain the native no-priority/no-current-openness boundary. It should not call the identity or the general filtered-commutator mechanism new. A paper can be independently useful as an explicit solution with a fully integral and finite-ideal proof even when its methods are classical; historical novelty of this application remains a bounded-search question.

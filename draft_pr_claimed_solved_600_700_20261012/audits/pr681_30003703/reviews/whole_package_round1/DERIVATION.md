## Exact mathematical target

Let F_n be the discrete free group on x_1,...,x_n, with integral lower central
series Gamma_1=F_n and Gamma_(r+1)=[Gamma_r,F_n]. Define A_n(k) as the kernel of
the action on F_n/Gamma_(k+1). Let R be the rational subalgebra of all
complex-valued functions on Hom(F_n,SL(2,C)) generated by the individual entry
functions a_ij(g). Let J be the ideal generated by a_ij(g)-delta_ij. Define D_n(k)
by identity on the entire quotient J/J^(k+1). All group brackets here are
ghg^-1h^-1. Matrix and associative brackets are UV-VU.

These are the actual definitions on OWR2018 printed pp.88-89 (physical PDF
pages46-47). In particular, the acted-on object is neither the character algebra
nor only the single graded piece J^k/J^(k+1). The n>=3 discussion reports equality
through k=4 and conjectures equality for all k>=1. The integral A definition and
the k+1 indexing also agree with Satoh arXiv:1607.05411v2, PDF pp.8-10; Theorem6.3
is on PDF p.17. Source versions, hashes and limits are in SOURCE_BINDINGS.json.

The precise claims reconstructed are:

* For every n>=4, x_4 -> x_4*w, with other generators fixed, is in
  D_n(5) minus A_n(5), where w=[[[x_1,x_2],[x_1,x_3]],x_1].
* For every n>=3, x_3 -> x_3*v, with other generators fixed, is in
  D_n(6) minus A_n(6), where v=[[[x_1,x_2],[x_1,[x_1,x_2]]],x_1].

## Universal identity, without a generic-point inference

In M_2(S), for any commutative unital ring S, Cayley-Hamilton gives
X^2-tX+dI=0, where t=tr X. Set U=[X,Y], V=[X,Z]. The identity
XU+UX=[X^2,Y]=tU holds exactly, and similarly XV+VX=tV. Therefore

    XUV=(tU-UX)V=tUV-U(tV-VX)=UVX,
    XVU=VUX.

Thus P(X,Y,Z)=[[[X,Y],[X,Z]],X]=0. Substitution Z=[X,Y] proves Q=0;
the substituted input has weight two, so Q has degree six, not five.
No diagonalization, division, characteristic restriction, smoothness, reducedness
or polynomial-coordinate identification occurs in this argument.

Burde arXiv:1411.0950v2, Eq.(4), PDF p.4, displays [z,[[z,x],[z,y]]]=0.
Taking z=X,x=Y,y=Z gives exactly -P. Theorem4.14 on PDF p.10 reports the
Razmyslov attribution for its place in an sl2 identity basis. The current paper
explicitly reports that historical attribution through the inspected Burde
source; it does not claim inspection of the original Razmyslov paper or novelty
of P. The universal arbitrary-matrix proof above supports the paper's stronger
ring formulation independently of that attribution.

## From zero leading class to actual ideal powers

For invertible A=1+u, B=1+z, the exact identity

    ABA^-1B^-1-1=(uz-zu)A^-1B^-1

follows by subtracting BA from AB and multiplying on the right. If the entries
of u and z are in I^r and I^s, r,s>=1, the right side belongs entrywise to
I^(r+s). Also A^-1-1=-uA^-1, so A^-1 and B^-1 are identity modulo I. Its
degree-(r+s) class is consequently the bracket of the respective leading
classes. This is an exact calculation in the original ideal filtration.

Apply it to the universal matrices M(g)=(a_ij(g)) in SL_2(R). The finite
generator matrices are I+B_i with B_i in M_2(J). Successive bracket weights in
w are (2,2), then4, then5. Successive relevant weights in v are2 and3, then5,
then6. Thus M(w)-I is in M_2(J^5) with degree-five class P, and M(v)-I is in
M_2(J^6) with degree-six class Q. The associated graded ring of the commutative
ring R is commutative, so the preceding identities apply to its matrices.
Zero in J^5/J^6 means membership in J^6; zero in J^6/J^7 means membership
in J^7, by the definition of the quotients. Hence

    M(w)-I in M_2(J^6),     M(v)-I in M_2(J^7).

This step does not infer ideal membership from arcs or from pointwise
vanishing in a jet sample. A singular or nonreduced ring would not invalidate
the calculation. The same conclusion descends through any quotient relations
in the function algebra. The generic finite-quotient computations are useful
checks, but are not needed to assume that gr_J R is a free polynomial ring.

## Integral obstruction in the discrete free group

Use the free associative formal power-series ring over Z, filtered by total
degree. The free-group assignment E(x_i)=1+X_i is a homomorphism, using the
geometric inverse for negative letters. In a complete filtered ring, the units
1+T_(>=r) form a subgroup; multiplication and inversion preserve that order.
The exact commutator identity above gives
[1+T_(>=r),1+T_(>=s)] subset 1+T_(>=r+s). Induction along the definition of
Gamma yields E(Gamma_r) subset 1+T_(>=r). This is only an inclusion; no
Magnus-injectivity theorem, dimension-subgroup converse, rational isolator or
BCH integrality theorem is required.

The same leading-bracket expansion now takes place in the free associative
algebra, where P and Q are not identities. For P, set U=XY-YX, V=XZ-ZX.
In (UV-VU)X-X(UV-VU), a word ending in Z cannot come from a final-X term.
The coefficient of XXYXZ is -1 from -X*(XY)*(XZ); XVU supplies no such word.
For Q, V=[X,[X,Y]]=X^2Y-2XYX+YX^2. The coefficient of XXXYXY is +1
from X*(X^2Y)*(XY) in XVU; neither final-X term nor -XUV contributes.
Therefore E(w) has a nonzero degree-five coefficient and E(v) has a nonzero
degree-six coefficient, proving w not in Gamma_6 and v not in Gamma_7 over Z.
Adding free generators preserves these coefficients verbatim.

An independent finite obstruction avoids formal series entirely. The six-by-six
integer upper unipotent matrices assigned to x_1,x_2,x_3 in the paper give
the exact full value I-E_16 for w. The seven-by-seven assignment gives I+E_17
for v. In UT_d(Z), the subgroup with its first r-1 superdiagonals zero has
commutators adding the minimum superdiagonal distances. Consequently Gamma_d
of UT_d is trivial. The displayed nonidentity images independently exclude
Gamma_6 and Gamma_7. Both complete integer matrix equalities were recomputed
in math_controls.py without importing any parent or native code.

## Automorphisms and the full quotient

If u omits x_t, both substitutions x_t -> x_t*u and x_t -> x_t*u^-1 fix u
and fix every other generator. Their two compositions are identity, proving
an actual discrete free-group automorphism. The spare generator conditions
are exactly n>=4 for w and n>=3 for v; the construction supplies no degree-five
rank-three case.

For the moving generator, the entry displacement is M(x_t)(M(u)-I). It is in
J^6 or J^7, respectively. All other generator entries are fixed exactly.
Entries of the finite generator matrices generate R: positive products use
matrix multiplication and negative generators use the adjugate formula with
determinant one. Polynomial substitution and telescoping products therefore
show identity on all of R/J^(k+1), not merely on the finite list of entries.
The trivial representation is fixed, so the augmentation ideal is preserved.
Restriction proves identity on the full J/J^(k+1), hence D_n(k) membership.

Finally x_t^-1*phi(x_t)=u is nontrivial modulo Gamma_(k+1), by the integral
obstruction. Thus phi is not in A_n(k). No assumptions about stability of the
Andreadakis filtration or ordinary lower central series of IA_n enter.

## Falsification boundaries and priority

The argument refutes the universal equality for each rank n>=3. It does not
claim failure in every degree, the rank-three degree-five case, larger matrix
sizes, trace algebras, or the ordinary IA lower central comparison. The explicit
3x3 control gives P=2E_13, so the matrix-size extension is false. An inner
free-group conjugation fixes all traces but changes component functions in
degree two; therefore prior trace-kernel results do not supply the required
component-kernel implication. Inaccessible journal contents cannot be inferred
from an earlier preprint or abstract.

The inspected Drensky-Findik v1 generic-matrix/completed relative-Lie result
concerns automorphisms of Lie algebras and their completions. The inspected
Shestakov-Umirbaev v2 algebra IA filtration and lifting/wildness statements
(PDF pp.6,19,21-22) likewise act on free algebras and PI quotients. Neither
selected statement itself gives a discrete free-group automorphism or an
actual power of this component augmentation ideal. An attempted identification
without those bridges transfers the central issue and is unsupported.
Conversely, furnishing the bridge here does not prove that no earlier author
furnished it. The current paper's bounded historical wording is essential.

The fresh controls check arbitrary symbolic M2 identities, the two integral
coefficients, both full UT witnesses, all4,096 triples in M2(F2),128 distinct
triples over F2[epsilon]/epsilon^2 and128 over Z/4, plus the failed3x3
extension. These are exact corroboration of the written proof, not a formal
proof certificate, firstness certificate or human peer review.

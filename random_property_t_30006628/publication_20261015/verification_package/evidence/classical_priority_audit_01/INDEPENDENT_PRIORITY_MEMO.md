# Independent priority implication memo

Written 2026-10-15 during the assigned classical audit, before reading any other family's verdict. This memo records my own source comparisons and deductions, not a new search for an unfinished central proof. The starting candidate is `source/PROOF.md` in the parent audit directory.

The exact target is fixed n >= 3, fixed inverse-closed finite generating alphabets in arbitrary nontrivial finitely generated factors, uniform cyclic syllable normal words, floor(Z_l^d) independent samples with replacement, fixed 1/3 < d < 1, and all sufficiently large integer lengths asymptotically. I have not replaced it by ordinary word-metric sampling, or by a question concerning all n >= 2.

Four priority attacks matter independently.

1. **Classical block transfer.** The criterion and transfer of property (T) are classical. Drutu--Mackay's actual published Lemma 12.2 already uses the common-suffix identity `(aw)(b^-1 w)^-1 = ab`. The candidate's additional task is choosing suffixes that remain in the inverse-fixed-point-deleted syllable alphabet. That extra task is substantive; the common-suffix mechanism itself is not new.
2. **All integer lengths.** Ashcroft's June 2021 thesis and actual published GGD 17 (2023) paper treat strict lengths through all residues with almost-equal blocks. The candidate's all-length construction has this clear antecedent. The new calculation, if accepted, concerns the different weighted syllable law; there is no first general all-length reduction here.
3. **Same-model strategy.** Montee--Steenbock's ESI Scientific Report 2024, p. 78, already proposes splitting relators respecting syllables and proving a spectral gap/transfer for their Model 2, citing Ashcroft 2202.12318. Thus the same-model plan has an explicit prior-project antecedent; the report's exact first public release date has not been verified. That report does not supply its proposed gap and transfer proofs.
4. **Hyperbolic/Markov generality.** Ashcroft 2202.12318v2 has a general deterministic language criterion, but its automatic application to random quotients uses a maximal component of a geodesic combing and an annular distribution. Those requirements cannot be omitted just because the syllable language has a primitive matrix. Its positive-weight random graph models also do not encode the new factor-forbidden pairs without additional analysis.

**Own deductions checking the strength of these attacks.**

Replacement is not a genuine obstruction to priority transfer: if M = floor(Z^d), the lost number of distinct sampled relators is at most the number of coincident pairs, whose expectation is <= M^2/(2Z). Markov gives P(distinct < M/2) <= M/Z -> 0. For any d' < d, eventually M/2 >= Z^d'. The first distinct samples are uniformly ordered without replacement. Hence any monotone quotient property known at d' on the *same support* transfers to replacement sampling at d. One must not claim newness merely from this convention.

Support is a genuine missing hypothesis for the proposed direct use of ordinary free-group theorems. In Z * Z * Z with radius-one alphabets {a_i, a_i^-1}, the syllable transition matrix has eigenvalues 4,-2,-2,0,0,0, while the ordinary free-reduction transition matrix has eigenvalues 5,1,1,1,-1,-1. Thus the two cyclic word counts are 4^l + 2(-2)^l and 5^l + 3 + 2(-1)^l. The relative support decays exponentially. Full-probability events for one law do not automatically hold after conditioning on this rare support; property (T) passes in the direction of *adding* relations, not deleting them.

Arbitrary factors alone are also not an adequate reason to dismiss all hyperbolic results. Given the finite alphabets and their inversion involutions, replace every non-fixed inverse pair by an infinite cyclic generator and every fixed point by an order-two generator, grouping these atomic generators by their old factor. The resulting free product H is virtually free, maps onto the original free product, and preserves this particular syllable sampling law word by word. However, the relators are still sampled from a proper constrained sublanguage of the geodesics of H. In general that sublanguage is not a maximal geodesic-combing component, nor the uniform law on an annulus of H. The reduction therefore does not by itself trigger Ashcroft's probabilistic theorems. Checking a generic deterministic spectral criterion would still require exactly the missing link-gap and degree estimates.

Unequal alphabets cannot simply inherit a uniform 3/4 mean-link gap. For part sizes (m,1,1), the nonzero eigenvalues of the letter adjacency matrix are lambda=(1+sqrt(1+8m))/2, 1-lambda, and -1. Its normalized Perron walk has negative eigenvalue -(1-1/lambda). Combining this with the unequal-length type eigenvalue -1/2 produces positive eigenvalue (1-1/lambda)/2. The resulting model-specific limiting gap is 1/2+1/(2lambda), approaching 1/2 as m grows. At sizes (6,1,1), lambda=4 and that gap is 5/8. This does not disprove the candidate; it identifies a real calculation needed beyond the balanced free-group precedent. A bounded exact-arithmetic witness is included separately.

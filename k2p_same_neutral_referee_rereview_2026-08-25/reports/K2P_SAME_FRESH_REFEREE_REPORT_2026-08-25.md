## 7. Scope and literature audit

The article's scope exclusions at lines 1801–1820 (PDF p. 25) are accurate.
It does not prove mixed-sign classification, stochastic or inheritance
boundaries, singular edges, levels above two, weak-class identifiability,
numerical stability, bit complexity, finite-sample inference, or equality of
complete stochastic images. The weak result establishes only sharpness of the
word “strongly.” I found no covert use of any excluded theorem.

The topology attribution is supported: Huber et al. prove the two underlying
level-two generator shapes and broader quarnet/blob-tree encoding
(<https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01510-5>). Englander et al. version 4
supplies the displayed-quartet and tree-of-blobs separation under JC/K2P; the
submission now explicitly transports its character-sector names into the
present `(0,C,G,T)` convention
(<https://doi.org/10.1101/2025.04.18.649493>). The recent Brits et al. theorem
is level one modulo triangles, not the present strong level-two equivalence
(<https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12919>). Other located level-two results concern
quarnets, quartet distances, JC, or distinguishability rather than this exact

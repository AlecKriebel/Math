## Success criteria

An ACCEPT recommendation requires no remaining load-bearing mathematical,
computational-completeness, or reproducibility defect after independent
falsification.  HOLD means no defeating counterexample is established but a
specific proof, semantic-replay, completeness, or reproducibility blocker
remains.  REJECT requires a counterexample or invalid load-bearing implication
that defeats the stated theorem or central finite classification.

# Five substantive attempts

All five attempts were written on 3 October 2026. Statement recovery, literature review, repository work, verification, and independent review are not counted as additional proof attempts.

1. [Pair injections and coning](attempts/turn_01.md). Proved T≤min(RG,RB,GB), the regime ab≤2c, and the exact common-core reduction. Recorded credited sharp coned K4 examples. General core concentration remained unproved.
2. [Link aggregation](attempts/turn_02.md). Proved the exact multiplicity identity and safe block aggregation. An explicit five-vertex example falsifies the tempting lossless link-resource bound. No global √2 proof resulted.
3. [Color-preserving compression](attempts/turn_03.md). Defined the natural simultaneous occupancy-blocked shift and proved it preserves each color count. A four-edge example for every r destroys all successful sets; four edges are minimal for this failure. The proposed monotonicity is false.
4. [Fractional ownership](attempts/turn_04.md). Disproved a raw squared-degree estimate, then proved a fractional assignment criterion and its exact quadratic dual. Gave an exact optimal ownership certificate for the obstruction example. The universal weighted estimate remains unproved.
5. [Complementary shadows](attempts/turn_05.md). Proved the complement identity, the regime d²a≤2bc, and the full restricted theorem n≤r+2. Small complementary dimension three is closed using two elementary parity/trade lemmas. Arbitrary complementary dimension remains open.

Final outcome: five attempts completed; unrestricted OWR Question 8 remains unresolved here. No counterexample to T²≤2RGB was found. The original exact-count question also remains unresolved.

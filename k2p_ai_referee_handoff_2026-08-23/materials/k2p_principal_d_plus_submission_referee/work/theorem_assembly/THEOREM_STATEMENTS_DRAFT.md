# Draft principal-\(\mathcal D_+\) theorem statements

Every statement labelled **Candidate** below is a target theorem, not an
established conclusion.  The purpose of writing it now is to expose all
quantifiers, domain restrictions, and dependencies before the remaining
certificates are built.

## 1. Model and relation

Let

\[
\mathcal D_+
=\{(s,g):0<s<1,\ 0<g<1,\ g>2s-1\},
\]

and require every inheritance probability to lie strictly between zero and
one.  Each edge has K2P Fourier spectrum \((1,s,g,s)\).

For a leaf-labelled standard semi-directed network \(N\), let
\(\mathcal M_+(N)\) denote its strict stochastic K2P image on this domain.
Write

\[
N\preceq_+N'
\]

when a relatively open subset of a top-dimensional regular stratum of
\(\mathcal M_+(N)\) is contained in \(\mathcal M_+(N')\), with a regular
physical target section over that source germ.  This is source-relative and
directed.  It is stronger than a common point and is not inferred from
Zariski-closure containment.

Write \(N\bowtie_+N'\) when the two images contain a common regular germ that
is relatively open and full-dimensional in both images.

## 3. Candidate local theorem

### Candidate Theorem L — complete bounded strong relation theorem

Let \(H,H'\) be any two labelled, directed, incoming-role-decorated primitive
supports arising from the complete strong level-2 four-/five-port completion
grammar, including every minimum repair, omitted role, and physical port
matching.  Then

\[
H\preceq_+H'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
H\cong H'\ \text{or}\ H\sim_T H',
\]

where \(\sim_T\) is ordinary triangle redirection with the required labelled
boundary transport.

This theorem is **not established**.  It requires both:

1. a graph-derived raw-universe partition with exact dimension upper bounds;
   and
2. closure of all 2,962 five-port restoration child requests for the 997
   current restoration parents, together with unique parent transports and
   coherent probes.

The 36-case direct overlay proves only one scoped input to this theorem.

## 4. Candidate global classification

### Candidate Theorem G — principal-\(\mathcal D_+\) K2P-SAME

Let \(N,N'\) be leaf-labelled binary standard semi-directed strongly
tree-child level-2 networks on the same taxon set.  Then

\[
N\preceq_+N'
\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
\begin{cases}
\operatorname{ToB}(N)=\operatorname{ToB}(N')
  &\text{as labelled decorated trees of blobs},\\
\text{each pair of corresponding factors is labelled-isomorphic}\
\qquad\text{or related by ordinary triangle redirection.}
\end{cases}
\]

In particular, the relation would be symmetric on this class and coincide
with labelled semi-directed isomorphism modulo coherent ordinary triangle
redirection.

This theorem is a **candidate only**.  Its proof may be promoted only after
the gate verifier reports every required necessity and sufficiency layer as
independently replayable and reports zero raw-ledger and restoration gaps.

## 5. Candidate generic-identifiability consequence

### Candidate Corollary I

Outside an explicitly certified proper algebraic/semialgebraic exceptional
subset of each top-dimensional source image, an exact K2P distribution on
\(\mathcal D_+\) determines the labelled standard semi-directed topology
modulo coherent ordinary triangle redirection.

The exceptional set must include, at minimum, singular source points,
vanishing bridge anchors, rank drops in chosen local slices, collisions of
finite target types, and every denominator or boundary factor used by the
restoration and gluing sections.  Merely saying "generic" is insufficient.

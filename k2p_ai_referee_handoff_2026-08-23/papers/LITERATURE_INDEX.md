  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s11538-025-01549-4>.
- Holtgrefe et al., *Characterizing Semi-Directed Phylogenetic Networks and
  Their Multi-Rootable Variants*,
  <https://doi.org/10.1007/s12064-025-00453-8>.

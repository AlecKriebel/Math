# Exceptional d=4 Hecke Yang--Baxter localization v1.1.0

This release is a formal-completeness and provenance revision of the first
public research note. The central matrix and theorem are unchanged.

## What changed

- made the conjugate-linear \(*\)-structure, tower compatibility, and
  quotient-tower injections explicit;
- replaced the informal dimension-three discussion with both exact
  Temperley--Lieb obstructions, each of trace norm \(1/18\);
- formalized the sitewise swap and faithful \((3,2)\)-generalized
  localization as a proposition;
- added the historical non-counterexample consequence and Lechner's
  index-\(4\), standard-braided consequence;
- corrected two equation-label defects;
- added version-specific code and source availability inside the PDF;
- revised the abstract, authorship metadata, AI disclosure, related-work
  section, webpage, and reproducibility instructions;
- extended the dependency-free exact verifier to check the complementary
  dimension-three obstruction and explicitly conjugate the original
  \(16\times16\) matrix into its spectator-factor form.

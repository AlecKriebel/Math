# Impact assessment of the 19 newly published follow-on papers

Inspected Math Puzzles rows **35–53** in the [publication sheet](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1ZljUv5Q98jNXLoHK8WjwrkzSm3dhHC1-7LElcOU7y20/edit?gid=1254632077#gid=1254632077). These are the 19 release-related publications; earlier OWR papers are excluded. Read the actual final manuscripts, confirmed each equals its deposited source archive, and checked that every public Zenodo file checksum matches the corresponding local publication payload. Five independent subject reviewers covered 17 papers; root covered two. Public packages match, but this is an impact assessment rather than a fresh proof audit.

**Impact** answers the user’s conditional question: assuming correctness, genuine novelty of the stated conclusion, and publication, how important is the principal result within its subfield? **Added** separately rates this particular paper’s contribution after crediting acknowledged upstream breakthroughs and established reductions. This distinction is necessary because a 9-level conclusion can be obtained in a 4-level consequence note. Scores are judgments with approximately half a point to one point of calibration uncertainty, not correctness probabilities. No score penalty is applied simply for AI assistance, Zenodo publication or lack of conventional refereeing.

The most substantial added work is sparse finite-field factorization (**7**), the multispecies Vlasov–Maxwell extension (**6.5**), bosonic operational secrecy repair (**6**), and singular metric rigidity (**6**). None of these papers warrants an added-contribution score of 9–10 on this reading. Several of their conclusions would be central breakthroughs if independently new.

## Scope and scoring decisions

- Binary automata: even a cube-root exponential lower bound is superpolynomial and refutes the canonical polynomial simulation target. The nonnegative hafnian result contains the entire unweighted general-graph FPRAS target via zero/one matrices. Thus both hypothetical theorem scores can be 9 while their added-contribution scores are 5 and 4.
- Exact TSP SDP: the new conclusion sharpens already superpolynomial lower bounds to the exponential order. It is a major subfield advance (8), rather than the first obstruction to a polynomial exact SDP.
- Sparse factoring: score the final main output-sensitive sparse theorem (7), not the separately inherited general dense factoring breakthrough (which would warrant 9 if independently new).
- Random-cone threshold: score the final main parameter theorem (5), not the inherited group-ring idempotent counterexample (which would warrant approximately 9 if independently new).
- Cubic metric rigidity: score the added singular metric-rigidity extension (7), not an allegedly first complex compactification comparison already established by prior work.
- Cost: distinguish the relation-level strict inequality from infimum cost over all group actions. Bosonic private capacity: distinguish the repaired GEN convention from the refuted literal convention. Rational-point undecidability: dimensions and input sizes vary. These are material mathematical limits, not generic caveats.

# Smooth hypersurfaces beyond the Chevalley-Warning range

Problem 20000125 · AIM-ALGEBRAIC_GEOMETRY-0125 · AIM Problem 9.5

## Result and attribution

For every prime p, every n≥2, and every d≥n+1, the complete proof constructs a geometrically smooth, geometrically integral degree-d hypersurface in projective n-space over F_p whose F_p-point count is not 1 modulo p. Finite deterministic coefficient selection is included.

The theorem and construction are attributed to Alper Ferudun, *Smooth Hypersurfaces Beyond the Chevalley-Warning Range over Prime Fields*, 6 September 2026, version 1.0: https://eulersolve.org/papers/aim-algebraic-geometry-0125/ ; DOI https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22514087 . The manuscript is an unrefereed, AI-assisted preprint. This packet records an independently reasoned mathematical audit, not a novelty claim, external acceptance, human peer review, or formal proof-assistant certification.

The literal [AIM Problem 9.5](http://aimpl.org/ratsubvarpos/9/) has no explicit smoothness hypothesis and asks a numerical-or-rational-connectedness disjunction. Its unsmoothed numerical alternative has norm-form witnesses. The stronger smooth theorem is distinct and also addresses the [2015 smooth-hypersurface question](https://mathoverflow.net/questions/220163). The proof retains the norm-form caveat and the n=1 smooth point-free example, which is not geometrically integral; no n=0 or all-extension-fields claim is made.

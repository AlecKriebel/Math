## Result and attribution

Adds a self-contained proof, mathematical audit, acceptance report, README, public source metadata, and artifact manifest for problem 30003386 (OWR-15212-005). Mutual common probability-kernel domination on the stated compact standard Borel interval/square spaces does not force deterministic componentwise maps in both directions: the reverse projection exists, but a forward map does not.

This is a checked rederivation of Alper Ferudun's prior interval/square construction, Theorem 3.1(1), pp. 4–5, in his unrefereed preprint dated 2 October 2026, [DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23107180](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23107180). No novelty or priority claim is made. The original question is Gershon Wolansky's *Multiphase Optimal Transport*, Oberwolfach Report 7/2017, printed p. 388, [DOI 10.4171/OWR/2017/7](https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2017/7).

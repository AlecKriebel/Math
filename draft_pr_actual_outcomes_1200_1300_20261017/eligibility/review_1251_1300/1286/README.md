# A rectifiable Anosov limit circle that is not C¹

This packet gives an affirmative answer to Tengren Zhang’s Problem 10 in Oberwolfach Reports 40/2018, printed p. 2527. For every closed oriented surface of genus at least two, it constructs a projective-Anosov representation into PGL(4, ℝ) with a rectifiable embedded limit circle that is not C¹, and hence is not C^{1,α} for any α > 0.

## Construction and scope

Choose two same-orientation marked cocompact Fuchsian representations whose translation lengths on one pants cuff are 1 and 2. Their tensor representation has first singular-value logarithmic gap 2 min(a₁,a₂), giving uniform domination in ordinary word length. The limit circle is the Segre image of the graph of the increasing boundary conjugacy. Its length is at most 2π in the stated round-metric normalization. A fixed-point secant estimate, the dense boundary orbit, and continuity of a hypothetical tangent field exclude C¹ embedded-submanifold regularity.

The image preserves a form of signature (2,2). No full-PGL Zariski-density, fixed-dimension generality, higher-root gap, or novelty claim is made. Those conditions are not part of the printed existence question.

## Result and attribution

This draft publishes the seven-file proof and acceptance packet for **30004145 / OWR-16933-007**.

For a smooth projective geometrically connected real genus-one curve with real points, fix a positive-degree unordered reduced totally real divisor, its linear-equivalence class, and its support counts on the real components. The resulting nonempty fixed-distribution component strongly deformation retracts onto a torus of dimension k − 1, where k is the number of occupied real components. Its universal cover is contractible and its fundamental group is free abelian of rank k − 1.

The nine-point plane cubic fundamental-group dichotomy was already stated by **Oleg Viro**, with initial examples and observations credited to **Ayşegül Öztürkalan**, in [Round dances of points on a curve](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46800), printed p. 1289 / PDF p. 45. This packet supplies an independent genus-one verification and makes no novelty or higher-genus claim.

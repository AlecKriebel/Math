## Accepted bounded result
For a smooth map from a second-countable 2n-dimensional manifold to R^n with smooth positive volume and locally finite pushforward, the singular part is exactly the pushforward of volume restricted to the critical set. In the finite-volume case, its mass equals the critical-set volume.

The conditional spectral criterion uses the exact multiplicity-weighted limiting joint eigenvalue measure. The stated compact Kähler application is confined to positive prequantum scalar Berezin–Toeplitz families that are exactly commuting and self-adjoint, with O(1/k) operator-norm perturbations and the cited scalar product and trace estimates.

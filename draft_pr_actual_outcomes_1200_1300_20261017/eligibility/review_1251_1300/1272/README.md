# Rational closure naturality for Problem 8 24

This packet gives a source-qualified affirmative answer to the rational-closure formulation of Hayman–Lingham Problem 8.24, record 2308024. Under the stated weight assumptions, the character space of the abstract weighted completion of rational functions with poles off a nonempty compact plane set is canonically homeomorphic to that set. The [proof](PROOF.md) is accepted by the separate [AI-assisted audit](ACCEPTANCE_REPORT.md) with the qualifications below.

## Exact scope

- Arbitrary nonempty compact plane sets are allowed for the abstract rational-function or rational-germ completion. The argument proves finiteness and definiteness of the norm, continuity of multiplication, automatic continuity of characters, spectral localization, and the homeomorphism.
- The completion need not be an injectively realized algebra of value functions. Its radical is exactly the kernel of the value map. The proof includes the singleton example showing why this qualification is necessary.
- For an actual Banach function algebra, the conclusion requires the stated dense rational subalgebra and continuous evaluations. This includes the rational closure inside a complete Dales–Davie algebra on a perfect compact set.
- The full Dales–Davie algebra is outside the claim whenever rational density has not been established. No density theorem for that larger algebra is supplied.
- The empty compact set is covered only by the explicit zero-supremum and zero-seminorm quotient convention in the proof.

## Established result and source limits

This is an elementary reconstruction of an established rational-density naturality argument. Abtahi–Honary and Feinstein–Kamowitz provide the inspected literature support and credit Dales–Davie (1973). The 1973 full text was not inspected. There is no novelty or priority claim.

The inspected problem source is [Hayman–Lingham arXiv:1809.07200v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1809.07200v2), PDF pages 196–197, printed pages 195–196. Its phrase “poles of X” is transparently corrected to “poles off X”. The exact wording of the 2019 published edition was not independently inspected; only its chapter metadata and subscription preview were available. The packet does not claim author confirmation of an interpretation beyond the inspected version.

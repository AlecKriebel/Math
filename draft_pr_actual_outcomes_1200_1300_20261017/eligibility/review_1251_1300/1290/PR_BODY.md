## Scoped result

Publish an attributed reconstruction of the known obstruction to an unrestricted nonreversible L2-gap extension of positive coarse Ricci curvature (Problem 4000005, AMR-039-0005).

The Bernoulli-prepending example retains the Gong–Wu attribution reported by Gong–Liu–Wen. It has coarse curvature exactly 1−a, mean-zero L2 spectrum the closed unit disk, spectral radius one, and every positive power of operator norm one, alongside strong decay for each fixed mean-zero observable. The proof distinguishes the three energy quantities and topological irreducibility from stationary-measure or Harris irreducibility.

The irreducible, aperiodic length-L truncations have mean-zero spectral radius zero and exact ordinary Poincaré gap 1−cos(π/(L+1)). Their finite-rank realizations converge strongly, without operator-norm convergence or a uniform exponential-decay prefactor.

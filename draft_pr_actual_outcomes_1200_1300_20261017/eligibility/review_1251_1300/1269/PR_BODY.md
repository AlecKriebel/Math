## Result and exact scope

Publishes the AI-assisted proof, separate mathematical audit, and scoped acceptance for problem **20002813 / AIM-REPRESENTATION_THEORY-0085**.

For the standard two-sided-orbit supercharacter theory of G = 1 + J, with J any finite-dimensional associative nilpotent algebra over any finite field, the canonical degree-weighted supercharacters equal the unnormalized additive Fourier orbit sums. Their pointwise product coefficients count additive decompositions of **one fixed target functional**, with a count constant on its two-sided orbit. These coefficients are nonnegative integers.

This settles only the standard algebra-group subquestion in the remark to [AIM Problem 1.1](https://aimath.org/WWN/supercharacters/supercharacters.pdf). The broader classification remains unresolved. The theorem does not cover arbitrary supercharacter theories on the same group, induction products, or Hopf multiplication. Conventional prefactor-normalized supercharacters are distinct from the canonical normalization used here.

Theorems A–D are accepted without mathematical correction:

- For nilpotent L with dim L' <= 2, both nilpotency classes agree in every positive characteristic, with the exact four-case formula 1, p, 2p-1, 3p-2.
- For class-two L with finite-dimensional L' and [x,L]=L' for some x, both classes are 1+(p-1)dim L'. The proved direct-sum closure and arbitrary central direct summands are retained.
- For dim L' <= 1 and p>2, both derived lengths agree, with the exact abelian, central and noncentral formulas. The noncentral formula and equality of the entire derived series also hold at p=2.
- Any counterexample to either specified question has a finite-dimensional example over a genuinely finite field of the same characteristic preserving the exact unequal pair of invariants. This is an unbounded reduction; a finite extension of the prime field may be required.

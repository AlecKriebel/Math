## What is proved

For every n≥3, equal pairs of path-rank arrays of U and M/U fail to classify Aut_Q(M)-orbits in the exact maximal-flat flag fiber. The explicit coordinate examples have incomparable orbit closures, separated by intersection dimensions with two intrinsic subspaces. The result concerns embeddings in a fixed complex representation, not varying ambient map tuples.

The n≤2 orbit classification and closure order are complete. For n≤4, finiteness is a corollary of Escolar–Hiraoka's existing commutative-ladder finite-type theorem, with orientation and field conventions made explicit. The general n≥5 finiteness question, arbitrary-n parametrization and closure relation remain unresolved by this work. Explicit n=3,4 orbit lists and closure orders are not provided.

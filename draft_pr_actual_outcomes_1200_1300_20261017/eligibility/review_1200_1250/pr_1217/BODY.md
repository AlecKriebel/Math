## Accepted existing proof

This draft adds a source-credited, AI-assisted, unrefereed proof-audit edition for 30002821 / OWR-13498-011, Optimality of Greedy Circle Packings. Alper Ferudun's version 1.1, dated September 30, 2026, proves the exact Propp-Kenyon target: the complete greedy line-tangent packing attains area pi*(zeta(3)/zeta(4)-1) in the unit tangent-disk gap. The audit identifies no missing step or theorem-changing repair.

The complete authored reconstruction appears once in PROOF.md, with a separate review roadmap in AUDIT.md. It retains the exact geometry, complete disjoint primitive family, all-degree coefficient positivity, compact finite maximizers, noncircular chain and cardinality inductions, deformation endpoints including k=2, countable and nonnegative integral limits, radius powers alpha>1, and scoped positive scale superpositions. Every written formula and example is retained, including the counterexample to arbitrary nondecreasing weights.

Attribution: Alper Ferudun, *The Ford-Circle Packing Has Maximum Area: An Answer to a Question of Propp and Kenyon*, version 1.1: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23049959. Original problem: https://doi.org/10.4171/OWR/2015/13, Problem 7, printed page 722.

The proof source publicly describes itself as unrefereed and AI-assisted. Acceptance is an independent internal AI audit, not external human peer review, journal acceptance, formal certification, novelty, priority or uniqueness. No nontangent-boundary, arbitrary-weight or finite alpha<=1 extension is claimed.

## Accepted scope and remaining gap

The corrected report proves an exact one-seed reformulation using fresh disjoint copies; elementary edge and final-edge lower bounds; a self-contained potential criterion and conservative finite-board/automorphism bound; and restricted fixed-pool, full-activation and unprepared-common-neighbour obstructions. It preserves the distinction between forcing an unspecified family member and a prescribed isomorphism type. Connectedness is not assumed. It constructs no subexponential seed sequence and proves no universal lower bound excluding one.

A single clique is an exponentially expensive seed under the proved lower bound. That lower bound cannot be multiplied by the number of components, and faster batched constructions remain possible. The fixed-pool/full-activation restrictions are essential and are not attributed to the earlier adaptive method.

The original source asks for subexponential dependence with d in the exponent. The inspected 2008 factor-1/4 versus factor-1/2 derivation caution is not a refutation of the stronger theorem. Gebauer's cited clique bound, followed by corrected amplification, yields only the exponential benchmark 2^((2/3+o(1))d). Its complete proof was not independently certified.

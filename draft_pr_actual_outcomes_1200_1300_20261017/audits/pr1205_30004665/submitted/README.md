## Result

For G = Spin_8(C) and reduced word (1,2,3,2,4,2,1,2,3,2,4,2), the canonical potential W has 22 distinct combined monomials, all with coefficient 1. Every inequality is essential, yet two monomials have X_6 exponent -2. This disproves the original OWR2021 converse in its specified canonical W coordinates.

The chamber-ansatz pullback has only exponents -1, 0, 1. Its later conjecture, the coefficient-based conjecture, and the conclusion of the published sufficient theorem are not refuted. The exponent-bound equivalence asserted on published page 1046 is false; no general replacement proof of that theorem is supplied. The older arXiv example-word discrepancy was already corrected in the published paper and is not a new error.

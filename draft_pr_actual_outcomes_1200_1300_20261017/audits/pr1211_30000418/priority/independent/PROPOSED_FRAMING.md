# Proposed paper wording after the independent priority audit

Suggested title: **A five-list obstruction on an eleven-vertex tree**

## Abstract

We exhibit an eleven-vertex tree T whose ordinary L(2,1) initial-segment palette-size parameter is5, whereas its arbitrary-list parameter is6. An explicit assignment of five-element positive-integer lists is infeasible, as shown by a short forcing argument. An ordinary labeling with the common palette {1,…,5} and the known Δ+3 tree list upper bound give the exact values. The construction contradicts the universal equality stated as Conjecture4 in Kohl's2006 Oberwolfach contribution and as Conjecture4.4 in her dissertation. Pendant-path extensions give infinitely many examples with unbounded diameter. No claim of historical priority, minimal order, or present openness is made.

## Attribution paragraph near the upper-bound proof

The upper bound χℓ^(2,1)(T)≤Δ(T)+3 is known: Kohl's2006 Oberwolfach contribution attributes the more general bound χℓ^(d,s)(T)≤sΔ(T)+2d−1 to Voigt, citing Kohl, Schreyer, Tuza and Voigt, *List version of L(d,s)-labelings*, Theoretical Computer Science349(1)(2005),92–98. We include the elementary greedy proof to make the exact value for the exhibited tree self-contained. [OWR, p.416 and ref.8 on417](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46037?nt=1), [TCS article](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.tcs.2005.09.032).

## Related-work paragraph

Separations between ordinary and list L(2,1) parameters on general graphs precede this construction. Fiala and Škrekovski's institution-hosted preprint *List Distance Labelings of Graphs*, listed as KAM-DIMATIA2001-530 and ITI2001-28, gives a ten-vertex graph with ordinary palette size5 and an infeasible five-list assignment in Figure4 on printed page14. That graph has cycles. The present statement concerns a tree and contradicts Kohl's universal tree equality. This comparison does not establish first-resolution priority for the tree problem. [2001 preprint](https://kam.mff.cuni.cz/kamserie/clanky/2001/s530.pdf), [series record](https://iti.mff.cuni.cz/series/2001.html), [Kohl conjecture](https://ems.press/content/serial-article-files/46037?nt=1).

## Historical-status note

The conjecture identification refers to the statement printed in the2006 sources. A bounded audit of original sources, author records, citation trails, later papers and revised surveys did not verify an earlier negative resolution for trees. Relevant inaccessible or unlocated full texts remain, so this is not a certificate that the conjecture was open immediately before this work or that the construction is historically new. The October2026 editorial edition dates are distinct from dates of earlier research and source publication.

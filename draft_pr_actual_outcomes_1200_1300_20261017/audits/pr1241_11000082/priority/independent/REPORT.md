## Exact comparison boundary

For each fixed closed genusg>=2 and fixed Johnson depthk>=3, the candidate asserts that for everyN there is a dilatation with at leastN ambient orientation-preserving Mod_g conjugacy classes represented in I_g(k). Genus, depth, and the constructed rank-two subgroup stay fixed beforeN varies. All pseudo-Anosovs are counted, including proper powers; one fixed value need not have infinite multiplicity.

[Farb's author-hosted volume](https://www.math.uchicago.edu/~farb/papers/mcgbook.pdf), printedp51/PDF58, asks precisely the bounded-multiplicity question. Its immediate discussion supplies the ambient Mod_g convention and the known Torelli/Johnson-kernel cases. Its separate Question7.7 concerns simple closed Teichmüller geodesics. Indexing is checked at printedp14/PDF21: I_g(1) is Torelli and I_g(2) is the Johnson kernel. The author-hosted standalone June18,2006 file retains Question7.6 (physicalPDF45, printed46). Neither document is a 2026 status update.

The positive public note matches every material target parameter. Its theorem coversk>=1, so it includes eachk>=3. It explicitly counts ambient classes and, in its proof, fixesg,k and the subgroup before increasing the trace-family size. It does not resolve the simple-length question or prove primitivity of its general families. The inspected orientation-preserving part uses the same mathematical architecture as the candidate: a separating filling pair, square tiling and affine twists, a signed saddle-holonomy lattice, the unique invariant disk to constrain ambient conjugators, bounded conjugacy fusion in a virtually free integral derivative group, a fixed deep rank-two subgroup, and equal-trace words. The trace-word construction and the fusion lemma are written differently. Architectural similarity alone proves no genealogy.

## Earlier printed ingredients versus an explicit resolution

The classical mechanism was available long before2026. [Horowitz1972](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/Pdf/horowitz.pdf), complete Example8.2 on printed648–649/PDF14–15, constructs arbitrarily large universal equal-character families of free-group words, distinct up to intrinsic conjugacy. It does not itself mention Johnson filtration or ambient mapping-class conjugacy. Thus citing Horowitz alone as a printed answer to Question7.6 would conflate a central ingredient with the target theorem.

[Leininger2004](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0304163), Theorems3.1–3.2/PDF14–15 and Proposition6.4/PDF26, supplies affine stabilizers, hyperbolic derivatives and uniqueness of an invariant Teichmüller disk, plus the two-twist classification. Those passages supply the geometric source interfaces, without an explicit deep-Johnson multiplicity theorem. [Farb–Leininger–Margalit2008](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0603675), introduction and Proposition4.8/PDF20, supplies deep lower-central words and their Johnson containment to prove bounds for the **least** dilatation. It does not state the target multiplicity conclusion in the inspected passages; a whole-text keyword scan found no multiplicity terminology. The 2006 question already describes unbounded multiplicity forI_g andI_g(2); these cases cannot be relabeled as allfixedk>=3.

[Aougab–Taylor](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1510.00995), Lemma2.2/PDF3–4, gives separating filling pairs in every relevant genus. Section4/PDF8–9 proves infinitely many ambient primitive ratio optimizers in a disk. Theorem5.1/PDF10 constructs ratio optimizers arbitrarily deep in the Johnson filtration. None of those inspected statements makes their dilatations equal or provides arbitrary multiplicity at a common value. Having infinitely many ambient classes, even in a fixed disk, is a distinct quantifier pattern.

The especially close title [Malestein–Putman, *Pseudo-Anosov dilatations and the Johnson filtration*](https://academicweb.nd.edu/~andyp/papers/PAJohnson.pdf), author-hosted version datedFebruary2,2015, was followed beyond discovery metadata. TheoremA/PDF2 gives an explicit lower bound and its remark improves an upper bound using shorter lower-central words. Its TheoremB concerns intersections of curves agreeing in nilpotent quotients. This is not an equal-dilatation multiplicity theorem. [Malestein2007](https://msp.org/agt/2007/7-4/agt-v7-n4-p08-s.pdf), PDF1–2, gives a pseudo-Anosov criterion for a surface with one boundary component from nilpotent-quotient data, and expressly distinguishes that detection problem from estimating dilatations.

Consequently, one may describe a **2026 assembly of old ingredients with an explicit ambient-conjugacy step**, rather than attribute that assembled theorem to any single old paper inspected here. This is an inference from the source comparison, not evidence that the assembly first occurred in2026. The Ferudun note is a positively located printed assembly, already available at the cutoff. No earlier explicit exact theorem was located in this bounded inspection.

## Trace and spectrum genealogy

The following primary texts were followed from the trace literature: [Kapovich–Levitt–Schupp–Shpilrain](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0409284), section5/PDF15–17, gives universal trace-polynomial and reversal identities and relates character equivalence to free-group translation equivalence; [Masters](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9812069), Theorem3.1/PDF5–6 and Lemma4.1/PDF6, applies the Horowitz mechanism to hyperbolic surface/three-manifold spectra and deals with nonconjugacy there. Neither passage is a theorem about ambientMod_g classes inside every fixed deep Johnson subgroup. [Anderson's author survey](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/29876/1/horowitz.pdf), PDF1 and6, documents the Horowitz–Randol–Masters genealogy and Fricke substitution mechanism. The original [Randol1980 officialPDF](https://www.ams.org/journals/proc/1980-078-03/S0002-9939-1980-0553396-1/S0002-9939-1980-0553396-1.pdf) could not be retrieved; its body was **not** inspected, and secondhand excerpts were not promoted to original-source proof inspection.

[Birman–Brinkmann–Kawamuro](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1001.5094), Example5.1/PDF24–26, constructs once-punctured genus-two examples with equal dilatation and distinguishes a pair using orientability and polynomial invariants. It is relevant positive evidence that coincident dilatations need not mean conjugacy; it gives no arbitrary-size all-depth closed-surface theorem in the inspected example. [Leininger's *Equivalent curves in surfaces*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0302280), opening problem, concerns homotopy classes of curves on a surface and lengths across metrics. The classes are surface-group classes, not pseudo-Anosov mapping-class classes. [Baik–Rafiqi–Wu](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1411.1385), opening scope, studies realization of Perron numbers as dilatations, without establishing the present multiplicity restrictions.

## Recent and dissertation checks

[Dahmani–Mj, *Class number for pseudo-Anosovs*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.12824), definition and Theorem1.2/PDF2–3, bounds the number of automorphism-conjugacy classes inside a single **commensurated-conjugacy** class, uniformly in the automorphism for fixed genus. Equal dilatation is necessary for commensurated conjugacy; the converse is not established there. This bound therefore cannot be substituted for a bound on the whole equal-dilatation fiber. Its genus-three pair is not the target all-depth construction. Primary arXiv metadata givesv4 August31,2024, journal reference2025, not a new2026 theorem.

[Jeffreys, *Meanders, hyperelliptic pillowcase covers, and the Johnson filtration*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2210.11332), Theorem1.4/PDF4 and section5/PDF25, gives ratio-optimizing pseudo-Anosovs arbitrarily deep in the filtration, with prescribed hyperelliptic component behavior. It does not assert common dilatation for unbounded families. Primary arXiv history confirmsv1 October20,2022 andv2 February25,2026; it also gives the journal reference Geometriae Dedicata218,93(2024). The2026 revision date and2024 journal date are preserved separately. The prior [2019/2021 square-tiled-surface paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1906.02016), Theorem1.2/PDF3, gives infinitely many primitive ratio optimizers in Abelian strata, which again is a distinct statement.

Jeffreys's [2020 Glasgow thesis](https://theses.gla.ac.uk/81526/), printed122/PDF133, was followed to the body. Its Question7.4 is the component-realization problem for ratio optimizers deep in the Johnson filtration; this is a different question number and question from Farb7.6. The institutional record dates the thesis July2020, deposited July20,2020. Only the recorded relevant thesis page and title/contents were inspected; this is not a184page thesis audit.

Farb's current author homepage, papers page and short books page were inspected; the linked2006 standalone chapter and final online volume were retained. No separately labeled answer/update to7.6 was found in those inspected listings. Margalit's [later problem list](https://margalit.droppages.net/papers/problems.pdf) was retained, its introduction and Johnson-filtration section consulted, and its extracted full text searched.

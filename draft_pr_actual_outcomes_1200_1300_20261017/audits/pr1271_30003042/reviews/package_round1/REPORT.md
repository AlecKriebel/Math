  2^16 ordered squarefree Clifford monomials have distinct half-valuation

  least value, and their nonzero coefficient prevents cancellation. This is
  a no-zero-divisors proof for the central simple even Clifford algebra,
  giving degree 256; it is not an inference from numerical experiments.
* With p=product(ti), psi=phi perpendicular <-p> has signed discriminant p^2.
  The codimension-one Clifford and maximal-Grassmannian identifications give
  C+(psi)=C0(phi) and X=OG9+(psi), with either component permissible. Over
  the specified ordinary SB function field the Clifford algebra splits.
  There is no assertion that the form becomes hyperbolic.

## Exact target and prior coverage

The target retains the original field and class: phi/F has dimension 17 and ind C0(phi) = 256; S is the ordinary Severi–Brauer variety of C0(phi); K = F(S); and X = OG8(phi). It asks whether e8 fails to belong to gamma5 K(X_K). The mathematical gate proves an instance of the opposite membership. It does not prove failure for every n >= 8, the exact gamma depth, an optimal replacement, or exact index 8 for every member of the older broader generic class.

The source chain is pointwise, not merely a contrapositive between universal assertions:

1. In the original construction, take an 18-dimensional form psi with trivial discriminant and division half-Clifford algebra A of degree 256. Over K = F(SB(A)), q = psi_K has trivial Clifford invariant. A nondegenerate codimension-one subform phi has C0(phi) isomorphic to A, and its maximal Grassmannian identifies with a component for psi. These are the definitions and identifications in *Around 16-dimensional quadratic forms*, §§1 and 4.
2. Devyatov–Karpenko–Merkurjev, Proposition 2.1, gives inclusion of the generic Chow image in the image for an arbitrary torsor. In top codimension, ind(E_gen/P) Z is contained in ind(E/P) Z, hence ind(E/P) divides ind(E_gen/P). The generic index bound 8 therefore applies to this older Clifford-splitting model. It is unnecessary to equate the two definitions of “generic.”
3. At d = 18, r = floor(d/4) = 4, index dividing 8 supplies transfers of the projective 3-space class and yields 8 l3 in T13 K(Y_K), where Y is the 16-dimensional quadric. The integral relation 8 l3 = l0 + h15 + 2 h14 + 4 h13 gives l0 in T13. Incidence with the rank-9 tautological bundle lowers the topological index by 8, giving e8 in T5. Theorem 4.3 then gives T5 = gamma5 in the original model. This is the specialization of the complete original implication on final-preprint p. 12.

Thus the target conclusion has exact prior theorem coverage **by composition**. All mathematical ingredients are prior results; spelling out their application is an explanatory/status contribution. The 2022 journal summary already prints the exceptional index values while referring to the 2021 preprint for their computation. For a conservative all-journal proof chain, the complete computation appeared in *Publicacions Matemàtiques* 67 (2023), 743–756. No claim of the earliest public combined inference is justified.

## Later analytic argument: exact role

The analytic paper's Theorem 4.1 proves i_t = t - 1 for d = 17, with t = 4. Its proof detects a nonzero coefficient of e8 in a mod-2 orbit product; it uses the monomial x7^4 x8^4 after the stated symmetric-polynomial reductions. Corollary 4.4 gives, for d = 18, exponents (i0,...,i8) = (0,1,2,3,3,3,4,4,4). In particular i4 = 3, so the fourth-Grassmannian index is 8. The last three terms use the source's n = 8 convention; the two maximal components have the same index.

The theorem is about isotropy indices, not the special class in the original K-ring. Obtaining the exact original gamma conclusion still uses the original implication and its field/Grassmannian identifications. The analytic paper is credited as a prior proof of the sufficient index input, not as a directly printed e8-in-gamma5 theorem. This family did not rerun source-author programs. Mathematical validity and new finite certificates belong to the completed mathematical gate, not to a newly claimed priority proof here.

## Claim, assumptions, and success criteria

For differentiation D on the complex Fréchet space H(C), with locally uniform convergence, the current target is a dense B contained in HC(D) such that each f in B obeys |(pf)^(n)(0)| → infinity for every nonconstant complex polynomial p. The same f must work for every p, including complex coefficients and degree one; no n-threshold uniform in p is required. This would disprove universal pointwise preservation, rather than composition P(f), differential action p(D)f, or a generic selected-family assertion. Finite tests cannot certify any of these infinite-dimensional assertions.

The retained finer target is: for every prescribed HC(D) input f, fixed nonconstant p and neighborhood W, there is h with D^n h → 0, f+h in W and p(f+h) not HC(D). The original theorem fixes p before constructing h. Its monomial special case uses one function for all positive monomials. The current simultaneous theorem uses a different argument and does not exchange the original quantifiers.

## Annular spaces and uniform tails

Set ell_n=(n+1)^(-1/2), u_n=log(n+e). The inequalities 0<ell_n≤1≤u_n≤n+1 are valid for all n≥0; at n=0 both bounds equal one. An eventually u_n-bounded jet sequence defines an entire exponential generating series: on every disk of radius r its tail is dominated by sum (n+1)r^n/n!, a convergent positive series. The finitely many unrestricted jets below M do not affect convergence or the uniform high-tail bound.

Derivative evaluation is continuous by the Cauchy estimates, so X_M is closed in a complete compatible metric on H(C); the baseline ell_n series proves nonemptiness. Equivalently X_M is homeomorphic to C^M times the product of the closed annuli: finite-coordinate convergence implies compact-open convergence after truncating a common factorial tail, and the inverse consists of continuous derivative evaluations. X_0 is a compact annulus product, with a unit-circle coordinate at zero. There is no assumption that D maps X_M to itself.

## High block density: zero, arbitrary size, complex phase

For any finite complex coefficient list, however large, u_(N+k) eventually exceeds every required fixed magnitude; N may be extremely large, but only existence is required. This rules out a missing tail or a difficulty approximating zero forced by the positive coefficient floor.

## Leibniz dominance and scalar nondensity

For n≥M+d, Leibniz gives sum_(j=0)^d c_j(n)_j a_(n-j), with (n)_j=n!/(n-j)!. There is no extra j!; it cancels against p^(j)(0)=j!c_j. At n≥2d, the leading modulus is at least |c_d|2^(-d)n^d/sqrt(n+1). The sum of all lower moduli is at most C_p n^(d-1)log(n+e). Their ratio is bounded by a fixed p-dependent constant times sqrt(n+1)log(n+e)/n, which tends to zero. Reverse triangle therefore yields an eventual positive multiple of n^(d-1/2), which tends to infinity. For d=1 this is the direct comparison sqrt(n) versus log(n). Zero lower coefficients, tiny nonzero leading coefficient, arbitrarily huge fixed lower coefficients, and every complex phase are covered; they affect only the onset.

The f-bounds are established without p. For each f the deterministic estimate applies to an arbitrary polynomial chosen afterward; no countable rational-polynomial surrogate and no uncountable Baire/probability intersection is used. An escaping scalar tail misses the unit disk; choose a small subdisk of that disk avoiding the finitely many early evaluations, including n=0. The whole scalar sequence is not dense. Continuous surjective evaluation would send a dense derivative orbit to a dense subset of C, so pf cannot be HC(D).

Approximate an arbitrary entire target by a polynomial P, then append the baseline after a sufficiently late M>deg P; it lies in X_M and is close on the desired disk. Relative HC density completes ambient density. Every X_M is closed and contains no polynomial, so density of polynomials forces empty ambient interior. Thus this particular B is meagre. This does not conflict with a residual common-preservation class. Scaling by a nonzero constant is a homeomorphism commuting with D; zero fails, and the constructed counterexamples defeat every nonconstant multiplier. The preserver classification follows.

## Original fixed-polynomial null-orbit proof

The elementary Baire existence proof using high zero-jet primitives is correct, and polynomial targets are dense. A finite prefix can be removed from a dense orbit because every nonempty open subset of H(C) contains infinitely many small constant translates and the finite removed set is closed. Thus increasing approximation indices exist, and adding a locally null derivative orbit preserves HC(D).

At a root alpha of p with multiplicity m≥1, write p(alpha+w)=sum_(j=m)^d q_j w^j. The coefficient A_k=q_m(k+m)!/k! is nonzero; all other correction indices in the (k+m)-th product derivative are strictly below k. If the determined scalar S_k is already outside the eta disk keep c_k=0; otherwise c_k=(eta-S_k)/A_k makes that scalar exactly the positive eta. Then |c_k|≤2eta k!/(|q_m|(k+m)!), independently of the large prior coefficients or cancellation in S_k. This tends to zero, is bounded uniformly by 2eta/(|q_m|m!), and gives an entire h with ||D^n h||_(R,alpha)≤exp(R)sup_(k≥n)|c_k|→0. Later corrections cannot alter already fixed product derivatives. The exact convergent Taylor-product formula gives zero for n<m and modulus at least eta afterward, so the eta/2, eta/4 scalar ball excludes the entire orbit. The uniform eta bound makes h as small as required before recursion; continuity of the recursive choices in eta is not needed. Disks centered at alpha exhaust C. The original theorem is valid for every prescribed input f in HC(D), with no unsupported growth assumption on its jets.

The new `independent_algebra.py` corroborates this algebra through ordinary polynomial multiplication, expansion into global coordinates and direct differentiation at nonreal roots: 96 cases, 1,344 recursion steps, root multiplicities 1/2/3/5, tiny and huge complex leading coefficients, huge cross coefficients, exact thresholds, zero targets and very small eta. Three executed altered recurrences fail the actual product barrier. Eighty separate exact radial tests include zero, small, boundary, huge and nonreal targets. These remain finite diagnostics, not proofs of the infinite conclusions.

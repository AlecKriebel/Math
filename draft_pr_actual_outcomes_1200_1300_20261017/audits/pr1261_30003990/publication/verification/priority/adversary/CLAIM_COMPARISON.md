# Claim comparison and route disposition

Target T: for fixed compact convex K subset R^n with interior and fixed polynomial f, the normalized Lebesgue hierarchy over global SOS densities of degree <=2r has 0<=H_r-min_K f<=C_(K,f)/r^2 for every integer r>=1. A single global square is enough. No claim about arbitrary measures, efficient discovery of a minimizer, unrestricted original-objective composition, or positive Theta for every objective is included.

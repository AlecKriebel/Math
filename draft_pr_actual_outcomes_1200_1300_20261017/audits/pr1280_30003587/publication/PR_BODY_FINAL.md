# Strong approximate innerness of finite abelian diagonal actions on Cuntz-Krieger algebras

Every finite abelian diagonal quasi-free action on a simple purely infinite Cuntz-Krieger algebra associated with a finite essential 0-1 matrix is strongly approximately inner, with implementing unitaries in the fixed algebra of the whole action.

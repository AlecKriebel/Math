## Positive prior overlap

The published critical characteristic-function rigidity is directly relevant. Brezis2002, Proposition1 and Corollaries1–2, imply that an integer-valued H¹ᐟ² function on a connected interval is constant almost everywhere. Remark3 explicitly traces the p=2,s=1/2 result to earlier work. Thus this mechanism is established background, including for positive-length E; it does not by itself force capacity-zero when E has length0. The author-hosted relevant pages, not the entire paper, were inspected. [Author paper](https://sites.math.rutgers.edu/~brezis/PUBlications/180.pdf).

Di Nezza–Palatucci–Valdinoci, arXiv1104.4345v3 (2011-11-19), Propositions3.4 and3.8, supplies the Fourier/Gagliardo and flat trace framework. The relevant statements and estimates were inspected; no full-paper audit is claimed. These are standard foundations, not a problem-specific resolution. [Record](https://arxiv.org/abs/1104.4345), [paper](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1104.4345).

Heinonen–Koskela's1998 framework already contains extended Borel densities, pointwise lower-semicontinuous majorants, weighted path-distance potentials, upper-gradient inequalities on every rectifiable curve, and compactness arguments for weighted length. Its inspected university-hosted manuscript uses Proposition2.17; Kallunki2001 cites2.15, so the numbering/version difference is retained. The statements use disjoint CLOSED plates, which excludes direct substitution of open F=R\E. These foundations substantially overlap the proof's preliminary mechanisms. [Publisher identity](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/BF02392747), [inspected manuscript](https://www.math.stonybrook.edu/~schul/past_teaching/mat678-fall11/QCControlledGeometry.pdf).

Bagby's fundamental energy theorem and Kallunki–Shanmugalingam's compact-plate capacity/modulus theorem produce a concrete route beyond background resemblance. The authored deduction smooths a finite-log-energy measure on a zero-length E into an absolutely continuous measure assigning E mass0, then restricts it to compact subsets of F. Signed energies tend to0, so separated condenser capacities, and hence subfamily moduli, become unbounded. It covers zero-length positive-capacity sets and never applies a separated theorem directly to touching plates. Definitions, normalizations and relevant theorem bodies were checked; see the separate deduction for the explicit hypotheses, estimates, cutoff at infinity and remaining full-target boundary. [Bagby official scan](https://www.iumj.indiana.edu/IUMJ/FTDLOAD/1968/17/17017/pdf), [Kallunki–Shanmugalingam official paper](https://www.acadsci.fi/mathematica/Vol26/kallunki.pdf).

The zero-length route is a **mathematical corollary assembled here from older general results**. It is not evidence that this precise assembly or the exact question's full resolution was published earlier. For the entire theorem, the hypothetical finite touching-family density must first yield an actual separating potential with the correct trace values. The accepted submitted proof provides that passage; merely citing the older separated-plate theorem would conceal it. A new alternative assembly should undergo its own adversarial review before use in a manuscript.

## Strongest indirect overlap

The important mechanism is not a touching-plate condenser identity. Hesse's compact-plate theorem is used only for compact E and compact sets F_j exhausting the real complement inside a rectangle. It supplies continuous potentials with a common energy bound. Tail convex combinations converge strongly in W^{1,2}; quasi-everywhere convergence retains the E plate value. The ordinary trace is the characteristic function of E. Critical trace rigidity kills its length, and the zero-trace theorem on the two half-planes then kills E in Sobolev capacity. Smooth small-capacity tests show zero modulus for every E-to-circle curve.

The audit gives every link, including Hesse's image-closure curve convention and the globally-versus-locally-rectifiable bridge. Thus the null-length positive-capacity case is included. The direct application of a compact-plate theorem to F=R\E would be invalid; the exhaustion and strong convergence are essential. See DERIVATION.md for the complete checkable deduction and exact source scopes.

This deduction differs materially from the submitted lower-semicontinuous majorant, pointwise path-distance, and small-circle proof. It shows that the full assertion fits established boundary-capacity theory. Whether the particular combination or the self-contained treatment is a publishable novel contribution remains a separate scholarly assessment.

- Björn–Björn–Shanmugalingam's 2007 characteristic-extension paper gives sufficient geometric conditions, including a strict dimension bound. It does not print the required critical converse for every compact real set.
- The inspected touching-Riesz-condensers paper works in n>=3 and studies signed-measure energy problems. It supplies no verified planar all-curves identification for this target.
- Weak-flatness lower bounds apply to continua crossing prescribed neighborhoods. A positive-capacity compact real set can be totally disconnected. The missing continuum hypothesis cannot be waived.
- Hedberg's 1974 removability and hyperplane-capacity criteria concern different condenser geometries and removability classes. The inspected theorem is not the target's E-to-real-complement assertion; no valid direct reduction was identified.
- Beurling/Dirichlet-space radial-limit results concern harmonic or analytic functions. They do not automatically identify the boundary values of an arbitrary pointwise separating Sobolev representative. That bridge must be proved, as it is in DERIVATION.md.
- Tamrazov's Gonchar condenser problem is a potentially misleading name overlap. Official abstract metadata was obtained, but its original body retrieval timed out; no theorem-level elimination or implication is claimed from the abstract alone.

# Conventions for the supplementary classical deductions

The frozen proofs use extended nonnegative Borel densities. Any restriction of
such a density to a region is interpreted piecewise: for example the Hesse
comparison density equals `rho(z)+epsilon` for `z in Q` and equals zero
otherwise. It is not an arithmetic multiplication of zero by infinity outside
Q. This is the pointwise restriction already intended in the frozen arguments;
the clarification changes no estimate or mathematical verdict.

Endpoint connectors are curves in the whole plane. Their interiors need not
avoid E, F, or the real axis. Endpoints contribute no arclength atoms. The main
note treats both globally rectifiable curves and continuous curves locally
rectifiable off their endpoints with actual endpoint limits. An auxiliary
constant path used in a distance construction has zero cost, including where
the density has an infinite value.

The complete compact-exhaustion/Sobolev deduction and the source-question Riesz
deduction are full-target alternatives. The energy-smoothing deduction is a
zero-length branch plus the outer finite-energy bridge; its positive-length
reduction is supplied separately. These deductions were assembled during this
2026 audit from classical foundations. They do not authenticate an earlier
explicitly printed resolution or clear a firstness/current-openness claim.

All initial and cross-challenge freezes remain unchanged. This is a current
expository addendum, not a retroactive edit to their records.

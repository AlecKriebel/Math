## The result I reviewed

The exact statement and every scope boundary are recorded in
`PRIMARY_SOURCE_CLAIMS.md`.  In condensed form, for binary standard
semi-directed strongly tree-child networks of level at most two on the same
labelled leaf set, with fixed `C/G/T` sectors, strict inheritance, and strict
principal-positive K3P edges, the source-relative regular containment relation
satisfies

`N <= N'  iff  N and N' are ordinary-triangle equivalent  iff  their images
share a regular germ full-dimensional in both`.

Ordinary-triangle equivalence means equality of the labelled reduced tree of
blobs, labelled mixed-graph isomorphism of corresponding nontriangle complete
factors, and coherent redirection of any remaining ordinary triangles.  Hence
there is no proper one-sided containment for this local relation.  The theorem
does not claim equality of whole stochastic images or numerical-parameter
identifiability.

The equivalence, topology-generic identifiability outside a proper
topology-dependent complex Zariski-closed set, and a terminating exact-real
reconstruction procedure also hold on the strict continuous-time cone.  The
algorithm has no claimed bit complexity, conditioning, finite-sample, or
statistical guarantee.  The three ordinary-triangle orientations share an
irreducible quartic `H_14` and a strict smooth rank-14 germ relative to it, not
an ambient rank-15 germ.  Finally, the weak-not-strong construction gives, for
all `n>=3`, nontriangle-equivalent networks with a common strict-CT regular germ
of dimension `6n-3`, proving sharpness of strong tree-childness.

The definitions and theorem lines are
`manuscript/sections/02_main_theorems.tex:4-86`,
`03_conventions_model.tex:5-169`, and `16_scope.tex:3-41`.

## Mathematical correctness

### 1. Cut recovery and the repaired former premise

The repair is mathematically substantive and adequate.  The paper correctly
separates three facts: a true cut has pointwise flattening rank at most four;
a source noncut is detected only generically; and containment within the strong
class forces equality of the two cut sets
(`01_introduction.tex:139-146`; `04_physical_topology.tex:66-159,274-362`).
It no longer asserts the withdrawn arbitrary-network pointwise converse.

The displayed-tree specialization gives a nonzero wrong-quartet determinant.
The balanced boundary reduction preserves enough labelled attachments to
exclude an all-switching displayed split, without invoking cut equality or the
fourteen-orbit result.  The reverse cut inclusion then uses the crossing-hull
and one-component-hull alternatives, compresses noncentral two-boundary
material to a strict physical K3P edge, and invokes only the 204 disclosed
one-active directions.  I found no use of a common bridge tree before it is
proved and no target-open or target-regular assumption hidden in the
source-relative relation.

Independently, I derived the displayed-tree minor
`p0^4 p1^4 p2 p3(1-u^2)` from the Fourier tree formula and counted the complete
808,642 balanced-word universe by a separate closed form.  The full cut
producer/verifier/mutation cone passed in ordinary and optimized modes.  The
former JC theorem is therefore not load-bearing.

## Scope, novelty, and literature positioning

**Pass; no mandatory literature correction.**  A bounded current search of
primary papers and official journal/preprint records found no result that
obviously subsumes the complete K3P directed-containment classification on the
strong level-2 class, the explicit irreducible `H_14` plus strict physical
smooth germ, or the all-`n` weak-class sharpness family.

The manuscript's comparisons are properly separated:

- level-one JC/K2P/K3P identifiability and triangle ambiguity do not give the
  present level-two classification;
- Englander et al. treats the binary triangle-free strongly tree-child level-2
  class under JC, with separate displayed-quartet tools under JC/K2P;
- Holtgrefe et al. and Allman et al. use quartet or concordance-factor data and
  different topology/model assumptions;
- Currie et al.'s complete triangle description is JC semialgebraic geometry,
  not the K3P `H_14` theorem here; and
- the practical four-taxon JC/K2P work addresses finite-data inference, not
  exact K3P containment.

One calibration is essential and already present: the numerical K3P
three-sunlet deficiency/rank-14 phenomenon was computationally foreshadowed by
Gross--Krone--Martin.  The defensible new local claims are exact rank
certification, the explicit quartic, its strict physical germ, and its role in
the global theorem (`01_introduction.tex:35-53`).  Likewise, the comparison
with Brits et al. must retain its convention-translation and
standard-admissibility qualification (`:54-58`).

The detailed claim-by-claim reconciliation and primary-source links are in
`results/LITERATURE_AUDIT.md`.  Same-author JC/K2P/tree--theta materials are
clearly labelled unreviewed companions and were not counted as independent
literature.

## Mechanisms and adversarial checks

1. **Carried target and exact identity** (source line 456). After a channel `s → u`, subtracting the new mark gives residual `x − s`. Therefore the log-factorial difference is exactly `log((x)_t/(x)_s)`. Enabledness of the previous target is automatic. The proof retains channel information where population displacements coincide, discards genuine self-channels before defining jump counts, and proves the reachable marked chain is irreducible. No inference from a displacement to a unique source or target is required.

2. **Scalar envelope** (lines 574–623). For fixed `q > 0`, concavity of `log p + C₀ + qpM` gives the optimizer `p=1` when `M ≥ −1/q`, and `p=−1/(qM)` otherwise. Thus the envelope tends to minus infinity even when an intermediate continuation probability vanishes. The exact episode recursion weights continuation by the designated channel probability `q_k p_k`; using only source probability would have been an error, but the manuscript does not do that. Backward induction is finite, and the fixed positive `q_k` need not be bounded uniformly across all networks or rate vectors.

3. **Normalized logarithms and top complexes** (lines 644–797). Extraction of each nonnegative integer coordinate to a fixed value or divergence is valid in finite dimension. A divergent coordinate with limiting normalized weight zero remains in `I`, which prevents confusing slow divergence with boundedness. The cases exhaust binary complexes: all complexes top; a top complex has two divergent particles; or every top complex has one divergent particle. In the last case a top unary source is enabled everywhere, or a bounded companion appears in a lower terminal, or the signed functional counting top divergent species minus distinct companions vanishes on every complex. Its negative coordinates are fixed along the extracted sequence, so it really diverges to positive infinity on the proposed sequence. This is the decisive finite combinatorial step; I found no missing top configuration under molecularity at most two.

4. **From sequence arguments to one finite exceptional set** (lines 826–930). Coercivity of the residual potential and finiteness of the mark set justify selecting a divergent subsequence if `K` were infinite. The same subsequence has a fixed target and fixed useful terminal. A contradiction to the definition of `K` follows from complete-episode drift tending to minus infinity. A global potential minimizer proves nonemptiness. The selected terminal is chosen before the episode, so the bounded episode endpoint is a stopping time, with no future-dependent choice.

5. **Expected returns and physical time** (lines 933–1090). At finitely many episodes, bounded jumps bound the populations and hence ensure integrability before the supermartingale estimate. Monotone convergence yields a finite expected number of episodes to `K`. The finite trace chain is irreducible, and its excursion-length estimate conditions at each excursion start; no unsupported independence of excursion lengths is used. Projection gives finite mean positive population return count. Nonexplosion is then established from infinitely many independent positive-mean holding times at a recurrent fixed state, before stationary CTMC theory is invoked. The uniform positive lower bound on mass-action exit rates gives finite physical mean return time. Thus the physical-time step is not circular.

The checks include zero complexes, disabled boundary sources, slow divergent coordinates of zero normalized weight, signed invariants, pure double sources, fixed but arbitrarily small positive rate constants, multiple channels with identical displacement, and finite or absorbing components. General multiple-linkage and higher-molecularity claims would break explicitly identified mechanisms and are correctly excluded.

## Follow-up: independent check of a proposed illustration

Checkpoint: 2026-09-14 UTC. Completion estimate: 100% of the bounded proposed-example check. No manuscript edit is recommended without normal editorial review; this is a checked candidate for the research notes.

Consider the directed cycle

`0 --2--> A+B --1--> B+C --1--> C --1--> B --1--> 0`.

The claimed structural facts withstand direct checking: three species, five distinct complexes, one strongly connected linkage class, and molecularity at most two. The displacements of `B+C→C`, `C→B`, and `A+B→B+C` are respectively `(0,−1,0)`, `(0,1,−1)`, and `(−1,0,1)`, which are linearly independent. Stoichiometric rank is three and deficiency is `5−1−3=1`. Neither `A` nor `2A` is a complex.

The population class is **all of N₀³**, not merely an unspecified infinite class. From zero, the enabled words `0→A+B` then `B→0` reach the singleton `A`; `0→A+B→B+C→C` reaches `C`; adding `C→B` reaches `B`. These words lift over any nonnegative residual, so concatenating them constructs any population from zero. Weak reversibility supplies a reverse population path for each enabled step. Consequently all populations communicate. The zero-source channel is always enabled, so none is absorbing.

At a positive deterministic population `(a,b,c)`, the five mass-action fluxes are `(2, ab, bc, c, b)`. Complex balance on this directed cycle requires every flux to equal two. In particular it requires `b=c=2` and `bc=2`, an explicit contradiction. Thus these rates have **no positive complex-balanced equilibrium**. This does not mean there is no positive equilibrium: the ordinary steady-state equations give `(a,b,c)=(3/2,4/3,2)`, with unequal cycle fluxes. The distinction prevents overstating the calculation.

The example is outside the pure-species hypothesis of the preceding Anderson–Cappelletti–Kim theorem. It also lacks the complete inflow/outflow collection and every pure double complex required in the two main structural results of [Anderson–Kim (2018), Theorems 1–2](https://people.math.wisc.edu/~dfanderson/papers/AndersonKimSIAP2018.pdf). The [strongly-endotactic all-outflow corollary, Corollary 7.1](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0304414920303239), does not apply directly because the network lacks `A→0` and `C→0`. It is full-dimensional and has three species, so a result restricted to one-dimensional stoichiometric subspaces or two species would not apply directly either.

This is therefore a clearer illustration beyond the **specific pure-species and complex-balanced sufficient conditions** than the current deficiency-zero two-species motivating example. The latter remains pedagogically useful for delayed boundary drift. **Limits:** I have not excluded every previous general analytical criterion, network transformation, or tailored Lyapunov proof for the candidate. Do not describe it as the first proof of recurrence for this particular network, as escaping all prior methods, or as independently establishing novelty of the full theorem.

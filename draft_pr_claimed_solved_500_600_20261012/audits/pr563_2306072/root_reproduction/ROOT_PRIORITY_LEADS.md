# Complementary root priority screen

This is a partial screen, not the priority verdict. Exact-term searches for nonmonotonic/nonmonotone support points and counterexamples in radial angle returned no relevant primary target argument; noisy unrelated results are not proof of absence. Broader searches located the following concrete leads and source coverage.

[Allu–Pandey2021, full official journal article](https://comptes-rendus.academie-sciences.fr/mathematique/item/10.5802/crmath.181.pdf), DOI10.5802/crmath.181: all nine article pages were screened, and the complete proofs of Theorems5,6 were read for scope. They classify support points in the restricted classes U and G, using each class's closed convex hull. They do not classify support points of the full normalized schlicht S or supply a globally nonmonotone omitted support slit. Thus this is not a verified prior resolution of Problem6.72. No claim to validate every unrelated proof is made. Local source download/hash is recorded separately and the full PDF will not be republished.

[Eremenko's institutional current list](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/uns1.html) was read and its [Hayman progress link](https://www.math.purdue.edu/~eremenko/haym.html) opened. The latter explicitly covers entire/meromorphic-function problems. Neither is a current-open certificate for this univalent-support question; absence from that list has no evidential force here.

Concrete priority leads passed to the separate source family: Ma–Zhang's maximum-radial-angle note DOI10.1016/0022-247X(91)90330-3; forward citation titles “On the behavior of the omitted arc of a support point of the class…” and “Asymptotic behavior of support points for planar curves.” A secondary catalogue was used only to discover the titles. Their mathematical scope needs the primary originals; no absence/exclusion conclusion is made from that record. The source family is also pursuing full Brown and Hibschweiler primary material and later degree-four/coefficient-extremal literature.

Historical-firstness is not established, and current openness is not asserted. Final priority acceptance is pending the complete independent source-family assessment.

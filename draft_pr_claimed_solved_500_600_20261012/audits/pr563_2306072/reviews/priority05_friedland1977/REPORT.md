## Concrete degree-four comparison

Corollary4.2, p149 equation(4.74), derives from the classical global Re a₄≤4 bound:

    Re[x²a₄+6x(1−x)a₃+2(7x²−12x+5)a₂]
       ≤14x²−30x+20, 0≤x≤1.

Koebe is the explicitly displayed global maximizer. The a₂weight becomes negative for5/7<x<1, so it would be wrong to dismiss this paper as merely necessary Schiffer equations or trivial positive coefficient bounds. This is close, actual global allS method work.

The normalized Koebe slit is the negative real ray from−1/4to∞. It has constant θ and β=0, so that explicitly constructed family does not itself witness either target failure.

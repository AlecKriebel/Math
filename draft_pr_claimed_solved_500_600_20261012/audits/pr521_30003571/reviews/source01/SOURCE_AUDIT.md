## Convention test: the r c discrepancy is real under the stated weight

The independent stationary example is in STATIONARY_CONTROL.md. It gives a globally positive product metric on O(2,2), all of whose fiber metrics are Fubini-Study. The defining normalized flow is exactly stationary. Its positive horizontal curvature c is constant along the fiber, A=0, and the logarithm of the determinant L2 integral has base Hessian -c. Published(14) then predicts c_t=(r-1)c, contrary to c_t=0.

This test falsifies a literal use of printed(14) with the O_E(r)-weight announced in Section3.3. It does not falsify the candidate, which derives its equation directly from defining(13). In that convention the scalar equation has coefficient one on Phi, and the candidate's central second-variation operator Delta_F+1-mean is consistent. A different choice of the O_E(1) weight and rescaled time requires a complete conversion; calling the isolated r coefficient a convention without specifying that conversion is insufficient.

The source ambiguity is not a reason to import (14) as an additional hypothesis or to change the candidate's target. The unresolved question explicitly concerns flow(13). It is also not enough to announce a correction of the full general curvature formula based only on this special control: the control establishes inconsistency in the announced notation, while the candidate only needs its directly derived special equation.

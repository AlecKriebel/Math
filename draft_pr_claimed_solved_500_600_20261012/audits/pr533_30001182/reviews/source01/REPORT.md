## 2. Lifting and restriction must not be conflated

The candidate's C4⊂M4 witness makes a mixed folded state downstairs become the restriction of a pure folded state upstairs. It proves that an upstairs extreme state can restrict to a non-extreme state and that the independence truth value changes. It does **not** prove that a downstairs extreme state lacks an extreme extension. Its downstairs mixture has an extreme upstairs extension already.

The polynomial inclusion C[x]⊂C[x,x^-1] supplies the other direction: all-copy evaluation at 0 has no positive extension, by Cauchy–Schwarz and xx^-1=1. This independently verifies literal non-lifting in the algebraic category. The common probability-space marginal obtained by evaluation at 1 is distinct from the nonextendible character; the example answers the unrestricted sets-of-extremes question, not failure to extend a full marginal that is already assumed to extend.

In the C* category, extension exists in the literal direction in item (1). State extension, averaging over finite symmetric groups, weak-* compactness, and an extreme point in the extension fiber give an extreme invariant extension. The fiber is a face because the downstairs invariant state is extreme. This standard deduction is proved in certificate C3. It does not imply ambient invariance, since it does not require restrictions of upstairs extremes to remain extreme. Thus the following three statements are consistent and should be reported separately:

| Statement | Unrestricted algebraic category | Universal unital C* category |
|---|---|---|
| Every extreme exchangeable state lifts extremely under any inclusion | False: Laurent localization obstruction | True: compact face/averaging argument |
| Every upstairs extreme restricts to a downstairs extreme | False in the candidate | False in the candidate |
| The proposed B,C independence is ambient-invariant | False in the candidate | False in the candidate |

## 3. Reconcile the source's classical sentence

The report compares its definition with the “classical (i.e. commutative)” case and invokes the usual product-measure conclusion. That conclusion is valid when the **sequence algebra/coproduct** is commutative, as in the tensor-product setting. It is not valid merely because the one-copy algebra A is commutative: the unrestricted free product of two nontrivial commutative algebras is generally noncommutative.

This is not a speculative distinction. Certificate C4 reconstructs the explicit prior C³ sample-algebra witness from DKW v3 Example 5.2.1, with operators Xi=e0i+ei0 and vacuum vector e0. It is an exchangeable state and is pure because its image contains all finite-rank matrix units. Here the C* image is the norm closure, the unitized compact operators; the algebraic finite matrix-unit span together with scalars is its dense subalgebra. But w(ai²)=1 while w(ai aj ai aj)=0 for i≠j, whereas a classical product state with that marginal would give 1. The same mechanism is in [Crismale–Fidaleo, arXiv:1310.1566](https://arxiv.org/abs/1310.1566), §7, Proposition 7.1 and the Boolean vacuum discussion. No private communication is used as evidence; the public DKW and CF texts contain the construction.

Therefore the source's classical comparison requires a commutative whole-sequence interpretation or a factorization-through-tensor-quotient condition. It cannot silently replace the displayed unrestricted free-product definition. The candidate uses that displayed definition in the algebraic category and its C* analogue, with all exchangeable states allowed, exactly as its proof says. The main example also has commuting B and C downstairs; that does not make arbitrary free-product witness copies commute.

## 6. Repairs, stronger scope, and publication wording

In particular the two-character decomposition proves non-extremality in all exchangeable states, but its components do not share the prescribed mixed full marginal. This is a checked specialization of prior DKW machinery; no exact prior published corollary was found and no novelty claim is made for it. It does not give distinct B,C or scalar intersection and is not required to validate the frozen package. Broadly saying that *every* faithful-only ambient-dependence variant is unresolved would therefore be stronger than this audit supports.

# Independent derivations and adversarial boundary analysis

Second full-package review, PR 533 / 30001182 / OWR-3392-009. Core derivations were made from the current manuscript and frozen author proof before the operator and extension review conclusions were read. The supplementary source certificates were then checked against the actual source hypotheses. This is an AI-assisted independent audit, not human review or a formal proof-assistant certificate.

## Claim and success/failure criteria

For a unital complex *-algebra A, F(A) is the countable unital free product and E(A) consists of all positive normalized states invariant under finite permutations. The relation asks for an extreme member w of E(A) matching the entire B*C law of phi. The claim is an injective state-preserving inclusion A into M4 for which that relation changes from false to true for the same distinct proper coordinate subalgebras B,C, having scalar intersection. A successful refutation could be an alternative extreme downstairs state, failure of positivity/purity upstairs, failure of a longer mixed word, an illicit GNS multiplication, or a missing source hypothesis. None was found. The alternative localization theorem needs absence of every positive extension, and the C* contextual theorem needs existence of an extreme invariant lift for every extreme invariant input.

## All-witness and all-word downstairs obstruction

Write p=(0,0,1,1), q=(0,1,0,1) in A=C4 and phi_t=(1-t)delta_00+t delta_11. For any candidate w and i different from j, exchangeability and the full B*C law give w(p_i)=w(q_j)=w(p_i q_j)=w(q_j p_i)=t. The positive square (p_i-q_j)^*(p_i-q_j) has expectation zero. In algebraic GNS this identifies P_i Omega and Q_j Omega.

This GNS step is legitimate for arbitrary positive algebraic states: if eta(x*x)=0, Cauchy-Schwarz with x and a*a x gives eta(x*a*a x)=0, hence ax is null. Therefore the null space is a left ideal and left multiplication acts on the quotient domain. We identify vectors on that domain, not operators on the whole Hilbert space.

Given two P indices, choose a Q index distinct from both. Given any Q index, choose a different P index. There are infinitely many copies, so all P_i Omega and Q_i Omega equal v. Each generator R is a projection and satisfies Rv=R(R Omega)=R Omega=v. Consequently a nonempty ordered word in these generators sends Omega to v. Its moment is t. The words and the unit span F(A), because 1,p,q,pq span each copy. Linearity forces w=(1-t)chi_0+t chi_1 on every element. For universal C* products, algebraic words are dense and continuous states agree on the closure. For 0<t<1 the two characters are distinct invariant states and this mixture is not extreme. This excludes every possible candidate; it is not merely a test of a preferred mixture.

The algebraic states used for these finite-dimensional copy algebras are compatible with universal C* products. In GNS, a copy element a obeys ||pi(a)[x]||^2 <= ||a||^2||[x]||^2, since ||a||^2 1-a*a has a square root in that copy and positivity applies after multiplication by x. Thus each copy acts boundedly and its representations complete to the universal product.

## State-preserving inclusion, quotient purity and the whole joint law

The diagonal inclusion is visibly unital and injective. The vector u=sqrt(1-t)e00+sqrt(t)e11 has norm one and its vector state restricts to phi_t. The coordinate algebras have dimensions two, their sum has dimension three, and their intersection consists exactly of scalars.

For any surjective unital *-homomorphism f:D->E and pure state eta of E, consider a positive convex decomposition of eta f. If x is in ker f, every component has zero value on x*x, hence on x by Cauchy-Schwarz. Each descends linearly to E; positivity follows by lifting a to z and a*a to z*z. Purity on E forces the components to be identical. This proves purity of the pullback for both algebraic states and C* states.

For a vector state on Md, the projection e=uu* has complementary projection of expectation zero in every component of a convex decomposition. Cauchy-Schwarz kills every block with 1-e on an end. The remaining block eTe is (u*Tu)e and each component has value one on e, so each is exactly the vector state. This is direct algebraic positivity, not an appeal to an unproved classification.

Folding all copies of M4 onto M4 is surjective and unchanged under permutations. Pulling the vector state back therefore gives a pure, exchangeable state, extreme even among all states. For any B*C word the folding image is precisely the original product of its diagonal letters in their original order. It belongs to A and the restricted vector state is phi_t. Equality follows for every word and every linear combination; continuity extends it to the C* product. Every full copy has the stated marginal.

## Algebraic nonextension

For R=C[x] inside S=C[x,x^-1], with both generators self-adjoint, all-copy evaluation at zero is a character and pure by the quotient argument. A positive extension would give eta(X^2)=0 and XY=YX=1 in a single copy. The Gram matrix of X,Y has off-diagonals one and first diagonal zero. Its determinant is -1 for every value of eta(Y^2), contradicting positivity. This is equivalent to Cauchy-Schwarz. Both algebras have the common evaluation-at-one state, but the forbidden character is a different marginal; the theorem does not claim nonextension of a marginal already extended by that inclusion.

## Extreme invariant C* lifting and free-product injection

For an equivariant unital C* inclusion D into E, a smaller invariant state extends by the ordinary state-extension theorem. Average over S_n. Each average extends the given state; a weak-* convergent subnet exists. A fixed finite permutation lies in every sufficiently large S_n, so the limit is invariant. The invariant-extension fiber is nonempty compact and convex. If the input is extreme invariant, restriction of any invariant decomposition shows the fiber is a face. An extreme point of the fiber exists by compact convex theory and is extreme in the larger invariant state space.

To see that the induced universal unital free-product map is injective, start with an arbitrary representation of the smaller product on H. For each copy, decompose its restriction into cyclic reducing representations. Extend each cyclic vector state to the larger copy and take GNS; its original cyclic orbit embeds isometrically as a reducing subspace for the smaller copy. This extends the copy representation to H plus a complement K_i. Choose an infinite cardinal large enough for all the spaces, and pad each K_i by copies of a nonzero representation of the larger algebra to that common dimension. Unitary identifications of complements fix H pointwise. The universal property produces one representation of the larger product extending the given representation on H. Supremum over all smaller representations proves universal-norm equality and injectivity. This is not the generally false claim that an arbitrary representation extends on its original H; the added spaces are essential. Algebraic injection follows independently from alternating tensor normal forms with nested vector-space complements.

Thus independence in the smaller C* ambient implies independence upstairs, while the converse fails in the displayed example. Extreme lifts exist while restrictions of some extreme states lose extremality. The Laurent obstruction belongs to the algebraic category.

## Boundaries and supplementary assertions

At t=0 or 1, the mixture is a character; the obstruction disappears. With coincident B=C allowed, C2 inside M2 gives the same effect with a faithful fair downstairs state. The original four-point state is nonfaithful (mass zero at 01 and 10) and the upper vector state is nonfaithful and nontracial. A tracial state on the M4 free product has normalized trace on each copy; the same cross-law collapse would force expectation of (p-q)^2 to be zero, while the matrix trace gives 1/2. The theorem provides no tracial witness.

Fixing a full marginal but retaining extremality in all E(A) leaves the theorem valid. Changing extremality to the fixed-marginal convex slice changes the answer for this example. Indeed, the forced mixture factors through the quotient h:F(A)->C2 with evaluations 00,11. Any positive decomposition kills ker h by squared-null positivity and factors through h. Prescribed marginal expectation t on p fixes the two quotient weights, so the mixture is extreme even in the set of all states with those full marginals, before imposing exchangeability.

The third-copy assumption matters. With two copies, the characters (first 10, second 01) and (first 01, second 10) are exchanged by swap; their fair mixture gives the desired correlated B*C law. The quotient onto their two evaluations has a unique invariant state. Positivity makes any invariant decomposition factor through that quotient, so this mixture is extreme swap-invariant. Its full marginal differs from phi, as allowed in the original unrestricted definition. The infinite-copy assertion excludes it via the third-index relation. Finite-permutation invariance is equivalent here to arbitrary-permutation invariance on words, since each word involves finitely many indices.

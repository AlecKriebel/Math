# Zero Filling and Persistent Free Subgroups of the Figure-Eight Knot

The four-page research note gives a negative answer to the literal universal
rank-two question on OWR8/2020 p.493. Figure-eight is hyperbolic and has no
finite rational surgery; its zero torus-bundle filling is metabelian and kills
a nontrivial second-derived word in every free rank-two subgroup. Zero is
included and the meridian infinity excluded. The separate search for some
positive hyperbolic example remains unresolved. Classical ingredients are
credited; historical firstness and continued-open status are not asserted.

Priority cannot be fully cleared. The full 2024 cyclic-persistence and 2019
vanishing-elements bodies were not inspected; some longer precursors were
scanned and selectively read. See CURRENT_PRIORITY_ASSESSMENT.json and the
paper for attribution and limits. AI tools were used extensively. This is
unrefereed, with internal AI reviews, no human peer review or formal certification.

## Replay

Extract the archive into a clean directory, keep its exact contents, and run:

    python3 -B run_all.py

Use Python3.10+ with assertions enabled and without PYTHONOPTIMIZE. No packages,
network access, third-party source PDFs or account credentials are required.
Seven registered jobs are actually executed in separate disposable copies.
Every resulting JSON is compared in full, with no normalized fields. The
original release verifier and topology control also rerun historical controls;
these are overlapping replays, not seven independent proofs. The semidirect
child job's complete generated result file is compared, rather than its short
stdout summary. The original extracted archive must remain unchanged.

The driver first checks exact outer inventory, all sizes/hashes, nested seals,
and a separately pinned exact 22-original-file gate. It requires the acquisition
snapshot's literal hash and head. The three original JSON manifests, seven
ordinary checksum files (including nested and early checkpoints), and every
original hash are checked. Ordinary checksum counts may overlap. Do not treat
checksums as authenticity, source classification, mathematical proof or priority.

seals. Their dated statements of pending work and old locator/flag wording are
historical. CORRECTIONS.md, CURRENT_CORRECTIONS.md, the current priority record
and the research note govern this release. In particular, the old source flag

Source locators, versioned read scopes and hashes are included; raw papers,
OCR, rendered pages, scratch/cache trees and account data are excluded. The
producer selects explicit authored paths and rejects lexical/resolved/archive
excluded components, escape, leaf symlinks, private filenames and duplicates.
Checkout ancestors outside this audit do not determine eligibility. Final
publication-adversary reports live in the repository beside this package to
avoid a self-referential archive; see the eventual acceptance guide there.

## PDF rebuild

The PDF is a separate deposit file; this archive contains its standalone TeX
source and build/visual receipts. With Tectonic0.16.9 and cached resources:

    SOURCE_DATE_EPOCH=1792022400 tectonic --only-cached --outdir . zero_filling.tex

Compare with BUILD.json. The fixed timestamp is solely a reproducibility setting,
not discovery or priority evidence. To rerun the archive's exact-inventory driver
after a PDF rebuild, use a separate working copy for the generated PDF. Do not
add build outputs to the exact frozen extraction.

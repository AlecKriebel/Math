The closest challenges:
* Sharifov (2010) optimizes all induced edge weights but permits disconnected
selections. The connected-input three-K3,3 example has relaxed optimum 27,
connected optimum 25, established in the paper. The missing connectivity
constraint cannot be dismissed because the input itself is connected.
* Weighted Connected Matchings (2022) uses the sum of matching-edge weights.
A square can have two perfect matchings with different assigned weights
and identical induced endpoint graph. Its binary-weight bipartite
NP-hardness is related prior work, not an asserted identity with our
unweighted induced-edge objective. A further reduction could be interesting;
none in the inspected source supplies the precise full beta conclusion.
* Havet--Nisse (2019) and Bai--Berczi--Nalam (2026) concern different ear
conventions or total-parity/matroid-join objectives. They are credited,
not counted as publications of an initial odd open prefix result.

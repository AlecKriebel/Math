## Independent reconstruction of the needed EGH chain

The necessary input is only: over C, every homogeneous overideal of a full regular sequence with degrees at least two has one monomial pure-power overideal matching the entire quotient Hilbert function. It is companion Corollary 1.2, obtained from Theorem 1.1. Neither partial-length Artinian reduction nor Betti domination is used by the follow-on note.

**Initial coefficients and low relations.** The skew Laurent construction is a domain, and localization over the independent generator squares makes it finite-dimensional over its center, hence division. Even row size is essential for the parity calculation identifying the center. Distinct rows commute, so the auxiliary square-root forms commute as whole forms. Their relation algebra is finite over the central polynomial variables. For a hypothetical curve of bounded degree in its characteristic set, the bounded Chow-form field of definition loses only a uniformly bounded number of generic coefficient blocks. Retained quadrics have separate simple zero valuations on the image curve; the resulting independent squareclasses force cover degree at least `2^(m−d_*)`, contradicting the bound. This does not assert that arbitrary generic quadrics remain independent over an arbitrary field containing all their coefficients.

**Simple factors and parameters.** Annihilator forms satisfy the linear/quadratic differential equations. A repeated nonlinear factor has gradient image in the characteristic set; restricting a nonconstant gradient map to a suitable plane curve bounds its image degree by `deg(p)(deg(p)−1)`, contradicting the small-curve exclusion. If every factor is repeated linear, logarithmic differentiation puts the ambient gradient in that set. Nonconstant gradient again gives a small curve; constant gradient makes a pure power, violating base-point-freeness. Thus every nonzero annihilator form has a simple factor.

For sums over M blocks, defective Jacobian rank has the advertised incidence bound. No component is contained there when M is large; complete-intersection Cohen–Macaulayness and generic smoothness establish reducedness. The nonzero homogeneous levels have the join homotopy description: the half-plane cover contracts one input factor and identifies the overlap with the product of both unit levels. A simple factor makes the cyclic unit-level cover connected. The compact-support Leray argument then uses the actual gap in fiber rows and real base dimension 2s. No local triviality of the whole nonproper submersion is silently assumed. Removing the singular cone subset respects the stated `M≥2h+4s+10` inequalities. Connected smooth cone gives integrality. Hamm–Lê gives simple connectivity of the smooth projective open from a smooth complete-intersection surface section; the circle-bundle group is abelian and has zero H1. Hurewicz then gives the required higher connectivity.

**Curves, Picard transport and division.** The Kummer valuation calculation works for composite b. The auxiliary cyclic cover has matching ramification indices at 0,1,∞ and a separate ramified value at 2; the normalized fiber product remains connected and makes the deck action étale. Degree, genus and base-point-free section identities give a genuine linearization. Generic degree-zero line bundles have the required two cohomology vanishings; evaluation trivializes their finite pushforward and yields commuting linear matrix polynomials. These matrices alone are not division coefficients.

The degree-one Picard action is free: a fixed line under a nonidentity cyclic subgroup can be linearized by rescaling and would descend with degree divisible by the subgroup order, contradicting degree one. Character circle maps use the integral lift of the abelian covering character and the Abel integral H1 isomorphism; no lift of the whole nonabelian cover is presumed.

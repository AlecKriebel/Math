1. BRANCH GEOMETRY AND FIRST EXHAUSTION (main.tex lines 140–221)
In B_m(u;a,b), u has direct unit connections to a and b. A child's entire
descendant branch has precisely the two external terminals u and a; no
edge jumps to b because original edges span only one or two parent steps.
The branches have disjoint interiors and no edges between them. Eliminating
the two child interiors therefore adds 2*kappa_m to the u-a conductance,
then u is in series with the unit u-b edge. The stated recurrence and
initial kappa_0=1/2 follow, including m=0.

The full L_{m,n} includes both descendant branches at x_0 and one sibling
branch at every x_i for 1<=i<n. Each branch contributes only to the
adjacent spine pair named in the manuscript. Thus A_m=1+2*kappa_m and
C_m=1+kappa_m, including the last pair x_{n-1},x_n, are exact. There is
no extra parallel copy of a terminal edge: every branch explicitly omits
its terminal edge. Skip-one spine edges remain unit edges.

Increasing either parameter preserves all prior vertices and edges. For
a fixed vertex whose distinguished-end ray first meets the spine at x_k,
choose n>k and n at least its depth below the relevant branch root. It
then belongs to L_{n,n}; its original parent/grandparent edges eventually
belong as well. No vertex identification or external terminal is used.
Indeed these L_{m,n} are also induced on their described vertex sets,
although that strengthening is unnecessary for the stated edge exhaustion.

For n=2 the upper suffix conductance is exactly C_m. Each extra upper
triangle produces T_C(t)=C+t/(1+t), so the exponent n-2 is correct.
Let B(C) denote its positive fixed point. Since C>=1 and B(C)>=C,
|T_C^(n-2)(C)-B(C)| <= 4^(-(n-2))*|C-B(C)|
                         <= 4^(-(n-2)).
The final inequality follows from B(C)-C=B(C)/(1+B(C))<1.
This bound is uniform in m and directly proves the diagonal assertion.
As C_m->1+1/sqrt(2), continuity gives B(C_m)->1+sqrt(2).

2. INDUCED EXHAUSTION AND INDICES (main.tex lines 226–281)
S_n consists of the binary descendant tree rooted at v_n to depth 2n.
Each old vertex's depth increases by one at the next stage, while the
cutoff increases by two, proving nesting. If a fixed vertex has depth d
below v_k, its depth below v_n is n-k+d for n>=k; this is <=2n once n is
sufficiently large. Thus the induced graphs really exhaust G.

At v_j the full remaining descendant depth is n+j. An off-spine child
has remaining depth n+j-1. Its reduced edge to v_j is c_{n+j-1}, in
series with its unit grandparent edge to v_{j+1}; adding the spine edge
gives 1+c_{n+j-1}/(1+c_{n+j-1})=a_{n+j}. For v_0 both children contribute,
giving c_n. At v_n the off-spine branch has only v_n as an attachment,
so it is electrically inert; there is no missing attachment to v_{n-1}
because sibling vertices are not adjacent in G. This checks every endpoint
case and the terminal index a_{2n-1}.

The sequence c_m lies in [1,c] with c=1+sqrt(2). Its map has derivative
2/(1+t)^2<=1/2 there, hence |c_m-c|<=sqrt(2)*2^(-m).
For a_m=1+c_{m-1}/(1+c_{m-1}),
|a_m-a|<=sqrt(2)*2^(-(m+1)), so
epsilon_n=sup_{m>=n+1}|a_m-a|<=sqrt(2)*2^(-(n+2)).
All upper-suffix arguments are at least 3/2 and below c. The manuscript's
looser derivative bound 1/4 applies. Since |a_{2n-1}-c|<1, its displayed
estimate is valid with C_0=1:
|u_{1,n}-c| <= (4/3)*epsilon_n + 4^(-(n-2)) -> 0.
No exchange of dependent infinite limits is needed.

4. FREE VERSUS WIRED AND FINAL ALGEBRA
BLPS Proposition 7.1 describes free currents obtained from finite
subnetworks; Proposition 7.2 separately identifies the unconstrained
finite-energy infinite-flow minimum as wired. Its Theorem 4.1 and Theorem
7.8 support the manuscript's unit-conductance edge marginal passage.

The original calculations delete external edges and preserve separate
boundary vertices. Reduction of finite interiors preserves terminal
response and is not wiring the infinite boundary. Cofinal finite
subgraphs give the same free resistance: for each fixed finite induced
ball B_r, eventually B_r is a subgraph of the exhaustion, while every
exhaustion graph lies in some larger ball. Rayleigh bounds give equality
of limits, with the inequalities in the proper direction.

For the square, any finite-support unit flow on J extends by zero to
each sufficiently large finite free exhaustion member of H, preserving
all divergences and energy. Thus R_H^F(o,v)<=R_J(o,v)<=E(theta_v).
This is a free upper bound. It never substitutes a wired resistance.
Whether the displayed currents have their claimed exact divergences and
energy was outside this review; the transfer itself is sound.

R_tree=1/(A+A/(1+A))=(3*sqrt(2)-2)/7,

# Bounded priority assessment, 22 October 2026

No authenticated earlier publication of the full arbitrary-vertex-transitive
expected-free-degree graph-square counterexample was found in the inspected
primary sources. This does not establish worldwide firstness, exhaustive
novelty or continuing openness. The proof and data were already public in
project PR1407; the note is dated 22 October 2026 and is not backdated.

normalization b_F=Edeg_FUSF/2-1. The printed source does not give that formula.

case. No new negative statement for the unimodular subclass is made.

Established ingredients:
- Grandparent graphs and their nonunimodular behavior are prior. Tang's actual
  v2 Section5.2 was read in full, including its path estimates, finite-ball
  resistance comparisons, connectivity and branching proofs. Those results
  and methods are credited. The inspected body does not give the complete
  expected-degree square comparison certified here.
- Kirchhoff/Thomson/Rayleigh and the free/wired exhaustion machinery are
  classical and explicitly credited to the actually read corrected BLPS body.
  This is a new specific comparison if priority holds, not a new general
  electrical or forest principle.
- Pete–Timár already prove component-count dependence for some Cayley graph
  generating sets. Their actual introduction explicitly distinguishes that
  from expected degree, which is group-invariant there. Their nonunimodular
  light-component result is also distinct. These known results are not
  presented as a prior publication of this full expected-degree comparison.
- Gaboriau's harmonic-projection invariant is stated in a transitive
  unimodular setting, not as a tracial invariant for the counterexample.
